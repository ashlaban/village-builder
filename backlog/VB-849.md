# VB-849: Help is carried: the village store's yearly filling is carried to the green at the moving round
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: physical
Phase: implement
Design ref: AGENTS.md, Deciding without Kim (the hard rule); design.md, Goods and the farming year (Help in hunger); backlog/VB-461.md
Goal: sim/help fillStores gives each household's quarter of its surplus to its village's store through the verb give, which settles a gift to a village at once (sim/exchange settleGift, Stores.move): the grain moves from every farmstead to the green with no one carrying it. VB-461 made gifts to a household fetched; this is the other direction.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] At the moving round each giving household's quarter is carried to where the store stands (`v.center`) by one of its members, `TRAVEL.load` a trip, as VB-461's fetch carries a gift home (a journey to the green, or a job kind if the haul of a common field, `sim/commons` brought, is the better fit); it is in the store only as each load arrives.
- [x] A household with no member who can go keeps its quarter (it stays at home, as the part above `HELP.storeTarget` does, VB-457).
- [x] `VB.count.giftUncarried` (VB-461) counts a gift to a village settled at once as well, and `tests/lib/invariants.js` holds it at 0.
- [ ] Numbers before and after: populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 and founders small 7, 42, 30 years: the store's grain at the start of winter, hungry household-days, trips a year to the store, growth.
- [x] Cost: households giving a village-year (about one each) times their trips; no new loop.

Noted by VB-461, 16 November 2026.

Implementation (16 Nov 2026):
- A new journey kind `haul` (`sim/agents` haulGift, haulStop), VB-461's fetch reversed and built on its parts (setOut, takeUp, unfetched, retarget,
  dropJourney): the verb `give` to a village from a household (`sim/commands` hauled) names its hauler (`haulerOf`, the member of 14 or more
  not on the road, split out of `fetcherOf`), sets the grain aside at the giving house (`gift: {to, village, haul, held, parts, stops: [house]}`)
  and he takes `TRAVEL.load` at most a trip to `v.center`, where each load goes into the store as it arrives, and goes again while any lies.
  He takes his meals from his household's store at setting out (journey provisions). With no hauler the verb refuses ("no one of the household
  can carry it") and `fillStores` leaves the quarter at home. A hauler who dies or changes household on his way out leaves what lies at the
  house to its giver (`unfetched`); a load on a dead back is lost as VB-291's (VB-850).
- `VB.count.giftUncarried` counts a gift from a household to a village settled at once as well (`settleGift`); the invariants hold it at 0 and
  check a haul as they check a fetch (its pawn of the giving household, the load bound for the green).
- Tests: `tests/fetch.js` (a quarter of 2 loads and a bit hauled, invariants each half hour; no free member: refused); `tests/exchange.js`
  waits for the haul.

Decisions: the giving household's own member hauls (a household's quarter is its act, every act is a pawn's); the haul is a journey, not a
job kind (the pawn leaves his work and his day as a fetcher does; a common field's haul is not this grain). Households of the village's own
farmstead as the green get no special case. No new loop; no new state field beyond the journey's gift.

Numbers (populous small, 15 years; before = the run's branch as the task began, scratch script sampling every hour; hungry = sum of
`hungryDays` at the end; store = all villages' grain at the first day of winter, mean of 15 years; trips a year = loads carried to the green):
| run | hungry hh-days | store at winter | haul journeys (trips) a year | growth |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| populous 7 | 27 / 42 | 6958 / 6931 | 0 / 20.5 (13.6 counted at the hour) | 1.52% / 1.58% |
| populous 42 | 7 / 22 | 7608 / 7553 | 0 / 32.9 (21.5) | 2.47% / 2.33% |
Hungry household-days rise by 15 each (of over half a million), the store at winter is within 1%, growth moves by 0.06 to 0.14 points either
way. The quarter now takes a day or more to reach the green, and the hauler's days away cost his household's work. Seeds 3 and 11 and founders
small 7 and 42, 30 years, were not taken (the container restarted mid-run, the machine is shared): owed to the collecting task, so that criterion
stays unticked. Households, not only averages: the giving households lose a hauler's days; none lost grain (unc 0).
Cost: one haul journey per giving household a year (about 20 to 33 a year on the small map), each a few short trips; no new loop.
