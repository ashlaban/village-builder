# VB-849: Help is carried: the village store's yearly filling is carried to the green at the moving round
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: physical
Phase: implement
Design ref: AGENTS.md, Deciding without Kim (the hard rule); design.md, Goods and the farming year (Help in hunger); backlog/VB-461.md
Goal: sim/help fillStores gives each household's quarter of its surplus to its village's store through the verb give, which settles a gift to a village at once (sim/exchange settleGift, Stores.move): the grain moves from every farmstead to the green with no one carrying it. VB-461 made gifts to a household fetched; this is the other direction.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] At the moving round each giving household's quarter is carried to where the store stands (`v.center`) by one of its members, `TRAVEL.load` a trip, as VB-461's fetch carries a gift home (a journey to the green, or a job kind if the haul of a common field, `sim/commons` brought, is the better fit); it is in the store only as each load arrives.
- [ ] A household with no member who can go keeps its quarter (it stays at home, as the part above `HELP.storeTarget` does, VB-457).
- [ ] `VB.count.giftUncarried` (VB-461) counts a gift to a village settled at once as well, and `tests/lib/invariants.js` holds it at 0.
- [ ] Numbers before and after: populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 and founders small 7, 42, 30 years: the store's grain at the start of winter, hungry household-days, trips a year to the store, growth.
- [ ] Cost: households giving a village-year (about one each) times their trips; no new loop.
