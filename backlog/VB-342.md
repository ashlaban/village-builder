# VB-342: Rain from a per-seed wind, a light erosion pass, and biomes placed by the rain
Status: needs-review
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Weather, seasons and daylight, Wind and rain
Goal: Give the generator a weather model that places biomes: the first batch of weather (Kim, 11 Nov).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A wind for each seed: a fixed direction, drawn from the seed on a stream of its own, kept as `w.wind` (a unit vector, the way the air moves).
- [x] A light erosion pass on the generator's coarse relief before the light and the tiles read it: stream power with a little creep; the coast does not move.
- [x] Rain carried by the wind: the air loads over the sea, drops a share over land and more where the ground rises, less where it falls; kept per land tile as `w.rain`, 0 to 1.
- [x] Moisture, so biomes, from that rain and the water of valley floors. The old moisture values are dealt out again in the order of wetness, so each biome keeps its share of the land and only its place changes; the biome rule is untouched.
- [x] Rivers by drainage basin (Kim, 11 Nov): the stems that become rivers are taken in the order of the catchment they carry, the trunk of the largest basin first, in place of long courses spread by noise; a source is still a hill, mountain or marsh tile, and the count per island and the spacing stand.
- [x] Lakes where basins pond (Kim, 11 Nov): after erosion, land more than 0.012 under the level its basin fills to before it spills towards the sea becomes lake, in patches of 27 coarse cells (about 3 tiles) or more, where that level is at most 0.12; the lakes the noise made below sea level stay.
- [x] Nothing in play reads the wind or the rain yet (the next batch: fronts, temperature, the harvest factor).
- [x] `tests/climate.js` (npm test): rain on the windward side of a ridge, erosion keeps the coast, moisture by wetness, the same seed the same climate. Invariants for `w.rain` and `w.wind`.
- [x] `tests/golden.json` re-recorded, every entry (every island changed); the tests that leaned on one seed's scene are listed below.

Implementation:
- `src/gen/climate.js` (new, kernel layer, before `gen.js`): `CLIMATE` (its numbers), `wind`, `drainage` and `catchment` on the coarse grid
  (a priority flood with gen/rivers' heap, now exported), `erode`, `rainfall`, `climate`, `moisture`, and `tiles`, which `gen.js` calls.
- `src/gen.js`: one call to `erode` after the elevation is made, one to `tiles` before the biome rule; `w.wind`, `w.rain`. Its line count is
  unchanged (547, the lint baseline).
- Method: the rain is the wind-ordered moisture transport of Red Blob Games' mapgen4, on a square grid walked with the wind so no sort is
  needed; erosion is an implicit stream-power step (stable for any strength), the drainage found afresh every third round because the
  flood is most of the cost.
- Why moisture is dealt out by rank: the biome thresholds, and with them the balance of meadow, forest and heath, were tuned on the noise's
  distribution. Dealing the same values out in a new order moves the biomes without moving their shares.

Rivers by basin (second change of the task, `src/gen/rivers.js`, `trace`):
- Before, every source-biome tile was a candidate, ranked by the length of its course (capped at 15) plus noise up to 10, so which basins
  got a river was largely chance. Now each basin is cut into stems (up the largest tributary at each fork); a stem's source is its
  highest source-biome tile; stems are taken by the catchment at their foot. `RIVERS.longEnough` and `RIVERS.noise` are gone.
- Medium seed 7: 28 sources before and after; river tiles 594 to 714 (courses start higher and follow the trunks); the largest catchment 1,549 in both.
- More river tiles are fewer farmable ones: on the 56 by 40 showcase island all three scenarios now fit at 10 of 110 seeds (12 with the
  climate alone, 18 on `main`). The numbers under Validation above were taken before this change; the two lines below are after it.
