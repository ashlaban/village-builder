# VB-342: Plan: crops and rotations
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: crops
Phase: plan
Design ref: Goods and the farming year; World and scale (Tile scale, Compression); Weather, seasons and daylight; Work: opportunities, plans and tasks (How a holder chooses); Starting scenarios
Goal: The game grows one grain. Kim's answers of 11 November (QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, this task's group) settle the shape of a system of several crops; make it concrete in design.md and write its implement tasks. No code.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `design.md`, Goods and the farming year, gains a section "Crops and rotations" (Proposed, but for the points Kim decided on 11 November, below): the crop table for the first batch (barley, winter rye, fallow, peas) with starting numbers for each: sowing and reaping windows in game days, hours a stage, base yield and seed ratio (barley as today's grain, 100 and 4:1; rye about 5:1, peas about 3:1, to be checked against sources and named), soil effect a crop, shelf life, and its source in the pot. Oats, and the fibre and ale crops (flax, hops, turnips, wheat), are named with the slice that brings them (slice 6 for the chains) and get no numbers.
- [ ] The same section states the rotation (a sequence of at most four steps a field, chosen from the few the culture's template offers), what fallow does (rest only: it restores soil, is not grazed, and does not count against the fields a hand can till), and how each layer of the plan chooses (section 7): the template's default rotation, foresight (what a poor, an ordinary and a good head each do about soil, the mix and the timing of sowing, with the traits it reads) and projects. Skill acts on choices and timing only, never as a multiplier on yield.
- [ ] Seed: seed a crop as a good in subtypes, reserved by the plan, eaten in hunger (VB-282's rule a crop), and how it changes hands (gift, dowry, the fair); a household sows only what it has seed for.
- [ ] The weather interface: what a crop asks (warmth, wetness and frost over its own window, a region), how the yield reads it, and what answers before slice 10 (a draw a year and region from the `weather` stream, in place of the one yearly factor), so that slice 10 replaces the answer and not the crops. Its cost named by Rules for cost 3, 4 and 9: sums kept a region, read by a field as a difference between sowing and reaping, no daily pass over fields.
- [ ] `design.md`, World and scale: the two readings of one grid (a site about 10 m across; land about 100 m across, about 0.87 ha a hex), with the arithmetic that a field's 75 net rations agree with it, and the Compression line "farmland a few hundred times" corrected to about 100 times in area. Starting scenarios: each scenario's preset of crops a village (founders: barley in both, rye in one, peas in the other; how generated villages draw theirs, by their ground).
- [ ] Interactions, each with what the plan decides or leaves to a named task: the work calendar (rye sown in the harvest's autumn; whether threshing may wait for winter; VB-333, VB-334, VB-023), soil and manure (VB-137), yield over time (VB-160, which weighs rotations as a cause), animals and fences (stubble grazing, winter rye standing while herds roam; VB-136, VB-139), the pot's sources, the fair and seed loans (slices 4 and 5), the food balance (VB-048, VB-284), the player's verbs and the plan panel (VB-253, VB-256), and the page's crop colours.
- [ ] Questions that remain go to `QUESTIONS.md`, each with a proposed answer; the implement tasks and a wrap-up are written in the `crops` arch with numbers, criteria, cost and `Waits for:` lines, as `needs-review` or blocked on Kim until Kim has read the plan (Kim, 11 Nov: the tasks come after the plan is read). `design.md`'s change log gains its row.
