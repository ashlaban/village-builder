Goal: No plan stays open with nothing posted and nothing waited for
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A village's plot cleared or roofed with nobody to allot it to (`plot.to` null: sim/expansion's finishStep, sim/cottage's finishCottage) does not stand open for ever: it is allotted at the next moving round to one then waiting, or after a stated time its tiles are released (owner, `st.used`, `w.dev`) with what was built on them accounted for (goal=no plan stays open with nothing posted and nothing waited for; ref=design.md section 7, Holders and plans; backlog/VB-255.md)
- [x] An invariant: no village plot stands cleared with nobody to have it longer than that time; a test on a tiny village (goal=the leak caught where it starts; ref=tests/lib/invariants.js)

Noted by VB-255, 16 November 2026.


## Implementation (VB-646)
- `sim/expansion` `reallot` (run by startPlots at each moving round): a village's plot with `open >= 1`, `to === null` is allotted afresh (`allot`, then
  finishCottage if it was already roofed); else the first round stamps `plot.vacant`, and after `VACANT_YEARS` (2) years `releasePlot` gives the
  ground back (owner null, `st.used`, `w.dev` 0, `costChanged`), and `sim/plans` drops the plan on event `plotReleased`. Cottage stages built and
  the walls' timber are left to the weather (told in the log).
- Invariant (tests/lib/invariants.js): no village plot stands vacant past 3 years (2 waited, 1 to the next round). Test: tests/plots.js (stamp, allot, release).
- Decision: the wait is two moving rounds (principle 19: the rounds in which someone might come for it); unclaimed means no one in demand in the village is open to take it.
- Numbers, populous small, 15 years: seed 7: no plot stood open, before and after. Seed 42: 2 plots, 86 plot-days open, longest 0.98 years, before and after
  identical (they were taken within a year): the leak does not show in 15 years of these seeds; the guard is for when it does.
