# VB-061: Talk: one partner per pawn and hour, with outcomes that pawns remember
Status: done (2026-10-31)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: social
Phase: implement
Design ref: Customs and culture (contacts weighted by time together). Kim, 2026-10-30 (VB-059): the first kind of interaction is talk; interactions happen when pawns meet; a small speech-bubble icon shows over the pair near the camera; talk is not logged as an event. Relationships review, 31 Oct 2026 (`docs/review-relationships.md`, section 7): each pawn picks one partner an hour, by familiarity and liking, rather than every meeting pair talking (which made nearly everyone a close friend and cost time quadratic in a crowd); a talk goes well or badly by the pair's compatibility, and leaves a memory (VB-060); every talk shows a bubble, a chat faint; the selected pawn shows a lens of the people it knows.
Goal: Turn meetings into interactions that build opinion, with partners chosen, and make them visible.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Between 6:00 and 22:00, each pawn of 4 or more, not carrying a load, starts a talk at chance 0.5 an hour, from a random pawn on; a pawn already talking this hour is left out, so each pawn talks at most once an hour.
- [x] Partners: the housemates in the house with it; outdoors, up to 8 pawns drawn from the 30-unit cells around it that `noteContacts` builds (within 30 units in x and y). The partner is drawn by weight (1 + min(tie, 1)) × (1 + opinion), × 2 when both are unmarried and 16–30.
- [x] The talk is a chat, a good talk or cross words by weights 1, 0.15·e^(2c) and 0.08·e^(−2c), c being the pair's compatibility, +0.15 between kin or housemates; a good talk × 2 for two unmarried 16–30s. Both remember it (VB-060), and their tie grows by 0.1; contact hours and the tie model are otherwise unchanged.
- [x] Each talk is recorded on both agents (`a.talk = { with, kind, at, until }`, half a game hour, starting within the half hour) for the page; not logged. `st.talks` counts talks.
- [x] The page draws a bubble over each talking pawn on screen: a faint "…" for a chat, a green ♪ for a good talk, a red ! for cross words, fading in and out. With a pawn selected, a line to each of the 20 people it knows best, to where they are (their house when at home), green for liked, red for disliked, stronger for a closer tie.
- [x] Tests (`tests/relations.js`): two pawns standing together talk within a few hours and both remember it; the tie grows by 0.1 a talk; no talk at night; in a crowded hour each pawn is in one talk at most; invariants hold.
- [x] Cost: populous medium at seed 7 after 2 years, `tests/perf.js`: 87.5 ms per game day with talk, against 65.7 after VB-124 without it and 87.2 before VB-124.
- [x] Reported, populous medium, seed 7, 2 years (`tools/sim.js --report relations`): 8.2 talks per pawn and day; per adult 3.3 friends and 1.8 disliked people outside the household; of all records 3% close friend, 13% friend, 80% neutral, 4% dislikes, 0.1% enemy. Ties: 57 per pawn as before, mean weight 7.8 (7.2 before), median 4.4 (4.3).
- [x] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
Notes / questions:
- The mood modifier "talked with a friend" waits for VB-062.
- Customs calibration (`npm run calibrate`) runs on its own synthetic people and ties, so talk does not change it; in the simulation, ties grow a little faster between those who talk most (mostly housemates). VB-063 reports the effect on customs over long runs.
