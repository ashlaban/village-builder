# VB-253: The player as head: every choice of the plan as a command
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Work: opportunities, plans and tasks (design.md, section 7); docs/review-work.md; design.md section 15 (Parity of command)
Goal: Parity of command (design.md, section 15): every act a pawn may take is the player's to command. A head's acts are the choices of its plan. Give each a verb, which the holder's own rule and the player both go through.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Verbs in `src/sim/commands.js`, each checking the act is possible and returning why not: set a target of the plan (within bounds the verb states: hay 0 to 3 winters, fields 0 to what the hands can work, the woodpile 0 to twice the winter goal); choose the tile of the next field or meadow (claims, reach, the farm's block: the checks of `chooseTile` and `chooseMeadow`, moved into the verb); set the order of the season's routines; choose or drop the project (VB-255's menu; until it lands, the land step only).
- [x] The holder's own rule issues the same verbs with `source: 'pawn'`; the player's go before it, and where the player has made no choice the holder's rule runs, as for any pawn (`tests/commands.js`: only the verbs call the acts they wrap).
- [x] Only the holder may issue them: a player in a pawn that is not the head is refused with the reason. A choice lasts until the season's plan is remade (order), the tile is used (tile) or the player clears it (targets, the project).
- [x] With no player commands the run is unchanged: `tests/golden.json` unchanged. A test plays a head at seed 7: a tile is chosen and the field is made there; a refused tile (claimed, out of reach) returns its reason; a raised hay target is met by the next winter.
- [x] `Sim` API entries for the page (VB-256). `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals.
Cost: none in a run without a player.

- Decisions (Deciding without Kim; none moves a Decided point):
  - The targets the holder's rule holds are values it reads at each review (the template's, `fieldsWanted`), not acts, so the rule issues
    no `target`; the verb sets the head's choice over them (`chosenTargets`, at each review and each season's plan). The rule's one act
    of the plan, the land step, goes through `project`.
  - A choice is the household's plan's: it stays when the player leaves the pawn or the head changes, until cleared (the criterion's words).
  - The head who gives up a project drops its stake with it (a stake is its household's project, the invariant of VB-158); the ground
    stays as the work left it.
  - A raised hay target makes the household rake and mow for it; it does not make the land step want more meadows (`wantsMeadow` reads
    `WANT` times a winter's need, a parameter of its own). Left as it is.
  - The night's plan choices. Covered: the cow wanted (`target` cow: 0 none, 1 wanted while it holds a meadow and has no cattle,
    `cowWanted` in `src/sim/exchange.js`); peat (dug against the woodpile's goal, `targets.wood`, so `target` wood moves it). Left out,

    verb belongs there, design.md "How the head chooses what to sow"); the quern (hewn and ground by `QUERN`'s constants, no choice in the
    plan); the boat and tar (begun by yearly rules of `sim/drying` and `sim/tar`, not by the plan: the head holds them off with `project`
