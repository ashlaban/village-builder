# VB-158: Households stake a claim before they clear, and may contest a weakened claim
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: claims
Phase: implement
Design ref: Claims, ownership and territory (Fields and meadows: Decided; Stakes: Decided 1 Nov). Review: `docs/review-claims.md`.
Goal: A field or meadow project owns its tile from its first hour, so nothing is ever contested between households. Make it a stake that the work turns into ownership. Depends on VB-042 and VB-156.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A field or meadow project stakes an unrealised claim (`why` `field` or `meadow`) on its tile instead of owning it; every work hour on it renews the claim; breaking the ground (or finishing the meadow) realises it as the household's.
- [ ] A stake that lapses (3 years without work) ends the project; a household that dissolves drops its stakes.
- [ ] Other households take in no staked tile. A household that finds no free tile within reach may contest the best tile staked by another household of its village whose claim is below `CLAIMS.contestBelow` = 0.5; the village's Disputed claims custom settles it at once. Under `first` the stake stands. The loser's holder remembers the winner's ("Took our claim", −0.15, cap −0.45, fading over 3 years), and the log tells it.
- [ ] `tests/stakes.js` on a tiny world: a stake becomes owned land when broken; a stake lapses; a contest under each `dispute` option.
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
