# VB-158: Households stake a claim before they clear, and may contest a weakened claim
Status: done
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: claims
Phase: implement
Design ref: Claims, ownership and territory (Fields and meadows: Decided; Stakes: Decided 1 Nov). Review: `docs/review-claims.md`.
Goal: A field or meadow project owns its tile from its first hour, so nothing is ever contested between households. Make it a stake that the work turns into ownership. Depends on VB-042 and VB-156.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A field or meadow project stakes an unrealised claim (`why` `field` or `meadow`) on its tile instead of owning it; every work hour on it renews the claim; breaking the ground (or finishing the meadow) realises it as the household's.
- [x] A stake that lapses (3 years without work) ends the project; a household that dissolves drops its stakes.
- [x] Other households take in no staked tile. A household that finds no free tile within reach may contest the best tile staked by another household of its village whose claim is below `CLAIMS.contestBelow` = 0.5; the village's Disputed claims custom settles it at once. Under `first` the stake stands. The loser's holder remembers the winner's ("Took our claim", −0.15, cap −0.45, fading over 3 years), and the log tells it.
- [x] `tests/stakes.js` on a tiny world: a stake becomes owned land when broken; a stake lapses; a contest under each `dispute` option.
- [x] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
- 2026-11-02 (done): built in `src/sim/stakes.js`. A project stakes its tile (`startProject`, `startMeadow`, and the settlers' first field in `src/sim/settle.js`); each step of work renews it (the kinds' new `worked` hook); `finishBreak` and `finishMeadow` realise it; a lapse ends the project (`lapsedStakes`, after `lapseClaims` each day); `extinct` drops a dissolved household's claims, and `farmToVillage` no longer hands the village a project's tile. A household with no free tile within reach contests the best stake of another household of its village below `CLAIMS.contestBelow`; the contest goes ahead only when the custom gives the tile to the newcomer, so under `first` and `kin` (a fresh tile has no last holder, so kin falls back to the first claim, as for borders) nothing happens and nothing is logged. When it is taken: the loser's project ends and its job is dropped with its hands freed (the kinds' new `project` flag, `src/sim/jobs.js`), its holder remembers the winner's (`claim`, "Took our claim", −0.15, cap −0.45, 3 years), its household leans to the rules that would have kept the tile (`aggrieve`), and the log tells it. New invariants: a project is staked by its household and not owned by it; every stake is its household's project. `tests/stakes.js` covers all of it on a tiny world, with a 40-day run in play.
- Measured before and after with a scratch script on the small map, counting the log lines day by day: populous seed 7, 20 years; founders seeds 7 and 42, 30 years; heirs seed 7, 20 years: population, households, fields and meadows identical; 421 projects, no contest and no lapse. Projects are finished within days, while a stake is contestable only after about 18 months unworked, so the contest stays dormant until something makes projects stall. Golden re-recorded: only `claimSeq` and the claims' `seq` numbers changed (founders and heirs, seeds 7 and 42, years 1–3), as the stakes take sequence numbers; no project stands at a yearly checkpoint.
