# VB-1616: The agenda: the played pawn's queue and open work, each with estimated hours against its deadline
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/ui2/HANDOFF.md; src/sim/estimate.js; src/sim/reasons.js (windowLeft, cause); src/sim/player.js; src/sim/projects.js
Goal: The player sees what a plan costs in hours and days before choosing it (Kim, 24 Nov: making the player's choices matter).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A module `sim/agenda`, a view computed when asked, as `sim/reasons` is: it writes nothing, draws nothing, and no simulation module reads it (a check as in `tests/reasons.js`). On the facade: `Sim.agenda(st)`, null with no played pawn.
- [ ] `queue`: each command of the player's queue in turn: its verb, its job's kind and tile (`site`, or -1), `hours {lo, mid, hi}` (the played pawn's own estimate of the work left with its own hands, `sim/estimate`; principle 14: what is shown of the future is some pawn's estimate), the walk to it where that is cheap to reckon, and `starts` and `ends` in game hours, the commands laid end to end from now (a command under way from its work left); a verb with no hours of work (a walk, going home) by its walk alone.
- [ ] `open`: the open work the played pawn may take and has not queued (from `sim/opportunities`, one entry a job): its key, kind, tile, `hours {lo, mid, hi}`, and `why`, what presses, in the words of `sim/reasons`' `cause` (export it if needed).
- [ ] Deadlines: an entry of either list carries `deadline: {t (game hour), what (a few words)}` or null: the sowing window of its field's crop, the expected frost for reaping and carrying in (as `windowLeft`, the head's estimate, never this year's draw), the haymaking window for mowing; and `verdict`: `in time`, `tight` (the range's `hi` passes the deadline) or `late` (its `mid` does), for a queued entry from its `ends`, for an open one as if begun now.
- [ ] What a step leads to: for a job that is a step of a project making a field (clearing, breaking; `sim/projects`), `then`: the steps left after it before the field can be sown, each with its kind and estimated hours, and the sowing deadline they lead to, with the verdict for the whole chain; so that the first spring's question, whether a field broken now can still be sown, has an answer. If the later steps' hours cannot be estimated before their jobs are posted, use the kinds' norm hours and say so in the entry (`rough: true`).
- [ ] `day`: the working hours left today and the hours of work queued.
- [ ] Cost: computed on a tap, over one household's open jobs; no loop over all pawns or all jobs of the island. No new work in a step.
- [ ] `tests/agenda.js` on a small constructed state, in `tests/suites.js`: two queued jobs lie end to end and their hours equal `sim/estimate`'s; a sowing that cannot end within its window is `late`; open work not queued is listed with its deadline; a field project's step carries `then`; the state is the same before and after a read (as `tests/reasons.js` shows it).
