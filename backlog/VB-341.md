# VB-341: The action bar: the played pawn's acts in three groups, and a tool mode on the map
Status: done (2026-11-11)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: The player and what they see; docs/mockups/action-menu.html (Menu)
Goal: Kim, 11 Nov, of the mock-up: implement its action bar in the game, the Actions button opening the second bar, which works as in the demo.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The dock's Actions (shown while a pawn is played) opens a second bar over the dock, as the mock-up's Menu draft: Myself, Household and
      Village, and Queue (the played pawn's sheet, with the queue's length); pressed again, or Esc with nothing else open, it goes.
- [x] Each group opens a list of acts above the bar: an icon, a name, a one-line note or, in red, why it is refused (shown, never only on
      hover), and a chevron where it leads to a list or a form. Every act is one the simulation has, through `Sim` (no new verb, no new state),
      by its basis (`design.md`, The player and what they see): Myself its own acts (Walk here, Go home, Rest an hour, the moving round's
      wishes, a match offered), Household its household's work by kind and the gift, Village the village's work by kind and the cause.
- [x] An act that needs a tile closes the list and puts the map in a tool mode: a banner under the top bar names the act and what to tap,
      the tiles that take it are marked, a tap queues the command, repeated work stays in hand until Done, and a refused tap says why.
- [x] The gift (who, how much, the floor's warning) is one form in the Household list; its household may also be chosen on the map.
- [x] Nothing the player could do before is lost: what the sheet's list held (work, going, causes, the gift, the round's wishes) is in the
      bar; the offers, the switches and the queue stay in the sheet.
- [x] Touch and mouse: targets of 44 px or more on a phone; both themes, the game's tokens and icon style.
- [x] Tests: jsdom (`tests/smoke.js`) and Chromium (`tests/browser.js`, `tests/browser-mobile.js`) open the bar and the groups, queue a tile
      act through the tool mode and a gift through its form; `tests/golden.json` unchanged.

Implementation:
- Impact note. Page only, but for one read-only field: `sim/opportunities` names each work item's tile (`site`, the job's or the one the kind
  would post at), which the tool marks and a tap matches. It reads `st` and writes through `Sim.queue`, `Sim.wish`, `Sim.answer`,
  `Sim.unqueue`, `Sim.setAuto`, as the sheet did; no new state, no new coupling between systems.
- `src/page/actbar.js` (new, after `page/play`): the bar's state (`barOpen`, `barMenu` {g, view}, `barMsg`), `paintBar`, `openBar`,
  `closeBar`, `menuHTML` (the groups from one call of `Sim.opportunities`, split by basis: work by its holder's type, causes to Village, the
  round's acts and offers to Myself), `actRow`, `workRows` (one row a kind; open at one place it is queued at once, at several the tool), and
  the tool: `startTool`, `endTool`, `toolSites`, `toolTap`, `paintBanner`, `drawToolMarks` (white hexagons in `L.hover`, redrawn when the
  sites or the zoom change). `renderActions` (page/play) calls `renderBar` four times a second with the opportunities it read (none while
  neither the sheet, the list nor the tool is open); the list is not redrawn while the player types in it, and the focused row keeps focus.
- `src/page/gift.js` (new): the gift moved from `page/play` (`renderGive` became `giveHTML`, `queueGift`, `pickGift` the tool's tap), with
  a stepper of five rations and "Add to the queue"; the checks and the floor's warning are as they were.
- `page/play`: the sheet keeps the pawn's head, needs, switches, offers, queue and refusal; `playClick` is the one handler of `data-play`
  buttons for the sheet and the bar; `oppRow` is only the round's read-only row; `walkPick` and `pickWalk` gave way to the tool.
  `page/input`: Actions toggles the bar, `openPanel` keeps it beside the card and the sheet and puts it away for People, Customs and Debug;
  Esc ends the tool, then the list, then the panels and the bar. `page/tileinfo`: a click goes to the tool first; no hover card over the
  list or the tool. `page/icons`: the mock-up's icons (24 px, stroked) and each job kind's.
- CSS (`src/head.html`): `.act-bar`, `.act-menu`, `.act-row`, `.act-form`, `.stepper`, `.act-banner`; with the bar open the panels, the
  play offer and (on a phone) the map's bar rise over it; the toasts go under the banner. The gift's old `.give-row` styles are gone.
- The old card list: its acts moved into the bar (not duplicated): one place for each act, and the sheet becomes the queue the mock-up shows
  beside the map. Lines: `page/play` 400 to 286; `page/actbar` 224 and `page/gift` 92 new.
