# VB-360: Skills: learnt by doing, faster with aptitude, fading unused
Status: done (2026-11-22)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The mill and the loom, the kiln and the quarry); `backlog/VB-185.md`
Goal: A skill a craft and pawn from 0 to 1: a novice works at 60% and a master at 130%; halfway in 400 hours; a tenth lost a year unused, read in closed form. Slice 6's plan (VB-185, 11 Nov), from Kim's answers in the session.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `sim/skills` owns a pawn's skills (`pawn.skills`: for each craft practised `{ level, at }`, the level and the time it was set; `@state`, owner, invariant 0 to 1; at most the crafts of `SKILLS.crafts`, the declared cap: spinning, weaving, smelting, smithing, pottery, building, milling).
- [x] `level(st, p, craft)` reads in closed form: the stored level less a tenth of itself a year since `at` (rule 3: no yearly pass). `practise(st, p, craft, hours)` raises it towards 1 so that 400 hours from 0 reach 0.5 (`SKILLS.half`), the hours counted times the pawn's aptitude stand-in (0.75 to 1.25, from the trait stand-ins of VB-249 until slice 7's traits).
- [x] A craft job's rate is the worker's rate times `0.6 + 0.7 × level` (`SKILLS.novice`, `SKILLS.master` 1.3). Field, wood and herd work have no skill yet (their rates stay as they are; a run changes only where a craft is worked, so `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` until the first craft lands).
- [x] The pawn panel lists the skills above 0.1 with a word (novice, practised, skilled, master).
- [x] Tests: the closed forms (halfway at 400 hours, a tenth a year), a save keeps them. Cost named: two reads a craft job step, nothing daily.
- [x] The numbers that move are reported before and after at the seeds and maps named (Working rules); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
