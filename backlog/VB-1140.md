# VB-1140: The page: a played holder hands the farm on, and a played heir sees the handover it is asked
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: acts
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Succession: Handed on, and taken up); AGENTS.md, Deciding without Kim, 14 to 16
Goal: The player can do on the page what the verb already lets its pawn do: offer its holding to the first in line, and answer a handover it is offered.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A played holder's panel has a control "Hand the farm on", which issues `offer` with the basis `handover` to the first in line (the one `heirRanking`'s asked line names), reason `retirement`; it is shown only when the verb's check passes, and says why not otherwise (no heir of age, an offer open already).
- [ ] The control names who is first in line and, as that pawn's own estimate (principle 14), whether it would take it (the line's `no`), with the next in line if it would not.
- [ ] A played heir asked a handover sees it as it sees a match offered (one presentation for an open offer: principle 15), with its pawn's advice, and answers yes or no; its no passes the offer on, and the page tells who took the farm.
- [ ] `tests/browser*.js` it touches pass; a screenshot under `docs/img/`.
