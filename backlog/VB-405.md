# VB-405: Plan: marriage that answers to scarcity, and how founders grow
Status: needs-review
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: Balance loops and targets (The balance review, 12 November); docs/balance/cards.md; docs/balance/findings.md; People, households and family records (Births, Courting)
Goal: The balance review found that marriage, not hunger, holds the population. The gate already works by delay (couples putting off marriage rose from none to 52 to 122 a decade; hungry households are under 1%), and its setpoint, the means a home must hold before a couple may marry (`MARRIAGE.means`, 1.5 years of food above the seed; VB-153), sets the food ratio late in the game and through it land concentration, the town's food and hunger. The review also found that growth has a ceiling of about 2.4% a year with the game's numbers, that the founders scenario reaches about 1% (10 people become 19 to 69 in 80 years, a third of households single-person, marriages barred by kinship), and that 10 to 1,000 in 150 years needs 3.1%. Decide with Kim how marriage and nursing respond to scarcity and how an island reaches 1,000, then write the tasks.

- [ ] The growth budget is written into the design as the yardstick: the ceiling (2.4% a year) and the brakes that must add up to it at the target, with the human cost of the mix chosen (the review's alternatives: by hunger alone about half of children die; by marriage at 18 alone about half never marry; marriage at 26 costs the fewest lives; a town at 3.3 times the farm's death rate drops life expectancy to 23).
