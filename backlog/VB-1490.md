# VB-1490: A field records its ground
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Making a field); Kim, 23 Nov (marginal land review)
Goal: A field knows the ground it was broken on (meadow, heath, hills, marsh), so later rules can read it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `fd.ground`: the ground a field lies on, a key of `BIOMES` (`src/gen/biomes.js`): `'meadow'`, `'heath'`, `'hills'` or `'marsh'`, read from `w.biome[tile]` when the record is made (the one constructor of VB-1505). It is the ground before the clearing: a tile whose scrub or forest the clearing turns to meadow (`clearScrub`, `src/sim/spread.js`) keeps the ground it had when the project began, so read it at the project's start where the two can differ, and carry it on the plan (plain data).
- [ ] An older save gets `fd.ground` at load from `w.biome[fd.tile]` (a field on a tile whose biome is not farmable reads `'meadow'`); `tests/saves.js` round trip holds.
- [ ] `fd.fert` and `fd.soil` are unchanged in meaning and value: `fert` stays the crop-neutral base, `fertility(w, tile)`. Nothing reads `fd.ground` yet but the invariant and the tests.
- [ ] `@state` line for the field record updated; an invariant in `tests/lib/invariants.js`: every field's `ground` is a farmable ground's key.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`, `tests/lib/probe.js`), with its line in `tests/suites.js`: a hill tile broken gives `ground: 'hills'` and `fert` 0.7; a meadow tile `'meadow'` and 1.
- [ ] No behaviour changes: `node tools/same.js --base REF` same but for the new field (say how it was compared), founders and populous, small, seeds 7 and 42, 5 years.
