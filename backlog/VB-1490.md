# VB-1490: Map overlays: one switch in the map bar, the three overlays behind it
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Claims, ownership and territory (Overlay); none for the switch itself: page only, no design mechanism moves.
Goal: The map bar has one button per overlay (Land L, Bedrock O, Walking time H), each drawn over the others, and no room for more. Give the page one overlay registry and one switch, so that a new overlay is one file and one entry. Page script only: no change under `src/sim` or to `st`.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A page registry (a new `src/page/overlays.js`, in `src/page.json` before the overlays that register): each overlay registers `{id, name, key, icon, group, set(on), refresh(), reset(), keyEl}`; `page/land`, `page/ground` and `page/reach` register and keep their drawing unchanged. `docs/architecture/modules.md`, Adding things, says how to add one.
- [ ] One overlay at a time: choosing one turns the current one off (its layer hidden, its key hidden); choosing the active one turns it off. The hex grid and regions stay separate toggles.
- [ ] The map bar (Kim's mock, variant A revised): in place of `landBtn`, `groundBtn` and `reachBtn`, a split control. Its left half shows the last-used overlay's icon and name, `aria-pressed`, and turns that overlay on or off in one click; its right half (an arrow, `aria-expanded`) folds a second line of the bar open or shut, above the first line. The second line is a `radiogroup` of one icon button per registered overlay, built from the registry, each with `title` "Name (KEY)" and `aria-label`; the active one is pressed. Folded, the bar is as wide as today or narrower.
- [ ] Keys: L, O and H keep their overlays; a registered overlay without a letter gets the next digit 1..9 in registry order; 0 turns the overlay off; [ and ] step to the previous and next overlay. `SHORTCUTS` and `aria-keyshortcuts` follow.
- [ ] The fold state and the last-used overlay are page state (never `st`); the second line starts shut on a narrow screen (`mobileUI`) and the buttons there are 44px on a coarse pointer; the mural theme styles the new control as it does the bar's buttons.
- [ ] The three key panels still show over the minimap, only the active overlay's.
- [ ] A browser check (in `tests/browser.js` or a new `tests/browser-overlays.js` with its line in `tests/suites.js`): each overlay on and off through the second line, through the left half and by key, no console error, never two layers visible; `node tests/smoke.js` passes.
- 23 Nov 2026 (Kim, in session): the overlays and the switch were chosen from a mock; one overlay shows at a time.
- Runs before VB-1491, VB-1492 and VB-1493, which add overlays through the registry.
