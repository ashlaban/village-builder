# VB-707: A debtor's refusal is a breach the village knows, and costs standing
Status: done (2026-11-28)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (1, 13, 16); design.md, Exchange, goodwill and obligations; People, households and family records
Goal: A pawn who refuses an ask from one it owes goodwill to, when it could have met it above its floor, breaks faith: a deed that lowers its standing for some years, and the creditor's memory.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The answer to an offer (VB-690) on the bases gift and help: when the pawn asked owes the asker at least the ask's worth and holds it above its floor, a no is a breach, judged by `propriety`; a negative term of standing with its half-life, and an event of the record.
- [x] The unplayed pawn weighs the breach in its answer (its honour trait sets how much); the played pawn is told before it refuses.
- [x] Numbers before and after, populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years: breaches a village-year, by the refuser's honour; goodwill outstanding; asks met.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim, the session on the social quantities): this is the conversion that makes goodwill worth holding.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim, the session on the social quantities): the duty runs both ways: a lord who refuses a follower in need what he could give commits a breach and loses the follower (VB-708).

## Notes (VB-707)
- Breach = a no to an `ask` (grain) or `help` (hands) by the pawn asked that owes the asker at least the ask's worth (help: hours x levy day rate OUTER.worth/OUTER.hours), could meet it (grain above its floor; spare hands), and whose custom (`norm proper`, food for food) allows. Deed `breach` in society: sum of rations refused, half-life 3 years, term -0.3(1-exp(-sum/20)) (named estimates, untuned); creditor memory `brokeFaith` (-0.3, cap -0.6, 4 years, a TRAITS.breach kind); record `faithBroken`; counter `faithBroken`.
- Unplayed: a debtor who could meet an ask meets it, the allowance aside, when its honour's breach weight (0.5 to 1.5) is at least 1 (honour from 0.5); below that it may refuse. Played: `Offers.warning` (view offer.warning, shown on the offer card) before it answers.
