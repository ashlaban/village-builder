# VB-707: A debtor's refusal is a breach the village knows, and costs standing
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-690, VB-705
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (1, 13, 16); design.md, Exchange, goodwill and obligations; People, households and family records
Goal: A pawn who refuses an ask from one it owes goodwill to, when it could have met it above its floor, breaks faith: a deed that lowers its standing for some years, and the creditor's memory.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The answer to an offer (VB-690) on the bases gift and help: when the pawn asked owes the asker at least the ask's worth and holds it above its floor, a no is a breach, judged by `propriety`; a negative term of standing with its half-life, and an event of the record.
- [ ] The unplayed pawn weighs the breach in its answer (its honour trait sets how much); the played pawn is told before it refuses.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years: breaches a village-year, by the refuser's honour; goodwill outstanding; asks met.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim, the session on the social quantities): this is the conversion that makes goodwill worth holding.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim, the session on the social quantities): the duty runs both ways: a lord who refuses a follower in need what he could give commits a breach and loses the follower (VB-708).
