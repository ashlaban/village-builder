# VB-1499: Map overlays: grazing and wear
Status: done (2026-11-23)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: Animals (grazing, the herds); Paths (wear); page only.
Goal: Two overlays. Grazing: the pressure on each pasture tile (`w.graze`, against the year's scale as `sim/grazing` reads it), so that overgrazed ground shows. Wear: the crossings on foot (`w.wear`) with the stage (`w.worn`: trodden ground, path, lane) told apart, so that the village's ways show at a glance.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Grazing: tiles with pressure banded from light to overgrazed, the band edges read from `sim/grazing`'s rules where it has them; ungrazed land is left as it is.
- [x] Wear: tiles banded by `w.wear` on a ramp, with the three stages of `w.worn` named in the key at the wear they begin at (read from `sim/paths`).
- [x] Registered with the registry of VB-1496 (one new file under `src/page/`, its line in `src/page.json`, an icon in `src/page/icons.js` in both icon sets, a key panel in `src/body.html`); no change to the switch itself. It gets the next free digit key.
- [x] Page script only: it reads `w` and `st` and writes neither; a sequential quantity uses one perceptually ordered ramp, readable in both themes over the map, and the key names the ends in the game's words and units.
- [x] Drawn again only when what it shows changed (as `page/ground` looks at most twice a second); a new world wipes it.
- [x] The browser check of VB-1496 covers it: on, off, no console error.
- 23 Nov 2026 (Kim, in session): the overlays and the switch were chosen from a mock; one overlay shows at a time.
- Grazing (`page/grazing`, group nature): five bands of `grazeOf` in cow-hours, edges from the rules that read the pressure: 1 (least shown), GRAZE.keepsOpen and GRAZE.thins (sim/spread), HERD.herding.downAt and twice it (sim/grazing milkFactor: overgrazed). RdPu ramp: a yellow one washed into the grass.
- Wear (`page/wear`, group ways): six bands of `w.wear` (1, a quarter of trodden, PATHS.at's three stages, 4 × lane); a tile is drawn in its `w.worn` stage's band at the least, since a stage holds until the count falls to PATHS.keep of its edge. Viridis ramp; a stage's row gives the crossings a day that keep it (at × ln 2 / halfLifeDays).
- Keys: five digit-less overlays and four digits, so the registry hands out 6 to 9 then Y, U, I (the free letters under them), the first not taken: the one change to the switch, asked for by the run.
- Both key panels reuse the reach key's style: no CSS added. Checked by screenshot at year 7 (founders, tiny, seed 7), both themes, kept outside the repository.
