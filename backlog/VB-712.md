# VB-712: Custom: herding by each farm or by turns for the village (byahjord)
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-710
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: animals
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Goods, production and animals: Animals, Customs of the animals; docs/plans/VB-141.md (section 2.4); design.md, Custom dimensions
Goal: A village may herd by turns of its farms, weighted by the head each keeps (Kim, 16 Nov); a hired herdsman comes later, as a third option. The herder fetches each herd from its yard and leads it home: nothing teleports.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A `DIMS` entry `herding` in `src/culture.js`: label "Herding", options `farm` ("Each farm its own") and `turns` ("By turns for the village"), malleability `m` 0.3, `farm` in every archetype; `SAID` phrases; interests in `sim/leanings` (a household with a herd and no child of 8 to 14 leans to `turns`; one with many head and children to spare leans to `farm`).
- [ ] Under `turns`, in a village with `HERD.rota.least` (3) or more farms that keep a herd, one farm a day posts the `herd` task, for all the village's herds; the others post none. The turn (`village.rota`: the farm whose turn it is and the days it has left; plain data, written only by `sim/grazing`) passes in the order of the farms, a farm herding one day for each `HERD.rota.per` (2) cow-equivalents it keeps, and at least one. With fewer farms, or when the farm on turn has nobody of `minAge`, each farm herds its own that day, as under `farm`.
- [ ] Nothing teleports: the herder walks to each yard and leads its herd out, keeps the herds on one pasture of the village's outland (chosen by `pasture`'s rule from the green, not from one house), and leads each home in the evening. A herd is out and herded only from when he fetched it; one not fetched is unherded (half the milk).
- [ ] Milk, strays and the bite of VB-709 read the shared pasture as they read a household's; the stints of VB-710 hold (the head beyond a stint stay home).
- [ ] State: `village.rota` gets its `@state` line, its owner, its place in saves and its invariants (the farm is a live farm of the village with a herd, or none; days left at least 0). A check on a small constructed state with its line in `tests/suites.js`: four farms keeping 4, 2, 2 and 0 cow-equivalents herd 2, 1, 1 and 0 days in a cycle of 4; the herds are fetched and brought home; a farm with nobody able falls back.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (`turns` against `farm`, forced), at populous small seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 (15 years): herder-hours a village-day and a year, the hours of children of 8 to 14 freed and what they did instead, milk a cow, the share of herd-days with `f < 1` (one pasture is more crowded than many), cattle in a crop, hungry days a household (the distribution); founders tiny seed 7 as the case of villages under 3 farms (unchanged). `HERD.rota.per` tried in at most five short runs.
- [ ] Cost (Rules for cost 9): villages herding by turns (some tens at `medium-ref`) × daily × one route through its farms' yards (one small bfs, kept for the day) and one `pasture` choice a week a village in place of one a household: a declared counter `rotaVillages`. Herders fall from one a household-day to one a village-day. Measured on a late save, counters reported.
- [ ] A hired herdsman is not built (Kim, 16 Nov: later, as a third option).
