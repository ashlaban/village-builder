# VB-455: Refactor: split sim/records: the event log (sim/eventlog) and the register of persons and households
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Design ref: none (code structure). docs/architecture/target-v2.md, sections 3.1 and 4.1 (the record), 4.4 (Membership); backlog/VB-413.md, VB-414, VB-415
Goal: `sim/records` (752 lines, 52 exports, the widest module after `sim/rules`) holds two things that share a name: the record of events (`record`, `log`, `personEvent`, the yearly fold; about 230 lines) and the register of persons and households (`person`, `marry`, `makeHousehold`, membership, the live indexes, `st.idx`, the kin queries; about 450 lines). The seam is clean: the event half calls one function of the register (`settlementIdOf`), and the register calls nothing of the event half. Their readers differ too: `log` is plumbing that 34 modules call; the register is the social core. Move the event half to a new module, `sim/eventlog` (Kim chose the name, 15 November).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A new module `sim/eventlog` (`@sys` and `@state` lines; kernel in `src/layers.json`; in `src/manifest.json` right after `sim/records`, whose `settlementIdOf` it reads) holds, moved verbatim: `record`, `log`, `personEvent`, `lifeEvent`, `indexEvents`, `atHousehold`, `atFarm`, `startFold`, `foldHistory`, `HISTORY` and what only they use (`FIELDS`, the fold's tables). It owns `st.record`, `st.recordYears`, `st.recordFold` and `pawn.events` in `src/owners.json`.
- [ ] Every caller reads these from `sim/eventlog`: only dependency lines change. `sim/records` exports none of them, and nothing is exported twice to spare a caller.
- [ ] `sim/records` keeps the register and is under 700 lines, so it leaves `tests/lint-baseline.json`; `tests/width-baseline.json` falls for it (52 to about 40) and holds `sim/eventlog` at its count if over 15. Its `@sys` line says what it now is.
- [ ] `practice` and `farmPractice` (the custom lookups) and `tieOf` and `scaleOf` (familiarity, read) stay where they are: they are VB-231's and VB-430's to move.
- [ ] The documents follow: `AGENTS.md` (Conventions: events are written by `log` and `personEvent`, now of `src/sim/eventlog.js`), `docs/architecture/modules.md`, `docs/architecture/ownership.md`; `tests/record.js` and the tools that name the module.
- [ ] Refactor rules of `BACKLOG.md`: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`, its line in the PR; the PR says what fell.
