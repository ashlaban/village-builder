# VB-1121: The player is the line: it goes on as the successor the customs name, at its pawn's death
Status: done (2026-11-19)
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Vision and scope (Player role; Which pawn); The player and what they see (After a death); Starting a game (Whom to play); Inheritance (Succession; Handover and shares)
Goal: The player follows its line by the customs of its village, from each death to the successor.
Decided (Kim, 17 and 18 November 2026, after the playtests of 16 November):
- The player is the line, not the person and not the farm. It changes pawn only at its pawn's death (Kim, 18 November; was, 17 November: at the handover): a pawn whose farm passes on in its lifetime is played on, retired.
- The successor is the one the inheritance customs name (patrilineal, matrilineal or other, as the village's customs have it), worked out beforehand and not chosen from a list.
- Succession applies to every played pawn, landed or not.
- The line, not the holding: where the farm goes to the widow under the customs, the successor is still the child.
- A successor who is a child is played as a child; a child may be played wherever the player takes a pawn, the same rule in each place.
- With no successor the game ends, and the player is shown the end-game summary.
- A successor who refuses the inheritance is still the successor: the player plays it without the farm.
- At the played pawn's death the game pauses and shows whom the player now plays.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] One function of the simulation names a pawn's successor, `successorOf(st, id)`, for any pawn, with or without a holding: the first of its line by the inheritance customs in force in its village (who can inherit, which child, a daughter's place, an heir to their own parents' farm), read from the rules `heirRanking` (`src/sim/heirs.js`) ranks by, but for the person and not for a household: the widow's right to the farm does not make her the successor, and a successor's own refusal, a larger farm of its own or being passed over for the holding (VB-824) does not unmake it. The notes say how far the line reaches when a pawn has no child (by the customs' order: the same ranking a farm of its would follow), and where it ends.
- [x] It is worked out beforehand, whenever asked: the played pawn's sheet shows "After you: Jon Gunnarsson, your eldest son, by Asklunda's custom", and a change of it (a birth, a death, a change of custom) is told.
- [x] In life nothing changes whom the player plays: when the played pawn's farm passes on (the heir takes over on marrying, the holder at 60, VB-824's offer when it lands), the player plays on as the same pawn, retired on the farm. A test holds it.
- [x] At the played pawn's death the game pauses and shows whom the player now plays: its successor. The list to choose from (`#succession`, `src/page/play.js`; `successors`, `src/sim/opportunities.js`) goes, and `Sim.succeed` takes the successor.
- [x] A child successor is played as a child: its sheet says what a child may do, who holds the farm meanwhile (the widow for life, kin in trust) and until when. One rule of who may be played, read wherever the player takes a pawn: Whom to play at the start (`src/page/choose.js`, today "Children are shown, and cannot be played") follows it too, so a child may be chosen there.
- [x] With no successor the game ends: the run stops, the break in the line is recorded, and the end-game summary is shown (the line's pawns in order with their years, the farms held, the people and villages of the island at the start and at the end, from the record and the state; no invented score), with the way back to the main menu and to watching the island on.
