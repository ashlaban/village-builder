# VB-1121: The player is the line: it goes on as the heir the customs name, at a handover and at a death
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Vision and scope (Player role; Which pawn); The player and what they see (After a death); Inheritance (Succession; Handover and shares)
Goal: The player follows its line by the customs of its village, and never sits out a retirement.
Decided (the session of 17 November 2026, after the playtests of 16 November): the player is the line, not the person; it changes pawn only when the holding passes; the heir is the one the inheritance customs name, patrilineal, matrilineal or other as the village's customs have it, and not chosen from a list; at the played pawn's death the game pauses and shows whom the player now plays.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] One function of the simulation names the played pawn's successor, `lineHeir(st, id)`: the first of `heirRanking` (`src/sim/heirs.js`) for the holding it holds, by the customs in force in its village (who can inherit, which child, a daughter's place, the widow, how many heirs), as the farm itself will pass. It is worked out beforehand, whenever asked: the played pawn's sheet shows "After you: Jon Gunnarsson, your eldest son, by Asklunda's custom", and says when a change of custom or a birth changes it.
- [ ] At a handover in life (the heir takes over on marrying, the holder at 60, or the holder's own handing on: `retire`, and VB-824's offer when it lands), the player goes on as the new holder in that step. The game pauses, the camera goes to the new pawn, and one notice says what happened and whom the player now plays, with that pawn's position as Whom to play shows it. The old pawn lives on, unplayed.
- [ ] At the played pawn's death the same: the game pauses and shows whom the player now plays, the heir the customs name. The list of successors to choose from (`#succession`, `src/page/play.js`; `successors`, `src/sim/opportunities.js`) goes; `Sim.succeed` takes the line's heir.
- [ ] Decide, and write in the notes with the reason, each case the decision leaves open: (a) a played pawn that holds nothing (a servant, a younger child, a wife): the heir the same customs would name for it (its spouse's holding, else its children by the custom's order, else its parents' line); (b) the widow who keeps the farm for life: she is the holder, so the player goes on as her, then as the heir after her; (c) an heir who is a child, whose farm kin hold in trust: the player goes on as the child, and the sheet says who holds for it and until when; (d) no heir at all: the break in the line is recorded, and only then is any adult offered, as Which pawn has it today; (e) the heir who renounces or is passed over (VB-824): the player follows the holding to the one who takes it.
- [ ] `design.md` is brought in line by this task (a design change decided in the session): Vision and scope, Player role ("the player goes on with another: ... the heir") and Which pawn (Decided, 3 Nov: "the player chooses among the pawn's kin ... the heir first"), and The player and what they see, After a death (Proposed), each with the date and what it was.
- [ ] Checks on small constructed states (`tests/tiny.js`): a played holder whose heir marries under "when the heir marries" is the heir after the step, paused; a played holder dies under "daughters first" and the player is the eldest daughter; the widow's case; no heir. A run with no player is unchanged (`tools/same.js` prints `same`).
- [ ] The skip stops at the change (it is a pause already), and `st.player` stays plain data (`tests/plaindata.js`, `tests/saves.js`).

Notes / questions:
- 16 Nov 2026 (playtest, founders small seed 7, 80 years in the page): Ingeborg Torstensdotter handed Östergården to her son Jon when he married in year 33, under "when the heir marries", and the player stayed in her, retired, for 14 years with nothing to decide, until she died at 74. After each death the page offered a list (her, sons of 10 and 7, three friends), with nothing to say what each choice meant. The headless playtest saw the same shape for a lone heir: 40 years unmarried with nothing to decide.
- Weighed and not taken, by the decision above: a free switch within the line at any time (it empties a death of meaning); staying in the retired pawn with a richer retirement (a design pass of its own; it may still come, for the pawn nobody plays); the choice among kin after a death.
- Principle 16: whom the player inhabits is not an act of a pawn, so no verb is added; `heirRanking` is every farm's rule. Close calls for the inbox: (a) and (c) above.
- One at a time with VB-824 (succession offered and taken up), which changes how a handover is made and by whom it is answered; whichever lands second builds on the other. VB-1105 keeps the labels a notice shares with Whom to play.
