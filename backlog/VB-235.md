# VB-235: The clock counts whole six-minute ticks
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: none (code structure). `AGENTS.md`, Conventions (Determinism; Time is in game hours).
Goal: `Sim.advance` (`src/sim/clock.js`) adds 0.1 to the float `st.t` and finds the hour as `Math.floor(st.t + 1e-9)`. The float drifts past that tolerance in year 2, and `left -= step` drifts with it, so the same seed gives different runs by the size of the chunks `advance` is given. Keep an integer count of six-minute ticks as the clock's source of truth and derive the hour, the day and `st.t` from it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `st.tick`, an integer count of six-minute steps since the run began, is the clock's source of truth; `st.t` is written in one place, from it (`st.tick / TICKS`, plus the part of a step the page has asked for so far). An hour begins when `st.tick % TICKS === 0`; nothing finds the hour, the tick or the year by adding a tolerance to a float: the six sites go (`clock.js:89-93`, `agents.js:180`, `stores.js:8`, `page/animate.js:69`). An animal's age in years is likewise compared from whole ticks: the four tolerances of `1e-6` in `sim/herds` go (`herds.js:75, 87, 105, 293`; Kim, 10 Nov). The eight tolerances on quantities (`help`, `hunger`, `claims`, `exchange`, `fields`) are not this task's.
- [ ] `Sim.advance(st, hours)` with a whole number of ticks runs exactly that many full steps. A test in `npm test` holds that populous, seed 7, tiny map, 2 years is the same state (the canonical hash of `tests/lib/canon.js`) by chunks of a year, a day, an hour and 0.1 hours, and that every `onHour` runs in the step that reaches its hour, over 60 simulated years of the clock's arithmetic alone.
- [ ] The page is unchanged in what it asks for: `speed.js` still passes frame-sized hours, and a part of a step still moves the agents (the Flag below is not solved here).
- [ ] A save keeps `st.tick`; a save without it loads with `st.tick = Math.round(st.t * TICKS)`. Invariant in `tests/lib/invariants.js`: `st.tick` is a non-negative integer and `st.t` agrees with it to within one step.
- [ ] `tests/golden.json` and `tests/counters.json` are re-recorded, and the PR lists the changed entries. Numbers before and after in the PR and here: populous and founders, small map, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years (`tools/sim.js`, `tools/balance.js`): living, households, deaths by hunger, grain per household at the lowest decile, so that households and not only the mean are compared; `node tools/bands.js` reports every band as before (in or out).
- [ ] Since the golden file is re-recorded here, the code kept only to give the old results goes in the same PR, each in its own commit with its changed entries listed: the binary search for the cyclic start in `talkHour` (`relations.js:219-222`; start from a random place in `st.living`), "the original order and random draws" in the moving round (`lifecycle.js:218`), "the original multiplication, including its floating-point rounding" in `Stores.villageDay` (`stores.js:83`), and `SPREAD` in `dailyTies` (`relations.js:31`; `(day + id) % every`). `registry.js:51` stays: a fixed order of contributions is the rule, not a fossil.
- [ ] `npm run verify` passes; `node tests/perf.js --counter-cost` unchanged within noise.
Notes:
- Flag: in the page a step is as long as a frame (`src/page/speed.js:25` passes `dt * speed / HOUR_SECONDS`), so the frame rate changes the run: populous, seed 7, 96 by 64, 3 years in chunks of 0.0731 hours ends with 171 living against 167 in whole steps. A seed reported from the page cannot be replayed headless, and it sits badly with Rules for cost, 7 (only the page interpolates). Whole steps in the page need the page to draw agents between two steps (at 1× a step lasts 0.2 s; `page/animate.js` draws `a.x`, `a.y` as they are). Kim decided on 10 November: the page runs whole steps and draws pawns between them, VB-244, after this one.
- 10 Nov 2026, from the code review of that day (places where the code is clever and a simpler way is better). Measured at `fb35e75`:
  - The arithmetic of `advance` alone (`t += 0.1`): `t` is off by 2.4e-8 hours at year 10, 3.9e-7 at year 40, 8.8e-7 at year 80. `Math.floor(t + 1e-9)` first misses an hour at year 2.00; over 60 years about 52,000 of 69,120 hours (75%) run `onHour` one step late. `Math.floor(t * TICKS + 1e-4)` never fails in 80 years, so the two clocks in one loop disagree: the autumn meetings' stages go by ticks, `movingRound` at noon by hours.
  - Populous, seed 7, 96 by 64, 3 years, the same seed by chunk: `advance(YEAR)` 3 times gives 3,785 record entries, 11,103.8 grain in the live households and 326 open jobs; `advance(DAY)` 144 times 3,752, 10,875.7 and 324; `advance(1)` 3,456 times 3,783, 10,839.1 and 326. All end with 167 living. Chunks of 1 and of 0.1 give the same state.
