# VB-350: Rotations: a field's courses, staggered over the farm, and the village's usual one
Status: done (2026-11-17)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: crops
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods and the farming year, Crops and rotations (design.md, section 8)
Goal: The rotation (The rotation), with the simplest head: every holder holds its village's usual rotation where it has the seed. A village with barley alone farms every year, as now.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `ROTATIONS` as rules: every year (barley); two courses (barley, fallow); three courses (fallow, rye, barley); barley and peas by turns; peas, rye, barley. A field holds `rotation` (a key) and `step` (`@state`, invariants: a known key, a step inside it, the field's crop the step's course).
- [x] When a field's crop is in, or its fallow year ends, it moves to the next step; a new field takes the step fewest of the farm's fields are at. A household holds only a rotation it has a field's seed for, every crop of it; short of a crop's seed, the field at that course lies fallow and the holder falls back to a rotation it has seed for (logged once, `atFarm`).
- [x] The plan reserves, of each crop, the seed for the fields its rotation sows with it next. `fieldsWanted` counts sown fields against the hands (`FIELDS.perHand`) and adds the fallow ones on top.
- [x] The village's usual rotation (`v.rotation`, `@state`, owner: say which module, by target-v2 section 4): the one most of its farms hold, updated at the autumn meeting and at no other time (Kim, 11 Nov), as a step of the meeting's work, a village a step; a new household starts with it if it has the seed. In this task every holder holds it.
- [x] Barley-only villages: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`. Numbers, populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years, with every village given rye by a test switch: fields a farm, rations by crop (`tools/balance.js` reports by crop), hungry household-years, soil; and whether households, not only averages, are better off. (`same.js` cannot print `same` once a state field is added: it reports 1,724 leaves added, `rotation: "every"` and `step: 0` on every field and a village's `rotation`, and none changed or gone; see the notes.)
Cost: a field a year at its turn; a village a year at its meeting, over its farms (bounded by the village's households). No daily work.
Notes / questions:
- 11 Nov 2026: written by the plan VB-345. Kim read and accepted the plan the same day (`QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, VB-345): its numbers are `design.md`'s starting values, Agreed.
- 17 Nov 2026, implementation (the run of 17 November; base 984607d):
  - `ROTATIONS` in `sim/rules` (a plain table, as `CROPS`): `every`, `two`, `three`, `peas` (barley and peas by turns), `peasRye`, each
    `{courses, label}`. Every field holds `rotation` and `step` (`sim/layout`, `sim/fields` broken; older saves get barley every year).
  - The steps (`sim/fields`): a field moves on when its last load is taken up (`hauled`), when its crop is lost (frost, left standing),
    when its sowing window closes with it unsown (on the day after, a field a farm on two days a year), and a fallow course on the
    autumn's first day after the summer it stood fallow (its rest mark), so that rye is sown on it that autumn. Barley every year never
    moves. The haul task carries its crop, so the last loads are booked as the crop reaped (`sim/kinds`, `sim/lying`).
  - The holder (`holdRotation`, at each review of its plan, before the fields wanted): it takes the village's usual rotation when it has
    every crop of it, a field's seed in store or a field bearing it; short of a crop's seed when a field at that course is in its window,
    it falls back on the rotation without that crop that keeps most of its crops (then one with fallow), logged once at the farm; a change
    waits while a field stands under a crop the new rotation has not. Fields are staggered again on a change (`restagger`), and a new
    field takes the step fewest fields are at, the course sown soonest among them (`placeField`).
  - Seed (`sim/hunger` seedNeed, `sim/plans` reserveSeed): of each crop of the rotation, the fields that sow it next (a fallow field's
    next sown course); and a field's seed of a crop no field bears or sows next. Barley's seed is the plan's target, as before; rye's and
    peas' the crop's own.
  - `fieldsWanted(st, hh, workable, bias)`: the sown fields by the mean net of the rotation's sown courses, times the head's bias, capped
    by the hands, then the fallow courses on top in their share (three courses: half as many again). The head's `headFields` became this
    one rule. The village overview's hands warning counts sown fields.
  - `v.rotation` (`@state`): owned by `sim/fields` (target-v2 4.3: a reading of its farms' fields), written only by `usualRotation`,
    which `sim/meetings` calls in each village's meeting step; the most held among its occupied farms, a tie keeping the one it has. A
    settlers' new village starts with its mother village's.
  - The test switch: `Sim.seed(w, seed, scenario, null, {rye: true})`, `--rye` in `tools/sim.js` and `tools/balance.js` (`sim/scenarios`
    giveRye): every village in three courses, each farm's fields staggered at the start, the rye course sown the autumn before and
    standing, a field's seed of rye for each fallow field taken from the barley its household brought. Founders' farms have no field at
    the start, so the switch leaves founders as it was. `tools/balance.js` reports grain by crop (the harvest's ledger entries name a
    crop other than barley) and fallow fields.
  - Cost: the review's rotation check is a few lookups over the farm's fields; the seed rule walks a farm's fields for each crop of its
    rotation when read (allocation-free; barley every year skips it); the meeting walks a village's farms once a year (`rotationWalk`,
    a new counter); `unsown` walks the fields on two days a year. Nothing daily, nothing over pairs.
