# VB-350: Rotations: a field's courses, staggered over the farm, and the village's usual one
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-349
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: crops
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods and the farming year, Crops and rotations (design.md, section 8)
Goal: The rotation (The rotation), with the simplest head: every holder holds its village's usual rotation where it has the seed. A village with barley alone farms every year, as now.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `ROTATIONS` as rules: every year (barley); two courses (barley, fallow); three courses (fallow, rye, barley); barley and peas by turns; peas, rye, barley. A field holds `rotation` (a key) and `step` (`@state`, invariants: a known key, a step inside it, the field's crop the step's course).
- [ ] When a field's crop is in, or its fallow year ends, it moves to the next step; a new field takes the step fewest of the farm's fields are at. A household holds only a rotation it has a field's seed for, every crop of it; short of a crop's seed, the field at that course lies fallow and the holder falls back to a rotation it has seed for (logged once, `atFarm`).
- [ ] The plan reserves, of each crop, the seed for the fields its rotation sows with it next. `fieldsWanted` counts sown fields against the hands (`FIELDS.perHand`) and adds the fallow ones on top.
- [ ] The village's usual rotation (`v.rotation`, `@state`, owner: say which module, by target-v2 section 4): the one most of its farms hold, updated at the autumn meeting and at no other time (Kim, 11 Nov), as a step of the meeting's work, a village a step; a new household starts with it if it has the seed. In this task every holder holds it.
- [ ] Barley-only villages: `tests/golden.json` unchanged. Numbers, populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years, with every village given rye by a test switch: fields a farm, rations by crop (`tools/balance.js` reports by crop), hungry household-years, soil; and whether households, not only averages, are better off.
Cost: a field a year at its turn; a village a year at its meeting, over its farms (bounded by the village's households). No daily work.
