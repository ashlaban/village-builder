# VB-1671: Invariant: every id resolves, and an ended party holds nothing
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/review-principles-2026-11-24/synthesis.md (Invariant catalogue, M1 and M3); tests/lib/invariants.js; design.md, Principles for decisions (Nothing teleports)
Goal: Every pawn, household, farm, village and holder id reachable in st names a live party or lies in a field declared as history, and a dead pawn or dissolved household holds nothing, checked by one general walk instead of per-collection checks added after each bug.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Id-typed fields of `st` (pawn, household, farm, village, holder) are declared once, beside `@state` or in `src/owners.json`, each as live or as history; one walk in `checkState` (`tests/lib/invariants.js`) fails on a live-declared id that names a dead pawn or a dissolved household, and on a pawn in two households.
- [ ] A dead pawn holds no stake, load or task, and a dissolved household holds no good in any store, herd, cart, claim, right or obligation (M3), checked in the same walk.
- [ ] An ending sweep (`tests/`, on a constructed village from `tests/tiny.js`): each pawn, then each household, is ended in turn at each phase of a day, and `checkState` runs after each.
- [ ] A breach the open repairs already cover (VB-1476, VB-1481, VB-1485, VB-1620) is listed in the check as known, with that task's ID, and not mended here; any other breach found gets a repair task.
- [ ] The per-collection checks the walk subsumes are removed, and the PR lists them.
