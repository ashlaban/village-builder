# VB-666: The supply outlook: how long food, fuel and hay last, season by season, from the plan
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/mocks/supply-doubt.html, docs/mocks/supply-scale.html (the UI review with Kim, 16 November); design.md section 7 (Holders and plans; The estimate); AGENTS.md, Deciding without Kim, 14 and 15
Goal: Every supply is read on one scale, time: will the household make it through the year. The simulation reckons it once, at the head's review, and the page only reads it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `Sim.supplyOutlook(st, householdId)` gives, for each supply (`food`, `fuel`, `hay`), plain data: `{ own, with, state, parts }`. `own` and `with` are how long the supply lasts from the start of the season we are in, in seasons, to the frame's end at 4 (a value of 4 or more is safe): `own` on the store alone, `with` with every intake the plan expects within the four seasons, spoilage taken off. `state` is `safe`, or the first season (0 to 3) whose need the likeliest outcome does not cover and whether it is short a little or badly (the bound a named tunable). `parts` holds the numbers behind it, for the page's i: the store, the need a season, each intake expected and when, what is expected to spoil.
- [ ] One scale for every supply, time (Kim): food is the larder, meals, flour and grain above the seed, over the household's daily need (`sim/hunger`); fuel is firewood and peat as one supply, the row being the need and not the good (free fuel as `sim/kinds` counts it, `PEAT.worth` a unit of peat), burnt by the season's use (`ECON.woodUse`), so a winter season costs several summers; hay is needed in winter only, so its outlook is the share of the coming winter covered (`Sim.winterNeed`), and its `state` names winter. A supply needed in one season only says so (`seasons: [3]`), for the page to draw it as one cell.
- [ ] What is still to come is the plan's, never the simulation's own knowledge of the future (principle 14): food's is the crop the head expects of the fields sown and those its plan will sow (the yield the plan already reckons with when it asks how many fields; no weather to come is read); hay's the meadows still to mow by the same reckoning; fuel's is the plan's goal for the season (`plan.targets.wood`), which the household cuts towards (Kim: connecting firewood to the household plan is sound), bounded by the trips its hands allow before winter, written with its formula. Spoilage is inside the projection (grain by `ECON.storeLoss`, hay by `HERD.hayLoss`; firewood does not spoil), and is not a separate figure of the outlook but for `parts`.
- [ ] Reckoned at the head's review and kept in its plan (`plan.outlook`: the centre only; the range is VB-669's), so that it is recomputed when the review already runs: on the planning day, and out of turn when a store crosses its target (the harvest brought in wakes it). The page reads the stored outlook and never computes one. `@state`, `src/owners.json`, the save, an invariant (each value finite and at least 0; `state` a known word), and what a household with no plan, no animals or no farm reads.
- [ ] The frame rolls by the season: an outlook made in an earlier season is read as stale and made again at the first review of the new one (the seasonal plan's first review, `seasonal`), so no pass at the turn of a season.
- [ ] Cost (Rules for cost, 9): named before building (households, times reviews a year, times the work of one outlook: three supplies, four seasons, the farm's fields and meadows) and measured on populous small at year 2 and on a late save (`--report perf`); nothing a step, nothing a day beyond the reviews that already run. A work counter if it loops over a growing collection.
- [ ] `node tools/sim.js --report outlook` prints the outlook of each household of a village beside what came of it a year on (the season it did go short in, or none), as a description with no target: how often the plan's reckoning was right.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): a household with a year's food and one with a month's; fuel that lasts the summer and not the winter; hay for half a winter with meadows still to mow; the outlook made again after a harvest is stacked; a save keeps it.
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged: nothing reads the outlook to decide. `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` but for `plan.outlook`; its line in the task file.
