# VB-666: The supply outlook: how long food, fuel and hay last, season by season, from the plan
Status: done (2026-11-17)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/mocks/supply-doubt.html, docs/mocks/supply-scale.html (the UI review with Kim, 16 November); design.md section 7 (Holders and plans; The estimate); AGENTS.md, Deciding without Kim, 14 and 15
Goal: Every supply is read on one scale, time: will the household make it through the year. The simulation reckons it once, at the head's review, and the page only reads it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `Sim.supplyOutlook(st, householdId)` gives, for each supply (`food`, `fuel`, `hay`), plain data: `{ own, with, state, parts }`. `own` and `with` are how long the supply lasts from the start of the season we are in, in seasons, to the frame's end at 4 (a value of 4 or more is safe): `own` on the store alone, `with` with every intake the plan expects within the four seasons, spoilage taken off. `state` is `safe`, or the first season (0 to 3) whose need the likeliest outcome does not cover and whether it is short a little or badly (the bound a named tunable). `parts` holds the numbers behind it, for the page's i: the store, the need a season, each intake expected and when, what is expected to spoil.
- [x] One scale for every supply, time (Kim): food is the larder, meals, flour and grain above the seed, over the household's daily need (`sim/hunger`); fuel is firewood and peat as one supply, the row being the need and not the good (free fuel as `sim/kinds` counts it, `PEAT.worth` a unit of peat), burnt by the season's use (`ECON.woodUse`), so a winter season costs several summers; hay is needed in winter only, so its outlook is the share of the coming winter covered (`Sim.winterNeed`), and its `state` names winter. A supply needed in one season only says so (`seasons: [3]`), for the page to draw it as one cell.
- [x] What is still to come is the plan's, never the simulation's own knowledge of the future (principle 14): food's is the crop the head expects of the fields sown and those its plan will sow (the yield the plan already reckons with when it asks how many fields; no weather to come is read); hay's the meadows still to mow by the same reckoning; fuel's is the plan's goal for the season (`plan.targets.wood`), which the household cuts towards (Kim: connecting firewood to the household plan is sound), bounded by the trips its hands allow before winter, written with its formula. Spoilage is inside the projection (grain by `ECON.storeLoss`, hay by `HERD.hayLoss`; firewood does not spoil), and is not a separate figure of the outlook but for `parts`.
- [x] Reckoned at the head's review and kept in its plan (`plan.outlook`: the centre only; the range is VB-669's), so that it is recomputed when the review already runs: on the planning day, and out of turn when a store crosses its target (the harvest brought in wakes it). The page reads the stored outlook and never computes one. `@state`, `src/owners.json`, the save, an invariant (each value finite and at least 0; `state` a known word), and what a household with no plan, no animals or no farm reads.
- [x] The frame rolls by the season: an outlook made in an earlier season is read as stale and made again at the first review of the new one (the seasonal plan's first review, `seasonal`), so no pass at the turn of a season.
- [x] Cost (Rules for cost, 9): named before building (households, times reviews a year, times the work of one outlook: three supplies, four seasons, the farm's fields and meadows) and measured on populous small at year 2 and on a late save (`--report perf`); nothing a step, nothing a day beyond the reviews that already run. A work counter if it loops over a growing collection.
- [x] `node tools/sim.js --report outlook` prints the outlook of each household of a village beside what came of it a year on (the season it did go short in, or none), as a description with no target: how often the plan's reckoning was right.
- [x] Tests on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): a household with a year's food and one with a month's; fuel that lasts the summer and not the winter; hay for half a winter with meadows still to mow; the outlook made again after a harvest is stacked; a save keeps it.
- [x] Behaviour unchanged: nothing reads the outlook to decide. `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` but for `plan.outlook`; its line in the task file.

Decided there, by Kim: one scale for supplies, how long they last; four seasons ahead, starting at the season we are in and rolling a season at a time ("will they make it through this year"); every intake within the frame counts; spoilage acts on the projected total and is inside it; fuel is one supply (firewood and peat); firewood's intake is the plan's; the outlook is the head's and is made at its review, which the harvest wakes. The head's doubt is added later (VB-669).

Implementation (17 Nov 2026):
- Impact: a new module `sim/outlook` (agency layer, after `sim/aims`) follows `sim/plans`' event `planReviewed` (whose payload now carries `why`) and writes one field, `plan.outlook` (owner `sim/outlook` in `src/owners.json`; `@state` in the module). It reads `sim/hunger` (dailyNeed, larderOf, seedPer), `sim/stores` (grain, the seed reserved), `sim/pot` (fresh food), `sim/kinds` (`fuelFree`, now exported), `sim/targets` (`hands`), `sim/herds` (`householdHay`), `sim/meadows` (`HAY`) and `plan.targets` (wood, hayWinters). No new coupling into the simulation: nothing reads the outlook. `Sim.supplyOutlook(st, id)` returns a copy of the stored outlook with `stale` (made in an earlier season) and `head` (whose estimate it is, principle 14); null with no plan or before a first review. The page only reads it (VB-667 draws it).
- Shape: each supply `{own, with, state, season, seasons, parts: {store, need (by season of the frame), intakes ([{what, season, n}]), spoilt}}`; hay's parts add `share` (of the coming stall covered).
- The reckoning: a walk a day at a time from today to the frame's end (four seasons from the start of the one we are in), once on the store alone (`own`) and once with the intakes (`with`); the value is where the store first fails the day's need, in seasons from the frame's start, capped at 4. Food: grain above the seed (spoiling by `ECON.storeLoss`'s tiers and the barn's factor, as `spoilGrain` does) and the meals, flour, sheaves, larder and fresh food (kept), eaten at `dailyNeed`; each field's crop comes in at its sowing day plus the crop's days to ripe (a field to sow: the first day of its crop's next window, today inside it), `FIELDS.expect × yield` less the seed it takes back (stooks: what lies in them, less the seed). Fuel: `fuelFree` (peat at `PEAT.worth`) burnt at `ECON.woodUse` of the day's season; on the first day of each season of the frame (today, for this one) the household cuts to the season's goal `plan.targets.wood[s]`, at most `hands × OUTLOOK.woodTrips × ECON.woodPerTrip` a season (the share left of this one); none with no farm or no wood in its village. Hay: the stall days (`HERD.stall`, autumn day 8 to spring day 4) at `householdHay` a day, counted as the winter's; meadows still in grass give `HAY × fert` (sim/meadows' `expectedHay`, the plan's reckoning of a meadow) at mid-summer, the mowing stopping at `hayWinters` winters' need; hay lying mown comes in a day after its mowing; spoiling by `HERD.hayLoss`. The value is the winter's season of the frame plus the share of the coming stall covered, 4 when all of it.
- New tunables (Proposed, of the view only): `OUTLOOK.little` 0.5 (a first short season covered at least this share is 'short', else 'bad'); `OUTLOOK.woodTrips` 6 (firewood trips a hand makes a season towards the goal).
- No plan: null. No herds: hay `state: 'none'`, own and with 4. Nobody to feed: food 'none'. No farm: the store alone, no intakes, no seed held back.
- Decisions (Deciding without Kim; in the inbox as `decision` notes): (1) `state` is a word (`safe`, `short`, `bad`, `none`) with the first short season in a field of its own, `season`, so that the invariant's "a known word" holds; (2) hay on the seasons scale is the winter's season plus the share of the coming stall covered, the whole stall (with its autumn and spring days) counted as the winter's; (3) a review for a window closing keeps the outlook it holds in the same season (cost: 1,561 of 5,087 household reviews at populous small seed 7 in two years were such); (4) food's intakes are the farm's present fields, not the field a land project will make, and the crop is the plan's `FIELDS.expect` of the yield without the field's fertility and soil (the plan's own reckoning when it asks how many fields).
