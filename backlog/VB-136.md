Status: ready
Priority: 2

Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Animals; docs/review-animals.md (review of 31 October 2026); answers in QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md under "VB-047: animals review".
Goal: Herds graze the outfield by day with a herder, children's work from 8 (Agreed); grazing keeps land open (Decided). Grazing pressure is counted where herds actually graze (review, G6).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A new type of work `herding` (child 8, daughter 6, son 5, retired 4, farmhand 3, farmer 2; Kim, 31 Oct, as P); kinds may set `minAge` (herding 8).
- [x] Each grazing day a household with a herd posts `herd` at 6:00: the herder walks its herds to a pasture tile within 8 steps (open land or wood, not a field or an unmown meadow, not another household's; best grass, least grazed, nearest) and stays 9 hours, then brings them home; the herds follow the herder on the map. `herd` is the game's first `background` kind (VB-251, done: attention `background`, at `rate` of the herder's hours, within `reach` steps of the pasture; `src/sim/kinds.js`), so the herder may take other work within reach meanwhile.
- [x] `w.graze` (`@state` documented): each grazing hour adds 1 a cow (0.25 a sheep) on the pasture tile and a quarter of that on its neighbours; it halves each game year, decayed lazily (Rules for cost, 3): stored with the game hour it was last written, or against a shared scale renormalised once a year, and read in closed form; no daily pass over the tiles (not faded with `w.wear` in `fadeWear`, `src/sim/paths.js`). A herd on the move adds a quarter crossing a head to the tiles it crosses, so cattle lanes wear in.
- [x] A herd without a herder by 10:00 stays by the farm; its cows give half their milk; if a stretch of fence beside a growing field of the village is broken (below 0.45), it gets into the crop with chance 0.2 a day: the field loses 10% of its yield, the event is logged, and the field's holder remembers "Cattle in our crop" (−0.1, cap −0.3, 2 years) about the holder of the household that keeps the stretch.

## Implementation notes (12 Nov 2026)
- Impact note: a new module `src/sim/grazing.js` (layer material, before `sim/herds`): the pressure `w.graze` (owner, `@state`), the day's
  pasture, the herds out with the herder, `unherded`, and the `herd` kind's record (as `sim/dig`'s `KIND`). It reads herds, households,
  meadows, `st.owner`, `st.used`; writes `w.graze` and two new herd fields, `herder` (pawn or null) and `herded` (day, -1 never). New
  coupling: `sim/agents` asks `sim/grazing` which herds a walking pawn drives (path wear) and clears them on arrival home; `sim/byre` reads
  `unherded` (milk, strays) and calls `sim/fields` `trample`; `sim/byre` emits `cattleInCrop`, which `sim/relations` remembers. No clock change.
- Rules (`src/sim/rules.js`): type `herding` in ROLES (child 8, daughter 6, son 5, retired 4, farmhand 3, farmer 2), `HERD.herding`,
  `HERD.stray`, `GRAZE` {keepsOpen 20, thins 200} for VB-091, the memory `crop` ("Cattle in our crop", -0.1, cap -0.3, 2 years), urgency
  `herd` 1.5; `stalled` moved here from `sim/herds` (re-exported there) so `sim/grazing` can sit below `sim/herds`.
- The kind: background, `reach` 3, `rate` 1, `minAge` 8 (read by `sim/jobs` score and fit), `window` from 6:00, posted until 10:00 on a
  grazing day for a household with animals; its pasture is drawn when first posted (bfs of 8 steps: grass by biome less pressure/100 less
  0.05 a step; own aftermath meadows allowed). `held` drives the herds out; `worked` adds the load (1 a head of cattle, 0.25 a sheep) an
  hour on the tile and a quarter on its neighbours. Path wear: a pawn driving herds treads 1 + 0.25 a head (`tread(w, i, n)`).
- `w.graze` is stored against the year's scale 2^((t - year start)/YEAR) and halved once a year at the year's first step
  (`grazingDay`, called by `herdsDay`); read in closed form by `grazeOf`. Saved in `WORLD`; invariants for it and the herd fields.
- Attention (VB-251) for its first use, `src/sim/agents.js`: a pawn with no task in hand may take a background task out of reach and sets
  out for it (kept, unworked, while its path ends within reach); a background task counts hours (`rate`), not work rate; it is let go
  outside working hours and by `goHome`; its work done, a pawn with nothing in hand goes home with the herds; pawns holding a side task are
  stepped while they hold it (`awake`); the matcher offers a herder full tasks within reach; an idle herder waits by its pasture
  (`idleAbout`). Fixed a VB-251 bug the herders showed: `freeHands` let go of a side task while iterating `j.claims`, skipping the next
  worker, whose job then lost it (an invariant failure in heirs 7 on day 6).
- G7 (`src/sim/byre.js` `stray`, in `byreDay`): for each cattle herd unherded on the day before, a broken stretch of its village's fence
  beside a standing crop (sown, growing, ripe) of the village lets it in with chance 0.2: the crop nearest the herd's spot gets
  `fd.trampled` (each takes 10% at the reaping), the event `cattleInCrop` is logged, and the field's holder remembers the keeper's holder.
  Milk: half for a herd unherded on the day before (`HERD.herding.milk`).
- Page: herds are drawn at their pasture while the herder minds them there, at its heels on the way, else by the farm
  (`src/page/animate.js`). Screenshot: `docs/img/vb136-herding-at.jpg` (populous small, seed 7, summer day 3, a herd on its pasture).
- Tests: `tests/herding.js` (new, in `npm test`); `tests/attention.js` (a full task in reach is now offered; the set-out case), `tests/milk.js`
  (half milk; the year's bound allows for unherded days), `tests/children.js` (child herd 8), `tests/record.js` (heirs 7 lives re-recorded:
  52 events from 66: fewer gifts and close friends, the same births, deaths and marriages).

## Orchestrator's decisions for Kim to overturn
- The stretch's keeper: the village fence is shared, so the keeper is the occupied farm of the village whose house is nearest the stretch
  (the fence shared out among the farms, as the gärdesgård was).
- Only cattle stray into crops; sheep do not. A stray is judged at midnight for the day before, and milk is halved on the morning after an
  unherded day (milking is at 6:00, before the 10:00 cutoff); never after a day in the byre.
- A background task counts the pawn's hours, not its work rate (a child of 8 minds the herd 9 hours, not 22).
- A herder may take a full task within reach (3 steps) of the pasture: VB-251's matcher offered none; the criterion says "may take other work
  within reach meanwhile".
- Numbers with no source: reach 3 steps; urgency 1.5 (as milking and the byre); grass by biome (meadow 1, marsh 0.8, heath 0.6, hills and
  forest 0.4, pine and shore 0.3); pressure over 100 counts against a tile as one grass grade; 0.05 a step; keepsOpen 20 and thins 200 from
  the measured range.
- A pasture may be the household's own meadow once mown and carried (aftermath grazing), not an unmown one.
