# VB-136: Herding and grazing pressure
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: animals
Phase: implement
Design ref: Animals; docs/review-animals.md (review of 31 October 2026); answers in QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md under "VB-047: animals review".
Goal: Herds graze the outfield by day with a herder, children's work from 8 (Agreed); grazing keeps land open (Decided). Grazing pressure is counted where herds actually graze (review, G6).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A new type of work `herding` (child 8, daughter 6, son 5, retired 4, farmhand 3, farmer 2; Kim, 31 Oct, as P); kinds may set `minAge` (herding 8).
- [ ] Each grazing day a household with a herd posts `herd` at 6:00: the herder walks its herds to a pasture tile within 8 steps (open land or wood, not a field or an unmown meadow, not another household's; best grass, least grazed, nearest) and stays 9 hours, then brings them home; the herds follow the herder on the map.
- [ ] `w.graze` (`@state` documented): each grazing hour adds 1 a cow (0.25 a sheep) on the pasture tile and a quarter of that on its neighbours; it halves each game year, faded daily as `w.wear`. A herd on the move adds a quarter crossing a head to the tiles it crosses, so cattle lanes wear in.
- [ ] A herd without a herder by 10:00 stays by the farm; its cows give half their milk; if a stretch of fence beside a growing field of the village is broken (below 0.45), it gets into the crop with chance 0.2 a day: the field loses 10% of its yield, the event is logged, and the field's holder remembers "Cattle in our crop" (−0.1, cap −0.3, 2 years) about the holder of the household that keeps the stretch.
- [ ] Set the "keeps open" and "thins" thresholds VB-091 reads; report the pressure map's range at populous medium.
- [ ] A test; idle shares before and after; re-record the golden file.
