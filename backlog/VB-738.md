# VB-738: The furnace: copper, tin and lead-silver ore smelted to metal
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-364, VB-736
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The metals and the stones: goods by purpose, kinds by property; Goods, recipes and facilities); `docs/plans/VB-702.md`; `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (17)
Goal: Ore becomes metal near the mine, and metal is what travels (principle "Place matters" by the cost of carrying).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A facility `furnace` (VB-361), built by a household in stages (Proposed: 40 hours, 3 loads of stone, 2 of clay). Recipes, skill smelting (VB-360): 4 loads of `ore/copper` and 8 of wood, 12 hours, a bar of copper; the same for tin; 4 loads of `ore/leadSilver` and 8 of wood, 12 hours, a bar of lead and 2 öre of silver (all Proposed).
- [ ] Metal is a good with kinds (VB-733): `metal/iron` (VB-701's `iron`, renamed in the registry and the store), `metal/copper`, `metal/tin`, `metal/lead`, `metal/bronze`; silver and gold are held by the öre (VB-741 declares the thing of worth; until it lands they are counted and nothing uses them). A worth row each (Proposed, in rations: a bar of copper 12, tin 20, lead 4; an öre of silver 20).
- [ ] The alloy: `bronze`, a recipe at the furnace with copper and tin in (`BRONZE.tin`, a seventh by weight) and bronze out, by whoever holds both.
- [ ] Lead is stored, never thrown away (nothing vanishes); its `tools/stocks.js` line says it waits for the church and the town.
- [ ] A head's target (VB-698) for a metal is what its own makings take, raised by what others ask (VB-699): no furnace runs with no one wanting metal.
- [ ] Invariants and `tools/stocks.js` lines for each metal; cost named: furnaces are tens at most, indexed by VB-361.
- [ ] Numbers, populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15, 30 years: furnaces built, bars made by kind, wood burnt against the village's wood (does a mining village strip its forest), metal carried between villages by kind, hungry household-days in mining villages against the others.
- [ ] The numbers that move are reported before and after at the seeds and maps named (Working rules); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
Notes:
- Numbers are Proposed by the plan of VB-702 (16 Nov) on Kim's answers to the shape, from general reading not checked by search: the task reports its own, found in at most five short runs.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim's answers after the plan of VB-702; section 14): Ore and metal are materials: not graded (D20); skill at smelting is rate only.
