# VB-719: Tilth on the field, farm and village cards
Status: done (2026-11-19)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: animals
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Yield over time; Soil); docs/review-yields.md (section 4); docs/review-yields.md (section 4.4)
Goal: The player sees what dung has built, on the one meter every level uses (principle 15), and is shown no harvest to come that is not a pawn's estimate (principle 14).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The field's card (`src/page/words.js`, where "Soil" and "Condition" are): a third row, label "Tilth" (Kim, 16 Nov), on the same meter as the other two (principle 15: a level is the meter, the same meter everywhere): value `round(tilthOf × 100)`, the hundredths of harvest it adds, `max` 40 (`TILTH.max`), an icon of the soil's family, title "What years of dung have left in the ground: the harvest gains this many hundredths". No note of years: no history is kept, and a reckoned "N years of dung" would not be what happened.
- [x] The farm's card and the village's card: the mean tilth of their fields, the same row and meter, so farms and villages compare at a glance. A farm or village with no field shows no row.
- [x] Whose estimate (principle 14): the row shows what the field holds now, as Soil and Condition do; it foretells nothing, so it names no pawn. Nothing on the page turns tilth into a harvest to come: where a card shows an expected harvest, that is a head's estimate (VB-351), which reads tilth as it reads soil, by its own bias, and says whose it is. If the page today shows an expected harvest computed by the simulation's own knowledge, the task notes it (a task of arch `acts` or a note for VB-351), and does not add tilth to it.
- [x] The page only reads: `tilthOf` through the `Sim` API (a read, no write to `st`); the lint passes. No log line and no event: tilth changes on every dunged field every spring (Rules for cost 8).
- [x] `node tests/smoke.js` and the `tests/browser*.js` that cover the cards pass; a case there, or in the words' own test, shows the row on a field with tilth 0 (the meter empty, the row shown) and with 0.12 (value 12).
- [x] A screenshot of a field's card and a farm's card with the row, under `docs/img/`, committed (`node tools/shot.js --help`): not committed (AGENTS.md, Kim 18 November: a screenshot to check one's work stays outside the repository).
- [x] Cost: one `exp` a field shown, when a card is drawn; nothing in the simulation's step (Rules for cost 7: the camera changes nothing).

Noted by VB-160, 16 November 2026.

Decisions (19 Nov): one `tilthStat(fields, of)` in src/page/meter.js serves the three cards (principle 15: one presentation); the farm's row is on its household's stats (an empty farm shows none, its fields being no one's to dung), the village's is the mean over its farms' fields, not its common fields. `Sim.tilthOf` joins the facade (a read). The farm card's expected harvest is already the head's reckoning (`Sim.harvestEstimate`, "The head's reckoning"), so nothing to note for VB-351. Checked in `tests/browser-fields.js` (0 and 12) and `tests/smoke.js` (farm and village rows).
