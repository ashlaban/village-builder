# VB-1582: The fetch is walked: the water job goes to the farm's nearest source or well, and is ranked so that it is done
Status: done (2026-11-23)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Water by the site); backlog/VB-1543.md; backlog/VB-626.md; backlog/VB-1542.md
Goal: a household's water costs the walk to it, and no farm draws where no water is
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Each farm has its source (design.md, Goods and the farming year, Water by the site, A farm's source): the tile it draws at, the class and the walk in hours, found by one search outward from the house that stops at the first source the household may draw at (a natural source; its village's well; its own), bounded at `WATER.search` (8 hours); marsh only by `WATER.marshShy`. Found at founding, when a well appears in its village, and once a year on the farm's planning day. Owner, `@state`, invariant (the tile is a source the household may draw at, or none), and the save.
- [x] The water job is posted at the farm's source, not at the house or the green: the walk out, `WATER.draw` (0.1 hours; the job's flat half hour goes), the walk home laden. A farm with no source in reach posts none and its panel says why. `village.well` is no longer read by the fetch.
- [x] Nobody draws where no water is: the starting villages' wells are sources on their greens' tiles (`w.fresh` well; `w.fresh` joins the `WORLD` list of `src/sim/saves.js`), and a farm that has stood for years more than `WELL.startWalk` from any source starts with a well on its house's tile where its ground has one (At the start). Digging is VB-1584.
- [x] Ranked so that it is done (How it is ranked): the walk is not taken off the score of a `vital` kind; at the morning's allotment the fetch heads the list of the member best fit and its hours count in that member's day. The household's panel shows the day's fetch in hours.
- [x] Marsh water costs `HEALTH.marsh` (0.25, by `HEALTH.frail`) to those the day's water covers, and the meal is cooked.
- [x] Checks on a small constructed state: a house beside a river fetches in about 0.36 hours; a house 7 steps of meadow off in about 1.95; a household with a good source within the half hour does not draw at a nearer marsh; a fetch whose walk is long is still taken before a nearer job of lower urgency.
- [x] Measured once, before and after (small, seeds 7 and 42; solo, founders and populous; 10 years): dry household-days, hours a household-day spent on water, deaths, and whether households, not only averages, are worse off; the farms with no source in reach, counted. A shortfall is a `tuning` note, not a reason to hold the task.
- [ ] Cost, named before building: households (250 at 1,000 living) x one fetch a day x one route from the house to its source, the same every day, so a hit in the route cache; no search for a source in any day. The source search: a farm at founding, at a well's finishing and once a year, about 250 a year, each stopping at the first source (tens to hundreds of tiles, bounded at 8 hours), with a work counter. `node tests/counters.js` and a late save (`--report perf`) before and after. (Unticked: the counters are reported below; the late save under `--report perf` is left to the night, fixtures and times not being a task's to run beside other agents.)
