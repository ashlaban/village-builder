# VB-169: Inhabit a pawn: the player's queued commands go before its own logic
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player and what they see (The player's commands, Needs and dark: Decided 7 and 8 Nov; The queue: Proposed, VB-079); Commands, time steps and views (Direct control: Decided); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, sections 4.1 (commands, `source: 'player'`) and 4.7, principle 15; `docs/review-life.md`, section 2.
Goal: The player inhabits one pawn and plays it by queueing commands, which go before the pawn's own logic; left to itself, the pawn does what its role's automatic behaviour chooses, and the player may switch that off (Kim, 7 Nov). Needs go on: at dark it goes home unless a command of the player's says otherwise (Kim, 8 Nov). This task is the simulation's side; the page is VB-210. It widens "take control" (VB-076, answer 3).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `st.player`: `null`, or `{pawn, auto, queue, refused, since}` (`@state` documented, owned by `sim/commands` in `src/owners.json`, saved with the state; invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js`: the pawn exists, the queue holds at most 8 commands, each with source `player` and the pawn as actor; `refused` is null or `{verb, why, t}`).
- [ ] Operations in `sim/commands`, on `Sim`: `inhabit(st, id)` (any living pawn, since whom to offer is the page's, by Which pawn: VB-210, VB-213; `auto` on; records a `player` event, "Played by the player"), `uninhabit(st)` (records it), `queue(st, cmd)` (refused with a reason when full, when the verb does not exist or when the actor is not the player's pawn), `unqueue(st, i)`, `setAuto(st, on)`.
- [ ] When the player's pawn has no player's command under way, the queue's head is issued with source `player` at the next 6-minute step. A command refused then is dropped and kept in `refused`. A command is under way until its act ends: `work` while the pawn holds that job, `walkTo` until it arrives, `goHome` until it is indoors, `idle` until `args.until`.
- [ ] The four planner sites (`docs/review-life.md`, section 2) leave the pawn alone while a player's command is queued or under way: the idle list of the 6-minute step (`src/sim/clock.js:95-104`), `lookUp` (`src/sim/jobs.js:130`), `callCooks` (`jobs.js:32`), and the end of work (`clock.js:40-46`). With `auto` off and the queue empty, the first three leave it alone too, and the end of work still sends it home.
- [ ] Tests (`tests/player.js`, in `npm test`):
  - inhabited with `auto` on and no commands, a populous tiny run over 5 game days hashes the same as the run with no player (the inhabited pawn is indistinguishable);
  - a queued `work` command is carried out before the planner's choice, and neither `lookUp` nor `callCooks` takes the pawn off it;
  - with `auto` off the pawn takes no job in working hours, and goes home at the end of work;
  - a `walkTo` queued for 19:00 is carried out, not cut off by the end of work;
  - a command refused when its turn comes is dropped with its reason;
  - save and load keep `st.player` (`tests/plaindata.js`, `tests/saves.js`).
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged when no one plays: `tests/golden.json` and the counters unchanged; `npm run verify` passes. Cost: one test of `st.player` per pawn at each site, no new loop.
