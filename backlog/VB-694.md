# VB-694: Plan: the metals and the stones: what each deposit is for
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: mill
Phase: plan
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (7, 9, 15); design.md, Goods, production and animals
Goal: For every generated deposit and rock: what it is made into, what uses that up, who needs it, and when it enters (which slice), written as tasks; none is left as map colour without a reason.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Bronze: copper and tin from different places make one good, so two places must trade (principle 9); what bronze is for beside iron, and whether it comes first.
- [ ] Silver and gold: standing, gifts and dowries, and the ship's silver (VB-188); what a household does with them before coin.
- [ ] The veins sim/prospect finds: how a vein is mined (a pit, a facility, hands), since today a find is recorded and nothing digs it.
- [ ] Flint, amber, soapstone, marble, slate, limestone, sandstone and granite: a use each, or the slice it waits for. Each good passes four tests: a source tied to place, a sink that recurs, a decision for a head, a reason to cross between households.
- [ ] How many villages have each deposit within reach, measured on populous small and medium at several seeds, so that scarcity is known before numbers are set.
- [ ] Implement tasks written with criteria; questions that move a Decided point go to `QUESTIONS.md`.
