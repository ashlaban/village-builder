Goal: A head short of hands offers a place, or an adult whose wish is service asks for one; the other answers; the servant walks there at the round and the year runs to the next (VB-634).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The offer (`place`, due at the round; at once for a household taken in, VB-695): on his asking day a head whose household is under `SERVICE.short` hands a field, or who lives alone, offers a place to one adult of his village or within `SERVICE.hours` whose wish has Service open (plan, section 3): kin first, then by his opinion of them, then the nearer, then the home that keeps the most hands. Or the adult whose best open rung is Service asks such a head, by the same order from its side (kin, opinion, nearness, the fewest hands a field). A head holds one open place offer.
- [ ] The answers: the adult says no at `SOCIAL.enemy` towards the head, when its home would be left under `SERVICE.short` hands a field without it, when the master's household is hungrier than its own (mean hunger of the members, read at the round), and never goes to a household where a parent lives; of several places it takes the best by its order. The head asked says yes while still short and not at enmity. The head of the home left is not asked (`moveWhy`). `takeServants` and `serveWhereShort` are removed; the move is `move`, reason `service` (VB-822), which sets the servant's flag and `servedSince` (VB-634).
- [ ] The year: at the round a servant stays without asking unless a better rung is open to it; one whose house is no longer short has Service open elsewhere, as today. Nobody is dismissed and no verb is added for it. A refusal is `SOCIAL.memories.refusedPlace` (-0.05, to -0.15, 1 year).
- [ ] The played pawn offers and answers as any; as head it is asked before a servant comes, and its pawn's judgement is shown as advice.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7 and 42: servants as a share of those of 18 to 30; lone holders with and without a servant; households under `SERVICE.short` at harvest; offers made, refused and by which of the three reasons; the share of servants who are kin of the master; growth. Whether households are better off: hungry household-days of the households short of hands, as a description.
- [ ] No share of servants and no count of years is set (VB-634). `tests/commands.js` and `tests/record.js` pass; tests on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): each reason for a no; kin before a stranger; a servant stays; a lone holder is served.
