Goal: A farm away from home is established over a season or more by people who walk there, sleep there and build it, and nobody lives at a place before it has a roof.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] State: `st.sites[]`, owned by `sim/settle`: `{id, leader, party {all, followers}, from (village), village (the small village joined, or none), house, field, plot (the tiles and their steps, as a plot's), booth, stage, since}`, with a live index, invariants and its line in `docs/architecture/ownership.md`. The stake on its tiles is the leader's (a claim's holder may be a pawn: `sim/stakes`, `sim/claims`), renewed by the work as a field's is.
- [x] Step 1, the verb `stakeSite` at the round, in place of `leave`: the leader, the party and the site as today (and as VB-786 searches); nobody moves, no grain is taken, no household or village is made. The round's provisions check goes to step 4.
- [x] Step 2: the leader calls `blaze` (VB-447) from the home green to the site; the party's adults take its jobs.
- [ ] Step 3, the site's jobs, owned by the site and taken by its party's members of 14 and over (but a nursing mother) at role priority 9 (`PLOT_PRIO`'s first): the booth (VB-784), the house tile and the first field cleared by the steps a plot has (fell 10, grub 6, clear 10 and up to 20 for stones, break 30), the first field sown in its season with seed carried from the party's stakes or their households, the cottage's foundation 30, walls 60 with 18 loads of timber (the site's felled trees, else timber trips to the nearest wood) and roof 30. A worker lodges at the booth (VB-783) when the walk there and back is over `AWAY.walk`, with a pack from the household it belongs to, and walks home for the next; with no job open it goes home and works there.
- [x] Step 4, the verb `leave`, tried when the roof's job ends and on the leader's household's planning day: the cottage finished (the roof on), and the provisions there (to the next harvest and `SETTLE.margin`, 6 days, counted from that day, and seed unless the field is sown; the first field may still be to break or sow, and follows from the household; from the stakes and the old households as today; short of it the move waits, and the log says why once a season). The household is founded with the leader as head, and the village where the site is a new one (named then); the party walks out with its loads as VB-675 has them.

The household and the village are founded when the farmstead is complete (answer 3 of the VB-445 questions, `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`): this replaces the plan's proposed founding moment. 27 Nov 2026 (Kim): complete means the cottage finished; the field can follow (design.md, Settling in steps, step 4). ## Decisions (VB-787, first part)
- `st.sites` lives in a new module `sim/sites` (the site, its jobs, their ends), not `sim/settle`, which keeps the round and the verbs `stakeSite` and `leave`: settle stays under 700 lines.
- The site's jobs are owned by the leader's household (the one that feeds its workers, Kim 18 Nov); `j.party` limits them to the party's members of 14 and over, not nursing, at `PLOT_PRIO.waiting` (sim/fit sitePrio). A member of another household takes none (rare: leader, spouse and siblings live at one home).
- The house tile is cleared first, then the cottage; the field is cleared and broken alongside and not sown at the site: it follows from the household (Kim, 27 Nov 2026).
- Lodging (sim/away siteLodgers): a worker whose walk there and back is over `AWAY.walk` lodges at the booth tile with the site's job keys; the lodger's own rule (stakeIfRough) raises the booth. Tried once a day a worker (`site.tried`).
- The walls' wood left at the move goes to the woodpile up to `ECON.woodWinter`, the rest burnt, as a plot's.
