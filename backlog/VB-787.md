# VB-787: Settling in steps: staked at the round, built from a booth, founded at the move
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-783, VB-784, VB-786, VB-447, VB-675
Priority: 2
Size: L
Arch: far
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Moving to new land (Settling in steps; Sleeping away from home); docs/plans/VB-445.md, section 5; design.md, Moving to new land; Clearing a new plot
Goal: A farm away from home is established over a season or more by people who walk there, sleep there and build it, and nobody lives at a place before it has a roof.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] State: `st.sites[]`, owned by `sim/settle`: `{id, leader, party {all, followers}, from (village), village (the small village joined, or none), house, field, plot (the tiles and their steps, as a plot's), booth, stage, since}`, with a live index, invariants and its line in `docs/architecture/ownership.md`. The stake on its tiles is the leader's (a claim's holder may be a pawn: `sim/stakes`, `sim/claims`), renewed by the work as a field's is.
- [ ] Step 1, the verb `stakeSite` at the round, in place of `leave`: the leader, the party and the site as today (and as VB-786 searches); nobody moves, no grain is taken, no household or village is made. The round's provisions check goes to step 4.
- [ ] Step 2: the leader calls `blaze` (VB-447) from the home green to the site; the party's adults take its jobs.
- [ ] Step 3, the site's jobs, owned by the site and taken by its party's members of 14 and over (but a nursing mother) at role priority 9 (`PLOT_PRIO`'s first): the booth (VB-784), the house tile and the first field cleared by the steps a plot has (fell 10, grub 6, clear 10 and up to 20 for stones, break 30), the first field sown in its season with seed carried from the party's stakes or their households, the cottage's foundation 30, walls 60 with 18 loads of timber (the site's felled trees, else timber trips to the nearest wood) and roof 30. A worker lodges at the booth (VB-783) when the walk there and back is over `AWAY.walk`, with a pack from the household it belongs to, and walks home for the next; with no job open it goes home and works there.
- [ ] Step 4, the verb `leave`, tried when the roof's job ends and on the leader's household's planning day: the roof on, the first field broken, and the provisions there (to the next harvest and `SETTLE.margin`, 6 days, counted from that day, and seed unless the field is sown; from the stakes and the old households as today; short of it the move waits, and the log says why once a season). The household is founded with the leader as head, and the village where the site is a new one (named then); the party walks out with its loads as VB-675 has them.
- [ ] `f.booth` and the booth's cottage jobs (`boothJob`, `boothWork`, `finishBoothStage`) go; a save with a household living in a booth loads with its cottage's stages as a site at step 3 whose household is already founded, and plays on (`tests/saves.js`).
- [ ] A leader who dies is followed by the party's next adult; other endings are VB-789's.
- [ ] The log and the record: the stake, the trail's end, the roof and the move each have their line and event, naming the pawn and the place; the village panel's count of those who wait shows the sites under way.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42, 15 years, and founders small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years: parties, days from the round to the move (median and longest), sites given up, villages founded and farms joined, hours worked at sites and hours the homes' own jobs lost, grain the homes gave (packs and provisions) against the 42 days of before, hungry days of the settlers and of their old households in the two years round the move, growth against Deciding without Kim 2. Whether households, not only averages, are better off.
- [ ] Cost (Rules for cost, 9): a site a party, a few under way on an island of 1,000, at most six jobs open each; nothing polled. `node tests/counters.js` before and after; a late save (`node tools/sim.js --load FILE --years 1 --report perf`) for the counters, times to the night.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state: a party of two stakes a wooded site four hours out, blazes, builds and moves, and the household exists only from the move; a home with no grain to spare sends no worker; a save at each step plays as the unbroken run.
- [ ] If the module passes 700 lines, the split task the lint asks for is opened and named.
