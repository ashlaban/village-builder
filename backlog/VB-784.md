# VB-784: A booth: a shelter built in a day, which sleeps four and rots
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: far
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Moving to new land (Settling in steps; Sleeping away from home); docs/plans/VB-445.md; design.md, Moving to new land (The booth)
Goal: A booth is something a pawn builds where it means to stay, and a stay there has a roof.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `st.booths[]`, owned by `sim/building`: `{id, tile, by (the pawn or household it was built for), built, cond, condAt}`; ids never reused, a live index, the field in `WORLD` or the saves as its kind needs, invariants, and a line in `docs/architecture/ownership.md`.
- [ ] A job kind `booth`: `BOOTH.hours` (12) at the tile, with `BOOTH.wood` (2) loads there first: felled on the tile where it is wooded, else fetched from the nearest wood within `WOOD.reach` as a timber trip is. Opened by a verb any pawn has (`raiseBooth`), on ground nobody else owns or has staked.
- [ ] A stay (VB-783) whose bed is a booth's tile has roof 'booth'; `BOOTH.sleeps` (4) at most, the verb `lodge` refusing the fifth; those it was built for and whom its builder lets in.
- [ ] It rots: its condition falls lazily, as a house nobody mends (`f.condition`, `f.condAt`), and at none it is gone and the tile is as it was (Principles, What does not wear needs a way out); a stay there renews nothing.
- [ ] The page draws a booth (one presentation, wherever a booth stands); a screenshot under `docs/img/`.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state: built in 12 hours with 2 loads; five pawns, four beds; gone after its years unkept. `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` (nothing builds one yet).
