# VB-784: A booth: a shelter built in a day, which sleeps four and rots
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: far
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Moving to new land (Settling in steps; Sleeping away from home); docs/plans/VB-445.md; design.md, Moving to new land (The booth)
Goal: A booth is something a pawn builds where it means to stay, and a stay there has a roof.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `st.booths[]`, owned by `sim/building`: `{id, tile, by (the pawn or household it was built for), built, cond, condAt}`; ids never reused, a live index, the field in `WORLD` or the saves as its kind needs, invariants, and a line in `docs/architecture/ownership.md`.
- [x] A job kind `booth`: `BOOTH.hours` (12) at the tile, with `BOOTH.wood` (2) loads there first: felled on the tile where it is wooded, else fetched from the nearest wood within `WOOD.reach` as a timber trip is. Opened by a verb any pawn has (`raiseBooth`), on ground nobody else owns or has staked.
- [x] A stay (VB-783) whose bed is a booth's tile has roof 'booth'; `BOOTH.sleeps` (4) at most, the verb `lodge` refusing the fifth; those it was built for and whom its builder lets in.
- [x] It rots: its condition falls lazily, as a house nobody mends (`f.condition`, `f.condAt`), and at none it is gone and the tile is as it was (Principles, What does not wear needs a way out); a stay there renews nothing.
- [x] The page draws a booth (one presentation, wherever a booth stands); a screenshot under `docs/img/`.
- [x] Tests on a constructed state: built in 12 hours with 2 loads; five pawns, four beds; gone after its years unkept. `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` (nothing builds one yet).

Noted by VB-445, 16 November 2026.


## Implementation (VB-784)
- `st.booths[]` (live only), `st.boothSeq`, owner `sim/building`; entries `{id, tile, by (household id), maker (pawn), guests, built (null while
  raised), wood, cond, condAt}`. `by` is the household and `maker` the pawn, since the household's pawns raise and sleep in it. A save without
  the fields gets none (saves.js); not a `WORLD` field (it is `st`, not the map). Invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js`, `tools/stocks.js` line.
- Verb `raiseBooth(tile, {guests})` (sim/commands): refused unless the tile is free ground (`freeFor`: nobody else's, unstaked, not used) with no
  booth. Wood and raising are posted by `boothWork` for the household: a `timber` trip (as the cottage's, one trip brings 3 units, BOOTH.wood
  is 2) felled on the tile where it is wooded (`onSite`), else from the village's wood (`v.wood`, which is within WOOD.reach), then the job kind
  `booth`, 12 hours.
- `lodge` (sim/away, small): roof 'booth' at a standing booth's tile; refused for a pawn it is not for and for the fifth; a stay under a booth that
  rots goes to roof 'none' (event `boothGone`, since building may not look up away).
- Rot: closed form `cond - years * BOOTH.wear` (1/3 a year: three years), the daily rule of `sim/building` removes the gone ones, a loop over live
  booths only (none, in every run today). The tile is as it was: nothing was written to it. A stay renews nothing.
- Page: `tent()` is the one presentation, used for a settlers' booth and for a booth on its tile (fades with condition; a ring of stakes while raised).
  Screenshot `docs/img/vb-784-booths.jpg` (two booths, one being staked, made by a script that adds them to a founders seed 7 state).
- `tools/same.js --base 33da017` differs only in the two new keys (`booths`, `boothSeq`) in every entry: no behaviour changed.
- Tests: `tests/booth.js` (staked, raised with its wood, five pawns four beds, rots away); the fetched-wood path is not tested (the tiny world has none).

## Decisions
- A booth is staked by `raiseBooth` and raised as the household's own job; a built-for household (not a single pawn) with `maker` kept. The
  `guests` list is the builder's letting in; no verb lets one in later (VB-787 or VB-790 may add one).
- `f.booth` (the settlers' flag) stays; VB-787 replaces it with `st.booths`.
