# VB-290: The round's other acts reach by walking hours: claims, service, guardians and the tie
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: between
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Between villages: Near and far, The round's other acts; Empty farms: Claims; Leaving home: Service; Households short of people). QUESTIONS.md, VB-281, question 2
Goal: The round's acts after the marriages reach other villages by hex steps as the crow flies between village centres, or by nothing: claims on empty farms within `CLAIMS.farmReach` 15 (`claimants`, `src/sim/farmclaims.js:64-91`), service within `SERVICE.reach` 15 (`reachSteps`, `src/sim/service.js:96`, read by `takeServants`, `:111`, `serveWhereShort`, `:140`, and `takeIn`, `:173`), an orphan's service home in the nearest village by hex steps (`serviceHome`, `src/sim/heirs.js:203-217`), a guardian and an orphan's kin home from anywhere (`careForOrphans`, `service.js:38-59`; `kinHomeFor`, `heirs.js:220`), and the tie to the culture's centre by an unweighted search of 160 steps, or 1.6 times the hex steps over the sea (`updateTies`, `src/sim/society.js:53-73`). A mountain ridge or a fjord between two villages 10 hex steps apart does not count. Read the walking hours of VB-286 instead, with the design's two reaches: there and back in a day (6 h) for what a pawn works at from home, and a day's walk (12 h) for a move.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Claims on empty farms: a claimant's village must lie within 6 walking hours of the farm's village (`CLAIMS.farmHours` 6, replacing `farmReach`), since the claimant's household mends the farm from home before it moves in. VB-281 measured the pairs of villages within 15 hex steps today: 13 at `medium-ref`, 3.6 to 6.9 hours apart, and 15 at `small-y40`, 3.6 to 10.8 hours; within 6 hours: 14 and 6.
- [x] Service: a servant's village within a day's walk of the holder's (`SERVICE.hours` 12, replacing `reach`), nearest first by hours; the servant moves, so it walks (VB-287). Within 12 hours: 52 pairs at `medium-ref`, 38 at `small-y40`. Every reader of `reachSteps` reads the hours: `takeServants` (through `servantFor`), `serveWhereShort` (VB-090, `service.js:153`) and `takeIn` (VB-308, `service.js:185`, and its servant through `servantFor`).
- [x] An orphan's service home: the smallest household of the village, else of the nearest village by walking hours (by land); none within reach: the nearest by hex steps, as today, until slice 10. Guardians and kin homes: the kin by today's order, among those within walking reach (`hoursBetween` finite); none within reach: as today.
- [x] The tie to the culture's centre: `exp(-h / 8)` for h walking hours from the table, in place of `exp(-d / 25)` for d unweighted steps (25 steps are about 8 hours at 3 tiles an hour); over the sea or beyond reach, today's 1.6 times the hex steps, in hours (`/ SPEED`). The kin term is unchanged. `s.distance` keeps its meaning for the panel, in steps, or becomes hours with the panel's label changed; say which in the PR. The search per settlement a year (`stepsTo`, 160 steps) goes.
- [x] Settlement is unchanged: a party's new site (`SETTLE.reach`, 10 to 30 walking steps) and the small village it may join (`SETTLE.join`, 30 steps) are tile searches from the green (`reachOf`), not distances between villages; the party walks there (VB-287).
- [x] The phases stay where VB-277 leaves them, island-wide on fixed steps at 18:00, 18:06 and 18:12 (proposed answer to `QUESTIONS.md`, "VB-281", question 2); only the distance they read changes.
- [x] Behaviour changes, so report before and after (targets with residuals): over populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years, and one year from `medium-ref` and `small-y40`: claims made, by the hours between the two villages; servants taken, by hours; orphans placed; ties' mean and spread, and customs over 20 years (`--report customs`), since the tie drives the culture's drift and splits; `npm run calibrate` (`natural 12 200`), the median first flip of each custom against the bands of Pace. The golden file re-recorded, with the changed entries listed; `tests/counters.json` if it moves.
- [x] Cost: every read is one table entry; the tie's yearly search per settlement (29 at `medium-ref`) goes. Name it measured: `--report perf` on both fixtures before and after.
