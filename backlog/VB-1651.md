# VB-1651: The played household's land projects side by side; a hand-planned start with nothing under way; the plan says why a task waits

Design ref: docs/ui2/HANDOFF.md (round 4, finding 1; For the next session, 1); backlog/VB-1650.md; backlog/VB-1472.md; src/sim/playerplan.js; src/sim/plans.js (landWhy, landStep, the verb project); src/sim/projects.js (ownOf, landProject); src/sim/agenda.js; src/sim/scenarios.js (seedFounders, solo)
Goal: A player who plans by hand never meets a plan that stands still without the screen being able to say why, and may have several fields and meadows under way at once.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The stall, as a test first (`tests/plan.js`, a small constructed state): the played pawn heads a household with a field project open that no task covers, automatic behaviour off; a `plant` task is added on a second tile and a `hay` task on a third. Today both stand for ever (`chainCmd` waits on `ownOf`); after this task each is begun in its turn and carried through.
- [x] Land projects side by side, for the played head only: the head's verb `project` with what `field` or `meadow`, from source `player`, is not refused because another project of the household's is under way (`landWhy`); one project a tile, and the tile's own checks, still hold. The holder's own rule (the land step, source `pawn`) and every unplayed head keep today's limit: how parallel projects are limited and ranked for them is VB-1472's, with Kim. The open steps of several land projects are each posted as their own job with keys that do not meet (`landProject` keys by kind and farm today); a run with one project at a time keeps its keys, so that `node tools/same.js --base REF` shows founders and solo with no played pawn unchanged. Whatever reads "the" project of a household (`ownOf`, `openOf`, `headView().project`, `dropProject`, the stakes, the seed reserved, `sim/agenda`) is right with several: `headView` gains `projects` (each `{id, what, step, tile}`; `project` stays the first), and a step done ends its own plan.
- [x] A task at a tile no longer waits for other projects: on a marked tile, or on any tile the verb would take (the page then needs no mark: `Sim.plan(st, 'add', {plant: tile})` is accepted for a tile where the head's `project` check passes, and the feeder begins the project there in the task's turn). A tile under 0 is refused (today `{plant: -1}` is accepted while no tile is marked, since `plan.tiles.field` is -1).
- [x] With automatic behaviour off, nothing is begun that the player did not plan: the played head's household begins no project by its own rule (the land step, the head's yearly look: read as `heldOff` does), while the player's own verbs go through. And the solo start for a hand planner: `Sim.seed(w, seed, 'solo', null, {site, hand: true})` seeds with no field or meadow project under way (today the first field's clearing is begun at seeding); without `hand` the seed is as today.
- [x] Why a task waits, for the page: each task of `Sim.planView(st)` gains `waits`: null while it is being worked or is what the plan does next, else plain data naming the one reason, of a small closed list declared in one place, e.g. `{why: 'hand', verb, target, since}` (a command queued by hand goes first), `{why: 'before', i}` (an earlier task is worked first), `{why: 'opens', t, what}` (its next step is not posted until its window opens, the crop is ripe, the hay is dry), `{why: 'refused', text}` (the verb's own reason: no room, not the pawn's to take, the head's check), `{why: 'sleep', until}`, `{why: 'routine', kind}`. `planView` also gains `idle`: null, or the reason the plan as a whole has the pawn do nothing now though it holds tasks. The same reads `nextOf` uses, no second rule; writes nothing. `Sim.agenda(st)` lays a waiting task's steps no earlier than its reason allows (an `opens` at its `t`), so the day strip shows nothing that will not happen.
- [x] A command queued by hand that cannot go on is not left at the head of the queue without a reason: `planView().idle` names it with the hour it was begun, so the page can offer to take it off (`Sim.unqueue`).
- [x] Tests on small constructed states (`tests/plan.js`, `tests/agenda.js`, `tests/plans.js` or `tests/projects.js`): two fields begun side by side by the played head and both carried to sowing; an unplayed head still refused a second; each `waits` reason; the `hand` seed; a save and a load go on the same. `npm run verify` passes; the lint and ownership baselines do not rise.
