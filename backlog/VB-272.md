# VB-272: Marriage offers: each match is an offer both households answer, the player's among them
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Marriage as a negotiation: The offer, Who negotiates, Consent, The player's doors; Leaving home); The player and what they see (Marriage). Survey and plan: `backlog/VB-233.md`.
Goal: The moving round's matcher (`marriageRound`, `src/sim/lifecycle.js`) marries each pair it finds at once, so the player has no say. Make each match an offer that the two who marry and their spokesmen answer. A side that is not the player answers yes at once, so a run with no player is unchanged. An offer the player must answer waits up to 7 game days, as plain data with a due time in `st.offers`, and the world goes on meanwhile. This is the first question answered through `Decisions.answer` (here `Sim.answer`; slice 5's Decisions module takes it over).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `spokesmanOf(st, id)` in `src/sim/records.js`: the holder of the pawn's household when the pawn lives there as a son or daughter of the house (a child of the holder, of the holder's spouse or of the holder's parent, not in service, and not the holder); otherwise the pawn itself.
- [ ] `st.offers`: the open offers, sorted by `due`, each `{id, man, woman, home, village (the man's), made, due (made + 7 × DAY), answers: [{key: 'man' | 'woman' | 'manSide' | 'womanSide', by (pawn id), yes: null | true | false}]}`. A spokesman who is also the one to marry gives one answer. Ids come from a counter in `st` and are never reused. Owner: `sim/lifecycle` (in `src/owners.json`; planned to move to Decisions in slice 5). `@state` line, invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js` (sorted by due; due at most 7 days after made; no pawn in two open offers; each answer's `by` is the man, the woman or their spokesman at the time the offer was made), and saved with the run.
- [ ] In `marriageRound`, where it issues `marry` today (`lifecycle.js:255`), the pair becomes an offer. Every answer whose `by` is not the played pawn is yes at once. If every answer is yes, the round marries them there and then, as today. Otherwise the offer goes into `st.offers`, the woman stays in `taken`, and the matcher goes on. A pawn in an open offer is not free to any man of the round, nor counted as unmatched by `kinPressure`. A refused offer leaves both unmarried until the next round.
- [ ] The verb `answer` in `src/sim/commands.js` (source `player`): target the offer's id, args `{yes}`. It checks that the offer is open and that the played pawn has an unanswered answer in it, and sets all of that pawn's answers. `Sim.answer(st, offerId, yes)` issues it.
- [ ] An offer closes when every answer is yes (the marriage), when one is no (refused), or at its due time, when its unanswered answers become yes, as the pawn would answer unplayed. The day's end looks only at `st.offers[0].due`, so nothing is polled between rounds (Rules for cost, 4). Once VB-241's calendar exists, this is one daily entry, guarded by an open offer. A marriage made at the close goes through the `marry` verb (source `round`). The round's handover for the home household then follows it, as at the round: the rule of `lifecycle.js:272-285` moves into one function that both call, and `refreshRoles` runs. If the verb refuses (one of them has died or married), the offer lapses, and its reason is logged.
- [ ] Events (`tests/record.js`): `offered` when an offer is stored, in the records of both who are to marry, naming the home's farm; `matchRefused` when one is refused, naming who refused; `offerLapsed`. A marriage at the close is the `married` event as today. Matches answered at once write nothing new.
- [ ] Consent's cost: when the played pawn refuses, as the one to marry, an offer whose spokesman on its side is another pawn, that spokesman remembers it: `SOCIAL.memories.refusedMatch` (`Refused the match I made`, v -0.2, cap -0.4, 3 years), in the spokesman's record of the pawn. When the player refuses as a spokesman, nothing is remembered.
- [ ] `skipStop` (`src/sim/notable.js`) stops on an offer that waits for the played pawn's answer (a new `SKIP` reason, `offer`), and `skipFrom` notes the open offers, so a skip that begins with one open does not stop on it at once.
- [ ] Tests (a new `tests/offers.js` in `npm test`). With no player, the golden file and `tests/counters.json` are unchanged (`roundPairs` included). When the played pawn is the man, his match waits: the woman is not married to anyone else in that round; yes marries them at once in the round's home, and the handover follows as it would have; no leaves both unmarried and logs the refusal; no answer marries them at the due time. When the player is head, a son's match waits for its answer, and the son's own answer is yes. When the played young pawn refuses its head's match, the head's opinion of it falls by 0.2. When the man dies with an offer open, it lapses. A save made with an offer open loads and plays the same as the unbroken run.
- [ ] Numbers in the PR and here. Marriages a year at `medium-ref` and `small-y40` after 1 year: unchanged, since there is no player (13 and 7 at `74ee065`). A scripted played run (founders, seed 7, small, 30 years, inhabiting the first holder and going on as heir): offers stored, answered yes, no and unanswered, and marriages of the played household against unplayed households.
- [ ] Cost (Rules for cost, 9): with no player, nothing (no offer is stored; the day's end tests one length). With a player: one stored offer for each match that touches the played pawn or a member it speaks for. At `medium-ref` that is 13 matches a year over 211 households, about 0.06 a household a year, and at most the played household's unmarried adults. Each match adds four `spokesmanOf` calls (O(1): the household's holder and two parents) to the round's 1,455 pair scorings at `medium-ref`. Name it measured: `node tools/sim.js --load medium-ref --years 1 --report perf` before and after.
