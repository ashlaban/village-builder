# VB-272: The gathering: villages choose where to meet, and one hosts
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-183
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Settlements, The gathering (10 Nov); `docs/review-markets.md`, sections 2.3 and 5
Goal: The first thing between villages: a yearly gathering at a host village's green. Villages offer to host and choose where to go, so hosts compete; the villages of one gathering are a district.
Acceptance criteria (draft; VB-183 confirms the numbers):
- [ ] At its autumn meeting (day 10) a village with a green may offer to host; every village then chooses the gathering it will attend that winter, a few days later (winter days 1 to 3, if Kim confirms question 12), by pull: travel hours to the host (on foot; boats as VB-183 answers), distance in customs (hard customs count most), kin living in the villages that chose it last year, the host holders' prestige, whether it was held last year, what the host poured (VB-283) and the disposition between the two villages (read from their holders' opinions and balances, never stored). VB-183 names the weights.
- [ ] A village attends one gathering. A gathering that fewer than three villages chose is not held; its villages choose again among those that stand, once. A village more than a day's walk from every host has none.
- [ ] The choice is village state with an owner (`src/owners.json`), in the `WORLD` list of `src/sim/saves.js`, with invariants: the host is an inhabited village with a green; every attending village is within reach.
- [ ] The district is a derived view (the villages of one gathering), never stored apart from the choice.
- [ ] A village's choice is a routine of its meeting (VB-257), so the player's pawn argues for it by advocacy as for any cause.
- [ ] Cost: one reach search a village a year (`Movement.reachFrom`), spread over the meetings' steps; a counter; measured on `medium-ref`.
- [ ] Reported (`tools/sim.js --report`): gatherings, their hosts and villages, by year; over 40 populous years on small and medium, how many stand and how often a host changes.
- [ ] A test; behaviour otherwise unchanged until VB-273 sends anyone (golden unchanged, or its changes listed).
