# VB-272: The gathering: villages choose where to meet, and one hosts
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-257
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Settlements, The gathering (10 Nov); `docs/review-markets.md`, sections 2.3 and 5
Goal: The first thing between villages: a yearly gathering at a host village's green. Villages offer to host and choose where to go, so hosts compete; the villages of one gathering are a district.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] At its autumn meeting (day 10) a village with a green may offer to host; every village then chooses the gathering it will attend that winter, a few days later (winter days 1 to 3), counted in hours of walking: the host with the fewest effective hours, if under 10. The walk (on foot, by fords and bridges; water is not crossed, so islands gather apart: Kim, 10 Nov); plus up to 4 for customs that differ (hard customs count double); less 0.5 for each household of kin in the villages that went there last year, at most 2; less or plus up to 2 for the disposition between the two villages (read from their holders' opinions and balances, never stored); less 1 if it was held last year; less up to 1 for the ale its host poured (0 until VB-283). A host's own walk is 0. Tunables in `src/sim/rules.js`.
- [ ] A village attends one gathering. A gathering that fewer than three villages chose is not held; its villages choose again among those that stand, once. A village more than a day's walk (10 hours) from every host has none. A host lodges 10 guests a farm (VB-273's lodging); until that rule is agreed, no limit by lodging.
- [ ] The choice is village state with an owner (`src/owners.json`), in the `WORLD` list of `src/sim/saves.js`, with invariants: the host is an inhabited village with a green; every attending village is within reach.
- [ ] The district is a derived view (the villages of one gathering), never stored apart from the choice.
- [ ] A village's choice is a routine of its meeting (VB-257), so the player's pawn argues for it by advocacy as for any cause.
- [ ] Cost: one reach search a village a year (`Movement.reachFrom`), spread over the meetings' steps; a counter; measured on `medium-ref`.
- [ ] Reported (`tools/sim.js --report`): gatherings, their hosts and villages, by year; over 40 populous years on small and medium, how many stand, how often a host changes, and the share of villages with no gathering (islands apart is accepted; the number is for slice 10).
- [ ] A test; behaviour otherwise unchanged until VB-273 sends anyone (golden unchanged, or its changes listed).
