# VB-1491: A village blazes a trail to a neighbour or a daughter it has kin in
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-447
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: far
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Moving to new land (Settling in steps); docs/plans/VB-445.md, sections 3 and 5
Goal: A village cut off by the wood from a neighbour or a daughter it has kin in opens the way, as a village duty decided at its meeting and carried out by its alderman.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] At its meeting a village decides to blaze a trail to a neighbour or a daughter it has kin in and cannot reach in a day there and back (6 hours, `SETTLE.hours`, by the villages' table), where wood with no path lies between; its alderman issues `blaze` (`sim/trails`, VB-447) from its green to the other's, a village duty (VB-314; principle 16).
- [ ] Whose hands: today `blaze` is worked by the blazer's household. A village's trail is the village's work: decide whether it is worked as a work day (`sim/workdays`) or owed as days of work (`sim/obligations`), and record why.
- [ ] A constructed check: two villages with kin between them, 8 hours apart through forest, are joined by a trail within a season; one with no kin there is not.
- [ ] Numbers before and after at populous small and medium, seeds 7 and 42, on the forested island: village pairs within 6, 12 and 24 walking hours at years 5 and 15, trails blazed, hours of blazing a village a year.
- Cost: one route search a meeting that decides it (bounded by `ROUTE.reach`); the jobs are a trail's (at most about 35 wooded tiles within 6 hours).
Notes:
- 23 Nov 2026: opened by VB-447 (its criterion "Who blazes, and when": the village's trail is a task of its own). VB-314 is done.
