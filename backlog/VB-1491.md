# VB-1491: Page: the overview's repaint is painted within the frame budget
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: src/page/chunks.js; docs/cost.md
Goal: A change to the overview's colours no longer costs a frame more than the painter's budget: the level-2 repaint goes through the same budgeted painting as the close levels, and the old picture stays up until the new one is ready.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `repaintTiles` no longer runs `chunkJob` to its end inside the frame for a level-2 chunk: the overview's repaint is painted by `schedule()` within its budget, and the chunk's old picture stays on screen until the new one is ready (as the close levels do since VB-1492).
- [ ] Measured in the browser on `#ps123` at 'as fast as it goes': the longest frame's painting time at a change of the overview, before and after, reported in the PR.
- [ ] The overview is never missing: a check in `tests/page.js` that every level-2 chunk stays resident and visible through a repaint.
- [ ] `npm test` and `npm run verify` pass.

Notes:
- Found while diagnosing the flicker of VB-1492 (23 November): a likely cause of stutter, not of flicker. Not yet measured on its own: a level-0 chunk took about 21 ms to paint in software-rendered Chromium, and a change can touch all six overview chunks in one frame.
- The yearly burst of tree changes (about 800 tiles in one step each spring) is the case to measure. Edits `src/page/chunks.js`, as VB-1490 does: one at a time.
