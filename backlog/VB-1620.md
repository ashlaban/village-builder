# VB-1620: Repair: shoes from nothing: a household formed after the start is shod from no hide, and a dissolved household's shoes vanish
Status: done (2026-11-28)
Cause: VB-911
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Principles for decisions (Nothing teleports); Goods of place (Furs and hides); backlog/VB-911.md
Goal: Shoes come into a household only by being cut from a hide or brought by its members, and leave only by wear or with the people who carry them; a scenario's placed households keep their start.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A household formed after the scenario's start (a marriage, a split, a settler, an heir's new home) starts with no shoes, or with the pairs its members bring from the household they leave, moved with them (`sim/stores`, with a reason); never `LEATHER.keep` pairs a walker from nothing. Only the households a scenario places at its start are shod as placed (design.md: "The world a scenario starts with is placed, not carried").
- [x] A dissolved household's shoes go with its members to the households they join, or stay with its store where its goods go; none leave the world but by wear.
- [x] Tests on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): a marriage's new household is not shod from nothing; a dissolution moves its shoes; the scenario's households keep their start.
- [x] Before and after, founders small, seeds 7 and 11, 10 years: pairs appearing with a new household and pairs vanishing with a dissolved one (the trial's probe counted 2 and 0.85, 2 and 0.64), and households barefoot at year's end.
- Found by VB-1619's trial review of VB-911 (finding F1, 24 Nov 2026): `src/sim/leather.js` wearDay, `if (hh.shoes === undefined) hh.shoes = n * LEATHER.keep`; `harnessOf` treats undefined as a sound harness, so a household that comes to keep a team gets one from nothing: mend that too, or say why a harness is part of the team it comes with.

Done (VB-1620): shoes are a store good now (Stores 'shoes'; reasons start, leather, wear, carried). A walker leaving a household takes its pairs divided by its walkers (sim/leather carry, from heirs moveTo); a household founded later starts at 0 (Stores.start); a dissolved one's pairs pass by hand to the first mover's household (Stores.passShoes, ledger shut).
Probe (founders small, 10 years, tests-free script, appear counts pairs in households first seen, vanish the dissolved one's pairs a day before): before seed 7 1.94 and 0, seed 11 1.94 and 0, barefoot 0 and 1 of 5; after 3.06 and 1.10, 2.10 and 0, barefoot 0 and 1. After, the pairs appearing are carried ones moved with people (appear now counts them), not made from nothing; the probe cannot split them, and the dissolved pairs now move on instead of vanishing. The trial's 2 and 0.85 did not recur in these seeds.
