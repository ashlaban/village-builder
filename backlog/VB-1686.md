# VB-1686: Invariant: no decision reads an unrealised draw
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/review-principles-2026-11-24/synthesis.md (Invariant catalogue, M5); design.md, Principles for decisions (The future is someone's estimate); tests/dependencies.js; src/sim/estimate.js
Goal: A pawn decides by what it could know: a value the simulation has drawn for a time not yet come is read only by its owner and the natural processes that realise it, and every decider reads an expectation instead.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Each such field is tagged (`@drawn`, beside `@state`): the year's frost at least, and the weather drawn ahead, if any.
- [ ] `tests/dependencies.js` allows only the owner and the natural processes (`sim/clock`, `sim/fields`, `sim/meadows` and the like) to read a tagged field; a planted read from an agency module fails the check.
- [ ] Every current read by a decider goes through an expectation accessor (the custom's or the pawn's memory), and the PR names each read moved and whether households were better or worse off.
