# VB-1615: Sleep for the played pawn: hours owed, the sleep verb, and what a lost night costs
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/ui2/HANDOFF.md; design.md, The player and what they see (Needs and dark); src/sim/player.js; src/sim/mood.js; src/sim/hunger.js (the health event)
Goal: The hours of a day are scarce for a player who plans by hand: working a night is a choice with a cost.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] State: the hours of sleep the played pawn owes (plain data, saved; on `st.player` or where target-v2 section 4 places it, with an `@state` line and its owner in `src/owners.json`). Each game hour awake adds `need / (24 - need)` hours (need 8: half an hour), each hour asleep takes one off, never under 0, capped. Asleep: indoors where it sleeps (its house, its booth, or its bed under the open sky before the booth stands; VB-1544) with no act in hand, or under the `sleep` verb. The values are one `@tunable SLEEP`.
- [ ] A verb `sleep` the player may queue (`register`, sim/commands; source `player`): the pawn leaves the job in hand (which keeps its progress), goes to where it sleeps and sleeps until `args.until` (an hour of the day; by default the start of work) or for `args.hours`; the command lasts until then, and the next in the queue is begun after it. Refused with its reason for the dead or one with no place to sleep.
- [ ] Planned work at night stays possible (a command still overrides dark; design.md, Needs and dark) and now costs sleep. With nothing queued, the pawn still goes home at dark and sleeps.
- [ ] Mood: a modifier `tired` (sim/mood, read from the state like `hungry`): nothing under a small allowance of hours owed, then by the hours owed, to a cap; a whole night lost is felt clearly (about as hungry is). Health: a smaller cost, only once a whole night or more is owed, at the day's health reckoning (the `health` event's `why` gains a term the page can show).
- [ ] A read for the page on the `Sim` facade: the hours owed, whether asleep, and the mood and health terms now.
- [ ] A run with no played pawn is unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base REF` on founders and solo. Unplayed pawns do not sleep by this rule yet: note it as an act only the player has (inbox `act`), with a follow-up task for every pawn if the cost allows (docs/cost.md).
- [ ] `tests/sleep.js` on a small constructed state, in `tests/suites.js`: the hours owed rise awake and fall asleep; the verb sleeps until its hour and the queue goes on; a night worked lowers mood by the tunable; health is touched only past the threshold; a save and a load keep the state; `tests/mood.js`, `tests/saves.js`, `tests/plaindata.js` and `tests/commands.js` pass.
