# VB-152: A guardian for an orphaned heir, and a servant for a holder who lives alone
Status: done (2026-11-01)
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: expansion
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion (a minor heir holds in name, with a guardian: the surviving parent or the nearest adult relative); Inheritance and expansion, Leaving home (Service: a landless adult may take service where labour is short; Agreed). Review: `docs/review-stability.md`; `docs/review-cities.md`, section 3.5.
Goal: Households of one are 14–31% of households late in a populous run and hungry in 35–73% of their years. Two causes are fixed here: a minor heir left with no adult in the house kept the farm alone and mostly starved (the guardian was looked for only among the adults of the house), and a holder who lives alone has nobody to share the work.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Daily, a household with no member of 18 or more takes in the ward's nearest landless adult relative who wants land (`st.demand`; by kinship, then the oldest), with spouse and the minor children who live with them, and that relative becomes the guardian (`setGuardian`). With no such relative, the farm stands empty as one that dies out (`extinct`), and each child goes to the nearest kin (`kinNear`, then any kin by kinship) or else into service in the village.
- [x] At the moving round, after marriages and the moves to new land, a holder of 18 or more who lives alone takes in an unmarried adult of 18 to 50 who wants land, as farmhand or maid (`p.servant`), from the holder's village first, then from the nearest village within 15 steps between centres; only from a household that keeps at least 2 working hands (14 to 59) without them; among those, the household that keeps the most hands, then the oldest. The servant still wants land and may marry the holder.
- [x] `SERVICE` in `src/sim/rules.js`; the module `src/sim/service.js`; a life event (`guardian`, `service`) and a log line for each.
- [x] `tests/guardians.js`: a guardian moves in; with none, the farm empties and no child is left alone; a lone holder takes a servant; a household that would keep fewer than 2 hands gives none.
- [x] Re-record the golden file: the heirs runs at seeds 7 and 42 change from year 1 to 3 (a servant goes to a lone holder).

That is the holder–holder marriage of VB-153, not service.
