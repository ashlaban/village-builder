Goal: Each harvest wears a field down; manure from the byre restores it (Agreed). Kim, 31 Oct: wear and manure, as proposed.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `fd.soil` per field, 1.0 at the start; each reaped crop multiplies its yield by the soil and lowers it by 0.08, to a floor of 0.75.
- [ ] The fodder job collects manure on the household's heap: 0.2 loads a head of cattle a stall day, 0.04 a sheep.
- [ ] Spring day 1–6: a field in stubble with manure on the heap posts `manure`, 1 h a load, any hands, tilling work; each load raises the field's soil by 0.04, to at most 1.15, the field with the lowest soil first.
- [ ] Page: a manure heap by the byre in spring; the tile card shows a field's soil.
- [ ] Founders and populous at seed 7, 10 years: soil per farm by herd size (reported); a test; re-record the golden file.
