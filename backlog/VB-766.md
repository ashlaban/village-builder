# VB-766: Crowding: a contribution to death.hazard by the people within 2 steps of a house
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: town
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Balance loops and targets (The balance review: Deaths; The growth budget: The town); Settlements, Towns and regions (Crowding); docs/plans/VB-189.md, section 5.7
Goal: The urban penalty is crowding (Kim, 16 Nov): living close costs lives, by a named rule with a physical meaning, wherever it happens, and stands in for disease until disease exists.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `CROWD` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each with its meaning: `steps` 2 (hex steps around a house that count as living close), `free` 36 (people within them that cost nothing: about the 99th percentile of a village house, 31 to 41), `per` 0.035 (hazard added for each person beyond), `max` 2.5.
- [ ] A household's crowding: the people living in houses within `steps` of its house, its own among them. Its factor, 1 + `per` x max(0, n - `free`), at most `max`, is compiled into an array: refreshed when a house is founded, left or rebuilt and when people move in or out at the round, never daily; one writer, an owner in `src/owners.json` (a cache; find its owner in target-v2 section 4 first), rebuilt on load and not saved. `tests/lib/invariants.js`: the array equals a fresh count.
- [ ] It contributes to `death.hazard` through the rules registry, declared before the first seed; the death roll reads the array (rule 6; `value()` allocates nothing). `explain` names it ("crowded: 44 people within 2 steps").
- [ ] No rule reads whether a place is a town (`design.md`, Towns and regions: a label no rule reads): the contribution is by household, wherever its house stands.
- [ ] A constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): ten houses within 2 steps of each other at five people each give 1.49; four give 1; the factor never passes 2.5; a house left lowers its neighbours' factor at the refresh.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 40 years, and founders small, seed 7, 50 years: the share of households above factor 1 and the highest factor by decade; deaths per 1,000; yearly growth (principle 2). Rural demography should barely move (the plan measured a median of 14 to 17 people within 2 steps, a 99th percentile of 31 to 41, a densest house of 52 on seed 7 at year 40: factor 1.56). Households, not only the mean: which households pay it.
- [ ] Parameterisation (at most five short runs, seeds and map named): `free` and `per`, so that under 2% of village households pay anything and ten plot households packed close pay about 1.5; the values tried and what each gave in the task file; the rest as a `tuning` note.
- Cost (rule 9): households (about 170 at `medium-ref`) x a refresh on a house's or a round's change x the 19 tiles within 2 steps, by an index of houses by tile; nothing a day. A declared counter for the refresh, before and after in `tests/counters.js`.
