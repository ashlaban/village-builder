# VB-1029: Faces age: hair greys, a beard comes at 18, the old have lines
Status: done (2026-11-18)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (15)
Goal: A person's age can be read from its face, so a life can be told in portraits.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Hair, beard and braid grey in three steps of six years, from an age that is the pawn's own (38 to 52, from its `look` seed, drawn after the other picks so that no look changes), to grey; the same colour on the map's figure and in the portrait (`agedLook`, `src/page/rigs.js`).
- [x] A beard comes at 18: a boy of a bearded look had his father's beard at three.
- [x] The portrait of one whose hair is two steps grey has lines under the eye and by the mouth, and at three across the brow: a hood or a married woman's headcloth hides the hair, not the face.
- [x] The map's figure is retinted when its age's step changes (`ageRig`, asked while the pawn is drawn), with no rebuild of the rigs; the portrait's cache key has the step.
- [x] `tests/browser-bonds.js` checks the steps, the beard, the figure and a married woman's portrait.

- 18 Nov 2026 (Kim, the session on the life story): chosen from the improvements a played life showed (Håkan Ivarsson at 26 and at 49 had one face).
