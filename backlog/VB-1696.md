# VB-1696: A stake names its store: title to grain where it lies
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Principles for decisions (Nothing teleports: title moves at once, custody by a journey); QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Archived 24 November 2026, question 4; src/sim/stores.js; tests/lib/invariants.js
Goal: Title moves at once and custody by a journey: a stake, a share or a wage owed is title to goods in a named store, never a number that travels with its holder.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `pawn.stake` (and any other title to goods held as a bare number) names the store its goods lie in; a pawn that moves leaves the grain where it lies, and what it eats on the road is what it carried (VB-637's wage, VB-672's pattern).
- [ ] An invariant: the titles on a store sum to at most its stock, and using goods requires custody (synthesis, M7).
- [ ] The save and an old save; `tools/stocks.js` lines for titles on a store.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (`--report accounts`, populous small seed 7, 50 years): the food account's `unexplained`.
