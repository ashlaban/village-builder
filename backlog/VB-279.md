Goal: The second good of place (Kim, 10 Nov), grounded as Kim asked in a need and in standing: a boat untarred is lost, tarred timber lasts, and a tarred farm is seen to be well kept.
Acceptance criteria (draft; VB-183 confirms the numbers):
- [ ] A resource reach test per village (pine forest within reach). A tar pit (*tjärdal*) is a facility one household builds and owns; burning is long work in the slack of early summer, from pine wood, giving tar by the barrel.
- [ ] A boat (VB-117) needs a barrel every second year: untarred for two years it fishes at half the catch, for four it is lost. This is the need that makes shore villages want tar.
- [ ] Tar on a farm's timber halves the upkeep hours its buildings ask for 6 years, where upkeep exists; if it does not yet, the criterion moves to the task that adds it, named here.
- [ ] A tarred farm counts toward its holder's standing only if the answer to `QUESTIONS.md` (VB-183, the question on prestige) says so.
- [ ] Tar is a keeping good. The page draws the pit smoking and tarred buildings darker.
- [ ] Numbers before and after as for dried fish, and boats lost for want of tar where no fair is in reach.
