Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A rung is one answer to 'where do I live next year': stay (as heir, or at home), marry onto a holding, a plot or an empty farm, a place in service, new land. A pawn's wish (VB-680) ranks them by its traits and means; it holds one open offer about its own future at a time, and when that is refused or lapses it makes the next. A match may ride on a place rung (a pair with a plot marries onto it).
- [ ] Offers on the bases of a life (VB-690's table) carry `due` the round; an urgent one (a ward, a household taken in) is due at once. The round's steps answer what is due, a village a step as today (rule 5), and make the moves as journeys.
- [ ] Whose yes: the pawn's own and the receiving head's always; the head left only for a marriage or a minor (Kim). An adult leaves for service or new land without leave; each case's rule is one function, read by every basis.
- [ ] The played pawn makes and answers these offers by the same verbs, through the year; `st.player.wishes` is gone into the pawn's wish.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7 and 42 (`tools/marriage-demography.js` where marriages move): marriages, service, claims, settlers, the never-married at 45 to 54, growth; and offers open at the round and answered (cost, rule 9; no pass over pairs).
