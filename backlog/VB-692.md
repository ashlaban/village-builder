# VB-692: The round concludes, it does not choose: offers about a life fall due at the moving round

Goal: The round's steps conclude the open offers on VB-690's queue, in an order that is stated; a pawn follows its own wish (VB-680) one rung at a time; a move needs the yes of the pawn and of the head it comes to, and of the head it leaves only for a marriage (as spokesman, where the custom gives him a voice) or one under age.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The round lasts `ASK.passes` (3) days, autumn days 11 to 13 (`src/sim/rules.js`, each number with its meaning), each village in its step as today and the starting village turning by the year. A village's step answers the offers on VB-690's queue that are due and addressed to its people, in the order `handover`, `match`, `land`, `party`, `place`; a pawn refused on a day may make its next offer that evening, due the next day; what is open after the last day lapses. The clock's table (`--report calendar`) shows the three days.
- [x] Today's choosers become askers, so that this task changes the frame and not the choices: the matcher's pick, the claim's first claimant, the plot, the settler who leaves and the servant placed are each made as an offer due at once, on VB-690's bases (`match` as VB-690 leaves it; `land`, `party`, `place`, `handover` added as rows of its table with today's rule as how the one asked answers), and concluded by the step. With `ASK.passes` set to 1 and no played pawn, `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`, or the differences are listed with their cause (the order of draws).
- [x] Whose yes is one function, `moveWhy(st, pawn, household, reason)` (plan, section 5), read by every basis and by `move` (VB-822): the pawn's own and the receiving head's, always; the head left only for a marriage (as spokesman) or for one under 18; a minor only with the grown pawn who keeps it. An adult leaves for service or new land without leave. A test for each case.
- [x] One open offer about its own future a pawn: `pawn.ask` (the offer's id or null; `@state`, `src/owners.json`, the save, an invariant in `tests/lib/invariants.js`: the offer exists, is open and is from or for that pawn). A pawn takes part only in the step of the offer it holds; its wish (VB-680; plan, section 3) names which.
- [x] The played pawn makes and answers these offers by the same verbs: it is asked when an offer is made and may answer until it is due, then its pawn's judgement answers (an offer made during the round keeps `MARRIAGE.offerDays`, 7); `st.player.wishes` is gone into the pawn's wish.
- [x] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7 and 42, with `ASK.passes` 3: marriages, service, claims, plots, settlers, the never-married at 45 to 54, growth; marriages a year and the mean age at first marriage within 5% of before, the never-married at 45 to 54 within 2 points (`tools/marriage-demography.js`), growth by principle 2; a target out is reported (before, after, residual, cause), not tuned beyond five short runs over this task's own parameters. Offers open at the round and answered, a day (`offersAnswered` in `VB.COUNTERS`; `tests/counters.json` re-recorded, the changes listed).
- [x] Cost (plan, section 7): at most one evaluation an open offer a day of the round; no pass over pairs is added, and the round's steps stay under the 60 ms a step of `docs/cost.md` (reported by the nightly bench, not measured here). `tests/commands.js` and `tests/record.js` pass; tests on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): the order of the bases in a step; a refusal on day 1 answered anew on day 2; a lapse after day 3.

- 17 Nov 2026 (VB-692, built). **What was done.** The round lasts `ASK.passes` 3 days (`src/sim/rules.js` `ASK`: `passes`, `day` 10
  (sday, 0-based: autumn days 11 to 13), `order`); the clock holds one row a day (`--report calendar`: season 2, sday 10, 11, 12). On each
  day a village's step first answers the offers due to its people (`answerDue` in `sim/round`: the basis's `where`, sorted by
  `ASK.order`, each concluded once through `sim/offers`' `conclude`, `VB.count.offersAnswered`); the first day then runs the round as
  before. Its choosers now ask: the matcher's pick (`match`, as VB-690 left it), the handover by custom (`handover`, `sim/marriage`), the
  first claimant and each plot (`land`, `sim/farmclaims`: asked of the village's eldest living holder until VB-314's alderman, `askLand`;
  the plot begun on the event `plotGranted` in `sim/expansion`), the settler's party (`party`, `sim/settle`) and the servant placed
  (`place`, `sim/service`), each an offer due at once whose answers are `moveWhy`'s and whose rule is today's. A match refused (unplayed)
  asks anew that evening, due the next day (`askAgain`: the best other within reach, the refuser left out, no go-ahead roll; not for the
  played pawn, not on the last day); after the last day `lapseRound` lapses the round's offers (`round: true`) still open.
  `moveWhy(st, id, hh, reason)` is in `sim/wishes` (it loads before `sim/farmclaims` and `sim/expansion`, which may not look up
  `sim/commands`; and the width baselines): the pawn's own yes ('self'; not a minor's under `care`), the receiving head's ('head'), the
  head left ('left': the spokesman for a marriage, the holder for a minor); a minor whose keeper lives with it in a standing household goes
  into service only with that keeper (the `move` verb refuses it alone), while a dissolved household's minors are placed as before. The
  match row adds the home's head where neither of the two nor a spokesman is he (no at enmity towards the one moving in).
  `pawn.ask` (`@state` in `sim/offers`, owner `sim/offers` in `src/owners.json`, saved with the pawn, absent when null): set when an offer
  with a subject is kept, cleared when it closes or lapses; `offerOf` refuses a second; invariants check both ways. The choosers skip a
  pawn holding an ask (claims, plots, settlers, service, the matcher). The played pawn is asked by `make` as before (kept
  `MARRIAGE.offerDays`), answers with `answer`, may make any of these with `offer` (`args.round` for one due at the round);
  `st.player.wishes` went into the pawn's wish in VB-680 (migration in `sim/saves`, invariant). `sim/offers` lost its dead `carrierOf`
  (sim/help has its own) and gained `conclude`: 15 exports, as before. Rows may set `memory: false` (a no told, nobody remembers it),
  `subject`, `where`, `what`.
