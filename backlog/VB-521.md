# VB-521: The talk round by turns, and encounters as one table: house, outdoors, pasture, occasion
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: talk
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, People, households and family records (Traits, talk and stories: Where pawns meet; Cost); docs/architecture/target-v2.md, section 4.4 (Encounters); backlog/VB-186.md
Goal: Talk and contacts are the game's largest social cost (55.3 ms a game day at medium-ref against a share of 55), and half of it is asking every living pawn every waking hour. Give each pawn a talk turn every fourth hour, let a talk stand for a longer conversation, and make who meets whom one table of kinds that venues join: the slice's saving, taken first so that stories and venues are built inside it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The talk round goes by turns (`design.md`, Where pawns meet): a pawn has a talk turn in the waking hours where `(hour + id) % SOCIAL.talk.every` is 0 (`every` 4, so four a day), and at its turn starts a talk at `SOCIAL.talk.chance` (1; VB-520 makes it sociability's). The hourly round walks only the pawns whose turn it is (the living bucketed by `id % every`, a cache with one writer, or an equal walk: not a pass over all the living with a skip). A pawn may still be drawn as a partner in any hour, and talks at most once an hour.
- [x] `SOCIAL.ties.talk` is 0.8 (it was 0.5): a talk stands for a longer conversation, and a day's talk builds the tie it built (6.6 talks × 0.5 before; about 4.2 × 0.8). The waking hours at home are counted at the turn, `every` at a time, so the separate hourly pass for `pawn.homeHours` goes; its invariant holds.
- [x] `sim/encounters` owns who meets whom (target-v2, section 4.4, Encounters; a module that `sim/relations`' talk asks for partners): a table `ENCOUNTERS` of kinds, each `{ present, sample, mix, said }`: `house` (the members inside) and `outdoors` (the cells of 30 units, moved here from `sim/society`'s `outdoors` and `sim/relations`' `partnersOf`, verbatim where they move), as built; `occasion`: a pawn standing at an occasion (`agent.occasion`, arrived) draws up to 8 of those who came, indoors or out, weighed by familiarity and liking plus `mix` (0.2; a kind of occasion may name its own), so that strangers are drawn; `pasture`: a pawn at a herding job draws from the herders within `ENCOUNTERS.pasture.reach` 120 units, kept in a second grid of that cell, filled in the pass that fills the first.
- [x] `Encounters.present(st, occasionId)` reads those who came; `said(st, occasionId, by)` is the one door by which something is made known to all present (VB-522's rite and VB-523's stories use it; nothing calls it in this task). Encounters read no plan and have only social effects.
- [x] Herders meet on the pasture (Kim, 12 Nov, on VB-136's question 3: this plan says whether, and it says yes): the heirs run of `tests/record.js` (seed 7, 3 years) reports gifts and close friends before and after (7 close friends before herding, 4 with it).
- [x] Counters: `talkTurn` (the pawns asked, 4 a pawn-day, where it would have been 16) declared in `VB.COUNTERS`; `talkWeighed` reported; `node tests/counters.js --update` records the intended change, listed in the task file. No time is a result here: the nightly bench gives talk's share (55.3 ms a game day at `medium-ref` on 10 November, share 55), and the profile's share of the run in `talkHour` is reported as a description (7.7% on populous small, year 1; 5.2% in the plan's trial).
- [x] Parameterisation (at most five short runs, populous small, seed 7, 3 years, `--report relations`): `every` 4 against 2 and 8, and the tie a talk 0.7, 0.8 and 1, so that the shares of close friends, friends, dislikes and enemies and the friends across villages stay within a tenth of before (2.8%, 7.2%, 6.7%, 0.2%; 0.35 a pawn) at the fewest turns; the values tried and what each gave. The plan's trial at `every` 4, chance 1 and 0.7 a talk gave 4.19 talks a pawn-day, 2.8%, 7.3%, 6.2%, 0.2% and 0.36, with marriages and births unchanged.
- [x] Customs keep their pace: the ties' weights relative to each other are what `Culture.dailyStep` reads, so report the customs changed at meetings and the causes taken up over 30 populous years (small, seeds 3, 7, 11) and 50 founders years (small, seeds 7, 42), before and after, with growth, marriages and hungry household-days; a custom moving more than a generation faster or slower is a `tuning` note, not a retune here.
- [x] Tests on a constructed state: a pawn talks only at its turn and at most once an hour; two guests in a host's house at an occasion can talk (today only housemates can indoors); two herders 100 units apart can, two at 150 cannot; `mix` lets two strangers at an occasion be drawn; a save made mid-day and loaded gives the same talks (`tests/saves.js`, `tests/plaindata.js`).

- 16 Nov 2026 (VB-521, implemented). Impact note: a new module `sim/encounters` (social layer, after `sim/traits` in the manifest),
  no state of its own; it reads `st.living`, `st.agents` (`inside`, `x`, `y`, `path`, `occasion`, `side`), `st.households[].members`
  and `st.occasions` (`come`, `kind`), and draws on the talk stream. `sim/relations`' talk asks it for partners
  (`partners(st, a, near, busy, round, found)`, which returns the kind's mix); `sim/clock` builds the hour's grid with its
  `outdoors` (moved verbatim from `sim/society`, which loses it, with the pasture grid filled in the same pass). New coupling: the
  pasture kind reads a herder by `agent.side.background.kind === 'herd'` (sim/agents' field, sim/grazing's kind), as plain data.
  - The round by turns: `SOCIAL.talk.every` 4, `chance` 1 (was 0.5 every hour). The turns are the living bucketed by `id % every`,
    a derived cache in `sim/relations` (one writer) rebuilt when `st.living` changes (its array, length or last id: ids only grow,
    so these detect every change); the hour walks only its bucket. At its turn a pawn counts `every` hours at home if inside
    (`pawn.homeHours`; the hourly pass over the living for it is gone; 16 waking hours / 4 = 4 turns, so the invariant's bound holds).
    Anyone may be drawn as a partner in any hour; `busy` still keeps one talk an hour.
  - `SOCIAL.ties.talk` 0.8 (was 0.5). Kinds: `house` and `outdoors` as built (verbatim, the draw shared by a `draw` helper);
    `pasture` (reach 120, Euclidean, up to 8 herders from a second grid of 120-unit cells) added to outdoors for a herder;
    `occasion` (a pawn at an occasion it has arrived at: up to 8 of `o.come` standing there, indoors or out, `mix` 0.2, a kind of
    occasion may name its own in `ENCOUNTERS.occasion.kinds`). The weight is `max(0, talkWeight) + mix`, so house, outdoors and
    pasture (mix 0) weigh as before. A member standing at an occasion is no longer counted among its housemates inside.
    `present(st, id)` (those who came and stand there) and `said(st, id, by)` (those who hear, everyone present but the speaker;
    nothing calls it yet) are exported.
  - Decisions (inbox, `decision`): pasture added to outdoors rather than replacing it; the mix as an additive floor; 0.8 kept over
    0.7 (both within a tenth); the turns as a closure cache keyed on `st.living`; occasion guests out of the house kind, and the
    talk stream for all draws. A defect found and noted as VB-561: the house kind and `homeHours` read `agent.inside`, which does
    not say which house (a pawn indoors at another farm counts as at home).
