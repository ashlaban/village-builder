# VB-718: Surplus dung goes to tilth
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: animals
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Yield over time; Soil); docs/review-yields.md (section 4); docs/review-yields.md (section 4.2)
Goal: The ratio of stock to arable matters above the soil's cap, and a field in courses does not lose tilth for wanting less dung (Kim, 16 Nov).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `TILTH.surplus` 2 in `src/sim/rules.js`: the most loads of manure a field takes in a year beyond what its soil asks (Kim, 16 Nov; an estimate, no source).
- [x] `manureWanted` (`src/sim/fields.js`): as today, the heap goes first to the farm's poorest field not yet sown, until its soil is at `SOIL.cap`. Once no field of the farm wants dung for its soil and the heap still holds a load, the job is posted for a field in the same stages the manure job takes today (not yet sown), the one with the least tilth (`tilthOf`, VB-717) first, for as many loads as it may still take this year. Those loads raise tilth only (`manureDone` already adds `TILTH.perLoad` a load; the soil stays at its cap; a fresh field above the cap takes surplus loads too and its soil is not lowered).
- [x] The year's count without a pass (Rules for cost 3 and 4): a field stores the surplus loads it took and the year it took them (`fd.surplus`, `fd.surplusYear`, or one pair; plain data, `@state`, owner `sim/fields`), read as 0 when the year is not this one; nothing resets it at the year's turn. Invariant: 0 ≤ the count ≤ `TILTH.surplus`, the year ≤ this year.
- [x] Nothing teleports and every act is a pawn's: surplus loads are carted by the same `manure` job (1 hour a load, the cart's rates), posted by the head on the plan's day as the others are; no new verb. A decision for the task, written in its file: whether surplus loads are as urgent as the loads to the cap (today the heap is carted before the hoe) or come after hoeing and sowing. Start with after: dung to spare must not make a household sow late. If the measure below shows no later sowing either way, keep the simpler.
- [x] A check on a small constructed state, with its line in `tests/suites.js`: a farm of 3 unsown fields at the cap with tilth 0.02, 0.05 and 0 and 5 loads on the heap spreads 2 on the third, 2 on the first and 1 on the second; with 10 loads it spreads 6 and 4 stay on the heap; a field below the cap is served first; a sown field takes none; next spring each may take 2 again.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (the base is VB-717's commit), in the task file and the PR, at populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42, 30 years, and founders small, the same seeds, 50 years: loads spread a field-year, to the cap and beyond it; loads that rotted on the heap; hours of the manure job a household-year and its share of spring days 1 to 6; the mean day of sowing (it must not move later by more than half a day; if it does, the surplus loads go after sowing); mean tilth by decade on farms with cattle and without, and by cattle a field; grain reaped a field by decade; hungry household-days and household-years; yearly growth a seed; and the households: the distribution of hungry days, and whether the farms without cattle are worse off than before (they should be unchanged). The drift at populous with surplus dung (reckoned: up to +6% of harvest at year 15 and +11% at year 30 on a well-stocked farm) is reported as measured. Long seeds not run here are owed to VB-662, the criterion unticked with its reason.
- [ ] `TILTH.surplus` tried in at most five short runs (1, 2 and 4 at the least; seeds and map named; what each gave in the task file). 2 stays unless a run shows a defect; a shortfall goes to `docs/tuning.md` as a row.
- [ ] Cost (Rules for cost 9): `manureWanted` already walks one farm's fields (a handful, capped by the farm's labour) when the job is posted in spring days 1 to 6; this adds one `tilthOf` a field on that walk, only when the heap still holds a load and no field wants soil: at most about 700 `exp` a spring day at `medium-ref`, six days a year. No new loop, no cache; the counter of VB-717 shows it. Measured on a late save, counters reported.
- [ ] Targets: `node tests/run.js verify --verbose` before and after, a line for each target moved; `tests/counters.js --update` only for an intended change, said in the PR.
- [x] `design.md` is not edited (Goods and the farming year, Yield over time, already says this); the `@state` line, `docs/architecture/ownership.md` and `tools/stocks.js` (the manure heap's line: what is carted beyond the cap) kept current.
