# VB-309: Held by kin when nobody can come: the farm in trust, its owner unchanged
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-308, Kim
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Households short of people (Held by kin when nobody can come; Proposed for Kim, VB-283); Handover and shares (Splitting land: the old household works the sibling's fields); QUESTIONS.md, VB-283; backlog/VB-283.md
Goal: Of the 56 household-years hungry over 0.3 of households short of people (VB-283's replay at `38b920d`), 42 had nobody to come (no free relative, no servant within reach); 28 of them had a parent's, child's or sibling's household in their village and 5 more within reach, 9 had none. When VB-308 finds nobody, the household's people move to their nearest kin household and that household holds the farm in trust until they can return. Waits for Kim's answers to `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-283 (the trust, and the orphan rule).
Acceptance criteria (as proposed; rewritten from Kim's answers):
- [ ] When VB-308's search finds nobody, the household's people move (`moveTo`) to its nearest kin household within walking reach, ranked as help ranks kin (`kinHouseholds`, `src/sim/help.js`: parents and children before siblings, then by id), that is not itself short of people. With none, nothing happens.
- [ ] The trust: the farm's owner (`st.owner`, `src/sim/ownership.js`), the household's holder and its line are unchanged; the trustee household works the fields as its own work (eligibility `member` of the trustee for the farm's tasks once VB-250 has landed, else the jobs posted to the trustee) and takes the harvest into its store; the house stands empty and decays as an empty farm's does. One record holds the trust (the farm and its trustee), with an index of live trusts kept by its owner (cost rule 1), named in `src/owners.json`.
- [ ] The trust ends at events, never by a daily check: the holder marries (the couple goes back), takes a servant at a moving round, or dies (succession as today); a ward's coming of age (`comeOfAge`, `src/sim/heirs.js`). Under Kim's answer to question 2, an orphaned heir with no guardian is held for the same way, in place of `extinct`.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (the replay, as VB-308): household-years over 0.3 of households short of people, after VB-308, fall to 15 or fewer on the ten runs, and none that had a kin household within reach stays hungry over 0.3 the year after it moved; the trustee households' hungry share before and after (they must not become the hungry ones: their household-years over 0.3 rise by less than the lone ones fall); farms in trust, how long, and how each ended; empty farms and land in use at year 30.
- [ ] Saves, invariants (`checkState`: a trust's farm and trustee live; its people live with the trustee), golden and counters re-recorded with the entries listed; a test on a constructed village.
Cost: one move and one record per case (the replay: at most 42 household-years on 350 run-years, so a few trusts a decade a run); the trust's end is checked at the events above, on their own steps; the trustee's work is its ordinary work over more fields. Name it measured on `small-y40`.
Order: after VB-308 and Kim's answers. Whichever of VB-309 and VB-034 (splitting land, slice 5) lands first builds the relation "a farm worked by a household that does not own it, its harvest shared"; the other reuses it (VB-034's sibling and the trust differ only in the share: half against all).
