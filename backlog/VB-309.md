Goal: Of the 56 household-years hungry over 0.3 of households short of people (VB-283's replay at `38b920d`), 42 had nobody to come (no free relative, no servant within reach); 28 of them had a parent's, child's or sibling's household in their village and 5 more within reach, 9 had none. When VB-308 finds nobody, the household's people move to their nearest kin household and that household holds the farm in trust until they can return. ## Decisions (Deciding without Kim)
- The relation is built here (VB-311 has not landed): the trustee posts the held farm's field work as its own job owner, so eligibility
  needs no new basis (the trustee's members are `member`s) and the harvest goes to its barn by the existing path; VB-311 or VB-034 may take
  the relation over as a grant of use.
- The people take their grain and animals to the trustee (the household's food goes with its mouths, as `handOn` does); at the return they
  take back their share of the trustee's grain by mouths (a store under one roof shared by those who eat from it); the animals stay.
- Only field work is done on the held farm: its meadows, byre, wood and house are not tended (nobody lives there).
- The trustee must not be short of people itself (two grown members, or no fields), so a trust never moves the shortage to the next farm.
- An orphan with no kin household by help's ranking (its parents are dead) is held by the household of its nearest grown kin by kinship
  within reach, as `extinct` sends children to kin (`kinHomeFor`), else the farm stands empty as before.
- At the holder's death the trust closes and succession runs on the held farm as today (an heir moves in; a minor heir alone is then
  taken by the orphan rule). A holder who inherits the trustee's farm hands the held one on (`handOn`), as one who marries onto another.
