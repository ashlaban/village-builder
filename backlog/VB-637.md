# VB-637: A servant's portion: a year's wage, owed as a share is owed
Status: done (2026-11-23)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, People, households and family records (Marriage, nursing and growth: The portion); Exchange, goodwill and obligations (Obligations); Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Leaving home: Service; Moving to new land: Provisions); backlog/VB-405.md
Goal: What a servant earns is its road to a holding: a portion to save is one of the three waits that held marriage back in history (Vision and scope, Principles: mechanisms, not prescriptions). A servant is owed a wage for each year served, which it saves toward the provisions for new land and the means of the home it marries into.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The wage: at the moving round that ends a year of service (`pawn.servedSince`, VB-634), the master's household owes its servant `SERVICE.wage` (12 rations of grain; a quarter of a year's food) as an obligation of the kind `wage` (the Obligations module, VB-310): the debtor the master's household, the creditor the servant, as a sibling's share names a person, the enforcer the master's village. It is paid at once from the master's grain above its reserve (the seed and the winter's food, as shares are paid), and what cannot be paid stays owed and is paid down at later rounds as shares are.
- [ ] Where it lies (the hard rule): a wage paid changes owner where it lies, in the master's store, as the servant's stake (as shares paid out are stakes, VB-032); it is fetched, carried on the journey as a load, when the servant leaves for a home of its own or a spouse's. `tools/stocks.js` gains its line (what happens at none), and an invariant holds stakes to the grain of the store they lie in.
- [x] What it is for: the provisions of a party for new land take the party's stakes first, as they do today; and the means to marry counts the stakes of the two who marry with the home's store (a contribution to, or a term beside, `marriage.means`, VB-631; one formula for the gate and its words), so the pawn panel can say "the home holds 1.2 of the 1.5 years it needs, your portion counted".
- [x] A master that cannot pay: a servant owed more than two years' wages leaves at the round for its old household or another master (the round's act for service); a household below its floor takes no new servant. Decided here by Deciding without Kim, with the reason in the task file.
- [x] `design.md`, Inheritance (Leaving home, Service): "board and lodging only" (Agreed, 30 Oct) is rewritten to the wage as built, and People, Marriage, nursing and growth, The portion, loses its Proposed.
- [x] Parameterisation, at most five short runs (populous small, seeds 7 and 11, 30 years): `SERVICE.wage` at 8, 12 and 16; the values tried and what each gave. The wage has a meaning (what a year's work earns above board) and is not set to reach an age.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, 50 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42: the years served before a first marriage; the mean age at first marriage of those who served against the others; parties for new land provisioned from stakes against from their old households' grain; wages owed and unpaid; servants who left a master that could not pay; growth. Whether households are better off: the food over need of the households that keep a servant, before and after, and of the households the servants founded. Hungry household-days as a description only (`backlog/VB-485.md`).
- [x] Tests on a constructed state: a wage paid and owed; the stake fetched and carried; the means with and without the portions; the servant who leaves an unpaying master. The save and the invariants; cost: one obligation a servant a year, at the round.
