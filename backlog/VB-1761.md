Goal: Whose every working hour of a pawn was is known exactly and cheaply, for the strip, the measure and the judgements that read the tally
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] At each leg's end its hours are booked to one part, decided by the step that opened the task: an outer levy's, owed; the household's within the levy left today, levied; beyond it at the head's call, demanded; of the pawn's own accord, given; a pastime, courting, a visit or rest, kept; levy hours the pawn kept for itself, taken. The night is the pawn's own and unbooked.
- [ ] An invariant (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): for every pawn-day the six parts sum to `H` (12 times its hands); checked on a constructed household and over populous small seed 7, 1 year.
- [ ] The season's tally (demanded, taken, given) by member on the household's plan, folded at the season's end into a yearly count; of the parts nothing is kept past the day but what `Sim.hours(st, pawn, from, until)` needs for the strip's six days behind (say what is kept and why).
- [ ] Sovereignty read from it (by right `1 - levy / H`; as lived `(demanded + given + kept) / H`, beside taken) through `Sim.hours`; no rule reads it as a switch.
- [ ] Cost: 1,000 pawns × 6 to 9 leg ends a day × one booking (under 0.5 ms a game day); the six days behind, 1,000 × 6 × 6 numbers.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10): `npm run verify` green, a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose (never skipped or quarantined) and named in the PR; no stuck pawn (no task held 48 hours without progress, as VB-1546's script counts); the stocks' accounts close (`node tools/sim.js --report accounts`: `unexplained` no larger than before).
- [ ] No village dies out: `node tools/sim.js --scenario founders|populous --map small --seed 7|11|42 --years 10`, all six runs: no settlement extinct by hunger and no collapse of its people (the living at year 10 against year 0, in the PR).
- [ ] Reported in the PR, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in those six runs, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed), before and after.
