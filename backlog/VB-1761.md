# VB-1761: A pawn's hours booked in six parts at each leg's end, and the season's tally

Touches: sim/levies (book, tally, fold); sim/legs (unleg, walked); sim/work; sim/pastimes (of); tests/lib/invariants.js; tests/levies.js
Goal: Whose every working hour of a pawn was is known exactly and cheaply, for the strip, the measure and the judgements that read the tally
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] At each leg's end its working hours (a clock hour times the pawn's hands) are booked by design.md's Whose an hour is: an outer levy's, hands lent (`helper`) and a day hired out by the head, owed, out of the levy left today; the household's or the village's work opened by the head, levied up to the levy left and demanded past it; the same of the pawn's own accord, levied up to the levy left and given past it; a pastime, courting, a visit, rest, leave, a self-hire and any working hour in no leg, kept; levy hours kept by a breach, taken; the head pair's household work given. A leg across the levy left is booked in two parts. The leg carries who opened it (head or pawn). The night is the pawn's own and unbooked.
- [x] An invariant (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): for every pawn-day the six parts sum to `H` (12 times its hands); checked on a tiny constructed household in the test; once, for the PR, over populous small seed 7, 1 year.
- [x] The season's tally (demanded, taken, given) by member on the household's plan, folded at the season's end into a yearly count; of the parts nothing is kept past the day but what `Sim.hours(st, pawn, from, until)` needs for the strip's six days behind (say what is kept and why).
- [x] Sovereignty read from it (by right `1 - levy / H`; as lived `(demanded + given + kept) / H`, beside taken) through `Sim.hours`; no rule reads it as a switch.
- [ ] Cost: 1,000 pawns × 6 to 9 leg ends a day × one booking (under 0.5 ms a game day); the six days behind, 1,000 × 6 × 6 numbers. (Counted, not timed on a shared machine: hoursBook 10.4 a living pawn-day on the counters' probe, 11.3 on populous small seed 7 over a year, a little over the 6 to 9 assumed; the time is the nightly bench's. The days behind are 6 × 9 numbers a pawn.)
- [x] No behaviour changes: `node tools/same.js --base REF` differs only in the new fields (the tally, the six days behind), listed; `npm run verify` passes.
