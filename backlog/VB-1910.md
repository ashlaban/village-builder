# VB-1910: What lies out degrades: a share a game day in the wet and after the frost, not all at the frost
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Risk, 27 Nov); Crops and rotations (Crops and the weather); sim/lying frost
Touches: sim/lying (frost, decay); sim/clock; sim/rules; sim/outlook; new tests/lyingdecay.js; docs/tuning.md
Goal: A late harvest or a wet autumn costs a household part of what lies out, more each game day it waits, so that bringing in is weighed against other work day by day (Kim, 27 Nov: degrade over time, without tracking the condition of every item).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A lying load's amount (sim/lying) falls by LYING.wetLoss a wet game day and by LYING.frostLoss a game day once the frost has come (Proposed first values 0.05 and 0.25; the task may try others, at most five short runs), the loss booked through the stores' reasons as spoilage is; a load under its quantum is dropped. No field is added to a load but what the amount already is.
- [ ] Which game days are wet comes from the season's weather the sim has; if the sim has no wet day yet the task says so, builds the frost's share only and leaves the wet share as a line of a named follow-up task.
- [ ] A head's estimate of what will be lost is reckoned by the same function as the loss (One quantity, one reckoning) and the outlook shows it.
- [ ] The crop still standing at the frost is unchanged by this task.
- [ ] Tests: the shares, a load dropped at its quantum, a save from before.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (founders small seeds 4 and 7, populous small seeds 7 and 42, 20 years): sheaves and hay lost a year, hungry household-days, growth; a shortfall goes to docs/tuning.md.
- [ ] npm run verify passes its hard checks.
