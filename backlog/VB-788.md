Status: ready

Size: S

Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Moving to new land (Settling in steps; Sleeping away from home); docs/plans/VB-445.md, section 6; design.md, The player, commands and views
Goal: The player has at each step of a settling what its pawn has, no more and no less.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] At the round a leader is offered the search's `SETTLE.shown` (5) best sites, each with its hours from home along the trail, its ground and its fields' fertility as the leader estimates them (Principles, The future is someone's estimate: the view says whose); a leader not played stakes the first; the played pawn answers within 7 days, as an offer of the round is answered, or the first is staked.
- [ ] The played pawn takes the site's jobs as any job, and uses `lodge` and `comeHome` (VB-783); the page shows its pack and its bed where a pawn's home is shown, by the presentation a store and a place have elsewhere (Principles, Like is shown alike).
- [ ] The move: from the first day `leave` may be made, the played leader chooses the day; a leader not played moves on that day.
- [ ] As a member of a party it does not lead, and as the head of a household that feeds a worker, the played pawn has no act that others lack: say so in the task file, with the check that showed it.
- [ ] `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` (no played pawn in the run); `node tests/smoke.js` and the browser test of the offers; a screenshot of the choice of sites under `docs/img/`.
