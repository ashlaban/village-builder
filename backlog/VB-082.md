# VB-082: Customs made legible: votes, leanings and causes
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Customs and culture (the whole section); Systems overview (Customs: the signature system, but slow and hard to see; finding 5).
Goal: Customs are the game's signature system, but the player sees one log line when a practice changes, and nothing of the argument before it: who leans which way, who argues, how close a vote was.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The meeting log shows, for each changed custom, the support for the old and the new option and the margin needed ("Any child 46% against Sons first 31%; needed 25% more"), and notes customs that came within 5% of changing.
- [ ] The pawn panel has a "Leans" line: the customs where the pawn's attitude differs most from its village's practice, and the cause it argues for, if any, with the years left.
- [ ] A pawn taking up a cause and a change of practice are notable events: a toast on the page, and a `cause` or `custom` event in the record of events once VB-075 is done.
- [ ] The golden text of the panels (VB-057), if done, is re-recorded; list the changes.
