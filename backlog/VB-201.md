# VB-201: Tighten medium steps to 60 ms and spread midnight work
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: Customs and culture (How attitudes move). `docs/roadmap.md`, sections 5.2 (no step over 60 ms, Decided, Kim, 3 November; revised 6 November) and 5.3 (rules 1, 3 and 5).
Goal: Outside the moving round, the steps over 50 ms are all at midnight, when the day's work runs in one 6-minute step. Measured on 3 November, populous small at seed 7, 30 game days from a save:
- at year 5, 2 of 30 midnights went over 50 ms (61 ms at most);
- at year 40, 13 of 30 did, the worst 138 ms on the year's first day and 102 ms on its second;
- at year 40, `dailyLife` took 39 ms a game day: `Culture.dailyStep` 17 ms, which walks every person and every tie, and its daily decay of every tie about 18 ms;
- `endOfDay` took 7 ms a game day, `refreshRoutes` 3 ms of it.
VB-195 (indexes of the living) and VB-127 (ties on a shared decay scale, at most 150 a pawn) remove part of this. Measure what is left, then spread or defer it, so no midnight goes over 60 ms. Blocked until VB-195 and VB-127 are done.
At the late reference, `medium-ref` (VB-203, 9 November, a whole game year from the save, three runs), 11 to 14 steps a year go over 60 ms, all of them midnights: the year's turn (96 to 103 ms on its first midnight, 72 to 83 on its second), the moving round's day (63 to 101 ms at its midnight; the round's own call 51 to 56 ms) and several late-autumn and winter midnights (63 to 87 ms). On `small-y40` none went over in a whole year (55 ms at most). Daily life and births costs 41 ms a game day at `medium-ref` against its share of 35 (`docs/bench/closure.md`); VB-127 takes the daily decay of every tie out of it first.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Remove the temporary medium-y60 400 ms allowance from design, roadmap, guidance and benchmark reports (done by Kim's answers of 7 November and VB-203; `medium-y60` is dropped).
- [x] Time every six-minute step of a whole game year on `medium-ref` and `small-y40` (`tools/bench.js` does it on `medium-ref`), without excluding spikes.
- [x] The moving round runs as whole phases, each on its own step and in today's order (marriages, kin pressure and handovers; demand, claims and plots; settlement; service, what is owed and prestige), each with its own ranking view; the culture's drift runs a settlement a step after midnight. This is the spread of `0bc77a3` (Kim, 10 November: whole phases meet the criterion; the village order within the marriages waits until that phase alone nears 60 ms, and gets a task then). No saved progress: the step of the day says what is due, and `tests/spread.js` checks each phase runs exactly once with uneven advances and with saves between phases and mid-night. Command validation and the order of each stream's draws within a phase are kept.
- [x] The demographic gate (Kim, 10 November): populous small, 30 years, at 12 seeds (1 to 12), before and after, with `tools/marriage-demography.js`: the means over the seeds of marriages a year, mean first-marriage age each within 5%, and the share of living women 45 to 54 never married within 2 percentage points of the mean before (Kim, 10 November, second answer: 1 woman in 50, in absolute terms). Each seed's never-married count is reported; seeds 7 and 42 are reported and do not gate. Only after this passes, re-record the golden file and list the changed entries; ratchet `tests/counters.json` and list the changed counters (`walkDaily`, `heirRank`, `roundRank`).
- [x] Measure first, on `small-y40` and `medium-ref`, a whole game year from each: the midnight step's time, and its parts by a CPU profile (`node --cpu-prof`), reported in the PR.
- [x] Then, for each part still over its share:
  - work that can wait a few hours is spread over the night's steps, a village per step (rule 5), as `Culture.dailyStep` per settlement;
  - a quantity that decays is read in closed form (rule 3);
  - yearly work at the year's turn (`newYear`'s pass over every household's ledger, VB-174's ledger) is spread over the year's first days.
- [x] No midnight step over 60 ms on `small-y40` and `medium-ref` in a whole game year, the round's day included (timed as `tools/bench.js` times steps).
- [x] Daily life and births at most its share, 35 ms a game day at `medium-ref` (`tools/bench.js`), or what VB-127 left of the gap named in the PR with a task for it.
- [x] If the order of the culture's draws changes, behaviour changes: re-record the golden file, list the changed entries, and report the drift of customs over 20 populous years before and after (`npm run calibrate`), each within the calibration's bands.
- [ ] `npm run verify` passes.
