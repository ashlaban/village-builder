# VB-446: Cattle lanes into the outland: the herds' way to pasture reaches the wood and is shared
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-780
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: far
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Worn paths; Animals (Herds, Grazing at low cost); Infield, outland and wilderness
Goal: On a forested island the herds' daily way to pasture reaches out of the village into its outland wood and wears a cattle lane there, a bad road made by use, where today's herds graze within 8 steps of home and their lanes stay inside the village.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Measured first, on the forested island (`COVER` of `src/gen.js` at about half forest) at populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 10 years: where the herds graze, how far from the house, and the path and lane tiles a village that lie in forest. Herds already wear the tiles they cross, a quarter crossing a head (VB-136); if that already gives lanes into the wood, the task closes with the numbers.
- [ ] If not: a village's herds reach pasture beyond today's 8 steps by one shared way, chosen a season from the green to the outland pasture's nearest tile and kept for the season, so that the crossings of all its herds fall on the same tiles; the way is plain data in `st` with an owner in `src/owners.json`.
- [ ] No new pass and no new route search a herder a day: the shared way is one search a village a season (VB-387 already holds the herders' routes out of the counters' residual and must not be set back); the cost is named in the plan and measured on a late save.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state: a season's driving over forest tiles brings them to the path's stage; a way no longer driven fades as any worn way does.
Notes / questions:
