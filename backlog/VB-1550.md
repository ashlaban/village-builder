# VB-1550: Water on the map: the fresh water's class, the depth to the ground's water and the walk to water as fields, on the tile card and an overlay
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Water by the site); backlog/VB-1543.md
Goal: the map knows where fresh water is and how deep a well would be, and shows both; nothing behaves differently
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `w.fresh` (Uint8 a tile: none, lake, brook, stream, river, marsh, well; codes and names in `src/world/`, read there and not through the `gen` facade) is laid by the generator from the lake biome, `w.river` by its kinds and the marsh biome; `@state` line; no rule reads it yet.
- [ ] `w.waterDepth` (metres a land tile, a sentinel for no well) is computed once at generation by the formula of design.md, Goods and the farming year, Water by the site (The water table): height 120 m at `w.he` 1, the rise above the nearest water surface by steps over land, moisture, the bedrock's factor, marsh 0.5, no well on mountain, snow and beyond `WELL.deepest`; its numbers are `@tunable`. The band of a depth (shallow, middling, deep, no well) is one function in `src/world/`.
- [ ] `w.freshWalk` (hours on foot over pathless summer ground to the nearest source that is not marsh) from one multi-source search at generation.
- [ ] The tile card names the tile's source or the nearest and the walk, and the well's band with its range of hours, never the metres. An overlay colours land by the walk to fresh water and marks the bands.
- [ ] No behaviour changes: `node tools/same.js --base REF` on the starting commit. A check on a small constructed map (`tests/tiny.js`): a tile beside a lake is shallow, a mountain has no well, marsh is a source of class marsh, the sea is none.
- [ ] Reported in the PR for Kim: the share of land and of siteable ground in each band on small and medium, seeds 1 to 8, against the design's (small: 31/28/24/17% of land, 19/41/34/6% of siteable), and a look at the overlay on two seeds.
- [ ] Cost, named before building: nothing a step. Once a generation: two multi-source searches over the land tiles (20,000 on medium) and three arrays a tile (0.5 MB on medium). Generation's counters reported before and after; times left to the night.
