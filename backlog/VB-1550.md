# VB-1550: Water on the map: the fresh water's class, the depth to the ground's water and the walk to water as fields, on the tile card and an overlay
Status: done (2026-11-23)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Water by the site); backlog/VB-1543.md
Goal: the map knows where fresh water is and how deep a well would be, and shows both; nothing behaves differently
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `w.fresh` (Uint8 a tile: none, lake, brook, stream, river, marsh, well; codes and names in `src/world/`, read there and not through the `gen` facade) is laid by the generator from the lake biome, `w.river` by its kinds and the marsh biome; `@state` line; no rule reads it yet.
- [x] `w.waterDepth` (metres a land tile, a sentinel for no well) is computed once at generation by the formula of design.md, Goods and the farming year, Water by the site (The water table): height 120 m at `w.he` 1, the rise above the nearest water surface by steps over land, moisture, the bedrock's factor, marsh 0.5, no well on mountain, snow and beyond `WELL.deepest`; its numbers are `@tunable`. The band of a depth (shallow, middling, deep, no well) is one function in `src/world/`.
- [x] `w.freshWalk` (hours on foot over pathless summer ground to the nearest source that is not marsh) from one multi-source search at generation.
- [x] The tile card names the tile's source or the nearest and the walk, and the well's band with its range of hours, never the metres. An overlay colours land by the walk to fresh water and marks the bands.
- [x] No behaviour changes: `node tools/same.js --base REF` on the starting commit. A check on a small constructed map (`tests/tiny.js`): a tile beside a lake is shallow, a mountain has no well, marsh is a source of class marsh, the sea is none.
- [x] Reported in the PR for Kim: the share of land and of siteable ground in each band on small and medium, seeds 1 to 8, against the design's (small: 31/28/24/17% of land, 19/41/34/6% of siteable), and a look at the overlay on two seeds.
- [x] Cost, named before building: nothing a step. Once a generation: two multi-source searches over the land tiles (20,000 on medium) and three arrays a tile (0.5 MB on medium). Generation's counters reported before and after; times left to the night.

- Not as written: `w.freshWalk` is not laid by the generator, which may not read the walking costs (`sim/walking` loads after it; `tests/dependencies.js`). `sim/water` lays it where a world enters a state (`makeState`; `load`, before the saved fields are put back), from the generated fields alone, so it is the same at seeding and after a load, and is not saved. A world shown with no state (the editor) has no walk: the card leaves the line out.
- Decided: where several neighbours are as near to water, the surface is the mean of theirs (the design names no tie rule; a plain search goes by the order tiles are read in). Shares against the design's, seeds 1 to 8: small, land 30/29/24/17% (31/28/24/17), siteable 18/42/35/6% (19/41/34/6); medium, land 39/28/19/15% (40/27/19/14), siteable 28/40/27/4% (29/39/27/4).
- Decided: the walk ends at the source's tile (a river's own tile 0; a lake's bank half a step of its ground from the water, with no slope down to the lake); a river's tile is crossed only at a ford; a step is `stepCost`'s on bare ground. No well's line on a river's tile; the sea's card says nothing of water.
- Measured, for Kim: the walk over pathless ground is long, since half the land is wood at 4 a tile and slopes weigh: small, median 3.4 hours one way, upper quartile 6.2, upper tenth 10.0; medium 2.8, 5.0, 7.8. The design's "inland, 7 steps of meadow, 0.875 h" is the open ground's case. 513 land tiles on small have none to walk to (the design counted 442 on islets; the other 71 lie inside snowfields, which nobody crosses).
