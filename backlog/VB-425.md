# VB-425: A child born on the road is born where its mother is, and the player's commands wait for the journey's end
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: between
Phase: implement
Design ref: backlog/VB-287.md (gaps for a follow-up); QUESTION_ARCHIVE / QUESTIONS.md VB-405, question 3 (Kim, 13 Nov: no teleportation)
Goal: VB-287 left two gaps against Kim's rule of 13 November (no teleportation): a child born to a mother on a journey appears at home (sim/births calls placeAtHome) and must be born where she is and travel with her; and the player's commands are refused while their pawn is on the road, where they should be held until it arrives (or refused with that reason, decided in the task).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A child born while its mother is on a journey (`sim/agents` phases `journey` and `road`) is placed at her tile, not at home, and arrives with her; a test on a constructed state (`tests/journeys.js`).
- [x] A command of the played pawn issued while it is on the road is either held until arrival or refused with the reason "on the road"; decided here by Deciding without Kim and written in the notes.
- [x] No other call of `placeAtHome` moves a living pawn that is on a journey (grep, listed in the notes).
- 13 Nov 2026 (implementation): `placeBorn(st, a, mother, hh)` in `sim/agents` (called by `sim/births` in place of `placeAtHome`): a child of a
  mother on a journey or the road is put at her position, outdoors, and `setOut` (no old household, so provisions from its own) sends it to its
  home as a journey of its own, so it walks the same route and arrives with her within a leg; otherwise it is placed at home as before.
- Decision (Deciding without Kim): the player's commands are refused with the reason, not held: `commands.js`' `free` already says
  "pawn N is on the road" for `work`, `walkTo`, `goHome`, `idle` and `attend`. Holding would keep the queue's head begun for up to days
  and block the pawn's other queued commands for no gain; the player may reissue on arrival. `tests/journeys.js` checks the reason.
- `placeAtHome` callers: `agents.js` moved (same farm, pawn indoors, not travelling), `placeBorn` (mother not travelling), `endJourney`
  (arrival), `scenarios.js` (setup, before any journey). None moves a living pawn on a journey.
