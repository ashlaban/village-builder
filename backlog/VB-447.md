# VB-447: Blazing a trail: a cheap way cut through forest, which fades unless walked
Status: done (2026-11-23)

Size: M

Goal: Forest too thick to walk through at any speed can be opened by cutting a trail, cheaply and for as long as the trail is used, so that a daughter settlement or a neighbour beyond the wood comes within reach.

- 15 Nov 2026, opened in the session with Kim on the journey scale; Kim: it runs before slice 10, in the arch `far`. Measured there (scratch copies, populous medium, seed 42, 8 tiles an hour, forest about half the land and rough ground about twice as dear): the median pair of villages 28.5 hours apart against 21.7 on today's ground, and 15 of 155 pairs beyond 67 hours. Dear forest isolates unless something opens it, and use alone will not: journeys are too few to wear a path.
- Kim, 15 Nov: paths should function as bad roads; roads should weigh heavily in rough ground and be dear to make. So two things, not one: a trail (this task: cheap, fades, on foot) and a built road (dear, kept up, for carts; with the bridges of VB-115).
- Grounds: blazed ways (trees marked with an axe, brush cut) were how forest tracks were made and kept in Scandinavia; a track not walked or cleared was lost to windfall and growth in a few years, which the yearly halving stands for.
- Settlement: a new village lies 10 to 30 walking steps from home by cost (`SETTLE.reach`), so with dear forest the sites beyond a wood fall out of the search. A trail brings them in. Kim, 15 Nov: settlers may build a farm on forest they clear, and settling needs several steps and a way of sleeping away from home; that mechanism is VB-445's to plan.
- Questions for Kim, with proposed answers: (1) May the player order a trail? Proposed: no; walking keeps it, and that is what makes it cheap and the built road dear.
