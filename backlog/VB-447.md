# VB-447: Blazing a trail: a cheap way cut through forest, which fades unless walked
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-445
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: far
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Worn paths; World and scale (Walking and terrain); Moving to new land; Between villages: distance and journeys
Goal: Forest too thick to walk through at any speed can be opened by cutting a trail, cheaply and for as long as the trail is used, so that a daughter settlement or a neighbour beyond the wood comes within reach.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] VB-445's plan (settling in steps) confirms or rewrites this task and carries its questions to Kim, since blazing is an act no existing system covers.
- [ ] A village, and a settlers' party, can open a blazing project from a tile to a tile: a job a tile along the cheapest route through the ground as it stands, each of `PATHS.blaze` hours (proposed 3 a tile of broadleaf or pine forest, 1 elsewhere; a tunable with that meaning), reached as jobs are, by a verb of `src/sim/commands.js`.
- [ ] A blazed tile is set to the path's stage by its count (`PATHS.at[2]`), through the owner of the tiles' wear; from then on it is a worn way like any other: it halves a year, and is kept only by being walked. Nothing new is stored for it, and no pass decays it.
- [ ] A trail carries a walker and a load on the back, not a cart: carts keep to built roads and open ground (the built road is another task of slice 10).
- [ ] Who blazes, and when: the task's plan names it. Proposed: settlers, to the site they chose, before they move (the first of the steps of settling, see the note); a village, to a neighbour or a daughter it has kin in and cannot reach in a day there and back.
- [ ] The cost is named before building (projects × tiles × hours), and a trail's tiles are bounded by the route search's reach.
- [ ] Numbers before and after at populous small and medium, seeds 7 and 42, on the forested island: village pairs within 6, 12 and 24 walking hours, and the sites a crowded village can find.
Notes / questions:
- 15 Nov 2026, opened in the session with Kim on the journey scale; Kim: it runs before slice 10, in the arch `far`. Measured there (scratch copies, populous medium, seed 42, 8 tiles an hour, forest about half the land and rough ground about twice as dear): the median pair of villages 28.5 hours apart against 21.7 on today's ground, and 15 of 155 pairs beyond 67 hours. Dear forest isolates unless something opens it, and use alone will not: journeys are too few to wear a path.
- Kim, 15 Nov: paths should function as bad roads; roads should weigh heavily in rough ground and be dear to make. So two things, not one: a trail (this task: cheap, fades, on foot) and a built road (dear, kept up, for carts; with the bridges of VB-115).
- Grounds: blazed ways (trees marked with an axe, brush cut) were how forest tracks were made and kept in Scandinavia; a track not walked or cleared was lost to windfall and growth in a few years, which the yearly halving stands for.
- Settlement: a new village lies 10 to 30 walking steps from home by cost (`SETTLE.reach`), so with dear forest the sites beyond a wood fall out of the search. A trail brings them in. Kim, 15 Nov: settlers may build a farm on forest they clear, and settling needs several steps and a way of sleeping away from home; that mechanism is VB-445's to plan.
- Questions for Kim, with proposed answers: (1) May the player order a trail? Proposed: yes, by parity of command, as head of a household for a settlers' party and through the village's meeting for a village's trail. (2) Does a trail need upkeep work beyond being walked? Proposed: no; walking keeps it, and that is what makes it cheap and the built road dear.
