# VB-151: Refactor: a cheaper route refresh and fewer allocations in hot loops
Status: done (2026-11-01)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: tooling
Phase: implement
Design ref: Time and calendar (1,000 pawns at speed). Review: `docs/review-balance.md`, Performance; `docs/review-stability.md`.
Goal: Late in a populous run a game day costs 4 times what it does early (small map, seed 7: 104 ms at 437 people, 446 ms at 1,034). Find the savings that change no behaviour.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `refreshRoutes` (`src/sim/routes.js`) works out the changed tiles' hex coordinates once a day instead of once per cached route, and skips a route's cost where the outcome is certain without it: a route of n steps costs between n cheapest steps (`ROUTE.minStep`) and n dearest (`MAX_STEP`, 3), so a changed tile nearer than n − 1 always drops it and one beyond n × 3.9 never does. The same routes are dropped as before.
- [x] The culture's daily drift (`dailyStep`, `src/culture.js`) draws each dimension's noise into one scratch array, and the hourly contact count (`noteContacts`, `src/sim/society.js`) lists the housemates indoors without a new array per household.
- [x] `tests/golden.json` is unchanged; `npm run verify` passes.
Notes / questions:
- 2026-11-01: populous tiny, seed 11, 48 days after year 60 (414 people), CPU profile of both versions on the same state: 7,014 → 6,298 ms (−10%); the route refresh 345 → 131 ms; the culture drift 403 → 355 ms.
- Tried and left out: pair keys below 2^30 for `st.contacts` (so V8 keeps them as small integers) measured within noise and would re-record the golden file's `contacts` entries.
- What is left is the social model: contact hours, talk, the daily decay of every tie and company for the idle are about 45% of a late day, and ties per pawn grow with the village (mean 68, up to 187 at 1,034 people), which is why a day's cost grows faster than the people. VB-127 (familiarity from talk, at most 150 ties a pawn) is the change that removes it.
