Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The responsibilities, each where it belongs (Kim, 18 November: a split follows them, not the line count): new ground made (`finishClear`, `clearStones`, `finishBreak`, `broken`), beside `sim/fieldplans` or a module of its own; a field's course in its rotation (`nextStep`, `restagger`, `placeField`, `takeRotation`, `outOfTurn`), beside `sim/rotations`; soil and manure (`wearSoil`, `surplusOf`, `manureWanted`, `manureDone`, `tilthOf`), a `sim/soil`; the crop year (hoed, sown, `standing`, reaped, carried, the frost, the day) stays in `sim/fields`. The task says which goes where, and why, before moving any.
- [x] `tests/width-baseline.json`'s line for `sim/fields` falls to 15 or less, or is gone; its readers import from the new homes.
- [x] Behaviour unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`.
Notes:
