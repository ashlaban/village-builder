# VB-2027: The storehouse as a place: threshed grain carried to it
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Settlements and look (every building drawn is one the sim has); VB-1898
Touches: sim/kinds (PLACES); sim/fieldwork (thresh)
Goal: the storehouse is where the grain keeps, so the jobs that carry grain home name it and carry to it
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Grain threshed and food carried home go to the storehouse place, not the barn (sim/kinds PLACES has no storehouse; fieldwork carries to barn): the jobs name the building that keeps them
- [x] A farm without a storehouse still keeps its grain, at the whole loss; tests show the place named in the job words
Decision: sheaves still go to the barn (unthreshed, threshed in it); the threshing is declared at barn and storehouse, and its words name where the grain goes (storehouse, or the house of a farm without one, which keeps it at the whole loss as before). No carry job for threshed grain exists to retarget.
