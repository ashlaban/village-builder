# VB-131: Herds as household data, at the farm and in the panels
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: animals
Phase: implement
Design ref: Animals; docs/review-animals.md (review of 31 October 2026); answers in QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md under "VB-047: animals review".
Goal: Each household may keep a cattle herd and a sheep flock (Agreed). Nothing in the game holds animals yet; this task makes them data, breeds them each spring, and shows them by the farms.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `st.herds[]` (`src/sim/herds.js`, `@state` documented): `{id, species 'cattle'|'sheep', household, home (tile), cull[], lean, unfed}`; id is the index, never reused. Cattle hold `head[]` of named animals `{id, name, sex, born, kind 'calf'|'heifer'|'cow'|'bull'|'steer'|'ox'}` (ids from `st.headSeq`); sheep hold counts `ewes`, `rams`, `lambs` (Kim, 31 Oct: named cattle, counted sheep). At most one herd per species and household.
- [ ] `hh.hay` (hay units) and `hh.larder[]` (food lots, used from VB-134) on every household.
- [ ] Founders (Kim, 30 Oct): the first farm keeps 2 cows, a bull and a pair of oxen, 4 ewes and a ram; the second 2 cows and 4 ewes; the third 2 cows; the lone farm none. Cows 4–8 years old, the bull 4, oxen 5 and 6, ewes 2–5, the ram 3. Hay for the spring days still in the byre plus one winter (26 stall days × 1.1) of each farm's need.
- [ ] Populous: 3 in 4 farms keep cattle: 2 cows (households of up to 5), 3 (6–7) or 4 (8–9), aged 3–10, and a heifer with chance 0.5; the farm with most cows in each village keeps the bull; a farm with 3 or more cows keeps a pair of oxen while the byre (6 cattle, a calf counting half) has room. Half the farms keep 4–8 ewes; the farm with most ewes in each village keeps a ram. Hay for the 5 stall days left at the start × 1.1. Heirs: A1 3 cows, bull, oxen, 6 ewes and a ram; A2 a cow and 3 ewes; A4 2 cows and a heifer; B1 3 cows, bull, oxen, 6 ewes and a ram; B3 a cow.
- [ ] Hay a stall day (`HERD.hay`): cow, bull and ox 1; heifer and steer 0.6; calf 0.4; ewe and ram 0.2; lamb 0.1. `winterNeed(st, hh)` = 21 stall days × the herd's daily need.
- [ ] Spring (spring day 3, at the start of the day): animals age: a calf of 1 becomes a heifer, or a steer, or a young bull when no bull of the village's reach (12 steps between houses) is under 8; a heifer of 3 a cow; a steer of 3 an ox. Cattle of 15 and more die; each adult animal dies with chance 0.03 (cattle) or 0.05 (sheep). Then a cow calves with chance 0.85 if a bull of 2 or more lives within 12 steps of her farm (Kim, 31 Oct, as P), the calf dying with chance 0.15; a ewe lambs with chance 0.9 if a ram is within reach, twins with chance 0.3, each lamb dying with chance 0.2. When the bull is another household's, the cow's holder remembers "Lent us the bull" (+0.05, cap +0.2, 3 years; `SOCIAL.memories.bull`) about the bull's holder. One log line per farm with births.
- [ ] A dissolved household's herds go to the household its first member moves to, merged into that household's herd of the species; with no such household they are lost.
- [ ] Page: a byre (*fähus*) beside the cottage of a household with a herd; in the grazing season (spring day 6 to autumn day 8), 6:00 to 20:00, its animals graze around the herd's home tile next to the farm, each with a slow wander and two poses; in the byre otherwise. Drawn only in view and above the zoom at which pawns show, from sprites that are rebuilt only when a herd changes; never through `visualDirty`.
- [ ] The settlers panel's household card lists the herds ("Cattle: 2 cows (Stjärna, Rosa), the bull Brante, 2 oxen, a calf · Sheep: 4 ewes, a ram, 5 lambs") and the hay with the winter's need.
- [ ] `tools/sim.js --report herds`: per household its herds by kind, ages and names, hay and winter need.
- [ ] `checkState`: every herd's household exists and is not dissolved (or the herd is empty); counts are whole and not negative; head ids unique; every head born in the past; kinds valid; hay and larder not negative.
- [ ] `tests/herds.js` in `npm test`: the founders' herds as above; after spring day 3 of year 2 at seed 7, calves and lambs are born; no cow calves without a bull in reach.
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
