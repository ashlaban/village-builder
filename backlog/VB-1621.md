# VB-1621: Repair: shoes last five years: LEATHER.shoeDays is read in game days, a week each
Status: ready
Cause: VB-911
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, 3. Time and calendar; Principles for decisions (Parameters, not patches); Goods of place (Furs and hides); backlog/VB-911.md; docs/tuning.md, the LEATHER row
Goal: LEATHER's wear is stated in a unit the calendar reads as meant, so that a walker wears a pair through in about as long as turnshoes last, and hides are used up by shoes and harness as VB-911 intended.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `LEATHER.shoeDays` is either stated in game days with its value reckoned from the calendar (a game day stands for about a week: about 32 game days for some eight months of wear), or kept in real days and converted where `wearDay` reads it; its comment names the unit. `harnessYears` and any other duration of `LEATHER` are checked the same way.
- [x] A test (`tests/leather.js`): a walker wears `YEAR_DAYS / shoeDays` pairs a game year as the unit says; the test fails with the value read in the other unit.
- [x] Before and after, founders small, seeds 7 and 11, 10 years: hides held and cut, shoes held against the target, hours of cobbling; populous small seed 7, 5 years. The `LEATHER` row of `docs/tuning.md` is corrected: the pile of hides was the unit, not the hunt's supply alone. Other shortfalls left for the tuning run.
- Found by VB-1619's trial review of VB-911 (finding F2, 24 Nov 2026): at shoeDays 240 read in game days a pair lasts 240 / 48 = 5 game years, 0.2 pairs a walker a year; the trial's probe held 48 and 53 hides against 4 and 3 cut.

Decision: kept in game days, 32 (8 of 12 months of a 48-day year); harnessYears is read times YEAR_DAYS, so fine. Measures (ledger cuts, hours = cuts x shoeHours) in the LEATHER row of docs/tuning.md. Populous shoes now 0.77 of target: left for tuning.
