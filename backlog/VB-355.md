- Decisions (Deciding without Kim):
  - The rotation is the farm's, set from any of its fields' cards. The design's row has the fields of one farm at different steps of the
    same rotation, and VB-350's state and invariant hold one rotation a farm; "a field's rotation" is read as that. The verb restaggers the
    farm's fields as the rule's change does (a field under a crop keeps it; the change is refused while a field stands under a crop the
    new rotation has not). A close call: surfaced.
  - The checks. `rotation`: only the head (`heads`), a farm of its own, a known rotation, a field's seed of every crop of it (in store or
    standing, as VB-350's `hasSeed`), and no field under a crop it has not (the "free field"). It has no window: a rotation is a plan, and
    each course keeps its own window. `sowOutOfTurn`: the field free (in stubble or hoed), the crop's sowing window open today, a field's
    seed of it in the store. The window is read strictly: the choice is made when the crop can be sown, not ahead. A close call: surfaced.
    `fallow` is a choice too (a year's rest: no seed, no window), and a field's own course puts it back to its turn.
  - Keeping a choice is an argument, not a source: `rotation({hold: true})` marks `farmstead.rotationBy`, and the rule (`rotationChoice`)
    then leaves that farm alone, the fall-back included: short of seed, the field waits unsown, as the design's row has it where no
    rotation serves. `rotation({rotation: null})` gives the choosing back. The page always passes `hold`; the rule never does, so the verb
    does the same for any source. A field out of turn is likewise left out of the rule's fall-back (`shortOf`).
  - A year out of turn keeps the field's step: when its crop is in, or lost, or its window closes unsown, the field goes on to the next
    step, as after any course (`nextStep` clears `turn`; on barley every year it goes back to barley).
  - The verbs sow nothing and move nothing: they set what the field is to bear, and the household's own work hoes and sows it from its
    store. No teleport.
  - Where the played head's farm gets rye: nowhere on founders today. Its farms hold barley alone, so the choices open there are two
    courses and a year's rest; rye and peas are refused with "no seed of rye: a field takes 25 and its store holds 0". VB-353 (ready) gives
    the three farms rye and the lone farm peas from the start, and from there the crop moves as grain moves (Seed: help, gifts, a start
    in life). Nothing was added here to hand the player seed.
  - A verb for the order of planting was not built: it is owed by VB-1123's plan, as its note of 18 Nov allows.
  - The card: the crop (its own glyph: rye and peas got one, fallow too; barley keeps the grain icon), the course, what is sown next
    with its days, and the grain held of each crop against a field's seed, as stats of the one kind above the soil's two meters; for the
    played head, a button for each rotation and each crop. A choice the verb would refuse today is dimmed, not disabled: pressed, it
    answers with the verb's own reason. Nothing was added to the Plan tab.
