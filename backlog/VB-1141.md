# VB-1141: An heir who holds a smaller farm takes the larger: it says no only for a place with more fields
Status: done (2026-11-21)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: acts
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Succession: Handed on, and taken up); docs/plans/VB-682.md, section 4.6
Goal: The heir's answer weighs the two places, as the design's line says: one who holds, or lives on a spouse's, smaller farm takes the larger, and its own passes on by the rule a holder who marries away follows.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The answer of one in line who holds a farm, or lives on a spouse's, is no only when that place has at least as many fields (a tie keeps its own); `heirRanking`'s asked line gives the answer and its reason, and the ranking is still its yes-sayers.
- [x] One who says yes to the larger hands its own farm on first, as a holder who marries away does (`handOn`: its next heir who can keep it, else the longest-waiting landless adult of the village, else it stands empty), and moves by `move`, reason `heir`, with spouse and children; farms are never combined.
- [x] At a death the same answer decides who renounces.
- [x] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7 and 42: heirs who changed farm, farms left empty, handovers by reason; and the tests of `tests/handover.js` extended with a smaller and a larger farm.
