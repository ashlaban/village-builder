# VB-1653: Wood in two steps: timber felled on a forest tile and carried home, firewood cut from it at the farmstead; both as plan tasks
Status: done (2026-11-24)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/ui2/HANDOFF.md (Kim's answers after round 4: Wood; round 4, finding 2); src/sim/chores.js (KINDS.wood); src/sim/building.js (KINDS.timber, fetchTimber); src/sim/forest.js; src/sim/stores.js; src/sim/playerplan.js (CHAINS, WAITS); src/sim/outlook.js; src/sim/carts.js
Goal: A player who plans by hand can keep the fire going: fell timber where they choose, and cut it to firewood at home; and every household's firewood goes the same two steps.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] One rule for every household (the player is not special): the firewood trip (`KINDS.wood`, sim/chores) becomes two steps. Felling: a tree felled on a forest tile and its load carried home as timber, a good the household holds (a stored quantity, loads: through `sim/stores` with a good and a reason, an owner in `src/owners.json`, an invariant, a `tools/stocks.js` entry, absent in an older save read as 0). Cutting: at the house, timber held is cut into firewood (`hh.wood`), a job kind of its own. The hours of the two steps for one load of firewood sum to today's trip, so the labour a load costs is unchanged; the urgency and `vital` rules of today's trip are carried over (a cold house cuts what timber it holds first, else fells).
- [x] Building is not changed here: the timber trips to a site (`KINDS.timber`, `fetchTimber`) stay as they are. A note, not code: whether a site at the farm should draw on the timber held at home is left to the task that makes the farmstead a construction (the next one), and said in this task's notes.
- [x] The player chooses the tile: a plan task `fell` at any forest tile with trees that the played pawn's household may fell on and can reach (the village's wood as today's rule draws it, or what the rule for a lone farm allows: say which, and refuse with the reason otherwise), `Sim.plan(st, 'add', {fell: tile, n?})`: n trips (default 1) felled there and carried home, the task leaving when they are home or the tile is bare (`which`). And a plan task `firewood` at the house tile: the timber held cut into firewood until it is all cut or the pile reaches the season's goal. Both are chains declared as data beside plant, hay and harvest (`CHAINS`), with their `waits` reasons (VB-1651) and laid into `Sim.agenda` as the others are.
- [x] A read for the page's warning, on the facade, plain data, writing nothing: how the fire stands for the played pawn's household: firewood held, timber held, the nights the pile lasts at the season's use, whether the house is cold now, and what would mend it in order (`fell` when no timber is held, `firewood` when timber is held but the pile is short). The page words it ("No firewood"; "Fell timber, then cut it to firewood at the farmstead").
- [x] The supply outlook's fuel (`sim/outlook`) counts timber held as firewood not yet cut only if a `firewood` task of the plan covers it, for a household planned by hand; for every other household as the head reckons today (say what you did).
- [ ] Tests on small constructed states (a line in `tests/suites.js` for a new script): an unplayed household keeps its pile through a year by the two steps; the played pawn fells on a chosen tile and cuts at home by its plan; the refusals; a save and a load go on the same. Report before and after, founders seed 7 on the small map for 10 years (`node tools/sim.js`, `node tools/balance.js`, `node tests/counters.js`): cold days, the hours on wood, the population; `node tests/counters.js --update` if the counters move by intent. `npm run verify` passes; baselines do not rise.
