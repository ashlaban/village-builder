# VB-164: Plan: needs, intentions and households that plan ahead
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: foresight
Phase: plan
Design ref: Economy and jobs; People, households and family records; Customs and culture (per-person attitudes). External review of 2 November (`docs/review-external.md`, point 4).
Goal: Pawns answer the jobs that are posted; households do not look ahead. `refreshJobs` posts work from rules of the moment, and matching ranks it by role, urgency and distance, so two farmers of different temper choose alike. Design three layers, each built on the one below, and keep job matching as the way intentions are carried out:
- needs: what a household (and a pawn) will lack, and by when: food to the next harvest, seed, firewood for the winter, hay, hands for the next season, a place to marry;
- intentions: what the household means to do about it, held over days or seasons (clear a field this year, take a servant at the moving round, sell a cow, marry off a daughter);
- actions: the jobs and commands (VB-076) that carry an intention out.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A short survey of the code: which rules today act as hidden needs or intentions (the fields a household wants in `src/sim/fields.js`, firewood and hay posting, the means to marry, the moving round), with file and function; and what of the colony-sim genre's approaches (utility scores, goal planners, schedules) fits a game of about 1,000 pawns and its speed budget (VB-088).
- [ ] Questions for Kim in `QUESTIONS.md`, each with a proposed answer: what is a need and how is it measured; who holds intentions (household holder, each adult, both); how long an intention lasts and what drops it; how personality and per-person attitudes (`Culture`) bend the choice; how the player's commands (VB-076, VB-079) enter, as an intention given to a household or as a job; and what the first, smallest step is (for example: firewood and food needs as numbers in a household's record, read by `refreshJobs` in place of today's thresholds, behaviour as close as the golden file allows).
- [ ] A `design.md` note is proposed for Kim, not written (implementing tasks do not change `design.md`).
