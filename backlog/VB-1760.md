Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `LEVY` (`@tunable`, a custom's norm read through sim/customs' one door): design.md's table (an adult member, whatever its position, 9.5, 10, 10.5 and 9.5 hours a day, spring to winter; a child of 7 to 13 6, 10, 7 and 3, times its hands by age; a lodger 0, and an adult-day in summer and one in autumn; an elder 0; the head pair not levied); a servant's contract gives its own row (its free day at the term is a dated grant, VB-1763); a child under 7 is not levied and not allotted.
- [ ] `levyOf(st, pawn, day)` (a new module `sim/levies`, `@sys`, owner of the member's levy on the household's plan): the head's grant if any (VB-1763), else the contract's, else the custom's, times the pawn's hands (age, health); compiled per member at the season's turn and when membership or a grant changes; read by the allotment as one array lookup; `Sim.levyOf`.
- [ ] The allotment's capacity for a member is its levy left today (its levy in force less the hours of the household's and the outer levies' work it has done today, at most the working day left; VB-1761 then reads it from the booking), not its working day (`dayHours`, `hoursLeft`, `takeDay`); the head pair, not levied, are allotted up to their working hours as today; each list past the levy is kept as the order in which a member gives, not assigned; work a household lives by (`vital`) is inside it and slotted first; levy hours the allotment leaves unassigned fall to the member for the day, and a later call inside the levy is no breach. Until VB-1762, a member's own hours go to its household's open work as today (Work beats idling), so this task moves the head's planning and not yet the hours worked.
- [ ] Until VB-1779, an `assign` or a call beyond a member's levy stays allowed as today and is not named a demand.
- [ ] A day worked under an outer levy (a day owed today, `sim/days`) comes out of the household's levy on the one sent.
- [ ] A bracket for the riskiest step, once and not committed (AGENTS.md: at most five short runs): the six runs of `tools/gate.js` with own hours given not at all (a local switch). If every village lives on levied hours alone, giving (VB-1762) cannot fail the gate at any value; if not, the floor the giving judgement must hold is reported in VB-1762's notes and as a `tuning` note on `GIVE`.
- [ ] Q8, its first half: the share of household hours worked today that lie beyond the levies, spring to autumn and in winter (populous small seed 7, 3 years), against 5 to 15% and under 5%; `docs/tuning.md`'s row for `LEVY` updated with it.
- [ ] Cost: 1,000 members × 4 compiles a year and one a grant; the allotment's read one lookup (under 0.1 ms a game day, 3.8).
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
