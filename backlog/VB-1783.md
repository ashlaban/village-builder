# VB-1783: What a pawn makes in its own hours is its own: a task a pawn owns, its material by its household's right, and the household's share by custom

Goal: A pawn can dig, fetch and make in its own hours for itself, keeps what it makes less what its village's custom gives the household, and goods cross between a pawn and its household only by a gift or a breach
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A task may be owned by a pawn (`{type: 'pawn', id}`; taken on the basis `self` by that pawn alone); its hours are booked `kept` (VB-1761); its good goes into the owner's store at the work (The good is made where the work is done), with a reason of its own.
- [ ] Material by the household's right: a pawn's own digging (sim/dig) and wood fetched take where its household may (its digging ground, the outland, the village's wood), and what they take is the pawn's; the household's stored clay and wood it uses only when given.
- [ ] The custom `ownMaking` (sim/customs, read through its one door): `own` (the default), `share` (`OWN.share`, 0.25 of each own making to the household's store at the making, with its reason), `household` (all of it while the pawn is an unmarried member); a check of each on a constructed household.
- [ ] `give` widens to persons: from a pawn's store to its household's or another pawn's, and from a household's store to a member's (a portion, material), answered by the receiver as today and booked with a good and a reason.
- [ ] A head that takes a member's goods unasked breaches: the verb `take` (a head on a member's store), the worth booked as goodwill the head owes the member, opinion following, as a demand of hours is (VB-1779); the default author issues it only when the household is hungry and the member refused its ask.
- [ ] Firing pots for oneself (VB-366's kind, owned by the pawn) practises pottery (`practise` of sim/skills) as the household's firing does, and its pots are graded by the maker's skill (VB-761): a check that forces the stream `making`.
- [ ] Cost: own makings are tasks like any, inside the jobs line; no pass of their own.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
