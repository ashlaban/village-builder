# VB-2042: A Walks map mode: the routes walked since it was switched on, as a heat map
Status: done (2026-11-28)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Worn paths (4. Sharing paths); docs/mocks/roads/heat.js and heat.py (the mock it builds on); Kim, 27 Nov, thread Redesign road layout for hex maps
Touches: sim/legs (setOut); new page/walks; src/body.html; new tests/walks.js
Goal: A player sees where the people actually walk, routes and the last legs into yards, at finer than a tile, at no cost while the mode is off.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A new overlay registered with registerOverlay, id walks, name Walks, group ways, beside Wear; Wear is unchanged.
- [x] Collecting starts when the mode is switched on and stops when it is switched off, and what it held is dropped then: with the mode off, sim/legs setOut does no work for it beyond one check of a null listener (Kim, 27 Nov: otherwise too expensive).
- [x] The sim does not read the page: sim/legs offers one listener slot (set by the page, null by default) called with the walk's points (a.path) after they are set; the simulation's results are the same with the listener set or not (tests/walks.js runs a seed both ways and compares the state's hash).
- [x] Each walk is drawn into a raster of the whole map, its cell a quarter of a hex's radius or finer, each cell counted once a walk; the counts halve each game year (PATHS.halfLifeDays), as wear does.
- [x] Drawn on a log colour ramp under the houses, blank where nothing has passed, redrawn at most twice a second; the key shows the ramp with its counts (1, 10, 100, ...) and the game days collected, icons and numbers first, no paragraph (Kim's rule: words a tap away).
- [ ] On a T world at speed, the frame time with the mode on is measured and reported against off; memory for the raster is at most 8 MB. (Memory held: the raster is made in 64-cell chunks only where a walk passes, capped at 8 MB, WALK_BYTES. Frame time not measured: the machine was shared by 4 agents, so a time is no result; left to the nightly bench.)
- [x] A screenshot of seed 3's village after one game year with the mode on, beside docs/mocks/roads/img/heat/heat-3.png, shows the same trunks.

Notes:
- The raster's cell is a fifth of a hex's radius; chunks of 64 by 64 cells (counts Float32 + canvas RGBA, 32 KB each) made only where walked, at most 8 MB; past that a walk over new land is not drawn and the key shows "!".
- The halving is lazy: a walk is added at 2^(days/half-life) and read back at 2^-(days/half-life), rebased past 2^16, so nothing is touched to fade it.
- The listener is called by setOut after a.path is laid; tests/walks.js runs a seed with and without it and compares the state's hash.
- tools/village-shot.js --walks: the mode on after --years, then one game year more. Seed 3 (10 farms, 60 by 42, --years 3 --walks) shows the mock's trunks: the long east-west trunk through the centre, the north-west to south-east diagonal and the spurs into the yards.
- Left: the frame time on a T world at speed, mode on against off (the nightly bench).
