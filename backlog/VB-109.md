# VB-109: A farm's fields as one contiguous block within 4 steps of the house
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Goods and the farming year: Making a field); Settlements and look. Kim, 30 Oct 2026 (VB-080, question 2): every generator places a farm's fields as one contiguous shape within 4 tiles of the house, not necessarily next to it; a field made in play prefers to touch that block.
Goal: `placeFields` (`src/sim/layout.js`) takes the most fertile free tiles among the house's neighbours, then at 2 steps, so a farm's fields can be scattered around the house with gaps between them. `chooseTile` (`src/sim/fields.js`) picks each new field on its own, by fertility, stones and distance within `REACH` (4). Fields should lie together, as an infield, which also gives each farm a simpler fence.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `placeFields(w, used, house, n, avoid)` returns n free farmable tiles that form one connected group (each touches another of them, by hex neighbour), all within 4 steps of the house over land; they need not touch the house. Among the groups it finds, it takes the one with the highest summed fertility less 0.15 per step from the house to each tile, found by growing groups from each candidate tile within 4 steps, taking the best neighbouring tile each time. It returns null when no group of n exists, as today.
- [ ] Every scenario uses it: founders, heirs and populous, for villages and lone farms, and the fourth field of VB-080 if that is done (the fourth joins the farm's block).
- [ ] Founders start with no fields (VB-010), so `chooseTile` decides their layout: it scores a tile that touches one of the farm's fields (or its project) +1, so a new field joins the block when a tile within `REACH` touches it; only when none does may it start a new block.
- [ ] A test on populous medium at seed 7: every farm's fields are connected and within 4 steps of its house. Founders at seed 7 after 10 years: the share of farms whose fields are connected (reported).
- [ ] The jsdom smoke test passes, and the fences (`makeFence`) still close around each infield.
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
Notes / questions:
- Fields up to 4 steps away mean longer walks than today (1–2 steps); the work arch's wrap-up (VB-108) sees the cost in the idle report.
