Decision (Deciding without Kim; a close call, noted in the inbox): **`attitude.malleability` has no term for a town, and none
replaces it** (4.4 and 6.1's Culture drift row, marked Proposed). The criterion offered two measures or "open". By principle 8
(mechanisms, not prescriptions) a rate set on a place is a patch where a mechanism already gives the effect: attitudes move by
contact, and a busy place's people meet more people, and more of other villages, at its venues (`design.md`, The gathering,
Customs drawn together). Crowding was not chosen as the measure: living close is not meeting. Whether contact alone is fast
enough is left open, as `design.md`'s open question on cities has it; if a term is added later it reads those a pawn met at
venues over a year. VB-834 measures it.
