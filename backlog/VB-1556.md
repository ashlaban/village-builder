Goal: The second ask of design.md's row A member's voice: a duty. A member asks its head for a type of work as its own: an offer on a new basis `duty`, answered by the head by the member's skill at the type (as the head sees it, `sim/estimate`) and by who holds the duty now; a yes gives the duty as the head's `assign` with a duty does (VB-1771), a no is a memory. A pawn asks of its own accord for the type it is most skilled at when it holds no duty of that type and has held none for a season, at most once a season. The played member asks through its Actions; the played head answers with advice.

- [x] The played member's Actions offer the ask; the played head answers it.
