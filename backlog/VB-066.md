# VB-066: The guardian of a minor heir, until the heir is 18
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion (Succession: a minor heir holds in name, with a guardian until 18: the surviving parent or the nearest adult relative).
Goal: `succession` sets `hh.guardian` to the oldest adult in the house, but nothing reads it, nothing clears it, and it is missing from the `@state` schema.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The guardian is the surviving parent if alive and in the household, else the nearest adult relative in the household by civil degree (the oldest on a tie), else the oldest adult in the household, else `null`.
- [ ] At the first daily step after the heir turns 18, `hh.guardian` becomes `null` and the heir gets the life event "Came of age and took charge of <farm>".
- [ ] The household panel shows "Guardian: <name>" while there is one. `@state` documents `hh.guardian` (pawn id or null).
- [ ] A test: the heirs scenario's farm A4 holder dies with children 8 and 5; the widow is guardian; 10 years later the guardian is cleared.
- [ ] Re-record the golden file if it changes and list the changed entries in the PR.
Notes / questions:
- Civil degree comes with VB-029; if it is not done, use the kinship coefficient to rank relatives and say so in the PR.
