# VB-066: The guardian of a minor heir, until the heir is 18
Status: done (2026-10-30)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion (Succession: a minor heir holds in name, with a guardian until 18: the surviving parent or the nearest adult relative).
Goal: `succession` sets `hh.guardian` to the oldest adult in the house, but nothing reads it, nothing clears it, and it is missing from the `@state` schema.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The guardian is the surviving parent if alive and in the household, else the nearest adult relative in the household by civil degree (the oldest on a tie), else the oldest adult in the household, else `null`.
- [x] At the first daily step after the heir turns 18, `hh.guardian` becomes `null` and the heir gets the life event "Came of age and took charge of <farm>".
- [x] The household panel shows "Guardian: <name>" while there is one. `@state` documents `hh.guardian` (pawn id or null).
- [x] A test: the heirs scenario's farm A4 holder dies with children 8 and 5; the widow is guardian; 10 years later the guardian is cleared.
- [x] Re-record the golden file if it changes and list the changed entries in the PR.
Notes / questions:
- Civil degree comes with VB-029; if it is not done, use the kinship coefficient to rank relatives and say so in the PR.
- Done 2026-10-30: relatives are ranked by the kinship coefficient (VB-029 not done). `hh.ward` (the minor's id) is new. The test `tests/guardian.js` runs 14 years because the heir under the heirs scenario's sonsFirst custom is Olof (5), who is 18 after 13. The "took charge" event is given only when the ward holds the farm; under a widow's life right nobody takes charge at 18, so the guardian is only cleared.
