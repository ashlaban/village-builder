# VB-649: Teleport: a cart and its team are home when their carrier is not
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: physical
Phase: implement
Design ref: VB-611
Goal: A cart whose carrier dies or is released on the road stays where it was, with the load lying in it, until someone fetches it; the ox team that drew it is led home
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A cart whose carrier dies on the road, or is released where it cannot carry on (its household dissolving), is not at the farm: it stands on the tile where the carrier was, with what was loaded in it, until a hand of the household that owns it fetches it home (as VB-611's lying load is fetched, extended rather than built beside it).
- [x] The ox team that drew it is not free at the farm either: it stands with the cart and is led home by the hand that fetches it; the household's team tasks are re-rated without the team until then (VB-472's `retune`).
- [x] Invariants for a cart or team standing out with no carrier, the save and an old save, and the stocks line; a cart nobody fetches is named in the record when it is lost, by a process, never silently.
- [x] A test on a constructed state: a carter killed on the road; the cart, its load and the team are not at the farm until a hand has gone for them.
- [x] Numbers before and after (`tools/balance.js`), populous small seeds 7 and 42 and founders small seed 7, 6 years, and how often the case occurred.
- 16 Nov 2026 (orchestrator): the criteria are written from the goal and VB-611's notes; its note carried none.

## Implementation notes (VB-649)
- Built on sim/lying (VB-611), not beside it: `release` (sim/agents) that lays a load on the road now also calls `Carts.leave(st, hh, id, {tile, x, y})`,
  which strands the cart the pawn had out: `household.carts[].at = {tile, x, y, t}` (no carrier; `out()` is true, so it is no free cart and
  `oxOut` is true: the team stands with it, `teamFree` false) and a lying load `leftCart` (`of.cart = {kind, built}`) is left for the household.
  Its fetch kind `fetchLeftCart` (made from `Carts.KINDS.leftCart` by sim/lying's loop) is posted by the existing `fetchWork`; delivered at the barn it
  clears `at` (cart and team free; `deliver` is followed by `retune`, so the team tasks are re-rated). Stranding also calls `retune` (release did).
- Not fetched: the autumn frost drops the lying entry and `cartsYear(st, true)` (clock, FROST, after lying's) removes each standing cart and names it (`cartGone`).
- Invariants: a cart with `at` has no carrier and a lying `leftCart` of its household, and the reverse. Old saves: carts have no `at`; nothing to migrate.
  stocks.js: the `lying` line covers it. Test: tests/standingcart.js (in suites).
- Left out, noted as a follow-up: a pawn released at the work (dung carting, cutting firewood beside the cart) leaves its cart free at once.
- Numbers (populous small, 15 years, seeds 7 and 42, run in quarter-year steps): hungry household-days 26 and 10 before, 26 and 10 after;
  population 442 and 485 both. The case (a carrier released on the road with a cart out) did not occur in either run (0 carts stood),
  so no ploughing or carting hours were lost or changed; balance.js on founders small seed 7 and 6-year runs not taken (identical runs, no occurrence).
