# VB-649: Teleport: a cart and its team are home when their carrier is not
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: physical
Phase: implement
Design ref: VB-611
Goal: A cart whose carrier dies or is released on the road stays where it was, with the load lying in it, until someone fetches it; the ox team that drew it is led home
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A cart whose carrier dies on the road, or is released where it cannot carry on (its household dissolving), is not at the farm: it stands on the tile where the carrier was, with what was loaded in it, until a hand of the household that owns it fetches it home (as VB-611's lying load is fetched, extended rather than built beside it).
- [ ] The ox team that drew it is not free at the farm either: it stands with the cart and is led home by the hand that fetches it; the household's team tasks are re-rated without the team until then (VB-472's `retune`).
- [ ] Invariants for a cart or team standing out with no carrier, the save and an old save, and the stocks line; a cart nobody fetches is named in the record when it is lost, by a process, never silently.
- [ ] A test on a constructed state: a carter killed on the road; the cart, its load and the team are not at the farm until a hand has gone for them.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (`tools/balance.js`), populous small seeds 7 and 42 and founders small seed 7, 6 years, and how often the case occurred.
- 16 Nov 2026 (orchestrator): the criteria are written from the goal and VB-611's notes; its note carried none.
