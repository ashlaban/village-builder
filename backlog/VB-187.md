Goal: Before stories are built, test them against the baseline of opinion averaging, so the design of slice 7 rests on measured spread, distortion and forgetting rather than on guesses.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A reduced scenario: a tiny map, three villages, abstract gatherings standing for a fair and an alehouse, and seeded stories. It is a tool, not part of the game: it touches no module of the simulation.
- [x] Three probes: the spread curve (the share of each village's adults holding a story, by day); distortion (the valence held against the valence at the source, by hops); strangers' opinion of the subject.
- [x] Both models on the same seeds, with the bands of target-v2, section 10.2, reported for each.
- [x] A short report in `docs/` with the numbers, and questions for Kim that the talk plan (VB-186) takes up.
