# VB-631: The means to marry: a rule of the registry, explained in the pawn panel
Status: done (2026-11-17)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, People, households and family records (Marriage, nursing and growth: The setpoint); backlog/VB-405.md
Goal: The means a home must hold before a couple may marry is the marriage valve's setpoint (Kim, 13 Nov). It becomes a rule of the registry, so that later contributions can move it with their cause, and it is explained where it binds: the pawn panel says how much of the means the home holds.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The rule: `marriage.means`, declared by `sim/matching` in `VB['sim/registry']` (combine `product`, base `MARRIAGE.means`, the context the home's household), with no contribution yet; `affords` reads it through `Rules.value`, once a home a round under its memo as today. It shows in `node tools/rules.js`. Nothing else of the gate changes.
- [x] One formula, two readers: `Sim.meansOf(st, householdId)` gives `{ held, asked, exempt }`, where `held` is the years of food the home holds above its seed for its members and one more (grain, grain in sheaf, meals and the larder, less what is reserved, over `YEAR_DAYS` times the daily need and an adult's ration), `asked` the rule's value, and `exempt` true for a home with fewer than two adults; `affords` is `exempt || held >= asked` by the same function, so the words and the gate cannot part.
- [x] `matchBar`'s reason names the numbers: "their home holds 0.9 of the 1.5 years of food it needs" (it was "their home cannot feed another mouth"); the player's wish for a match and the offer show it as they show the reason today.
- [x] The pawn panel, for an unmarried pawn of the least age or more (18 for a woman, 20 for a man), says what stands between it and a marriage, the first that holds of: too hungry to marry; no holding to marry onto (no place under the residence custom in force); "Cannot marry yet: the home holds 0.9 of the 1.5 years it needs"; else "The home has the means to marry: it holds 2.1 of the 1.5 years asked", or "The home needs a spouse's hands: no means are asked". It is what the home holds today, counted, and no forecast, so no estimate is named (principle 14). `node tests/smoke.js`, the `tests/browser*.js` touched, and a screenshot under `docs/img/`.
- [x] Behaviour is unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` against the commit the task starts from; `ruleValue` rises by the homes checked at a round, reported from `node tests/counters.js`.
- [x] Tests (`tests/means.js`, on populous seed 7 with the stores set by hand, not tiny.js; the exempt home is made by ageing its members): a home just under and just over the means, with the reason's numbers; the exempt home; `meansOf` and `affords` agree over the probe's households; the rule's explanation (`Rules.explain`) gives the base and no parts.
- [x] `docs/architecture/modules.md` or the rule's comment says where a later contribution joins (a custom, a head's caution, a servant's portion: VB-637).
