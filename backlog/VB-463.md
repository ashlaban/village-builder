# VB-463: The alderman's keep: days of work or a share of the surplus, by the village's practice
Status: done (2026-11-19)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Households and other bodies (The alderman's keep; Roads to the elite); QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-404 (answer 3) and VB-406 (answer 3); backlog/VB-404.md
Goal: The office is the first road to the elite (Kim, 13 November), and it needs an income: Kim, 15 November: both kinds, as a practice the meeting can move.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A practice "What the alderman is kept with" (days of work, default; a share of the surplus; nothing; malleability 0.4), in the customs' table, held and voted as any practice.
- [x] Days: at each autumn meeting with an alderman in office, each other farm's household of the village owes his household `OFFICE.keepDays` (4) days to the next autumn, as days owed with the source `keep` (VB-462). A vacancy ends the days left; a new alderman's begin at his election.
- [x] A share: at the moving round each other household of the village sets aside `OFFICE.keepShare` (0.1) of its surplus, reckoned as the store's quarter is and beside it; it is his where it lies, and a member of his household fetches it, a back-load a journey (nothing teleports); what is not fetched by the next moving round returns to its household. Through `sim/stores`, with the reason `keep`.
- [ ] Both are income from others for the class (VB-318): the grain as received, the days through the fields' hours. (undone: the days feed hoursOther already (VB-462); the grain received is booked only in the ledger with reason `keep`, as VB-318 is not built: it reads that.)
- [x] Events and log: the keep owed and given, a year's sum a village (routine lines folded into yearly counts, rule 8).
- [ ] Numbers, populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 and founders small seeds 7, 42, 30 years, for each option of the practice forced in turn and for the default left to drift: the keep a village-year in rations (days at their fields' harvest share), against the alderman's household's need (the plan's size: about 0.7); his household's total food against need (about 1.5 wanted); hungry household-days of the payers, by household; growth. Parameterisation (Deciding without Kim, 3; five short runs): `keepDays` 2, 4, 7 and `keepShare` 0.05, 0.1, 0.17. (undone: not measured; owed to the tuning run, the five parameter runs too.)
- [x] Cost (rule 9): one obligation a farm a village a year; a fetch journey a paying household a year under the share. No daily work.
Notes:
