# VB-463: The alderman's keep: days of work or a share of the surplus, by the village's practice
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-314, VB-462
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Households and other bodies (The alderman's keep; Roads to the landed class); QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-404 (answer 3) and VB-406 (answer 3); backlog/VB-404.md
Goal: The office is the first road to the landed class (Kim, 13 November), and it needs an income: Kim, 15 November: both kinds, as a practice the meeting can move.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A practice "What the alderman is kept with" (days of work, default; a share of the surplus; nothing; malleability 0.4), in the customs' table, held and voted as any practice.
- [ ] Days: at each autumn meeting with an alderman in office, each other farm's household of the village owes his household `OFFICE.keepDays` (4) days to the next autumn, as days owed with the source `keep` (VB-462). A vacancy ends the days left; a new alderman's begin at his election.
- [ ] A share: at the moving round each other household of the village sets aside `OFFICE.keepShare` (0.1) of its surplus, reckoned as the store's quarter is and beside it; it is his where it lies, and a member of his household fetches it, a back-load a journey (nothing teleports); what is not fetched by the next moving round returns to its household. Through `sim/stores`, with the reason `keep`.
- [ ] Both are income from others for the class (VB-318): the grain as received, the days through the fields' hours.
- [ ] Events and log: the keep owed and given, a year's sum a village (routine lines folded into yearly counts, rule 8).
- [ ] Numbers, populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 and founders small seeds 7, 42, 30 years, for each option of the practice forced in turn and for the default left to drift: the keep a village-year in rations (days at their fields' harvest share), against the alderman's household's need (the plan's size: about 0.7); his household's total food against need (about 1.5 wanted); hungry household-days of the payers, by household; growth. Parameterisation (Deciding without Kim, 3; five short runs): `keepDays` 2, 4, 7 and `keepShare` 0.05, 0.1, 0.17.
- [ ] Cost (rule 9): one obligation a farm a village a year; a fetch journey a paying household a year under the share. No daily work.
Notes:
- 15 Nov 2026: written by VB-404's plan. Kim, 15 November: both kinds, as a practice; and an alderman is not thereby landed. The sizes are the plan's: 14 farms × 4 days × 3 rations, or a tenth of a village's surplus of about 1,700 rations, are each about 170 rations, 0.7 of the need of a household of 5 (240), against the class's 1.25: the keep must be joined by rents, clients' days or hired hands' harvest to make an alderman landed.
- 15 Nov 2026: It replaces the keep answered on 13 November (a quarter of what households give the village store; `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, VB-404, answer 3), which the store's target (VB-457) cut to about 21 rations a year (VB-406, answer 3).
