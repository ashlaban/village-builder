# VB-313: Refactor: Decisions as a module, the meeting's votes asked through it
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Customs and culture, Meetings, culture and splits; Exchange, Loans, obligations and cases (Cases at the meeting); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, section 4.5 (Decisions)
Goal: Slice 5 adds questions with several voices: a case, the alderman's election. Today the only such question, a custom's change at the meeting, is voted inside `Culture.meeting` (`src/culture.js`). Make one module that poses a question to a body, runs its procedure and keeps the ballots, and move the custom votes into it unchanged, so the case and the office are new kinds of question, not new voting code.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A module `sim/decisions` with `ask(st, question)`: a question names its body (a village), its kind, its options and the kind's evaluation, found by kind in a registry the posing module fills at load (a pending question is plain data); `procedureFor(st, body, kind)` returns the procedure from the body's practices, with the kind's default.
- [ ] Two procedures now: `vote` (voices weighted as today's meeting weighs them, under "Who speaks at the meeting"; a margin) and `agent` (one pawn decides); a tie escalates to the body's presiding pawn when it has one (VB-314), else the question's fallback.
- [ ] The meeting's custom votes are asked through it, with the same voices, weights, margins and order of draws: `tests/golden.json` unchanged.
- [ ] Each decision keeps its ballots (support for each option) and margin for the year, bounded (the last 30, like `st.meetingLog`), so VB-082 can show them.
- [ ] Refactor rules: behaviour unchanged, `npm run verify` passes, no renames of `Culture`'s API; the PR says what fell or moved.
Cost: unchanged: the same votes, now through one call.
Order: beside VB-310 and VB-311. Runs one at a time with VB-278 (the meetings a village a step), which edits the same lines of `src/sim/lifecycle.js`; whichever lands second follows the other. VB-314, VB-315 and VB-082 build on it.
