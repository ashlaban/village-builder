## Implementation notes (VB-853)
Tests only; nothing under `src/` changed. `tests/lib/probe.js` gains `island(seed, size)` and `scenario(name, seed)`: small generated
islands, 36 by 26 (as `tests/page.js`), 48 by 34 for heirs (on 36 by 26 and 30 by 22 it throws "Not enough room for the debug
villages"). Each is a tenth of a second to generate and seed.

Decision: the scenario cases use these small generated islands rather than `tinyWorld` maps. `Sim.seed` runs all three scenarios on a
`tinyWorld` island too, but a populous year there costs about twice as much (the hand-drawn all-meadow land fills up), and
`saves` asserted that a save loads into a world generated again from its seed and size, which a `tinyWorld` cannot show.
`tests/page.js` already uses a generated 36 by 26 island in verify.

Where each assertion went:
- `saves` (was 43 s at night, the default and tiny maps over 2+1 years): now in INTEGRATION, small. Each key of the state and the
  changed tiles agree after save, JSON, load into a regenerated world and advance: founders 42 (saved at 0.6 y, through the autumn
  meetings and the round), heirs 7 and populous 7 (a few days in), on small islands; the mid-hour save with the idlers
  drawn and the mid-day save between talk turns: on the probe village (10 days + 0.7 h and + 10.5 h, 3 days on). The refusal of
  format-2 and format-3 saves: on the probe. Deleted: length (years before the save) and seed 42 of heirs (it does not seed on a small
  island). Length is not a rule.
- `clock --extended` (populous on the tiny map, 2 years by chunks of 2 y, a day, an hour, 0.1 h): now `clock --integration`, the probe a
  year and a day by chunks of the whole, a day and 0.1 h (same tick count, `st.t`, invariants and state hash). The hour-chunk falls from
  the year case and stays in the 2-day case of `npm test`. The long run is deleted.
- `spread --extended`: the same assertions as its probe case in `npm test` (the round's steps, the nights, the meetings' order and
  saves between phases), on the populous tiny map. Deleted: the probe case holds each rule, with its two villages (the meetings' order
  still wraps in one year of two). The load into a generated world is now `saves`'.
- `paths --extended`: worn-in start of populous and heirs and none for the founders, on small islands (thresholds scaled to the land:
  populous more than 5 trodden and a path, against 60 and 10); a populous season, not a year, for wear only where pawns walk, on fields,
  never on houses, greens or water, stages fitting counts, more than 20 worn tiles; then lanes laid by hand round every house and
  green and a green laid on open land, for: a house draws nothing, only its door sends a way to it, at most three non-adjacent ways
  onto a green and every other trail side joins the way beside it (now asserted to happen at least once), trodden ground draws
  nothing, every trail tile has segments within a tile, `thin` drops a decoration on a lane and keeps one clear (now asserted to
  have a lane). Stays at night, as `paths --extended`, saying so in its first line: the default island's year, where the quota "more
  than a quarter of the houses have a way to their door" is a statistic of a whole island's traffic (1 of 10 on the small island
  after a year, since its houses mostly face the green).
- `indexes --extended` (founders 10 y, populous 3 y on 180 by 130, heirs 5 y; compared each year): now `indexes --integration`,
  compared at the seed and every season of founders (4), heirs (2) and populous (1) on small islands, then after each of 7 deaths
  by `Sim.killPawn` (a holder whose widow and heir take over; a whole household, the holder last, which ends it) and 2 days on. The
  short runs bring few events by themselves (2 born, none died), so the deaths are made; the record's other mutations stay in the
  probe case of `npm test`. The long runs are deleted.

Validation: each script passes in each mode; `paths --extended` passes on the default island (unchanged numbers: worn in
377/211/52, after a year 832/418/150, 352 segments). Times on this machine at load 12 to 25 on 4 cores are descriptions: user CPU
`saves` 4.7 s, `clock --integration` 2.3 s, `paths --integration` 1.7 s, `indexes --integration` 3.0 s (wall 5 to 17 s under that
load). The night loses `saves`, `spread --extended`, `clock --extended` and `indexes --extended` (about 110 s measured in the 245-second
verify).
