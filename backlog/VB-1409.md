# VB-1409: Refactor: a journey's kinds as one table (what arriving, resting, falling and a new home do)
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: none (code structure). design.md, Between villages (A journey; The suitor's visit); docs/architecture/target-v2.md section 2; backlog/VB-422.md
Goal: A journey's kind is its stay's why (move, carry, fetch, haul, visit), and sim/away asks it in a dozen places: endJourney is a chain of four tests, legDue, dropStay and the change of home (retarget) each test it again, and sim/agents' describe and sim/visits and sim/gifts read it too. As sim/kinds does for job kinds, one table in sim/away says for each kind what its arrival, its morning on the road, its bearer's death and its household's move do; the functions that carry a journey ask the table. This is the journeys' part of VB-422's split, done first so that the split moves one concern.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `sim/away` declares the journey kinds once, as code (never saved state: a stay keeps only its `why`, a string): for each of `move`, `carry`, `fetch`, `haul` and `visit`, what it does on arrival (today's chain in `endJourney`), on the morning its rest ends (`legDue`: the guest's days), when its pawn dies away (`dropStay`: the gift laid down) and when its pawn's household changes under way (`retarget`); a kind with nothing of its own for one of these names none and gets the common course.
- [ ] `endJourney`, `legDue`, `dropStay` and `retarget` ask the table and hold no test of `j.why` against a kind's name; the sub-conditions that are state, not kind (`j.back`, `j.gift`, `j.stay`), stay in the kind's own function. What remains outside the table is listed in the task file with its reason (the reads in `sim/agents` describe, `sim/visits` and `sim/gifts` may stay reads of the string, or go through a small read `sim/away` exports, whichever the layers allow: say which).
- [ ] A lodger's stay is not a journey though it shares `st.away` and may carry the same word (`why: 'visit'` is both the suitor's journey and a lodger's caller's word, sim/away's `@state`): how the two are told apart today is found and kept, and said in a line where the table is declared.
- [ ] The order of the tests in `endJourney` is part of the behaviour (a carrier who has delivered ends as a move does): the table gives the same course for every state a stay can be in; the `@state st.away` comment names the table as where a kind's course is read.
- [ ] Not in this task: no change to the stays' record, to the verbs `lodge`, `comeHome`, `visit`, `sendVisit`, to provisions or to legs in `sim/agents`; the split of `sim/agents` and of journeys out of it stays VB-422's; no new module unless `sim/away` would cross the lint's line (then the split task AGENTS.md asks for).
- [ ] Behaviour is unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` at `--scenario founders,heirs --seeds 7,42 --map small --years 3` and at a run in which every kind is seen (a move between villages, a gift carried, a gift fetched and hauled, a suitor's visit, a death on the road if one can be found: count them with a scratch watcher on `personEvent` kinds or `st.away`, and say which run shows which; the kinds no run shows are covered by the focused check). The lines are in the result; no save leaf changes.
- [ ] A focused check on a small constructed state that walks each kind through arrival, the morning, a death and a change of home against the table, in `tests/suites.js`; `npm run format` and `npm run verify` pass; the lint, width and ownership baselines do not rise.
