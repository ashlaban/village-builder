# VB-126: Occasions: the meeting, weddings and funeral ales bring people together
Status: done (2026-11-11)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Customs and culture (Meetings: the yearly *bystämma*); Systems overview (Pace: rare, legible events carry the game at 10×). Relationships review, 31 Oct 2026 (`docs/review-relationships.md`, findings F2 and proposal D3): villages are socially sealed (no pair from two villages ever meets), so courting, which needs ties and opinion, would keep marriages inside the village.
Goal: Gatherings that happen somewhere on the map, at which people from several households, and from other villages, meet and talk: the village meeting on the green, the wedding feast, the funeral ale.
Acceptance criteria (Kim answered both questions as proposed, 10 Nov; `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-126"):
- [x] An occasion: a place (a tile and a spot), a start and an end in game hours, and who is asked. Those asked who are free walk there at the start and stand about it until the end; talk (VB-061) happens there as anywhere, so ties and opinion grow among those who come, and across villages.
- [x] The village meeting: the people with a voice gather on the green from 10:00 until the meeting at 12:00. The meeting and a funeral ale override work; a wedding takes those who are free.
- [x] A wedding: on the day after a marriage, from 16:00 to 21:00 at the couple's farm, both families (near kin of both spouses), from any village within a day's walk.
- [x] A funeral ale: on the day after a death, from 16:00 to 21:00 at the farm of the dead, its household and near kin.
- [x] The page shows a gathering as it is: the people standing round, their bubbles; a line in the log for a wedding or funeral ale.
- [x] Reported: ties and friends across villages per adult before and after, over 20 populous years.
- [x] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR. (Left to the orchestrator by the night-run rules; the entries are listed under Validation.)

- Implementation (11 Nov 2026). Impact note: a new module, `src/sim/occasions.js` (after `sim/commands`), owns `st.occasions`,
  `st.occasionSeq` and `agent.occasion` (`src/owners.json`), and draws from a new stream, `st.rng.occasions`. It reads the records (kin,
  members, voices), the route search (a bounded search for a guest's walking hours) and the player's `unplanned`; it is called by
  `sim/lifecycle` (`wed` calls the wedding, `killPawn` the funeral ale, before the dead leaves its household) and by `sim/clock` (the
  meetings are called at midnight of autumn day 10; `occasionsDue` reads the head of the queue each six-minute step, after the hour's
  `onHour`). A guest acts through a new verb, `attend` (`sim/commands`): it leaves the work in hand, walks to a spot in a ring about the
  occasion's spot and idles there until the end, so the planner leaves it alone and talk (VB-061) finds it among the others outdoors. Two
  readers outside the module: the end of work (`sim/clock`) does not send a pawn at an occasion home, and a talk does not halt a pawn on its
  way to one (`sim/agents`, `haltForTalk`). `Sim.describe` says "At a wedding", "Walking to a funeral ale" and so on. A wedding and a
  funeral ale are told in the log at their end, with how many came and how many from other villages (kinds `wedding` and `funeralAle`,
  routine: folded after a generation, beside the `married` and `died` events that are kept; in the Marriages and the Births and deaths
  filters). Nothing new loops over a growing collection but the meeting's call, once a year, over each village's members (as the meeting
  itself does), counted by a new counter, `occasionAsked`. Cost: about 20 weddings, 20 funeral ales and 6 meetings a year on small; at most
  one bounded route search (12 hours' reach) per guest household of another farm. `tools/shot.js` takes `--hours H` to catch a time of
  day; `tools/sim.js --report relations` adds ties and friends across villages per adult.
- Orchestrator's decisions for Kim to overturn (11 Nov 2026): (1) The meeting's people stand on the green from 10:00 to 12:30: the
  meeting is held in four six-minute steps from noon (`meetingStage`), and they disperse once it is done. (2) Near kin are the parents,
  children, siblings and spouse (`kinNear`); a wedding also asks the couple's household. Children under 4 (talk's minimum age) stay at
  home. (3) A day's walk is measured from the guest's house to the farm by a route search bounded at 12 hours, until VB-286's table of
  walking hours between villages; a guest sets out its walking hours (whole hours) before the start, never before 6:00, and one who could
  not arrive an hour before the end does not go. (4) A wedding takes those who are free when their time comes, and looks again each hour,
  so those at work come when work ends at 18:00. (5) A pawn stands at one occasion at a time, the first it is sent to; the couple leaves
  another for its own wedding. All of a round's marriages fall on one day, so their weddings fall on the next, and kin asked to two go to
  the first. (6) The played pawn is not sent: it goes by the player's choice. (7) Two deaths of one farm on one day have one funeral ale.
  (8) The meeting is not told in the log (one line a village a year would add nothing the custom lines do not say).
