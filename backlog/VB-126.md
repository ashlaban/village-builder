# VB-126: Occasions: the meeting, weddings and funeral ales bring people together
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Customs and culture (Meetings: the yearly *bystämma*); Systems overview (Pace: rare, legible events carry the game at 10×). Relationships review, 31 Oct 2026 (`docs/review-relationships.md`, findings F2 and proposal D3): villages are socially sealed (no pair from two villages ever meets), so courting, which needs ties and opinion, would keep marriages inside the village.
Goal: Gatherings that happen somewhere on the map, at which people from several households, and from other villages, meet and talk: the village meeting on the green, the wedding feast, the funeral ale.
Acceptance criteria (Kim answered both questions as proposed, 10 Nov; `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-126"):
- [ ] An occasion: a place (a tile and a spot), a start and an end in game hours, and who is asked. Those asked who are free walk there at the start and stand about it until the end; talk (VB-061) happens there as anywhere, so ties and opinion grow among those who come, and across villages.
- [ ] The village meeting: the people with a voice gather on the green from 10:00 until the meeting at 12:00. The meeting and a funeral ale override work; a wedding takes those who are free.
- [ ] A wedding: on the day after a marriage, from 16:00 to 21:00 at the couple's farm, both families (near kin of both spouses), from any village within a day's walk.
- [ ] A funeral ale: on the day after a death, from 16:00 to 21:00 at the farm of the dead, its household and near kin.
- [ ] The page shows a gathering as it is: the people standing round, their bubbles; a line in the log for a wedding or funeral ale.
- [ ] Reported: ties and friends across villages per adult before and after, over 20 populous years.
