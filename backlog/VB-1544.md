Goal: A design pass; no code. A pawn's wish at the moving round (`sim/wishes` `chooseWish`) ranks its rungs by two things: ambition (new land above a plot) and hunger (stay above a match). Whom the pawn is tied to and what it thinks of its head weigh nothing. Propose what they would add, as mechanisms (Principles: mechanisms, not prescriptions; a wait is the thing waited for): a pawn who courts ranks the match with its partner first and declines others; one with close friends or kin in a village ranks farms and plots there above others; one who thinks ill of its head ranks service and leaving above staying. For each: the rule, its parameter with a meaning, what it reads, its cost, and what it moves of the growth targets (marriages, the age at marriage, households founded), measured by a mock or a short experiment (at most five runs).
