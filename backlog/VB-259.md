# VB-259: Meals and foods: a ration is only the unit
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods and the farming year (Food, Meals), design.md section 8; Kim, 10 November
Goal: "Ration" does two jobs: it is the unit (one adult's food for one day; grain, milk and debts are counted in it) and it is a good a household stores (`hh.rations`, cooked from grain by `cook`, `src/sim/stores.js`). Kim, 10 November: a ration is only the unit. What a household stores are foods, each worth so many rations, and meals, which the cook makes from them. Make that the shape of the stores before fish, gathered food and mixed meals arrive (VB-117 to VB-119), with nothing else changed.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The household's cooked store is `hh.meals`, counted in rations; the good's name in `sim/stores` is `'meals'`, in every operation, reason and ledger line, in `src/sim/` and `src/page/`, and in the tools and tests that name it (`grep -rn "rations" src tools tests` then finds only the unit). The page says "meals" for the cooked store and keeps "rations" as the unit ("12 rations of grain", "Owes 34 rations").
- [x] One table of foods, `FOODS` in `src/sim/rules.js`: for each food its worth in rations a unit, its shelf life in days (none for grain), its source for a meal's variety (grain and flour are one source) and whether it is eaten uncooked. Rows today: grain (1, none, `grain`). Every place that puts food into a household's store under another reason (milk, butter, salted and fresh meat: `src/sim/byre.js`, `src/sim/herds.js`) is listed in the PR with what it puts and where; these keep going where they go today, and each gets its row, so the table names every food the game has. No food moves differently.
- [x] `cook` takes foods and makes meals through one function that reads the table (today: grain only, 1 to 1).
- [x] Saves: the field is renamed, so an older save is refused as any other build's (VB-214's rule; `tests/saves.js`). `@state` lines, `src/owners.json` and the invariants follow the name.
- [x] Behaviour is unchanged: every number of `node tools/sim.js --scenario populous --seed 7 --map small --years 3` is the same before and after. `tests/golden.json` is re-recorded only for the field's name; the PR lists the changed entries and shows they differ in nothing else. Counters unchanged. `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals (the page changes).
- [x] The PR's impact note, and what fell: places that treated the cooked store and the unit as one.
