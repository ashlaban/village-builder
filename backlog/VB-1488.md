# VB-1488: Refactor: the interface's shared pieces: a theme styles the pieces, a component composes them
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: L
Design ref: Kim, 23 November; VB-1484
Goal: A theme with its own shapes is the styling of a few shared pieces, not a rule for each component; the look of Glass and Mural may shift to get there.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The shared pieces are named and listed once, beside the token list at the head of `src/head.html`: panel, head, button (with its pressed and primary states), icon button, row, meter; a piece more only with its reason in the notes. Each is a class (or the element, for `button`) a component's markup carries.
- [ ] Each theme styles each piece once, in its own section; a rule under a theme's selector names a piece, not a component. The components (cards, dock, action bar, top bar, drawer, menus, the screens) compose pieces and carry layout only. What must stay a theme's rule for one component is counted in the notes with its reason; the aim is Mural's 113 `:root[data-theme="mural"]` rules down to 25 or fewer, and Glass's 14 split-off rules down to its pieces.
- [ ] Mural's own images survive as its styling of the pieces: the band is its panel, black letter its head, the medallion its icon button, the leaf its button, the beads its meter. Hand tuning of single components beyond that goes.
- [ ] One rule for pressed: every pressed, chosen or primary button takes the theme's pressed look (the six exceptions of VB-1484 go). One rule for corners: no literal `border-radius` that a theme's tokens should say (the nine radii VB-1484 left literal follow the tokens); circles and pills may stay.
- [ ] The look may change, and Kim judges it: screenshots before and after, side by side, of the menu, Options, the choice of whom to play, a household card, the same with the action menu open, a village card, the drawer and a history tab, in Glass light, Glass dark and Mural at 1440 by 900, and the menu and a card at 390 by 844; saved outside the repository at the path the result names, never committed. The notes list each change of look a person would notice, by theme.
- [ ] `tests/themes.js` checks the new rule: a theme's rule names only pieces (or is on the counted list); `tests/page.js` follows any markup change. `npm run verify`, `node tests/page.js`, `node tests/smoke.js` and the browser tests the change touches green.
- [ ] `docs/architecture/modules.md`, Adding things: a new theme is its tokens and its styling of the pieces; a new component uses pieces.
