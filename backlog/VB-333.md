# VB-333: The harvest is the year's peak: reaping 15 hours a field and threshing 20
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (Goods and the farming year: Field work, Tilling power). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Field work (11 Nov)
Goal: Three fields cost a household about 215 pawn-hours a year, under a fifth of two hands' working time, and less than its water and cooking; the hoeing is 61 of them, the reaping 17 and the threshing 19, so the crowded time of the grain year is the spring and the harvest is over in a day or two. Kim, 11 Nov: grain should take a larger share of the year, by the reaping and threshing hours, which also makes the harvest the peak.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `CROPS.barley.hours` reap 5 to 15 and thresh 6 to 20 (`src/sim/rules.js`, since VB-346 the kinds read them from there); both keep `hands: Infinity`, so the whole household reaps and threshes together. Nothing else in the two kinds changes.
- [x] The harvest still comes in: with the reaping three times as long, a household of two hands reaps three fields in about two days in place of one. Report crops lost to frost before and after; if they rise, the reaping's urgency against the frost (`reapFrostPerDay`) is the lever, not the hours.
- [x] Threshing is the barn's work of late autumn and winter: 20 hours a field's sheaves. The means to marry already counts grain in sheaf; report whether any household goes hungry with sheaves unthreshed in the barn, and if so the thresh job's urgency rises with the household's hunger (one line, in the kind's urgency).
- [x] Numbers before and after (populous and founders, small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years; `tools/balance.js` at seeds 3, 7, 11, 42): pawn-hours by field job and their share of the hands' year, the days from ripe to reaped, to the barn and to threshed, crops lost to frost, fields left unsown, hungry household-days and deaths, idle hours; whether households, not only averages, are worse off, and which (the short-handed, the lone holder). The golden file and the work counters are re-recorded and their changed entries listed; the bands of `tests/lib/bands.js` reported, none re-set.
