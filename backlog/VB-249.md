# VB-249: Windows and the due-queue, then planning days by diligence
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-248
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Work: opportunities, plans and tasks (design.md, section 7); docs/review-work.md, sections 2 and 4
Goal: Every holder is reviewed every working hour. Most of that is polling for work that falls due at a known hour or cannot have changed. Open the time-bound kinds from a due-queue, and review each plan on its holder's planning day.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Each time-bound kind declares its window (water from 5:00, cooking from 14:00, milking from 6:00, fodder, threshing, and the haul from the hour the stooks are dry); a due-queue in `st`, plain data with one writer (`sim/plans`), opens each at its hour (Rules for cost, 4). No task of these kinds waits for a review.
- [ ] A holder's review runs on its planning day: every day, every second or every third, by the head's diligence (a stand-in until VB-254: one number per pawn from a new seeded stream `traits` in `SYSTEMS`, `src/sim/rng.js`; the thirds of its range give the three cadences), offset by the holder's id so reviews spread over days and over the hours of the working day (rule 5).
- [ ] A review also runs at once when a window is about to close (sowing, the harvest before the frost) and when a store crosses a target: `sim/stores` sets one dirty flag per holder and `sim/plans` reads and clears it, at most one such review per holder a day. No other call from stores to plans.
- [ ] A member without a task still acts on the household's opportunities, the plan's tasks first (`Opportunities.forPawn`), so a ripe field found between reviews is not left.
- [ ] Behaviour changes. Before and after, populous small, 3 years, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42: farmers' idle share by season, fields sown and reaped in their windows, hungry household-days, and the worst household's hungry days (not only the mean); in the PR and in this file. No seed's hungry household-days rise by more than a tenth. Re-record the golden file and the counters and list the changes.
- [ ] Cost measured: `oppAsk` a living pawn-day falls at least fivefold; "jobs refresh and matching" (`tools/bench.js`) at `medium-ref` and `small-y40`, before and after. Invariants for the due-queue (sorted, no entry for a dissolved household). `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals.
Cost: households × 25 calls × 1 / cadence a day for reviews (14 to 42 times fewer than today), plus about 5 due-queue entries a household a day.
