# VB-1627: Land first: the solo settler lands with nothing built, and the Farmstead is placed from the build menu as a plan task carried through to a raised booth
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/ui2/HANDOFF.md; backlog/VB-1623.md; backlog/VB-1625.md; src/sim/scenarios.js (landAlone); src/sim/building.js (roofless, raiseBooth, stakedWork, KINDS.timber, booth); src/sim/cottageplan.js; src/sim/settle.js (settleFarm); src/sim/playerplan.js (CHAINS, WAITS); src/sim/landing.js; src/sim/herds.js (homeTile); design.md (Settling in steps; Sleeping away from home)
Goal: A player who plans by hand begins the solo game with goods, animals and nothing built, and raises the first shelter where they choose, by their own plan.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] With `hand: true` (`Sim.seed(w, seed, 'solo', null, {site, hand: true})`) nothing is under way at the landing: no booth staked, no cottage plan, no field (today `landAlone` still stakes the booth and makes the cottage plan). The farm record stays, on the landing tile, as the camp: a new field `farmstead.camp` (true while nothing stands; an owner in `src/owners.json`, `@state`, an invariant, saved), the household's goods, herd, cart, water and routine as today. A camp is roofless: its nights cost the open sky's cold and damp (`roofless`, sim/building), which today holds only while a staked booth is unraised.
- [x] A plan task places it: `Sim.plan(st, 'add', {farmstead: tile})`, a chain declared beside the others (`CHAINS`). Refused with the reason when there is no camp, or the tile is not one a house may stand on (water, a river, used or owned by another, not walkable from the camp), or it lies farther from the landing than a named bound (a tunable, 3 steps to begin with, the reach of the site's reading: `LANDING.soilSteps`); one such task at a time.
- [x] Carried through with no further act: in the task's turn the head (the played pawn) places the farmstead by a verb of its own (an act of the pawn's; registered by the module that carries it out): the house tile becomes the tapped tile if it differs from the landing's (what follows the house moves with it: the used tiles, the tile's owner, the herd's home tile, the farm's water, the travel cost; the landing tile is given back), and the booth is staked there by `raiseBooth`'s existing rule; then the timber is fetched and the booth raised by the pawn's own work as the plan feeds it (a timber trip's job stands at the wood, not at the tile: the chain needs its own way to find its jobs). When the booth stands, `camp` is cleared, the household sleeps under it and the task leaves with `which: 'raised'`. The cottage is not begun: with automatic behaviour off it waits for a later task (the ladder's next rung), so make sure nothing begins it for a played head.
- [x] `planView().tasks[i].waits` gives its reasons as for the other chains (the timber not in, the sleeping hours), and `Sim.agenda` lays its steps into the days.
- [x] Reads for the page, on the facade, plain data, writing nothing: `Sim.buildMenu(st)`: what the played head may build now, `[{what: 'farmstead', ok, why, hours, timber}]` (one entry for now; a registry others will add to); `Sim.placeWhy(st, what, tile)`: null or why that tile will not take it. The page draws nothing on a camp but the goods and animals: say in your result which field tells it a camp from a booth staked and a booth raised.
- [x] Unchanged without `hand`: the solo start as today, the settler households of `sim/settle`, founders (`node tools/same.js --base REF --scenario founders,solo`). A save and a load in the middle of the chain go on the same.
- [x] Tests on small constructed states (a line in `tests/suites.js` for a new script): the camp's cold nights; the refusals; the chain from the tap to the raised booth on the landing tile and on a tile two steps off; the herd and the water follow the house. `npm run verify` passes; baselines do not rise.

Kim's question, with that answer proposed: how far from the landing may it be placed.
