# VB-286: Walking hours between villages: a table compiled a village a step
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: between
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Between villages: Distance, Near and far). Survey and numbers: backlog/VB-281.md
Goal: Every act that reaches another village reads a different distance today, or none: hex steps as the crow flies between centres (claims, `CLAIMS.farmReach` 15; service, `SERVICE.reach` 15; the nearest village for an orphan's service home), an unweighted search of up to 160 steps (the tie to the culture's centre), breadth-first steps (settlement), a route search bounded at 12 hours from each guest's house to the farm (an occasion's guests, `hoursFrom`, `src/sim/occasions.js:47-52`, VB-126, which keeps it "until VB-286's table"), and nothing at all (the matcher's women, kin help, guardians). Kim decided on 10 November that the distance between villages governs what passes between them. Give the game one measure: the walking hours between two villages' greens by the route search's costs (terrain, slope, worn paths), compiled into a small table that every later task reads. This task builds the table and its reader only; nothing reads it yet, so the run is unchanged.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `reachCosts(w, from, maxCost, targets)` in `src/sim/routes.js`: a Dijkstra search from tile `from` by `stepCost`, over passable tiles (a target tile may be closed, as `findRoute`'s ends may), stopping at `maxCost` or when every target is settled; it returns the cost to each target reached. It reuses the module's scratch buffers and heap (no allocation per tile), counts its settled tiles in a declared counter `VB.count.reachSettled`, and agrees with `findRoute` + `routeCost` on every pair of greens at `medium-ref` and `small-y40` (a test, to 1e-9).
- [x] `st.villageHours`: one row per village id, `row[u]` the walking hours from village v's green to u's (the cost over `SPEED`, 3 meadow tiles an hour), `null` beyond `ROUTE.reach` (200 meadow steps, 67 hours) or with no route by land. Plain data (arrays of numbers and `null`) in `st`, not in `st.w`, saved with the run, owner `sim/routes` (`src/owners.json`), `@state` line. Costs are the same both ways (`stepCost` is symmetric), so writing v's row also writes column v of every other row.
- [x] `hoursBetween(st, a, b)`: 0 for the same village, else the table's entry (`Infinity` for `null`). Allocates nothing.
- [x] Refresh, a village a step (Rules for cost, 5): an entry of VB-241's calendar on autumn day 9, from noon, village k's row on step noon + k, by VB-277's window rule when there are more than 60 villages (village k on noon + floor(k × 60 / villages)). Day 9 comes before the meetings (day 10), whose tie may read the table (VB-290), so the rows are at most a year old, and worn paths and felled woods, which change costs slowly, enter within the year. A village founded by settlement (`settle.js`, `found`) or a split gets its row at once. The seed builds the whole table, and so does a load of a save without it (all saves before this task); the save format stays.
- [x] Invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): one row per village, each of length the number of villages; the diagonal 0; symmetric to 1e-9; every other entry `null` or a number in (0, 67]. Equality with a fresh search is a test at the refresh, not an invariant, since costs drift between refreshes.
- [x] A report: `node tools/sim.js --report reach [--json]` prints, for the table: villages, pairs, pairs by land and within reach, the hours' spread (least, quartiles, most), each village's nearest neighbour, and pairs within 12 hours. The numbers at `medium-ref` and `small-y40` go in this file (VB-281 measured: 29 villages, 406 pairs, 268 by land, 247 within 67 h; median 41 h, nearest neighbour median 5.2 h, at most 16.0; and 23 villages, 253 pairs, 193 by land, all within 67 h; median 27 h, nearest neighbour median 6.2 h, at most 9.6).
- [x] Behaviour unchanged: nothing reads the table. `tests/golden.json` changes only by the new key `villageHours` in each state entry, listed in the PR; `tests/counters.json` only by the new counter. `tests/saves.js` and `tests/spread.js` (a save between two villages' rows goes on as the unbroken run) pass.
- [x] Cost (targets, residuals reported): a row's search at `medium-ref`, measured by VB-281's prototype, 2.4 ms median and 19 ms at most (1.3 and 6.2 at `small-y40`), against 60 ms a step; the whole table 95 ms at `medium-ref` (43 at `small-y40`), once a year spread over 29 steps, so about 2 ms a game day of the year's mean. The table holds villages² numbers (841 at `medium-ref`; 3,600 at 60 villages). `node tools/sim.js --load` on each fixture `--years 1 --report perf`.
Notes:
- 11 Nov 2026 (backlog curation): VB-126's per-guest route search (`hoursFrom`, `sim/occasions`) added to the distance measures, as a reader that moves onto the table.
- 10 Nov 2026 (VB-281's plan): written. Rules for cost 2 and 9: pairs of villages are few (29 at `medium-ref`), so a dense table is cheaper than any index. Why not the route cache: it holds tile-to-tile paths under an LRU of 60,000 and drops them daily where a tile's cost changed; a lookup between two greens would search, or miss, at every use. Why not `findRoute` per pair: a pair with no land route explores the whole island before it fails; 406 searches took 414 to 930 ms at `medium-ref`. One Dijkstra a green finds all the greens it reaches at once.
- Orchestrator's decision (10 Nov 2026), for Kim to overturn: "refreshed when a path changes" is met yearly, a village a step, rather than on every change of a tile's cost. Paths change by stages (walking 1.05 to 1.3 times faster) over years, and a daily refresh of the table would cost a row search per changed tile's neighbourhood every day for a change of a few per cent.
- After VB-241 (the calendar entry). Independent of VB-277 to VB-280; one at a time with VB-278 if both edit `sim/clock.js`'s table on autumn days 9 and 10. VB-287, VB-288 and VB-290 read the table; VB-126's occasions are a reader that moves onto it, in place of `hoursFrom` (no task holds that move yet, and the table's hours are green to green, not house to farm).
