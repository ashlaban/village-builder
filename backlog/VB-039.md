# VB-039: Food help from kin and the village commons
Status: needs-review
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: courting
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs, Known gaps (hunger); Customs (who eats first). Kim, 2026-10-30: a hungry family can request support from kin and the village commons, if there is a surplus; individual pawns have different thresholds for how much buffer food they are comfortable with.
Goal: A founders run at seed 7 has Hagen (6 people) at 0 grain and hunger 1.0 for years while Östergården next door holds 1,500 grain for one person. Let food move between households.
Acceptance criteria (proposed; VB-036 confirms the numbers):
- [ ] Each adult has a buffer comfort: days of their household's food they want in store before they give, drawn once at birth or seeding from 20–60 days (uniform, from `st.rand`). A household's buffer is its holder's.
- [ ] A household asks for help when its grain above the seed reserve (VB-049) covers fewer than 3 days. It asks kin households first (households holding a parent, child or sibling of a member; closest first), then the village store.
- [ ] A household gives at most its grain above its buffer and its seed reserve, and at most what was asked for 12 days. Each gift is a ledger entry in both households and a life event for both holders.
- [ ] Village commons store: the village holds a grain store (`v.grain`); see the questions for how it is filled. It gives on the same rule, with the village's buffer.
- [ ] A test on constructed households: a household with a surplus above its buffer gives, one below its buffer does not; kin are asked before the village.
- [ ] A founders run over 50 years at seeds 7 and 42 reports hungry days and hunger deaths before and after (reported, not asserted). Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
Notes / questions:
- How is the village store filled? Options: a share of each harvest (a tithe to the village), gifts from households above their buffer at the moving round, or only common fields once those exist. See `QUESTIONS.md`.
- Does help create a debt the receiver repays after a good harvest, or is it a gift? Does it raise ties between the households (it plausibly should, for customs drift)?
