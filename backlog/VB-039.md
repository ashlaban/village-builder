# VB-039: Food help from kin and the village commons
Status: done (2026-10-31)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: courting
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs, Known gaps (hunger); Customs (who eats first). Kim, 2026-10-30: a hungry family can request support from kin and the village commons, if there is a surplus; individual pawns have different thresholds for how much buffer food they are comfortable with.
Goal: A founders run at seed 7 has Hagen (6 people) at 0 grain and hunger 1.0 for years while Östergården next door holds 1,500 grain for one person. Let food move between households.
Acceptance criteria (Kim, 2026-10-30: the proposed values are accepted as starting values):
- [x] Each adult has a buffer comfort: days of their household's food they want in store before they give, drawn once at birth or seeding from 20–60 days (uniform, from `st.rand`). A household's buffer is its holder's.
- [x] A household asks for help when its grain above the seed reserve (VB-049) covers fewer than 3 days. It asks kin households first (households holding a parent, child or sibling of a member; closest first), then the village store.
- [x] A household gives at most its grain above its buffer and its seed reserve, and at most what was asked for 12 days. Each gift is a ledger entry in both households and a life event for both holders. A gift is not repaid; it raises the tie between the two holders by 0.5 and, once VB-060 is done, their opinion of each other by 0.1. (The opinion part waits for a question; see the note of 31 October below.)
- [x] Village commons store: the village holds a grain store (`v.grain`, `@state` documented). At each moving round, every household with grain above its buffer and seed reserve gives 25% of that surplus to its village's store. The store gives on the same rule as a household, keeping no buffer of its own.
- [x] A test on constructed households: a household with a surplus above its buffer gives, one below its buffer does not; kin are asked before the village.
- [x] A founders run over 50 years at seeds 7 and 42 reports hungry days and hunger deaths before and after (reported, not asserted). Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
