# VB-903: A cause the played pawn takes up on its own is shown, and may be laid down at once without the wait
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Customs and culture (Meetings, culture and splits); The player and what they see (The meeting)
Goal: The player learns when its pawn takes a side, and can still choose its own cause.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] When the played pawn takes up a cause by the culture's natural draw (`advocate(..., natural)`, `src/culture.js`, the entrepreneur's draw), a skip stops on it (a new reason of `SKIP`, `src/sim/notable.js`): "Gunnar Olofsson has taken up a cause: a daughter's share, equal to a son's", with the Agenda one click away.
- [ ] A cause laid down within its first season (`dropCause`) does not start the wait of `causeFree`: the pawn may take up another at once. The rule is every pawn's (principle 16); a pawn without a player keeps its cause as now, so a run with no player is unchanged (`tools/same.js` prints `same`).
- [ ] The Agenda says, for a cause the pawn took up on its own, that it did ("You took this up on your own in year 4, autumn"), and until when it may be laid down without the wait.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`, the draw forced with `fix` on its stream): the played pawn takes up a cause; the skip stops; laid down in the season, `argue` for another passes its check; laid down after it, `argue` is refused with the wait as today.
- [ ] The other choices the automatic behaviour makes for the played pawn that are not work (a gift by the holder's allowance, a project, a wish at the round, a cause) are listed in the notes, each with whether a skip stops on it and why.

Notes / questions:
- 16 Nov 2026 (playtest, founders small seed 7, 80 years in the page): in year 4 Gunnar Olofsson, played with his automatic behaviour on, took up "a daughter's share, equal to a son's" on the eve of the round; nothing told the player, and the cause the player then chose was refused: "You may take up another in 5.0 years; laying this one down does not shorten the wait." His son did the same in year 66. The automatic switch is all or nothing: off, the pawn does no work unless every task is queued.
- Weighed and not taken: a second switch (work automatic, causes the player's), which gives the player a choice its pawn does not have (principle 16); and asking the player before the pawn takes it up, as an offer is asked, which delays the played pawn's act against its unplayed self (The player's commands, Decided 7 Nov: with the automatic behaviour on, the pawn does exactly what it would unplayed). The rule above keeps both. A close call for the inbox.
- Runs one at a time with VB-901, which adds reasons to `SKIP` too.
