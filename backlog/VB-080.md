# VB-080: A food balance that feeds a family before animals

Design ref: Goods and the farming year (Food, Seed); Systems overview (Food balance; finding 2); Known gaps.
Goal: A household of 6 eats 192 rations a year with four young children and 288 when all are over 14. A meadow field gives about 78 in an ordinary year, less 20 of seed, so three fields give about 170 and four about 230. In the populous scenario a quarter of household-days in year 2 are hungry, and 9 people die of hunger in 4 years. Animals (VB-048) are the last arch to start; every task until then is measured on a hungry baseline.
