## Implementation (the page)
- `tests/lib/dompage.js`: the jsdom harness, taken out of `tests/smoke.js` (the PixiJS stand-ins, the canvases, the hand-driven frame
  loop, the chunks painted in full or left blank, the state the page runs), with three additions: `patch` changes the page's text
  before it runs, `painted` may give a count for each level of detail, and a `jsdomError` (an error the page throws in a handler or a
  timer, which jsdom reported and went on from) fails the test. `tests/smoke.js` now loads the page through it and is otherwise as it was.
- `tests/page.js`, in `INTEGRATION`: the page on a 36 by 26 island. The page's own sizes start at 120 by 85 (3.4 s to build in jsdom),
  so the test adds a size `x: [36, 26]` to `SIZES` and to the hash's pattern in the page's text, and opens `#sx18` (founders, seed 18:
  two villages, ten people). It checks: whom to play opens and Watch only closes it; some days run as fast as it goes, then at 30x,
  where the shown clock is no more than 5 minutes behind the simulation; the Land, Ground and Reach overlays on and off, with their keys; the
  People sheet lists the farms, not a household in folds; a lived-in farm's card has its food meter and the Stores, Land and Animals tabs with icons; a pawn's card has
  its twelve trait icons and no household tabs, and its History tab dated lines; a village's Customs tab has a charter whose phrase
  opens its ways; the Culture pane is titled by its culture with a row a village; Debug's moving round runs, its kill reports a death
  and raises the toast; the family tree draws and its line and descendants views show their person; played as a holder, the action bar
  opens and each group lists its acts. Any error the page logs or throws fails it.
- Decision: founders, not populous. Populous on the showcase island (56 by 40, 143 people) took 6.3 to 6.9 s of CPU here, three fifths
  of it in frames at speed and in drawing; founders on 36 by 26 takes 3.1 s. Founders is the early game's reference (Deciding without
  Kim, 6), and the panels a populous island adds (the goodwill rows, merged toasts) stay in `smoke`'s tiny populous map at night.
- Days are run at the uncapped speed, then 10 frames at 30x: a frame at 30x costs as much to draw as one uncapped and runs about a
  fiftieth of the hours. One chunk of the nearest level is painted in full; `smoke` and the Chromium scripts paint the rest.
