# VB-406: The village store: why most of its grain rots
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: Balance loops and targets (The balance review, 12 November); docs/balance/cards.md; docs/balance/findings.md; Goods and the farming year (Help in hunger)
Goal: Each year a household gives the village store a quarter of its surplus (`HELP.storeShare`, `fillStores`, `src/sim/help.js`), and the store loses a fifth a year (`ECON.villageStoreLoss`, `villageDay`, `src/sim/stores.js`). Measured on populous small (seeds 3, 7, 11; 50 years), 10 to 11% of the grain harvest goes into village stores, 68 to 85% of that rots there, and 10 to 22% is given back out: the store takes in far more than hunger asks of it, since hungry households are under 1%. Measure its flows, say why, and propose the rule that would make it a store and not a sink. This task changes no behaviour: its proposal goes to Kim as a question.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Measured a year and a village, populous small, seeds 3, 7 and 11, 50 years, and founders small, seeds 7 and 42, 50 years: grain in, grain given out (to how many households, how much each), grain rotted, the store at the year's end in years of the village's need, and what the giving households would have lost to their own rot (`ECON.storeLoss`) had they kept it.
- [ ] The cause is stated with numbers: how much of the loss is the rate (a fifth a year), how much the filling rule (a quarter of every surplus, every year, whatever the store holds), and how much is that little is asked (help asks kin first, and once a season).
- [ ] Two or three candidate rules are tried on the same runs and compared (rotted, given out, hungry household-days, the store's size): for example filling only up to a target (a share of a year of the village's need), a lower share, or the store's surplus poured as the village's feast or lent as seed. Each with its effect on the golden file named, none committed.
- [ ] The proposal is a question in `QUESTIONS.md` with the numbers, and this file's notes carry the tables; a tuning task is written (blocked on Kim) for the rule proposed.
- [ ] If VB-403's report exists it is used and its village-store lines checked against this task's; if not, the measuring script is kept in `tools/` only if it is worth keeping.
