# VB-449: The head's day: a household's work is allotted, not bid for
Status: done (2026-11-15)
Priority: 1
Size: M
Design ref: design.md section 7; docs/review-jobs.md section 6 B
Goal: The player and the reader can see who does what today and why, and the per-step auction over household work goes (Kim, 15 November: B first).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `design.md` section 7 says how the day is allotted (Matching, Several hands, More urgent work and the cook's call rewritten; the noise and the poor-fit wait gone), as Kim agreed on 15 November.
- [x] A household's day is saved state with one writer: for each member an ordered list of task keys, made each morning, at the household's review and when its or its village's tasks change; a loaded run goes on as the unloaded one.
- [x] Best fit first, each member up to a day's working hours, as many hands on a task as finish it in a day, the rest of what a member may do after; no random draw.
- [x] A pawn takes the first task of its list it can; a pawn with an empty list looks at the household's opportunities as before (once a day); the round is left for help between households.
- [x] The hourly look up and the cooks' call are gone from the calendar: the 1.5 times and 1 test is made when the day is allotted, and a task with a deadline today calls its hand.
- [x] New work counters for the allotment; checks on a small constructed state; `npm run verify`.
- [x] Numbers before and after at populous small, seeds 7 and 42, and founders small, seed 7: living, hungry days, grain, idle share by season, ms a day and the jobs line, and whether households are better off.

Implementation notes (15 Nov 2026):
- `src/sim/jobs.js`: `allot` (the head's day), `allotChanged`, `takeDay`, `helpers`; `planRound`, `lookUp`, `callCooks` and the poor-fit wait are gone, and the calendar's two hourly rows with them. `refreshJobs` and `lookForWork` note the holders whose tasks were posted or taken down and allot their days at their end; each morning every household's.
- State: `plan.day = {made, lists: {pawn id: [job keys]}}` on a household's standing plan, owner `sim/jobs` (`src/owners.json`), written whole by `allot`; saved with the plans; its invariant in `tests/lib/invariants.js`. A plan without a day (an old save, a new household) is allotted when a member first looks for work.
- Tunable: `MATCH.day.horizon` (working days in which a task's hands should finish it). Counters `dayPair`, `dayTake` replace `matchBid`, `lookUpJob`, `cookFarm`.
- `src/sim/stores.js`: a woodpile falling under `MATCH.urgency.woodLow` wakes the head, as grain crossing its reserve does: without the hourly look up nothing else told the head.
- `src/sim/occasions.js`: one who cannot set out because it carries a load is looked at again at the next step, not in an hour. Found by `tests/routines.js`: a hauler whose trips took the hour was carrying at every hourly look, and a village of one voice had no quorum. On `main` the same could happen; the timing hid it.
- `tests/lib/invariants.js`: a load in phase `work` is allowed for a kind that stacks (firewood beside a cart). `main` has the state too (963 agent-steps in 40 days of heirs, seed 7); the check passed only by when it was run.

Decisions (Deciding without Kim):
- The join factor stays, inside the allotment (a further hand counts for 0.8). Without it every member's best task was the hoeing, the spring raking was missed and `tests/head.js` lost its hay; the factor is what spreads hands over tasks that can be worked at once. A close call: the review proposed dropping it.
- The allotment does not start idle members; they take their tasks when their pause ends, as before. Starting them made `refreshJobs` an act with side effects on every caller. A close call.
- A task with a deadline today (`late`: the meal) goes first in its hand's list whatever its fit, and calls the hand: the general rule in place of the cooks' own.
- Help between households is untouched (the round, its two-hour wait, its noise): a task of its own.

Parameter runs (principle 3; populous small seeds 7 and 42, 4 years; founders small seed 7, 8 years):
- `horizon` 1, before the join factor: `tests/head.js` out (1.94 winters of hay for 2).
- `horizon` 0.5 and 0.25, before the join factor: the head's raised hay target had no effect (the raking missed).
- `horizon` 1 with the join factor: hungry household-days 50 (seed 7), 6 (seed 42); founders' grain 483 at year 8.
- `horizon` 0.5 with the join factor: 42, 5, and 508. Kept.
