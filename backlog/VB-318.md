# VB-318: Class: a derived view with hysteresis, and what landed changes
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-316
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies, Households and other bodies (Class; What landed changes); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, sections 4.6 (Class) and trace 7.1 (step 11)
Goal: A household that owns farms its old families work, and lives on rents and full stores, is the implicit elite. Name it, as a view the simulation does not store as truth, and let it change only what the design says: its holder leaves the field work to others and its voice weighs more.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A derived class per live household, recomputed after each village's meeting, a village a step (rule 5): tenant (works more land than it owns), cottager (a cottage with one field or none), peasant (owns most of what it works), landed (rents received plus stores at the year's end at least 1.5 times its year's need, three year ends running; it leaves landed after three year ends running below 1.1). The hysteresis counts are the only state, kept by the view's owner in `src/owners.json`.
- [ ] Landed acts through rule contributions only (`VB['sim/registry']`): the holder's priority for field work falls to 2 of 10, and its prestige gains 0.2 (`refreshPrestige`); nothing else reads the class (a categorical view feeds a rule only through hysteresis, AGENTS.md).
- [ ] The household panel shows the class (VB-321 adds the rest); `node tools/sim.js --report households` prints it.
- [ ] Numbers (populous and founders small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 30 years): households by class by decade, landed households per 1,000 living, and elite turnover (landed households that leave landed or dissolve within 30 years, and whether a landed farm stays landed after its holder's death); field work hours of landed holders before and after. Golden and counters re-recorded, entries listed.
Cost: one comparison a household a year, a village a step; the priority contribution is compiled per household at that cadence (rule 6).
Order: after VB-316 (rents).
