# VB-318: Class: a derived view with hysteresis, and what elite changes
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies, Households and other bodies (Class; What elite changes); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, sections 4.6 (Class) and trace 7.1 (step 11)
Goal: A household that owns farms its old families work, and lives on rents and full stores, is the implicit elite. Name it, as a view the simulation does not store as truth, and let it change only what the design says: its holder leaves the field work to others and its voice weighs more.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A derived class per live household, recomputed after each village's meeting, a village a step (rule 5): tenant (works more land than it owns), cottager (a cottage with one field or none), peasant (owns most of what it works), and, beside it, a livelihood (Kim, 15 Nov; `design.md`, Class): elite when its income from others in the year is at least `CLASS.eliteIn` (1.25) times its year's need at three year ends running, no longer after three running under `CLASS.eliteOut` (0.75); landed by the same two numbers on its income from land alone, even if it is elite already. Income from others: rents received, the keep in grain (VB-463), interest received, and for each field it owns and works, its harvest times the share of the year's work hours that hands not of the household gave (VB-462's two numbers a field: hired, days owed), less wages paid. Income from land: the rents and that harvest less wages, without the keep in grain and interest. Its own members' harvest and its stores do not count. The two hysteresis counts and the year's two incomes are the only state, kept by the view's owner in `src/owners.json`; the panel explains the number by its parts.
- [x] The class is shown and feeds no rule (Kim, 24 Nov; design.md, A label is read, never a switch: a rule reads the measure): what elite stood for acts through rule contributions only (`VB['sim/registry']`), each reading the measure behind it, a household's income from others against its need: the holder's priority for field work falls as that measure rises (to 2 of 10 at the level that made a household elite), and its standing rises with it (to 0.2 there; `refreshStanding`). Nothing reads the class.
- [x] The household panel shows the class (VB-321 adds the rest); `node tools/sim.js --report households` prints it.
- [x] A test on a constructed state: a household with large stores and no income from others is neither elite nor landed (the review's count of 45 to 100 in 200 under the old rule); an alderman with the keep alone (0.7) is not elite; with the keep in grain and interest to 1.3 he is elite and not landed; a household whose rents and hired hands' harvest reach 1.3 is both; one elite by the keep becomes landed as well when its land's income alone passes 1.25.
- [ ] Numbers (populous and founders small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 30 years): the elite households' income from others by its parts (rent, keep, interest, others' hours), how many of them are landed, the households between 0.75 and 1.25 on each measure, households by class by decade, elite and landed households per 1,000 living, and elite turnover (elite households that leave it or dissolve within 30 years, and whether an elite farm stays elite after its holder's death); field work hours of elite holders before and after. The counters re-recorded (`node tests/counters.js --update`), their entries listed. NOT MEASURED tonight (no long runs on shared cores); the report is `--report households` plus a probe to run in the tuning pass.
Cost: one comparison a household a year, a village a step; the priority contribution is compiled per household at that cadence (rule 6).
Order: after VB-311 (own and use), VB-319 (hired hands) and VB-463 (the keep); rents are read from the obligations and are none until VB-316 lands.
