# VB-318: Class: a derived view with hysteresis, and what landed changes
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-311, VB-319, VB-463
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies, Households and other bodies (Class; What landed changes); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, sections 4.6 (Class) and trace 7.1 (step 11)
Goal: A household that owns farms its old families work, and lives on rents and full stores, is the implicit elite. Name it, as a view the simulation does not store as truth, and let it change only what the design says: its holder leaves the field work to others and its voice weighs more.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A derived class per live household, recomputed after each village's meeting, a village a step (rule 5): tenant (works more land than it owns), cottager (a cottage with one field or none), peasant (owns most of what it works), landed by Kim's three conditions (15 Nov; `design.md`, Class): it owns at least one field or meadow, and its income from others in the year is at least `CLASS.landedIn` (1.25) times its year's need at three year ends running; it leaves landed after three running under `CLASS.landedOut` (0.75). Income from others: rents received, the keep in grain (VB-463), interest received, and for each field it owns and works, its harvest times the share of the year's work hours that hands not of the household gave (VB-462's two numbers a field: hired, days owed), less wages paid. Its own members' harvest and its stores do not count. The hysteresis counts and the year's income are the only state, kept by the view's owner in `src/owners.json`; the panel explains the number by its parts.
- [ ] Landed acts through rule contributions only (`VB['sim/registry']`): the holder's priority for field work falls to 2 of 10, and its prestige gains 0.2 (`refreshPrestige`); nothing else reads the class (a categorical view feeds a rule only through hysteresis, AGENTS.md).
- [ ] The household panel shows the class (VB-321 adds the rest); `node tools/sim.js --report households` prints it.
- [ ] A test on a constructed state: a household with large stores and no income from others is never landed (the review's count of 45 to 100 in 200 under the old rule); one that owns no land is not landed whatever it is paid; an alderman with the keep alone (0.7) is not, and with a tenancy's rent beside it is.
- [ ] Numbers (populous and founders small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 30 years): the landed households' income from others by its parts (rent, keep, interest, others' hours), the households between 0.75 and 1.25, households by class by decade, landed households per 1,000 living, and elite turnover (landed households that leave landed or dissolve within 30 years, and whether a landed farm stays landed after its holder's death); field work hours of landed holders before and after. Golden and counters re-recorded, entries listed.
Cost: one comparison a household a year, a village a step; the priority contribution is compiled per household at that cadence (rule 6).
Order: after VB-311 (own and use), VB-319 (hired hands) and VB-463 (the keep); rents are read from the obligations and are none until VB-316 lands.
