# VB-439: Walking costs: each ground its own, roads clear the ground, slopes a little steeper
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, World and scale (Walking and terrain); src/sim/rules.js TERRAIN, PATHS, SLOPE
Goal: Thick forest with no way through is much slower than a road through it, which is a little slower than meadow, which is a little slower than a road over meadow; steep ground turns routes aside a little more.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Each kind of ground has its own cost: meadow 1, shore 1.15, heath 1.25, pine 1.6, hills 1.8, broadleaf forest 2, marsh 2.5, mountain 3.5 (`TERRAIN`; they were 1, 1, 1, 1.5, 2, 1.5, 2, 3).
- [x] A worn tile costs `(1 + (TERRAIN - 1)(1 - PATHS.clear[stage])) / PATHS.speed[stage]`, `clear` [0, 0.25, 0.75, 0.8], `speed` [1, 1.03, 1.1, 1.15] (it was the terrain over [1, 1.05, 1.15, 1.3]). A road is a path or a lane, stages 2 and 3, from 200 crossings (`PATHS.at`).
- [x] Thick forest 2, its road 1.14 (path) or 1.04 (lane), meadow 1, its road 0.91 or 0.87: the order Kim asked for; `tests/routes.js` holds it.
- [x] `SLOPE.grade` 1.5 → 2: a step at the median, 90th and 99th percentile of rise is slowed 1.3, 2.0 and 4.1 times (it was 1.2, 1.7, 2.9); the cap of 6 is reached from a difference of 0.26 (it was 0.34).
- [x] Numbers before and after are below; `design.md` (Walking and terrain; Worn paths) says what the code does.

## Decisions
Kim asked for this tuning (13 November) and gave the order and the words (significantly, slightly, a bit); the numbers are the agent's
reading of them, each a named tunable, and no run chose them.
- **A road clears the ground rather than only speeding it.** Dividing the terrain's cost by a speed could not give the order asked for:
  a forest lane at 2 / 1.3 is still half again slower than meadow. So wear takes away a share of the cost above meadow (`clear`).
- **The lane's speed fell from 1.3 to 1.15**, so that a forest road stays slower than open meadow and a meadow road is slightly, not much, faster.
- **A ford and a bridge keep their own cost**, divided by the stage's speed as before: wading is not cleared by wear.
- **Hills fell from 2 to 1.8**: the slope's factor now carries most of what makes hills slow.
