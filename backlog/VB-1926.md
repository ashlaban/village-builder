# VB-1926: The head's call is urgent or not: bring the load in, or drop everything
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: plans
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Plans at four scales (The head calls; The cut and the call; A load in hand); Principles for decisions; docs/plans/four-scales/README.md
Touches: sim/day (call); sim/jobs (allot, slotIn); sim/reasons; page/play; tests/call.js
Goal: A head's call to a member is non-urgent by default (the member brings its load in, arrives within about an hour) or urgent (drops the load, comes now), and the panel names which and its cost before the call.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A call carries `urgent` (default false). A non-urgent call to a member with a load in hand lets it finish its leg and bring the load to its body's store, and the member plans to be at the called task within about a game hour of the call (a target the plan aims at; nothing lapses if it is later); an urgent call sets the load down as a pile at once (the stray pile's rules) and sends the member now.
- [ ] The deadline call (a meal due today) is non-urgent unless its head or the rule marks it urgent; a head's call beyond the member's levy is still a demand (design.md, The levy and own hours) whichever kind it is.
- [ ] The play panel and the call's cost line name the kind and its cost before the call (the wait, or the load left and the walk home), as an icon and label with the sentence one tap in (design.md, section 15, Icon and label first).
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state: a member carrying a load is called non-urgent and the load reaches the store before it arrives; called urgent, the pile lies where it stood and it arrives sooner; `tests/call.js` and `tests/reasons.js` pass.
