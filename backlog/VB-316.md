# VB-316: Foreclosure: a field passes, and its old family farms it as tenants
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-311, VB-315
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Land and rights (Foreclosure; Tenancy); Exchange, Loans, obligations and cases (Remedies: "Debts are paid with"); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, trace 7.1 (steps 7, 8 and 10)
Goal: The heart of the alderman's road: a ruling of land. A field splits off the debtor's farm and passes to the creditor's household, while the debtor's household stays on it as tenant, paying rent. Repeated, one household comes to own farms its old families still work.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Under "land", or under "labour, then land" for labour owed and not given within its year (heard at the next meeting), an upheld case takes a field when the debt left is at least half a field's worth (60 rations, `SHARES.perField` / 2): the debtor's field of least worth (soil, then id) is split off and its `own` transferred to the creditor's household (VB-311, basis `foreclosed`); its `use` stays with the debtor's household. The field's worth above the debt is booked as goodwill to the debtor's holder; the debt is met. One case takes one field a hearing.
- [ ] The house and yard go last, with the last field: the old family is then the tenant of the whole farm.
- [ ] Rent: an obligation of kind `rent` each year, a quarter of the holding's harvest, delivered when it is threshed, and 2 days' labour (8 work hours each) at the owner's harvest, taken on the basis `debtor`; unpaid rent is overdue and can be pressed like a loan.
- [ ] The debtor's household keeps every task of the field through its `use`: sowing, weeding, harvest as now.
- [ ] Memories: the debtor's holder and spouse of the creditor's holder ("Took our field"), fading over 10 years; the village sees it (the log line of Exchange, What the player sees).
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous and founders small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 30 years): fields foreclosed, land Gini (fields owned by live households), tenancy share (fields worked under tenancy), households owning more than one farm; hungry household-days of tenant households, by household. Golden and counters re-recorded, entries listed.
Cost: a split and a title per ruling, a few a decade a village; rent is one obligation a tenancy a year in the due-queue.
Order: after VB-311 and VB-315. VB-317 (tenancies over time) and VB-318 (class) follow.
