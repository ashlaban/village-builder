Goal: a pawn working away has its water walked for, as at home; the player can send the played pawn to drink
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A booth's water is fetched: a job of the booth's (as its raising is), posted each day at the site's source (`sim/drinking` `sourceFor` from the booth's tile, or a source found once for the booth), walked as the farm's fetch is; the day it is not in, those who sleep in the booth end their day by their own thirst (`sim/thirst` `dayEnd`, given false), not watered by the roof (`sim/hunger` `nightCost` gives a booth no dry day today: water from nowhere).
- [ ] A pawn of the booth that drinks at the site's source by the verb `drink` takes the booth's fetch from there when the booth's water is not in (design.md, Water by the site, The thirsty pawn: "drinks and fetches at the site's source in the same way"); checked on a small constructed state.
- [ ] A lodger at a site with no roof (`roof: 'none'`, why `work`) is told what its water is: today it goes to drink at 36 hours and ends two days of three thirsty; decide by AGENTS.md whether the site's fetch covers it or it drinks each morning, and record the reason.
- [ ] The played pawn's Actions list (`sim/opportunities`, `page/actbar`) offers Drink, with the verb's own reason where it is refused (no source in reach; on the road); the panel's "Thirsty" line stands beside it. `node tests/smoke.js` and the action bar's browser test pass.
- [ ] Measured once, before and after (founders small seeds 7 and 42, populous small seed 7; 10 years): pawn-days ended thirsty at a booth and at a roofless site, health lost there, hours a booth's day spends on water.
