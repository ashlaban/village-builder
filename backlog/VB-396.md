# VB-396: Tutorial: a main-menu button that teaches the game on a very small founders island
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Starting a game: the scenario and whom to play; The founders' first year; The player and what they see
Goal: A new player learns who they are, how time, the action bar and the queue work, and what the first year asks, without reading anything first.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The main menu has a Tutorial button, beside Quick start, that raises one fixed, very small founders island (seed 68, 40 by 28 tiles) and plays the scenario's suggested holder at once, with no start screen and no choice of whom to play.
- [x] The game starts paused with the camera on the played pawn's farm.
- [x] Four coach marks on the real interface (who you are, time, the action bar, taking work), each advanced by the player doing what it names; the tutorial can be left at any step.
- [ ] Then a card of the first year's goals by season, each with a "Show me" that points at the control; a goal is ticked from the state, whoever met it.
- [ ] The tutorial is the page's own state: nothing of it enters `st`, a save or the simulation.
- [ ] `tests/browser-start.js` covers the button and the steps; `npm run verify` passes.
