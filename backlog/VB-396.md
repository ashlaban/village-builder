# VB-396: Roads over fields and meadows, a green open on every side, and roads that look right
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Worn paths (4, Sharing paths: lanes form between farms, the well, the fields and the commons wood); Generated villages. Kim, 12 November 2026: roads pass over fields and meadows but not buildings, the green connects to roads from all sides, and roads look nice.
Goal: A road stopped at the edge of every field and meadow, since no built tile wore a path, and the green met roads on two or three sides only. Roads should run where people walk, over fields and meadows too, never through a house; meet the green from any side; and look like roads: no loose ends, meshes or kinks, and a colour and width that read on crops and grass.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Fields and meadows count crossings and are walked faster by their stage, as open land is (Kim: wear and speed, no loss of crop); a house and the green wear nothing. A house has a mark of its own (`w.dev` 3), set by the generator, by a house raised in play and on loading an older save.
- [x] The roads over fields and meadows are drawn: over the field art, joined to the roads of the open land with nothing painted twice.
- [ ] The green meets roads from every side: a village's street arms may leave the green on any of its six sides (Kim: layout too), and every worn tile next to the green joins the roads that cross it.
- [ ] The look (Kim: loose ends and gaps, meshes and kinks, colour and width, and what else the shots show): judged on `tools/village-shot.js` shots of several seeds, before and after, as generated and after some years.
- [ ] Golden file re-recorded, the changed entries listed; numbers before and after (worn tiles by stage; the growth bands of populous) in the notes; `npm run verify` once at the end.
- Batch 1 (12 November), wear, speed and drawing. `sim/paths` `bars(w, i)` is where no path wears (water, `w.dev` over 1); `sim/routes` `tileCost` gives a field or meadow 1 over its stage's speed. Drawing: fields are opaque sprites over the map chunks, so `gen/paths` `drawOver` paints the chunk's trails again on a clear canvas, kept only inside the panes of worn fields and meadows (0.92 of the tile's radius, as the field art is cut), and `page/chunks` shows it in a layer above the fields (`L.ways`) with its chunk. No new state: the canvas is the page's.
- Decided without Kim: an unworn field beside a road's loose end still draws the end towards it, as a house does (the way was going there); the mark of a plot's house is set when the cottage stands, not when its ground is cleared.
- Golden: every scenario's entries move (routes and wear). `tests/record.js`: heirs seed 7 at 2.5 years, 68 events became 72. Populous small seed 7, worn in, trodden/path/lane over land that can wear: 501/216/22, after a year 735/342/38 (the count now includes fields and meadows; the figures before are to add in the wrap-up).
