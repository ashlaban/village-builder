# VB-680: Every pawn has wishes at the round: the player's wishes become the pawn's
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: acts
Phase: implement
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (16)
Goal: Each pawn who comes to the round holds its own wishes, the round reads them alike for all, and the player's are the same record on the pawn it plays.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The wishes live on the pawn (owner named in `src/owners.json`), written by the pawn's own choosing before the round (from ambition, opinion of a partner, its household's means) and by the `wish` verb for the played pawn; `wishOf` reads one place.
- [x] The round's rules read the wish where they now read 'as the pawn would'; the default wish gives today's outcomes as nearly as the mechanism allows, and the differences are reported.
- [x] VB-392 (the wish to settle) is built on this, as a wish every pawn has. Numbers before and after: marriages, moves out, claims and service, populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years.

## Implementation (16 Nov 2026)

- **`sim/wishes`**, a new kernel module after `sim/traits`, owns `pawn.wish` (`src/owners.json`): absent or null for the default
  wish, else `{rank, farm, partner}`. `rank` is `WISH.rungs` (`src/sim/rules.js`: heir, match, farm, plot, party, service, stay, the
  default being today's order of the round's steps; plan VB-682, section 3) in the pawn's order; a rung after `stay` is one it would
  not take. `farm` and `partner` name the one farm or partner wished, or null. The default is stored as nothing, so the save grows
  only by the pawns whose wish differs.
- **The pawn's own choosing** (`chooseWishes`) runs once a year at the start of the moving round (`roundPhase` step 0, before the first
  village's round), over the living of an age to marry but the played pawn (`wishWalk` in `VB.COUNTERS`, one walk of the living a
  year): ambition at `WISH.landAmbition` (0.75) or more ranks New land above A plot; a pawn at a hunger of `HUNGER.marryBelow` or more
  ranks Stay above Marry ("not this year"). Its household's means enter as its hunger (what the household could feed it); ambition as
  the ranking; opinion of a partner: see the decisions.
- **The `wish` verb** (`Sim.wish`, sim/commands) writes the played pawn's wish on the pawn through `setWish`: the kinds of VB-212 and
  VB-274 (out, claim, serve, match), `settle` (VB-392) and `rank` (a whole ranking). `st.player.wishes` is gone; an older save's
  wishes load as its pawn's (`sim/saves`). Playing a pawn starts it from the default wish; a pawn the player leaves keeps its wish
  until its own choosing at the next round. `wishOf` (moved from sim/records to sim/wishes) is the one read, by kind; `Sim.wishesOf`
  gives all the kinds for the page.
- **The round reads them alike**: farmclaims, heirs (`staysWhy`, `handOn`), service and opportunities read `wishOf` for any pawn;
  the matcher (`villageRound`) leaves out any pawn whose wish declines a match, and makes any wished match first (`wishedMatches` in
  sim/matching, beside `matchBar`; it was the played pawn's only); settle reads the wish to settle for the leader and each sibling.
- **VB-392**: `settle` true heads the ranking with New land and passes the draws of `SETTLE.chance` (leading) and `SETTLE.follow`
  (following); false ranks it after Stay and never leads or follows; null draws as before. The page's round panel has a New land row
  (As you would, Lead or follow, Never), `docs/img/vb-680-round-wishes.png`.
- Invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): a wish's ranking holds each rung once, its farm exists, its partner is of the other sex; a
  living pawn's wish is never the default written out; the player has no wishes of its own.
- Tests: `tests/wishes.js` (rewritten on the pawn's wish; new section 10: own choosing by ambition and hunger, the played pawn
  skipped, an unplayed pawn's wish read as the played pawn's, a whole ranking), `tests/settle.js` (new section 5: the three values of
  the wish to settle, for the leader and a sibling, with the draws forced either way; and its unit block's fake state given the
  `heirPlots` index it lacked, which had made the script fail on its first block at the base commit), `tests/browser.js` (the New land
  wish set from the panel).

## Decisions (Deciding without Kim)

- **Opinion of a partner** is not an input of the own choosing yet: today no rule makes a pawn wish for one partner, and the plan
  gives that rung to courting (VB-036: a courting pawn's match rung is the one it courts). The opinion still decides the answer
  (`unplayedNo`, no at enmity). VB-036 writes `partner` through `setWish`. Close call (a `decision` note).
- **Staying declines the rungs that leave home** (out false = Stay above farm, plot, party and service). Today a wish to stay only
  kept the pawn out of `st.demand`; now asked alone, as a claimant, it is no claimant either. No outcome changes (the round reads
  claimants from `st.demand`). Moving out (out true) declines the heir's rung.
- **The hunger gate stays a rule** (Decided, 13 Nov: a marriage needs no hunger), read by the matcher for every pawn, the played one
  too; the own wish's "not this year" repeats it as the pawn's judgement, so a played pawn cannot marry hungry by wishing.
- **The draws stay** for the default wish to settle: VB-696 replaces them with a threshold (plan, section 9.2). Here the wish only
  passes or refuses them, as VB-392 asked.
- **The wished match is read for every pawn**, and the round's code no longer names the played pawn but for the answer it waits for.
