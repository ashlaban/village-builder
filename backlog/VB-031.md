# VB-031: Clearing a new plot
Status: needs-review
Priority: 1
Size: L
Arch: expansion
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion, Clearing a new plot (Decided; details Agreed, next batch); Customs (Cleared land belongs to).
Goal: When adults want to move out, nothing is claimable and there is room, the village clears a new plot: a house tile and its first fields. Who gets the plot follows the village's "Cleared land belongs to" custom (`newLand`), which today has no effect. Depends on VB-003 (owners per tile) and VB-030 (answers); if either is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria (proposed, from the design; VB-030 confirms the numbers):
- [ ] Trigger, checked at the moving round: at least one person in `st.demand` in the village, no empty farm they may claim (VB-033), and a free site. One project per village at a time.
- [ ] Site: a house tile and 2 field tiles within 4 steps of the green; not owned, not the green, not the village's last commons-wood tile, passable land, in the village's own region or a neighbouring one. The tiles are owned by the village while the project runs (`setOwner`).
- [ ] Jobs owned by the village: fields as in VB-010 (`clear` 10 h + 20 h × stones/255, `break` 30 h); forest tiles: fell 10 h (firewood and timber), grub out stumps 6 h. The cottage is VB-032.
- [ ] Who works: those waiting for the plot rank these jobs high, farmhands and maids next, landholders treat them as a lower village duty (role priorities in `ROLES`, numbers from VB-030). Each pawn's hours on the project are recorded.
- [ ] Who gets it, by `newLand`: `village` the village allots it, married couples first, then by age; `labour` whoever worked most hours; `intended` the person the project was started for (the first in `st.demand` when it started).
- [ ] Stumps, turned soil and the progress show on the map; the village panel shows the project and its progress.
- [ ] A test: a constructed village with one waiting couple and a free site starts a project; each `newLand` option picks the expected person.
- [ ] Founders runs over 50 years at seeds 7 and 42 report plots cleared, marriages and population per decade (reported, not asserted). Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
Notes / questions:
- The job hours in the design predate the long-work notes; VB-030 asks whether they grow with them.
