# VB-776: Plan: a third mock: does a shared facility gather makers?
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M

Phase: plan
Design ref: docs/plans/VB-189.md, sections 2, 3 and 10; docs/cities/mock-town-emergence.md (parts one and two; docs/cities/mocks/); design.md, Settlements, Towns and regions (No town before the ship; What the town waits for)
Goal: Say whether anything gathers makers into a town before the ship (Kim, 16 Nov: no town before the ship is accepted; then a third mock on a shared facility), and what result would reopen the town's design.
Acceptance criteria:
A third mock, as throwaway research code beside the first two (`docs/cities/mocks/`), its findings appended to `docs/cities/mock-town-emergence.md` as part three. No game code changes.
- [ ] The question: does a facility that is costly to build and worth sharing (a kiln, a bloomery; VB-361's facilities, the costs of `design.md`, The mill and the loom, and The metals and the stones) gather makers, where free movement and dealing at gatherings alone did not (the second mock: the fullest village holds 10% to 22% of an island's makers; a place begun gathered comes apart in a generation)?
- [ ] It answers, each with the run that gives it: (1) the fullest village's share of the island's maker households at years 30 and 60, with and without the facility, at the real maps' sizes and at four times; (2) whether a place begun gathered now holds (the second mock: 4.6 maker households at year 10, 1.4 at year 60); (3) whether the leading place locks in or wanders, over 10 seeds; (4) how the answer moves with the facility's cost (0.5, 1, 2 and 4 times a household's year of spare hours), its capacity (2, 4, 8 makers) and who may use it (its builder only; any maker for a due; the village's); (5) whether the due can be paid in the game's terms with nothing teleporting (a share of the make, carried); (6) what stops the place: custom, food, fuel, or ore within a walk; (7) what the place's measures read (outsiders, dealing days, work that is not farming) and whether any now falls into two groups that would give "town" a cut point.
- [ ] It compares, in the same toy and by the same measures, the two other candidates as far as a toy can: an elite household as buyer and patron (slice 5: one household that wants 5 to 20 times a farm's goods and feeds those who serve it) and outside demand (the ship: a buyer at one harbour taking a fixed worth a year). One table: the fullest place's share under none, each alone, and all together.
- [ ] What result reopens the town's design (`design.md`, What the town waits for): a variant, at parameters within a factor of two of the game's planned costs, in which the fullest place holds a third or more of the island's maker households and still holds them at year 60, in most of 10 seeds. Then a `question` note for Kim with the mechanism named, and a `task` note for the plan that designs it. If no variant does, the finding stands and the town waits for slice 5 and the ship: one paragraph for `design.md` in the task file, and a `task` note only if a row must change.
- [ ] Method, what was invented (every invented number listed, as parts one and two do), what a toy cannot show, and the seeds; every table is reproduced by `node docs/cities/mocks/NAME.js` in under a minute.
- [ ] The document says at the head of part three that nothing in it is evidence about the game beyond a factor of two.
- Cost: none in the game.
