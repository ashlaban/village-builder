- [x] The question: does a facility that is costly to build and worth sharing (a kiln, a bloomery; VB-361's facilities, the costs of `design.md`, The mill and the loom, and The metals and the stones) gather makers, where free movement and dealing at gatherings alone did not (the second mock: the fullest village holds 10% to 22% of an island's makers; a place begun gathered comes apart in a generation)?
- [x] It answers, each with the run that gives it: (1) the fullest village's share of the island's maker households at years 30 and 60, with and without the facility, at the real maps' sizes and at four times; (2) whether a place begun gathered now holds (the second mock: 4.6 maker households at year 10, 1.4 at year 60); (3) whether the leading place locks in or wanders, over 10 seeds; (4) how the answer moves with the facility's cost (0.5, 1, 2 and 4 times a household's year of spare hours), its capacity (2, 4, 8 makers) and who may use it (its builder only; any maker for a due; the village's); (5) whether the due can be paid in the game's terms with nothing teleporting (a share of the make, carried); (6) what stops the place: custom, food, fuel, or ore within a walk; (7) what the place's measures read (outsiders, dealing days, work that is not farming) and whether any now falls into two groups that would give "town" a cut point.

## Notes (VB-776, 16 November 2026, in the night's orchestrated run)

**What was made.** `docs/cities/mocks/share.js` (the third mock, tables D1 to D7) and part three of
`docs/cities/mock-town-emergence.md` (findings 26 to 38, the measures, the test, method, what was invented, what remains a toy).

**The answer.** No. Nothing gathers makers into a town before the ship at parameters near the game's, and the reopening test was not
met. The fullest village's share of the island's maker households, years 30 and 60, `populous-small-7-y30` (129 households):

**The paragraph for `design.md`** (written into Towns and regions, What the town waits for, as Proposed, under the night's rule 1;
No town before the ship gained one Proposed sentence; a row in `docs/design-changelog.md`): the third mock did not reopen the
section; a shared facility is no longer a candidate (the planned ones cost 0.01 to 0.15 of a household's year of spare hours and
change nothing; a costly one keeps the landless out of the craft and does not fill a place); the elite's household adds its own
custom and no more; a ship's buyer on known days feeds the makers within half a day of the harbour; what gathered makers was demand
at a point that only residents can serve, of the order of all the island's farms give its makers. The ship's plan chooses.

**Decided here** (each a `decision` note in the inbox):
1. "Within a factor of two of the game's planned costs" is read as a facility of up to 0.3 of a year of spare hours (twice a
   windmill's 140 hours). The task's own sweep (0.5 to 4 years) is 17 to 132 times a bloomery and so lies outside the test; it was
   run and reported, and could not have reopened the design by the task's wording. Why: the planned costs are in the backlog
   (VB-364, VB-368, VB-362, the mill), and the test names them.
2. A third counts only at a place of three maker households or more. Why: a costly facility leaves islands with a dozen makers,
   where one village with four is already a third; `design.md` already counts "villages with three of them".
3. The two rows of `design.md` were edited as Proposed rather than left as a paragraph here. Why: the row said "which a third mock
   tries first", which is now false, and the night's rule allows it.
4. In the toy a builder's heir keeps the facility and its due; 66 to 100% of dues then go to a household that is no maker. A toy's
   assumption, surfaced because it is a rent (Deciding without Kim, 13) that slice 6's tolls would create if a facility were dear.

**What was tried and dropped.** The first run of the whole script took 85 s; seeds were cut (the sweeps to 2, the medium map to 3,
four times the households to one seed, the village's capacity rows and the planned-cost row of D3 dropped) to some 165 runs. A
time is a description here: 73 s for the whole script with a load of 12 on the night's 4 cores, where a run took 0.15 s early in
the night and 1.5 s late; each table by `--only` took 12 to 35 s under that load before the cut. On a quiet machine the count of
runs gives about half a minute; that was not measured.

**Validation.** `node docs/cities/mocks/share.js` prints D1 to D7; `node docs/cities/mocks/move.js --only base|feedback|lock|days`
print what they printed before. `npm run verify` on the merged result: see the commit.
