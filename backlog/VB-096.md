# VB-096: Walking costs by terrain
Status: done (2026-10-30)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: tooling
Phase: implement
Design ref: World and scale (Terrain; Uses of regions); Systems overview (Work: walking ignores terrain: mountains cost the same as meadow).
Goal: Routes in `src/sim/agents.js` are breadth-first searches over passable tiles, so a pawn crosses a hill or a mountain as fast as a meadow and takes no detour round it. Give terrain a cost, in the route search and in walking speed.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Each biome has a step cost (meadow, heath, shore and fields 1; forest 1.5; hills and marsh 2; mountains 3; snowfields closed), in a `TERRAIN` table in `src/sim/rules.js`.
- [x] The route search finds the cheapest path (Dijkstra or A* with the hex distance as the bound), with the same cap and fallback as after VB-064; cached routes are invalidated when a tile's biome changes (felling, clearing).
- [x] A pawn walks a tile at its normal speed divided by the tile's cost.
- [x] A test: a route round a hill is chosen over one across it when it is cheaper; snowfields are never crossed.
- [x] Report mean firewood and field walk times before and after, founders and populous medium at seed 7. Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.

- Done 2026-10-30. The search moved out of `src/sim/agents.js` into a new module `src/sim/routes.js` (`tileCost`, `passable`, `stepCost`, `findRoute`, `route`, `costChanged`, `refreshRoutes`); `VB['sim/agents'].route` still names it. A step between two tiles costs the mean of their costs, so a route costs the same both ways and the cache answers the way back with the way there, reversed. One A* search up to `ROUTE.reach` (200 meadow steps) replaces the two breadth-first searches of 60 and 200 steps; beyond it a pawn still walks straight and `routeFallbacks` counts it. Houses and fields cost 1 whatever the biome under them; the two ends of a route may be closed tiles.
- Invalidation: `clearTile` (felling a wood to nothing, clearing a plot) marks the tile in `st.routeDirty`; at the end of the day `refreshRoutes` drops each cached route that the tile could change: those for which steps from the start to the tile plus steps from the tile to the end, times the cheapest step, are within the route's cost.
- Speed: route search took about 40% of the simulation's time (each new pair of tiles ran a 60-step breadth-first search of up to 11,000 tiles). Populous medium at seed 7, 2 years (`tests/perf.js`): 129.6 to 88.1 ms a game day; the search no longer shows in the profile. Cached routes after a year: 3,697 to 2,991.
- Mean walk to the job, hours (founders seed 7 over 2 years; populous medium seed 7 over 1): firewood 1.40 to 1.52 and 1.34 to 1.40; fields 0.50 to 0.50 and 0.48 to 0.48; water 0.43 to 0.43 and 0.74 to 0.74.
- Golden entries changed: every `founders` and `heirs` entry at seeds 7 and 42 from year 1 on; the `seed` entries only by the new `routeDirty` key. `world`, `calibrate` and `apiKeys` are unchanged.
