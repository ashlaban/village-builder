# VB-096: Walking costs by terrain
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: tooling
Phase: implement
Design ref: World and scale (Terrain; Uses of regions); Systems overview (Work: walking ignores terrain: mountains cost the same as meadow).
Goal: Routes in `src/sim/agents.js` are breadth-first searches over passable tiles, so a pawn crosses a hill or a mountain as fast as a meadow and takes no detour round it. Give terrain a cost, in the route search and in walking speed.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Each biome has a step cost (meadow, heath, shore and fields 1; forest 1.5; hills and marsh 2; mountains 3; snowfields closed), in a `TERRAIN` table in `src/sim/rules.js`.
- [ ] The route search finds the cheapest path (Dijkstra or A* with the hex distance as the bound), with the same cap and fallback as after VB-064; cached routes are invalidated when a tile's biome changes (felling, clearing).
- [ ] A pawn walks a tile at its normal speed divided by the tile's cost.
- [ ] A test: a route round a hill is chosen over one across it when it is cheaper; snowfields are never crossed.
- [ ] Report mean firewood and field walk times before and after, founders and populous medium at seed 7. Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
