# VB-311: Land rights: own and use held apart, st.owner the user's cache
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Land, ecology and rights, Land and rights (Slice 5's rights; Owner and user); Claims, ownership and territory (Ownership, Decided); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, section 4.3 (Land)
Goal: Foreclosure, tenancy and splitting between heirs all need a holding whose owner is not the household that works it. Today `st.owner` (`src/sim/ownership.js`) holds one party a tile, and every rule of work reads it as the one who works the tile. Keep that reading, so the hot paths do not change, and record the owner beside it only where the two differ.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `sim/ownership` (or a module `sim/land` that takes it over, if `sim/ownership` would pass 400 lines) owns `st.owner` as the user of each tile (the one who works it), and a new `st.titles`: a Map of holding to `{owner, since, basis, rent}` for holdings whose `own` lies with another party. A holding is a farm (its house and yard tiles), a field or a meadow, named by its tiles as today's farmstead and field records name them.
- [ ] Operations: `ownerOf(st, tile)` (the title's owner, else the user), `holdingsOf(st, party, 'own' | 'use')`, `split(st, farm, field, why)` (a field becomes a holding of its own), `transfer(st, holding, 'own', to, basis, why)`, `grant(st, holding, 'use', to, rent, why)` and `end(st, holding, why)`; each marks `st.landDirty`.
- [ ] The claims, the share rule (`SHARES`) and the land each holds (Claims, Land and tax) count `own`, and every rule of work keeps reading the user; until a title exists the two are the same, so nothing in play changes.
- [ ] A test on a constructed village (`tests/lib/tiny.js`): split a field off a farm, pass its `own` to a neighbour with the farm's household keeping `use`; the field's work still goes to its user; `ownerOf` and `holdingsOf` answer both ways; ending the title returns the field to one holder.
- [ ] Saves, plain data, invariants (`checkState`: a title's holding exists and its owner and user are live parties, or the owner a village), `src/owners.json`; `tests/golden.json` unchanged.
Cost: none in play until slice 5's remedies make titles: a title a foreclosed field or let farm, so tens at `medium-ref` after decades; no per-tile array is added.
Order: beside VB-310 and VB-313. VB-316 (foreclosure), VB-317 (tenancies) and VB-034 (splitting between heirs) build on it; VB-309 (a farm in trust) may reuse its `grant` of use.
