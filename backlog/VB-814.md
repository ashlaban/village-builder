Goal: Bulk goes by water (principle 9: trade over land, by river, and late by sea): a second kind of boat that makes a load between villages cheap, and rivers as ways inland.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The large boat: one more kind of the one item (`BOATS.large`): crew 4 at the oars, 900 rations or 12 people beyond them, 0.35 a tile of water, on the sea within `nearLarge` 8 tiles of land (the small boat's 3 leaves 40 to 42 pairs of villages apart at seeds 11 and 42: report how many 8 joins); 120 hours and 20 loads of timber at a landing, tarred as the fishing boat is (2 barrels). A head begins it as any project (VB-678), when its own count of loads that waited for a boat passes a named number; nobody is given one.
- [ ] A crew: the large boat puts out only with 4 aboard who chose to go (members of the household, or others by the help and hire that exist: say which in the task file). With fewer it stays.
- [ ] Rivers: a small boat goes on a river of the widest kind (`gen/rivers` `kind` River: 52 to 190 tiles a medium island) at `river` 0.5 a tile down and 0.75 up; a ford is a portage of `portage` 3; a bridge is passed. A village on such a river has a landing on it (the plan counted 3 to 4 more villages with a landing a map).
- [ ] A constructed check: 900 rations cross 10 tiles of water in one journey with 4 aboard and the boat is refused with 3; a small boat goes up 12 river tiles past one ford in the hours the rule gives.
- [ ] Numbers before and after as VB-813's, and: large boats built, by which households (principle 13: who gained by it is reported, not set); loads carried by water a year; villages joined at `nearLarge` 8.
- Cost (rule 9): as VB-812; the river adds its tiles (at most 190) to the water searched.
