Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `node tools/gate.js` (it starts with `tools/help.js` and answers `--help`) runs the six runs (founders and populous, small map, seeds 7, 11 and 42, 10 years; side by side where the machine allows) and prints a line a run: the settlements at year 0 and 10, each one extinct and why (hunger, or another cause, read from the record's last deaths), the living at year 0 and 10 by settlement, the tasks held over 48 hours without progress (VB-1546's count, now a function of the tool), the stocks' `unexplained` (the accounts report), hungry household-days and deaths by hunger; `--json`.
- [x] `--out FILE` keeps the result; `--base FILE` compares and exits non-zero when the gate fails: a settlement extinct by hunger that the base keeps alive; a settlement collapsed; more tasks held over 48 hours than the base; `unexplained` larger than the base's beyond a tolerance the help states. Hunger and deaths are printed beside the base and never fail.
- [x] The bound for "a collapse of its people", decided by AGENTS.md, Deciding without Kim, and surfaced to Kim as a close call: a settlement collapses when its living at year 10 are under half its living at year 0 and under half the base run's at year 10, so a decline the base shares is not charged to the task. Written in the tool's help and in `docs/testing.md`.
- [x] The base recorded at the commit the tool lands on and kept where the next task finds it (a file under `docs/gate/`, or the fixtures' local cache keyed by the sources, as `docs/testing.md` prefers: say which), its numbers in the PR.
- [x] `tests/gate-tool.js` (a line in `tests/suites.js`): the comparison on constructed results (a settlement extinct, a decline under and over the bound, shared and not shared by the base, a stuck task) fails and passes as stated, without running the simulation.
- [x] The wall time of the six runs reported (about 9 s a year of populous small: some 10 to 15 minutes in all); `docs/commands.md` gets its line.
