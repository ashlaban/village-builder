# VB-700: Salt: boiled at a salt spring, and salting needs it
Status: done (2026-11-23)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (7, 9, 16); design.md, Goods, production and animals
Goal: Salt is boiled from brine at a salt spring with wood, carried home, and used up by salting meat, fish and butter; a household without salt keeps its meat a shorter time by drying or smoking.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A job kind at a salt spring tile within reach: brine boiled with firewood into salt, a load carried home (nothing teleports); the good `salt` in the goods registry with its weight, keeping and worth; its target is the head's (VB-698).
- [x] Salting uses salt by the ration of meat (a tunable with its physical meaning); without salt the meat is dried or smoked and keeps a named shorter time, so a household without salt is worse off, not barred.
- [x] Sea salt is not made (a cold shore: a note says why, or the task decides otherwise and says so); the spring's yield does not run out.
- [x] Numbers before and after (`tools/balance.js`), populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years: villages with a salt spring in reach, meat spoilt, hungry household-days by whether the village has salt; the invariants and `tools/stocks.js` line for `salt`.
