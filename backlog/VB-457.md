# VB-457: The village store fills to a target, and the commons are sown under it
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Help in hunger); backlog/VB-406.md; QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-406 (15 Nov)
Goal: VB-406 measured the village store as a sink: 79 to 83% of what comes in rots and 1 to 3% is given out, because a quarter of every surplus goes in every year whatever the store holds. Decided by Deciding without Kim, 10 (a bound on a Decided rule): the filling stops at a named target, and the commons follow the same target. The mechanism (a quarter of the surplus, at the moving round) stays.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `HELP.storeTarget` (0.5, years of its village's need) is a named tunable in `src/sim/rules.js`; `fillStores` (`src/sim/help.js`) takes a household's quarter only up to what the store lacks of the target, shared among the givers in proportion to what each would have given; the rest stays at home. `design.md`, Help in hunger, gains the bound in its Decided line (a design line edited by this task, by Deciding without Kim, 10).
- [ ] A common field is sown only in a year its village's store is under the target at the sowing decision, and lies fallow, grazed as meadow, otherwise; a field already sown is harvested. The decision is taken once a year, on the village's planning day (rule 4: no polling).
- [ ] A test on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): a store at the target takes nothing and the household keeps its quarter; a store just under it takes only the difference; a common field is not sown with the store at the target and is sown the year after it falls under.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, `node tools/store-flows.js`, populous small seeds 3, 7, 11 and founders small seeds 7, 42, 50 years: rations in, given out (households helped), rotted, the store in years of need, the commons' share of the inflow, hungry household-days; and growth (Deciding without Kim, 2). VB-406's patch of the filling alone gave rot down 74% with hunger unchanged; the commons rule has not been run, and this task's run is its first.
- [ ] Parameterisation (Deciding without Kim, 3; at most five short runs): targets 0.25, 0.5 and 1.0, each with what it gave; the row of `docs/tuning.md` (VB-406) is rewritten with the value kept and the residual.
- [ ] The alderman's keep is not touched: VB-314's plan sets it from the surplus offered (`QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, VB-406, answer 3).
