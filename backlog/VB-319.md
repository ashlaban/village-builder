# VB-319: Hired hands: a day's work for a wage at the peaks
Status: blocked

Goal: A landed household's fields are worked by others: tenants' and debtors' days, servants, and hands hired for the day. Add the last basis, so landless adults earn grain at the peaks and households short of hands get their harvest in.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The basis `hired` in VB-250's table of bases: at sowing, haymaking and harvest, a holder whose field task has waited 2 hours for hands hires a landless adult of its village with no task of its own, for the rest of the day; a landed household (VB-318, once done) asks first, and a household below its floor does not hire.
- [ ] The wage, 3 rations for a day (8 work hours; less for part of a day, in whole rations), is an obligation of kind `wage` (VB-310) paid at the day's end from the household's grain through `sim/stores` (reason `wage`); unpaid, it is overdue like a loan.
- [ ] The hired pawn's household (or the pawn, if a lodger) receives the grain; events for hiring are routine and fold into yearly counts (rule 9).
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years): days hired a year, grain paid, harvest losses (fields not reaped by the frost), the idle share by season (`design.md`, How busy), hungry household-days of landless adults' households. Golden and counters re-recorded, entries listed.
Cost: the `hired` basis is checked only for tasks that have waited 2 hours, from a per-day index of who was hired by whom, so matching stays one lookup a check; a share of the 2 ms this slice claims for eligibility (`docs/roadmap.md`, 5.2).
