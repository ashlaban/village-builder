# VB-319: Hired hands: a day's work for a wage at the peaks
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-310, VB-470
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, Loans, obligations and cases (Hired hands); Work: opportunities, plans and tasks (Who may take a task: "the hired (a wage) come with slice 5"); the worth table (a day's work 3 rations)
Goal: A landed household's fields are worked by others: tenants' and debtors' days, servants, and hands hired for the day. Add the last basis, so landless adults earn grain at the peaks and households short of hands get their harvest in.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The basis `hired` in VB-250's table of bases (rewritten 15 Nov): at sowing, haymaking and harvest, a head whose estimate (VB-476) says a field task will not be finished in its window with its own hands and the help it was given (VB-470) hires a landless adult of its village whose own day has the hours, for the hours it lacks, at most the rest of the day; a landed household (VB-318, once done) asks first, and a household below its floor does not hire. The hired hours are a leg on the hired basis.
- [ ] The wage, 3 rations for a day (8 work hours; less for part of a day, in whole rations), is an obligation of kind `wage` (VB-310) paid at the day's end from the household's grain through `sim/stores` (reason `wage`); unpaid, it is overdue like a loan.
- [ ] The hired pawn's household (or the pawn, if a lodger) receives the grain; events for hiring are routine and fold into yearly counts (rule 9).
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years): days hired a year, grain paid, harvest losses (fields not reaped by the frost), the idle share by season (`design.md`, How busy), hungry household-days of landless adults' households. The counters re-recorded (`node tests/counters.js --update`), their entries listed.
Cost: hiring is weighed when the head's day is allotted and a task's estimated hours are not covered, from a per-day index of who was hired by whom; no new loop (the pairs the allotment weighs already); a share of the 2 ms this slice claims for eligibility (`docs/roadmap.md`, 5.2).
Order: after VB-310 and VB-470 (help is asked for first; hiring is what a head does when help falls short). Independent of the debt chain.
