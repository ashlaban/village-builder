# VB-710: Custom: grazing on the commons, by land share or open: stints as the right graze
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-311, VB-709
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Goods, production and animals: Animals, Customs of the animals; docs/plans/VB-141.md (sections 2.1, 2.2, 5); design.md, Land and rights (Grazing on the commons), Custom dimensions; docs/architecture/target-v2.md, section 4.3
Goal: Grazing on the village's outland is the right graze, held by the village and granted to its households by the custom (Kim, 16 Nov): by land share, with the tenant's share its own, no stint for the landless and strangers' herds admitted only under open.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A `DIMS` entry `grazing` in `src/culture.js`: label "Grazing on the commons", options `byLand` ("By land share") and `open` ("Open to all"), malleability `m` 0.4; `byLand` in the Old Nordic archetype, and in each other archetype the value its other land customs suggest, said in the task file; `SAID` phrases; interests in `sim/leanings` (a household whose herds exceed its stint leans to `open`; one within its stint whose pasture was crowded, `f < 1` of VB-709, leans to `byLand`; a landless household with animals leans to `open`). The third option, `meeting`, is VB-716.
- [ ] The commons' capacity of a village: the sum of `HERD.herding.grass` over its outland tiles that are grazable, times `HERD.commons.cap` (0.25 cow-equivalents a tile at full grass), computed in the village's step of the autumn meeting (Rules for cost 5), never daily.
- [ ] The stint under `byLand`: capacity × the household's share of the village's tiles in use (fields and meadows, by the tile's user, so a let holding counts for its tenant and not for its owner; Kim, 16 Nov), in cow-equivalents (cattle 1, a sheep `HERD.herding.sheep`); a household with no land has 0. It is recorded as a `graze` right on the village's outland, grantor the village, holder the household, basis `custom`, term one year, through `LandRights.grant` (target-v2 4.3; VB-311), with `hh.stint` its cache, written only by `sim/grazing`. The grant is a pawn's act (principle 16): the alderman's where there is one, else that of the holder who presides at the meeting, through a verb in `src/sim/commands.js`.
- [ ] `pasture` and the herd's load honour it: on the village's outland a household's herds load `min(head, stint)` cow-equivalents; the head beyond it stay at the farm as an unherded herd does (half the milk, may stray) or graze the household's own meadows' aftermath; the least valuable head stay first, in the count's order. Nothing caps the herd itself.
- [ ] Strangers: under `byLand` a tile of a village's outland is not grazable to a household of another village; under `open` it is, as today. A herd turned away is logged once a household a year (`log`, with its event kind, the herder and the place); no grievance yet (VB-297).
- [ ] State: `hh.stint` gets its `@state` line, its owner in `src/owners.json`, its invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js` (not negative; 0 for a landless household under `byLand`; absent under `open`; a village's stints sum to at most its capacity) and its line in `tools/stocks.js` (at none: the herd stays at the farm).
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state with its line in `tests/suites.js`: three farms of 6, 3 and 0 tiles on a commons of capacity 6 get stints of 4, 2 and 0; a tenant gets its holding's share and its owner none; a farm of another village is turned away under `byLand` and admitted under `open`; with every village forced to `open`, behaviour equals today's (`node tools/same.js --base REF`).
- [ ] Numbers before and after (`byLand` against `open`, forced), at populous small seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 (15 years) and founders tiny seed 7: commons pressure a village (the mean, and the share of tiles over `downAt`), the share of herd-days with `f < 1`, head kept at the farm, milk a cow, herd sizes by land held (none, 1 to 3 tiles, more), hungry days a household (the distribution), in the task file and the PR; who gains and who loses, by class. `HERD.commons.cap` tried in at most five short runs (0.15, 0.25 and 0.4 at the least): kept where a village's stints together leave its commons near `downAt`, not over it, at the season's end.
- [ ] Cost (Rules for cost 9): 211 households at `medium-ref` × once a year, a village a meeting step × its households and their fields and meadows (tens of tiles): a declared counter `stintHouseholds`. On choosing a pasture (at most once a week a household) one lookup of `hh.stint` and of the tile's village, inside the existing `pastureTiles` counter. The custom's attitudes: about 0.3 ms a game day (an estimate). Measured on a late save (`node tools/sim.js --load FILE --years 1 --report perf`), counters reported.
- [ ] An impact note in the PR (size M, a new state field): the modules and state read and written, and the new coupling of `sim/grazing` to land rights and to the customs in force.
