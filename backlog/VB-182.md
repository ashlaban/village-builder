## Plan (2026-11-08)

Run interactively (started by Kim from the app): the questions are in `QUESTIONS.md` ("VB-182") and asked in the session.

**Survey.**
- Help today is one module, `src/sim/help.js` (68 lines). Each day `shareFood` (`help.js:43`, called at `src/sim/clock.js:55`) takes every
  household whose grain above its seed reserve covers fewer than 3 days of its need, and asks for 12 days' food: first its kin
  households, closest first (`kinHouseholds`, `help.js:15`: those of members' parents and children, then siblings), each giving what
  lies above its seed reserve and its holder's buffer (`surplus`, `help.js:9`; `pawn.buffer` is 20 to 60 days, drawn at birth,
  `src/sim/records.js:54`); then the village store, which gives the rest. `gift` (`help.js:29`) moves the grain, writes the two holders'
  `gift` events, and raises their ties (familiarity) by 0.5 each way (`help.js:39`); opinion does not change. At the moving round,
  `fillStores` (`help.js:59`, from `src/sim/lifecycle.js:128`) moves a quarter of each household's surplus to the village store.
- VB-174 leaves `sim/stores` (`src/sim/stores.js`) as the one writer of food: `put`, `take` and `move` with a good and a reason, each
  writing a ledger entry on each side (`gift` entries name the other side by farm or village name, not by household id); help already
  calls them. VB-204 (ready) folds the routine kinds into yearly totals and keeps gifts as entries, so a gift's entry can carry its deal.
- VB-178 leaves `sim/relations` as the one writer of ties and memories: `strengthenTie` (`relations.js:17`), `opinionOf` (standing plus
  timed memories, read in closed form, `relations.js:84`), the seasonal `fadeRelations` (`relations.js:114`, from
  `lifecycle.js:276`) and `forget` at a death (`lifecycle.js:32`). Goodwill and its coupling to opinion go here.
- The rules registry (`src/sim/registry.js`) has one rule, `death.hazard` (`src/sim/deaths.js:6`); `value` allocates nothing, so the
  allowance's context must be a reused object.
- Commands (`src/sim/commands.js:20`) have no verb that moves goods; the meeting's verbs show an actor that is a body
  (`{settlement}`), which the village store's gift can follow.
- Holders change in `sim/heirs`: `transfer` (`heirs.js:156`, a handover at retirement or death) and `succession` (`heirs.js:287`, a
  widow's), where a share of goodwill would pass.
- The panel's relations list is `showRelations` (`src/page/words.js:189`), from `Sim.relationsOf` and `Sim.reasonsOf`.

**What goodwill needs.** A signed balance per pair that has dealt, with the time it was last booked and when it opened; sparse (only pairs
that dealt), capped per pawn, decaying in closed form (no daily pass), pruned at the seasonal pass that already walks the living. Measured
(fixtures at `4ee11e9`, stale): 33 to 75 gifts between holders a year at populous small, seed 7 (350 to 420 living), 112 pairs in 8
years, at most 9 counterparts a pawn; one year from `small-y40`, 163 gifts among 129 pairs (729 living), at most 5; from `medium-y60`,
486 among 364 pairs (2,531 living), at most 6. A gift is about 22 rations. The scratch scripts are not kept; the numbers are in
`QUESTIONS.md`.

**Approach.** A design pass, no code: Kim answered seven questions in the session, approved the design text, then the task list and two
points on the player's gift (questions 8 and 9, archived with the rest). Help keeps its own rules (whom it asks, in what order, for how
much); Exchange's gift decides whether a gift goes ahead (the floor, the allowance) and settles it; `sim/relations` keeps goodwill and
declares `allowance`. Behaviour is kept first (VB-216), then the booking is added as state that nothing reads but the panel (VB-217,
VB-218); goodwill acts only from slice 4 (VB-220).

*Rejected:*
- **Allowance as help's buffer alone** (no floor, no terms): it would satisfy the golden file but leave the registry's second rule
  empty, and the floor is Decided (3 Nov) for every deal.
- **Help asking for the allowance:** the allowance's sum differs from today's `surplus` in the last bits, so the golden file would move.
  Help proposes today's size and Exchange checks it within 1e-9 (VB-216's note).
- **One store of balances keyed by pair** (like `st.contacts`): reading one pawn's balances, for the cap and the panel, would need an
  index beside it, a cache with its own invariant. Mirrored per-pawn maps, like `pawn.ties`, with one writer, read directly.
- **Goodwill acting in slice 1:** Kim chose book-and-show (question 3), keeping the roadmap's "done when".
