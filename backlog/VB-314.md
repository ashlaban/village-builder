# VB-314: The alderman: chosen at the meeting, presides, the village's agent
Status: blocked

Goal: The alderman's road starts with the office. Give each village an alderman, chosen at its meeting, who decides ties, speaks for the village in its loans and gifts, and gains standing by it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A new practice "How the alderman is chosen" (elected by the meeting, default; by turns among the farms, a year each in the order of their founding; malleability 0.3).
- [ ] At the autumn meeting, when the office is vacant (death, retirement, leaving the village, or a turn's end), the village chooses among its holders through `sim/decisions` (VB-313): elected, each voice for the holder it thinks most of by opinion and prestige; by turns, the next farm's holder. A village of one farm has its holder as alderman.
- [ ] The alderman is held on the village as a role written by one owner (the membership owner, `sim/records` or `sim/membership`; an edge once Kim decides the bodies question), in `src/owners.json`; he gains 0.3 prestige while in office (`refreshPrestige`).
- [ ] He casts the deciding voice of a tie in the village's decisions, and he is the village's agent: the village store's help is booked as goodwill between him and the receiving holder (The gift, Who books: no longer nothing), and the village's loans (VB-312) are made by him.
- [ ] Events: chosen, and the office vacated; the village panel names the alderman.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years): aldermen a village and their years in office; the goodwill booked to them; golden and counters re-recorded, entries listed.
Cost: one question a village when vacant; nothing daily.
Order: after VB-313; VB-315 (cases, a tie to the alderman) after it.
