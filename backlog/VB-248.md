Goal: Several choices and targets a household makes are hidden in the rules that post jobs (`docs/review-work.md`, section 1.2). Move them into the plan record, each read where it is read today, so a culture, a head's foresight or the player can later change them in one place.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Targets on the standing plan, written by `sim/plans` at each review with today's values: the woodpile's goal by season (`woodGoal`, `src/sim/kinds.js`), hay for `ENOUGH` winters (`hayWanted`, `src/sim/meadows.js`), the fields wanted (`fieldsWanted`, `fieldsWorkable`, `src/sim/fields.js`). `wood.available`, the meadow work and the land step read the plan's targets, not the constants.
- [ ] The land step: `startProject` and `startMeadow` are called by the plan's review (once a day at `WORK[0]`, as now), and the tile comes from one function per kind (`chooseTile`, `chooseMeadow`) behind a seam that takes a tile given in the plan in its place. No verb gives one yet (VB-253).
- [ ] The cull: `herd.cull` stays where it is, filled at the autumn count; the plan records that the count was held this year. Nothing reads it yet.
- [ ] Reservations: `sim/stores` keeps a count per holder, good and plan (`reserve`, `release`, `reserved`, each with a reason, as its other operations); `seedReserve` (`src/sim/hunger.js`) becomes the plan's reservation of seed for its unsown fields and its project, and its readers (`cookWanted`, `floorOf`, `src/sim/help.js`, `src/sim/farmclaims.js`, `src/sim/lifecycle.js`) read the reserved count. An invariant: a holder's reservations of a good never exceed its stock of it, and no reservation names a plan that is gone.
- [ ] `@state` lines for the new fields; `tests/golden.json` unchanged; counters unchanged. `npm run verify` passes.
- [ ] The PR says what fell: the constants and call sites no longer read outside the plan.
Cost: a few numbers per holder, written at the review; no new loop.
