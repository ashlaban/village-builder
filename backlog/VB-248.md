# VB-248: The hidden plans made explicit: targets, the land step and the seed's reservation, golden unchanged
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Work: opportunities, plans and tasks (design.md, section 7); docs/review-work.md, sections 1.2 and 5 (task 2)
Goal: Several choices and targets a household makes are hidden in the rules that post jobs (`docs/review-work.md`, section 1.2). Move them into the plan record, each read where it is read today, so a culture, a head's foresight or the player can later change them in one place.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Targets on the standing plan, written by `sim/plans` at each review with today's values: the woodpile's goal by season (`woodGoal`, `src/sim/kinds.js`), hay for `ENOUGH` winters (`hayWanted`, `src/sim/meadows.js`), the fields wanted (`fieldsWanted`, `fieldsWorkable`, `src/sim/fields.js`). `wood.available`, the meadow work and the land step read the plan's targets, not the constants.
- [x] The land step: `startProject` and `startMeadow` are called by the plan's review (once a day at `WORK[0]`, as now), and the tile comes from one function per kind (`chooseTile`, `chooseMeadow`) behind a seam that takes a tile given in the plan in its place. No verb gives one yet (VB-253).
- [x] The cull: `herd.cull` stays where it is, filled at the autumn count; the plan records that the count was held this year. Nothing reads it yet.
- [x] (partly; see the notes: the stock invariant is not added, and the seed is counted at read) Reservations: `sim/stores` keeps a count per holder, good and plan (`reserve`, `release`, `reserved`, each with a reason, as its other operations); `seedReserve` (`src/sim/hunger.js`) becomes the plan's reservation of seed for its unsown fields and its project, and its readers (`cookWanted`, `floorOf`, `src/sim/help.js`, `src/sim/farmclaims.js`, `src/sim/lifecycle.js`) read the reserved count. An invariant: a holder's reservations of a good never exceed its stock of it, and no reservation names a plan that is gone.
- [x] `@state` lines for the new fields; `tests/golden.json` unchanged; counters unchanged. `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals.
- [x] The PR says what fell: the constants and call sites no longer read outside the plan.
Cost: a few numbers per holder, written at the review; no new loop.
