Goal: Make the budget a target the game can be held to. The review of the work since 3 November found three things, and Kim answered on 7 November:
- the late reference is the year populous medium reaches 1,000 living (VB-202 makes it), not year 60;

Notes:
- 7 Nov 2026 (review of the work since 3 November): written. It waits for VB-202's fixtures.
- 7 Nov 2026, Kim's answers (all four proposals):
  1. Basis: at 30× a game day lasts 1.6 s, and the simulation may take at most a quarter of the page's main thread once every slice is built: 400 ms a game day at the late reference. The systems built so far may use half of it, 200 ms; each later slice's plan claims its line from the other half.
  2. History ratio: cost per living pawn-day at `small-y40` at most 1.25 times `small-y5`; `medium-y60` against `medium-y5` reported nightly.
     Rationale, given in the session:
     - The ratio exists for rule 1: it catches loops whose work grows with what has accumulated (the dead, ended unions, records, ledgers) rather than with the living. To see that, it needs two states far apart in history. `medium-ref` is now about year 14, only nine years past year 5, so a loop that doubles the cost by year 60 would show only a few percent there.
     - `small-y40` is 35 years past `small-y5`, more than a generation, with 0.76 dead records per living pawn. It is cheap, it is already loaded in verify, and VB-088's history check asserts the work counters on the same pair at the same 1.25. The wall-clock ratio and the counters then judge the same pair.
     - On 5 November (VB-195, paired runs) small's ratio was 0.291 against 0.282 ms a living pawn-day, 1.03, while medium-y60 against medium-y5 was 1.32 on 7 November. The two differ mostly in density: medium's living grow 2.8 times against small's 1.9, and pair costs (talk, the marriage market) grow per pawn with density. That is a cost of people met, which the mean budget at `medium-ref` already bounds; it is not history. The counters by name (`roundPairs` scaling exponent 2, for example) say which loop grows and why.
     - Caveat: small's living also nearly double between the two saves, so a little density stays in the ratio; the named counters separate the two.
  3. `medium-y60`: a stress fixture, reported nightly against twice the reference's lines (3 November: "a large map may reach about 2,000 at half the speed"), and never gating. Its temporary 400 ms step allowance has ended: no 6-minute step over 60 ms on `medium-ref` and `small-y40`.
  4. Save and load: 0.5 s each, at `medium-ref`.
