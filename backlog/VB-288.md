# VB-288: Distance governs the round's matching: women within reach, nearer ones preferred
Status: done (2026-11-16)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: between
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Between villages: Ease of mixing, Over the sea; Leaving home: Marriage). Survey: backlog/VB-281.md
Goal: A village's round (VB-277) matches its own men with the unmarried women of every village, and the score knows nothing of how far apart the two live: it adds 2 for the same village, else 2 × the lesser of the two villages' ties to their culture's centre (`matchScore`, `src/sim/matching.js:17-22`). So a woman 60 hours' walk away, or on another island, scores as one next door when both villages lie near the centre. VB-281 measured the marriages of the last ten years in the fixtures' histories: at `medium-ref`, of 118, 47 within the village, 17 within a day's walk (12 h), 34 further and 22 over the sea; at `small-y40`, of 70, 19, 10, 33 and 8. Kim decided on 10 November that distance governs the mixing and close villages mix easily. Make the pool the women within walking reach, and the score prefer the nearer.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The pool. A village's round offers its men the unmarried women (by today's tests) of the villages within walking reach of it: `hoursBetween(st, v, u)` finite (by land, at most `ROUTE.reach`, 67 h; VB-286). A woman of a village over the sea or beyond reach is not in the pool. The pool is built from the villages within reach and their living, not from the island's living filtered.
- [x] The score's village term is `2 × exp(-h / 8)` for h walking hours between the two villages (`MARRIAGE.nearHours` 8, a tunable): 2 within the village, as today; 1.21 at 4 h, 0.94 at 6 h, 0.45 at 12 h, 0.10 at 24 h, 0.01 at 41 h. It replaces the term of the two villages' ties, which keep their use in the culture's drift. The scale 8 h is the tie's own (its decay is `exp(-d / 25)` in steps, about 8 h of walking at 3 tiles an hour).
- [x] Kin pressure (`kinPressure`, as VB-277 leaves it): an unmatched pawn is pushed only when it has no allowed partner among the free pawns of the other sex within reach, but would have one under the next looser limit within reach; the plenty pull is unchanged.
- [x] The player's wish for a partner (VB-274, landed 11 Nov: through `matchBar`, `src/sim/matching.js`): a wished partner beyond reach is refused with that reason, shown where the wish is set.
