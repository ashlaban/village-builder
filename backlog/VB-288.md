# VB-288: Distance governs the round's matching: women within reach, nearer ones preferred
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-286, VB-277
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: between
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Between villages: Ease of mixing, Over the sea; Leaving home: Marriage). Survey: backlog/VB-281.md
Goal: A village's round (VB-277) matches its own men with the unmarried women of every village, and the score knows nothing of how far apart the two live: it adds 2 for the same village, else 2 × the lesser of the two villages' ties to their culture's centre (`marriageRound`, `src/sim/lifecycle.js:245-249` at `fd74348`). So a woman 60 hours' walk away, or on another island, scores as one next door when both villages lie near the centre. VB-281 measured the marriages of the last ten years in the fixtures' histories: at `medium-ref`, of 118, 47 within the village, 17 within a day's walk (12 h), 34 further and 22 over the sea; at `small-y40`, of 70, 19, 10, 33 and 8. Kim decided on 10 November that distance governs the mixing and close villages mix easily. Make the pool the women within walking reach, and the score prefer the nearer.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The pool. A village's round offers its men the unmarried women (by today's tests) of the villages within walking reach of it: `hoursBetween(st, v, u)` finite (by land, at most `ROUTE.reach`, 67 h; VB-286). A woman of a village over the sea or beyond reach is not in the pool. The pool is built from the villages within reach and their living, not from the island's living filtered.
- [ ] The score's village term is `2 × exp(-h / 8)` for h walking hours between the two villages (`MARRIAGE.nearHours` 8, a tunable): 2 within the village, as today; 1.21 at 4 h, 0.94 at 6 h, 0.45 at 12 h, 0.10 at 24 h, 0.01 at 41 h. It replaces the term of the two villages' ties, which keep their use in the culture's drift. The scale 8 h is the tie's own (its decay is `exp(-d / 25)` in steps, about 8 h of walking at 3 tiles an hour).
- [ ] Kin pressure (`kinPressure`, as VB-277 leaves it): an unmatched pawn is pushed only when it has no allowed partner among the free pawns of the other sex within reach, but would have one under the next looser limit within reach; the plenty pull is unchanged.
- [ ] The player's wish for a partner (VB-274, if it has landed): a wished partner beyond reach is refused with that reason, shown where the wish is set.
- [ ] Behaviour changes, so report before and after (targets with residuals): the demography of VB-201 and VB-277 (populous small, 30 years, seeds 1 to 12, `tools/marriage-demography.js`): marriages a year and mean first-marriage age, each within 5%; the share of women 45 to 54 never married within 2 points; each seed's never-married count; seeds 7 and 42 reported. Marriages by the walking hours between the two villages (the same village, up to 6 h, 6 to 12, 12 to 24, 24 to 48, over 48, over the sea), against VB-281's ten-year shares above. Customs over 20 years at seeds 7 and 42 (`--report customs`), the marriage limit included, since kin pressure now counts within reach. Households with an unmarried adult over 30 at year 30. The golden file re-recorded, with the changed entries listed; `tests/counters.json` re-recorded with `roundPairs` listed. If the demography is out, say by how much and why, and do not tune here: the suitor's visit (VB-289) and the score's scale are Kim's.
- [ ] Cost (target): pairs scored a year, `VB.count.roundPairs`, at `medium-ref` and `small-y40`, before and after: fewer, since a man scores only the women within reach (Rules for cost, 2: pairs meet only among those who can meet). The `scaling.roundPairs` target (+0.85 out on `main`) and `history.roundPairs` (+2.1) reported with their residuals. Each pair's term is one table read (VB-286), no search.
