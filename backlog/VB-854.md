## Implementation notes (VB-854)
Tests only; nothing under `src/` changed. Each of the twelve scripts now runs on the small generated islands of `tests/lib/probe.js`
(`scenario`, `island`; VB-853), and moves from EXTENDED to INTEGRATION in `tests/suites.js`. What took years is constructed.
Two keep a night mode, `heirs --extended` and `hunger --extended`, which say so in their first lines.

A rule found red: `stores` failed on the run's branch before this task (its first lines asserted grain + meals unchanged by cooking,
but since VB-009 grain cooked unground gives 1 / `ECON.unground` a ration). The assertions now read the foods table: 4 grain cooked
give `4 * FOODS.grain.worth` meals, and the yearly total of cooking is `-10 * (1 - worth)`. The rule (cooking converts at the food's
worth) is what `meals` asserts too.

Decision: scenes found on the 120 by 85 or default island are found again on the small island at another seed, as VB-853 did, rather
than built on a `tinyWorld`: `offers` seed 42 (was 54: a son of the house whose bride speaks for herself) and, for its scene 11,
seed 13, three villages, men 23 and 6 and woman 17 (was seed 61, four villages, men 32 and 8, woman 24); `occasions` seed 11 (was 3; at
3 and 7 the small island's first round marries one couple, and the scene needs two).
