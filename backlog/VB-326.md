# VB-326: The village's wood is an area: each firewood trip fells a tree on a random tile of it
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Land, ecology and rights (Land and ecology: Trees per tile, The village's wood; Infield, outland and wilderness: The commons wood). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Consistency review (10 Nov)
Goal: A village cuts all its firewood on one tile, its commons wood (`v.commonsWood`; each farm's `f.woodlot` is that tile), until the tile is bare; a bare tile is meadow for good (`clearTile`, `src/sim/forest.js`), and the village moves to the next. A household fells about 11 trees a year and a tile regrows 2 to 2.7, so regrowth never meets the cutting: populous small, seed 7, lost 623 of 2,463 forest tiles in 40 years (plots included) and the median walk to the wood went from 4 to 9 steps. Kim decided on 10 November: the wood a village is allotted is larger, an area, and a pawn cuts its tree on a random tile of it. Sized to the village's hearths, the allotment's regrowth meets its cutting, and the wood thins instead of vanishing.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The allotment: `v.wood`, the village's wooded tiles for cutting, plain data in the saved state, owned by `sim/territory` (`src/owners.json`, `@state`), replacing `v.commonsWood` and `f.woodlot`; a save with the old fields loads with an allotment reckoned at load. Its size is `WOOD.perHousehold` tiles (5) for each live household of the village, at least `WOOD.least` (6) and at most `WOOD.cap` (150, the declared cap of Rules for cost 2). Five, because a household fells 11 trees a year and a tile of 60 to 80 regrows 2 to 2.7.
- [ ] Where: the nearest wooded tiles by walking steps from the village's centre within `WOOD.reach` (12 steps: 4 hours each way on meadow, so a trip of 3 hours' cutting fits the 12 working hours; today's 24 steps is 8 hours each way), first those in the village's own land, then forest no other village holds or claims (`freeFor`), never a house, field, meadow or plot tile. Reckoned at the autumn meeting's land stage (`landMeeting`, which moves the wood today), when a farm is founded in the village, and when a tile of it is cut bare or built on; never daily. The village's outland claim counts the allotment's tiles outside its land as need, wooded tiles first, as it does today for a village with no wood of its own (`src/sim/territory.js:64-65`).
- [ ] Cutting: a firewood job's site is drawn when it is posted, from the stream `rng.forest` (a new name in `SYSTEMS`, `src/sim/rng.js`): evenly among the allotment's tiles with more than a quarter of their trees, and if there is none, among those with any. `fellTree` fells on the job's site. A tile cut bare still becomes meadow (design, Cleared woodland) and leaves the allotment.
- [ ] A village with no wooded tile in reach has an empty allotment: no firewood job is posted, as today, and the log says so once a year (`kind: 'noWood'`). What that costs its households is VB-327's. Generated villages: `finishLand` (`src/sim/layout.js`) places a village only where at least `WOOD.least` wooded tiles lie within `WOOD.reach` of its centre, as `newSite` already asks a wood of a settlers' site (which reads the same two numbers after this task); the seeds whose villages move are listed.
- [ ] The page reads the allotment where it read the commons wood: the tile panel names the village a wooded tile is cut by, the Land overlay marks the allotment, and worn paths to the wood (`src/sim/paths.js:45`) follow the tiles cut.
- [ ] Invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): every tile of an allotment is wooded, in no other allotment and not in `st.used`; its length is within the cap. A test (`tests/wood.js`, in `npm test`): a village of 10 households over 30 years loses no tile of a full allotment to firewood, and its standing trees level off; with `WOOD.perHousehold` forced to 1 tiles are cut bare, as today.
- [ ] Cost named and measured (rule 9): a draw is one index into the allotment for each posted job (households × posts a day); the reckoning is one bounded search a village a year. The routes are the risk: house × allotment tile pairs replace house × one tile, up to 75 routes a farm of a 15-farm village against `ROUTE.cacheMax` 60,000; `routeSearch`, `routeEvict` and the step time are reported at `small-y40` and `medium-ref` before and after, and if evictions rise the draw is kept for a season a farm.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous and founders, small, seeds 7 and 42, 40 years): forest tiles and standing trees by year, tiles cut bare by firewood and by plots apart, the walk to the wood (median, longest), firewood hours a household, household-days with an empty pile, villages with an empty allotment. The golden file is re-recorded and its changed entries listed.
