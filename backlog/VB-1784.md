Goal: Any pawn with own hours and any head can hold aims that span years in its plan record, through one verb the default author uses too
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `sim/aims` (`@sys`): the registry of aim kinds, each declared at load by the module whose state its work changes (`holders`, `why`, `measure`, `estimate`, `season`, `weigh`, `lesser`, `ends`: aims.md 3.1), as sim/makings is; it holds no kind's rule; a kind declared by the test only.
- [ ] An aim is a year step of its holder's plan record (VB-1752): `what: {aim: {kind, for, args}}`, `since`, `by`, `waits`, `tally`, `author`, `kept`, `state`; an invariant that its `for` is its holder or a member of it, and that a pawn holding one has own hours (none of the head pair).
- [ ] The project menu's saved key `plan.aim` is renamed `plan.menu`, a commit of its own; `FORMAT` raised: a save of an older build is refused, no migration.
- [ ] The verb `aim` (`form`; `revise` its `by`, its args or its `for`; `drop`; `keep`): any pawn on its own aims, a head on its household's, never one pawn on another's own; its check the kind's `why` (position and possibility, never need) and the actor's position, never whether it is played; the default author forms no aim of a kind for a body that holds an authored one.
- [ ] `Sim.aims(st, body)`: a body's aims through the actor view.
- [ ] No behaviour changes with only the test kind: `node tools/same.js --base REF` differs only in the plan record's leaves and `plan.menu`, listed in the PR; `npm test` and `npm run verify` green.
