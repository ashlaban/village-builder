# VB-1784: The aim record and the verb aim: a pawn's and a household's plans at the year and beyond
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-1781, VB-1755, VB-1757, VB-1778
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: plans
Phase: implement
Design ref: docs/plans/four-scales/aims.md, section 3 (3.1 to 3.6) and decisions 1 to 9; design.md section 7, Aims and personal goods (An aim; Who holds one; How an aim becomes work; Progress and the estimate; At risk, waiting, dropped; Choosing an aim; The verb aim); Plans at four scales (The plan record; A step at risk, and the take-back); section 15, Decision points
Touches: new sim/aims; sim/plans; sim/projectmenu; sim/wishes (enqueue, seek, nextAsking); sim/saves; new tests/aims.js
Goal: Any pawn and any head can hold aims that span years, see them measured and estimated, and have them written into each season's steps, through one verb its default author uses too
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `sim/aims` (`@sys`): the registry of aim kinds, each declared at load by the module whose state its work changes (`holders`, `why`, `measure`, `estimate`, `season`, `weigh`, `ends`: aims.md 3.1), as sim/makings is; it holds no kind's rule. A kind declared by the test only.
- [ ] An aim is a year step of its holder's plan record (VB-1752): `what: {aim: {kind, for, args}}`, `since`, `by`, `waits`, `tally` (hours and worth this season, folded into `last` at the season's end), `author`, `kept`, `state`; an invariant that its `for` is its holder or a member of it and that a pawn holding one has own hours (none of the head pair). The project menu's `plan.aim` is renamed `plan.menu`; `FORMAT` raised.
- [ ] The verb `aim` (ops `form`, `revise`, `drop`, `keep`): any pawn on its own aims, a head on its household's; its check the kind's `why` and the actor's position, never whether it is played; the default author forms no aim of a kind for a body that holds an authored one.
- [ ] The review: a household's aims at its head's first review of a season; a pawn's at its asking day (sim/wishes' queue widened from those who seek to every adult with own hours). Each aim is measured, estimated by its holder (a centre and a range, the head's lean, the pawn's own), and is under way (its kind's `season` writes its steps, each with `why` the aim, sized to the spare: aims.md 3.3), at risk (a decision point for its author; unanswered by its next review, postponed, scaled down or dropped by its weighing under `AIM[kind].keep`; a kept aim left), waiting (`waits` named, no steps), done or failed (`ends`).
- [ ] No aim ends by a count of years (A wait is the thing waited for): a check on a constructed state that an aim whose estimate never reaches its `by` is postponed, keeps its `waits`, and is dropped only when its score falls under `keep`.
- [ ] Decision points added to VB-1757's list: an aim to form, an aim at risk, a member's aim bearing on the head's plan for it.
- [ ] `Sim.aims(st, body)`: a body's aims with their measure, the holder's estimate (lo, mid, hi) and what each waits for, through the actor view.
- [ ] Cost: about 700 pawns × 4 asking days a year and 200 households × 4 reviews, each tens of reads (under 0.1 ms a game day at the late reference); done and dropped aims leave the live list at the season's fold; a work counter in `VB.COUNTERS`.
- [ ] `npm test` and `npm run verify` green; with no kind declared but the test's, behaviour is unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base REF` differs only in the plan record's and the asking queue's leaves, listed in the PR.
