# VB-1637: The byre: an outbuilding that keeps the animals' condition through the winter
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: backlog/VB-1631.md; src/sim/ladder.js (OUTBUILDINGS); src/sim/byre.js; src/sim/herds.js; src/sim/rules.js (HERD.byre); src/sim/upgrade.js; src/sim/aims.js (barn, for the like of a making)
Goal: A farm's animals winter better under a roof, and the roof is built.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Read first, and say in the notes, how the stall winter works today (sim/byre: the places `HERD.byre`, the lean, the autumn count, what a farm with a booth or a camp does with its animals): the byre is today assumed at every farm.
- [ ] The byre becomes a building: `farmstead.byre` (an owner, `@state`, an invariant, saved; every farm that stands at seeding in every scenario has one, so that founders, heirs and populous start as today, and an older save reads as having one), an entry `byre` in `OUTBUILDINGS` (timber about 20 loads and 60 hours to begin with, tunables; places `HERD.byre`). A farm without a byre (a hand start; a settler's new farm, if the sim's rule for those farms makes none: say what you found and decided) winters its animals in the open: the stall winter's lean comes faster by a named factor (a tunable), with the same rules otherwise.
- [ ] Built by the plan: `Sim.plan(st, 'add', {outbuilding: houseTile, what: 'byre'})` (if the chain `outbuilding` does not exist yet when you merge, add it so that the barn's task, built beside this one, can share it), the timber at home first; `Sim.farmView().outbuildings` lists it with `built`, `ok`, `why`, `planned`.
- [ ] One rule for every household (Kim): an unplayed head whose farm has animals and no byre builds one by its own rule before its first winter when it has the hands, after its cottage.
- [ ] Report before and after, founders seed 7 on the small map for 10 years (the animals through each winter, the hay, the population); `node tools/same.js` is expected to differ only where a farm has no byre: say where. A `tuning` note for what is out.
- [ ] Tests on small constructed states; a save and a load go on the same; `npm run verify` passes; baselines do not rise.
