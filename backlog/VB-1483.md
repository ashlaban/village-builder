# VB-1483: The growing year by band and height: a field's thaw and first frost from its band's curve and the lapse over the land
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: roads
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Climate and travel beyond walking (Climate: The year's curve); docs/plans/VB-190.md 5.1; backlog/VB-807.md
Goal: A field in the north of the map, or on high ground, thaws later and frosts earlier than one in the south by the sea, by its band's curve and the lapse.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `thawDay` and `frostDay` (sim/weather) take a field's band (`sim/climate bandOfRow`) and its height: the shift of the band's curve
  from the middle world's 59 degrees (`growDays`), less `CLIMATE.lapse` degrees at the top of the land's height (`w.hp`), rounded to whole
  days; the sowing window reads its field's, and the frost takes each band's crops on its own day (one entry a band, not a pass a day).
- [ ] Measured: the middle world's bands move the thaw by 0.39 and -0.42 days on the small map (rounding to none) and 0.58 and -0.66 on
  the medium (a day): report populous small seeds 7 and 42, 30 years, and populous medium seed 7, 10 years, before and after; reference
  numbers may move on medium, which is why VB-807 left this out.
- [ ] `tests/latitude.js` gains a field in the north band and one on high ground; `npm run verify`.
