# VB-114: Generated villages start with their worn paths
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Settlements and look (Generated villages: "Worn in: after the farms stand, the generator walks a few simulated years of daily trips (fields, well, wood, the neighbours) so the village starts with its paths and lanes"; Decided, 30 Oct). Kim, 30 Oct 2026: paths are part of world generation.
Goal: After VB-094 a populous or heirs village starts on clean grass and needs years to show its lanes, while it is meant to have stood for generations. The generator should wear the paths in, so a generated village looks like one that has grown in play.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `wearIn(w, villages, farmsteads, years)` in `src/sim/paths.js`, called by the settlement generator for every scenario but founders (who arrive on unmade land): season by season for `years` (3), each farm's daily trips (to each field, the green and well, its woodlot, and the other houses of its village) are routed over the current wear, with the speed-up of VB-110, and add their crossings; wear fades as in play between seasons, and stages are recomputed, so routes merge onto paths as they form.
- [ ] The trips per day are set from a grown run: populous medium at seed 7, the tiles per stage after 5 years of play from a worn-in start are within 25% of the worn-in counts (reported in the PR).
- [ ] It costs under 300 ms for populous medium at seed 7 (reported).
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
