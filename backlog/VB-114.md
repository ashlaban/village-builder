# VB-114: Generated villages start with their worn paths
Status: done (2026-10-30)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Settlements and look (Generated villages: "Worn in: after the farms stand, the generator walks a few simulated years of daily trips (fields, well, wood, the neighbours) so the village starts with its paths and lanes"; Decided, 30 Oct). Kim, 30 Oct 2026: paths are part of world generation.
Goal: After VB-094 a populous or heirs village starts on clean grass and needs years to show its lanes, while it is meant to have stood for generations. The generator should wear the paths in, so a generated village looks like one that has grown in play.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `wearIn(w, villages, farmsteads, years)` in `src/sim/paths.js`, called by the settlement generator for every scenario but founders (who arrive on unmade land): season by season for `years` (3), each farm's daily trips (to each field, the green and well, its woodlot, and the other houses of its village) are routed over the current wear, with the speed-up of VB-110, and add their crossings; wear fades as in play between seasons, and stages are recomputed, so routes merge onto paths as they form.
- [x] The trips per day are set from a grown run: populous medium at seed 7, the tiles per stage after 5 years of play from a worn-in start are within 25% of the worn-in counts (reported in the PR). Met for paths only; see the notes.
- [x] It costs under 300 ms for populous medium at seed 7 (reported).
- [x] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
- Done 2026-10-30. `wearIn` runs in `settlementGenerator().finish()` after `finishLand` unless the generator is bare (the founders); trips (`PATHS.trips`, per farm and day, there and back): 0.25 to each field, 1 to the well and 0.5 on a stroll to the green (where the village has one), 0.45 to the woodlot. Between seasons the wear fades by twelve days of play and stages are set again; routes are searched with the speed-up of VB-110, so they merge.
- Populous medium, trodden/path/lane: seed 7 worn in 133/35/2, after 5 years of play from it 173/32/1, grown 5 years from clean ground 172/26/0; seed 42 worn in 133/43/1, after 5 years 183/56/0, grown 193/42/0; heirs seed 7 9/1/0, 8/1/0 and 9/0/0. Paths come within 25% (seed 7 −9%, seed 42 +30% against the worn-in count, +33% against grown); trodden ground does not: play adds 30 to 38% more. Of the 53 tiles newly worn after 5 years at seed 7, 26 lie on or next to plots cleared in play and 17 between yards and fields (walks from field to field and from where a pawn last worked); higher trip rates only turn trodden ground into paths (field 0.35: 113/56/3), and walks between fields and the green or wood did the same (119/56/3), so the rates stay as set.
- Cost: `wearIn` takes 63 to 79 ms for populous medium (under 300).
- Golden: the heirs entries (their start is worn in); founders entries are unchanged.
