Goal: A claim on a farm or a plot is a pawn's offer to the village's alderman (VB-314), due at the round, answered by the custom in force.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The ask (`land`, due at the round): a landless adult (or a betrothed pair, VB-825) whose best open rung it is asks the alderman (VB-314) of the farm's village for a named farm, or of its own village for a plot. Of the farms it may claim (as built: claimable, within `CLAIMS.farmHours`, and allowed by that village's claims custom) it names the one with the most fields, then the best kept. With no plot site at home it may ask the alderman of a small village within reach (`SETTLE.smallFarms`, `SETTLE.join`, kin there first) for a plot there: this replaces a party joining a small village unasked (`smallVillage`, sim/settle).
- [ ] The answer (a no names the line, in the log; the claimants' order and the plot's cap are the custom's, as before; `startPlots`' loop is kept as the fallback for those who rank new land above a plot and those not yet asked, and `claimFarms`' for the round's own, so not replaced): the alderman answers by the custom in force and adds no judgement of his own. Among several asking for one farm the custom's order decides exactly as `claimants` does today (kin of the old family while the birthright stands, villagers, anyone, the meeting's support; couples before singles; then the oldest); a plot goes as `startPlots` gives it today. A no names the line of the custom that stood in the way, in the log and in the asker's record; no memory is booked against the alderman. `claimFarms`' and `startPlots`' loops over `st.demand` are replaced by the asks due.
- [ ] Numbers (only populous small seeds 3, 7 at 30 years, and 7 at 14, measured; the rest owed, see the note) before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7 and 42: claims and plots a decade, the wait in rounds before land, asks refused by the custom's line, small villages joined; growth by principle 2. With no played pawn the claims and plots of before are expected, but for the small villages now asked.
