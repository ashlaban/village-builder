# VB-1124: Page: a game info panel: every action, where it applies, what it needs and what it gives
Status: done (2026-11-23)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: backlog/VB-1123.md (Kim's fifth answer of 18 Nov); src/sim/kinds.js, src/sim/commands.js, the goods registry; AGENTS.md, Deciding without Kim, 15 and 16
Goal: A player can look up what can be done in the game: each action, where it applies, what must be there first, and what it produces.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A panel, opened from the main menu and from plan mode (a link in a tile's menu and in the legend), lists every action in the game: its icon and name, where it applies (which tiles, the house, the shore), its preconditions (a tool, a material in store, a season's window, a facility), what it takes (days of work by a hand of middling skill, goods) and what it produces.
- [ ] It is read from what the simulation declares (the job kinds of `sim/kinds`, the verbs of `sim/commands`, the goods registry, the crops), not written by hand beside them: an action added to the game appears in it with no change to the page. Where a registry does not yet say where an action applies or what it needs, the task adds that to the declaration, for the rule and the panel to read alike, and says which it added. **Partly (VB-1537):** the job kinds, goods registry, FOODS and CROPS are read, and every kind now declares `at`, `needs` and `gives` (of `PLACES` and `THINGS` in `sim/kinds`, checked at load); the verbs of `sim/commands` carry no name to list, and no rule reads the declarations yet.
- [x] An action's row leads to the things it names (a good, a crop, a facility), each with what makes it and what uses it, so that a chain can be followed (clay, dug at a bank, to pots, fired at the house).
- [x] What a pawn must know or guess is not given away: the panel says what an action is and needs, as the rules have it; what it will bring on this farm this year is an estimate, and stays in plan mode with whose it is (principle 14).
- [x] Icons before words (principle 15): an action, a good and a crop are shown by the icons they have everywhere; numbers sit behind the meter or in a row's details.
- [x] The page only reads; `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`. A functional test in the page's tests; a screenshot in the PR.
