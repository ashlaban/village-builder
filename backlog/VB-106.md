# VB-106: Neighbours help with each other's work, and it builds ties
Status: blocked
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: work
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Jobs, owners and roles: Who may take a job). Kim, 30 Oct 2026 (jobs review): help between households is allowed, and it builds relationships.
Goal: A household's jobs can only be taken by its own members, so one farm's field waits while the next farm's adults stand idle. Neighbours helped each other at harvest and with heavy work, and the help bound households together.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] An adult (14 or more) may take a job of another household in their own village, of the kinds plow, sow, weed, reap, haul, thresh, clear, break and mend (not cook, water or wood), once the job has waited 2 hours.
- [ ] A helper bids only when it has no bid on a job of its own household or village in the same round; the helper's bid is multiplied by 0.5, plus 1 if the helper is near kin of the household's holder (`kinNear`) and plus the helper's opinion of the holder (VB-060).
- [ ] Each completed help job raises the tie between the helper and each adult of the helped household by 0.2, and their opinion of each other by 0.05 × (1 − opinion), with the VB-060 helpers. A life event for the helper names the farm helped; no log line.
- [ ] Help is a gift: nothing is owed, no food or grain moves.
- [ ] Matching stays within the village: with VB-071 done, a pawn bids on its household's, its village's and its village's other households' jobs only.
- [ ] A test: two households in one village, one with 3 idle adults, the other with an unhoed field waiting 3 hours; a helper hoes it, and their tie and opinion rise.
- [ ] Populous medium at seed 7, 3 years: help jobs per year, the share of field work done by helpers, `--report idle` (VB-101) and hungry household-days, before and after (reported, not asserted).
