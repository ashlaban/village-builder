Goal: A pawn with the aptitude, the material and buyers in sight forms the aim of living by a craft and keeps at it in its own hours, bound by its buyers, and its skill and its pots show it
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The kind `craft` (declared by sim/pottery for pottery): its `why` a pawn with own hours, not of the head pair; formed on the pawn's aim day by the weighing of aims.md 3.5, its demand read from a count made once a district, good and season at the gathering's review (a work counter); its measure the worth got from others in the last year (`pawn.trade`) against its need. A standing state: at `AIM.craft.lives` (0.5) its card and its wish change (VB-1789), its steps go on; it ends only at founding (VB-1789) or dropped by its weighing.
- [ ] The target for exchange, one function (VB-1797 reuses it): pieces placed in the last year (`pawn.trade.placed`) plus the known shortfall of the district's buyers, less what it holds, at most what its own hours make; no firing beyond it.
- [ ] Its season steps: clay dug, wood fetched and firings, taken up across days (VB-1783); the own-hours judgement (VB-1762) weighs the aim's lack beside giving, a pastime, courting and rest; hours booked `kept` and tallied on the aim. Its estimate is the pawn's own, by `grownBy` and the pieces it placed.
- [ ] A check on a constructed district (aims.md 8.1), reachable by its numbers: a second son of aptitude 0.6 with clay in reach and six clayless households places about 16 pots a year and reaches "able" (0.25, about 160 hours) within five game years, his pots then mostly sound; with two clayless households he places about 5, is not "able" in five years, and his aim waits for `buyers` (the card says so); an idle brother forms none; under `memberMaking: household` neither does.
- [ ] Reported from the gate's six runs: craft aims formed, standing, at `lives`, waiting and on what; pots made in own hours by grade; own hours put to aims; hours given by those with an aim against those without. Ten-year counts are small: they are judged at the wrap-up's 30 years.
- [ ] What "living by a craft" is, and the pot's worth: Kim's question 5 (`QUESTIONS.md`, block "VB-1472, VB-1785, VB-1786, VB-1792, VB-1794") is built with its proposed answer standing; if Kim answers otherwise, the change is said in this task's notes before it starts, or opened as a task if it has landed.
- [ ] Cost: about 700 weighings a season inside VB-1793's review, reading the district's count; the makings are tasks like any.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
