Goal: A pawn with the aptitude, the material and buyers in sight forms the aim of living by a craft and spends its own hours on it, and its skill and its pots show it
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The kind `craft` (declared by the pots' module for pottery; another craft whose making exists may declare it later): for a pawn with own hours, not of the head pair and not the heir to the home farm; formed at its asking day on the weighing of aims.md 3.5 (`AIM.craft`: aptitude, level, own hours by the levy in force, material in reach by its rights, the share the custom leaves it, households within its gathering's reach short of the good with no maker, ambition); its measure the worth got from others in the last year (`pawn.trade`, VB-1787; 0 until it lands) against its need, done at `AIM.craft.lives` (0.5).
- [ ] Its season steps: clay dug, wood fetched and firings, pots into the pawn's store up to its own target, as tasks the pawn owns (VB-1783); the own-hours judgement (VB-1762) weighs the aim's lack beside giving, a pastime, courting and rest; the hours booked `kept` and tallied on the aim.
- [ ] Its estimate is the pawn's own, by the reckoning of the doing: `grownBy` of sim/skills over the hours it has been putting in, the odds of its grades at that level, the worth a piece fetched it last year.
- [ ] A check on a constructed household over six game years (aims.md 7.1): a second son of aptitude 0.6 with clay in reach and two farms of his village short of pots forms the aim and reaches "able" in about four years at the levy's values, his pots then mostly sound; an idle brother in the same position forms none; under the custom `household` neither does.
- [ ] Reported (populous small, seeds 7, 11 and 42, 10 years): craft aims formed, held, dropped and waiting, and on what; pots made in own hours by grade; own hours put to aims as a share of own hours; hours given by those with an aim against those without, beside Q9's thirds.
- [ ] Cost: about 700 weighings a season inside VB-1784's review; the makings are tasks like any.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
