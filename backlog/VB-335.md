# VB-335: Sheaves come home by the load: 30 rations on the back, 90 on a cart
Status: needs-review
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (Goods and the farming year: Field work, Tilling power). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Field work (11 Nov)
Goal: A field's stooks come home in one job of 0.7 hours and one carry, whatever the field bore (`KINDS.haul`, `deliver` puts all of `fd.held` in the barn): up to 100 rations in one armful, where the design's load is 30 rations on the back and 90 on a cart with two oxen (Exchange, Deals between strangers; Between villages, Kin help). Kim, 11 Nov: yes, the harvest is carried by the load.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A haul job carries one load: `TRAVEL.load` rations on the back (30; VB-291's tunable, defined here if this lands first), taken from `fd.held`, which falls by it; the field stays in stooks, and its haul job stays open, until `fd.held` is 0. The kind keeps `hands: Infinity`: each hand that joins carries its own load, so a household of three brings a field in in one or two trips each.
- [x] A household with an ox team (`hasTeam`, `src/sim/herds.js`) carts: its first hand on the job carries `TRAVEL.cart` (90) a trip, as the first hand at the hoe drives the team (`withOxen`); the team is in one place at a time, the same rule. The page draws the cart if it has the art, else the load as today.
- [x] `Stores.sheaf` takes the load, not the field: `hh.unthreshed` holds one entry a field as today (the loads of one field add to its entry), so threshing and the ledgers are unchanged. The harvest log line is written when the last load of the farm's last field is in.
- [x] Hay is the same question and is not changed here: `KINDS.hay` carries a meadow's 30 units in one job of 4 hours; say in the task's report what a load of hay should be, as a question for Kim.
- [x] Cost (rule 9): three or four jobs' walks a field in place of one, in the days after reaping; `walkStep`, `routeSearch` and the step time in autumn at `small-y40` before and after.
- [x] Numbers before and after (populous and founders, small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years; `tools/balance.js` at seeds 3, 7, 11, 42): pawn-hours by field job and their share of the hands' year, the days from ripe to reaped, to the barn and to threshed, crops lost to frost, fields left unsown, hungry household-days and deaths, idle hours; whether households, not only averages, are worse off, and which (the short-handed, the lone holder). The golden file and the work counters are re-recorded and their changed entries listed; the bands of `tests/lib/bands.js` reported, none re-set.

## Implementation notes
- 12 Nov 2026. `TRAVEL = {load: 30, cart: 90}` in `sim/rules.js` (VB-291 had not defined it). `KINDS.haul` gets `load(st, a, j, cap)` and `again(j)`: in
  `stepWork` (sim/agents), when a hand's share of the 0.7 hours is done, it takes a load from the field (`fields.carried`: `fd.held` falls, the
  field is `hauled`, stubble, when it reaches 0) and walks it to the barn; the job's progress goes back to 0 and the other hands go on loading, so
  each hand that joins carries its own load. The cart: `cartFree` (agents): the household has a team (`hasTeam`) and no member has it out, at the
  plough (`withOxen`) or on the road with more than `TRAVEL.load`; then the load is `TRAVEL.cart`. A carrier stays among `j.claims` (the invariant),
  and `freeHands` leaves a hand on the road alone. `dropJob` drops only the job it was given (it deleted a re-posted job of the same key).
- `Stores.sheaf(st, hh, n, seq = -1)` returns the entry's number; `fd.bin` holds it, `hh.threshed` (lazy, set by `thresh`, so no seed entry moves)
  counts entries threshed, so a field's loads add to one entry of `hh.unthreshed` and threshing is unchanged. The ledger's first 'harvest' entry of
  a field carries `first: true`, for `tools/balance.js` to subtract the seed once a field (it subtracted it from each entry, which read as no grain).
- The harvest log line is written by the deposit that finds no field ripe or in stooks and no other hand on the road with a load. A pawn who dies on
  the road loses its load (`fd.held` was taken at the pickup); the page draws the load as before (no cart art; `src/page/` unchanged).
