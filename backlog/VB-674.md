# VB-674: Teleport: herds handed on, given and paid in are led, not spliced
Status: done (2026-11-22)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: physical
Phase: implement
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (the hard rule)
Goal: An animal that changes household changes owner where it stands and is led to its new byre by someone who walks; its hay is carted; a herd with no household to go to is not deleted.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Every path that moves an animal between households (sim/herds passHerds, startHerd, payInAnimals; their callers in sim/cottage and sim/heirs) changes the owner at once and the place by a `lead` journey, as VB-390's cow; the hay that goes with it is a load carried or carted.
- [x] A herd whose household ends with nobody to have it stays on its farm until fetched or lost by a process named in the record (with VB-650).
- [x] A test on a constructed state for each path; invariants for an animal owned by one household and standing at another.
- [x] Numbers before and after (`tools/balance.js`), populous small seeds 7 and 42 and founders small seed 7, 6 years.
- 16 Nov 2026: Found by the consistency review of 16 November 2026 (code only); the lines named are leads, to be confirmed before work starts.
- 22 Nov 2026 (done): the moves are a new module, `sim/droving` (passHerds, startHerd, payInAnimals out of sim/herds, its width 28 to 25). A herd standing away is a herd record owned by its new household, `herd.at` its farm, on `st.herdsAway`, listed by no household: nobody milks, feeds or works it until it is home (the kind `drove`, any season: its hay is fetched apart). Hay goes as a `hay` load lying in the old barn (sim/lying, fetchHay, rots at the frost as other lying loads). A herd with nobody: stays at its farm until the autumn frost, then taken in by the farm's household or lost to the winter (`herdLed`, `herdDeath`). Exchange's cow (VB-390) is led already: `payInAnimals(..., led)` puts her in the herd at once.
- Left: the hay of a household that ends with nobody still goes with its barn (VB-650, in VB-1175); wool sheared on summer day 1 from a flock standing away goes to its owner's store (teleport, noted). Numbers: populous small 7, 42 and founders small 7, 6 years: hungry household-years 1.1% to 0.84%, food from animals 9.9% to 11.0% (populous; founders 15.0% to 13.7%), within the run's noise; herds stood away 21, 27 and 4 times, median 1 to 4 days, at most 34.
