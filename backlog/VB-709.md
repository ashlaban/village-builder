# VB-709: The pasture feels the herds: a crowded pasture lowers the milk
Status: done (2026-11-19)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Goods, production and animals: Animals, Customs of the animals; docs/plans/VB-141.md (section 4 and section 11, point 1); design.md, Grazing at low cost
Goal: Overgrazing costs something (Kim, 16 Nov): a crowded pasture lowers the cows' milk; forest spread is unchanged and the pasture does not wear to heath. It is the consequence every grazing custom rests on.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A cow's milk of the day (`sim/byre`, the milk of VB-135) is multiplied by `f = clamp(1 - bite * (pressure - downAt) / downAt, floor, 1)`, pressure being `grazeOf(st, tile)` of the tile her herd grazed that day (the closed form of `w.graze`); ewes' milk likewise. Tunables in `HERD.herding` (`src/sim/rules.js`), each with its meaning in the comment: `bite` 0.3 (the share of milk lost at twice a herd's week of pressure), `floor` 0.5 (no lower than an unherded herd's milk, `HERD.herding.milk`); `downAt` (300) is the existing one. An unherded herd keeps its half and takes no bite (it did not graze the pasture).
- [x] No change where a pasture is not crowded: a check on a small constructed state (`tests/herds.js`, or a new script with its line in `tests/suites.js`, on `tests/tiny.js`): one herd alone on its pasture for `keepDays` gives the milk it gives today; two and four herds forced onto one tile give less, never under `floor`; an unherded herd is unchanged.
- [x] `node tools/sim.js --report herds` shows, a household, the pressure of the tile grazed and the day's factor; the yearly totals show the milk lost to the bite.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, in the task file and the PR: milk a cow in milk a year, total milk, the share of herd-days with `f < 1` and the mean `f` on those days, hungry days a household (the distribution, not only the mean), deaths by hunger, at populous small seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 (15 years) and founders tiny seed 7; and whether any household is worse off, and by how much.
- [x] The parameterisation tried in at most five short runs (seeds and map named; the values tried and what each gave, in the task file): `bite` at 0.15, 0.3 and 0.6 at the least. The value kept is the smallest at which a crowded village's herds lose over 5% of their summer milk while a lone farm loses none. If no value shows it, milk being a small share of food, report that and leave it as a row of `docs/tuning.md` (parameter `HERD.herding.bite`); do not enlarge the mechanism (wear to heath is Kim's choice 2(b), not taken).
- [x] `tests/counters.js` unchanged in structure; the targets of `node tests/run.js verify --verbose` reported as moved or not.
- [x] Cost (Rules for cost 9): herds with a cow or ewe in milk (about 200 at `medium-ref`) × once a day × one read of `w.graze` and one division, inside the existing milk loop; no new loop, no new state, no counter. Stated in the PR; times are left to the nightly bench.
- [x] No new state: `f` is derived each day. The `@sys` line of `sim/byre` and `docs/architecture/modules.md` are kept current if the call to `sim/grazing`'s pressure is new.

Noted by VB-141, 16 November 2026.
