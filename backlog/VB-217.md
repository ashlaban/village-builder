# VB-217: Goodwill booked between the two holders
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: kin-help
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, goodwill and obligations (Goodwill, Booking: Decided 3 Nov; The gift: Unit and decay, Who books, Allowance, Agreed 8 Nov); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, section 4.4 (Relations: goodwill); `docs/roadmap.md`, section 5.3 (rules 1 to 3); VB-182's plan.
Goal: Goodwill becomes state: a kin gift books its customary worth between the two holders, and the balance decays in closed form, is capped, pruned, and half of it passes at a handover. Nothing reads it yet but the panel (VB-218) and allowance's `goodwill` term, which is 0 until slice 4 (VB-220), so the golden file changes for the booking only.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `sim/relations` owns `pawn.goodwill` (`src/owners.json`, an `@state` line): a Map of the other pawn's id to `{v, t, since, why}`, created at a pawn's first booking, so pawns that never dealt are unchanged. `v > 0` means the other owes this pawn `v` rations as of game hour `t`; the other pawn holds the mirror, `-v` with the same `t`, `since` (when the balance opened, or last changed sign) and `why` (the last booking's reason, `help` here). Tunables `GOODWILL` in `src/sim/rules.js`: `halfLife` 4 years, `forget` 1 ration, `cap` 32, `limitDays` 30, `acts` false.
- [x] Operations in `sim/relations`, on `Sim` where the page reads them: `balance(st, a, b)` (closed form, `v * 0.5 ** ((st.t - t) / halfLife)`, no write), `standing(st, a, b)` (`since`, or null), `book(st, creditor, debtor, rations, why)`, `transfer(st, from, to, share, why)`, and `goodwillOf(st, id)` for the panel (`[{other, balance, since, t, why}]`, largest first). Booking a new counterpart when a pawn has `cap` drops its smallest balance, on both sides.
- [x] `sim/exchange`'s `settle` books a gift between two holders: the giver is owed its worth (grain 1 a ration). The village store, its filling and a household without a holder book nothing (Who books).
- [x] Balances under `forget` are dropped at the seasonal pass, `fadeRelations` (`relations.js:114`), on both sides; a new work counter `goodwillWalk` in `VB.COUNTERS` counts the balances it visits.
- [x] Handovers (`sim/heirs`): at a retirement and at a holder's death (`transfer` with reason `retirement` or `death`, and a widow's `succession`), half of the old holder's balances pass to the new holder, merged with any it has with the same counterpart; a balance with the new holder itself is dropped by the share that would pass to it. Other handovers (married away, a claim) pass nothing. A pawn's death ends its other balances on both sides. In `die` (`src/sim/lifecycle.js:31-37`), `forget` runs before `succession`: pass the share first.
- [x] Allowance's `goodwill` term declared by `sim/relations`: while `GOODWILL.acts` is false it is 0 and its label says it comes with slice 4; when true, what the giver owes the receiver plus, less what the receiver owes beyond `limitDays` of its household's need (VB-220 switches it on).
- [x] Invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`, `checkState`): each balance has its mirror; no pawn has more than `cap`; `v` finite and non-zero, `since <= t <= st.t`; no balance with a dead pawn or with oneself. Saves keep it (`tests/saves.js`, `tests/plaindata.js`).
- [x] A test, in `tests/exchange.js` or a new `tests/goodwill.js` in `npm test`: a kin gift books both sides; the balance halves in 4 years; a reverse gift nets against it and resets `since` when it changes sign; the 33rd counterpart drops the smallest; a retirement passes half; a death ends the rest.
- [x] The golden file re-recorded, and the PR lists the changed entries: `node tests/golden.js --dump` before and after differs only in `goodwill` fields. `tools/sim.js` (founders 7 small, 10 years; populous 7 small, 8 years) prints the same yearly lines; `tools/balance.js` gives the same numbers.
- [ ] Numbers in the PR and here: balances at populous 7 small after 8 years (pairs, counterparts per pawn, largest, median), and after one year from `small-y40`; the save's JSON and gzip size before and after at both.
- [ ] Cost line: about 500 bookings a year at `medium-y60` (486 holder gifts measured), each at most `cap` steps, and the seasonal pass over a few balances a living pawn: under 0.01 ms a game day; under 50 KB more JSON in the save at `medium-y60`. Measured on `small-y40` (`--report perf`) and reported.
- [ ] Impact note in the PR (a new state field): `sim/relations` writes `pawn.goodwill`; `sim/exchange` and `sim/heirs` call its operations; nothing in the simulation reads a balance while `acts` is false. `npm run verify` passes.
Notes:

## Implementation notes (8 Nov 2026)

- Commits: `cc2a919` (the balances, booking by a kin gift, the seasonal pass), `2aff406` (handovers, deaths, allowance's goodwill term,
  invariants, `tests/goodwill.js`), `e7c7ca5` (the golden file), `bea1b10` (the owner entry).
- Names: `goodwillSince` for the interface's `standing` (`sim/relations` already has `standing`, kinship) and `passGoodwill` for its
  `transfer` (`sim/heirs` has a `transfer`). `addGoodwill(st, a, b, x, why, opened)` is the signed form under `book`.
- The `Sim` API is left alone, so that the golden file's `apiKeys` hash stays as it was; VB-218 adds the page's reads (its file says so).
- Allowance's `goodwill` term is relations' quantity but is contributed by `sim/exchange`, with source `sim/relations`, because its limit
  needs the receiving household's need (`dailyNeed`, in `sim/hunger`, which loads after `sim/relations`); `sim/deaths` contributes
  hunger's terms the same way. The terms are summed as slack, goodwill, reserve; the goodwill term is an exact 0 while `GOODWILL.acts` is
  false, so the sum is today's to the bit (`tests/exchange.js` still finds it equal to `surplus`).
- A balance passed at a handover keeps its age (`since`) when it opens a new pair for the new holder; merged into an existing one it
  keeps that one's age unless the sign changes. Its `why` is `handover`.
- In `killPawn` the balances end after `succession`, so a dead holder's share passes first; `forget` (memories) is unchanged.
- `tests/counters.js`' fixture has no kin between its households, so it books one balance by hand to exercise `goodwillWalk`.
  Re-recording `tests/counters.json` showed that `ruleValue` had risen by one read in its 900 pawn-days with VB-216 (the yearly filling's
  allowance check), inside the test's 10% tolerance; VB-216's note is corrected.
