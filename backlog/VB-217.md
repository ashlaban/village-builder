# VB-217: Goodwill booked between the two holders
Status: done (2026-11-08)
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: kin-help
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, goodwill and obligations (Goodwill, Booking: Decided 3 Nov; The gift: Unit and decay, Who books, Allowance, Agreed 8 Nov); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, section 4.4 (Relations: goodwill); `docs/roadmap.md`, section 5.3 (rules 1 to 3); VB-182's plan.
Goal: Goodwill becomes state: a kin gift books its customary worth between the two holders, and the balance decays in closed form, is capped, pruned, and half of it passes at a handover. Nothing reads it yet but the panel (VB-218) and allowance's `goodwill` term, which is 0 until slice 4 (VB-220), so the golden file changes for the booking only.
Acceptance criteria (revised 8 Nov 2026 with Kim's decisions while building it: `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-217"):
- [x] `sim/relations` owns `pawn.goodwill` (`src/owners.json`, an `@state` line), made at a pawn's first booking, so pawns that never dealt
  are unchanged, and gone at its death.
- [x] One decaying number a pair (Goodwill is one decaying number: Decided 8 Nov): a Map of the other pawn's id to `{v, t}`, `v > 0`
  meaning the other owes this pawn, read as `v * 0.5 ** ((st.t - t) / halfLife)` with `halfLife` in years (`GOODWILL.halfLife`, 4,
  tunable from 2 to 20); the other pawn holds `{v: -v, t}`. No step decays it (Kim: decay when read), so no loop walks the balances.
- [x] Forgetting: a balance that has decayed under `GOODWILL.forget` (1 ration) reads as 0, and is dropped when either of its pawns next
  books, when a pawn reaches the cap of 32 (the smallest goes to make room), or at a death. Work at each is at most the two pawns' maps,
  bounded by the cap, so no work counter is needed (`AGENTS.md`: counters are for loops over growing collections); `goodwillWalk` goes.
- [x] Operations: `balance`, `book`, `addGoodwill` (signed), `passGoodwill`, `endGoodwill`, `goodwillOf` (`[{other, balance}]`, for the panel,
  VB-218 adds it to `Sim`).
- [x] `sim/exchange`'s `settleGift` books a gift between two holders: the giver is owed its worth (grain 1 a ration). The village store, its
  filling and a household without a holder book nothing (Who books).
- [x] Handovers (`sim/heirs`): at a retirement and at a holder's death (`transfer` with reason `retirement` or `death`, and a widow's
  `succession`), half of the old holder's balances pass to the new holder; other handovers pass nothing. In `killPawn` the balances end
  after `succession`, so the share passes first.
- [x] Allowance's `goodwill` term, contributed by `sim/exchange` for `sim/relations`, 0 while `GOODWILL.acts` is false (VB-220 switches it on).
- [x] Invariants (`checkState`): each balance is mirrored exactly (`-v`, the same `t`); none with the dead or oneself; `v` finite and
  non-zero; `t <= st.t`; at most `cap`. Saves keep them.
- [x] `tests/goodwill.js` (in `npm test`): booking, decay halving in 2, 4 and 20 years and a loss proportional to size, netting, the cap,
  forgetting when touched, a save, a retirement and a death.
- [x] The golden file re-recorded: against `00a7229`, the dumps differ only by added `goodwill` lines. `tools/sim.js` (founders 7 small,
  10 years; populous 7 small, 8 years) prints the same yearly lines; `tools/balance.js` the same numbers.
- [x] `tests/history.js` passes with no new exception in `tests/cost-baseline.json`.
- [x] Numbers here and in the PR: balances and save size at populous 7 small, years 8 and 40.
- [x] Impact note in the PR (a new state field): `sim/relations` writes `pawn.goodwill`; `sim/exchange` and `sim/heirs` call its operations;
  nothing in the simulation reads a balance while `GOODWILL.acts` is false. `npm run verify` passes.
Notes:
- 8 Nov 2026 (Kim, while building it; `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-217"): goodwill is one decaying number a pair, decaying toward zero
  in proportion to its size; dealings are for stories. Built first as `{v, t, since, why}` read in closed form with a seasonal pass to
  forget, then as one number decayed by a seasonal step (`287768a`); that step failed `tests/history.js` (6 times the work per living
  pawn-day at `small-y40` as at `small-y5`, limit 1.25, though 179 visits in 30 days), so Kim chose decay when read, `{v, t}`, with no
  step. Help in hunger becomes a seasonal ask (VB-222), and the save line is restated (the Flag below).
- Flag (8 Nov 2026): the plan's save line, under 50 KB more JSON at `medium-y60`, was counted from one year's pairs, but balances
  accumulate: a typical one takes about 18 years to fall under a ration at a 4-year half-life. Measured at populous 7 small, year 40:
  456 pairs, 90 KB of JSON (16 KB gzipped alone), about 0.25% of the save; `medium-y60`, with three times the gifts, would hold about
  270 KB, 0.2% of its 136 MB. Kim chose to restate the line, not the design (8 Nov): under 0.5% of the late save, about 300 KB at
  `medium-y60`, measured on fresh fixtures by the wrap-up (VB-219). The roadmap's line (section 3.1) and VB-219's are restated with it.
