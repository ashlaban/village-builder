# VB-132: Hay meadows, made and placed
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: animals
Phase: implement
Design ref: Animals; docs/review-animals.md (review of 31 October 2026); answers in QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md under "VB-047: animals review".
Goal: A hay meadow is built land that has to be found, claimed and cleared before it yields hay (Decided). Households with animals need meadows for their winter hay; generated villages need theirs at the start.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `f.meadows[]` (`@state` documented): `{id, tile, stage 'grass'|'mown'|'aftermath', fert, raked, mown (day), held}`; a meadow tile is owned by the household, in `st.used`, and `w.dev` is set (paths do not wear it, the settlement layer draws it).
- [ ] Site value (`meadowFert`): meadow 1, marsh 0.8, heath 0.6; × 1.3 when the tile touches a river or open water (wet banks and shore meadows).
- [ ] Choosing a tile: open meadow, marsh or heath within 6 walking steps of the house, not a river or water tile, unowned and unused, not next to a house or green; best by site value − 0.06 a step − 0.3 × stones / 255, + 0.3 next to one of the farm's meadows, − 0.5 next to one of its fields (room for the field block).
- [ ] A household with animals and enough fields (where fields are made, `st.makeFields`) starts a meadow project when its meadows' expected hay (30 × site value each) is below 1.2 times its winter need: claim, then clear for 4 h + 10 h × stones / 255, which takes the worst stones (down to 80 of 255) into the household's stone; no breaking. One land project at a time, fields first.
- [ ] Populous and heirs: each farm with animals gets meadows for 1.2 times its winter need at the start (at most 4), placed by the same choice; founders start with none.
- [ ] Page: a meadow is drawn as tall flowering grass; the tile card names a meadow and its holder.
- [ ] `tests/meadows.js` in `npm test`: founders seed 7 make at least one meadow per farm with animals by the end of summer of year 1, each within 6 steps and on a valid tile; populous small seed 7 starts with meadows for every farm with animals.
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
