## Implementation notes (agent run, 16 Nov 2026)
- VB-473 already met the first criterion's reading half: `placePawns` and the other page scripts (cards, words, play) read a place through
  `Sim.placeOf` / `Sim.poseOf`, and no page script wrote `agent.x`/`y`. What was left, and done here: `prevX`, `prevY`, `prevStride`,
  `prevIn`, `prevN`, `notePlaces`, `drawBetween`, `stepTick` and `drawingBetween` are gone from `page/speed.js`; the page keeps only
  `stepShare`. `Sim.poseOf(st, a, at)` takes a fractional tick and works the walker's place along its leg at `at`, clamped to the leg's
  due and its last hour (read only); `placePawns` draws every pawn at `st.tick + stepShare`, so a walker is ahead of the simulation by
  less than a step, where it was a step behind. Rows of field work use the same `at`. The clock shows the same time (ahead, not behind).
- A cut does not jump: a cut is made at the step's time, which is never behind the drawn time, so the pawn goes on from where it was drawn
  (at most the share of a step ahead). A walk begun this step is drawn at its start until its tick.
- Tests: `tests/browser.js` now checks each pawn drawn within a step of `placeOf` and, frame to frame, that none moves further than the
  steps run allow (a jump); `tests/browser-speed.js` likewise, and its clock check allows the clock up to a step ahead.
