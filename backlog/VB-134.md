# VB-134: The byre, the autumn count, slaughter and salt meat
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: animals
Phase: implement
Design ref: Animals; docs/review-animals.md (review of 31 October 2026); answers in QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md under "VB-047: animals review".
Goal: Hay sets how many animals a household can winter, and the hard winter limit is Decided: the household slaughters what its hay cannot carry, and animals die when hay runs out. Meat is salted by a household job before it keeps (Kim, 30 Oct).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Stall period: autumn day 9 to spring day 5 (21 days); at the start of each stall day every herd eats its need from `hh.hay`.
- [ ] Fodder: each stall day a household with a herd posts `fodder` at the byre, 0.5 h + 0.15 h a head of cattle + 0.03 h a sheep, one hand, a new type of work `husbandry` (mistress 9, maid 10, daughter 9, farmhand 4, farmer 3, son 3, retired 3; Kim, 31 Oct, as P); a stall day without it adds a lean day to the household's herds (read by milk in VB-135).
- [ ] The autumn count (autumn day 8): old animals go (cows and oxen of 12, bulls of 10 when a younger bull is in reach, ewes with chance 1/7, extra rams and steers beyond the oxen's replacements, male calves and ram lambs not kept for breeding); then, while the winter's need × 1.1 exceeds the hay (with hay still on the meadows) or the byre holds more than 6 cattle (a calf counts half), the least valuable animal goes, in order: ewe lambs, ewes, heifer calves, heifers, an extra bull, cows (oldest first), the only bull in reach, the only ram in reach, oxen last. One log line per farm names what goes.
- [ ] Slaughter: one job per animal at the byre, cattle 3 h, sheep 1 h, two hands, husbandry with the men's rank (farmer 8, farmhand 8, son 7) and the women's 2; it gives fresh meat that keeps 3 days: a cow 15 rations, an ox or bull 18, a heifer or steer 10, a calf 5, a sheep 3, a lamb 1.5 (Kim, 31 Oct).
- [ ] Salting: while fresh meat is in the larder, `salt`, 1 h per 8 rations (at least 1), one hand, household work in the house; it makes salt meat that keeps a year. Meat past its day spoils, with a log line.
- [ ] The meal eats larder food within 6 days of spoiling first, then cooked rations and grain, then the rest of the larder when still short.
- [ ] When the hay left will not last the stall days still to come, the household slaughters at once (urgency 3) what it must to make it last; that meat is lean, half the above. When a stall day's need is not met two days running, the herd loses an animal a day, the least valuable first, which gives nothing.
- [ ] `checkState`: larder lots have a kind, a positive amount and a date; cull entries name animals that exist.
- [ ] `tests/byre.js` in `npm test`: a household with hay for half its herd culls to fit at the count, by the order above; a household with no hay slaughters at once and loses animals if nobody does; a fed herd loses none to hunger over 3 years of founders at seed 7.
- [ ] `tests/perf.js` reports the animals' share; under 10 ms a game day at populous medium.
