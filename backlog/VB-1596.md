Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `sim/expansion` `findSite` (a plot's site in a village) weighs the site's daily fetch as the round's search does (`sim/water` `siteWater` with the village: the least of fresh water, the village's well and a well of its own), at a weight stated in the task's notes against the plot score's own scale; no site is refused for its water.
- [x] Cost, named before building: the candidates of one village's search within `REACH`, a plot at a time; the village well's walk by one bounded search a candidate (`sourceSettled`), or one search outward from the green shared by all candidates if the counter shows it matters.
- [x] A check on a small constructed state: of two plot sites alike but for the walk to the village's well, the nearer is chosen.
- [x] Measured once, before and after (populous, small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): the daily fetch of farms founded on plots, by quartile, and their dry days in their first two years.

Noted by VB-1583, 23 November 2026.
- Built, 23 November 2026 (VB-1596). `sim/expansion` `findSite`: a site's score loses `SETTLE.perFetch` (2.4) a daily hour of its fetch for 2 people (the couple a plot is cleared for; one trip a day at any size until VB-1586), from `sim/water` `plotFetch` (new: the least of `siteFetch`, fresh water and a well of the site's own, and the walk to the village's well). `findSite(st, v, false)` for the two callers that ask only whether there is room (`sim/aims`, `sim/settle` `markCrowded`). `tests/sitewater.js`, part 4.
- Decided, the weight: the round's own, 2.4 a daily hour, not a second parameter. Against the plot score's scale: 2.4 is an hour's nearness to the green (8 steps at `HOUSE.far` 0.3), so a step further from the village's well (0.26 h a day) costs 0.63 beside the step's own 0.3; from a house on the rim (0.36 h) to a well of its own at its dearest (0.89 h) the water moves a score by 1.3, about `HOUSE.offGreen` (1.4) and less than the land can (`HOUSE.byLand` 1.5 a point of fertility). Close call: the design's reason for 2.4 ("twice an hour of nearness, which is paid once") read on this scale gives 4.8, which would put the water above everything else in the score; not tried.
- Decided, the cost: the counter showed it matters, so the walks to the well are one search outward from the green, shared by the sites and kept within `REACH` (at most 127 tiles a search), not `siteWater`'s search a site. A search a site settled 6,450 tiles in the two villages' round of `tests/counters.js` (`sourceSettled` 0.039 -> 7.25 a pawn-day); the shared one 160 (0.165, recorded with `--update`). So the fresh water is the land's walk (`w.freshWalk`), as in the round's search, not a source found as walked now. Populous small, 15 years: `sourceSettled` 19,195 -> 22,109 (seed 7), 20,084 -> 24,244 (seed 42).
- Measured, populous small, 15 years, before -> after, farms founded on plots. Seed 7: 39 -> 41 farms; the fetch a day q1/median/q3 0.75/1.14/1.53 (longest 2.94) -> 0.74/0.96/1.23 (3.06); steps from the green 3/4/4 -> 2/3/4; drawing at a well 20 -> 24; dry household-days of the first two years 929/3642 (26%) -> 524/3603 (15%), hungry 11% -> 18%. Seed 42: 41 -> 45 farms; the fetch 0.72/0.96/1.25 (2.18) -> 0.70/1.03/1.40 (4.21); steps 2/3/4 both; at a well 24 -> 28; dry 759/3455 (22%) -> 772/3831 (20%), hungry 16% -> 18%.
- Households: not clearly better off. Seed 7's new farms walk less and are dry on fewer days; seed 42's middle farm walks a little further (the runs part early: 29 -> 24 villages, and the farms are other farms), and hunger is up 2 to 7 points on both, which the water does not explain. Two seeds; left to the tuning run with VB-1583's note.

The check now asks it of households with a winter to feed (`winterNeed` above 0), and that there are three.
