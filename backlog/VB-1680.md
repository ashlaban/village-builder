# VB-1680: Invariant: a tunable states its unit
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: docs/review-principles-2026-11-24/synthesis.md (Invariant catalogue, M13); tests/lint.js; src/sim/rules.js; design.md, Principles for decisions (Parameters, not patches)
Goal: Every named parameter says what it is measured in (hours, days, steps, a share, per year), so a quantity is not read in the wrong unit.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The lint finds an `@tunable` whose comment names no unit; today's are recorded in a baseline that only falls (`tests/lint.js --update`).
- [ ] The tunables of the cases below get their units, and the baseline falls by at least those.
- [ ] A grep lint finds a day count compared with hours outside `DAY`, `SEASON_DAYS`, `YEAR_DAYS` and `YEAR`, or the PR says why it could not be made precise.
