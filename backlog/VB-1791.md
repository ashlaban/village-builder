# VB-1791: A pawn's store moves with its keeper, and passes at death by custom

Goal: A pawn's goods go where it goes only by its journey, and are someone's the moment it dies
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Custody: the store lies at its keeper's house. A move (the verb `move`, every reason) carries it as the pawn's load on the move's journey, up to `TRAVEL.load`; the rest stays at the old house as a pile with the pawn's title (sim/lying), fetched by the stray-pile method (VB-1749). A check on a constructed state: a servant holding 30 rations moves to a master two hours away, carries its load, the rest is fetched; no ration made or lost.
- [ ] At death title passes at once by the custom `personalEstate` (sim/customs, one value `kin`): the spouse; else the children in equal shares into their stores, a child not of age's share to the household that keeps it (its minder's), a holder's or a holder's spouse's to its household; else the household the pawn lived in; else its nearest kin's household; else its village's store. Custody unchanged. A check for each step of the order, and an invariant that no dead pawn's store holds anything.
- [ ] Cost: one passing a death and one carrying a move, where each already runs.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
