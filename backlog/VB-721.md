- [x] The mechanism, not a multiplier (principle 8): a wet field (a marsh's edge, a wet hollow, by the wetness slice 10 gives a tile) loses fertility to its wetness; a ditch takes away a share of that loss. Marsh stays 0.5 by its biome unless slice 10's plan moves the wet part of that into wetness.

- [x] Upkeep: a ditch silts up; the gain fades unless it is cleared every few years (stored as a value and the time it was set, read in closed form: Rules for cost 3), so drainage is kept by work, as tilth is by dung.
