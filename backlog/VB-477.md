# VB-477: The timetable in the page: the day, its ranges, and the head's verbs
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-476
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/review-blocks.md; design.md section 7 (A leg, and its end, to The timetable); docs/mocks/blocks/README.md; design.md section 15 (parity of command)
Goal: The player runs a household against a timetable it can read, doubt and change.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `Sim.timetable(st, household, viewer)`: each member's list laid end to end with its walks, each task {lo, mid, hi}; worked out on demand, never in a step.
- [ ] The head's verbs in `src/sim/commands.js`: reorder a member's list, move a task to another member, call a member off; each checks it is possible and returns why not; the allotment keeps the head's choices until the day is next allotted, and says so.
- [ ] Before a call, the panel names its cost: the walk home in hours and the load left.
- [ ] An audit of the page: every place that shows hours left or a time of finishing shows the inhabited pawn's estimate; progress so far stays exact. The pawn panel, the cards and the idle line are named in the task's notes with what each shows.
- [ ] A control of the page's speed, "until my task ends" (Kim, 15 Nov): the clock runs at its fastest and stops at the end or cut of the inhabited pawn's leg, or at anything that concerns its household (a call, a request for help, a death, an occasion, a store crossing a target). Not a verb: the simulation is the same run with or without it.
- [ ] The page's functional tests; `node tests/smoke.js`.
