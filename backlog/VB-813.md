Goal: Over the sea opens (Decided, 10 Nov: closed until slice 10's boats): what reaches between villages reaches across water for those with boats, and Kim sees what that does to reach, to regions and to where makers could be.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The table by boat: for each village with a landing, the hours over water to every other landing, searched once when the village is founded (the water does not change), saved; `hoursBetween(st, a, b, { boat: true })` is the lesser of the land table's hours and (the walk to the landing + `put` + the water + the walk from the far landing). It is an estimate (principle 14: what a boat's owner knows of the way); the journey itself is VB-812's legs.
- [ ] The acts between villages read it for a household that has a boat lying at its landing, and for the season: the round's choice of a wife, the suitor's visit, service, guardians and kin homes, kin help, who may come to a rite and to the gathering, the goer to a green (VB-769) and the walk to a door (VB-770) where those exist. The row "Over the sea" of `design.md`, Between villages, is rewritten by this task: no act between villages with no way by land or by a boat one of the two households has. Water that is not open closes it (VB-808).
- [ ] The goer's bound stays a day there and back, in hours: a boat changes who is inside it, not the bound (the plan measured 18 to 30 pairs of villages within 6 hours on foot and 21 to 37 by boat). The boat is away for the day, and the head weighs the fishing lost against the dealing, by its own estimate.
- [ ] `tools/reach.js` and the report of regions and market potential (VB-771, where it exists) show each village's people in reach three ways, on foot, by boat and over the ice of a normal winter, at 3, 6 and 12 hours, and a household's estimate of a green names the mode it reckoned with. The Reach overlay (`src/page/reach.js`) gains the same three ways as one choice, shown alike (principle 15); `node tests/smoke.js` and its browser test.
- [ ] Reported for the town's question (VB-189 handed it over: reach sets where makers are): the people within 3 hours of each village on foot, by boat and in winter, against the mock's figure (three or more makers in 73% of villages with 160 to 320 households within 3 hours). The plan measured 38 to 58 people within 3 hours of the median village and no change by boat: say what the built game gives.
- [ ] Numbers before and after on populous medium, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42, and small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years: marriages by land, by boat and over ice; the never-married share on the small islands; kin gifts; villages at a gathering; the spread of customs between islands (the tie to the culture's centre); growth by island (principle 2), and whether households on the small islands, not only the mean, are better off.
- Cost (rule 9): villages with a landing (10 to 20) x one search of the water (5,000 to 7,000 tiles) at founding; 900 numbers saved on medium; a lookup where one is made today.
