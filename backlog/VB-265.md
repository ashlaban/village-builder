## Implementation notes
- `tests/lib/targets.js`: `target()` takes `extra` fields kept in the record (a share, the per-seed values, the before range).
- `tools/bench.js --record`: after a run, one record per budget line with a limit (`bench/medium-ref/mean`, `longest-step`, `moving-round`, `meetings`,
  `save`, `load`, `gzip`) and one per system share (`bench/medium-ref/share/<system>`, ms a day against its budget), each with the share of the
  whole step in the same run (`share`, `shareOfDay`, `wholeMs`); noise `runs`, n 1 (a single run's spread is unknown). The drift flag is unchanged.
- `tools/bench-paired.js` (new, `--help`): before and after checkouts (default both this one), alternated, 3 runs each, each in a fresh
  process of that checkout's own `tools/bench.js --fixture`; median and range of both sides for the mean, longest and p99 step, ms a living
  pawn-day and every system's share of the whole step; `noise` when the after median lies in the before range, else `faster` or `slower`.
  `--record` appends the mean and the longest step with noise kind `paired` (the before range). Timings are never pass or fail.
- `tools/bands.js`: after the full runs, one record per band and run (`bands/<band>/<run>`), the mean over the seeds against the band's range,
  noise twice the standard error over the seeds (`se`, per-seed values and `inBand` in the record). `--record` appends them.
- `tools/marriage-demography.js`: seeds now run one per core; prints three TARGET lines (`demography/marriages-per-year`, `first-marriage-age`,
  `never-married`) against `docs/bench/demography-reference.json` (the means of 12 seeds at `756c693`, written by `--make-reference`), within 5%,
  5% and 2 points; noise twice the standard error over the seeds; `--record` appends them.
