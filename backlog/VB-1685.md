# VB-1685: Invariant: a cache equals its derivation after every verb, with no lag
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/review-principles-2026-11-24/synthesis.md (Invariant catalogue, S1); tests/lib/invariants.js (allowAgeLag); docs/architecture/target-v2.md, principle 2; docs/architecture/ownership.md
Touches: tests/lib/owner-caches.js (checkRoles); tests/lib/invariants.js (checkState, checkCaches); sim/lifecycle (dailyLife); sim/heirs (serve, refreshRoles); sim/spread (clearScrub); tests/lint-rules.js; tests/lib/clock.js (setClock); new tests/caches.js; tests/suites.js
Goal: Every derived cache (pawn.household, hh.members, pawn.role, st.owner and the others declared as caches) equals a fresh derivation after every command, the owner told after the write, with no allowance for lag.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A pawn's role is right on the day its age changes it, and the `allowAgeLag` allowance is removed from `tests/lib/invariants.js`.
- [ ] On constructed states, a test issues each registered verb and compares each declared cache with a fresh derivation after it. (Partly: `tests/caches.js` checks after each verb carried, 27 of 68 verbs reached on the probe village and founders in 45 days plus generic commands by hand; the other 41 are listed in its NOT_YET, a list that only shrinks, and need commands built for them: VB-1703.)
- [x] A lint, or a test, finds a `...Changed(` notification that comes before the write it reports in its function.

## Notes
- From the principles review of 24 November (synthesis, S1). Would have caught VB-1369, 1460, 1506, 440 and PRs #135, #201, #273, #281.
- Birthday: `dailyLife` refreshes the roles of children turned 14 that day; `setClock` (tests) refreshes roles as the days jumped over would have.
- The `move` verb refreshes the roles of both homes after the move (it lagged until its caller's refresh: the trust's move left a holder `farmer` in kin's household).
- Crowding inside the round's batch (VB-1380) is compared at the batch's end, not inside it: a deferral within one synchronous call, kept for cost (decision).
- Six declared caches are not compared (`CACHES_UNCOMPARED` in tests/lib/invariants.js, each with its reason): memos and day-compiled indexes by design.
- `clearScrub` (sim/spread) told the route cost before its tree writes; reordered (the cost reads biome and dev only, so no behaviour change).
- The lapsed-ground invariant allows a tile taken into use today (it leaves the set at the day's visit, as sim/spread documents); the earlier birthday refresh exposed it on heirs seed 42.
- `notedBefore` (tests/lint-rules.js) is syntactic: the key argument's text written as `[key]` after the call in the same function, stopped by a return on the path.
