# VB-1685: Invariant: a cache equals its derivation after every verb, with no lag
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/review-principles-2026-11-24/synthesis.md (Invariant catalogue, S1); tests/lib/invariants.js (allowAgeLag); docs/architecture/target-v2.md, principle 2; docs/architecture/ownership.md
Touches: tests/lib/owner-caches.js (checkRoles); tests/lib/invariants.js (checkState); sim/lifecycle; new tests/caches.js; tests/suites.js
Goal: Every derived cache (pawn.household, hh.members, pawn.role, st.owner and the others declared as caches) equals a fresh derivation after every command, the owner told after the write, with no allowance for lag.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A pawn's role is right on the day its age changes it, and the `allowAgeLag` allowance is removed from `tests/lib/invariants.js`.
- [ ] On constructed states, a test issues each registered verb and compares each declared cache with a fresh derivation after it.
- [ ] A lint, or a test, finds a `...Changed(` notification that comes before the write it reports in its function.
