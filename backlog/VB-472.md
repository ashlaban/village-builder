# VB-472: Work as a block: a worker is not stepped
Status: done (2026-11-16)
Priority: 1
Size: M
Design ref: docs/review-blocks.md; design.md section 7 (A leg, and its end, to The timetable); docs/mocks/blocks/README.md
Goal: Work costs an event at its start, its end and a change of hands, not a step every 6 minutes (Rules for cost 3, 4).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A task's state: `done`, the step it was reckoned at, the summed rate of its hands at work; `doneOf` is the only read of progress outside its owner. Reckoned, and its end queued again, when a hand joins or leaves, when a hand's rate changes (the meal), at the end of work and at a cut.
- [ ] The queue of ends is plain data in `st`, sorted by step, bounded by the tasks under work, saved; a loaded run goes on as the unloaded one (`tests/saves.js`).
- [ ] The hooks `worked` (a stake renewed, a claim worked, a plot's hours by pawn, grazing by whole hours) run at the reckoning with the hours since, and give the same totals over a day as today.
- [ ] A pawn at work stands at its task's spot for talk and nearness; `a.work` (the rows) is no longer simulation state; the page draws the rows from the task and the time (`tests/smoke.js`, the browser tests it touches).
- [ ] The invariant of VB-471 holds; new invariants for the task's fields and the queue; the work counters' structure updated (`walkStep` falls).
- [ ] Numbers before and after at populous small, seeds 7 and 42, and founders small, seed 7: living, hungry household-days, grain, the hours a household-year by kind, the idle share; `--report perf` and the counters early and on a late save.
