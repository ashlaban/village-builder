Goal: Kim (10 Nov, code review): marriage is where opinion first changes a decision; until now it chooses only whom a pawn talks and walks to. In VB-272's first version every unplayed side says yes, and only the player can refuse. Here opinion, as VB-240 derives it (kin standing, compatibility shown with familiarity, memories of what happened), enters in two places: between the two young, and between the two households' spokesmen. The criteria below are the proposed answer to the question under "VB-275" in `QUESTIONS.md`, which this task waits for.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The young: the matcher's score gains `MARRIAGE.liking` (1.0) × the mean of the two young's opinions of each other. Opinion is 0 between strangers, since affinity grows with familiarity, so liking moves a pair by at most 1, against the same-village bonus of 2 and the noise of 1.5. An unplayed young pawn answers no to an offer when its opinion of the other is at or below `SOCIAL.enemy` (-0.5).
- [ ] The spokesmen: an unplayed spokesman answers no when its opinion of the other side's spokesman is at or below `SOCIAL.enemy`. A young pawn who speaks for itself is the other side's spokesman here.
- [ ] A young pawn's no to a match its spokesman made costs the spokesman's opinion of it, as VB-272 does for the player (`refusedMatch`), now for unplayed pawns too.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, in the PR and here: at `medium-ref` and `small-y40` after 4 years each, and populous small at seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 for 15 years. Report marriages a year; offers refused a year, by whom (young, spokesman); the share of marriages whose two young like each other (mutual opinion of `SOCIAL.friend`, 0.25, or more) and dislike each other (`dislike`, -0.2, or less); the mean age at first marriage; living and households; and whether the households of refused matches are worse off (unmarried adults at 30). `tests/golden.json` and `tests/counters.json` are re-recorded, with the changed entries listed.
- [ ] Cost: two `opinionOf` calls for each pair the matcher scores (1,455 pairs a year at `medium-ref`, `VB.count.roundPairs`), and four for each match. `opinionOf` reads the pawn's record of the other and their tie, without allocating. Measure the round's step at `medium-ref` against 60 ms (`node tools/sim.js --load medium-ref --years 1 --report perf`).
