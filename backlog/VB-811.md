# VB-811: Sledges: the winter's vehicles, and heavy hauling kept for the snow
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-810
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: roads
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Climate and travel beyond walking (Modes; The ground by season); Carts; docs/plans/VB-190.md, section 5.3
Goal: Winter is the season of heavy carrying: a sledge on snow carries more than a cart (target-v2, 4.2) and needs no way, so a head keeps timber, stone and the far wood for the snow.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `SLEDGES` in `src/sim/rules.js`: a hand sledge carries 90 rations at 0.85 of the walker's pace, an ox sledge 450 at 0.7 (an ox cart's 300 at 0.6: runners on snow drag less than wheels on a soft way; from memory and unchecked, as the carts' loads are); built as carts are (8 hours and 2 loads of timber; 20 hours and 4), worn and mended as carts are. In `sim/carts`, as two more kinds of the one item: no new module.
- [ ] A sledge runs only on a tile where snow lies or ice bears (VB-808), over any ground a walker crosses, with no need of a way; an ox sledge takes no step steeper than `steep` 2. A carter takes the household's best vehicle for the day's ground: a sledge while snow lies in its band, a cart otherwise. Carts are laid up while snow lies and for the soft days between the thaw and firm ground (wheels sink: the break-up is the season when a load goes on the back).
- [ ] The head holds heavy hauling for the snow (principle "Every act is a pawn's": the head chooses; principle "The future is someone's estimate": by its own estimate of the hours): timber and stone for a project, and firewood from the far wood, are fetched in winter when the head reckons the sledge's trips cost fewer hours than the cart's now, and the project can wait. A named tunable `SLEDGES.wait`, the hours saved that make a load wait; on a world with no snow in a normal winter no head waits.
- [ ] If VB-649 (a cart and its team are home when their carrier is not) is open, a sledge has the same fault and its criterion covers both; a sledge left away lies where it was left.
- [ ] A constructed check: in frost a load of 450 crosses 6 tiles of forest with no way in one trip by ox sledge, and after the thaw in 15 trips on the back; a sledge is refused on bare ground.
- [ ] Numbers before and after on the middle and the northern world, populous medium at year 0, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 (`node docs/plans/VB-190-water.js ROOT medium 3,7,11,42`, the plan's measures), and years on populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years, and founders small, seed 7, 50 years: timber and stone hauled by season, hours of hauling a household, sledges built, projects finished a year, firewood short-days, hungry days by household, growth.
- Cost (rule 9): the carts' cost; one more compiled array only if the ox sledge's slope bar needs it (else the walker's winter array serves).
