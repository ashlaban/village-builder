Goal: What a new household starts with is carried or carted there by those who move, or fetched afterwards; nothing is in the new store before someone brought it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Settlers' provisions travel with the party as loads (on the back, or a cart), and are in the new store when the party arrives, not before; what the party cannot carry stays owned by the new household at the old farm until fetched.
- [x] A restored farm's and a cottage's start (grain, wood) changes owner at the old home and is fetched by the new household's hands.
- [x] Tests on constructed states; numbers before and after for founders and populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 10 years: villages founded, hungry days in a new household's first year. Runs one at a time with VB-445 (settling in steps), whose plan should read this.
