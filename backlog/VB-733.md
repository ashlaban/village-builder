# VB-733: Goods by kind: the registry's material and kinds, and the stone heap by its rock
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The metals and the stones: goods by purpose, kinds by property; Goods, recipes and facilities); `docs/plans/VB-702.md`; `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (17)
Goal: Principle 17 in the goods registry and the stores: kind is the subtype, carried from the material into the good, with no record for each item; the stone heap is its first user, the quern its first reader.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The goods registry (`sim/stores`; target-v2 4.3) gains `material` (the class: stone, metal, clay, wood, ore, amber; `class` keeps its meaning, custom's class of exchange) and, for a good with kinds, its list (at most 8) with the properties its use reads, as named tunables of `sim/rules.js` with a physical meaning. Declared at load, sealed with the rules registry, never saved. Properties are compiled at load into one frozen row a good and kind; a reader allocates nothing (`tests/` shows it as the rules registry's `value()` is shown).
- [ ] A store's count of a good with kinds is `{[kind]: n}`, as `household.grain` (VB-347): `put` names the kind; a take of `good/kind` takes it; a take of `good` (any kind) falls on the kind of least customary worth first (`Culture.norm('worth', 'good/kind')`, one row a kind). The ledger counts the good whatever its kind, as it does grain. `@state`, the invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`), `tools/stocks.js` and saves (an old save's number becomes granite) follow.
- [ ] The stone heap: `household.stone` is `{[rock]: loads}`. A quarried load is of its tile's bedrock (Decided, 11 Nov); a picked or cleared load is granite. The foundation and the well take any kind.
- [ ] The quern reads its stone (the plan's D8): it is hewn from a load of sandstone or granite on the heap and is of that rock, no longer of the rock under the green; `wantsStone` asks for a load of a rock that can be hewn, from a quarry of it within reach. The quern's two kinds and speeds move into the registry's rows (`quern/sandstone`, `quern/granite`), its first reader.
- [ ] Quarrying on flat ground (the plan's D7): `quarry(w, t)` is hills and mountains only, which hides a third of the limestone and sandstone villages have within 12 steps (89 against 67 villages, 61 against 46, of 189; limestone was quarried on flat ground on Gotland and Öland). Widen it to heath (thin soil over rock) as a named tunable, `BUILD.quarryOn`; report the quarry columns of `docs/plans/VB-702-reach.js` (or `--report ground`, VB-735) before and after, and keep the change only if it adds villages with a limestone or sandstone quarry in reach.
- [ ] The page (principle 15): the heap shows its loads by rock, one presentation for a good's kinds wherever they appear (an icon of the good, the kind as its tint and label); `node tests/smoke.js` and the browser tests it touches.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years, and founders small seed 7: querns by rock, households with no working quern, the share of grain eaten as flour, stone loads quarried by rock, hungry household-days. Cost: `node tests/counters.js` unchanged but for intended lines; save size at the end of the runs.
- [ ] A note on VB-366 (pots), VB-698 (targets) and VB-416 (the registry split out of `sim/stores`) of what they should read from this, in the task file and as inbox notes for the orchestrator.
- [ ] The numbers that move are reported before and after at the seeds and maps named (Working rules); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
