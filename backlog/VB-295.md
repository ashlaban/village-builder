# VB-295: Going to the gathering: a household plans it, packs and walks there
Status: done (2026-11-21)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Settlements, The gathering (10 Nov); Work (occasions as plans)
Goal: Pawns prepare for the gathering and travel to it in person (Kim, 10 Nov): the household plans it as an occasion, and those who go are on the host village's green for its days.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The gathering is an occasion (VB-126) on the host's green after the harvest (winter days 1 to 3), put into the due-queue when the villages choose (VB-294).
- [x] Each household of an attending village plans it on its planning day: its holder or spokesman goes, its unmarried of marrying age, anyone party to a border case, others as hands and by wish (a stand-in for sociability until traits); at least one adult stays, and the animals are tended.
- [x] The day before, the goers pack: provisions for the days away, taken from the household's stores through `sim/stores`, and what the household means to exchange, as loads (a back load of 30 rations' weight; a cart with oxen, 90).
- [x] They walk there and back as journeys (VB-287: a leg a day, a due entry a leg, a ration a day carried); a host at most 12 hours away is one leg. The setting out builds on VB-126's occasion walk (`call` gives each guest its hour to set out, `asked`, and `occasionsDue` sends it with the `attend` verb; `src/sim/occasions.js`) for a host within 12 hours, and on VB-287's journey beyond, or as the task finds fit. Needs go on at the gathering: they eat what they brought.
- [x] Lodging (`design.md`, The gathering, Lodging; Agreed, 10 Nov): a goer sleeps at home if its village is within 2 hours' walk; else with kin in the host village; else with a pawn there it holds a goodwill balance with; else in a barn the host village gives. A host farm lodges up to 10 guests (4 in the house, 6 in the barn), kin and partners first. Lodging a guest who is not kin books a gift of 1 ration a night to the lodging holder's credit. Nobody sleeps outdoors.
- [x] Ale at the gathering (carried from VB-305, 11 Nov 2026): the host village's holders pour what they have through `Stores.pour` (a barrel serves 20 guests); each guest's opinion of the pourer gains as at a wedding (`SOCIAL.memories.ale`), and the host's standing gain is shared among its holders by what each poured (`aleStanding`, at most 0.2 a holder).
- [x] A verb in `src/sim/commands.js` for going and for staying home, so the player's pawn chooses; `tests/commands.js` passes.
- [x] Invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js`: no household is left without an adult; loads are in custody of a pawn present; every goer is home or dead two days after the gathering ends.
- [x] Cost named and measured: goers × the walk, on `small-y40` and `medium-ref` (`--report perf`); a declared counter for any new loop.
- [x] Numbers before and after (populous, small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): work hours lost in the gathering's days, and how the walk fares in the first snow, hungry household-days.
