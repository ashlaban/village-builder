# VB-1111: The player's gift is carried: the grain does not reach the other farm at once
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: physical
Phase: implement
Design ref: Vision and scope, Principles for decisions (Nothing teleports); Exchange, goodwill and obligations (The gift)
Goal: A gift of the player's, like every gift, reaches the other farm only by someone's journey.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The gift form (`src/page/gift.js`) names the carrier: an adult of the giving household who may carry (the check of `give`'s `carrier`, VB-291), the player's own pawn first, or another chosen in the form; or, where none may, the gift changes owner where it lies and waits for the receiver to fetch it. Decide which is the default and write why.
- [x] `give` without a carrier no longer moves grain in the step it is given: `settleGift` (`src/sim/exchange.js`) is reached only by a carried load's arrival or a fetch. Every caller of `give` is checked (`tests/commands.js`).
- [x] Numbers before and after, as the hard rule asks (`AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim): days from a gift to its arrival, and the receiving farm's food on the day of the gift and on arrival, at founders small seed 7.
- [x] A check on a small constructed state: a gift with a carrier arrives when the carrier does; the giver's store falls when the load is taken up, the receiver's when it is set down.

Implementation (18 Nov 2026):
- What the run's branch already had: since VB-461, VB-849 and VB-861 the verb `deliver` has a gift with no carrier fetched by the
  receiver's household (`sim/away` fetchGift) or hauled to the green, a load a trip; `settleGift` was left with the gift within one
  farmstead, and with a store-to-store gift no caller makes. So the teleport of the 16 November note (21 to 121 days at once) was
  closed for a form that names no carrier; what was missing was the form's carrier, the bound on a carried load, and the checks.
- The form (`src/page/gift.js`, giftWay) gains "Carried by", shown once a household is chosen: the members the verb takes as carrier
  (`Sim.giftCarriers`, which asks `sim/offers` giftOf, the verb's own check, so the page holds no rule), the played pawn first and
  marked "(you)", then "One of <farm>, who fetches it". Under it one line says what happens. Between two households of one farmstead
  it says that nothing is carried.
- Decision, the default: carried by the giver's own, the played pawn first; fetched when no one of the household may carry. Why: the
  player's gift is unasked, and an unasked gift is brought by the giver's own, as the good-year gift is (design.md, The good-year
  gift: "carried there by one of its own"); what answers an ask is fetched by the asker (VB-861). The carrying costs the giver its
  walk, which is what makes the gift a deed. A close call: design.md, The player's gift, says "Its gift is fetched as any"
  (Proposed, VB-461), so fetching stays one choice away, and is the only way for more than a back-load. Surfaced as a `decision`,
  and VB-1130 asks the design line to say so.
- Decision, a physical bound (Deciding without Kim, 8: hard limits are for physical facts): the verb refuses a carried gift over
  `TRAVEL.load` (30 rations), "one pawn carries at most 30 rations: a larger gift is fetched". Help's far gift and the good-year gift
  already kept to it by their own rule; the verb did not, so the form could have sent 100 rations on one back. A carrier within one
  farmstead is refused too (a farmstead is one place). No new tunable.
- `settleGift`: the verb refuses a store-to-store gift ("a village store gives only to a household"), so `settleGift` is reached
  only for two households of one farmstead, where nothing is carried; a carried load's arrival and a fetch book through `receive`,
  as before. The criterion's wording ("reached only by a carried load's arrival or a fetch") is met in its sense, no grain changes
  place in the step it is given, not in its letter: the arrivals never went through `settleGift`. `tests/commands.js` holds the
  callers of `give` at the two there are (`sim/help`, `page/gift`) and `settleGift` at one call, after the carried, hauled and
  fetched branches; `giftUncarried` stays 0 (the invariant).
- `Sim.TRAVEL` is exported by the facade, for the form's "30 rations". No state field, no new module, no new loop.
