# VB-962: A pawn's pack is carried as grain, lies where it fell, and comes home as grain
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: physical
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Moving to new land, Sleeping away from home (Food); AGENTS.md, the hard rule
Goal: Nothing of a pawn's pack appears or vanishes but by a process: it lies where its bearer fell, and comes home as the grain it was
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A pawn who dies away from home (a journey or a stay) leaves what is left of its pack where it fell (sim/lying, as a bearer's gift is laid, VB-850), for its household to fetch; nothing of the pack vanishes with the stay (`sim/away` dropStay).
- [ ] The pack is carried as the grain it was taken as (by crop, `parts`), and what is left of it goes back into the household's grain when it is home, not into its meals: no grain becomes meals without a cook, and none of it is lost on the way (today a ration of grain, 1 / ECON.uncooked of it, comes back as one ration of meals).
- [ ] `node tools/same.js --base REF` lists the differences with their cause; numbers before and after at populous small, seed 7, 2 years (`tools/balance.js`): grain into and out of the packs.
- [ ] A test on a constructed state (`tests/away.js`): a lodger who dies at its bed leaves its pack lying there; one who comes home puts the grain back into the store.
