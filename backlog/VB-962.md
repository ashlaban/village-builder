# VB-962: A pawn's pack is carried as grain, lies where it fell, and comes home as grain
Status: done (2026-11-17)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: physical
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Moving to new land, Sleeping away from home (Food); AGENTS.md, the hard rule
Goal: Nothing of a pawn's pack appears or vanishes but by a process: it lies where its bearer fell, and comes home as the grain it was
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A pawn who dies away from home (a journey or a stay) leaves what is left of its pack where it fell (sim/lying, as a bearer's gift is laid, VB-850), for its household to fetch; nothing of the pack vanishes with the stay (`sim/away` dropStay).
- [x] The pack is carried as the grain it was taken as (by crop, `parts`), and what is left of it goes back into the household's grain when it is home, not into its meals: no grain becomes meals without a cook, and none of it is lost on the way (today a ration of grain, 1 / ECON.uncooked of it, comes back as one ration of meals).
- [x] `node tools/same.js --base REF` lists the differences with their cause; numbers before and after at populous small, seed 7, 2 years (`tools/balance.js`): grain into and out of the packs.
- [x] A test on a constructed state (`tests/away.js`): a lodger who dies at its bed leaves its pack lying there; one who comes home puts the grain back into the store.

Noted by VB-783, 17 November 2026.

## Implementation notes (VB-962)
- `sim/away`: a stay's pack now also holds `parts`, the grain it was taken as by crop (`provide` passes `into` to `takeSpare`). What is left
  (`packLeft`: rations left times 1 / ECON.uncooked, in the crops' mix) goes home as grain into the household's store (`packHome`; its
  village's if the household is gone), at a lodger's return and a journey's arrival; a pawn who dies away lays it where it fell
  (`layPack`, through `layGift`, the lying load `leftGrain`, owner the pawn's household). No new mechanism. An old save's pack has no
  `parts`: it comes home as nothing (rare, a stay open at the save).
- Tests: `tests/away.js` (a pack taken as grain, back in the barn not meals, a lodger dying at its bed lays it); `journeys` and `fetch`
  now find the receiver's lying load among two (the bearer's pack lies beside it).
- `same.js` populous seed 7 tiny 2 years: 65 keys differ, cause: leftover packs come home as grain (barn) instead of meals.
- Numbers, populous small, 15 years, grain of packs coming home: seed 7 2446 grain (before: the same packs came home as about 1834 meals,
  a quarter of the grain lost to the cooking it never had), seed 42 2687 (about 2015 meals). Packs laid where a bearer fell: 0 at both
  seeds (no pawn died away with a pack left in these runs); before, such a pack vanished. Verify green.
