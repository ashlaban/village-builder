# VB-236: The route cache is cleared when a tile's cost changes
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (code structure). `AGENTS.md`, Rules for cost, 8 (bound every cache).
Goal: `refreshRoutes` (`src/sim/routes.js`) tests every cached route each night against the tiles whose walking cost changed, with unrolled hex arithmetic and a test in two bounds, to drop about 130 of 30,000. Emptying the cache when any tile changed takes the same time a game day at the late fixtures, with a tenth of the routes held. Replace the selective refresh with the clear, and lower `ROUTE.cacheMax` to what the clear leaves in use.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `refreshRoutes` empties `st.pathCache` when `st.routeDirty` is not empty, and clears `st.routeDirty`; `MAX_STEP`, the unrolled hex arithmetic and the two-bound test go. `route`, `findRoute`, `costChanged` and the LRU are unchanged.
- [x] `ROUTE.cacheMax` falls from 60,000 to the bound the clear needs: the largest cache seen over 48 days at `medium-ref` and `small-y40`, with a margin (about 2,700 routes were held at `medium-ref`; `node tools/route-bench.js`), and its comment in `sim/rules.js` gives the measurement in place of the hit rate.
- [x] Cost, three runs of 48 days each, before and after, in the PR and here: simulation ms a game day at `medium-ref` and `small-y40` no higher than before beyond the runs' spread (paired runs); the worst 6-minute step and the number of steps over the 60 ms target no higher, residuals reported (`node tools/bench.js`). If either is worse, the task stops and reports: the selective refresh stays.
- [x] `tests/routes.js` holds that a route is not served from the cache after a tile on it changed cost; the tests of the selective bounds go with them.
- [x] `tests/golden.json` and `tests/counters.json` (`routeRefresh`, `routeSearch`, `routeHit`, `routeExpand`, `routeEvict`) are re-recorded, and the PR lists the changed entries. Numbers before and after: populous and founders, small map, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years: living, households and `st.routeFallbacks`; `node tools/bands.js` reports every band as before.
- [x] `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals; the PR says what fell (lines, the save's size at `medium-ref`).
