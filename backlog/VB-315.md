# VB-315: Weeding in two rounds a crop, of 5 hours each
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (Goods and the farming year: Field work, Tilling power). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Field work (11 Nov)
Goal: A growing field asks for a round of weeding every 6 days, 2 hours each (`ECON.weedEvery`, `KINDS.weed`): four or five rounds a crop, 30 hours of work for three fields and as many again walking there and back, more than its reaping. Kim, 11 Nov: two rounds a crop, with longer hours each, to cut the walking.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Two rounds: a field's weeding falls due `ECON.weedDays` after it sprouts ([6, 15] days of growth, of its 26) in place of every `weedEvery` days; `KINDS.weed.hours` 2 to 5. `fd.weedPosted` and `fd.weedOpen` keep their meaning; a round still open when the next falls due, or at ripening, counts as missed (`fd.weedMiss`), as today.
- [ ] A missed round cuts the yield by `ECON.weedCut` 0.2 (it was 0.08 a round of four or five), to the same most of 0.4 for both, so a field never weeded loses what it loses today.
- [ ] Children's part is unchanged (weeding is theirs at 5, `ROLES.child.except`).
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous and founders, small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years; `tools/balance.js` at seeds 3, 7, 11, 42): pawn-hours by field job and their share of the hands' year, rounds weeded and missed, the yield lost to weeds, weeding's work and walking hours, hungry household-days and deaths, idle hours; whether households, not only averages, are worse off, and which (the short-handed, the lone holder). The golden file and the work counters are re-recorded and their changed entries listed; the bands of `tests/lib/bands.js` reported, none re-set.
Notes:
