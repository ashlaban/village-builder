# VB-315: Default and cases at the meeting: pressing, the hearing, labour owed
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-254, VB-312, VB-314
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, Loans, obligations and cases (Pressing; Cases at the meeting; Remedies; Partial failure); Customs and culture, Slice 5's practices; Work, Who may take a task (`debtor`); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, sections 4.5 (Enforcement, Opportunities) and trace 7.1 (steps 5 to 7)
Goal: A defaulted loan must end somewhere. The creditor presses, extends or forgives; a pressed debt is heard at the debtor's village meeting, by the village's own procedure; upheld, the debtor's household works it off. Land as a remedy follows in VB-316.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] On its planning day after a default, the creditor's holder chooses: press, extend a year, or forgive. It presses more the larger the debt against its own year's need and the higher its ambition (the stand-in trait of slice 3), less the higher its opinion of the debtor's holder; forgiving books the debt's worth as goodwill to its credit. Three verbs in `sim/commands` (`press`, `extend`, `forgive`), so the player as holder chooses by them.
- [ ] A pressed debt is a case (plain data, kept by a module `sim/enforcement` that owns it) on the agenda of the debtor's village's next meeting (autumn day 10), heard in that village's meeting step through `sim/decisions` (VB-313).
- [ ] Two new practices: "Who judges a case" (the meeting's voices, default; the alderman; malleability 0.3) and "One's own cause" (the parties stand aside, default; the parties vote; 0.35). A voice upholds by its opinion of the creditor's holder against the debtor's, its household's own position (owing leans to the debtor) and its leaning on "Debts are paid with"; a tie goes to the alderman (VB-314), and with none the case is extended.
- [ ] A new practice "Debts are paid with" (labour; labour, then land, default; land; malleability 0.5). This task carries out labour: upheld, the debtor household's adults owe a day of 8 work hours for each 3 rations left, within a year, as an obligation of kind `labour`; the creditor's plan opens them as its own tasks, taken on the basis `debtor` (VB-250's table of bases), and each hour worked is booked against the debt. Not upheld: extended a year, and the creditor's holder remembers it.
- [ ] Partial failure: a debtor household dissolved passes its obligations to its successor (the household that takes its farm, else the one its last holder's heir lives in; else lapsed, remembered by the creditor's holder); a creditor dissolved, likewise.
- [ ] Events and log lines for pressing, forgiving, the hearing and its ruling, with the ballots kept by VB-313.
- [ ] Invariants (a case's obligation is defaulted and live; labour owed at most the days the debt is worth); numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 20 years): defaults, pressed, forgiven, cases heard and upheld, labour days owed and given; hungry household-days of debtor households, by household. Golden and counters re-recorded, entries listed.
Cost: the creditor's choice on its planning day per defaulted loan; a few cases a village a year, each heard over at most 60 voices in the village's own step; the `debtor` basis reads a compiled per-pawn index of labour owed (pawn to creditor), so a task's eligibility stays one lookup. Measured on `small-y40`: no step over 60 ms.
Order: after VB-312, VB-314 and VB-250 (slice 3's eligibility). VB-316 adds land.
