Goal: The match list holds only people the pawn could marry, so the wish is a real choice.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `match()` (`src/sim/opportunities.js`) leaves out, before it takes the best by tie, those the round's rules bar for good or for now by who they are: kin within the custom's limit (`kinOk`), the retired, and (when VB-1107 lands) kin by marriage. Bars that may lift (the means, hunger, a home) stay listed with their reason, as now. The list fills to `MATCH_PARTNERS` from the next by tie.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state: a son whose strongest tie of the other sex is his widowed mother sees her nowhere in the list, and sees the next unmarried woman he knows.
- [ ] Cost: as now, at most `MATCH_PARTNERS` checks past those left out; no pass over every pawn.
