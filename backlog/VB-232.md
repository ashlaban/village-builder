## Implementation notes
- `contribute` to an undeclared rule waits in a map and attaches when the rule is declared; `seal` fails naming rules never declared. A spec's `order: [keys]` sorts the contributions at seal and must name each exactly once. `death.hazard` (`['hunger', 'frailty']`) and `allowance` (`['slack', 'goodwill', 'reserve']`) declare theirs, equal to the load order they had, so the floating-point sums are unchanged.
- Lint (`tests/lint.js`): a line that contributes names a `source` equal to its own module. The `hunger` and `frailty` terms now live in `sim/hunger`.
- Goodwill: its `source` became `sim/exchange` (the term stays in `sim/exchange`). Its limit needs the household's `dailyNeed` (`sim/hunger`), and `sim/relations` loads before `sim/hunger`, so moving it home would be an upward lookup; `sim/exchange` already owns the allowance's deals.
- Ranges: optional on a sum rule's term (`explain` checks one only when given), required on a product rule's (checked when the rule is declared or the term attaches). `ALLOWANCE_BOUND` is gone with its three ranges, and the `need` terms' `[-1e9, 1e9]` too. allowance's clamp is `[0, Infinity]` (a clamp may be open on one side).
- Fell: `ALLOWANCE_BOUND` and its imports in `sim/hunger`, `sim/help`, `sim/exchange`; one wrong `source` label x2 and one x1 corrected; 5 invented bounds; deaths no longer imports `HUNGER`/`HEALTH`. Upward calls by layer: 25 to 24 (`sim/hunger`'s lookup of `sim/relations`), baseline lowered by 1. The registry grew by about 25 lines.
