# VB-389: The fields a household wants are counted net of seed
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: animals
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods and the farming year (Seed). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-284, question 1
Goal: fieldsWanted (src/sim/fields.js) counts the gross yield: FIELDS.want 1.1 of the need at 75 a field, of which 25 is seed, so the plan nets 0.73 of the need. A formula that computes something other than it says is a fix, not tuning (AGENTS.md, Deciding without Kim, 3).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `fieldsWanted` is `ceil(FIELDS.want × rations / (FIELDS.expect × fieldYield − seedPerField))`, still capped by 2 fields a hand; `FIELDS.want`, `FIELDS.expect` and the yield keep their values.
- [ ] A test: a household of 2 adults and 2 children wants 4 fields (3 today), a lone adult 2 (1 today).
- [ ] Numbers before and after (the runs of `docs/bench/food-balance.md`: populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 and 30 years; founders small at four seeds): fields a household, the farms' share of need in an ordinary year, land taken and when villages fill (`FIELDS.room`), hours in the fields, crops lost to frost (VB-333), hungry household-years, and growth by `tools/bands.js`. The golden file and the field jobs' counters are re-recorded and listed.
