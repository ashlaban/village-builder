# VB-389: Balance accounts: a report of where the food and the people go
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: Balance loops and targets (The balance review, 12 November); docs/balance/cards.md; docs/balance/findings.md
Goal: The balance review needed numbers the game cannot report in one place, and found by hand-written scripts that about 15% of a year's gross produce has no outlet in the measured split (it is neither eaten, brewed, given nor counted as rotted). Give the game one report, `node tools/sim.js --report accounts [--json]` (from a run or `--load`), that closes the yearly accounts and prints what is left unexplained, so that the balance map's properties are read from the game and not from documents. It reads only: no new state, nothing saved, nothing the simulation reads.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Food, a game year, village by village and in all: produced by source (grain by crop, fish, gathered food, meat, milk); then seed, eaten (as meals, uncooked, fresh, from the larder), given to other households, given to the village store, received, brewed, rotted in households, rotted in the village store, and the change in stores. The account closes: produced plus received equals the outlets plus the change in stores, and the remainder is printed as its own line, `unexplained`, with its share of the produce. Read from the stores' ledgers and movements (`src/sim/stores.js`) as the run goes, in the tool; a household keeps three years of ledger, so the tool accumulates at each year's end.
- [ ] The 15%: the report is run on populous small, seeds 3, 7 and 11, 30 years, and the task file says where the unexplained share goes (a movement with no reason, sheaves never threshed, the larder, a counting error of the review's scripts) or raises it as a Flag with a question in `QUESTIONS.md`.
- [ ] People, a game year: births, deaths by cause, marriages, marriages postponed by reason (no home, the home lacks the means, hunger, the kin limit, no partner in reach), moves between villages, households founded and dissolved; and from them the growth rate, births a woman (period rate), the shares dying before 1 and before 15, the mean age at first marriage of women and men, and the share of those aged 45 and over who never married.
- [ ] The spread between households, at the year's end: food produced over need (p10, p50, p90, the share under 1.0 and under 1.67), days of food held (p10, p50, p90), the Gini of fields owned and of grain held, the share of single-person households, and the households hungry over 30 days of the year.
- [ ] Goodwill: gifts in the year, goodwill outstanding a household, live pairs, and the share held by the three largest creditors. Labour: the idle share and the walking and carrying share of adults' working hours by season, from the `idle` report's own counting.
- [ ] The balance map's properties as one table at the end (`docs/balance/cards.md`, The balance properties): each with its value this year, or `no mechanism yet` (share extracted, tenancy, town and craft share, elite share, sold, goodwill from feasts, disease).
- [ ] `--json` gives the same as data, a record a year. `--help` describes it, and `AGENTS.md`, Commands, gains its line.
- [ ] A test (`tests/review-tools.js` or one beside it, in `npm test`) runs it on the tiny map for 3 years and asserts that the food account closes within 1% once `unexplained` is counted, and that the people account's births less deaths equals the change in the living.
- [ ] Numbers in this file and the PR: the table for populous small (seeds 3, 7, 11; years 10, 30, 50) and founders small (seeds 3, 5, 7, 11, 42; year 50), beside the balance review's (`docs/balance/findings.md`, Measured on the real game), and each difference explained.
- [ ] Cost (rule 10): the tool's own bookkeeping, outside `st`; the golden file, the work counters and the saves are unchanged.
Notes:
- 12 Nov 2026: written from the balance review of that day (`docs/balance/cards.md`, `docs/balance/findings.md`); the numbers quoted are measured on populous small, seeds 3, 7 and 11, 50 years, by `docs/balance/mocks/measure/scripts/`, unless a mock is named. Mock numbers mix the game's rules with invented ones: directions and rough sizes are findings, exact figures are not.
- The review's scripts are a starting point, not code to keep: `docs/balance/mocks/measure/scripts/analyze.js` (reads a save), `events.js` (counts the record by decade), `aggregate.py`.
- What the review measured, to compare against: growth 1.2 to 2.2% a year; births a woman 6.1 to 6.9; 20% dying before 1 and 34% before 15; first marriage at 20 to 21 (women) and 22 to 23 (men), almost nobody never married; of the grain harvest, seed 33 to 35%, eaten 26 to 30%, rotted in households 14 to 16%, to the village store 10 to 11%, brewed 8%, given 8%; food produced over need 1.64 to 1.86 with fish, meat, milk and gathered food (39 to 52% of all food), 18 to 28% of households under 1.0; hungry households 0 to 0.8%.
- `tools/balance.js` leaves fish out and gives 1.05 to 1.52; say in its `--help` how the two differ, or fold it into this report.
- The `hours` report counts 102 to 128% of working hours, so it cannot measure spare capacity; the idle report is the one to use, and the over-count is worth a line in the PR.
- May run beside anything: it adds a report to `tools/` and a test.
