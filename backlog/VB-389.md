# VB-389: The Customs tab of the village card: the customs as a charter, voices as faces
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Kim, session of 12 November 2026 (customs pane review); design.md, Customs
Goal: The customs drawer shows 14 cards of labels and support bars, about 4,300 px in a 585 px sheet (398 px on a phone), with a native select and a wide comparison table. Kim chose: the customs belong on the village's card, written as a charter, with the people who speak at the meeting shown by the way they favour.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The card of a village with customs (a settlement of `st.cenv`) has a third tab, Customs, beside Details and History; the Customs chip in its links goes. The tab chosen stays as the selection changes; on a card that has no Customs tab, Details shows.
- [ ] The tab is headed "The customs of <village>", names the culture, how many of its people speak at the meeting and the days to the next meeting, and then tells the 14 customs as sentences under four heads (Land, Inheritance, Household, Meeting); the option in force is a phrase in each sentence.
- [ ] A phrase is marked: tinted where the village's practice differs from its culture's custom, underlined gold where contested, red where set to change. Set to change: another option leads and passes the meeting's margin now. Contested: it leads and is within 10 points of that margin. An option that leads from further off is not marked. A key under the charter names the marks.
- [ ] A phrase opens (click, Enter or Space; again to close) the custom's options, each with a verdict where it has one (In force; the culture's custom; More support, but cannot win yet; Close to winning; Wins at the next meeting) and the faces of those with a voice who favour it most, at most 8 and then a count. No percentages. A face whose first and second leanings are within 0.15 is ringed and says "wavering towards <its second>"; one with over 1.5 times an even share of the say is "a strong voice"; the played pawn is "You".
- [ ] The meeting's margin is one function of `Culture`, read by the meeting, the cause note of the played pawn and this tab (today the note has its own copy, without the small-meeting factor); `tests/golden.json` is unchanged.
- [ ] The page only reads: no write to `st`. Redrawn when the day, the village, a practice or the open phrase changes, not every frame. Styles use the theme's tokens (light and dark) and the card's existing pieces (tabs, small-caps heads, tags); tap targets on a coarse pointer as elsewhere.
- [ ] Tests: `tests/smoke.js` (the tab opens on a village, 14 phrases, a phrase opens its options and every voice stands by exactly one, the tab is absent on a pawn's card) and the Chromium tests, desktop and mobile (the tab opens, a phrase opens, the sheet fits). `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass; `index.html` rebuilt.
- 12 Nov 2026 (session with Kim): the design was explored as mocks in the running page and chosen step by step: the customs first as what is unusual or changing, then what the player can change; four heads are fine; the charter; D (a tab of the village card); no Notable and no You section (the marks carry it); faces only, no percentages; a wavering voice names where it leans. A note on a face (why it leans, and how far it is from changing) is VB-391; the culture's pane is VB-390. Until VB-390 the dock's Customs drawer stays as it is.
- Cost (rule 9): one pass over a village's members a redraw (a day, or a click), 14 customs times its voices; nothing in the simulation.
