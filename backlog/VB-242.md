# VB-242: The meetings and the moving round, a village a step: survey and tasks
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Leaving home); Customs and culture (the meetings). `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, section 3.3. Kim, 10 Nov 2026 (code review).
Goal: `movingRound` (`src/sim/lifecycle.js`) handles every village in one six-minute step, and to stay near the 60 ms limit it keeps a dependency-tracked cache of heir rankings that is invalidated by reading the event record. Kim decided on 10 November that each village holds its own meeting and its own round, on the same days as today, a village a step: the game is about locally supported community, and a village's step is small enough to rank heirs afresh. Survey what the split touches and write the implement tasks.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A survey, in this file: what `meetingStage` and `movingRound` read and write across villages today, with file and function: the matcher's pool (unmarried men and women of the whole map, the same-village bonus, the settlement tie), `places`, `homes`, `handed`, `means` and `putOff`, `kinPressure`, the handovers and retirements (`lifecycle.js:256-264`), `countDemand`, the land meeting, and `changedSince`. For each: whether it is a village's own, or reaches another village and how.
- [ ] Kim's decisions (10 Nov 2026) made concrete, in `design.md` (Leaving home; Customs and culture) as rows Decided, 10 Nov: (1) all villages meet on autumn day 10 and move on day 11, as today, each village in its own six-minute step, in the order of village id; (2) a village's round matches its own unmarried men, and a woman of any village may be chosen, by today's score; a woman taken in an earlier village's round is no longer free; (3) a lone farm (a one-farm village) is a village here. Say what the order of villages changes against today (which man asks first), and whether the order should turn from year to year so that no village is always first.
- [ ] Implement tasks, with numbers and criteria, each of size S or M: the meetings a village a step (VB-206's stages become stages of a village); the round a village a step; the heir cache's end: with a village a step, `heirRanking` is called afresh, `roundHeirs`, the parameters `nested`, `ownFirstSet`, `watch` and `family`, and `changedSince` go, or the survey says what must stay and why. Each task names its steps as entries of VB-241's calendar, and that a save between two villages' steps goes on (`tests/saves.js`).
- [ ] The cost named (Rules for cost, 9): villages, households a village and heir rankings a village's step at `medium-ref` and `small-y40`; the longest step expected, against 60 ms. VB-201 (steps within 60 ms) brought in line: its lines for the meetings and the round are rewritten or closed by these tasks.
- [ ] VB-233 (marriage as a negotiation) brought in line: an offer is made at the man's village's round.
Notes:
- Kim, 10 Nov 2026, in the code review, all three as proposed: the same days and a village a step; a village's own men with women from anywhere; two tasks (VB-241 and this one). "The game is centered around this theme of locally supported community."
- Measured at `fb35e75` on the published fixtures, three rounds each, the heir cache as it is against a fresh ranking at every ask with every household counted as changed after each change: the final state is identical. `medium-ref` (1,026 living, 227 households): 58, 69 and 42 ms a round with 604 rankings, against 78, 86 and 68 ms with 4,008. `small-y40` (695 living, 173 households): 53, 43 and 24 ms with 486, against 87, 91 and 109 ms with 5,098. The cache saves 20 to 60 ms once a year, and the round is over 60 ms with it.
- What the cache is (`heirs.js:20-147`, `lifecycle.js:149-264`): `heirRanking` has seven parameters, four for the cache; a household's memo is trusted while `hh.members` is the same array of the same length (it leans on how `moveTo` replaces one array and appends to the other); `watch` records which rankings depend on which pawns and spouses; `changedSince` finds what changed by reading the event record since a mark, so a change that writes no entry, or one that names no household, leaves a stale ranking with nothing to show it.
- Not a "Plan:" task of an arch: it runs beside the slices, as VB-228 to VB-232 do.
