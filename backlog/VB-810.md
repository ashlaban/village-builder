# VB-810: What a cart needs underfoot: carts keep to ways and open ground
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: roads
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Carts (Underfoot); Climate and travel beyond walking (Modes); docs/plans/VB-190.md, section 5.3
Goal: A cart goes where wheels can go, so a way is worth having and a far field without one is carried from on the back (principle "Place matters": the cost of carrying makes a route worth having).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `CARTS.under` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each with its meaning. A hand cart crosses meadow, field, stubble, shore, heath, a green, a ford and a bridge, and any other ground only on a path or a lane (stages 2 and 3) or a built way; an ox cart crosses meadow, field, stubble, shore and a green, a ford of a brook or stream, a bridge, and other ground only on a lane or a built way. Neither takes a step whose slope multiplies its cost by more than `steep` 2 (hand cart) or 1.6 (ox cart). Marsh is closed to wheels but on a causeway.
- [ ] A second and third compiled array beside the walker's (rule 6), refreshed with it; the route cache's key gains the mode (foot, hand cart, ox cart) and keeps its bound of 8,000 entries for all three. A carter's route is searched on its cart's array; where the cart has no route, or one more than `detour` 1.5 times the walker's in hours, the pawn takes the next best: the hand cart, then the back, and the head's count of loads that went on the back (`cartMiss`) is not raised by it (the lack is of a way, not of a cart): counted apart, as `wayMiss`, which VB-815's estimate of a way reads.
- [ ] A cart's crossing wears a tile as `PATHS.cartWear` 3 walkers do (a named tunable: wheels and hooves cut a way faster than feet), so a carted way deepens into a lane.
- [ ] A constructed check: a field across 4 tiles of forest with a trodden way is carted from on the back, with a path by hand cart, with a lane by ox cart; a step steeper than `steep` turns the cart round by the next way or sends the load on the back.
- [ ] Numbers before and after on populous medium at year 0, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 (`node docs/plans/VB-190-water.js ROOT medium 3,7,11,42`, the plan's measures), and years on populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years, and founders small, seed 7, 50 years: the share of loads by ox cart, hand cart and back; `wayMiss` a household a year (the distribution); hours of carrying a farm; lanes and paths by tile count at year 10 and 30; grain, hungry days by household, growth (principle "The island grows without the player"). At most five short runs for `steep`, `detour` and `cartWear` (seeds and map named), the values tried in the task file, the rest as `tuning` notes.
- Cost (rule 9): two more arrays of a number a tile (0.45 MB on medium), refreshed where the walker's is; a carter's search as a walker's. `routeSearch` and `routeExpand` before and after by mode.
