# VB-102: Several hands on one field job
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: work
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Jobs, owners and roles; Long work and processing: Tilling, Harvest, Multi-day jobs). Jobs review, 30 Oct 2026.
Goal: A job takes one pawn (`j.claim`), so a 20-hour field has one hoer and a 5-hour reap one reaper, however many hands the household has. In populous medium, seed 7, year 1, plowing waits 2.7 hours at the median and 27 hours at the 90th percentile in the sowing window, while farmers are idle for 35% and sons for 71% of spring work hours; 69% of that idle time is spent with no open job the pawn may take. Harvest and tilling were work for the whole household.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `KINDS[kind].hands`: how many pawns may work one job at once. Plow, sow, weed, reap, haul, thresh, clear and break have no limit (`Infinity`), so the whole household can take part, ordered by role priority; cook, water, wood and mend take 1.
- [ ] `j.claim` becomes `j.claims`, the list of pawns working it (`@state` documented); the page's job counts in the settlers panel read it. A job is open while it has fewer claims than hands.
- [ ] Each worker adds their own progress to `j.done` at their own rate (hunger, health, and children's rate once VB-104 is done). When the job is done, every worker is released; the one who finished does the completion and any carrying.
- [ ] Bidding: a pawn's bid for a job that already has k workers is multiplied by 0.8^k, so a free job is preferred to joining one; the poor-fit wait applies as before.
- [ ] Workers on one field start on different rows (row offset by worker), so they do not stand on each other.
- [ ] A test: a household of 3 adults with one stubble field in the sowing window hoes it with more than one pawn, in under 60% of the elapsed hours of one.
- [ ] Populous medium at seed 7 and founders at seeds 7 and 42, 3 years: the `--report idle` numbers (VB-101), fields sown per year and hungry household-days, before and after (reported, not asserted).
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
