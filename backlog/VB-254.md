# VB-254: Foresight: stand-in traits, a head's horizon and its bias
Status: needs-review
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Work: opportunities, plans and tasks (design.md, section 7); docs/review-work.md; design.md section 7 (foresight, Traits before slice 7)
Goal: Layer 2 of how a holder chooses: a head plans from an estimate of what its household needs, and heads differ in how good it is. This is where a capable head's household does better for a reason the player can read.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Stand-in traits: `wits`, `husbandry`, `diligence`, `ambition` and `generosity`, one number in 0 to 1 per pawn and trait, drawn at birth (and for the living at the start of a run) from the stream `traits`, uniform, so neutral on average; read through one function, `Traits.of(st, pawn, name)`, which slice 7 re-points to the real traits. `@state`, invariants, the save. (VB-249's diligence becomes one of these.)
- [x] Skill as head: the mean of wits and husbandry, plus 0.02 a year as head up to 0.2, capped at 1.
- [x] Horizon: a head of skill under 0.33 reviews targets only when a store has crossed one (it reacts); from 0.33 it projects each store to the season's end at the review; from 0.67 a season further (wood and hay for the winter from early summer, seed for next spring before threshing ends).
- [x] Bias: each head has one multiplier per store target (wood, hay, seed, fields), drawn once when it becomes head from the stream `traits`: within ±30% × (1 − skill). It multiplies the template's target in that household's plan; the template is untouched. The household panel's plan (VB-256) shows the head's target beside the custom's.
- [ ] (unmet in part: no template order is enforced for any head, since VB-023 was closed unmerged on 11 Nov, so a head under 0.1 has nothing to depart from; `headView` reports `keepsOrder`. The B3 check holds: 3/3/3 crops at seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 for a head of skill 0.5) The template's order: heads of skill under 0.1 do not keep it (sowing first is not enforced for them); all others do. `tests/heirs.js`'s criterion (VB-023) holds for a head of skill 0.5, forced with `fix`.
- [ ] (unmet: the mean of hungry household-days rose from 23.75 to 36.25, and the top third did better than the bottom third at seeds 3 and 42 only; see the table below) Numbers before and after at populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42: hungry household-days, households that run out of wood or hay, and both split by the head's skill in thirds; the top third does better than the bottom third at every seed, and the mean of hungry household-days over the seeds moves by at most a tenth (the stand-ins are neutral on average).
- [x] No per-review randomness: a review draws nothing (the bias is stored), so reviews stay staggered freely. `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals.
Cost: a few multiplications per review; five numbers per pawn in the save (about 40 bytes a pawn: name it in the PR against the save's line).
