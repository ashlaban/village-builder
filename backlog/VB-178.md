Goal: `pawn.ties` has five writer modules: talk (`sim/relations`), gifts (`help.js:37`), marriage and death (`lifecycle.js:117`, `30`), the daily turning of contacts into ties and the kin floor (`lifecycle.js:234-241`), the seeding of ties (`society.js:35-40`) and a newborn's (`births.js:29-30`). Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `sim/relations` owns `pawn.ties` and `pawn.rel`, with operations to strengthen a tie, set it (marriage, the kin floor) and drop a pawn's ties (a death); the daily rule of contacts and ties moves out of `dailyLife` into `sim/relations` or `sim/society`.
- [ ] The culture fields of a new pawn (`att`, `anc`, `advocate`, `prestige`, `interest`) are set by one function, used by seeding (`society.js`) and births; `culture` owns `att`, `anc` and `advocate`.
- [ ] `node tools/writes.js --field pawn.ties` (and `pawn.rel`, `pawn.att`, `pawn.anc`, `pawn.advocate`) lists only the owner and that one function, apart from the save codec.
- [ ] Refactor rules of `BACKLOG.md`: behaviour unchanged, `tests/golden.json` unchanged, `npm run verify` passes.
