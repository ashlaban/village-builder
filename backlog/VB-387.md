## Measurement and conclusion (12 Nov 2026, agent run; nothing changed under `src/`)
Criterion 1 (the cause, measured). `refreshRoutes` (`src/sim/routes.js`) is not a daily test of every route: it returns at once on a day with no
changed tile (`st.routeDirty` empty) and otherwise adds the cache's size to `routeRefresh` and tests each route against that day's changed
tiles (a wear stage change from `fadeWear`, or a felled tree). So a route that has not changed is tested only on a day when some tile
changed, which is the design (a batch test per route against the day's changed tiles, in place of an index from tile to routes: VB-236).
- Populous small seed 7, 3 years, `--report counts`, before VB-136 (`2003881`) -> now (the merge of `claude/backlog-orchestration-t73ngj`):
  routeRefresh 654,311 -> 729,087 (12.66 -> 14.13 a living pawn-day, +12%); routeSearch 23,106 -> 30,268 (+31%); routeExpand 595,738 ->
  772,287 (+30%); routeHit 183,811 -> 179,978. By day: days with a changed tile 124 -> 137 (+10%); the cache's mean size on those days
  5,277 -> 5,322 (+1%); its size at the end 6,291 -> 6,707 (+7%). So the rise in routeRefresh is the number of days with a changed tile,
  not a larger cache; routeEvict stays 0 (cap 60,000).
- Counters' tiny probe (30 days, 900 living pawn-days): routeRefresh 3,294 (base 0.837, limit 0.920) with herds, 0.75 without
  (672 entries): days with a changed tile 8 against 3, cache 332 against 259 routes at the end. The tiny probe has so few dirty days that
  five more of them, each testing a cache of about 350, are the whole +294%. The extra dirty days are herders treading new ground:
  `fadeWear` changes the stage of the tiles they cross (a real change of walking cost), so the routes must be tested.
- The herders' routes (home to pasture and back) are already one cached route, reused for the way back (`route()` reverses the stored one)
  and, with the kept pasture, across days; none is tested daily on its own account.
Criterion 2: already so for the kept pasture: a route is tested only on a day a tile changed its cost; "not daily" holds. What would cut the
test further is VB-236's index (the routes through a changed tile, so only those are tested, instead of every cached route against each changed
tile); it has not landed and was not built here. Left for VB-236: with it routeRefresh falls to the routes through changed tiles.
Criterion 3: not met by a change; the residual is reported: routeRefresh +294%, routeExpand +44%, routeSearch +29% on the tiny probe
(from VB-136), cause as above: herders' new activity (more route searches while a pasture is chosen) and their wear, which makes more days
with a changed tile on a tiny, nearly quiet map. `tests/counters.json` is not touched.
Criterion 4: no behaviour change (nothing edited), so `tests/golden.json` is unchanged. ms a day: not re-measured (no change).
Conclusion: no caching fault; the residual is the honest price of herding (a walk out and back a day by one pawn a household, and the wear it
makes) until VB-236. Status left `needs-review`.
