# VB-115: Building bridges
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-190
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: roads
Phase: implement
Design ref: World and scale (River courses: crossing at fords and, later, bridges). Kim, 30 Oct 2026: bridges need construction; the rule (a bridge tile is passable) comes with VB-112, building them later, with resource management.
Goal: After VB-112 a river is crossed only at fords, which can mean long detours to a field, a woodlot or the next village. A village should be able to build a bridge where the detour costs it most.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Written by slice 10's plan (VB-190, 16 Nov 2026) on Kim's answers of 12 November (`QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, VB-115) and the principles of 16 November; `design.md`, Climate and travel beyond walking (Built ways, passage and upkeep); `docs/plans/VB-190.md`, sections 5.5 and 8.
- [ ] A bridge is a third kind of built way in the facilities' registry, on VB-815's rules: one stretch of one river tile, 6 loads of timber felled in the village's wood and 40 hours of village work at the priority of the green and the well, twice that over a river of the widest kind (Kim, 12 Nov); `w.crossing` becomes 2 when it stands, and the compiled ground reads it.
- [ ] Where and when (Kim, 12 Nov: where a river cost the village's members most; made the alderman's estimate by principles 14 and 16): once a year, with VB-815's reckoning of ways, the alderman weighs at most 8 sites, the river tiles where the village's worn ways and the straight lines to its fields, its wood and the neighbouring greens meet the river; for each, the hours a year saved against the nearest ford, by the crossings counted at that ford (`w.wear`) and the loads that lacked a way (`wayMiss`). He puts the best to the meeting if its saving over `WAYS.payback` 5 years exceeds its hours; Kim's figure of 200 extra steps a year is the floor below which nothing is proposed. At most one way a village at a time.
- [ ] It decays 5% a year and is closed below 20% (Kim, 12 Nov), read in closed form from when it was last mended; mended by the village's custom `wayDuty` as any built way. An ox cart crosses a bridge and not a ford of a river (VB-810).
- [ ] Over another holder's land it needs the right of way (VB-817), where that is built.
- [ ] The map draws it; a screenshot under `docs/img/`. A constructed check: a village whose fields lie across a river 12 steps from the ford proposes the bridge at the nearer site and builds it, and the route to the fields then crosses it; with the ford beside the fields it proposes nothing; unmended it closes after 16 years.
- [ ] Numbers before and after on populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years, and populous medium, seed 7, 15 years: bridges proposed, built, kept and lost; hours of walking a farm in the villages that built one; hours between the villages a bridge joins; hungry days by household; growth (principle 2).
- Cost (rule 9): 25 to 30 villages x once a year x at most 8 sites x 2 searches bounded at the village's reach of daily work; under 500,000 tiles settled a year, a village a step.
