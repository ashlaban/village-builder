# VB-115: Building bridges
Status: done (2026-11-14)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: roads
Phase: implement
Design ref: World and scale (River courses: crossing at fords and, later, bridges). Kim, 30 Oct 2026: bridges need construction; the rule (a bridge tile is passable) comes with VB-112, building them later, with resource management.
Goal: After VB-112 a river is crossed only at fords, which can mean long detours to a field, a woodlot or the next village. A village should be able to build a bridge where the detour costs it most.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Annual gathering may elect a wood-and-stone bridge construction project.
- [x] Bounded placement survey selects shared farm journey shortcuts or useful ford improvements.
- [x] Bridge becomes passable on completion and appears in the map’s church mural style.
Notes / questions:
- Depends on VB-112 (fords and the crossing values); worn paths (VB-094) show where people cross.

Implementation (14 November, user request supersedes slice dependency):
- Annual meeting may choose a bridge routine. Construction costs 24 loads of wood and 16 of stone, contributed from unreserved household stores when the project starts, and 192 shared duty hours. Construction happens from a bank; only completion opens the crossing and invalidates routes.
- Placement samples at most eight occupied farms and their first field and meadow. Radius-10 endpoint maps score at most 64 nearby river tiles for saved walking steps; a used ford also earns a convenience benefit. No per-candidate A* searches or daily placement scans. Existing bridge projects exclude their sites.
- Choice: local walking-step savings are a cheap approximation to terrain-weighted travel; remote improvements and unsampled fields can be missed. Materials are committed to construction at inception, like building supplies, rather than transported individually.
- Focused constructed river test covers shared detours, charging, completion and route invalidation. Survey measured below 1 ms per village on the small fixture; this is not a late-save whole-year measurement.
- Final validation: `npm run verify`: 105/105 passed; 16 advisory targets out. `node tests/bridges.js`: passed, including ford convenience, about 0.38 ms per village after the suite. `git diff --check` and rebuilt artifact check passed. No cached late fixture was available, so the requested whole-year late-game placement budget remains unverified.

Follow-up: increased bridge costs fourfold at the user’s request: 24 wood loads, 16 stone loads and 192 person-hours. The gathering still gates on available unreserved materials and a useful site; it does not forecast harvest labour, food security or payback. Routine priority remains occupied-farm count, rather than measured bridge benefit.

Artwork: timber deck, stone abutments and oxide rails in the farm palette, oriented across the river; rebuilt with settlement visuals on completion and load. Page smoke and real Chromium checks cover the settlement renderer.
