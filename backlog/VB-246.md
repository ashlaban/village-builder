# VB-246: A benchmark that shows the three engine workarounds still pay
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (test infrastructure). Kim, 10 Nov 2026 (code review).
Goal: Three places in `src/` are written as they are because of the JavaScript engine, not the problem: `decode` gives an object all its keys before its values (`saves.js:155-167`) and `fadeRelations` copies a record's memories instead of deleting keys (`sift`, `relations.js:162`), both to keep objects out of dictionary mode (VB-205); and `driftPerson` is a function of its own to avoid repeated deoptimisation (`culture.js:190`, VB-195). Nothing notices if an engine update makes one useless or harmful.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `tests/perf.js --engine` (in verify, reporting, and failing only as below) times each of the three as it is against its plain form, on `small-y40` if the fixture is there and on a populous small run of 5 years if not: (1) a year of simulation after `Sim.load` with `decode` as it is, against a `decode` that sets each key in turn; (2) a year with `fadeRelations` as it is, against one that deletes the faded keys; (3) `Culture.dailyStep` over 48 days with `driftPerson` apart, against the loop written in place. The plain forms live in the test, not in `src/`.
- [x] It prints the three ratios, and fails when a workaround is slower than its plain form by more than the runs' spread in three runs of three: the message says which, and that the workaround can go. It does not fail when a workaround merely stops helping; it says so.
- [x] The three comments in `src/` name the test in place of the old measurement. Nothing in `src/` changes otherwise: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`.
- [x] The numbers of the first run in the PR and here, with the Node version.
