# VB-393: A boat is built only by a household with a surplus and two free hands
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods of place and side crafts. QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-300, question 1
Goal: Every eligible shore household builds a boat (64 and 71 households at populous small, seeds 7 and 42, in 15 years); a boat doubles the catch, so they fish less and summer idle rises (28.1 to 32.7% and 32.7 to 36.9%). The boat becomes rarer and dearer; the catch is unchanged.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The boat's kind is taken only by a household with food above its floor and a year's need, and two hands free of field work in the season; the catch with a boat is unchanged.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): boats built, summer idle, the catch and dried fish racked, hungry household-days in shore villages.
- [ ] Its note names what replaces it: the boat as a project chosen by the holder's values (VB-255), and tar to keep it (VB-301).
