# VB-393: A boat is built only by a household with a surplus and two free hands
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods of place and side crafts. QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-300, question 1
Goal: Every eligible shore household builds a boat (64 and 71 households at populous small, seeds 7 and 42, in 15 years); a boat doubles the catch, so they fish less and summer idle rises (28.1 to 32.7% and 32.7 to 36.9%). The boat becomes rarer and dearer; the catch is unchanged.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The boat's kind is taken only by a household with food above its floor and a year's need, and two hands free of field work in the season; the catch with a boat is unchanged.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): boats built, summer idle, the catch and dried fish racked, hungry household-days in shore villages.
- [x] Its note names what replaces it: the boat as a project chosen by the holder's values (VB-255), and tar to keep it (VB-301).
Notes:
- 12 Nov 2026: written from Kim's answers in the discussion of principles of that day (`AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim); the question and its numbers are in `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md` as the Design ref names.

Implementation notes (13 Nov 2026):
- `boatable(st, hh, f)` in `src/sim/drying.js` replaces the bare hands test of the yearly boat round: a household begins a boat only with grain above its
  floor (`floorOf`, which holds the seed reserve) and a year's need besides (`dailyNeed * YEAR_DAYS`), and `BOAT.hands` working hands that its fields do
  not need (`hands - ceil(fields / FIELDS.perHand)`). The catch with a boat is unchanged (`BOAT.catch` 2). No new state or counter.
- What replaces it: the boat as a project chosen by the holder's values (VB-255), and tar to keep it (VB-301, landed: an untarred boat leaks at 2 years and
  is lost at 4, and without the fair only the pit's household tars). This rule stands until VB-255.
- Reading of the criteria: "food" is grain (`grainOf`), not the larder.
