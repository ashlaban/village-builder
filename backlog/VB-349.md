## Implementation notes
- `src/sim/rules.js`: row `peas` (yield 60, seed 20, sow spring days 1-10, ripe 18, wear 0, new key `gain` 0.04, weeds 2, hours plow 20, sow 2, weed 5, reap 10, thresh 10) and `FOODS.pulses` (worth 1, no shelf date, source `pulses`, held as grain of its crop).
- `src/sim/fields.js` `wearSoil`: a crop with `gain` mends the soil to at most `SOIL.start`, never lowering a manured field (as fallow does).
- `src/sim/pot.js`: peas above their reservation are the food `pulses` in the pot, a source of their own: in a lean pot (2 or 3 sources) they go in first, up to the share that leaves grain a source, else after the grain. The grain cooked skips peas (`takeSpare(..., skip)` in `sim/stores`, threaded to `split`); the seed fallback is as before. A household with no peas takes the old path.
- Decision: threshing is a job of the barn over sheaves of every crop mixed, so it keeps barley hours (20); peas `thresh: 10` stands in the row, as rye does, unread until sheaves are told apart by crop.
- Peas are eaten uncooked as grain (hunger reads `grainOf`) and spoil as grain does.
- Tests: `tests/peas.js` (fast): the row; a forced field of peas at seed 7 run by the simulation (sown spring day 1, reaped 18 days on, 56 rations, soil 0.90 to 0.94, hours 20/2/5/10); soil to at most 1.0, manured field kept; frost; the pot (two sources, 10% less food; peas alone one source; peas held for seed not cooked). `tests/grain.js` and `tests/rye.js` list the new crop.
