# VB-476: The head's estimate, and the day allotted by it
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/review-blocks.md; design.md section 7 (A leg, and its end, to The timetable); docs/mocks/blocks/README.md
Goal: Households differ by their heads' judgement, for a reason the player can read.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `estimate(st, pawn, task)` gives {lo, mid, hi, unseen (the causes not reckoned with)}: computed when read, nothing stored but the lean (one more bias of VB-254's, drawn once). The order in which skill as head brings causes in (hunger and health, the crop's size, each hand's skill) is a named table.
- [ ] The range is honest: over a run, a head's tasks end inside its range at about the share the range claims, for poor and good heads alike (reported in thirds of skill).
- [x] `allot` (sim/jobs) mans a task until its estimated hours are covered and counts each member's believed hours; it weighs no more pairs than today (`dayPair`).
- [x] A notable event when a sowing or harvest window is missed and the estimate was outside its range: which cause the head did not reckon with.
- [ ] On a constructed state: a poor and a good head with the same heavy crop; the poor head mans it as a normal one. Numbers at populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42: hungry household-days and crops lost to frost by thirds of the head's skill.
