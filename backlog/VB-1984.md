# VB-1984: A boat-load is dealt at its landing
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-770, VB-813, VB-814
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: town
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Settlements, Towns and regions (A town before the ship; Where a town grows); Climate and travel beyond walking (Modes and vehicles; Reach by mode and season); docs/plans/VB-1924.md, sections 2 and 8
Touches: new sim/boatdeal; sim/fair; tools/sim.js; new tests/boatdeal.js
Goal: A household's boat-load is dealt at doors near the landing it came to, by the head's own weighing of the carrying, so that makers gather at the landing most boats reach
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A head planning the walk to a door (VB-770) or a goer to a green (VB-769) weighs the way by boat (the table by boat, VB-813) beside the way on foot, and takes the cheaper, with the household's boat and its load (150 rations in the small boat, 900 in the large, VB-812, VB-814); nothing is moved without the boat and the rowers.
- [ ] At the far landing the load is dealt at doors within a short carry: each door's cost is the walk there and back carrying the load in back-loads of 30 (or the household's cart, if it came), read by VB-770's own weighing; no rule, weight or bonus favours a landing or a place. A door the head did not know is learned as VB-770 learns one: a household seen dealing at that landing (at most 4 a good).
- [ ] The exports (fish, butter, firewood, hay; design.md, section 17) are carried by the same reckoning: a head holding them over its targets packs them as a boat-load when its estimate of a landing's doors covers the rowing.
- [ ] A test on a constructed state: two villages across water, a maker at the landing and one 6 tiles inland with the same goods; a boat-load from the other village is dealt with the one at the landing; with the inland maker's goods twice as wanted, the head's weighing (not a rule) decides.
- [ ] Reported by `tools/sim.js --report boats` (with `--help`): boat journeys a year by village, worth dealt at each landing, and the leading landing's share of the island's maker households (VB-839's measure), populous medium with boats, seeds 7 and 42, 60 years: against the plan's expectation of a quarter to a third (design.md, Towns and regions, A town before the ship); a shortfall is a `tuning` note naming the carrying cost as the lever, and does not hold the task.
- [ ] Cost (rule 9): about 100 households with a landing x 2 to 6 trips a year, each a journey with one leg by water, constant a leg; under 0.1 ms a game day, a year's mean, counted by a counter in `tests/counters.json`; times are the night's.
