Goal: The main building can be built up past the cottage, and its size matters to those who live in it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Room: each rung of the main building has a housing capacity (the people it sleeps without crowding: the booth's is `BOOTH.sleeps`; name the cottage's and the timber house's as tunables beside the rungs, `@tunable`, chosen so that an ordinary founders household fits its cottage). A household with more people living in the house than its rung's capacity is crowded: each of its members who is not of the head's own family (its tenants: lodgers and servants as the sim knows them; say exactly who, from the household's own records) carries a mood modifier `crowded` (sim/mood, as `tired` was added: VB-1615), one rule for every household.
- [x] The timber house: a rung `timber` after `cottage` in `RUNGS`: more room, and the day's firewood less by a named factor (a tunable, 0.8 to begin with) than a cottage's; built from the cottage by stages of its own (timber about 30 loads and 150 hours to begin with, tunables), the household living in the cottage meanwhile. State: the rung the main building stands at must now be stored where it cannot be derived (an owner in `src/owners.json`, `@state`, an invariant, saved; an older save reads as the rung derived today).
- [x] Built by the plan: `Sim.plan(st, 'add', {upgrade: houseTile, what: 'timber'})` through the existing `upgrade` chain (the timber at home first); `Sim.farmView().next` offers it on a cottage.
- [x] One rule for every household (Kim): an unplayed head builds its cottage up to a timber house by its own rule when its household is crowded and it has the hands and the season for it (through the makings or `ownRung`, sim/upgrade: say which), never while another of its large projects is under way.
- [x] Report before and after, founders seed 7 on the small map for 10 years and populous seed 7 small for 5 (`node tools/sim.js`, `node tests/counters.js`): the households crowded, the timber houses built, the mood, the firewood hours, the population; record what is out in `docs/tuning.md` through a `tuning` note; `node tests/counters.js --update` if the counters move by intent.
