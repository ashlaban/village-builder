# VB-1928: A kiln and bathhouse in the yard dries wet sheaves, malts barley and is the bath
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Settlements and look (The yard and beyond); Goods and the farming year (Risk); backlog/VB-1910.md; backlog/VB-1911.md
Touches: sim/facilities; sim/building; new sim/kilnhouse; tests/facilities.js; new tests/kilnhouse.js
Goal: A farm with a kiln and bathhouse dries its sheaves in a wet autumn so that less is lost, malts barley and gives a bath; a farm without one loses what VB-1910's wet autumn takes.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A kind declared in the facility registry (VB-1911): the kiln and bathhouse, built in stages from timber and stone, an owner, a condition, one fire for the three uses; it enables the recipes drying sheaves and malting and houses a bath, and gives no flat bonus.
- [ ] In a wet autumn (the weather VB-1910 reads) a household with the building dries the sheaves that lie out, at a cost in wood and hours per load, so that the share lost is less than the share lost without it; the numbers are set in the task and noted.
- [ ] Malting barley and the bath are one task each, each using the fire's wood; the bath's effect is the health or mood the design gives a bath, or none, as design.md says.
- [ ] tests/kilnhouse.js on a constructed wet autumn: with and without the building, the grain lost; the wood used; the numbers before and after on populous small seeds 7 and 42, 15 years.
