# VB-1960: Rent carried to an heir who lives elsewhere
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: physical
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, Handover and shares; Vision and scope, Principles for decisions (Nothing teleports)
Touches: sim/heirland (payRent); tests/heirsplit.js
Goal: A rent of split fields owed to an heir in another household is paid where it lies and fetched, not left owed
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A rent owed to an heir who no longer lives in the household that owes it (its own house built, or married into another) changes owner where it lies, in the debtor's barn, and is fetched by a pawn of the owner's household (the one mechanism VB-1175 collects for shares and gifts, not a second beside it); `sim/heirland` payRent pays it so, in place of leaving it owed.
- [ ] tests/heirsplit.js: a rent owed when the house stands is fetched: the new household's grain rises only when the carrier arrives.
