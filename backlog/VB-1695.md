# VB-1695: Review: dead bands weighed against the cost of undoing
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Principles for decisions (What can be undone costs something to undo); QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Archived 24 November 2026, question 11
Goal: A reversible choice is held by the real cost of reversing it, which the chooser weighs; a named dead band remains only where the world charges no such cost.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Every slack, hold or dead band on a reversible choice is listed (at least `FIELDS.slack`, `P.hold`, and those of PRs #199 and #234), with the cost of reversing that choice in the world (the hours to break a field again, a move's loads).
- [ ] For each, the chooser weighs that cost; the slack is then set to what remains (zero where the cost suffices) or kept with a written reason.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous small seeds 7 and 42, 10 years): how often each choice flips, and the work it costs.
