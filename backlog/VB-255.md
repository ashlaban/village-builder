# VB-255: Projects: a head with a surplus takes one, by its values
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-123, VB-254
Priority: 2
Size: M

Goal: Layer 3 of how a holder chooses: a head whose year is secured turns to something more, and which thing depends on who it is. An ambitious head takes land; a generous one builds for its heir.
Decided (Kim, 10 November; `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-255: the barn as a project"): the barn and the base weights, as in the criteria.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Surplus: at the review after the autumn count, a head has a surplus when every target of its plan is met, its store holds food beyond the year's need and the seed (the means to marry's measure, 1.5 years, is the first value), and its household's adults were idle over a third of their work hours in the last season (read from a count the matcher keeps per household and season, not from the record).
- [ ] The menu, each an opportunity computed for the holder (never stored): a field beyond `fieldsWanted`, up to what the hands can work; a claim on land (a stake, by the rules of the stakes as built); a farm or cottage for the heir (the heir an adult at home with no holding; the steps of a plot and a cottage as built); a barn: a second store building on the farm's block, one per farm, built by the steps of a plot's cottage for 40 timber and its hours, drawn on the map beside the house (`src/page/`, so `npm run verify`); a farm with a barn loses half as much grain to spoilage (`ECON.storeLoss`, `src/sim/hunger.js`) and half as much barn hay (`src/sim/byre.js`). `@state` for the farm's barn, an invariant, and a test of both halvings.
- [ ] The choice, when the head has a surplus and no project: each open option's weight is a base weight times the head's lean: base 1 for each, but 2 for the heir's farm when the head has two or more potential heirs (those the custom of inheritance would rank, adults or not, so that one would be left without the home farm); the lean is `1 + ambition` for the field and the claim, `1 + generosity` for the heir's farm, 1 for the barn; one draw from the stream `plans` (new in `SYSTEMS`). Made at most once a year per holder, and when a project ends. One project per holder at a time.
- [ ] A project is a project plan (VB-123): its steps open in turn, what it will spend is reserved, and it is reviewed at once, and dropped with its reservations released, when a step becomes impossible or the surplus is gone for a whole season.
- [ ] Dried fish for the fair (moved from VB-300, 11 Nov 2026), once VB-300 has landed: in a shore village a project to make more dried fish than the household eats, aimed at the fair; the target is stated in rations.
- [ ] Events: taking and finishing a project are notable events of the head and the household (`log`, `personEvent`, with kind, pawn and place; `tests/record.js`).
- [ ] Numbers at populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42, before and after: projects taken and finished by kind, the share of heads who ever had a surplus (by skill in thirds), land held by the top tenth of households, heirs who start with a farm, hungry household-days (the mean and the worst household), and the idle share by season. Heads in the top third of ambition take more land than the bottom third at every seed; hungry household-days do not rise at any seed by more than a tenth. Re-record the golden file and list the changes.
- [ ] Cost measured at `medium-ref`: the menu is computed only for a holder with a surplus and no project. `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals.
Cost: holders with a surplus × 1 a year × at most 4 options.
