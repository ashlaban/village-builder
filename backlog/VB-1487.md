# VB-1487: A lone holder takes the ranks of the roles its household lacks
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Design ref: backlog/VB-1484.md; src/sim/roles.js (the roles' ranks); design.md, Who may take a job
Goal: a household of one does the household's whole round, and solo's dry days fall to the couples' 2 to 5%
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A general rule, not a case for water: for each kind of work, a household's holder ranks it as the highest-ranking role would that the household has no working member of (no mistress: her household 9), and at its own rank where the role is filled. Which roles count as absent (none of that role alive, of age and at home) is stated in the code and in design.md beside the roles.
- [ ] The rule is read where the ranks are read, once, with no branch for solo or for a kind; a household of two or more with every role filled is unchanged (`node tools/same.js --base REF` on founders and populous, or the differences listed and explained: widowers and widows are expected to change).
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state (tests/tiny.js): a holder alone ranks water as a mistress does; with a mistress added, as a farmer.
- [ ] Measured before and after on solo small, seeds 7 and 42, 4 years, and on founders and populous small seed 7: dry days, hungry days, health, grain at each year's end, and hours by job kind. VB-1484's counterfactual (rank 9 for water alone): 129 to 8 and 115 to 16 dry days, health 100.
- [ ] Report whether households were better off, and what the lone holder gave up to do the round (field and hay hours): two of VB-1484's counterfactuals that brought the water forward by other means cost 31 and 71 hungry days, untraced. If hunger rises, say why before the task is closed.
