## Implementation notes (VB-993)
- `release` (sim/agents): a pawn released with a cart out and no load laid, on a tile other than its farm's house, now strands the cart
  there by the same `standCart` (extracted from VB-649's code: `Carts.leave(.., {tile,x,y})` plus the lying `leftCart`). No new mechanism.
- Test: tests/standingcart.js, a manure cart's pawn released away from the farm. Invariants as VB-649 (unchanged, hold).
- Numbers (populous 120x85 map, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years, 3 h steps): carts stood away before 0 and 0, after 472 and 594; carts lost at the
  frost (cartGone) 0 and 0 before, 9 and 7 after; living at the end 178 and 183 before, 179 and 184 after. Dung and firewood hours lost
  to fetching were not measured separately (no counter): the case is frequent, not rare, so this is owed to the tuning/bench run.
