# VB-212: The moving round's acts: the player's wish stands in for its pawn's own
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-210
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player and what they see (The round's acts: Decided, 8 Nov: "the same rules should apply to player controlled pawn as to other pawns to the extent possible"; Marriage: Decided, 8 Nov); Inheritance and expansion (Leaving home, Empty farms, Households short of people, Moving to new land); `docs/review-life.md`, section 3.
Goal: The big acts of a life (moving out, claiming an empty farm, going into service, settling new land) happen at the moving round, by the round's rules. Where the round asks what a pawn itself wants, the player's wish should stand in, and the round should decide as it does for anyone, at the round.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A survey in this file of every place the round reads what a pawn wants, with file and line, each sorted as either the pawn's own wish (the player's may stand in) or a rule (it stays). It starts from `wantsOut` (`src/sim/heirs.js:371-379`), which builds `st.demand`, from which claims (`heirs.js:320`, `src/sim/farmclaims.js:120`) and settling parties (`src/sim/settle.js:226`) are chosen; and service, chosen by `src/sim/service.js:98`. Proposed sorting:
  - rules: alive, adult, not retired, not a holder, not restoring a claim;
  - the pawn's own wish: the prospect of an heir's place or a place if married.
- [ ] `st.player.wishes` (`{out, claim, serve}`: each `null` for "as the pawn would", or the player's choice; `claim` a farm id or `false`), set by `Sim.wish(st, kind, value)` and kept across rounds until changed; owned by `sim/commands`; invariants added. The round reads it at the points the survey names as the pawn's wish, and nowhere else.
- [ ] The pawn panel (VB-210) offers the wishes under "at the moving round", with the rule that bars each now if one does.
- [ ] Tests: a landless adult whose player wishes to stay is left out of `st.demand`, and so neither claims nor leaves; an heir whose player wishes to move out enters it, where the survey sorts the heir's prospect as its wish; a wish never lets a pawn past a rule (a holder never enters `st.demand`); with no player, the golden file is unchanged.
- [ ] Marriage is unchanged: the round matches the player's pawn as anyone (Decided, 8 Nov).
