# VB-212: The moving round's acts: the player's wish stands in for its pawn's own
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player and what they see (The round's acts: Decided, 8 Nov: "the same rules should apply to player controlled pawn as to other pawns to the extent possible"; Marriage: Decided, 8 Nov); Inheritance and expansion (Leaving home, Empty farms, Households short of people, Moving to new land); `docs/review-life.md`, section 3.
Goal: The big acts of a life (moving out, claiming an empty farm, going into service, settling new land) happen at the moving round, by the round's rules. Where the round asks what a pawn itself wants, the player's wish should stand in, and the round should decide as it does for anyone, at the round.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A survey in this file of every place the round reads what a pawn wants, with file and line, each sorted as either the pawn's own wish (the player's may stand in) or a rule (it stays). It starts from `wantsOut` (`src/sim/heirs.js:371-379`), which builds `st.demand`, from which claims (`heirs.js:320`, `src/sim/farmclaims.js:120`) and settling parties (`src/sim/settle.js:226`) are chosen; and service, chosen by `src/sim/service.js:98`. Proposed sorting:
  - rules: alive, adult, not retired, not a holder, not restoring a claim;
  - the pawn's own wish: the prospect of an heir's place or a place if married.
- [x] `st.player.wishes` (`{out, claim, serve}`: each `null` for "as the pawn would", or the player's choice; `claim` a farm id or `false`), set by `Sim.wish(st, kind, value)` and kept across rounds until changed; owned by `sim/commands`; invariants added. The round reads it at the points the survey names as the pawn's wish, and nowhere else.
- [x] The pawn panel (VB-210) offers the wishes under "at the moving round", with the rule that bars each now if one does.
- [x] Tests: a landless adult whose player wishes to stay is left out of `st.demand`, and so neither claims nor leaves; an heir whose player wishes to move out enters it, where the survey sorts the heir's prospect as its wish; a wish never lets a pawn past a rule (a holder never enters `st.demand`); with no player, the golden file is unchanged.
- [x] Marriage is unchanged: the round matches the player's pawn as anyone (Decided, 8 Nov).

The wishes only narrow (Kim, 10 Nov): `out` alone decides `st.demand`; a wish for a farm or against service does not put an heir into it.

Implementation (10 Nov 2026): `st.player.wishes` (`{out, claim, serve}`, each `null` at `inhabit`), set by `Sim.wish(st, kind, value)` (`wish` in `src/sim/commands.js`, which checks the shape only: the round decides), kept until changed, and read through `wishOf(st, id, kind)` in `src/sim/records.js` (below `sim/farmclaims` in load order, which `sim/commands` is not) at the four points the survey names: `staysWhy`, `claimants`, `handOn`, `takeServants`. `staysWhy` takes the wish as its last argument, so `Sim.opportunities` can ask what the pawn itself would do: the played pawn's `leave` carries `own`, and each of the round's acts says what bars it now, the wishes counted in. The pawn panel (`roundBox` in `src/page/play.js`) offers the three wishes under "At the moving round" with those reasons, and says that a couple moves together. An older save's player loads with no wishes (`src/sim/saves.js`); invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js`.

Impact note: reads `st.player` in `sim/records` (the reader), and through it in `sim/heirs`, `sim/farmclaims` and `sim/service`; writes `st.player.wishes` only in `sim/commands`, its owner. No new module; one new state field, saved with the player. New coupling: the three round modules read the player's wishes, through one function. No abstraction added: a verb would not fit, since a wish is not an act and is not checked against the rules when made.

Cost: `wishOf` is two comparisons a call, at the round only: once per living pawn in `countDemand`, once per claimant candidate, once per waiting adult in `takeServants`. Nothing is added to a step.

Validation (10 Nov 2026): `npm run verify` passes (98 scripts); `npm test` passes (84 scripts, with the new `tests/wishes.js`: the criteria's four cases, a farm wished for and none, service declined, no wishes hashing as no player over a round and a day, saved and loaded). Golden file: the behaviour entries are unchanged; `apiKeys: Sim` changes, for the new `Sim.wish`. `tests/browser.js` sets a wish from the panel and takes it back. No numbers before and after: with no player, and with a player who makes no wish, the run is unchanged.
