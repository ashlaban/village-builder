Goal: With VB-119 merged (Kim, 12 November), fresh fish spoils and the poorest households are hungrier: over 30 years at populous small 120,000 to 200,000 rations of fresh fish spoil (40 to 46% of the fish landed for food) while 35,000 to 56,000 are dried, and household-years hungry on over 0.3 of their days went 0/0/0/0 to 6/0/5/2 at seeds 3, 7, 11, 42. Kim: drying the fish and cooking the seed are this separate task.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The surplus is dried, not lost: the drying want (`DRY`, `src/sim/drying.js`, VB-300) grows from a quarter of the winter's need to the winter's fish share of the pot (`sim/pot`, VB-119), and its season runs to the end of autumn; fresh fish a household holds beyond what it will eat before it spoils goes to the rack (`hh.rack`) while the rack has room and the season is open.
- [ ] Fishing for the pot waits while the fresh fish held covers the next 2 days of the pot: the fish job's urgency counts fresh fish, not only grain (today a household with grain fishes daily and the catch spoils). Fishing for the rack (`rackfish`) is not held by it.
- [ ] The seed a short household eats is cooked like other grain: the cook cooks from the seed what the coming day's meal would take from it (as `feedHousehold` does), so `ECON.rawGrain` (1.25) falls only on days the cook is missed. The seed reservation's rules are otherwise unchanged (`Stores.reserved`, a crop each, VB-347).
- [ ] Measured by VB-119's runs (populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42), before and after: household-years hungry on over 0.3 of their days (the aim: back to what it was before VB-119, 0/0/0/0, or the residual explained household by household), hungry days, grain at harvest, fish landed, dried and spoilt fresh (the aim: well under the 40 to 46% of today), the share of mixed pots, days eaten uncooked, summer idle hours; and whether households, not only averages, are better off, and which are not.
- [ ] Tests (`tests/pot.js`, `tests/drying.js` or their like): a household holding two days of fresh fish posts no fishing for the pot; a catch beyond what it can eat is on the rack the next day and none of it spoils while the rack has room; a short household's seed is eaten cooked when a cook is free. Invariants as they are; the golden file re-recorded and its changed entries listed; `npm test` passes and the hard checks of verify.
- [ ] Cost (rule 9): one comparison a household when the fish job is asked and one when the day's fresh fish is counted; no new loop.
