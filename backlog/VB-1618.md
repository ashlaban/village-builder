# VB-1618: Evaluate independent review: reviewed tasks against held-out ones, and Kim's decision
Status: blocked
Waits for: Kim
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: backlog/VB-1615.md (Evaluation method); tools/review.js eval; docs/agents/usage
Goal: The decision whether independent review stays, is narrowed or stops, made on the evidence the method of VB-1615 fixed before the data came in.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The window is closed: at least 60 tasks of the control arm done since VB-1615's merge (single-task sessions and nights alike), and 14 days past the last of them; the eligibility rule and the holdout unchanged meanwhile (`tools/review.js`, `HOLDOUT`).
- [ ] `node tools/review.js eval` run and its output in this file: per arm the tasks done and observed, the escapes (tasks whose `Cause:` names them, made within 14 days, not opened by their own review) and escapes a task; the ratio, reviewed against control, with its exact 90% interval; median cost and minutes a task; for the reviewed arm the findings by grade, the confirmed catches, the false blockers, the tasks merged with a finding unresolved, the cost a catch and the break-even.
- [ ] By kind (plan; M or L behaviour; S with new structure), where the catches concentrate; and the share of tasks done at the first attempt by arm (`node tools/agent-usage.js --history docs/agents/usage`).
- [ ] Kim's decision, proposed by VB-1615: keep review for all eligible tasks if the break-even holds and the ratio's upper bound is at most 1.25; narrow it to the kinds where catches concentrate if it holds only there; stop it if more than half of blocking findings are false blockers or the break-even fails in every kind. Recorded here; `HOLDOUT` set to false (or the review removed) by the task that carries the decision out.
- Power, stated before the data (VB-1615): about 60 control and 120 reviewed tasks detect a halved escape rate of 15% with a power of about 0.4; the escape comparison guards against harm, the break-even carries most of the decision.
- Threats: the arm is in the run files and the PR, not the task file, so whoever writes a `Cause:` line does not see it; an implementer who knows a review comes may work differently (the cost by arm shows it); the replay of 17 to 24 November made 72% of tasks eligible, partly from merges inflating reconstructed diffs.
