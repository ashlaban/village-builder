# VB-1618: The player's plan: a daily routine, sleeping hours and an ordered list of tasks that feed the queue
Status: done (2026-11-24)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/ui2/HANDOFF.md (Kim's direction, 24 November; Next); src/sim/player.js (the queue, playerStep, the sleep verb); src/sim/agenda.js; design.md, The player and what they see
Goal: A player plans the day and the days ahead by hand, and the hours are scarce (Kim, 24 Nov).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] State `st.player.plan` (plain data, saved, with its `@state` line, its owner and an invariant; absent in an older save reads as empty): `{routine: [kind...] (job kinds seen to every day, in order), sleep: null | {from, until} (hours of the day, e.g. 22 and 6), tasks: [{key (a job's key), kind, site (its tile or -1)}] (in order, at most PLAN_MAX, about 24), day, done: [kind...] (the routine kinds seen to on game day `day`)}`.
- [x] One act on the `Sim` facade, `Sim.plan(st, op, args)`, true or why not, the player's as `queue` and `setAuto` are: `add {key, at?}` (a posted job the pawn is eligible for, whether or not it may take it this minute; no key twice), `remove {i}`, `move {from, to}`, `routine {kinds}` (kinds of `sim/kinds` only), `sleep {from, until}` or `sleep null`. And a read `Sim.planView(st)`: the routine with, for each kind, whether it is done today and whether a job of it is open; the sleeping hours; the tasks with whether each one's job still stands; and what the plan has the pawn on now (`routine`, `task`, `sleep` or null, with its kind or key).
- [x] The feeder, in `playerStep`, only with automatic planning off (with it on the plan is kept and not followed), and only when the player's queue is empty: a command queued by hand goes before the plan, as an order for now.
  1. In the sleeping hours, a pawn not asleep and not under a command queued by hand leaves the plan's work in hand (which keeps its progress) and sleeps until `until`, by the `sleep` verb. Work ordered by hand is not broken off: that is how a night is worked, at sleep's cost (VB-1615).
  2. Else the first routine kind not done today that has an open job of the pawn's household it may take: begun by the `work` verb and marked done for the day when begun. A kind with no job open yet is tried again later in the day.
  3. Else the first task whose job stands and may be taken now. A task whose job is finished or gone leaves the plan; the last one so dropped is kept for the page (`plan.last {key, kind, t}`), as `refused` keeps the last refusal.
  A plan-fed command goes through the queue and the verbs as any command does (source `player`), marked so the feeder and the page can tell it from one queued by hand.
- [x] A task broken off for the night is taken up again in the morning without the player's doing; a test shows a clearing of more than a day's hours done over two days with a night slept between, and the hours owed back near zero in the morning.
- [x] Nothing moves without a pawn: every act is a verb the pawn carries out. A run with no played pawn, and one played with automatic planning on and an empty plan, are unchanged (`node tools/same.js --base REF`, founders and solo).
- [x] `tests/plan.js` on a small constructed state, in `tests/suites.js`: each op and its refusals; the routine is seen to once a day in order; the order of tasks is kept; a hand-queued command goes first; sleep by the plan's hours; a finished task leaves; a save and a load keep the plan and go on the same. `tests/player.js`, `tests/saves.js`, `tests/plaindata.js`, `tests/commands.js` pass.

## Notes
- 24 Nov 2026 (Kim, in session): the played settler is planned by hand, automatic off; a daily routine for the necessities; sleep is a planned act; night work at a cost. This is Kim's word for the capability; how a manual and an automatic plan meet is for later and not this task's.
- Measured before (solo, probe island seed 7, automatic off, nothing queued): nobody fetched water or cooked; the settler walked to drink every other day and lost about a point of health a day.
- VB-1619 (the agenda lays the plan into days) follows this and reads the same rule: keep the feeder's choice of what comes next in one function the view can call without writing.
- 24 Nov (VB-1618, decisions): the plan is its own module, `sim/playerplan` (owner of st.player.plan; `nextOf` the one read-only choice VB-1619 can call), so `sim/player` grows by the feeder only. The plan is written lazily: a pawn played with no plan has no `plan` field, so saves and hashes are unchanged.
- A plan-fed command carries `plan {from, kind, key}`. A hand command queued behind a plan-fed head drops the plan's commands at once (a task keeps its progress and stays planned; a routine kind begun stays done for the day). The feeder pre-checks each verb, so it never fills `refused` step after step.
- In the sleeping hours the plan always queues `sleep` (also for a pawn already in bed), so nothing else is fed then; with no sleeping hours the plan works on through the night.
- Routine `open` in the view = a job of the kind posted for the household; the view also gives `next` (what would be fed) and `followed` (automatic off).
- A task is matched by its job key: a job finished and posted again under the same key in the same step would be taken as the same task (a clearing's next tile reuses `clear:fN`; seen only across a review, after the task had left).
- same.js founders,solo seeds 7,42: same; no pawn is played there, the played-with-automatic-on case is tests/player.js part 1 (hash unchanged), and the feeder runs only with a plan and automatic off.
