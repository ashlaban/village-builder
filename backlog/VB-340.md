Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] New archipelago opens the start in place of the form: the three built scenarios as picture cards in a row (name, one line, people at the chosen size, generations on record), the chosen one marked; no scenario that is not built is offered.
- [ ] The annotated picture: the showcase island (`buildDemo`, `page/loader`) is re-seeded with the chosen scenario and shown in a framed view of the stage, the camera on a village of it; three or four callouts, each an icon and a few words, are anchored to real things of that run and follow the camera: founders, the houses, the well, a meadow tile with no field, forest; heirs, the empty farm, a farm lived in, a field, the ruin (a second small view); populous, a village's green, a field, and the island whole (a second small view). A scenario the showcase island has no room for falls back to another seed of `DEMO.seeds`.
- [ ] Who starts, from the showcase run and marked as an example: 30 people or fewer, every farm as a small card (the farm's icon, name, its people's portraits; an empty farm and a ruin as dashed cards; founders without farm names); more, an age pyramid, women to the left and men to the right, with the count.
- [ ] Story archetypes: VB-339's cards, unchanged in look. Here a card is a look: pointing at or tapping one lights up those people in Who starts (portraits, or their share of each bar) and selects nothing; the layout does not move.
- [ ] The year ahead in four short steps for the chosen scenario, and what is unusual in its stores and customs, as an icon and a line each.
- [ ] Map size and seed in the bottom bar beside Back and Raise the land; Raise the land builds the world as Generate did (`build`), and VB-339's mode follows. The error for a map with no room is shown in the bar.
- [ ] One column under 860 px; no sideways scroll at 400 px. The principles of Starting a game hold.
- [ ] The page's functional tests cover it: each scenario can be chosen and raised from the start; the callouts exist and sit inside the view; pointing at an archetype changes no element's size. `npm run verify` once; `index.html` rebuilt; no change under `src/sim/`; `tests/golden.json` unchanged.
