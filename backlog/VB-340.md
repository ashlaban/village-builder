- Implementation (11 Nov 2026): `seedHeirs` (`src/sim/scenarios.js`) looks for village A on a landmass of 1,500 tiles first and, where none
  has them, on one of the requirement scaled to the map as founders' `minLand` is (60 tiles on 56 by 40); village B's rules were left as
  they were, since none of them is what fails (at seeds 43 and 54 the villages are laid out with too few farms, "Not enough room for the
  debug villages", which is not a size rule). So heirs seeds on the showcase at 8 of the 10 seeds of `DEMO.seeds`, not at 43 and 54;
  the start uses `DEMO.start` = 7, 12, 15, 26, 28, 29, 75, 98, the seeds where all three scenarios fit (the menu's island keeps its ten).
  `src/page/start.js` (new) is the start: `openStart` (New archipelago, whom to play's Back, and a build that found no room, whose message
  goes in the bar) keeps an open game aside (`aside`: its world, run and painted overview, and the camera; `stagePainted` is now kept by
  `swapWorld`), raises the showcase for the chosen scenario on the start's seed (the menu's own where it is in `DEMO.start`, whose run is
  reused) and the other two behind it, each once (`runOf`); `startBack` (Back, or Esc) brings the game aside back before the main menu,
  so Back to game returns to it, to whom to play if that was open. All changes of the stage go one after another (`onStage`); `build`
  waits for them and drops the start and the game aside only once its run is seeded (`dropStart`), so a map with no room leaves both as
  they were. The picture is a frame in the sheet through which the stage shows (`#startView`, no veil); `startCamera` (page/frame, each
  frame) frames the village's houses and callouts first and then keeps the frame's world in it as the sheet scrolls or the map is dragged
  and zoomed through it (on a wide screen; on a phone the page scrolls over it); `placeCallouts` puts each mark at its thing, or at the
  edge with an arrow when the thing is outside, and its words beside it on the first side with room that covers no other. Callouts:
  founders, a house, the well, the nearest meadow tile with no field, the nearest forest; heirs, the empty farm (its years read), a farm
  lived in (the number lived in, in words), a field, the ruin (its years and village read; an edge callout when out of view); populous,
  the largest village (its name and farms), a field, and the number of villages in words in the picture's corner. The cards: the picture
  is the game's own icons of the framed village (its village icon and farms, an empty one greyed), the name, a line, people at the chosen
  size from `START_PEOPLE` and the generations on record read from that scenario's run. Who starts and the archetypes read the shown run
  (`storyCards` unchanged; an archetype pointed at, or tapped, re-renders Who starts only; lit portraits change opacity and filter, lit bars
  a gradient stop). The year ahead: the mock's for founders and populous; for heirs the waiting heir's and the unmarried holder's ages
  read. The rows: founders' stores (the scenario's), and the customs read from each village's practice against the old Nordic one. The
  bar: size, seed with Another, the error, Back and Raise the land (`#genForm` submits to `build` as Generate did; `#genScenario` is now a
  hidden input the cards set, so the tools that fill the form still work). While a menu screen is up the game's keys wait, and a click
  through the frame picks nothing.
