# VB-340: The start: scenario cards, the annotated picture, who starts and story archetypes
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, The player, commands and views, Starting a game (11 November); docs/mocks/setup.html (step 1)
Goal: Build step 1 of Starting a game in place of the generation form.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] New archipelago opens the start in place of the form: the three built scenarios as picture cards in a row (name, one line, people at the chosen size, generations on record; populous's people as a range for each size in a table that a test checks against `Sim.seed` runs), the chosen one marked; no scenario that is not built is offered.
- [ ] The annotated picture: the showcase island (`buildDemo`, `page/loader`) is re-seeded with the chosen scenario and shown in a framed view of the stage, the camera on a village of it; three or four callouts, each an icon and a few words, are anchored to real things of that run and follow the camera: founders, the houses, the well, a meadow tile with no field, forest; heirs, the empty farm, a farm lived in, a field, and a callout at the picture's edge towards the ruin on the far shore; populous, a village's green, a field, and the number of villages in words. One view, no inset (Kim, 11 Nov). Heirs fits the 56 by 40 showcase island: `seedHeirs` (`src/sim/scenarios.js`) keeps its 1,500-tile landmass where one exists and otherwise falls back to the requirement scaled to the map, as founders' is (`minLand`); `tests/golden.json` unchanged, since maps where it fits today take the first branch; a test seeds heirs on the showcase size at every seed of `DEMO.seeds`. A game that is open is kept aside while the start shows the showcase and is restored by Back to game, and Whom to play's Back returns to the start the same way.
- [ ] Who starts, from the showcase run and marked as an example: 30 people or fewer, every farm as a small card (the farm's icon, name, its people's portraits; an empty farm and a ruin as dashed cards; founders without farm names); more, an age pyramid, women to the left and men to the right, with the count.
- [ ] Story archetypes: VB-339's cards, unchanged in look. Here a card is a look: pointing at or tapping one lights up those people in Who starts (portraits, or their share of each bar) and selects nothing; the layout does not move.
- [ ] The year ahead in four short steps for the chosen scenario, and what is unusual in its stores and customs, as an icon and a line each.
- [ ] Map size and seed in the bottom bar beside Back and Raise the land; Raise the land builds the world as Generate did (`build`), and VB-339's mode follows. The error for a map with no room is shown in the bar.
- [ ] One column under 860 px; no sideways scroll at 400 px. The principles of Starting a game hold.
- [ ] The page's functional tests cover it: each scenario can be chosen and raised from the start; the callouts exist and sit inside the view; pointing at an archetype changes no element's size. `npm run verify` once; `index.html` rebuilt; under `src/sim/` only the heirs landmass fallback; `tests/golden.json` unchanged.

  finds no room on the showcase island at any seed of `DEMO.seeds`. `seedHeirs` (`src/sim/scenarios.js`) puts village A on a landmass of
  at least 1,500 tiles; the showcase (`DEMO`, 56 by 40) averages 838 land tiles over its ten seeds, and `Sim.seed(wd, seed, 'heirs')`
  throws "No village site found" at all ten. Founders (10 people) and populous (130 to 146) fit at all ten. Heirs fits at 84 by 60 at
  seeds 7, 15, 26, 28, 75 (5 of 10), at 96 by 68 at 6 of 10, at Tiny (120 by 85) at all ten; founders and populous fit at every one of
  those seeds at 84 by 60. So the criterion "a scenario the showcase island has no room for falls back to another seed of `DEMO.seeds`"
  cannot be met for heirs, which is the stop the task and its brief name. Nothing was changed in the code.
- Measured on the way (Chromium, headless, the page's own `G.generate`, `Sim.seed`, `paintOverview` and `swapWorld`, one call each): a
  re-seed of the 56 by 40 showcase takes 0.18 to 0.25 s (generation 115 to 160 ms, seeding 11 to 50 ms, the overview and the swap 20 to
  65 ms); at 84 by 60, 0.18 to 0.49 s (heirs 0.18 s, populous 0.49 s, generation being most of it); 96 by 68, about 0.5 s. Re-seeding at
  each card is fast enough at either size; and generation can be kept for a seed when only the scenario changes, leaving 30 to 90 ms.
