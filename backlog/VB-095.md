# VB-095: Family tree by scope, fast at full scale
Status: needs-review
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: claims
Phase: implement
Design ref: Commands, time steps and views (Family tree: shows the genealogy of any entity, from one pawn to a whole culture: Decided; Performance: must stay fast: Decided, how Open; Keeping the family tree fast: Proposed); Claims, ownership and territory (One scope model: Agreed, 30 Oct).
Goal: `layoutTree` in `src/body.html` lays out every pawn ever born on each open and draws every node. It takes about 10 ms for the 818 founding pawns of populous medium, and calls `S.unions.filter` per pawn, inside a sort comparator too, so it grows with pawns × unions; a 150-year run holds several thousand records and the SVG holds a node for each. Show the tree for a scope (VB-043) and draw only what is on screen. Depends on VB-043; if it is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria (draft, pending `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-095):
- [ ] The tree opens on a scope: by default the selected pawn's village, its living members and the generations above and below them as answered (proposed: 2 up, 1 down). A level control switches to household, village or culture, and the selected pawn's line.
- [ ] Branches outside the scope fold behind a "+ n more" node that opens them in place.
- [ ] Spouses, children and generations come from the indexes of VB-052, not from scans of `st.unions` or `st.pawns`.
- [ ] Only nodes within the visible window plus a margin are in the SVG; panning adds and removes nodes.
- [ ] Measured on a state of at least 6,000 records (a 150-year founders run, or a populous run with records copied): open under 100 ms, panning above 50 fps in the Chromium test (VB-025) if it is done. Report before and after.
- [ ] The tree's content (father, mother, spouse with kinship, children, siblings, life events; dead greyed, heirs marked) is unchanged.
