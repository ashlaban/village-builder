# VB-736: Mining a found vein: rock ore dug from a pit by fire-setting
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-733
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The metals and the stones: goods by purpose, kinds by property; Goods, recipes and facilities); `docs/plans/VB-702.md`; `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (17)
Goal: The veins `sim/prospect` finds are worked: a found vein is a source a head can choose to dig, the first use of the upland ores.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A job kind of `sim/dig` at a vein tile the village has found, within `DIG.reach` of the house, where `mayDig` allows (its own land, its village's outland, the wilderness): a load a trip to the household's store, the digger's household owning it (Kim, 16 Nov). `MINE`: 3 hours and 1 load of firewood from the household's pile a load of ore at the surface; 6 hours and 1.5 loads once the tile holds less than half of what it began with (Proposed). The tile's loads fall; an endless tile does not.
- [ ] Ore is a good with kinds (VB-733): `ore/bog` (today's `bogOre`), `ore/iron`, `ore/copper`, `ore/tin`, `ore/leadSilver`, each with its richness (metal a load); gold is dug as metal (VB-741 gives it its use). The head's target for each (VB-698) is what its smelting takes; with no furnace and no one asking, none is dug.
- [ ] Rock iron ore goes into VB-701's bloom at a named better yield (`ore/iron` richer than `ore/bog`: Proposed 3 loads a bloom against 4).
- [ ] Nothing teleports: ore is carried home by the digger, a load a trip; a verb in `sim/commands.js` for the act, with its check; the played pawn has it.
- [ ] Invariants (ore counts whole and not negative; a tile's loads never rise but bog iron's), `tools/stocks.js` lines (what happens at none), a work counter if a loop over veins is added. Cost named: `sim/dig`'s kept search, one more want a household.
- [ ] Numbers, populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15, 30 years: veins found and worked, loads dug by kind, wood burnt in pits against the village's wood, villages that mine, years a worked vein would last at the rate seen (the design asks about three generations: report, and a `tuning` row if far off), hungry household-days before and after.
- [ ] The numbers that move are reported before and after at the seeds and maps named (Working rules); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
