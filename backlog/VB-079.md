## Plan (2026-11-07)

Run interactively (started by Kim from the app): the plan is shown, and the real choices are asked; whatever is not answered goes to
`QUESTIONS.md` with a proposed answer.

**Context.**
- Pawns act only through `issue` (`src/sim/commands.js:157`); the planner is today's one source of work, at four sites: idle pawns bid
  every 6 minutes (`src/sim/clock.js:95-104`, `src/sim/jobs.js:193`), working pawns look up each hour (`jobs.js:130`), cooks are called
  off other work (`jobs.js:32`), and all go home at the end of work (`clock.js:40-46`). A player is a second caller of the same verbs,
  and those four sites are where the planner must step aside.
- Jobs are posted by one loop per farm, field by field, in a fixed order (`jobs.js:45-94`); a job's id is its posting order
  (`jobs.js:22`) and matching visits jobs by id (`jobs.js:126`, `181`). Moving the rules into the kinds keeps the golden file only if
  the posting order is kept.
- The meeting has no ballots: support is the mean of attitudes weighted by voice and prestige (`src/culture.js:240-248`), and practice
  moves when the leading option beats it by a margin (`culture.js:249-268`). A pawn's one lever is advocacy over years
  (`culture.js:212`, the debug verb `argue`, `commands.js:136`). An answer has to be a new term in that mean.
- `recordOf` filters the whole record per query (`src/sim/records.js:19-26`): ambitions cannot read it in a step (110,278 events at
  small year 40, VB-199).
- Saves are format 1 and other formats are refused (`src/sim/saves.js:102-113`), in Node only. `docs/bench/budget.md` (5 November,
  stale fixtures): save 1.09 s, load 0.32 s, 19 MB of JSON at medium-y5; save 6.0 s at medium-y60.
- The pawn panel is rebuilt every 0.25 s of real time (`src/page/animate.js:144-150`, `src/page/words.js:166`), about 4 game hours at
  30×; what it shows of a pawn's opportunities is computed at that rate, in the page.
- The successor list can be built from what exists: heirs (`src/sim/heirs.js:19`), kin (`records.js:177-193`), ties
  (`src/sim/relations.js:259`).
- Already decided: the player role, which pawn, ambitions that grant nothing, the omniscient actor view as a seam, the floor warning
  (`QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, 3 November); saves in IndexedDB, the last 3, other builds refused (30 October); a controlled pawn left alone
  by the planner until released (VB-076, answer 3).

**Approach.** A design pass, no code. It writes:
- the proposed rows of `design.md` section 15, "The player and what they see", marked Proposed, with the reasoning and costs in
  `docs/review-life.md`;
- slice 2's implement tasks in the roadmap's order (behaviour-preserving first):
  - the kinds' `available` refactor (golden unchanged);
  - `Opportunities.forPawn` with the actor view;
  - inhabiting a pawn (VB-169 rewritten);
  - the meeting's answers (`Decisions.answer`);
  - the player's succession;
  - saves in the page (from VB-077's answers);
  - ambitions;
- the wrap-up VB-078, rewritten as a played life over three generations.

VB-077 is closed by this plan, since its one open criterion is the save task. VB-098 and VB-099 stay in the arch, `needs-review`; the
wrap-up stops waiting for them, and ambitions take only what they need of VB-099. The fourth door, offers in Exchange, comes with slice 1's
gift (a note in VB-182), not here.

*Rejected:*
- **One large "player" task (VB-169 widened to everything):** too large for one run, and the roadmap asks for the behaviour-preserving
  refactor first.
- **`Opportunities.forHolder` for every holder now:** slice 2 needs one pawn on demand. Every holder every hour is slice 3's cost risk
  (roadmap, section 3.3), and its plan designs the cache.
- **Advocacy as the player's only voice at the meeting:** it acts over years through others' attitudes, so the pawn's own voice would
  never count at the sitting it attends.

**Impact.** The plan itself changes no module. The tasks it writes would touch:
- `sim/kinds` and `sim/jobs` (the refactor);
- a new `sim/opportunities`, read-only;
- `sim/commands`, `sim/clock` and `sim/jobs` (the player as a source: one new state field, `st.player`);
- `culture` and `sim/lifecycle` (ballots at the meeting);
- `sim/saves` and the page;
- a read-only ambitions view.

The new coupling is one test at each of the four planner sites ("is this pawn the player's, and held?"). Each task's impact note says
why the existing pieces do not fit where it adds one.

**Cost.**
- Without a player: nothing a game day. The planner's test is one comparison per idle or working pawn at the four sites, and the
  counters are unchanged.
- The refactor: `refreshJobs` runs at 14 hours a day (hours 5 to 18) over every farm. It must post through a callback, without arrays
  per kind, and keep "jobs refresh and matching" within 5% of before (33.5 ms a day at medium-y5, measured below).
- `forPawn`: one pawn, on demand, in the page (its household's and its village's open work, the empty farms and households it could
  go to); under 1 ms a call at the late reference, at most four calls a second.
- Answers: one term per answered custom per sitting. Succession: once per death of the player's pawn, over its kin and at most 150 ties.
- Ambitions: read from the state on demand and at the new year. They never call `recordOf`; events of the player's line come through
  VB-199's pawn index once it lands.
- Saves: a main-thread freeze of about 1.1 s at medium-y5 today, until VB-199 and VB-204 bring a save under 0.5 s (a question).
- Slice 2's line in the budget table: 0 ms a game day of simulation; the page's opportunities under 1 ms per panel refresh.

**Tests.** None added by this plan; the golden file and counters are unchanged. `node tests/backlog.js` checks the new files and rows.

**Measurements (before, for the tasks' criteria).**
- (a) Jobs refresh and matching, ms a game day: medium-y5 and small-y40, 30 days, `--report perf`.
- (b) Open jobs a pawn may take (its household's and village's) at the working hours: medium-y5 and small-y40. This sizes what
  `forPawn` reads.
- (c) `recordOf` for one pawn: ms a call and the record's length, at small-y40.

Save and load are taken from `docs/bench/budget.md` (5 November). All four fixtures are stale (golden hash), which is usable, and is said
with every number.

**Risks.**
- The refactor's posting order: the golden file is the test.
- A planner site missed would let the planner override the player; the inhabiting task names all four, with a test for each.
- A played run is deterministic only given the player's commands and their times. The wrap-up scripts the player in Node.
- Decided against decided: autosave at each moving round (30 October) against save under 0.5 s (3 November). Until VB-199 and VB-204,
  the autosave would freeze the page for twice the budget. Both lines are owned elsewhere, so this is a question, not a flag.
