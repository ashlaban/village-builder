# VB-274: The player's wish for a match, carried to the moving round
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-272
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Marriage as a negotiation: The player's doors); The player and what they see (Marriage; The round's acts).
Goal: As the one to marry, the player may wish for a match, carried to the round as its other wishes are (VB-212). A wish narrows what the round does for the played pawn and never takes it past a rule. The other side answers the match as it would any offer.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `st.player.wishes.match`: `null` (as the pawn would), `false` (not this year), or a pawn id (that partner). Set by `Sim.wish(st, 'match', value)`, kept until changed, and cleared when the played pawn or the wished partner marries or dies. An older save's player loads with `match: null`. Invariants added.
- [ ] With `false`, the played pawn is left out of the round's men or women, and so is not counted by `kinPressure`. With a pawn id, at the start of the man's village's round and before the matcher's loop, the pair becomes an offer (VB-272), but only if the round's rules allow it. Both are alive, single, of marrying age (`MARRIAGE.menFrom`, `womenFrom`), fed (`HUNGER.marryBelow`), not retired and not in an open offer. Their kin degree passes `kinOk` under the man's village's custom. `homeFor` gives a home, and that home affords the match (`affords`, the means to marry). The pair's score, with the noise at its mean (`MARRIAGE.noise` / 2), is at least `MARRIAGE.minScore`. No draw is made from `st.rng.society` for it. The played pawn's own answer is yes, since the wish is its yes. The other answers follow VB-272. If the wish fails a rule, the played pawn enters the matcher as usual.
- [ ] `Sim.opportunities` says, for the wished partner, the rule that bars the match now if one does (as `roundBox` does for the other wishes). The pawn panel offers the wish under "At the moving round": "not this year", "as I would", or a partner. The partners listed are the unmarried pawns of the other sex, of age, whom the pawn knows best by tie, at most 8, each with the rule that bars it if one does.
- [ ] Tests (`tests/wishes.js`): `false` keeps the played pawn unmarried through a round that would have married it; a wished partner who passes the rules becomes an offer at the round and is married at the close; a wished sister, a partner with no home, and one 30 years older make no offer, and the played pawn enters the matcher as usual; with no player and with no wish, the golden file is unchanged. A browser test sets the wish from the panel.
- [ ] Cost: one pair checked a round for the played pawn (O(kin degree)), nothing between rounds.
