# VB-274: The player's wish for a match, carried to the moving round
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Marriage as a negotiation: The player's doors); The player and what they see (Marriage; The round's acts).
Goal: As the one to marry, the player may wish for a match, carried to the round as its other wishes are (VB-212). A wish narrows what the round does for the played pawn and never takes it past a rule. The other side answers the match as it would any offer.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `st.player.wishes.match`: `null` (as the pawn would), `false` (not this year), or a pawn id (that partner). Set by `Sim.wish(st, 'match', value)`, kept until changed, and cleared when the played pawn or the wished partner marries or dies. An older save's player loads with `match: null`. Invariants added.
- [x] With `false`, the played pawn is left out of the round's men or women, and so is not counted by `kinPressure`. With a pawn id, at the start of the man's village's round and before the matcher's loop, the pair becomes an offer (VB-293), but only if the round's rules allow it. Both are alive, single, of marrying age (`MARRIAGE.menFrom`, `womenFrom`), fed (`HUNGER.marryBelow`), not retired and not in an open offer. Their kin degree passes `kinOk` under the man's village's custom. `homeFor` gives a home, and that home affords the match (`affords`, the means to marry). The pair's score, with the noise at its mean (`MARRIAGE.noise` / 2), is at least `MARRIAGE.minScore`. No draw is made from `st.rng.society` for it. The played pawn's own answer is yes, since the wish is its yes. The other answers follow VB-293. If the wish fails a rule, the played pawn enters the matcher as usual.
- [x] `Sim.opportunities` says, for the wished partner, the rule that bars the match now if one does (as `roundBox` does for the other wishes). The pawn panel offers the wish under "At the moving round": "not this year", "as I would", or a partner. The partners listed are the unmarried pawns of the other sex, of age, whom the pawn knows best by tie, at most 8, each with the rule that bars it if one does.
- [x] Tests (`tests/wishes.js`): `false` keeps the played pawn unmarried through a round that would have married it; a wished partner who passes the rules becomes an offer at the round and is married at the close; a wished sister, a partner with no home, and one 30 years older make no offer, and the played pawn enters the matcher as usual; with no player and with no wish, the golden file is unchanged. A browser test sets the wish from the panel.
- [x] Cost: one pair checked a round for the played pawn (O(kin degree)), nothing between rounds.

Implementation notes (11 Nov 2026):
- The match rules moved out of `marriageRound` into a new module `src/sim/matching.js` (after `sim/heirs` in the manifest; no state, no owner): `kinOk`,
  `matchScore` (the matcher's score, with the noise a fraction of its amplitude), `matchRules(st)` (place, homeFor, affords, with their caches) and
  `matchBar(st, a, b)` (the first rule that bars a match, or `{man, woman, home}`). The round and `Sim.opportunities` read the same rules. A new module was
  needed: `heirs.js` stands at its 400-line limit. The round's own lines changed little: the context block is now `matchRules(st)`, the loop's score is one call
  to `matchScore`, and a wished pair comes first in the loop with no scan and no draw (the `goAhead` draw is skipped for it too); the played pawn's own
  answer is set yes (the `man` or `woman` key only; a played head's answer for a member stays open, so an offer waits).
- `st.player.wishes.match`; `Sim.wish(st, 'match', v)` takes null, false or a living pawn of the other sex; `clearMatchWish` (sim/commands, the owner)
  is called by `wed` and `killPawn`. Older saves load with `match: null` (`saves.js`). Invariant in `tests/lib/invariants.js` (null, false or a living pawn
  of the other sex, the played pawn living).
- `Sim.opportunities`: for a played single adult, `{verb: 'match', when: 'round'}` items: one with `target: null` (the pawn may wish), then up to 8 partners by
  tie (`relationsOf`), the wished one added if not among them, each with `why` from `matchBar`. The Actions sheet shows "A match": As it would, Not this year, partners.
- Cost: one `matchBar` a round (one pair; `kinDegree` is bounded); the panel's items run `matchBar` for at most 9 pairs on a rebuild, sharing one `matchRules`
  (a lazy `roundHeirs`), only for a played single adult.
