# VB-1542: A member's voice: leave asked of the head
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-1540
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: `design.md` section 7, A member's voice, Own time, The player as head; section 5, The round as offers; `docs/review-agency.md`, section 4
Goal: A member has no way to ask its head for anything, and a played pawn that is not the head can only act on opportunities. Build the first ask of design.md's row A member's voice: leave. A member asks its head for free hours today (from now, or from an hour named, to the end of the working day): an offer on a new basis `leave` in the table of bases (`sim/offers`), contributed by the module that weighs it. The head answers by its day: no when its estimate (`sim/estimate`) says the household's tasks with a window closing or a deadline today want the member's hours; else by its opinion of the member and its own diligence. A yes takes the member out of the day's allotment for those hours (`allot` goes around it as around one away) and they are its own time (`sim/pastimes`, VB-1539); a no is a memory of the member's. A pawn asks of its own accord when it courts, or has not sat with a close friend or near kin for `VOICE.days` (6), at most once in that time, and then spends the leave on that visit. The played member asks through its Actions; the played head is asked and answers, its pawn's judgement shown as advice.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The basis `leave` with its check, its answer rule and what a yes does, each through verbs; `VOICE` a `@tunable`; the member's leave is plain saved state with one owner and ends with the working day.
- [ ] A new test on a constructed household holds: a head with a deadline today says no; a head with a free day says yes by opinion; on a yes the day is allotted around the member and it chooses a pastime; on a no the member remembers it; a pawn asks at most once in `VOICE.days`.
- [ ] The played member's Actions offer the ask with the head's likely answer as its pawn's estimate; the played head gets the offer to answer with advice (`sim/player`, `sim/offers`).
- [ ] Cost named before building (entities × cadence × work) and its counter added; asks a pawn-day reported.
- [ ] Reported, populous small, seeds 7 and 11, 2 years, before and after: leaves asked, given and refused a pawn-year, hungry household-days, the harvest missed, courting pairs that marry. Whether households were better off is said in a line.
- [ ] `npm run format`, `npm run verify` and `node tests/smoke.js` pass.
