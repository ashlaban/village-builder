# VB-093: A village windmill
Status: needs-review
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: food
Phase: implement
Design ref: Long work and processing (Milling at a mill: much faster; a water mill by a river, or a windmill, likely dearer to build; often owned by the village and built as a commons job: Decided; Building jobs: Decided); Economy and jobs (water mills need rivers, which the world does not generate yet: Open).
Goal: Once households grind on a hand quern (VB-009), milling costs a household of 6 about 60 work hours a year. The design adds a mill that is much faster, often owned and built by the village. The world has no rivers yet, so the first mill is a windmill. Depends on VB-009 (flour and the `grind` job), VB-032 (building jobs and timber), VB-010 (the stone store), VB-031 (village building jobs and their priorities) and VB-039 (the village store); if any is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria (draft, pending `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-093):
- [ ] Trigger, checked at the moving round: a village of 3 or more farms with no mill, and a free open tile within 6 steps of the green, hills preferred, starts a mill project. The tile is owned by the village (`setOwner`).
- [ ] Building jobs owned by the village, taken by its adults at the village-duty priority of VB-031: foundation 20 h (uses 10 loads of stone), frame 80 h (uses 20 loads of timber), sails and millstones 40 h. Progress persists across days; materials come from the village's and then its households' stores, and a step waits while they are short.
- [ ] Milling: a `mill` job of the household (walk to the mill with grain, grind, carry flour home) at 1 work hour per 20 units of grain, 5 times the quern. Households of the owning village mill free; a household of another village within 10 steps may use it for a toll of 1 in 16 of its grain, which goes to the owning village's store (`v.grain`, VB-039). A household chooses the mill when the walk there and back takes less than the quern hours it saves.
- [ ] The mill's condition falls by 5% a year; below 60% the village posts a mend job (10 h); below 30% it stops milling.
- [ ] The map shows the mill by stage (foundation, frame, sails); the sails turn while someone mills. The village panel shows the mill and its condition.
- [ ] A test: a constructed village of 3 farms with stores of stone and timber builds a mill, and a household then mills there.
- [ ] Populous medium at seed 7, 10 years: mills built, quern and mill hours per household-year, and tolls paid (reported, not asserted).
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
