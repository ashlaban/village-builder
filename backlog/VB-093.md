# VB-093: A village windmill
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-009, VB-346, VB-349
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Long work and processing (Milling at a mill: much faster; a water mill by a river, or a windmill, likely dearer to build; often owned by the village and built as a commons job: Decided; Building jobs: Decided); Economy and jobs (water mills need rivers, which the world does not generate yet: Open).
Goal: Once households grind on a hand quern (VB-009), milling costs a household of 6 about 60 work hours a year. The design adds a mill that is much faster, often owned and built by the village. The world has rivers since VB-111 (`src/gen/rivers.js`), so the first mill may be a water mill or a windmill: slice 6's plan (VB-185) chooses. Depends on VB-009 (flour and the `grind` job), VB-032 (building jobs and timber), VB-010 (the stone store), VB-031 (village building jobs and their priorities) and VB-039 (the village store); if any is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria (rewritten by slice 6's plan, VB-185, 11 Nov):
- [ ] A facility kind `mill` (VB-346) the village builds as a commons job, decided at the moving round by a village of 3 farms or more with no mill: a water mill on a river tile within 6 steps of the green where there is one (100 work hours), else a windmill on a free open tile within 6 steps, hills preferred (140 hours, as agreed on 30 Oct); both take 10 loads of stone, 20 of timber, a pair of millstones and a bloom of iron for the fittings, from the households' stores in turn.
- [ ] Millstones: `hewMillstones`, 30 work hours from 2 loads of sandstone, gives the pair (a good a sandstone village sells: 60 rations, Proposed); a village without sandstone gets them by exchange before it can build.
- [ ] `grindAtMill`: a household carries grain to the mill and grinds 5 times as fast as the sandstone quern; households of other villages within 10 steps may use it for a toll of 1 in 16 to the owning village's store. A household with a mill in reach makes no new quern. Wind and the river's flow are ignored at first.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous small and medium, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years): mills built, of which kind and when; hours of grinding saved against the hours of building; toll taken; villages that wanted a mill and lacked millstones or iron.
