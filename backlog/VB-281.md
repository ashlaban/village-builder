Goal: VB-242's survey found that the matcher's women, claims on empty farms, settlement and service reach between villages with no cost of distance beyond a fixed reach, and nobody walks. Kim decided on 10 November that distance between villages governs this mixing: easy between close villages, costly to prepare for a long trip, and made by a pawn that physically travels. What a trip costs in the game's goods (design, Provisions and the season): a day's walk is 12 hours (6:00 to 18:00); a move of h hours takes ceil(h / 12) days on the road and as many rations from the household left. A visit takes 2 × ceil(h / 12) + 1 days away, a ration and 12 working hours a day: the nearest neighbour (median 5.2 h at `medium-ref`) is within a day's walk and needs no visit; a village 13 to 24 hours away costs 5 days (a ration and 60 hours of work), the median pair at `medium-ref` (41 h) 9 days, and the furthest within reach (67 h) 13 days, more than a season of 12 days.

Cost (Rules for cost, 2, 4, 7 and 9), measured on the fixtures with a prototype (scratch, not committed):
- The table (rule 2): villages² hours, 841 numbers at `medium-ref` (3,600 at 60 villages). A row is a Dijkstra from the green by `stepCost`, bounded at `ROUTE.reach`, stopping once every green is settled: 2.4 ms median and 19 ms at most at `medium-ref` (11,600 tiles settled at the median), 1.3 and 6.2 ms at `small-y40`; the whole table 95 and 43 ms. A row a step on one day (rule 5): within 60 ms, about 2 ms a game day over the year. `findRoute` per pair is no substitute: 406 searches took 414 to 930 ms at `medium-ref`, since a pair with no route by land explores the island before it fails.
- Journeys (rule 4): due entries sorted by time, the day's 6:00 reading only the first. About 26 journeys a year at `medium-ref` and 10 at `small-y40` (the moves measured above), one route search each, a leg a day (about 3), so some 80 due entries a year; visits (VB-289) add at most the unmatched men with the means, 1 or 2 a year at `medium-ref`; carried gifts (VB-291) about 21 at `medium-ref` and 35 at `small-y40`.
- The walk (rule 7): the agent's own walk, awake only while it walks, whether or not the camera sees it: under 1,000 walk steps a journey.
- The pairs (rule 2): VB-288's pool holds only women within reach, so `roundPairs` falls (its scaling and history targets are out on `main`, +0.85 and +2.1).
