# VB-281: Plan: reaching another village: distance governs the mixing, and the pawn travels
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: plan
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Leaving home: A village's round); Kim, 10 Nov 2026 (QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-242, question 2); backlog/VB-242.md (survey)
Goal: VB-242's survey found that the matcher's women, claims on empty farms, settlement and service reach between villages with no cost of distance beyond a fixed reach, and nobody walks. Kim decided on 10 November that distance between villages governs this mixing: easy between close villages, costly to prepare for a long trip, and made by a pawn that physically travels. Settle the details so implement tasks can be written.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A survey, in this file, building on `backlog/VB-242.md`: every act that reaches another village today (the matcher's choice of a woman, claims on empty farms, plots and settlement, service, kin help, visits), with file and function, what distance it reads today (a fixed reach, a bonus, none) and whether anyone walks for it.
- [ ] `design.md` rows, Proposed for Kim: one measure of distance between two villages (walking time by the route cache, not tiles as the crow flies), how the ease of mixing falls with it, what "preparing for a long trip" costs (food carried, days away from the farm's work, the season it can be made in), and which acts need the pawn to travel and arrive before they happen (a suitor's visit, a claimant walking to the farm, a servant's move).
- [ ] The questions the survey cannot settle, in `QUESTIONS.md` with proposed answers: at least whether a wife from another village needs a visit first, and whether the island-wide steps of VB-277 (demand, claims, settlement, service at 18:00 to 18:12) become a village's own once travel exists.
- [ ] Implement tasks, each of size S or M, with numbers, criteria and their order against VB-277 and VB-272.
- [ ] The cost named (Rules for cost, 2, 4 and 9): village pairs are few (26 villages at `medium-ref`), distances are compiled once and refreshed when a path changes, a journey is a due entry and not a poll.
Notes:
