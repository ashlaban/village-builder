# VB-367: Peat: cut in summer and burnt in place of wood
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The mill and the loom, the kiln and the quarry); `backlog/VB-185.md`
Goal: A household short of wood near a peat bog cuts peat in summer; dried, two loads burn as one of wood. Slice 6's plan (VB-185, 11 Nov), from Kim's answers in the session.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `dig` (VB-359) takes peat too: 3 hours a load, in summer only, into `hh.peat`; a load cut in one season burns from the next (it dries).
- [x] The hearth burns wood first and then peat, 2 loads for 1 of wood (`PEAT.worth` 0.5). A household cuts peat when its nearest wood is more than `PEAT.far` (8) steps off or its pile is under the winter goal at summer's end, up to the shortfall.
- [x] Peat does not grow back in a game. Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years): forest tiles lost (VB-326's measure), trees felled a household, peat cut, cold houses (VB-327).
- [x] The golden file is retired (AGENTS.md): no re-record; `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
Notes:
- Numbers are Proposed by slice 6's plan (11 Nov) on Kim's answers to the shape; the hours are in the game's work hours (576 an adult a year) and, but for the textile shares, from general reading not checked by search: each task reports its numbers and the wrap-up tunes them.

Implementation (13 Nov):
- `sim/dig` takes peat: `DEPOSIT.PEAT` among its goods; `wants(st, hh, good)`; the want (`peatTrips`) is summer only, the pile's shortfall
  (wood less reserved plus `PEAT.worth` of the peat held, wet or dry) against the plan's winter goal, in trips; peat is chosen when the village's
  wood lies over `PEAT.far` steps from the house (`digAt.far`, read at the yearly search) or from day `PEAT.late` (6) of summer. `PEAT` is in
  `sim/rules` beside `WOOD`.
- Decision (Deciding without Kim, 7): a trip brings `ECON.woodPerTrip` (3) units of peat, as a firewood trip brings 3 of wood, so a unit
  burns as 0.5 of wood and a 3-hour trip is worth 1.5 loads of wood. The tile falls by 1 a trip. Peat never grows back (`left()` only regrows bog iron).
- Drying (rule 3): `hh.peat` holds all peat, `hh.peatCut` {key, n} the units cut in the season keyed year*4+season; `Stores.peatDry` is what is
  held less what was cut this season: no pass dries it. The stores' `dug` writes both, with the ledger's good `peat`, reason `dig`.
- Burning: `warmHouse` burns wood, then dry peat for what the day lacks, `PEAT.worth` of wood a unit, reason `burn` (routine). The firewood job
  counts peat held in its pile (`fuelFree` in `sim/kinds`) so that a household with peat cuts less wood; urgency of the peat job is the
  pile's shortfall of its goal, at least the upkeep's.
- Also: `hh.peat` defaults to 0 in old saves; owners, invariants (`peat` a store, the totals' goods, deposits dug), `tools/stocks.js`
  line, the farm card shows peat; `tests/peat.js` (in verify). `tests/review-tools.js` used `household.peat` as its made-up field: now `household.lime`.
- Cost (rule 9): no new loop; the search runs once a household-year in summer when peat is wanted (counted in `digGround`), the burn adds one
  comparison on a cold-pile day.
