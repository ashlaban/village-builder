# VB-156: The village's outland, claimed by need
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: claims
Phase: implement
Design ref: Claims, ownership and territory, Infield, outland and wilderness (Decided 1 Nov). Review: `docs/review-claims.md`.
Goal: A village holds only its green, its commons wood, its plots and its empty farms; the land between villages is nobody's, and a household of one village may make a field beside another's green. Give each village an outland (*utmark*), its common pasture, wood and land to take in, claimed by need at the meetings, so villages grow towards each other and the island fills in, readable on the map. Depends on VB-042.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `src/sim/territory.js`, at each autumn meeting after the customs (`landMeeting`): a claim placed at an earlier meeting on a tile still free becomes village land (a year and a day; disputes are VB-157); then each village with an occupied farm claims free tiles (unowned, on land, not mountain or snow) within `CLAIMS.outReach` = 8 walking steps of its green, nearest first, until its outland and claims reach `CLAIMS.outPerFarm` = 8 tiles an occupied farm; when it holds no forest, the nearest forest tile within reach comes first. Villages claim in turn, one tile each, so none takes a contested strip first.
- [ ] A village claims a tile another village has claimed only when the tile lies nearer its own green (walking steps); a tile another village owns is never claimed.
- [ ] Outland: village land that is not in use (`st.used`), not the green and not a plot. `outland(st, v)` lists it.
- [ ] Generated villages (populous, heirs) start with their outland already theirs; the founders claim theirs at the start, unrealised, so it is theirs at the first meeting.
- [ ] Taking in land: a household's new field or meadow and a village's plot may use unowned land nobody else claims, or the village's own outland, never another village's land or claims (`freeFor(st, tile, village)`, used by `chooseTile`, `chooseMeadow`, `findSite` and `roomAt`).
- [ ] Settlers found villages in wilderness only: owned and claimed tiles are taken land for `newSite`.
- [ ] The commons wood is the nearest forest in the village's own land when it holds forest, else the nearest forest no other village holds (`villageWood`).
- [ ] `tests/territory.js` on tiny worlds (VB-155): two villages 10 steps apart claim towards each other and stop where their land meets; the outland grows with farms; a household never takes in another village's tile; a village with forest in reach gets its wood; settlers' sites avoid village land.
- [ ] Reported in the PR: populous tiny at seed 7, owned land at the start and after 10 years, the cost of `landMeeting`, and a screenshot with the Land overlay.
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
Notes / questions:
- Numbers are Proposed (`CLAIMS` in `src/sim/rules.js`): a farm holds 6–8 tiles of infield, so 8 tiles of outland a farm makes the outland about as large again; history had it larger.
