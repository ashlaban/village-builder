# VB-339: Whom to play: the person chosen on the game itself once the land is raised
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, The player, commands and views, Starting a game (11 November); docs/mocks/setup.html (step 2)
Goal: Build step 2 of Starting a game as a mode of the game's own page, in place of the small play offer.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] After a new game is generated (not after a load, and not for the showcase island) the page is paused in a choosing mode, in place of `#playOffer`: the stage's other interface is put away as for the menu screens, the map is dragged and zoomed as always, and the camera rests on the suggested pawn's farm (`suggestedPawn`).
- [ ] Clicking a farm's house tile, or its fields, selects that household: a panel of the menu's family (`.panel`) shows the farm's icon (`farmIcon`), its name and village, its food in days (`foodDays`) and its living members as portraits (`faceImg`), children among them but disabled (under `ADULT`). An empty farm or a ruin can be selected and says no one lives there.
- [ ] Clicking a person shows their details in the same panel: name, age, position; one line on the life; whom the player would go on as after a death (the first of `Sim.successors`, or that the line would break); "Can" and "Ahead" as icon chips. Who speaks at the meeting is read from the custom in force in that village, not from a table of the page; the heir shown is the one the game's succession names.
- [ ] Story archetype cards: for the scenario, the positions worth beginning from (founders: a founding holder, a founder's wife, the farmhand or the maid; heirs: an ageing holder, the heir who waits, a younger child, the widow, the heiress; populous: a holder, an heir, a younger child, a servant). A card has the position's icon, its name, one line and up to six portraits of those who hold it, with "and N more"; an archetype no one holds is not shown; the suggested one is starred. Clicking a card selects such a person and moves the camera to their farm; another click goes to the next. One function builds the cards, so VB-340 reuses it unchanged.
- [ ] A bar with Back (to the menu's New archipelago screen, the game left as it is), Watch only (closes the mode with no one played) and Play as NAME (`playAs`, then the Actions panel and Follow as the offer did). The game starts paused in the mode and takes the speed it had before on leaving.
- [ ] On a narrow screen (under 900 px) the map is first, the household's panel under it, the archetypes last, and the bar stays at the bottom of the screen; no sideways scroll at 400 px.
- [ ] The principles of Starting a game hold: nothing in the layout moves on hover; no text needed is only in a `title`; no numbers that are not read from `st`.
- [ ] The page's functional tests cover it: a new game opens the mode; a click on a farm, then a person, then Play as plays that pawn (`st.player.pawn`); Watch only leaves `st.player` null; a load does not open it. The tests that relied on `#playOffer` are moved to the mode.
- [ ] No change under `src/sim/` beyond a read-only helper if one is needed; `tests/golden.json` unchanged. `npm run verify` once; `index.html` rebuilt.
