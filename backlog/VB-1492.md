# VB-1492: Page: a repainted chunk stays on screen until its replacement is painted
Status: done (2026-11-23)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: src/page/chunks.js; docs/testing.md
Goal: Squares of the map no longer flicker to the overview at high speed: refreshTrees and refreshPaths mark the level-0 and level-1 chunks near a changed tile stale, which stay on screen until their replacement is painted and swapped in the same frame.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `repaintTiles` and `refreshPaths` mark the level-0 and level-1 chunks near a changed tile stale instead of dropping them; `wantedList()` and `schedule()` treat a stale chunk as wanted; a finished job drops the old chunk and adds the new one in the same frame.
- [x] A chunk marked stale again while its repaint is in flight is added and left stale, so it is painted once more; the in-flight job is not cancelled.
- [x] A check in `tests/page.js`: after a tile is added to `st.treeDirty`, the chunk covering it stays visible every frame until it is replaced.
- [x] Measured at 'as fast as it goes' on `#ps123`, 20 s (the scratch script of the diagnosis): frames that show a coarser level fall from 63 of 109 (9 episodes) towards none. Result: 11 of 107, 1 episode; that episode is the loader's opening glide (still running when the script starts measuring), passing level 1 before its destination is painted; after it, no frame showed a coarser level.
- [x] `npm test` and `npm run verify` pass.
Notes:
