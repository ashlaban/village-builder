# VB-1492: Map overlays: woodland and wild food
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-1490
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: Forests and wood; Wild food (forage, fishing); page only.
Goal: Two overlays. Woodland: `w.trees` against `w.treesMax` on forest tiles, so that felling and regrowth show. Wild food: the forage stock of forest and heath tiles (`w.forage`, as of `w.forageYear`) and the fish stock of river and lake tiles (`w.fish`, as of `w.fishDay`), each read through its module's own reading of the stock today if it has one (`sim/forage`, `sim/fish`), never recomputed on the page.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Woodland: tiles with `treesMax > 0` banded by trees standing as a share of full cover; the key names bare, thinned and full.
- [ ] Wild food: forage tiles and fish tiles banded by stock 0..1, the two told apart in the key (two ramps or a mark); a tile with neither is left as it is.
- [ ] Registered with the registry of VB-1490 (one new file under `src/page/`, its line in `src/page.json`, an icon in `src/page/icons.js` in both icon sets, a key panel in `src/body.html`); no change to the switch itself. It gets the next free digit key.
- [ ] Page script only: it reads `w` and `st` and writes neither; a sequential quantity uses one perceptually ordered ramp, readable in both themes over the map, and the key names the ends in the game's words and units.
- [ ] Drawn again only when what it shows changed (as `page/ground` looks at most twice a second); a new world wipes it.
- [ ] The browser check of VB-1490 covers it: on, off, no console error.
- 23 Nov 2026 (Kim, in session): the overlays and the switch were chosen from a mock; one overlay shows at a time.
