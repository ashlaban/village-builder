# VB-1682: Sweep: a chance that stands for a judgement becomes a threshold
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Principles for decisions (A wait is the thing waited for: a judgement is never a die; The simulation has no hand of its own); QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Archived 24 November 2026, question 2; tests/commands.js
Goal: No pawn's yes or no is a draw: chance is for what nobody decides (weather, a birth, a death, a find), and a judgement is a threshold on what the pawn weighs.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Every `rnd(` in an agency-layer module is listed in the PR as nature's (kept) or a judgement's (moved), with its reason.
- [ ] Each judgement's draw becomes a threshold on what the pawn weighs (its traits, its opinions, its stores, its need), its parameter named with a meaning; the courting refusal already done this way (`leaveBelow`, VB-682) is the pattern.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous and founders small, two seeds each, 30 years): growth, marriages a year per 1,000, and whether households were better or worse off; at most five short runs a parameter.
- [ ] VB-523's telling and believing are left to VB-523, which is built that way.
