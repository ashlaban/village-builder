# VB-762: Workmanship of tools: a farm's ironwork is forged, and has a grade
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The metals and the stones: Workmanship; The mill and the loom: Skills); `docs/plans/VB-702.md`, section 13; `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (17)
Goal: Kim's own example holds in play: a shoddily made spade breaks more easily and has a worse edge, so whose smith forged a farm's tools matters to its fields.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A farm's tools are one set with one grade of workmanship, that of its ironwork: the household's tools gain `grade` beside their condition (`@state`, invariant, saves: an old save's set is sound). Wooden tools, with no iron in them, are sound.
- [ ] `forge`: a craft job at home, skill smithing, `TOOLS.forgeHours` (12) and `TOOLS.forgeIron` (half a bloom) (Proposed): it makes the set's ironwork anew, at full condition, and draws its grade by the smith's skill (VB-761). Posted when a household with iron has tools with no ironwork, or when its head estimates that its best smith's work would be worth more than the set it has (VB-761's `expectedWorth` against the set's grade; principle "The future is someone's estimate"), at most once a winter. Mending (VB-121, VB-701) restores condition and keeps the grade.
- [ ] What the grade scales (Kim: "will break more easily, and can have a worse edge"): the hours of field work that wear a tenth of condition are multiplied by `WORKMANSHIP.life` of the set's grade; the share of the work's rate that the tools give (VB-121: 0.3 of it, by condition) is multiplied by `WORKMANSHIP.work`. Named so in the rules, with nothing else read.
- [ ] A forged set is a good that can cross households (`tools/iron`, graded, one row of custom's worth: Proposed 30 rations): a head with no smith may get one from another by an offer, carried home by a pawn; the old ironwork is a tenth of a bloom of scrap in the store (nothing vanishes).
- [ ] Numbers, populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years: sets forged by grade and by the smith's skill; tools' mean condition and the field work's mean rate by the set's grade; winter hours of mending and forging a household; households that got a set from another's smith; hungry household-days, before and after. Up to five short runs for the tunables.
- [ ] The numbers that move are reported before and after at the seeds and maps named (Working rules); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
Notes:
- The plan of VB-702 (16 Nov 2026), section 13 and decision D23: VB-121 mends by tenths and never makes a set; a grade redrawn at each tenth would flicker, and a mean of mendings is not one of five grades, so the grade is drawn when the set is forged. A close call. Numbers Proposed.

Noted by VB-702, 16 November 2026.
- 23 Nov 2026 (VB-761, workmanship, landed): a made piece's grade is drawn by `VB['sim/workmanship'].draw(st, maker, craft)` (stream `making`; the maker is the pawn who finished the job, passed to a kind's `done` as its fifth argument); a graded good declares `graded: {work: [properties]}` in `declareGood`, and reads its row by grade from `Goods.kindRow(good, kind).grades[grade]` (`life`, `work`, `worth`, the properties scaled); the `worth` rule takes `grade` in its context; `jobs.allot` gives a making of a graded good to the household's most skilled hand at its craft; `expectedWorth(st, id, craft)` is the head's estimate. The forged set's grade beside its condition, drawn at smithing.
