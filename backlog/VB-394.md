# VB-394: Hay comes home by the load
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Goods and the farming year; Animals (Hay and the hard winter limit). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-335 (what a load of hay is)
Goal: Sheaves come home by the load (TRAVEL.load, TRAVEL.cart); KINDS.hay still brings a meadow's 30 units home in one job of 4 hours and one carry, 30 cow-days on one back.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Hay comes home by the load as sheaves do: 10 units on the back, 60 on a cart with two oxen (new `TRAVEL` values beside `load` and `cart`), 1.5 work hours a load, the household's hands joining as at the sheaves.
- [ ] The cost is named (the same walks as VB-335's sheaves) and the hay job's counters re-recorded and listed.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years; founders small, the same seeds): hours of haymaking a household-year, hay in the barn at the first stall day, animals culled for want of hay, and whether short-handed households are worse off (VB-335's sheaves left them so: `docs/patches/VB-335.patch`).
