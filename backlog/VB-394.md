# VB-394: Hay comes home by the load
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Goods and the farming year; Animals (Hay and the hard winter limit). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-335 (what a load of hay is)
Goal: Sheaves come home by the load (TRAVEL.load, TRAVEL.cart); KINDS.hay still brings a meadow's 30 units home in one job of 4 hours and one carry, 30 cow-days on one back.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Hay comes home by the load as sheaves do: 10 units on the back, 60 on a cart with two oxen (new `TRAVEL` values beside `load` and `cart`), 1.5 work hours a load, the household's hands joining as at the sheaves.
- [x] The cost is named (the same walks as VB-335's sheaves) and the hay job's counters re-recorded and listed.
- [x] Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years; founders small, the same seeds): hours of haymaking a household-year, hay in the barn at the first stall day, animals culled for want of hay, and whether short-handed households are worse off (VB-335's sheaves left them so: `docs/patches/VB-335.patch`).
Notes:
- 12 Nov 2026: written from Kim's answers in the discussion of principles of that day (`AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim); the question and its numbers are in `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md` as the Design ref names.

Implementation (13 Nov 2026):
- `TRAVEL.hay` is 1/3 unit of hay a ration of load and the new `TRAVEL.hayCart` is 60: 10 units on the back (30 rations), 30 on a hand cart, 60 on an
  ox cart (it would be 100 by the ratio, so the cart's is capped). `KINDS.hay.hours` is 1.5 and `CART.reload` 1.5 (a first and each further load take the
  same); the hands join as before (`hands: Infinity`, the job stays open while `meadow.held > 0`). A meadow's 30 is three trips on the back or one cart.
  Before VB-394 the carts' work had set 6 / 18 / 60 and 4 hours for the first load and 0.5 for the next; this makes the loads as the question asked.
- Cost (rule 9): the same walks as the sheaves': a pawn walks barn-meadow-barn once a load; meadows about 3 to 5 a farm, 3 loads a meadow on the back
  (1 on a cart), so a farm's year is about 10 to 15 walks of the meadow's distance, a few hundred pawn-steps a year; no new loop, no new counter.
- Counters (populous-run probe, `tests/counters.js`, per pawn-day, against `tests/counters.json`): routeSearch 0.3871 -> 0.3904, routeExpand 1.6475 baseline
  (in on main) -> 1.663 (out), routeRefresh 2.286 -> 3.082, walkPoint 8.134 -> 8.011, matchBid 10.02 -> 9.795; routeSearch, routeRefresh, walkPoint, matchBid
  and oppAsk were out on `main` already (6 out then, 7 now). Not re-recorded: they are targets out, and raising a ceiling to make a residual vanish is not
  for an agent (AGENTS.md, Working rules); the hay job has no counter of its own.

  barn at the first stall day, autumn day 9; lean culls are the emergency slaughters for want of hay; before is `main`):
