# VB-555: A child on the way: births while courting, the offer out of the round, and the custom Children born outside marriage
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: talk
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, People (Births; Courting; Traits, talk and stories: Courting); Customs and culture (Custom dimensions: Children born outside marriage); Kim's answers of 30 October (QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-036)
Goal: Some children are born outside marriage, from courting, with the man she was with as father; the couple often marries quickly, and such a child inherits by custom.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Conception while courting, in `sim/births`' step for the women it already walks (a courting woman is reached from `pawn.courting`; no new loop): `COURT.conceive` 0.08 a woman-year, by the age curve of married women and times (1 − hunger), not while pregnant or nursing; `pregnantBy` is the man she courts. The true father is recorded and known to all (Kim, 30 Oct).
- [ ] The offer out of the round: `COURT.known` 12 days after conception a due entry (`st`, plain data) makes the couple's offer by the round's acts and under its rules (ages, a place to live, the kin limit, the means; hunger of 0.5 or more postpones it, a day at a time for at most a season); refused or not allowed, the couple is offered again at the round as VB-036 has it.
- [ ] The child: `pawn.outside` is true for a child born to an unmarried mother; it lives in its mother's household; an event of the record (`bornOutside`, with its kind, the child, both parents and the place), which is VB-523's story kind "a child outside marriage" (kin, -0.3, about the father and the mother) if stories have landed. When its parents marry each other `outside` becomes false, with an event (legitimation by the later marriage, as medieval law had it).
- [ ] The custom, the sixteenth (`src/culture.js`, `outside`): inherit like other children; after children born in marriage (default); do not inherit; malleability 0.5; self-interest pulls a pawn born outside marriage, and the parent of one, toward the first (Kim, 30 Oct). `sim/heirs` reads it through the customs in force where it ranks children. An old save gains the block from its culture's default (`sim/saves.js`; `tests/saves.js`); `tests/browser-mobile.js` and whatever counts the customs follow.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7, 42: births while courting as a share of births (about 5%; in 3 to 8%); the share of such couples married within a year (reported against Kim's "about 3 in 4, if they can": 0.6 to 0.9; never set); children outside marriage alive at 15 and how they inherited; mothers' households' hungry days against others' (whether households are worse off); growth and marriages.
- [ ] Parameterisation (at most five short runs): `conceive`, toward the 5%; the values tried and what each gave. Tests on a constructed state with `fix`: a courting woman conceives and a non-courting unmarried one never does; the offer is made 12 days on and the child is then born in marriage; a couple that cannot marry has a child outside marriage; each option of the custom in `sim/heirs`; legitimation. Invariants for `pawn.outside` and the due entry. Cost: nothing new a step; a due entry a pregnancy.
