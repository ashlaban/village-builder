# VB-523: Stories: an event told where people talk, believed by trust and fit, sharpened, and forgotten
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-519
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: talk
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, People, households and family records (Traits, talk and stories: Stories); docs/architecture/target-v2.md, sections 4.4 (Stories) and 10.2; docs/gossip-experiment.md; backlog/VB-186.md
Goal: Gossip as Kim decided it on 3 November, model B: a story is an event and who was in it, known first to those who were there, told in talk by its salience, believed by the hearer's trust in the teller and the story's fit, sharper at each retelling, and forgotten untold. What a pawn has heard of another moves its opinion as a memory, so a stranger judges at about a third of a witness. Lies come in the next task.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `sim/stories` owns stories (target-v2, section 4.4, Stories; `design.md`, People, Stories): `st.stories`, the live ones by id, each `{ id, kind, subject, other, t, v, by, held }` (the event's kind, the pawn it is about, the other party or null, when, the valence at the source, the pawn who first told it, how many hold it); a story nobody holds is dropped (rule 1: no walk over stories ever told). `pawn.known`: at most `STORY.keep` 8 entries `{ story, v, hops, s, t, zeal: [home, away], first }`. `@state`, `src/owners.json`, the `WORLD` list of `src/sim/saves.js`, invariants (a known story is live; `held` equals the holders; at most 8; values within bounds; the dead hold none).
- [ ] Kinds, a table `STORY.kinds` (each `{ event, domain, v }`): a claim taken (law, -0.5), cattle let into a crop (law, -0.3), a work day shirked (law, -0.2), a match refused (kin, -0.3), a quarrel (kin, -0.2; VB-520's event, if it has landed), help given in hunger (dealings, +0.4), ale poured or a feast laid out (dealings, +0.3). A story is made by a subscriber to the kind's event (`sim/events`, declared at load: `claimTaken`, `cattleInCrop` and `alePoured` exist; where a kind has no event yet, the act that writes its memory emits one): `Stories.witness(st, kind, subject, other, present)`. A later slice adds a kind by one line.
- [ ] Who knows it first-hand: the parties, the adults of their households, and those at the occasion where it happened (`Encounters.present`); `Encounters.said` at a meeting or a rite makes the speaker's most salient story known first-hand to all who came.
- [ ] Telling, in `talk` (VB-521's turn), each side once: at chance `STORY.tell` 0.3 × (0.5 + the teller's sociability) it weighs the stories it holds, |v| × zeal × strength × `negative` 1.3 for bad news × (1 + min(1, the hearer's tie to the subject / 8)), against `background` 1 for other talk, and tells the one drawn, or none. The zeal is the one for the hearer's village: home if it is the teller's own, else away. A story about the hearer is told only by its close friend.
- [ ] Saturation: told to one who holds it, the teller's zeal for that audience is multiplied by `stifle` 0.5 and the hearer's strength restored; under `quiet` 0.05 it is not weighed.
- [ ] Hearing: the told valence is the teller's × (1 + `sharpen` 0.08), with noise of spread 0.05, within -1..1. The hearer believes at chance `belief` 0.6 + 0.5 × (trust − 0.5) + 0.5 × fit, where trust is 0.2 + 0.6 × min(1, its tie to the teller / 8) + 0.5 × its opinion of the teller, within 0..1, and fit its opinion of the subject times the story's sign. Believed, it holds the story (one more hop, both zeals 1), making room by dropping the entry of least |v| × strength.
- [ ] Hearsay moves opinion through memory, not a new read: a believed story writes a memory of the subject of kind `heardIll` or `heardWell`, of the told valence × `hop` 0.8 to the power of its hops, to at most ±0.4 a subject, fading over `STORY.fade` 120 days (`sim/relations`' `remember`, given a value). So `opinionAt` costs what it costs.
- [ ] Forgetting, in closed form (rule 3): strength is 0.5 to the power of (days since `t` / (`half` 40, or `halfFirst` 120 first-hand) / (0.5 + the pawn's wits)); read when the story is weighed or room is made, and an entry under `forget` 0.2 is dropped then. No daily pass; the day a pawn's ties are renormalised (every 4 years) also drops its forgotten entries.
- [ ] `tools/sim.js --report stories`: stories made by kind a year, held a pawn, hops, and for the stories of a run the three probes of the experiment (the spread curve by village and day, the valence held against the source's by hops, strangers' opinion of the subject). `tools/gossip.js` stays as the reduced model; a note in its `--help` says where the game's numbers live.
- [ ] A reduced scenario asserts the bands that one story can show (`tests/lib/bands.js`, an `assert`; `tests/tiny.js`: three villages, two within an ale evening's reach or, until VB-522 has landed, sharing a green; one across water): held by over half its village's adults within 12 days in at least 7 of 10 seeds; by over half the neighbouring village's within 48 days in at least 7 of 10; never across the water; held 144 days on by under half of those who held it at its peak. The full runs' `report` bands are the wrap-up's.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7, 42: the shares of close friends, friends, dislikes and enemies; the share of held opinions at ±0.8 or beyond (the polarisation band: under 0.25) and villages split over one subject; gifts and help a year (a household spoken ill of may be helped less: say whether households, not only averages, are worse off); growth, marriages, hungry household-days.
- [ ] Parameterisation (at most five short runs, seeds and map named): `belief` (0.45, 0.6, 0.75) and `tell`, on the reduced scenario's bands; the values tried and what each gave; the rest to `docs/tuning.md` by a `tuning` note.
- [ ] Cost (rule 9): about 4,200 talks a day at 1,000 living × 2 sides × 0.3 × up to 8 stories weighed; a hearer does one lookup and writes only when it first believes. Counters `storyWeighed` and `storyTold` declared, reported a living pawn-day. The save: at most 8 entries a pawn; report the JSON's size on a 30-year populous small save before and after, against the save's line.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state, chances forced with `fix`: a witness tells and a hearer believes; saturation halves the zeal for that audience only; a ninth story drops the weakest; strength a half-life on is a half, and three half-lives for a first-hand one; a slow pawn forgets sooner than a sharp one; the hearsay memory is discounted by hops and capped; a story dies with its last holder; a save keeps them (`tests/saves.js`, `tests/plaindata.js`).
