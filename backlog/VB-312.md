# VB-312: A day without water: no cooking and a little health
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-308
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (Goods and the farming year: Water). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Consistency review (11 Nov, stocks)
Goal: The water a household fetches is read by nothing but the job that fetches it (`hh.waterDay`, `src/sim/kinds.js:161-166`): in populous small, seed 7, no water was fetched on 3.9% of household-days over 15 years and nothing followed. Kim, 11 Nov: a day without water costs something, as an empty woodpile does: no cooking that day, and a small cost in health.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] No cooking: the cook job is offered only once the day's water is in (`cookWanted` also asks `hh.waterDay === c.day`), so the meal of a dry day is eaten uncooked at VB-308's 0.75. The water job's urgency rises from 14:00, the cook's hour, to above cooking's (`waterLate`, 1.5 times `cookEvening`), so the hand that would cook fetches the water first.
- [ ] Health: at the end of a day with no water fetched each member loses `HEALTH.dry` (1; times `HEALTH.frail` under 5 and from 60) and the meal restores nothing that day, in `updateHealth` beside the cold's term; a starting value, half the cold's, to be tuned by the numbers below. `hh.dry`, 0 to 1 over the last 12 days, is kept as `hh.cold` is (one log, an invariant, a save without it loads with 0) for the panel ("no water") and for VB-062's mood.
- [ ] A farm with no village well draws at its own house, as today; nothing here makes water scarce. The first day of a new household counts as fetched.
- [ ] Tests (beside VB-308's): a household whose water job is never taken cooks nothing, eats at 0.75 and its frail members' health falls; with water, a run is as VB-308 left it.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous and founders, small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): dry household-days by season and why the job went undone (unclaimed, no hand in working hours), meals uncooked for want of water, health and deaths in the households it touches; whether households, not only averages, are worse off. The golden file is re-recorded and its changed entries listed. Cost: one comparison a live household a day in the pass that burns the wood.
