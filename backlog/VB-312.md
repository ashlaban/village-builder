# VB-312: Grain loans: borrowed when help falls short, repaid at the harvest
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-310
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, Loans, obligations and cases (Grain loans; Interest and due; Overdue and default); Customs and culture, Slice 5's practices ("Loans of grain are repaid"); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, sections 4.1 (a loan is one command), 4.5 (Exchange) and trace 7.2
Goal: Kim, 10 Nov: grain loans are slice 5's. Help in hunger gives; a household still short after help has nowhere to turn. Let it borrow from a household with grain to spare, owing it back at the harvest with a quarter more, so lean years move grain to the hungry and debts to those with full stores.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] When help in hunger has gone round (`src/sim/help.js`; VB-308 where done) and a household is still short of reaching the next harvest with its floor, its holder asks to borrow, once a season: from the households of its own village in order of grain to spare (most first, then by id), then from its holder's trade partners met at a gathering (VB-302, once done).
- [ ] A lender spares its grain above its floor and its own need to the next harvest; it lends at most what the borrower lacks, in whole sacks of 10 rations; it refuses a household whose holder it holds below −0.3 in opinion, and one with no field and a loan unpaid. The village store lends too, booked by its alderman once VB-314 is done.
- [ ] A loan is one command (a verb `lend` in `src/sim/commands.js`, covered by `tests/commands.js`) that moves the grain through `sim/stores` with the reason `loan` and creates the obligation, or does nothing and says why.
- [ ] A new practice "Loans of grain are repaid" (with a quarter more, default; as lent; malleability 0.4) in `src/culture.js`, read where the loan is made. The loan is due when the borrower's next harvest is threshed (the end of autumn); it is paid then from grain above the floor, in part if it must; unpaid, it is overdue and due again at the next harvest; unpaid after the second, defaulted (VB-315 hears it).
- [ ] Events: a loan, a repayment and a default, each with its pawns and households (`log`, `personEvent`; `tests/record.js`).
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous, small, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42, 15 years; `tools/balance.js`, `--report hunger`): loans a year, grain lent, repaid, defaulted; hungry household-days and the worst household's hungry share, by household and not only on average; the stores of the richest tenth. Golden and counters re-recorded, entries listed.
Cost: a short household asks once a season and looks over its village's households (about 30 at most), so at `medium-ref` a few hundred comparisons a season; the dues are in VB-310's queue. Measured on `small-y40`.
Order: after VB-310. VB-315 (cases) follows.
