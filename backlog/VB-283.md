# VB-283: Ale: brewed from grain to spare, and poured by the host
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-126, VB-277
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, Goods of place and side crafts (What each good is for: Ale, Agreed 10 Nov); Settlements, The gathering (What the host gains)
Goal: Slice 4's third good (Kim, 10 Nov), grounded in standing: what a host pours is what its guests remember, and surplus grain gets a use other than rotting.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Brewing is a side craft: long work in the house, from grain above the household's floor and its reserve only, 10 rations of grain and 4 hours give a barrel, worth 14 rations; ale keeps one season.
- [ ] At an occasion (VB-126: a wedding, a funeral ale; the gathering, VB-273) the host household pours what it has, a barrel serving 20 guests; the ale is used up through `sim/stores` with a reason.
- [ ] Each guest's opinion of the host gains 0.05, fading over 3 years; the host's prestige gains 0.05 a barrel poured in the last 3 years, at most 0.2; at the gathering the gain is shared among the host village's holders by what each poured. The pours of the last 3 years are bounded state with an owner and an invariant.
- [ ] At the gathering the host village's holders pour; a host that pours nothing loses pull the next year (VB-272).
- [ ] Ale is in the class of food and is not a keeping good.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous, small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): grain brewed against grain spoilt, hungry household-days (no household brews below its floor), prestige by household.
