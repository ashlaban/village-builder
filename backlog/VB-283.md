## The survey: what happens today to a household short of people
A household short of people is, here, one with at most one member of 16 or more (the bands' adult) on its first hungry day of the year; the rules below read 18.
- **Kin help** (`shareFood`, `kinHouseholds`, `src/sim/help.js`; VB-219): once a season, when its spare grain covers fewer than 3 days, a household asks the
  households of its members' parents, children and siblings, anywhere on the island, then the village store, for food to the next harvest. A kin household gives
  only what it holds above its seed reservation and its holder's buffer (20 to 60 days). In spring the kin of a lone adult seldom have any: 41 of the 56 rows
  below had a kin household in the village and were hungry all the same. Nobody moves.
- **Inheritance** (`succession`, `heirFor`, `transfer`, `src/sim/heirs.js`): when the lone holder dies, the farm goes to the first heir; a minor heir with no adult
  in the house is passed over for an adult in line if there is one, else holds in name with a guardian (`setGuardian`). A lone adult is never joined to kin while alive.
- **The guardian** (`careForOrphans`, `src/sim/service.js`, every day from `dailyLife`, `src/sim/lifecycle.js`, over every live household): only a household with
  no member of 18 or more. Its ward's nearest relative in `st.demand` (kinship, then the oldest) moves in with spouse and minor children; with none, `extinct`
  (`src/sim/heirs.js`): the farm stands empty, the village holds it, the old family keeps a birthright (`birthright`, `sim/farmclaims`), and the children go to
  their nearest kin (`kinNear`, `kinHomeFor`) or into service in the village (`serviceHome`). A minor holder with one grown housemate (a sister of 19) gets
  nothing: the housemate is the guardian (`guardianOf`).
- **Service** (`takeServants`, `src/sim/service.js`, at the moving round's last phase, `roundPhase` 3): only a household of exactly one member, its holder of 18 or
  more: an unmarried landless adult of 18 to 50 in `st.demand`, whose household keeps 2 working hands, from the village or a village within 15 steps, through
  the `serve` command. Once a year, and only when such a person exists: 1 of the 56 rows had one within reach.
- **Since VB-282** a household that would go hungry eats its seed, so the lone adult with 3 to 6 fields who starved beside 50 to 120 grain of seed (VB-270's
  shape) is mostly gone: the hungry lone household is now one with 1 field (35 of 56 rows), short for real (grain 0 on its first hungry day in all 56).

## The plan
1. **VB-307** (S, ready): the guardian rule reads a queue of households whose membership changed, in place of a daily walk over every household (behaviour-preserving,
   golden unchanged). The hook the next task reads.
2. **VB-308** (M, after VB-307): at the sign (a household with at most one member of 18 or more and fields, whose seasonal ask for help falls short), a free relative
   from `st.demand` moves in, else a servant by `takeServants`' rule, at once. Land unchanged: people come to the farm. Reaches 14 or 15 of the 56 rows; criterion:
   at least 10 fewer.
3. **VB-309** (M, after VB-308 and Kim): with nobody to come, the people move to their nearest kin household, which holds the farm in trust (owner and holder
   unchanged) until they can return. Reaches 33 of the 42 left; criterion: 15 or fewer rows of households short of people over 0.3 on the ten runs.
   Order against VB-090 and VB-034: VB-307 and VB-308 go before VB-090 (blocked behind VB-250) and one at a time with it and with VB-290, all in `sim/service`;
   VB-308 and VB-090 share the placement helper (village first, then within reach), whichever lands second. VB-309's trust is the relation splitting land has
   (VB-034, slice 5: the old household works the sibling's fields for half the harvest); whichever lands first builds it.

## Cost (Rules for cost, 4 and 9)
- Checked at the membership change, not polled: VB-307 removes today's daily walk of `careForOrphans` over every live household (about 150 on populous small,
  250 at `medium-ref`, every day) for one queue entry a departure (deaths and moves; the households that became short of people were 7 a year on populous 3).
- VB-308 adds no loop: the sign rides on help's ask, at most once a season a household and only below 3 days of spare grain; the grown count is over its members;
  only a household short of people searches `st.demand` (a kinship memo, as `careForOrphans`) and `takeServants`' list, about 2 household-years a run-year.
- VB-309: one move and one record a case, a few a decade a run; the trust ends at events (marriage, the round, coming of age, death), with an index of live trusts.

## Orchestrator's decisions for Kim to overturn
- The ward is not planned for on its own: VB-270's 25 ward rows are lone adults whose death passed the farm in name to a child (above). A minor holder with one grown
  housemate counts as short of people in VB-308, as the task asked.
- Kin first, then a servant, at the sign of help falling short (VB-308 ready on it): this widens two Decided rules of 1 November (the guardian, the servant for the
  lone holder) in their own direction, a second occasion beside the round and a household of at most one grown member, and reverses nothing; the reasoning is in
  VB-308's file. The membership change alone is not the sign: 97 to 98% of household-years short of people with fields are not hungry over 0.3.
- What becomes of the household when nobody can come moves Decided points (a holder who leaves the farm; the orphan rule's empty farm), so it is Kim's
  (`QUESTIONS.md`, VB-283), and VB-309 waits for it.
