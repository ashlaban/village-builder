# VB-561: Talk indoors: a pawn inside another house is not at home
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: talk
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Where pawns meet; src/sim/encounters.js (house); src/sim/agents.js placeByDoor
Goal: The house kind of sim/encounters, and pawn.homeHours, read agent.inside, which does not say which house: a pawn indoors at another house (moved at night, waiting for its journey's first leg, VB-287) counts as inside its own, so its housemates may draw it and its hours at home grow
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A pawn is present in the house kind of `sim/encounters` only when inside its own household's house (by its position at that house's door, or a field that names the house it is in, owned by `sim/agents`); one indoors at another house is not drawn by its housemates and draws none of them.
- [x] `pawn.homeHours` counts a turn only when the pawn is inside its own house; the invariant holds.
- [x] A test on a constructed state: a pawn waiting indoors at another farm for its journey is not drawn by its housemates at home; one at home is.
- [x] Numbers before and after (populous small, seed 7, 3 years, `--report relations`; `node tests/counters.js`), and how many pawn-hours were counted at home that were not.

Noted by VB-521, 16 November 2026.

## Notes (VB-561 run)
- Decision: "at its own house" is read by position, not a new field (the task allowed either): `atHome(st, a)` in `sim/encounters` = indoors and within `st.w.R + 12` of the door spot of its household's house (the radius `placeByDoor` already uses). No new state, so no invariant or WORLD entry. `sim/relations` uses it for `homeHours`; `partners` returns mix 0 and no one for a pawn indoors elsewhere.
- Test: `tests/encounters.js` section 5 (constructed state).
- Numbers (populous small, seed 7, 3 years): pawn-hours counted at home that were not: 20 of 255,604 counted (0.008%). The run diverges after the change (draws differ): year 3 end 372 living, was 374; 14231 -> 13847 'all' row second column. `tests/counters.js`: 53 counters, 2 out (heirRank, roundRank, +0.000225) as before the change.
- Households vs averages: not measured per household; the effect is too small to move any.
