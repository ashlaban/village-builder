# VB-561: Talk indoors: a pawn inside another house is not at home
Status: done (2026-11-16)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: talk
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Where pawns meet; src/sim/encounters.js (house); src/sim/agents.js placeByDoor
Goal: The house kind of sim/encounters, and pawn.homeHours, read agent.inside, which does not say which house: a pawn indoors at another house (moved at night, waiting for its journey's first leg, VB-287) counts as inside its own, so its housemates may draw it and its hours at home grow
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A pawn is present in the house kind of `sim/encounters` only when inside its own household's house (by its position at that house's door, or a field that names the house it is in, owned by `sim/agents`); one indoors at another house is not drawn by its housemates and draws none of them.
- [ ] `pawn.homeHours` counts a turn only when the pawn is inside its own house; the invariant holds.
- [ ] A test on a constructed state: a pawn waiting indoors at another farm for its journey is not drawn by its housemates at home; one at home is.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous small, seed 7, 3 years, `--report relations`; `node tests/counters.js`), and how many pawn-hours were counted at home that were not.
