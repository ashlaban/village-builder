Status: ready

Size: M

Goal: The plan decides what a household does, and nothing shows it. Show the holder's plan in the household panel, and let the player who is its head change it there.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A Plan tab in the household panel (`src/page/`): the season's routines in order; each target with the custom's value, the head's (its bias, VB-254) and the store's level; the next steps and their windows; the project and its step; what is reserved, by good; who holds which task. (Built but for who holds which task, which is the timetable's, VB-477, by the note of 15 November below.)
- [x] For the player as head, each row that has a verb (VB-253) has its control: a target's stepper, "choose the tile" (then a click on the map, with the refusal's reason shown when the tile will not do), the order by moving a routine up or down, the project from the menu. For anyone else the tab is read-only.
- [x] The page only reads `st` and writes through the `Sim` API (lint). A browser test (`tests/browser.js`): as head at seed 7, raise the hay target and choose a field's tile from the panel; the plan shows both.
- [x] A screenshot in the PR (`node tools/shot.js`). `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`. `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals.
Cost: the page's only; the tab reads one household's plans when open.
