# VB-1555: The fetch grows with the household: trips by hand for six, a cart's barrel for eighteen
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Water by the site); backlog/VB-1543.md; design.md, Carts
Goal: a large household's water costs more trips or a cart, and a small one's as much as six
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A day's water covers people (design.md, Goods and the farming year, Water by the site, The fetch grows with the household): a trip by hand covers `WATER.hand` (6; a child under 12 carries 3), a trip with a cart's barrel `WATER.cart` (18); `hh.waterFor` holds how many today's loads cover, beside `hh.waterDay`, with its invariant; the day's water does not keep.
- [x] A household above 6 posts the trips it needs, each a job of its own; the head takes the cart where one is free, the way carries it and it is the cheaper in hours (the walk both ways at 0.8 of the pace, 0.3 hours to fill), by the rule of Taking a cart; a cart trip wears the cart as any trip does.
- [x] The household cooks once the first load is in, and is dry by the part of its people the day's water did not cover (`hh.dry`, and the health of those not covered until VB-1554 makes it the pawn's own).
- [x] Checks on a small constructed state: households of 1 and 6 make one trip, of 7 two by hand or one with a cart, of 19 a cart's and one by hand; the hours match the design's table within the walk's rounding; with only one of two trips made, the meal is cooked and the dry part is as the people uncovered.
- [x] Measured once, before and after (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 10 years): the households above each threshold, trips a household-day, cart trips and carts built for want of one, hours a household-day on water by household size, dry household-days.
- [x] Cost, named before building: households x trips a day (one for most, two or three above 6 people; none above 18 in today's runs unless the measure finds them) x one cached route; the choice of cart a comparison a trip.
