# VB-195: Indexes of the living: no step walks the dead
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (code structure). `docs/roadmap.md`, sections 5.3 (rule 1) and 5.4 (indexes of the living); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, principle 2 (one writer per cache, checked against its source).
Goal: The 6-minute step walks every agent ever made, the dead included (`advance` in `sim/clock`). In `src/sim`, 19 loops walk every pawn record, 26 every household, 7 every agent and 5 every union; `Culture.dailyStep` walks every person. At year 40 of populous small, 1,274 pawn records stand behind 725 living (0.76 dead per living pawn, perhaps 4 to 5 by year 150), so the dead cost almost as much as the living, and the share grows every generation. The owners keep indexes of the live ones, and the loops in a step or a day walk them. Blocked until VB-088 is done (its history check measures this task).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `st.living`: the ids of living pawns, in id order, kept by the functions that add a pawn (births and the seeding modules) and the one that kills it (`killPawn`), and by nothing else. `st.liveHouseholds`: the ids of households not dissolved, in id order, kept by the functions that found a household and the one that dissolves it (`sim/heirs`; if VB-175 is done, its one founding act; if not, the three founding sites in `sim/cottage`, `sim/farmclaims` and `sim/settle`). Agents are walked through `st.living`, since an agent's id is its pawn's id.
- [ ] Every loop in a step, an hour or a day that walks a whole collection walks its index instead, in the same order. Loops that need the dead keep the full arrays: the family tree, kinship, the record, saves and the page's lists. The PR lists each loop it found (grep, or `tools/writes.js`) and what it now walks.
- [ ] The indexes are caches, so each has one writer and a check against its source: `checkState` (`tests/lib/invariants.js`) asserts each equals a scan, every day in `tests/invariants.js`; `tests/golden.js` leaves them out of the fingerprint (`DERIVED`, beside `jobsBy`); a save and load keeps or rebuilds them (`tests/saves.js` passes). If VB-173 is done, its owners table lists them in its cache category.
- [ ] `tests/golden.json` unchanged: the order of every walk is kept, so behaviour is the same. `npm run verify` passes.
- [ ] `walkStep` and `walkDaily` per living pawn-day at year 40 against year 5 (VB-088's history check) at 1.05 or below, and their entries leave `tests/cost-baseline.json`.
- [ ] Before and after on `small-y40` and `small-y5`, 30 game days each: ms per game day, ms per living pawn-day, and their ratio.
- [ ] When done: sets VB-201 `ready` if VB-127 is done too.
Cost: two arrays of about 1,000 ids at the late reference. A death or a dissolution costs one splice of at most 1,000 entries, about 30 a year. A step then walks about 1,000 agents instead of 1,000 plus the dead.
