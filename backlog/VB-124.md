# VB-124: Faster contacts and ties, with the same results
Status: done (2026-10-31)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: tooling
Phase: implement
Design ref: Vision and scope (up to about 1,000 pawns); `docs/review-relationships.md`, findings F4 and proposal A.
Goal: Contact hours (`noteContacts`) and the daily tie step (`dailyLife`) took about 55 of the 87 ms of a populous medium game day: every pair of outdoor pawns was compared each hour, pairs were kept under string keys `"i:j"` that `dailyLife` split again, and the ties were walked twice a day to decay and to prune them.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Outdoor pairs are found from cells of 30 map units; each pawn meets the later pawns of its own and the 8 cells around in agent order, so pairs are counted, and the Maps filled, in the same order as before.
- [x] `st.contacts` keys are numbers, `pairKey(i, j)` = i × 2^21 + j with i < j (`pairKey`, `pairOf` in `sim/society`); `@state` updated.
- [x] One pass decays the ties and notes those that fall below 0.02; only those are checked for dropping, after the day's contact and the kin floor.
- [x] Behaviour unchanged: every tie, attitude, union and log line is the same. `tests/golden.json` changes only in `contacts`, whose keys are now numbers: decoded back to `"i:j"`, the 12 changed dumps match the old ones line for line, in order.
- [x] `tests/perf.js`, populous medium at seed 7 after 2 years (845 alive): 87.2 ms per game day before, 65.7 after (−25%); moving round 165 → 133 ms, meetings 62 → 47 ms.
Notes / questions:
- What is left of the cost is the pairs themselves: idle pawns gather on one green hex, and co-presence there is quadratic in the size of the crowd. Talk with one partner per pawn (VB-061) and familiarity from talk rather than co-presence (`docs/review-relationships.md`, proposal B) remove that.
