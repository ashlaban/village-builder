# VB-440: Developing a tile tells the route cache
Status: done (2026-11-14)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: roads
Phase: implement
Design ref: World, climate and travel (Worn paths); src/sim/routes.js, tileCost
Goal: A developed tile walks as open ground (`tileCost`, `src/sim/routes.js`: cost 1, whatever its biome), so developing one changes its walking cost. Three acts developed a tile without telling the route cache, so routes cached past it stayed stale until something else dropped them: a household's broken field (`sim/fields`, `broken`), its new meadow (`sim/meadows`, `addMeadow`) and a village plot's broken field (`sim/expansion`, `finishStep`). Every other writer of `w.dev` already calls `costChanged`. Kim, 14 November: found by the review of PR #134, whose research is not merged; this fix is taken from it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The three acts call `costChanged(st, tile)` when the tile was not developed before, and not when it was (its cost does not change then).
- [x] A test in `tests/routes.js` (fast): each act marks its tile in `st.routeDirty`; a second break of developed ground marks nothing. It fails without the fix.
