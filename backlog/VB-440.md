## Decision and impact

User requested batch one of the path-network proposal. This establishes the cost separation and compilation, not the later junction graph.
Preference remains equal to walking cost so Decided path speeds and game balance are preserved. No new player action or tuning run.
`sim/routes` owns all derived scratch buffers; no authoritative state, world fields, exports or save format changes. Physical movement still
uses `stepCost`; Dijkstra accumulates walking cost separately from its choice labels. Cache refresh and path-stage cadence are unchanged.
The compiler touches only searched tiles and their six neighbours, with two new work counters. Slope rows use Float64 to preserve arithmetic.
No new scans over pawns, cached routes or whole-world tiles occur during a tick.

### Measurements

Founders and populous, seed 7, tiny, one year, existing counters before/after:

| Scenario | Searches | Heap removals | Cache entries checked | Cache hits | New compiled tiles | New compiled slope edges |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| founders | 504 / 504 | 9,799 / 9,799 | 2,133 / 2,133 | 1,920 / 1,920 | 17,959 | 240 |
| populous | 6,643 / 6,643 | 161,086 / 161,086 | 43,094 / 43,094 | 21,900 / 21,900 | 290,983 | 162 |

New counters are work after seeding; most relief is already compiled by generation. Every pre-existing counter is identical before/after.
The controlled 56-by-40 mixed-terrain probe runs 200 fixed pairs per sweep. Before and after have the same SHA-256 route checksum
`e6ff1883135182348c47fc3f4475053e2bd7c61ab46a8b7976e0a1d69847983b` and 127,551 heap removals per sweep. New code compiles
101,650 tile costs per sweep and 13,058 slope edges on the first sweep, then zero slope edges on all warm sweeps.

First sample: median warm sweep 74.34 ms before, 62.22 ms after (16.3% lower). A second sample after verification: 67.61 ms before,
66.88 ms after (1.1% lower); first sweep 110.05 / 97.89 ms. Timings include hashing and JIT/GC noise; these two samples do **not**
establish a reliable end-to-end speedup. Cache-refresh work is identical (199 entries checked, 103 retained); second-sample time
1.42 / 1.68 ms is diagnostic noise on a tiny operation, not evidence of an invalidation optimization.

Cost tradeoff: new scratch arrays use approximately 85 bytes per world tile when slopes are allocated, in addition to the existing
search scratch; they are bounded by world size and reclaimed with the world. Tile costs rebuild lazily per search, so this batch
avoids stale terrain after generation/editor/load without requiring new change publishers. There is no new per-pawn or daily world scan.
Late-save fixtures were absent; no late-game speedup or whole-game CPU saving is claimed. Existing cost targets and their limits remain
unchanged. The 18 advisory residuals from verify remain for their existing tasks; this batch adds no policy that changes simulation work.

### Deferred

The comfort policy, terrain-dependent willingness to detour, sparse junction graph, shared-destination routing, cache-invalidation
optimization and traffic-induced mud are later work. The route-failure bypass is already tracked by VB-329; it is not changed here.
Activating a non-neutral policy must revisit preference budgets versus physical reach cutoffs, equal-choice ties and cache invalidation;
separate arrays alone are not a completed preference behaviour. The current symmetry and Decided walking/path speeds stand.
