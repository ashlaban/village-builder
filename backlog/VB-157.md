# VB-157: Borders: disputes between villages, cairns and grudges
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: claims
Phase: implement
Design ref: Claims, ownership and territory, Infield, outland and wilderness (Borders, Border grudges: Decided 1 Nov); Customs (Disputed claims). Review: `docs/review-claims.md`.
Goal: Where two villages' outlands meet, both claim the same tiles. Settle them by custom, mark the settled border, and let the losers remember it, so borders are stories. Depends on VB-156.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] At the meeting, a tile two or more villages claim goes to the winner under the Disputed claims custom (`settle`, VB-042): the practice both villages hold, or the culture's custom when they differ, or the first claim when they are of different cultures. The losers' claims are dropped.
- [ ] Each settled tile that touches the loser's land gets a cairn (`st.cairns`, a `Map` from tile to `{a (winner), b (loser), t}`); the log tells it once per pair of villages and meeting ("Ulsäter and Rönnes set cairns between them; Ulsäter keeps 3 tiles, by the first claim").
- [ ] Each holder of a losing village remembers each holder of the winning village: a memory `border`, "Took our land", −0.1 per meeting lost, cap −0.3, fading over 3 years (`SOCIAL.memories`).
- [ ] `tests/borders.js` on a tiny world: two villages claim one strip; each `dispute` option gives the expected winner; cairns stand on the border; the holders' opinions fall.
- [ ] Reported in the PR: disputes and cairns per meeting in populous tiny, seed 7, years 1–10.
