# VB-157: Borders: disputes between villages, cairns and grudges
Status: done (2026-11-02)
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: claims
Phase: implement
Design ref: Claims, ownership and territory, Infield, outland and wilderness (Borders, Border grudges: Decided 1 Nov); Customs (Disputed claims). Review: `docs/review-claims.md`.
Goal: Where two villages' outlands meet, both claim the same tiles. Settle them by custom, mark the settled border, and let the losers remember it, so borders are stories. Depends on VB-156.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] At the meeting, a tile two or more villages claim goes to the winner under the Disputed claims custom (`settle`, VB-042): the practice both villages hold, or the culture's custom when they differ, or the first claim when they are of different cultures. The losers' claims are dropped.
- [x] Each disputed tile, once settled, gets a cairn (`st.cairns`, a `Map` from tile to `{a (winner), b (loser), t}`); the log tells it once per pair of villages and meeting ("Ulsäter and Rönnes set cairns between them; Ulsäter keeps 3 tiles, by the first claim").
- [x] Each holder of a losing village remembers each holder of the winning village: a memory `border`, "Took our land", −0.1 per meeting lost, cap −0.3, fading over 3 years (`SOCIAL.memories`).
- [x] `tests/borders.js` on a tiny world: two villages claim one strip; each `dispute` option gives the expected winner; cairns stand on the border; the holders' opinions fall.
- [x] Reported in the PR: disputes and cairns per meeting in populous tiny, seed 7, years 1–10.
- 2026-11-02 (Kim, border pressure): as first built, villages almost never disputed a border: over 20 years at populous tiny seeds 3, 7 and 42, no dispute and no cairn, since villages claim free land nearest first and a claim becomes land after a year. Kim chose border pressure. Built: a village still short of outland once no free tile is left in its reach may claim the outland of a neighbour that holds more outland a farm than it does (any, if the neighbour's farms all stand empty), nearest first, at most `CLAIMS.pressPerMeeting` = 4 tiles a meeting; the holder's right counts as the oldest claim and is realised, so under `first`, `strongest` and `kin` the holder keeps it and under `meeting` the village with more voices takes it; a village with no occupied farm cannot defend its land. A cairn keeps the loser off a tile for `CLAIMS.cairnYears` = 3. The proposal said "nearer its own green"; measured, that almost never held (the first land grab already gives each tile to the nearer village), while villages short of outland were common (4 to 6 of 8 to 10 at year 20), so the test is outland a farm instead.
- 2026-11-02 (done): measured, day by day for 20 years on populous tiny: seed 7, 7 border meetings, all Rönnes pressing Ultuna and losing by the first claim (years 2, 3, 6, 8, 10, 14, 19); seed 3, 3 (Skogby pressing Halvik, years 12, 19, 20); seed 42, none. Under the default custom no land changed hands. Memory `border`: "Won the border from us", −0.1 a meeting, cap −0.3, 3 years. `tests/borders.js` checks each option. Golden re-recorded: only the new, empty `st.cairns`.
