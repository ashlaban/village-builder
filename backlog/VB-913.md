# VB-913: A lapsed wish for a match is told, with why
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, Marriage as a negotiation (The player's doors)
Goal: A wish that fails is told, with the reason, so the player can wish again.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] When `clearMatchWish` (`src/sim/commands.js`) clears the played pawn's wish because the wished partner married another, a personal event says so ("Your wish for Ingeborg Håkansdotter lapsed: she married Ragnar Björnsson at Vallen's round"), and the skip stops on it.
- [ ] When the round passes a wish by (the pair's score below the round's lowest, the other's village matched first, a rule of the round that barred them that day), the played pawn is told the reason the round had, in one line, and the wish stands for the next round unless it was cleared.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state for each: the wished partner marries another; the round passes the wish by on its lowest score.
