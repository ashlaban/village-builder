## Implementation notes (agent run)
- `sim/agents`: `walkHours(st, a)`, `placeOf(st, a)`, `doneOf(st, j)`, `dueOf(st, j)` (the tick whose step finishes the work, null with no hands); read-only.
  They read two new plain, saved fields written beside the stepping, which the stepping itself does not read (behaviour unchanged):
  `agent.leg` {t, x, y, seg} (set by `walkTo`, and by a journey leg that goes on), and `job.cast` {t, done, rate, key} (set when the
  hands or their rate differ, a hand is gone, or the hours began anew). The cart's part in carting is in the rate (`Carts.cartingRate`, a
  read-only twin of `carting`, which takes a cart).
- A walk begun before the step's walking moves its pawn that step: its start is the step before (`anchor`); a walk begun by a side task's
  step likewise (stepAgents).
- Test: `tests/legs.js` (in `npm test`), on the probe state over 12 days: walks 1506 steps, `placeOf` within 3e-13 of `a.x, a.y`; 321
  tasks, 266 ended, every one within 1 step of what was foretold; `doneOf` within a step's work of `j.done` (a hand leaving mid-step
  is told after the hands before it). Nights, meals, hands joining and leaving, and journeys' legs occur in the run; 80 days also pass.
- `node tools/same.js --base 4ae3e1a` (founders, heirs; 7, 42; tiny; 2 years): the only differing leaves are the new `.leg` and `.cast`.
