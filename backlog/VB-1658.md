## Notes
- The own rule (sim/upgrade ownRung, a calendar entry each working hour, counter campLook): a camp farm whose head is not held off (sim/projects heldOff) places by sim/camp placeOwn (the played plan's farmstead tile if it takes one, else the landing, else the nearest in bfs order) and, booth raised, issues beginCottage for played and unplayed alike (one path; its home timber into the walls).
- Fence: the generator's village fence of the solo landing encloses the one house tile (sim/layout finishLand); a camp's landing takes it down at seeding (makeCamp). A farm placed in play gets no fence, as a settler's (sim/cottage) gets none: laying one at placing would be a fence without work. The village's centre stays at the landing.
- Menu: `Sim.buildMenu` is sim/agenda's wrapper, adding `work` (the plan's estimate, the chain's steps by the pawn's own estimate, mid; 23.9 against `hours` 12 on the tiny land).
- The gap: the last timber load lies at the booth before the staked booths are next asked (stakedWork, the island ask at each review hour), so the wood step stays open and the raising unposted for up to an hour (overnight after 18:00). A real wait of the hourly ask: shown as `opens` at that hour ('the raising of the booth'), not `unposted`; behaviour unchanged (closing it would move the solo start's raising).
