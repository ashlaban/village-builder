  **What was built.** `sim/asking` `askMatch` asks for either side: the man's on every asking day; a woman's from the asking day of season `ASK.womenFrom` (1, the summer) until the round, if nobody asks her (`herSideAsks`); a woman who holds a farm and speaks for herself as a man. The score is `sim/matching` `sideScore`: `matchScore` plus `MARRIAGE.headsLiking` (0.5) a point of the asking spokesman's opinion of the other spokesman; the noise is drawn once for each candidate weighed and the best one's is kept on the offer (`fancy`). The means are no longer read at the asking. `sim/marriage`: the basis `match` takes an offer from either side (`asker: 'woman'` where hers asks; its subject is the one who asks, its step the village of the one asked); `whyNo` is each answer's rule (one to marry: enmity, courting another unless it yields, "not this year"; a spokesman, and one who speaks for itself: the score as it reads it, with the fancy for the side that asked and the noise at its mean for the side that answers, and the means of the home). `sim/round`: `villageRound`'s loop over men and women is gone, with `MARRIAGE.goAhead`; `answerDue` answers the matches of a village's step best first as the side asked reads them (`matchRead`), a courting pair's own before any arranged one. What is left of a village's round: its store, the played pawn's wished match, kin pressure, and the visits of its men who hold no offer. `roundPairs` is 0.
  **Memories and events.** `refusedSuit` and `marriedAgainst` are rows of `SOCIAL.memories`; VB-036 built its memory as `refusedCourting` (not `refusedUs`, which is the row for goods), and it is unchanged. A refusal is told by `log` (kind `matchRefused`, with `by`, a new field of the record, and `reason`: enmity, courting, wish, score) and in the lives of the two (`personEvent`); a match made against a wish is the kind `marriedAgainst`, in the log and in the life of the one who yielded. A home that cannot feed another mouth is told as `putOff` (as the matcher told it), remembered by nobody.
  **Decisions (Deciding without Kim), each a close call another agent could have made the other way:**
  1. *The best first, then the next.* "Her side answers the best offer it holds and refuses the rest" is built as: the offers one is asked by are answered best first; the first that all say yes to marries, and the rest lapse at that ("has married": no refusal, no memory). If the best comes to nothing (one of the four says no, or the home cannot feed another), the next best is answered in its turn, rather than refused unread.
  2. *One who asks is asked by nobody, and one asked asks nobody.* With both sides asking, a pawn could else be in an offer of its own and in another's, and marry whichever village's step came first. So `ASK.held` (5) counts those asking one who does not ask.
  3. *A spokesman's enmity is in the score, not a no of its own.* As built (VB-275) a spokesman said no at enmity towards the other spokesman; the plan lists what a spokesman weighs (the score with his opinion of the other, the means, later the terms) and keeps "no at enmity" only for the one to marry. Enmity now costs a spokesman's reading 0.25 to 0.5 of a point. A courting pair's own offer keeps the rule as VB-036 built it.
  4. *The played pawn's asking is any pawn's.* VB-823 left the played pawn's own asking empty ("it asks by the verb"), since the round's matcher still matched it. With the matcher gone its asking, and that of one it speaks for, makes the offer its wish allows (as it would; the one it names; not this year), and its rungs of land likewise; the player answers. A wished partner is that pawn's offer and no more: the side asked may take a better one.
  5. *The round run at once holds the askings first.* `Sim.movingRound` (the drawer's button, the tests) first holds the asking of each who seeks, as its last before the round and due at the round's first step, so that the debug round still marries. It is a debug verb.
  6. *The test asked for in the criteria* ("her side takes the better of two offers and the other spokesman remembers") contradicts the fourth criterion and the plan (not booked when hers took a better offer). Built by the plan: `tests/suit.js` checks that a better offer taken leaves no memory, and that a spokesman who refuses a suit is remembered.
