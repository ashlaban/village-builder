# VB-693: An arranged match is a spokesman's offer, and her side takes the best

Goal: Every marriage is an offer made by a pawn (the pair's own, VB-036, before any arranged one) and answered by the two and their spokesmen, by opinion, means and terms.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The asking (VB-823's asking day): the spokesman of a member whose wish is to marry (`spokesmanOf`; itself where it speaks for itself) offers `match` to the best-scoring partner the gate allows (age, the kin limit, a home under the residence custom) among VB-288's pool narrowed by VB-289 (the villages within a day's walk, a village visited since the last round, his last gathering when VB-298 has landed), at most `ASK.pool` (40) candidates, the nearest villages first, and none who holds `ASK.held` (5) open offers. The man's side asks; a woman's side that holds no offer asks from the summer asking day, and a woman who holds a farm and speaks for herself from the first. A courting member's spokesman arranges nothing (VB-036).
- [ ] The score is `matchScore` with two changes: the noise is drawn once, at the asking (`st.rng.society`), and kept on the offer; and `MARRIAGE.headsLiking` (0.5) times the asking spokesman's opinion of the other spokesman is added. It must reach `MARRIAGE.minScore`. The village's matcher (`villageRound`'s loop over men and women) and `MARRIAGE.goAhead` are removed; `roundPairs` falls to 0 and `asksWeighed` counts the candidates read.
- [ ] The answers, at the round: her side answers the best offer it holds (the score as her spokesman reads it: the same terms, his opinion of the other spokesman, the noise at its mean) and refuses the rest. Each of the four of `answersOf` weighs: a spokesman, that score reaching `minScore`, the means of the home (`marriage.means`, VB-631) and the terms when VB-276 has landed; one to marry, no at `SOCIAL.enemy` towards the other, no while courting another unless its opinion of its spokesman is above its opinion of the one it courts (it yields), no under 'not this year' (plan, section 3). The gate is read at the round by `marry`, unchanged.
- [ ] Consequences in opinion, rows of `SOCIAL.memories`: `refusedMatch` as built; `refusedSuit` (-0.1, to -0.3, 2 years: a spokesman, of the spokesman who refused his offer; not when hers took a better offer); `marriedAgainst` (-0.3, 5 years: a member who yielded, of its spokesman); VB-036's `refusedUs`. Events for a refusal and for a match made against a wish, by `log` and `personEvent`.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7 and 42: marriages a year and the mean age at first marriage within 5% of before, the never-married at 45 to 54 within 2 points (`tools/marriage-demography.js`), growth by principle 2; a target out is reported (before, after, residual, cause), not tuned beyond five short runs over this task's own parameters; expected and to be told from a fault: first marriages about a third of a year earlier (the go-ahead roll's delay). The share of offers refused, by who refused; marriages against a wish; marriages by the walking hours between the two villages, as VB-288 reports them; households with an unmarried adult over 30 at year 30 (whether households, not only averages, are better off).
- [ ] Parameterisation, at most five short runs (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): `MARRIAGE.headsLiking`, `ASK.held` and the season from which a woman's side asks; the values tried and what each gave, the rest as a `tuning` row.
- [ ] Cost (plan, section 7): candidates read a year are at most seekers x 4 askings x `ASK.pool`, each one table read (VB-286); `scaling.roundPairs` and `history.roundPairs` are reported on `asksWeighed` with their residuals (before: exponent 2 against 1.15, and 13.2 against 1.25). `tests/commands.js` and `tests/record.js` pass; tests on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`), chances forced with `fix`: her side takes the better of two offers and the other spokesman remembers; a courting member refuses, and yields when it holds its spokesman dearer; a woman's side asks in summer; the cap of 5.
