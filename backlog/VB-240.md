# VB-240: Opinion is derived: compatibility shown by familiarity, and memories only of what happened
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Traits, talk and stories; Exchange, goodwill and obligations (The gift: Goodwill and opinion). Kim, 10 Nov 2026 (code review).
Goal: Opinion is kin standing plus timed memories, and nearly all memories come from talk, whose kind is drawn from the pair's fixed compatibility: at the late fixtures the memories' sum correlates 0.72 with compatibility, and the friend and fell-out events they cause are 83% of the record. Kim decided on 10 November that opinion is derived: kin standing, plus compatibility shown as two pawns grow familiar, plus memories of things that happened; that talk grows the tie only; and that only becoming close friends or enemies is an event.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `opinionOf(st, a, b)` is the kin standing's value (`SOCIAL.situational`, as today), plus affinity, `SOCIAL.affinity.weight` (0.6) × `compat(st, a, b)` × min(1, `tieOf(st, a, b)` / `SOCIAL.affinity.known` (16)), plus the memories of a's record of b, clamped to -1..1. Goodwill's gratitude stays slice 4's (VB-220) and adds to the same sum.
- [ ] Talk writes no memory: `chat`, `goodTalk` and `crossWords` leave `SOCIAL.memories`, and `talk` no longer calls `remember`. A talk still adds `TIES.talk` to both ties, still draws its kind from compatibility for the bubble (`agent.talk.kind`, the same draws from `st.rng.talk`), and still halts the two. `bull`, `border` and `claim` stay, and a new kind is added as they were.
- [ ] `pawn.rel` holds a record only while it holds a memory of an event; `fadeRelations` keeps its seasonal pass over what is left (name its cost at `medium-ref` in the PR: about 400 memories against 67,000). A load drops the three talk kinds from older records.
- [ ] Events: `SOCIAL.closeFriend` is 0.45. A pawn's record gets "Became close friends with" when its opinion of another reaches `closeFriend`, and "Became the enemy of" at `enemy` (-0.5), each left only `SOCIAL.back` past its line; `friend` (0.25) and `dislike` (-0.2) stay as the page's labels and make no event. Since opinion now moves with the tie and not only at a `remember`, the band is checked where the tie changes (a talk, a job done together, the day's hours at home, the season's kin floor) and at `remember`: say in the PR where the band is kept and that no step walks all pairs for it (Rules for cost, 2). `notable.js` and `tests/record.js` follow.
- [ ] `talkWeight` and `companyFor` read the new opinion; `companyFor` takes the pawn's tie scale once, as `talkHour` does.
- [ ] `design.md`: a row "Opinion" under Traits, talk and stories with the formula and Kim's decision (Decided, 10 Nov), and The gift's "Goodwill and opinion" row brought in line (gratitude adds to the derived sum). `docs/architecture/modules.md` and the `@state` line of `pawn.rel` follow. The open question on a "Worked together" memory (`QUESTIONS.md`, VB-039 and VB-106, question 2) gets a note that work together already grows the tie, which now carries it.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, in the PR and here, at `small-y40` and `medium-ref` after a further 4 years each, and populous small at seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 for 15 years: per pawn of 12 and over, friends, close friends, disliked and enemies (median, 90th centile, share with none); events a living pawn-year by kind, and the record's size; talks a day; living and households (the culture's drift reads ties, which talk still feeds: say whether customs moved, `node tools/bands.js`). Expected from the fixtures as they stand: about 3 friends and 4 disliked a pawn, and enemies seldom without an event.
- [ ] Cost: `node tools/bench.js` before and after; "talk and contacts" (51 ms a game day at `medium-ref`) is expected to fall, with its residual reported against its share of the budget, since half of `talk`'s own time writes the two memories. `tests/golden.json` and `tests/counters.json` re-recorded, the changed entries listed.
- [ ] Invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): no memory of a kind outside `SOCIAL.memories`; a record's band agrees with its opinion within `back`. `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals.
