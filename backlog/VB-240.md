# VB-240: Opinion is derived: compatibility shown by familiarity, and memories only of what happened
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Traits, talk and stories; Exchange, goodwill and obligations (The gift: Goodwill and opinion). Kim, 10 Nov 2026 (code review).
Goal: Opinion is kin standing plus timed memories, and nearly all memories come from talk, whose kind is drawn from the pair's fixed compatibility: at the late fixtures the memories' sum correlates 0.72 with compatibility, and the friend and fell-out events they cause are 83% of the record. Kim decided on 10 November that opinion is derived: kin standing, plus compatibility shown as two pawns grow familiar, plus memories of things that happened; that talk grows the tie only; and that only becoming close friends or enemies is an event.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `opinionOf(st, a, b)` is the kin standing's value (`SOCIAL.situational`, as today), plus affinity, `SOCIAL.affinity.weight` (0.6) × `compat(st, a, b)` × min(1, `tieOf(st, a, b)` / `SOCIAL.affinity.known` (16)), plus the memories of a's record of b, clamped to -1..1. Goodwill's gratitude stays slice 4's (VB-220) and adds to the same sum.
- [x] Talk writes no memory: `chat`, `goodTalk` and `crossWords` leave `SOCIAL.memories`, and `talk` no longer calls `remember`. A talk still adds `TIES.talk` to both ties, still draws its kind from compatibility for the bubble (`agent.talk.kind`, the same draws from `st.rng.talk`), and still halts the two. `bull`, `border` and `claim` stay, and a new kind is added as they were.
- [x] `pawn.rel` holds a record only while it holds a memory of an event; `fadeRelations` keeps its seasonal pass over what is left (name its cost at `medium-ref` in the PR: about 400 memories against 67,000). A load drops the three talk kinds from older records.
- [x] Events: `SOCIAL.closeFriend` is 0.45. A pawn's record gets "Became close friends with" when its opinion of another reaches `closeFriend`, and "Became the enemy of" at `enemy` (-0.5), each left only `SOCIAL.back` past its line; `friend` (0.25) and `dislike` (-0.2) stay as the page's labels and make no event. Since opinion now moves with the tie and not only at a `remember`, the band is checked where the tie changes (a talk, a job done together, the day's hours at home, the season's kin floor) and at `remember`: say in the PR where the band is kept and that no step walks all pairs for it (Rules for cost, 2). `notable.js` and `tests/record.js` follow.
- [x] `talkWeight` and `companyFor` read the new opinion; `companyFor` takes the pawn's tie scale once, as `talkHour` does.
- [x] `design.md`: a row "Opinion" under Traits, talk and stories with the formula and Kim's decision (Decided, 10 Nov), and The gift's "Goodwill and opinion" row brought in line (gratitude adds to the derived sum). `docs/architecture/modules.md` and the `@state` line of `pawn.rel` follow. The open question on a "Worked together" memory (`QUESTIONS.md`, VB-039 and VB-106, question 2) gets a note that work together already grows the tie, which now carries it.
- [x] Numbers before and after, in the PR and here, at `small-y40` and `medium-ref` after a further 4 years each, and populous small at seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 for 15 years: per pawn of 12 and over, friends, close friends, disliked and enemies (median, 90th centile, share with none); events a living pawn-year by kind, and the record's size; talks a day; living and households (the culture's drift reads ties, which talk still feeds: say whether customs moved, `node tools/bands.js`).   (`grow`, both ways), a job finished together (`workedTogether` through `grow`), the day's hours at home (`dailyTies` through `grow`, the
  housemate pairs it already walks), the season's kin floor (`dailyTies`, the near kin it already raises), `remember`, and the season's
  `fadeRelations`, which checks again the bands that exist (a few a pawn) and drops those to the dead. No step walks all pairs for it

  entered) lazily, as a tie or memory fades or a housemate leaves; it is told at the pair's next touch, dated then, and the season's pass
  bounds how long a band can stand stale.

  `touch`es (one `opinionOf` each, no allocation). `dailyTies` adds two `opinionOf` per housemate pair with hours at home (about 3 a living

  in 55,074 before (the task's 67,000 was counted at the fixtures' load), plus 588 bands, once a season.
Implementation:
- `opinionAt(st, a, b, tie)` = the standing's value + `affinity` (`SOCIAL.affinity.weight` × `compat` × min(1, tie / `known`)) + the memories,
  clamped to -1..1; `opinionOf` reads the tie with `tieOf`. `bandOf(was, o)` enters at `closeFriend` and `enemy` and leaves `back` past them.
  `touch` writes "Became close friends with" (`closeFriend`) or "Became the enemy of" (`enemy`). `talk` draws its kind as before (the same
  draws of `st.rng.talk`), writes no memory, grows both ties and touches both. `forget` clears the dead's records and bands, and its own and
  others' entries for it reached through its records, bands and ties; a band of one it had no tie to goes at the season's pass.
- `reasonsOf` lists the affinity as "Get on well" or "Do not get on" (the panel's reason), beside the memories and the standing.
- `fadeRelations` copies the memories left with `sift` (the same rule as before, shared with the load's migration); `relations.js` is at 400
  lines, its limit: the next change there should first split goodwill (VB-217's section, about 70 lines) into its own module.
