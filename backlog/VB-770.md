# VB-770: The walk to a named household to deal
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-302, VB-698
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: town
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Settlements, Towns and regions (The walk to a door); Exchange (The fair); docs/plans/VB-189.md, section 5.3
Goal: Between gatherings, a pawn who lacks a thing walks to someone who has it (Kim, 16 Nov: on other days a pawn may walk to a named household to deal; the hard rule).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A household remembers, for each good, at most `DEAL.known` 4 households it has dealt with or seen dealing (on a green, at a door), the least recent dropped (rule 2); plain data with an owner, in the save, with invariants. It is the household's knowledge: a household it has never met is not known, whatever it holds.
- [ ] On its planning day a head that is under its target of a good it cannot make (VB-698), and knows such a household within a day there and back (6 walking hours, `design.md`, Between villages, Near and far), plans a walk when its estimate of the gain exceeds the walk at `DEAL.hour` (VB-769's, or defined here if this lands first): a journey of VB-287, a pawn carrying what the household means to give. Nothing teleports: the goods go and come on the pawn's back.
- [ ] At the door: an offer by VB-690, then a swap by VB-302's command, or a refusal that both remember (the refused does not walk there again for that good for `DEAL.sulk` 1 year). The verb is any pawn's (`src/sim/commands.js`), and the played pawn's: an opportunity "Walk to <household> for <good>", with the reason when it is not possible.
- [ ] Nothing here is counted for a place's label (the label reads greens). Events: the walk and the deal, by `log` with kind, pawn and place; folded into yearly counts.
- [ ] Reported, populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years, before and after: walks a household a year, the customary worth dealt at doors and its share of all dealing (the mock: 60% to 77%, a toy's figure), refusals, hours walked to deal (the hours report); hungry household-days and growth; which households walk.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state: a head lacking iron who knows a smith 2 hours away walks and deals; one who knows none does not walk; a refusal is remembered; a walk longer than a day there and back is not planned.
- Cost (rule 9): about 1.8 walks a household a year (the first mock): 300 journeys a year at `medium-ref`; the weighing at the planning day over at most 4 known households a good under target. A counter for the weighing, before and after.
