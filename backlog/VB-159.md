# VB-159: Hay spoils in the barn, by winters held
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: animals
Phase: implement
Design ref: Animals (Hay and the hard winter limit: hay in the barn spoils as grain does; Kim, 2 Nov 2026); docs/review-balance.md (grain spoiling in store).
Goal: Grain in store spoils by years of need held (`ECON.storeLoss`, `spoilGrain` in `src/sim/hunger.js`), but hay in the barn (`hh.hay`) keeps for ever. Haymaking stops at two winters' need, yet a household whose herd shrinks (the autumn count, a death, a sale) keeps a barn it never uses up. Kim, 2 Nov 2026: hay spoils in tiers, as grain does.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `HERD.hayLoss` = [0.05, 0.25, 0.5] (in `src/sim/rules.js`): each day, hay up to one winter's need of the household's animals now (`winterNeed`, `src/sim/herds.js`) loses 5% a year, the second winter's worth 25% a year, the rest 50% a year, compounded daily as in `spoilGrain`. A household with no animals has a winter's need of 0, so all its hay is in the last tier.
- [ ] Hay lying mown on the meadows does not spoil by this rule.
- [ ] `hh.haySpoilt` counts the hay lost since the start (`@state` line updated, invariant: a finite number ≥ 0 in `tests/lib/invariants.js`); the household panel shows it beside the hay, as "spoilt" is shown for grain.
- [ ] A test on a constructed household: with exactly one winter's need in the barn, a year loses about 5%; with four winters' need, about 5% + 25% of one winter's worth + 50% of two; with no animals, hay halves in a year.
- [ ] `tests/hay.js` and `tests/byre.js` still pass; report from `tools/balance.js` (4 seeds) whether hungry household-days or animals culled for want of hay change.
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
