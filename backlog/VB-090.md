# VB-090: Landless adults take service where labour is short
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: expansion
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion, Leaving home (Service: a landless adult may take service where labour is short; Agreed); People, households and family records (Household and family: servants live in households not their own; Agreed).
Goal: Only the founders' and populous servants, and the members of a farm that dies out (`extinct`, `src/sim/heirs.js`), ever live as servants. A landless adult otherwise stays at home for life, however many hands the farm already has, while a farm of an old couple with four fields has too few. Let landless adults move into service where labour is short, so work and mouths spread across a village.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Hands per field of a household: members aged 14–59, not retired, divided by its fields (at least 1). A household is short of labour below 1 hand per field and has hands to spare above 1.5.
- [ ] At the moving round, after handovers and after `st.demand` is counted: each unmarried pawn in `st.demand` whose household has hands to spare, and would still have at least 1 hand per field without them, may move. Oldest first, they go as a servant (`p.servant = true`, role farmhand or maid) to the household of their own village with the fewest hands per field, if it is short; otherwise to the shortest household of the nearest village within 15 steps by `hexSteps` between centres; otherwise they stay. Each household takes at most one new servant per moving round.
- [ ] A servant whose household is no longer short may move again at a later moving round under the same rule; nobody moves back home by this rule.
- [ ] Service is board and lodging only: the servant eats from the new household's store and works its jobs, as servants do today. Wages wait for money.
- [ ] A life event and a log line name the farm the servant moved to (`moved`).
- [ ] The placement shares one helper with VB-065 (village first, then the nearest village), whichever is built first.
- [ ] A test on a constructed village: a household of 2 old holders with 4 fields and a household with 4 adult landless children and 2 fields; after the moving round one child serves at the first farm.
- [ ] Populous medium at seed 7, 5 years, and founders at seeds 7 and 42, 30 years: servants per year, hands per field (lowest, median, highest) and hungry household-days, before and after (reported, not asserted).
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
