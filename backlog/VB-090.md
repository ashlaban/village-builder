# VB-090: Landless adults take service where labour is short
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion, Leaving home (Service: a landless adult may take service where labour is short; Agreed); People, households and family records (Household and family: servants live in households not their own; Agreed).
Goal: Only the founders' and populous servants, and the members of a farm that dies out (`extinct`, `src/sim/heirs.js`), ever live as servants. A landless adult otherwise stays at home for life, however many hands the farm already has, while a farm of an old couple with four fields has too few. Let landless adults move into service where labour is short, so work and mouths spread across a village.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Hands per field of a household: members aged 14–59, not retired, divided by its fields (at least 1). A household is short of labour below 1 hand per field and has hands to spare above 1.5.
- [x] At the moving round, after handovers and after `st.demand` is counted: each unmarried pawn in `st.demand` whose household has hands to spare, and would still have at least 1 hand per field without them, may move. Oldest first, they go as a servant (`p.servant = true`, role farmhand or maid) to the household of their own village with the fewest hands per field, if it is short; otherwise to the shortest household of the nearest village within 15 steps by `hexSteps` between centres; otherwise they stay. Each household takes at most one new servant per moving round.
- [x] A servant whose household is no longer short may move again at a later moving round under the same rule; nobody moves back home by this rule.
- [x] Service is board and lodging only: the servant eats from the new household's store and works its jobs, as servants do today. Wages wait for money.
- [x] A life event and a log line name the farm the servant moved to (`moved`).
- [x] The placement shares one helper with VB-065 (village first, then the nearest village), whichever is built first.
- [x] A test on a constructed village: a household of 2 old holders with 4 fields and a household with 4 adult landless children and 2 fields; after the moving round one child serves at the first farm.
- [x] Populous medium at seed 7, 5 years, and founders at seeds 7 and 42, 30 years: servants per year, hands per field (lowest, median, highest) and hungry household-days, before and after (reported, not asserted).
- [x] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.

## Orchestrator's decisions for Kim to overturn (VB-090, the implementation)
- The event and log line are service's (`service`, "goes into service with ... at <farm>, which was short of hands"), through the `serve`
  command as every servant's move, not a new `moved` event: the criterion's `moved` is read as "a life event naming the farm moved to",
  and VB-308 uses `moved` for kin who come as members, not servants.
- "Otherwise to the shortest household of the nearest village": the nearest village that has a short household able to take one (not
  already given a servant this round), ranked [steps, hands per field, id]; a nearer village whose short farms all took one does not stop
  the search.
- A household with no fields is never a target (with 0 fields, "at least 1" would make an old couple with no land short); it may still be
  a source.
- The lone holders' servants (`takeServants`, unchanged) come first in the round and count toward "one new servant a household a round".
- "Nobody moves back home" is read as never to a household where the pawn's living father or mother is a member.
- Candidates are those of `st.demand` who would serve by the existing service rule (`unfit` null: 18 to 50, free, unmarried; the player's
  `serve` wish), so a pawn over 50 does not go into service, as for the lone holders.
- Reach stays `SERVICE.reach` 15 hex steps: VB-290 (walking hours) has not landed; it reads `reachSteps`, the one helper.
