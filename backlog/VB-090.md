# VB-090: Heir to their own parents' farm, as a custom
Status: needs-review
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: expansion
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion, Succession (someone first in line for their own parents' farm steps behind the other relatives; this is a custom: Decided); Custom dimensions (Heir to their own parents' farm: options listed; default and malleability Proposed).
Goal: `heirRanking` in `src/sim/heirs.js` puts a relative who is first in line for their own parents' farm behind the others, always. The design makes this a custom with two options, so that farms may be combined where the custom allows.
Acceptance criteria (draft, pending `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-090):
- [ ] A new `DIMS` entry `ownFarm`, "Heir to their own parents' farm", with options `behind` "Steps behind other relatives" and `keeps` "Keeps their place", and a value in each `ARCHETYPES` entry (Old Nordic `behind`, malleability 0.6). It shows in the customs panel and changes at meetings like the others.
- [ ] Under `behind`, heir ranking is as today (golden file unchanged for this option). Under `keeps`, the relative keeps their place in the ranking.
- [ ] What happens when such a relative inherits under `keeps` follows the answer to `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-090 question 2.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed family: a nephew who is first in line for his own parents' farm steps behind his cousin under `behind` and does not under `keeps`.
- [ ] Re-record the golden file (a new custom changes the attitude arrays) and list the changed entries in the PR; rerun `node tests/calibrate.js natural 2 50` and report whether the pace of the other customs moved.
