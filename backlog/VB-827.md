Goal: The played pawn makes and answers the offers about a life through the year where it sees them, and every pawn's open ask is shown alike.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The pawn panel shows, for any pawn, its open ask (basis, to whom, due when) and the offers it holds unanswered, each as one row of one presentation for every basis (principle "Like is shown alike"), with an icon a basis and links to the pawns and the place.
- [ ] For the played pawn: its wish as the ranking of the rungs, each marked open or shut with the fact that shuts it, reordered through the `wish` verb; a button on an open rung makes the offer (the `offer` verb); each offer it holds has yes and no (the `answer` verb, through `Decisions.answer`) and, as advice, what its pawn would answer and why ("Olof would say no: he thinks ill of her father"), named as that pawn's judgement and never as what will happen (principle "The future is someone's estimate"). As head it sees the same for each member it speaks for.
- [ ] The village log tells an offer made, answered and lapsed by the events the simulation writes; nothing is written by the page.
- [ ] `node tests/smoke.js` and the `tests/browser*.js` it touches; a screenshot under `docs/img/` (`node tools/shot.js`).
- [ ] The page reads `st` and writes through the `Sim` API only (the lint).
