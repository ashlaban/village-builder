# VB-060: Relationships between pawns: tie, opinion and courting
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: social
Phase: implement
Design ref: Customs and culture (How attitudes move: contacts and ties). Kim, 2026-10-30 (VB-059): a relationship holds the existing tie (familiarity), an opinion from −1 to 1 (liking) and a courting flag; kin links stay in the family records.
Goal: Today `p.ties` maps another pawn's id to a tie weight, used by customs drift. Give each known pair a relationship record that interactions (VB-061), courting (VB-036) and help (VB-039) can read and change. Behaviour must not change yet: nothing reads opinion or courting in this task.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `p.rel`: a `Map` from the other pawn's id to `{ opinion, courting }`, beside `p.ties` (which keeps the tie weight and its uses unchanged). `@state` documented. A record exists while the pair has a tie; it is removed when the tie is dropped (below 0.02, as today) and opinion is within 0.05 of 0.
- [ ] Starting values: opinion 0.3 between kin (`kinNear`), 0.1 between members of one household, 0 otherwise; courting false. Newborns get records with their household.
- [ ] Opinion drifts back towards its starting value with a half-life of 2 years.
- [ ] Helpers in `sim/society`: `relOf(st, a, b)` (a record or null), `changeOpinion(st, a, b, d)` (clamped to −1..1, symmetric unless stated), `setCourting(st, a, b, on)`.
- [ ] The pawn panel lists the pawn's five strongest relationships with tie and opinion in words (dislikes, neutral, likes, close friend).
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged for the simulation: `npm run verify` passes, and the golden file changes only by the added `rel` fields (re-record it and list the changed entries in the PR).
