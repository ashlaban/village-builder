# VB-060: Relationships between pawns: opinion from kin and timed memories
Status: done (2026-10-31)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: social
Phase: implement
Design ref: Customs and culture (How attitudes move: contacts and ties). Kim, 2026-10-30 (VB-059): a relationship holds the existing tie (familiarity) and an opinion from −1 to 1 (liking); kin links stay in the family records. Relationships review, 31 Oct 2026 (`docs/review-relationships.md`, section 7): opinion is the sum of timed memories, as in RimWorld and Crusader Kings III, so it can be explained and does not saturate; the starting values below were replayed over real contact hours and accepted.
Goal: Today `p.ties` maps another pawn's id to a tie weight, used by customs drift. Give each pawn an opinion of the others it has dealings with, which talk (VB-061), help (VB-039, VB-106) and courting (VB-036) read and change, and show it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `p.rel`: a `Map` from the other pawn's id to `{ mem, band }`, beside `p.ties` (which keeps the tie weight and its uses unchanged). `mem` holds at most one memory per kind, `{ v, t }`; a record exists while it holds a memory. `@state` documented; checked by `checkState`.
- [x] Opinion of a about b (`opinionOf`), −1 to 1: the standing's value (spouse, parent or child +0.3, sibling +0.2, housemate +0.1) plus each memory as it is now. A memory gains its value at each event, up to its cap, and fades linearly to 0 over its days from the last event: chatted +0.03 (cap +0.2, a year), a good talk +0.1 (cap +0.5, 3 years), cross words −0.12 (cap −0.6, 3 years). Opinion has a direction. Tunables in `SOCIAL` (`src/sim/rules.js`).
- [x] Compatibility of a pair (`compat`), −1 to 1, from the world seed and the two ids, so it needs no memory: two uniform draws less 1, less 0.02 a year of age gap beyond 5 years (at most 0.4). VB-061 weights the outcome of a talk by it.
- [x] Faded memories are forgotten daily, and records with none left or about the dead are dropped; a death ends the relationships of others with the dead.
- [x] Entering friend (0.25 and up) or dislike (−0.2 and down) is a life event of the pawn ("Became friends with …", "Fell out with …"); leaving needs 0.1 past the line, and is not told.
- [x] Words: close friend (0.6), friend (0.25), neutral, dislikes (−0.2), enemy (−0.5). The pawn card lists the five people the pawn knows best by tie, and any it dislikes besides, with kin, the opinion in words and as a number (×100), and the largest memory.
- [x] `tools/sim.js --report relations`: per village, the records and their words, and friends and disliked people outside the household per adult.
- [x] Behaviour unchanged for the simulation: the golden file changes only by the added empty `rel` fields (32 dumps compared).
Notes / questions:
- The courting flag moves to the courting tasks (VB-036): nothing sets it before courting exists.
- Replayed over 2 populous years with talk as in VB-061: of a pawn's relations with other households 5% disliked, 82% neutral, 11% liked, 2% close; within the household 1% disliked, 49% liked, 31% close. The wrap-up (VB-063) tunes at full scale.
