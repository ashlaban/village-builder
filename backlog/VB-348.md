## Decisions (Deciding without Kim)
- Fallow is a row of `CROPS` (`fallow: {fallow: true, sow: [summer], mend: 0.08, hours: {plow: 10}}`), not a table of its own: a field's course is
  a key of one table, as the design's rotation says ("a course is a crop of the table or fallow"), and every reader of `CROPS[fd.crop]` (the
  window, the hours) reads it alike. A row without `seed` is a course, not a grain: `sim/stores` builds `CROP_LIST` from the rows with seed, so
  there is no `grain/fallow`. The course `fallow` (`fd.crop`) is not the stage `fallow` (a field gone to grass on an empty farm, VB-033).
- When fallow rests the soil: the criterion says "at the year's end", but in fallow, rye, barley the rye is sown on the fallow field in
  autumn, before the year ends. So the rest is earned in the summer and paid at the year's end, whatever the field bears by then: a fallow
  field of an occupied farm is marked on the summer's first day (`fd.rest` 0.5), tilling raises the mark to 1, and on the year's last day
  (winter day 12) the mark times 0.08 is added to the soil, to at most `SOIL.start`; a field manured above 1.0 keeps its soil. A field put to
  fallow after the summer earns nothing until the next summer, so a course from one autumn to the next earns 0.08 once.
- Fallow's tilling is the `plow` job (stage `stubble` kept, `fd.rest` 1), posted once a summer; it is not grazed (fields are never in the pasture,
  `st.used`) and nothing abandons it or makes it a meadow (no rule drops an unsown field); the test checks it stays in stubble.
- Rye's one round of weeding: `weeds: 1` on the row, counted in `fd.weeds` (absent for barley, which is weeded every `weedEvery` days as before);
  the round falls when the field is growing (autumn days 4 to 9), and a round still open `weedEvery` days later counts as missed.
- The frost spares a winter crop standing green (`winter: true`); stooks of any crop are lost to it as before. A ripe rye field still standing on
  autumn day 1 (`lost: [2, 0]`) is lost, logged as a lost crop (event kind `frost`, the kind of a crop lost in the field).
- "Refused, with the reason": sowing is a job, not a verb, so the refusal is `sowWhy(hh, fd, c)` (exported by `sim/kinds`): the course is not
  sown, out of the window, not hoed, or less than a field's seed of the crop ("the household holds 24 of the 25 rations of rye a field's seed
  takes"); the sow job is posted only when it is null. Seed is taken from the crop's own grain (`grain/rye`).
- Sheaves carry their crop: `household.sheafCrops`, absent while every barn entry is barley's (so barley runs keep their state), else the crop of
  each entry of `unthreshed`; threshing puts `grain/<crop>`.
- The weather factor: `reaped` multiplies every crop by `st.weather`, so rye reads the year it is reaped in (summer), as barley does.
- Left to VB-350 (rotations): the standing plan reserves only barley's seed, so a household holding rye for a rye field eats it when hungry or
  spoils it below a field's seed; the test holds the rye back by hand, as the plan will. Changing a field's course is a plain write of
  `fd.crop` (tests); VB-350 gives it its rule.
