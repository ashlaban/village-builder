# VB-146: Founding a new farm from a crowded village
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: L
Arch: expansion
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion, Moving to new land (Decided, 1 Nov: who leaves, distance; the rest Proposed). Review: `docs/review-expansion.md`.
Goal: A village whose land within 6 steps is taken leaves its landless adults waiting for life: in populous small at seed 7, 8 of 11 villages are full by year 30 with 20–33 adults waiting. Let a waiting adult of a crowded village leave with their family and siblings who follow, and found a village of one farm on good land 10 to 30 steps away, living in a booth while they build the cottage.
Acceptance criteria (Kim, 1 November 2026: landless only, 10–30 steps; the numbers below are Proposed starting values, in a `SETTLE` record in `src/sim/rules.js`):
- [ ] Crowded: at the moving round, after `startPlots`, a village is crowded when adults of it are in `st.demand` with no plot and no cottage coming, and `findSite` returns null. `v.crowded` holds how many such adults wait (0 when the village is not crowded).
- [ ] Each such adult counts the moving rounds waited in a crowded village (`p.landWait`); it is 0 again for anyone not waiting in a crowded village. Aged 18–45, they leave at the round with a chance of 0.3 at `landWait` 1, 0.6 at 2 and 0.8 from 3 (`st.rand`), oldest `landWait` first, then oldest, then lowest id.
- [ ] The party: the settler, their living spouse and their children under 18 in the same household, and each unmarried sibling (full or half) who is also waiting in the same village and in no other party, with a chance of 0.5 each, at most 2. Followers are servants (`p.servant`), farmhand or maid.
- [ ] Provisions: 36 days of the party's rations (`ECON.ration`) and seed for one field (`ECON.seedPerField`). The party's stakes come first; then each old household, the settler's first, gives grain above `SHARES.reserveBase + members × SHARES.reservePerMember`, as far as needed. Short of it, nobody moves, nothing is taken, and the log says the family could not spare the grain (once per settler and round).
- [ ] The site: a house tile 10 to 30 walking steps (`bfs` with `walkable`) from the home village's centre, farmable meadow or heath, not wooded, off rivers, unowned, not in `st.used`, at least 3 steps (`hexSteps`) from every house, green and field and from every tile of a plot or field project; with a block of 3 fields from `placeFields` (avoiding every yard) and a wood within 24 steps (`nearestWood`). Score: `siteScore` + 3 × the block's summed fertility − 0.15 × steps. The block's mean fertility must be at least 0.9 × the mean fertility of the home village's fields (1.0 if it has none). The search stays under 30 ms on populous small (reported).
- [ ] Founding: a new village (next id; centre the house; no green, well or fence; `commonsWood` the nearest wood; `plots: []`; `founded: { t, from, by }`), and a new Culture settlement with the same id, the mother's culture and a copy of the mother's practice. Name: the founder's given name in the genitive (an `s` added unless it ends in one) with `torp`, `ryd`, `boda`, `hult` or `mark`, the first not taken; if all are taken, `villageName`.
- [ ] The booth: a new farmstead on the house tile with no fields, `booth: { i: 0, done: 0 }` for the cottage stages, named for its surroundings as a lone farm; a new household (holder the settler) with the provisions as grain; house owned by it (`setOwner`); its field tile starts as the farm's field project (`f.project`, clearing). The party moves in (`moveTo`, a "moved" life event naming the new village); followers are servants. The log names the settler, the party, the mother village, the new village and the steps.
- [ ] Cottage jobs: while `f.booth` stands, the household posts the stages `foundation` (30 h), `walls` (60 h), `roof` (30 h) at the house, owned by the household; progress persists while a job is dropped. When the roof is on, `f.booth` is null and the log says so.
- [ ] `@state` lines for `v.crowded`, `v.founded`, `p.landWait` and `f.booth`; invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js`: every village has a settlement with its id; `v.crowded` is a non-negative integer; `f.booth` is null or has `i` from 0 to 2.
- [ ] A test (`tests/settle.js`, in `npm test`): a constructed crowded village with one waiting adult (`landWait` 3) and a sibling founds a village 10–30 steps away; the new household sows its first field in the spring after its field is broken; a village with room founds nothing; a party without provisions stays.
- [ ] Populous small at seeds 7 and 42, 30 years: villages founded, people in them, adults waiting with no room, by decade, before and after (reported).
