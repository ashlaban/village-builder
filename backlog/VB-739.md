Goal: Copper has a good of its own that recurs, and bronze is the good two places make together: each a decision for a head (Kim, 16 Nov).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The kettle: a good `kettle/copper`, one a household, `{kind, made}` read in closed form (worn out 30 years after it was made; no pass). Beaten from 2 bars of copper, 16 hours, skill smithing (VB-360); worth 40 rations (Proposed). A brewing with a kettle gives 2 barrels for 6 hours where one without gives 1 for 4 (`BREW`; the kettle's property is what it holds). The head's target for one (VB-698): a household that brews.
- [ ] The cast pot: `pot/bronze`, a kind of VB-366's pot (counts by kind, VB-733): cast from 2 bars of bronze in a mould of 1 load of clay, 12 hours, skill casting; it covers the store as any pot and does not break; worth 60 rations (Proposed). It is movable worth: given in a dowry (VB-276 reads the worth table) and divided among heirs as the animals are (VB-140).
- [ ] Each is carried: the kettle and the pot go to their new household by a pawn's journey (a dowry goes with the bride).
- [ ] The page (principle 15): the kettle and the pots by kind in the household's store, as VB-733 shows kinds.
- [ ] Invariants, `tools/stocks.js` lines; cost named: one record and one count a household.
- [ ] Numbers, populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15, 30 years: kettles and bronze pots by village, barrels of ale brewed a household with and without a kettle (and guests served: does the kettle move standing to copper villages), copper and tin carried between villages, bronze pots passed by dowry and by inheritance.
- [ ] The numbers that move are reported before and after at the seeds and maps named (Working rules); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
