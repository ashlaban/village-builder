# VB-452: A house's ground searches walk a kept list of its reach, not a new search each ask
Status: done (2026-11-15)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: none (cost; AGENTS.md, Rules for cost 6 and 8). src/sim/routes.js, reachTiles; src/sim/fish.js, src/sim/forage.js, src/sim/deer.js, ground
Goal: The fishing, gathering and hunting grounds of a house (`ground` in sim/fish, sim/forage and sim/deer) each ran a breadth-first search of the house's walking reach at every ask: when the household's work is refreshed and again for each idle pawn looking for work. The search's order depends only on where a pawn can stand, which play does not change; only the test of each tile (stock, cover, building) does. The order is searched once a house and reach and kept; the tests stay live, so the grounds found are the same.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `reachTiles(w, from, steps)` in sim/routes gives the search's tiles in its order, kept beside the world (never in `st`, never saved); the three grounds scan it.
- [x] Behaviour unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base c5b7ee3 --scenario founders,heirs,populous --seeds 7,42 --map tiny --years 3` prints `same`.
- [x] A daily invariant (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): each kept list of a live farm is what a new search finds. A test in `tests/routes.js` (fast) of the list, and of the lists searched again when a tile's standing changes.
- [x] Measured before and after, below.
Notes:
- 15 Nov 2026: from Kim's report of the simulation's slowdown. A CPU profile of populous tiny, seed 7, 4 years, against 10 November (`5ce315e`) put the time under the jobs functions at about 19 ms a game day against 4; the ground searches were its clearest single item.
- Decision (Deciding without Kim: no Decided point moved, nothing new for the player): the list is kept in a `WeakMap` by world in sim/routes' closure, not in `st` or on `w`. It is derived from the world alone (as sim/fish's `lakeSize`), so a loaded run cannot drift from it; on `w` it would have been a new world leaf for `tools/same.js` and the saves to step round. Where a pawn can stand: not water, not a river away from its crossings, not snow; a house can be stood on (`passable`). No act of play changes those today. Should one (a bridge built), `costChanged` compares the tile's standing with what the lists were searched under and drops them all; the invariant checks the lists daily against new searches.
- Bound (Rules for cost, 8): one list a house and reach asked for (three reaches: 6, 5, 6 steps), at most 127 tiles of 4 bytes each; a house is never unbuilt, so at most three lists a house ever built, about 1.3 kB a house.
- Numbers, populous tiny, seed 7, 4 years, one run at a time, sampled CPU time in ms a game day, before and after: the three `ground` functions with what they call 2.83 and 0.34; `refreshJobs` 6.23 and 4.67; `lookForWork` 1.92 and 0.95; the whole run with its generation 55.6 and 50.9.
