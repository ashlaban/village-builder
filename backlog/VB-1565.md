# VB-1565: A helper who goes home is let go of the task it worked: no work credited to a hand not there
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Design ref: `design.md` section 7, A leg, and its end; The cut and the call; Help between households; `docs/review-agency.md`, section 8
Goal: Mends a red check. `node tests/oxen.js` fails on the run's branch after its merge with main (`OXEN FAIL: rates 0 1.96, 1 1.01, 2 0.95, 3 0.98, 4 1.31, 5 1.04`): household 4, which has no ox team, shows 1.31 hours of ploughing done a pawn-hour, above a hand's pace (1.18). It passes on main alone and on the branch before the merge, so it is an interaction of the two: main's day's end (VB-1546 the afternoon allotment, VB-1547 a core task finished past the day's end, `eveningCall` in `sim/jobs`, the day's end in `sim/clock` and `sim/agents`) with this branch's own time (`sim/pastimes`, `ownTime`, called from `sim/clock`) or VB-1554's change in `refreshJobs`. Traced so far (founders, seed 7, the probe island of `tests/oxen.js`, game day 50): pawn 3, wife of household 1, lent as a `helper` (`sim/hands`) to household 4's `plow`, is seen with `a.job` still that task while its phase is `go`, `work`, then `home`, then `null`: it walks home without being released from the task's claims, and the task is credited 11.35 hours of work for 6.0 hours in phase `work` that day (a task's progress is reckoned in closed form from the rate of the hands in `j.claims`, `sim/work`). Find which path sends a hand home (or leaves it free) without `release` (`sim/agents`), mend it there so that one rule lets go of work at the day's end for a member and a helper alike, and add the invariant: no agent holds a full task (`a.job`) in phase `home` or `null`. A script that prints the day-by-day tally is in the notes.
