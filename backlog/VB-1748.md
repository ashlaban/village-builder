Goal: A load in hand is a good, not a promise: what a pawn carries exists from the work, and a carrier cut on the road leaves the good where it stands
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The kinds whose `deliver` makes their good or its effect (the mocks counted ten; listed in the PR from `grep -n "deliver:" src/sim`) make it at the work or load leg: the stones leave the tile, the catch and the game are rolled, the bog and the clay are dug when the work is done; `deliver` only puts the load into the store. A kind left at the door says why in its record.
- [ ] A good made or set down away from a store lies as a pile with an owner at a tile (sim/lying, as a fallen carrier's load lies today), for the goods with no store of their own (the catch, game, gathered food, dug clay, ore and peat, salt, a basket of stone, seed carried to a field); water stands in a container (the barrel, VB-1586). Each has a way out (rot, frost, fetched) and an entry in `tools/stocks.js`.
- [ ] A trip's yield accrues with the work: a fishing, hunting or gathering trip cut before its end brings home what its hours so far have yielded; only the hours not yet yielding are lost (a check on a constructed trip cut at half its hours).
- [ ] Q3: the share of carrying tasks cut between the work's end and the delivery, populous small seed 7, 3 years, and each of these goods' yearly amount before and after; a kind whose amount moves by 5% or more is named (section 4's refutation: it keeps its `deliver` hook, said in its record).
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10): `npm run verify` green, a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose (never skipped or quarantined) and named in the PR; no stuck pawn (no task held 48 hours without progress, as VB-1546's script counts); the stocks' accounts close (`node tools/sim.js --report accounts`: `unexplained` no larger than before).
- [ ] No village dies out: `node tools/sim.js --scenario founders|populous --map small --seed 7|11|42 --years 10`, all six runs: no settlement extinct by hunger and no collapse of its people (the living at year 10 against year 0, in the PR).
- [ ] Reported in the PR, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in those six runs, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed), before and after.
- [ ] The teleport lines of VB-1175 this mends (the cart taken at the field, from VB-1586; any other) are ticked there with this task's ID.
