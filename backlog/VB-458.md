# VB-458: The feast: a host with plenty lays out food at a wedding or a funeral ale, and the guests' holders are booked goodwill
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Exchange, goodwill and obligations (The gift: The feast, What a feast books); backlog/VB-407.md
Goal: Today an occasion pours ale and gives the host standing; no food is served and no goodwill booked, and about a fifth of the grain harvest rots. Build the feast of VB-407's plan on the wedding and the funeral ale, which exist (sim/occasions).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `FEAST` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each with its meaning: `above` 2 (years of the household's need, over its seed, that it keeps before it feasts), `share` 0.25 (of what it holds beyond that), `perGuest` 3 (rations one person eats and drinks at a sitting). The threshold is a rule of the registry, so the household's panel can say why it does or does not feast.
- [ ] When a wedding or a funeral ale at a farm reaches its hour, a host above the threshold serves `min(share × excess, perGuest × present)` rations through `sim/stores` (a good and the reason `feast`); those present, the host's own members too, have eaten that day's meal, so their households do not feed them again for it. Nothing is served to those who did not come, and nothing is served that is not eaten.
- [ ] Each guest household with a living holder is booked goodwill to the host holder's credit for the customary worth of what its members ate and drank (the ale poured too, at its grain), by the gift's booking (`sim/exchange`); a household without a holder books nothing; the host's own members book nothing. Standing stays `pourAle`'s.
- [ ] An event `feast` (`log`, with its kind, the host pawn and `atFarm`): the host, the guests' count, the rations served; the holder's panel lists the balances it made as any goodwill.
- [ ] Invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): a feast never takes a host below its seed and 2 years; rations served equal rations eaten. A test on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): a host below the threshold serves nothing; a host above it serves the lesser of the two bounds; a guest who stayed at home owes nothing; a guest household's meal is not eaten twice.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small seeds 3, 7, 11 and founders small seeds 7, 42, 30 years: feasts a village-year, rations feasted and rotted as shares of the grain harvest (`tools/balance.js`; VB-403's report if it exists), goodwill outstanding a household (88 rations today), hungry household-days, growth. Whether households, not only averages, are better off: the helped households a year, before and after.
- [ ] Parameterisation (Deciding without Kim, 3; at most five short runs): `above` 1.5, 2, 3 and `share` 0.25, 0.5, with what each gave; the values kept are Kim's 2 and 0.25 unless a run shows a collapse, and the others go to `docs/tuning.md`.
- [ ] Cost (rule 9): no new loop. Occasions a year (weddings and funerals, tens on populous small) × those present (at most 20 and the household) × one booking each; counted by a work counter `feastServed`; goodwill's cap of 32 counterparts a pawn holds.
