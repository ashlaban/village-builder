# VB-458: The feast: a host with plenty lays out food at a wedding or a funeral ale, and the guests' holders are booked goodwill
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Exchange, goodwill and obligations (The gift: The feast, What a feast books); backlog/VB-407.md
Goal: Today an occasion pours ale and gives the host standing; no food is served and no goodwill booked, and about a fifth of the grain harvest rots. Build the feast of VB-407's plan on the wedding and the funeral ale, which exist (sim/occasions).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `FEAST` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each with its meaning: `above` 2 (years of the household's need, over its seed, that it keeps before it feasts), `share` 0.25 (of what it holds beyond that), `perGuest` 3 (rations one person eats and drinks at a sitting). The threshold is a rule of the registry, so the household's panel can say why it does or does not feast.
- [x] When a wedding or a funeral ale at a farm reaches its hour, a host above the threshold serves `min(share × excess, perGuest × present)` rations through `sim/stores` (a good and the reason `feast`); those present, the host's own members too, have eaten that day's meal, so their households do not feed them again for it. Nothing is served to those who did not come, and nothing is served that is not eaten.
- [x] Each guest household with a living holder is booked goodwill to the host holder's credit for the customary worth of what its members ate and drank (the ale poured too, at its grain), by the gift's booking (`sim/exchange`); a household without a holder books nothing; the host's own members book nothing. Standing stays `pourAle`'s.
- [x] An event `feast` (`log`, with its kind, the host pawn and `atFarm`): the host, the guests' count, the rations served; the holder's panel lists the balances it made as any goodwill.
- [x] Invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): a feast never takes a host below its seed and 2 years; rations served equal rations eaten. A test on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): a host below the threshold serves nothing; a host above it serves the lesser of the two bounds; a guest who stayed at home owes nothing; a guest household's meal is not eaten twice.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small seeds 3, 7, 11 and founders small seeds 7, 42, 30 years: feasts a village-year, rations feasted and rotted as shares of the grain harvest (`tools/balance.js`; VB-403's report if it exists), goodwill outstanding a household (88 rations today), hungry household-days, growth. Whether households, not only averages, are better off: the helped households a year, before and after. (Not all taken, 17 Nov: populous small seeds 7 and 42 at 15 years, as the run asked, and founders 7 and 42 at 30, on a shared machine; populous seeds 3 and 11 at 30 years are owed, noted as VB-997 for VB-662.)
- [x] Parameterisation (Deciding without Kim, 3; at most five short runs): `above` 1.5, 2, 3 and `share` 0.25, 0.5, with what each gave; the values kept are Kim's 2 and 0.25 unless a run shows a collapse, and the others go to `docs/tuning.md`.
- [x] Cost (rule 9): no new loop. Occasions a year (weddings and funerals, tens on populous small) × those present (at most 20 and the household) × one booking each; counted by a work counter `feastServed`; goodwill's cap of 32 counterparts a pawn holds.

- 17 Nov 2026 (VB-458, implementation): the rule `feast` (`sim/occasions`, context `{hh}`): the household's grain (base) less its seed reserve (`seed`) and `FEAST.above` years of its need (`keep`); above 0 it feasts; `Sim.explainFeast(st, hh)` gives its parts for the panel (the panel's line is VB-996). At an occasion's end (`finish`, after `pourAle`), `feast` lays out `min(share × generosity × excess, perGuest × need − ale)` rations of grain above the seed (`Stores.takeSpare`, reason `feast`, the entry carrying guests, booked, keep and left), where need is the day's rations of those who came (a child 0.5) and ale the poured barrels' grain (`BREW.grain`). Each who came and has not feasted that day eats its part by its ration (`pawn.feast = {t, n, host}`, owned by `sim/occasions`); `feedHousehold` (`sim/hunger`) gives a pawn who feasted in the last day a full meal from nothing. Each guest household with a living holder, not the host's, owes the host's holder what its members ate plus its share of the ale at its grain (`relations.book`, the gift's booking between the two holders). Event `feast` (`log`, host pawn, `atFarm`, n = rations; the text names the guests' count). Counter `feastServed` (a pawn served). Test `tests/feast.js` (verify) on the probe state; invariants in `checkState`: a feast of the last day left its host at or above its keep, and its rations equal what its eaters ate, each one feast a day.
- Decisions (Deciding without Kim; noted in the inbox): (1) a feast too small to give each who came a day's food is not laid out (the meal is either eaten there or at home, never split); (2) the sitting is `perGuest` a ration of need, less the ale's grain, as the design's line counts ale in the 3 rations, where the criterion wrote `perGuest × present`; (3) goodwill is booked only at a feast, the ale in it; an occasion with ale and no food books as before (memory and standing only); (4) the food is grain above the seed, a ration of grain a ration of food (no cooking work); (5) the booking is the gift's (`book` between the holders), without the gift's tie gain, since the guests already remember the ale; (6) generosity scales the share, 0.5 to 1.5 (`TRAITS.acts.generosity.feast`, VB-520's note).

  The feast works but is small: about 13 rations a feast, the sitting binding at nearly every one (a wedding brings a handful of guests), so it eats under half a percent of the harvest while a third rots, and the goodwill it books (about 45 rations a year on populous small) is lost in the noise of help's. Households: hungry days and the helped households a year move within the seeds' noise (seed 42's hungry days 10 to 21 is a few households in one bad spell); no household is worse off by rule, since a feast leaves its host above its seed and 2 years. Growth is the targets' as before (both populous seeds above 1%). The shortfall goes to the tuning run (inbox `tuning`); the harvest home (VB-459) is the occasion that brings feasting to scale.
