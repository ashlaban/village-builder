# VB-320: Debtors' politics: those who owe lean against the creditors' practices
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-316
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, Loans, obligations and cases (Debtors' politics); Customs and culture, How attitudes move (Self-interest); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, sections 5.5 and 6.1 (Land concentration; Debtors' politics)
Goal: Land concentration is a reinforcing loop; its opposer is the meeting, where debtors vote and the debt custom can drift back. Give slice 5's practices the self-interest pull every custom has, so the opposer exists and its strength can be measured.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Self-interest on "Debts are paid with" and "Loans of grain are repaid": a pawn whose household owes (an open loan, rent, or labour) is pulled towards "labour" and "as lent", one whose household is owed towards "land" and "a quarter more", in proportion to the debt against the household's year's need, through the existing interest term (`refreshInterests`, `src/sim/society.js`).
- [ ] On "One's own cause" and "Who judges a case", none (no household gains by them in a known direction); stated in the code's comment.
- [ ] Numbers (populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 40 years; `npm run calibrate` for the pace): the share of villages whose practice of "Debts are paid with" left its default and when; land Gini and tenancy share with and without the pull. The counters re-recorded (`node tests/counters.js --update`), their entries listed.
- [ ] The office (VB-404's plan): a pawn whose household pays the alderman's keep is pulled towards the lesser keep on "What the alderman is kept with" (a share of the surplus, then days of work, then nothing), in proportion to what it paid against its year's need, and towards "by turns" on "How the alderman is chosen"; the alderman's household the other way. Numbers: the share of villages whose keep left its default and when, and the alderman's years in office, with and without the pull (the review's mock had no brake on offices).
Cost: two more terms in the yearly interests, per pawn; nothing daily.
Order: after VB-316.
