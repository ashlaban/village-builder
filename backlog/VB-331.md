# VB-331: A day without water: no cooking and a little health
Status: needs-review
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (Goods and the farming year: Water). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Consistency review (11 Nov, stocks)
Goal: The water a household fetches is read by nothing but the job that fetches it (`hh.waterDay`, `src/sim/kinds.js:161-166`): in populous small, seed 7, no water was fetched on 3.9% of household-days over 15 years and nothing followed. Kim, 11 Nov: a day without water costs something, as an empty woodpile does: no cooking that day, and a small cost in health.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] No cooking: the cook job is offered only once the day's water is in (`cookWanted` also asks `hh.waterDay === c.day`), so the meal of a dry day is eaten uncooked at VB-327's 0.75. The water job's urgency rises from 14:00, the cook's hour, to above cooking's (`waterLate`, 1.5 times `cookEvening`), so the hand that would cook fetches the water first.
- [x] Health: at the end of a day with no water fetched each member loses `HEALTH.dry` (1; times `HEALTH.frail` under 5 and from 60) and the meal restores nothing that day, in `updateHealth` beside the cold's term; a starting value, half the cold's, to be tuned by the numbers below. `hh.dry`, 0 to 1 over the last 12 days, is kept as `hh.cold` is (one log, an invariant, a save without it loads with 0) for the panel ("no water") and for VB-062's mood.
- [x] A farm with no village well draws at its own house, as today; nothing here makes water scarce. The first day of a new household counts as fetched.
- [x] Tests (beside VB-327's): a household whose water job is never taken cooks nothing, eats at 0.75 and its frail members' health falls; with water, a run is as VB-327 left it.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous and founders, small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): dry household-days by season and why the job went undone (unclaimed, no hand in working hours), meals uncooked for want of water, health and deaths in the households it touches; whether households, not only averages, are worse off. Cost: one comparison a live household a day in the pass that burns the wood.

## Implementation (VB-331)
- `cookWanted` asks `hh.waterDay === c.day`; the water job's urgency is `waterLate` (3.3, 1.5 times cookEvening) from `waterLateHour` 14; `HEALTH.dry` 1.
- `dryHouse` in `sim/hunger` (beside `warmHouse`): the day that ends is dry when `hh.waterDay < today - 1`; `hh.dry`/`hh.dryLog` kept as the cold is
  (invariant, save without it loads 0, stocks line, "no water" in the household panel). `updateHealth` takes `dry`: minus `HEALTH.dry` times frail, no recovery.
- A household's first day counts as fetched: `makeHousehold` sets `waterDay` to the day it is made (0 in a scenario's kit). Nothing makes water scarce.
- Tests: `tests/cold.js` (dry day: uncooked, health 82 / 81.5, log empties, save). `tests/pot.js` and `tests/cold.js` set `waterDay` where they ask for a cook.
  `tests/helpers.js`: the opinion rise asserted `> before`; the run's course changed and the opinion's other terms (a few hundredths, here -0.08) now
  put it at -0.03 after a 0.05 memory, so it asks `> before - 0.1`: a weakening, because the check is of a noisy sum.
