# VB-075: A typed record of events
Status: done (2026-11-02)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: core
Phase: implement
Design ref: People, households and family records (Life events); Systems overview (Chronicle: missing; finding 6).
Goal: The village log keeps the last 40 lines of text (`log` in `src/sim/records.js`), and pawns keep text events. Nothing can ask "every death in Ulsäter", "what happened at Norrgården" or "when did this custom change", which is what the family stories, the panels, notifications and the tests need.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `st.record` is an append-only array of events `{t, kind, pawn, other, household, farm, village, n, text}`; fields that do not apply are absent. `@state` documented. Kinds at least: `born`, `died` (cause in `text`), `married`, `moved`, `inherited`, `share`, `cause`, `custom` (a meeting or reckoning change), `split`, `harvest`, `frost`, `empty` (a farm stood empty), `settled` (scenario start), `year`.
- [x] Every `log(...)` and `personEvent(...)` call site also writes its event; `st.events` and `p.events` are unchanged, so the page needs no change.
- [x] `Sim.recordOf(st, {pawn, household, farm, village, kinds, from, to})` returns matching events in time order.
- [x] A test: after 10 founders years at seed 7, every pawn ever born has a `born` event, every dead pawn a `died` event, and every union a `married` event.
- [x] The PR reports the record's length and JSON size after 50 founders years and 3 populous medium years.
- [x] Re-record the golden file (a new key) and list the changed entries in the PR.
- Done 2 Nov 2026, in two commits. One event per happening: where a person event and a log line tell of the same thing (a move, a marriage, service, a claim, an inheritance, a plot), `false` on one of them says the other records it; a parent's "Child born" is the child's `born` event. Seeding writes the history before the start (births, deaths, marriages of the people on record), so the record answers for every pawn, not only those born in play. `died` keeps its cause in `text`, as asked; other events keep their log line or person event's text.
- 40 kinds: the 14 asked for and stake, stakeLapsed, plot, plotStarted, cottage, field, meadow, hay, hayRotted, slaughter, spoiled, herdBirths, herdDeath, claim, claimEnded, gift, stayed, noLand, left, guardian, service, border, kinBarred, putOff, friend and fellOut. A `log` call without its event is an error, and `tests/record.js` checks every call in `src/sim` statically.
- Size, medium map, seed 7: founders after 50 years 3,687 events, 0.41 MB of JSON (0.04 MB gzipped), 19% of the save; populous after 3 years 22,602 events, 2.39 MB (0.26 MB gzipped), 16% of the save. `friend` and `fellOut` are 63% of the events in founders and 83% in populous (12,899 and 5,876); if the record grows too large for saves, they are the first to drop or to keep only for the last few years.
