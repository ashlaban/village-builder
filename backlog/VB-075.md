# VB-075: A typed record of events
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: core
Phase: implement
Design ref: People, households and family records (Life events); Systems overview (Chronicle: missing; finding 6).
Goal: The village log keeps the last 40 lines of text (`log` in `src/sim/records.js`), and pawns keep text events. Nothing can ask "every death in Ulsäter", "what happened at Norrgården" or "when did this custom change", which is what the family stories, the panels, notifications and the tests need.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `st.record` is an append-only array of events `{t, kind, pawn, other, household, farm, village, n, text}`; fields that do not apply are absent. `@state` documented. Kinds at least: `born`, `died` (cause in `text`), `married`, `moved`, `inherited`, `share`, `cause`, `custom` (a meeting or reckoning change), `split`, `harvest`, `frost`, `empty` (a farm stood empty), `settled` (scenario start), `year`.
- [ ] Every `log(...)` and `personEvent(...)` call site also writes its event; `st.events` and `p.events` are unchanged, so the page needs no change.
- [ ] `Sim.recordOf(st, {pawn, household, farm, village, kinds, from, to})` returns matching events in time order.
- [ ] A test: after 10 founders years at seed 7, every pawn ever born has a `born` event, every dead pawn a `died` event, and every union a `married` event.
- [ ] The PR reports the record's length and JSON size after 50 founders years and 3 populous medium years.
- [ ] Re-record the golden file (a new key) and list the changed entries in the PR.
