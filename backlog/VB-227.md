# VB-227: Jobs: refresh and matching within their share
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (code structure). docs/roadmap.md, section 5.3 (rules 4, 5 and 6); docs/bench/closure.md (VB-203).
Goal: At `medium-ref`, "jobs refresh and matching" (`refreshJobs`, `callCooks`, `lookUp` and `planRound` in `tools/perf.js`) costs 35.1 ms a game day, the mean of three whole-year runs on 9 November at `9f130e6` (33.2 to 36.5), against its share of 30 ms (VB-203, `docs/bench/closure.md`). Spring costs most: 47.5 ms a day in one run, against 30 to 34 in the other seasons. Find which entry point and loop carry it, and cut it to the share.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Measure first, on `medium-ref` and `small-y40`, a whole game year each: the ms a game day of each of the four entry points, by season, and a CPU profile (`node --cpu-prof`) of the largest.
- [x] Each loop over a growing collection it finds has a declared work counter (`VB.COUNTERS`), asserted at tiny scale in `tests/counters.json`.
- [x] (order-preserving cuts only; the changes of behaviour, rules 4 and 5, are VB-249's: Kim, 10 Nov 2026) A change by the rules for cost for each part over the share: post a job when it falls due rather than polling for it (rule 4), compile a value read in an inner loop (rule 6), spread work over steps (rule 5). An order-preserving change leaves the golden file unchanged; a change of behaviour re-records it and reports `tools/sim.js` before and after at seeds 7 and 42.
- [x] (residual about +3 ms, about 33 against 30; the target moves to VB-249: Kim, 10 Nov 2026) Jobs refresh and matching moved towards the target of 30 ms a game day at `medium-ref` (`tools/bench.js`, whole year), paired runs before and after, and the counters; the residual reported, with the task or `docs/bench/closure.md` line that closes what is left.
- [x] `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals.

Impact note: no new module, state field or coupling. `sim/jobs`' review builds `builtTiles` once at the hour of the land looks and hands it
to `startProject` and `startMeadow` (sim/fields, sim/meadows: an optional last argument of `chooseTile` and `chooseMeadow`); `meadowJobs`
(sim/meadows) takes `want` as a boolean or a function asked only when a raking or mowing could be posted; `byreJobs` (sim/byre) walks
`hh.herds` without building arrays and counts the cows in milk only when a milking could be posted; `jobsFor` returns the household's or
the village's set as it stands when the other is empty; `dailyNeed` and `larderOf` (sim/hunger) are loops, summing in the same order.
Three work counters: `matchBid`, `lookUpJob`, `cookFarm`. `tests/golden.json` unchanged.
