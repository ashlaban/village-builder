# VB-1107: Marriage to kin by marriage: no step-parent and stepchild
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Design ref: People, households and family records (Kinship; Marriage, nursing and growth: The gate); Inheritance (Marriage)
Goal: No match is offered that every culture of the setting forbade: a step-parent and a stepchild, a parent-in-law and a child-in-law.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A custom, "Kin by marriage who may marry", in the customs' catalogue (`src/culture.js`) with options from strictest to loosest: (a) none within the Church's fourth degree of affinity, as it held from 1215 (the spouse's blood kin to third cousins, civil degree 8); (b) none in the direct line (a step-parent and stepchild, a parent-in-law and child-in-law); (c) no bar. The Nordic default (b). Step-siblings marry under every option (canon law set no bar between them).
- [x] `kinOk` (`src/sim/matching.js`) reads it, wherever it is asked: the round's matcher, the player's wish for a match, and `sim/opportunities`. Affinity runs through a marriage that has ended by death too; the degree is the civil degree between the pawn and its spouse's blood kin.
- [x] A check on a small constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): a widow and her late husband's son by an earlier marriage are not matched under (a) and (b), and are under (c).
- [x] Marriages and growth over 30 years, founders small seeds 7 and 42 and populous small seed 7, before and after, in the notes (principle 2; the effect is expected to be small).
- [x] Cost: the affinity look-up reads the two pawns' spouses' kin, through `kinDegree`; no pass over pairs.
