# VB-1107: Marriage to kin by marriage: no step-parent and stepchild
Status: needs-review
Priority: 1
Size: S
Design ref: People, households and family records (Kinship; Marriage, nursing and growth: The gate); Inheritance (Marriage)
Goal: No match is offered that every culture of the setting forbade: a step-parent and a stepchild, a parent-in-law and a child-in-law.
Acceptance criteria (as proposed in `QUESTIONS.md`, "VB-1107"; Kim's answer may change them):
- [ ] A custom, "Kin by marriage who may marry", in the customs' catalogue (`src/culture.js`) with options from strictest to loosest: (a) none within the Church's fourth degree of affinity, as it held from 1215 (the spouse's blood kin to third cousins, civil degree 8); (b) none in the direct line (a step-parent and stepchild, a parent-in-law and child-in-law); (c) no bar. The Nordic default (b). Step-siblings marry under every option (canon law set no bar between them).
- [ ] `kinOk` (`src/sim/matching.js`) reads it, wherever it is asked: the round's matcher, the player's wish for a match, and `sim/opportunities`. Affinity runs through a marriage that has ended by death too; the degree is the civil degree between the pawn and its spouse's blood kin.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): a widow and her late husband's son by an earlier marriage are not matched under (a) and (b), and are under (c).
- [ ] Marriages and growth over 30 years, founders small seeds 7 and 42 and populous small seed 7, before and after, in the notes (principle 2; the effect is expected to be small).
- [ ] Cost: the affinity look-up reads the two pawns' spouses' kin, through `kinDegree`; no pass over pairs.

Notes / questions:
- 16 Nov 2026 (playtest, founders small seed 7, 80 years in the page): in year 25 the round offered Ingeborg Torstensdotter, 52, widowed twice, a match with Sven Hansson, 23, the son of her second husband, raised in her house from 14. The offer gave no hint of the tie. `kinOk` judges kin by blood alone (`kinDegree`), and the design's Kinship row judges the marriage limit by civil degree of blood (Decided).
- Why it goes to Kim: The gate (Decided, 13 Nov, Kim: "keep the gate, add nothing") says nothing else holds a marriage back than its four conditions; kin by marriage is a fifth bar, or a wider reading of "kin". The question is in `QUESTIONS.md`, "VB-1107".
