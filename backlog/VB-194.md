# VB-194: The bench history and the budget table, reported nightly
Status: done
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (measurement). `docs/roadmap.md`, sections 5.2 (the budget and its table, Decided, Kim, 3 November) and 6 (bench history, microbenchmarks).
Goal: The counters catch extra work, but they do not show what a game day costs on a real machine, and they do not show slow drift over many commits. A nightly bench on the two medium fixtures does both. It is appended to a history and never fails. The budget table shows each system and each measure against its line, so every slice's plan can name its line and its wrap-up can measure it. Blocked until VB-193 (the fixtures) is done.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `tools/bench.js` runs on `medium-y5` and `medium-y60` (VB-193). For each, it calls the moving round and the meetings once (timed, one call each), then advances 30 game days, timing each 6-minute step from the tool. It reports:
  - ms per game day (mean) and ms per living pawn-day;
  - the longest step, the 99th percentile of steps, and the hour of the five longest;
  - the moving round and the meetings, one call each;
  - heap used at the end;
  - save time (`Sim.save`, `JSON.stringify` and gzip, each), MB of JSON and gzipped, and load time (parse and `Sim.load`);
  - ms per system per game day, as `tools/perf.js` divides them.
- [x] Microbenchmarks in the same run: A* (`findRoute`) on 1,000 random reachable pairs of tiles of the medium map, in µs per search and ns per expanded tile. The rules registry (VB-197) adds `Rules.value` in ns per call.
- [x] Each run appends one line to `docs/bench/history.jsonl`: date, commit, Node version, CPU model and cores, the fixtures' stamps, and the numbers above. A number 20% or more worse than the median of the last five lines on the same CPU model is flagged in the output (`FLAG`, with the five values). The tool always exits 0.
- [x] `docs/bench/budget.md`, written by `tools/bench.js --budget` from the last history line, never edited by hand. One row per measure of the budget (`docs/roadmap.md`, section 5.2) and one per system, with its budget (blank until a plan sets one), year 5 and year 60. The ratio of ms per living pawn-day at year 60 to year 5 is the budget's flat-in-history line. A slice's plan adds its system's budget to a table at the top of `tools/bench.js`.
- [x] `tools/nightly.js` (VB-193) runs the bench after the fixtures. The nightly run commits the history line and the table in its PR.
- [x] The first history lines and the budget table in the PR and in this file.
Cost: an estimated 5 minutes nightly (two loads, 60 game days at up to 300 ms each, two saves, the microbenchmarks).
