# VB-221: The player's gift from the pawn panel, with the floor's warning
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, goodwill and obligations (The gift: The player's gift, Decided 3 Nov and Agreed 8 Nov); The player and what they see (What the player sees); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, section 4.7 (offers in Exchange, the player's fourth door); VB-182's plan.
Goal: The player's fourth door: while it plays a holder, the pawn panel lets it give grain to any household, by the verb `give` with source `player`, as any pawn's gift goes. Its pawn's allowance does not bind it; below the floor it is warned, by name, who goes hungry.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] While the player plays a pawn whose household it may give for (its holder; who else may is the household's kind's rule, today the holder only), the panel offers "Give grain" to a household: its kin households and those of its village listed first, any other chosen from the map. An amount in whole rations, at most what the household holds above its seed reserve.
- [x] The gift is queued as a command `give` (VB-216), source `player`, and settles as any gift: through `sim/stores`, with the gift's events, booked as goodwill to the giver's credit when both households have a holder (VB-217).
- [x] Below the floor, before the gift is queued, a warning that names who would go hungry: the household's members whom its feed order serves last, within the floor's 20 days, read from `sim/hunger` without writing; the player confirms or cancels.
- [x] A test of the simulation side (in `tests/exchange.js`): a `give` of source `player` passes the floor and the allowance and settles; the names in the warning match the feed order's last group.
- [ ] A screenshot in the PR. Golden file unchanged; `npm run verify` passes; `index.html` rebuilt. (The screenshot is `docs/img/vb-221-give-warning.png`; the golden file changed in `apiKeys / Sim` only; `npm run verify` as a whole is left to the PR's Checks: Kim, 10 Nov, the code first.)

Implementation (10 Nov 2026).
- Simulation: `sim/hunger`'s `feedGroups(st, hh, mem)` is the feed order that `feedHousehold` had inline (one rule, two readers), and
  `servedLast(st, hh)` the last group with anyone in it. `sim/exchange`'s `giftWarning(st, from, n)` gives `null`, or `{below, days,
  hungry}`: rations under the floor after the gift, the floor's days, and `servedLast`. It reads only. `Sim` exports `giftWarning` and
  `kinHouseholds`. No state field, no verb and no rule changed: `assessGift` already let source `player` pass.
- The Actions sheet (`#actionSheet`, bottom bar's Actions, shown while a living pawn is played; `src/page/play.js`, `renderActions`):
  the played pawn's top part, the gift's box, then what it may do and the wishes. It is rebuilt only while open. A pawn's card keeps
  one line (`#cardPlay`): "You play this pawn" with a button to its actions, or after the played pawn's death "Go on as". Taking the
  new game's offer, going on as a successor and loading a save with a played pawn open the sheet. Debug has "Play as them" and "Stop
  playing" for the selected person.
- Give grain: a household from kin (`kinHouseholds`), then the rest of its village, or "Choose on the map": the next click takes the
  household of a pawn, of a farm's name, of a farm's house or of land a household owns (`Sim.ownerOf`). Whole rations from 1 to the
  grain above the seed reserve. Give asks `Sim.giftWarning`; with a warning the player confirms or cancels; then `Sim.queue` with the
  verb `give`. A household without a holder is said to be owed no goodwill. The box is its own element, not redrawn while the player
  types in it.

Not built: who else than the holder may give for a household (the kind's rule; today the holder only). The warning names the whole
household where the feed order is "equal", as the rule says: everyone is served last together.

Numbers: no behaviour of the simulation changes for a run without a player's gift (`node tests/golden.js`: the state hashes match;
the one changed entry is `apiKeys / Sim`, for the two new exports).
