Goal: The player's fourth door: while it plays a holder, the pawn panel lets it give grain to any household, by the verb `give` with source `player`, as any pawn's gift goes. Its pawn's allowance does not bind it; below the floor it is warned, by name, who goes hungry.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] While the player plays a pawn whose household it may give for (its holder; who else may is the household's kind's rule, today the holder only), the panel offers "Give grain" to a household: its kin households and those of its village listed first, any other chosen from the map. An amount in whole rations, at most what the household holds above its seed reserve.
- [ ] The gift is queued as a command `give` (VB-216), source `player`, and settles as any gift: through `sim/stores`, with the gift's events and, once VB-217 is done, booked as goodwill to the giver's credit.
- [ ] Below the floor, before the gift is queued, a warning that names who would go hungry: the household's members whom its feed order serves last, within the floor's 20 days, read from `sim/hunger` without writing; the player confirms or cancels.
- [ ] A test of the simulation side (in `tests/exchange.js`): a `give` of source `player` passes the floor and the allowance and settles; the names in the warning match the feed order's last group.
- [ ] A screenshot in the PR. Golden file unchanged; `npm run verify` passes; `index.html` rebuilt.
