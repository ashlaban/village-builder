## Implementation (VB-876, 17 November 2026)
Started from 125c9d3. Code moved verbatim (line ranges sliced from the old file, then Prettier); no renames.
- `sim/lifecycle` (118 lines, was 703): deaths and retirement (`killPawn`, `retireHolder`, `dailyDeaths`) and the daily life step (`dailyLife`).
- `sim/meetings` (184): the year's meetings (`villagesOfStep`, `meetingStage`, `villageMeeting`, `culturesYear`, `routinesMeeting`,
  `holdMeetings`) and the night's culture drift (`nightCulture`, `NIGHT_STEPS`); owner of `st.meetingLog` and `st.lastMeeting`.
- `sim/marriage` (190): `wed` (the marry verb's act), `handover` (the handover by custom), and the basis `match` of sim/offers.
- `sim/round` (236): the moving round (`movingRound`, `roundPhase`, `villageRound`, `kinPressure`), reading `villagesOfStep` from
  sim/meetings and `handover` from sim/marriage.
Load order: lifecycle, marriage, meetings, round, before sim/births; all four in the agency layer.

Decisions (Deciding without Kim, 12):
- The split follows what VB-682's tasks will change: VB-692 and VB-823 rewrite the round (askers, the round only answering) and touch
  `sim/round` alone; VB-822 changes `wed` (its `move`) and VB-824 makes `handover` an offer, both in `sim/marriage`, beside the basis
  `match`, which the asking will make offers of. Deaths and the day's step, which none of them touches, keep the name `sim/lifecycle`.
- `nightCulture` goes with the meetings, not the daily step: it is the culture's drift (Culture.settlementStep), as the meetings are its
  yearly change, and shares their imports.
- `villagesOfStep` lives in sim/meetings and is exported for sim/round, rather than moving to sim/occasions (another module's file).
- `kinOk` was re-exported by sim/lifecycle only for tests: the tests now read it from sim/matching, its owner.
- clock's CALENDAR owners, tools/perf.js, tools/moving-profile.js and tools/writes.js name the new modules; docs/architecture/modules.md updated.

What fell: the 703-line module becomes four, the largest 236; sim/lifecycle's imports fall from 29 modules to 14 (marriage 12,
meetings 10, round 20); six unused imports gone (`DAY`, `dailyNeed`, `larderOf`, `reserved`, `kinDegree`, `heirFor`) and the
`kinOk` re-export. 25 lines added in all (three module headers and import lists).
