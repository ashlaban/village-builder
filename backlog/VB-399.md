# VB-399: Village names by site: one generator set for seeded and founded villages (batch 1: seeded)
Status: done (2026-11-12)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: Naming of farms
Goal: One set of village name generators (site, then mother and founder in batch 2), grounded in the Swedish place-name layers; names drawn from a stream keyed by the centre tile, so a word change never moves the layout.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] One function `villageName(ctx, taken, rand, order)` in `src/sim/names.js` for every village, with generators `(ctx, rand) => [names, best first]` as the farms have; `ctx` is `{w, site, founder, mother}`, and a village with no mother is an old one (Kim: no `old` flag).
- [x] A `site` generator for the old layer: a first part from the ground within 5 steps (trees, pine, shore, stone, marsh, open land; the lie of the land, crops, beasts, size and colour anywhere) and an ending that fits the site (-by, -sta, -inge anywhere; -lunda in broadleaf wood, -åker on open land, -berga by hills, -vik and -näs by water, -ö on an island of at most 250 tiles), joined by four rules (the link -e- of some trees before -by and -berga, no vowel against a vowel, no part joined to itself, three of a letter made two).
- [x] Sacred and central names are rare (Kim): the largest seeded village of a map, if it has 8 farms, is its old centre (-tuna, or -vi by hills), and no other village is; where the ground suits -lunda or -åker, 8% of villages bear a god's name (Fröslunda, Torsåker).
- [x] A medieval ceiling (Kim): no ending or custom attested only from the 1600s.
- [x] Seeded villages are named when all are laid (`nameVillages` in `src/sim/layout.js`), from a stream of the centre tile's own (`tileRand` with a salt), so a change of words moves nothing but names.
- [x] Tests in `tests/names.js`: the joining rules, every ending's kinds, endings only where the ground suits them, the same site the same name, taken names passed over, one old centre a map. `tests/golden.json` re-recorded for names only.
- Still to do, each a task of its own once Kim has seen this one. Batch 2: founded villages through `villageName` (`mother`: Norrby, Sörby, Väsby, Ösby, Nyby; `founder`: the genitive with -torp, -böle, -ryd, -boda, -säter, -mark by the site), and `founderName` removed. Batches 3 and 4: farm generators (position, age and size, a feature at the house; office and bynames where the roles exist).
- 12 Nov 2026 (session with Kim). Kim: seeded and founded villages share one set of generators, as a seeded village was founded too; names may change; research first; a medieval ceiling; sacred names rare, one centre a map; no `old` in `ctx`.
- Research (an Opus sub-agent, 12 Nov): datings from sv.wikipedia.org, *Svenska ortnamnsefterled*, the one source fetched; the rest from the model's knowledge, unchecked (Pamp, *Ortnamnen i Sverige*; *Svenskt ortnamnslexikon*). Layers: old habitation endings take a bare stem (-sta, -by, -inge, -lunda, -åker, -berga; -tuna and -vi for central and holy places); the medieval colonisation endings take a person's genitive (-torp, -böle, -ryd, -boda, -säter, -mark; batch 2); nature endings of any period follow the site (-vik, -näs, -ö; -sund, -holmen, -hamn and -ed wait for a test of the site's shape). Found wrong in the old stock: `nes` (Norwegian; `näs`), `säter` and `tuna` on any seeded village, `Ul` (the god Ull) before any ending; in the farms' words, for batch 3: `Berga` among open land, `Bråten` (Norwegian), `hult` (Småland; -böle is the archipelago's). `gen.js` names islands with `nes` and `ey` too: not touched here.
- Decided on the way (Deciding without Kim, 4 and 7): the layout's stream is still drawn as it was when names came from it (`drawnName` in `src/sim/layout.js`, the old 11 and 8 parts, held as the village's name while the villages are laid). The first attempt took the draws out, which laid every seed's villages anew: 15 scripts of verify pinned to their seeds failed (browser, goods, causes, counters, player, record, marriage, projects, occasions, ale, herding, start, offers and others). A naming change should not move the worlds; Kim had agreed to the re-roll, so this is for Kim to overturn. The price is 8 lines of old words kept in the layout.
- A culture's `namePrefs` still holds the farms' order only: no culture exists at seeding, and Kim left cultures that differ out of this work.
- Cost (rule 9): a village named once, at seeding: one search of 91 tiles and a flood of at most 250, 48 draws. Nothing in a step.
- Validation: `npm test` green; `npm run verify`: every script but `tests/hunger.js`, which fails the same way on `main`. `tests/golden.json`: 65 entries differ, all of them names of villages (in `villages`, `cenv`, `households`, `record`), e.g. Högtuna → Valby, Alsta → Furuberga, Ulby → Bredlunda, Rönsta → Sandvik. `tests/record.js`: the hash of the lives of heirs 7 re-recorded (the same 68 events; village names in their text).
