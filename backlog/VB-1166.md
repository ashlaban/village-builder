# VB-1166: Mural theme: the family tree, a person's row and the supply bars fitted
Status: done (2026-11-18)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: VB-1161; Kim, 18 November
Goal: The family tree and the lists of people read as one piece with the map in the mural theme.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The family tree: a person is a square plaster tag in an ink line, the chosen one in a thick red ochre line, the one the tree is drawn about in a thicker ink line; marriage and descent in ink; the chosen person's name a head; the card's parts divided by dotted ink.
- [x] A person or a place in a list (`.row`: who lives here, the People sheet, the Culture pane's villages): its picture a small medallion on an ochre ground, its name in the scroll's hand, rows parted by a dotted line.
- [x] A supply's seasons (food, fuel, hay on a farm's card): square cells in an ink line, what is held in green earth; red is kept for a shortfall.
- [x] Glass is unchanged: every rule is under `:root[data-theme="mural"]`.

## Notes

Kim, 18 November: the family tree next, and the person's medallion and scroll in the lists in the same batch.

Decisions:
- A tree's person is a square tag, not a medallion with a scroll: the tree has no portraits, and up to some hundred people a view; a
  tag holds the three lines of text it shows. A close call.
- In a list a person's name is in the scroll's hand (serif italic) but has no scroll drawn round it, where the mock-up drew one: a
  card lists a dozen rows and a sheet many more, and a frame on each made the list heavy. The links above the list (the chips) are the
  full medallion and scroll. A close call, surfaced.
- The supply cells were the accent, which is red ochre here and read as a warning on a safe store: they are green earth (VB-1161's
  rule: red is for pressed and for a warning).

Not looked at: the People sheet open (the script's shot closed it), though its rows are the same `.row`; the tree with many people
(Everyone) and its Path mode.
