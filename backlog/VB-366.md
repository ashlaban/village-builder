# VB-366: Pots: fired clay keeps the store from damp and mice
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The mill and the loom, the kiln and the quarry); `backlog/VB-185.md`
Goal: A household fires pots from clay in a pit; with pots, half of the grain store's yearly loss is saved; pots break, so the need lasts. Slice 6's plan (VB-185, 11 Nov), from Kim's answers in the session.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `fire`: at home, 4 work hours, 1 load of clay and 1 of wood, gives 4 pots (`hh.pots`, through `sim/stores`); skill pottery. Posted while the household has clay and under `POTS.want` (8) pots. No facility: peasant ware is fired in a pit.
- [ ] With `POTS.per` (1 pot for each 60 rations in store) the grain store's yearly loss (`sim/hunger`'s share) is halved, in proportion to the store the pots cover. A quarter of the pots break a year, taken on the planning day.
- [ ] The worth table gains a pot at 3 rations (Proposed). Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): grain lost in store a household, pots held, hungry days; whether households without clay get pots.
- [ ] The numbers that move are reported before and after at the seeds and maps named (Working rules); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
