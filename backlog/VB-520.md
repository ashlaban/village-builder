# VB-520: Traits act: the two rules each trait changes, and chemistry from values, temper and customs
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M

Design ref: design.md, People, households and family records (Traits, talk and stories: The twelve traits and the rules each changes; Chemistry); Balance loops and targets (Like seeks like); backlog/VB-186.md
Goal: A trait earns its place by changing at least two rules, as a contribution neutral at 0.5 and never a choice made for the player. Wire each trait to the rules that exist today, and make the pair's compatibility what target-v2 sketched: likeness in values and sociability, temper as friction, the customs between two pawns (Like seeks like, Decided 12 Nov and not yet built), beside the seeded term.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Each trait contributes to the rules of `design.md`'s table (People, The twelve traits and the rules each changes), linear in the trait and neutral at 0.5: as a contribution to a rule of `sim/registry` where the rule is declared there, and as a named weight (`TRAITS.acts.<trait>.<rule>`: the value at 0 and at 1) where a pawn chooses. This task builds those whose rule exists today:
  - generosity: the holder's own reserve in allowance, 40 to 0 days (VB-484's, if it has landed: then only checked here); the share a host lays out at a feast, 0.5 to 1.5 times `FEAST.share`, if VB-458 has landed (else a line in VB-458's notes); (VB-484 had not landed: built here, and VB-484 is met; VB-458 has not landed: its line is below, for the orchestrator to write)
  - piety: the anchor's pull in `culture`'s daily step, 0.5 to 1.5 times (the rite's attendance is VB-522's);
  - ambition: the pressure a pawn needs before it wishes to move out or claim a farm at the round, 1.5 to 0.5 times;
  - honour: the value a memory of `claim`, `border`, `refusedMatch`, `crop` and `stayedAway` is written at, 0.5 to 1.5 times (the lie's judgement is the lies task's);
  - sociability: the chance to start a talk at its turn, min(1, 0.5 + sociability), and `SOCIAL.seek`, 0.25 to 0.75 (telling a story is VB-523's);
  - temper: friction in chemistry (below), and the quarrel: a talk of cross words becomes one at chance `SOCIAL.talk.quarrel` 0.05 × the two tempers' sum; both remember it (`quarrel`: -0.1, to -0.3, a year) and it is an event of the record (`quarrel`, with its kind, both pawns and the place), so a story kind for VB-523;
  - diligence: the planning day (built; re-pointed by VB-519) and the idle wait, `IDLE.wait` 4 to 2 hours;
  - openness: `attitude.malleability`, 0.5 to 1.5 times, and the chance to take up a cause, 0 to 2 times;
  - body: health regained on a fed day, 2.5 to 3.5; and the aptitudes' learning rate and cap of skills, 0.75 to 1.25 times each, by the family the skill belongs to (heavy work: body; crafts: hands; field and herd: husbandry), if VB-360 or VB-475 has landed, replacing their stand-in; (neither has landed: the skills are VB-360's)
  - wits and husbandry: a head's skill (VB-254), unchanged in form.
- [x] Every trait has at least two rules it moves once the slice is done: a table in the task file, trait by rule, with the task that built each, and those this task leaves to VB-522, VB-523 and the lies task. `tools/rules.js` lists the contributions. Where a rule is explained to the player (the allowance, why a household does or does not feast), the trait's label is named.
- [x] Chemistry (`design.md`, Chemistry): `compat` becomes `C.personal` 0.7 × the seeded term + `C.likeness` 0.5 × likeness − `C.friction` 0.3 × (the two tempers' sum − 1) − `C.customs` 0.3 × the customs between them, less the age gap as today, within -1..1. Likeness is (0.17 − the mean absolute difference over the four values and sociability) / 0.17, within -1..1. The customs between them is the share of customs on which the two lean to different options, each custom weighed by 1 − its malleability; a pawn's leaning (its strongest option a custom) is compiled as a byte a custom where `culture` writes its attitudes (rule 6), owned by `culture`. Nothing is stored per pair; `compat` allocates nothing.
