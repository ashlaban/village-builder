# VB-984: A booth's maker lets a pawn in
Status: done (2026-11-19)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: far
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Moving to new land (The booth); VB-784
Goal: Whom a booth sleeps is the maker's to say, after it is built as well as when it is staked.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A verb `letIn(booth, pawn)` any pawn who made a booth has: adds the pawn to its `guests` (a right, which moves at once, Deciding without Kim); lodge's refusal for a pawn it is not for lifts. Test on a constructed state: refused before, lodged after; refused for one who did not make the booth.

Noted by VB-784, 17 November 2026.

Decisions: the verb is registered by sim/building, which owns `st.booths` and carries out the act (its exports stay at 15); target the booth's id, `args.pawn` the one let in. Refused: no such booth, the actor not its maker, no living pawn, or the booth open to the pawn already (its household, or let in before). No cap on `guests`: beds stay `BOOTH.sleeps`, and a duplicate is refused. design.md, Who may sleep where, names the verb.
