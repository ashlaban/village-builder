# VB-032: Constructing a new farmstead
Status: needs-review
Priority: 1
Size: L
Arch: expansion
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion (Clearing a new plot: build the cottage; Leaving home: a start in life); Long work and processing (Building: Decided); Settlements and look (Farmstead).
Goal: A new farmstead is built on a cleared plot (VB-031) or on a ruin (VB-033): foundation, log walls, turf roof. When the roof is on, the new owner and their spouse form a household there. Inheritance shares, which today pile up unused in `p.stake` and `p.owed`, become the household's start in life. Depends on VB-031 and VB-030; if either is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria (Kim answered the VB-030 questions on hours and shares on 2026-10-30):
- [ ] Building jobs owned by the village (cleared plot) or the claimant's household (ruin): prepare the foundation, raise the walls (uses timber from felling), lay the roof. Hours (Kim, 2026-10-30): foundation 30 h, walls 60 h, roof 30 h, 120 h in all. Progress persists across days.
- [ ] The map shows foundation stones, rising walls and the roof by stage.
- [ ] When the roof is on: a new farmstead record (house, the plot's fields, village, name per VB-026), a new household with the new holder and spouse; they move in (`moveTo`), with a "moved" life event.
- [ ] Start in life: the new holder's and spouse's `stake` moves into the household's grain; `owed` shares stay a debt of the farm they came from and are paid down at the moving round from grain above that farm's reserve (VB-049). The share event text no longer says "splitting land comes in a later batch" once VB-034 is done.
- [ ] `ids are never reused`: farmstead and household ids are appended.
- [ ] A test: a finished plot with a waiting couple gives a household that sows its fields the next spring.
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
