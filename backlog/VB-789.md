# VB-789: A site given up: the stake lapses, the work stands, a roofed cottage is an empty farm
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: far
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Moving to new land (Settling in steps; Sleeping away from home); docs/plans/VB-445.md (If it fails); design.md, Empty farms; Claims, ownership and territory
Goal: A settling may fail, and what was built is left in the world for someone else.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A party ends when its leader weds onto a holding, is given a plot or a farm, or dies with no adult of the party left; its workers come home (VB-783), its site is closed (the live index), and its stake is left to lapse by the claims' own rule. Nothing is removed: felled tiles stay felled, the booth rots (VB-784), the walls stand and weather.
- [ ] The search (VB-786) scores a site by the work left on it, so a half-made site ranks above bare ground beside it: its hours done count as nearness does, a named tunable (`SETTLE.made`, proposed 1 a 40 hours done).
- [ ] A cottage whose roof is on and whose party ended is a farm with no household: it is claimed and mended as an empty farm is (Empty farms), by anyone the custom allows.
- [ ] Numbers, populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years: sites given up and why, sites taken up again, roofed cottages claimed.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state: the leader marries onto a farm at the next round and the site is found by the next party; a roofed cottage is claimed.
