# VB-438: Slopes by Tobler's hiking function, symmetric around the flat
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, World and scale (Walking and terrain); src/sim/rules.js SLOPE
Goal: Walking slows on a slope by the factor a walker's speed falls by, the same up and down, on any terrain.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A step's cost is the mean of its two tiles' terrain costs times `exp(SLOPE.tobler × S)`, `S = SLOPE.grade ×` the elevation between them, at most `SLOPE.max`; 1 on the level, the same up and down.
- [x] `SLOPE = { tobler: 3.5, grade: 1.5, max: 6 }` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each with its meaning; `grade: 0` switches slopes off.
- [x] The route cache's bound on the dearest step (`MAX_STEP`) follows: the dearest terrain times `SLOPE.max`.
- [x] `tests/routes.js` holds the formula, the symmetry, the cap, the proportion on hard ground and that a ramp is cheaper than a cliff.
- [x] Numbers before and after are below; `design.md`, Walking and terrain, says what the code does.

## Decisions (Kim, 13 November, asked in the session)
Tobler (1993): a walker makes `6 exp(-3.5 |S + 0.05|)` km an hour at slope `S`. Kim asked for it symmetric around 0, so the 0.05 is dropped
and the speed falls by `exp(-3.5 |S|)`.
- **It multiplies the terrain cost** (it was added to it): a slope in forest or hills costs in proportion, and a worn path is slowed in proportion.
- **`grade` 1.5, where the median step costs what it did.** Read physically (1,400 m of relief, tiles of 150 m) `grade` would be 9.3: the
  median step a slope of 0.3 and three times as slow, the 90th percentile 15 to 45 times. The heightmap is far steeper than ground is. The
  level is left to a tuning run; only the shape changed.
- **The cap is 6**, where Tobler's 6 km an hour has fallen to 1 (a slope of 0.51, an elevation difference of 0.34); it was +2, three times meadow.

## Implementation notes
- `slopeCost` (added) became `slopeFactor` (multiplied) in `src/sim/routes.js`; nothing outside the module and its test read it. No state changed.
- One `Math.exp` a step where the two tiles differ in elevation (rule 6, compile what is hot: not compiled; see the cost below).
- The factor is convex, so a climb spread over several steps is now cheaper than the same climb in one (it cost the same before).
