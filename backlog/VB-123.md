# VB-123: A job as a few steps: go, work, fetch, carry
Status: blocked
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Jobs, owners and roles: Multi-step jobs; Long work and processing: Building jobs). Job review, second pass, 30 Oct 2026.
Goal: Every job has one of three shapes, set by the kind's flags: walk there and work; walk there, work and carry home; walk home and work inside. Building (VB-032, VB-058, VB-093, VB-115) needs materials fetched to the site first, and a mill needs grain carried there and flour carried back; none of these fit. A job made of a few steps does.
Acceptance criteria (proposed; see `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-123):
- [ ] A kind lists its steps: `go` (to the site, the house or a named place), `work` (hours, animation), `fetch` (a good, from a store or a place) and `carry` (a load, to a place). Today's three shapes become [go, work], [go, work, carry home] and [go home, work inside], and the `field`, `inside`, `carry` and `to` flags go.
- [ ] Agents run a job's steps in order; a job's progress is kept per step; several hands (VB-102) and interrupts (VB-107) work as before.
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged for today's kinds: `tests/golden.json` unchanged.
- [ ] Field-making (`src/sim/fields.js`) and plot clearing (`src/sim/expansion.js`) become one project model on the steps, used by the first building task.
