# VB-123: Project plans: steps, reservations and reconsideration
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-252
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Jobs, owners and roles: Multi-step jobs; Long work and processing: Building jobs). Job review, second pass, 30 Oct 2026.
Goal: A project is held in three shapes today: a land project is `f.project = {tile, stage, hours}` (`src/sim/fields.js`, `src/sim/meadows.js`), a plot is `village.plots[]` (`src/sim/expansion.js`) and a restoration is `f.claim` (`src/sim/farmclaims.js`): each is a plan with steps, each step opening when the one before is done. Make them one: a project plan in `st.plans`, whose steps reserve what they spend and are reconsidered when they become impossible. The job's own shapes (go, work, fetch, carry) are left as built until a building needs materials fetched (slice 6).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A project plan (`kind: 'project'`, written by `sim/plans`): a holder (a household, a village or a claimant), a list of steps each with its opportunity kind, its place and what it spends, and the index of the open step. A step's task is made when the step before is done.
- [ ] The land project, the meadow project, a village's plot with its cottage, and a claimed farm's restoration become project plans with the steps they have today, in that order of commits, each with `tests/golden.json` unchanged (the state's shape changes: list the golden entries that move, with behaviour the same; `tests/saves.js` passes). Their readers read the plan through `sim/plans`.
- [ ] Reservations: a step reserves what it will spend (seed for the new field; timber when a step spends it) through `sim/stores` (VB-248), released when the step is done or the plan dropped.
- [ ] Reconsideration: when a store or a right changes so that the open step is no longer possible (the stake is contested away, the reserved good is lost), the plan is reviewed at once: the step reopens if it can, else the plan is dropped and its reservations released, with a notable event. `contest` (`src/sim/stakes.js`) goes through it.
- [ ] The acceptance scenario of Test C (`docs/architecture/stress-tests.md`, section 5), as a test with a test-only project that spends 40 timber over three steps: the household's field work and needs go on beside it; a second project cannot reserve the same timber; with the timber removed mid-way the plan is reviewed and dropped, and nothing stays reserved.
- [ ] Invariants: every project plan's holder exists; its open step is within its steps; its reservations match the store's. No loop walks finished plans: a finished or dropped plan is removed (Rules for cost, 1). `npm run verify` passes.
