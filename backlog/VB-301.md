# VB-301: Tar: burnt in pine villages, needed for boats and timber
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-299, VB-300, VB-306
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, Goods of place and side crafts (10 Nov) (First goods, Agreed 10 Nov; What each good is for)
Goal: The second good of place (Kim, 10 Nov), grounded as Kim asked in a need and in standing: a boat untarred is lost, tarred timber lasts, and a tarred farm is seen to be well kept.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A resource reach test per village (pine forest within reach). A tar pit (*tjärdal*) is a facility one household builds and owns; burning is long work in the slack of early summer, from pine wood, giving tar by the barrel (12 work hours and a load of pine wood a barrel; worth 8 rations).
- [ ] A fishing boat (VB-300) needs a barrel every second year: untarred for two years it fishes at half the catch, after four it is lost. This is the need that makes shore villages want tar.
- [ ] Tar on a house slows the wear of its condition by about a third and raises the cap of the prestige a well-kept house gives by about a fifth (VB-306), for 6 years a barrel.
- [ ] Tar gives no prestige by itself: only through the well-kept house (Kim, 10 Nov).
- [ ] Tar is a keeping good. The page draws the pit smoking and tarred buildings darker.
- [ ] Numbers before and after as for dried fish, and boats lost for want of tar where no fair is in reach.
