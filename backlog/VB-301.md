# VB-301: Tar: burnt in pine villages, needed for boats and timber
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, Goods of place and side crafts (10 Nov) (First goods, Agreed 10 Nov; What each good is for)
Goal: The second good of place (Kim, 10 Nov), grounded as Kim asked in a need and in standing: a boat untarred is lost, tarred timber lasts, and a tarred farm is seen to be well kept.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A resource reach test per village (pine forest within reach). A tar pit (*tjärdal*) is a facility one household builds and owns; burning is long work in the slack of early summer, from pine wood, giving tar by the barrel (12 work hours and a load of pine wood a barrel; worth 8 rations).
- [x] A fishing boat (VB-300) needs a barrel every second year: untarred for two years it fishes at half the catch, after four it is lost. This is the need that makes shore villages want tar.
- [x] Tar on a house: VB-306 built the effect (`HOUSE.wearTarred` 0.067 against `wear` 0.1, `termTarred` 0.18 against `term` 0.15, `tarYears` 6, read from `f.tarredUntil`, `src/sim/rules.js`); this task has a household that holds a barrel use it on its house, through `sim/stores`, setting `f.tarredUntil` 6 years on.
- [x] Tar gives no prestige by itself: only through the well-kept house (Kim, 10 Nov).
- [x] Tar is a keeping good (declared by VB-299: `tar`, `src/sim/stores.js`). The need term for tar (`sim/exchange`, a stub returning 0 "from VB-301") is switched on: worth its worth when a boat or the house is due. The page draws the pit smoking and tarred buildings darker.
- [x] Numbers before and after as for dried fish, and boats lost for want of tar where no fair is in reach.

- 13 Nov 2026 (decisions, Deciding without Kim): (1) Burning draws only among the pine tiles of the village's wood (`B.PINE` with trees
  standing), the first in the wood's order (own land first, nearest the centre first); the firewood draw is left as it is, so a tar village
  burns its pine as firewood too and a village whose pine is felled burns no tar until it regrows or the wood is reckoned again. (2) One pit
  a village: dug by its household with the most working hands (at least 2, no project plan under way), as a project plan of one step
  (`tarpit`, 12 hours, 2 hands) on the wood's first pine tile, begun at summer day 0. (3) Without the fair (VB-302) a barrel never leaves
  the pit's household: it keeps one barrel for its house and one for its boat, and only it tars. The other households' boats leak and are
  lost: that is the need the fair answers. (4) A boat is tarred as it is built; it is due a barrel from half a year (`TAR.lead`) before it
  leaks; a house is due while it is untarred. Tarring takes 4 hours, outside winter. (5) The need term lives in `sim/tar` (the stub in
  `sim/exchange` is removed): a barrel is worth its customary worth (8) for each house or boat due beyond the barrels held, nothing beyond;
  giving one up costs as much. The pit's and the tarring's hours, the lead and the stock are this task's, Proposed, in `docs/tuning.md`.

Cost named before building (rule 9):
- The pit round: once a year at summer day 0, each farm of each village (`tarAsk`) until one holds a pit, and a walk of the village's wood
  (at most `WOOD.cap` tiles, usually a few) for its first pine tile. The boat round of spring walks each farm once more (`tarAsk`) for a
  boat to be lost.
- Burning: asked with the household's other farm kinds, in early summer only (6 days a year), for the pit's household only, after
  comparisons; the pine tile is the wood's walk again. Tarring: two comparisons a household. No loop over history, no per-pawn collection.

  the other counters as on the base. Late saves not run (night-run rules).
