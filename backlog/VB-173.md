# VB-173: Only a field's owner writes it: a static check
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: ownership
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (code structure). Architecture review of 3 November 2026 (`docs/review-architecture.md`, section 2, point 1); Rules 1 and 3 of `docs/architecture/target-architecture.md`; layer 6 ("architectural fitness tests") of `docs/architecture/stress-tests.md`, section 14.
Goal: Every field the simulation keeps has one owner module; other modules read it freely and change it only through the owner's operations. Enforce it statically, as `tests/commands.js` allows only the command verbs to call the acts they wrap, so a new write past the owner fails `npm test`. VB-172 names the owners and how they are declared; blocked until it is done and Kim has reviewed its map.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Owners are declared in one table, `src/owners.json` (Kim's answer 4 of 3 November, `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-172: the ownership map"), keyed by the fields as `tools/writes.js` names them (`household.grain`): each field's owner module, or `signal`; with the creation modules (`sim/scenarios`, `sim/layout`, `sim/people`), which may write the records they seed, and the exempt save codec (`sim/saves`). The check reads it, so a new owned field needs no change to the check.
- [ ] The first entries, the boundaries Kim chose (the map, `docs/architecture/ownership.md`, section 7): `st.owner` (`sim/ownership`) and `st.claims` (`sim/claims`), which pass today; household and village food (`household.grain`, `rations`, `unthreshed`, `ledger`, `spoilt`, `village.grain`, `pawn.stake`, `owed`, `owedFrom`: `sim/stores`, VB-174); membership (`pawn.household`, `household.members`, `pawn.servant`: `sim/records`, VB-175); a pawn's movement (`agent.path`, `phase`, `anim`, `inside`, `idleUntil`, `x`, `y`: `sim/agents`, VB-177); ties and attitudes (`pawn.ties`, `pawn.rel`: `sim/relations`; `pawn.att`, `anc`, `advocate`: `culture`, VB-178); the crop (`farmstead.fields`: `sim/fields`, VB-179); and the page flags (`st.visualDirty`, `biomeDirty`, `rigDirty`, `treeDirty`, `pathDirty`, `landDirty`) as `signal` (Kim's answer 5), which any module may write.
- [ ] The check reuses the scan of `tools/writes.js`: in `src/sim` and `src/culture.js`, a write (assignment, compound assignment, `++`/`--`, `delete`, or a mutating call) on an owned field outside its owner and the creation modules fails, naming the file, line, field and owner. Writes to a new record before it is pushed are creation and are not checked. `// lint-allow: owner <reason>` exempts a line, as the clock rule does.
- [ ] Known violations are kept in a baseline that may only go down (as `tests/lint-baseline.json`), so the check lands before the refactors that clear them (VB-174 to VB-179); each of those empties its fields' entries.
- [ ] `tools/writes.js` prints the owner column from the table, and marks writes past the owner.
- [ ] Part of `npm test`, under 1 s; a test plants a write outside the owner and sees it fail.
- [ ] `CLAUDE.md`, Conventions: one line on the rule (reads free, writes through the owner, page flags are signals) and the check.
- 3 Nov 2026: VB-172 is done and Kim answered its questions; owners are declared in `src/owners.json`, not on the `@state` lines (answer 4). Set `ready`; it may land before the refactors, with their writes in the baseline.
- 3 Nov 2026 (target-v2, section 11.2): the owners table gains a "cache" category, so `pawn.household`, `hh.members`, `pawn.role` and `st.owner` are checked like fields: one writer each, and a check that each equals what it is derived from (principle 2).
