# VB-173: Only a field's owner writes it: a static check
Status: blocked
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: ownership
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (code structure). Architecture review of 3 November 2026 (`docs/review-architecture.md`, section 2, point 1); Rules 1 and 3 of `docs/architecture/target-architecture.md`; layer 6 ("architectural fitness tests") of `docs/architecture/stress-tests.md`, section 14.
Goal: Every field the simulation keeps has one owner module; other modules read it freely and change it only through the owner's operations. Enforce it statically, as `tests/commands.js` allows only the command verbs to call the acts they wrap, so a new write past the owner fails `npm test`. VB-172 names the owners and how they are declared; blocked until it is done and Kim has reviewed its map.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Owners declared as VB-172 settles (proposed: on the `@state` line); the check reads them, so a new owned field needs no change to the check.
- [ ] In `src/sim`, an assignment, a compound assignment, or a `push`, `splice`, `set` or `delete` on an owned field outside its owner fails, naming the file, line, field and owner. `// lint-allow: owner <reason>` exempts a line, as the clock rule does.
- [ ] Known violations are kept in a baseline that may only go down (as `tests/lint-baseline.json`), so the check lands before the refactors that clear them.
- [ ] Starts with the fields VB-172's review settles first (proposed: tile owners, which pass today; household membership; household and village food).
- [ ] Part of `npm test`, under 1 s; a test plants a write outside the owner and sees it fail.
- [ ] `CLAUDE.md`, Conventions: one line on the rule (reads free, writes through the owner) and the check.
