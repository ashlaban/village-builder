# VB-306: A well-kept house gives prestige, up to a cap; tar helps
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, Goods of place and side crafts (A well-kept house, Decided 10 Nov)
Goal: Kim, 10 Nov: a well-maintained house should give prestige, up to a cap, and tar makes that source easier (a slightly higher cap, a little less decay). It grounds tar in standing and gives upkeep a reward.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A house's condition, 0 to 1, is state with an owner and an invariant; it wears down with time and rises with upkeep work, which is offered as ordinary work of the type upkeep. If buildings already have a condition (empty farms decay), that one is used and no second is added.
- [ ] The holder's prestige gains a term that follows the house's condition, 0.15 times the condition (a holder's prestige is 1.6); a holder's spouse gains the same. The condition wears 0.1 a year, read in closed form from when it was last raised (rule 3: no daily pass); 4 work hours of upkeep raise it 0.1, offered once it is under 0.8. It is read where prestige is read; nothing is stored beside the condition.
- [ ] A tarred house (VB-301): the term is 0.18 times the condition and the wear 0.067 a year, for the 6 years a barrel lasts. Until VB-301 no house is tarred.
- [ ] The pawn panel names the term among prestige's parts; the page may draw a neglected house greyer.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous, small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): upkeep hours, prestige by household, and whether voices at the meeting shift.
- [ ] The open point of `design.md` (what a more prestigious house brings) is updated.
