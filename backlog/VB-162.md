# VB-162: A village overview: food, people, idle hands and warnings
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-256
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player, commands and views; Systems overview. External review of 2 November (`docs/review-external.md`).
Goal: To see why a village struggles, the player now opens household after household. One panel per village shows its state at a glance and warns of trouble ahead, from numbers the simulation already keeps.
Decided (Kim, 10 November; `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-162 and VB-165"): what the overview shows, as in the criteria.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Selecting a village (its label, or a key from VB-165) opens a Village panel with: food in store and its days of need, this year's harvest against a year's need, people by age band (0–14, 15–49, 50+) and the change since a year ago, households hungry on any day in the last 30, and the share of adult work hours idle over the last 30 days.
- [ ] Warnings, each with the households it concerns (clicking one selects it): food in store short of the days to the next harvest; a household hungry 5 of the last 30 days; a holder over 60 with no heir in the household; a farm with fewer working adults than its fields need.
- [ ] The numbers come from one function in the simulation (`villageSummary(st, v)`), so `tools/sim.js --report villages` prints the same numbers; a test checks the function against a hand count on a tiny world.
- [ ] The page only reads `st` (lint); the panel costs under 1 ms to build at populous medium.
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged: the golden file is unchanged.
