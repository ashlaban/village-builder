  Decisions (Deciding without Kim; noted in the inbox):
  - The criteria's "last 30 days" is read as the last 12 days, a season (HUNGER.days, what a pawn's hunger log holds; the game's year
    is 48 days), and "hungry 5 of the last 30 days" as 2 of the 12, the same share (`OVERVIEW` in sim/overview). No new state.
  - "This year's harvest" is the latest harvest: this calendar year's from autumn, when it comes in, last year's before (in spring
    this year's would always read 0).
  - Whose estimate (principle 14): the played pawn's; with none (tools, a watched game), the village's eldest living holder's. The
    estimate is the pawn's foresight as sim/plans reckons a head's (`horizonOf` its skill): a pawn that reacts looks a day ahead, one
    with a horizon of one season to the season's end, two a season further, never past the next harvest; and the fields a hand can
    till by its own household's plan (`targets.perHand`, its head's bias), FIELDS.perHand without one. The card says whose reckoning
    and how many days ahead.
  - People a year ago are those then alive whose household (the last, for the dead) is of the village: who moved in during the year
    counts as of it then. It walks every pawn record when the card is drawn (never in a step; a work counter, `overviewWalk`).
  - "Over 60" is 60 and on; an heir "in the household" is one of `heirRanking`'s, alive and a member.
  - VB-667's supply cells and chips are not built (it is blocked): the village store is a stat beside the food, and a warned farm's
    row carries the warning's icon; VB-927 makes them VB-667's when it lands.
