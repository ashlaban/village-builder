Goal: What the ice opens is used: the acts that weigh distance read the hours of their own season, and a cold island can gather when its ice bears.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Each act between villages that reads `hoursBetween` names the season it happens in: the suitor's visit and the winter rites read the winter table while ice bears; the moving round (autumn day 11), claims and service read the land table; kin help reads the day's. The list of callers and what each reads is in the task file; no caller reads a table by default.
- [ ] A custom of the culture, `gatherDay` (principle 5; grounded in the winter fairs held when the ice bore, as the Disting at Uppsala in February): `harvest` (winter days 1 to 3, as Decided on 10 November, the start of every culture of today) and `midwinter` (winter days 8 to 10). It is a practice the villages' meetings can change as any custom; a culture founded on a northern or far north world starts with `midwinter`. Proposed: it moves the gathering's Decided date for those cultures only, and Kim may strike it.
- [ ] Who gathers reads the hours of the gathering's own days: over the ice at midwinter where it bears in a normal winter.
- [ ] Numbers before and after on the northern world, populous medium, seeds 3, 11 and 42 (islands apart by water: 76 to 132 pairs of villages with no route by land), 20 years: villages at each gathering, goods dealt, marriages between islands, by custom; and on the middle world, where nothing should move.
- Cost (rule 9): no new loop; a table lookup where one is made today.
