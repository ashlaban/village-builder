# VB-125: Idle pawns seek company, and stop to talk
Status: done (2026-10-31)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: social
Phase: implement
Design ref: Relationships review, 31 Oct 2026 (`docs/review-relationships.md`, proposal D2 and section 7): idle pawns walk to where someone they like is idling, and stop and face each other briefly to talk, so friends show as groups on the map. Dwarf Fortress shows the alternative: friendships at random, as dwarves talk to whoever they run into.
Goal: An idle pawn chose between the green and its own yard at random, so who met whom was a matter of where one happened to stand. Let liking steer where the idle go, and let talk show.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] An idle pawn (`idleAbout`), at chance 0.5 (`SOCIAL.seek`), draws up to 4 (`SOCIAL.seekSample`) pawns idling outdoors in its village and walks to one of them, by weight (opinion + 0.05, never below 0) × (1 + min(tie, 1)); so never to someone it dislikes. With none, it walks to the green or its yard as before. The idle pawns of each village are indexed once a game hour, and a pawn drawn is checked to be idling still.
- [x] A pawn idling outdoors that talks stops where it is until the talk is over; the page turns an idle talking pawn to face the other.
- [x] Tests (`tests/relations.js`): an idle pawn walks to a friend idling in its village, and never to someone it dislikes.
- [x] Reported, populous medium, seed 7, 2 years (`--report relations`): per adult 4.8 friends and 2.6 disliked people outside the household (3.3 and 1.8 without), at about the same rate of talk (8.3 talks per pawn and day, 8.2 without); of all records 3.6% close friend, 15.3% friend, 75.7% neutral, 5.3% dislikes, 0.1% enemy. `tests/perf.js`: about 100 ms per game day against about 90 without (two runs each; the company search costs about 8 ms a day).
