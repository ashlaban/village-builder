# VB-1538: Refactor: split sim/goods by concern
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-733
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Conventions (a module over 15 exports; VB-415); `docs/architecture/modules.md`; target-v2 section 4.3 (goods)
Goal: sim/goods holds three concerns: how a good is kept in store (`KEEPING`, `keepingOf`, `dayLoss`, `shares`, `tierLoss`, `keepsDays`), grain's subtype ids by crop (`GRAIN`, `CROP_LIST`, `CROP_GOOD`, `GOOD_CROP`, `isGrain`, `grainGood`), and the registry of goods with their materials and kinds (`declareGood`, `goodOf`, `goods`, `kindRow`, `kindsOf`, `baseOf`, `kindIn`; VB-733). The split follows them: keeping is read by the stores and the outlook alone and is the first candidate for a module of its own; grain's ids are the crop's side of the registry's subtypes and may join the kinds once a crop is a kind (principle 17).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] sim/goods at 15 exports or fewer, split by the concerns of the goal, not by the count; each new module's `@sys`, `src/manifest.json`, `src/owners.json`, `src/layers.json` and `docs/architecture/modules.md` follow; `tests/width-baseline.json` loses its sim/goods line.
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`; `npm run verify` passes.
