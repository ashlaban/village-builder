# VB-1538: Reasons shown: who chose a pawn's act, and why, on its card
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: `design.md` section 7, Reasons shown; `docs/review-agency.md`, section 4; `docs/architecture/target-v2.md` section 4.5, Agency below the head
Goal: A pawn's card says what the pawn does, not who chose it or why (`sim/agents` `describe`; `sim/jobs` `idleLine` answers for the idle only). Build the view of design.md's row Reasons shown: for a living pawn, who chose its present act (the head's day with its place in the member's list, the head's call, a day lent or owed, the village's work, the player's order, its own choosing, or none) and one line of why, and show it on the pawn's card beside what it is doing. It is a derived view, computed when asked: it reads the day's lists (`plan.day`), the task's basis and what the kind's record already declares (its window, `late`, the store and target it fills); prefer those to a new hook on every kind (`docs/review-jobs.md`, 4.3, on the hooks). What presses is the head's estimate (`sim/estimate`), never the simulation's own knowledge. Standing duties (VB-1540) and pastimes (VB-1539) are built beside this task: leave their reasons to them (a `duty` and a pastime read as the head's day and own choosing until then).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A read-only view (a module of its own or beside `explainIdle`; `Sim` exposes it) returns for a pawn `{by, head?, place?, why}`: every `by` of the row but `duty` is reached by a constructed state in a new test listed in `tests/suites.js` (`tests/tiny.js`, `tests/lib/probe.js`).
- [ ] It writes nothing to `st`, draws from no stream and is not called by any simulation module (the dependency check or a test holds it); nothing is added to saved state unless a `by` cannot be told without it, and then the field has an owner, an `@state` line and passes `tests/saves.js`.
- [ ] The why names a cause for at least: a kind with a window or a deadline today, field work whose window is closing, and a kind that fills a store under its target; other kinds say who chose and the place in the list.
- [ ] The pawn's card shows the reason in one line under what the pawn is doing, the same wherever a pawn's doing is shown (Principles: like is shown alike); the idle line is that line for a pawn with no work. `node tests/smoke.js` and the affected browser tests pass.
- [ ] `npm run format` and `npm run verify` pass; behaviour unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`.
