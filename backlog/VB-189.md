  - How a town comes about (cities review, question 1). How does a town come about? **P:** both ways, as in Sweden: a yearly fair at the site of highest market potential grows into a market place (*köping*) once 3 households there live by trade or craft; the Crown charters a market place of 5 such households as a town (*stad*), and without the Crown a market place of 10 with a church counts as one.
  - The urban penalty (cities review, question 7). The urban penalty. **P:** the death hazard times 1.8 in towns (Stockholm had 52 deaths per 1,000 a year in the 1750s). The review's arithmetic shows a town of historical size (5% of the people) absorbs only a small share of the countryside's surplus of births; the main brake must stay late marriage and celibacy, which the game barely has (0–5% of women 45–54 never married, against 10–20% in the European marriage pattern).
  - Scenarios (cities review, question 9). Scenarios. **P:** first "No land left" (a full island, buildable now: it shows the ceiling and the two problems above), then "The fair meadow" with Phase 1; the other five (`docs/review-cities.md`, section 6) as their phases are built.
