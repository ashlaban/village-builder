# VB-775: Rites on a village's own days, so that neighbours attend each other's
Status: done (2026-11-19)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: town
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, People (Where pawns meet: The season's rite); Customs (Rules of a culture); Settlements, Towns and regions (Rites on a village's own days); docs/plans/VB-189.md, section 5.4
Goal: More days on which neighbours meet and may deal, by custom and with nobody calling one (Kim, 16 Nov: rites on each village's own days, as a parish's feast day, so neighbours can attend each other's).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Each village with a green keeps three rite days of its own. They are drawn once, at the village's founding or the scenario's start, each within `RITE.spread` 5 days after the season's day of VB-522 (spring 1 to 6, summer 6 to 11, winter 7 to 12), from a seeded stream (`src/sim/rng.js`); plain data on the village, in the save, with invariants (three days, each in its window). Nothing staggers them against the neighbours' but chance (principle 8).
- [x] `PRACTICES.riteDays` in `src/culture.js`, a rule of the culture as VB-713's hunting is (not voted, no drift): `own` ("Each village its own days"; the one every culture starts with) and `common` ("The same days everywhere": VB-522 as built). Decided by principle 5 and written in the task file: it is a custom, so cultures may come to differ and Kim reviews it with the others; it is not a dimension of `Culture.DIMS` because nothing yet gives a pawn a stake in it, and the days themselves already differ by village.
- [x] Under `own`, the adults of another village within `RITE.reach` 2 walking hours of a green may come to its rite, at VB-522's chance by piety times (1 - walk / `RITE.reach`); they walk there and home. A rite's calendar entries are per village (the due-queue), not one day for all.
- [x] The rite's leader, ties and what is said are VB-522's, unchanged; guests from another village meet those present at the venue's `mix`.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (`common` against `own`), populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7 and 11: rites a village-year, the share of those present from another village, ties and friends across villages a pawn, hours of work lost (the hours report), marriages across villages; and, once VB-769 exists, the greens' share of dealing (the mock: 36% to 50%, a toy's figure). Growth and hungry household-days.
- [ ] Parameterisation (at most five short runs): `RITE.spread` (3, 5, 8), for the share of rites that a neighbour within reach can attend without missing its own; values tried in the task file.
- [x] Tests on a constructed state: two villages an hour apart with different days, a devout adult attends both; under `common` it attends its own only; a village 3 hours away sends nobody.
- Cost (rule 9): 3 rites a year x villages (25 to 30) x those asked (the village and the villages within 2 hours, each one chance, by the table of walking hours, no search); through the due-queue, nothing polled. The counter `occasionAsked` before and after.

Noted by VB-189, 16 November 2026.
