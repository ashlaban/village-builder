# VB-1104: Customs swing back within a decade: measure it against the Decided pace and mend the cause
Status: done (2026-11-19)
Priority: 1
Size: M
Design ref: Customs and culture (Pace; How attitudes move; Meetings, culture and splits)
Goal: A custom that passes holds on the scale of generations, as Pace has it, unless an effort as large moves it back.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Measured over founders small seeds 7 and 42 (80 years) and populous small seed 7 (40 years): each change of practice by village and of custom by culture, its dimension, the causes argued for it at the meeting that passed it, and whether it goes back within 10 and 30 years. A table in the notes; a throwaway script, or a report of `tools/sim.js` if it is worth keeping.
- [x] The mechanism found and written in the notes: what support a passed practice keeps once the causes behind it lapse (Culture.P.causeEvery, 5 years), what pulls attitudes back (drift to the culture's custom, the founders' seeded attitudes), and how the culture's custom follows its villages' practice, including why one village of two changed the custom "across the Nordic culture" in the playtest, and whether that line says what happened.
- [x] Mended by `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim 11 (enforcing a Decided point needs no question): where a formula computes something other than it says, fixed and reported before and after (principle 3); where the swing is a balance point, a named parameter with a meaning (a practice's hold once in force, a margin to undo it) tried in at most five short runs, the values and what each gave in the notes. A shortfall outside the task's reach is a row of `docs/tuning.md`.
- [x] Reported before and after at the seeds above: changes per 30 years and the share reversed within 10, against Pace's table (natural drift: about 3 generations for the easiest custom; a coalition of 20% of the voice: 1.1 to 2.1 generations).

Pace (Decided): customs change on the scale of generations, villages are stable and larger cultures slower.
- For the player a cause is the one act that reaches the village, and one that passes and lapses in five years teaches that it does not matter. Small cultures may rightly move faster (Pace: 100 people first change in about 2.2 generations); the measure says whether this is that, or a defect.
- 16 Nov 2026 (the parallel session "Gameplay review and feedback": founders small seed 7, 75 years headless, one played pawn; its evidence added at the user's request): 12 of its 118 skip stops were custom changes, mostly "Across the Nordic culture", swinging back and forth over 75 years.
- 17 Nov 2026 (reviewed after the merge with main, 354 commits on from the playtests' base; the code facts checked again): stands (`src/culture.js` changed by one line since).
- 19 Nov 2026, done. Mechanism: a cause pulls its advocate (P.advocacyPull) and its contacts (persuasion) for 5 years; once it lapses, attitudes relax within a few years to anchors that still hold the old way (P.anchor 0.35 a year; adults' anchors drift 0.02), so a change one cause carried kept no support of its own, and went back at a village by returnMargin (0.12) or at the reckoning by the small margin it passed with. The culture's custom is its villages' support weighted by size, tie and centre; in founders the centre (tie 1, counted twice) is two thirds of it, and the reckoning's margin (0.25 times 1.44 at 18 people) is far below what that village's own meeting needs to leave the custom (0.85 times fewVoices), so the centre's mood alone moved "the culture", while villages that clearly preferred the old kept it: the line "Across the Nordic culture" did not say what happened.
- Mended: (1) a practice's hold, `Culture.P.hold` 0.5: at the meeting or reckoning that passes a change, each voice of a village taking it up that favours the new way moves its anchor half way to its attitude (it carried the change in public), so the change outlasts its cause; (2) `P.fewVoices` (a bare 0.1, now 0.2) and `P.smallCulture` (0.02) named; (3) the culture's line reads "The Nordic custom: X is now "Y" (was "Z"), taken up in A; B keeps "Z"". Runs (culture changes back within 10 years at f7/f42; populous village meetings back within 10): t1 no hold, fewVoices 0.2, returnMargin 0.25, smallCulture 0.05: 0.45/0.5, 0.30 of fewer; t2 hold 0.5: 0.25/0.29, 0.65; t3 t2 + returnMargin 0.25: founders unchanged, 0.65 (kept 0.12); t4 hold 0.5 + fewVoices 0.2: 0.25/0.2, 0.62 (kept); t5 hold 0.8 + fewVoices 0.2: 0.33/0.25, 0.53, more changes at f7.
- Before -> after (changes, per 30 years, back within 10 of those closed): founders 7 culture 15 (5.6), 6/14 -> 5 (1.9), 1/4; village meetings 8 (3.0), 2/7 -> 4 (1.5), 0/4; villages following the culture 25 (9.4), 9/22 -> 6 (2.3), 1/4. Founders 42 culture 5 (1.9), 2/4 -> 6 (2.3), 1/5; meetings 12 (4.5), 4/8 -> 8 (3.0), 0/6; following 7 (2.6), 2/4 -> 5 (1.9), 1/4. Populous 7 (no culture change either way): meetings 35 (26.3), 16/22 -> 21 (15.8), 8/13. A cause was argued at 3 of 8 founders 7 meeting changes before, 2 of 4 after. Against Pace: founders' culture (18 to 48 people, 16 customs) changes each custom about once in 260 years after, and its changes mostly hold past 10 years.
