# VB-104: Children work from age 7, at reduced efficiency
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: work
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Jobs, owners and roles: Role priorities: children herd). Kim, 30 Oct 2026 (jobs review): children can work from the age of 7, at reduced efficiency.
Goal: Children (role `child`, under 14) have no job priorities and are idle for 100% of work hours. Children worked from about 7: fetching water, weeding, helping at the harvest, later herding (the animals arch).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Children of 7 to 13 take jobs: the `child` role's priorities are water 6, weed 5, haul 4, reap 3; children under 7 take none. Adults' priorities stay higher, so a child does a job only when no adult of the household takes it first.
- [ ] Work rate by age: 0.3 at 7, rising by 0.1 a year to 0.9 at 13, multiplying the progress of every job they work (with hunger and health, as for adults); from 14 the pawn has an adult role at full rate, as today. The rate lives in `ROLES.child` as `rate: [from, perYear]` or a `CHILD` table in `src/sim/rules.js`.
- [ ] Children walk at full speed and carry at the carrying speed, as adults do.
- [ ] The pawn panel shows a working child's job like an adult's.
- [ ] A test: a household of two parents and a child of 9 in summer; the child takes water or weeding; a child of 6 takes nothing.
- [ ] Populous medium at seed 7 and founders at seed 7, 3 years: `--report idle` (VB-101), weeding rounds missed and hungry household-days, before and after (reported, not asserted).
