# VB-098: Notifications for notable events
Status: done (2026-11-09)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Systems overview (Presentation: Notifications missing; Pace: rare, legible events carry the game at 3× and 10×; finding 6). Proposed.
Goal: A death, a marriage, a farm left empty or a change of custom passes unseen unless the player is looking at the right panel. Notifications draw the eye to them; VB-082 (customs) and VB-084 (run until something happens) need the same list of notable events. Depends on VB-075 (the record of events); if it is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] One function in the simulation, `notable(st, ev)`, decides from a record event whether it is notable, by the answered list (a death, birth or marriage in the selected pawn's household or line; the death of a landholder; a farm emptied, claimed or newly built; a change of practice or of the culture's custom; a death from hunger).
- [x] The page shows each as a toast (bottom left, 6 s, at most 3 at once); a click selects the pawn, farm or village and moves the camera to it.
- [x] At high speed, toasts of one kind within a game day merge ("3 births in Ulsäter").
- [x] A small list turns each kind on or off; the choice is kept in `localStorage` (page state, not `st`).
- [x] VB-082 and VB-084 use `notable` if they are done after this task, or are changed to use it.
- [x] The smoke test checks that a death in the heirs scenario raises a toast and no console errors.

- 9 Nov 2026, built:
  - Impact: one new module, `sim/notable` (after `sim/deaths` in the manifest), a derived view with no state. It reads a record event, `st.pawns` (household, parents, `retired`), the kin index's `holds` and farm names, through `sim/records` and `CAUSES` of `sim/deaths`; it writes nothing. It is not in `sim/records` because that loads before the causes of death, and because the record's owner should not decide what the player is told. On `Sim`: `notable`, `NOTABLE`, `eventLine`. One page file, `src/page/notices.js`, which only reads `st`.
  - `Sim.notable(st, ev, about = null)` gives the reasons an event is notable, as keys of `Sim.NOTABLE`, or null: `family` (a birth, marriage or death in the household of pawn `about`, or to its line: itself, its forebears and its descendants, 4 generations each way), `holder` (the death of a pawn who held the household it died in, and had not retired), `hunger` (a death of hunger or starvation), `farm` (the kinds `empty`, `claim`, `cottage`, `left`), `custom` (`custom`, `split`). An event may have several reasons, so a death from hunger in the family still shows with `family` turned off. The criterion wrote `notable(st, ev)`: the third argument is the selected pawn, since the simulation does not know the selection; once `st.player` exists (VB-169) it can default to the played pawn.
  - `Sim.eventLine(st, ev)` tells an event in one sentence: its own text, a death as the village log told it (the record keeps only the cause), and the births, marriages and deaths seeded as history, which carry no text. VB-099's village log needs the same.
  - The page: each frame the record's new events are judged for the pinned pawn (or the one pinned a frame before, so the death of the selected pawn, which ends the selection, is still its own). A toast is a button at the bottom left, beside the left sheet while one is open and under the clock at phone width; it lasts 6 s of real time, also while paused; a fourth replaces the oldest, with no queue. Events of one kind in one village on one game day share a toast while it is up ("3 deaths in Högnes"), which in practice happens at 10× and above. A click selects the newborn or the groom, else the farm's house, else the village (its block opened in the People list, the camera on its centre), and takes the toast away. The list is in the menu, under Game, built from `Sim.NOTABLE`; the kinds turned off are kept in `localStorage` (`vb.notices.off`). A debug skip tells nothing.
  - Departures, for Kim: (1) beyond the answered list, a party leaving for new land (`left`: a farm founded) is told under farms, and a village's customs parting from its culture (`split`) under customs. (2) A cottage built on a cleared plot was logged as a second `moved` event beside the one its holder's move records, so a new farm could not be told from a move: `finishCottage` now logs it with the kind `cottage`, as the roofing of a settler's cottage is. (3) Whether a dead pawn held land is read from the kin index, which is right for deaths of the session and says no for the earlier deaths of a loaded save; toasts and the skip (VB-234) only judge new events, but a chronicle that wants to mark landholders' deaths in old history needs the `died` event to say so (a note in VB-099).
  - VB-082 will announce through `notable` (a note there: a pawn taking up a cause is not in this list). VB-084, done on 30 October, has no "run until something happens"; that became VB-234, which shares `notable`.

It runs in the page's frame, not in a step, and allocates only for births, marriages and deaths. No loop over a growing collection, so no counter.
