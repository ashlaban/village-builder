# VB-098: Notifications for notable events
Status: needs-review
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: Systems overview (Presentation: Notifications missing; Pace: rare, legible events carry the game at 3× and 10×; finding 6). Proposed.
Goal: A death, a marriage, a farm left empty or a change of custom passes unseen unless the player is looking at the right panel. Notifications draw the eye to them; VB-082 (customs) and VB-084 (run until something happens) need the same list of notable events. Depends on VB-075 (the record of events); if it is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria (draft, pending `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-098):
- [ ] One function in the simulation, `notable(st, ev)`, decides from a record event whether it is notable, by the answered list (proposed: a death, birth or marriage in the selected pawn's household or line; the death of a landholder; a farm emptied, claimed or newly built; a change of practice or of the culture's custom; a death from hunger).
- [ ] The page shows each as a toast (proposed: bottom left, 6 s, at most 3 at once); a click selects the pawn, farm or village and moves the camera to it.
- [ ] At high speed, toasts of one kind within a game day merge ("3 births in Ulsäter").
- [ ] A small list turns each kind on or off; the choice is kept in `localStorage` (page state, not `st`).
- [ ] VB-082 and VB-084 use `notable` if they are done after this task, or are changed to use it.
- [ ] The smoke test checks that a death in the heirs scenario raises a toast and no console errors.
