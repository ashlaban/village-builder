Status: needs-review
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: Systems overview (Chronicle: a typed record of events, missing; Fun: a growing cast and a memory; finding 6: one record from which the log, the panels, notifications and the tests all read). Proposed.
Goal: VB-075 keeps every event as a typed record but leaves the page unchanged. A chronicle shows that record where the player looks: what happened to this farm, this family, this village, across generations. Depends on VB-075; if it is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria (draft, pending `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-098):

- [ ] A farm's history follows the farm across households: every holder, handover, empty year and claim since the start.
- [ ] Names in the history are links that select the pawn, farm or village.
- [ ] The village log in the left panel becomes a view of the record (its last 40 village events); `st.events` and the 40-line text log are no longer written, and VB-075's test still passes.
- [ ] Building a farm's history after 150 founders years takes under 20 ms (reported).
- [ ] The smoke test opens each tab with no console errors.
