# VB-278: Dried fish: a food that keeps, made where there is water to fish
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-117, VB-277
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, Goods of place and side crafts (10 Nov) (First goods, Agreed 10 Nov)
Goal: The first good of place (Kim, 10 Nov), grounded in a need: food through the winter and variety in the pot.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A resource reach test per village (fishing water within 6 steps of a house, as VB-117), refreshed when its land changes; a village without it cannot make the good.
- [ ] Drying is a side craft: long work on a drying rack at the farm, in slack hours, turning fresh fish (VB-117) into dried fish that keeps to the next summer.
- [ ] Dried fish is a food source at cooking (Mixed meals) and a keeping good, in lots of 10 rations.
- [ ] The fishing boat (`design.md`, Goods of place, The fishing boat; Agreed 10 Nov): a household by the sea or a lake of 20 tiles or more may build one as long work of 20 hours from timber; a facility on a shore tile, with an owner and invariants. A trip in it brings 8 rations where the bank gives 4. Its tarring is VB-279's: until then it does not decay. Nobody travels in it.
- [ ] A household makes more than it eats only as its holder's project aimed at the fair (slice 3's projects); the target is stated in rations.
- [ ] Hours at the craft raise the pawn's skill at it, if skills exist by then; else a note for slice 7.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous, small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): idle hours in the slack seasons, hungry household-days in shore villages and inland ones separately.
