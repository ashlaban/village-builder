# VB-278: The meetings a village a step: each village's meeting in its own six-minute step
Status: needs-review
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: between
Phase: implement
Design ref: Customs and culture (Meetings, culture and splits: A village's meeting, Decided, 10 Nov). Survey and cost: `backlog/VB-242.md`.
Goal: The year's meetings (`meetingStage`, `src/sim/lifecycle.js:60-89`) run in four stages for the whole island from noon of autumn day 10 (VB-206). Stage 0 refreshes everyone's roles, prestige and interests, stage 1 every settlement's tie, stage 2 the entrepreneurs, every settlement's meeting and every culture's year, and stage 3 is the land meeting. Kim decided on 10 November that each village holds its own meeting in its own step, in the order of village id, and a lone farm is a village. VB-206's stages become the stages of one village; then come the cultures' reckoning and the land meeting, which are not any one village's.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The steps. On autumn day 10, the villages' steps run from noon, village k on step noon + k, while there are at most 60 villages; with more, village k runs on noon + floor(k × 60 / villages), as VB-277's round does. The cultures' years run at 18:00 and the land meeting at 18:06. `meetingStage(st, n)` stays the entry point for each step, so `tools/perf.js` and `tools/bench.js` ("Meetings, its call") read the longest step. In VB-241's calendar the meetings are one entry for the day, whose `run` reads the step of the day. `holdMeetings(st)` (the drawer, the tests) runs every village and then the cultures' years, without the land meeting, as today.
- [x] A village's step, `villageMeeting(st, v)`, in this order:
  1. Its living's interests: `refreshInterests` for the village's living, plus the living of no settlement at the first village's step, as `nightCulture` does. Each household is ranked once a step (a memo by household), not once per pawn. The clearing of the dead's interests (`refreshInterests`, `society.js:97`, a walk of `st.pawns`, Rules for cost, 1) moves to the death.
  2. Its tie to its culture's centre: `updateTies` for one settlement, whose kin count reads the near kin of its people who live in the centre.
  3. Its entrepreneurs: its living adults, drawing from `st.rng.culture` in `st.living` order within the village.
  4. Its meeting (`Culture.meeting`) and its log lines, put at the head of `st.meetingLog` at its step.
  Roles and prestige are refreshed for the whole island at the first village's step (0.3 ms; every meeting reads them, and deaths refresh them anyway).
- [x] The meeting's occasion (VB-126) follows the village's own step: each village's people stand on its green for its meeting and are free after it. Today `callMeetings` (`src/sim/occasions.js:86-97`, at midnight of autumn day 10, `clock.js:53`) holds every village's meeting occasion from 10:00 to 12:30 (`OCCASIONS.meeting`, `occasions.js:18-21`, "until its four steps are done"), which a village whose step falls after 12:30 would miss. `tests/occasions.js` (the voices on the green at 11:00, each occasion ending at 12:30, nobody held at 13:00) follows.
- [x] At 18:00, each culture's year (`Culture.cultureYear`), its log lines, and "The meetings changed nothing this year." when no line of this year was written, which the step reads from `st.meetingLog[0].year`. Then `st.lastMeeting`. At 18:06, the land meeting, unchanged.
- [x] Saves: `tests/spread.js` checks that each village's step, the cultures' step and the land meeting each run exactly once, with uneven advances. A run saved between villages 0 and 1, in the middle of the window, and between the last village and 18:00 goes on as the unbroken run. `tests/saves.js` passes. No progress is saved.
- [x] The draws reorder, so behaviour changes. Report before and after (targets with residuals): `npm run calibrate` (`natural 12 200`), the median first flip of each custom against the bands of `design.md` (Pace); customs over 20 populous small years at seeds 7 and 42 (`node tools/sim.js --report customs`): the customs in force, the mean support of the options in force, and the least of them. `tests/counters.json` re-recorded if it moves, with the changed counters listed. Note in the PR that the meeting log now lists the villages' lines newest village first.
- [ ] Cost (Rules for cost, 9; targets, residuals reported). The longest village step at `medium-ref` and `small-y40`, against 60 ms. Expected under 15 ms: the largest village holds 111 of 1,005 living at `medium-ref` and 69 of 636 at `small-y40`, and the island-wide stages took 33, 52 and 16 ms (`medium-ref`) and 45, 15 and 10 ms (`small-y40`), cold, one call each. The cultures' and the land meeting's steps are as VB-206 measured them (under 5 ms, and 6 to 14 ms). Walks: the village's living once a step, so the island's living once a year in all, against three walks of the living and one of every pawn ever today. **Unmet in part (VB-278):** the fixtures `medium-ref` and `small-y40` are not built in a task (they are night work, and none is cached on this machine), so the longest village step there is not measured; the nightly bench reads it ("Meetings, its call"). Measured instead, as a description (populous medium, seed 7, year 2's meetings, 860 living, 22 villages; the machine shared): every village step under 5 ms, the cultures' step under 5 ms, the land meeting 49 ms (before: the island stages 25, under 5 and 6 ms, the land meeting 49 ms). Walks and rankings, counted: heirRanking calls on the meetings' day 635 before, 180 after (tiny map seed 7: 87 to 20); `meetingWalk` 2 × living a year (the villages' living once, and the walk of the living for the households of none at the first village's step); no walk of `st.pawns`.
