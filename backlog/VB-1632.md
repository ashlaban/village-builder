# VB-1632: The cottage as a plan task: a played head builds the booth up to a cottage from the upgrade, drawing first on the timber held at home
Status: done (2026-11-24)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: backlog/VB-1627.md; backlog/VB-1631.md; backlog/VB-1625.md; src/sim/ladder.js (RUNGS, farmView); src/sim/cottageplan.js; src/sim/building.js (boothWork, fetchTimber, KINDS foundation, walls, roof); src/sim/planchains.js (CHAINS); src/sim/plannext.js (WAITS); src/sim/camp.js; src/sim/stores.js (timber)
Goal: A player who plans by hand turns the booth into a cottage by one task of the plan.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A plan task builds the next rung of the main building: `Sim.plan(st, 'add', {upgrade: houseTile, what: 'cottage'})` (a chain `upgrade` in `CHAINS`, its `what` a rung of `RUNGS` that follows the one standing; only `cottage` is built by this task, the others refused with the reason "not yet"). Refused with the reason when the farm is still a camp, the rung does not follow the one standing, or one is already planned or under way.
- [x] Carried through with no further act: in the task's turn the head (the played pawn) begins the cottage by an act of its own (the cottage plan made as `landAlone` and `settleFarm` make it, through a verb or the making registry: say which and why), and the plan feeds the foundation, the walls' timber, the walls and the roof to the pawn until the roof is on; then `f.booth` is cleared as today and the task leaves with `which: 'roof'`. `waits` gives its reasons (timber not in, sleeping hours, a stage's own conditions), and `Sim.agenda` lays its steps.
- [x] Timber: the walls take the timber the household holds at home first (`household.timber`, VB-1625; through `sim/stores` with a reason), and only what is then lacking is fetched by timber trips. Say whether this holds for every household or the played one, and report founders seed 7, small, 10 years before and after if it is every household.
- [x] Nothing begins a cottage for a played head with automatic behaviour off except this task; `Sim.farmView(st, f).next` says so for the page: an entry's `ok` and `why` are the task's own check (today the cottage reads `ok: true` after the booth though nothing can begin it), and gains `planned` (whether a task of the plan covers it).
- [x] Unplayed booth households and the solo start without `hand` build their cottage as today (`node tools/same.js --base REF --scenario founders,solo`, unless the timber rule above is for every household: then the report above).
- [x] Tests on a small constructed state (`tests/camp.js` or a new script with its line in `tests/suites.js`): from a raised booth to the roof on by the plan alone; the timber at home used first; the refusals; a save and a load in the middle go on the same. `npm run verify` passes; baselines do not rise.
