Acceptance criteria:

- [x] Bound repair steps, discovered records and inconsistency queue, including repeated notifications while paused.

Decision: extend the existing research engine outside the production manifest and saved state. Use lexicographic Lifelong
Planning A* with zero heuristic; the hex graph's six incoming/outgoing neighbours supply the dependency index. Each growth
call processes at most 64 queue entries by default; an indexed heap holds at most one inconsistency per discovered tile.
The lowest remaining inconsistency clips the certified landmark region while repair is incomplete. No game parameters change.

## Implementation

RepairTree uses lexicographic (preference, physical time) g/rhs labels with a zero-heuristic LPA* queue. Cheaper edges
offer candidates directly, even off the current parent path; worsened parents rescan six incoming edges. Equal-value
replacement parents are installed immediately. The indexed heap has one entry per discovered record, including while
hundreds of notifications arrive without repair. The six-neighbour world index supplies reverse dependencies.

Normal growth and repair share a 64-entry request budget (8 in the smaller trial). The smallest pending inconsistency
clips the valid landmark potential. Direct tree certificates require the fully certified inner region; arbitrary queries
retain exact A* and its physical-budget fallback. Closing the root or an unrepresentable opening at a full cap resets safely.
There is no new production module, coupling, saved state, game parameter or native work counter. Research phase counters
include repair pops, rhs scans/offers, queue updates, cap stops/resets and maximum processed entries per call.

## Measured results

48-day live Populous runs on Tiny/Small seeds 7/42, Large seed 7, a Small five-year save, one repeated Small 42 run and
Tiny 7/42 at 8/256 limits: 128733 cache misses, 128724 found routes, nine no-route cases. All four alternatives matched
native preference, physical time and reachability; no sampled geometry changed.

Default demand repair reduces tree work 60–79%, query-plus-tree work 18–27% versus rebuilding and 16–23% versus control.
Large: tree work 303347 → 82000, total work 1010271 → 739347, resets 1264 → 0; time 2489 → 2139 ms (14% better).
Large exit time 2367 → 1972 ms (17% better). However demand repair remains 1.31–1.71× control time: not enabled in production.
Keeping exploration costs memory: Large observed records 8122 → 17414 across the pool, plus lookahead/heap-index fields.
All real trials had zero repair resets; bounded trials hit their caps safely. Every measured growth batch respected 64/8 entries.

No production target or outcome moves in this task. No thresholds or counter baselines change. The intended plain-A* speed
goal remains missed; the before/after repair gains and remaining overhead are reported, not hidden by node counts.
Full explanation and raw phase timings: docs/village-tree-repair.md and docs/bench/village-tree-repair.json.
