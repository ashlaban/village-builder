The five: the first five households with a head (0 to 4); a head is given a task only while its authored hours (hours left of its plan's tasks) are under a working day, else the levelling has no load to level; the open tasks of daily kinds are left to the default author (its reserve).
- Measured (populous small seed 7, 2 years), the five households: default 5 missed (plow 2, sow 3); levelled by key 16 (mow 7, plow 5, 4 past their `by`), +220%; all households 27 against 35. Residual's cause: taken by key, the five heads' working day fills with digging, wood and the like before the mowing and ploughing (missed with 0 hands, never authored); the variant `--order deadline` (windowed tasks first) gives 9, none by a window, all 9 authored tasks past their `by` worked by the head alone (hay, wood, dig, fish).
