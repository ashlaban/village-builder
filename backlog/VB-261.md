For each: whether it stays a hard check (correctness: build, lint, golden, determinism, saves, invariants) or becomes a target reported with its residual; where residuals are recorded and shown over time; what a PR must say. Questions for Kim with proposed answers; then the tasks to build it, and AGENTS.md and roadmap section 5 brought in line.
