# VB-261: Walking costs more on a slope
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, World, climate and travel, the row Walking and terrain (the slope cost is Kim's, 10 Nov; the row is not yet rewritten)
Goal: Routes prefer flat ground and a pawn walks slower on a steep step: stepCost (src/sim/routes.js) adds SLOPE.perRise for each unit of elevation between the two tiles (w.he), at most SLOPE.max, the same up and down, so the way back stays the way there, reversed.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `SLOPE = { perRise: 6, max: 2 }` in `src/sim/rules.js`, with a comment that says what each number does; `perRise: 0` switches slopes off.
- [x] `stepCost` adds `slopeCost`: `perRise` times the elevation difference of the two tiles, at most `max`, the same up and down; water counts as sea level; a worn path does not flatten a slope. Routes and walking speed both follow, as they do for terrain.
- [x] `ROUTE.minStep` stays the search's lower bound (a slope only adds); `MAX_STEP` of `refreshRoutes` grows by `SLOPE.max`.
- [x] `tests/routes.js`: raised ground is walked round, the way back costs the same, a step costs the same up and down, level high ground costs the terrain alone, the cap holds, `perRise: 0` restores the straight way.
- [x] `tests/golden.json` re-recorded (every entry changes: routes and so walking times differ from the first day), and the pinned life hash of `tests/record.js`. `tests/counters.json` holds unchanged.
Notes:
- 10 Nov 2026, asked by Kim directly, with three decisions: symmetric (not dearer uphill), slope slows walking as well as steering routes, and the strong default (6 a unit of rise, cap 2) to be tuned with the balance.
- `design.md`'s row "Walking and terrain" (Agreed, 30 Oct) does not yet name slopes: a design pass rewrites it.
- Hills (2) and mountains (3) already stand for steep ground by biome; they are left as they are, so high ground is now dear twice over where it is also steep. To weigh when tuning.
- The rise per step depends on the map size (the same land in fewer tiles): median 0.064, 90th percentile 0.18, most 0.68 at 96 by 68 tiles; 0.038, 0.11 and 0.50 at 190 by 135. A typical step so costs about 0.2 to 0.4 more, a steep one about 1, the steepest 2.
