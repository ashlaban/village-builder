# VB-763: Workmanship in the page: one mark for a grade, and a good's table of kind by grade
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The metals and the stones: Workmanship; The mill and the loom: Skills); `docs/plans/VB-702.md`, section 13; `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (15, 17)
Goal: The player reads a piece's workmanship at a glance, by one mark that is the same wherever a graded good appears (principle 15).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] One mark for workmanship (principle 15), drawn by one function wherever a graded good appears (a store's row, a deal, a gift, a dowry, a pawn's dress, a farm's card, the tile panel): at the corner of the good's icon (the icon the good, its tint and label the kind: VB-733). Sound has no mark; shoddy a notch cut from the icon; good one pip, fine two, masterwork three. The grade's word is its label; the word in the page is workmanship, never grade alone (a house has a grade).
- [ ] Counts by grade: a store's row for a good and kind shows its count and one strip of five segments, the pieces by grade; the same strip wherever pieces by grade are shown.
- [ ] A good's table, opened from any icon of it: kind by grade, each cell with what it changes (how long it lasts, how well it works, its worth by custom), read from the registry's compiled rows (VB-761), with no number written in the page.
- [ ] A pawn's skill at a craft (VB-360's panel) shows the same strip as the odds of its next piece, labelled as that pawn's own expectation (principle 14).
- [ ] The played pawn can name the cell it gives, deals or takes into use, by the verbs VB-761 has (principle 16: no choice only the player has).
- [ ] `node tests/smoke.js` and the browser tests it touches; a screenshot under `docs/img/`.
