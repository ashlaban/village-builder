# VB-1560: Refactor: split sim/jobs by concern (the posted jobs, the weighing, the head's day)
Status: done (2026-11-23)
Priority: 1
Size: M
Design ref: AGENTS.md, Conventions (a module's length); docs/architecture/target-v2.md, section 2
Goal: sim/jobs holds the posted jobs alone; the weighing and the head's day are modules of their own
Acceptance criteria:
`sim/jobs` went to 702 formatted lines with VB-1551 (water first in the allotment, the vital kinds' fit). It holds four responsibilities; the split follows them, not the line count.

- [x] The posted jobs stay in `sim/jobs`: `post`, `sweep`, `refreshJobs`, `islandJob`, the hourly asking of the kinds' rules and `lookForWork`, which calls the others in order.
- [x] Weighing a job for a pawn moves to a module of its own (proposed `sim/fit`): `urgency`, `prioOf`, `plotPrio`, `standOf`, `joined`, `floored`, `tooYoung`, `score`, `takeBar`, `matchBar`, `appraise`, `bid`, `fit`, `explainIdle`, `idleLine`: read-only, drawing nothing, read by the matcher, the head's day and the Actions list (`sim/opportunities`).
- [x] The head's day moves to a module of its own (proposed `sim/day`): `allot`, `allotChanged`, `eveningCall`, `takeDay`, `dayHours`, `hoursLeft`, with the `@state plan.day` line; it reads the weighing and the posted jobs and writes only `plan.day`.
- [x] The duty round and the idle pawn's company (`dutyRound`, `idleAbout`, `assign`, `slot`, `mergeById`) go where their concern lives: with the days owed and the hands lent, or stay with `lookForWork`; the task says which and why.
- [x] No behaviour changes: `node tools/same.js --base REF` on the starting commit; `tests/lint.js` passes with `sim/jobs` under 700 lines and no module over 15 exports that was not; `docs/architecture/modules.md` and the `@sys` lines updated; `src/owners.json` names the owner of `plan.day`.

Noted by VB-1551, 23 November 2026.

Done by VB-1560, 23 November 2026 (`same` against 75fc4a0; `sim/jobs` 702 to 235 lines, 15 exports to 3; 29 imports to 16).
- Load order `sim/fit`, `sim/day`, `sim/idle`, `sim/jobs`: the board notes the holders whose tasks changed and calls `allotChanged(st, changed, c)` at the end of a review or a look, as before. The other way (the clock calling the allotment after `refreshJobs`) would make the layering strict but change what `refreshJobs` does for its 13 test callers: left, a close call.
- `jobsFor` (with `mergeById`) and `workCommand` are `sim/fit`'s: the jobs a pawn weighs and the one command that takes one, read by `sim/day` and `sim/opportunities`; `sim/day` could not read them from `sim/jobs`, which loads after it.
- `allotted` (whom the head allots today) is `sim/fit`'s too, since `standOf` reads it; `sim/day` imports it.
- `dutyRound` is `sim/day`'s: a pawn on duty takes of the creditor's day as `takeDay` takes of its own, by the same command. Not `sim/days` or `sim/hands`: it serves both, and both load before the weighing, which reads them (a cycle).
- `idleAbout` and `IDLE` are a module of their own, `sim/idle`: it reads nothing of the jobs or the weighing (company, pose, the legs), so neither `lookForWork` nor the day is its concern. `slot` went with `allot`, its one caller.
- Removed, a commit of its own: `assign`, called by nothing since the head's day (VB-466). Changed, a commit of its own: the morning's asks are an argument of `allot` (`asking`), not a variable of the module's closure.
- Left: `takeDay`'s `missed` repeats `allotted` but for the commanded pawn (VB-1566); `docs/architecture/ownership.md`'s tables of 18 November still name `sim/jobs`.
