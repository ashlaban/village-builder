# VB-1560: Refactor: split sim/jobs by concern (the posted jobs, the weighing, the head's day)
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Design ref: AGENTS.md, Conventions (a module's length); docs/architecture/target-v2.md, section 2
Goal: sim/jobs holds the posted jobs alone; the weighing and the head's day are modules of their own
Acceptance criteria:
`sim/jobs` went to 702 formatted lines with VB-1551 (water first in the allotment, the vital kinds' fit). It holds four responsibilities; the split follows them, not the line count.

- [ ] The posted jobs stay in `sim/jobs`: `post`, `sweep`, `refreshJobs`, `islandJob`, the hourly asking of the kinds' rules and `lookForWork`, which calls the others in order.
- [ ] Weighing a job for a pawn moves to a module of its own (proposed `sim/fit`): `urgency`, `prioOf`, `plotPrio`, `standOf`, `joined`, `floored`, `tooYoung`, `score`, `takeBar`, `matchBar`, `appraise`, `bid`, `fit`, `explainIdle`, `idleLine`: read-only, drawing nothing, read by the matcher, the head's day and the Actions list (`sim/opportunities`).
- [ ] The head's day moves to a module of its own (proposed `sim/day`): `allot`, `allotChanged`, `eveningCall`, `takeDay`, `dayHours`, `hoursLeft`, with the `@state plan.day` line; it reads the weighing and the posted jobs and writes only `plan.day`.
- [ ] The duty round and the idle pawn's company (`dutyRound`, `idleAbout`, `assign`, `slot`, `mergeById`) go where their concern lives: with the days owed and the hands lent, or stay with `lookForWork`; the task says which and why.
- [ ] No behaviour changes: `node tools/same.js --base REF` on the starting commit; `tests/lint.js` passes with `sim/jobs` under 700 lines and no module over 15 exports that was not; `docs/architecture/modules.md` and the `@sys` lines updated; `src/owners.json` names the owner of `plan.day`.

Noted by VB-1551, 23 November 2026.
