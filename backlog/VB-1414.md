Goal: Kim, 21 Nov 2026: a small boat and a cart are much smaller constructions than a building, and it makes no sense that a household cannot build a cart once a building is begun (a cart would help). A cart (12 to 30 hours), a boat (20) and a tar pit (12) are small constructions: begun and built beside any other project of the household, in a booth as in a house, with no limit on them but each one's own test. The large projects (a field, a meadow, a barn, a plot for an heir) keep today's limit of one until VB-1472.
