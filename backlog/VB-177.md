# VB-177: Refactor: a pawn's movement only through `sim/agents`
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: ownership
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (code structure). VB-172's map, sections 5.6 and 7.3; Kim's answer 1 of 3 November (`QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-172: the ownership map").
Goal: `sim/agents` writes 21 of the 26 sites of a pawn's movement; four other modules write past it. A talk stops an idle pawn by setting its path, animation and idle time directly (`relations.js:162`), past the `idle` verb; the dawn places pawns indoors by their door (`clock.js:37`); a death stops the pawn (`lifecycle.js:28`); and the verbs `goHome` and `idle` set fields themselves (`commands.js:40`, `45`). A pawn the player holds (VB-169) should change only by its commands and the rules every pawn has, through one module.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Operations in `sim/agents` for each: a pawn halted for a talk until a time, placed by its door, stilled at death; the verbs call agents' functions.
- [ ] `node tools/writes.js --field agent.path` (and `phase`, `anim`, `inside`, `idleUntil`, `x`, `y`) lists only `sim/agents`, apart from seeding and the save codec.
- [ ] Refactor rules of `BACKLOG.md`: behaviour unchanged, `tests/golden.json` unchanged, `npm run verify` passes.
