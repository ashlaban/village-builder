# VB-1101: Skip stops where the player has a choice: the round's eve only with one, and building news no stop
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player, commands and views; The player and what they see (Skip to the next event)
Goal: Every skip stop is something the player wants to see or must answer.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The eve of the moving round (`SKIP.round`, `skipStop` in `src/sim/notable.js`) stops a skip only when the played pawn, or a member it answers for as head, has a choice the round will read: a farm stands empty that it may claim, service it may take or decline, a match it may wish for, a move out the custom allows, an offer it may make. The note names the choice ("Backgården stands empty: you may claim it"), and the camera goes to it. With no choice, the skip runs on.
- [ ] A skip stays bounded: where no stop has come for a year, it stops at the eve with a note of the year in brief (births, deaths, the harvest against need, the household's food). Decide the bound and write it in the design's Skip row (Agreed, 9 Nov, so the agent's to change; a close call for the inbox).
- [ ] A farm's building news that asks nothing (a house raised onto stone, a barn finished) no longer stops a skip; a farm inherited, emptied, claimed or newly settled in the village still does.
- [ ] The skip's note and the toast of the same event are one notice, not two (the playtest saw each stop's text twice).
- [ ] Measured headless, a played pawn with its automatic behaviour on and every offer answered as unplayed, on founders small seed 7 over 80 years: the stops by reason before and after, in the notes (the playtest: 163 stops, 80 of them the round's eve, 8 a house on stone). `skipStop` still writes nothing to `st`: a skipped run is the run played (`tools/same.js`).

Notes / questions:
- 16 Nov 2026 (playtest, founders small seed 7, 80 years in the page, as Gunnar Olofsson, his widow and his son): the stops were births 24, deaths 17, custom changes 20, new farmsteads 7, offers 6, a house on stone 8, and the eve of the round 80. At those 80 the player had something to set only a handful of times; the stop taught the player to click on without looking, and so missed the stops that mattered.
- Whether a choice exists is what `sim/opportunities` already computes for the action bar's "At the moving round" (forPawn); read it, do not copy it.
- Runs one at a time with VB-1102 and VB-1103, which touch `src/sim/notable.js` too.
- 16 Nov 2026 (the parallel session "Gameplay review and feedback": founders small seed 7, 75 years headless, one played pawn; its evidence added at the user's request): 118 stops in 75 years: the round's eve 74 (both lives held a farm, so every wish was barred), farm 17 (mostly a house on stone or a new farmstead), custom 12 (mostly "Across the Nordic culture", swinging), family 12, offers 2, hunger 1. Its other skip findings are VB-1108 (events lost in the stopping step), VB-1109 (moves between homes), VB-1112 (a cause's end), VB-1113 (a lapsed wish), VB-1114 (help received) and VB-1115 (frost on the played farm): all add or mend stops, one at a time with this.
- 17 Nov 2026 (reviewed after the merge with main, 354 commits on from the playtests' base; the code facts checked again): stands: `skipStop` still stops at every eve (`why: 'round'`), and `SKIP.offer` now covers every offer (VB-690). With wishes now every pawn's (`src/sim/wishes.js`, VB-680) and offers made through the year (the asking day, VB-823), "has a choice the round will read" is: an offer it holds unanswered, or a rung of its wish that is open (VB-827 names the rungs).
