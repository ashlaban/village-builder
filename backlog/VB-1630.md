# VB-1630: A camp left on automatic behaviour places its farmstead and plans its cottage by its own rule
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: src/sim/camp.js; src/sim/scenarios.js (landAlone); backlog/VB-1627.md
Goal: A hand start switched to automatic behaviour still gets a roof.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] With automatic behaviour on (or a head not played), a household with a camp (`farmstead.camp === true`, sim/camp) and no booth staked places its farmstead by its own rule: its head issues `placeFarmstead` on the landing tile (or the best tile within `LANDING.soilSteps` by `placeWhy`) at its first working hour, and the booth is raised as a staked booth is.
- [ ] Once the booth stands (`camp === false`) with automatic behaviour on, the cottage plan is made as `landAlone` made it before VB-1627, so a run left on automatic is not roofed by a booth forever.
- [ ] The same for a played head whose player has turned automatic behaviour on: from a camp it places its farmstead and, the booth raised, begins its cottage (through `beginCottage`, sim/upgrade), as an unplayed head would.
- [ ] The landing's fence: when the house moves off the landing tile (`placeFarmstead`, sim/camp `moveHouse`), whatever of the farm was laid out around the landing tile at seeding (its fence, yard, any tile kept for it) moves with the house or is not made until the farmstead is placed; nothing of the farm is left standing around the tile given back. Say what was found there.
- [ ] `Sim.buildMenu(st)`'s entry gives the whole work as the plan reckons it: `hours` the raising alone as today, and `work` the hours with the timber trips (the agenda's estimate), so the menu and the plan's estimate agree.
- [ ] While a booth is staked and its timber is in, the farmstead task's step does not report `unposted` between the timber's arrival and the raising job's posting (found in the page: "nothing is open to work" shown briefly): say what the gap is and close it, or name it with a `HOLDS` rule if it is a real wait.
- [ ] A test on the tiny world of tests/camp.js: hand start, automatic on, the booth raised and the cottage begun within a season.
