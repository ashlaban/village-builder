# VB-1728: Land rights: titles follow their parties' ends
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Land, ecology and rights, Land and rights (Tenancy; Foreclosure)
Touches: sim/heirs (extinct); sim/ownership (farmToVillage); new tests/titles.js
Goal: A title whose owner or user household ends would leave a dead party holding land; the operations of VB-311 exist to pass or end it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A household that dissolves, leaves at the moving round or passes at inheritance ends or moves the titles it holds (sim/ownership `end`, `transfer`), so `checkState`'s rule (a title's owner and user are live parties) holds once VB-316, VB-317 or VB-034 make titles in play.
- [ ] `farmToVillage` keeps a titled holding's user (a tenant goes on working a let field of a farm that stands empty).
- [ ] A test on a constructed village (`tests/titles.js`): the owner's household dissolves; the title follows its estate or ends.
