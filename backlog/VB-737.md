# VB-737: Prospecting as work: a head who wants ore sends a pawn to the hills
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-736
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The metals and the stones: goods by purpose, kinds by property; Goods, recipes and facilities); `docs/plans/VB-702.md`; `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (17)
Goal: A village that needs ore can look for it, by a pawn's act, not only stumble on it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A verb `prospect` in `sim/commands.js` (any pawn; the played pawn too): a day's journey to an upland tile within reach, 8 hours of work there, carrying its meal; the find is by `sim/prospect`'s rule (hours / 60 within 2 tiles of a vein), and the knowledge is the village's as today.
- [ ] A head chooses it when its target for an ore or a metal (VB-698) is above what it holds and its village knows no vein of that ore; at most `PROSPECT.days` (Proposed 3) a household a year, in the slack seasons.
- [ ] Where it looks is the head's estimate (principle "The future is someone's estimate"): upland tiles nearest its house not yet worked near, never the simulation's knowledge of where the vein lies.
- [ ] Numbers, populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15, 30 years: finds a decade by village with and without the verb; days spent prospecting; the share of veins within 30 steps of a village that are found by year 30.
- [ ] The numbers that move are reported before and after at the seeds and maps named (Working rules); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
