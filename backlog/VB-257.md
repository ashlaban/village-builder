# VB-257: The meeting sets the village's routines, and a practice for when it does not
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Work: opportunities, plans and tasks (design.md, section 7); docs/review-work.md, section 2 (The village)
Goal: The village's only work today is the fence's stretches and plots, and the meeting sets nothing about work. The design has the meeting set the village's routines afresh each year from the village work available, and a practice say what follows when it does not decide.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The village's standing plan (VB-258) holds this year's routines, at most four, set by the yearly meeting as a decision of the meeting (a command of the meeting, as its other decisions) from the village work available that year. The fence's stretches are the first routine, and the only one until VB-058 (the green and well), VB-100 (a common field) and VB-120 (work days) each add theirs: this task builds the list, the choice and the failure.
- [ ] A routine chosen is a source of tasks for the village's adults on the `villager` basis; a routine not chosen posts nothing that year.
- [ ] A meeting that does not decide (no quorum, or a tie): a practice of the culture says what follows, declared with the customs before the first seed: `nothing` (the default: no village work that year) or `asLastYear`. Each culture of the generated worlds holds `nothing`; a test forces each.
- [ ] The consequence is visible: an unmended fence lets the cattle into the crops as it does today, and the meeting's failure is a notable event of the village, named in its log.
- [ ] With every meeting deciding and the fence chosen, the run is as today; report how often a meeting fails at populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42, and the crops lost in those years. Re-record the golden file if it changes and list the entries.
- [ ] Invariants (at most four routines; each of a known kind). `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals.
Cost: one decision per village a year.
