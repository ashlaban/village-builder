Implementation notes (VB-894):
- `src/page/plan.js`: `planSites(what, hh)` reads once at the tool's start the tiles within the rule's reach (a bfs of 4 steps for a field, 6 for a meadow, the rules' own REACH) that `fieldWhy` or `meadowWhy` lets through, and gives them to the tool's `sites`, which `drawToolMarks` (page/actbar) already marks. `drawPlanMarks` (called from page/animate beside it) draws the tiles the plan names in gold while the farm's Plan tab is open, read from `st.plans.households` (no copy per frame). The named tile is told by a "Show on map" link (`data-plan="fly"`, flyTo) with the tile's number as a small note, for anyone's tab; the head's controls are as before.
- Decision: reach constants are copied into the page (`PLAN_REACH`) since the modules do not export REACH; a follow-up could export them. The page reads `VB['sim/fields']`, `sim/meadows`, `sim/hex`, `sim/routes` for these pure checks, as the tests do, rather than widening the `Sim` facade.
- Validation: `tests/browser.js` checks the marked tiles are all ones the rule takes, includes the one it then clicks and names, and finds the link. Screenshot `docs/img/vb894-marks.png`.
