# VB-276: Marriage terms: a dowry and a morning gift, booked in customary worth
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Marriage as a negotiation: Terms, The player's doors; Leaving home: A start in life). `docs/roadmap.md`, section 3.4 (goods, worth). Slice 4's plan (VB-183) confirms or rewrites the numbers below.
Goal: In the first version (VB-293), a marriage's only terms are the means to marry and a start in life. Slice 4 brings goods and customary worth, and with them the two terms Kim accepted on 9 November. The dowry (*hemföljd*) comes from the bride's side and is counted against her share of the inheritance. The morning gift (*morgongåva*) comes from the groom's side and is hers if she is widowed. As head, the player also proposes a match for a member, with its terms.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The dowry: at the close of an offer, the bride's spokesman's household gives the couple's home livestock and stores, by default half her share of the household's movable worth as the inheritance custom would divide it that day, in customary worth. It is recorded on the bride as an advance and subtracted from her share when that household's estate is next divided. A household that cannot pay it in full gives what it has above its seed; that does not bar the match.
- [ ] The morning gift: from the groom's household, by default the lesser of one cow's worth (40 rations, the worth table of slice 4) and a tenth of its movable worth. It is recorded as hers, and if she is widowed it goes with her before any division.
- [ ] The offer (VB-293) carries its terms, `{dowry, gift}`, in customary worth. Unplayed spokesmen propose the defaults and answer yes to any terms within half to twice them. The player as spokesman sets the terms its side gives when proposing, and answers the other side's terms with the offer.
- [ ] As head, the player may propose a match for a member at the round: a wish as VB-274's, for that member, with terms. The other side answers it as an offer.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, at `medium-ref` and `small-y40` after 4 years, and populous small at seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 for 15 years. Report dowries and gifts a year and the worth moved; new households' hungry days in their first 3 years; widows' hungry days; and whether households that married off several daughters are worse off. The golden file is re-recorded, with the changed entries listed.
- [ ] Cost: per match (13 a year at `medium-ref`), one valuation of two households' goods. Nothing between rounds.
