## Implementation notes
- `feedHousehold` (`src/sim/hunger.js`): after the larder and the grain above the reservation, a day that still lacks takes `min(lack, grain - spare)` of the grain below
  the reservation through `Stores.take(..., 'seedMeal')` (a new reason in `KINDS` and `ROUTINE` of `sim/stores`, so a daily meal folds into the yearly total and
  writes no ledger entry). If even that cannot cover the day, the household still eats all it has and is hungry as before. A day that is covered never touches
  the seed, and a day with grain above the reservation is as it was. The order: larder, meals, spare grain, rest of the larder, seed.
- The fields left unsown are told by `eatSeed`: event kind `seedEaten` (`atFarm`; `HISTORY` in `sim/records`, kept a generation), once a season at most per
  household. The one new field is `household.seedSeason` (the season index last told, -1 at the start; an older save has none and tells at once; `@state` in
  `sim/scenarios`). The count told is the fields the grain now left cannot sow.
- What else reads the reservation is unchanged (`reserved()`, `floorOf`, the allowance's slack, help, claims, the means to marry). A household that ate its seed
  holds less than its reservation, so kin help and the deals treat it as short of seed exactly as they treated a household that held less than the reservation
  before: its floor is above its grain, so it gives nothing, and what it asks and receives is as today. Nothing was added to treat it better.
- The band: `PROBES.hungryHouseholdShare` in `tests/lib/bands.js` (household-years after year 3, present 12 days or more, hungry on over 0.3 of their days; target
  at most 0.05) replaces the worst household in `hungry-households`; `worst-household` is a new `watched` band (0..1, never out) that keeps the worst share in the report.
  The reduced run (`tests/bands.js`) follows, and reads 0 on the four tiny seeds.
- Decisions made by the orchestrator, Kim's delegate for this run, for Kim to overturn: only eating reaches below the reservation, only on a day that would
  fall short, and only for what that day lacks; no tuning of yields, reserves or needs. The new measure and its 0.05 target are Kim's change of a target
  (10 Nov, QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-270 question 2), not made here.
- Replay and cohort: `docs/bench/hunger-causes.js` as before; new `docs/bench/seed-eaten.js` (shares, deaths on a day the stores covered the need or the seed was
  eaten, fields in the ground after the spring's sowing, and the cohort). Its cohort is, before, households hungry on a day their stores covered the need
  (reserve-blocked) and, after, households that ate seed in the year; they are not the same households, so read the cohort as what became of a household with
  such a day. Tests: `tests/hunger.js` (the seed test now asserts eating one ration of seed, the one told event, and hunger when nothing is left), `tests/record.js`
  (heirs 7's lives: 75 to 77 events and a new hash, behaviour changed).
