# VB-1109: Fix: the played pawn's move between homes stops the skip
Status: done (2026-11-18)
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player and what they see (Skip to the next event)
Goal: When the played pawn or its household gains or loses a member or a farm, the skip stops there.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A skip stops (`stopsFor`, `src/sim/notable.js`) when an event moves the played pawn or a member of its household between homes, or brings one in: a child going to kin who hold its farm in trust, a ward coming back of age and the trust ending, a child or a servant joining the household, the household taking another farm in trust or giving one up. The kinds are named in one set beside `FARM`, with why each stops.
- [x] A check on a small constructed state for each kind: the event stops the skip when it concerns the played household, and not when it concerns another.
- [ ] Measured with VB-1101's headless count: the stops by reason before and after.
