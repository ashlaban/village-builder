# VB-442: Rebuild only the farms that changed, not every farm, fence and pawn
Status: done (2026-11-22)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: none (the page's drawing; src/page/settlement.js, buildFarms; src/page/animate.js, updateSettlement)
Goal: `buildFarms` destroys and redraws the whole settlement layer (fields, fences, houses, herds, every pawn's rig) whenever `st.visualDirty` is set or `st.rigDirty` holds a pawn. The simulation asks for that on about 85 steps a game year on populous tiny (255 of 34,560 steps in 3 years, seed 18): a field broken (71 sets), a meadow finished (49), a building raised (27), a plot's or cottage's step (27 each), scrub cleared (25), a birth (16), a death (10). At the uncapped speed that is several whole rebuilds a second of wall time, each some hundreds of sprites and about 80 Graphics, for a change to one farm or one pawn. Rebuild what the change touched: the farm, the fence or the rig.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A change to one farm (a field broken, a meadow, a building, a plot) redraws that farm's sprites and leaves the others; a birth, a death or a wedding remakes the rigs it names (`st.rigDirty` already names them) and leaves the others.
- [x] How the simulation says which farm changed is decided in the task: the page reads `st` and writes only its flags (AGENTS.md, Conventions), so a set of dirty farms beside `rigDirty`, cleared by the page, is the existing pattern (`rigDirty`, `treeDirty`, `landDirty`); a whole rebuild stays for a load and a new game.
- [x] Measured before and after on the quick start at the uncapped speed in Chromium: sprites and Graphics made a game year, and game years reached in 100 seconds.
- [x] The page's checks pass (`tests/smoke.js`, `tests/browser.js`), with VB-441's check of the renderer's Graphics contexts.
