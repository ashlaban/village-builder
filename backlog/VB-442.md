Median segments were one step. Segment shortcuts saved zero settled vertices, added relaxations, and junction handles
increased numeric storage. Keep flat routes. The saved study, raw results and maintained probe are now in this branch.
See docs/path-segment-sharing-study.md and docs/bench/path-segment-sharing.json. VB-443 measures the next alternative.

Validation: all replay objectives and physical tie costs matched native routing. No production behavior changed by this task.
