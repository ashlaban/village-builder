Goal: One record and one module carry every claim on a household's days, and a levier's power shows in the terms, not in a rule
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `sim/days`' work (the days called, the day's duty, the basis `owed`) moves into `sim/levies` (a move commit, then the change); the obligation of kind `days` gains its terms (`names`, `notice`, `substitute`, `commute`, `carry`, `onBreach`), its window, its named days and the days worked; `debt`, `keep` (sim/keep) and `patron` (sim/followers) get the terms design.md gives (the levied names, in the season the levier asks); `routine` and `due` are accepted (`routine` laid by VB-1768; `due` tested on a constructed levy, since no estate exists yet).
- [ ] The levied head names its days within the window at the notice's days ahead, by its own rule (the day whose displaced work is least, by its estimate) or through the verb `name`; a day not named in time is named by its default author. The verb `levy` (a levier's agent lays a levy with its terms) replaces `callDays`.
- [ ] On a named day the one sent works the levier's tasks on the basis `owed`; each hour is booked `owed` (VB-1761) and comes out of its household's levy on it; a met, lapsed or breached levy leaves the live index and is pruned with its events (Rules for cost 1 and 9).
- [ ] An outer levy's days come off the household's labour budget (VB-1778) in its window; `Sim.levies(st, body)` gives the levies a body lays and bears, with their terms, through the actor view.
- [ ] An estate's levy comes on top (design.md, Outer levies): a check on a constructed state that a `due` laid on a village adds to its households' routine lots rather than replacing them.
- [ ] `st.daysCalls` and `st.daysToday` go, or become the levies' compiled index (derived, `DERIVED` in sim/saves); `FORMAT` raised (sim/saves): a save of an older build is refused, as `docs/architecture/modules.md` (Saves) says; no migration is written.  the cases of `tests/days.js` move to `tests/levies.js`.
- [ ] Q10: the windowed hours a household's days owed displace when it names them three days ahead, against the calendar's days (populous small seed 7, 3 years), against 70% fewer: reported.
- [ ] Cost: a few hundred open records and about 200 households × 2 to 4 lots a year, through sim/obligations' due-queue (under 0.5 ms a game day).
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
