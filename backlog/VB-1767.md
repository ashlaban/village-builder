# VB-1767: Outer levies as obligation records with terms: sim/days goes into sim/levies, and the levied names its day
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-1763, VB-1766
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: plans
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md section 9, Outer levies (One record, Terms, Who names the day, Worked); docs/plans/four-scales/README.md, 3.3 (How levies relate to sim/obligations; the OuterLevy type), 3.10 step 7, section 4 Q10
Touches: sim/days; sim/levies; sim/obligations (create, KINDS, setCalled, due, fulfil, lapse); sim/keep; sim/followers; sim/eligibility; tests/days.js; tests/levies.js; tests/obligations.js
Goal: One record and one module carry every claim on a household's days, and a levier's power shows in the terms, not in a rule
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `sim/days`' work (the days called, the day's duty, the basis `owed`) moves into `sim/levies` (a move commit, then the change); the obligation of kind `days` gains its terms (`names`, `notice`, `substitute`, `commute`, `carry`, `onBreach`), its window, its named days and the days worked; `debt`, `keep` (sim/keep) and `patron` (sim/followers) get the terms design.md gives (the levied names, in the season the levier asks); `routine` and `due` are accepted (`routine` laid by VB-1768; `due` tested on a constructed levy, since no estate exists yet).
- [ ] The levied head names its days within the window at the notice's days ahead, by its own rule (the day whose displaced work is least, by its estimate) or through the verb `name`; a day not named in time is named by its default author. The verb `levy` (a levier's agent lays a levy with its terms) replaces `callDays`.
- [ ] On a named day the one sent works the levier's tasks on the basis `owed`; each hour is booked `owed` (VB-1761) and comes out of its household's levy on it; a met, lapsed or breached levy leaves the live index and is pruned with its events (Rules for cost 1 and 9).
- [ ] `st.daysCalls` and `st.daysToday` go, or become the levies' compiled index (derived, `DERIVED` in sim/saves), with a save migration; the cases of `tests/days.js` move to `tests/levies.js`.
- [ ] Q10: the windowed hours a household's days owed displace when it names them three days ahead, against the calendar's days (populous small seed 7, 3 years), against 70% fewer: reported.
- [ ] Cost: a few hundred open records and about 200 households × 2 to 4 lots a year, through sim/obligations' due-queue (under 0.5 ms a game day).
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10): `npm run verify` green, a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose (never skipped or quarantined) and named in the PR; no stuck pawn (no task held 48 hours without progress, as VB-1546's script counts); the stocks' accounts close (`node tools/sim.js --report accounts`: `unexplained` no larger than before).
- [ ] No village dies out: `node tools/sim.js --scenario founders|populous --map small --seed 7|11|42 --years 10`, all six runs: no settlement extinct by hunger and no collapse of its people (the living at year 10 against year 0, in the PR).
- [ ] Reported in the PR, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in those six runs, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed), before and after.
