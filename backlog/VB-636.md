# VB-636: Settling families come with the ship
Status: blocked

Size: M

Goal: There is no immigration before the ship, and nobody appears who has not travelled (Kim, 13 Nov). With the ship, families of another culture settle: they shorten a founders' island's road to 1,000 and reopen pools that closed. Written by VB-405's plan for slice 8's plan to confirm.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Who comes: a family of another culture, a couple of 20 to 35 with up to 3 children, unrelated to anyone on the island (their parents known only from the patronymic, as the founders' are), with customs drawn from the world beyond that differ from the island's on at least 3 dimensions.
- [ ] How it comes, with nothing teleported: as passengers of a ship that calls (slice 8's voyage). It lands at a town's harbour (the harbour waits for VB-1924, which designs how a town grows before the ship) with what it brought in the ship, 42 days of rations a head and seed for a field, and walks, as a journey with its loads carried or carted, to the nearest village within a day's walk of the harbour that has a plot site or an empty farm and whose customs let an outsider claim it. Where there is no such village the family does not land. An invariant (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): every pawn added after the seeding has a mother on record or a voyage it arrived by.
- [ ] How often (Proposed, from the review's mock; VB-188 sets it): `SHIP.settlers`, the chance that a calling ship carries a family, such that one family lands in 2 years while fewer than 300 live on the island (`SHIP.settleBelow`) and one in 10 years after. In the mock a family every 2 years brought an island of ten to about 1,000 at year 150, and for the first 60 years only to about 850.
- [ ] The family settles as a claimant does: the farm or plot is claimed, mended or built by the round's and the claims' own acts; it is a household of the village, with a vote, and its customs pull on the village's as any household's do.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, founders small, the band's sixteen seeds, from the year the ship first calls: the year 1,000 is reached (measured or by arithmetic, as VB-633's band prints it) with and without the families; the seeds standing still; marriages between the families' children and the islanders'; the villages' customs that moved; the line "those who arrive, less those who leave" of the growth budget (`design.md`, Balance loops and targets, The growth budget: 0.13 of a point at 1,000) as measured. Whether households are better off: the hungry days of the villages that took a family in against the others, as a description.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state: a family lands and walks; no village to take it, no landing; the invariant refuses a pawn with no mother and no voyage.
