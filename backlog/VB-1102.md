# VB-1102: History folds routine events: a year's help, frost and lambing as one line each
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player, commands and views; The player and what they see (The chronicle)
Goal: A History tab reads as a life: what mattered stands out, and the routine is counted.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The History tabs of a person, a farm and a village (`Sim.chronicle`, `src/sim/notable.js`; `src/page/history.js`) fold the routine events of one kind within one year into one line with a count and the places: "Helped at Ängsgården, Södergården and Nygården: 23 days", "Frost took 3 crops", "47 lambs born; 5 ewes lost over the winter". The routine kinds are one named list, written in the notes with why each is routine.
- [ ] A folded line opens, in place, to the events it holds; the filters (Births and deaths, Marriages, Land and farms, Customs and meetings) work as now.
- [ ] The record is unchanged: the fold is the view's. A check on a small constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): a pawn with 10 help events and a birth in one year shows two lines, the birth first by time.
- [ ] Measured on the playtest's case (founders small seed 7, Jon Gunnarsson, year 35): the lines of that year before and after, in the notes (about 40 before, nearly all "Helped at").
- [ ] Cost: the view folds only the years it shows; nothing kept in `st`.
