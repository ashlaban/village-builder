Goal: A History tab reads as a life: what mattered stands out, and the routine is counted.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The History tabs of a person, a farm and a village (`Sim.chronicle`, `src/sim/notable.js`; `src/page/history.js`) fold the routine events of one kind within one year into one line with a count and the places: "Helped at Ängsgården, Södergården and Nygården: 23 days", "Frost took 3 crops", "47 lambs born; 5 ewes lost over the winter". The routine kinds are one named list, written in the notes with why each is routine.
- [x] A folded line opens, in place, to the events it holds; the filters (Births and deaths, Marriages, Land and farms, Customs and meetings) work as now.
- [x] The record is unchanged: the fold is the view's. A check on a small constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): a pawn with 10 help events and a birth in one year shows two lines, the birth first by time.
- [ ] Measured on the playtest's case (founders small seed 7, Jon Gunnarsson, year 35): the lines of that year before and after, in the notes (about 40 before, nearly all "Helped at").
- [x] Cost: the view folds only the years it shows; nothing kept in `st`.

Done (VB-1102), decisions:
- Routine list `ROUTINE` in `src/sim/notable.js`: helped (a day among neighbours), frost, herdBirths, herdDeath (each takes one crop or beast, repeats through the year), harvest (once a farm a year). Births, deaths, marriages, land, customs never fold. `received` grain lines are not a record kind found here (VB-1114 owns them).
- The fold is `foldRows`, reached by `Sim.chronicle(st, q, group, limit)`; without `limit` rows are raw (existing callers unchanged). It folds from the newest until `limit` lines, so only the years shown; the rest is `rows.rest`. Nothing in `st`. Page: a fold is a `<details>`.
- The birth-vs-fold test reads "birth first by time" as chronological; newest first the fold comes first.
- Measure NOT matching the ~40: my rerun (founders small seed 7, 36 years, Jon Gunnarsson id 13, year index 35) had 7 lines before, 2 after (the playtest's save was a different run: the page's 80 years). Left unticked.
