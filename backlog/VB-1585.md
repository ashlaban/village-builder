# VB-1585: The thirsty pawn: a pawn's own thirst, and the verb by which it leaves its work to drink and fetch
Status: done (2026-11-25)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Water by the site); backlog/VB-1543.md; backlog/VB-1542.md
Goal: no pawn goes without water for want of a place on a list
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `p.drank`: the hour a pawn last drank; thirst is read from it in closed form and nothing ticks (design.md, Goods and the farming year, Water by the site, The thirsty pawn). Owner, `@state`, invariant, and a save without it loads as having drunk.
- [x] A pawn drinks at home at the meal when the day's water is in, at a source when it draws there, and by the verb `drink`, registered with `sim/commands` (`check` names why not: no source in reach), which any pawn has and the played pawn may be given.
- [x] At `THIRST.thirsty` (24 hours) the pawn's panel says so. At `THIRST.leave` (36 hours) the pawn leaves whatever it does, a task that rank never interrupts as well (mowing, reaping), walks to the nearest source it may draw at and drinks; where its household's water is not in, it takes the fetch from there. Children under 7 and the bedridden do not go.
- [x] The dry day's health is the pawn's own: who ended the day without a drink loses `HEALTH.dry`, who drank loses none; the uncooked meal stays the household's. Marsh water drunk costs `HEALTH.marsh`.
- [x] A pawn working away at a site drinks and fetches at the site's source. Water on a journey stays Open and is noted where the design names it. (Unticked, VB-1585: the drink at the site's source is built and checked; the fetch at a site is not: a booth has no fetch of its own and its water still comes with its roof, so there is nothing for the pawn to take from there. VB-1601.)
- [x] Checks on a small constructed state: a pawn mowing with its household's water never fetched leaves at 36 hours, drinks and brings the water home; a pawn with no source in reach is refused with the reason; a log line names the event, the pawn and the place.
- [x] Measured once, before and after (solo small seeds 7 and 42; founders small seed 7; 10 years): dry household-days, pawns that left work to drink and what they left, health lost to thirst, deaths.
- [x] Cost, named before building: a number a pawn; the test where the day's end already visits the living (1,000 x once a day x a comparison); a due entry only for a pawn that ended a day without a drink (tens a day at most), each one route to the farm's source; no pass of its own.
