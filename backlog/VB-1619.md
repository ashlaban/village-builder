# VB-1619: The agenda lays the player's plan into days: routine, tasks and sleep by the hour, each task's end and verdict
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/ui2/HANDOFF.md; src/sim/agenda.js; src/sim/sleep.js
Goal: The player sees what the plan makes of today and the next days before choosing it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `Sim.agenda(st)` gains `days`: today and the next two days, each a list of segments `{from, to (game hours), what: 'sleep' | 'routine' | 'task' | 'queue' | 'free', kind, key, site}` in order, laid by the rule the feeder follows (VB-1618: hand-queued commands first, the sleeping hours, the routine once a day, the tasks in order, a task broken off at the sleeping hour and taken up in the morning), each piece of work at the pawn's own estimate (`mid`), walks as the view reckons them now. With no sleeping hours in the plan, work is laid through the night.
- [x] `plan`: each task of the plan with `hours {lo, mid, hi}`, `starts`, `ends`, `deadline`, `verdict` and `then`, as `queue` entries have them, from where the layout puts it; and `owed`: the hours of sleep owed at each day's end by the layout (VB-1615's rule), so that a plan with no sleep shows its cost before it is paid.
- [x] The routine's hours: each routine kind's estimated hours a day (`routine: [{kind, hours}]`), so the page can say what the necessities take of a day (a far brook against a well).
- [x] The layout and the feeder agree: a test runs a plan of a routine, sleeping hours and two tasks for two days and compares what the pawn did hour by hour with what `days` said at the start (the kind of each hour; the estimate's error allowed at the joins).
- [x] The view still writes nothing and is read by no simulation module; its cost is bounded by the plan's length and three days, with no loop over the island; `tests/agenda.js` extended.
