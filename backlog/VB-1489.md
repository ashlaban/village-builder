Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: backlog/VB-1484.md; src/sim/chores.js:104 (the water job's window); design.md, Goods and the farming year (Water)
Goal: the end of the work day does not cost a household its water
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The general rule, stated in design.md and in the code once: a task of the kinds a household lives by, once begun, is carried to its end though the work day ends meanwhile. The kinds are a declared list on the job kinds' records (proposed: water, cooking, the wood of a cold house; the task settles the list and gives its reason for each), not a test for water.
- [ ] Water may be fetched after dark: the water job's window runs past the work day's end (src/sim/chores.js, `available` and `window`), to an hour the task proposes, and a pawn takes it then when the household's water is not in.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state: water claimed at 17:30 is delivered; water not in at 19:00 is fetched that evening.
- [ ] Sleep and rest are not shortened unseen: report the hours of rest lost, before and after, on solo small seed 42 and populous small seed 7, with dry days and hungry days.
