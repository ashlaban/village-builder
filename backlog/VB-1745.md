# VB-1745: A controller per pawn: the automatic switch and the queue are the pawn's, not the player's
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-1743, VB-1744
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: plans
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md section 15, A controller for any pawn and Direct control; docs/plans/four-scales/README.md, 3.6 (auto, queue) and 3.10 step 2; docs/review-jobs-2026-11-25/README.md, section 6 (What an LLM agent could play requires, points 1 and 7)
Touches: new sim/controller; sim/player (inhabit, uninhabit, queue, unqueue, setAuto, playerStep, playerDied, goOnAs); sim/records (commanded, unplanned); sim/saves; page/play; page/actbar; tests/player.js; tests/commands.js; new tests/controller.js
Goal: Any number of pawns may have their own logic off and be driven through verbs at once, and a pawn driven by nobody behaves as before
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A controller record per pawn, owned by a new module `sim/controller` (`@sys`, `@state`, `src/owners.json`, manifest before sim/player): its automatic switch, its queue of at most `QUEUE_MAX` commands and the queue's bookkeeping (`refused`, the begun head) as `st.player` holds them today; sparse (only a pawn with a controller holds one), plain data, saved, and ended at a death as `playerDied` ends the queue today.
- [ ] `unplanned(st, id)` and `commanded(st, id)` (sim/records) keep their names and callers and read the pawn's controller, so no rule reads `st.player` to ask whether a pawn plans for itself.
- [ ] Verbs `auto` (on or off) and `queue` / `unqueue`, registered for any pawn for itself; `Sim.setAuto` and `Sim.queue` issue them for the played pawn, so the page is unchanged; the queue step runs every controller's queue (from an index of the pawns with a non-empty queue, kept by the owner), not only the played pawn's.
- [ ] `st.player` keeps the frame and the played line's record (`pawn`, `left`, `ended`, `last`, `days`) and, until VB-1746, the player's plan. `FORMAT` raised (sim/saves): a save of an older build is refused, as `docs/architecture/modules.md` (Saves) says; no migration is written.
- [ ] The path's acceptance test (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, section 4): in `tests/controller.js`, on a tiny constructed state, an empty controller (automatic on, nothing queued) on every pawn runs to the same state as with none, but for the controller records; two pawns with automatic off at once are both left alone by the planner. Once, for the PR: the same on populous small seed 7, 2 years.
- [ ] Cost: a controller is read where `st.player` is read today (one lookup); no new pass.
- [ ] `npm run verify` and `node tests/smoke.js` pass; `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` for unplayed runs.
