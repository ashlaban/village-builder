# VB-237: Refactor: stores refuses a holder that is not a household, a village or a pawn
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: none (code structure). `AGENTS.md`, Conventions (Food moves through `src/sim/stores.js` operations).
Goal: `type(holder)` (`src/sim/stores.js:18`) names a holder by the fields it has: `farmstead` makes a household, `center` a village, and everything else a pawn. `put` on an agent or a farmstead object is therefore accepted and writes a `stake` field onto it. The module that guards food movements should refuse what it does not know.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `type(holder)` returns `'pawn'` only for a pawn, by a field every pawn has and no other holder does (`'stake' in holder`, or the like, with pawns given `stake: 0` where they are made if they are not already), and throws `stores: not a holder` for anything else. Households and villages are told as today.
- [x] `tests/stores.js` holds that `put`, `take` and `move` throw for an agent record, a farmstead and `{}`, and leave them unchanged.
- [x] `put`, `take` and `set` work the holder's kind out once a call, not three times (pass it on).
- [x] Refactor rules of `BACKLOG.md`: behaviour unchanged, `tests/golden.json` and `tests/counters.json` unchanged, `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals.
