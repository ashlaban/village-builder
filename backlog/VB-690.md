# VB-690: Offer and answer: one verb pair for every crossing between households
Status: done (2026-11-16)
Priority: 1
Size: M
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (16, the hard rule); design.md, Exchange, goodwill and obligations (The gift)
Goal: One `offer` verb and one `answer` verb, a table of bases, and the settling that follows a yes (the right at once, the goods set aside where they lie, a fetch or a carry posted); the marriage offer and the gift go through it with behaviour unchanged.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] An offer is plain data {id, from, to (pawns), basis, gives, wants, due} in a due-queue in `st` (rule 4), with its owner in `src/owners.json`, its invariants and its place in the save; a lapsed or answered offer leaves the queue (rule 1).
- [x] A table of bases, each a row: what may be offered and wanted, how the pawn asked weighs it (sim/exchange's floor and allowance for goods; the match's rules for marriage), and what a yes books (goodwill, an obligation, nothing). A want with nothing given is an ask; a gift wants nothing.
- [x] `offer` and `answer` are verbs any pawn issues; an unplayed pawn answers once, when the offer falls due, by its basis's rule, which is that pawn's own judgement; the played pawn is asked and answers as it likes, with its pawn's judgement shown as advice (the player's exemption from floor and allowance is this, restated: Kim, 16 Nov). Only yes or no; a counter-offer is the player's alone, as a new offer the other way.
- [x] A refusal is a memory of both pawns, from which opinion follows; no other penalty.
- [ ] The ask travels at once, as news (the hard rule's exemption; Kim, 16 Nov, who may yet change it: the offer would then gain the place it must be made at, and nothing else). Goods never do: a yes sets them aside and posts the journey (VB-461's custody, used here and not built beside it). Not yet: VB-461 has not landed on the run's branch, so a yes delivers
  through the existing paths (a gift with a carrier, and every ask, is carried by `carryGift`, the carrier taking the grain at the start and
  walking it there; a gift with no carrier is settled as before, which `same` requires). Moving the settle onto VB-461's custody is VB-861.
- [x] The marriage offer (`st.offers`, sim/lifecycle) and `give` (sim/commands, sim/exchange) are moved onto it; `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` with no played pawn. tests/commands.js covers both verbs.
- [x] Cost (rule 9): offers open at once are few (asks a season, a gift a household-year, the round's matches); one evaluation an offer at its due time; a declared counter.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim, the session on the household boundary): the shape and its seven examples (a hungry head's ask of a brother, the good-year gift, help from the store through the alderman, a loan, a swap, hands at harvest, a marriage) are Kim's and the reviewer's; VB-460, VB-312, VB-302, VB-470 and VB-276 each add a basis to this, not a verb. After VB-461 where the two touch the same lines of sim/help; one at a time with VB-228 and VB-681 (sim/commands).

## Implementation notes (VB-690, 16 November 2026)
- New module `sim/offers` (social layer, loaded before sim/exchange): owns `st.offers` and `st.offerSeq` (moved from sim/lifecycle in
  `src/owners.json`). An offer is `{id, from, to, basis, gives, wants, made, due, answers[]}` plus its basis's fields (`parties` of goods,
  a match's `village`), kept in due order; one answered at once is never kept and takes no id (so with no player the queue stays empty,
  as before). `make` draws up the answers, the unplayed answer at once when it is due now, the played pawn's window extends `due` by the
  basis's wait; `dueOffers` (the clock's day-end entry, now owned by sim/offers) answers what is still open by each pawn's judgement.
- The table of bases is `Offers.BASES`, each row contributed by the module whose rules weigh it (`Offers.basis(name, row)`, at load):
  `gift` and `ask` in sim/exchange (floor and allowance through `assessGift`; a yes books goodwill through `receive`), `match` in
  sim/lifecycle (the round's rules; `unplayedNo`; a yes marries, and past the round brings the handover). A row: books, wait, check,
  answers, weigh, settle, and optionally refused, lapsed, opened.
- Verbs (sim/commands): `offer` (any living pawn; `given` answer keys only from the round, for the played pawn's wish), `answer` (any
  living pawn with an answer still to give; the player-only check is gone), `give` (the gift's shorthand, kept for sim/help and the
  page: it makes a gift offer), and `deliver` (source 'offer' only: what a yes settles; wraps `settleGift` and `carryGift`, so
  tests/commands.js's static check still holds).
- The round makes each match with `offer` from the man's spokesman to the woman's (source 'round'); the at-once yes marries through
  `marry` as before; the handover of a match answered later follows as before.
- Refusals: of goods, both remember (`refusedUs` -0.1 for the one refused, `refusedThem` -0.05 for the one who refused; new
  SOCIAL.memories) and an `offerRefused` event is told; a match keeps its existing rule (the spokesman remembers `refusedMatch`), which
  `same` requires.
- Page: the played pawn's sheet shows any basis (`A gift is offered`, `You are asked`, `A match is offered`) with the pawn's own answer as
  advice ("Anders Björnsson would answer yes", principle 14); the action bar's row is generic. Screenshot: `docs/img/vb690-offer.jpg`.
- Saves: an offer saved in the old shape (`man`, `woman`, `home`) is read as the basis `match`.
- New tunable: `HELP.answerDays` 1 (the played pawn's window for goods). New counter: `offerWeighed`.

## Decisions (Deciding without Kim)
- Help's gifts (source 'help') carry the receiver's yes: help answers a hungry household's own ask (sim/help), so the receiver said yes
  in asking. Without it a played pawn's household would wait a day for its food help, and tests/wishes.js (no wishes change nothing)
  showed it. VB-691 turns help into a head's ask proper.
- The match's offer is made by the man's spokesman to the woman's, and the spokesman's own answer is still asked (the round proposed it,
  not he): behaviour as before; VB-682's plan replaces the round's choosing.
- A gift to the played pawn is asked (window 1 day, `HELP.answerDays`); unanswered, its pawn's judgement answers (a gift is taken). A
  match left unanswered is now answered by the pawn's judgement too (before: yes); the same unless the pawn is at enmity.
- Gifts from a household with no holder and from the village store keep their actors (`{household}`, `{village}`): VB-691 and VB-673
  give them a pawn (the head, the alderman).
