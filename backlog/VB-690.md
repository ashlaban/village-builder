# VB-690: Offer and answer: one verb pair for every crossing between households
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (16, the hard rule); design.md, Exchange, goodwill and obligations (The gift)
Goal: One `offer` verb and one `answer` verb, a table of bases, and the settling that follows a yes (the right at once, the goods set aside where they lie, a fetch or a carry posted); the marriage offer and the gift go through it with behaviour unchanged.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] An offer is plain data {id, from, to (pawns), basis, gives, wants, due} in a due-queue in `st` (rule 4), with its owner in `src/owners.json`, its invariants and its place in the save; a lapsed or answered offer leaves the queue (rule 1).
- [ ] A table of bases, each a row: what may be offered and wanted, how the pawn asked weighs it (sim/exchange's floor and allowance for goods; the match's rules for marriage), and what a yes books (goodwill, an obligation, nothing). A want with nothing given is an ask; a gift wants nothing.
- [ ] `offer` and `answer` are verbs any pawn issues; an unplayed pawn answers once, when the offer falls due, by its basis's rule, which is that pawn's own judgement; the played pawn is asked and answers as it likes, with its pawn's judgement shown as advice (the player's exemption from floor and allowance is this, restated: Kim, 16 Nov). Only yes or no; a counter-offer is the player's alone, as a new offer the other way.
- [ ] A refusal is a memory of both pawns, from which opinion follows; no other penalty.
- [ ] The ask travels at once, as news (the hard rule's exemption; Kim, 16 Nov, who may yet change it: the offer would then gain the place it must be made at, and nothing else). Goods never do: a yes sets them aside and posts the journey (VB-461's custody, used here and not built beside it).
- [ ] The marriage offer (`st.offers`, sim/lifecycle) and `give` (sim/commands, sim/exchange) are moved onto it; `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` with no played pawn. tests/commands.js covers both verbs.
- [ ] Cost (rule 9): offers open at once are few (asks a season, a gift a household-year, the round's matches); one evaluation an offer at its due time; a declared counter.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim, the session on the household boundary): the shape and its seven examples (a hungry head's ask of a brother, the good-year gift, help from the store through the alderman, a loan, a swap, hands at harvest, a marriage) are Kim's and the reviewer's; VB-460, VB-312, VB-302, VB-470 and VB-276 each add a basis to this, not a verb. After VB-461 where the two touch the same lines of sim/help; one at a time with VB-228 and VB-681 (sim/commands).
