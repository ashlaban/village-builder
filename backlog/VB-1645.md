# VB-1645: Sleep for every pawn: hours owed, the verb for a pawn's own logic, and the cost
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: src/sim/sleep.js; src/sim/player.js (sleepStep, the verb sleep); docs/cost.md
Goal: A lost night costs any pawn as it costs the player's: the player's pawn is no exception (principle 15)
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Every pawn owes sleep by SLEEP (sim/sleep): the hours owed moved from `st.player.sleep` to the pawn (or where target-v2 section 4 places it), the played pawn's carried over; reckoned by transitions (going in, going out, the sleep verb's start and end), not by a pass over the living each step, or with a declared work counter if a pass is kept (docs/cost.md 1.3).
- [x] The verb `sleep` open to a pawn's own logic (sources beyond 'player'), and a pawn's own rule that sleeps one that owes a night (one that worked late as a lent hand, a traveller) when it may.
- [x] Mood `tired` and the health term read for every pawn; before and after on founders and populous (tools/balance.js, tools/sim.js --report accounts): the households' health and the gifts (the gift's allowance reads mood) reported, and a late save's perf.
- [x] tests/sleep.js extended to an unplayed pawn.

Notes:
- `pawn.sleep` {owed, asleep, at, until}, owned by the new sim/sleepers (agency layer, before sim/player); an old save's st.player.sleep is adopted.
- Reckoned by an hourly pass over the living (counter sleepReckon, 24 a pawn-day, O(1) each) and each step for the played pawn (its page
  read is unchanged); transitions alone would miss the many ways a pawn goes home. A close call, noted.
- A rest on the road (a journey's wait leg) counts as asleep, else every journey night would owe.
- Own rule, in working hours, once owed >= SLEEP.health.over and the pawn is free: `sleep` for ceil(owed - mood.over) hours, source 'pawn'
  (sleepOwn: 823 in 3 years, populous 7); not the played pawn with automatic off.
- After (populous small 7, 3y, at midnight): 1.3% of pawn-days owe a whole night (wives 3.5%, maids 4.4%, farmers 2.7%, infants 0); mean
  health 99.0. Accounts 15y populous 7/42: growth 1.9/1.5% -> 2.8/2.5%, gifts 2256/2288 -> 2468/2216, hungry30 7 -> 8: within noise.
- Late save (populous 7, year 15, one year): sleepers is not its own row of --report perf (it sits in the rest of Sim.advance).
