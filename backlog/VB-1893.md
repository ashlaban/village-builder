# VB-1893: A call does not set a load down: a hand sent home with a load in hand brings it in first
Status: needs-review
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: plans
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md section 7, Plans at four scales (A load in hand); Finishing what was begun (25 Nov, Kim); docs/plans/four-scales/README.md, 3.2 (The rule for a load in hand)
Touches: sim/agents (release, goHome); sim/day; tests/methods.js
Goal: A load in hand reaches its owner's store unless its carrier cannot walk on or its caller chose to set it down, having been shown the cost
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A pawn sent home (the verb `goHome`, by any source but the pawn's own inability to walk on) with a load in hand (`a.load > 0`, a task's hand) hauls it to its owner's store first, by the rule for a load in hand (`sim/methods` RULE), and then goes home; it is set down as a pile only when the command says so (`args.setDown`), or the pawn cannot walk on (death, illness, its household gone: `fellIll`'s goHome and `release`'s other callers keep today's behaviour).
- [ ] The head's call (`dayCall`, `call` after VB-1779) of a member with a load in hand: its check says, before it is issued, that the member brings the load in first, and with `setDown` what setting it down costs (the pile where it stands, and the walk out and back to fetch it, by the head's estimate); run, it keeps the load unless `setDown`.
- [ ] A check on a constructed state (`tests/methods.js` or `tests/day.js`): a hand hauling a load home is called off by its head: the load reaches the store and the hand is then off; with `setDown` the load lies as a titled pile where it stood; a queued `goHome` of a controller's pawn behaves the same.
- [ ] Set-downs counted (the event or the pile's reason) before and after on populous small seed 7, 2 years: those from a call fall to zero without `setDown`; reported.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10): `npm run verify` green, and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774); hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs reported.
