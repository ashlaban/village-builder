# VB-1893: A call does not set a load down: a hand sent home with a load in hand brings it in first
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: plans
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md section 7, Plans at four scales (A load in hand); Finishing what was begun (25 Nov, Kim); docs/plans/four-scales/README.md, 3.2 (The rule for a load in hand)
Touches: sim/agents (release, goHome); sim/day; tests/methods.js
Goal: A load in hand reaches its owner's store unless its carrier cannot walk on or its caller chose to set it down, having been shown the cost
Kim (27 November 2026): there are two kinds of call. An **urgent** call: drop everything and come now (the load is set down where the
member stands, the cost shown first). A **non-urgent** call: arrive within the hour or so (the load is brought in first). The non-urgent
call is the default; `setDown` below is the urgent call. Who may make an urgent call: only the head's call and the pawn's own
controller (the rules' summons, such as occasions, pastimes and drink, are never urgent). A member on a non-urgent call is off from the
delivery, the walk home with the load booked as the task's work; on an urgent call, off from the call.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A call has a kind, `urgent` or not (default not; the command's `args.urgent`, which replaces the `setDown` of the first draft). A pawn sent home (the verb `goHome`, by any source but the pawn's own inability to walk on) with a load in hand (`a.load > 0`, a task's hand) on a non-urgent call hauls it to its owner's store first, by the rule for a load in hand (`sim/methods` RULE), and then goes home; an urgent call sets it down as a pile where it stands and the pawn comes at once; the pawn that cannot walk on (death, illness, its household gone: `fellIll`'s goHome and `release`'s other callers) keeps today's behaviour.
- [ ] Only the head's call (`dayCall`, `call` after VB-1779) and the pawn's own controller may make a call urgent; the rules' summons (`sim/occasions`, pastimes, drink) are never urgent and wait for the load to be brought in, as the day's end and a lent hand's hours already do.
- [ ] The head's call of a member with a load in hand: its check says, before it is issued, that on a non-urgent call the member brings the load in first and about when it arrives, and on an urgent call what setting the load down costs (the pile where it stands, and the walk out and back to fetch it, by the head's estimate).
- [ ] The called hand is off from the delivery on a non-urgent call (the walk home with the load booked as the task's work, VB-1761's six parts) and from the call on an urgent one.
- [ ] A check on a constructed state (`tests/methods.js` or `tests/day.js`): a hand hauling a load home is called off by its head: non-urgent, the load reaches the store and the hand is then off; urgent, the load lies as a titled pile where it stood and the hand comes at once; a queued `goHome` of a controller's pawn behaves the same.
- [ ] Set-downs counted (the event or the pile's reason) before and after on populous small seed 7, 2 years: those from a call fall to zero but for urgent calls; reported.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10): `npm run verify` green, and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774); hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs reported.
