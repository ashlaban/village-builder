# VB-099: A chronicle: the history of a pawn, farm and village
Status: done (2026-11-09)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Systems overview (Chronicle: a typed record of events, missing; Fun: a growing cast and a memory; finding 6: one record from which the log, the panels, notifications and the tests all read). Proposed.
Goal: VB-075 keeps every event as a typed record but leaves the page unchanged. A chronicle shows that record where the player looks: what happened to this farm, this family, this village, across generations. Depends on VB-075; if it is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A "History" tab in the pawn, household and village panels lists their events from `Sim.recordOf`, newest first, with year, season and day, and a filter by kind (births and deaths, marriages, land and farms, customs).
- [x] A farm's history follows the farm across households: every holder, handover, empty year and claim since the start.
- [x] Names in the history are links that select the pawn, farm or village.
- [x] The village log in the left panel becomes a view of the record (its last 40 village events); `st.events` and the 40-line text log are no longer written, and VB-075's test still passes.
- [x] Building a farm's history after 150 founders years takes under 20 ms (reported).
- [x] The smoke test opens each tab with no console errors.

Two things found on the way: founding a household records a `moved` event for each member beside the log's own event of the same move (a settler's `left`, a claimed farm's `moved`), so a history will show some moves twice unless one is dropped; and a `died` event does not say whether the pawn held land (VB-098 reads it from the kin index, which forgets past holders on load).
- 9 Nov 2026, implementation. Impact: `sim/notable` gains two read-only views of `st.record`, `chronicle(st, {pawn | farm | village}, group)` and `villageLog(st, n)`, with `CHRONICLE` (the four filters), all on `Sim`; no state of their own, and no step calls them. `sim/records`' `log` writes the record only; `st.events` is gone from new states and dropped from older saves on load. A new page script, `src/page/history.js`, draws the tab and turns the names in a line into links; `page/words` calls it when a card is filled and uses it for the village log. No new coupling between systems: the page reads through `Sim` only.
- Choices made where the criteria were silent:
  - The household's panel is the farm's card (a household has no card of its own), so its History is the farm's, which follows the farm across households.
  - "Every holder since the start": seeding records a `holds` event for each household's holder, since no earlier event named the first holder. A save made before this has none, and its farms' histories begin at their first handover.
  - The duplicate move VB-098 found: a member's coming to a household is recorded as `joined`, not `moved` (the pawn's own life event keeps `moved`). A pawn's history drops a `joined` beside another event of the same hour at the same farm; a farm's, a village's and the log leave out `joined` and the other personal kinds (`friend`, `fellOut`, `cause`, `player`, `share`, `gift`), whose sentences have no subject.
  - An empty spell is told as a note on the `empty` event ("Empty for 3 years", or "Empty since, 1 year" while it lasts), counted to the next event that settles the farm, not as a line a year.
  - The tab chosen stays as the selection changes, so a name followed in one history opens the next. A dead pawn's name opens the family tree, as every link to the dead does.
  - The tab lists the newest 200 lines and says how many older ones a filter by kind would reach.
  - The left panel's Recent events now lists the log's last 40 lines, where it showed 6 of them.
  - The filters: births and deaths (`born`, `died`); marriages (`married`, `putOff`); land and farms (`holds`, `inherited`, `empty`, `claim`, `moved`, `joined`, `cottage`, `left`, `stayed`, `settled`, `plot`, `plotStarted`, `field`, `meadow`, `stake`, `border`, `service`, `guardian`); customs (`custom`, `split`, `cause`). Harvests, hay, herds and gifts show under All only.
- Cost: a history is one pass over the record, when its tab is open and the record has grown since it was last drawn. Founders seed 7 after 150 years (2,807 events; the settlement had died out): a farm's history 0.21 ms at worst. On the late saves: `small-y40` (104,874 events) 3.3 ms a farm, 3.4 ms a village, 2.2 ms a pawn; `medium-ref` (70,125 events) 2.1, 2.4 and 1.0 ms; the village log under 0.1 ms. The criterion is 20 ms.
