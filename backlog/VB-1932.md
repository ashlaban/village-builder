# VB-1932: Fell and Cut firewood are always available; the head plans the winter's wood
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md section 7 (The task as a plan; The player's plan); Kim, UI thread 27 Nov 2026: "The task to cut trees should always be available. The task to cut timber to firewood should always be available on non-reserved timber. It is the responsibility of the head to make sure the household has enough resources to make it through the winter; they can use available actions to schedule their goals"; playtests of 27 Nov (/mnt/project-files/ui-plan/playtest-window-acts-2026-11-27.md: Cut vanished all summer and winter)
Touches: sim/chores (firewood, pileOf, timberOf); sim/planview (fireView); page/ui2wood (fireAct, woodActs); page/ui2hud (storeLeft); tests/firewood.js; tests/handplan.js; design.md
Goal: An action is never withheld because of a goal: Fell may be planned on any tile whose trees the household may fell, and Cut firewood whenever timber not reserved by a project is at hand, whatever the woodpile holds against the season's goal; keeping enough wood for the winter is the head's planning (the player's, or an unplayed head's own rule using these same actions)
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Sim.plan(st, id, 'add', {firewood: house}) is accepted whenever unreserved timber is home, also when the pile holds the season's goal (no "the woodpile holds the season's goal" refusal), and refused only with no unreserved timber, saying so (tests/firewood.js)
- [ ] A player's cut task cuts the unreserved timber home and ends when none is left; it no longer ends with 'full' at the season's goal (tests/firewood.js)
- [ ] Timber reserved by a project (a building's held-back timber, sim/chores fuelFree) is never cut by it (test)
- [ ] Sim.plan add {fell: tile} is accepted on any tile sim/territory woodWhy allows, in every season and whatever the pile holds (test)
- [ ] The house callout and the Stores firewood row show Cut whenever unreserved timber is home and no cut task is planned, and a fellable tile always shows Fell (page check in docs/ui2/playtest.js or a jsdom test)
- [ ] An unplayed head still decides when to fell and cut by its own rule (sim/chores ask/hold), now through these actions: tests/firewood.js and tests/handplan.js pass for head-planned households
- [ ] design.md section 7, The player's plan, cites the principle "Acts are on offer; goals are the planner's" (Principles for decisions, Decided 27 Nov) where it says what the firewood and fell tasks refuse
