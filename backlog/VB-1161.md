## Notes

Kim, 18 November, on two rounds of mock-ups (a session's scratch files, not committed): the interface is drawn in another style than the
map and pawns (the map is "a church mural": flat pigment in a dark contour, on mottled plaster); of three variations of each piece Kim
chose the B set throughout: stencil icons (one pigment, the parts held apart by gaps), the vault band (lime plaster between two sawtooth
borders) with its head in black letter (Grenze Gotisch), ringed medallions for buttons, counted beads for the meter, the medallion with
its name on a scroll for a person's row. A trait's small meter is five beads (Kim: the same meter everywhere, principle 15). The theme is
chosen in Options, from the main menu, and kept in localStorage; Glass stays the default until Mural covers every screen (Kim).

The theme's grammar, which the tasks that follow keep to (principle 15):
- A panel is the band; a head is black letter in red ochre.
- A medallion is a thing pressed; a scroll is a name or a word; the two together are a link or a labelled button.
- Pressed, chosen and primary are one look: filled with red ochre.
- A level is counted beads, filled as far as the level, the last in part; a mark is a wedge over them.
- Tones: green earth (good, and a plain level), yellow ochre (short), dark red (bad).

## Implementation

- `src/page/options.js` (new, in `src/page.json` after `saves.js`): the theme, read from localStorage at load and set as `data-theme` on
  the root (Glass sets none), the Options screen and its radio group (click, arrows, Esc).
- `src/body.html`: the menu's Options entry; `#optionsScreen`, with the saves overlay's class so it sits and scrolls as Saves does.
- `src/head.html`: Grenze Gotisch added to the fonts; the Options styles; the mural theme, as rules under `:root[data-theme="mural"]` at
  the stylesheet's end. The generic button rule is under `:where()`, so it only replaces the base `button` rule and every more specific
  button (the link-like ones that reset border and background) is left as it was. The band is drawn as background layers, not pseudo
  elements, so it needs nothing of a panel's position or overflow.
- `src/page/meter.js`: the meter's span carries `--lo` and `--hi`, where its fill starts and ends; the beads are a full-width fill
  masked to ten rounds and clipped to that span, so the signed meter and the part-filled bead need no other markup.

Decisions (Deciding without Kim, 12: tooling and presentation within what Kim chose):
- Options takes the menu card's place rather than opening beside it as the mock-up drew it: the same behaviour as Saves, and it fits a phone.
- Black letter is used for the heads of panels and screens (the clock, a card's name, a sheet's or screen's title), not for running text.
  Kim was asked whether a pawn's name at the card's size reads well and has not answered: a close call, surfaced in the PR.
- A plain level's beads are green earth, not the accent: the accent is red ochre in this theme, and red is kept for pressed and for a warning.
