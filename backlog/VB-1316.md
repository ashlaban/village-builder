# VB-1316: The retired's voice: advice to the head, and a grandchild's match
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Inheritance (The retired); docs/plans/VB-1122.md, sections 3 and 4
Goal: A retired pawn, played or not, has a say in its household's year and its grandchildren's matches.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The basis `advice` of `offer` (VB-690): a retired member offers the head one of the head's verbs (`target`, `rotation`, `routines`, `sowOutOfTurn`) with its args, once a year at the head's spring planning day; check: the actor lives in the head's household and the verb's check holds for the head. The head answers yes when its opinion of the adviser × (the adviser's standing / the village's mean) reaches `ADVICE.heed`, and on a yes issues the verb itself; a no books nothing.
- [x] An unplayed retired advises where the head's plan departs from the template's by more than `ADVICE.gap` on a store, by its own estimate (principle 14).
- [x] A grandchild's match: under "Who makes a match" by the heads (with or without consent), a retired grandparent in the house wishes a match for a grandchild of age, carried to the head as advice and weighed the same way; under "the couple", no say.
- [ ] (moved to VB-1484, so that the simulation's side lands first) The page: the played retired pawn's sheet lists its decisions of the plan's section 4 (advise the plan, a grandchild's match, the meeting, the keep), and a year with none skips to the next thing asked of it.
- [x] Tests on a constructed state: advice taken and refused by opinion; a grandparent's match wish carried to the head. Founders small 30 years seed 7: advice offered and taken a year, reported.

Noted by VB-1122's plan, 19 November 2026 (`docs/plans/VB-1122.md`).

Decisions (23 Nov 2026):
- New module sim/advice (after sim/asking: a match advised is asked by askMatch); `routines` is the village's verb, so the head's `order` stands for it.
- The unplayed retired advises hay alone, the custom's (unbiased template) as its estimate: a chosen target lasts until cleared, and fields follow the household's food, so a fixed number would go stale (close call).
- ADVICE.gap 0.25 never fired (a head's bias is at most 0.3 × (1 − skill)); 0.1. ADVICE.heed 0.3 and 0.25: none taken (a head's opinion of a retired parent about 0.29, standing ratio about 0.8); 0.2. Runs: heirs small seeds 7 (6 y) and 42 (10 y).
- The grandchild's partner: of those who seek in its village, the one the grandparent thinks best of (ADVICE.fancy 0.1); none arose in the runs (a retired grandparent with an unmarried grandchild of age in the house is rare).
- Founders small seed 7 is down to 3 living by year 30 with or without this change: no advice there; reported at heirs and populous instead.
