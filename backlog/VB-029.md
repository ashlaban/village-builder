# VB-029: Marriage limit by degree, opening up when spouses are scarce
Status: done (2026-10-31, f26576d)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: courting
Phase: implement
Design ref: People, households and families (Kinship: Decided limit by degree); Customs, How attitudes move (Scarcity: Decided, strength Open). Kim, 2026-10-29: judge the marriage limit by degree; the kinship coefficient is only shown; fewer marriages should make people more open to closer kin marrying; siblings never marry; degree is counted the civil way (both lines added), with a fourth, loosest option allowing uncle–niece; plentiful allowed spouses pull the limit slowly back towards stricter.
Goal: `kinOk` compares Malécot's coefficient with 1/64, 1/16 or 1/8. In a closed population from 10 founders the average coefficient between two people tends towards about 1/20, so the stricter options bar most pairs within a few generations, although they are distant kin by descent. Judge the limit by degree instead, and let a shortage of allowed spouses push the custom towards closer kin.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A helper `kinDegree(st, a, b)` (civil counting): for each common ancestor, add the generations from each person up to it; the pair's degree is the smallest such sum. Siblings and half-siblings 2, uncle–niece and aunt–nephew 3, first cousins 4, first cousins once removed 5, second cousins 6, third cousins 8; no common ancestor on record: no limit. A person never marries a direct ancestor or descendant.
- [x] `kinOk` uses the degree: "Third cousins or further" (`second`) allows degree 8 or more, "Second cousins or further" (`first`) 6 or more, "First cousins or further" (`close`) 4 or more, and a new loosest option, "Uncle–niece or further" (`uncle`), 3 or more. Degree 2 or less is never allowed, whatever the custom. Existing option keys and labels in `culture.js` are unchanged; the new option is listed last and starts with no support.
- [x] Under the default (`first`), first cousins once removed (degree 5) are now barred; today's coefficient test allows them (1/32 < 1/16). The PR reports how many heirs-scenario marriages this changes.
- [x] `kinship` (the coefficient) is still computed and shown for spouses and in the family tree, and is no longer used for the limit.
- [x] Scarcity: at each moving round, an adult who would marry (single, men 20+, women 18+, not retired, with a home under the residence custom) but finds no allowed partner, while one would be allowed under the next looser option, adds self-interest pressure on `kinLimit` towards that option, through the existing self-interest pull (`refreshInterests`), with the same weight as other self-interest pulls. The custom itself changes only at the yearly meeting, as for other customs.
- [x] Plenty: each adult who marries at the moving round, to a spouse also allowed under the next stricter option, adds pressure on `kinLimit` towards that option at half the weight of the scarcity pressure, so the limit can swing back, slowly.
- [x] The moving round counts adults blocked only by kin, per settlement; the meeting log shows the count.
- [x] Tests: degrees on a constructed pedigree (siblings, half-siblings, first cousins, uncle–niece, first cousins once removed, second and third cousins); siblings, half-siblings and direct lines never marry under any option, including `uncle`; with many blocked adults in one settlement, attitudes move towards the looser option within a few years, and back towards the stricter option more slowly when every marriage is between distant kin (seeded).
- [x] Re-record the golden file (marriages in the heirs scenario may change) and list the changed entries in the PR.
