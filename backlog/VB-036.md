Goal: Courting, beside the round's matchmaking and never in its place: two unmarried pawns who have come to like each other court, and their wish is offered at the moving round before the matcher chooses, as the player's wish for a match is. A head may refuse; the couple then keeps courting, remembers it, and may leave for service. - 18 Nov 2026 (implemented). What was built, where it parts from the criteria as written, and what was decided (Deciding without Kim):
  - The module: `src/sim/courting.js` (layer social, after `sim/matching`), owning `pawn.courting`, `pawn.courtingSince`,
    `pawn.courtRefused` ({by, t}: the head who refused the pair, read once by the next round) and the index `st.courtships` (the man of
    each couple; a cache with one writer, saved, seeded for a save from before). `COURT` is `minAge` 16, `opinion` 0.4, `tie` 1.5,
    `end` 0.2, `years` 3 and `leaveBelow` 0 (the threshold of the refusal's criterion, named: principle 3). Not a stored quantity: no
    line in `tools/stocks.js`. Counters `courtCheck` and `courtWalk`; record kinds `courting`, `courtingEnded` (its `reason`: cooled,
    years, married, died) and `leftRefused`, in the Marriages filter of a history.
  - Starting: `sim/relations`' `talk` emits `goodTalk` (the two and their opinions as its band check read them); courting makes one
    check (`courtCheck`, 0.36 a pawn-day in the counters' 30 days) and, if the gate allows, the two court, a life event of both. No
    pass over pairs.
  - Ending: `touch` emits `courtTouched` only for a pair that courts (one compare a touch); the round's first step reads the couples
    (`review`). The years do not end a courtship while the pair's offer is open (decided: they are about to be answered).
  - The pair's own offer is `sim/asking`'s `askCourted`: at the event `courting` and on either's asking day, due at the round, the
    basis `match` marked `courted` by `sim/marriage` from who courts whom (not from what the offer says). The two's yes is their own
    answer at the round, by their judgement (they like each other), not given in the making: so the played pawn's answer waits for
    the player like any. The means of the home and the two's hunger are read where it settles; unmet, it lapses and they wait.
  - One who courts is in no arranged match: the round's matcher passes it by, `askMatch` neither asks for it nor asks it, `matchBar`
    names it ("is courting another"), and to a match arranged before the courtship began it says no, unless it holds the spokesman
    who made it dearer than the one it courts (design.md, Courting, The pair's own offer). What it remembers of yielding
    (`marriedAgainst`) is left to VB-693.
  - A refusal: `SOCIAL.memories.refusedCourting` (-0.2 to -0.4, 3 years), not `refusedUs`: that name was taken by `sim/offers` for a
    refusal of goods, at other values (-0.1 to -0.3, 2 years). At the next round `leavers` gives those who hold that head under
    `COURT.leaveBelow`, and `serveWhereShort` lets each ask for a place whatever the hands at home; it still needs to be fit to serve
    and a head within reach who is short. A no of one of the two is not a head's refusal.
  - The invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): symmetric; both living, unmarried, of opposite sex, 16 or more; never kin of degree 2
    or less; the index holds each courting man once. The custom's kin limit is read where a courtship starts and where the offer is
    made, and is not an invariant: a village may tighten it under a couple, who then wait.
