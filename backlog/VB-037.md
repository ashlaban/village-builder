# VB-037: Founders start with a looser marriage limit
Status: done (2026-10-30, d178764)

Design ref: Customs and culture (Closest kin who may marry); Starting scenarios (Founders). Kim, 2026-10-30: the founders scenario's default marriage permissions are more permissive: "First cousins or further" (`close`).
Goal: With 10 unrelated founders, the third generation are mostly cousins, so the Old Nordic default ("Second cousins or further") bars most marriages before scarcity can loosen the hardest custom to change. Start the founders scenario at `close`.

- [x] The heirs and populous scenarios are unchanged: their golden entries do not change.
