# VB-022: Daily meals that always happen (unblocks VB-009)
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: food
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs, Long work and processing (Milling at home). Not checked tonight: the design snapshot could not be read in the nightly run.
Goal: Make the daily `cook` job reliable, so that moving food from grain to rations (VB-009) does not create hungry days. In the founders runs the cook job is missed on the first days of spring (`cookDay` stays -1 for days 0-4), because everyone is busy with sowing work.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Households hold `rations` (`@state` documented): food cooked and ready to eat. Daily food is eaten from rations at midnight; a household that has grain but no rations eats grain (above the seed reserve, VB-049, if it is done) at 1:1, so no household goes hungry because a cook was missed.
- [ ] Starting rations: every household starts with 2 days of rations for its members, in every scenario.
- [ ] The cook makes the next day's food: the `cook` job turns grain (above the seed reserve) into rations at 1:1, enough for the next day's need of the household's members, and is posted from 14:00 while the household has less than that.
- [ ] Cook may interrupt: from 14:00, if the household has less than the next day's rations, its member with the highest `cook` priority who is at work stops their job (its progress is kept, as at the end of the day) and goes home to cook.
- [ ] Founders runs over 3 years at seeds 7, 42, 3 and 11 have no more hungry days than before this task; report the numbers.
- [ ] A test: a household whose cook was busy all day still eats; with no grain and no rations it is short.
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
