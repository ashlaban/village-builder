# VB-695: Kin care is offered and answered: the ward, the household taken in, the farm in trust; a guardian acts as head
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: acts
Phase: implement
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (16); backlog/VB-682.md; backlog/VB-690.md
Goal: The kin's head offers, or the household in need asks (basis `care`, due at once); a refusal of kin in need is remembered; and a guardian acts as head of the ward's farm until the heir is of age.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The three are offers on VB-690 (basis `care`, due at once, so a chain of asks is one call, as today): for a household left with no grown member, each landless grown relative in today's order (kinship, then the oldest; within walking reach first) offers to come as guardian, with its family; else each kin household's head, nearest kin first, is asked to take the children and hold the farm in trust. A household of at most one grown member whose ask for food fell short (VB-308) asks the same way: a relative to move in, else a servant (`place`, due at once), else kin to hold the farm in trust. The people of a dissolved household (`extinct`) each ask for themselves, `care` to their nearest kin and else `place` in the village, a child through the grown member who keeps it or through the kin who offer. No household is moved by a rule: `careForOrphans`, `takeIn` and `extinct` call no `moveTo`; the moves are `move` (VB-822), reasons `guardian`, `care` and `service`.
- [ ] The answers: a relative asked to come says yes when free to move (as built: unmarried, landless, 18 to 50, its home keeping a grown member) and not at enmity with the household's grown members. A kin head says no only when his own household's mean hunger is `HUNGER.marryBelow` (0.5) or more, or when he is at `SOCIAL.enemy` with the asker and their kin degree is over 2. A child's yes is not asked. A no is `SOCIAL.memories.refusedKin` (-0.4, 5 years), held by each grown member refused and by a ward of 12 or more; a yes is `tookUsIn` (+0.3, 5 years), beside what help books.
- [ ] sim/plans, sim/aims and the head's verbs read the guardian as head while a holder is under 18; the played guardian has the head's verbs.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7 and 42: orphaned households and how each ended (a guardian, kin, service, empty); households taken in and by whom; trusts begun and ended; refusals by reason; children one household further than before; hungry days of wards' farms; growth by principle "The island grows without the player".
- [ ] `tests/commands.js` and `tests/record.js` pass; tests on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): each answer's reasons; a hungry kin head refuses and the next is asked; a sibling at enmity still takes a sibling in; a dissolved household's adult asks for itself; a save during a guardian's journey plays as the unbroken run.
