# VB-107: More urgent work interrupts a job
Status: done (2026-10-30)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: work
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Long work and processing: Multi-day jobs: a job keeps its progress when pawns stop to answer something more urgent). Jobs review, 30 Oct 2026.
Goal: A pawn keeps its job until it is done or the work day ends: a farmer hoeing a 20-hour field does not stop when the last firewood runs out or a ripe field waits. VB-022 lets the cook leave other work after 14:00; this task makes that a general rule.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] At every full hour of the working day, each pawn in the `work` phase of a job (not walking to it, not carrying home) bids, noise-free, on the open jobs it may take. If the best bid is more than 1.5 × its current job's bid plus 1, it releases the current job, which keeps its progress, and starts the new one.
- [x] A pawn switches at most once per hour; a job finished within the hour is not left.
- [x] VB-022's cook rule stays as it is, or becomes a case of this rule if the numbers give the same behaviour; the PR says which.
- [x] The pawn panel's life events are unchanged; the event log does not record switches.
- [x] A test: a son hoeing when the household's woodpile drops below 8 (wood urgency 2.2) leaves the field for the woods; the field keeps its progress. (A farmer, as first written, never switches with these numbers; answered 30 Oct: keep the rule, test a son.)
- [x] Populous medium at seed 7, 3 years: switches per pawn per year, `--report idle` (VB-101) and hungry household-days, before and after (reported, not asserted).
- [x] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
