Goal: The engine that reads a task's next leg from the world exists, is fuzzed clean, and is cheap enough to carry every kind
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A new module `sim/methods` (`@sys`; manifest after sim/kinds and sim/legs): the nine primitives (`go`, `fetch`, `work`, `load`, `haul`, `unload`, `return`, `wait`, `mind`); facts read from the world (where the pawn stands, what it carries, a cart or tool held, a pile at a tile, a stage done, a store's level), none stored on the task; and `next(st, goal, pawn)`: 1. the rule for a load in hand; 2. the stray method; 3. the first method whose `when` holds, read from its last step to its first, taking the last step whose precondition holds and whose effect does not; all effects hold: done; no precondition holds: blocked, with its reason. Methods are data declared by the kind's domain (`KINDS[kind].methods`); `next` allocates nothing on its hot path.
- [x] The engine and the fuzz of the mocks are the starting point: `git show 4651420:docs/mocks/plans/task/micro.js` (Study A, parts 2, 3 and 5), read and rebuilt in the game's conventions, not copied.
- [x] A fuzz test kept in `tests/methods.js`, on constructed states and seeded (a short count in `npm test`; the 10,000 runs of Q1 at night if slower): the firewood goal of 3.2, declared in the test, under random cuts (the night, a call, a death, an illness), two goals for one cart, two owners and piles that rot: no stuck task, no false `blocked`, no good outside a store, a pile or a hand; the five rows of the firewood table each a check.
- [x] Cost, named before building: microseconds an ask on the constructed state, and the day reckoned at the late reference (1,000 pawns × 9 leg ends × 1.6 asks) against 5 ms (3.8); the measured Q4 is VB-1750's.
- [x] Wired to nothing: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`; `npm run verify` passes.
