Touches: new sim/methods; sim/chores (KINDS); sim/kinds (KINDS); sim/agents (arrived, ended, complete, release); sim/jobs (lookForWork); new tests/methods.js
Goal: A pawn doing the wood goes on from whatever the world holds after any cut, and nothing of the task's progress is kept on the task
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A new module `sim/methods` (`@sys`; manifest after sim/kinds and sim/legs): the nine primitives (`go`, `fetch`, `work`, `load`, `haul`, `unload`, `return`, `wait`, `mind`); facts read from the world (where the pawn stands, what it carries, a cart or tool held, a pile at a tile, a stage done, a store's level), none stored on the task; and `next(st, goal, pawn)`: 1. the rule for a load in hand; 2. the stray method; 3. the first method whose `when` holds, read from its last step to its first, taking the last step whose precondition holds and whose effect does not; all effects hold: done; no precondition holds: blocked, with its reason. Methods are data declared by the kind's domain (`KINDS[kind].methods`); `next` allocates nothing on its hot path.
- [ ] The wood's kinds (felling timber, cutting firewood; sim/chores) are goals with methods: the five rows of the firewood table (3.2) each give the legs the table gives, a check each in `tests/methods.js`; the tree falls at the work leg; timber lying at the tile is a pile with title (sim/lying) and is taken up from there by any eligible pawn.
- [ ] The rule for a load in hand: a pawn carrying a body's goods hauls them to that body's store, past the end of the working day if need be, and sets them down as a pile only when it cannot walk on (death, illness, its household gone); a call sets a load down only when the caller says so. The stray method: a pile of a good the goal uses, owned by the goal's owner and lying where its kind neither makes nor wants it, is fetched to the owner's store before the kind's own method; piles are indexed by owner, never scanned.
- [ ] A fuzz test kept in `tests/methods.js`, on constructed states and seeded (a short count in `npm test`; the 10,000 runs of Q1 at night if slower): the wood's goal under random cuts (the night, a call, a death, an illness), two goals for one cart, two owners, piles that rot: no stuck task, no false `blocked`, no good outside a store, a pile or a hand.
- [ ] A kind with `methods` goes through `next`; a kind without runs on its phases as today, through one switch in sim/agents, so the later tasks of step 3 convert kinds a group at a time.
- [ ] Cost, named before building: `next` is asked at a leg's end, 1,000 pawns × 6 to 9 leg ends a day × about 1.6 asks; budget 5 ms a game day at the late reference (3.8). A work counter for its asks in `VB.COUNTERS`; measured on populous small, 2 years, and on a late save (`node tools/sim.js --load FILE --years 1 --report perf`), against the budget.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10): `npm run verify` green, a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose (never skipped or quarantined) and named in the PR; no stuck pawn (no task held 48 hours without progress, as VB-1546's script counts); the stocks' accounts close (`node tools/sim.js --report accounts`: `unexplained` no larger than before).
- [ ] No village dies out: `node tools/sim.js --scenario founders|populous --map small --seed 7|11|42 --years 10`, all six runs: no settlement extinct by hunger and no collapse of its people (the living at year 10 against year 0, in the PR).
- [ ] Reported in the PR, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in those six runs, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed), before and after.
- [ ] Reported: the wood felled and cut a year and household-days with the wood run out, before and after.
