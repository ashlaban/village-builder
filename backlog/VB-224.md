# VB-224: Refactor: a handover ranks only the households of its candidates' lines
Status: done (2026-11-08)
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (code structure). `docs/roadmap.md`, section 5.3 (rule 2, no pass over pairs; rule 1, cost follows activity); Kim's answer of 8 November 2026 in the VB-202 session: fix it first.
Goal: VB-202's fresh fixtures fail `tests/history.js`: heir-ranking work per living pawn-day at `small-y40` is 1.66 times that at `small-y5` (limit 1.25; recorded excess 1.35, which may only fall). A full `heirRanking` outside the moving round (a holder's death, a hand-on, a meeting's check) asks `ownFirstOf(st, hh)`, which ranks every other live household to learn whether a candidate is first in line for a farm of their own. The window of 30 days holds one such handover at `small-y5` (88 households ranked) and three at `small-y40` (173 each): a pass over handovers times households. A candidate can only be a direct heir of a farm whose line is one of its ancestors, one to four generations up (`lineOf` follows four generations), so ranking only those farms gives the same claims for every candidate.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `sim/records` indexes farms by line beside `holds` (`st.idx.lines`, caught up with new farmsteads, and `noteLine` where a farm's line is written), and `householdsOfLine(st, id)` returns the live households whose farm's line, or with none its holder, is `id`.
- [x] `heirRanking` without `ownFirstSet` asks only the households of its candidates' ancestors' lines; the round's shared `ownFirstSet` is unchanged. For every live household of a late state, the ranking equals the ranking given the full `ownFirstOf(st)` (a test, on a tiny state in `npm test` and on the fixtures in verify).
- [x] The index's invariant in `checkState`: each live household with a line is found by `householdsOfLine` under it.
- [x] Behaviour unchanged: `tests/golden.json` unchanged, `npm run verify` passes; `tests/history.js` passes on VB-202's fixtures, and its heir-ranking excess in `tests/cost-baseline.json` is lowered or removed with `--update`. Changed work counters (`tests/counters.json`) listed in the PR.
Cost: a handover ranks 1 + (the farms of its candidates' lines) households, against 1 + every live household; the ancestor walk is at most 30 pawns a candidate.
