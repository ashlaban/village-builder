# VB-1527: An abandoned field goes back to its ground
Status: done (2026-11-23)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Land and ecology (Abandoned fields, Forest spread, Fresh land); Kim, 23 Nov: abandoned fields revert to their correct terrain type
Goal: A field nobody holds does not stay farmland for good: it returns to the terrain it lies on.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The defect: a field let go (`letGo` of `src/sim/fields.js`, VB-768) left its tile `w.dev` 1 for good: walked as a field whatever its ground, never drawn as its terrain, never wild for forage or deer, and forest that grew over it stood on "developed" ground.
- [x] The mechanism (`src/sim/spread.js`, beside the forest's front): a tile let go is lapsed ground (`st.lapsed`), tended to the day it was let go; `LAPSE.years` (8, when scrub would stand on open land beside a wood) untended and not taken into use again, it is wild ground of its terrain: `w.dev` 0, its walking cost its ground's, drawn as its biome. Hills are hills, heath heath, marsh marsh; a meadow's field, and a field broken from a wood, is meadow that the forest's spread takes where a wood seeds it. Its stones do not come back (stone-free, as the design had it).
- [x] Taken in again before then (a field, a meadow, a plot), it leaves the lapsed ground and nothing reverts. Ground reverted counts as never farmed: a field broken there starts fresh (`SOIL.fresh`), a long fallow of 8 years; surfaced as a close call.
- [x] An older save: its let-go tiles (developed, in nobody's use, no house or green) are found once and lapse from the day of the load.
- [x] A work counter (`lapsedTile`), a check on a small constructed state (`tests/forest-spread.js`), the invariant (a lapsed tile is `w.dev` 1), saves round trip.
- [x] Kim, 23 Nov (second instruction): reverting is a natural property, not tied to a pawn or a household. A field of any farm, held or empty, unused `LAPSE.years` (`fd.used`: sown, reaped, a fallow course, reclaimed, broken) leaves the farm on the year's last day and is wild at once (`lapseFields` of `src/sim/fields.js`, counter `fieldLapse`); a field let go lapses from its last use, not from the day it was let go. A farm under restoration keeps its fields.
- [x] `design.md`, Abandoned fields, rewritten by the orchestrator (Kim's instruction of 23 Nov), the years Proposed.
