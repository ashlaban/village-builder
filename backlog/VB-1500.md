# VB-1500: Map pixels: a check that the baked map is the same at two commits
Status: done (2026-11-23)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: `docs/testing.md`; `AGENTS.md`, Commands (No golden file)
Goal: Nothing checks the map's pixels: `tools/same.js` compares the leaves of `w` and `st`, so a refactor of the bake, the marks, the rivers' or the paths' drawing has no proof. A tool that bakes the same chunks at a base commit and in the working tree, headless, and compares their hashes, is that proof for the terrain split (the four tasks that wait for this one).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A tool (`tools/samepixels.js`, or a `--pixels` mode of `tools/same.js`; it starts with `tools/help.js` and answers `--help`) bakes, headless, at `--base REF` (a worktree of that commit, as `tools/same.js` does) and in the working tree, and prints `same` or each chunk that differs, with exit 1.
- [x] What it bakes, at seeds 7 and 42 on a small map with founders, at year 0 and after 2 years: `bakeWater`; every chunk of the overview (level 2) through `bakeChunk`; the level 0 and level 1 chunks round each village, with the rivers, the worn paths (`drawChunk` and `drawOver`) and `decorateChunk` drawn on a canvas of `@napi-rs/canvas` (a devDependency already; no new dependency).
- [x] It also bakes a chunk after a cover is set by hand (`gen/cover` `set`) and after `restore`, so that a colour left stale shows.
- [x] It finds the drawing functions on either side of the split without being told: `ArchGen` and `VB['gen/...']` today, `VB['draw/...']` after VB-1503; the tasks that move them keep it working at both commits.
- [x] It catches a change: a test (in `tests/suites.js`, on a small constructed world, in the tier its time allows) changes one pigment, or one mark's place, and the hashes differ; unchanged, they agree.
- [x] `docs/commands.md` and `docs/testing.md` have its line; `npm run verify` passes.
