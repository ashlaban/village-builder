# VB-403: Balance accounts: a report of where the food and the people go
Status: done (2026-11-22)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: Balance loops and targets (The balance review, 12 November); docs/balance/cards.md; docs/balance/findings.md
Goal: The balance review needed numbers the game cannot report in one place, and found by hand-written scripts that about 15% of a year's gross produce has no outlet in the measured split (it is neither eaten, brewed, given nor counted as rotted). Give the game one report, `node tools/sim.js --report accounts [--json]` (from a run or `--load`), that closes the yearly accounts and prints what is left unexplained, so that the balance map's properties are read from the game and not from documents. It reads only: no new state, nothing saved, nothing the simulation reads.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Food, a game year, village by village and in all: produced by source (grain by crop, fish, gathered food, meat, milk); then seed, eaten (as meals, uncooked, fresh, from the larder), given to other households, given to the village store, received, brewed, rotted in households, rotted in the village store, and the change in stores. The account closes: produced plus received equals the outlets plus the change in stores, and the remainder is printed as its own line, `unexplained`, with its share of the produce. Read from the stores' ledgers and movements (`src/sim/stores.js`) as the run goes, in the tool; a household keeps three years of ledger, so the tool accumulates at each year's end.
- [x] The 15%: the report is run on populous small, seeds 3, 7 and 11, 30 years, and the task file says where the unexplained share goes (a movement with no reason, sheaves never threshed, the larder, a counting error of the review's scripts) or raises it as a Flag with a question in `QUESTIONS.md`.
- [x] People, a game year: births, deaths by cause, marriages, marriages postponed by reason (no home, the home lacks the means, hunger, the kin limit, no partner in reach), moves between villages, households founded and dissolved; and from them the growth rate, births a woman (period rate), the shares dying before 1 and before 15, the mean age at first marriage of women and men, and the share of those aged 45 and over who never married.
- [x] The spread between households, at the year's end: food produced over need (p10, p50, p90, the share under 1.0 and under 1.67), days of food held (p10, p50, p90), the Gini of fields owned and of grain held, the share of single-person households, and the households hungry over 30 days of the year.
- [x] Goodwill: gifts in the year, goodwill outstanding a household, live pairs, and the share held by the three largest creditors. Labour: the idle share and the walking and carrying share of adults' working hours by season, from the `idle` report's own counting.
- [x] The balance map's properties as one table at the end (`docs/balance/cards.md`, The balance properties): each with its value this year, or `no mechanism yet` (share extracted, tenancy, town and craft share, elite share, sold, goodwill from feasts, disease).
- [x] `--json` gives the same as data, a record a year. `--help` describes it, and `AGENTS.md`, Commands, gains its line.
- [x] A test (`tests/review-tools.js` or one beside it, in `npm test`) runs it on the tiny map for 3 years and asserts that the food account closes within 1% once `unexplained` is counted, and that the people account's births less deaths equals the change in the living.
- [x] Numbers in this file and the PR: the table for populous small (seeds 3, 7, 11; years 10, 30, 50) and founders small (seeds 3, 5, 7, 11, 42; year 50), beside the balance review's (`docs/balance/findings.md`, Measured on the real game), and each difference explained.
- [x] Cost (rule 10): the tool's own bookkeeping, outside `st`; behaviour (`node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`), the work counters and the saves are unchanged.

The differences: food over need is gross of seed here (seed an outlet) and counts the common fields; the review's was net of seed, which
also explains most of the households under 1.0. Grain rots more in households and less goes to the store: the store's target (VB-457,
after the review) stops the filling, so the grain stays home and rots there. Growth and the late child deaths: starvation is the largest
cause of death late (73 / 140 / 206 in years 41-50), while no household is hungry 30 days and food is 2.7 to 3.3 times need: a row in
docs/tuning.md (with VB-1374's fall in growth); it is the game's, not the review's counting.

The 15% (review's lines against our grain account, years 26-30): the review counted seed, grain cooked and raw, grain rotted in
households, to the store, brewed and given, 76 / 88 / 90% of the grain produced. The rest is the review's scripts' counting: flour into
the pot 14 / 14 / 5% (the quern grinds most grain before it is cooked, VB-009; the scripts read only grain cooked), the growth of stores
10 / 0.5 / 9%, stakes of those who died 4 / 1 / 1% (VB-1476), grain rotted in the store 5% (not an outlet of the households, counted
nowhere); less the gifts between households, which the scripts counted as an outlet only (6 to 8%: on the island a gift is both given and
received). No movement without a reason was found but the stake at death.
