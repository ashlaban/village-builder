# VB-054: State invariant checker in the long runs
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: tooling
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (test infrastructure)
Goal: Most bugs in this code show up as inconsistent state long before they show on screen (a pawn in two households, a holder who is dead, a job claimed by nobody). Check the invariants automatically, so nightly work cannot break them silently.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `tests/lib/invariants.js` exports `checkState(st)`, returning a list of violations, each naming the record (e.g. `households[3]: holder 17 is dead`). At least: every living pawn is a member of exactly one undissolved household, and its `household` field names it; every undissolved household's holder is alive and a member; dead pawns are in no household; every union has a start before its end and at most one open union per pawn; grain, wood, hunger and (once VB-050 is done) health are finite and within range; every claimed job is the `job` of the claiming agent; every farm with a household has `state` `occupied`, and every empty farm `derelict` or `ruin`; tile indexes on records are within the map; once VB-003 is done, every house and field tile has an owner.
- [ ] `tests/invariants.js`, in `npm run verify`: runs founders and heirs at seeds 7 and 42 for 20 years and populous tiny at seed 7 for 5 years, calling `checkState` after every game day; fails with the first violations and the day they appeared.
- [ ] A broken state made by hand (a pawn removed from its household's members) is reported.
- [ ] CLAUDE.md says: a new state field or record gets its invariants here.
