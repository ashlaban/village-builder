# VB-213: The player's succession: going on as another after a death
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Vision and scope (Which pawn: Decided, 3 Nov); The player and what they see (After a death: Proposed, VB-079).
Goal: When the player's pawn dies, the player goes on with another (Decided, 3 Nov): among the pawn's kin by blood or marriage, its household and its close ties, the heir first; if none is alive, any adult, and the break is recorded.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `Sim.successors(st, id)`: the living, in this order without repeats:
  1. the heirs of the farm the pawn held (`heirRanking`, `src/sim/heirs.js:19`);
  2. its kin by blood or marriage, nearest degree first (`kinDegree`, `src/sim/records.js:193`), kin of any age;
  3. its household;
  4. its close ties by opinion (`relationsOf`, `src/sim/relations.js:259`, at most 150).

  Each entry has `{id, why}` (heir, son, wife's brother, household, friend). The list is empty when none is alive. It reads no full list of pawns or records.
- [x] When the played pawn dies, `st.player` keeps it, with an empty queue, until the player chooses; the world does not wait. The page pauses (page state) and offers the list, or any living adult when it is empty. The choice inhabits the successor with a `player` record event naming the link ("went on as the heir, Erik Olofsson"), or "a break in the line" when the list was empty.
- [x] Tests: the heir comes first; kin by marriage are listed; an empty list gives a break; with no player, the golden file is unchanged. A browser check kills the played pawn from the debug drawer and chooses its heir.
Notes:
- 8 Nov 2026 (VB-079, slice 2's plan): written. The record of a line's lives (VB-099) waits for Kim; this task keeps only the events it writes.

Implementation (10 Nov 2026): a death drops the pawn's ties and opinions and ends its marriage, and its farm passes on, so what the succession needs is noted when the played pawn dies: `playerDied` (`src/sim/commands.js`, called by `killPawn` before those) empties the queue and sets `st.player.left = {t, held, spouse, ties}` (the household it held, its spouse, up to 12 of those it thought friends, opinion 0.25 and up, best first; Kim, 10 Nov). `successors(st, id)` and `succeed(st, id)` are in `src/sim/opportunities.js` (they need `heirRanking`, which loads after `sim/commands`); the write is `goOnAs` in `sim/commands`, the owner of `st.player`. Order: who holds the farm now and then `heirRanking`, as `heir`; kin by blood to civil degree 4, first cousins (Kim, 10 Nov), and by marriage (the spouse, the spouse's kin to degree 2, the living spouses of its own kin to degree 2), nearest first, blood before marriage at a degree, then the oldest, each with its word (`son`, `wife's brother`, `brother's wife`; `kin` beyond the named ones); the household; the friends. At most 40. For a living pawn, or one the player does not play, the farm, spouse and friends are read as they are now. The chosen link is written to the dead pawn's events ("The player went on as the heir, Erik Olofsson", "... as its wife's brother, ...", "A break in the line: the player went on as ..."), and the successor gets "Played by the player". With successors alive only they may be chosen; with none, any living adult. Page: `offerSuccession` (`src/page/play.js`, each frame, the list read twice a second) pauses once at the death and shows the list in a box; a successor's card, or any adult's when the list is empty, offers "Go on as".

Impact note: one new state field, `st.player.left`, written only in `sim/commands`, saved with the player (older saves load with `null`); invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js`. New coupling: `sim/lifecycle`'s `killPawn` calls `sim/commands`' `playerDied`; `sim/commands` reads `sim/relations` for the friends. No new module.

Cost: `playerDied` is one comparison a death unless the played pawn dies. `successors` is asked by the page only, while a choice is open: it walks the pawn's ancestors to 4 generations and their descendants within degree 4, one heir ranking, the household and at most 12 ties; no list of all pawns or records.

Not built: kin through a spouse who died before the pawn (the union has ended, and finding it would scan the unions); a pawn the player leaves by choice has no succession.

Validation (10 Nov 2026): `npm run verify` passes (99 scripts), with the new `tests/succession.js` (the heir first; kin by blood and by marriage with their links, nearest first; friends last; an empty list gives a break; saved and loaded; another pawn's death is the same with a player as without). Golden file: the behaviour entries are unchanged; `apiKeys: Sim` changes, for `Sim.successors` and `Sim.succeed`. `tests/browser.js` kills the played holder from the debug drawer, sees the page pause and offer the heir first, and goes on as it. No numbers before and after: with no player a run is unchanged.
