# VB-213: The player's succession: going on as another after a death
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-210
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Vision and scope (Which pawn: Decided, 3 Nov); The player and what they see (After a death: Proposed, VB-079).
Goal: When the player's pawn dies, the player goes on with another (Decided, 3 Nov): among the pawn's kin by blood or marriage, its household and its close ties, the heir first; if none is alive, any adult, and the break is recorded.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `Sim.successors(st, id)`: the living, in this order without repeats:
  1. the heirs of the farm the pawn held (`heirRanking`, `src/sim/heirs.js:19`);
  2. its kin by blood or marriage, nearest degree first (`kinDegree`, `src/sim/records.js:193`), kin of any age;
  3. its household;
  4. its close ties by opinion (`relationsOf`, `src/sim/relations.js:259`, at most 150).

  Each entry has `{id, why}` (heir, son, wife's brother, household, friend). The list is empty when none is alive. It reads no full list of pawns or records.
- [ ] When the played pawn dies, `st.player` keeps it, with an empty queue, until the player chooses; the world does not wait. The page pauses (page state) and offers the list, or any living adult when it is empty. The choice inhabits the successor with a `player` record event naming the link ("went on as the heir, Erik Olofsson"), or "a break in the line" when the list was empty.
- [ ] Tests: the heir comes first; kin by marriage are listed; an empty list gives a break; with no player, the golden file is unchanged. A browser check kills the played pawn from the debug drawer and chooses its heir.
