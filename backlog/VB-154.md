# VB-154: Room between villages: new villages farther apart, and room for each new farm
Status: done (2026-11-01)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: expansion
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion, Moving to new land (the new village); Land and ecology (the outland, *utmark*). Review: `docs/review-stability.md`; `docs/review-expansion.md`.
Goal: The open land between villages fills too quickly: in the baseline runs the nearest neighbouring village is 11–22 steps away at year 10 and 7–9 by year 60–80. Settlers found villages 10–30 steps from home with a house and fields as close as 3 steps to other farms, and three settlers in four join a small village, which grows into a hamlet of 4 farms in the gap.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A new village keeps `SETTLE.apart` = 5 steps (was 3) from every other farm's house, green, field, field project and plot: no new house or field within 4 steps of them.
- [x] A new village's house lies at least `SETTLE.centreApart` = 12 hex steps from every village centre, lone farms included.
- [x] A new farm needs room (`FIELDS.room` = 6): that many free farmable tiles (unowned, unused) within FIELD_REACH walking steps of its house besides its first field, for more fields, meadow and pasture: for a village's plot and a settler who joins a small village (`findSite` and `roomAt`, `src/sim/expansion.js`) and for a new village (beyond its block of fields and away from other farms; `newSite`, `src/sim/settle.js`). So a hamlet stops growing once the land round it is in use.
- [x] `tests/spacing.js`: every site offered has room; with a room no farm can have there is none; a new village's site keeps its distances and has room.
- [x] The golden file is unchanged: no farm in the 3-year founders and heirs runs is sited where the new rules bite.
