# VB-208: Refactor: each job kind says where it is available, and `refreshJobs` posts from them
Status: done (2026-11-09)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (code structure). `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, section 4.5 (Opportunities: "each kind's record in `KINDS` gains `available(st, holder)`; today's posting rules in `refreshJobs` move there almost line for line"), principle 4; `docs/roadmap.md`, section 3.2 (the first step of the jobs rework); `docs/review-life.md`, section 3.
Goal: Today one loop in `refreshJobs` (`src/sim/jobs.js:45-94`) holds every rule for when a job is posted: hoe and sow in the sowing window, weed, reap, haul, reclaim, manure, firewood below the goal, threshing, water, cooking, mending, and the jobs of meadows, the byre, plots and claims through their modules. Slice 2 needs "what could be done here" for one pawn (VB-209) and slice 3 for every holder, so the rule must live with each kind. Move each kind's posting rule into its `KINDS` record as `available`, and make `refreshJobs` a loop that asks the kinds, with behaviour unchanged.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Each kind posted from `refreshJobs` has `available(st, holder, at, c, post)` in `src/sim/kinds.js` (or a shared one for the kinds a module posts together, `meadowJobs`, `byreJobs`, `plotJobs`, `claimJobs`, delegating to that module's function). Here `at` is the field, meadow or farm it is asked about. It calls `post(key, site, extra)` for each job it would post, so no array is made per call. Its comment states the rule in words.
- [x] `refreshJobs` keeps today's order: farms in order, then for each farm its fields, its meadows and the farm itself, asking at each the kinds of that scope in today's order; then village fences, plots and claims. Job ids, and so matching, are unchanged.
- [x] What is a choice rather than an availability stays out of `available`: `startProject` and `startMeadow` (a household looks for land at the start of work) are called where they are today, and named in a comment as slice 3's hidden plans (VB-164).
- [x] Refactor rules of `BACKLOG.md`: `tests/golden.json` and the work counters unchanged; `npm run verify` passes; the PR says what fell (the lines of `refreshJobs`, and the rule for each kind now in one place beside its record).
- [x] Cost: "jobs refresh and matching" within 5% of before, from `node tools/sim.js --load tests/fixtures/medium-y5.gz --years 1 --report perf` and the same on `small-y40`, before and after on one machine (on 7 November, 30 days: 33.5 ms a game day at medium-y5, 22.8 at small-y40; stale fixtures). `sim/kinds` stays under 400 lines; if it would not, the field kinds' rules go into a module of their own listed before it.
Notes:
- 8 Nov 2026 (VB-079, slice 2's plan): written. It runs before VB-169 and VB-209, which build on it; VB-169 also edits `sim/jobs`, so they run one after the other.
- A kind's `available` reads the household's stores and the field's or meadow's stage as the loop does today; `LandRights.may` and typed goods arrive with slices 3 and 5. Keep the signature open to a holder that is a village (fences, plots) as well as a household.
- Why the order matters: a job's id is its posting order (`jobs.js:22`), matching visits jobs by id (`jobs.js:126`, `181`), and two new jobs on different fields can appear in the same hour (reaping one field and hauling another), so asking kind by kind across all fields would renumber them.
- 9 Nov 2026, implementation:
  - `sim/kinds`: each kind posted from `refreshJobs` has `available(st, holder, at, c, post, f)` in its record, with its rule in words above it.
    `holder` is the household (or the village), `at` the field, farm or village asked about, and `f` the household's farm. The sixth
    argument is needed because a field does not know its farm, and a field job's key and `extra` name the farm. The rule calls
    `post(key, site, extra)` once for each job it would post. A rule shared by several kinds names each job's kind (`post(key, site, extra,
    kind)`). A job that the farm's household does not own also names its owner (`..., kind, owner`).
    - Own rules: plow, sow, weed, reap, haul and reclaim at a field; manure, wood, thresh, water and cook at the farm; mend at the farm
      (`f.fence`) and at a village (`v.fence`, with the farm argument left out).
    - Shared rules, each delegating to its module's function as before: `meadowWork` (rake, mow and hay; `meadowJobs` and `hayWanted`),
      `byreWork` (fodder, slaughter, salt and milk; `byreJobs`), `plotWork` (fell, grub and a plot's steps and cottage stages; `plotJobs`),
      `claimWork` (restore; `claimJobs`), `landProject` (clear, break and meadow; the farm's land project) and `boothWork` (the cottage
      stages; `boothJob`).
    - Clear and break are also a plot's steps, and the cottage stages are also a plot's. A record holds the household's rule, and its
      comment names the second rule (`plotWork`).
    - The meadow rule is asked once at the farm, not once at each meadow. Whether hay is wanted belongs to the household and is worked out
      once for all its meadows, as before, so the jobs' order is the same.
    - `RECLAIM` and `cookWanted` moved beside their kinds. `callCooks` in `sim/jobs` now imports `cookWanted`.
  - `ASK` in `sim/kinds` lists `[kind, rule]` entries in today's order, for each place the rules are asked: `field`, `farm` (meadows, manure,
    byre), `farmAfterPlans` (land project, booth, wood, thresh, water, cook, mend), `village` (mend), and `island` (plots, then claims).
    `refreshJobs` asks each list in turn, through one `post` closure and one `ask` closure made per call. The default owner is the farm's
    household, shared by the farm's jobs as before. A village's or plot's job gets a new owner object for each job, as before, so the
    saves' shared references do not change.
  - Field kinds declare `stages`: the field stages at which each can be available. `ASK.fieldAt` gives a stage's rules in the order of
    `ASK.field`, and `refreshJobs` asks only those at each field. Without this index, `refreshJobs` alone was about 30% slower than the inline
    loop (six indirect calls a field an hour; see Cost below). No two field kinds share a stage, so at most one field rule is asked at a
    field. Reclaim is listed at two stages. The posting order is unchanged. The rules still check the stage themselves, so a caller can ask a
    single kind (VB-209).
  - `startProject` and `startMeadow` are choices, not availabilities. They stay in `refreshJobs`, between the farm's two lists, where
    they were. A comment names them as slice 3's hidden plans (VB-164).
  - What fell: `refreshJobs` went from 50 lines (`jobs.js:45-94`) to 28, including its comments. Its 43 lines of posting, over farms, villages,
    plots and claims, became an 11-line loop over the farms and two lines for the villages and the island. It holds no posting rule. Each rule is now one place, in its
    kind's record. `sim/jobs` went from 224 to 197 lines and no longer imports from `sim/hunger`, `sim/byre` or `sim/expansion`.
    `sim/kinds` went from 199 to 313 lines, under the 400 limit, so the field kinds keep their rules in it.
