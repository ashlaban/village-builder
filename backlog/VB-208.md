# VB-208: Refactor: each job kind says where it is available, and `refreshJobs` posts from them
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (code structure). `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, section 4.5 (Opportunities: "each kind's record in `KINDS` gains `available(st, holder)`; today's posting rules in `refreshJobs` move there almost line for line"), principle 4; `docs/roadmap.md`, section 3.2 (the first step of the jobs rework); `docs/review-life.md`, section 3.
Goal: Today one loop in `refreshJobs` (`src/sim/jobs.js:45-94`) holds every rule for when a job is posted: hoe and sow in the sowing window, weed, reap, haul, reclaim, manure, firewood below the goal, threshing, water, cooking, mending, and the jobs of meadows, the byre, plots and claims through their modules. Slice 2 needs "what could be done here" for one pawn (VB-209) and slice 3 for every holder, so the rule must live with each kind. Move each kind's posting rule into its `KINDS` record as `available`, and make `refreshJobs` a loop that asks the kinds, with behaviour unchanged.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Each kind posted from `refreshJobs` has `available(st, holder, at, c, post)` in `src/sim/kinds.js` (or a shared one for the kinds a module posts together, `meadowJobs`, `byreJobs`, `plotJobs`, `claimJobs`, delegating to that module's function). Here `at` is the field, meadow or farm it is asked about. It calls `post(key, site, extra)` for each job it would post, so no array is made per call. Its comment states the rule in words.
- [ ] `refreshJobs` keeps today's order: farms in order, then for each farm its fields, its meadows and the farm itself, asking at each the kinds of that scope in today's order; then village fences, plots and claims. Job ids, and so matching, are unchanged.
- [ ] What is a choice rather than an availability stays out of `available`: `startProject` and `startMeadow` (a household looks for land at the start of work) are called where they are today, and named in a comment as slice 3's hidden plans (VB-164).
- [ ] Refactor rules of `BACKLOG.md`: `tests/golden.json` and the work counters unchanged; `npm run verify` passes; the PR says what fell (the lines of `refreshJobs`, and the rule for each kind now in one place beside its record).
- [ ] Cost: "jobs refresh and matching" within 5% of before, from `node tools/sim.js --load tests/fixtures/medium-y5.gz --years 1 --report perf` and the same on `small-y40`, before and after on one machine (on 7 November, 30 days: 33.5 ms a game day at medium-y5, 22.8 at small-y40; stale fixtures). `sim/kinds` stays under 400 lines; if it would not, the field kinds' rules go into a module of their own listed before it.
Notes:
- 8 Nov 2026 (VB-079, slice 2's plan): written. It runs before VB-169 and VB-209, which build on it; VB-169 also edits `sim/jobs`, so they run one after the other.
- A kind's `available` reads the household's stores and the field's or meadow's stage as the loop does today; `LandRights.may` and typed goods arrive with slices 3 and 5. Keep the signature open to a holder that is a village (fences, plots) as well as a household.
- Why the order matters: a job's id is its posting order (`jobs.js:22`), matching visits jobs by id (`jobs.js:126`, `181`), and two new jobs on different fields can appear in the same hour (reaping one field and hauling another), so asking kind by kind across all fields would renumber them.
