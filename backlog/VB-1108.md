# VB-1108: Fix: a skip loses the events of the step that stopped it
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player and what they see (Skip to the next event)
Goal: No event that concerns the played pawn passes a skip unread.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A skip goes on reading the record where the last one stopped (`skipStop`, `src/sim/notable.js`): the page keeps the last stop's cursor (`from.n`) and the next `Sim.skipFrom` starts from it, or a step's later stops are queued and shown in turn; decide which and write why. Events that pass while the game runs at a speed, not skipping, are not read back by the next skip.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state: two events that stop a skip in one step (a wedding of the played pawn, and a farm taken over by its son the same hour) stop it twice, in the record's order.
- [ ] `skipStop` still writes nothing to `st`.
