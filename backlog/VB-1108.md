# VB-1108: Fix: a skip loses the events of the step that stopped it
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player and what they see (Skip to the next event)
Goal: No event that concerns the played pawn passes a skip unread.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A skip goes on reading the record where the last one stopped (`skipStop`, `src/sim/notable.js`): the page keeps the last stop's cursor (`from.n`) and the next `Sim.skipFrom` starts from it, or a step's later stops are queued and shown in turn; decide which and write why. Events that pass while the game runs at a speed, not skipping, are not read back by the next skip.
- [x] A check on a small constructed state: two events that stop a skip in one step (a wedding of the played pawn, and a farm taken over by its son the same hour) stop it twice, in the record's order.
- [x] `skipStop` still writes nothing to `st`.

Notes / questions:
- 16 Nov 2026 (the parallel session "Gameplay review and feedback", `claude/wizardly-noether-5mfey1`: founders small seed 7, 75 years headless through the Sim API, one played pawn with its automatic behaviour on; filed here at the user's request, the code facts checked on `main`): `skipFrom` sets `n: st.record.length`, and `startSkip` (`src/page/play.js`) calls it at every skip, so the events after the stopping one in the same step are never read. Seen in year 16, autumn day 11, noon: the stop was "Hans Andersson and Ingeborg Torstensdotter marry"; in the same step "Jon Gunnarsson takes over Östergården as Ingeborg marries away", an `inherited` event that concerns the played pawn, went untold.
- Runs one at a time with VB-1101, VB-1109, VB-1112, VB-1113, VB-1114 and VB-1115, which add stops in `src/sim/notable.js`.
- 17 Nov 2026 (reviewed after the merge with main, 354 commits on from the playtests' base; the code facts checked again): stands (`skipFrom`, `src/sim/notable.js`:261, still starts at the record's end).
- 18 Nov 2026, implementation. Decided: the cursor is kept, not a queue of stops. `Sim.skipFrom(st, last)` takes the last skip's
  cursor (the page's `skipLast`, set when a skip ends, dropped on a load), and goes on with it when the same pawn is played and no
  step has run since it was last asked (`last.at === st.t`): the unread events of the stopping step come first, and the cursor's
  hunger, offers and occasions stand as judged, so a second stop of another kind in that step is told too. Why not a queue: a queue
  would judge a step's later events at the first stop and show them later, when the player may have acted on the first (the cursor
  judges each when it is read, by the pawn's household and village then); and `skipStop` already is a cursor, so the change is one
  argument. What the record gains while the game stands between the two skips (`pass`: the player's own acts, which it saw) is
  passed; once a step has run at a speed, or another pawn is played, the skip begins at the record's end, as before.
  `skipStop` writes only to the cursor (`at`, `end`, `pass`), never to `st`; `tools/writes.js` hints `from` in `sim/notable` as a
  local object, since `at` and `end` are names of state fields elsewhere (Deciding without Kim, 12).
- Close call (noted as `decision`): a command refused in the same step as another stop is still not told by the next skip. The
  cursor's `t` moves to the new skip's start, because keeping it would stop the next skip on a refusal the player was already
  shown while the game stood. Left for VB-1135 (noted).
