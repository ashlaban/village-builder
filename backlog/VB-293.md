# VB-293: Marriage offers: each match is an offer both households answer, the player's among them
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Marriage as a negotiation: The offer, Who negotiates, Consent, The player's doors; Leaving home); The player and what they see (Marriage). Survey and plan: `backlog/VB-233.md`.
Goal: The moving round's matcher (`marriageRound`, `src/sim/lifecycle.js`) marries each pair it finds at once, so the player has no say. Make each match an offer that the two who marry and their spokesmen answer. A side that is not the player answers yes at once, so a run with no player is unchanged. An offer the player must answer waits up to 7 game days, as plain data with a due time in `st.offers`, and the world goes on meanwhile. This is the first question answered through `Decisions.answer` (here `Sim.answer`; slice 5's Decisions module takes it over).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `spokesmanOf(st, id)` in `src/sim/records.js`: the holder of the pawn's household when the pawn lives there as a son or daughter of the house (a child of the holder, of the holder's spouse or of the holder's parent, not in service, and not the holder); otherwise the pawn itself.
- [x] `st.offers`: the open offers, sorted by `due`, each `{id, man, woman, home, village (the man's), made, due (made + 7 × DAY), answers: [{key: 'man' | 'woman' | 'manSide' | 'womanSide', by (pawn id), yes: null | true | false}]}`. A spokesman who is also the one to marry gives one answer. Ids come from a counter in `st` and are never reused. Owner: `sim/lifecycle` (in `src/owners.json`; planned to move to Decisions in slice 5). `@state` line, invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js` (sorted by due; due at most 7 days after made; no pawn in two open offers; each answer's `by` is the man, the woman or their spokesman at the time the offer was made), and saved with the run.
- [x] In `marriageRound`, where it issues `marry` today (`lifecycle.js:255`), the pair becomes an offer. Every answer whose `by` is not the played pawn is yes at once. If every answer is yes, the round marries them there and then, as today. Otherwise the offer goes into `st.offers`, the woman stays in `taken`, and the matcher goes on. A pawn in an open offer is not free to any man of the round, nor counted as unmatched by `kinPressure`. A refused offer leaves both unmarried until the next round.
- [x] The verb `answer` in `src/sim/commands.js` (source `player`): target the offer's id, args `{yes}`. It checks that the offer is open and that the played pawn has an unanswered answer in it, and sets all of that pawn's answers. `Sim.answer(st, offerId, yes)` issues it.
- [x] An offer closes when every answer is yes (the marriage), when one is no (refused), or at its due time, when its unanswered answers become yes, as the pawn would answer unplayed. The day's end looks only at `st.offers[0].due`, so nothing is polled between rounds (Rules for cost, 4). Once VB-241's calendar exists, this is one daily entry, guarded by an open offer. A marriage made at the close goes through the `marry` verb (source `round`). The round's handover for the home household then follows it, as at the round: the rule of `lifecycle.js:272-285` moves into one function that both call, and `refreshRoles` runs. If the verb refuses (one of them has died or married), the offer lapses, and its reason is logged.
- [x] Events (`tests/record.js`): `offered` when an offer is stored, in the records of both who are to marry, naming the home's farm; `matchRefused` when one is refused, naming who refused; `offerLapsed`. A marriage at the close is the `married` event as today. Matches answered at once write nothing new.
- [x] Consent's cost: when the played pawn refuses, as the one to marry, an offer whose spokesman on its side is another pawn, that spokesman remembers it: `SOCIAL.memories.refusedMatch` (`Refused the match I made`, v -0.2, cap -0.4, 3 years), in the spokesman's record of the pawn. When the player refuses as a spokesman, nothing is remembered.

When the man dies with an offer open, it lapses. A save made with an offer open loads and plays the same as the unbroken run.

Implementation (10 Nov 2026, VB-293):
- `spokesmanOf(st, id)` (`src/sim/records.js`): the holder when the pawn lives in its household, not in service, as a child of the holder, of the holder's spouse or of one of the holder's parents; otherwise itself.
- `marriageRound`: at the `marry` call, with a pawn played, the match's answers are made (`answersOf`: the man's and the woman's, and a `manSide`/`womanSide` answer only when another speaks for them); every answer not by the played pawn is yes. All yes: the round marries them as before. Otherwise `makeOffer` stores it (sorted by due, `id` from `st.offerSeq`), the man joins the woman in `taken`, and the matcher goes on. At the round's start the pawns of open offers are put in `taken` and their men skipped, so they are free to no one and count as matched in `kinPressure`. With no player none of this runs: no `spokesmanOf` call, no answer is made, the round is the old round (so the cost criterion's "four `spokesmanOf` calls a match" is zero without a player).
- The verb `answer` (`src/sim/commands.js`, source `player`): refused unless the actor is the played pawn, living, `args.yes` a boolean, the offer open and the pawn with an answer still to give in it; `Sim.answer(st, offerId, yes)` issues it. `answerOffer` sets all the pawn's answers; a no as the one to marry, with another as spokesman on its side, makes that spokesman (if living) remember `refusedMatch` of the pawn (`SOCIAL.memories.refusedMatch`: -0.2, cap -0.4, 3 years). The offer closes at a no or when all are yes.
- `closeOffer`: a no logs `matchRefused` (the text names who refused); otherwise the `marry` verb (source `round`), then `handover` for the home with a fresh `roundHeirs(st).rank` and `refreshRoles`; a refused verb logs `offerLapsed` with its reason. `dueOffers(st)`, called at the day's end (hour 0, after `dailyLife` and `dailyBirths`), closes the offers whose due time has passed, their unanswered answers yes; it reads only `st.offers[0]`. `MARRIAGE.offerDays` is 7.
- Events: `offered` in both pawns' lives (as `married` is, a `self` telling each and one log line naming the home's farm), `matchRefused`, `offerLapsed`; all three notable (kept, `HISTORY` 0) and in the chronicle's Marriages filter.
- Skip: `SKIP.offer`; `skipFrom` notes `offer: st.offerSeq` (the next offer's id), and `skipStop` stops once on each offer from that id on that waits for the played pawn. A note of the next id rather than a list of the open ids keeps `tools/writes.js` able to place the writes (a list's push was two unresolved writes, over the test's 20).
- Saves: `st.offers` is saved with the state; a save without it (all saves before this task) loads with none open and `offerSeq` 0, so the save format stays 3 and the fixtures load.
- Invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): sorted by due, made no later than now, due after made and at most 7 days on, ids unique and below `offerSeq`, man and woman pawns of their sex, no pawn in two open offers, the answers `man` and `woman` by them and at most one spokesman's answer a side by another pawn, none a no and not all yes (an offer closes then). Who spoke for whom when the offer was made is checked in `tests/offers.js` (a spokesman may change while it is open).
