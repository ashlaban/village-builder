# VB-078: Wrap-up: a life: a played line over three generations, commands and saves
Status: done (2026-11-11)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: wrap-up
Design ref: The player and what they see (VB-079); Commands, time steps and views; Systems overview (findings 4 and 6); `docs/review-life.md`.
Goal: Check slice 2 as a whole once its implement tasks are done. A line can be played over three generations through the player's doors. A played run that is saved and loaded many times plays exactly like one that never was. Commands carry out all pawn work, and the slice costs the simulation nothing.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A scripted player in Node (`tools/sim.js --player SCRIPT`, or a test helper) plays the founders scenario at seeds 7 and 42 for 75 years. It inhabits the first holder, queues work from `Sim.opportunities` on some days, argues one cause, sets one wish at a moving round, and goes on as a successor at each death. It answers each offer of marriage that waits for it (VB-293), refusing one, and wishes for a match once (VB-274). Report the pawns played and how each followed the last (heir, kin, household, tie, or a break), the causes argued and the meetings' outcomes, the offers answered, refused and left to their due time, and how the line's households fared against unplayed households (hungry days, farms held, children).
- [x] The same played run, saved and loaded every 5 years (with `st.player`), ends with the same golden hashes as the unbroken played run. The same holds for populous medium over 5 years, saved every year, with no player.
- [x] Every job a pawn takes in those runs goes through a command (counted by source: planner, player, round, meeting, debug). The played pawn, for one game day with the automatic behaviour off, does only what the queue and the dark told it.
- [x] Cost: with no player, the golden file and counters are as before the slice. With a player, the game day at small-y40 and medium-y5 is reported against unplayed, target within 1%, with the residual (and its cause) if out. `Sim.opportunities` is reported in ms a call there (target under 1 ms; at `medium-ref`), with the residual. Report save sizes and times (VB-214) and the record's size per decade, and propose limits for any that grows faster than linearly.
- [x] The budget table (`docs/bench/budget.md`) gains slice 2's line: 0 ms a game day of simulation.
