## Implementation (18 November 2026)
Cause: the test's waiting, not the page. A camera glide (`flyTo`, `src/page/cards.js`) lasts 1.1 s of frame time, and
`src/page/frame.js` counts a frame for at most 50 ms; under 20 frames a second, as headless on a shared machine, the glide outlasts
the clock, and the test read the camera after fixed waits (1.8 s after Play as, 1.5 s after Show on map), mid-glide. All three
messages are that one reading: Play as and Show on map read short of their tile, and the People sheet's "before" was a camera still
gliding, so its "after" differed.

Fix, in `tests/browser.js` only: the test hook gains `gliding()` (whether a glide is on), and the three checks wait for the glide
to end (`glided()`) before reading the camera. No check or threshold changed: within 1 tile of the farm, a move of at most 1 world
unit at the sheet, within a tile of the project's tile. No file under `src/` changed, so no screenshot.

Not measured: the frame rate during the failing runs; the cause is inferred from the code and from the outcome below.
