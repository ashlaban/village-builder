# VB-1406: Refactor: one due-queue helper (sim/due) for the sorted queues
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: none (code structure). docs/architecture/target-v2.md sections 2 and 4.1; docs/cost.md (Rules for cost, 4)
Goal: Eight modules each hand-roll a list sorted by due time: st.workDue and st.legDue (sim/agents, two near-identical binary searches, slotOf and legSlot), st.plans.due (sim/plans, a sort after each change), st.asking (sim/wishes), st.visits (sim/visits), st.offers (sim/offers), st.occasions (sim/occasions), st.obligationsDue (sim/obligations). One pure helper holds the search, the insert, the removal and the taking of what is due; each queue stays the state of its owner, in its saved shape.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `sim/due` is a pure helper in the kernel layer (no state of its own, no domain import, no randomness): where an entry stands in a list sorted by a due key and a tie key, its insert, its removal, and the taking of the entries due by a time. It allocates nothing on the paths `st.workDue` and `st.legDue` use (the step's hot path): no closure or array made per call.
- [x] Each of the eight queues uses it where its order is one the helper gives (by due then id; or by due, after those already due at the same time), and keeps its owner, its `@state` line and its saved shape: no change to `src/owners.json` writers, to `src/sim/saves.js` or to a save's leaves. A queue whose order or cadence the helper cannot give without changing behaviour stays as it is, and the task file says which and why in a line.
- [x] `slotOf` and `legSlot` of `sim/agents` are gone; no module is left with its own binary search over a due list.
- [x] Behaviour is unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` at the defaults and at `--scenario founders --seeds 7 --map small --years 3`; both lines are in the result. `node tests/counters.js` is unchanged.
- [x] A focused test on constructed lists (ties, removal of an absent entry, an empty list, the take) with its line in `tests/suites.js`; `@sys`, `src/manifest.json`, `docs/architecture/modules.md` name the module; `npm run format` and `npm run verify` pass; the lint and width baselines do not rise.
