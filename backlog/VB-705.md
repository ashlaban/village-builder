# VB-705: Standing is earned by deeds, by every pawn: holding a farm is one deed among others
Status: done (2026-11-23)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (1, 13, 16); design.md, Exchange, goodwill and obligations; People, households and family records
Goal: Standing is the sum of a pawn's public deeds, each a named term that fades, with holding a farm one of them; a gift seen by others is a deed.
