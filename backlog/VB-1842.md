# VB-1842: A task at a tile on a pawn's own plan is dated, at risk and taken back as a task by key
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: plans
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md section 7, Plans at four scales (A step at risk, and the take-back)
Touches: sim/authored (weighed, byOf, reviewAuthored); sim/estimate (estimate)
Goal: A step its author cannot finish in time is not lost silently, whatever its kind
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A task at a tile on a pawn's own plan (a chain of `sim/planchains`: the foundation, the barn, the farmstead) is estimated by its head from the steps its chain has left, so that the verb `plan`'s add and date check it against its `by` and an undated one is given a `by`, as a task by key is (`sim/authored`, `weighed`, `byOf`).
- [x] At the household's review such a task is at risk when its estimate says it is not done by its `by`, and taken back past it unless kept, as a task by key (`reviewAuthored`); a test on a small constructed state shows both.
- [x] Cost: the chain's estimate read only for the planners' chain tasks at the review (`riskCheck`), none in a run with nothing authored.

Noted by VB-1755, 26 November 2026.

Decisions: a goal task is estimated by Estimate.goalEstimate (sum of goalSteps, as agenda goalParts); an undated one waiting on a window (crop to ripen, hay to dry) is dated from that window day, else a harvest was taken back at once. A taken-back goal step leaves once goalGone says so. tests/plan.js tasks that never finish now keep.
