# VB-2045: A seed loan lent in the crop short
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Loans (Loans of seed); backlog/VB-1973.md
Touches: sim/loans (seedShort); sim/gifts (fetchGift); sim/offers (delivery)
Goal: Seed lent for a sowing is of the crop the fields want
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A seed loan names the crop short (rye for a rye shortfall): the lender gives that crop where it holds it, so the seed lent can be sown; a loan of grain keeps the lender's proportion.
- [x] Measured: populous small seeds 7, 11 and 42, 10 years: seedLentFreed (before to after; lent, sown): 7: 562 to 435 (2000, 1428 to 2270, 1805); 11: 2280 to 1570 (4340, 2020 to 3850, 2270); 42: 1680 to 770 (4090, 2410 to 3760, 2960).

Noted by VB-1973, 28 November 2026.

Decisions: seedShort returns `by` (the short of each crop); the settled loan's deliver carries `crops` (cropsFor: each crop short in order, whole rations, at most its short), fetchGift takes those from 'grain/<crop>' first and the rest of the sack's rounding in the lender's proportion; a grain loan is unchanged. Much is still freed unsown (seed 11: 1570 of 3850): the cause is not the crop, left undone.
