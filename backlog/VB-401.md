# VB-401: Farm names, batch 3: position, feature and age generators, drawn by weight
Status: done (2026-11-12)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: Naming of farms; backlog/VB-399.md
Goal: More kinds of farm name, grounded in the customs of Swedish village farms, and a village whose farms mix them.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Three new farm generators in `src/sim/names.js`: `position` (Övergården, Uppgården and Nedergården by 0.08 of height above or below the village centre; Mellangården, Hemgården and Innergården within 1 step of it; Yttergården and Bortgården 3 steps and more out), `feature` (what lies at the house: Strandgården, Sjögården, Kärrgården, Myrgården, Backgården, Bergsgården, Stengården, Hällgården, Lundgården, Skogsgården, Ängsgården, Hagagården, Vallgården) and `age` (Nygården and Nybygget for a farm built in play; Storgården for the farm of most fields in its village, Lillgården for that of fewest, Gammelgården for any old one).
- [x] `compass` offers the direction only; the middle farm went to `position` and the new farm to `age`. `owner` takes a woman's name bare (Ingridgården), as the villages do (VB-400).
- [x] The order of the generators is drawn by weight for each farm (Kim): a culture's `namePrefs.farm` stays a list, the preferred first, and the first of n weighs n, the last 1 (`farmOrder`); every culture starts with compass, owner, position, landmark, feature, age. A lone farm is still named by its surroundings alone.
- [x] The landmark words corrected: `Berga` among the hills, `Bråten` and `Hult` out (Dungen, Svedjan), `Bryggan` out (a croft's), `Vallen` in, the prefix `Ros` out (gentry of the 1600s).
- [x] Seeded farms are named from a stream of the house tile's own, as farms built in play are; the layout's stream is drawn as it was (`drawnFarm`), so only names change in `tests/golden.json`.
- [x] Tests in `tests/names.js`: each generator against the ground, the height and the steps; a given order; the weights over 2,100 orders; no village of 6 farms named by one kind alone.
- 12 Nov 2026 (session with Kim): farms drawn by weight, as villages; names may change in the golden file.
