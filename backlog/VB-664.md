# VB-664: The panels' shared pieces: icons for goods and traits, one meter, one household view
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Principles (Like is shown alike, pictures before words); the UI review of 16 November (this task's notes)
Goal: Things of one kind are shown one way, and by icon, image or meter before text: the pieces every panel task builds on.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A meter, one component of the page (`src/page/`): a level on its scale, with marks for a target or a threshold (the custom's and the head's target on a store; the means asked of a home), and a signed form about a centre. Every level the panels show uses it; an exception is named, with its motive, in the task's notes. The scale is the meter's argument: it shows today's ranges until VB-665 settles them, and changes with one line then.
- [ ] Icons for goods (`src/page/icons.js`, or drawn from the map's own art as `page/portraits` does): grain, sheaves, hay, firewood, stone, clay, ore, peat, wool, dried fish, tar, water, and the cart and the boat. A stat row (icon, value, meter where it is a level, the word as its title and label for a screen reader) replaces the sentences of the household's stores.
- [ ] Icons for the twelve traits, one a trait, shown on the pawn's card for the traits `Sim.traitLabels` names today (traits are not hidden: Kim, 16 November); the word is the icon's title. Whether every trait is shown, and how its strength, is decided here and written in the notes.
- [ ] One household view: the household's detail (members, stores, fields and work, animals and larder) is on its map card, as tabs; the People sheet becomes a list for finding a person, farm or village, each row opening its card (Kim, 16 November). A member's row has one layout wherever it is listed. Food is counted in one unit on the card, the sheet and the played pawn's sheet.
- [ ] One control for one meaning: a tab, a filter and an act do not share the pill; "show on the map" is the target icon everywhere; red is kept for a warning (the action menu's "Doing it now" and "Taken" take another colour).
- [ ] Work kinds whose icons cannot be told apart in the action menu (hoe, rake, grind, cart manure) get icons of their own, or the reason they share one is noted.
- [ ] The tile and its region's numbers ("Tile 60, 75 in region 53") leave the player's cards for Debug; "Child (barn)" no longer reads as the building (VB-255's barn).
- [ ] Desktop only (Kim, 16 November): the phone's layout is not made worse, and is not designed here. The page only reads `st` (lint); `node tests/smoke.js`, the `tests/browser*.js` touched; screenshots before and after under `docs/img/` (the farm card, the pawn card, the People sheet).
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`.
Cost: the page's only; a card reads one household when open.
