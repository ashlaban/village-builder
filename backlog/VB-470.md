Goal: Help between households is an act the player can make and receive, with a size in hours.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A head whose estimate says a task will not be finished in its window with its own hands asks named households of its village for hands and hours ("two hands for half a day"), nearest kin and best friends first; a verb in `src/sim/commands.js`, so the player as head asks the same way.
- [ ] The head asked answers by kinship, its opinion of the asker and its own day (its members' believed hours not given to its own tasks); it may give fewer hours than asked. The player as head is asked, and answers, through the same verb.
- [ ] A helper's hours are a leg like any other, on the helper basis; goodwill and the tie are booked as today (VB-106), by the hours given.
- [ ] The helper round's fallback goes: no bid on a neighbour's job that has waited two hours, no noise; `MATCH.help.waitHours`, `MATCH.noise` and `MATCH.join` go where nothing reads them.
- [ ] Numbers before and after at populous small, seeds 7 and 42: helpers' hours a household-year, households helped, ties formed by help, hungry household-days, crops lost to frost.
