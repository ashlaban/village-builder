# VB-470: Help is asked for
Status: done (2026-11-23)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/review-jobs.md sections 4.1, 6 B; design.md section 7 (Help between households), section 9
Goal: Help between households is an act the player can make and receive, with a size in hours.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A head whose estimate says a task will not be finished in its window with its own hands asks named households of its village for hands and hours ("two hands for half a day"), nearest kin and best friends first; a verb in `src/sim/commands.js`, so the player as head asks the same way.
- [x] The head asked answers by kinship, its opinion of the asker and its own day (its members' believed hours not given to its own tasks); it may give fewer hours than asked. The player as head is asked, and answers, through the same verb.
- [x] A helper's hours are a leg like any other, on the helper basis; goodwill and the tie are booked as today (VB-106), by the hours given.
- [x] The helper round's fallback goes: no bid on a neighbour's job that has waited two hours, no noise; `MATCH.help.waitHours`, `MATCH.noise` and `MATCH.join` go where nothing reads them.
- [x] Numbers before and after at populous small, seeds 7 and 42: helpers' hours a household-year, households helped, ties formed by help, hungry household-days, crops lost to frost.

- 23 Nov 2026 (VB-470 built): `sim/hands` (new): the basis `help` of sim/offers (the ask is the `offer` verb, Kim 16 Nov), `st.handsToday` (today's lent hands), `askFor`, `shortOf`. The ask is made at the morning's allotment, once a day, for today's hours: the asked heads' days are allotted by then, so each answers by its own (`plan.day.free`, new: the horizon hours the head gave each member no task).
- The shortfall: a household's windowed field work (hoeing and sowing to its crop's sowing window, reaping and carrying to the frost), its windows in order, against the work its hands were given in the horizon kept up to each window's end; help kinds without a window (weeding, threshing, mending) are not asked for.
- The answer: willing by HELP.factor + kin + opinion > 0 (the round's weight, kept); it lends adults of a field-work role with free hours, the most free first, for the hours asked or fewer. A no for want of hands is not remembered; an unwilling no is (VB-690); neither is logged unless the player is one of the two (record cost). The played head asked has 3 hours to answer.
- A lent idling pawn is told at once and stops idling; one with nothing of the asker's to take goes back to its own day. Its hours are booked as it works and, at the end, the unreckoned block's; its tie grows by MATCH.help.tie per `tieHours` 4 (new) given, at the end of the help, in place of per task.
- `MATCH.noise` and `MATCH.help.waitHours` are gone; `MATCH.join` stays: the head's allotment reads it (the spread of hands, VB-102). Without the round, a member the day left out (commanded, away) had no work when free again: `takeDay` now allots its day anew (tests/player.js).
