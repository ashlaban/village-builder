# VB-336: The ox team's advantage: an ard tills several times faster than a hoe
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (Goods and the farming year: Field work, Tilling power). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Field work (11 Nov)
Goal: A team of two oxen makes the first hand at the hoe or at breaking 2.5 times as fast (`HERD.team.rate`, design: "perhaps 2–3 times faster than a hoe", Agreed). On three fields that saves about 30 hours a year, against wintering two oxen on hay; 7 of 53 households of three fields kept a team (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, year 3). Kim, 11 Nov: the advantage can be raised.
