# VB-174: Household and village food in `sim/stores`, every movement recorded
Status: done
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: ownership
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (code structure). VB-172's map, `docs/architecture/ownership.md`, sections 5.3, 5.7 and 7.1; Kim's answers of 3 November (`QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-172: the ownership map", questions 1 to 3 and 7).
Goal: Household and village food has ten writer modules and no owner, and only harvests, gifts, meat, milk and butter leave a record. Give it one owner, a new module `sim/stores`, through which every other module changes food, and record every movement in the ledger, so a household's balance can be read from it (VB-092, `tools/balance.js`). Kim chose to record everything in this task (answer 3), so it changes behaviour on purpose: the golden file is re-recorded, and only the ledgers may change in it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `src/sim/stores.js` (`VB['sim/stores']`, an `@sys` header, under 400 lines), listed in `src/manifest.json` after `sim/records` and before `sim/hunger`. It owns `household.grain`, `rations`, `unthreshed`, `ledger` and `spoilt`; `village.grain`; and paid `pawn.stake`; owed shares and their debtor remain obligations in heirs/cottage (later target-v2 decision below).
- [x] Operations, each naming why: `put(st, holder, good, n, why)`, `take(st, holder, good, n, why)` (returns what it took, never more than is held), `move(st, from, to, good, n, why)` (what one loses the other gains), `cook(st, hh, n)`, `sheaf(st, hh, n)`, `thresh(st, hh)`. A holder is a household, a village or a pawn. A negative or non-finite amount, or a dissolved household, throws: it is a bug in the caller.
- [x] The rules stay where they are and call the operations: meals and the feed order (`sim/hunger`), gifts and the village store (`sim/help`), the kinds' sow, cook, haul and thresh (`sim/kinds`), shares and handing on (`sim/heirs`), owed shares (`sim/cottage`), provisions (`sim/farmclaims`, `sim/settle`), grain spoiling (`spoilGrain`), and the village store's spoilage (`clock.js:62`, which moves out of the clock; Kim's answer 7). Pruning the ledger to three years (`clock.js:107`) moves too.
- [x] `node tools/writes.js --field <f>` lists only `sim/stores` for each owned field, apart from seeding (`sim/scenarios`) and the save codec.
- [x] Every movement writes a ledger entry `{t, kind, n, ...}` on each side: the kinds already there (`harvest`, `gift`, `meat`, `milk`, `butter`) unchanged, and new ones for seed, meal, cooking, spoilage, share (with the pawn), handing on and provisions. The village store gets a ledger of its own (`village.ledger`, pruned like a household's); a pawn's stake and owed share are entries in its household's ledger naming the pawn.
- [x] Conservation, as a new test (`tests/stores.js`, in `npm test`): for founders 7 and populous 7 (tiny map) over 3 years, each household's change in grain, rations and sheaves over a year equals the sum of its ledger entries for that year, within 1e-6 per entry; the same for each village store.
- [x] Invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): every ledger entry has a known kind, a finite `n` and a time no later than `st.t`; `village.ledger` exists. `@state` lines updated.
- [x] The golden file is re-recorded, and the PR lists the changed entries: only the `households` and `villages` keys change, and `node tests/golden.js --dump` shows only ledger lines differing. `tools/sim.js` (founders 7 small, 10 years; populous 7 small, 8 years) prints the same yearly lines before and after. `tools/balance.js` gives the same numbers.
- [x] Costs reported: ledger entries a household a year, the save size, and the verify time, before and after, at populous 7 small after 8 years.
- [x] Impact note in the PR (a new module and a new state field); `npm run verify` passes.

Implementation report (6 November 2026):
- Stores owns grain, rations, sheaves, paid stakes and both food ledgers. Every
  call names its good and reason. Owed shares remain in heirs/cottage under the
  later target-v2 decision, rather than becoming goods.
- Nutrition, production, gifts, shares and provision rules retain their order.
  Village spoilage and annual pruning move out of the clock. Old format-1 saves
  gain empty village ledgers; no historical transactions are invented.
- Conservation: 22,311 food entries reconcile annually over three years at
  founders 7 and populous 7 tiny. Invalid quantities and dissolved recipients
  throw; transfers are capped and conversions preserve combined food.
- Canonical golden dumps change only ledger lines: household/village hashes
  after years 1–3, plus village hashes at seeding, for founders/heirs seeds 7/42.
  No pawn, world, calibration or public-API hash changes. Only the new storeWalk
  counter is added; existing counter and cost baselines are unchanged.

  apart from measured timing. The eight-year balance JSON is identical for all
  household-years. Decoded eight-year states match after excluding ledgers.

  Raw save bytes: 8,701,753 → 13,030,614; gzip: 1,671,326 → 2,218,225.

  three owed-share sites leave the goods-only boundary. No exemption is added.
