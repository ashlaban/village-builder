# VB-634: Service by the year: a servant has no home to marry into
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, People, households and family records (Marriage, nursing and growth: Service and marriage); Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Leaving home: Service); backlog/VB-405.md
Goal: A servant does not marry while in service (Kim, 13 Nov): service is by the year, from round to round, the master's house is no home to bring a spouse to, and a servant marries at the round by leaving service for a place. No share of servants and no count of years is set.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] No home in the master's house: `matchRules`' `homeFor` gives no home where the home it would choose is one in which that spouse lives as a servant (`pawn.servant`), under every residence custom (under "with whoever holds land" a servant already has no place; under "at the husband's home" and "at the wife's home" the master's household was returned). `matchBar` says why: "Olof is in service: his master's house is no home for a couple". A servant marries at the round by leaving service for the spouse's place or a place of its own, as today; a test shows the servant flag is cleared by that move.
- [x] The year: `pawn.servedSince` (game hours; set where a pawn enters service, null otherwise and on leaving; `@state`, `src/owners.json` under the owner of `pawn.servant`, the save, an old save gaining the time of its load for those in service) with its invariant (`tests/lib/invariants.js`: set exactly when `servant` is true). Scenario servants are seeded as serving since the start.
- [x] Not within the year: `matchBar` takes the occasion, and for an offer out of the round it refuses a pawn in service ("in service until the moving round"). The round's own offers are unchanged. VB-555's offer out of the round (a child on the way) reads this bar; if VB-555 has not landed, the bar and its test are here and a note in `backlog/VB-555.md` names it; if it has, its due entry waits for the round and the child may be born outside marriage, which is reported.
- [ ] The pawn panel of a servant says since when it serves and that it marries by leaving service; `node tests/smoke.js`.
  Panel line written (src/page/words.js); `tests/smoke.js` fails in this sandbox at `win.cam` undefined (line 71), before reaching the panel: not run through.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (Working rules), populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and heirs small, 30 years, seeds 7, 42: by the residence custom in force, marriages a year per 1,000; married pawns living as servants in a master's household (the leak this closes; if it is none before, say so: the change is then a guard, and the other numbers are unchanged); servants as a share of those of 18 to 30; the mean age at first marriage of those who ever served against the others; growth. Whether households are better off: the hands a field of the households that kept, and lost, a servant to marriage. Hungry household-days as a description only (`backlog/VB-485.md`).
  Not taken as named (30 years, four seeds, heirs): populous small at 15 years, seeds 7 and 42, as the run asked; the rest is owed to VB-638. See the notes.
