# VB-1115: The harvest window as the head's estimate: frost takes the crop left standing, and says so
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: crops
Phase: implement
Design ref: Crops and rotations (Crops and the weather); Principles for decisions (The future is someone's estimate)
Goal: A harvest lost for want of hands reads as that, and the player can see it coming.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The frost's line (`fieldsFrost`, `src/sim/fields.js`) says what was lost and how it stood: "Frost took the barley at Östergården, ripe and not yet reaped" (or "in stooks", or "still growing"), one line a farm, not one a field.
- [ ] The played household's sheet shows the head's estimate (principle 14, the pawn's own reckoning) once a crop is ripe: the fields still to reap against the days of work before the frost, by the hands it has ("two fields standing; frost in about 3 days"), and says whose estimate it is.
- [ ] A loss to frost on the played household's farm stops the skip.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state: a farm with two ripe fields and one hand on autumn's last day loses both; one line, naming them as ripe and unreaped.
