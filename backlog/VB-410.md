# VB-410: A cart is drawn: beside the house, and with the pawn that has it out
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Carts
Goal: A player can see whether a farm has a cart, which kind, and when it is on the road
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A hand cart or an ox cart stands by the house of a farm whose household has one home (`household.carts`, a cart whose pawn is at no job with it), drawn inside the farm's own tile, as the yard and the woodpile are; a farm with several shows them.
- [ ] A pawn out with a cart is drawn with it: pushing or drawing the hand cart, walking beside the oxen and the ox cart; the load on it reads as what the pawn carries (sheaves, hay, logs). The cart by the house is gone meanwhile.
- [ ] A cart below the condition at which it is mended (`CART.mendAt`) looks worn.
- [ ] The household's card names its carts and their condition (`src/page/words.js`, as it names the fishing boat).
- [ ] The page only reads `st`: which cart is out is read as `sim/carts` reads it (a read exported from the module, not a second rule in the page).
- [ ] `node tests/smoke.js` passes, with a check that a farm with a cart draws one; a screenshot of a village with carts is in the PR (`node tools/village-shot.js`).

Notes (the distance review, 13 November): carts were built without art (`src/sim/carts.js`; design.md, Carts, "Not yet"). The art is
drawn on canvases in `src/page/farmart.js` (the yard, the stooks, the byre); a pawn's load is `a.carry` (`sheaf`, `hay`, `logs`), and
a load above `TRAVEL.load` means a cart. Dung is carted with no carried load (`KINDS.manure.carted`): the cart stands on the field then.
