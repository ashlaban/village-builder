Status: needs-review
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Carts
Goal: A player can see whether a farm has a cart, which kind, and when it is on the road
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A hand cart or an ox cart stands by the house of a farm whose household has one home (`household.carts`, a cart whose pawn is at no job with it), drawn inside the farm's own tile, as the yard and the woodpile are; a farm with several shows them.
- [x] A pawn out with a cart is drawn with it: pushing or drawing the hand cart, walking beside the oxen and the ox cart; the load on it reads as what the pawn carries (sheaves, hay, logs). The cart by the house is gone meanwhile.

- [x] The household's card names its carts and their condition (`src/page/words.js`, as it names the fishing boat).
- [x] The page only reads `st`: which cart is out is read as `sim/carts` reads it (a read exported from the module, not a second rule in the page).
- [x] `node tests/smoke.js` passes, with a check that a farm with a cart draws one; a screenshot of a village with carts is in the PR (`node tools/village-shot.js`).
