- 18 Nov 2026, implementation (VB-1106):
  - **The rule: the age of account, `SOCIAL.account` = 12.** A child below it cannot quarrel, and nobody holds its nature against
    it. Of the two the criterion offers, "cannot quarrel" rather than "remembered more lightly": a lighter memory would be a second
    scale of the same thing with nothing for the head or the player to decide by it (principle 7), and a scolding leaves no grudge.
    In `quarrelMaybe`, a pair with one below the age returns before the draw: no memory, no event; the talk is still cross words,
    with its bubble. In `opinionAt`, between such a pair chemistry counts only where it is liking (the affinity term is not below
    0), both ways: this is what the note of 16 November asks ("dislike between an adult and a small child however it arises"),
    since Gunnar disliked Jon, 3, by "Do not get on" alone. Standing, memories of things that happened and gratitude count as
    between any two. The rule holds for any pair with one below the age, two children as well: one rule, read from `pawn.age`.
  - **Why 12**, not 7 (canon law's age of reason): the playtest's pairs are of 3, 5, 6 and twice 8, and the criterion asks that
    they no longer dislike or quarrel; 12 is the lawful age of the Norse and Anglo-Saxon laws, below which a child answered for
    nothing it did. A physical fact of childhood (principle 8: hard limits are for physical facts), so a rule and no custom.
  - **Why chemistry weighs on a parent and child at all** (found, not changed): `compat` takes up to 0.4 off for the age gap, the
    whole of it from 25 years, so every parent and child lose 0.24 of opinion, nearly the 0.3 their standing gives. From 12 this
    still holds: in the after run a child of 12 "Became the enemy of Gunnar Olofsson (46)" in year 13, the year the rule let go.
    Left for VB-1125 (noted in the inbox).
  - **The margin, `SOCIAL.back`: 0.1 to 0.2.** The hysteresis was there (VB-190) and too narrow: a tie halves every year and a
    "Worked together" memory fades in one, so opinion swings by more than 0.1 with the seasons. At 0.2 a close friendship is left at
    the friend line (0.45 − 0.2 = 0.25: no longer close friends when no longer friends) and an enemy at −0.3. Tried, populous small
    seed 7, 20 years, with the age of account in: 0.1, 169 of 851 tellings a second telling of the same pair; 0.15, 49 of 626; 0.2,
    7 of 527 (close friends 147, 40, 7). Three runs of the five.
