Goal: The player sees what makes a place a market place and what a household lives by, as meters and icons before words (principle 15), with every estimate named as someone's (principle 14).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The settlement card shows the place's three measures (outsiders, dealing days, work that is not farming) as meters, the same meter as everywhere (principle 15), and its label as an icon and a word ("market place"), with since when; a village with no label shows the measures alone.
- [ ] The household card shows its ladder label (side craft, craft household, specialist) as an icon, and its food by source as one bar (the head's memory, VB-767), with the head's estimate named as the head's (principle 14).
- [ ] A map layer of where dealing happened in the last year: greens by worth dealt, and the walks to doors where VB-770 exists.
- [ ] The strata as a stacked graph by decade (the work line; heads beside it; household work apart), in the island's report.
- [ ] The chronicle calls a day with much dealing a market ("A market at Ängby's rite: 14 households dealt"); crowding is explained where it binds ("crowded: 44 people within 2 steps").
- [ ] The page only reads `st` and the derived views. `node tests/smoke.js` and the `tests/browser*.js` touched; a screenshot under `docs/img/`.
- Cost: drawn on opening a card or the report; nothing in a step.
