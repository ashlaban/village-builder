# VB-824: Succession is offered and taken up: the holder hands on, the heir answers
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: acts
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Succession: Handed on, and taken up; Handover and shares); docs/plans/VB-682.md, section 4.6
Goal: A handover in life is the holder's offer, which the first in line answers; an inheritance at a death is taken up or renounced by the heir; no farm changes hands by the calendar.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] In life: at the time the timing custom gives (the heir's marriage; the holder at 60), the holder offers `handover` (VB-690) to the first of `heirRanking`, due at the round, giving the holding and wanting its keep on the farm for life (retired there, as built). The round's `handover` loop over a village's farms is replaced by the offers due. A played holder may offer at any time, by the same verb.
- [ ] The heir answers: yes, unless it holds a farm, lives on a spouse's, or is betrothed to a place (VB-825, if landed) with more fields; a no passes the offer to the next in line at once, and books no memory. The passing-over rules of the ranking (`ownFirst`, "keeps their place") are unchanged, and a test shows the answers give the same heir as the ranking does today in each of its cases.
- [ ] At a death: the inheritance falls at once to the first in line (a right), as `succession` does today. The heir takes it up by `move`, reason `heir` (VB-822), with spouse and children, or renounces it when it holds a place with more fields, and it falls to the next; with nobody to take it the farm stands empty, as today. A minor heir is not asked: the guardian rule stands (VB-695).
- [ ] Shares, the reserve and what is owed are untouched (Handover and shares).
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7 and 42: handovers a decade by reason (marriage, age, death), offers refused and renounced inheritances, farms left empty, holders of 60 or more still holding. With no played pawn the same heirs are expected; differences are listed with their cause.
- [ ] `tests/commands.js` and `tests/record.js` pass; tests on a constructed state: an heir with a larger farm refuses and the next takes it; a played holder hands on early; a death with a renouncing heir.
