# VB-147: Settlers join small villages nearby before founding
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: expansion
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion, Moving to new land (Where: Decided, 1 Nov). Review: `docs/review-expansion.md`.
Goal: Settlers who leave a crowded village (VB-146) should prefer a small village nearby that has room, so a lone farm grows into a hamlet by followers, before they found yet another farm. Depends on VB-146 (done).
Acceptance criteria (Kim, 1 November 2026: any village of 1 to 3 farms that is not crowded, including the generated lone farms):
- [ ] Before founding, a settler looks for a village other than their own, with 1 to 3 farmsteads, whose centre is within 30 walking steps of their village's centre, and that is not crowded: `findSite` returns a site, none of its own adults is in `st.demand` without a plot, and fewer than 2 plots and booths are under way there.
- [ ] Of these, villages where a living near kin of the settler (`kinNear`) lives come first, then the nearest by walking steps, then the lowest id.
- [ ] The party builds a booth on the house tile `findSite` gives and takes its field tile as the field project, as in VB-146, in that village; the log says they join it.
- [ ] Provisions as in VB-146.
- [ ] A test: a crowded village with a lone farm 15 steps away with room: the settler joins the lone farm's village; with the lone farm crowded, the settler founds.
- [ ] Populous small at seeds 7 and 42, 30 years: farms founded and farms added to small villages, by decade (reported).
