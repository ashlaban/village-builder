Goal: A player who plans by hand sets the house on stone and raises a barn, each by one task of the plan.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The ladder's shape follows Kim: the foundation is not a rung of the main building but a property of it (`farmstead.grade`, as today: `ground` or `stone`): `RUNGS` loses `stone`, and `Sim.farmView` gains `foundation: {grade, needs {stone, hours}, ok, why, planned, building}` beside `rung`, `next` and `outbuildings`; a house of any rung from the cottage up may be set on stone, and keeps it when built up.
- [ ] Prestige: find what the sim has for a household's or a house's standing (`HOUSE`'s standing term, sim/house; rank, sim/society) and say in the notes what a stone foundation gives today (wear 0.08 against 0.1, the standing term 0.2 against 0.15) and whether that already is the prestige Kim means; if nothing reads the standing term as prestige, propose in the notes (a `question` note for Kim, with a proposed answer) rather than invent a mechanism.
- [ ] Plan tasks: `Sim.plan(st, 'add', {foundation: houseTile})` carries the stone's gathering (picked or quarried, as `stoneWork` posts it) and the raising through; `Sim.plan(st, 'add', {outbuilding: houseTile, what: 'barn'})` carries the barn's stages and timber through (the timber at home first, as the cottage's, VB-1632). Each refused with the reason where the head's own check refuses; each with its `waits` (the raising in the slack of the year is a real wait: name it; say whether the played head's own plan may raise outside the slack as VB-1632 decided for the cottage); `which` values; laid into `Sim.agenda`.
- [ ] One rule for every household (Kim): unplayed heads set their house on stone and raise their barn by their own rule exactly as today (`node tools/same.js --base REF --scenario founders,solo`); with automatic behaviour off the played head begins neither except by its tasks.
- [ ] Tests on small constructed states (a line in `tests/suites.js` for a new script); a save and a load in the middle go on the same; `npm run verify` passes; baselines do not rise.
