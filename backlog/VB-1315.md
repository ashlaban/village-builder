# VB-1315: The keep (undantag): an obligation of the new holder, met at the table, pressed when short
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Inheritance (The retired); docs/plans/VB-1122.md, section 3
Goal: A retired pawn's keep is the new holder's obligation, which the retired can press when it is not met.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `handOn` books an obligation of kind `keep` (`sim/obligations`) from the new holder's household to each retired pawn (the old holder and its spouse): one adult's rations, `KEEP.wood` loads a winter, one cow's milk where the farm has cows; ended at the pawn's death. Met at the table while it lives in the house: no goods move (the farmstead is one place).
- [ ] A day short: a retired member hungry on a day the household's store held its ration (counted in the hunger step's walk of members, no new pass). `KEEP.shortDays` in a year press the keep: the memory "Kept me short" (−0.2, 3 years) of the holder, and the case at the next meeting by the pressed debt's procedure.
- [ ] Pressed `KEEP.reclaim` years running, the meeting's verdict may give the farm back to the old holder (the close call of the plan, section 8, noted in the inbox or the PR).
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state: the keep booked at a handover and ended at a death; a household that eats while its retired goes hungry presses the keep and the case is on the agenda. Founders small 50 years seeds 7 and 42: keeps pressed and cases a decade, reported.
- [ ] Invariants and a `tools/stocks.js` line where the keep's reservation is a stored quantity; cost per the plan, section 6.
