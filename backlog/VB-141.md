Goal: Customs candidates from the design and the review: grazing on the commons (by land share, open, or by the meeting), hunting rights, how hay from common meadows is shared, how hard to stock for winter ("to the hay" or "keep the herd"), and herding by each farm or by turns for the village (*byahjord*). Slice 5 adds land rights (VB-311): grazing on the village's outland and commons is the right `graze`, held by the village and granted to its households by the custom, so a household's share of the commons can follow the land it owns (and a landed household's share grow with it).
