# VB-143: Heir rankings cached in the moving round, and the yearly ties without a whole-island search
Status: done (2026-10-31, c8fae47)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: tooling
Phase: implement
Design ref: Vision and scope (up to about 1,000 pawns); Systems overview (Across systems: cost; a budget per system). Review: `docs/review-balance.md` (31 October 2026), section 1.
Goal: The moving round still took about 1.3 s in one step on populous medium at seed 7 in year 1, and the meetings 60–120 ms. In the round's marriage match the woman's place (`homeFor`'s default argument `placeIfMarried(st, f.id)`) and `kinPressure`'s checks ranked every farm's heirs for every pair of candidates, each ranking ranking all other farms again; `wantsOut` and the handover loop did the same per pawn and per household. In the meetings `updateTies` searched up to 160 steps from every village and scanned every pawn once per village. Behaviour must not change.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] In `movingRound`, `placeIfMarried` is cached per pawn with one `ownFirstOf` until the next marriage changes the families; the handover loop computes `ownFirstOf` once and again only after a handover; the demand count passes one `ownFirstOf` to `firstHeirs` and `wantsOut` (a new optional argument), as `st.countDemand` does.
- [x] `updateTies` finds the steps between a village and its culture's centre with `stepsTo` (`src/sim/hex.js`), the same breadth-first search stopping at the target, and groups the living pawns by settlement once.
- [x] The page's smoke loop marks the cooking households in one pass over the agents, not one pass per house.
- [x] Behaviour unchanged: `tests/golden.json` is unchanged, and a 3-year populous medium run at seed 7 hashes the same before and after (pawns, households, unions, demand, log, herds).
Notes / questions:
