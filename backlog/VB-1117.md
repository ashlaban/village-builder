# VB-1117: A father's History holds his children: births and their deaths
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player and what they see (The chronicle); People, households and family records
Goal: A father's life, as the chronicle tells it, has his children in it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A father's History (`Sim.chronicle` for the pawn, `src/sim/notable.js`) tells each of his children's births and each death of a child of his, as the mother's does: by reading the child's father where the event names the child, or by naming the father on the event (`src/sim/births.js`). Decide which, by cost: the chronicle reads only the pawn's events.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state: a father with two children, one of whom dies, has three lines for them.
- [ ] The record's lines are unchanged (`tests/record.js`), unless the event gains the father's id, which is then reported.
