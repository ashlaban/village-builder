- 18 Nov 2026 (VB-693): opened by it. With the matcher gone `roundPairs` is 0, and this is the round's one remaining pass over pairs: for each free man of the village, and each free woman of it, `pool.some(...)` twice (the custom's limit, and the next looser one). It stops at the first partner found, so it is short where partners are many and long where they are few.
