Acceptance criteria:

- [x] Measured on populous small at years 5 and 40 and at `medium-ref`: reads a round, and their share of the round's step.
- [x] If it grows faster than the living (Rules for cost, 2): bounded, e.g. by reading a pawn's candidates nearest first up to `ASK.pool`, as an asking does, the numbers before and after and the kin pushes unchanged on the seeds named.
- 18 Nov 2026 (VB-693): opened by it. With the matcher gone `roundPairs` is 0, and this is the round's one remaining pass over pairs: for each free man of the village, and each free woman of it, `pool.some(...)` twice (the custom's limit, and the next looser one). It stops at the first partner found, so it is short where partners are many and long where they are few.

Done 19 Nov 2026. Counter `kinPairs` (VB.COUNTERS, tests/counters.json). A year loaded from each fixture: small-y5 6 reads (12 villages,
0.015 a living), small-y40 83 (23 villages, 0.107 a living), medium-ref 68 (38 villages, 0.067). It grows faster than the living (7 times a
living from year 5 to 40), but in absolute terms it is 83 can() calls a year against roundWalk's 15,284 records: under 1% of the round's
walk, too small to time apart. Decision: not bounded; the counter's target (+10% against its baseline) watches it.
