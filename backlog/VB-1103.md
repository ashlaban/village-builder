# VB-1103: A cause the played pawn takes up on its own is shown, and may be laid down at once without the wait
Status: done (2026-11-18)
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Customs and culture (Meetings, culture and splits); The player and what they see (The meeting)
Goal: The player learns when its pawn takes a side, and can still choose its own cause.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] When the played pawn takes up a cause by the culture's natural draw (`advocate(..., natural)`, `src/culture.js`, the entrepreneur's draw), a skip stops on it (a new reason of `SKIP`, `src/sim/notable.js`): "Gunnar Olofsson has taken up a cause: a daughter's share, equal to a son's", with the Agenda one click away.
- [x] A cause laid down within its first season (`dropCause`) does not start the wait of `causeFree`: the pawn may take up another at once. The rule is every pawn's (principle 16); a pawn without a player keeps its cause as now, so a run with no player is unchanged (`tools/same.js` prints `same`).
- [x] The Agenda says, for a cause the pawn took up on its own, that it did ("You took this up on your own in year 4, autumn"), and until when it may be laid down without the wait.
- [x] A check on a small constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`, the draw forced with `fix` on its stream): the played pawn takes up a cause; the skip stops; laid down in the season, `argue` for another passes its check; laid down after it, `argue` is refused with the wait as today.
- [x] The other choices the automatic behaviour makes for the played pawn that are not work (a gift by the holder's allowance, a project, a wish at the round, a cause) are listed in the notes, each with whether a skip stops on it and why.

Done notes (VB-1103):
- Decided: the grace is a named tunable `Culture.P.causeGrace` = 0.25 year (a season); a natural cause's event carries `reason: 'natural'` (an existing record field) so skipStop tells it from the player's `argue`.
- Other automatic choices of the played pawn, and whether a skip stops (read from `skipStop`): a gift by the holder's allowance (`gift`): no, not in `stopsFor`, a routine act the notice tells; a project (farm kinds): only by events in its village or household, as before; a wish at the round: yes on the eve, `aids.choice` (VB-1101); an offer it must answer: yes (`offer`); a cause: now yes (`cause`).
- Close call: a cause laid down in its first season frees the wait for every pawn alike; a second switch and an ask-before were weighed and not taken.

Notes / questions:

His son did the same in year 66. The automatic switch is all or nothing: off, the pawn does no work unless every task is queued.

The rule above keeps both. A close call for the inbox.
