# VB-004: Ownership map overlay
Status: done (2026-11-01)
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: claims
Phase: implement
Design ref: Claims, ownership and territory (Overlay: Decided). Kim, 2026-10-29: a simple first version is acceptable. Review: `docs/review-claims.md`.
Goal: A toggle button, "Land", next to Grid and Regions, that tints each owned tile by its owner and shows the claims on the land. Depends on VB-042.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A toolbar button "Land" turns the overlay on and off; off by default.
- [x] Village land takes a light tint in the village's colour and household land a stronger tint in the same colour, outlined in the household's own shade; unowned tiles stay untinted. Unrealised claims show as a dashed edge in the claimant's colour; tiles claimed by two or more show hatched; cairns (VB-157) show as small stone piles on their tiles. Tints stay readable over the terrain at play zoom.
- [x] The overlay reflects ownership and claims changes (`st.landDirty`) on its next redraw and clears the set; a full redraw on change is fine.
- [x] The tile panel lists the claims on a tile: who, why, unrealised or realised, and strength.
- [x] `tools/shot.js`: a reusable screenshot of any scenario, seed and map after some years, with the Land overlay on (Playwright on the built page).
- [x] Smoke test toggles it on and off with no console errors.
Notes / questions:
- Out of scope: filters by entity level (VB-044), chunk caching (VB-045).
- 2026-10-30: VB-003's questions are answered; VB-003 is done.
- 2026-11-01 (claims review): renamed "Land"; claims, contested tiles and cairns added from VB-044, as the outland makes them the overlay's main content; the tile panel's claims moved here from VB-042.
- 2026-11-01 (done): the Land layer sits above the fences and below the grid; one `Graphics`, redrawn whole at most twice a second while `st.landDirty` holds tiles (and cleared without drawing while the overlay is off). A village's colour is a golden-angle hue by id; a household's outline the same hue at its own lightness. `tools/shot.js` takes `--scenario`, `--seed`, `--size`, `--years`, `--land`, `--close N` and `--at X,Y,Z`. Screenshot: `docs/img/claims-land-start.jpg` (populous tiny, seed 7, at the start, before the outland).
