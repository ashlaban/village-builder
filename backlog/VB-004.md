# VB-004: Ownership map overlay
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: claims
Phase: implement
Design ref: Claims, ownership and territory (Overlay: Decided). Kim, 2026-10-29: a simple first version is acceptable. Review: `docs/review-claims.md`.
Goal: A toggle button, "Land", next to Grid and Regions, that tints each owned tile by its owner and shows the claims on the land. Depends on VB-042.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A toolbar button "Land" turns the overlay on and off; off by default.
- [ ] Village land takes a light tint in the village's colour and household land a stronger tint in the same colour, outlined in the household's own shade; unowned tiles stay untinted. Unrealised claims show as a dashed edge in the claimant's colour; tiles claimed by two or more show hatched; cairns (VB-157) show as small stone piles on their tiles. Tints stay readable over the terrain at play zoom.
- [ ] The overlay reflects ownership and claims changes (`st.landDirty`) on its next redraw and clears the set; a full redraw on change is fine.
- [ ] The tile panel lists the claims on a tile: who, why, unrealised or realised, and strength.
- [ ] `tools/shot.js`: a reusable screenshot of any scenario, seed and map after some years, with the Land overlay on (Playwright on the built page).
- [ ] Smoke test toggles it on and off with no console errors.
Notes / questions:
