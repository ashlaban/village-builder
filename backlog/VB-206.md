# VB-206: The meetings spread, so no step goes over 60 ms
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: Customs and culture (the meeting). `docs/roadmap.md`, section 5.3 (rule 5, stagger and spread).
Goal: Once a year, at noon on autumn day 10, one 6-minute step runs every village's meeting and then the land meeting (`holdMeetings` and `landMeeting`, `src/sim/clock.js:29`). `holdMeetings` (`src/sim/lifecycle.js`) works in four stages:
1. it refreshes roles, prestige, interests and ties over the whole population;
2. it picks the year's entrepreneurs;
3. it holds each settlement's meeting;
4. it runs each culture's year.
On 9 November (VB-203, `medium-ref`, a whole game year, three runs) the meetings' call took 38 to 50 ms and its noon step 60 ms in one run, at the limit; at `medium-y5` 33 ms. VB-201 covers the moving round (the next day) and midnight, but not this step. Measure it again here and, if the step is over 60 ms, spread it; it grows with the living, so a step at the limit at 1,000 is over it as the population grows.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Measure first, on `medium-ref` and `small-y40`: the step's time and each part's (the four refreshes, the entrepreneurs, the settlements' meetings, the cultures' years, the land meeting).
- [x] If the step is over 60 ms, its parts are spread over the day's steps, a village per step (rule 5). Progress is kept as plain data in `st`, so a save between steps resumes it. The meeting log and the order of each village's draws are kept. If the whole-population refreshes are the cost, say whether they can run per village or lazily.
- [x] If the order of the culture's draws changes, behaviour changes. Then re-record the golden file, list the changed entries, and report the drift of customs over 20 populous years before and after (`npm run calibrate`), each within the calibration's bands.
- [x] No step of the meeting day over 60 ms on `medium-ref` and `small-y40`; `npm run verify` passes.
