# VB-206: The meetings spread, so no step goes over 60 ms
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-203
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: Customs and culture (the meeting). `docs/roadmap.md`, section 5.3 (rule 5, stagger and spread).
Goal: Once a year, at noon on autumn day 10, one 6-minute step runs every village's meeting and then the land meeting (`holdMeetings` and `landMeeting`, `src/sim/clock.js:29`). `holdMeetings` (`src/sim/lifecycle.js`) works in four stages:
1. it refreshes roles, prestige, interests and ties over the whole population;
2. it picks the year's entrepreneurs;
3. it holds each settlement's meeting;
4. it runs each culture's year.
On 7 November the bench timed this at 41 ms at medium-y5 and 276 ms at medium-y60, over the 60 ms step limit. VB-201 covers the moving round (the next day) and midnight, but not this step. Measure it at the new reference and, if it is over 60 ms, spread it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Measure first, on `medium-ref` and `small-y40`: the step's time and each part's (the four refreshes, the entrepreneurs, the settlements' meetings, the cultures' years, the land meeting).
- [ ] If the step is over 60 ms, its parts are spread over the day's steps, a village per step (rule 5). Progress is kept as plain data in `st`, so a save between steps resumes it. The meeting log and the order of each village's draws are kept. If the whole-population refreshes are the cost, say whether they can run per village or lazily.
- [ ] If the order of the culture's draws changes, behaviour changes. Then re-record the golden file, list the changed entries, and report the drift of customs over 20 populous years before and after (`npm run calibrate`), each within the calibration's bands.
- [ ] No step of the meeting day over 60 ms on `medium-ref` and `small-y40`; `npm run verify` passes.
