# VB-400: Village names, batch 2: founded villages through villageName (mother and founder generators)
Status: done (2026-11-12)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Naming of farms; backlog/VB-399.md
Goal: founderName and its five endings go; founder and mother are generators of villageName, chosen at random by weight.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A village founded in play is named by `villageName` with `ctx = {w, site, founder, mother}`, as a seeded one is (VB-399); `founderName` and its five endings are gone from `src/sim/names.js` and `src/sim/settle.js`.
- [x] `founder`: the founder's given name with an ending of the colonisation layer that fits the site, the endings in a random order by weight: -torp and -böle anywhere, -ryd in wood, -boda on open land, -säter and -mark 12 steps or more from the mother village, -vik, -näs and -ö by the shore as an old village has them. A man's name in the genitive (an `s` unless it ends in s, x or z), a woman's bare (Kim: female founders too; the medieval form, as Gunnilbo).
- [x] `mother`: the by to the north, south, west or east of the mother village (Norrby, Sörby, Väsby, Ösby), then Nyby, the little one of the mother's name (Lillvalby), Nytorp, Nyböle, Utby.
- [x] The order of the two is random by weight (Kim: the founder's preferred, meaning weighed higher, not always first): `VILLAGE.prefs`, founder 3 and mother 1; the site's names come last, when both are taken. An old village (no founder, no mother) draws nothing for the order, so VB-399's names stand.
- [x] The name is drawn from a stream of the site tile's own, never from `st.rng`. Tests: `tests/names.js` (a man's, a woman's and a name in s; the mother's; 3 in 4 by the founder over 400 streams; the site when the others are taken), `tests/settle.js` (the founded village bears one of the offered names).
- 12 Nov 2026 (session with Kim): founder first, female founders too, and "prefer" is a weight, not an order.
- Decided on the way (Deciding without Kim, 3 and 5): the weights 3 and 1 and the distance of 12 steps are named in `VILLAGE` (`src/sim/names.js`); no balance depends on them. -hult went (Småland's, not the archipelago's); -böle came (Uppland's and Åland's).
- Not done: Över- and Ytter- with the mother's name (they want a waterway to lie along); a culture's own weights (`namePrefs` holds the farms' order only).
- Cost (rule 9): one founding, one search of 91 tiles and a flood of at most 250. Nothing in a step.
- Validation: populous tiny, seed 7, 25 years: Elinsäter (from Sandsta, by Elin), Astridmark and Margitryd (from Frötuna), Andersryd (from Sandsta), Cecilianäs (from Bredby). `tests/golden.json` unchanged (no village is founded in its runs). No number, target or household moves: the simulation reads no name, and the naming draws from no stream of `st.rng` (the old one drew from `st.rng.settle` only when a founder's five names were all taken).
