# VB-817: The right of way: a built way across others' holdings is granted by the village and agreed by each holder
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-815
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: roads
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Climate and travel beyond walking (Passage); Land, ecology and rights, Land and rights (Passage; Slice 5's rights: `pass` with built ways); docs/architecture/target-v2.md, section 4.3 (`grant`, `via`); docs/plans/VB-190.md, section 5.5
Goal: Kim's decision 22 (3 Nov): the village grants passage for its land; every other holder must agree. A built way holds the rights it crosses.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A `pass` right in the land rights of VB-311, granted under the holder's `own`: by the meeting's vote for the village's commons and outland; by a household's head for its field, meadow or yard; by the other village's meeting for its land. A facility that is a built way lists them (`via`), and is not begun until it holds one for every tile of its stretch.
- [ ] Asking is an offer from the alderman (or the head who builds) to each holder, answered by that head's own judgement: its estimate of what the way saves its own household (its counted crossings and loads), less what it loses (`WAYS.strip` 5% of a crossed field tile's yield, a named tunable), plus what its goodwill and opinion of the asker allow (the allowance rule as it stands; a favour granted books goodwill as help does). The played head is asked as any head is, through the offers it already sees.
- [ ] A refusal: the alderman takes the next best line that avoids the holding if one saves enough, else the way is not built, and the record says who refused. A right ends with the way; when a holding passes to an heir the right stands.
- [ ] A constructed check: a track over the commons and one neighbour's meadow is built when the neighbour gains by it, and bends round when it does not and owes the asker nothing; a way with a tile lacking a right is never begun.
- [ ] Numbers: ways asked, agreed, bent and refused over 30 years on populous small, seeds 7 and 42; who refused whom (households, not only counts).
- Cost (rule 9): a few offers a way, a few ways a village a decade.
