Goal: design.md says how a head judges whether a place is worth the walk, in hours and by its own estimate, for each kind of daily work, and the tasks that replace the step reaches are written.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `design.md` says what a head judges: for each kind of daily work that has a reach in steps today (the list is `docs/plans/VB-444.md`, section 3: wood 12 and 27, fishing 6, foraging 5, the hunt 6, the herds' pasture 8, the stone 12, a farm's fields 4, the common meadow 3), whether the place is worth the walk, in hours there and back by the route's cost against what the trip brings, as the head's own estimate by its skill (principle 14), inside its plan (Work: opportunities, plans and tasks). One rule for all of them, not one a kind (Working rules: the third special case).
- [ ] Which reaches are not a judgement and stay distances, with the reason: spacing and layout (`SETTLE.apart`, `centreApart`, `greenReach`, `FIELD_REACH` where it is room, not a walk), the claim on outland (`CLAIMS.outReach`: a right, not a trip), breeding.
- [ ] What bounds the search for places to judge, since a judgement over every tile is a pass (Rules for cost 2 and 6): a compiled reach map a household or a village, refreshed at its cadence, with its cost named: entities, cadence, work a call.
- [ ] The player has what its pawn has (principle 16): what the player sees and may overrule of the judgement, through the plan panel (VB-256).
- [ ] Numbers that show the need: at the journey scale (VB-780), how often the step reach cuts off a place a day's work could reach, and how often it sends a pawn on a trip that does not fit the day (the farthest wood, 5.4 hours out by its path).
- [ ] The implement tasks are written with numbers and criteria and set `ready`.
