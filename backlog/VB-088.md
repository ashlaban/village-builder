# VB-088: A cost budget in the test runs: scaling and history checks
Status: blocked
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (measurement). `docs/roadmap.md`, sections 5.2 (the budget: cost per living pawn-day at year 60 at most 1.25 times year 5) and 6 (scaling exponent, history check); `CLAUDE.md`, Rules for cost, rules 1 and 2.
Goal: Performance is measured once per task, by hand. This task first asked for wall-clock timing in the verify run, but timing at year 2 missed the late game, and timing flakes. Check work instead, from the counters (VB-192): how it grows with the number of people (scaling) and with history (year 5 against year 40). Then rules 1 and 2 of the Rules for cost are tested in every run, and a loop over the dead or over pairs fails when it is added. Blocked until VB-192 (the counters) and VB-193 (the fixtures) are done.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Scaling, `tests/scaling.js` in `npm test`, under 5 s: tiny worlds (`tests/lib/tiny.js`) with 2 and with 4 identical villages side by side (the same map strip repeated, the same household sizes), 30 game days and one moving round each. For each counter, the exponent log(W4 / W2) / log(N4 / N2) of its work against the living. Over 1.15 fails.
- [ ] History, `tests/history.js` in `npm run verify`, under 30 s: from `small-y5` and `small-y40` (VB-193, through `loadFixture`), 30 game days and one moving round each. For each counter, its work per living pawn-day at year 40 against year 5; over 1.25 (the budget's line) fails. It runs on a stale fixture, saying so, and skips with a notice when there is none.
- [ ] The history test also times each 6-minute step of its two windows from outside (`Sim.advance(st, 0.1)`, timed in the test, since modules read no clock) and prints the longest, the 99th percentile and the hour of the five longest; reported, never failing (timing belongs to the bench, VB-194).
- [ ] Known excesses are kept in `tests/cost-baseline.json`, as the lint keeps its baseline: an entry per counter and check, which may only go down (`--update` lowers it); a counter without an entry must pass. Expected today: the walks over the dead (`walkStep`, `walkDaily`), the moving round's rankings (`roundRank`) and, in the scaling check, its pairs (`roundPairs`, quadratic across villages). VB-195 and VB-196 lower them.
- [ ] Both print every counter's exponent or ratio, passing or not (`node tests/run.js verify --verbose`).
- [ ] The numbers at `HEAD` in this file.
- [ ] When done: sets VB-195 `ready`.
Cost: about 5 s in `npm test` and 30 s in verify, in parallel with the other scripts.
