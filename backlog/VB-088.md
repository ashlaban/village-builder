## Implementation, 5 November 2026

Impact: tests and test helpers only; no simulation, page, saved state, golden state or work-counter changes.
`tests/lib/cost.js` reads `VB.count` and pawn lifetimes; the simulation never reads these measurements.
The history windows use isolated workers, one per fixture, to meet the 30-second cost target.
Only the parent may fetch fixtures; neither worker writes them. No new coupling between simulation systems.

- `tests/scaling.js` runs in test and verify: two/four repeated 28-by-28 terrain strips, three farms per village,
  six people per household, identical farm geometry and family age/sex patterns. The layout is asserted equal.
  Mean living populations are 36 and 72. Every declared counter and the derived `roundRank` is exercised.
- `tests/history.js` runs in verify: fresh `small-y5` and `small-y40` fixtures stamped `4df5766`, each with
  one explicit moving round at the saved hour, then 30 days (7,200 six-minute calls). The windows end before
  the next scheduled moving round. Living pawn-days are integrated from birth/death times: 11,704.5 / 21,781.
  Stale fixtures are identified and used; missing fixtures skip the comparison; incompatible formats fail.
- `tests/cost-baseline.json` records only measured excesses. Both scripts accept `--update` to lower existing
  entries and remove fixed ones; a new excess or an increased one fails without writing the file.
  `tests/cost-rules.js` exercises that policy, non-finite/zero work, coverage and linear/quadratic growth.
- In addition to the expected historical pawn walks and rankings, the measurements expose route invalidation
  at both scales, A* tile expansions just over the scaling limit, and marriage pairs over the history limit.
  These are recorded explicitly rather than widening the general 1.15 / 1.25 thresholds.
- VB-195 is now ready. VB-196 can lower rankings and pairs; VB-198 can lower route-cache work.
