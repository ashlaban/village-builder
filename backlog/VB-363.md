# VB-363: Dress: clothing wears out, and a worn dress costs health in winter and standing
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The mill and the loom, the kiln and the quarry); `backlog/VB-185.md`
Goal: Each person wears a length of cloth out in about 3 years; without sound dress winter costs a little health and standing falls. Slice 6's plan (VB-185, 11 Nov), from Kim's answers in the session.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `pawn.dress`: `{ worth, at }`, read in closed form: 1 when new, 0 after `DRESS.years` (3) (rule 3). On the household's planning day a pawn whose dress is under `DRESS.mend` (0.3) takes a length of cloth from the store (reason `dress`); children take half a length.
- [x] Under 0.1 (rags): the cold's health loss in winter is `DRESS.cold` (1.5) times, and standing loses `DRESS.shame` (0.1). Above 0.7 with dyed or fine cloth: nothing yet (slice 8).
- [x] The scenarios' first people start with dress of worth 0.3 to 1, drawn; the generated households with fleeces and a loom by their sheep.
- [x] Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): people in rags by year and household, health and deaths in winter, cloth used and left, whether households without sheep get cloth at the fair or by gift. Cost: no daily pass; the read is closed form at the death roll's cadence.
- [x] The numbers that move are reported before and after at the seeds and maps named (Working rules); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
Notes:
- Numbers are Proposed by slice 6's plan (11 Nov) on Kim's answers to the shape; the hours are in the game's work hours (576 an adult a year) and, but for the textile shares, from general reading not checked by search: each task reports its numbers and the wrap-up tunes them.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim's answers after the plan of VB-702; section 14): A dress carries the grade of its cloth: `pawn.dress` gains it, and `DRESS.years` is multiplied by the grade's life. Cloth is given out by rank, the holder and spouse first and in the best (13.5). 'Fine cloth' in the second criterion is a kind of cloth (slice 8), not the grade fine.
- 23 Nov 2026 (VB-761, workmanship, landed): a made piece's grade is drawn by `VB['sim/workmanship'].draw(st, maker, craft)` (stream `making`; the maker is the pawn who finished the job, passed to a kind's `done` as its fifth argument); a graded good declares `graded: {work: [properties]}` in `declareGood`, and reads its row by grade from `Goods.kindRow(good, kind).grades[grade]` (`life`, `work`, `worth`, the properties scaled); the `worth` rule takes `grade` in its context; `jobs.allot` gives a making of a graded good to the household's most skilled hand at its craft; `expectedWorth(st, id, craft)` is the head's estimate. `pawn.dress` carries its cloth's grade; `DRESS.years` times the grade's `life`; dress by rank, the holder and spouse first in the best.
