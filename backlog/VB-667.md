# VB-667: The supply component: season cells in the Stores tab, and chips in the farm rows
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/mocks/supply-doubt.html, docs/mocks/supply-scale.html; AGENTS.md, Deciding without Kim, 14 and 15
Goal: The player sees at a glance whether a household will make it through the year, for each supply, and in a village's list which farms will not.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The Stores tab of a farm's card (`src/page/cards.js`) opens with Supplies: a row for food, fuel and hay, each its icon, its word, its season cells, its state in a few words ("safe", "short in spring", "short in winter") and an i. The cells are the four seasons ahead from the season we are in, under a header of the seasons as icons (a sprout, a sun, a leaf, a snowflake; `src/page/icons.js`). A cell is filled by how much of that season's need is covered: solid from the store (`own`), striped from what is still to come (`with`); a cell the likeliest outcome does not cover has a red frame. Read from `Sim.supplyOutlook` (VB-666); the page computes nothing of it.
- [ ] A supply needed in one season only (hay) is one wide cell for that season, after that season's icon, in place of four (Kim).
- [ ] The i opens the numbers under the row (`parts` of the outlook: the store, the need, what is expected and when, what is expected to spoil), and for hay a sentence first: it is eaten by the animals while they stand in the byre over winter; out of winter they graze, and none is needed (Kim). There is no mark for when a supply is laid in, and no separate mark for spoilage (Kim).
- [ ] The section says whose reckoning it is ("By Gunnar's reckoning, at his last review": principle 14). The farm card's Details shows the same three rows in place of today's Food, Firewood and Hay stats, and the played pawn's sheet the same for its household: one presentation of a supply, wherever it is shown (15).
- [ ] The component is a piece of `src/page/meter.js`, beside the meter, with its argument the outlook; `docs/architecture/modules.md` names it.
- [ ] A farm's row (`farmRow`: a village's card, the People sheet) shows its people as an icon and a number, and a chip for each supply about to fall short: the supply's icon and the icon of the first season it is short in, gold for a little, red for badly. A supply that is safe has no chip, and a farm with nothing to worry about shows none. The chips replace the dot and the words "Fed", "Short of food" (Kim: "Chips are now good. Use them"); a title on the row still says how the household eats today.
- [ ] Desktop (the phone's layout is not made worse). The page only reads `st` (lint). `node tests/smoke.js` checks the cells, the opened numbers and a chip on a constructed short household; the `tests/browser*.js` touched pass; screenshots before and after under `docs/img/`.
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`. Cost: the page's; a village's card reads one stored outlook a farm.
