# VB-667: The supply component: season cells in the Stores tab, and chips in the farm rows
Status: done (2026-11-17)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/mocks/supply-doubt.html, docs/mocks/supply-scale.html; AGENTS.md, Deciding without Kim, 14 and 15
Goal: The player sees at a glance whether a household will make it through the year, for each supply, and in a village's list which farms will not.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The Stores tab of a farm's card (`src/page/cards.js`) opens with Supplies: a row for food, fuel and hay, each its icon, its word, its season cells, its state in a few words ("safe", "short in spring", "short in winter") and an i. The cells are the four seasons ahead from the season we are in, under a header of the seasons as icons (a sprout, a sun, a leaf, a snowflake; `src/page/icons.js`). A cell is filled by how much of that season's need is covered: solid from the store (`own`), striped from what is still to come (`with`); a cell the likeliest outcome does not cover has a red frame. Read from `Sim.supplyOutlook` (VB-666); the page computes nothing of it.
- [x] A supply needed in one season only (hay) is one wide cell for that season, after that season's icon, in place of four (Kim).
- [x] The i opens the numbers under the row (`parts` of the outlook: the store, the need, what is expected and when, what is expected to spoil), and for hay a sentence first: it is eaten by the animals while they stand in the byre over winter; out of winter they graze, and none is needed (Kim). There is no mark for when a supply is laid in, and no separate mark for spoilage (Kim).
- [x] The section says whose reckoning it is ("By Gunnar's reckoning, at his last review": principle 14). The farm card's Details shows the same three rows in place of today's Food, Firewood and Hay stats, and the played pawn's sheet the same for its household: one presentation of a supply, wherever it is shown (15).
- [x] The component is a piece of `src/page/meter.js`, beside the meter, with its argument the outlook; `docs/architecture/modules.md` names it.
- [x] A farm's row (`farmRow`: a village's card, the People sheet) shows its people as an icon and a number, and a chip for each supply about to fall short: the supply's icon and the icon of the first season it is short in, gold for a little, red for badly. A supply that is safe has no chip, and a farm with nothing to worry about shows none. The chips replace the dot and the words "Fed", "Short of food" (Kim: "Chips are now good. Use them"); a title on the row still says how the household eats today.
- [x] Desktop (the phone's layout is not made worse). The page only reads `st` (lint). `node tests/smoke.js` checks the cells, the opened numbers and a chip on a constructed short household; the `tests/browser*.js` touched pass; screenshots before and after under `docs/img/`.
- [x] Behaviour unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`. Cost: the page's; a village's card reads one stored outlook a farm.

Implementation (17 Nov 2026):
- Impact: page only. `src/page/meter.js` gains the supply component beside the meter: `suppliesHTML(O, only)` (the Supplies section: whose
  reckoning it is, a header of the four seasons ahead as icons, a row a supply with its icon, word, season cells, state in words and an i;
  the numbers under the row when its i is open) and `supplyChips(O)` (the farm row's chips). Its argument is `Sim.supplyOutlook(st, id)`
  (VB-666); it reads `own`, `with`, `state`, `season`, `seasons` and `parts`, and computes nothing of the outlook. `src/page/icons.js`
  gains `seasonIcon(k)` (a sprout, a sun, a leaf, a snowflake, from the mocks). `src/page/cards.js`: the Stores tab opens with the
  component; the farm card's Details (`houseStats`) shows it in place of the Food, Firewood and Hay stats (`foodStat`, `hayStat` and
  `hungerTone` fell); the Animals tab shows the hay row in place of the hay stat; `farmRow` shows the chips and the people as an icon and a
  number, with how the household eats today (`hungerWord`) as the row's title. `src/page/play.js`: the played pawn's sheet shows the
  component for its household in place of food and firewood. CSS in `src/head.html`; `docs/architecture/modules.md` names the component.
- The cells: frame season k (from the season the outlook was made in) is filled solid to `own - k` and striped to `with - k` (each
  clamped to the cell), and framed red where `with < k + 0.98` (the mock's rule). Hay (`seasons: [3]`): one wide cell after the icon of
  its season (the frame season whose need is not 0), filled by `own`/`with` less that season (4: full), framed red unless safe.
- Decisions (Deciding without Kim; in the inbox as `decision` notes):
  (1) the goods tiles VB-668 put under Supplies (grain, flour, meals, sheaves, dried fish, firewood, peat) stay, under the three rows: they
  are what the food and fuel rows count, and Kim set aside only the chain of goods, not the counts;
  (2) the Animals tab shows the hay row in place of its hay stat, so that hay has one presentation (15);
  (3) "Cold" (no firewood today) stays a stat in Details beside Water and Hungry days: it is today's state, not the outlook, as the
  row's title is for food;
  (4) a stale outlook (made in an earlier season) is drawn with its cells dimmed and its frame from the season it was made in, the line
  saying "at his review in the <season>"; a supply nobody needs (no herds, nobody to feed) reads "none needed" over an empty cell; no
  outlook yet (before the head's first review) is a line saying so;
  (5) the i is open per supply (food, fuel, hay) across every panel that shows the component, as page state, like "All materials".
- Not built here: VB-893 (the plan panel's stores by the component, and the year-ahead graph) and VB-927 (the village store as a supply
  and the warnings as chips) are not small additions: the village store has no outlook to read (the simulation reckons a household's
  only), and the year-ahead graph is a new drawing. Both stay for their tasks.
