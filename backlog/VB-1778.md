Goal: A parent's levy is the budget its child plan runs within, and a household sees at a season's start that its plan does not fit
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The labour budget of a household for a season (sim/levies): the sum of its members' levies (`levyOf`) over the season's days, less the days of its outer levies (8 work hours each; today the days owed of `sim/days`), computed at the season's first review and when membership, a grant or an outer levy changes; read by `Sim.plans` beside the season's steps.
- [ ] Each season step's hours left (`left`: lo, mid, hi, by the head's estimate; VB-1752) is set against the budget; a season that does not fit is seen at its first review and answered by the head's default author in the order VB-1759 states: hands asked (sim/hands), a target lowered (never for an absence shorter than the step's window), or more asked of the members (a demand, VB-1779); which, and when, said in a line of the record.
- [ ] A check on a constructed household: a season whose targets' hours exceed its budget is flagged at its first review and answered; one that fits is left alone.
- [ ] Cost: 200 households × 4 seasons a year × about 30 rows (about 500 reads a day at the late reference); no pass of its own.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
