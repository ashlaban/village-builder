# VB-772: A landless maker asks for a plot where its custom is best
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-770
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: town
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Settlements, Towns and regions (A landless maker asks for a plot; Plots); docs/plans/VB-189.md, sections 5.6 and 11; backlog/VB-682.md
Goal: A maker may move to its buyers (Kim, 16 Nov): the landless with a craft, a younger son or a servant, may ask for a plot elsewhere for custom's sake, through the offers of the reworked moving round; a landed maker does not.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Who may ask: an adult with a craft (a skill of VB-360 above `MAKER.skill`, proposed 0.3) who holds no land and heads no farm: a younger son, a servant. A maker with a farm does not ask, and no rule moves it.
- [ ] Among the rungs of its wish (VB-680, VB-692) it holds "a plot at V", valued by its own estimate (principle "The future is someone's estimate"): its custom at V, from the households it has dealt with or seen dealing (VB-770's memory), by village, shared with the makers of its craft it knows of there; against its kin where it lives, the walk from V to the nearest deposit of its material it knows, and the fields to be had there. Villages weighed: those within a day's walk it has been to, at most `MAKER.weigh` 6.
- [ ] It asks through the offers of the reworked moving round (VB-690, VB-692, VB-697; Kim's answers to VB-682): an offer at any time of the year, the round its due date; one village a year, one rung at a time; refused, it may ask another the next year. The move needs the pawn's yes and the receiving side's, which for a plot is the alderman's answer by the custom (VB-697) or the head's who gives it room. Its household walks there (VB-287). The played pawn asks by the same verb.
- [ ] A plot given is a plot as `sim/expansion` lays one: no kind of its own, no rule about fields.
- [ ] Reported, populous small and medium, seeds 7 and 42, 40 years: would-be makers a decade who asked, were given, and where; moves a maker in 30 years (the mock: 0.1 to 1.3); the fullest village's share of the island's maker households (the mock: 10% to 22%; a third or more, held for decades, would contradict the plan's finding and is surfaced); would-be makers who found no place.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state: a landless smith who has dealt with eight households of V and two at home asks V; a smith with a farm asks nobody; a refusal leaves it where it is, free to ask elsewhere next year.
- Cost (rule 9): would-be makers, a few a year x at most 6 villages weighed x its known households (at most 4 a good): under 50 evaluations a year.
- If VB-682's plan changes what `docs/plans/VB-189.md`, section 11, assumes of the round, this task is rewritten before it is taken.
