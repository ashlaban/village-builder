Decisions (Deciding without Kim):
- A loaded game opens paused, not at a saved speed: a save keeps no speed, and keeping one is a change of the save's format for no gain the pause does not give.
- The farm's History is one event read from both farms, not a second `left` event for the farm left: Rules for cost, 8 (bound history), and one happening is one event. It adds one field to the record.
- A coming is told at the farm come to as well as the leaving at the farm left (the criterion names both), but only for a move that names the farm left; `joined` stays a personal event everywhere else.
- Which routines are "done daily" is the page's list (`PLAN_DAILY`: the four the criterion names): `sim/kinds` has no word for it. Close call: the daily ones keep their arrows inside the fold, since the verb takes any order and the player has what its pawn has (16); another agent could as well have shown them without controls.
- A tile's facts are shown by pointing (the criterion's first way), desktop first (15). On a touch screen, with no pointer to hover, the first tap still names the tile; a tap that shows and a second that names is plan mode's to settle with the estimate (VB-1026).
- The village's name is moved on the map for every view, not only in the chooser: like is shown alike (15).
- `tests/browser.js` plays a holder whose farm has free land within a field's reach: on tiny populous, seed 7, the first household's reach is all owned, and the tile part of the plan panel's test (VB-256, VB-894) was failing on the base commit for it.
