# VB-699: A head reads the asks: what others want raises its target
Status: done (2026-11-23)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (7, 9, 16); design.md, Goods, production and animals
Goal: Demand is the asks that are open, read by a head as its own estimate (principle 14), with no price and no market state.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A household that lacks a good it needs and cannot make (no deposit, no shore, no pine in reach) asks for it: an offer on the basis `swap` or `gift` wanting the good, open until met or a year old.
- [x] On its planning day after the harvest a head reads the asks for the goods it can make, from the households it has a tie to and those of the villages its village gathers with, and sets its target to its need plus what it judges it can place (its hands to spare, the load it can carry to the gathering); the estimate is the head's, with its skill and lean (VB-476's pattern), and the panel says so.
- [x] Cost (rules 1, 2, 4): the open asks are indexed by good, live ones only, with a cap a household; a head reads its goods' lists once a year, bounded by the villages it gathers with; a declared counter; measured on `small-y40` and `medium-ref`.
- [x] Numbers before and after, populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years: asks open and met by good, goods made beyond need and what became of them (swapped, given, spoilt), hours spent on them.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim, the session on the material economy): principle 9 by mechanism: place decides who can make, the asks decide who does.
- 18 Nov 2026, amended to agree with VB-1123 (plan mode; Kim's decisions of 18 Nov): the stepping of targets leaves the game: no head, played or not, sets a target by hand, and the Plan tab's steppers go with the tab. "The head's target" in this task is the level the head reckons for itself, never one that is set. The level stays (Kim, 18 Nov): the player reckons it in their head, and a head not played reckons it as a margin it prefers over what survival needs, by its traits and skill, 1.5 times the baseline to start with. So this task's rule, a head raising its target by the asks it reads, stands as a reckoning; what plan mode shows of it is an action to make the good on a member's bar, chosen at the house or at the place that gives it, and the played head places that action itself where the automatic head's reckoning would.
- 23 Nov 2026 (built, `sim/asks`): at each review a household asks for each fair good it needs, lacks and cannot make (the place's bound
  beyond the need 0); one ask a good, `ASKS.cap` 3 a household, a year at most, met when it next holds its need. Today that is tar and dried fish.
- Close call: the ask is kept in its own index `st.asks.open[good]`, not in `st.offers`: an ask has nobody to answer until a maker meets it at
  the fair (basis `swap`) or by a gift; it carries that basis. Ties beyond the head's village and its gathering's district are not read yet.
- Close call: the lean is `plan.head.bias.hours` (VB-476's), so no new draw; skill decides what it hears (from 0.5 the district's, else its village's).
- Close call: each maker reckons the whole of an ask it hears of, with no share among makers: dried fish, asked rarely, is over-reckoned.
