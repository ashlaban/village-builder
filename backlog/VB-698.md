# VB-698: A target on every stored good: the head decides what to make beyond its need
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (7, 9, 16); design.md, Goods, production and animals
Goal: What a household makes of each good is its head's target: its own need, which the head's rule sets as today, plus what the head means to give or exchange; the played head sets it by the verb it has.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `target` covers every stored good a household can make (the goods registry's rows with a maker: dried fish, tar, peat, clay, bog iron, quern stones, butter, salt meat, ale; later crafts add theirs), with bounds from what the place and the hands allow (`targetBounds`); the constants that sized production go, or become the default target's terms.
- [x] The unplayed head's target is its need by default, so `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`, or the difference is reported; nothing is made for exchange until a head has reason (the task that reads the asks).
- [x] Cost (Kim: keep computation feasible; rules 5, 6, 9): a target is one number a household and good, set on the head's planning day and read by the `available` rules as a compiled value; no new loop, no pass over goods in a step; the review's work is counted before and after (`node tests/counters.js`) on `small-y40`.
- [x] VB-644 (dried fish for the fair) becomes this target for dried fish, with no project of its own.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim, the session on the material economy): the general rule in place of VB-644's special project (the third special case would have been tar, then iron).
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim, the check of the new chain): raised to priority 1 as a foundation that other tasks wait for: VB-699, VB-700, VB-701, VB-644.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim's answers after the plan of VB-702; section 14): A target is on a good, or a kind of it, never on a grade of workmanship. Whether to make a graded good or get it reads the head's estimate of what its best hand's piece will be worth (the plan of VB-702, 13.5; VB-761 gives the mean).

## Implementation, 16 November 2026

Impact: a new module `sim/targets` (material layer, after `sim/stores`): the registry of the goods a household makes (code, sealed with
the rules registry; no saved state), `target`, `need`, `bounds`, `view`, and `hands` (moved from `sim/drying`, which re-exports it).
New state: `plan.targets.goods` (written only by `sim/plans`: compiled at each review, at a retarget and when the head sets one) and
`plan.chosen.goods` (the head's choices, by the verb `target`, which already existed). Reads: each maker reads its good's target where it
read its own constant or rule (`sim/drying` rackfish and the rack's room, `sim/tar` burntar, `sim/dig` wants, `sim/quern` hewing,
`sim/kinds` brewWanted, `sim/byre` churning and salting). No new coupling but makers → `sim/targets` (downward); `sim/targets` reads
`st.plans` (state, not a call). Why a registry and not a branch per good in `sim/plans`: nine goods now and every later craft adds
one (Deciding without Kim, 4; the third special case was tar, then iron).

- The made goods: dried fish, tar, peat, clay, bog iron (`bogOre`), the quern, butter, salt meat, ale; each declared by its maker with
  its need (the default target), its top bound beyond need (place and hands) and its held stock.
- Defaults (the household's rule as before): dried fish `Drying.wanted`; tar the barrels a pit's household keeps (1, 2 with a boat);
  clay and bog iron `DIG.stock`; ale `BREW.stock`; a quern 1 (a working quern, a held one counted while not yet due for renewal);
  butter, salt meat and peat `Infinity` (no cap of their own: the milk's share churned, all fresh meat salted, peat cut to the fuel
  pile's goal, which the woodpile's target already sets). DIG.stock, BREW.stock and the dried fish wanted become the default's terms.
- Bounds (`targetBounds` in `sim/plans` asks `Targets.bounds` for a made good): [0, need + what the place and the hands allow beyond
  it]. Hands: the household's working hands times `TARGET.spareHours` (Proposed 200: about a month of a hand's slack a year), at each
  good's hours a unit (a rack trip and its hanging for a bank's catch, twice from a boat; a barrel's hours at the pit; a load's dig;
  a barrel's brewing). Place: no dried fish beyond need where the village does not fish; no tar beyond the barrels kept without a pit
  in a wood with pine; nothing dug beyond the stock where the digging ground has none; no quern beyond its own (no store holds a loose
  stone yet: VB-866). Butter and salt meat: what is held and what the cows give over the butter's keeping days, or the fresh meat and
  the herds' carcasses.
- Whole goods (tar, quern, ale, clay, bog iron) take whole targets; a refused target names its bounds.
- A target above the need is read at once: a head that sets dried fish above its need fishes for the rack and lays catch by it until
  the target is held. That is VB-644's surplus for the fair, with no project of its own; who sets it, and why, is the task that reads
  the asks (VB-699). Nothing is made for exchange until then: every unplayed head's target is its need.
- `Sim.headView(st, id).goods`: each made good's held stock, target now, the custom's (need), the head's choice, bounds and unit. The
  page's plan panel does not show it yet (VB-865).
- Cost: a target is one number a household and good, compiled on the head's planning day (9 reads a review, `planReview` times 9),
  read by the makers' `available` rules as one map lookup; no new loop in a step and no pass over goods in one. Bounds are read only by
  the verb and the page's view.

Decisions (Deciding without Kim):
- A by-product's default target is `Infinity`, not a number: butter and salt meat are what the milk and the meat give, and peat is
  cut against the fuel pile's goal, which is the woodpile's target; a finite target only caps them (peat: or asks for more, in
  summer). Any number would have changed today's churning and salting (3, 11: no patch; the head's verb is the way to set one).
- The quern's target counts working querns (1 by default); 0 lets it wear out. Querns beyond its own need a store of loose stones (VB-866).
- `sim/targets` is a new module, not a section of `sim/stores` or `sim/plans`: both are over their size lines with split tasks open
  (VB-416, VB-431), and the registry is its own concern (goods with a maker), read by the makers below `sim/plans`.

## Validation

- `tests/made-targets.js` (fast): on a tiny world at seed 7 with herds, a review compiles every made good's target to its need; the
  verb refuses beyond the bounds and half a whole good; clay above its stock is dug on, none at 0; fresh meat is not salted at a target
  of 0; a quern target of 0 fetches no stone; ale 3 is read by the view with its bounds; a save holds the targets and choices; cleared,
  the need again; invariants hold. `tests/head.js`: its "no such target" case now names wool (ale is a target).
- Invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): `plan.targets.goods` absent or one non-negative value a made good, whole where counted
  whole; `plan.chosen.goods` null or non-negative, and the compiled target is the choice.
- `node tools/same.js` (founders and heirs, seeds 7 and 42, tiny, 2 years): only the new field `plan.targets.goods` differs.
  Populous small seed 7, 3 years: the same to the end of year 2; diverges in year 3. With the target read live in place of the
  compiled one the run is the same throughout: the difference is the compiling (a dried fish need that moves with the household
  between its reviews, a boat built), at most a review's cadence (1 to 3 days) late.
- `node tools/balance.js --scenario populous --seeds 3,7,11,42 --map small --years 10 --from 3`, before and after: 2,428 and 2,434
  household-years; made/need by household size within run-to-run noise (size 5: 1.91 → 1.94; size 6: 1.82 → 1.80; size 8: 1.42 → 1.39);
  hungry household-years 0 to 2% at every size in both. Households are neither better nor worse off: the change is a delay of a
  day or two in when a target moves, not a different target.
- Counters on `small-y40` (each tree's own fixture, populous small seed 7, made for its sources; a year on, `--report counts`), per
  living pawn-day before and after: `planReview` 0.1874 → 0.1872 (7,116 → 6,986 reviews a year); `oppAsk` 12.96 → 13.26;
  `digGround` 1.281 → 1.292; `tarAsk` 0.0284 → 0.0245; `fishReach` 0.0106 → 0.0138. The two runs part from year 3 (above), so these
  are two islands of 792 and 787 living, not one; no counter moves beyond that. The compile adds 9 map writes a review (about
  63,000 a year here, 1.7 a living pawn-day), each a read of the maker's need; no new loop in a step. `node tests/counters.js` passes
  unchanged (no counter declared or re-recorded). Times are left to the nightly bench.
