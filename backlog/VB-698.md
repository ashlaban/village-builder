# VB-698: A target on every stored good: the head decides what to make beyond its need
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (7, 9, 15); design.md, Goods, production and animals
Goal: What a household makes of each good is its head's target: its own need, which the head's rule sets as today, plus what the head means to give or exchange; the played head sets it by the verb it has.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `target` covers every stored good a household can make (the goods registry's rows with a maker: dried fish, tar, peat, clay, bog iron, quern stones, butter, salt meat, ale; later crafts add theirs), with bounds from what the place and the hands allow (`targetBounds`); the constants that sized production go, or become the default target's terms.
- [ ] The unplayed head's target is its need by default, so `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`, or the difference is reported; nothing is made for exchange until a head has reason (the task that reads the asks).
- [ ] Cost (Kim: keep computation feasible; rules 5, 6, 9): a target is one number a household and good, set on the head's planning day and read by the `available` rules as a compiled value; no new loop, no pass over goods in a step; the review's work is counted before and after (`node tests/counters.js`) on `small-y40`.
- [ ] VB-644 (dried fish for the fair) becomes this target for dried fish, with no project of its own.
