Implementation notes (11 Nov 2026):
- New module `src/sim/forage.js` (after `sim/fish`; layer in `src/layers.json` beside fish): `w.forage` (stock 0..1) and `w.forageYear` (the game
  year it was set) per tile; `stock` reads full once the year has turned (spring), so no pass (Rules for cost, 3); `gather` (2.5 rations x stock,
  stock - 0.2); `ground(st, house)`: the nearest forest, pine or heath tile (not under a field or yard) within 5 walking steps, in breadth-first
  order, with at least 0.2 left. `initForage` at seeding and at the load of an older save; both fields join `WORLD` in `sim/saves`. Tunables
  `FORAGE` in `sim/rules`.
- Kind `gather` in `src/sim/kinds.js`: type `foraging`, 1 hand, 3 hours, carried home in baskets to the door, anim `forage`; asked last in
  `ASK.farmAfterPlans`, after `fish`, and listed after `fish` in the summer and autumn `ROUTINES` of `sim/plans`. Posted from summer day 6 to the
  end of autumn; one job, two under `FISH.secondDays` (12) days of spare food. Urgency is fishing's: the line moved into one helper,
  `spareUrgency`, that both kinds use. The baskets go into `hh.meals` through `Stores.put(..., 'gathering')`, a new routine reason beside `catch`,
  so fish and gathered food are counted apart in the yearly totals.
- Roles: `foraging` ranks as before, `gather: 3` for farmhand and son (their foraging is 6 and 7): farmer 3, wife 4, farmhand 3, maid 5, son 3,
  daughter 6, retired 3, child 5.
- Page: a `forage` pose (the `gather` stoop with a basket on the arm) and a basket carried home (`src/page/rigs.js`). `tools/shot.js` gets
  `--follow KIND` (looks at the first pawn at work on a job of that kind). Screenshot: `docs/img/vb118-gathering-follow.jpg` (populous 7, tiny
  map, summer day 6, 07:10; the gatherer in the forest at the centre).
- Invariants: `w.forage` in [0, 1] and `w.forageYear` not after this year. New test `tests/forage.js` (in `npm test`); `tests/children.js` knows a
  child's gather 5.
- Work counter `forageGround` (tiles searched for a ground): one breadth-first search of up to about 90 tiles per ask, like `fishGround`; not
  measured on a late save.
