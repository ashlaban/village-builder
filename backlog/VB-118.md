Goal: The woods give only firewood, and women and children are idle most of late summer. Gathering is light work for them that adds a little food and, with VB-119, makes a meal go further.
Acceptance criteria (proposed; see `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-118):

- [ ] Posted from summer day 6 to the end of autumn, one job per household, two when food is short (as VB-117); the same urgency as fishing.
- [ ] `foraging` ranks as in VB-117; gathering is an exception at 3 for son and farmhand.
- [ ] Until VB-119, gathered food goes straight into rations.
