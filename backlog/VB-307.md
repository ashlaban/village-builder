# VB-307: Short of people: checked when a household's membership changes, not daily
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Households short of people (Short of people, checked at the change; Proposed, VB-283); backlog/VB-283.md
Goal: `careForOrphans` (`src/sim/service.js`) runs every day from `dailyLife` (`src/sim/lifecycle.js`) and walks every live household to find one with no member of 18 or more (Rules for cost, 4: schedule, don't poll). A household can only come to have no grown member when its membership changes, so note the household at the change and let the guardian rule read only the households noted. Behaviour-preserving; it is also the hook VB-308 reads.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A queue in `st` as plain data (`st.peopleCheck`, household ids, in the `WORLD` list of `src/sim/saves.js`), with its operations in `sim/records`, its one writer: every site that takes a member out of a household notes it (`leaveHousehold`, which deaths and `moveTo` go through; `clearMembers`; a household made by `makeHousehold` with no grown member, if any can be). Grep the sites that write `household.members` and `pawn.household` and list them in the task file.
- [ ] `careForOrphans` reads the queue (in ascending household id, as `st.liveHouseholds` is ordered) at the same place in `dailyLife`, and empties it; a dissolved or empty household is skipped, as today. No other daily pass over households is added.
- [ ] `tests/golden.json` unchanged (`node tests/golden.js`), `tests/counters.json` unchanged but for the walk this removes, if a counter counts it; the queue's invariant in `tests/lib/invariants.js` (ids of live households, no duplicates); a load in the middle of a run gives the same run (`tests/saves.js`).
- [ ] `src/owners.json`, the `@state` lines and `docs/architecture/ownership.md` name the new field and its owner.
Cost: today one check of `members.some(adult)` a live household a day (about 150 households on populous small, 250 at `medium-ref`, times 48 days a year); after, one entry a membership departure (deaths and moves: the replay counts 7 households a year becoming lone on populous small seed 3, and 804 over the ten full runs' 350 years), each checked once. Name it measured: `--report counts` on `small-y40` before and after.
Order: first of VB-283's three; before VB-308, which reads the same queue. One at a time with VB-090 and VB-290, which edit `sim/service`.
