Goal: Crops and the weather. The weather is being built on a parallel branch (Kim, 11 Nov; its first batch, rain from a per-seed wind, names "fronts, temperature, the harvest factor" as its next): this task takes the question a crop asks from there if it has landed, and builds the season's draw only if it has not.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] One question, answered by one module (the weather's, if it exists; say who owns it by target-v2 section 4): for a place and a season, warmth and wetness in −1 to 1; for a place and a year, the thaw and the first autumn frost. Until the weather answers it a region, a draw for the archipelago at each season's start from the stream `weather`, two years kept in `st` (plain data, in `WORLD`, with invariants), and nothing older.
- [ ] Each crop's row holds its reading (Crops and the weather, the table): barley, rye, peas; the meadows read hay's. The harvest is computed once, at reaping, as now, with the reading in place of `st.weather`. Barley's reading is tuned so its mean and spread over 1,000 drawn years are today's factor's (0.75 to 1.2, mean about 0.975): the test prints both.
- [ ] No ground is tilled or sown before the thaw (spring day 1 to 4); the frost falls on autumn day 8 to 12, most often 11; a cold summer ripens barley and peas up to 2 days later, a warm one a day earlier. The plan's closing-window rules (VB-322) read the year's frost, not a constant.
- [ ] The spring's forecast line goes (Kim, 11 Nov); a line a season names its weather, through `log` with an event kind `tests/record.js` knows.
- [ ] `tests/golden.json` re-recorded, listed. Numbers before and after, populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years: hungry household-years, crops lost to frost, the correlation of barley's and rye's harvests in a village, hay. `node tools/bands.js` once after.
Cost: 10 draws a year; a reading a field at its reaping. No daily pass. If the weather's sums a region exist, a field reads a difference of two of them.
