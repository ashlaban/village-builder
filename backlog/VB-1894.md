# VB-1894: A planner who falls ill or is called away gives its authored steps back to the household's day at once
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: plans
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md section 7, Plans at four scales (Authors: a step whose holder cannot work returns to the pool at once; Replanning: the day's lists); docs/plans/four-scales/README.md, 3.4, 3.5, section 4 Q7
Touches: sim/authored (takeBack); sim/steps (takeBack); tests/authored.js
Goal: No authored task waits on a holder who cannot work, unless its controller kept it
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] When a member on its own plan cannot work (the events `fellIll` and `leftDay`: ill, lent, on leave, away; and `died`), the open authored steps of its own plan are taken back at once by the default author, as `takeBack` does at a review (the step the household's, due by its head's estimate for the hands its day allots, a task by key slotted into the day as it stands, the card saying why: "its holder fell ill" and the like), unless kept; at a death, in place of today's dropping of the steps (`sim/steps`' `died` handler), whose tasks by key wait for the next allotment.
- [ ] A kept step of an ill or away planner stays, at risk, and the card warns; a planner with no other hand in its household (VB-1879) keeps its steps.
- [ ] A check on a constructed household (`tests/authored.js`): a planner holding two tasks by key falls ill: within the hour both are in a member's list of the household's day and their cards say they were taken back; the same planner, one step kept: that one stays on its plan.
- [ ] Q7 (`tests/day.js` 7) still holds with the reaper on its own plan: a three-day illness costs no more vital hours than today's allotment, within 10%.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10): `npm run verify` green, and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774).
