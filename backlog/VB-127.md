# VB-127: Familiarity from talk and time at home, not from standing near each other
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: talk
Phase: implement
Design ref: Customs and culture (How attitudes move: contacts weighted by time together, plus kin). Relationships review, 31 Oct 2026 (`docs/review-relationships.md`, findings F1, F4 and proposal B); Kim kept the tie model unchanged in VB-061 and left this for later.
Goal: The tie counts the hours two pawns spend within 30 map units or in one house, sleeping hours included. Housemates tend to about 70 while the kin floor is 0.4, so a pawn's household is about 95% of its social influence; and counting a crowd is quadratic in its size (150 pawns on a green make 11,000 pairs an hour), now the largest single cost of a game day.
Acceptance criteria (Kim, 2 Nov 2026: the proposals accepted; `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, VB-127):
- [ ] The tie grows from talk (VB-061), from waking hours at home (once a day per household, from each member's hours inside), and from working one job together (VB-102), instead of from every hour near each other: +0.5 per talk, +1 per 24 waking hours under one roof, +0.5 per job worked together; the kin floor stays at 0.4. `noteContacts` and `st.contacts` go.
- [ ] Ties are stored against a shared decay scale (weights divided by 0.5^(t / year), renormalised every few years), so the daily pass over every tie goes; `Culture.dailyStep` divides by the sum of a pawn's weights, so the shared scale cancels there.
- [ ] A pawn keeps at most its 150 strongest ties; kin are always kept.
- [ ] The kin floor and the starting ties are set on the same scale as the new tie.
- [ ] Customs: the calibration of `design.md` (Pace) is rerun in the simulation over 100 founders and 20 populous years, before and after; the PR proposes retuning if a custom moves more than a generation faster or slower.
- [ ] Cost: `tests/perf.js` before and after; target a game day of populous medium under 60 ms.
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
