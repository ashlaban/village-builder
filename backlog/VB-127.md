# VB-127: Familiarity from talk and time at home, not from standing near each other
Status: done (2026-11-09)
Priority: 2

Arch: talk

Design ref: Customs and culture (How attitudes move: contacts weighted by time together, plus kin). Relationships review, 31 Oct 2026 (`docs/review-relationships.md`, findings F1, F4 and proposal B); Kim kept the tie model unchanged in VB-061 and left this for later.
Goal: The tie counts the hours two pawns spend within 30 map units or in one house, sleeping hours included. Housemates tend to about 70 while the kin floor is 0.4, so a pawn's household is about 95% of its social influence; and counting a crowd is quadratic in its size (150 pawns on a green make 11,000 pairs an hour), now the largest single cost of a game day.

- 9 Nov 2026, implementation:
  - `sim/relations` owns familiarity whole. A tie grows by `SOCIAL.ties.talk` (0.5) a talk, by `home` (1) per 24 waking hours two housemates spend in their house, and by `work` (0.5) for each pair that finishes one job together (`workedTogether`, called by `sim/agents` `complete` before the hands are freed). Waking hours are the talk hours (6 to 22): `talkHour` counts `pawn.homeHours` for each pawn inside, and `dailyTies` at midnight adds the lesser of two housemates' hours, once per pair of each live household, and starts the hours again. `noteContacts`, `st.contacts`, `pairKey` and `pairOf` are gone; the outdoor cells that talk draws partners from stay in `sim/society` as `outdoors`, built in the talk hours only. The gift's tie (`HELP.tieGain`), the starting ties (household 1, near kin at least 0.5), a newborn's (1 with its household) and the wedding's (2) go through `addTie` and `raiseTie`; the wedding now raises the spouses' tie to at least 2 instead of setting it to 2, so it never lowers a tie talk has built.
  - Decay scale: each pawn's ties are stored on its own scale, `pawn.tieT`; the tie now is the stored weight halved for every year since then (`tieOf`), so no step decays them (rule 3). Every 4 years (`renorm`), on a day spread by id (`(day + id * 73) % 192`, 73 being 192/φ², so any run of days takes an even share of the pawns), a pawn's ties are put back on today's scale, and those under 0.02, not to kin, or to the dead go. `Culture.dailyStep` is unchanged: it divides by the sum of the pawn's stored weights, so the scale cancels. The readers that need the value go through `tieOf` or the pawn's scale: talk's and company's weights, the meeting's support for a claim (`sim/claims`), and `relationsOf` (the page).
  - The scale is a pawn's, not one shared by all: one shared scale needs every tie of every pawn renormalised on one day, about 150,000 at `medium-ref`, a midnight step VB-201 is trying to shorten; per pawn it is spread over 192 days. `Culture.dailyStep` sums one pawn's ties at a time, so it cancels all the same.
  - Cap: `put` keeps at most `cap` (150) ties a pawn; a new tie at the cap takes the place of the weakest that is not to kin, unless it is weaker still and not to kin. Kin are always kept.
  - Kin floor: kept at 0.4, on the pawn's scale. Raising every pawn's near kin each midnight walked every living pawn's kin (3 ms a day at `small-y40`, `kinNear` allocating), so each pawn raises them once a season, on its own day, to 0.4 and a season's fading (0.476): they stay at 0.4 or above until its next turn. A newborn's kin are raised at the birth, both ways; an older save's at the load.
  - A death removes the dead from the ties of those it knew (`dropTies`, as before); with the cap and forgetting one side of a tie can go alone, so a pawn may hold a tie to the dead until it next renormalises. Nothing reads it: talk and company draw the living, `Culture.dailyStep` skips the dead.
  - State: `pawn.tieT` and `pawn.homeHours`, owned by `sim/relations` (`src/owners.json`, `@state` lines), with invariants in `checkState` (`checkTies`: ties to other pawns, living or dead since the pawn's `tieT`, finite and above 0, at most the cap; `tieT` no later than now; `homeHours` whole and within the talk hours). Older saves: `st.contacts` is left out of the decoded state, and `migrateTies` gives each pawn a scale from the load, trims any over the cap and raises kin to the floor.
  - Counters: `contactPairs` goes; `walkTies` now counts the ties walked by renormalising and by making room under the cap; `tiePairs` (new) the pairs given a day at home or a job together. `tests/counters.json` re-recorded: `walkTies` 12.82 to 0.054 a living pawn-day, `walkDaily` 6.0 to 5.0, `contactPairs` 61.8 gone, `tiePairs` 2.88; the rest move with the run (`routeRefresh` 1.02 to 1.44, the others within 10%).
  - `tests/relations.js`: the scale (a tie read a year later is half; a talk adds 0.5 as of now; renormalising keeps every tie and drops those under 0.02), the kin floor a season after it is raised, a day at home, a job together, and the cap.
