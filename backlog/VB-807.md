# VB-807: Climate: a world's latitude, three bands, the year's curve and the winter's dates
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: roads
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Climate and travel beyond walking (Climate; The winter's dates); Weather, seasons and daylight (Temperature); docs/plans/VB-190.md, sections 5.1 and 6
Goal: Climate by latitude (Kim, 3 Nov, decision 16: colder further north): a world has a latitude, a place has a band, and the winter's dates are known days that fall due, so that later tasks change the ground a few times a year and never daily.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `src/sim/climate.js` (`sim/climate`; find its owner in target-v2 section 4.2 first, and say in the task file where the weather's module of VB-354 ends and this begins): `CLIMATE` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each value with its meaning: the four climates (southern 57, middle 59, northern 62, far north 65 degrees), `degPerRow` 0.015 (the map's span north to south, as compressed as its distances), the curve (`janAt56` 0.5, `janPerDeg` -1.25, `julAt56` 17, `julPerDeg` -0.15, `coldest` winter day 7), `swing` 3 degrees (what a hard or a mild winter moves the cold half of the year), and the thresholds `snow` -1, `lake` 60, `sound` 300, `soundShut` 150 (degree-days of frost, a game day counting 7.6), `rot` 20 and `open` 60 (degree-days of thaw).
- [ ] `w.lat`, set at generation from the climate chosen (the middle one where none is given, so every world of today is the middle world and no seed changes); in the save. Three bands by row (thirds of the map), each read at its middle row and at sea level. The generation screen offers the four climates beside size, seed and scenario (`node tests/smoke.js` and the browser test that opens the screen).
- [ ] The year's dates in closed form, once a year on autumn day 1, from the curve and the winter's warmth (VB-354's question for the season, -1 to 1): for each band `frost` (snow lies, the ground bears), `lakeBears`, `soundShut`, `soundBears`, `thaw` (ice stops bearing, snow goes, the ground is soft) and `open` (water open, the ground firm), each a whole game day or none. They go into the due-queue as plain data (`st.climate`, in `WORLD`, with an owner in `src/owners.json` and invariants: the dates in order, none in a band whose curve stays above freezing, at most 6 a band); a date falling due sets the band's state and emits one event. Nothing reads the state yet but the log (a line a band's change, through `log`, with an event kind `tests/record.js` knows) and VB-354's thaw and frost, which become the band's (and later by `lapse` 8 degrees over the land's height for a field on high ground: a named tunable, reported, with a `tuning` note).
- [ ] A constructed check (`tests/tiny.js`): the dates of the plan's table for the bands at 58, 59, 60, 62 and 65 degrees in a normal, a mild and a hard winter (`node docs/plans/VB-190-ice.js` prints them); a world at 57 has no date in a normal winter.
- [ ] Reported, with no target: over 200 drawn winters at each climate, the days snow lies, lake ice bears and sound ice bears (median and range), and the share of winters with each.
- [ ] Numbers before and after on populous medium at year 0, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 (`node docs/plans/VB-190-water.js ROOT medium 3,7,11,42`, the plan's measures), and years on populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years, and founders small, seed 7, 50 years: they are the middle world's, where the thaw and the frost fall where VB-354 put them, so growth, grain and hungry days should not move; say so with the figures. The northern world's are reported beside them (principle 6: no target).
- Cost (rule 9): 3 bands x 6 dates a year, computed once; nothing a day (rule 4). No pass over tiles.

Noted by VB-190, 16 November 2026.
Decisions (22 Nov 2026):
- `CLIMATE` lives in `sim/climate`, not `sim/rules.js`: only that module reads it (VB-419), and rules.js would have passed its 700 lines.
- `st.climate` is state saved whole, not in `WORLD` (that list is `w`'s tile arrays); `w.lat` goes in the save beside the seed.
- The winter's warmth is wanted on autumn day 1, before winter is drawn: `winterAhead` draws winter's season then, from the same stream
  in the same order (nothing else draws from it), so no number moves; its line is still told as winter begins.
- The thaw and first frost move by the world's latitude (`shiftOf`: southern -1/+1 days, northern +2/-2, far north +3/-3), not by a
  field's band: by band the medium map's middle world would move a day (close call; VB-1483 does the band and the lapse).
- The thaw is one date a band at 20 degree-days (no ice bears); a sound's earlier rot at 10 is not a seventh date.
- Medium at year 0 is not run: `w.lat` does not enter generation (`tools/same.js` shows the world entries unchanged).
