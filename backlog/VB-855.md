## Implementation notes (VB-855)
Tests only; nothing under `src/` changed. The eleven scripts run on the small generated islands of `tests/lib/probe.js` (`island`,
`scenario`; VB-853) and move from EXTENDED to INTEGRATION in `tests/suites.js`. One keeps a night mode, `soil --extended`, which says
so in its first lines.

Three were red on the run's branch before this task (so red at night), and now assert what the branch does:
- `hay`: its constructed barns (`{ hay, haySpoilt, herds }`) threw in `spoilHay`, which reads `st.farmsteads[hh.farmstead].barn` since
  VB-255; they now have `farmstead: null` (no farmstead, so no barn building). The rules asserted are unchanged.
- `dig`: `KINDS.dig.urgency(null, null, ...)` threw, since VB-367 reads the job's good (peat weighs the pile's shortfall); it is now
  asked of a clay job, and still equals the house's upkeep (`MATCH.urgency.repair`).
- `drying`: founders at seed 7 on the small map built no boat in a year and a half. Since VB-393 a boat is begun only by a household
  with a year's need above its grain floor and two free hands, and since VB-255 only by one with no other project, at spring day 3;
  none of founders or populous at seed 7 on the small island begins one in five years (0 boats each year). The run now counts boats and
  asserts dried fish made and eaten; the boat's beginning, timber, work, building and catch stay asserted on the constructed scene, which
  calls `boatYear` on a fresh founders state. The shortfall is a tuning note (below).

Decision (close call): the soil's ten-year run stays at night as `soil --extended` rather than deleted: the farms with cattle keeping
their soil (at least 0.95) and the farm without falling below them is a balance of a decade's crops and dung, and in four years on the
small island fresh fields (soil 1.4, above the cap 1.15) take no manure at all, so nothing tells the farms apart. What a run shows of the
mechanism is now in verify: a year on, every field's soil set below the cap (1, 0.9, 0.8 in turn), the spring's days 1 to 6 cart the heap
of each farm with two loads or more onto its fields, its poorest first, and no farm without a heap gains soil.
