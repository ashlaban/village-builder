# VB-252: The seasonal plan: the culture's year as a template, golden unchanged
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Work: opportunities, plans and tasks (design.md, section 7); docs/review-work.md, section 2; design.md section 7 (the year)
Goal: Layer 1 of how a holder chooses (design.md, section 7): the culture's year as a template. Today's order and targets are constants spread over the kinds and the role rankings. Put them in one table per culture, which the review reads, with today's values, so that the order (VB-023), a head's foresight (VB-254) and the player (VB-253) each change one row.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A template per culture, declared as rules of `VB['sim/registry']` (declared before the first seed or load; never in saved state): for each season, the routines in their order, and a target for each store (the woodpile by season, hay in winters, seed, fields per hand). Its first values are today's (`woodTarget`, `woodWinter`, `ENOUGH`, `fieldsWanted`'s numbers), the same for every culture.
- [ ] The seasonal plan, a second plan per household beside the standing one (`kind: 'season'`), made at each season's first review from the template: its steps are the season's routines in order; the plan's targets (VB-248) are copied from it. The review reads steps and targets from the plan, never from the template directly, so a later change to one household's plan touches no other.
- [ ] The order is recorded and not yet enforced beyond what the role rankings do today: `tests/golden.json` unchanged. (VB-023 enforces the first order: sowing before the next hoeing.)
- [ ] The template's value read in an inner loop is compiled to an array at the season's turn (rule 6); `value()` allocates nothing.
- [ ] `node tools/sim.js --household N --report plans` prints a household's plans: targets, steps, next review. Invariants: one seasonal plan per occupied household, of the current season. `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals.
Cost: one small record per household a season.
