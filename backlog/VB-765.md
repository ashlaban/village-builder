## Notes (VB-765)
- Class of each job kind is a table in `tools/strata.js` (a test fails on a kind without one). Calls: brewing, fish drying/racking, tar, quern making, digging are craft (not farming); fell/grub, salt, slaughter, boats and carts are farming; quarry/pick, wells, mending and fetching for building are household work.
- Not-farming is not ~0 today but 100 to 120 per 1,000 (brewing and fish drying), since "all craft hours" counts. The criterion's "near 1,000 / 0 / 0" is out by that; no allowance written.
- Heads: the craft's share of food over 3 years is not measured; the tool uses the craft's share of hours (half or more) and says so. "Product left the household" cannot be told: printed as such.
- Sink: M from pawns alive at a year's start and end in another village; S from gift entries in the 3-year ledgers whose giver is a household of another village. Populous: births exceed deaths, so M/(d-b) is infinite and S/c (2.7 to 9.3 people at years 21-30, seed 7) binds.
- Bands (populous medium, nightly): work line sampled twice a day (10:00, 15:00) in `runScenario`, only where `strata` is set.
- Today (every-hour samples; farming / not farming / elite per 1,000, years 1-10 and 21-30 or 41-50): populous small seed 7: 889/111/0, 878/122/0; seed 42: 897/103/0, 897/103/0; founders small seed 7 (year 50): 885/115/0, 891/109/0. Every household a farm in the heads line.
