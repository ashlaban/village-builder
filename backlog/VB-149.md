# VB-149: Fresh land yields more for its first crops

Goal: Virgin and burnt-over ground gave far more than old fields for its first crops; settlers went to "more fertile ground". Give a field broken on ground never farmed a soil above manure's cap that wears down crop by crop. Depends on VB-146 (done).
