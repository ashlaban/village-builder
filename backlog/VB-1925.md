# VB-1925: The generator lays exactly one salt spring a map, picked by the seed
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, World and scale (Ground); section 17 (Salt is scarce); src/gen/geology.js (VEINS salt row, lay); tests/geology.js
Touches: gen/geology (VEINS, lay); tests/geology.js
Goal: Every generated map holds exactly one salt spring, on the tile and island the seed picks, and a saved world keeps the springs it has.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `lay` in `src/gen/geology.js` places exactly one salt spring on a map (one tile, a vein of size 1, endless as today), on a tile of the salt host that the seed picks among all the islands' hosts; the same seed gives the same spring (the test's two generations agree). Where no tile of the map fits the host, the spring goes on the land tile that fits it best (the task says how); the map never has none.
- [ ] The other kinds' draws are unchanged: the other deposits and rocks of seeds 7, 42 and 3 on the test islands are tile for tile as before the change (the salt row's draws are kept in step as the generator already does for hosts with none).
- [ ] `tests/geology.js`: on seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 and 99 and on a map of several islands, the count of salt tiles is exactly 1; the existing check that the spring does not run out stays.
- [ ] A saved world is not regenerated: loading a save made before the change keeps its springs (none, one or several), checked on a constructed old state; the editor's `setDeposit` still places salt by hand.
