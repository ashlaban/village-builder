# VB-083: Seasons on the map
Status: blocked
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: Open questions (Seasons on the map, such as snow in winter); Systems overview (Presentation: seasons missing).
Goal: The yearly round is the shape of the game, but a map in winter looks like a map in summer. Seasons on the map mark time passing at a glance, at every speed. Depends on VB-025 (a real-browser check catches drawing errors); if it is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A tint per season over the painted land: fresh green in spring, full green in summer, ochre and russet in autumn on broadleaf and meadow, bare broadleaf in winter; pine stays green.
- [ ] Snow on land from winter day 2 to spring day 3, and lakes iced over; fields and roofs under snow.
- [ ] The change is spread over frames like the tree-stage repaints, and costs under 2 ms a frame on a medium map in the Chromium test (VB-025).
