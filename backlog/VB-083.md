# VB-083: Seasons on the map
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: roads
Phase: implement
Design ref: Open questions (Seasons on the map, such as snow in winter); Systems overview (Presentation: seasons missing).
Goal: The yearly round is the shape of the game, but a map in winter looks like a map in summer. Seasons on the map mark time passing at a glance, at every speed. Depends on VB-025 (a real-browser check catches drawing errors); if it is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Rewritten by slice 10's plan (VB-190, 16 Nov 2026; `design.md`, Climate and travel beyond walking; `docs/plans/VB-190.md`, sections 5.1, 5.2 and 8). The earlier criteria put snow on fixed dates (winter day 2 to spring day 3); the dates are now the band's, from `sim/climate` (VB-807) and the ground by season (VB-808).
- [ ] A tint per season over the painted land: fresh green in spring, full green in summer, ochre and russet in autumn on broadleaf and meadow, bare broadleaf in winter; pine stays green. The turn of the colours follows the band's thaw and first frost, so the north of a map turns before its south.
- [ ] Snow on land, fields and roofs from the band's frost to its thaw; none on a world or in a winter that has none. Lakes and rivers iced from the frost, and the sea within 3 tiles of land where the sound shuts; ice that bears and ice that does not (the freeze-up and the break-up) are told apart at a glance, the same way on every water (principle "Like is shown alike"), and the map's key says which is which.
- [ ] Winter ways: a lane stays visible as trodden snow (Worn paths, 6), and a way worn over the ice shows as trodden snow while the ice bears.
- [ ] A boat is drawn at its landing or shore tile and, under way, on the water with those aboard; a sledge and a cart are drawn with the pawn that takes them (Carts, Not yet: a cart is not drawn). One drawing a kind.
- [ ] The page reads the band's state and the vehicles' places and writes nothing (Rules for cost 7). The change on a date is spread over frames like the tree-stage repaints, a band at a time, and costs under 2 ms a frame on a medium map in the Chromium test (VB-025); `node tests/smoke.js` and the `tests/browser*.js` it touches.
- [ ] Screenshots under `docs/img/` (`node tools/shot.js`): one island at midsummer, at the freeze-up, at midwinter with the ice bearing, and at the break-up, on the middle and the northern world.
