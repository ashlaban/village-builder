# VB-767: A head's remembered yield of each source of food
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: town
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Fields a household wants); docs/plans/VB-189.md, section 5.5
Goal: A head knows what each of its household's sources of food has brought, in poor years and on average, so that an estimate of next year's food is the head's own (principle 14; Kim, 16 Nov).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A yearly tally for each household of rations by source: fields, animals (milk and meat), fish, hunting and gathering, deals and craft, days worked for others, gifts and help. It is added where `src/sim/stores.js` already books a food operation with its reason (no new pass); a reason that fits no source is listed in the task file.
- [ ] At the head's planning day after the harvest the tally closes into a ring of `FIELDS.memory` 8 years a source and starts again. Plain data on the household with an owner in `src/owners.json`, in the save, with invariants: at most 8 x 7 numbers, none negative, the year's tally equal to the booked operations of a constructed year. A new head keeps the household's ring: the memory is the farm's, told to the heir (a close call, said in the task file).
- [ ] `expects(st, hh, source)` returns `{ poor, mean, years }`: the second lowest and the mean of the ring. With fewer than 3 years, `poor` is 0 and `mean` half the mean so far (a head does not lean on what it has barely seen).
- [ ] `caution(st, hh)`: 0 bold to 1 cautious, from the head's bias on fields (`plan.head.bias.fields`, VB-254), as 0.5 + (bias - 1) / (2 x `Traits.HEAD.bias`), within 0 and 1; VB-476's lean replaces it when that exists. No new trait (the plan's close call). The mapping is written in the task file with the spread it gives on populous small, seed 7.
- [ ] Nothing reads it yet: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`. No line in `tools/stocks.js` (no stored good). `tools/balance.js` (or a report of `tools/sim.js`) prints a household's memory beside the food by source it measures from outside, and the two agree on a tiny run.
- Cost (rule 9): one addition for each food operation already booked; 170 households x once a year x 7 sources to close; 56 numbers a household in the save (under 15 KB at `medium-ref`).
