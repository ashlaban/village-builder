Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A visitor (`sim/away`, a journey with `why: 'visit'`) who has arrived asks a household of the village visited for a bed, by the rule the `lodge` verb's guest already has (`lodgeWhy`: the head's yes, by its opinion of him); with a yes he sleeps in that house for his nights there (roof `house`, the host's fire), and stands at its door; with no house that will have him he sleeps at the green as today.
- [x] Which house he asks first is a rule with a reason written in the task file (the house of the woman he came for, or of kin, before any other); the host and the guest meet as those at home do (`src/sim/relations.js`, Where pawns meet).
- [x] Invariants for the visit's bed (`tests/lib/invariants.js`, the stays away), and a check in `tests/journeys.js` on the three-village state: a visitor with a host sleeps under its roof, one refused sleeps at the green.
- [x] Numbers before and after at populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years: visits a year, the visitors' health on their return, deaths on a visit.

Decisions (VB-1151):
- Whom he asks: a visitor asks at most `AWAY.asks` (3) households of the village, kin of his (kinship above 0) first, then the house of an unmarried woman of its asking queue (the woman he came for), each by her id; the first head whose opinion of him is not below 0 (`hostWhy`, shared with `lodgeWhy`) hosts him. Reason: a house takes in kin and the family of the match he is there to make; no other house has a reason to. Read from `askingIn`, a few dozen at most: no new loop over a growing collection. No kin or woman to ask: the green, as before.
- Host and guest meet as at home do: the guest stands outdoors at the host's door (walkLeg to its house tile), so the outdoors encounter draws them (`sim/encounters`); no new encounter kind. Warmth is the host's (`nightCost`, roof `house`).
- The stay's `bed` is set only while he is a guest at a door; `to` stays his home house; invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js` for it.
- Numbers (populous small, 30 years, before -> after): seed 7: visits a year 0.77 -> 1.10, health on return 77.2 -> 93.4, deaths on a visit 0 -> 0; seed 42: 1.20 -> 1.37, 84.6 -> 95.0, 1 -> 0. Every visitor who arrived was hosted (23/23 -> 33/33; 36/36 -> 41/41 by the opinion rule). The visit counts differ by the runs diverging, not by a rule that sets out visits.
