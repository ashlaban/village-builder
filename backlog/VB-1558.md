# VB-1558: Cost: a task newly due is slotted into the day, not the day weighed anew
Status: done (2026-11-24)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: `design.md` section 7, The day's work (the slotting, 23 Nov); `docs/cost.md`; `backlog/VB-1554.md` (the measure)
Goal: VB-1554 measured the day's allotment as the largest part of the jobs line (31%): a household's day is allotted 4.4 times a day, and half of the pairs weighed are for a task the due-queue has just opened (the milking 18%, the morning with the water 18%, the cooking 15%). Kim, 23 November: a task newly due may be slotted in. When the due-queue opens a task for a household (`refreshJobs`, the due-queue's part), weigh each member for that task alone and put it into the day's lists as they stand (`plan.day.lists`), by its fit among the tasks already there and within the member's hours left (`plan.day.free`); a task with a deadline today (`late`) goes first in its hand's list and calls it, as `allot` does. The whole day is weighed anew only at the morning, at the head's review, when a member looks for work (`lookForWork`, `takeDay`'s missed member) and when a task of the household or its village is posted or taken down otherwise.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A task the due-queue opens is slotted, not the day allotted anew: `dayPair` a pawn-day at populous small, seed 7, falls by at least a quarter from its value at the task's start (`node tests/counters.js`, before and after, `--update` for the intended change); seeds 3, 11 and 19 reported. *Unticked: it falls by 18%, 21.10 to 17.32 (seed 7); 19.56 to 16.03 (3), 20.50 to 17.06 (11), 24.55 to 20.37 (19); on the probe 9.39 to 7.67 (recorded). The due-queue's openings no longer allot a day at all; what is left are the allotments the rule keeps: the morning 27% of the pairs, 14:00 23% (VB-1546, added after VB-1554's measure), a member's look 21%, the reviews about 20%.*
- [x] The slot and `allot` share the fit, the join factor, the hands' cap: no second copy of the rule (a test on a constructed household holds that a task slotted lands where the morning's allotment would have put it when nothing else changed).
- [x] A test holds: the milking opened at its hour reaches a member's list without the other lists changing; the meal's hand is called from work as before; a task slotted for a member with no hours left goes to the next fit.
- [x] Behaviour changes, so reported before and after at seeds 7, 3, 11 and 19, populous small, 2 years: hungry household-days, the harvest missed, meals cooked late or not at all, cows not milked. Whether households were better off is said in a line.
- [x] `npm run format` and `npm run verify` pass; `sim/jobs` over 700 formatted lines names or opens its split task.
