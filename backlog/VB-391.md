# VB-391: An ask for help that brings nothing is not the season's ask
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: Exchange, goodwill and obligations (The gift; Lumps and occasions). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-219, question 2
Goal: Under the seasonal ask, deaths from hunger in households of one rose from 0 to 14 over 16 runs: a lone person whose ask brings nothing (no kin with a surplus, the store empty) waits a whole season. An ask that moved no goods marks nothing worth remembering, so it does not count.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A seasonal ask that moves no goods at all (no gift from kin, nothing from the village store) is not recorded as the season's ask: the household asks again on a later day, at most once a day. An ask that brought anything counts as today.
- [ ] No event and no goodwill is booked for an ask that brought nothing (Lumps and occasions).
- [ ] A test: a lone household with no kin's surplus and an empty store asks again when the store is filled later in the season.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, by VB-219's 16 runs (seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42, populous small, 15 years, four ways of stepping the clock): hungry household-days, deaths from hunger and starvation, those in households of one (14 today), asks made, and the cost of the daily check against hunger and meals' line.
