# VB-391: A voice's note on the Customs tab: how far it leans, and why
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: Kim, session of 12 November 2026 (customs pane review); backlog/VB-389.md
Goal: Kim: a face first shows a small note on why the person leans as they do, and a percentage: how much the player needs to convince them.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A face of an opened custom (VB-389) is a button; chosen, a note under the ways says: the person's name, as a link to their card; how far they lean, as one pair of numbers ("leans 46% to “Equal shares”, 41% to “Children first”": their first and second way, from `att`); and the strongest pull towards the way they favour. Chosen again, or another phrase opened, the note goes.
- [ ] The pulls are those of `driftPerson`, weighed as it weighs them, for the favoured way: their own cause ("Argues this cause"), what they learned young ("Raised to it"), the people they deal with ("Close to <name>, who favours it", the tie that weighs most among those who favour it, else "Those around them lean this way"), a cause argued near them ("Swayed by <name>’s cause"), their own interest ("It serves their interest"). Where no pull is towards it: "Nothing pulls them further this way".
- [ ] One read-only function of `Culture` gives the pulls (not enumerable, as the wiring operations); it writes nothing, draws no randomness and is called only when a face is chosen. `tests/golden.json` unchanged.
- [ ] Tests: a rule test of the function (a person raised to a way, one with a tie who argues a cause, one arguing their own); `tests/smoke.js` and `tests/browser.js`: a face chosen shows the note with two percentages and a reason, its name opens the person's card. The hard checks of verify pass but for `tests/hunger.js` (VB-389's flag); `index.html` rebuilt.
- 12 Nov 2026 (session with Kim): the note names the strongest pull (Kim: yes); one number is enough for how far (Kim).
- Cost (rule 9): one pass over one person's ties and one over their village's members, a click.
