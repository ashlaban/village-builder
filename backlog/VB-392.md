# VB-392: The player's wish to settle new land
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player, commands and views (The player and what they see). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-212, questions 1 and 2
Goal: The moving round draws a chance for each waiting adult to lead a party to new land, and for a sibling to follow; the player's wishes (st.player.wishes: out, claim, serve) have no field for it, so the played pawn settles only by the draw.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `st.player.wishes` gains `settle`: `true` passes the draws of `SETTLE.chance` (leading a party) and `SETTLE.follow` (following a sibling), `false` never leads or follows, `null` draws as now; the round's other rules (age, provisions, land within reach, the village crowded) decide as for anyone.
- [ ] The wish is set by a command, as the other wishes, and shown with them in the page; a married couple is as today (the pawn panel says the spouse's choice may move the pawn).
- [ ] A test of the three values; the golden file unchanged (no played pawn in it).
