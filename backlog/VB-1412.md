# VB-1412: One order for every due-queue (due, then id), and removal by key
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: none (code structure). backlog/VB-1406.md; docs/cost.md (Rules for cost, 4)
Goal: VB-1406 kept each queue's order, so sim/due has two inserts (by due then id; by due, after those due at the same time) and st.offers, st.occasions, st.obligationsDue and st.asking still remove by scanning (indexOf, findIndex). With breaking changes allowed (Kim, 21 Nov 2026), every queue is ordered by due then a unique id, sim/due loses append and after, and every removal is a search by key.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Every due-queue (`st.workDue`, `st.legDue`, `st.plans.due`, `st.asking` and `st.askingBy`, `st.visits`, `st.offers`, `st.occasions`, `st.obligationsDue`) is ordered by due, then an id no two entries share (a visit's and an asking's: its pawn); `sim/due` exports no `append` or `after`; no `indexOf` or `findIndex` removes from a due-queue (an occasion whose due moves is removed before it moves).
- [ ] Behaviour may change only in the order of entries due at the same time: `tools/same.js` against the starting commit, and each difference traced to such a tie or reported; numbers before and after at founders and populous, seed 7, small, 5 years (marriages, offers answered, occasions held).
- [ ] `tests/due.js` extended; `npm run verify` passes; baselines do not rise.
Notes / questions:
- 21 Nov 2026: from Kim's question whether the freedom to break changes VB-1406; one global scheduler in place of the eight queues was considered and rejected (each queue is drained at its own point of the step, and that order is behaviour).
