# VB-1736: Patronage in a case: a client leans its voice to its patron's side at a hearing
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, Loans, obligations and cases (the hearing, VB-315); patronage (VB-708 leanOf)
Touches: sim/cases
Goal: A client's voice at a hearing leans to its patron's side, as it does in other decisions of the meeting
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] When a case is heard at the meeting (VB-315's hearing, the practice "Who judges a case"), a voice whose patron's holder is a party adds `PATRON.lean` (0.3, of the weight of its own leaning) for its patron's side, as in a decision of the meeting (VB-708's `leanOf`); under "One's own cause" the parties' households stand aside or vote as the practice says, and a client is not a party.
- [ ] A test on a constructed state: a client of the creditor's holder upholds more often by the lean; one whose tie decayed does not.
- [ ] Cost: one more term a voice in a hearing.
