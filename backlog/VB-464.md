Goal: Kim agreed patronage on 12 November and its lasting form on 13 November: as first worded the owed work was cleared in a year or two and was negligible in the review's mock.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Help's order of asking (`src/sim/help.js`) gains the patron: after kin within a day's walk and before the village store, a hungry household asks the household of its village with the most grain above the feast's threshold (`FEAST.above`, VB-458); it gives by the gift's rule (its floor, its allowance), carried as help is (VB-461), and goodwill is booked between the two holders.
- [ ] The tie: at the patron's planning day after harvest, each household whose holder owes its holder more than `PATRON.above` (30) days of the client's need in goodwill owes `PATRON.days` (2) days to the next autumn, as days owed with the source `patron` (VB-462). The days do not pay the balance down. No tie is stored: it is read from the balance each year, so goodwill's decay ends it.
- [ ] The vote: in a decision of the meeting (`sim/decisions`, VB-313) a client's holder adds a term to its voice for the side its patron's holder takes, `PATRON.lean` (0.3, of the weight of its own leaning); in a case where the patron is a party, the same, under "One's own cause".
- [ ] The household panel says "Client of Olof Persson (owes 2 days a year)" and "Patron of 3 households"; a log line when a tie begins and ends.
- [ ] A test on a constructed state: a balance over the threshold gives 2 days and a lean; one that has decayed under it gives neither; the days leave the balance as it was.
- [ ] Numbers, populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 40 years: patrons and clients a village by decade, a tie's length in years (the plan expects 4 to 8), days given, votes a lean turned, households helped by a patron against by the store; hungry household-days by household; and whether patrons are the alderman's household (the two roads joining).
- [ ] Cost (rule 9): one pass over a holder's balances (at most 32) a year on its planning day; one more term a voice in a vote. No daily work.
