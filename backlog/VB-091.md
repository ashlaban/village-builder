Acceptance criteria (draft, pending `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-091):

- [ ] A new `DIMS` entry `outside`, "Children born outside marriage", with options `equal` "Inherit like other children", `after` "Inherit after children born in marriage" and `none` "Do not inherit", and a value in each `ARCHETYPES` entry (Old Nordic `after`, malleability 0.5).
- [ ] `heirRanking` reads it for the children of both parents: under `after`, children born outside marriage follow every child born in marriage; under `none`, they are left out (their own children too).
- [ ] If the parents later marry, their child counts as born in marriage, as the answer to `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-091 question 2 decides.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed family for each option.
