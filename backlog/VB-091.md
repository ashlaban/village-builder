# VB-091: Forest spread into open land
Status: blocked
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: animals
Phase: implement
Design ref: Land and ecology (Forest spread: Decided; Grazing: Decided; Grazing at low cost: pressure feeds forest spread, Agreed); Making a hay meadow (a meadow left unmown and ungrazed falls under the forest-spread rule); Inheritance and expansion, Empty farms (meadow goes on to scrub and young forest).
Goal: Forest turns nearby meadow, and fields by way of meadow, into scrub and then woodland, unless grazing or farming keeps the land open (Decided). Today trees only grow back on tiles that still have some (`growTrees`, `src/sim/forest.js`); open land never closes, so the commons stay open for ever and grazing has nothing to hold back. Depends on the grazing-pressure task that VB-047 writes ("herding and grazing pressure"); until that exists, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The front: once a game day, open tiles (meadow, heath, and the grass of an empty farm's fields) next to a forest tile, or to a scrub tile at half cover or more, gain trees. They reach a quarter of full cover (scrub) in 8 years and full cover in 30, as regrowth does. At full cover the tile becomes broadleaf forest, or pine if most of its forest neighbours are pine. Its `treesMax` is drawn as for generated forest.
- [ ] Scrub is a meadow or heath tile with trees: it is drawn with the existing tree stages (`treeStage`), and its trees can be felled for firewood like a forest tile's.
- [ ] Kept open: a field in use, a house, a green, a hay meadow mown that year, a tile with work on it in the last year (a clearing job or a realised claim), and a tile whose grazing pressure is at or above the "keeps open" threshold. Pressure at or above the "thins" threshold removes trees at a fifth of the growth rate.
- [ ] Clearing: scrub below half cover adds 10 h to a `clear` job (VB-010); at half cover or more the tile is felled (10 h) and grubbed (6 h) as forest (VB-031).
- [ ] The front is kept as a set of tiles, like `st.regrow` (`@state` documented), and only that set is visited each day; the PR reports the set's size and the daily cost on populous medium at seed 7.
- [ ] VB-067's interim rule (empty fields go to scrub only near woodland) is replaced by this one.
- [ ] Founders and populous medium runs at seed 7 over 50 years report open, scrub and forest tile counts per decade (reported, not asserted).
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
