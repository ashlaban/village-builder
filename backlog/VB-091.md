# VB-091: Forest spread into open land
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: animals
Phase: implement
Design ref: Land and ecology (Forest spread: Decided; Grazing: Decided; Grazing at low cost: pressure feeds forest spread, Agreed); Making a hay meadow (a meadow left unmown and ungrazed falls under the forest-spread rule); Inheritance and expansion, Empty farms (meadow goes on to scrub and young forest).
Goal: Forest turns nearby meadow, and fields by way of meadow, into scrub and then woodland, unless grazing or farming keeps the land open (Decided). Today trees only grow back on tiles that still have some (`growTrees`, `src/sim/forest.js`); open land never closes, so the commons stay open for ever and grazing has nothing to hold back. Depends on the grazing-pressure task that VB-047 writes ("herding and grazing pressure"); until that exists, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The front: once a game day, open tiles (meadow, heath, and the grass of an empty farm's fields) next to a forest tile, or to a scrub tile at half cover or more, gain trees. They reach a quarter of full cover (scrub) in 8 years and full cover in 30, as regrowth does. At full cover the tile becomes broadleaf forest, or pine if most of its forest neighbours are pine. Its `treesMax` is drawn as for generated forest.
- [x] Scrub is a meadow or heath tile with trees: it is drawn with the existing tree stages (`treeStage`), and its trees can be felled for firewood like a forest tile's.
- [x] Kept open: a field in use, a house, a green, a hay meadow mown that year, a tile with work on it in the last year (a clearing job or a realised claim), and a tile whose grazing pressure is at or above the "keeps open" threshold. Pressure at or above the "thins" threshold removes trees at a fifth of the growth rate.
- [x] Clearing: scrub below half cover adds 10 h to a `clear` job (VB-010); at half cover or more the tile is felled (10 h) and grubbed (6 h) as forest (VB-031).
- [x] The front is kept as a set of tiles, like `st.regrow` (`@state` documented), and only that set is visited each day; the PR reports the set's size and the daily cost on populous medium at seed 7.
- [x] VB-067's interim rule (empty fields go to scrub only near woodland) is replaced by this one.
- [x] Founders and populous medium runs at seed 7 over 50 years report open, scrub and forest tile counts per decade (reported, not asserted).
- [x] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR. (The golden file is retired, VB-408: nothing to re-record; `docs/testing.md` stands.)

Implementation (13 Nov 2026):
- New module `sim/spread` (material layer, loaded after `sim/grazing`, which it reads, and before the fields, meadows, plots and
  kinds that call it). It owns `st.front` (a Set of tiles, `@state` in the module) and `w.tended` (Int32Array, the day of the last work
  on a tile plus 1; in the save's WORLD list). `initFront` makes the front once at seed (one pass over the tiles) and at the load of an
  older save; `spreadForest` visits only the front, once a game day (sim/clock, after `growTrees`).
- The front holds open land (meadow or heath, not water, not a green or house: `w.dev` < 2) beside forest or thick scrub (half cover
  or more), and every tile with scrub. A tile that no longer qualifies is dropped when visited (lazily; a felled neighbour needs no
  scan). It is joined by a forest tile cut bare (`sim/forest` emits `cutBare`; the kernel module does not call up a layer) and by the open
  neighbours of a tile that becomes thick scrub or forest (added after the pass, visited from the next day).
- Rates: as regrowth, full cover in `SPREAD.years` (30) game years, linearly, so a quarter (scrub) in 7.5. `treesMax` is drawn by
  `gen/cover` `fullTrees` for the forest it will become (pine where most forest neighbours are pine), at its first growth; at full
  cover the biome is set and `treesMax` redrawn if the kind changed. Walking costs are told (`costChanged`); the page is told
  (`treeDirty`, `biomeDirty`). Stones are untouched.
- Kept open: grazing at or above `GRAZE.keepsOpen`; at or above `GRAZE.thins` the trees go at `SPREAD.thin` (a fifth) of the rate;
  work within `SPREAD.tendedDays` (a year: a plot's steps, a field's clearing and breaking, a meadow made, a field reclaimed); a
  household's field (in use, or to be reclaimed); its meadow mown within the last game year (`YEAR_DAYS`, rolling rather than the
  calendar year: a meadow mown last summer stays open through spring, before its next mowing); other land it uses; an empty farm's fields and meadows while a
  claimant restores it (`Projects.forEach`, made once a pass on first need). A house and a green leave the front.
- Scrub: a meadow or heath tile with trees. The page draws it as wood by its tree stage (`gen.js` decorateChunk treats it as
  broadleaf for the drawing; one line, `gen.js` is at its lint baseline). Thick scrub (half cover or more) is in a village's wood
  (`sim/territory` reckonWood, `woodland`) and cut for firewood as forest is; `fellTree` puts a scrub tile in the front, not in
  `st.regrow`. The invariant on a village's wood accepts it.
- Clearing: `scrubHours` adds `SPREAD.scrubHours` (10) below half cover and `FELL_HOURS + GRUB_HOURS` (16, now exported by
  `sim/forest`, which owns felling) at half or more, to a field's clearing (`clearHours`) and a meadow's; a plot fells and grubs thick
  scrub as steps of its own (`stepsFor`). `clearScrub` takes the trees off when the clearing is done.
- Decision (Deciding without Kim, 4: extending a system): reclaiming a field the forest has grown into at half cover or more costs the
  felling and grubbing besides Kim's `RECLAIM` hours (a field gone to forest is meadow again once reclaimed). Reason: the clearing
  rule above, applied to the other clearing job, so that a farm empty 30 years is not cleared as cheaply as one empty 8.
- VB-067's interim rule (`nearForest`) is gone: an empty farm's field is 'scrub' when the trees on its tile reach a quarter of full
  cover (`SPREAD.scrub`), 'fallow' when they are cleared or thinned off; a scenario's ruin laid out in scrub gets a quarter's trees.
- Cost: the front visited once a day, a counter `frontTile` (VB.COUNTERS; its key added by hand to `tests/counters.json` at 0, the
  probe has no forest). The grazing scale is taken once a pass (`yearScale`), not per tile.
