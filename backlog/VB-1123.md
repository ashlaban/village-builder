# VB-1123: Page: plan mode: the household's season planned on the map, in place of the Plan tab
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-1765
Priority: 2
Size: L
Design ref: docs/mocks/plan-mode-l.html with its screenshots docs/mocks/img/plan-mode-l.png and plan-mode-l-2.png (the latest; and -a to -k); design.md section 7 (The plan panel; a design pass comes first); principles "The future is someone's estimate", "Like is shown alike" and "Every act is a pawn's"
Goal: A head plans its household's season where the land is: who does what and when, what does not fit and why, and what the plan is expected to bring against what the household needs.
Acceptance criteria:
Kim's decisions are marked with their day (17 or 18 Nov 2026). Where a point is the mocks' and not Kim's, it says "proposed".

The frame
- [ ] **A moment** (17 Nov). On the played head's planning day and at each season's first review the game reminds the head that the plan is due; an option turns the reminders on or off (the bell), and with them on the game pauses and offers the mode. The head may open it at any other time. Leaving it goes on with the plan as it stands. A pawn that is not a head opens it read-only.
- [ ] **It replaces the Plan tab** (18 Nov): `src/page/plan.js` and the tab go when the mode is built; nothing is changed in the tab meanwhile.
- [ ] **One plan is shown, the head's** (18 Nov). No switch to the custom's plan. Two buttons, apart: undo (the last change back) and reset (the custom's plan). Reset, the reminder and going on are icons with a tooltip.
- [ ] **A key** (18 Nov): a button unfolds a legend of the marks, and every icon says what it is when pointed at. Proposed: the legend is open the first time the mode is.
- [ ] **Toggles move nothing** (18 Nov): a mark shown or hidden, the legend, a breakdown, a menu or a card change no other part's place or size. Marks have fixed slots; what unfolds lies over the map.

The strip: a member's season
- [ ] **A row a member**: the name, a slot for a person's problem, the season's days on the one meter (the daily chores, what the plan asks, what is free; red and over when it asks more than there is), and under the name the daily chores.
- [ ] **One bar of the season a member** (18 Nov): the planned actions lie on it one after the other, each with its icon and name. The grey foot is the share of each day the chores take. A window's end is a pin with the action's icon. A bar's uncertain end is striped (the head's estimate), and the next action is drawn at the end of that uncertainty; but it is started when the last is done, and the plan then shifts to show what happened (18 Nov). Where more would fit, the bar says so: the free stretch and the days of work it holds.
- [ ] **Work in three lengths** (18 Nov, yes to the proposal). What recurs daily is a chore: a pill under the member's name with its days in a season, handed to another by dragging it. What takes half a day or more is a bar. What is shorter and does not recur is gathered into one block of small work, which opens into its parts and is laid out on the day's strip (VB-477). The cut at half a day is the drawing's; what mechanism stands behind it is left for future work (18 Nov).
- [ ] **The seasons ahead** (18 Nov): after the twelve days, a cell a later season with what is known of it if this plan holds (the routines the calendar and the fields give, what this season carries over) and the days that asks against the days the member has. The next season may be planned as this one is; those after show the known load only (proposed; see Planning ahead, below).
- [ ] **Only known events** (18 Nov): a member who comes or leaves is shown from or to the day that is known (hired at the round and coming on a day; a marriage accepted and its day), and not before it is known. Who left is the past, and is in the last plan's outcome.
- [ ] **Skill on the bar** (18 Nov): a work bar carries the member's skill at the job on a fixed scale of four, novice to master, not a comparison within the household; pointed at, the level, the number and what the job grows it to.
- [ ] **A person's card** (18 Nov): a click on a member opens a quick card: skills on the one meter with the same scale and what this season adds, aptitudes, health, mood, the season's days.

Acting
- [ ] **Actions are chosen at a tile** (18 Nov). A click on a tile opens a menu there: the actions that tile takes, each shown in the members' hands (skill, the days of work, a made good's grade), one that cannot be done with its reason, and the tile's details as a pseudo-action ("About this tile": soil, the estimated yield with its range, the walk, the seed). What has no field is on the tile it happens at: the house (brewing, crafts, a feast, asking for a hand), the river (fishing), the bank (clay).
- [ ] **Replace** (18 Nov): with an action's bar selected, the action chosen next takes its place. The menu says what it replaces.
- [ ] **Delete** (18 Nov): a selected bar is removed by Backspace, by Delete, or by a delete button on the bar.
- [ ] **A new action** goes to the bar selected, else to the first free stretch of the member chosen in the menu (proposed, in place of the day's pointer of mocks E to J).
- [ ] **A missing need warns and can be met** (18 Nov): an action whose needs are not met (pottery with no clay) can still be placed; it carries the red mark, in the menu with what is missing, then on its bar and its tile. The menu offers the action that meets the need ("Dig clay first"), which is placed before it and linked to it: the second waits for what the first brings. Nothing teleports: the first's days include the carrying.
- [ ] **Planting is a field at a time** (18 Nov): "Plant" replaces "Hoe and sow"; each field is planted on its own, with the crop the head chooses for it (two fields of one crop and two of another), in an order the head sets.
- [ ] **Every change is a verb any head has** (17 Nov; principle "Every act is a pawn's"): the mode gives the player no choice the automatic head lacks. The verbs the mode needs and the game lacks are added for every head, or the choice is left out and said so: an action given to a member (`assign` or so; 18 Nov: yes); an action removed, replaced or moved in the order; a chore handed over; an action placed that waits for a need, and the link. `setTarget` goes (below). The room for the player is in judgement: the automatic head plans by custom and its own reckoning and gets by; a player who reads the mode does better (17 Nov).

The map
- [ ] **The farm's land is one territory** (18 Nov): one outline round each group of its joined tiles, with no tile's border inside; its land keeps its brightness and all other land is dimmed.
- [ ] **A tile at rest carries one mark or none** (18 Nov). A meadow, or a tile an action is planned on: one disc, its picture the next step, its colour the member who has it, a yellow ring for a change of the head's. A field: its crop (a picture and a colour a crop), its place in the order of planting, and a ring for how far the crop has grown; the details in the tile's menu. A tile that cannot take what is in hand: a cross, and why when pointed at. A tile chosen for a new field is outlined as planned, not yet the farm's. No tint at rest.
- [ ] **An overlay for the action in hand** (18 Nov): while "Break a field" is chosen, the open tiles in reach show their fertility, stronger for richer soil, with a small key on the map. Other actions that choose a tile get theirs the same way.
- [ ] **A problem is marked where it is** (18 Nov): a red exclamation mark on the tile it concerns, on its bar, or by the person's name when it is a person's (ill, or asked for more days than there are). A click opens that tile's or person's menu, which begins with the problem, why, and what could be done, each with what it would give (the last proposed). There is no box of problems.

The outcome
- [ ] **What is shown is the threshold of survival** (18 Nov: "and has always been"): what the household needs to get through the year, by the head's estimate. Nothing is stepped: the stepping of targets leaves the game, here and everywhere. A head still holds a level it fills a store to: the player reckons it in their head; a head not played reckons it as a margin it prefers over what survival needs, moved by its traits and skill, 1.5 times the baseline to start with (18 Nov). The margin is a named tunable (principle "Parameters, not patches"), and the mode shows the threshold, not the margin.
- [ ] **Food is one need of several sources** (18 Nov): one bar, a colour a source (grain, peas, dried fish, milk and butter, and what else is eaten); the i opens the breakdown, a row a source with its icon. Hay and firewood are needs of their own.
- [ ] **A need's bar**: solid for what is in store, striped for what this plan is expected to add, the mark of the need, and a pointer where a poor year would leave it (18 Nov). The i shows the numbers.
- [ ] **A change shows on the outcome** (18 Nov): an action added, removed or moved updates the estimates at once, and the need it touches shows the change (what was lost or gained against the bar before, the row marked) until the next change.
- [ ] **The surplus and what it is for** (18 Nov): what the plan leaves above the food's need, in a likely year and a poor one, and its uses, which are actions at the house: a feast and crafts. What else there is to do with it is looked up, not listed here: the game info panel (VB-1124; Kim's answer of 18 Nov) holds every action, where it applies, what it needs and what it gives, and the surplus line and the menus link to it.
- [ ] **The loop is closed** (17 Nov): for the plan the last review made, what the head estimated beside what came of it. What has happened is shown as it was, with no estimate's mark.
- [ ] **Every estimate is the head's** (principle "The future is someone's estimate"): it has a range where the head's judgement gives one, a head of poorer judgement sees a poorer estimate, and the mark of an estimate (the wits icon in the mocks) is explained in the legend.

How it is judged
- [ ] **Usability comes first** (17 Nov): a player who has not read the design finds, in the mode, what will not fit this season, why, and what to change. The plan names how this is checked (a playthrough as a new holder, written up, before and after). No one but the mocks' author has yet tried them.
- [ ] **As little text as will do** (17 Nov; principle "Like is shown alike"): a routine, a good, a member, a skill, a crop, a window and a state are shown by the icon and the one meter the page has, reused. New glyphs only for new concepts, added to the one set (drawn so far: go on, undo, reset, the key, a problem, skill's four bars, a jar, a pod; a glyph a crop is still owed, since barley and rye share one).
- [ ] **One presentation** (principle "Like is shown alike"): a member, an action, a need and a field look the same here, on the farm's card and on the day's strip; the season's bar and the day's are one object at two lengths.
- [ ] **Cost** (rules 7 and 9): the estimates are worked out on demand while the mode is open, never in a step; the plan names their cost (households, times the season's actions, times one estimate, times the seasons looked ahead) and measures it on founders and on a late save.
- [ ] A design pass first: `design.md` section 7 (the plan panel, the targets and their steppers) is rewritten for what is decided here, and the open tasks that build on the tab or on stepped targets are confirmed, rewritten or closed (the notes below list those found on 18 Nov).
- [ ] The mode's functional test in the page's tests; a screenshot in the PR.

## Walks through the interface
Each walk is a requirement told as what a player does and sees. The household is the mocks': Norrgården, year 3, spring; Torsten 32, the head, and Märta 29; four fields, six meadows; Karin, 14, hired at the moving round, comes on the first day of summer. After each step: the mock and frame that draws it. They were checked on 18 Nov by a script that opens each mock and looks for the step's part (46 parts found; the two not found are meant: a hidden mark's slot, and the overlay off at rest). "Not drawn" means no mock shows it; "drawn, not working" means the picture is there and the click does nothing. The walks marked **(Kim's)** are the cases Kim gave.

1. The player clicks "Rake meadows" on Märta's bar. It takes the dark frame, a delete button appears at its corner, and the six meadows it covers are ringed on the map. (L, frame 1)

## Planning ahead
Kim, 18 Nov: how feasible is planning ahead, assuming the last season's plan held? The calendar's part is cheap and sure: each crop's windows and each field's stage give the routines of the seasons ahead; `sim/outlook` already projects the supplies four seasons on, and the plan's `ahead` (VB-254) looks a season on for wood and hay. Not there: a season's order and hands made before its first review. To build: the review's order-making callable for a season not begun, on a projected state, on demand while the mode is open. The limit: ranges compound, so the second season ahead is wide; births, deaths, leavings not yet known and the weather are not in it. Not measured.
