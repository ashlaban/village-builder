# VB-1649: The plan's plant task: a field carried through from clearing to sowing as one task
Status: done (2026-11-24)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/ui2/HANDOFF.md (Known after the third batch); src/sim/playerplan.js; src/sim/agenda.js (then, layout); src/sim/projects.js; src/sim/fieldwork.js
Goal: The first spring's choice is whether to start a field, not four visits to one tile.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A task of the plan may be a plant task: `{plant: tile, kind: 'plant', site: tile}` beside the tasks by key, added by `Sim.plan(st, 'add', {plant: tile, at?})`: accepted for a tile that is a field of the played pawn's household not yet sown this year and still within reach of its sowing window, or the place of its household's open project making a field (whether or not that step's job is posted yet); refused with its reason otherwise, and no tile twice.
- [x] The feeder takes, for a plant task, whatever step is open at that tile that the pawn may take now, in the order the work goes (clearing, breaking, then the field's own work up to sowing: the hoeing or ploughing and the sowing), by the `work` verb as any task; between steps, while the next job is not posted yet, the task stands and the feeder goes on to the next task. The task leaves the plan when the field is sown, or when its window has closed with the field unsown (kept in `plan.last` with which). A field already broken starts at its hoeing: the same task, shorter.
- [x] `Sim.planView(st)` shows a plant task with `step` (the kind of the step open or next) and `stands`; `Sim.agenda(st)` lays it into `days` step by step and gives its `plan` entry the hours of all steps left, `ends` at the sowing's end, the sowing window as `deadline` and the chain's `verdict` (what `then` gives a single step today), `rough` where a step's job is not posted yet.
- [x] A plain task by key that is a step of a field project is unchanged (the page chooses which to add).
- [x] `tests/plan.js` and `tests/agenda.js` extended on a small constructed state: a plant task on a project's tile is carried from clearing to a sown field over the days with no further act of the player; one added on a broken field takes only the hoeing and sowing; the refusals; a window closed drops it; a save and a load go on the same. A run with no played pawn is unchanged (`node tools/same.js --base REF`, founders and solo).

## Notes
- 24 Nov 2026 (Kim, in session): "agreed (so a plant task that is shorter if the field is already broken)", to the question whether a planned field should carry itself through clearing, breaking, hoeing and sowing as one task. Kim's word for the capability; no question is open.
- Known from VB-1646: a task by key is matched by its job's key only, and a clearing posted again under the same key would be taken for the same task; a plant task is matched by its tile and has no such fault.
- Decisions (VB-1649): a plant task matches the household's jobs at its tile by kind (clear, break, plow, sow), never by key; it is
  checked each step by `plantGone` in planDay. Its `last` is {plant, kind, site, t, which}: besides `sown` and `closed`, `fallow` (the
  field the project made lies fallow this year: nothing to sow) and `gone` (no field nor project there now, e.g. the project dropped).
- A project's tile never drops for its window while the project is open (the deadline shown is this year's, else next year's, as
  `then`); once broken, the field drops `closed` if this year's window is past. A sown, fallow, overgrown or shut-window field is refused.
- The agenda lays a plant task by parts: a hoeing or sowing not posted waits for the window's opening; days' segments carry the step's
  kind and key (null while not posted); the entry adds `steps`, `rough`, `step`, `plant` and has `then: null`. Plain entries unchanged.
- `same.js --base 383e9c9 --scenario founders,solo`: same.
