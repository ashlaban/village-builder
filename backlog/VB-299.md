# VB-299: Goods for the fair: quanta, three classes and keeping goods
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, The fair: barter between villages (10 Nov); Goods, Goods of place and side crafts (10 Nov); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, sections 4.3 and 5.2
Goal: Kim, 10 Nov: goods fall into classes, and custom says which exchanges across them are proper; keeping goods are accepted beyond need. The quanta and the worth table are Agreed (10 Nov).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A registry entry per good the fair trades (grain, butter, salt meat, dried fish, tar, ale, cattle by kind, a sheep; no wool or cheese, which are not goods yet, and no labour days, which are promises and come with slice 5): its quantum, how it perishes, its class, and whether it is a keeping good. Declared before the registry seals; nothing of it in saved state.
- [x] `Culture.norm('worth', good)` with fixed rates, in rations as the one unit of account: grain, a sack of 10 rations, 10; salt meat, butter and dried fish, a lot of 10 rations, 15; a barrel of tar 8; a barrel of ale 14; a cow 40, an ox 50, a heifer or steer 20, a sheep 6.
- [x] `Culture.norm('proper', fromClass, toClass)`: within a class ordinary; food for an animal admired; an animal taken for food while the giver's household is below 60 days of need is a hard bargain, allowed and booked as one; land is in no deal.
- [x] A keeping good is worth its customary worth to a taker with no need of it, up to 30 rations' worth held at once by a household. Keeping goods: butter, salt meat, dried fish, tar, a sheep.
- [x] `Needs.value` for these goods (`design.md`, The fair, Need): food 1 a ration when stores reach the next harvest with the reserve, in a line to 3 at empty stores and 0.5 beyond two years' need, and 1.2 times for a food the pot lacks, up to a fifth of the need; an animal 1.5 times its worth with none of its kind, 1 otherwise, 0 without hay for the winter; tar its worth when a boat or the house is due; ale its worth with an occasion ahead, half otherwise. It allocates nothing in an inner loop.
- [x] `tools/rules.js` lists the new rules and their owners; a test of each class rule; behaviour unchanged until VB-302 uses them (golden unchanged).

Orchestrator's decisions for Kim to overturn:
- Tar's class: the second class, named `wares` (animals, cloth and labour; tar beside them as a made good that keeps). So `proper` treats
  tar given for food in dearth as a hard bargain, like an animal. Ale is in the food class but gives no rations (its need is its own term).
- `proper` is read from one side: the side giving `from` and taking `to`, with that side's days of food; VB-302 calls a deal by the worse of
  its two sides' words, and books a hard bargain as one (the word is here; the booking is VB-302's). "Days of need" counts grain and the larder against the household's daily need.
- "The reserve" in the food curve: the seed reserve plus the holder's buffer days (`HELP.bufferDays`, the allowance's reserve), so the
  reach is seed + (days to the next harvest + buffer) × daily need; in autumn the next harvest is a season off, as help's askDaysAt.
  The fall to 0.5 beyond two years' need is a step, not a second line.
- "A fifth of the need" for a food the pot lacks: a fifth of the need to the next harvest, and the source counts as lacking below it.
- An animal's kind: the same kind of cattle (a cow for a cow, an ox for an ox), any sheep for a sheep. "Hay for the winter": the
  household's hay against the herd's daily hay with the new head, times the stall days still to come, or a whole stall period outside
  it. Note for VB-302: in summer the hay of the coming winter is not yet made, so a fair held then values animals at 0 for households
  short of last winter's hay; if the gathering falls before haymaking, that rule needs the expected hay.
- A keeping good's value is the greater of its need and its worth for the quanta that fit under 30 rations' worth held (`kept`, which
  VB-302 must keep), not a mix per quantum.
- Dried fish keeps a year (YEAR_DAYS) until VB-300 sets it; grain's perishing is `ECON.storeLoss[0]` a year (more beyond a year, by sim/hunger).
- `Culture.norm` takes no `st` or place yet (one table everywhere); norms that vary by place and learn (slice 8) will add the context.
