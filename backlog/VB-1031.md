# VB-1031: A gathering is told as it begins: a notice, and a skip stops for a wedding or a funeral ale
Status: done (2026-11-18)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (14, 15); design.md, Where pawns meet
Goal: The player sees a gathering that concerns its pawn while it is on, not after everyone has gone home.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `Sim.gatherings(st, id, seen)` (`src/sim/notable.js`): the occasions that have begun, are not over and concern pawn `id`, each once; it reads `st.occasions` and writes nothing to `st` (`seen` is the caller's own list). An occasion concerns a pawn that is asked to it or has set out for it; one held for someone of its household or line; the meeting and the rite of its village.
- [x] The page tells each as it begins, in a notice whose click goes to the spot; the list of notices has a switch for them (`src/page/notices.js`).
- [x] The skip to the next event stops as a wedding or a funeral ale that concerns the played pawn begins, the camera on the spot (`skipStop`, the reason `occasion`); one that was on when the skip began does not stop it.
- [x] `tests/skip.js` (case 6) and `tests/browser-gathering.js`; the simulation unchanged (`node tools/same.js`).

- 18 Nov 2026 (Kim, the session on the life story): found while playing a life (founders, seed 2938): every picture of a rite, an ale evening, a wedding or a funeral showed an empty yard, because the record's line, which the skip and the notices follow, is written when the occasion ends. The gatherings themselves were on the map all along. Kim chose: stop when it begins, a mark on the map, and who is there (the last two are VB-1032).

## Implementation and validation (18 Nov 2026)
- Decision, measured: Kim's choice read "skip stops when an occasion that concerns the played pawn begins". Over the 24 years of Håkan Ivarsson (founders, small, seed 2938, headless, offers answered yes) that made 181 stops for occasions (81 ale evenings, 71 rites, 24 meetings, 5 funeral ales) beside the 45 there were (24 round eves, 13 family, 5 farm, 3 custom): a skip that stops every six weeks for a rite is no skip. So only a wedding and a funeral ale stop it (5 stops in that life); the rite, the meeting and an ale evening are told by the notice. A close call another could have made the other way; a switch a reason is the general answer if Kim wants them back.
- No new record events and no new state: the simulation's run is the same (`node tools/same.js --base 5ee0f75`: same); `skipFrom`'s cursor, which is the page's, gains `occ`.
- Cost: one judgement an occasion a caller (the kin test only then), a scan of `st.occasions` (the few called and not over) a step of a skip and a frame of the page.
