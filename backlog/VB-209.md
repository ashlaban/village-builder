# VB-209: What a pawn may do here: `Opportunities.forPawn` and the actor view
Status: ready

Size: M

Phase: implement
Design ref: The player and what they see (What it may do here: Proposed, VB-079); What a pawn knows (Decided: omniscient, read through an actor view); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, sections 4.5 (Opportunities) and 4.7, principles 4, 15 and 16; `docs/review-life.md`, section 3.
Goal: The player chooses its pawn's commands from what the pawn may do here, for whom and on what basis; slice 3's planner will ask the same question for every holder. Build the read-only question for one pawn, on demand, and the actor view it reads through.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A new module `sim/opportunities` (`@sys` line; listed in `src/manifest.json` after the modules it reads; under 400 lines) with `actorView(st, id)`, the seam that filters nothing for now (it returns what the pawn may read, today the whole state), and `forPawn(st, view, id)`, read-only, writing nothing to `st` and drawing no random numbers. Exposed as `Sim.opportunities(st, id)`.
- [ ] `forPawn` lists, each with `{verb, target, args, holder, basis, fit, why}` (`why` null when the command would be accepted now):
  - the work of its household and its village, from each kind's `available` (VB-208), matched to the posted job, with the planner's fit and reasons (`explainIdle`, `src/sim/jobs.js:152`), best fit first; basis `member`, `servant` or `villager`;
  - go home, idle, and walk to a tile, offered only on tiles the walking rule opens (`walkable`, `src/sim/routes.js`; VB-168's note);
  - a cause: each custom's options other than its village's practice, and dropping its cause if it has one (VB-211 issues them);
  - the moving round's acts, marked "at the moving round": moving out (`wantsOut`, `src/sim/heirs.js:371`), claiming each empty farm of its village it may claim (`src/sim/farmclaims.js`), taking service (`src/sim/service.js`), each with the rule that bars it now, if one does;
  - nothing for marriage (Decided, 8 Nov: the round matches the player's pawn until courting, VB-036).
- [ ] A test (`tests/opportunities.js`, in `npm test`): on a tiny map over a game day, every open job a pawn would bid for in `planRound` is listed as work with `why` null, and every work item listed with `why` null is a job it would bid for (the list and the planner agree); `forPawn` leaves `st` unchanged (hash before and after) and draws nothing.
- [ ] Cost: under 1 ms a call at small-y40 and medium-y5, reported with `medium-y60` (stress), for a holder and for a landless adult; no loop over all pawns, households or the record (the village's farms and households only).
- [ ] Golden file and counters unchanged; `npm run verify` passes; `node tests/dependencies.js` passes (the module reads only earlier ones).
