# VB-209: What a pawn may do here: `Opportunities.forPawn` and the actor view
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player and what they see (What it may do here: Proposed, VB-079); What a pawn knows (Decided: omniscient, read through an actor view); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, sections 4.5 (Opportunities) and 4.7, principles 4, 15 and 16; `docs/review-life.md`, section 3.
Goal: The player chooses its pawn's commands from what the pawn may do here, for whom and on what basis; slice 3's planner will ask the same question for every holder. Build the read-only question for one pawn, on demand, and the actor view it reads through.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A new module `sim/opportunities` (`@sys` line; listed in `src/manifest.json` after the modules it reads; under 400 lines) with `actorView(st, id)`, the seam that filters nothing for now (it returns what the pawn may read, today the whole state), and `forPawn(st, view, id)`, read-only, writing nothing to `st` and drawing no random numbers. Exposed as `Sim.opportunities(st, id)`.
- [x] `forPawn` lists, each with `{verb, target, args, holder, basis, fit, why}` (`why` null when the command would be accepted now):
  - the work of its household and its village, from each kind's `available` (VB-208), matched to the posted job, with the planner's fit and reasons (`explainIdle`, `src/sim/jobs.js:152`), best fit first; basis `member`, `servant` or `villager`;
  - go home, idle, and walk to a tile, offered only on tiles the walking rule opens (`walkable`, `src/sim/routes.js`; VB-168's note);
  - a cause: each custom's options other than its village's practice, and dropping its cause if it has one (VB-211 issues them);
  - the moving round's acts, marked "at the moving round": moving out (`wantsOut`, `src/sim/heirs.js:371`), claiming each empty farm of its village it may claim (`src/sim/farmclaims.js`), taking service (`src/sim/service.js`), each with the rule that bars it now, if one does;
  - nothing for marriage (Decided, 8 Nov: the round matches the player's pawn until courting, VB-036).
- [x] A test (`tests/opportunities.js`, in `npm test`): on a tiny map over a game day, every open job a pawn would bid for in `planRound` is listed as work with `why` null, and every work item listed with `why` null is a job it would bid for (the list and the planner agree); `forPawn` leaves `st` unchanged (hash before and after) and draws nothing.
- [x] Cost: under 1 ms a call at small-y40 and medium-y5, reported with `medium-y60` (stress), for a holder and for a landless adult; no loop over all pawns, households or the record (the village's farms and households only).
- [x] Golden file and counters unchanged; `npm run verify` passes; `node tests/dependencies.js` passes (the module reads only earlier ones).
Notes:

- 10 Nov 2026, implementation. Impact: a new module, `sim/opportunities` (124 lines, after `sim/service` in the manifest), with `actorView(st, id)` and `forPawn(st, view, id, near)`, on `Sim` as `opportunities(st, id, near)`. No state. It reads households, farms, villages, jobs, the culture's practice and `st.demand`; it writes nothing. New coupling: it calls the kinds' `available` (`sim/kinds`), the planner's fit (`sim/jobs`), the verbs' checks (`sim/commands`) and the round's rules (`sim/heirs`, `sim/farmclaims`, `sim/service`), all earlier modules.
- Four rules learnt to say why not, so the list reuses them and copies none; each keeps its old boolean beside it, and the golden states are unchanged: `staysWhy` beside `wantsOut` (`sim/heirs`), `barred` beside `free` (`sim/farmclaims`), `unfit` and `handsLeft`, which `takeServants` now uses (`sim/service`), and `fit`, the row `explainIdle` built (`sim/jobs`).
- Choices made where the criteria were silent:
  - An item has two fields more than the seven named: `kind` (a job's kind, to tell the work in words) and `when` (`now`, or `round` for the moving round's acts).
  - Work is asked from each kind's `available` for the pawn's farm and village, as `refreshJobs` asks it, then the posted jobs of the two are added, since a plot's steps and a claimed farm's restoration are asked island-wide. Work that is available but not posted (outside working hours) is listed with the reason "not given out yet".
  - The job a pawn holds is listed as "doing it now".
  - A walk is offered only for the tile asked about (`near`, as in target-v2's signature): there is no list of every open tile.
  - Dropping a cause is listed as the verb `dropCause`, which does not exist yet (VB-211 issues causes). The player's queue refuses it until then.
  - Moving out, claiming and service are wishes for the round: `leave` carries no party or site, which the round reckons.
  - Service is offered with holders of the pawn's own village who live alone; the round also looks at villages within reach.
- One approximation, for cost: whether the pawn is first heir to a farm. A pawn the round last counted as wanting out (`st.demand`) is not asked again; any other is checked against the first heirs of its own village's farms only, and only once the cheaper bars pass. An heir to a farm in another village, or one who became an heir since the last round, is found at the round, which decides in any case.
- The reused rules `guardian` and `claimOf` scan the live households and the farmsteads for one id each (a few microseconds), as they do for the round; the criterion's "no loop over all households" holds for everything written here.
- Cost, a call at 10:00, the best of three, over every such adult of the save (the bench has no `medium-y60` since 8 November, so `medium-ref` stands in):
