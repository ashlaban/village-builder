Kim, 30 Oct 2026: work 6:00–18:00; dark about 6 hours at the equinoxes (22:00–4:00), 4 at midsummer, 8 at midwinter; pawns sleep in the dark; twilight of 2 hours (accepted when merging main, `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`).
Goal: Work runs from 7:00 to 17:00 (`WORK` in `src/sim/rules.js`) and the page dims at fixed hours (`evening` in `src/body.html`). The design now has a longer, fixed working day because nights are short, and a dark part of the day that changes a little with the season.
