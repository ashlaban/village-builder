# VB-105: Daylight by season, with a fixed short night
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: work
Phase: implement
Design ref: Time and calendar (Daily rhythm: work from 7:00 to 17:00). Kim, 30 Oct 2026 (jobs review): daylight changes with the season; the part of the day that is night stays a fixed, compressed block of game hours.
Goal: Work runs from 7:00 to 17:00 every day of the year (`WORK` in `src/sim/rules.js`), and the page dims at fixed hours (`evening` in `src/body.html`). At about 59° north the day lasts some 18 hours at midsummer and 6 at midwinter; the long summer days carried haymaking and harvest, the short winter days left little outdoor work.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Night is fixed: 21:00 to 05:00 every day; pawns are home and indoors, as now after 17:00.
- [ ] Day length by day of the year: L = 12.25 + 6.25 × cos(2π (doy − 15) / 48) hours, longest on summer day 3 and shortest on winter day 3, and at most the 16 hours outside the night. Sunrise is 13:00 − L/2 and sunset 13:00 + L/2: about 10:00–16:00 at midwinter, 05:00–21:00 at midsummer, 08:00–18:00 at the start of spring.
- [ ] `daylight(c)` in `src/sim/rules.js` returns `[sunrise, sunset]` for a calendar date and replaces `WORK` everywhere it is read (`sim/clock`, `sim/jobs`, `sim/agents`): work starts at sunrise, jobs are posted from an hour before, and everyone goes home at sunset. `Sim` exports `daylight` in place of `WORK`.
- [ ] Cooking is posted from 14:00 as today (VB-022), or from sunset − 3 hours when that is earlier.
- [ ] The page dims and lights the windows from the sunset hour and brightens at sunrise, reading `Sim.daylight`, in place of the fixed 16:00 and 8:00.
- [ ] Populous medium at seed 7 and founders at seeds 7 and 42, 3 years: work hours per adult per season, `--report idle` (VB-101), fields sown, crops lost to frost and hungry household-days, before and after (reported, not asserted).
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
