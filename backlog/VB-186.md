- 15 Nov 2026, the two tasks waiting for an ID (arch `talk`, implement, priority 2):

  **A. Lies and counter-stories, by pawns and by the player; and what the player hears** (size M; waits for VB-523 and VB-520). Ref: `design.md`, People (Traits, talk and stories: Stories, the rows Lies, Exposure and What the player hears); target-v2, sections 4.4 (Stories) and 7.3; Kim's decision 23. Goal: pawns and the player alike may lie, and a discovered lie is the source of a counter-story about the liar, of higher valence, by how the culture judges lies; the player reads what its pawn has heard as that pawn's belief. Criteria:
  - [ ] `Stories.invent(st, teller, about, kind, why)` makes a story with `lie: true` that the teller holds first-hand and tells as any other; one lie about one pawn at a time. The flag is read only by `expose` and by reports: no pawn's choice and no page text reads it (a test greps the `Sim` API's story view for it).
  - [ ] Who lies: at its talk turn a pawn that holds another as an enemy (`pawn.bands`, -1) invents a story about it at chance `STORY.lie` 0.005 × (1 − its honour) × (0.5 + its ambition), of a negative kind of `STORY.kinds` drawn by weight; a verb of `sim/commands` (`slander`, with whom and the kind), which refuses a lie about oneself, a second lie about the same pawn and a pawn under 16, and returns why.
  - [ ] The player: the same verb from the action bar's Myself group ("Speak ill of", a party and a kind in one form), with one line saying what it risks; the played pawn is exposed as any liar is.
  - [ ] Exposure: a lie told to a hearer who holds a first-hand memory of the subject (not hearsay), of a kind in the story's domain and of the other sign, is not believed. The hearer then holds first-hand a counter-story of kind `lied` about the story's `by`, of valence −min(1, `counter` 1.5 × |the lie's| × (0.5 + the hearer's honour) × (0.5 + its culture's mean honour)); the teller is told it at once and its zeal for the lie goes to 0. `lied` is a story kind as any other, in every domain. An event of the record (`lieExposed`, with its kind, the liar, the hearer and the place).
  - [ ] Honour's second rule and ambition's are thereby built (the table of VB-520 is completed in its file or here).
  - [ ] What the player hears: `Sim.heard(pawnId)` (the actor view) gives, for each story the pawn holds: whom it is about, the kind in words, from whom it last heard it, how many mouths, and how well it is remembered (a word); never the flag. The pawn panel's Heard list shows them under a line that says whose belief it is. A story about the played pawn's household, heard by the played pawn, is a notable event (`sim/notable`; a toast; it stops Skip to the next event). `node tests/smoke.js`, the `tests/browser*.js` touched; a screenshot under `docs/img/`.
  - [ ] The reduced scenario of VB-523 gains the lie bands as an `assert` (`tests/lib/bands.js`): with three living witnesses a slander reaches fewer of the two villages than a true story of its kind in at least 7 of 10 seeds, and its counter-story more than the lie in at least 7 of 10 (the experiment: 10 of 10 each).
  - [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7, 42: lies invented and exposed a 1,000 pawn-years, the reach of lies, true stories and counter-stories, liars' close friends and enemies before and after exposure, the shares of opinions at ±0.8 or beyond; growth, marriages, hungry household-days; whether the households of those lied about, not only averages, are worse off (help received).
  - [ ] Parameterisation (at most five short runs): `lie` and `counter`; the values tried and what each gave. Tests on a constructed state with `fix`: only an enemy is lied about; an honourable pawn never lies; a witness exposes and a stranger does not; the counter-story's valence by the hearer's honour; the player's verb and its refusals. Cost: one chance a turn for pawns with an enemy (0.2% of relations); a lookup of first-hand memories by subject on hearing a lie.

  **B. A child on the way: births while courting, the offer out of the round, and the custom Children born outside marriage** (size M; waits for VB-036). Ref: `design.md`, People (Births; Courting; Traits, talk and stories: Courting); Customs and culture (Custom dimensions: Children born outside marriage); Kim's answers of 30 October (`QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, VB-036). Goal: some children are born outside marriage, from courting, with the man she was with as father; the couple often marries quickly, and such a child inherits by custom. Criteria:
  - [ ] Conception while courting, in `sim/births`' step for the women it already walks (a courting woman is reached from `pawn.courting`; no new loop): `COURT.conceive` 0.08 a woman-year, by the age curve of married women and times (1 − hunger), not while pregnant or nursing; `pregnantBy` is the man she courts. The true father is recorded and known to all (Kim, 30 Oct).
  - [ ] The offer out of the round: `COURT.known` 12 days after conception a due entry (`st`, plain data) makes the couple's offer by the round's acts and under its rules (ages, a place to live, the kin limit, the means; hunger of 0.5 or more postpones it, a day at a time for at most a season); refused or not allowed, the couple is offered again at the round as VB-036 has it.
  - [ ] The child: `pawn.outside` is true for a child born to an unmarried mother; it lives in its mother's household; an event of the record (`bornOutside`, with its kind, the child, both parents and the place), which is VB-523's story kind "a child outside marriage" (kin, -0.3, about the father and the mother) if stories have landed. When its parents marry each other `outside` becomes false, with an event (legitimation by the later marriage, as medieval law had it).
  - [ ] The custom, the sixteenth (`src/culture.js`, `outside`): inherit like other children; after children born in marriage (default); do not inherit; malleability 0.5; self-interest pulls a pawn born outside marriage, and the parent of one, toward the first (Kim, 30 Oct). `sim/heirs` reads it through the customs in force where it ranks children. An old save gains the block from its culture's default (`sim/saves.js`; `tests/saves.js`); `tests/browser-mobile.js` and whatever counts the customs follow.
  - [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7, 42: births while courting as a share of births (about 5%; in 3 to 8%); the share of such couples married within a year (reported against Kim's "about 3 in 4, if they can": 0.6 to 0.9; never set); children outside marriage alive at 15 and how they inherited; mothers' households' hungry days against others' (whether households are worse off); growth and marriages.
  - [ ] Parameterisation (at most five short runs): `conceive`, toward the 5%; the values tried and what each gave. Tests on a constructed state with `fix`: a courting woman conceives and a non-courting unmarried one never does; the offer is made 12 days on and the child is then born in marriage; a couple that cannot marry has a child outside marriage; each option of the custom in `sim/heirs`; legitimation. Invariants for `pawn.outside` and the due entry. Cost: nothing new a step; a due entry a pregnancy.
