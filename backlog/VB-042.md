# VB-042: Tile claims, realised and unrealised
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: claims
Phase: implement
Design ref: Claims, ownership and territory (Claims: Decided; Compact tile data: Proposed).
Goal: Owners exist once VB-003 is done; claims do not. New fields (VB-010), cleared plots (VB-031) and empty farms (VB-033) all start with a claim, so give claims one representation. Depends on VB-003 and VB-041; if either is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria (Kim answered VB-041 on 2026-10-30):
- [ ] `st.claims`: a short list per tile, held sparsely (a `Map` from tile to an array), since most tiles have none. A claim is `{ by: {type, id}, state: 'realised' | 'unrealised', since, strength, why }`; `type` is pawn, household or village (all three may claim).
- [ ] Expiry: an unrealised claim's `strength` starts at 1 and falls linearly to 0 over 3 game years without work on the tile; work on the tile, or a renewal by the claimant (`renewClaim`, also offered at the moving round to claimants who still want the tile), sets it back to 1. At 0 the claim lapses and is dropped.
- [ ] Disputed claims: when two unrealised claims meet on one tile, the village's practice of a new custom, "Disputed claims" (`dispute`, a `DIMS` entry with a value in each `ARCHETYPES` entry), decides which stands. Options: `first` the first claim stands; `strongest` the claim with the most strength (most recently worked or renewed); `kin` kin of the tile's last owner first; `meeting` the meeting decides by voice-weighted support. Old Nordic default `first`, malleability 0.7. It appears in the customs panel and changes at meetings like the others.
- [ ] Helpers `claimsOn(st, tile)`, `addClaim(st, tile, claim)`, `renewClaim(st, tile, by)`, `dropClaim(st, tile, by)`, `realise(st, tile, by)` (a realised claim makes the claimant the owner via `setOwner`). Exported and documented with `@state`.
- [ ] VB-010, VB-031 and VB-033 use these helpers when they are implemented, or are changed to use them if already done.
- [ ] The tile info panel lists the claims on a tile.
- [ ] A test of the helpers and of realising a claim.
