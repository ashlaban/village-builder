# VB-245: Idle pawns wait 2 to 4 hours before strolling again
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: none (a tunable). Kim, 10 Nov 2026 (code review).
Goal: `idleAbout` (`src/sim/jobs.js`) sends an idle pawn to someone it likes, the green or its yard, and has it look again after 0.75 to 2 hours. At `medium-ref` that is about 5,460 strolls a game day and 40 ms of the day's 204, a fifth of the budget, with about 650 of 1,031 living idle outdoors at each hour's count. Kim decided on 10 November that an idle pawn waits 2 to 4 hours.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `idleAbout` has the pawn look again after `IDLE.wait[0] + rnd * (IDLE.wait[1] - IDLE.wait[0])` hours, with `IDLE.wait = [2, 4]` in `sim/rules.js` (0.75 to 2 today, written in `jobs.js:195`). A pawn still takes work the moment the hourly refresh offers it one it bids for: say in the PR how an idle pawn hears of new work during its wait (`awake` in `sim/agents` wakes those whose pause ends by the hour's end), and that no job waits longer for hands than before (hours from a job's posting to its first hand, median and 90th centile, before and after).
- [ ] Numbers before and after, in the PR and here: `node tools/bench.js` at `medium-y5` and `medium-ref` (ms a day, "agents: idle strolls", "talk and contacts", the longest step); strolls and companies weighed a day (`idleStroll`, `companyWeighed`); talks a day, since those out strolling meet; populous and founders, small map, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years: living, households, hunger deaths, grain per household at the lowest decile; `node tools/bands.js` every band as before.
- [x] Two screenshots of a village at midday in summer and in winter, before and after (`tools/shot.js`), with a sentence on whether it still looks lived in.
- [ ] `tests/golden.json` and `tests/counters.json` re-recorded, the changed entries listed. `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals.

Implementation notes (night run):
- `IDLE = { wait: [2, 4] }` in `sim/rules.js` (exported); `idleAbout` reads it. How an idle pawn hears of work: `awake` lists a pawn in working hours if its pause ends by the hour's end, so
  a new job is offered to it when its wait ends; the hourly refresh and `lookUp` still reach those walking or working. A pawn asked to an occasion is not affected: `visit` (sim/occasions) issues
  `attend` to any pawn with `canGo` (no job, phase null or home), which does not read `idleUntil`, and `attend` wakes it; so a waiting pawn is still asked.
