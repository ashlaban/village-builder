Goal: A pawn holds title to goods in a store of its own that lies where it sleeps, moves only with it, and passes to someone when it dies
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `pawn.stake` becomes `pawn.goods` (`@state`, owner sim/stores): the fields a household's store uses for the goods a pawn may hold (grain; pots by grade once VB-366 lands; clay, wood, cloth, butter and the foods the fair deals), read through the stores' reads as any holder's; every reader of the stake reads it (the wage, the means to marry, a party's provisions, a founding, the settlers' walk); `KEEPING.stake` goes, and "stake" no longer names a pawn's grain (the word is left to land, sim/stakes). `FORMAT` raised (sim/saves): a save of an older build is refused, as `docs/architecture/modules.md` (Saves) says; no migration is written.
- [ ] A pawn holds no animal and no good that does not wear (silver, gold, amber, a bronze pot): a put of one into a pawn's store is refused with its reason (aims.md 5.6).
- [ ] Custody: the store lies at its keeper's house. A move (the verb `move`, every reason) carries it as the pawn's load on the move's journey, up to `TRAVEL.load`; the rest is left at the old house as a pile with the pawn's title (sim/lying), fetched by the stray-pile method (VB-1749). A check on a constructed state: a servant holding 30 rations moves to a master two hours away, carries its load, and the rest is fetched; no ration made or lost.
- [ ] The head pair holds none: a pawn that becomes its household's holder or the holder's spouse (a founding, a handover, a marriage onto the holding) puts its store into the household's, title at once where it lies, as a founding does with stakes today; a check of each of the three.
- [ ] At death (Nothing is left to no one): title passes at once by the custom `personalEstate` (sim/customs, one value `kin`: the spouse; else the children, in equal shares into their stores, or their households' for a holder or a holder's spouse; else the household the pawn lived in; else its nearest kin's household; else its village's store); custody unchanged; a check for each step of the order, and an invariant that no store of a dead pawn holds anything.
- [ ] `tools/stocks.js` counts the pawns' stores in the accounts (in, out, where held), and the stocks' `unexplained` does not grow on founders small seed 7, 10 years.
- [ ] Cost: about 700 stores of a few numbers at the late reference, read where a move, a wage, a marriage or a death already runs; nothing in a step; the save's growth reported.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
