Goal: VB-277 ranks heirs afresh after each marriage or handover inside a village's round: rankings a round went 478 to 2,016 (medium-ref) and 468 to 3,586 (small-y40), up to 430 in one step, because under land residence every woman's place is ranked again. Clear only the memos of the households a marriage or handover touches, behaviour unchanged.
Acceptance criteria:
- [~] After a marriage only the touched households' rankings are cleared (handover still clears all: handOn moves children, claims and holders, too wide to bound); `node tools/same.js` prints `same` (founders, heirs, populous; seeds 7, 42; small; 5 years).
- [ ] (not measured: no fixtures in the worktree, they are a night job; counters only) Rankings a round and the longest marriage step at `medium-ref` and `small-y40`, before and after, on a quiet machine; the 60 ms step target reported.

## Notes (VB-432)
Implementation: `heirRanking` and `ownFirstOf` take a `deps` Set and fill it with the pawns the ranking read (line, holder, every candidate before the filter, their spouses, the ancestors that pick the nested farms). `matchRules` keeps `readers` (pawn -> households whose cached ranking read it); `touched(pawns, homes)` drops the rankings of the homes and of the readers of the pawns, and the places of those pawns and of the members of cleared households; handed and means are cleared whole (cheap, they read cached rankings). `villageRound` uses it after a marriage where neither holds a farm nor is held in trust (so wed's handOn and goBack do not run); any other marriage, and every handover, still `clear()`s all.
Checked: a temporary comparison of every cached ranking against a fresh one, populous seed 7 / founders seed 3 / heirs seed 5, small, 5-8 years: 0 stale. `same.js`: same.
Numbers (populous seed 7 medium 10 years, `--report counts`): heirRank 14,522 -> 13,931 (-4%); populous small seed 7 6 years 2,029 -> 2,014. Rankings a round and longest marriage step at medium-ref and small-y40 not measured (fixtures are a night job). The saving is small at year 10; the rest is handovers' full clear and rank(h) of first-time households. A follow-up (handover partial clear) is VB-539.
