Goal: A gift of the player's, like every gift, reaches the other farm only by someone's journey.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The gift form (`src/page/gift.js`) names the carrier: an adult of the giving household who may carry (the check of `give`'s `carrier`, VB-291), the player's own pawn first, or another chosen in the form; or, where none may, the gift changes owner where it lies and waits for the receiver to fetch it. - [ ] Numbers before and after, as the hard rule asks (`AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim): days from a gift to its arrival, and the receiving farm's food on the day of the gift and on arrival, at founders small seed 7.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state: a gift with a carrier arrives when the carrier does; the giver's store falls when the load is taken up, the receiver's when it is set down.
