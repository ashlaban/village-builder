mends half), in the house, in winter or when below a quarter; a pawn takes them by the verb `work`, as every job (decision: the criterion's
"verb" is met by the job kind, not a new verb). A slaughtered beast of the cattle now gives the hide its kind declared (reason `slaughter`).
The hide's need term moved from `sim/deer` (DEER.leather, a head) to `sim/leather` (a year of the wear). Numbers are named estimates
(docs/tuning.md row). Test: tests/leather.js. Before/after, founders small 10 years, seeds 7 / 11: hungry days and deaths unchanged; mean
grain at year 10 470 -> 362 / 417 -> 426 (runs diverge); ms/day 7.3 -> 7.0 (seed 7). Left: hides pile up (76 / 58 held at year 10: the hunt
brings far more than leather uses); going barefoot or a worn harness costs nothing yet; both noted for the tuning run.
