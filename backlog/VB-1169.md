# VB-1169: Cost: the asking walks the whole queue for each match asked: an index of the unmarried by village
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: AGENTS.md, Rules for cost (1, 2); backlog/VB-693.md
Goal: A match asked reads the unmarried of the villages within reach, not the island's queue.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The unmarried of an age to be asked are kept by village, as a cache with one writer (the module that writes `st.asking`: sim/wishes), written where the queue is written (a birth's coming of age, a death, a marriage, a move); `askMatch` and `sim/visits`' `farSingles` read only the villages within a day's walk, or visited, in place of the whole queue.
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base REF` on founders and populous small; the candidates weighed in the same order (nearest villages first, then as the queue held them).
- [ ] `askWalk` and `visitWalk` before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 7 and 42 (before, VB-693: 364,000 and 984,000 `askWalk` in 30 years), and at `medium-ref`; the invariant of the new cache in `tests/lib/invariants.js`.
- 18 Nov 2026 (VB-693): opened by it. A match asked walks the island's queue of seekers once (`askWalk`), so the work is seekers times the queue; VB-693 let a woman's side ask as well, which doubled it (populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42: 1.22 million reads before, 2.44 million after). Small beside a day's work today, but it grows with the square of the unmarried.
