# VB-716: Grazing on the commons: the meeting allots, the alderman carries it out
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-710, VB-313, VB-314
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Goods, production and animals: Animals, Customs of the animals; docs/plans/VB-141.md (section 2.1); design.md, Custom dimensions
Goal: Under the meeting's allotment a village sets its stints each year (Kim, 16 Nov), which is its remedy for the landless under by land share.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `meeting` ("The meeting allots") is the third option of the custom `grazing` (VB-710), with its `SAID` phrase and its interests in `sim/leanings` (a landless household with animals, and one whose herd is larger than its land share allows, lean to it).
- [ ] Under `meeting`, at the village's autumn meeting, a decision through `sim/decisions` (VB-313) allots the year's stints: in proportion to the cow-equivalents each household winters (what its autumn count kept), cut pro rata so that they sum to at most the commons' capacity; the alderman (VB-314) carries it out, granting each `graze` right through the verb of VB-710. A village with no alderman allots nothing and holds to `byLand` for the year, said in its meeting's log.
- [ ] Another village's herds are closed out, as under `byLand`.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state with its line in `tests/suites.js`: households wintering 6, 3 and 1 cow-equivalents (the last landless) on a commons of capacity 5 get 3, 1.5 and 0.5; on one of capacity 20 each gets its head.
- [ ] Numbers, `meeting` against `byLand` (forced), at populous small seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 (15 years): stints and head kept by land held, the herds of the landless, commons pressure, milk a cow, hungry days a household (the distribution); who gains, by class.
- [ ] Cost (Rules for cost 9): VB-710's yearly pass (the counter `stintHouseholds`) plus one decision a village a year; a third option adds about 0.1 ms a game day to the custom's attitudes at `medium-ref` (an estimate, measured by VB-715).
