Goal: Passage as Decided on 3 November (fields and yards are never crossed; a practice for meadow and pasture), read where a tile lies, so that one ground serves every walker.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A field from its sowing to its reaping, and a farm's yard, is closed to a route that goes through it, for every pawn alike, its holder too (a route may start or end there, as at a house); a field tile with a path or a lane on it stays open along it (the way between the strips, as the villages' fenced lanes were). The closing and the opening are tile cost changes on the days of sowing and reaping (`costChanged`). Bringing the code into line with a Decided point (principle 11): the cost is reported, not asked about.
- [ ] No tile is cut off: where closing a field would leave a house, a field or the green with no way to the others, that field stays open (the test VB-402 uses for a house that would close a way), and the count of such fields is reported.
- [ ] The practice `passage` (a custom, with the customs' drift and the meeting's vote): `free` (anyone may cross another's meadow and pasture: the start of every culture, and what the code does today) and `ways` (a meadow from spring day 1 to its mowing is crossed only along a path or a lane). It is read where the tile lies, by the village whose land it is, so the compiled ground stays one for all walkers. What moves a village to `ways`: say in the task file what the households that lose hay to trampling lean on, by the leanings that exist; a named tunable for hay lost a crossing, 0 until measured.
- [ ] A constructed check: a route from house to green goes round a sown field and through it after reaping; along a lane it crosses it all year; a field that would cut a house off stays open.
- [ ] Numbers before and after on populous medium at year 0, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 (`node docs/plans/VB-190-water.js ROOT medium 3,7,11,42`, the plan's measures), and years on populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years, and founders small, seed 7, 50 years: hours of walking a farm, route lengths (median, 90th percentile), fields left open by the cut-off test, tiles worn to a path at year 10, `routeSearch` and `routeExpand`, the days a year the route cache is emptied, hungry days by household, growth (principle 2). If growth at the median seed falls below the target, the figures and the cause go in the task file and the PR's Targets section; nothing is tuned to hide it.
- Cost (rule 9): fields (about 600 at `medium-ref`) x 2 tile changes a year, each one array write and the day's cache emptying that already happens.
