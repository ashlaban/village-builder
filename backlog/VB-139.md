Goal: One wild grazer, roe deer (Kim, 30 Oct): meat and hides when hunted; forest edges and meadows; can damage crops.
Acceptance criteria:

- [x] A field next to forest whose fence stretch is broken loses yield to deer with a small daily chance in summer (logged).
- [x] Hunting: an autumn and winter job by urgency of food, as fishing (VB-117), for men; a kill gives meat and lowers the stock.
- [x] Page: a few deer at forest edges near the camera at dawn and dusk.
- [x] A test; re-record the golden file (retired by VB-408: `docs/testing.md` stands; `tests/deer.js` in the integration tier).

Implementation (13 Nov 2026):
- `src/sim/deer.js` (new, after sim/byre): `st.deer` = {head[], since[], cap[], edges[][]} per region of `w.regions`, owned by sim/deer, made by
  `initDeer` in `makeState` (and for older saves in sim/saves). A forest-edge tile is a wood tile (forest or pine) with open land beside it;
  the capacity is `DEER.perEdge` (0.3) an edge tile, recounted at the year's first step (`recount`, keeping the deer). The stock grows
  logistically at `DEER.growth` (0.35 a year), read in closed form (Rules for cost, 3). A new run starts every region at its capacity.
- Grazing: each day a region's deer add `DEER.graze` (1.5 cow-hours) a deer over `DEER.spots` (3) of its edge tiles in turn, through
  sim/grazing's `graze` (the owner of `w.graze`). Work: regions × 3 a day.
- Crops: in summer (calendar guard), a standing crop beside a wood and beside a broken stretch of its village fence (below
  `DEER.crop.below`, 0.45, the cattle's) is browsed with `DEER.crop.chance` (0.04) a day times its region's deer against the capacity;
  `fd.browsed` counts it (sim/fields' `trample(fd, true)`), and the reaping takes `DEER.crop.loss` (5%) a time. Logged as `deerInCrop`
  (kept a generation, as `cattleInCrop`). Nobody is blamed: the deer are nobody's.
- Hunting: kind `hunt`, its record in sim/deer, registered in sim/kinds with fishing's urgency by spare food. Foraging work for the men
  alone (`hunt: 0` for the wife, maid, daughter, retired and child; farmer 3, farmhand 6, son 7 from foraging), from 14; autumn and
  winter; a 4-hour trip to the nearest forest edge within 6 steps whose region holds a deer or more. On coming home: a kill with 0.3 times
  the deer against the capacity, one deer fewer and 3 rations of fresh meat (a sheep's) into the larder, salted as the slaughter's
  (ledger reason `hunt`, routine, folded yearly). The `deer` random stream draws the kills and the crops.
- Page: `src/page/deer.js` (new): roe deer art (summer red-brown, winter grey-brown; grazing and looking up), drawn within 2.5 hours of
  the dark's start or end, near the camera (zoom over 0.3), on the open ground beside an edge tile in view, a group of one to three on a
  tile with chance 0.12 times the region's density, by a hash of the tile and the day; at most 12. Reads `st` only.
- Tunables `DEER` in sim/rules beside FISH and HERD: that took sim/rules over its baseline, so VB-419 (Refactor: split sim/rules by concern)
  was opened, as the lint asks. sim/kinds grew by three lines: VB-418 (each domain declares its own job kinds) was retitled to name
  sim/kinds, which it splits, so the lint finds it. sim/records' one line is covered by VB-372.
- Counters `deerGround` (tiles searched for a hunting ground) and `deerCrop` (fields tested for deer); `tests/counters.json` re-recorded.

Decisions (Deciding without Kim):
- Hides are left out: a hide no system reads would be a stock read by nothing; VB-420 adds them with a use. (Extending a system, 4.)
- No deer move between regions (no teleportation: none appears where it did not live); a region that gains its first forest edge gains
  no deer until deer can move, and one that loses all its edges loses its deer at the yearly count. A region hunted below one deer keeps
  its remainder and grows back from it.
- "For men" is by the roles' exceptions (no rule of sex exists), so the retired, of either sex, do not hunt; sons from 14 (youths hunted).
- The crop damage needs a wood beside the field and a broken village fence stretch beside it, as the cattle's straying does; its chance
  scales with the region's deer, so hunting protects the crops.
- All numbers are starting values with no source but a roe deer's size and the land reading of a tile (about 2 ha); left for the tuning run.
