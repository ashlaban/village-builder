## Implementation notes
- `SHARES.worth` (cow 40, ox 50, heifer 20, steer 20, ewe 6) and `SHARES.start.ewes` (2) in `src/sim/rules.js`. `src/sim/herds.js`: `giveAnimal`
  (one animal, only when the giver keeps a cow and its winter hay; the hay above the giver's winter need goes with it, up to the animal's own
  need), `startHerd` (a cow and two ewes), `payInAnimals` (the cheapest first, never past what is due).
- `sim/cottage.finishCottage`: after the household is founded, `startHerd` from the holder's old household. `sim/heirs.transfer` and
  `sim/cottage.payOwed`: what grain cannot cover of a share is paid in animals to a sibling who has another household.
- No new state, no new loops over growing collections (per-event, a few herd lookups).

## Orchestrator's decisions for Kim to overturn
- A sibling with no household of their own cannot hold animals (pawns hold grain only), so for them a share short of grain stays a debt in grain;
  animals pay only to a sibling in another household. Giving pawns a stake in animals would be new state.
- The old household's "winter hay" is `hh.hay` against its remaining herd's need over the stall period (`HERD.stallDays`); an old household
  lacking it gives no animals.
- Only cows, oxen, heifers, steers and ewes are valued (the worth table); calves, bulls, rams and lambs are not given in shares.
