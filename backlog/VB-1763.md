Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Verbs, each checked by position: `grant` (a head on a member: a standing grant by season, or a dated one, leave); `ask` and `answer` with the bases `leave` and `lighter` (a member to its head; answered once by the head's need and its opinion; a no is a memory, as today); `breach` (a member takes hours within its levy). `assign` and `call` (today `dayGive` and `dayCall`) beyond the member's levy are a demand, named as such by the check before it is issued; the member complies or refuses by its own judgement.
- [ ] sim/voice's leave by rule (`st.leaves`) becomes its default author's `ask` and the head's dated grant on the household's plan; `st.leaves` goes, with a save migration.
- [ ] Goodwill: hours demanded are booked as goodwill the head owes the member, hours taken as goodwill the member owes the head, in units of a day's work (The gift); opinion follows as for any goodwill; the tally of VB-1761 counts them.
- [ ] Decision points added to VB-1757's list: work at risk with the levies spent (demand, or not), a demand received, own hours to spend (give, a pastime, courting, rest, or take), an ask to answer.
- [ ] Q9, its second half: demands under 5% of levied hours and falling mostly on households of one or two levied members (populous small seed 7, 3 years): reported.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10): `npm run verify` green, a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose (never skipped or quarantined) and named in the PR; no stuck pawn (no task held 48 hours without progress, as VB-1546's script counts); the stocks' accounts close (`node tools/sim.js --report accounts`: `unexplained` no larger than before).

- [ ] Reported in the PR, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in those six runs, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed), before and after.
