# VB-1693: Design: a dependant's charge, a named living minder in place of the ban
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Principles for decisions (Whoever cannot keep itself is someone's charge; Nothing is left to no one); QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Archived 24 November 2026, question 7; backlog/VB-1602.md
Touches: design.md; sim/offers; sim/gifts; sim/exchange; sim/away; sim/visits; tests/lib/invariants.js (checkState)
Goal: Every child too young to keep itself, and every bedridden pawn, has a named living minder, an obligation that passes at once at the minder's death; a minder may leave, and weighs what leaving costs the dependant, instead of a verb's check forbidding it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `design.md` gains the rows (Proposed): who is a dependant (the age, from the child's own keeping: VB-1602's under 7), who is named its minder by default (by custom), how the charge passes at the minder's death (Nothing is left to no one) and at a move, and what a minder's absence costs the dependant (water, food, health) and how the minder weighs it.
- [ ] The ban built by VB-1602 (the last grown member may not leave; `mindsWhy` in the verbs' checks) is replaced by that weighing; no verb's check refuses a minder for its charge alone.
- [ ] An invariant in `checkState`: every dependant has a living minder (synthesis, M11, form 3).
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous small seeds 7 and 42, 10 years): days a child under 7 is alone at home, its dry and hungry days, and the lone parents' hungry days (VB-1602 saw hungry child-days double on seed 7 under the ban).
- [ ] VB-1602 and VB-1606 are closed by it or rewritten against it; the line of VB-1240 on a household of children alone is answered.
