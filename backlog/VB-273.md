# VB-273: An offer of marriage in the page: the player's question, answered yes or no
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-272
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Marriage as a negotiation: The player's doors); The player and what they see (Marriage; What the player sees; Skip to the next event).
Goal: VB-272 stores an offer that waits for the player in `st.offers`, and `Sim.answer` answers it. The player has to see it and answer it in the page. The world does not wait: the 7 days run at whatever speed is set.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `Sim.opportunities` (VB-209) lists the open offers that wait for the played pawn: for each, the offer's id, the two who would marry (name, age, household's farm), the home and its farm, the player's part (`the one to marry` or `head of the household` for whom), the other answers given so far, and the game days left.
- [ ] The pawn panel (`src/page/play.js`) shows each such offer as a question above the queue, with Yes and No buttons that call `Sim.answer`. It is gone once answered or closed. The panel then says how the offer ended (married, refused by whom, lapsed and why) until the next offer or the next round.
- [ ] A notice (`src/page/notices.js`) when an offer is made, which opens the pawn panel. A skip that stops on an offer (VB-272's `offer` reason) shows the same question.
- [ ] `tests/browser.js` plays a pawn to a round where its match waits, answers No from the panel, and finds both unmarried and the refusal in the History tab. A screenshot of the question under `docs/img/` (`node tools/shot.js`).
- [ ] With no player, nothing in the simulation changes: the golden file is unchanged.
- [ ] Cost: none to the simulation. The page reads `st.offers` (a few entries at most) while the panel is open.
