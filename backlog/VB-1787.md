# VB-1787: A pawn's goods at the fair: a lot with its owner, and a pawn's income from others
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-1782
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: plans
Phase: implement
Design ref: docs/plans/four-scales/aims.md, section 5.5 and decisions 22 and 23; design.md section 9, The fair: barter between villages; section 7, Aims and personal goods (Trade); section 11, The gathering (Who goes)
Touches: sim/fair; sim/gathering; sim/stores (record); tests/fair.js
Goal: A pawn can take its own goods to the gathering, or send them with its household's dealer, swap them for goods of its own, and its year's income from others is known
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A lot of `pawn.load` names its owner (a pawn or a household); a pawn with goods in its store over its own target is among who goes to the gathering, with that reason; a member who does not go may ask its household's dealer to carry its lot (an ask answered by the dealer's opinion), and each swap is booked to the lot's owner's store; goodwill as change is booked between the persons who dealt.
- [ ] No swap gives a pawn an animal or a good that does not wear (VB-1782): none is proposed to a pawn's lot.
- [ ] `pawn.trade` (`@state`, owner sim/stores): `{year, got: [this, last], gave: [this, last]}`, the customary worth a pawn got and gave for its goods, folded at the year's turn; read by `Sim` for the card.
- [ ] A check on a constructed gathering: a son's six pots swapped for grain, the grain in his store, his income booked, nothing made or lost by the stocks' accounts.
- [ ] Reported (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 10 years): pawn lots at the fair, and the worth pawns dealt against households'.
- [ ] Cost: a few dozen pawn lots a gathering beside the households'.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
