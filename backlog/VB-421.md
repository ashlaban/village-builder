# VB-421: A village whose wood in reach is gone: its households cut further, or leave
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (Fuel); Land, ecology and rights (The village's wood). backlog/VB-326.md, backlog/VB-327.md
Goal: A village's allotment (VB-326) holds only wooded tiles within WOOD.reach (12 steps); when plots, fields and other villages' cutting leave none, no firewood job is posted and, since VB-327, its houses are cold every day: health falls by 2 a winter day (3 for the frail), no meal is cooked, and the households die out. Find first why the allotment empties (tiles cut bare, built on, held by another village), then give such a village an answer by the existing systems: firewood cut beyond WOOD.reach at the cost of the longer walk, as the job's hours already reckon, or households that walk away to a village with wood (no teleportation: they travel, Kim 13 Nov). Numbers before and after at populous small seed 42, 30 years: villages emptied, cold household-days, deaths in cold houses.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Why the allotments of Kornåker and Berginge (populous small, seed 42) empty, measured and written here.
- [x] A village with an empty allotment is answered by an existing system (cutting beyond `WOOD.reach`, or households leaving on foot), decided by AGENTS.md, Deciding without Kim, and written here.
- [x] Numbers before and after at populous small seed 42 (and 7, 3, 11), 30 years: villages emptied (the `villages` band), cold household-days (`cold-households`), deaths in cold houses, growth.
Notes:
- 13 Nov 2026: opened by VB-327, whose numbers found it (`backlog/VB-327.md`, Validation). Before VB-327 Kornåker's 4 households lived 10 years with an empty pile and nothing followed.
- 13 Nov 2026, why the allotments empty (populous small 42, before this change; the wooded tiles within 12 steps of each centre followed day by day, with the village whose wood held each when it went):
  - Kornåker: 6 wooded tiles in reach at the start, 3 of them already in a neighbour's wood (generated villages take their wood in order, `finishLand`), so its allotment was 3 tiles for 3 to 4 households (it should be 20). About 44 trees a year from 3 tiles that regrow 6 to 8: all three cut bare in year 4 (by its own cutting), and the empty allotment came at year 4.5.
  - Berginge: 27 wooded tiles in reach, 4 in another village's wood; it held 23 for 9 households at the start (45 wanted) and grew to 16 (80 wanted). 16 households' 176 trees a year from 23 tiles that regrow about 55: the trip's even draw thinned all of them together, and 5 went bare in year 12 and 18 in year 13. Empty at year 13.9.
  - So neither was built on or taken by another village in play: the allotment was short of its size (reach held too few tiles for the hearths) and was cut bare, the end the design's area exists to prevent. The same at seed 11 (Hammaråker, Lundinge, Åssta) and 3 (Linåker).
- 13 Nov 2026, decision (Deciding without Kim): a village whose reach holds fewer wooded tiles than its area's size (`woodSize`) takes the rest beyond `WOOD.reach`, up to `WOOD.far` (27 steps: 9 hours' walk out on meadow and the trip's 3 hours' cutting fit the 6:00 to 18:00 day; the laden walk home may run past 18:00, as a delivery does today), its own land first and nearest first, after every tile within reach (`reckonWood`, `src/sim/territory.js`). Reason: it is the existing firewood job, whose walk the agent walks and whose bid already falls with distance (`MATCH.distanceWeight`), so the longer trip costs its hours and nothing new is built; it fills the area to its size before the near tiles are cut bare, rather than after; nobody moves, so it does not depend on VB-287 and nobody appears anywhere (Kim, 13 Nov). Households leaving would need a household move between villages, which no system makes today. The design's Decided parts (an area, larger; a tile drawn at random) stand; the change is to the Agreed reach: `design.md`, Land and ecology, The village's wood, still says "A village with no forest in reach has no wood"; it should read "within a day's walk (27 steps)", for the next design pass (agents do not edit design.md). A generated village or a settlers' site still needs `WOOD.least` tiles within `WOOD.reach`. The yearly line now says "no wood within a day's walk". Test: `tests/wood.js`, a village with forest only beyond reach gets its full area from 13 to 27 steps, nearest first, and none when the forest begins beyond `WOOD.far`.
