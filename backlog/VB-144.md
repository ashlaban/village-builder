# VB-144: Ewes' milk and wool
Status: ready

Design ref: Animals (Herds: sheep give wool, lambs, meat, manure); docs/review-balance.md (balance review, 1 November 2026), section 2; QUESTIONS.md, "Balance review: the tuned values", question 7.
Goal: Sheep give only lambs, meat and dung today. Walter of Henley (13th century) counts 20 ewes on salt-marsh pasture as giving the cheese and butter of 2 cows, and the *Hosebondrie* milks them only until August; wool was the other reason to keep sheep, spun and woven in winter.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A ewe that lambed this spring is in milk from turn-out to the end of summer at 0.05 rations a day (`HERD.eweMilk`), milked with the cows by the same job, 0.03 hours a ewe more; the milk goes where cows' milk goes (fresh and butter, `src/sim/byre.js`).
- [ ] On summer day 1 each sheep of a year or more gives a fleece: `hh.wool` counts fleeces (`@state`, invariant ≥ 0). Spinning and weaving wait for the winter crafts (VB-121); until then wool only accumulates and is shown in the household panel.
- [ ] `tests/milk.js` reports ewes' milk for founders 7, year 1, and checks it stops at the end of summer.
