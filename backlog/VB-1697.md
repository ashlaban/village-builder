Goal: A household that dies out stands as an estate until its last good has passed: what VB-650 left in the closed record passes with the farm to whoever takes it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Every good a closed household's stores still hold (the list in VB-1175's line from VB-650) stays at the farmstead, owned by the estate, and passes with the farm to the household that restores or takes it; none is dropped when the record closes.
- [x] What no one takes is used up by a named process (rot, as lying loads), with an event of the record.
- [x] `tests/estate.js` extended: a household dies out with each good in store; the next holder of the farm owns them where they lie.
- [x] VB-1175's line from VB-650 is ticked.

## Notes
- From the principles review (Kim, 24 Nov: P2, the remaining goods passing with the farm).
- 28 Nov 2026. Every other good of the stores (the wood under a trip, timber, stone by rock, peat, tar, hides, salt, wool, yarn, cloth by
  grade, iron, clay, ore, the rack, flour, meals, larder and ale lots) is taken out as handed on and lies at the house as one pile, kind
  `estate`, owned by the dead household (`of.goods`; its `n` the sum of their units). Put in at once for an heir on the same farm; else
  taken up by the household founded there (sim/membership foundHousehold -> sim/heirs passEstate, event `estate`). Not fetched: it passes
  with the farm, not with the herds' heir (the invariant's fetch rule exempts it). Unclaimed, the frost wears it as any lying load, each
  good by the same share, and names it ("household goods ... whose household is gone").
- Close calls: whole-numbered goods (tar, hides, wool, yarn, clay, ore, cloth, ale) the frost has worn are put in floored. Wet peat passes
  as peat (its drying season is not carried). An old save's closed records keep their goods as before (no migration).
- Revision (review F1): the frost wears an estate good by good in its own unit, (1 - LYING.frostLoss) a day, each gone under one of its
  unit (ESTATE_LEAST, sim/lying wearEstate), never by the rations' quantum on a sum of unlike units. Left (advisory F2-F4): the worn
  fractions of whole goods floored at the taking up, and the estate's food worn unbooked (the dead household's ledger is shut); a store
  wearing as an open pile (decay by good, a design question, the design note); household.fresh not taken (takeGoods' list kept by hand).
