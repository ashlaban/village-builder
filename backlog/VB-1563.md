Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/cost.md, 1.3 (rules 6 and 8); backlog/VB-1554.md (the measure)
Goal: The second part of the jobs line (VB-1554's profile, populous small, seed 7, 2 years): the commands takeDay issues are 22% of it (takeDay 23%, of which the verb work's run 79%: start 58%, of that walkTo and setOut 41%, findRoute 25%; release 11%), about 1.27 route searches a pawn-day against 1.9 hits. A pawn that takes a task walks from where it stands to the task's site, and most such walks are between the farm's door and its own fields, day after day.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The measure first: of the routes begun by `work` commands with source `planner`, the share that are searched (`routeSearch`) against found (`routeHit`), by from-and-to class (door to own field, field to field, to the village's task, elsewhere), by counters and one CPU profile, as a description.
- [ ] The largest class is cut by the rule it breaks (compile what is hot, bound every cache): `routeSearch` a pawn-day falls by at least a third at populous small (`node tools/sim.js --scenario populous --seed 7 --map small --years 2 --report counts`, before and after; `node tests/counters.js --update`), with no route cache unbounded.
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged (`node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`): a route found from a cache is the route a search finds.
- [ ] A cache added is derived, not saved, has one owner, and a test holds it equal to the search on a constructed state.
- [ ] `npm run format` and `npm run verify` pass.
