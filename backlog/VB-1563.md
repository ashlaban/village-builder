## Notes (VB-1563)
- The measure (populous small, seed 7, 2 years; 37,448 searches of `route`): by tile class of from and to, house to wild ground 22%, to or from the green 16%, door to own field 14%, field or meadow to field 8%, field to door 8%, wild to house 11%, house to house 7%, door to another's field 5%, the rest 9%. The cache was emptied on all 96 days (13 tiles changed a day): 42% of the searches were of a pair the cache held the day before. CPU: `route` 5.0% of the simulation, `findRoute` 4.8%.
- The rule broken: the cache was emptied whole when any tile changed, throwing away routes no change could touch. Cut, for every class alike: an A* pops only tiles whose key is at most the goal's, and a tile's key is at least (hexSteps(from, t) + hexSteps(t, to)) x minStep; a route whose every changed tile lies beyond that bound is the route a search finds now. Routes are set aside at the emptying (`st.routeKept`, with a log of each day's changed tiles by 16-tile cell) and tested only when asked for again: testing every route each day cost what it saved (measured, 90 ms against 120 ms).
- `routeSearch` 1.161 to 0.627 a pawn-day (-46%); `routeExpand` 18.8 to 14.4; routes taken back counted as hits (`routeHit` 1.66 to 2.20); new `routeCheck` 7.7. CPU over three profiles each: `route` 5.0% to 4.5%, `findRoute` 4.8% to 3.7%.
- `ROUTE.keepDays` 16 (tried 1, 4, 8, 16, 48: `routeSearch` 0.80, 0.72, 0.67, 0.63, 0.60): bounds the log and what is set aside (with `cacheMax`).
- `same.js` prints same: populous, founders, heirs at seeds 7 and 3, small, 4 years, and the default tiny runs. `tests/routekeep.js` holds every route taken back equal to a fresh search on a grid whose ground changes daily (and fails when the test is switched off).
- Target out: `scaling.routeCheck` 1.28 (limit 1.15). The checks per take are flat (11.7 to 11.9), but routes asked grow 1.14 in the probe (as `routeSearch` did on main, 1.138) and a larger share is taken back at 4 villages (34% to 37%); `routeSearch` itself fell from 1.138 to 1.07.
