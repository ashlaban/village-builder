# VB-402: Roads over fields and meadows, a green open on every side, and roads that look right
Status: done (2026-11-12)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Worn paths (4, Sharing paths: lanes form between farms, the well, the fields and the commons wood); Generated villages. Kim, 12 November 2026: roads pass over fields and meadows but not buildings, the green connects to roads from all sides, and roads look nice.
Goal: A road stopped at the edge of every field and meadow, since no built tile wore a path, and the green met roads on two or three sides only. Roads should run where people walk, over fields and meadows too, never through a house; meet the green from any side; and look like roads: no loose ends, meshes or kinks, and a colour and width that read on crops and grass.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Fields and meadows count crossings and are walked faster by their stage, as open land is (Kim: wear and speed, no loss of crop); a house and the green wear nothing. A house has a mark of its own (`w.dev` 3), set by the generator, by a house raised in play and on loading an older save.
- [x] The roads over fields and meadows are drawn: over the field art, joined to the roads of the open land with nothing painted twice.
- [x] The green meets roads from every side (Kim: layout too): no route crosses a house, so roads bend round buildings; at most three houses stand on the six tiles round a green, never two side by side; a village's street arms grow with its size and may leave the green on any side; every worn tile next to the green joins the roads that cross it.
- [x] The look (Kim: loose ends and gaps, meshes and kinks, colour and width, and what else the shots show): judged on `tools/village-shot.js` shots of several seeds, before and after, as generated and after some years.
- [x] Golden file re-recorded, the changed entries listed; numbers before and after (worn tiles by stage; the growth bands of populous) in the notes; `npm run verify` once at the end.

- Batch 2 (12 November), the green and the houses; Kim's three answers: routes go round houses, at most three houses on the green's rim, arms grow with size.
  - Routes: `sim/routes` `tileCost` is Infinity on a house (`w.dev` 3); a walk still starts or ends at one (the search's closed ends). `passable` (what the reach searches of `sim/hex` count steps over) still counts a house, so a house stays within reach of its neighbours; a new `through` is what a route may cross. A house raised in play tells the route cache (`costChanged`): a plot's house when the plot starts, a settler's when the farm is made.
  - Where a house may stand (`sim/hex`, with the other searches over the grid): `cutsOff` refuses a site that would leave itself or a neighbouring house no open tile, or whose open neighbours would no longer reach each other round it within 10 steps; `rimFull` refuses a fourth house on a green's rim, or one beside another there. Both hold in the generator (`layVillage`) and in play (`sim/expansion` `findSite`; `cutsOff` in `sim/settle` `newSite`). Placing by hand (`sim/byhand`) is not held to them.
  - Street arms (`layStreet`): 2 from 4 farms and one more for every 6 farms over that (3 from 10, 4 from 16, at most 6); a new arm leaves well away from the others while such a side is free, then on any side no arm has taken. The street's length is as before, so more arms are shorter arms. `STREET.armFarms` is the tunable (it replaces `STREET.lane`).
  - A village that comes to one farm is a lone farm: its centre is the house and the ground meant for the green is free again. It happened before too (populous small seed 11 had one) with the centre left on the empty green tile, which `tests/owners.js` would have refused had it run that seed.
  - Decided without Kim: the reach searches keep counting a house as land, as above (otherwise no search ends at a house); the bound of 10 steps on the way round (a longer way round a house counts as none; `CUT_REACH`).

- Batch 3 (12 November), Kim's two further points and the mess that remained.
  - Houses: a way ends at a house's door and never goes on through it (`gen/paths` `door`): the most worn tile beside the house sends a way in under the yard's edge; a house on the green has its door on the green. Drawing only.
  - The green: every way onto it, and each rim house's door way, runs to the open ground by the well, one meeting place; no fence runs along the green (`makeFence`, `open`), which the open rim had left fenced on its own.
  - A stronger pull of roads on the route search was built as a preference apart from the walking time (`PATHS.seek`), measured and taken out again (Kim: the discrepancy between path-finding cost and travel cost as small as possible). Populous small, seeds 7 and 42, two years, trodden/path/lane tiles: no pull 1052/534/125 and 910/477/121; 15/30/45% cheaper 1013/525/123 and 884/457/129; 25/45/60% 950/531/124 and 832/452/136; 40/65/80% 856/516/143 and 765/441/144, with 4 to 6% of trips longer in tiles and 1.0 to 1.4% more walking time. The trips that wear a village's ways are 3.2 tiles long on average, so there is seldom a longer way to take: the mess was in the drawing.
  - Instead, at no cost: among routes that cost the same the search takes the one nearest the straight line (`STRAIGHT`, a thousandth of a step a tile's width off it, in the search's key only), so the trips between two places wear one line.
  - What is drawn: ways from the stage of a lane (`LOOK.from` 3; Kim: a threshold where roads appear, heavy wear becomes roads, and the finer net of ways pawns use is not shown). The reason for a lane and not a path: one household alone makes some eleven crossings a day of the tile at its door (`PATHS.trips`: water 2, a stroll 1, three fields at 0.5, the wood 0.9, there and back), and a path is four a day, so every household drew its own and a village's middle was a net of them; a lane is sixteen a day, a way several households share. Populous small seed 7 after a year: 1,160 trail segments drawn from a path, 234 from a lane. Trodden ground and paths are walked, counted, quicker and named on the tile's card as before, and not drawn. A tile keeps to its two most worn neighbours (`keeps`), and at a meeting of three ways or more one main way runs through and the others join it at its middle.
