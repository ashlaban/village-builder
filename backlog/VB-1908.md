## Notes
- The yard is ladderHTML itself, so the build panel and the house callout's Build up show the same view; the selected tile is hud.yard (page state), its card under the tiles.
- What a building gives and its stages come from sim/ladder's tables (OUTBUILDINGS gives and kinds, RUNGS sleeps, FOUNDATION) and BUILD's stage hours; nothing new in the sim. The foundation's number is its standing term over the ground's (+33%).
- The missing thing's icon is read from farmView's why by its words (no cottage: the house; no wood: timber; under way, planned, not the head); anything else falls back to a lock.
- The tiles mark their open state with aria-expanded, not aria-pressed: panels.css greys every button[aria-pressed=false] and colours the pressed ones.
- The timber chip showed the hoe (actIcon('timber') has no path); it now uses goodIcon('timber').
- Test: tests/browser-yard.js (extended): the first view, the card and Build, and no text node of more than three words before the card's i.
