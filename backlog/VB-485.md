- 16 Nov 2026 (VB-485, implementation): `sim/plans`' `project` puts the head's horizon in place of the template's look-ahead: the
  template's targets (`woodWinter`, two winters of hay) are what a head that projects to the season's end keeps (skill 0.33 to 0.67), so
  it wants them as they are on any day. A head that reacts (under 0.33) wants them less what the rest of the season will burn
  (`ECON.woodUse`) and the stall days left in it will feed, never under nothing; a far-seeing head (0.67 and up) wants the larger of the
  template's target and what it projects to burn and feed to its horizon's end (`burnAhead`), so more only where the template looks less
  far; with today's ECON that projection (at most 25 loads; under a winter of stall) never passes the template's (30 to 40 loads; two
  winters), so such a head plans as the template does. The difference is kept in `targets.ahead` as before and taken off at the next
  review. `headFields` takes the fields the household's food asks before the cap (`fieldsWanted(st, hh, Infinity)`), times the bias,
  then caps at `workable`. `retarget`: a new head's targets made at once, at the event `newHead` (a succession) and in `startHeads`:
  the season's plan with its bias, and the fields it wants. `T.seed > 0` restored in `tests/lib/invariants.js`: no path makes it 0 now
  (the bias is at least 0.7; the target is made at the plan's making); `npm test`'s continuity and verify's invariants hold.
