# VB-210: Playing a pawn in the page: Play as, what it may do, the queue and the switch
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-209
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player and what they see (What the player sees, What it may do here: Proposed, VB-079); Which pawn (Decided, 3 Nov); `docs/review-life.md`, sections 2 and 3.
Goal: With VB-169 the simulation takes a player's commands and VB-209 says what a pawn may do; this task makes it playable in the page. The pawn panel offers "Play as", and while the player plays a pawn it shows what the pawn needs, what it is doing, what it may do here (each a button that queues the command), the queue, and the automatic switch.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] "Play as" on the pawn panel of any living adult (Which pawn), and on the start of a new game the scenario's suggestion: the first holder in founders and heirs, a holder in populous. "Stop playing" on the played pawn's panel.
- [ ] While playing, the pawn panel shows (design.md, What the player sees):
  - its hunger and health, and its household's food in days of need and its firewood;
  - what it is doing, and the queue, each command with a button that removes it;
  - the automatic switch;
  - the last refused command and why;
  - what it may do here, from `Sim.opportunities`, grouped as work, going, causes and at the moving round; each with its basis, and greyed with its reason where `why` is set.
  A click queues the command; "Walk here" queues a walk to the next open tile clicked on the map.
- [ ] A "Follow" switch keeps the camera on the played pawn (page state, not `st`); the played pawn is marked on the map, so it can be found among others.
- [ ] The page reads `st` and writes only through `Sim`, as the lint requires; the panel is rebuilt at its present rate (`src/page/animate.js:144`), and `Sim.opportunities` is called at most once per rebuild.
- [ ] `tests/browser*.js`: play as a pawn, queue a job, see it carried out, switch the automatic behaviour off and see the pawn idle in working hours; no console errors. The phone layout (`tests/browser-mobile.js`) shows the panel usable at its width.
- [ ] Screenshots before and after of the pawn panel at the same scenario, seed, year and view (desktop at 1440×900 and a phone), in `docs/img/vb-210-*.png`, linked here and in the PR. Golden file unchanged.
