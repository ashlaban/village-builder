# VB-210: Playing a pawn in the page: Play as, what it may do, the queue and the switch
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player and what they see (What the player sees, What it may do here: Proposed, VB-079); Which pawn (Decided, 3 Nov); `docs/review-life.md`, sections 2 and 3.
Goal: With VB-169 the simulation takes a player's commands and VB-209 says what a pawn may do; this task makes it playable in the page. The pawn panel offers "Play as", and while the player plays a pawn it shows what the pawn needs, what it is doing, what it may do here (each a button that queues the command), the queue, and the automatic switch.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] "Play as" on the pawn panel of any living adult (Which pawn), and on the start of a new game the scenario's suggestion: the first holder in founders and heirs, a holder in populous. "Stop playing" on the played pawn's panel.
- [x] While playing, the pawn panel shows (design.md, What the player sees):
  - its hunger and health, and its household's food in days of need and its firewood;
  - what it is doing, and the queue, each command with a button that removes it;
  - the automatic switch;
  - the last refused command and why;
  - what it may do here, from `Sim.opportunities`, grouped as work, going, causes and at the moving round; each with its basis, and greyed with its reason where `why` is set.
  A click queues the command; "Walk here" queues a walk to the next open tile clicked on the map.
- [x] A "Follow" switch keeps the camera on the played pawn (page state, not `st`); the played pawn is marked on the map, so it can be found among others.
- [x] The page reads `st` and writes only through `Sim`, as the lint requires; the panel is rebuilt at its present rate (`src/page/animate.js:144`), and `Sim.opportunities` is called at most once per rebuild.
- [x] `tests/browser*.js`: play as a pawn, queue a job, see it carried out, switch the automatic behaviour off and see the pawn idle in working hours; no console errors. The phone layout (`tests/browser-mobile.js`) shows the panel usable at its width.
- [x] Screenshots before and after of the pawn panel at the same scenario, seed, year and view (desktop at 1440×900 and a phone), in `docs/img/vb-210-*.png`, linked here and in the PR. Golden file unchanged.

- 10 Nov 2026, implementation: a new page script, `src/page/play.js` (after `page/history.js` in `src/page.json`). "Play as" on the card of any living adult (18 and over) and "Stop playing" on the played pawn's, through `Sim.inhabit` and `Sim.uninhabit`. A new game offers the scenario's suggestion over the toasts (`#playOffer`: the holder of the first household with a living adult holder, which is the first holder in founders and heirs and a holder in populous); it goes when a pawn is played, a panel opens or another world is built. While playing, the card shows hunger and health, the household's food in days of need (grain above the seed reserve, cooked rations and the larder, over a day's rations of its living) and its firewood, what it is doing, the automatic switch (`Sim.setAuto`), Follow, the queue (each with Remove, `Sim.unqueue`), the last refused command with its reason and time, and what it may do here from one `Sim.opportunities` call per rebuild, grouped as work, going, causes and at the moving round, each with its basis or, greyed, its `why`. Work and going queue through `Sim.queue` (idle for an hour from the click); "Walk here" waits for the next tile clicked on the map (`page/tileinfo` asks first), asks `Sim.opportunities` with that tile and queues the walk only where it is open, else says why and waits for another. Causes and the round's acts are shown read-only until VB-211 and VB-212. Follow keeps the camera on the pawn, or its house while it is indoors (page state; a drag lets go). The played pawn has a gold pointer over its head (or its house), the same size on screen at any zoom. The card is rebuilt at its present rate (`page/animate`, four times a second), its HTML replaced only when it changes. `docs/architecture/modules.md` names the script.
