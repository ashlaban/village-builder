# VB-771: A place's label: outsiders, dealing days and work, shown and reported
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-769, VB-765
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: town
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Settlements, Towns and regions (A market place and a town are labels; What a place's label reads); docs/plans/VB-189.md, section 5.1
Goal: A market place is a label read from what people do, which no rule reads (Kim, 16 Nov): households of other villages dealing on a village's green, with dealing days beside it; "town" gets no cut point until something makes one.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `placeMeasures(st, v)`, a derived view (target-v2, section 4.6), from the yearly counts VB-769 folds: over the last 3 years, **outsiders** (households of other villages that dealt on its green in a year), **dealing days** (gathering days on which at least `PLACE.deal` 3 households dealt there) and the share of its people's work that is not farming (VB-765's split). Never stored as truth.
- [ ] The label: **market place** when outsiders are `PLACE.enter` 12 or more at `PLACE.years` 3 year ends running; dropped when under `PLACE.leave` 6 at 3 year ends running (the numbers Proposed, read off a toy model). **No label "town" exists**: no cut point is defined until something makes one, and the code holds none.
- [ ] No rule of the simulation reads the label or the measures: a lint line (`tests/lint.js`) allows `placeMeasures` and the label only in the page and in tools.
- [ ] `tools/places.js` (with `--help`): every village by decade with its three measures, its label, its people and its sink (VB-765); the island's count of market places; the fullest place's share of the island's maker households (the mock: 10% to 22%).
- [ ] Reported, populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years, and the test bed (VB-778) if it exists: the histogram of outsiders by village-year (does it fall into two groups, as in the mock: under 3 at 87% of village-years, 24 and more at hosts?), and whether 12 and 6 sit in the gap; if they do not, the values that would, as a `tuning` note.
- [ ] A `report` entry in `tests/lib/bands.js`: market places an island, reported nightly, never failing.
- Cost (rule 9): 25 to 30 villages x once a year, from yearly counts; outside the step.
