Goal: src/culture.js holds the catalogue of customs and practices (DIMS, ARCHETYPES, PRACTICES), the daily drift of leanings with its causes (drift, driftPerson, entrepreneurs, WEIGH), and the norms on goods (WORTH, CLASSES, PROPER, norm). The golden file is gone (VB-408): they become ordinary exports of the module each ends in, and the width baseline says the true number.
