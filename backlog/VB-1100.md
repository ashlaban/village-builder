# VB-1100: The offer shows what it would bring: who moves, each farm and heir, and food after
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, Marriage as a negotiation (The offer; The player's doors); Principles for decisions (The future is someone's estimate)
Goal: The player answers a match knowing what it brings: the people who move, the farms held, held in trust or left, the heirs after, and the food of the home that receives them.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] One function of the simulation, `offerPreview(st, offerId, viewer)`, says what a yes would bring, read from the rules the round's marriage runs (`homeFor` and the handing on of `src/sim/matching.js`, the trust and handover of `sim/trust` and `sim/heirs`), never from a copy of them in the page: where the couple lives; each pawn who changes household and where to (the spouse, the children who follow, a ward); for each farm either of them holds or is first heir to, who holds it after (taken over by whom, held in trust by whom, left empty); the first heir of the home after; and the home's food in days of need after the move, and before it.
- [x] The offer in the Queue sheet shows it before Yes and No, pictures before words (`AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim, 15): the portraits of those who move with where they go, each farm as its icon with its holder after, food before and after as the one meter. It says whose estimate it is (principle 14: the played pawn's; the actor view filters nothing yet, so today it equals the outcome).
- [x] A check on a small constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): the case of the playtest, a widowed holder with five children whose eldest is a minor marries onto a widow's farm; the preview names the five children who move, the minor's farm held in trust by the receiving home, and the food after; after a yes, the state is what the preview said.
- [x] The skip's stop for an offer (`skipStop`, `src/sim/notable.js`) opens the offer where it is answered, so the player does not look for it in the Queue sheet.
- [x] Cost: computed when the sheet is shown, never in a step; nothing added to `st`.

Notes / questions:
- 16 Nov 2026 (playtest, founders small seed 7, 80 years in the page): in year 16 the round offered Ingeborg, a widow holding Östergården with three children, a match with Hans Andersson, a widower holding Södergården with five. The offer read "would marry and live at Östergården". A yes brought Hans's five children into the house (food 43 to 19 days of need), made his 14-year-old take over Södergården and then go "to kin at Östergården, who hold the farm in trust", so the player's household held two farms; Hans died weeks later. All of it was told only afterwards, in the village log. In year 58 the same kind of offer could be judged only from memory of the first.
- Runs one at a time with VB-690 (offer and answer as one verb pair), which moves the marriage offer onto a general offer; whichever lands second builds on the other. The preview is the marriage basis's first; a later basis (a loan, a gift) may add its own.
- 16 Nov 2026 (the parallel session "Gameplay review and feedback": founders small seed 7, 75 years headless, one played pawn; its evidence added at the user's request): Jon (58) was offered his widowed stepsister Margit (54); under the custom "the widow keeps the farm for life" a yes held back his heir, his nephew Håkan, and her daughter Ingrid (17) moved in with her. The offer said neither.
