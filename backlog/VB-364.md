# VB-364: The bloomery: bog ore smelted with wood to iron

Goal: A household with bog ore builds a bloomery and smelts ore and wood into iron blooms, the good tools are mended with. Slice 6's plan (VB-185, 11 Nov), from Kim's answers in the session.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A facility kind `bloomery`: built by a household on its own land or the outland within 3 steps of its house, 20 hours, 4 loads of stone and 2 of clay (`BLOOM`); a household builds one when it has 4 loads of bog ore and its tools want iron (VB-121), or as its side craft's project.
- [ ] `smelt`: 10 work hours with 2 hands at the bloomery, 4 loads of bog ore and 6 of wood from the owner's stores, gives 1 bloom (`hh.iron`, in blooms, through `sim/stores`, reason `smelt`); skill smelting raises the yield to 1.5 at mastery (Proposed). Offered in autumn and winter.
- [ ] The worth table gains a bloom at 30 rations (Proposed). Rock iron, mines and a smithy as a facility are not built: they wait for a mine (slice 8 or a later half).
- [ ] Numbers (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): bloomeries built and by whom, blooms made and held, wood burnt against the village's wood (VB-326), bog ore dug against its regrowth (VB-359).
- [ ] The golden file is re-recorded where behaviour changes, its changed entries listed; `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
