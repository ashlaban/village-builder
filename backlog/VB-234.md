# VB-234: Skip to the next event that concerns the player's pawn
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player and what they see (Skip to the next event: Decided as a principle, 9 Nov; what stops it Proposed); Time and calendar (Speeds).
Goal: A run lasts 40 to 80 hours and a pawn's adult life many of them, most of it routine. Kim decided on 9 November (gameplay review) that the player may skip until the next event. The page runs the simulation at the uncapped speed until something happens that concerns the played pawn, and pauses there with the camera on it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A "Skip" control beside the speeds, offered while the player plays a pawn: the simulation runs at the uncapped speed (VB-084) until a stop, then the page pauses (it does not drop to 1×), moves the camera to what happened and says why it stopped.
- [ ] What stops it comes from one function in the simulation over the record of events and `st.player`, shared with VB-098's `notable`; the page only reads it. It stops on: an event that concerns the played pawn, its household or its line (a birth, marriage or death; a farm inherited, emptied or claimed; hunger in the household; a change of practice in its village); a question that waits for the player's answer (an offer of marriage, once VB-233's tasks are built); a refused command; an empty queue with the automatic behaviour off; and the eve of the moving round.
- [ ] A skip never changes what the simulation does: a run skipped to a day has the same golden hashes as one played to that day (rule 7 of the Rules for cost).
- [ ] A skip always ends: the eve of the next moving round at the latest, so never longer than a year.
- [ ] `tests/browser*.js`: play a pawn, skip, and see the page paused at a named event with no console errors.
