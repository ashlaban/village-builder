# VB-713: Hunting rights: a rule of the culture and the right hunt
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-311
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: animals
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Goods, production and animals: Animals, Customs of the animals; docs/plans/VB-141.md (section 2.5; section 6, points 6 and 7); design.md, Land and rights
Goal: Who may hunt where is a rule of the culture first (Kim, 16 Nov), a voted custom later: free as built, on the village's own land, or by the village's leave.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `PRACTICES.hunting` in `src/culture.js` (a rule of the culture: not voted on, no drift): options `free` ("Anyone may hunt"; the base), `landed` ("On the village's own land") and `leave` ("By the village's leave"); it is in a new culture's practices and in loaded saves (`sim/saves`), and is shown with the culture's customs on the page.
- [ ] `hunt` is a kind of `RightKind` (target-v2 4.3; VB-311), beside `gather` and `fish`. `huntGround` (`sim/deer`) checks it for each candidate tile: under `free`, as today; under `landed`, only tiles the hunter's household or its village holds; under `leave`, those, and only for a household holding a `hunt` right for the season, granted by the village's alderman through a verb (principle "Every act is a pawn's"). With no alderman (VB-314 not landed, or the office vacant) `leave` acts as `landed`, said in the log once a village.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state with its line in `tests/suites.js`: a household between its own village's wood and a neighbour's hunts both under `free`, only its own under `landed`, and neither under `leave` without the grant.
- [ ] Numbers on one fixture (populous small seed 7, 15 years), each option forced: hunts a year, kills, meat a household, the deer stock by region at the end, and which villages lose their hunting (those with no forest edge of their own). Under `free`, behaviour equals today's (`node tools/same.js --base REF`).
- [ ] Cost (Rules for cost 9): hunts (a few a household a year) × the candidate tiles of a hunt ground (within `DEER.hunt.reach`) × one lookup of the tile's holder; nothing daily, no counter.
- [ ] Not a voted custom yet: the task file names what would make villages differ (a second game animal, claims between villages), for Kim's later review of the customs.
