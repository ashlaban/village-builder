# VB-554: Lies and counter-stories, by pawns and by the player; and what the player hears
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: talk
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, People (Traits, talk and stories: Stories, the rows Lies, Exposure and What the player hears); target-v2, sections 4.4 (Stories) and 7.3; Kim's decision 23
Goal: Pawns and the player alike may lie, and a discovered lie is the source of a counter-story about the liar, of higher valence, by how the culture judges lies; the player reads what its pawn has heard as that pawn's belief.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `Stories.invent(st, teller, about, kind, why)` makes a story with `lie: true` that the teller holds first-hand and tells as any other; one lie about one pawn at a time. The flag is read only by `expose` and by reports: no pawn's choice and no page text reads it (a test greps the `Sim` API's story view for it).
- [x] Who lies: at its talk turn a pawn that holds another as an enemy (`pawn.bands`, -1) invents a story about it at chance `STORY.lie` 0.005 × (1 − its honour) × (0.5 + its ambition), of a negative kind of `STORY.kinds` drawn by weight; a verb of `sim/commands` (`slander`, with whom and the kind), which refuses a lie about oneself, a second lie about the same pawn and a pawn under 16, and returns why.
- [x] The player: the same verb from the action bar's Myself group ("Speak ill of", a party and a kind in one form), with one line saying what it risks; the played pawn is exposed as any liar is.
- [x] Exposure: a lie told to a hearer who holds a first-hand memory of the subject (not hearsay), of a kind in the story's domain and of the other sign, is not believed. The hearer then holds first-hand a counter-story of kind `lied` about the story's `by`, of valence −min(1, `counter` 1.5 × |the lie's| × (0.5 + the hearer's honour) × (0.5 + its culture's mean honour)); the teller is told it at once and its zeal for the lie goes to 0. `lied` is a story kind as any other, in every domain. An event of the record (`lieExposed`, with its kind, the liar, the hearer and the place).
- [x] Honour's second rule and ambition's are thereby built (the table of VB-520 is completed in its file or here).
- [x] What the player hears: `Sim.heard(pawnId)` (the actor view) gives, for each story the pawn holds: whom it is about, the kind in words, from whom it last heard it, how many mouths, and how well it is remembered (a word); never the flag. The pawn panel's Heard list shows them under a line that says whose belief it is. A story about the played pawn's household, heard by the played pawn, is a notable event (`sim/notable`; a toast; it stops Skip to the next event). `node tests/smoke.js`, the `tests/browser*.js` touched; a screenshot under `docs/img/`. (Smoke and tests/browser.js pass, the latter now opens the form and queues a slander; no screenshot committed, Kim 18 Nov.)
- [x] The reduced scenario of VB-523 gains the lie bands as an `assert` (`tests/lib/bands.js`): with three living witnesses a slander reaches fewer of the two villages than a true story of its kind in at least 7 of 10 seeds, and its counter-story more than the lie in at least 7 of 10 (the experiment: 10 of 10 each).

- Decisions (VB-554): a first-hand memory's domain is STORY.domains; the only good law memory is `worked`, and kin has none, so a kin bond counts as the kin domain's good knowledge (else a kin lie could never be exposed); a first-hand true story of the other sign counts too; the subject always knows better.
- One counter-story a lie (`s.counter`), each holder's valence its own; the liar holds it first-hand, a re-teller as hearsay; the hearer also remembers the liar (`lied`, cap −0.6, 360 days).
- Lies draw from the `stories` stream (no new system); the culture's mean honour is read from st.cenv as sim/traits reads it (sim/traits is at 15 exports).
- The rite (`spoke`) exposes a lie to those who know better, as a talk does. `heardOfUs` and `lieExposed` are record events, kept 2 years, personal.
- Lie bands, 10 seeds (liesRun): the lie reached less than the true story in 9 of 10, its counter-story further than the lie in 9 of 10.
- Every trait act is 1 at 0.5 (tests/traitacts.js): honour.lie is [2, 0] and STORY.lie 0.0025, the design's 0.005 × (1 − honour) unchanged.
