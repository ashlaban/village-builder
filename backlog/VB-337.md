# VB-337: Review tools: a household's hours by job, and the stocks nothing reads
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Balance loops and targets; AGENTS.md, Commands. Reviews of 10 and 11 November (VB-326 to VB-336)
Goal: The consistency reviews of 10 and 11 November answered their questions with scratch probes that were written, run and thrown away: one sampled every pawn each six-minute step to find that three fields cost a household 215 pawn-hours a year and its water 121; another listed by hand, quantity by quantity, what reads the woodpile, the water and the stone heap, and found that nothing does. Kim, 11 Nov: make the first two of these measurements tools, since every later change to balance asks the same questions.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Hours: `node tools/sim.js --report hours [--json]` (a module `tools/hours.js`, as `tools/idle.js` is for `--report idle`, starting with `tools/help.js`): over the run's years, pawn-hours by job kind, split into work and walking or carrying (an agent with a job whose phase is not `work`), for each household and season, and idle working hours beside them. Printed: the mean and the spread by household for each kind, as hours and as a share of the working hours of the household's hands (members of 14 to 59, not retired) and of all its workers (children from 7 at their rate); `--household N` prints one household's table; `--fields N` keeps the households with N fields, and `--team` splits those with an ox team from those without.
- [x] It samples as `tools/idle.js` does, outside the simulation: it steps the run and reads `st.agents`, writes nothing to `st`, and adds no counter to the modules; the same seed gives the same run with or without the report (a test asserts the state hash is equal). Its cost is the tool's own: said in its help (about a minute a year of populous small when it samples each step; a sample an hour is the default if the numbers agree within 2%).
- [x] The farming year's days ride with it: for each field, the day it is hoed, sown, ripe, reaped, in the barn and threshed, read from the stage changes (not from an end-of-day sample, which misses a field hoed and sown in one day), reported as the median and the latest by household group, with fields never sown and crops lost to frost.
- [x] Stocks: `node tools/stocks.js [--json]`, built on the writes map (`tools/writes.js --json`): for each stored quantity of a household, a farm, a village and a tile (a table in the tool names them: grain, meals, sheaves, the larder, wood, stone, hay, manure, the day's water, fence and house condition, a village's grain, a tile's trees and stones), the modules that add to it, those that take from it, and those that read it without writing it; a quantity with no reader in the simulation but its own job kind's `available` and `urgency` is marked `read by nothing`, and one read only by the page `shown only`. Today it must mark the woodpile, the day's water and stone (until VB-327, VB-331 and VB-332 land), which is its first test.
- [x] What happens at none is the part a scan cannot find: the tool's table has a line for each quantity, written by hand and checked by a test in `npm test` that fails when a quantity of the `@state` lines of households, farms and villages has no line; a new stored quantity then cannot be added without saying what its lack costs.
- [x] `AGENTS.md`, Commands, names both; `docs/architecture/modules.md` lists the two tools. A new state field's checklist (Working rules: "A new state field or record gets its invariants") gains: and, if it is a stored quantity, its line in `tools/stocks.js`.
- [x] The numbers of the two reviews are reproduced as the tools' first run and recorded in the task file: populous small, seeds 7 and 42, year 3, households of three fields (hoeing 61 + 13, weeding 30 + 28, reaping 17 + 9, water 121, cooking 120, firewood 103), and the three quantities read by nothing. `npm test` passes; nothing under `src/` changes, so the golden file is unchanged.

- Orchestrator's decisions for Kim to overturn:
  - Hours: a household-year counts when the household lived through the whole year sampled; its fields and ox team are those at the end
    of the spring; idle hours are those of members of 7 and over, not retired, without a job in the working hours; workers are members of
    7 and over, not retired, at `workRate` (60 and over count fully while not retired); the spread is the 10th, 50th and 90th percentile
    of the household-years' hours; the shares are of the group's summed hands' and workers' hours. `--from Y` added to sample year Y on.
  - Hours: a pawn's hour counts for its own household whoever's job it is on (help and service included).
  - Hours, the farming year: a crop gone from sown, growing or ripe to stubble without reaping counts as lost to frost; the threshing day
    is when the barn's lot of that field (oldest first) is threshed; days are told as season and day of the sampled year.
  - Stocks: besides the task's list, the table has ale, clay and bog iron ore, and a tile's fish, wild food and deposit, as quantities of
    the @state lines. A read counts only outside the functions that change the quantity (a store call on its good or a direct write), so
    a read to decide how much to move (a new household's first wood) is not a use; a take is a use unless its reason is a loss (spoilage,
    burn) or a transfer (gift, share, handingOn, provisions, start), or its function is one of the line's `losses` (hay spoiling).
    `read by nothing` wins over `shown only` when its own kind reads it; `shown only` is for a quantity nothing in the simulation reads,
    its own kinds included, that the page reads.
  - Stocks: the `page` mark counts the page's direct reads of the field; what it shows through a `Sim` function (the larder) is not seen.
