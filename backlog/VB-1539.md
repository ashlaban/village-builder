- The playtest's numbers stand. The job is posted at 5:00 every day; the lone farmer ranks it at his household-work rank 2 times its urgency (2.8 before 10:00, 2, 6.6 from 14:00), against 4.5 to 10.7 for the work he takes instead; it lies 3rd to 15th of his day's list and the day's long tasks run past 18:00 before he reaches it. The pair's mistress ranks it 9: their dry days are harvest days and a day owed.
- Day's order: every task the settler took after 14:00 with the water open came from a list made before 14:00 (102 of 102, seed 42; 67 of 68, seed 7), and in 85 and 46 of them water outranked the task by then: the 14:00 step reaches no list unless the day is allotted anew, and on a dry day the cook (which needs the water) is not posted to cause it (src/sim/jobs.js:198 allots everyone only at 5:00).
- Claimed and not finished: all 7 taken at 17:24-17:54 and released at 18:00 (sim/clock's end of work); an hourly sample saw none of them (VB-626's 1-4% is a floor).
- Counterfactuals (in memory; small; to 4 years): seed 42, water's urgency 3.3 all day: 51 dry, dies day 100 of starvation (31 hungry days, cause not traced); farmer's rank of water 9: 8 dry, health 100, lives; water `late`: 50 dry, 71 hungry days, lives; the day allotted anew at 14:00: 69 dry, health 100, lives. Seed 7, rank 9: 16 dry, health 100.
