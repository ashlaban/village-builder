# VB-1539: Own time: a pawn with no task chooses a pastime
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: `design.md` section 7, Own time; section 4, Where pawns meet; `docs/review-agency.md`, section 4; `docs/architecture/target-v2.md` section 4.5, Agency below the head
Goal: A pawn with no task strolls and waits (`sim/jobs` `idleAbout`, `sim/relations` `companyFor`): a fifth to two thirds of working hours by role and season, read on the card as "Strolling" or "Resting". Build design.md's row Own time: a new module `sim/pastimes` (Social layer; listed after what it reads) owns a table of pastimes, each saying who may choose it, where it is spent, with whom, what weighs for it and its words for the card; the first table is the row's (company, a visit, the green, the fire, play, by the herd). `idleAbout` moves out of `sim/jobs` into it and becomes the choice among them: a weighted draw from the stream `talk`, by sociability, ties and liking, and the walk. A pastime is a walk and a wait: it lasts as the idle wait did, is cut as any leg is, makes and uses no good, and its only effects are those of the encounter it puts the pawn in (`sim/encounters`); it reads no plan, no store and no mood. It is chosen through a verb `pastime` (`sim/commands`), which the played pawn has in its Actions under Myself. Nothing teleports: a visit is walked.
