# VB-100: Common fields worked by the village
Status: needs-review
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: Economy and jobs, Goods and the farming year (Built land: fields and meadows can be private or common: Decided); Jobs, owners and roles (Owners: common fields and meadows are owned by the village; Who may take a job: adults of the owning village: Decided); Commons (common fields and meadows: Decided).
Goal: Every field is a household's today. The design has common fields owned by the village and worked by its adults. With a village store (VB-039), a common field gives a village food to help hungry households in a poor year. Depends on VB-039 (the village store) and VB-042 (tile claims); if either is not done, mark this blocked. Common meadows come with hay in the animals arch.
Acceptance criteria (draft, pending `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-100):
- [ ] When a village makes a common field, by the answered rule (proposed: at the moving round, a village of 3 or more occupied farms whose store holds less than 10 days of food for its members, with a free meadow tile within 3 steps of the green; at most one common field per 3 farms), it claims the tile for the village (VB-042) and posts VB-010's `clear` and `break` jobs, owned by the village.
- [ ] A common field passes through the same stages as a household's; its jobs are village jobs, taken by the village's adults at the village-duty priorities of VB-058 (farmhand and son 6, farmer 5, wife, maid and daughter 3).
- [ ] Seed comes from the village store and the harvest goes into it.
- [ ] The village panel lists its common fields and their stage.
- [ ] A test: a village with an empty store makes a common field within two years, and its harvest reaches the store.
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
