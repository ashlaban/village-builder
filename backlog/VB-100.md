# VB-100: Common fields worked by the village
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs, Goods and the farming year (Built land: fields and meadows can be private or common: Decided); Jobs, owners and roles (Owners: common fields and meadows are owned by the village; Who may take a job: adults of the owning village: Decided); Commons (common fields and meadows: Decided).
Goal: Every field is a household's today. The design has common fields owned by the village and worked by its adults. With a village store (VB-039), a common field gives a village food to help hungry households in a poor year. Depends on VB-039 (the village store) and VB-042 (tile claims); if either is not done, mark this blocked. Common meadows come with hay in the animals arch.
Acceptance criteria (Kim's answers of 10 November; `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-100"):
- [x] When a village makes a common field, as a routine its meeting may choose (VB-257) when this holds: a village of 3 or more occupied farms whose store holds less than 10 days of food for its members, with a free meadow tile within 3 steps of the green; at most one common field per 3 farms; it claims the tile for the village (VB-042) and posts VB-010's `clear` and `break` jobs, owned by the village.
- [x] A common field passes through the same stages as a household's; its tasks are the village's, taken by its adults on the `villager` basis.
- [x] Their priority is a custom (Kim): a new dimension `commons` in `DIMS` (`src/culture.js`) with three options, `own` (the commons after a household's own work: the village-duty priorities of VB-058 less 2), `fair` (those priorities: farmhand and son 6, farmer 5, wife, maid and daughter 3) and `first` (those priorities plus 2), with a value in each `ARCHETYPES` entry (`fair`, but `first` where the archetype already favours the village's claims); it is voted and drifts as the other customs do. The village's custom in force gives the priority; compiled to an array when the custom changes (Rules for cost, 6).
- [x] Help received moves it (Kim): a person whose household was given food while hungry leans towards the next higher option of `commons`, as a term of the person's interests (`refreshInterests`, `src/sim/society.js`, where a lost dispute's grievance and `kinPush` already lean persons), in proportion to the days of need given in the last year and fading with them. It is read from the household's goodwill balances as kept (no new record, no daily pass: the pressure is computed on the person's drift day). The numbers are the agent's starting values: `npm run calibrate` before and after, each custom within the calibration's bands, and over 30 populous-small years at seeds 7 and 42 the villages that gave help in a hungry year hold `first` more often afterwards than those that did not (reported).
- [x] Seed comes from the village store and the harvest goes into it.
- [x] The village panel lists its common fields and their stage.
- [x] A test: a village with an empty store makes a common field within two years, and its harvest reaches the store.
