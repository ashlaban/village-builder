Goal: Bronze has a village use that the meeting chooses (Kim, 16 Nov; principle 16), before the church gives the bell its own.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A decision of the yearly meeting (through VB-313's Decisions), proposed when the village's holders together hold `BELL.bronze` (20 bars) of bronze or its copper and tin, and the village has no bell; carried out by the alderman (VB-673): he asks each holder for its share, which is carried to the green, and a pawn with the skill casts it there (60 hours, a pit, 4 loads of clay and 10 of wood; Proposed). Every hour and every load is a pawn's.
- [ ] The bell is the village's, one record a village on the green's tile. While it hangs, each of the village's holders has a term of standing at the gathering (Proposed 0.05, with VB-705's deeds), and the gathering's log names it. It cracks with chance a hundredth a year (the village's own stream of `sim/rng`), and is recast from its own metal with a tenth lost.
- [ ] No church use: a note on VB-188 (slice 8's plan) that the bell exists and what the church adds.
- [ ] Invariants (the bell's record; bronze conserved from holders to bell), a `tools/stocks.js` line.
- [ ] Numbers, populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15, 40 years: villages with a bell and the year each was cast, where its copper and tin came from (own veins, other villages), meetings that proposed one and failed for want of metal.
- [ ] The numbers that move are reported before and after at the seeds and maps named (Working rules); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
Notes:
- Numbers are Proposed by the plan of VB-702 (16 Nov) on Kim's answers to the shape, from general reading not checked by search: the task reports its own, found in at most five short runs.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim's answers after the plan of VB-702; section 14): The good is declared graded (the plan of VB-702, 13.3): its making draws a grade by the maker's skill at the craft the criteria name, its numbers are the sound row, and the report gives pieces by grade. The bell's chance to crack is divided by the grade's life and its term of standing takes the grade's worth, under the term's cap.
