# VB-329: No pawn walks where there is no route: a job with no way by land is refused
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: World, climate and travel (Worn paths). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Consistency review (10 Nov), pawns placed, question 2
Goal: When the route search finds no way within `ROUTE.reach` (200 meadow steps), or none by land, `route` returns the goal alone and counts a fallback (`src/sim/routes.js:124`), and `walkTo` then draws one straight leg to it over whatever lies between, the sea included (`src/sim/agents.js:45-56`). No pawn did so in 12 years of populous small, seed 7 (`fallbacks: 0`), but nothing forbids it: a job bid weighs distance by hex steps as the crow flies (`src/sim/jobs.js:98`). Kim, 10 Nov: a job with no route by land is refused.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A pawn bids for no job whose site it has no route to: the bid asks the route cache (`route`, cached, so a known pair costs a lookup) only for the job it would otherwise win, and a job refused for want of a route is not asked about again by that household that day (rule 4: no polling). The verbs that send a pawn somewhere (`src/sim/commands.js`: the player's Walk here, `goHome`) return "no way there" for a goal with no route.
- [ ] `walkTo` with no route does not move the pawn: it stays where it is, idle, and `st.routeFallbacks` counts it. `tests/lib/invariants.js` holds the count at 0 over the invariants' runs, so a fallback is a failure with the pair named, not a walk over water.
- [ ] Moves to a new home are VB-287's (a journey with no route by land does not start; `design.md`, Between villages, Over the sea); this task does not touch `moveTo`.
- [ ] A test (`tests/routes.js` or beside it, in `npm test`): a job posted on another island gets no bid and its owner's pawns take other work; a goal beyond `ROUTE.reach` is refused; a pawn told to walk there is told why not.
- [ ] Cost (rule 9): at most one route lookup for each winning bid, which `walkTo` makes today anyway; `routeSearch` and `routeHit` before and after at `small-y40` and `medium-ref`. Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): fallbacks (0 expected before and after), jobs refused for want of a route, and the golden file unchanged or its changed entries listed.
