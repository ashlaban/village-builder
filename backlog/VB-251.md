## Implementation notes (11 Nov 2026)
- A kind declares `attention` (`full` by default), and for `background` a `reach` (steps) and a `rate` (share of the pawn's hours); documented in `src/sim/kinds.js`. No game kind declares one.
- `agent.side`: absent, or `{partial, background}` (jobs or null); absent when empty, so the golden state is unchanged (made lazy for that, instead of a field on every pawn). Held tasks list the pawn in `j.claims`.
- `src/sim/agents.js`: `sideWhy`, `offerWhy`, `hold`, `unhold`, `dropSides`, `reachAfter`, `stepSide`. The `work` verb holds a non-full job as a side task (checked: slot free; background within reach of the pawn and of its full task's site) and a full one as before, dropping a background task the new site takes out of reach (the urgency switch). `bid` offers a pawn that holds a background task only partial tasks within reach; `lookUp` never switches to a side task. A step drops a background task when the pawn leaves reach or the job leaves the board, and works it at `rate`; death and `freeHands` let go. Dropping removes the pawn from `j.claims`, so the task is open for others with its progress.
- `tests/attention.js` (in `npm test`): a test-only kind of each attention. Invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js`.
- Validation: `node tests/golden.js` matches; counters unchanged expected (no kind uses it).
