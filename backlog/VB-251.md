Goal: A pawn holds one task. The design lets it hold one partial and one background task besides (minding the herd while doing something that can be put down). Build the shape; herding (VB-136) is its first use.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A kind declares its attention: `full` (the default, every kind today), `partial` (left and returned to within the hour) or `background` (done at the side, at a rate the kind states). A pawn may hold one background task and one partial task at once, and never a background task beside a full one that takes it out of reach (the kind states its reach in steps).
- [ ] The matcher offers a pawn that holds a background task only partial tasks within reach; a background task is dropped, and reopened for others, when its holder leaves reach or takes full work by the urgency switch.
- [ ] A test with a test-only kind of each attention: the pair is held, the reach is kept, the drop reopens the task. No kind of the game changes attention here, so `tests/golden.json` is unchanged.
- [ ] `@state` line and invariants (at most one of each per pawn; a held task names a living pawn). `npm test`, `node tests/golden.js`.
Cost: none until a kind uses it; one more field per pawn.
