# VB-2001: Stooks and mown hay degrade a share a day after the frost, as lying loads do
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Risk)
Touches: sim/fields (fieldsFrost, stooksDecay); sim/meadows (meadowsFrost, hayDecay); sim/outlook (food, hay, frostDays); sim/lying (exports); sim/clock (CALENDAR)
Goal: Sheaves and hay lying out lose a share each game day, not all at the frost (Kim, 27 Nov)
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Stooks in the field (sim/fields fieldsFrost, fd.held) and mown hay on the meadow (sim/meadows meadowsFrost, m.held) lose LYING.frostLoss a game day once the frost has come, by sim/lying's lossOver, gone under LYING.quantum, in place of all at the frost; the crop still standing is lost at the frost as now.
- [x] The outlook's stooks and mown hay counted less what lossOver takes by the season's end.
- [x] Tests: a share a day of stooks and of mown hay; numbers before and after (founders and populous small, 20 years): sheaves and hay lost a year, hungry household-days.

Noted by VB-1910, 28 November 2026.

Measured (seed 7, small map, 20 years; a scratch probe: what the frost took of stooks and mown hay plus every 'lying' booking, a year; hungry household-days the sum of hungryDays):
founders: sheaves lost 178.3 -> 42.1 a year, hay 30.8 -> 10.8, hungry household-days 6 -> 0; populous: sheaves 2706.7 -> 808.0, hay 130.6 -> 103.6, hungry 872 -> 37.
Decisions: the loss is booked to the field's or farm's holder as a lying load's is (event lyingSpoilt, reason 'lying'), so one account of what lay out; tests/stores.js's food reconciliation leaves 'lying' out, as it moved no store.
The daily steps run from the frost's day to the year's end (frostSeen), as lying decay does; worn-out stooks take the field on to its next course and are told as the frost's; a new counter outDecay.
