# VB-163: Long-run viability tests in the verify run
Status: needs-review
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: tooling
Phase: implement
Design ref: Systems overview. External review of 2 November (`docs/review-external.md`).
Goal: The wrap-ups report long-run numbers once, by hand; nothing fails when a later change starves a village in year 30. A verify test runs several scenarios and seeds for decades and fails when a village collapses or growth leaves its band, so a change that breaks the society is caught by the change, not by the next review.
Questions: in `QUESTIONS.md`, "VB-162, VB-163 and VB-165: the external review's new tasks" (the bands: a floor against collapse, or the design's targets).
Acceptance criteria (draft, as proposed):
- [ ] `tests/viability.js` in `npm run verify`: populous small at seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 for 30 years, founders small at the same seeds for 50 years, heirs small at seeds 7 and 42 for 30 years.
- [ ] It fails when: a scenario's population ends below its start; any village that held 3 or more households at year 5 is empty at the end; deaths from hunger exceed 2% of all deaths after year 3; any household is hungry on more than 30% of its days in a year after year 3; the invariants of `tests/lib/invariants.js` fail at any year end.
- [ ] It prints, per scenario and seed, the population at years 1, 10, 20, 30 (and 50), births, marriages, deaths by cause, and the share of hungry household-days, whether it passes or fails (`--verbose`).
- [ ] The whole test takes under 60 s on its own; if it cannot, it runs the seeds in parallel like `tests/run.js`, or takes fewer seeds, and says which in the PR.
- [ ] The numbers at `HEAD` are recorded in this file when the task is done.
