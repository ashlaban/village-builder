# VB-163: Bands: reduced scenarios asserted in verify, full runs reported nightly
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: Balance loops and targets (Bands, Decided, Kim, 3 November). `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, sections 6.2 (calibration and bands) and 8 (Kim's decision 9); `docs/roadmap.md`, section 3.0 (this task's floors become the first asserted bands). External review of 2 November (`docs/review-external.md`).
Goal: The wrap-ups report long-run numbers once, by hand, and nothing fails when a later change starves a village in year 30. Kim decided (3 November, decision 9) that full simulations cost too much to assert: they run nightly and are reported, never failing. Reduced scenarios (a tiny map, a few years, toy setups) are asserted in `npm run verify` and fail outside their band. Build both on one table of bands, starting with the floors against collapse drafted here on 2 November. Later slices add their loops' bands to the same table: kin help in slice 1, the town's natural decrease in slice 9.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] One table of bands, `tests/lib/bands.js`, in target-v2's `Loop` shape (section 6.2) cut to what is needed now: `{id, probe, kind, report?: {lo, hi, afterYear, scenario, map, years, seeds}, assert?: {lo, hi, afterYear, setup, years, seeds}}`. `probe` names a function of a finished run, kept in the same file and fed by yearly samples taken during the run.
- [x] The first bands, the floors drafted on 2 November:
  - the population at the end at or above the start;
  - no village that held 3 or more households at year 5 is empty at the end;
  - hunger causes at most 2% of deaths after year 3;
  - no household is hungry on more than 30% of its days in any year after year 3;
  - `checkState` (`tests/lib/invariants.js`) passes at every year end.
- [x] Reduced scenarios, `tests/bands.js` in `npm run verify`:
  - proposed: populous on the tiny map (120 by 85) at seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 for 8 years, measured at about 11 s a seed (12 years took 17 s on 3 November), each seed in its own process;
  - at most 45 core-seconds in all, and verify's total time reported before and after;
  - each `assert` band checked; a failure names the band, the seed and the value;
  - if a floor fails on today's code at reduced scale, the PR reports it and proposes the band's reduced value rather than loosening it silently.
- [x] Full runs, `tools/bands.js`:
  - the runs drafted on 2 November: populous small at seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 for 30 years; founders small at the same seeds for 50; heirs small at seeds 7 and 42 for 30; in parallel, one per core;
  - each `report` band printed as in or out;
  - per run, the population at years 1, 10, 20, 30 (and 50), births, marriages, deaths by cause and the share of hungry household-days;
  - results appended to `docs/bench/bands.jsonl`; the tool never fails;
  - `tools/nightly.js` (VB-193) runs it for a commit not yet run; if VB-193 is not done, this task adds `tools/nightly.js` with this step alone.
- [x] The reduced and the full numbers at `HEAD` in this file.
- [x] `AGENTS.md`, Commands: `tests/bands.js` and `tools/bands.js`; a line in Working rules that a slice's wrap-up adds its loops' bands to `tests/lib/bands.js`.
- [x] When done: VB-187 (the gossip experiment, which runs beside the slices from slice 0 on) is set `ready`: it waited for this harness.
Cost: about 45 core-seconds in verify; the full runs an estimated 15 to 20 minutes on 4 cores, nightly.

- 10 Nov 2026, implementation (VB-163): `tests/lib/bands.js` holds the table (`BANDS`, one entry per floor and full run, e.g. `population/populous`; the populous entries carry the `assert`), the probes (`PROBES`), the reduced setups (`SETUPS`), the full runs (`FULL`) and `runScenario`, which advances a day at a time and samples each year end: living, live households per village, births, marriages and deaths by cause read from the record appended that year, each household's hungry days over its days present, and `checkState`. Deaths by hunger count the causes `hunger` and `starvation` (as `sim/notable` does). A household present fewer than 12 days of a year is not judged on it, and its share is capped at 1 (one made or dissolved during a day may be fed that day and not be counted present). `tests/bands.js` (verify) runs each seed in its own process, in parallel; `tools/bands.js` runs the full ones, one per core, and appends to `docs/bench/bands.jsonl`; `tools/nightly.js` calls it with `--if-new`. Day-at-a-time runs are not the same runs as `tools/sim.js`'s year-at-a-time ones (the clock's 0.1-hour steps round differently), so their numbers differ from `tools/sim.js` at the same seed.
