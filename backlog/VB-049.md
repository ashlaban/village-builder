Design ref: Goods and the farming year (Seed: a quarter of the expected harvest); Inheritance and expansion (A reserve of seed is never paid out). Kim, 2026-10-30: for now, pawns reserve seed for all fields.
Goal: `feedHousehold` (`src/sim/hunger.js`) eats all grain when food is short, seed included, and the `sow` job needs `seedPerField + 10` grain. A farm that runs dry then never sows again: at seed 7, Hagen (6 people) has hoed fields, 0 grain and hunger 1.0 for 7 years and more (review, 2026-10-30).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `seedReserve(st, hh)`: `ECON.seedPerField` for each field of the household's farm that is not yet sown this year (stage `stubble` or `plowed`); 0 for fields already sown, growing or reaped. Exported from `sim/hunger`.
- [x] `feedHousehold` shares out only grain above the reserve; the reserve is never eaten. A household whose grain is at or below its reserve is short, and its members' shortfall and hunger follow as today.
- [x] The `sow` job is posted when the household's grain covers the field's seed (`hh.grain >= ECON.seedPerField`, without the `+ 10`), and `complete` takes it from the reserve.
- [x] The household panel shows the seed set aside.
- [x] A test on constructed households: with grain 50 and 3 unsown fields (reserve 60), nothing is eaten and all 50 grain is still there after a day; the household then sows 2 fields; with grain 100, 40 is eaten.
- [x] A founders run over 50 years at seeds 7 and 42 reports per decade: population, hungry days, hunger deaths, fields sown (reported, not asserted). Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
