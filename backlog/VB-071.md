# VB-071: Jobs indexed by owner, so matching looks only at jobs a pawn may take
Status: done (2026-10-30, dd34cb5)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: tooling
Phase: implement
Design ref: Vision and scope (up to about 1,000 pawns); REVIEW.md, Performance.
Goal: `planRound` in `src/sim/jobs.js` bids every idle pawn against every open job: 818 pawns × 660 jobs is 127,000 bids per 6-minute step at 7:00, nearly all of them failing at `mayTake`. A pawn may only take jobs of its own household and village, about 5–15.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `st.jobs` keeps, besides the map by key, an index from owner (`household:<id>`, `village:<id>`) to its open jobs, updated wherever a job is posted or deleted (`post`, `refreshJobs`, `complete`, `deposit`, `extinct`). `@state` documented.
- [ ] `planRound` bids each idle pawn against its household's and its village's jobs only, in increasing job id, which is the order `st.jobs` iterates today, so the random draws in `bid` happen in the same order.
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged: `npm run verify` passes and `tests/golden.json` is unchanged.
- [ ] Report in the PR the ms per game day of populous medium at seed 7 over 10 days, before and after (`tools/sim.js` if VB-068 is done).
- Nightly 2026-10-30: `jobsBy` is kept out of the golden fingerprint (`DERIVED` in `tests/golden.js`) as it only indexes `st.jobs`; `golden.json` is unchanged. populous medium, seed 7, 10 days: 324 ms per game day before, 330 after (818 pawns), so matching was not the cost at this size; the index is still in place for 1,000 pawns.
