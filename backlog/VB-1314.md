# VB-1314: The holder's choice to hand on, weighed; holding out costs the heir's opinion
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Inheritance (Handover and shares: Timing); docs/plans/VB-1122.md, section 5
Goal: An unplayed holder hands on when it chooses, by a weighing of named parameters; a holder who holds out pays in its heir's opinion.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `HANDOVER` in `sim/rules` with the plan's first values (custom 0.8, from 55, years 0.05, weak 0.3, wish 0.3, opinion 0.4, keep 1.0, at 1, grudge 0.05 a year up to 0.3), each a tunable with its meaning; `sim/marriage`'s `handover` weighs them at the heir's wedding into the house and the holder's birthday, as VB-824 calls it, in place of "at the custom's time unless its opinion of the heir is ill".
- [ ] `farmstead.expected` = `{heir, since}`: set when the custom's time comes and the holder does not offer, cleared at the handover or when the first in line changes; its invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js`, plain data, saved.
- [ ] The heir's opinion of a holder holding out: −min(grudgeMax, grudge × years since `expected.since`), derived in the opinion (no memory stored, no daily pass); a played holder pays it too. A test on a constructed state: a holder at odds with its heir holds on at the wedding and hands on at 60; the grudge grows a year and ends at the handover.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (founders small 50 years seeds 7 and 42; populous small 30 years seeds 3, 7, 11, 42): marriages and handovers a decade by reason, holders of 60 and over still holding, the median years from the custom's time to the handover; written in the task file.
- [ ] Cost: one weighing a holder a year and one at a wedding; the opinion's read allocates nothing.

Noted by VB-1122's plan, 19 November 2026 (`docs/plans/VB-1122.md`).
