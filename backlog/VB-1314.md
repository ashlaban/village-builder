# VB-1314: The holder's choice to hand on, weighed; holding out costs the heir's opinion
Status: done (2026-11-19)
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Inheritance (Handover and shares: Timing); docs/plans/VB-1122.md, section 5
Goal: An unplayed holder hands on when it chooses, by a weighing of named parameters; a holder who holds out pays in its heir's opinion.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `HANDOVER` in `sim/rules` with the plan's first values (custom 0.8, from 55, years 0.05, weak 0.3, wish 0.3, opinion 0.4, keep 1.0, at 1, grudge 0.05 a year up to 0.3), each a tunable with its meaning; `sim/marriage`'s `handover` weighs them at the heir's wedding into the house and the holder's birthday, as VB-824 calls it, in place of "at the custom's time unless its opinion of the heir is ill".
- [x] `farmstead.expected` = `{heir, since}`: set when the custom's time comes and the holder does not offer, cleared at the handover or when the first in line changes; its invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js`, plain data, saved.
- [x] The heir's opinion of a holder holding out: −min(grudgeMax, grudge × years since `expected.since`), derived in the opinion (no memory stored, no daily pass); a played holder pays it too. A test on a constructed state: a holder at odds with its heir holds on at the wedding and hands on at 60; the grudge grows a year and ends at the handover.
- [x] Numbers before and after (founders small 50 years seeds 7 and 42; populous small 30 years seeds 3, 7, 11, 42): marriages and handovers a decade by reason, holders of 60 and over still holding, the median years from the custom's time to the handover; written in the task file.
- [x] Cost: one weighing a holder a year and one at a wedding; the opinion's read allocates nothing.

Noted by VB-1122's plan, 19 November 2026 (`docs/plans/VB-1122.md`).

Decisions (19 Nov 2026, the build):
- `expected` is kept on the household (`household.expected`), not the farmstead: a farm outlives its households, and a re-peopled farm would carry a stale grudge; cleared at an offer, a new first in line at a weighing, nobody grown in line, and a new head ('newHead'). A change of first in line between weighings is seen at the next (heirRanking is too dear for the opinion's read). Close call.
- The weighing holds under every custom; under 'death' the custom's time never comes (`due` 0), so an old holder may still hand on by years, weakness and opinion. Close call.
- `keepShare`: a ration a day for the holder and its spouse living there, over last calendar year's sheaves (ledger) and milk and butter (totals), at most 1; 0 with no year on record (a first year, a constructed state). `hand`: under AGES.hands[1]. `wish`: married in the house, or courting where neither holds a farm. The offer's reason: 'marriage' when the heir is married in the house, else 'retirement'.
- tests/handover.js's old case 3 (a holder of 62 at enmity holds out under 'age60') no longer holds: at 62 the weighing reaches 1.05 even at -1.
- Numbers (base -> after; populous small 30 y, seeds 3/7/11/42; handed on by decade): at the wedding 22/17/18 -> 12/18/13, 17/20/17 -> 8/15/8, 24/21/13 -> 11/8/7, 13/16/13 -> 7/9/10; at 60: 0 -> 2, 1, 1, 0; holders of 60+ still holding 8/3/6/9 -> 4/12/8/9; median years from the custom's time to the handover 0 in every run (the handovers in life are mostly at the wedding). Founders small 50 y seeds 7 and 42: one fewer handover at the wedding on seed 7 (2 -> 1), seed 42 the same. Living at the end 557/656/466/657 -> 586/681/494/677 (seed 42's after with the sim/away fix below). Row in docs/tuning.md (the keep holds most holders under 55 at the wedding).
