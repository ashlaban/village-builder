# VB-1553: Wells are dug: a household's at its house and a village's on its green, their hours by the depth; village.well becomes a tile
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-1552
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Water by the site); backlog/VB-1543.md
Goal: a household far from water can buy its time back by digging, and a village's well costs what its ground asks
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A well's hours and stone are by the depth of its tile (design.md, Goods and the farming year, Water by the site, Wells): `WELL.flat` 16 + `WELL.perMetre` 4 a metre, a load of stone and one more for every 2 m; one rule, read by the household's well and the village's. The plan shows the band's range of hours until water is struck, never the metres ahead.
- [ ] A household's well: a project at the house (long work, as a cart's building), on the house's tile, begun by the head when the fetching it saves in `WELL.payYears` (2 years) is more than its hours at the band's middle, with no project under way and the stone on its heap; the played head may begin it when it likes, through a verb that says why not (no well in this ground, one already, a project under way).
- [ ] The village's well: sim/green's well step takes its hours and stone from the depth of the green's tile in place of `BUILD.wellHours` and `BUILD.wellStone`, the green's tile is chosen where the water is shallowest among those it may take, and a green whose ground has no well is laid out without one.
- [ ] `village.well` becomes the well's tile (none where there is none); every reader moves (the page's sweep and curb, the village card, the portraits, `sim/paths`, `sim/layout`, `sim/byhand`); a save with the boolean loads. A finished well sets `w.fresh` to well on its tile and the farms it changes find their source again.
- [ ] Checks on a small constructed state: a well in 2 m of ground takes 24 hours and 2 loads, in 10 m 56 hours and 6; a household inland digs and its fetch falls to 0.1 hours a trip; a household beside a river does not dig; no well is begun on ground with none.
- [ ] Measured once, before and after (small, seeds 7 and 42; founders and populous; 15 years): wells dug by households and by villages, their hours by band, hours a household-day on water, dry household-days; whether households are better off.
- [ ] Cost, named before building: one project a household or a village at most; the head's test of whether a well pays on its planning day, a comparison of numbers the farm's source already holds (250 households x a seventh a day x no search).
