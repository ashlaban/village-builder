# VB-1553: Design: standing duties, a mechanism that does not delay work with a window
Status: blocked
Waits for: Kim
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: `design.md` section 7, Standing duties, The day's work, Types of work; `docs/review-agency.md`, section 4; `docs/review-jobs.md`, section 5.2 (the long-lived assignment)
Goal: The head's day has no memory: each morning `allot` (`src/sim/jobs.js`) weighs every member for every task afresh and breaks ties by a task's id, so nobody is the one who sees to the wood. Build design.md's row Standing duties: the types of work (`KINDS[kind].type`) of the tasks the morning's allotment gives a member within its hours are its duties, kept on the plan's day (`plan.day.duties`, written by `allot` only); at the next morning's allotment a pair whose type the member holds as a duty has its fit times `MATCH.keep` (1.25, a tunable of `sim/roles` beside `join`). A duty no task of the morning answers lapses; a much more urgent task still takes the member (keep is under `switchRatio`). The head's verb `duty` (a member, a type of work, set or lift) pins one: a set duty stands until lifted, the member leaves or the head changes, and with no choice made the rule runs (parity: the planner's rule and the played head pass the same check). A member's card names its duties.
