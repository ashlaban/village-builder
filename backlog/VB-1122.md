# VB-1122: Plan: the retired holder's life, and whether a holder may hand on by choice
Status: needs-review
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: plan
Design ref: Inheritance (Succession; Handover and shares); The player and what they see; backlog/VB-824.md; backlog/VB-1121.md
Goal: A retired pawn, played or not, has a life with decisions in it; and it is decided whether handing on in life is a holder's own act.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] What the retired (undantag) hold and may do, for every pawn (principle 16), grounded in the Nordic retirement contract (principle 5): the keep the contract names (food, a room, firewood, a cow's milk) as an obligation of the new holder, and what follows when it is not met; a voice with the heir (its opinion weighed in the head's plan, by its standing) and at the meeting under the custom of who speaks; causes argued; matches for grandchildren where the custom gives the old a say; gifts from what is its own; work it still does. Each is an existing verb opened to the retired or a new one, with its check.
- [ ] What the player sees and decides as a retired pawn, listed against the playtest's 14 years: at least one decision a year that matters, or the plan says why not and what the skip does instead.
- [ ] By Kim's answer to the question in `QUESTIONS.md`, "VB-1122": whether and when a holder hands on by its own choice, how that sits with the timing custom (When the heir takes over) and with VB-824's offer, and what the player's pawn may do.
- [ ] Cost named (Rules for cost, 9), and the implement tasks written with numbers and criteria and set ready; questions that move a Decided point go to `QUESTIONS.md`.

Notes / questions:
- 18 Nov 2026 (Kim, in the session on VB-1121): the player succeeds only at death; a pawn whose farm passes on in its lifetime is played on. Kim asked whether one should be able to pass the farm on before death at all.
- 16 Nov 2026 (playtest, founders small seed 7, 80 years in the page): Ingeborg Torstensdotter's farm passed to her son when he married in year 33, by the custom "when the heir marries"; she was "Retired (undantag)" for 14 years, her acts at the round all barred, with nothing to decide until she died at 74.
- As built: the farm passes in life by the timing custom alone (the heir's marriage, the holder at 60), at the round; the `retire` verb is reached only from the Debug panel. VB-824 (ready) makes the handover the holder's offer, which the heir answers, and lets a played holder offer at any time.
