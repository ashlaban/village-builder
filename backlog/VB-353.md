Goal: Two facility kinds a household or a village builds: a brick kiln fires clay into brick and roof tiles, a lime kiln burns limestone to lime; both eat wood. Slice 6's plan (VB-185, 11 Nov), from Kim's answers in the session.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Facility kinds `brickKiln` and `limeKiln`: 30 hours and 6 loads of stone each. `burnBrick`: 6 loads of clay and 8 of wood, 16 work hours, gives 4 loads of brick or of tiles (the owner's plan says which). `burnLime`: 6 loads of limestone (quarried, VB-332's heap by kind) and 10 of wood, 16 hours, gives 4 loads of lime. Skill pottery for brick, building for lime.
- [ ] `hh.brick`, `hh.tiles`, `hh.lime` through `sim/stores`; the worth table gains a load of brick or tiles at 10 rations and of lime at 14 (Proposed). A kiln is built only by a holder whose project needs its product (VB-354, VB-355), or to sell it at the fair.
- [ ] Numbers (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years): kilns built, loads burnt, wood eaten against the village's wood.
- [ ] The golden file is re-recorded where behaviour changes, its changed entries listed; `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
