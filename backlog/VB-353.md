# VB-353: The scenarios' crops: founders, heirs and populous
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: crops
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods and the farming year, Crops and rotations (design.md, section 8)
Goal: The scenarios' crops (Decided: a mix, by presets, a backup household; Agreed: rye and peas in founders).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Founders (`seedFounders`): the village's three farms bring barley and rye and start in three courses; the lone farm brings barley and peas and starts with barley and peas by turns; each brings a year's food in barley and seed for the fields it wants by its rotation. One farm of the village carries a field's seed of peas as reserved seed, not sown. With no lone farm, the third farm grows the peas.
- [x] Heirs: village A barley and rye in three courses, village B barley and peas. Populous: a seeded draw a village from the land within its reach (rye where forest is most of it, peas where meadow is, both from 12 farms, barley alone on heath and high ground; about a third barley alone): the shares are rules, and the draw is on the scenario's own stream.
- [x] Generated fields start at staggered steps with the crops their day of the year asks for (a rye field sown the autumn before stands green at the start).
- [x] Numbers (rations by crop not tabulated; see decisions) before and after at the bands' seeds: `node tests/bands.js`, and populous small at seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 for 15 years (rations by crop, hungry household-years, whether households are better off). `npm run verify`, its targets reported.
Cost: none in play.
Notes / questions:
- 11 Nov 2026: written by the plan VB-345. Kim read and accepted the plan the same day (`QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, VB-345): its numbers are `design.md`'s starting values, Agreed.

Decisions (VB-353):
- Founders: no fields at the start, so each farm carries its rotation by `village.rotation` and, for the third farm where no lone farm fits, `farm.firstRotation` (rotationOf reads it); the backup is `farm.keeps = ['peas']` (the village's last farm; the middle one with no lone farm), a field's seed held by seedNeed and reserveSeed, eaten only by the hunger rule. Each farm brings seed for ceil(wanted/courses) fields a crop.
- Populous draw (`CROP_DRAW`, one draw a village after the villages are laid, so the layout is unchanged): forest share of land within 8 steps >= 0.25 rye, meadow >= 0.3 peas, 12 farms both, a third barley alone. Tried forest 0.5, 0.35, 0.25 (small and medium, seeds 3, 11, 42, 92 villages): rye in 0, 3, 14. Result at 0.25: peas 37, barley alone 32, rye 14, both 9.
- Fix found: fieldsFrost left a lost crop's weeds on the field going fallow (invariant `weeds 2`); cleared there.
- Cost, populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, year 15 before -> after: living 439, 436, 433, 491 -> 448, 440, 455, 485; hungry-days summed over years 14, 18, 33, 30 -> 54, 67, 43, 49; mean grain a household about 20% lower (750-870 -> 580-740). Founders: five or six fields a farm (fallow on top) delay the meadows and hay (tests meadows, hay, notable, soil, newfields adjusted to year 2 and 4 years). Households better off: no, slightly hungrier; a tuning matter.
