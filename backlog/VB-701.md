# VB-701: Iron tools, a first version: a bloom from a pit, and a farm's tools wear
Status: done (2026-11-23)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (7, 9, 16); design.md, Goods, production and animals
Goal: A household with bog ore smelts it with wood in a pit into blooms; a farm's tools wear with field work and are mended with iron; worn tools slow the work.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A job kind at the farm: ore and firewood into a bloom (loads and hours as tunables); `iron` in the goods registry; a farm's tools a condition from 0 to 1 that field work wears and mending restores with iron and winter hours.
- [x] Worn tools slow ploughing, reaping and felling by a named factor, never bar them (a wooden spade still digs); the head's target for iron is its mending need (VB-698).
- [x] VB-364 (the bloomery as a facility, better yield) and VB-121 (winter crafts) build on this and say what they change.
- [x] Numbers before and after, populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years: villages with bog ore in reach, tools' mean condition by whether the village has iron, field hours a ration, hungry household-days. Parameterisation in at most five short runs.

Build the wear and the rate so that each can take a multiplier from the set's grade.
- 18 Nov 2026, amended to agree with VB-1123 (plan mode; Kim's decisions of 18 Nov): the stepping of targets leaves the game: no head, played or not, sets a target by hand, and the Plan tab's steppers go with the tab. "The head's target" in this task is the level the head reckons for itself, never one that is set. The level stays (Kim, 18 Nov): the player reckons it in their head, and a head not played reckons it as a margin it prefers over what survival needs, by its traits and skill, 1.5 times the baseline to start with. Nothing else in this task changes by it.
- 23 Nov 2026 (done, sim/ironwork): the tools are `hh.tools` on the household (as VB-121 and VB-762 read them), undefined for sound; a new household's set is sound. Worn: `TOOLS.kinds` plow, break, reap, fell, wood, timber (the plough, spade, sickle and axe; mowing left out), `TOOLS.wear` 0.001 an hour of work done (VB-121's 0.01 a 10 hours), worn in `sim/work` settle; the rate `1 - share + share × tools` in `manning` and the head's estimate. A village's own work (common fields) is not slowed.
- `TOOLS.share` 0.15, not VB-121's 0.3 (run 1 of 5): at 0.3 tools worn to nothing (most villages have no bog in reach) cut mean grain about a fifth and raised hunger (seed 11: 418 living at year 15 before, 326 after); 0.15 is the floor VB-121's plan gives a household without iron (85%), which VB-121 may raise back with its ceiling. Numbers: docs/tuning.md.
- Smelt: `IRON` 4 loads of bog ore and 6 of firewood a bloom, 10 hours, 2 hands, autumn and winter, at the farm (VB-364's numbers for the pit). Mending: winter, 4 hours, 0.1 bloom for 0.1, only with iron. Iron's target the mending need; a keeping good of the fair (worth 30) with a need term; `ASKS.cap` 3 to 4 (one a good it cannot make; asks.js counts a new ask before the open asks of later goods, so a cap below the goods' count could be passed).
- Left: iron gathers with the smelters (24 to 56 blooms held a seed at year 15) while tools elsewhere sit at 0.07 to 0.21: the fair and the asks carry little of it; a tuning row.
