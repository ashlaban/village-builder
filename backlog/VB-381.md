Goal: The lint holds a module's exports to 15 and a function's formatted length to 60 lines, each with a baseline that only falls (`tests/width-baseline.json`, updated with `node tests/lint.js --update`, a ratchet as the lint's), and no cap on a module's length. A function is a declaration, expression or arrow with a block body; a one-line arrow is not counted. Counted from the source (`export` declarations and the names of `export { ... }`).
