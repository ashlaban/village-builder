# VB-441: Active effort preference for paths and persistent wet ground
Status: done (14 Nov 2026)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Worn paths; World and scale
Goal: Prefer easier established routes without inventing daily rain or changing walking speeds.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Active effort preference changes route choices at equal movement speeds, grounded in cited research and history.
- [x] Paths reduce obstruction but never automatically drain wet ground; generated moisture used without inventing daily rain.
- [x] Walking speeds and physical reach budgets stand; numeric tests verify effort choices, detour limits, reach and cache invalidation.
- [x] Legacy route/hour caches rebuilt on load; current saves deterministic.
- [x] Before/after route and wear changes, household outcomes and cost reported without screenshots or tuning unrelated systems.
- [x] Focused tests, build and full verify run; residuals documented.

## Decision

Flag: the active choice policy increases route-search work in the measured runs; no speedup is claimed and no existing cost target
was raised. The pre-existing straight-route fallback (VB-329) occurs twice before and three times after in populous tiny seed 42,
two years; its repair remains with VB-329. The full measured effects and household outcomes are in `docs/path-effort-policy.md`.

User explicitly requested activating the effort model after the neutral scaffolding of VB-440. This authorizes route preference beyond
fastest time; Decided stage speed bonuses and base speed are preserved. No permission question is needed. Daily rain is not modelled:
st.weather is a yearly harvest-yield factor. Use the generator's moisture (regional rainfall and valley wetness), not this harvest factor.
All policy coefficients are named under PATHS.effort. Biome mappings and willingness to exchange time for effort are game approximations,
not medieval measurements; research and mapping are documented in docs/path-effort-policy.md.


## Implementation

- `PATHS.effort` is active by default. Surface factors are sourced from physiological/archaeological research; biome assignments,
  residual obstruction, wetness proxy and the time/effort exchange are explicitly labelled game approximations.
- `sim/routes` compiles active choice costs separately from movement, uses physical-time secondary ordering, and keeps straightness
  out of the admissible heuristic. Queries keep their physical budget with a measured fastest-route fallback when necessary.
- Cache invalidation covers preference as well as time; newly attractive off-route paths invalidate affected routes. No new daily
  terrain scan or per-pawn policy state. Foot traffic alone does not improve drainage or soft sand/marsh substrate.
- Save metadata marks the route policy; legacy cached routes and village hours rebuild on load. No new authoritative world/state fields.
- Added `routeBudgetFallback`, initial reference 0 on the counter fixture. Existing counter baselines and all target limits stand.
- No daily rain/saturation/drainage facility model exists. Generated moisture is used; the annual yield-weather factor is excluded.

## Validation and measurements

- `npm test`: 53/53 passed; `npm run verify`: 106/106 passed, 17 advisory targets out, zero hard failures. Subprocess tests run with
  the same environment override used in VB-440. Format, build freshness, ownership, lint rules and diff whitespace checks pass.
- Active preference tests isolate effort by equalising all stage speeds. A forest detour takes physical cost 16.5 instead of 15,
  reducing choice cost from 21.99 to 19.14. Physical duration is 82.5 rather than 75 game minutes at SPEED 12; the cost is not minutes.
  An excessive detour is rejected; wet-ground, no automatic drainage, fixed soft substrate, bridge footing, off-route cache updates,
  physical-budget fallback, new/legacy save loading and two-day deterministic save continuation all pass.
- Independent small-world Dijkstra checks the choice minimum and physical time through changes of terrain, wear, heightmap, slope
  rules, bridges and fords. `tests/villagehours.js` confirms the table agrees with actual chosen-route walking hours.
- `tools/path-effort-probe.js`: founders/populous, seeds 7/42, tiny, two years, against the VB-440 source snapshot. 14 of 246 sampled
  initial trips choose longer lower-choice-cost routes; all four final wear hashes change. Numeric probe repeated at seeding for
  founders/populous seed 7 yields identical outputs apart from timing. No screenshots were made or needed.
- End population before/after: 12/12, 11/11, 139/138, 146/146; hungry pawn-days: 41/42, 0/12, 52/60, 23/0. Individual household
  outcomes change in 5, 5, 26 and 28 compared households. No claim of uniformly improved households or long-term balance.
- Route searches before/after: 1,002/1,022; 918/912; 11,613/12,386; 9,309/9,529. Heap removals: 17,293/22,752; 20,570/22,576;
  307,151/377,800; 302,973/340,722. Cache entries checked: 5,304/4,555; 5,503/5,240; 134,941/117,329; 120,714/107,971.
  Search work increases about 10–32%; cache-refresh work decreases about 5–14%. There is no general performance win claimed.
- The 17 advisory target residuals remain reported, not hidden by baseline updates. Policy parameters await a user-started tuning run;
  no speed, agricultural yield, social reach or other balancing number was changed to compensate.
