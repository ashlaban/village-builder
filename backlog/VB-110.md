# VB-110: Worn paths speed walking, and routes prefer them
Status: done (2026-10-30)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: Land and ecology (Worn paths: faster walking, sharing paths; Decided, 30 Oct). Kim, 30 Oct 2026 (merging main): paths are drawn first (VB-094), the speed-up comes in this follow-up.
Goal: VB-094 counts crossings and draws trodden ground, paths and lanes, but walking ignores them. The design wants paths to speed walking, so routes merge onto them and the merged path wears deeper.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Walking is 5% faster on trodden ground, 15% on a path and 30% on a lane than on the bare terrain, multiplying the terrain speed of VB-096.
- [x] The route search counts the faster walking in its step costs, so a pawn takes a path when it is quicker even if it is longer. Cached routes are dropped when a tile changes stage, at most once a game day for each tile.
- [x] A test: two farms with a lane between them that bends around a meadow; a pawn takes the lane when it is quicker than the straight way, and the straight way when it is not.
- [x] Founders at seed 7 after 20 years and populous medium at seed 7 after 5: tiles per stage and mean walk time to fields, well and woodlot, against VB-094's numbers (reported); the cost of route search per game day, before and after.
- [x] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
