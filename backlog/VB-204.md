# VB-204: Food ledgers: routine movements folded into yearly totals
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (code structure). `docs/roadmap.md`, sections 5.2 (save and load under 0.5 s) and 5.3 (rule 9, bound history: routine events fold into yearly counts); VB-174 (`sim/stores`), `docs/review-vb174.md`.
Goal: VB-174 made `sim/stores` write a ledger entry for every food movement and keep it three years. By its own measure, at year 8 of populous small:
- entries kept per occupied household-year went from 27 to 227;
- the save went from 8.7 to 13.0 MB of JSON, and from 1.67 to 2.22 MB gzipped.
Save is the budget line furthest over (6.9 s against 0.5 s at year 60), and VB-199 is meant to bound history.

Counted on 7 November (populous small, seed 7, at year 4: 87 occupied households, 15 villages), 63,640 entries were kept, 4.3 MB of JSON. 95% of them are routine:
- spoilage 21% (every household and village, every day);
- meals 36% (rations and grain, almost every day);
- cooking 25%;
- milk and butter 10%;
- threshing 2.4%.
The other 5% are gifts, seed, harvests, meat, shares and provisions.

Who reads the ledgers:
- `tools/balance.js`: harvest, gift, meat, milk and butter by kind over a year;
- the herd summary in `sim/herds`: milk and butter this year;
- tests: `tests/stores.js` reconciles each holder's year, and `help`, `milk`, `regress` and `weeding` read entries.

Fold the routine kinds into yearly totals and keep the others in full.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Routine kinds (`meal`, `cooking`, `spoilage`, `thresh`, `milk`, `butter`) add to a yearly total per holder, kind and good (an amount and a count), instead of writing an entry each. Other kinds stay entries, as today. The totals are kept for the same three years as the entries and pruned with them. `sim/stores` owns them (`src/owners.json`), with an `@state` line and invariants in `checkState`.
- [ ] `tests/stores.js` still reconciles every holder's year: the change in its quantities equals its entries plus its totals.
- [ ] `tools/balance.js --json` (populous, seeds 7 and 42, small, 8 years) and the herd summary give the same numbers before and after.
- [ ] Entries kept per occupied household-year, and the save's MB (JSON and gzipped), before and after: at year 8 of populous small (VB-174's measure), and on `medium-ref` if VB-202 is done.
- [ ] The prune at the new year walks live households and villages only (rule 1). A dissolved household's ledger is emptied once, when it dissolves; if that is not possible, say why.
- [ ] Golden file re-recorded for the ledger lines only, with the changed entries listed. `npm run verify` passes.
Cost: one addition per routine movement, into a total found by kind, with no allocation per movement. About a dozen totals per holder-year instead of about 200 entries.
