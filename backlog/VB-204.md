# VB-204: Food ledgers: routine movements folded into yearly totals
Status: done (2026-11-09)
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (code structure). `docs/roadmap.md`, sections 5.2 (save and load under 0.5 s) and 5.3 (rule 9, bound history: routine events fold into yearly counts); VB-174 (`sim/stores`), `docs/review-vb174.md`.
Goal: VB-174 made `sim/stores` write a ledger entry for every food movement and keep it three years. By its own measure, at year 8 of populous small:
- entries kept per occupied household-year went from 27 to 227;
- the save went from 8.7 to 13.0 MB of JSON, and from 1.67 to 2.22 MB gzipped.
Save is the budget line furthest over (6.9 s against 0.5 s at year 60), and VB-199 is meant to bound history.

Counted on 7 November (populous small, seed 7, at year 4: 87 occupied households, 15 villages), 63,640 entries were kept, 4.3 MB of JSON. 95% of them are routine:
- spoilage 21% (every household and village, every day);
- meals 36% (rations and grain, almost every day);
- cooking 25%;
- milk and butter 10%;
- threshing 2.4%.
The other 5% are gifts, seed, harvests, meat, shares and provisions.

Who reads the ledgers:
- `tools/balance.js`: harvest, gift, meat, milk and butter by kind over a year;
- the herd summary in `sim/herds`: milk and butter this year;
- tests: `tests/stores.js` reconciles each holder's year, and `help`, `milk`, `regress` and `weeding` read entries.

Fold the routine kinds into yearly totals and keep the others in full.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Routine kinds (`meal`, `cooking`, `spoilage`, `thresh`, `milk`, `butter`) add to a yearly total per holder, kind and good (an amount and a count), instead of writing an entry each. Other kinds stay entries, as today. The totals are kept for the same three years as the entries and pruned with them. `sim/stores` owns them (`src/owners.json`), with an `@state` line and invariants in `checkState`.
- [x] `tests/stores.js` still reconciles every holder's year: the change in its quantities equals its entries plus its totals.
- [x] `tools/balance.js --json` (populous, seeds 7 and 42, small, 8 years) and the herd summary give the same numbers before and after.
- [x] Entries kept per occupied household-year, and the save's MB (JSON and gzipped), before and after: at year 8 of populous small (VB-174's measure), and on `medium-ref` if VB-202 is done.
- [x] The prune at the new year walks live households and villages only (rule 1). A dissolved household's ledger is emptied once, when it dissolves; if that is not possible, say why.
- [x] Golden file re-recorded for the ledger lines only, with the changed entries listed. `npm run verify` passes.
Cost: one addition per routine movement, into a total found by kind, with no allocation per movement. About a dozen totals per holder-year instead of about 200 entries.
Notes:
- 7 Nov 2026 (review of the work since 3 November): written. Slice 1 (VB-182) books gifts and goodwill; gifts stay entries.
- 9 Nov 2026, implementation (in the session with Kim):
  - `sim/stores`: `ROUTINE` kinds (`meal`, `cooking`, `spoilage`, `thresh`, `milk`, `butter`) of a household or village add to `holder.totals`, `{year, kind, good, n, count}`: the calendar year of the hour the clock is working through (`yearOf`, as `cal(hour).year`, so midnight's work at the year's turn belongs to the new year). The search runs back over this year's totals only; a total is allocated once per holder, year, kind and good. A pawn's stake movement of a routine kind would stay an entry (none exist today).
  - `prune` walks `st.liveHouseholds` and the villages, and drops entries and totals older than the last three years. `close` empties a household's ledger and totals when it dissolves (`sim/heirs`, `extinct`): nothing can reach a dissolved holder afterwards, since `type` refuses it.
  - `totals` is owned by `sim/stores` (`src/owners.json`), with `@state` lines and invariants in `checkState` (routine kinds only, one per year, kind and good, in year order, within the last three years; a dissolved household holds none). Older saves load with empty totals and keep their routine entries until they are pruned; their dissolved households' ledgers are emptied on load (`tests/fixtures.js` found them in the published fixtures).
  - Readers: the herd summary (`sim/herds`) and `tools/balance.js` (`Stores.yearSum`) read milk and butter from the totals; `tests/milk.js` counts milking days from a total's count.
  - `tests/stores.js` reconciles every holder from the seed to noon of each of three new years' first days (away from midnight at the year's turn): its change in grain, rations and sheaves equals its entries plus its totals. Before, it reconciled each year from a snapshot at the year's turn, which a yearly total cannot split.
