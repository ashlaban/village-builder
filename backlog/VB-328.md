# VB-328: Timber: a felled plot yields its wood, building fells trees, and a new household's first wood comes from somewhere
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-326
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Clearing a new plot); Land, ecology and rights (Land and ecology: Timber). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Consistency review (10 Nov)
Goal: Wood appears and vanishes at three places. Felling a plot's tile sets its 60 to 80 trees to 0 and yields nothing (`finishStep`, `src/sim/expansion.js:158`), though the design says the felling yields firewood and timber and the cottage uses the timber (Clearing a new plot, Agreed 30 Oct). A cottage or a booth on open ground is built of no timber at all. And every new household starts with 16 loads of firewood from nowhere (`ECON.startWood`, `makeHousehold`, `src/sim/records.js:154`), settlers at a bare booth included.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A felled plot tile: its standing trees become the plot's wood, `plot.wood` in loads (3 a tree, `woodPerTrip`). The cottage's walls take `BUILD.timber` loads of it (18: six trees); what is left goes, when the plot is given, to the woodpile of the new household up to its winter goal (40) and then to the households that worked the plot, by hours, each up to its winter goal; the rest is burnt where it lies (the log says how much).
- [ ] A cottage or booth with no felled tile of its own: before the walls step the village (a plot) or the household (a booth) fells `BUILD.timber` / 3 trees in the village's wood, as firewood trips whose load goes to the site (a job kind `timber`, type wood, 3 hours and the carry, on VB-326's drawn tile); the walls step waits for them. No fence, well or green takes timber yet.
- [ ] A new household's first wood is taken from the piles of the households its members leave, up to `ECON.startWood` (16) in all and never leaving a pile under `woodLow` (8); a cottage's household counts its plot's wood first. Settlers carry none (their loads are food, VB-287) and cut on arrival: with VB-327 their first winter needs 7 trips. The scenarios' first households keep their 16.
- [ ] Stores: wood moves through an operation with a reason (`plot`, `timber`, `start`), as food does through `src/sim/stores.js`, so the ledger shows where a pile came from.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years): plots finished and how long they take, cottages built, trees felled for timber, the first winter of new households and of settlers (wood in store by day, cold days with VB-327). The golden file is re-recorded and its changed entries listed.
