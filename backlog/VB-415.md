Goal: The exports half of VB-381, landed before the move to TypeScript since counting exports needs no formatter, and with VB-414's mechanism in place of a ratchet that only falls: a wide module may still gain an export, but only with an open task that splits it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `tests/lint.js` counts each module's exports on its loaded namespace (constants included, as VB-381 counts them). A module over 15, and over its count in `tests/width-baseline.json`, fails unless an open task titled "Refactor: ..." names its key (`splitTask`, VB-414); with one it prints a line and passes.
- [x] `tests/width-baseline.json` holds the modules over 15 at today's counts; `node tests/lint.js --update` lowers it and drops a module that is back at 15. A new module starts at 15.
- [x] `sim`, the facade, is not counted. `sim/rules` is counted like the rest.
- [x] `AGENTS.md`, `docs/gates.md` and `docs/architecture/modules.md` say so; VB-381 and question 4 of "VB-055: the move to TypeScript" in `QUESTIONS.md` get a note.
