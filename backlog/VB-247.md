# VB-247: A job names its farm, field and meadow by id
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: none (code structure). `AGENTS.md`, Conventions (ids are array indexes; `st` is plain data).
Goal: Every other record of `st` names another by its id, but a job holds objects: `job.farm`, `job.field`, `job.meadow`, `job.stretch`, and an `owner` object shared between jobs and fences; `st.idlers` holds agent objects. These are the shared objects the save codec's first pass exists to find (2,932 references in a two-year save).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A job holds `farm`, `field`, `meadow` and `plot` as ids (`field` and `meadow` as the id within their farm, beside the farm's id) and `stretch` as its fence's place (`{village | farm, index}`); its `owner` is two fields, `ownerType` and `ownerId`, as a fence's is. A helper in `sim/agents`, where jobs are owned, gives the record for a job (`farmOf(st, j)`, `fieldOf(st, j)`, and so on), and every reader goes through it. `st.idlers.by` holds agent ids. `st.cenv.people` is the one shared object left (it is `st.pawns`): either `sim/society` reads `st.pawns` and the field goes, or it is rebuilt on load (VB-239's `DERIVED`).
- [ ] After this, a save of populous, seed 7, small map, 2 years holds no `$ref` and no `$id`; a test holds that for the tiny map too. `encode`'s first pass, `$id`, `$ref` and `$arr` go from `sim/saves.js`, and `encode` fails, naming the path, on an object reached twice; `tests/codec.js` follows. `FORMAT` rises by one (the fixtures are remade by the nightly workflow; `node tools/fixtures.js make` for the task's own checks).
- [ ] `tools/writes.js`: the `ALIAS` entries for `job.field`, `job.meadow`, `job.farm`, `job.plot` and `job.stretch` go or follow the helpers, and the ownership check still places every write through them (no new unresolved write, VB-243).
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged, though not a "Refactor:" by the rules of `BACKLOG.md`, since the golden file moves: `tests/golden.json` changes only if the canonical text of a job changes its hash (it will: ids for back-references); list the entries and show that no number of the simulation moved (living, households, grain; populous and founders, small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years).
- [ ] Cost: `node tools/bench.js` before and after, since the planner reads a job's field and farm in its bids ("jobs refresh and matching", 37.6 ms a day at `medium-ref`): no higher beyond the runs' spread; save and load no slower. The PR says what fell (lines of the codec, the save's size).
- [ ] `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals.
