# VB-611: Teleport: a load lies where its carrier fell
Status: done (2026-11-16)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: physical
Phase: implement
Design ref: VB-581
Goal: A load whose carrier dies or whose household dissolves lies where it was and is fetched, not booked at the barn from the road
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A load whose carrier dies on the road (`stillAtDeath`), or whose household dissolves (`stopWorkFor`), lies where the carrier fell or stood, as a lying load with its good, amount and tile (and owner), and the haul's or hay's next hand fetches it from that tile and carries it home; it is not booked at the barn from the road.
- [x] The lying load has its invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js`, its place in the saved world (`WORLD` in `src/sim/saves.js`), and a line in `tools/stocks.js`. (16 Nov: in the saved state, `st.lying`, not `WORLD`: `WORLD` holds the per-tile arrays, encoded apart from the state, and a lying load names its field or meadow, whose sharing only the state's encoding keeps; see decisions.)
- [x] A test on a constructed state: a hand dies mid-haul; the load is not in the barn until another hand has carried it there.
- [x] Numbers before and after at populous small, seeds 7 and 42, and founders small, seed 7: grain, hungry household-days.

## Implementation notes (VB-611, 16 Nov 2026)
- New module `sim/lying` (material layer; owner of `st.lying`, `st.lyingSeq`, and `job.left` of its fetch tasks). `release` in `sim/agents`
  (reached by `stillAtDeath` and `stopWorkFor`) puts a load on its way home down where the hand is (`placeOf`, the tile under it): in its
  own field or meadow back with the stooks or the mown hay, as VB-581 built; anywhere else as a lying load `{id, kind, n, tile, x, y, t,
  owner, of}` (`lay`), `of` holding what the kind's delivery reads of its task (field or meadow, farm, village, common, dest, step, from, and
  `src`, the task's tile). The old `deposit(st, a, a.job.farm)` from the road is gone, and `deposit` lost its `farm` argument. A task with no
  more loads to give (`again` false: the field's last load, firewood, the well's stone) is taken down there, as the delivery took it down.
- Fetching: one kind for each kind that carries a load (`carry` and `load` or `stack`): `fetchHaul`, `fetchHay`, `fetchWood`,
  `fetchWellstone` (`Lying.fetchKind`, registered in `sim/kinds`). Site: the tile the load lies on; 1 hand; `LIFT` 0.5 h to take the load up;
  its load is what lies, up to the cart or back the fetcher has (`take`; the task stays open while some is left, `job.left`); carried home
  as the kind carries (its `to`, `carry`, `back`) and delivered by the kind's own `deliver` with the task's tile as it was (a felled tree's).
  Posted by one rule (`Lying.fetchWork`) at the owner's review, in `ASK.farmAfterPlans` (a household's loads) and `ASK.village` (a common
  field's sheaves, the well's stone); a role ranks it as the kind it fetches for (`r.except[K.fetch]`, so a child fetches sheaves at 4).
- Expiry: the autumn frost (a `CALENDAR` entry beside the meadows' frost, owner `sim/lying`) rots every load still lying, as it rots the
  stooks and the mown hay, and tells it (`frost` event, at the household or village that owned it). So the list holds at most a year's loads
  put down on the road, and an entry leaves it with its last load taken. No new loop over a growing collection: the rule walks the list
  only when it is present (absent when empty), and it is tiny (0 in all six-year runs below).
- Older saves: no `st.lying` is no load lying (absent until the first); tested in `tests/setdown.js`.
- Invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): absent or a non-empty list; ids distinct, rising, within `lyingSeq`; times in order and within the
  last year; a kind that has a fetch kind; a load above 0; a tile of the map; an owner that exists; a task tile.
- `tools/stocks.js`: a line `lying` (tile), with what happens at none. `docs/architecture/modules.md`: the module.
- Tests: `tests/setdown.js` (case 3 rewritten: a hand of the household killed by `killPawn` mid-haul on the road; the load lies at the tile it
  fell on with its amount, kind and owner; the household's rule posts `fetchHaul` there; another hand walks there, takes it up and carries
  it home, stepped by the simulation; the barn rises by the load only as it arrives, and the list is empty; invariants hold. Case 4: the
  household dissolving (`stopWorkFor`): the load lies; saved and loaded with its field shared; an older save without the field loads;
  case 5: the frost rots it and tells it). `tests/service.js` (a dissolving household's loads lie, not booked), `tests/children.js` and
  `tests/kinds.js` (the fetch kinds' rank).

## Decisions (Deciding without Kim)
- A kind of its own for fetching, not the haul's or hay's task: a load lying on the road is not in the field, and the haul's task stands on
  the field's tile (its site, its spot, `setDown` and the side tasks' reach read it). One generic fetch kind a carrying kind covers haul and
  hay as the criterion asks, and firewood and the well's stone as well, which the same `release` booked from the road. Close call.
- Saved in the state, not in `WORLD`: `WORLD` is the per-tile arrays, encoded apart; the load names its field or meadow (object sharing,
  kept by the state's encoding). The criterion's point, that it is saved, is met. Close call.
- Expiry by the autumn frost, the process that already takes stooks and mown hay still out: no new parameter, and a bound on the list.
- A load owned by a household that dissolves (its own people's loads when it dies out, a helper's load for it) is fetched by nobody and
  rots at the frost: its owner is gone, and nothing passes a gone household's goods on (its barn is closed too, `Stores.close`). Before,
  the load was booked at the barn just before the barn was closed, so the grain was lost then too. Close call; principle candidate noted.
- `LIFT` 0.5 h to take up a load already bundled: a named guess, under the haul's 0.7 h of loading from the stooks. Not tuned: no load was
  laid in the measured runs.
- The cart a dying carrier had is still freed at its farm at once (`Carts.leave`), and the ox team with it: a teleport left, noted as a task.
