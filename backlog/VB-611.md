# VB-611: Teleport: a load lies where its carrier fell
Status: done (2026-11-16)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: physical
Phase: implement
Design ref: VB-581
Goal: A load whose carrier dies or whose household dissolves lies where it was and is fetched, not booked at the barn from the road
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A load whose carrier dies on the road (`stillAtDeath`), or whose household dissolves (`stopWorkFor`), lies where the carrier fell or stood, as a lying load with its good, amount and tile (and owner), and the haul's or hay's next hand fetches it from that tile and carries it home; it is not booked at the barn from the road.
- [ ] The lying load has its invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js`, its place in the saved world (`WORLD` in `src/sim/saves.js`), and a line in `tools/stocks.js`.
- [ ] A test on a constructed state: a hand dies mid-haul; the load is not in the barn until another hand has carried it there.
- [ ] Numbers before and after at populous small, seeds 7 and 42, and founders small, seed 7: grain, hungry household-days.
