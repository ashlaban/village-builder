# VB-317: Tenancies over time: handover, end, letting again, marrying onto one
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-316
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Land, ecology and rights, Land and rights (Tenancy); Inheritance, Empty farms; Marriage (residence "with the land")
Goal: A tenancy made by foreclosure must live on: through the tenant's handover, its end when the tenant household dies out or leaves, and a new tenant after it. A vacant tenancy is a home, so it lets a landless couple marry, as the cities review's *landbor* did.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] At the tenant household's handover, the tenancy passes with the household if the owner's holder agrees: it does unless its opinion of the heir is below 0. Refused, the tenancy ends at the next moving round.
- [ ] A tenancy ends when the tenant household dissolves or leaves at the moving round, or is refused at a handover. The owner's household then works the land itself if its hands allow (`FIELDS.perHand`), or lets it again: a farm to a landless couple or adult of its village, in the order the Claims on empty farms custom gives (birthright first under "kin first"), as an empty farm is claimed (`src/sim/farmclaims.js`); a field alone to a household of the village with hands to spare.
- [ ] Under "with the land", the moving round's matcher counts a vacant tenancy as a home for a landless couple (`homeFor`, `src/sim/matching.js`), with the same means test as a farm.
- [ ] Events: a tenancy passed on, ended, let; the household panel's lines follow in VB-321.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous and founders small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 30 years): tenancies made, passed on, ended, let again; marriages onto a tenancy; landless adults over 25 and the never-married share at 45. Golden and counters re-recorded, entries listed.
Cost: work at a handover and at the round, per tenancy, on their own steps; nothing daily.
Order: after VB-316. Runs one at a time with VB-277 (the moving round a village a step) and VB-288 (distance in matching), which edit the same files.
