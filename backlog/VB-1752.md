Goal: Every body's plan is one record of steps, and every task can say which step it serves, up the chain
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The plan record (3.4's `Plan` and `PlanStep`; owner sim/plans; plain data, saved, `@state`; an invariant that every step's `why` resolves and every `who` is within the planner's authority): one for every live household and village, and for a pawn once it holds a step; the order of steps is saved behaviour; a season or year step carries `left` (lo, mid, hi: the hours still to do by the planner's estimate), which the labour budget (VB-1778) sets against the levies.
- [ ] The household's season targets (sim/seasonal) and its projects' step lists (sim/projects) are steps of its plan, written by the default author (`author: 'custom'`); the code that read the old parts reads the steps; the old parts go; `FORMAT` raised (sim/saves): a save of an older build is refused, as `docs/architecture/modules.md` (Saves) says; no migration is written.
- [ ] A posted task names the step that opened it (an id on the job), at the body's review or by the due-queue; `Sim.why(st, task)` gives the chain of steps upward and `Sim.plans(st, body)` the record, both through the actor view; the card's reason (Reasons shown) reads the chain.
- [ ] Cost: season plans 200 households × 4 a year × about 30 rows (about 500 a day at the late reference); a step is read by id; done steps leave the live lists and a season's are folded at its end.
- [ ] If the steps re-represent today's targets and projects with no change of behaviour, `node tools/same.js --base REF` differs only in the plan record's leaves (listed) and the gate is not run; each change of behaviour is named, and then the gate applies.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
