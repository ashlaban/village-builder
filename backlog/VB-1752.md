Goal: Every body's plan is one record of steps, and every task can say which step it serves, up the chain
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The plan record (3.4's `Plan` and `PlanStep`; owner sim/plans; plain data, saved, `@state`; an invariant that every step's `why` resolves and every `who` is within the planner's authority): one for every live household and village, and for a pawn once it holds a step; the order of steps is saved behaviour.
- [ ] The household's season targets (sim/seasonal) and its projects' step lists (sim/projects) are steps of its plan, written by the default author (`author: 'custom'`); the code that read the old parts reads the steps; the old parts go, with a save migration (`tests/saves.js`).
- [ ] A posted task names the step that opened it (an id on the job), at the body's review or by the due-queue; `Sim.why(st, task)` gives the chain of steps upward and `Sim.plans(st, body)` the record, both through the actor view; the card's reason (Reasons shown) reads the chain.
- [ ] Cost: season plans 200 households × 4 a year × about 30 rows (about 500 a day at the late reference); a step is read by id; done steps leave the live lists and a season's are folded at its end.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10): `npm run verify` green, a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose (never skipped or quarantined) and named in the PR; no stuck pawn (no task held 48 hours without progress, as VB-1546's script counts); the stocks' accounts close (`node tools/sim.js --report accounts`: `unexplained` no larger than before).
- [ ] No village dies out: `node tools/sim.js --scenario founders|populous --map small --seed 7|11|42 --years 10`, all six runs: no settlement extinct by hunger and no collapse of its people (the living at year 10 against year 0, in the PR).
- [ ] Reported in the PR, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in those six runs, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed), before and after.
