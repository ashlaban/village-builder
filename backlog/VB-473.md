Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Design ref: docs/review-blocks.md; design.md section 7 (A leg, and its end, to The timetable); docs/mocks/blocks/README.md
Goal: A step of the simulation touches only the pawns whose leg is due.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A walk leg holds its route's points with the hours to each; its end is its due step; a cut treads the tiles entered so far and leaves the pawn where its hours reach. Wear over a day is as today's at populous small, seed 7 (the sum of `w.wear`, and the worn tiles, within a hundredth).
- [x] A wait (`idle`) is a leg due when the pause ends; the start of the working day is one event for those waiting for it.
- [x] Journeys between villages (`st.journeys`) use the same queue; `st.awake`, `wake`, `sleep` and the loop of `stepAgents` are gone.
- [x] Every reader of a place outside `sim/agents` (`sim/relations`, `sim/society`, `sim/jobs`, `sim/lifecycle`) reads `placeOf`; `agent.x` and `agent.y` are written only at a leg's start and end. Talks a pawn-day and ties formed, before and after, within a twentieth.

- [x] The queue is bounded by the living; invariants; saves.
- [x] Numbers before and after at populous small, seeds 7 and 42, and founders small, seed 7: living, hungry household-days, grain, the hours a household-year by kind, the idle share; `--report perf` and the counters early and on a late save.

## Implementation notes (agent run, 16 Nov 2026)
- `sim/agents`, a section "legs": every living pawn is in one leg (`agent.leg`): a walk `{kind 'walk', t, x, y, seg, pace, h, end, due}`
  (h: the hours from its start to each point of `a.path` past `seg`, at the pace fixed as it begins; `end` the step those hours reach,
  whole steps; `due` its end, or on a journey the night's stop), a wait `{kind 'wait', t, due}` (a free pawn until its pause ends, in
  the working hours; or a traveller resting on the road until 6:00), or work `{kind 'work', t, x, y, seg 0}` (its end is its task's, in
  `st.workDue`); none (null) for a free pawn waiting for the working day, and for the dead. `begin`, `waitLeg`, `roadWait` set a leg and
  queue it; `unleg` takes it out; `halt` cuts a walk where its hours reach (`along`, by halving), treading the tiles entered so far
  (`treadTo`), and `stopAt` writes the pawn's place, facing and stride: a.x and a.y are written only at a leg's start and end (and by
  `placeAtHome`, `placeByDoor` for one standing at home).
- `st.legDue`: the pawns whose walk or wait is under way, by due then id, each once (binary search, splice); bounded by the living (an
  invariant); plain data, saved; built from the pawns' state when absent (`legsOf`: a fresh run, an older save). `stepAgents` takes the
  tasks due (`dueWork`), then the legs due: a walk arrives (`walkEnd`, `arrived`) or stops for the night, a rest on the road sets out on
  the next leg, a wait ends and its pawn, if it wants work, is offered to the planner. The working day's start (WORK[0]) is one event:
  `dayStart` walks the living once a day for the free pawns with no leg. After the planner, `rest` gives those it left free a wait to the
  next step, as the stepping polled them. `st.awake`, `wake`, `sleep`, `anchor`, `moveAgent`, `stepSide` and the loop are gone.
- Every path that stops or changes what a pawn does updates its leg at once: `walkTo` cuts the walk in hand; `release` waits a freed
  pawn, or re-paces a walk that goes on (`repace`: a load put down); `freeHands` cuts a hand on its way and waits it; `stopWorkFor`,
  `haltForTalk`, `idle`, `moved`, `placeBorn`, `setOut`, `startLeg`, `retarget`, `delivered`, `endJourney`, `stillAtDeath` (a death cuts
  the walk where it had come to and unlegs the pawn); `standStill` (exported; tests that place pawns by hand use it).
- `st.stepAt` {moving, walked} replaces `st.awake.moving` (non-enumerable, not saved): `workTick` (work and places reckoned to the step
  before until the step's legs begin), `walkTick` (a walk begun before or while the legs are taken moves this step), `pollTick`. Between
  steps, and in a fresh or loaded run, both are the tick.
- Journeys: `st.journeys` keeps its records in the order they set out, with no `due`; their legs are their pawns' (a walk that stops at
  the last step before WORK[1], a wait on the road until 6:00). `dueJourneys`, `stopJourneys`, `queueJourney` and their two calendar
  entries are gone.
- A background task (herding) is in `st.workDue` like a task under work and ends there (`sideEnded`); its rate (`minding`) is constant over
  its holder's leg (standing free; at other work within reach; a walk that begins and ends within reach), and `sideCheck` looks at reach as
  the holder's leg begins and ends; `dropJob` lets its holders go at once; the end of the working day lets every holder go (sim/clock).
- Readers of a place: `sim/society` `outdoors(st, placeOf)` (the clock passes `placeOf`, so society makes no upward call) fills each
  outdoors pawn's place, which `sim/relations` reads for cells and nearness; `sim/jobs` company (`placeOf(mate)`); `sim/lifecycle` a death
  on the road. The page reads `Sim.placeOf` and `Sim.poseOf` (read only; see decisions); `tests/browser.js` and `tests/browser-speed.js`
  compare drawn places with `placeOf`.
- Counters: `walkStep` is now the legs a step takes from the queue, `walkAwake` the living looked at by the day's start, `walkPoint` the
  points trodden at a walk's end or cut; new `walkPlace` (places worked out along a walk). `tests/counters.json`: only these three
  re-recorded; every other counter left at the run branch's baseline (the probe's values are the base's to three digits).
- Tests: `tests/legs.js` rewritten (walks end at their due, never later; places at a walk's start and end; waits; tasks; a cut treads only
  the tiles entered and the next walk starts where it stopped; an older save with no legs loads with every pawn in its leg and goes on);
  `tests/attention.js` steps the legs to walk into and out of reach; `tests/journeys.js`, `tests/relations.js`, `tests/living.js`,
  `tests/regress.js` (the underflowing walk is now a leg a step long), `tests/speed.js` adapted. Invariants: the queue (living, distinct,
  sorted, each due after its start), each living pawn's leg as what it does, a walk's hours and end, a free pawn with no leg only when its
  next look falls outside the working hours, no leg for the dead, no `st.awake`; `st.workDue` may hold background tasks, and one minded is
  in it; journeys in id order with no due.
- `sim/agents` is 1,227 lines (VB-422 splits it, waiting for this task); its exports fell from 32 to 31 (width baseline lowered).

## Decisions (Deciding without Kim)
- Two due-queues: the pawns' walks, waits and journeys' legs in `st.legDue`; tasks' ends in `st.workDue`, as VB-472 built it. One queue
  of the pawns' legs; a work leg's end is its task's, shared by its hands. Close call.
- A background task's reach at a leg's start and end: a walk ending out of reach lets the task go as it begins (the stepping let go at the
  step the pawn left reach); one beginning and ending in reach minds it all the way. Close call.
- A pawn freed by `release` (an occasion, an order, a household dissolving) looks for work at its next step, where the stepping found it at
  the next hour's rebuild of the awake list. Close call.
- The page: no shim that writes `st` (one made the page's state differ from headless, `tests/browser.js`); the page reads walkers through
  `Sim.placeOf` and `Sim.poseOf`, and the simulation writes facing and stride at a leg's end only. Drawing from legs between steps stays
  VB-474's. Close call (the task's note allowed merging).
- A journey's night stop is its walk's due, the last step before WORK[1], where the stepping stopped it at the 18:00 entry before the
  step's walking: the same place, a step's record earlier.
- A fresh run is between steps (work and walks reckoned from the tick now; the stepping took the step before).
- Teleport found and fixed: a pawn released on its way to a task and sent on a journey (`moved`, `carryGift`) walked its old route to the
  task's site as its first leg, then was placed at its new home; `setOut` now cuts the walk in hand. Teleport found and left for its task:
  `release` books a load on its way home at the barn from the road (VB-335): VB-581 (physical).
