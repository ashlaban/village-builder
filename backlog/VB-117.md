# VB-117: Fishing in rivers, lakes and the sea
Status: needs-review
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: food
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Goods and the farming year: Food from the wild, Mixed meals). Job review, second pass, 30 Oct 2026 (`REVIEW.md`; answers in `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`).
Goal: Adults stand idle 55–79% of work hours and the rivers (VB-111) feed nobody. Fishing fills slack hours from spring to autumn, makes a village by water eat better, and is what a household short of food turns to: a lean summer sends the boys to the river.
Acceptance criteria (proposed; see `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-117):
- [ ] A `fish` kind in `src/sim/kinds.js` (VB-116), type `foraging`, 1 hand, 3 work hours, carried home: at a shore tile (walkable land next to a river, lake or sea tile) within 6 steps of the house, the nearest with fish.
- [ ] Each river and lake tile holds a fish stock from 0 to 1; a trip brings home 4 rations × the stock and lowers it by 0.1; stocks recover 0.02 a day. The sea and lakes of 20 tiles or more do not run down. Stocks are documented with `@state` and checked in `tests/lib/invariants.js`.
- [ ] Posted from spring day 3 to the end of autumn (ice in winter): one job per household, two when its grain above the seed reserve covers under 12 days of its need.
- [ ] Urgency 0.2 while grain above the seed reserve covers 24 days or more of the household's need, rising linearly to 2.0 at none, so fishing fills spare hours in good times and comes first in a lean summer.
- [ ] Roles rank `foraging`: farmer 3, wife 4, farmhand 6, maid 5, son 7, daughter 6, retired 3, child 5 (children from 7 at their rate); fishing is an exception at 2 for wife, maid and daughter.
- [ ] Until VB-119, fish go straight into the household's rations; with VB-119 they are fresh food.
- [ ] A page animation: a pawn at the bank with a rod or net.
- [ ] Populous medium and founders at seeds 7 and 42, 3 years, weather held at 1.0: idle shares, hungry household-days, fish landed per household and stock left per village water, before and after (reported).
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
