# VB-117: Fishing in rivers, lakes and the sea
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Goods and the farming year: Food from the wild, Mixed meals). Job review, second pass, 30 Oct 2026 (`REVIEW.md`; answers in `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`).
Goal: Adults stand idle 55–79% of work hours and the rivers (VB-111) feed nobody. Fishing fills slack hours from spring to autumn, makes a village by water eat better, and is what a household short of food turns to: a lean summer sends the boys to the river.
Acceptance criteria (numbers decided by Kim, 10 November; `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-117 and VB-118"):

- [x] Each river and lake tile holds a fish stock from 0 to 1; a trip brings home 4 rations × the stock and lowers it by 0.1; stocks recover 0.02 a day. The sea and lakes of 20 tiles or more do not run down. Stocks are documented with `@state` and checked in `tests/lib/invariants.js`.
- [x] Posted from spring day 3 to the end of autumn (ice in winter): one job per household, two when its grain above the seed reserve covers under 12 days of its need.
- [x] Urgency 0.2 while grain above the seed reserve covers 24 days or more of the household's need, rising linearly to 2.0 at none, so fishing fills spare hours in good times and comes first in a lean summer.
- [x] Roles rank `foraging`: farmer 3, wife 4, farmhand 6, maid 5, son 7, daughter 6, retired 3, child 5 (children from 7 at their rate); fishing is an exception at 2 for wife, maid and daughter.

Implementation notes (11 Nov 2026):
- New module `src/sim/fish.js` (after `sim/paths` in the manifest): `w.fish` (stock 0..1 as of `w.fishDay`) and `w.fishDay` per tile, read in
  closed form (`stock`: + 0.02 a day, at most 1; Rules for cost, 3: no daily pass); `landCatch` (4 rations × stock, stock − 0.1 where it runs
  down); `runsDown` (a river tile, or a lake of fewer than 20 tiles, from `lakeSizes`, worked out once a world from the generated water and
  kept on `w`, not saved); `ground(st, house)`: the nearest bank (passable land, not a river tile) within 6 walking steps, in breadth-first
  order, whose richest neighbouring water has a stock of at least 0.1. `initFish` at seeding (`sim/layout`, beside `initWear`) and at the
  load of an older save; `fish` and `fishDay` join `WORLD` in `sim/saves`. Tunables `FISH` in `sim/rules`; urgency numbers in `MATCH.urgency`.
- Kind `fish` in `src/sim/kinds.js` (type `foraging`, 1 hand, 3 hours, carried home to the door, anim `fish`), asked last in
  `ASK.farmAfterPlans` so other jobs keep their order; its key names the bank, so a fished-out ground is left at the next review. Posted
  from spring day 3 to the end of autumn; a second job under 12 days of spare food. The catch goes into `hh.meals` through
  `Stores.put(…, 'catch')`, a new routine reason (folded into the yearly totals, so fish landed are `totals` of kind `catch`).
- `sim/plans` `closing`: the first day of winter is a closing day (every holder reviewed), so fishing is taken down at the ice instead of
  standing until each household's planning day.
- Roles: `foraging` in every role's `work` as decided; `fish: 2` in the exceptions of wife, maid and daughter.
- Page: `rod` and `catch` parts in `src/page/rigs.js`, a `fish` pose (the rod held out over the water, its tip dipping now and then) and a
  string of fish carried home; a pawn fishing faces its water (`src/page/animate.js`).
- Invariants: stocks in [0, 1] with a day not after today, and below 1 only where they run down; a fishing job stands on a bank next to its
  water. New test `tests/fish.js` (in `npm test`): the kind and ranks, stocks and recovery, the sea, the ground, the season, the urgency line,
  the second trip, the catch into meals, a run with fish landed, and a save keeping the stocks. `tests/children.js` knows a child's fish 5.
- Work counter `fishGround` (tiles searched for a ground): about 6,700 a day, 21 per living pawn-day at populous small, year 1. Each call is
  a breadth-first search of about 100 tiles, at a review or a member's look, not per hour; not measured on a late save.

Households are better off, not only the average: hungry household-days fall to none in three runs of four and by half in the fourth; mean
grain at the end rises (founders 7 332 → 378, populous 7 303 → 329, populous 42 274 → 314; founders 42 249 → 267). The son's idle share falls

1.5 to 2.6 rations on average: the rivers by the villages run down to about half their stock, and the fishers walk on to the next bank.
