Notes:
- Plan (22 Nov 2026, VB-875; Deciding without Kim): mood changes the answer to an offer, through the allowance of a gift (sim/relations' rule, the sum of slack, goodwill, opinion and reserve): a term `mood` (sim/exchange, beside goodwill and opinion), `MOOD.gives.days` (10) of the giving household's need × (mood − 50) / 50. So every gift, ask and help a holder weighs reads its mood, and the panel shows the term ("Mood: low"). One decision, not the rate of work: work is read every hour of every worker (cost rule 1), and it would compound hunger with hunger's own mood term, a spiral.
- `sim/mood` `level(st, id)`: the number `of` gives, made without the list, since the registry's value must not allocate; `of` and `level` share one function of the terms. tests/mood.js holds level = of, the term on the probe (calm 0, grieving −3 days' need, married more, the receiver's mood unread), and that sim/exchange is the one reader.
- Close call: the giver's mood only, not the receiver's (a cheerful asker is not more deserving); the gift's allowance only, not a swap's `dealAllowance`.
- Numbers (populous small, 3 years): seed 7 the term read 1,113 times, +3.5 rations on a mean allowance of 99 (up on 84% of reads, down on 10%, −16.5 to +12); gifts 381 to 377, 8,259 to 8,046 rations; no ask was made either way (asks 0). Mood by season unchanged (means 53.7, 53.7, 53.4, 53.4 to 53.6, 53.6, 53.4, 53.4; p10 50, p50 and p90 55; low 1.0, 1.3, 2.5, 3.5% to 1.4, 1.8, 3.0, 3.7%). Seed 42 (and its notes): term +3.0, up 1,022, down 193 of 1,345.
- Households: hungry household-days over the 3 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42: 549, 8, 36, 81 before; 580, 9, 50, 105 after; at 5 days (one run each of 3, 11, 42) 513, 50, 105. Seeds 11 and 42 come out the same at 5 and at 10 days, so the change is a run's divergence (a gift flipped early), not a dose; not better off, not shown worse. A row in docs/tuning.md asks whether low spirits should hold back help in a hungry winter.
- Agent usage (`tools/agent-usage.js`, to the PR): 59 model calls in about 15 minutes, no sub-agents; context 84.5k at the first call, about 177k at its peak; fresh input about 110, cache writes about 115k, cache reads about 8.3M, output about 30k tokens; weighted cost about 1.13M.
