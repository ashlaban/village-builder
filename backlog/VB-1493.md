# VB-1493: Refactor: the map's colours out of the world, into a paint cache of the page
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: `docs/architecture/target-v2.md` 3.1 (the layers; Player and presentation); `docs/architecture/modules.md`
Goal: Last step of the terrain split: `generate` builds the map's colours, hillshade, brush texture and coast distance into `w` in every run, headless too, and the simulation keeps the colours up through `setBiome`. They become a cache the page builds from the world and recolours itself, so the world holds terrain and run state only, and the generator and the simulation know no colour.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `draw/paint` builds a world's paint from the world alone: `tr`, `tg`, `tb` per tile, `shade`, `cdist` and `chex` per coarse cell, and the brush texture `tile` (`makeTile` moves with it). `generate` no longer makes them, and `w` no longer holds them: their `@state` lines, `tools/stocks.js` (`NOT_STOCKS`) and `tests/lib/tiny.js` follow. `macro`, `hrnd` and `elev` stay in `w`: they are terrain (meadow stones read `macro`, `sim/layout` reads `hrnd`), so a seed's world and a save are unchanged.
- [ ] The pigments (`c`, `c2`, `shade`) and the sea's and the lake's colours are `draw/pigments`; the biome table of `world/biomes` holds `key`, `name` and `water`. `setBiome` writes the biome alone, in a world module; `sim/saves` sets the loaded biome as it sets the other arrays.
- [ ] The cache is one per world (the start screen holds several islands, the editor its own), kept by the page (`paintOf(wd)` in `page/chunks`, a WeakMap), not in a module's closure; `bakeWater` and `bakeChunk` take it as an argument.
- [ ] Recolouring is explicit (Kim, 23 November: no cache that mends itself while it bakes): the page recolours a tile where it learns the biome changed: the tiles of `st.biomeDirty` before their chunks are repainted, the editor's strokes, `restore` (page/editplace), and a load onto a world already painted. The pixel check, not the bake, asserts that no colour is stale: after each of those four, the cache equals one built anew.
- [ ] The colour formula is today's to the bit (`mc` in double precision from `csample(w.macro, ...)` and `w.hrnd`, stored to Float32): the pixel check of VB-1489 prints `same`, also after a cover set by hand and after `restore`.
- [ ] `node tools/same.js --base REF` differs only by the seven fields gone from `w`, listed in the result; `npm run verify`, `node tests/smoke.js` and the browser tests of the map and the editor pass.
- [ ] No module before `sim.js` in the manifest names a colour: `grep -n "tr\[\|\.c2\b\|shade" src/gen.js src/gen src/world` finds only the relief (`elev`). `docs/architecture/modules.md` follows.
Notes:
