# VB-691: A head reads the asks: what others want raises its target
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-690, VB-682
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (7, 9, 15); design.md, Goods, production and animals
Goal: Demand is the asks that are open, read by a head as its own estimate (principle 14), with no price and no market state.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A household that lacks a good it needs and cannot make (no deposit, no shore, no pine in reach) asks for it: an offer on the basis `swap` or `gift` wanting the good, open until met or a year old.
- [ ] On its planning day after the harvest a head reads the asks for the goods it can make, from the households it has a tie to and those of the villages its village gathers with, and sets its target to its need plus what it judges it can place (its hands to spare, the load it can carry to the gathering); the estimate is the head's, with its skill and lean (VB-476's pattern), and the panel says so.
- [ ] Cost (rules 1, 2, 4): the open asks are indexed by good, live ones only, with a cap a household; a head reads its goods' lists once a year, bounded by the villages it gathers with; a declared counter; measured on `small-y40` and `medium-ref`.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years: asks open and met by good, goods made beyond need and what became of them (swapped, given, spoilt), hours spent on them.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim, the session on the material economy): principle 9 by mechanism: place decides who can make, the asks decide who does.
