Goal: Help in hunger is asked for by a head and given by a head, through VB-690's offer and answer, down the ladder: kin, then the alderman for the store (VB-314; until then the store answers by its rule).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] On its planning day (or the day its store falls under the ask's threshold) a short head offers on the basis `gift`, wanting what it lacks, to its kin in the order sim/help asks them today, then to the alderman; each answers by its floor and allowance; the midnight pass goes.
- [ ] The played head asks by the same verb and is asked as any head; an ask refused is remembered (VB-690).
- [ ] The grain is set aside and fetched or brought (VB-461). Numbers before and after, since an ask now waits for a planning day: hungry household-days, days from ask to meal, populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 and founders small seeds 7 and 42, 15 years.
