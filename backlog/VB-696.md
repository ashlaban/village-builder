# VB-696: A party for new land is called and joined
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: acts
Phase: implement
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (16); backlog/VB-682.md; backlog/VB-690.md
Goal: A party is those who said yes to a leader's call; nobody is drawn. VB-392's wish is this, for any pawn.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The rung: New land is open to a waiting adult of `SETTLE.ages` in a crowded village (`v.crowded`, as built) once it has waited `SETTLE.patience` (2) times its ambition's factor (`TRAITS.acts.ambition.settle`), rounded up, rounds there: 3 at ambition 0, 2 at 0.5, 1 at 1; from ambition 0.75 it ranks above A plot (VB-680's ranking; plan, section 3). `SETTLE.chance` and `leaveChance` are removed.
- [ ] The call: such a pawn offers `party`, due at the round, to its spouse and to each unmarried sibling waiting in the village, the eldest first, at most `SETTLE.maxFollow` (2). The spouse says yes unless she, or a child she keeps, is at a hunger of `HUNGER.marryBelow` (0.5) or more; the provisions are counted at the move (VB-445's plan: `design.md`, Moving to new land, Provisions), not at the call. A sibling says yes when it holds no open offer of its own and its opinion of the caller is `SETTLE.followLiking` (0) or more. `SETTLE.follow` is removed. Minor children are not asked. A party short of provisions is not called, and the log says why, once a pawn a year, as today.
- [ ] What follows the yeses is VB-445's (`design.md`, Settling in steps): the call is made and answered at its step 1, in place of "the leader is found as before"; the site, the trail, the booth and the move, and when each falls, are its tasks'; the party's people change household by `move`, reason `settle` (VB-822), at the step that plan names, with what they carry (VB-675). Until VB-445's tasks land the party leaves at the round by the `leave` verb, as today. Joining a small village is not this task: it is the Plot rung, asked of that village's alderman (VB-697).
- [ ] The played pawn calls or joins by the same verbs; VB-392's wish is this rung in its ranking.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7 and 42: parties called, refused and by whom, their size, the rounds waited before leaving by ambition, villages founded, growth by principle "The island grows without the player" (founders the reference). Parameterisation, at most five short runs (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 20 years): `SETTLE.patience` and `SETTLE.followLiking`, towards the parties a decade of before, counted by this task; the values tried and what each gave.
- [ ] `tests/commands.js` and `tests/record.js` pass; tests on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): the threshold by ambition; a hungry spouse says no and the party is not formed that round; a sibling with an offer of its own stays.
