# VB-121: Winter crafts: tools and fence poles
Status: needs-review
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: work
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Jobs, owners and roles: Winter crafts). Job review, second pass, 30 Oct 2026.
Goal: Winter is 79% idle. On a medieval farm winter was when tools were made and mended and fence poles cut. Work in winter that prepares spring gives the slack season a purpose, and a lazy winter shows in a slow sowing.
Acceptance criteria (proposed; see `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-121):
- [ ] Each household has tools in a condition from 0 to 1 (`hh.tools`, `@state`, invariants), starting at 1. Every 10 work hours of tilling, tending and harvest work lowers it by 0.01; that work goes at (0.7 + 0.3 × tools) of the worker's rate.
- [ ] A `craft` kind, type `crafts`, inside, 4 work hours, 1 hand: restores 0.1 of the tools; posted in winter while tools are below 1, and in autumn while they are below 0.6.
- [ ] A `poles` kind, type `wood`, 4 work hours, carried home: brings 4 fence poles from the woodlot and fells a tree; posted in winter while the household's poles are below 8. Mending a fence stretch uses a pole; a village's mending draws on its households' poles in turn.
- [ ] Roles rank `crafts`: farmer 6, farmhand 7, son 7, retired 5, wife 2, maid 1, daughter 1, child 0.
- [ ] Populous medium and founders at seeds 7 and 42, 3 years: winter idle share, tools at the start of spring, hoeing hours per field, before and after (reported).
