# VB-121: Winter crafts: tools and fence poles
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-360, VB-364
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Jobs, owners and roles: Winter crafts). Job review, second pass, 30 Oct 2026.
Goal: Winter is 67 to 76% idle (farmers, populous small, four seeds; `design.md`, How busy, measured 10 Nov). On a medieval farm winter was when tools were made and mended and fence poles cut. Work in winter that prepares spring gives the slack season a purpose, and a lazy winter shows in a slow sowing.
Acceptance criteria (rewritten by slice 6's plan, VB-185, 11 Nov):
- [ ] Each household has tools in a condition from 0 to 1 (`hh.tools`, `@state`, invariants), starting at 1. Every 10 work hours of tilling, tending and harvest work lowers it by 0.01; that work goes at (0.7 + 0.3 × tools) of the worker's rate.
- [ ] Mending and iron: a `craft` kind, type `crafts`, inside, 4 work hours, 1 hand, skill smithing, restores 0.1; posted in winter while tools are below their ceiling, and in autumn while they are below 0.6. Without iron the ceiling is 0.5 (wooden spades and ards: work at 85%); each 0.1 mended above 0.5 takes a tenth of a bloom from `hh.iron` (VB-364), so a household with iron mends to 1.
- [ ] A `poles` kind, type `wood`, 4 work hours, carried home: brings 4 fence poles from the village's wood (VB-326) and fells a tree; posted in winter while the household's poles are below 8. Mending a fence stretch uses a pole; a village's mending draws on its households' poles in turn.
- [ ] Roles rank `crafts`: farmer 6, farmhand 7, son 7, retired 5, wife 2, maid 1, daughter 1, child 0.
- [ ] Populous medium and founders at seeds 7 and 42, 15 years: winter idle share, tools at the start of spring with and without iron, hoeing hours a field, households that get iron by exchange; before and after.
