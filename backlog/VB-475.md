# VB-475: Time taken from causes: the crop's size, the hand's skill, mishaps
Status: done (2026-11-22)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/review-blocks.md; design.md section 7 (A leg, and its end, to The timetable); docs/mocks/blocks/README.md; Long work and processing
Goal: A task takes longer for reasons the world can show, and the only new randomness is small and has a cause.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Reaping's and threshing's hours are the kind's times the field's crop over a normal crop's, between a half and one and a half: named tunables; the carrying already goes by loads.
- [x] Skill at a type of work: one stand-in number by pawn and type from the stream `traits` (as VB-254's traits, until slice 7); it does not grow. A hand's rate is multiplied by 0.9 to 1.1 by it.
- [x] Mishaps: at a work leg's start the hand's rate is lowered by a draw between 0 and a most that falls from a fifth to a twentieth with its skill, from a new stream `mishap` (`SYSTEMS`, `src/sim/rng.js`); a hand of middling skill averages the norm, shown by a test that forces the draws with `fix`.
- [x] Up to five short runs for the tunables (seeds and map named), each written down with what it gave.
- [x] Numbers before and after at the bands' seeds: the farming year's days (hoed, sown, reaped, threshed), crops lost to frost, hungry household-days, growth; and whether households, not only averages, are better off.
