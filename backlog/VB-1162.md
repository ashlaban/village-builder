# VB-1162: Mural theme: the bars' and the acts' icons as stencils
Status: done (2026-11-18)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: VB-1161; Kim, 18 November
Goal: The mural theme's icons are stencils, as the map's marks are painted.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] An icon can carry a second drawing, a stencil (`stencil`, `src/page/icons.js`); the mural theme shows it and Glass the line drawing, by the stylesheet alone, so changing theme redraws nothing.
- [x] Stencils for the top bar, the dock, the map's bar and the panels' heads (`ICON`, 21), the speeds, and the action bar's groups and acts (`ACT_PATHS`, 36).
- [x] On a pressed button the stencil's gaps take the button's ground (`--ico-bg`).

## Notes

Kim, 18 November: the stencil manner of the mock-ups (B) for every icon; black letter is fine at a card's size (VB-1161's close call, answered).

A stencil is drawn on a 24 box whatever its icon's own box, and scaled; its shapes are filled in the icon's colour, a shape of class `l`
is a line in that colour (a handle, a stem), one of class `c` a cut through a shape in the ground's colour. The gaps between shapes are
a thin stroke in the ground's colour, `--ico-bg`, which the theme sets to the red ochre where a button is pressed, chosen or primary.

Decisions:
- Both drawings are in each icon's markup and the stylesheet shows one, rather than icons drawn anew when the theme changes: the page
  builds its panels as strings at many places, and none of them need know the theme. The cost is the stencil's markup in Glass too, a
  few hundred bytes an icon shown.
- Family is a tree with a round for each of three, and Culture a banderole (a mural's speech scroll), not the boxes and the rolled
  sheet of the line icons: the theme's own images where it has them (a close call: the meaning is the same, the picture is not).
- A job kind with no icon of its own takes the hoe in both drawings, as before.
