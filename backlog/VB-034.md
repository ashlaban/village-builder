# VB-034: Splitting land between heirs
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-310, VB-311
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, Handover and shares (Splitting land: designed as a custom, behaves like a buy-out until splitting exists); Land, ecology and rights, Land and rights (Splitting between heirs)
Goal: The `heirCount` option `split` today pays shares like `buyout` and says "splitting land comes in a later batch". Make it divide the farm's fields between heirs, so a second heir can found a household (VB-032) without clearing new land. The split is `split` and `transfer` of VB-311's land rights.
Acceptance criteria (Kim answered on 2026-10-30; rewritten by VB-184, 11 Nov 2026):
- [ ] At a handover under `split`, the fields are divided between the heir and the siblings who want land, weighted by `daughterShare`; a farm keeps at least 1 field, and no split leaves a part with less than 1 field.
- [ ] Each sibling's fields are split off the farm and their `own` passes to the sibling (VB-311, basis `inherited`); a sibling who receives fields gets a building site next to them (VB-032) and becomes holder of a new household when it is built, which then takes their `use`. Until then the old household keeps the `use`, works the fields and gives the sibling half their harvest, as an obligation of kind `rent` (VB-310) with a half share in place of a tenancy's quarter.
- [ ] The relation "a holding worked by a household that does not own it, its harvest shared" is the one VB-316 (a tenancy) and VB-309 (a farm in trust) use; whichever of them lands first builds it, and the others reuse it.
- [ ] A test on a constructed farm with 4 fields and 3 children.
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR; numbers before and after (heirs and populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years, under `split` set by `tests/lib/tiny.js`'s `setPractice` or a scenario option): households founded on split land, fields per household, landless adults over 25.
