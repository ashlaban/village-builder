Design ref: Inheritance, Handover and shares (Splitting land: designed as a custom, behaves like a buy-out until splitting exists); Land, ecology and rights, Land and rights (Splitting between heirs)
Goal: The `heirCount` option `split` today pays shares like `buyout` and says "splitting land comes in a later batch". Make it divide the farm's fields between heirs, so a second heir can found a household (VB-032) without clearing new land. The split is `split` and `transfer` of VB-311's land rights.
Acceptance criteria (Kim answered on 2026-10-30; rewritten by VB-184, 11 Nov 2026):
- [x] At a handover under `split`, the fields are divided between the heir and the siblings who want land, weighted by `daughterShare`; a farm keeps at least 1 field, and no split leaves a part with less than 1 field.
- [x] Each sibling's fields are split off the farm and their `own` passes to the sibling (VB-311, basis `inherited`); a sibling who receives fields gets a building site next to them (VB-032) and becomes holder of a new household when it is built, which then takes their `use`. Until then the old household keeps the `use`, works the fields and gives the sibling half their harvest, as an obligation of kind `rent` (VB-310) with a half share in place of a tenancy's quarter.
- [x] The relation "a holding worked by a household that does not own it, its harvest shared" is the one VB-316 (a tenancy) and VB-309 (a farm in trust) use; whichever of them lands first builds it, and the others reuse it.
- [x] A test on a constructed farm with 4 fields and 3 children.
- [x] Numbers before and after (heirs and populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years, under `split` set by `tests/lib/tiny.js`'s `setPractice` or a scenario option): households founded on split land, fields per household, landless adults over 25.
  Measured 28 Nov 2026 (small map, `setPractice` heirCount split held every year; before = night-base, after = this branch): split plots / households / fields per household / landless over 25 / population. heirs 7: 0/12/3.75/1/17 -> 0 (3 inherited titles open)/11/3.00/0/20; heirs 42: 0/7/2.86/0/9 -> 0/8/3.88/0/10; populous 7: 0/147/3.76/18/530 -> 1 (1 title)/144/3.70/24/552; populous 42: 0/148/4.22/29/605 -> 0/143/4.22/27/623. Splits are rare: few heirs have a grown sibling in the village with no farm or plot and a house site by its fields.

- The heir owns as a pawn (title owner `{type: 'pawn'}`, code 0) until its household; the rent is the obligation `rent`, a load at a time as the sheaves come in (`sheavesIn`), paid at the moving round above the reserve, as shares. The relation is built here on VB-311's titles (rent 0.5); VB-316 reuses it with 0.25. VB-309's trust (done first) is not this relation, so it is not reused (review, 28 Nov): a trust moves no own and no use of any field (owner, holder and line stay the same, `st.trusts`), the trustee takes a whole farm's work, people and harvest while the owning household cannot work it, and nothing is shared back; the split is a title on single fields, owned by one party and used by another household, the harvest shared. A trust that should share its harvest with the ward would be the title with a rent, a change to VB-309.
- The house is a village plot of a house only (`plot.split`), its alone; built, the fields pass to the new farm (`sim/fields` passField) and the titles end. An heir who dies first: its fields go back to the farm (design.md does not say; noted). `farmToVillage` became `farmTo` (also used by foundHousehold), which carries the use of fields split off.
