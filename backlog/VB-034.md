# VB-034: Splitting land between heirs
Status: needs-review
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: expansion
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion, Handover and shares (Splitting land: designed as a custom, behaves like a buy-out until splitting exists).
Goal: The `heirCount` option `split` today pays shares like `buyout` and says "splitting land comes in a later batch". Make it divide the farm's fields between heirs, so a second heir can found a household (VB-032) without clearing new land. Depends on VB-032 and VB-030; if either is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria (proposed; VB-030 decides how land is divided):
- [ ] At a handover under `split`, the fields are divided between the heir and the siblings who want land, weighted by `daughterShare`; a farm keeps at least 1 field, and no split leaves a part with less than 1 field.
- [ ] A sibling who receives fields gets a building site next to them (VB-032) and becomes holder of a new household when it is built; until then the fields are worked by the old household and the harvest is shared (VB-030).
- [ ] Fields change owner (`setOwner`) to the new household.
- [ ] A test on a constructed farm with 4 fields and 3 children.
