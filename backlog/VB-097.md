# VB-097: Any allowed work beats idling, so posted jobs get done
Status: needs-review
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: work
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Jobs, owners and roles: Matching; Goods and the farming year: Fuel). Jobs review, 30 Oct 2026.
Goal: A bid is role priority × urgency − 0.15 × distance + noise, and a job gets no bid when that is at most 0. A firewood job with a nearly full woodpile has urgency about 0.1, so a farmer 6–8 steps from the woodlot never bids (7 × 0.1 − 0.9 to 1.2 < 0): on a summer evening in populous medium, seed 7, 110 of 119 open wood jobs could not get a farmer's bid. The jobs stay posted (about 42 unclaimed at the end of each work day in year 1) while 62% of adult idle time in summer is spent next to open jobs nobody bids for. Firewood is then cut when the pile runs low, mostly in autumn and winter, not laid in during the slack summer.
Acceptance criteria (draft, pending `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-097):
- [ ] A pawn with role priority 3 or more for a job it may take always bids at least `MATCH.floor` = 0.1 plus the noise; distance and urgency still order the jobs above the floor. Poor fits (priority 2 or less) keep the 2-hour wait and get no floor. The number lives in `MATCH` if VB-086 is done, else beside the other matching numbers in `src/sim/jobs.js`.
- [ ] Firewood for the winter: from summer day 0 to the end of autumn the wood job is posted while the pile is below `ECON.woodWinter` = 40 (the winter's use, 1.6 × 12 ≈ 19, plus autumn's, plus a margin), and below `woodTarget` (30) in the rest of the year.
- [ ] The cut of firewood per year does not change by more than 10% (it follows use, not the target); VB-024's report, if done, shows it.
- [ ] Populous medium at seed 7 and founders at seed 7, 3 years: `--report idle` (VB-095), unclaimed jobs at the end of the day, the woodpile at the start of winter, and hungry household-days, before and after (reported, not asserted).
