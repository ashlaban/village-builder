# VB-009: Hand-quern milling and making rations
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs, Long work and processing (Milling at home: Decided). Kim, 2026-10-29: milling makes more food from grain; a household job turns grain or flour into rations; flour needs less input than grain.
Goal: Food stops being eaten straight from grain. Households grind grain to flour on a hand quern, and a household job turns flour or grain into rations; flour goes further.
Acceptance criteria (rewritten by slice 6's plan, VB-185, 11 Nov):
- [x] Households hold `grain`, `flour` and `meals`. Harvests, seed, starting stores and inheritance shares stay in grain.
- [x] The quern is a thing: `hh.quern`, `{ rock, made }` or null. Households of the scenarios' start have one (of their village's bedrock where that is sandstone or granite, else granite). A quern wears out `QUERN.years` (20) after it was made, read from `made` (rule 3); a household without one eats its grain unground (1.25 grain a ration, as today's cooking from grain).
- [x] Making one: `hewQuern`, 12 work hours at home from a load of sandstone or granite on the heap (VB-332's heap by kind), winter work, skill building; wanted from 2 years before the old one ends, or at once with none. The worth table gains a quern at 25 rations (Proposed), so a household on limestone or slate gets one at the fair.
- [x] `grind` (at home): 1 work hour for 4 units of grain on sandstone, 20% slower on granite (`QUERN.granite` 0.8), 1 grain to 1 flour; posted while grain exceeds the seed reserve and flour is below 10 days of the household's need. Priorities: mistress of the house and maid 7, daughter 6, retired 3, others 1.
- [x] The daily `cook` turns flour into meals at 1:1, or, when flour runs short, grain at 1.25 a ration; daily food is eaten from meals. The household panel shows grain, flour, meals and the quern with its stone and years left.
- [x] A test shows the same grain feeds a household about 25% longer with a quern than without. Founders, 3 years, seeds 7 and 42: no more hungry days than before; households without a quern by year (populous small, 30 years).

Decisions (Deciding without Kim):
- The heap keeps no kinds (VB-332 built one number, `hh.stone`): a load on the heap is taken as of the village's bedrock (the rock under its
  green), the same rock the start's querns are of. Rule 4 (extending a system); a heap by kind is a follow-up.
- The fair is not built (VB-302 blocked): the quern is declared a good at 25 rations, so the fair trades it when it comes; until then a
  household on limestone or slate whose quern wears out, or a new one there, eats its grain unground. Reported below; follow-up proposed.
- Skill building waits for VB-360 (skills, ready, not built): hewing is plain `upkeep` work until then.
- Hewing is winter work for a replacement; with no working quern it is posted in any season ("at once").
- The start's querns are of every age below 16 years, by household id (`(id * 7) % 16`): no draw, since the seeding stream is shared.
- Children (under 14) do not grind: their work is their listed kinds (tests/children.js); "others 1" is the adult roles.
- Flour is a stock of at most 10 days: it does not spoil, and stays in a household that dissolves, as meals do.
