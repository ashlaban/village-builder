# VB-009: Hand-quern milling and making rations
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-332
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs, Long work and processing (Milling at home: Decided). Kim, 2026-10-29: milling makes more food from grain; a household job turns grain or flour into rations; flour needs less input than grain.
Goal: Food stops being eaten straight from grain. Households grind grain to flour on a hand quern, and a household job turns flour or grain into rations; flour goes further.
Acceptance criteria (rewritten by slice 6's plan, VB-185, 11 Nov):
- [ ] Households hold `grain`, `flour` and `meals`. Harvests, seed, starting stores and inheritance shares stay in grain.
- [ ] The quern is a thing: `hh.quern`, `{ rock, made }` or null. Households of the scenarios' start have one (of their village's bedrock where that is sandstone or granite, else granite). A quern wears out `QUERN.years` (20) after it was made, read from `made` (rule 3); a household without one eats its grain unground (1.25 grain a ration, as today's cooking from grain).
- [ ] Making one: `hewQuern`, 12 work hours at home from a load of sandstone or granite on the heap (VB-332's heap by kind), winter work, skill building; wanted from 2 years before the old one ends, or at once with none. The worth table gains a quern at 25 rations (Proposed), so a household on limestone or slate gets one at the fair.
- [ ] `grind` (at home): 1 work hour for 4 units of grain on sandstone, 20% slower on granite (`QUERN.granite` 0.8), 1 grain to 1 flour; posted while grain exceeds the seed reserve and flour is below 10 days of the household's need. Priorities: mistress of the house and maid 7, daughter 6, retired 3, others 1.
- [ ] The daily `cook` turns flour into meals at 1:1, or, when flour runs short, grain at 1.25 a ration; daily food is eaten from meals. The household panel shows grain, flour, meals and the quern with its stone and years left.
- [ ] A test shows the same grain feeds a household about 25% longer with a quern than without. Founders, 3 years, seeds 7 and 42: no more hungry days than before; households without a quern by year (populous small, 30 years).
