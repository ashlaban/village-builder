Goal: On the forested island a crowded village finds land to settle: on forest, as far as there and back in a day along a trail.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A site may be wooded (broadleaf or pine): `siteScore`, the block of fields (`placeFields`), the room (`FIELDS.room`) and `SETTLE.fertility` read a wooded tile as the meadow it becomes when grubbed; the first field's project fells, grubs and breaks it (the steps a plot has), and the house tile is felled and grubbed before the cottage's foundation. A field broken there is fresh land.
- [x] The reach is hours: `SETTLE.hours` (6) from the green, by one bounded search (`reachCosts`'s, with a wooded tile that is not a path at a path's cost: the trail the party would blaze), which replaces `reachOf`'s count of steps; `SETTLE.reach` goes (its lower bound never bound: `centreApart`, 12 hex steps, stays and is the only near bound); `SETTLE.perStep` becomes `SETTLE.perHour` (1.2). The log's "within a day's walk" says what the search does.
- [x] A small village is joined within `SETTLE.join` hours (6) by the villages' table (`hoursBetween`), in place of 30 walking steps.
- [x] Until VB-787 lands the party still moves at once; with VB-447 not built its walk is over pathless ground, which the task says in its numbers.
- [x] Cost (Rules for cost, 9): one bounded search a crowded village where someone leads, a round: at most 7,057 tiles over open meadow, against the 67 hours of a row of the table; a counter `settleReach`; `node tests/counters.js` before and after. No search when nobody leads.
- [x] Numbers before (VB-780's island, meadow sites within 10 to 30 steps) and after, populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42, 15 years, and medium, seeds 7 and 42, 5 years: villages founded and farms joined, the sites' hours from home and their ground (meadow or forest), adults waiting with no room at the end, growth against Deciding without Kim 2, hungry days of the settlers' households in their first two years against the others (a description). The plan's year-0 counts to compare with: with meadow only 0 of 30 villages find a site on medium at seed 7; within 6 hours along a trail every village has wooded sites.
- [x] Up to five short runs on `SETTLE.hours` (3.75, 6, 12 named in the plan) if the first value founds no villages or scatters them; the values tried and what each gave in the task file, the rest to `docs/tuning.md`.
- [x] Tests on a constructed state: a village ringed by forest finds a wooded site and none beyond 6 hours; a site 11 hex steps from a centre is refused.
