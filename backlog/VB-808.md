# VB-808: The ground by season: walking costs compiled, snow, frozen marsh and bearing ice
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-780
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: roads
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Climate and travel beyond walking (The ground by season; Cost); docs/plans/VB-190.md, sections 5.2 and 6
Goal: Frozen water opens travel where it is cold enough (Decided, 3 Nov), and the cost grid changes on the winter's few dates, never daily.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The cost of crossing a tile on foot is compiled into one array a world (`sim/walking`; rule 6: `tileCost` is read in the search's inner loop), rebuilt for a band's rows when a date of VB-807 falls due and for one tile when `sim/routes.costChanged` sees it; not saved, rebuilt on load; an invariant that it equals the rule computed afresh. `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` for this step alone, before any winter value is read.
- [ ] `WINTER` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each with its meaning: `ice` 1 (lake, river and sound ice, a tile, with no slope), `marsh` 1.5 (frozen marsh), `snow` 1.3 (what deep snow multiplies a tile's cost by where no lane is trodden), `soft` 1.3 (the same for the days between the thaw and firm ground). From `frost`: snow and frozen marsh. From `lakeBears`: lake tiles and every river tile are crossed on foot (a river needs no ford). From `soundBears`: the sea within `near` 3 tiles of land in that band. From `thaw`: no ice bears. A walker never ends a day's leg on ice: a journey's leg stops at the shore if it cannot cross before 18:00, and a date falls due at 4:00, when nobody is out.
- [ ] The route cache is emptied on a date, as on any day a tile changes cost; a journey under way plans its next leg on the day's ground. `routeSearch`, `routeExpand` and `reachSettled` before and after in `tests/counters.js`; the count of days a year on which the cache is emptied, before and after.
- [ ] The winter table of hours: on the day `lakeBears` falls due in the middle band, a row a step, each village's hours to the others on the ground of a normal winter of its climate (not this year's: it is what people know of the winter ways, principle 14), bounded at 24 hours; `hoursBetween(st, a, b, season)` answers from it while ice bears in the village's band and from the land table otherwise. In `st`, saved as the land table is. No table where no band has ice in a normal winter.
- [ ] If boats travel (VB-812): a boat leg is refused on water that is not open (from `frost` on lakes and rivers, from `soundShut` on the sea, until `open`), with its reason; else this criterion is VB-812's.
- [ ] A constructed check: two shores of a lake 6 tiles wide and 20 round are 6 steps apart while the ice bears and 20 before and after; a marsh tile costs 1.5 in frost; a pawn whose leg would end on the ice stops at the shore; on the thaw's day no route crosses the lake.
- [ ] Numbers before and after on populous medium at year 0, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 (`node docs/plans/VB-190-water.js ROOT medium 3,7,11,42`, the plan's measures), and years on populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years, and founders small, seed 7, 50 years, on the middle and the northern world: hours to the nearest village and between the median pair, pairs within 6, 12 and 24 hours, people within 3 and 12 hours of the median village, summer against winter (the plan measured, with lakes, rivers and marsh frozen, 2.2 to 2.8 hours to the nearest village against 2.7 to 3.0, and 46 to 77 pairs within 12 hours against 35 to 61); marriages between villages by season, kin gifts carried, hungry days by household, growth (principle 2).
- Cost (rule 9): a band's rows of the array (about 19,000 tiles on medium) x at most 6 dates a year; the winter table 22 to 30 rows x 5,000 to 8,000 tiles settled where lakes freeze and 15,000 to 17,000 where sounds do (the plan's measure), once a year: a third to seven tenths of the land table's cost on the middle world, the whole of it or more in the far north; if the night shows it over the slice's line, the bound falls to 12 hours. Owed to the night as a `task` note for VB-818: the row and the table on `medium-ref`.
