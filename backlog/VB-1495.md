# VB-1495: Breaking forest to a field: felled, grubbed, and its wood carted home
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-1494
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Making a field); Kim, 23 Nov: breaking forest to field is fine
Goal: Forest is farmable ground for a household's field, at the cost of felling and grubbing, and its wood comes home.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Kim, 23 November 2026: breaking forest to a field is fine: a costly conversion that yields all the remaining wood as resources to cart home. Forest and pine get rows in `GROUND` (`src/sim/crops.js`), so `farmable` holds for them where a household's own field may lie; a village's wood (`sim/territory`, `woodTaken`) and the claims on it are respected as `freeFor` already decides, and a tile leaving a village's wood is reckoned as when a plot takes one.
- [ ] The project: a field on a wooded tile is felled and grubbed before it is cleared and broken, as a plot's is (`stepsFor` and `stepHours` of `src/sim/expansion.js`: `FELL_HOURS`, `GRUB_HOURS`), as steps of the household's field project (`fieldProject`), not hours hidden in `clearHours` (thick scrub's `scrubHours` path stays for scrub). One rule for the plot and the household's field if the code allows it; at the third special case propose the general rule.
- [ ] The wood: every tree standing on the tile comes out as timber or firewood by the rules that fell a tree today (`sim/forest`: `fellTree`, `clearTile`; VB-328 for a felled plot's timber), lies at the site, and is carried or carted home by pawns through `src/sim/stores.js` operations with a good and a reason. No teleport: nothing reaches the store without a walk. `tools/stocks.js` balances.
- [ ] The field made: `fd.ground` is `'forest'` or `'pine'` (VB-1490), its biome meadow after the clearing as `clearScrub` leaves it; its fertility a named value a wood in `GROUND` (proposed: forest 0.9, pine 0.6, estimates, with a `tuning` note) and its soil fresh (`SOIL.fresh`, VB-149: burnt-over and virgin ground). The head's choice (VB-1494) weighs the felling and grubbing hours against the wood gained and the yield: the wood's worth enters the score as a named term.
- [ ] `design.md` is not edited (the orchestrator rewrote Making a field on 23 Nov). The page shows the steps as any project's; the card's ground wording covers the two woods (VB-1493).
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state: a household with only forest in reach makes a field there: the steps in order, their hours, the wood in its store after the carrying, `ground` and `fert` of the field.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, founders and populous, small, seeds 7 and 42, 10 years: fields made by ground, hours felling and grubbing, timber and firewood brought home, the forest's tiles lost, hungry days a household, growth; households out of wood in winter. Whether households were better off.
- [ ] Cost: no new loop over tiles; `node tests/counters.js --update` if a counter moves, named.
