# VB-292: The player's journey: the played pawn may make a visit
Status: done (2026-11-18)
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Between villages: The player's journey); The player and what they see. `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-281", question 3 (Kim, 10 Nov: yes)
Goal: The suitor's visit (VB-289) is an act the round gives to unmatched men. By parity of command (design, section 15, Kim, 10 Nov: every act a pawn may take is the player's to command unless a gameplay reason is written against it), the player may order it: for its own pawn, and as head for an unmarried son or daughter of the house. Decided (Kim, 10 Nov; `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-281", question 3): yes, at the same costs and in the same season.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A verb `visit` in `src/sim/commands.js` (source `player`): target a village; it checks that the actor is the played pawn, or one the played pawn speaks for (`spokesmanOf`), unmarried and 18 or more, that the village lies beyond a day's walk and within reach, that it is winter or the visit can be due on winter day 1, that the actor has no open journey, and that the household can give the visit's food above its seed and means; it returns why not. It stores the journey as VB-289's round does, due at the next 6:00 in winter.
- [x] An opportunity (`src/sim/opportunities.js`): "Visit <village> (N days, R rations)" for each village beyond a day's walk and within reach that holds a free person of the other sex the pawn could marry, nearest first, at most 5.
- [x] The pawn panel lists it with the opportunities; a visit under way shows the days left. No new art.
- [x] A played woman may visit too: since the round's men choose, her visit puts her in the pool of the visited village's men at the next round (`p.visited` read from the woman's side), as a man's visit puts that village's women in his. Tests in `tests/journeys.js`: a played man's visit puts that village's women in his pool; a played woman's visit puts her in that village's men's pool; a refused visit says why.
- [x] No player, no change: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`, and the counters are unchanged.

Decision (principles 16 and 11): the verb's check gained no test of who the player is; whether the player may order it is checked by the player's own entry, `orderVisit` (`Sim.orderVisit(st, who, village)`): the played pawn, or one it speaks for (`spokesmanOf`), with the visitor as the verb's actor, at once and not queued (a plan takes no time, as the head's plan choices and an answer). Only women: a man guest would enter the pool as one of the other villages' men (kin pressure) and change runs with no player; his visit already brings the visited village into his own village's pool.
