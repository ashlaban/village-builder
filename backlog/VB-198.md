# VB-198: The route cache bounded
Status: blocked
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: World and scale (Walking and terrain). `docs/roadmap.md`, sections 5.3 (rule 8, bound every cache) and 5.4; VB-097's first criterion, moved here.
Goal: `st.pathCache` keeps one route for every pair of tiles ever walked, and it is saved with the state. In populous small at seed 7 it holds 8,212 routes at year 5 and 25,723 at year 40 (114,874 tiles, 1 MB of the save). On every day a tile's walking cost changes, `refreshRoutes` tests every route: 91 ms over 30 game days at year 40. Bound the cache, as rule 8 asks, and measure what that saves. Blocked until VB-193 (the fixtures) is done.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Measure on `small-y40` and `medium-y60` first: entries, MB in the save, `routeHit`, `routeSearch` and `routeRefresh` per game day (VB-192), and ms per game day in `refreshRoutes`.
- [ ] Bound the cache at `ROUTE.cacheMax` entries (start at 20,000, VB-097's figure; tune by the hit rate), dropping the least recently used: a hit moves its entry to the end of the Map, and an insert past the bound drops the first. The order is the Map's, so it survives a save; `checkState` asserts the bound.
- [ ] Hit rate, searches a day, ms a game day and the save's MB before and after, on both fixtures. The hit rate falls by at most 5 points, or the bound is raised until it does.
- [ ] A dropped route may be searched again and come out different from the stale copy, so routes can change: run the golden check, and if it changes, re-record it and list the changed entries (its runs are short, so it may not change).
- [ ] VB-097 points here for its first criterion and keeps HPA* for the day routes cost too much.
- [ ] `npm run verify` passes.
Cost: one Map delete and set per hit; `refreshRoutes` then tests at most `cacheMax` routes a day.
