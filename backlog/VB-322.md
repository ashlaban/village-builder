# VB-322: Fields due: a household is reviewed out of turn when a field is ripe or still to sow
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: docs/bench/hunger-night-11nov.md (the proposed fix A, with its diff); backlog/VB-249.md; design.md, Work: opportunities, plans and tasks
Goal: The night's investigation (11 Nov) found that since VB-249 plow, sow and reap wait for the household's next review, so hoed-to-sown and ripe-to-reaped each lose up to a day, and fields lost at the frost rose from 4.7-6.6% of those sown to 6.8-9.0% on all six seeds. Fishing hides the hunger this causes; the harvests are still lost.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `reviewWhy` (`src/sim/plans.js`) reviews a household out of turn at the first review hour of any day one of its fields is ripe, and of each day of the sowing window while one of its fields is still to hoe or sow (the report's `fieldsDue`, fix A); at most one such review per household a day, beside VB-249's other reasons. No other change to planning days, and no hourly review (the report's variant B costs 64% more `oppAsk` and works no better).
- [x] The check allocates nothing and walks only the household's own fields (Rules for cost 2 and 6); no new loop over all households beyond the review hour's own.
- [x] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 (and 5 and 13 if time allows), in the PR and here: fields lost at the frost as a share of fields sown (8.2/6.9/8.5/5.8% at the tip before; the base before VB-249 was 4.7 to 6.6%), ripe fields not reaped, the mean sowing day, household-years hungry on over 0.3 of their days (`docs/bench/lone-households.js`), and the worst household. Frost losses fall on every seed.
- [x] Cost: `oppAsk` a living pawn-day in `node tests/counters.js` rises by no more than a tenth (the report measured 8.13 to 8.61), and VB-249's gain (39.1 before it) is kept; `planReview` reported. `tests/golden.json`, `tests/counters.json` and the record's anchor re-recorded, the changed entries listed.
- [x] A test in `tests/plans.js`: a household of cadence 3 whose field ripens the day after its review is reviewed that day at the first review hour and reaps; one whose hoed field waits in the sowing window sows the same day.
Cost: households with a field due x one review a day, in the sowing window and the harvest: at most one extra review a household on those days (about 20 days a year).
