# VB-084: A 30× speed and an uncapped speed
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Time and calendar (Speeds: pause, 1×, 3×, 10×; Pace of play); Open questions (Faster speeds or skipping nights).
Goal: At 10× a year takes about 4 minutes, so a 150-year run is about 10 hours at full speed, much of it uneventful. The simulation takes about 0.2 s per game day on populous medium, so 30× costs about 13% of a core.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A 30× speed.
- [ ] An uncapped speed: each frame advances the simulation as far as a frame budget of 12 ms allows, so the speed is set by the machine; the speed buttons show it as "Max" with the speed reached (for example "Max (41×)").
- [ ] The clock and the picture stay in sync at every speed (VB-002), and frames stay above 30 fps on populous medium (in the Chromium test once VB-025 is done; until then measured by hand and reported in the PR).
