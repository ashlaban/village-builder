# VB-084: A faster speed and running until something happens
Status: needs-review
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Time and calendar (Speeds: pause, 1×, 3×, 10×; Pace of play); Open questions (Faster speeds or skipping nights).
Goal: At 10× a year takes about 4 minutes, so a 150-year run is about 10 hours at full speed, much of it uneventful. The simulation takes about 0.2 s per game day on populous medium, so 30× costs about 13% of a core.
Acceptance criteria (draft, pending `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-084):
- [ ] A 30× speed.
- [ ] "Until something happens": run at the highest speed the frame rate allows until a notable event (a death, birth or marriage in the selected pawn's family, a meeting that changes a practice, a farm emptied), then drop to 1× and select it.
- [ ] The clock and the picture stay in sync at every speed (VB-002), and frames stay above 30 fps on populous medium in the Chromium test (VB-025).
