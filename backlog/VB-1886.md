Noted by VB-1843, 27 November 2026.

Q12 over seeds 7, 11, 42 (populous small, 2 years), windows missed by the five, default 5+2+7 = 14: key arm 16+15+22 = 53 (+279%), deadline arm 9+4+4 = 17 (+21%). All households: default 27+26+48 = 101, key 124, deadline 102 (+1%). By key is far worse; by deadline is within about 20% of the default on the five, 1% on all: not yet shown better or worse than 10%.
The by misses of the deadline arm: 9+4+2 = 15 of the 17 (the rest plow misses). Never taken back, none: the default author takes back only a step found at risk at an earlier review and past its `by`. 7 of 15 were never found at risk (the head's estimate said on time); 8 were first found at risk after the `by` (hour 418 to 2074), and the task was gone 4 to 11 hours later. Each was finished within a day of its `by`, by the head alone (1 hand; one with 0). Cause: the at-risk check runs at the household's review, which found the shortfall only once the `by` was past, so the take-back (needs a review before the `by` day passes) never fired; no rule changed.
tools/levelled.js: --detail now also prints the by, the hour first at risk, the take-back and the hour the task was gone (read-only).
