# VB-823: The asking day: a pawn who seeks makes its offer through the year
Status: done (2026-11-17)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: acts
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (The round as offers); docs/plans/VB-682.md, sections 2, 3 and 7
Goal: Offers about a life are made all the year (Kim, 16 Nov): each pawn who seeks reviews its wish on a day of its own once a season and, holding no open offer, makes one; the round only answers.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A due-queue `st.asking` of `{t, pawn}`, plain data in order of `t` (Rules for cost, 4), owned by the module that owns the wish (VB-680): `@state`, `src/owners.json`, the `WORLD` list of `src/sim/saves.js`, and invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): at most one entry a living pawn, none for the dead, the married who hold a place or the under-age. An entry is written when a pawn becomes a seeker (comes of age to marry; is grown and landless; is widowed; leaves service; is refused at the round) and at each asking for the next season, and is dropped where the pawn marries, takes a place or dies. The asking day is season day `id mod SEASON_DAYS`, at 6:00. A step reads only the head of the queue.
- [x] At an asking the pawn's wish is reviewed (the ranking of `docs/plans/VB-682.md`, section 3) and, if `pawn.ask` is null, the offer of its best open rung is made by that basis's asking rule: VB-692's askers, called for one pawn in place of the round's walk. A son or daughter of the house has its `match` made by its spokesman on the member's day. `ASK` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each with its meaning: `passes` 3 (VB-692), `held` 5 (the most open offers one pawn is asked by on one basis; the next asker turns to its next best), `pool` 40 (the most candidates weighed at one asking, the nearest villages first).
- [x] An offer whose asker or asked has died, married or taken a place lapses where it is next read (an asking of either, or the round), with no pass over the open offers; the asker's `pawn.ask` is cleared and it asks again on its next day.
- [x] The played pawn is asked when an offer to it is made and may answer on any day until it is due; its own asking day makes no offer for it (it asks by the verb when it likes), and its spokesman, if unplayed, asks for it as for any member unless its wish says not this year.
- [x] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7 and 42: marriages, service, claims, plots and settlers a decade, against VB-692's; the askings a year by season and by rung; the share that found nobody to ask; offers open on the round's first day. Expected: outcomes close to VB-692's, since an offer made earlier is answered at the same round; differences reported with their cause.
- [x] Cost (rule 9; plan, section 7): seekers (about 60 to 120 a 1,000 living) x at most 4 askings a year x at most `ASK.pool` candidates, one table read each; counters `asksMade` and `asksWeighed` in `VB.COUNTERS`, `tests/counters.json` re-recorded with the changes listed; times left to the nightly bench.
- [x] Tests on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): the day by id; no offer while one is open; the cap of 5; a lapse when the asked marries another; a save in mid-year plays as the unbroken run (`tests/saves.js`). `tests/commands.js` and `tests/record.js` pass.

- 17 Nov 2026 (VB-823, built). **What was done.** `st.asking` (`@state` and owner `sim/wishes`, `src/owners.json`): the due-queue
  `{t, pawn}` in order of `t`, one entry a living pawn who seeks or will: of 18 or more but the married who hold a place (the holder or
  the holder's spouse), and a child, written at its birth (event `born`) for the first asking day after its 18th birthday. `seek` keeps
  it right at the events `born`, `died` (the dead dropped, the widowed written), `wed`, `newHead` and `rehoused` (every `move`: into
  service, out of it, into a founded household); `nextAsking` takes the head and writes the pawn's next season; `seedAsking` at the end
  of seeding (`startStreams`) and for an older save (`sim/saves`). The asking day is season day `id mod SEASON_DAYS` at `ASK.hour` (6).
  The new module `sim/asking` (after `sim/meetings`, layer agency) runs it from a daily entry of the clock at 6:00 (`--report calendar`,
  row 43), reading only the head of the queue: `askFor` lapses the pawn's open offer if its people have died, married or taken a place
  (`staleWhy`) and asks nothing more that day; else it reviews the wish (`chooseWish`, the per-pawn body of `chooseWishes`; not the
  played pawn's) and walks its rungs to Stay: Heir (read only before a farm is asked: an heir asks no rung that leaves home), Marry (a
  man of age: `askMatch`, by his spokesman), An empty farm (`claimableFarm`, `askLand` with the round's due), and the rest (plot, new
  land, service) left to the round's walk. Each offer is due at the round's next day (`dueRound`: autumn sday `ASK.day` noon, or day 2
  or 3 when made during the round), `round: true`, so the round's steps answer it (VB-692's `answerDue`), now first lapsing the stale
  (`staleWhy`), and a match lapsed because she married another asks anew that evening as a refused one does.
  `askMatch(st, m, due, {not, only, roll})` is VB-692's matcher for one man: the unmarried women of 18 or more read from the queue
  (every one of them is in it, an invariant), within walking reach (VB-288), sorted by the walking hours between the villages, at most
  `ASK.pool` (40) weighed (kin limit, a home, the score with its draw), none asked by `ASK.held` (5) open matches; the go-ahead draw
  (`roll`) at the asking, the means at the asking. The round's ask anew after a refusal (`askAgain`) now calls it (35 lines fell from
  `sim/round`). `ASK.hour`, `held`, `pool` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each with its meaning. A woman may be asked by up to `ASK.held` open
  matches: `matchWhy` (`sim/marriage`) and `matchBar` (`sim/matching`) refuse the sixth (they refused the second); the invariant of the
  open offers follows. The played pawn: its own asking makes no offer ('played'); one whose spokesman is another, unplayed, is asked for
  by him unless its wish is not this year (wishOf match false); one whose spokesman is played is the player's to ask. An offer to the
  played pawn waits for its answer until it is due (VB-690's `make`, unchanged). Counters: `askings`, `asksMade`, `asksNobody`,
  `asksWeighed`, `askWalk` in `VB.COUNTERS`.
