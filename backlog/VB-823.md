# VB-823: The asking day: a pawn who seeks makes its offer through the year
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-692
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: acts
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (The round as offers); docs/plans/VB-682.md, sections 2, 3 and 7
Goal: Offers about a life are made all the year (Kim, 16 Nov): each pawn who seeks reviews its wish on a day of its own once a season and, holding no open offer, makes one; the round only answers.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A due-queue `st.asking` of `{t, pawn}`, plain data in order of `t` (Rules for cost, 4), owned by the module that owns the wish (VB-680): `@state`, `src/owners.json`, the `WORLD` list of `src/sim/saves.js`, and invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): at most one entry a living pawn, none for the dead, the married who hold a place or the under-age. An entry is written when a pawn becomes a seeker (comes of age to marry; is grown and landless; is widowed; leaves service; is refused at the round) and at each asking for the next season, and is dropped where the pawn marries, takes a place or dies. The asking day is season day `id mod SEASON_DAYS`, at 6:00. A step reads only the head of the queue.
- [ ] At an asking the pawn's wish is reviewed (the ranking of `docs/plans/VB-682.md`, section 3) and, if `pawn.ask` is null, the offer of its best open rung is made by that basis's asking rule: VB-692's askers, called for one pawn in place of the round's walk. A son or daughter of the house has its `match` made by its spokesman on the member's day. `ASK` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each with its meaning: `passes` 3 (VB-692), `held` 5 (the most open offers one pawn is asked by on one basis; the next asker turns to its next best), `pool` 40 (the most candidates weighed at one asking, the nearest villages first).
- [ ] An offer whose asker or asked has died, married or taken a place lapses where it is next read (an asking of either, or the round), with no pass over the open offers; the asker's `pawn.ask` is cleared and it asks again on its next day.
- [ ] The played pawn is asked when an offer to it is made and may answer on any day until it is due; its own asking day makes no offer for it (it asks by the verb when it likes), and its spokesman, if unplayed, asks for it as for any member unless its wish says not this year.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7 and 42: marriages, service, claims, plots and settlers a decade, against VB-692's; the askings a year by season and by rung; the share that found nobody to ask; offers open on the round's first day. Expected: outcomes close to VB-692's, since an offer made earlier is answered at the same round; differences reported with their cause.
- [ ] Cost (rule 9; plan, section 7): seekers (about 60 to 120 a 1,000 living) x at most 4 askings a year x at most `ASK.pool` candidates, one table read each; counters `asksMade` and `asksWeighed` in `VB.COUNTERS`, `tests/counters.json` re-recorded with the changes listed; times left to the nightly bench.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): the day by id; no offer while one is open; the cap of 5; a lapse when the asked marries another; a save in mid-year plays as the unbroken run (`tests/saves.js`). `tests/commands.js` and `tests/record.js` pass.
