- [ ] The holder's prestige gains a term that follows the house's condition, up to a cap (VB-183 names it: of the size of a holder's other small terms). It is read where prestige is read; nothing is stored beside the condition.
- [ ] A tarred house (VB-279): the cap is about a fifth higher and the wear about a third slower, for as long as the tar lasts.
- [ ] The pawn panel names the term among prestige's parts; the page may draw a neglected house greyer.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous, small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): upkeep hours, prestige by household, and whether voices at the meeting shift.
