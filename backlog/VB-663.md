Notes:
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim, in the session reviewing the backlog): asked for when VB-331 was closed. Measurements and tuning do not generally block work: a task is marked done, and what is left to look at is collected here.
- How it works. A task that is done but leaves something unexplained adds a line to a free slot (a sub-agent of an orchestrated run leaves a `task` note naming this task, and the orchestrator writes the line). An item is small: about an hour, a throwaway script, the seeds and map named, nothing changed under `src/`. Its answer is a paragraph under its line; where it finds a defect or a teleport it opens a task and goes on. An item may be addressed to Kim ("For Kim:"), who answers under it.
- At most ten items. An agent that takes this task works the items there are and ticks them; with ten ticked it is done, and the next item opens a new collection. Until then it stays ready, and its items may be taken a few at a time.
- A measure that only wants the night's fixtures is not an investigation: it goes to VB-662.
