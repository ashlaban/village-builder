# VB-343: Finding ores: a village knows an upland vein once its pawns come on it at work
Status: done (2026-11-11)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: Goods, Goods of place and side crafts (In the ground: Known and found)
Goal: Kim, 11 Nov (VB-342's questions): bedrock, clay, peat and bog iron are known to all; the upland ores (iron, copper, lead and silver, tin, gold) are found. Knowledge is the village's: a played pawn sees what its village knows, a watcher sees everything, the unfound drawn faint.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The upland ores (iron, copper, lead-silver, tin, gold) are marked `hidden` in gen/geology; every deposit tile has a vein id (`w.vein`,
      `w.veinKind`), and `w.veinNear` names the hidden vein within 2 tiles over land of each land tile.
- [x] `st.finds`, a Map of village id to the vein ids it has found, owned by `sim/prospect` (`src/owners.json`), saved, with its
      invariant; a save without it loads with none found.
- [x] Finding: when a pawn finishes a job, standing within reach of a hidden vein its village does not know, the vein is found with
      chance work hours / `PROSPECT.hours` (60), from a stream of its own (`st.rng.prospect`). The whole vein is then known to that
      village only; the record gains an `oreFound` event (kept for good, under Land and farms), which names the finder and the village.
- [x] Views: a played pawn sees the ores its village knows, in the tile panel and the Ground overlay; a watcher sees all, a vein no
      village has found drawn faint, with "Not yet found" in the key and "which no village has found" in the tile panel.
- [x] `tests/prospect.js` (npm test); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks; golden entries listed below.
Notes:
- Kim's answers in the session (11 Nov): build finding ores first of VB-342's decisions; knowledge by village; a played pawn sees its
  village's, a watcher everything with the unfound faint.
- Impact. New module `sim/prospect` (kernel layer, after `sim/records`): reads `w.deposit`, `w.vein`, `w.veinKind`, `w.veinNear` and the
  pawn's household and village; writes `st.finds` and the record, and sets `visualDirty`. New coupling: `sim/agents` calls
  `prospect.worked` where a job is completed (`complete`, and a background job's end in `stepSide`). No abstraction beyond one list a
  village; a household's or a pawn's own secret was weighed and not built (Kim: by village).
- Cost (rule 9): one array read a finished job (`w.veinNear[tile]`), and only near a vein a search of the village's finds, at most the
  map's hidden veins (4 to 7 on the small maps measured; the list's declared cap). No pass, no tally: the chance is drawn as the work
  ends (rule 3). The page's overlay looks for a change twice a second while it is on (a sum over the villages' lists).
- Not built: prospecting as long work (the answer's "or prospects"), herding near a vein (only finished jobs count), telling another
  village of a find, and any use of a found vein. Founders who never work in the uplands find nothing, so prospecting should come with
  the first good that needs ore.
- Numbers, small map, 30 years (`w.veinNear` reach 2, 60 hours): populous seed 7, 4 hidden veins, 2 finds by 2 of 21 villages (copper in
  year 1, iron in year 25); populous seed 42, 6 hidden veins, 4 finds by 4 of 23 villages (lead-silver in years 1, 1 and 12 by three
  villages, iron in year 19); founders seed 11, 7 hidden veins, 2 farms, no find. No balance moves: nothing reads a find yet.
- Golden file, re-recorded: `world/7` and `world/42` gain `vein`, `veinKind` and `veinNear`; every `founders` and `heirs` entry (seeds 7
  and 42, seed and years 1 to 3) gains `finds` and the `prospect` stream in `rng`. No other entry changes.
- Validation: `npm test` 104 scripts pass; `npm run verify` 122 scripts pass, 11 cost targets out (`scaling.*`, `history.*`), as before
  this change.
