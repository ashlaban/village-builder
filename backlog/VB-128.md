# VB-128: Report: who is hungry and why
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: tooling
Phase: implement
Design ref: Systems overview (Across systems: food balance).
Goal: Populous medium at seed 7 in year 6 (at `243d9dd`) has a median household grain of 324 and still 32 of 120 households (239 people) hungry for over 30 days, and 35 deaths from starvation; the mean hides a wide spread (10th percentile 137, 90th 662). VB-039 (food help) and later tuning need to see who is short and why, in one command, instead of a scratch script each time.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `node tools/sim.js --report hunger` (text and `--json`) lists, for the year reached (or `--at Y`), the households with more than 30 hungry days: id, village, members by age band, fields (and how many were sown), grain at the harvest and now, hungry days by season, and the deaths from starvation or hunger in it.
- [ ] It ends with a summary: share of households and of people hungry, and the grain quartiles of all households, so a tuning change is compared by numbers.
- [ ] The report is listed in the `tools/sim.js` header comment and in `CLAUDE.md`.
- [ ] A line in `tests/tools.js` runs the report and checks that it prints the summary.
