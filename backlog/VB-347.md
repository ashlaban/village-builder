## Implementation notes
- Shape: `household.grain` and `village.grain` are `{[crop]: rations}`, keys of `CROPS` (barley alone); an empty store is `{}`. The good
  `'grain/<crop>'` (`Stores.grainGood(crop)`) is one crop's grain, `'grain'` any crop's, as the registry names `'cattle/cow'`. The ledger and
  the yearly totals count grain as `'grain'` whatever its crop (so they are unchanged). A pawn's stake stays one number of no crop.
- `sim/stores` (the functions changed or added, for VB-300 beside it): added `grainOf(holder, crop?)` (the one reader of the total;
  allocation-free, the crops added in `CROPS`' order from 0, so one crop's total is its number to the last bit), `grainGood`, `partsOk`,
  `partsSum`, `takeSpare(st, hh, n, why, into)`, `CROP_LIST`, `STAKE_CROP`, and the internal `takeGrain`, `weight`, `split`,
  `setHouseholdGrain`, `setVillageGrain`, `setGrain`, `isGrain`; changed `record` (a crop's grain recorded as `grain`), `field`, `set`
  (`setFood` became `setMeals`, `setVillage` went), `crossed` (reads `grainOf`), `put` (a `parts` argument: `'grain'` is put by its crops),
  `take` (an `into` argument: the crops it took), `move` (carries the crops), `cook` (grain through `takeSpare`), `thresh` (puts
  `grain/barley`), `start` (grain by crop), `villageDay`, `brew` (through `takeSpare`), `reserve` (grain is reserved by its crop),
  `reserved` (`'grain'` sums the crops' keys; a following rule gets the crop). No other function of the module changed.
- Takes of `'grain'` fall on each crop in proportion to what is held; the last crop with weight takes what is left, so one crop takes
  exactly n. `takeSpare` weighs each crop by what is held above its own reservation (the cook, the meal's grain above the seed, the brewer).
  `eatSeed` and spoiling take by what is held (VB-352 orders the seed eaten). Gifts (`sim/exchange`: the giver's take now comes before the
  receiver's put, which gets its crops; the events and ledgers are as before), shares, handing on and the village's filling use `move`;
  settlers' and claimants' provisions collect the crops of their takes (`into`) and found the household with them (the command
  `foundHousehold` takes grain by crop).
- Reservations: the standing plan's seed is `grain/barley` (`sim/plans`, `SEED`), counted at read by `seedNeed(st, hh, crop)` (the fields
  that bear or last bore the crop); a land project holds `grain/barley` (`sim/fields`, `sim/meadows`, the old-save path in `sim/saves`).
  The readers of the seed (`reserved(st, hh, 'grain')`) read the sum, as before.
- Readers: every `hh.grain`/`v.grain` read in `src/` goes through `Stores.grainOf` (`Sim.grainOf` for the page and the tools). Sowing checks
  and takes the field's crop (`grainOf(hh, fd.crop)`, `grain/<crop>`).
- Saves: the codec is generic; `oldGrain` in `sim/saves` turns an older save's number into `{barley: n}` and its plans' `grain` reservation
  into `grain/barley`. No world field: the `WORLD` list is unchanged. `src/owners.json` unchanged (the same fields, the same owner).
- `@state`: `household.grain, village.grain` in `sim/stores`; the households' and villages' lines in `sim/scenarios` say `{crop: rations}`.
- Checks: `tests/lib/invariants.js` (every crop of `CROPS`, none below 0, the sum is `grainOf`; a household's reservation goods are crop
  goods); new `tests/grain.js` (in `npm test`: loads the modules with rye added to `CROPS` in its own process: proportional takes, spoiling,
  a crop's take, moves, the gift verb, stakes, the cook and the meal by reservation, the village store, an old save loading as barley);
  `tests/stores.js` (one crop exact to the last bit, the refusals); the tests that set or read `hh.grain` by hand use `{barley: n}` and
  `Sim.grainOf`. `tools/stocks.js` reads `grainOf` calls as reads and crop goods and `takeSpare` as grain.
