# VB-347: Spinning and weaving: fleece to yarn to cloth, a loom, and who does it
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-144, VB-345
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The mill and the loom, the kiln and the quarry); `backlog/VB-185.md`
Goal: Fleece is spun into yarn as background work indoors and yarn woven into cloth on a loom the household builds; a practice says whose work it is. Slice 6's plan (VB-185, 11 Nov), from Kim's answers in the session.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Goods: `hh.yarn` (skeins) and `hh.cloth` (lengths), through `sim/stores` with reasons; cloth does not perish; the worth table gains a length of cloth at 40 rations and a skein at 12 (Proposed).
- [ ] `spin`: background work (it is held beside minding the house, cooking and the byre, and worked while indoors), 40 work hours a skein from one fleece (`TEXTILE.spinHours`), skill spinning; posted while the household has fleeces and under `TEXTILE.yarnStock` (4) skeins.
- [ ] The loom: `hh.loom`, built at home as long work, 12 hours and 2 loads of timber, once. `weave`: partial work at the loom, 12 work hours a length from 2 skeins, skill weaving; posted while the household has 2 skeins.
- [ ] The practice "Spinning and weaving are" (`Culture`): women's work (default) or everyone's indoor work, malleability 0.4; it sets the role ranks of `spin` and `weave` (women's: wife 7, maid 7, daughter 6, retired woman 5, others 0; everyone's: the same, and men 4).
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): the idle share by season and by sex, skeins and lengths made a household, fleeces left unspun. The hours are scaled to the game's 576 work hours a year so that dress for six takes about a third of one adult's year (acoup, "Clothing, How Did They Make It?", III: spinning about 85% of cloth's labour; checked by search on 3 Nov). The golden file is re-recorded and listed.
- [ ] The golden file is re-recorded where behaviour changes, its changed entries listed; `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
Notes:
- Numbers are Proposed by slice 6's plan (11 Nov) on Kim's answers to the shape; the hours are in the game's work hours (576 an adult a year) and, but for the textile shares, from general reading not checked by search: each task reports its numbers and the wrap-up tunes them.
