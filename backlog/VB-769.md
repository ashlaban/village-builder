# VB-769: Dealing on the green on a gathering day, and goods carried there on an estimate
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-302, VB-522
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: town
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Settlements, Towns and regions (Nobody calls a market; Dealing on the green; A household's estimate of a green); Exchange (The fair); docs/plans/VB-189.md, section 5.2
Goal: A market is a gathering day with much dealing, and nobody called it (Kim, 16 Nov): pawns bring goods to gatherings that happen anyway, each by its own estimate of who will come.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The fair's dealing (VB-302) runs at every occasion held on a green: the village's meeting, the season's rite (VB-522), the yearly gathering; among those present who brought goods, by VB-302's rules unchanged (proposals in seeded order, partners first, then 4 others; need decides; customary worth books). No occasion is created for dealing, and no pawn, village or calendar calls one. An ale evening is at a farm and is not such an occasion.
- [ ] Everyone taking part walks there with what it means to give (the hard rule): goods dealt change hands on the green and are carried home.
- [ ] A household's estimate of a green: the customary worth it dealt there, by kind of day (a village's own day; a yearly gathering), blended `DEAL.keep` 0.7 old to 0.3 new at each visit; at most `DEAL.greens` 8 greens a household, the least visited dropped (rule 2); a green never visited at `DEAL.unseen` 0.5 of its own green's, until stories carry it (VB-523). Plain data with an owner, in the save, with invariants.
- [ ] On the planning day before an occasion the head packs a load for its own green if it holds anything over its targets (VB-698). It sends a goer to another village's green only to deal when its estimate there exceeds the walk there and back at `DEAL.hour`: a day's work, 3 rations for 8 hours, times the season's busy share (`design.md`, How busy). The goer is a pawn who chose it through a verb of `src/sim/commands.js`, the played pawn's too ("Go to the rite at <village> to deal"), with the estimate shown as the head's (principle 14).
- [ ] One record line a green a day (households that dealt, those of other villages, worth by good), folded into yearly counts (rule 8); "a market" is what the chronicle calls a day with `DEAL.market` 8 or more households dealing.
- [ ] Reported, populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years, before and after: the share of customary worth dealt on greens by kind of day, at the yearly gathering and (with VB-770) at doors; goers who came only to deal; a host's calendar of dealing by day of the year; hungry household-days and growth; whether the households that deal, not only the average, are better fed. The mock's 23% to 40% of makers' dealing at gatherings is a toy's figure, for comparison only.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state: two households at a rite with goods each lacks deal; a household with nothing over its targets packs nothing; a goer is not sent when the walk costs more than the estimate; the estimate moves by 0.3 of the difference.
- Cost (rule 9): a rite or meeting day: about 170 households present island-wide x 12 proposals = about 2,000 evaluations over the occasion's hours, about 100 a six-minute step; 4 such days a year a village, and the yearly gathering as VB-302 has it; the estimate is 16 numbers a household, written on a visit and read on a planning day, no pass. Counters for proposals on a green and for goers, before and after; under 0.1 ms a game day over the year at `medium-ref` is the claim the wrap-up measures.
