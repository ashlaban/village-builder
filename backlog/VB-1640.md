Goal: A household may have as many projects under way as its people can work; the limit is its hands, not a rule.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] List first, in the task's notes, every place a project is refused or held because another of the household's is under way (the guards VB-1472 names: `Projects.ownOf` read by `landWhy`, the land step, `sim/aims`' menu, `buildStep`, `barnWhy`, `heirWhy`, the well's and the drain's checks, `byreWhy`, the cottage's and the foundation's checks, the `HOLDS.project` wait, the one outbuilding task a house), with what becomes of each.
- [ ] The checks (what a verb refuses, for every head, played or not, and for the player's plan tasks) no longer refuse a project because another is under way: a field beside a meadow beside a barn beside the foundation. What still refuses: the same making twice where one of a making is the rule (one cottage, one foundation, one barn, one byre, one well), one project a tile, and each project's own conditions. The `side` plans of VB-1623 (the played head's second field) become the general case: every open step's job has a key that cannot meet another's, for any head; a run with one project at a time keeps its keys.
- [ ] A head's own rule is its own choice and is not changed here: where the land step, the yearly look or `ownRung` begins something only while the household has no large project under way, that stays as the head's prudence (so `node tools/same.js --base REF --scenario founders,solo` and populous seed 7 small for 2 years show no change), written in the code as the head's choice and not as a refusal. How heads should choose among parallel projects (by hands, stores, the season) stays VB-1472's, with Kim: add what you learned to that task's notes through a `decision` note, and do not edit its file.
- [ ] The played head: several buildings and land projects planned at once are each worked in the plan's order; `Sim.farmView` reports each under way (`building` for the main building, each outbuilding's and the foundation's own `building`); no `why` anywhere says "has a ... under way" for a different making. `Sim.headView().projects` lists them all.
- [ ] `design.md` section 7 is not edited by this task (read-only): a `question` note only if a Decided point there still states the limit, with its text.
- [ ] Tests on small constructed states (`tests/farmtasks.js`, `tests/plans.js`, `tests/head.js`): a barn, a byre and the foundation under way together on a played farm, all carried through; a field and a barn together for an unplayed head when issued by the verb; the refusals that remain. A save and a load go on the same. `npm run verify` passes; baselines do not rise.
