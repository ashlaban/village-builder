# VB-122: The idle report measures waits from when a job is free, and counts switches
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: tooling
Phase: implement
Design ref: none. Job review, second pass, 30 Oct 2026 (`REVIEW.md`, gap 8).
Goal: `tools/idle.js` measures a job's wait from when it was first posted, so a 20-hour field that is worked over two days counts the night and the second morning as waiting, and a job taken within the hour it was posted is never seen. Work-arch tasks also report job switches (VB-107), which each had to count with its own script.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The wait of a job is measured for each spell in which it has free hands and nobody working it, from the moment it became free (posted, or left by its last worker) to the moment a pawn takes it; spells outside work hours are not counted.
- [ ] The report adds switches per pawn per year: a pawn at work on a job with work left that takes another job within the hour, with the cook's call after 14:00 (VB-022) counted apart.
- [ ] `--json` gives the same; `tests/tools.js` checks that waits are non-negative and switches are counted.
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged: `tests/golden.json` unchanged.
Notes / questions:
- The work arch's measurements in `REVIEW.md` (second pass) and VB-102 and VB-107 used a scratch script that already did both; this makes it the standard report.
