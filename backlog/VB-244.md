# VB-244: The page runs whole steps and draws pawns between them
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Rules for cost, 7 (only the page interpolates). Kim, 10 Nov 2026 (code review).
Goal: `src/page/speed.js` passes `dt * speed / HOUR_SECONDS` hours to `Sim.advance`, so a simulation step is as long as a frame and the same seed gives a different run by the frame rate: a seed reported from the page cannot be replayed headless. Kim decided on 10 November that the page runs whole six-minute steps and draws each pawn between its last two places.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `advanceSimulation` (`src/page/speed.js`) keeps the part of a step the frames have not yet used, in the page, and asks `Sim.advance` for whole steps only (VB-235's ticks); at "Uncapped" it asks for one step at a time as today. `Sim.advance` refuses, or rounds and says so in its `--help`d contract, a number of hours that is not whole steps.
- [ ] The page draws each living pawn, and what it carries or leads, between its place at the last step and the one before, by the share of the step's time gone (`src/page/animate.js`); a pawn that went indoors, came out or was placed (a move of more than a step's walk) is not drawn between. Nothing of the interpolation is written to `st` (the lint's page rule). The clock shown advances smoothly.
- [ ] A test in verify (jsdom or Chromium, `tests/browser.js`): the same seed run in the page at two frame rates for a game day ends in the same state as `Sim.advance` headless (the canonical hash); and a pawn's drawn place never lies outside the segment between its two last places.
- [ ] `tools/shot.js` screenshots or a short capture at 1× and 3× in the PR, with a sentence on how the walk reads against today's.
- [ ] VB-235's third criterion (a part of a step still moves the agents) is undone here: `advance` and `stepAgents` take whole steps only, and the remainder field goes from `st` if VB-235 put one there. `tests/golden.json` unchanged (headless runs already use whole steps).
- [ ] The frame's cost: `node tests/browser-speed.js` no slower than before beyond its spread. `npm run verify` passes.
Notes:
