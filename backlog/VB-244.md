# VB-244: The page runs whole steps and draws pawns between them
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Rules for cost, 7 (only the page interpolates). Kim, 10 Nov 2026 (code review).
Goal: `src/page/speed.js` passes `dt * speed / HOUR_SECONDS` hours to `Sim.advance`, so a simulation step is as long as a frame and the same seed gives a different run by the frame rate: a seed reported from the page cannot be replayed headless. Kim decided on 10 November that the page runs whole six-minute steps and draws each pawn between its last two places.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `advanceSimulation` (`src/page/speed.js`) keeps the part of a step the frames have not yet used, in the page, and asks `Sim.advance` for whole steps only (VB-235's ticks); at "Uncapped" it asks for one step at a time as today. `Sim.advance` refuses, or rounds and says so in its `--help`d contract, a number of hours that is not whole steps.
- [x] The page draws each living pawn, and what it carries or leads, between its place at the last step and the one before, by the share of the step's time gone (`src/page/animate.js`); a pawn that went indoors, came out or was placed (a move of more than a step's walk) is not drawn between. Nothing of the interpolation is written to `st` (the lint's page rule). The clock shown advances smoothly.
- [x] A test in verify (jsdom or Chromium, `tests/browser.js`): the same seed run in the page at two frame rates for a game day ends in the same state as `Sim.advance` headless (the canonical hash); and a pawn's drawn place never lies outside the segment between its two last places.
- [x] `tools/shot.js` screenshots or a short capture at 1× and 3× in the PR, with a sentence on how the walk reads against today's.
- [ ] VB-235's third criterion (a part of a step still moves the agents) is undone here: `advance` and `stepAgents` take whole steps only, and the remainder field goes from `st` if VB-235 put one there. `tests/golden.json` unchanged (headless runs already use whole steps).
- [x] The frame's cost: `node tests/browser-speed.js` no slower than before beyond its spread. `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass, its targets reported with their residuals.

Impact note (for the PR):
- Reads and writes: `src/sim/clock.js` (`ticking` takes whole steps, `stepsIn` refuses others; `step` always ends its step), `src/sim/agents.js`
  (`stepAgents(st, c)`, a step's dt fixed), `src/sim/commands.js` (`skip` refuses parts of a step), `src/sim/saves.js` (a part in a save is
  dropped). The page: `src/page/speed.js` keeps `stepShare` (the part of the next step asked for), the run it belongs to, and each agent's
  place, stride and indoors flag before the frame's last step (typed arrays in the closure); `src/page/animate.js` `placePawns()` computes the
  drawn place of every agent once a frame, read by the rigs, the oxen at the plough and the milked cow, the selection ring, talk bubbles, the
  lens and the played pawn's mark (`talk.js`, `play.js`); `pose` takes the drawn stride. The page now reads `VB['sim/rules']` (`SPEED`,

- Cost: one copy of the agents' places per frame that runs a step, and one pass over the agents per frame (both O(agents), no allocation once
  the arrays have grown); uncapped and skips do not copy (they draw where the last step left the pawns).
Implementation:
- Whole steps: `advanceSimulation` adds `dt * speed / HOUR_SECONDS / 0.1` to `stepShare`, runs its whole part a step at a time
  (`Sim.advance(st, 0.1)`), noting the places before the last; uncapped and skip run a step at a time as before. `drawingBetween()` is true
  only while `st` is the run stepped there and its clock is still the page's last step (a load or a debug skip draws where pawns are).
- Drawn place: between the place before the last step and the place now, by `stepShare`; a pawn indoors before or now, new since the copy, or
  moved more than a step's longest walk (`SPEED * HW / TICKS / ROUTE.minStep`, a lane, plus 1%) is drawn where it is.
- The clock shows the time the pawns are drawn at (`tick - 1 + stepShare` steps while drawing between, else `tick`), floored to 5 minutes:
  smooth, and at most a step and 5 minutes behind `st.t` (`tests/smoke.js`, `tests/browser-speed.js` allow that lag now).
- Callers brought in line: `tests/clock.js` (frame-sized chunks gone; parts refused and leave the state unchanged), `tests/spread.js`
  (uneven whole steps; saves at 12.0, 12.1, 12.2 and 12.1, 0.5), `tests/interrupts.js` (0.55 to 0.6), `tests/player.js` (end of work - 0.2),
  `tests/saves.js` (3000.75 to 3000.7), `tests/lib/clock.js` (`setClock` refuses a time between steps), `tests/speed.js` (whole steps only).
  No tool asked for a part of a step.
