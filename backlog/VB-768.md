# VB-768: Fields sized against the head's estimate of all its food
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-767
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: town
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Fields a household wants); Settlements, Towns and regions (A craftsman's food); docs/plans/VB-189.md, section 5.5
Goal: A household fed partly by something other than its fields holds fewer fields, by its head's own judgement (Kim, 16 Nov: the head sizes its fields against an estimate of all the household's food, by the head's trait).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `fieldsWanted` (`src/sim/fields.js`) subtracts from `FIELDS.want` x the year's rations what the head trusts its other sources to bring: the sum, over the sources that are not fields, of `c` x poor + (1 - `c`) x mean (`expects` and `caution` of VB-767), before dividing by a field's cautious net yield. The least is 0 fields when the rest covers the need, else 1; the most is what its hands can till, as now. Gifts and help count at their poor year only, whatever the head's caution (a head does not plan on charity: a close call, said in the task file).
- [ ] Nothing is given up in one year: a household lets go at most `FIELDS.letGo` 1 field a year, and a field let go is left as fields are left today (fallow, to be claimed). A household with fewer than 3 years of memory sizes its fields as today.
- [ ] The scenarios' starts (`src/sim/scenarios.js`: seed for the fields wanted) and the plan's review (`src/sim/plans.js`: `T.fields`) read the same function; where the fields wanted are explained to the player, the estimate is named as the head's (principle 14: "Ragnhild counts on 60 rations of fish in a poor year").
- [ ] **Numbers before and after** (this changes every household with a second source of food, today's fishers and cow keepers among them): founders small, 50 years, seeds 7 and 42; populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42. Reported: fields a household; grain harvested and grain in store a head; food by source against need (`tools/balance.js`); hungry household-days; yearly growth against principle 2 (a median seed that shrinks is a collapse and may be tuned away; anything between is a target out, reported and left). **Households, not only averages:** the hungry days of the households that let a field go against those that did not, and of the fifth with the most food from other sources; how many households hold no field at the end.
- [ ] Parameterisation (at most five short runs, seeds and map named): `FIELDS.memory`, `FIELDS.letGo` and which year is "poor" (the lowest, the second lowest, the lower quartile), with what each gave; the rest as a `tuning` note.
- [ ] A constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): two households with the same nets and the same eight years, a cautious head and a bold one: the bold one wants fewer fields; a household with no second source wants what it wants today; one good year lets no field go. `tests/counters.js` unchanged, or re-recorded with the entries listed.
- Cost (rule 9): no new loop; read at the planning day's review where `fieldsWanted` is already computed (170 households x their planning days x a sort of 8 numbers for each of 6 sources).

No target is stepped; the size is the head's own.
