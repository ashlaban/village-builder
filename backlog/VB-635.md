# VB-635: Founders: the couples' ages, and what a pool of ten costs
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-633
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, People, households and family records (Marriage, nursing and growth: Founders); Starting scenarios; Balance loops and targets (The growth budget: Founders, measured against it); backlog/VB-405.md
Goal: A founders' island is held by the size of its pool, not by the marriage gate: in VB-405's four seeds 47% of the years the unmarried waited had nobody free to marry and 22% only kin. Report it over the band's sixteen seeds, and try the one thing within the Decided start, the founding couples' ages.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The report first, with VB-633's `--report waiting` and `node tools/bands.js --runs founders --years 80` (sixteen seeds, small): growth by seed and by 20 years; the seeds that stand still (no more living at year 80 than at year 40) and those that died out; the waiting years by cause; the share of household-years with one member; the years each village spent at each option of the kin limit. VB-405's four seeds (3, 7, 11, 42; 80 years) are the figures to compare with: growth 1.1%, 0, 2.6%, 2.2%; nobody free 47% of waiting years, kin 22%, the score 6%, no home 1%, the means none.
- [ ] The founders' ages become named values in `src/sim/rules.js` with their meaning (`FOUNDERS`: the man's age 23 to 33, the wife 1 to 6 years younger and at least 19, the two servants 16 to 19), read by `sim/people`; with the values as built, `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`.
- [ ] Parameterisation, at most five short runs (founders small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 80 years; the values tried and what each gave, in the task file): younger couples (the man 20 to 26, the wife 18 to 23), which lengthen the years a founding wife bears children and bring the second generation closer in age; the servants' ages. A value is kept only if more of the four seeds grow and none that grew before now stands still; otherwise the ages stay and the trial is reported.
- [ ] What is not changed, being Decided: ten founders, five women and five men, four couples and two servants, all unrelated; the kin limit's start at first cousins; no one arrives before the ship (`design.md`, Starting scenarios; People, Marriage, nursing and growth: Founders).
- [ ] If the ages change: the tests that count the founders' start pass or follow, the fixtures are rebuilt by the night (they are keyed by the sources), and the numbers before and after are given over the band's sixteen seeds at 50 years (median growth, seeds above their start, seeds below half), the three growth targets named with their residuals.
- [ ] What is left goes to Kim as it is: a `tuning` note for a value the trial points to but did not keep, and a `decision` note that says how many of sixteen islands of ten stand still or die out by births alone, since the levers left are the number of founders (Decided) and the ship's year (slice 8).
