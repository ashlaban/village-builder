# VB-1476: A pawn's grain stake vanishes at its death
Status: done (2026-11-28)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: src/sim/stores.js (stakes), src/sim/cottage.js payOwed; AGENTS.md, Conventions (food moves through sim/stores)
Goal: A pawn's grain stake (sim/stores: one number of no crop; filled by shares paid to it at the moving round, sim/cottage payOwed, and
spent as provisions when it founds or settles) stays on the pawn when it dies. Nothing moves it: the grain leaves the world with no movement
and no reason, against AGENTS.md, Conventions (food moves through sim/stores operations, each with a good and a reason). VB-403's accounts
found it as the whole of what their food account could not explain: on populous small seed 7 the stakes of those who died were 149 rations
in year 6, 750 in year 7, and up to 8,435 (23% of the produce) in year 50; the report now books them as `died holding`.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] At a pawn's death the title to its stake passes at once, with its reason, where the design's inheritance sends what a person holds (the
  household it lived in, else its heir's); the grain stays in the store it lies in and is fetched by the new owner (Kim, 24 Nov: title moves at once, custody by a journey).
- [x] An invariant: no dead pawn holds a stake.
- [ ] `node tools/sim.js --scenario populous --seed 7 --map small --years 50 --report accounts`: `died holding` is 0 every year; numbers
  before and after (growth, food over need, hungry households), seeds 3, 7, 11.
- [ ] Why the stakes grow so large late in the run (shares paid to pawns who then live on in another's household?) is said in the PR.

## Notes
- Flag: Kim, 24 Nov (QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Archived 24 November 2026, question 4): title moves at once, custody by a journey. The stake as title in a named store is VB-1696; this task is its case at death.
- Decided: at death (sim/lifecycle leaveStake, before the pawn leaves its household) the stake moves into the household it lived in (reason handingOn, estate, as sim/heirs lays an estate), else the line's successor's household, else the first village's store; a stake already lies in its household's store, so no journey is owed.
- Why stakes grow late (read from payOwed, not measured): `own` is null when the pawn lives in the debtor's own household, so the share goes to the pawn's stake and nothing spends it until it founds or settles; stakes of those who stay and die accumulate.
- Unticked: the 50-year numbers (criteria 3), see the result.
