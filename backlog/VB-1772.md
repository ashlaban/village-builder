# VB-1772: Dated steps: work from a day and by a day, taken up on its day, with further hands when its window will be missed

Design ref: design.md section 7, Plans at four scales (The days ahead, How a step becomes work, Replanning); docs/plans/four-scales/README.md, 3.1 (a date, a dated step), 3.5 (The head's review) and 2.2 (the week serialises what the day parallelises)
Touches: sim/plans (reviewed); sim/day (allot); sim/hands (askFor, shortOf); sim/levies (grant); tests/day.js; tests/hands.js
Goal: What a household commits to beyond today is held as dates, not hours, and windowed work is manned to finish in its window
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A dated step (`from`, `by`, a goal or a kind and a place) in a household's or a pawn's days ahead, written by the default author (a window opening, a project's step) or through `plan`; on its `from` day its tasks open and the day's allotment weighs them as any; nothing plans hours beyond today.
- [ ] At the head's review a dated step its hands cannot finish by its window's end, by the head's estimate, is given further hands: members first, then hands asked of a neighbour (sim/hands); the rule reads the head's own range, not a constant band.
- [ ] Leave granted for a day ahead (VB-1763) and an outer levy's named days are dated steps, shown on the strip's days ahead; the rule binds three days, the page shows six.
- [ ] Windows missed a year by kind (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 3 years), before and after.
- [ ] Cost: on the staggered planning day; 200 households × 0.7 reviews a day × the few dated steps open.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10): `npm run verify` green, a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose (never skipped or quarantined) and named in the PR; no stuck pawn (no task held 48 hours without progress, as VB-1546's script counts); the stocks' accounts close (`node tools/sim.js --report accounts`: `unexplained` no larger than before).
- [ ] No village dies out: `node tools/sim.js --scenario founders|populous --map small --seed 7|11|42 --years 10`, all six runs: no settlement extinct by hunger and no collapse of its people (the living at year 10 against year 0, in the PR).
- [ ] Reported in the PR, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in those six runs, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed), before and after.
