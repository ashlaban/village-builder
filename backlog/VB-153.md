# VB-153: Two holders may marry, and a couple marries only with the means
Status: done (2026-11-01)
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: expansion
Phase: implement
Design ref: People, households and family records (marriage needs a place to live, by the residence custom); Inheritance and expansion. Review: `docs/review-stability.md`; `docs/review-cities.md`, sections 3.1 and 3.5.
Goal: Under the residence custom "with the land" a couple needs exactly one place, so two people who each hold a farm, or are first heir to one, could not marry: lone settlers and heiresses stayed single for years (populous tiny seed 3, year 15: 19 households of one, nearly all women of 25 to 35 who had held a farm alone for 7 to 14 years). Marriage was also early and almost universal (women first married at about 19; 0–5% of women of 45–54 never married), so hunger was the only brake once the land filled.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Two people with a place may marry. A holder keeps their farm and a spouse who is only first heir elsewhere moves to it and leaves that line (`heirRanking` passes over an heir whose spouse holds another farm). Of two holders, the couple lives where the other farm is best handed on (an heir who can keep it, then a landless adult of its village, then a child or nobody), then on the farm with more fields, the man's on a tie; with two first heirs, on the farm with more fields.
- [x] A holder who marries away hands the farm on (`handOn`, `src/sim/heirs.js`): to its next heir who can keep it (an adult, or a minor with a grown housemate as guardian), else to the landless adult of its village who has waited longest (a claim: `transfer` reason `claim`, no shares owed on it), else to a minor heir (sim/service then brings a guardian or empties it), else the farm stands empty and its grain and animals go with the holder. The holder's minor children go too, unless one of them keeps the farm.
- [x] When no adult is left in the house to be a minor heir's guardian, a farm passes to the first adult in the line of heirs, if there is one (`heirFor`), at a death as at a marriage: a child no longer inherits a distant farm and moves there alone.
- [x] The means to marry (`MARRIAGE.means`, 1.5 as tuned in the stability review; 1 at first): the chosen match goes ahead only when the household the couple will live in holds food (grain, rations, grain in sheaf, the larder) above its seed for a year of its members and one more; otherwise they wait, and the log says how many couples of a village put it off.
- [x] Service and guardians (VB-152) no longer take an adult with a plot coming.
- [x] Nobody married to a holder is allotted a plot (`allot`), and a cottage whose holder has married onto a farm meanwhile stands empty; `transfer` and `finishCottage` no longer take along a child who holds a farm of their own (found by `tests/invariants.js`: a woman who held a farm moved into her new husband's cottage, and her farm's holder was no longer a member). `tests/plots.js` and `tests/fresh.js` chose a holder's wife as a landless villager; they now choose unmarried or unlanded adults.
- [x] `tests/marriage.js`: two lone holders marry and hold one farm; with an empty store they put it off; a holder and a first heir of her village live on her farm and he leaves his line. `tests/guardians.js` sets up its orphans directly, since a grown heir in line now comes first.
- [x] Re-record the golden file: the heirs runs change from year 1.
Notes / questions:
- Kim, 2026-11-01 (stability review): two holders may marry (`QUESTIONS.md`, Towns question 5, as proposed), and marriage needs means; no later minimum ages.
- "The other farm goes to its next heir": read as an heir who can keep it. Handing a farm to a child with no adult in the house emptied it at once (populous tiny seed 3, 30 years: 15 farms handed on at marriages, many to a child brother, and 17 farms emptied for want of grown kin); with the order above, 18 hand-ons and 11 emptied, most in famine years or where nobody in the village waited for land. Claiming such empty farms is VB-033.
- Over 30 years at populous tiny seed 3, 62–99 couples put off marrying for want of means against 62–68 marriages.
- 2026-11-01, follow-up: as first built, the means rule kept the lone from marrying (six long runs: lone holders of 20–49 at the end 39 → 53 and 39 → 92 on the small map, households of one up at 4 of 6 runs): a lone holder or a new settler's farm rarely holds a year of food for two. Historically the check bore on young couples setting up on a full farm, not on a widower or a lone settler, who remarried quickly. A home with fewer than two adults is now exempt; `tests/marriage.js` checks both sides. Re-recorded the golden file (heirs runs).
- 2026-11-01, tuning: at 1 year the check seldom bound, since the moving round falls just after the harvest, when stores are at their highest: births and deaths from hunger stayed near the baseline. At 1.5 years (homes of fewer than two adults still exempt), over the four tiny-map runs of 80 years with VB-154: deaths from hunger per 1,000, mean of the decades, 1.36 before and 1.09 at 1 year → 0.78; hungry days per household a year 2.57 and 2.24 → 1.75; households of one after year 20 at 6–12% (20–25% before); women's mean age at first marriage 21–22.5 (19–20.6 before).
