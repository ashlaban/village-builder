# VB-196: The moving round under 50 ms a step on the late fixtures
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion; People, households and family records (marriage). `docs/roadmap.md`, sections 5.2 (no step over 50 ms, Decided, Kim, 3 November) and 5.3 (rule 5, stagger and spread).
Goal: In populous small at seed 7, the moving round takes 37 ms in one call at year 5. At year 40 it takes 485 to 539 ms called right after loading the save, and 897 ms in its place in year 41: the game freezes once a game year (3 November, this container). A CPU profile of one round on the year-40 save puts 444 of its 511 ms in `heirRanking`. Most of that is `ownFirstOf` (423 ms), which the round recomputes after every marriage, and `handsOnWell`, which ranks heirs again for each pair the marriage loop weighs (`both`, 382 ms). `kinPressure` takes 119 ms. Bring the longest step of the round under 50 ms. Blocked until VB-193 (the fixtures) is done.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] First, keeping behaviour: the round's caches (`own`, `places`, `handed`, `means` in `movingRound`; the rankings `kinPressure` uses) are refreshed only for what a marriage changes: the two families' households and the farms they hold or are first heirs to. They are no longer dropped whole after every marriage. `tests/golden.json` unchanged. The round's time and `roundRank` (VB-192) on `small-y40` and `medium-y60` reported, before and after.
- [ ] Only if the round still takes over 50 ms in one call on `medium-y60`: spread it, one village's marriages, handovers and claims per 6-minute step on the round's day, in village order (rule 5), with the parts that span villages (matches across villages, `settleRound`) in a last step. This changes behaviour: re-record the golden file and list the changed entries. Report marriages a year, the mean age at first marriage and the share of women 45 to 54 never married, for populous small at seeds 7 and 42 over 30 years, before and after; each within 10%.
- [ ] The longest single step on the round's day under 50 ms on `small-y40` and `medium-y60`, timed as VB-088's history check times steps. `roundRank` and `roundPairs` in `tests/cost-baseline.json` lowered.
- [ ] The cost named in the PR: heir rankings per round, before and after (households times calls).
- [ ] `npm run verify` passes.
