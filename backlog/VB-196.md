# VB-196: The moving round within the late-fixture step gates
Status: done
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion; People, households and family records (marriage). `docs/roadmap.md`, sections 5.2 (no step over 60 ms, Decided, Kim, 3 November; revised 6 November: small 60 ms, medium temporarily 400 ms until VB-201) and 5.3 (rule 5, stagger and spread).
Goal: In populous small at seed 7, the moving round takes 37 ms in one call at year 5. At year 40 it takes 485 to 539 ms called right after loading the save, and 897 ms in its place in year 41: the game freezes once a game year (3 November, this container). A CPU profile of one round on the year-40 save puts 444 of its 511 ms in `heirRanking`. Most of that is `ownFirstOf` (423 ms), which the round recomputes after every marriage, and `handsOnWell`, which ranks heirs again for each pair the marriage loop weighs (`both`, 382 ms). `kinPressure` takes 119 ms. Bring the longest step under 60 ms on small-y40 and temporarily under 400 ms on medium-y60; VB-201 restores medium to 60 ms. Blocked until VB-193 (the fixtures) is done.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] First, keeping behaviour: the round's caches (`own`, `places`, `handed`, `means` in `movingRound`; the rankings `kinPressure` uses) are refreshed only for what a marriage changes: the two families' households and the farms they hold or are first heirs to. They are no longer dropped whole after every marriage. `tests/golden.json` unchanged. The round's time and `roundRank` (VB-192) on `small-y40` and `medium-y60` reported, before and after.
- [x] The direct round is under the temporary 400 ms gate on `medium-y60` (329.952 ms), so village scheduling is deferred to VB-201. When VB-201 changes ordering, preserve the original safeguards: plain-data progress, save/load determinism, exactly-once decisions, and 30-year demographic comparisons for populous small seeds 7 and 42 (marriages/year, first-marriage age, women 45–54 never married), each within 10%, before accepting golden changes.
- [x] The longest single step on the round's day is under 60 ms on `small-y40` (53.713 ms) and temporarily under 400 ms on `medium-y60` (333.546 ms), timed as VB-088's history check times steps. The earlier cache stage lowered `roundRank` and `roundPairs` growth baselines; this continuation lowers `heirRank` further and retains the ceiling for unchanged pair work.
- [x] The cost named in the PR: heir rankings per round, before and after (households times calls).
- [x] `npm run verify` passes.
