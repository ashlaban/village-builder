# VB-310: Refactor: Obligations as a module, the heirs' shares its first kind
Status: done (2026-11-18)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, Loans, obligations and cases (Obligations); Inheritance, Handover and shares (Shares still owed); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, section 4.4 (Obligations)
Goal: Slice 5's loans, rents, labour owed and wages are obligations. Today the one kind there is, a sibling's share still owed, lives on the pawn (`pawn.owed`, `pawn.owedFrom`) and is paid by a loop over pawns at the moving round (`src/sim/cottage.js`). Give every explicit claim one owner, `sim/obligations`, with a due-queue, and move the shares into it without changing behaviour, so the loans that follow add a kind, not a module.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A new module `sim/obligations` (VB-012 convention, `@sys` and `@state` lines, under 400 lines, in `src/owners.json` as the owner of what it writes; remove the planned name if `plannedOwners` holds it) keeps `st.obligations` (array by id, ids never reused) of `{id, kind, debtor, creditor, owes, due, every?, enforcer, status, delivered, since}`, with `debtor` and `creditor` as plain party refs (`{kind: 'household' | 'village' | 'pawn', id}`), `owes` as rations or work hours, and `status` one of open, part, met, overdue, defaulted, forgiven, lapsed.
- [x] A live index of open obligations by debtor and by creditor, and a due-queue `st.obligationsDue` sorted by hour then id; a step touches only what is due (rule 4); met, forgiven and lapsed ones leave both and are told once in the record (rule 1). Both are caches with one writer, rebuilt and compared in a test.
- [x] Operations `create`, `fulfil` (part or whole, with a reason, goods through `sim/stores`), `forgive`, `transfer` (to a successor) and `due(st, party)`; no other module writes the records.
- [x] The heirs' shares become obligations of kind `share` (debtor: the farm's household; creditor: the sibling as a pawn), paid at the moving round from grain above the reserve in the same order as today (by the sibling's id), with the same events; `pawn.owed` and `pawn.owedFrom` go, and every reader (cottage's start in life, the panels, the tools) reads `due` instead.
- [x] Saves (`WORLD` in `src/sim/saves.js`), plain data (`tests/plaindata.js`), invariants (`checkState`: every live obligation's parties exist, delivered at most owed, the due-queue holds exactly the open ones), a counter for the due step in `VB.COUNTERS`.
- [ ] Refactor rules (`BACKLOG.md`): `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`, counters unchanged but the new one, `npm run verify` passes; the PR says what fell (the pawn fields, the loop over the living at the round). (Unticked: it prints differing leaves, since the state fields moved, and one record line was added on purpose, the lapse of a dead sibling's share; the leaves are named in the notes, and no others differ. Counters and verify hold.)
Cost: open shares at `medium-ref` are counted in the PR (tens expected); the round's loop over the living (`payOwed`, `st.living`) becomes a walk over the open shares; the due-queue step is a comparison a step when nothing is due.
Order: first in slice 5, beside VB-311 and VB-313 (different files). VB-312 (loans) and VB-319 (wages) add kinds to it.

- Decisions (Deciding without Kim), each also in the inbox:
  - A share's term ends with the moving round's first day and goes on a year; the payment stays the round's (`payOwed` pays every open share whenever a round is held). Paying from the queue's step would move the payment out of its place in the round (after service, before prestige) and stop the debug round from paying. The step only moves a share to its next term; overdue is for kinds with one term (VB-315).
  - What a dead sibling was owed lapses at the next round, told once on its record. Before, the share stayed on the dead pawn, never paid: only the record gains a line.
  - `oweShare` keeps `pawn.owedFrom`'s quirk: when a second farm comes to owe a sibling, what the first still owes passes to it; and a share whose debtor household dissolved stays open, unpaid. VB-1155 mends both (a change to behaviour, with numbers).
  - `WORLD` in `src/sim/saves.js` lists tile fields; `st.obligations` is state, saved by the codec like every `st` field, so nothing was added there. An obligation is a claim, not a stored quantity: no line in `tools/stocks.js`.
  - `lapse` is an operation beside the four named, since `lapsed` is a status and nothing else reaches it.
