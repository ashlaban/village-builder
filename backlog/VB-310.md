# VB-310: Refactor: Obligations as a module, the heirs' shares its first kind
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, Loans, obligations and cases (Obligations); Inheritance, Handover and shares (Shares still owed); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, section 4.4 (Obligations)
Goal: Slice 5's loans, rents, labour owed and wages are obligations. Today the one kind there is, a sibling's share still owed, lives on the pawn (`pawn.owed`, `pawn.owedFrom`) and is paid by a loop over pawns at the moving round (`src/sim/cottage.js`). Give every explicit claim one owner, `sim/obligations`, with a due-queue, and move the shares into it without changing behaviour, so the loans that follow add a kind, not a module.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A new module `sim/obligations` (VB-012 convention, `@sys` and `@state` lines, under 400 lines, in `src/owners.json` as the owner of what it writes; remove the planned name if `plannedOwners` holds it) keeps `st.obligations` (array by id, ids never reused) of `{id, kind, debtor, creditor, owes, due, every?, enforcer, status, delivered, since}`, with `debtor` and `creditor` as plain party refs (`{kind: 'household' | 'village' | 'pawn', id}`), `owes` as rations or work hours, and `status` one of open, part, met, overdue, defaulted, forgiven, lapsed.
- [ ] A live index of open obligations by debtor and by creditor, and a due-queue `st.obligationsDue` sorted by hour then id; a step touches only what is due (rule 4); met, forgiven and lapsed ones leave both and are told once in the record (rule 1). Both are caches with one writer, rebuilt and compared in a test.
- [ ] Operations `create`, `fulfil` (part or whole, with a reason, goods through `sim/stores`), `forgive`, `transfer` (to a successor) and `due(st, party)`; no other module writes the records.
- [ ] The heirs' shares become obligations of kind `share` (debtor: the farm's household; creditor: the sibling as a pawn), paid at the moving round from grain above the reserve in the same order as today (by the sibling's id), with the same events; `pawn.owed` and `pawn.owedFrom` go, and every reader (cottage's start in life, the panels, the tools) reads `due` instead.
- [ ] Saves (`WORLD` in `src/sim/saves.js`), plain data (`tests/plaindata.js`), invariants (`checkState`: every live obligation's parties exist, delivered at most owed, the due-queue holds exactly the open ones), a counter for the due step in `VB.COUNTERS`.
- [ ] Refactor rules (`BACKLOG.md`): `tests/golden.json` unchanged, counters unchanged but the new one, `npm run verify` passes; the PR says what fell (the pawn fields, the loop over the living at the round).
Cost: open shares at `medium-ref` are counted in the PR (tens expected); the round's loop over the living (`payOwed`, `st.living`) becomes a walk over the open shares; the due-queue step is a comparison a step when nothing is due.
Order: first in slice 5, beside VB-311 and VB-313 (different files). VB-312 (loans) and VB-319 (wages) add kinds to it.
