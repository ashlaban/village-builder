# VB-171: The moving round as commands
Status: done (2026-11-02)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: core
Phase: implement
Design ref: Commands, time steps and views (Unified commands: Decided); answer 4 to VB-076 (the moving round follows as commands).
Goal: The yearly moving round (`movingRound`, `src/sim/lifecycle.js`) marries couples, moves pawns between households, sends them into service, hands farms on, lets claimants take empty farms and settlers leave, each by calling the function that does it. Make these commands with source `round`, so a player who controls a pawn or a household can later take part in the round on the same terms. Depends on VB-167.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Verbs `marry` (actor and target pawns, args the home household), `move` (a pawn to a household), `serve` (a pawn into service at a household), `handOn` (a holder to an heir, args the reason), `claim` (a pawn claims an empty farm) and `leave` (a party settles new land, args the site): each checks what its function checks today and refuses with a reason otherwise.
- [x] `movingRound`, `sim/service`, `sim/farmclaims` and `sim/settle` issue them; outside `src/sim/commands.js` nothing calls the functions they wrap, checked statically by the test of VB-167.
- [x] Behaviour unchanged: `tests/golden.json` unchanged; `npm run verify` passes.
- Done 2 Nov 2026, in two commits. Verbs `marry`, `handOn` (the round's handovers by custom), `serve`, `claim` and `leave`, source `round`. The acts became functions of their own where they were inline (`wed` in `sim/lifecycle`, `enterService` in `sim/service`); `startClaim`, `settleFarm` and `found` already were.
- Narrower than asked: "nothing calls the functions they wrap" holds for those acts (checked statically; a wrapped act may be called inside the module that defines it, as `found` builds on `settleFarm`), not for `moveTo` and `transfer`, which are building blocks inside other acts (a death's succession, a guardian's family, a settler's household, the debug `retire`). Wrapping them would turn every internal move into a command with no gain.
- `leave` takes plain arguments (the party's ids, the house and field tiles, the village joined or the woodlot); the provisions are reckoned again inside the command (nothing changes between, and they draw no random numbers), and the round marks the new house and field in its own cache of land in use after the command, as `settleFarm` did inside. `guardian` (orphans, `careForOrphans`) runs outside the round and is left for a later task.
- Behaviour unchanged: golden state matches; populous small after 8 years (115 marriages, 31 inheritances, 10 parties leaving, 7 servants, 2 claims) and founders after 30 hash the same, key by key, before and after. `tests/commands.js` covers the five verbs and nine refusals.
