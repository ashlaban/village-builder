# VB-1739: A job's farm, field, meadow and stretch held as ids, and agent.job as the job's key
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: backlog/VB-247.md (its notes of 22 and 25 Nov); AGENTS.md, Conventions (ids are array indexes; st is plain data)
Touches: sim/jobs (post); sim/agents; tools/writes.js; tests/codec.js
Goal: A job names the records it works on by id, and a pawn its job by key, so a save holds none of these as shared objects
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A job holds `farm`, `field`, `meadow` and `plot` as ids (`field` and `meadow` as the id within their farm, beside the farm's id) and `stretch` as its fence's place (`{village | farm, index}`); a helper in `sim/agents`, where jobs are owned, gives the record (`farmOf(st, j)`, `fieldOf(st, j)`, ...), and every reader goes through it.
- [ ] `agent.job` and `agent.side.*` name the job by its key, not the object; a pawn holding a job after `dropJob` (hay, 3 pawn-hours a year at populous seed 7) keeps its behaviour: the dropped job is kept where it is read, or the change is named with its numbers.
- [ ] `tools/writes.js`: the `ALIAS` entries for `job.field`, `job.meadow`, `job.farm`, `job.plot` and `job.stretch` go or follow the helpers; no new unresolved write (VB-243).
- [ ] Shared references in a save of populous, seed 7, small, 2 years, before and after (880 after VB-247: 429 job.farm, 259 agent.job, 113 job.meadow, 39 job.stretch, 28 job.field, 12 others); `FORMAT` rises by one.
- [ ] `node tools/same.js --base REF` differs only in these back-references (ids for objects): no number of the simulation moved (living, households, grain; populous and founders, small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years).
- [ ] `npm run format` and `npm run verify` pass.
