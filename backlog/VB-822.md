# VB-822: `move`: one act for every change of household, with its reason
Status: done (2026-11-17)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: acts
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (The round as offers); docs/plans/VB-682.md, section 5
Goal: Every change of household goes through one verb, `move`, with a reason and the minors who follow, so that the journey, the event and (from VB-692) whose yes it needs are written once; the acts that also change a right keep their verbs and issue it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A verb `move(actor, target, {reason, with})` in `src/sim/commands.js`: the actor changes to household `target`; `reason` is one of marriage, heir, service, guardian, care, home, settle, found; `with` lists the minors who follow. Its check says why not: not living, no such live household, lives there already, an unknown reason, a minor in `with` whom the actor does not keep (its child, its ward, or a child of the household it moves with). Whose yes it needs is `moveWhy`, added by VB-692; here the check holds only what makes the move possible.
- [x] `move` is the only caller of `moveTo` (sim/heirs), which `tests/commands.js` checks. The ten call sites go through it: `wed` (sim/lifecycle), `transfer` and `handOn` and `extinct` (sim/heirs), `enterService`, `careForOrphans` and `takeIn` (sim/service), `holdInTrust` and `endTrust` (sim/trust), `foundHousehold` (sim/membership).
- [x] What stays apart, each issuing `move` for its people: `marry` (a union), `handOn` (a holding), `goToKin` and `goBack` (a trust), `leave`, `foundHousehold` and `finishCottage` (a household founded). The verb `serve` is removed: reason `service` sets the servant flag (and `servedSince`, if VB-634 has landed) and writes the service event. The guardian's coming, kin taken in and the placing of a dissolved household's people thereby gain a verb and an actor (three acts found with no verb; who chooses them is VB-695's).
- [x] The `moved` event carries its reason; the log lines and life events are unchanged in text.
- [x] Behaviour unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` (the seeds and maps it runs by default). The PR says what fell: the call sites, the two copies of `setsOut`, the verbs.
- [x] `tests/commands.js` covers `move` (each refusal, each reason, the minors who follow); `tests/record.js` passes; one at a time with VB-681 and VB-690 in `src/sim/commands.js`.

Noted by VB-682, 16 November 2026.

## Implementation (VB-822, 17 November 2026)
- `move` in `src/sim/commands.js` replaces `serve`. Check: not living, no such live household, lives there already, `there is no reason X
  to move`, `with` not a list, a follower not living or `not a minor whom N keeps`. Its run calls `moveTo` (sim/heirs) for the actor and
  then each follower; reason `settle` walks on its stakes (`carry` false, as sim/settle's foundings did); reason `service` sets the
  servant flag and writes the service event and log line, with the issuer's words (`args.event`, `args.line`, `args.other`) or the plain
  ones a dissolved household's people had; the one `setsOut` is there.
- `moveTo` takes the reason and puts it on the `joined` record entry (`reason` added to the record's FIELDS in sim/records).
- Call sites: `wed` (now sim/marriage) `marriage`; `transfer` `heir` (heir, then the spouse with the minors, or the heir with them);
  `handOn` `marriage` (the giver with its minor children); `extinct` `care` or `service`, each its own; `careForOrphans` `guardian` and
  `takeIn` `care` (one `moveFamily`: the lead, then the spouse with the children); `enterService` is gone, its words built by `serving`
  and issued by `serve` in sim/service (three callers); `holdInTrust` `care`, `endTrust` `home`; `foundHousehold` `found`, or `settle`
  for settlers. `opportunities` checks the service item with `move`; its item keeps the name `serve` (an opportunity, read by the page).
- What fell: ten direct `moveTo` calls; the two copies of `setsOut` (sim/heirs, sim/service); the verb `serve` and `enterService`;
  sim/heirs no longer imports `setServant` or `journeyOf`, sim/service no longer `setServant` or `moveTo`.
- Decided (Deciding without Kim): "a child of the household it moves with" is read as its spouse's child (a step-child); a spouse is
  no minor, so a family moves as two moves, the lead first and then the spouse with the children, which keeps the order of the old
  calls. A follower is not required to live in the actor's household (the criterion does not ask it; moveWhy, VB-692, may).
- `tests/commands.js`: `moveTo` is called only by the verb (statically, sim/heirs included); each refusal, each of the eight reasons, the
  minors who follow, the reason on the record, the servant made only by service.
