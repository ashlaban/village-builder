# VB-822: `move`: one act for every change of household, with its reason
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: acts
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (The round as offers); docs/plans/VB-682.md, section 5
Goal: Every change of household goes through one verb, `move`, with a reason and the minors who follow, so that the journey, the event and (from VB-692) whose yes it needs are written once; the acts that also change a right keep their verbs and issue it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A verb `move(actor, target, {reason, with})` in `src/sim/commands.js`: the actor changes to household `target`; `reason` is one of marriage, heir, service, guardian, care, home, settle, found; `with` lists the minors who follow. Its check says why not: not living, no such live household, lives there already, an unknown reason, a minor in `with` whom the actor does not keep (its child, its ward, or a child of the household it moves with). Whose yes it needs is `moveWhy`, added by VB-692; here the check holds only what makes the move possible.
- [ ] `move` is the only caller of `moveTo` (sim/heirs), which `tests/commands.js` checks. The ten call sites go through it: `wed` (sim/lifecycle), `transfer` and `handOn` and `extinct` (sim/heirs), `enterService`, `careForOrphans` and `takeIn` (sim/service), `holdInTrust` and `endTrust` (sim/trust), `foundHousehold` (sim/membership).
- [ ] What stays apart, each issuing `move` for its people: `marry` (a union), `handOn` (a holding), `goToKin` and `goBack` (a trust), `leave`, `foundHousehold` and `finishCottage` (a household founded). The verb `serve` is removed: reason `service` sets the servant flag (and `servedSince`, if VB-634 has landed) and writes the service event. The guardian's coming, kin taken in and the placing of a dissolved household's people thereby gain a verb and an actor (three acts found with no verb; who chooses them is VB-695's).
- [ ] The `moved` event carries its reason; the log lines and life events are unchanged in text.
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` (the seeds and maps it runs by default). The PR says what fell: the call sites, the two copies of `setsOut`, the verbs.
- [ ] `tests/commands.js` covers `move` (each refusal, each reason, the minors who follow); `tests/record.js` passes; one at a time with VB-681 and VB-690 in `src/sim/commands.js`.
