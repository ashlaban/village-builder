# VB-302: The fair: barter between strangers at the gathering
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-295
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, The fair: barter between villages (10 Nov); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, sections 4.5 and 5.1 to 5.4
Goal: Kim, 10 Nov: a barter economy until coin, and the fair is the one its gathering bears. Households of different villages exchange what their places give.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A swap is one command that changes everything or nothing (target-v2, 4.1): goods move through `sim/stores` with a good and a reason, custody from load to load, and the residual in customary worth is booked between the two pawns who dealt.
- [ ] `accept = floorOk && gain + allowance >= 0` for both, with `Needs.value` for gain; a keeping good counts at its worth up to 30 rations' worth held; a hard bargain is allowed and booked as a breach (VB-299).
- [ ] Finding deals: each day of the gathering, pawns with a load propose in seeded order (a stream of their own in `src/sim/rng.js`), first to those they hold a balance with, then to 4 others present, on each of the 3 days; one pawn deals for its household, from its load (30 rations' worth on the back, 90 with a cart and two oxen; animals walk); the work grows with those present, not with their pairs (rule 2), and has a counter.
- [ ] Counted and reported in the fair's log line: deals, rations' worth moved by good, deals that failed for want of change, hard bargains.
- [ ] The player's pawn proposes and answers through the same verb; its allowance does not bind it, and the floor warns (The gift, The player's gift).
- [ ] Invariants: goods conserved over a fair; no household below its floor by a deal unless the player's.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous, small, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42, 15 years, `tools/balance.js` and `--report hunger`): hungry household-days, spoilt grain, the stores of the richest tenth, by household and not only on average. A small fair is an accepted result (The fair, A small fair is the right result).
Notes:
- 10 Nov 2026: written from Kim's answers to the markets review (`docs/review-markets.md`; `QUESTIONS.md`, "VB-183: the gathering, the fair and goods of place"). Its numbers were settled by Kim's answers of 10 November (`QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-183"; `design.md`, sections 8, 9 and 11).
- Cost named (Kim agreed, 10 Nov): at most 60 dealing pawns a gathering times about 12 proposals a day, spread over the hours of the fair's days; with the gathering the slice claims 2 ms a game day and no step over 60 ms on `medium-ref`.
