# VB-092: Store views for households and villages
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player, commands and views (Store views: resource stores can be inspected for every level and dimension of entity; Decided); Claims, ownership and territory, One scope model for overlays, stores and trees (Agreed, 30 Oct).
Goal: Stores can be seen only one household at a time, in the household panel. The design wants them for any level of entity, through the same scope model as the ownership overlay (VB-043). Build the first version: households and villages. Depends on VB-043; if it is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `Sim.storesOf(st, scope)` returns, for the households in a scope (`inScope`, VB-043) with `dim` "owns", one row per household and a total row. The columns are the stores that exist when it runs: grain, meals (`hh.meals`, VB-259), the larder's foods (`hh.larder`, by `FOODS`), seed set aside, unthreshed sheaves, firewood, stones (`hh.stone`, VB-010, done), and, once their tasks are done, flour (VB-009) and shares owed. A village scope adds the village store (`v.grain`, VB-039) as its own row.
- [x] A "Stores" panel opens from the village panel and the household panel: a table of those rows, with each store in days of the household's food where that applies (grain, meals, the larder's foods, flour) and in plain units otherwise. It updates once a game day, not every frame.
- [x] Selecting a household row selects that household, as clicking its farm does.
- [x] A test: on the heirs scenario at day 0, the village A totals equal the sums over its households, and the household rows equal the household panel's values.
- [ ] Building the rows for the largest village on populous medium takes under 5 ms (reported in the PR). Unticked: a time on a shared machine is a description, left to the nightly bench; described once, populous medium seed 7 at year 2, its largest village (12 households): 1.2 ms a build, most of it inScope's scan of the 56,000 tiles' owners.
- [x] No simulation change: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`.

Notes (VB-092, 25 Nov):
- `storesOf` lives in `sim/scopes` beside `inScope` (a scope's view, read-only); `Sim.storesOf` and `Sim.inScope` join the facade. Rows hold raw units and the row's need (rations a day); the page divides, so the sim stays free of presentation.
- Columns: grain, seed (part of the grain), flour, meals, the larder's FOODS (`held: 'larder'`), sheaves, firewood, peat, stone (loads over rocks), shares owed (`owed`, debtor the household, kind share). Fresh food (`hh.fresh`) is not a store column: the criterion names the larder. The panel hides a column at zero in every row, grain always shown.
- The village row's grain is in days of its households' need; the total row sums the households only, so the village store stays its own row.
- The panel is a pane of the dock's drawer (`page/storeview`), rebuilt once a game day; from a household it shows that household's row, with a button to its whole village.
- No work counter: `storesOf` is a view's read on a click and once a game day while open, not recurring simulation work.
