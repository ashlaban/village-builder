# VB-092: Store views for households and villages
Status: blocked
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: claims
Phase: implement
Design ref: Commands, time steps and views (Store views: resource stores can be inspected for every level and dimension of entity; Decided); Claims, ownership and territory, One scope model for overlays, stores and trees (Agreed, 30 Oct).
Goal: Stores can be seen only one household at a time, in the household panel. The design wants them for any level of entity, through the same scope model as the ownership overlay (VB-043). Build the first version: households and villages. Depends on VB-043; if it is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `Sim.storesOf(st, scope)` returns, for the households in a scope (`inScope`, VB-043) with `dim` "owns", one row per household and a total row. The columns are the stores that exist when it runs: grain, seed set aside, unthreshed sheaves, firewood, and, once their tasks are done, stones (VB-010), rations (VB-022), flour (VB-009) and shares owed. A village scope adds the village store (`v.grain`, VB-039) as its own row.
- [ ] A "Stores" panel opens from the village panel and the household panel: a table of those rows, with each store in days of the household's food where that applies (grain, rations, flour) and in plain units otherwise. It updates once a game day, not every frame.
- [ ] Selecting a household row selects that household, as clicking its farm does.
- [ ] A test: on the heirs scenario at day 0, the village A totals equal the sums over its households, and the household rows equal the household panel's values.
- [ ] Building the rows for the largest village on populous medium takes under 5 ms (reported in the PR).
- [ ] No simulation change: the golden file is unchanged.
