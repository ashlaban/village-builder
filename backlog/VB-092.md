# VB-092: Store views by scope
Status: needs-review
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: claims
Phase: implement
Design ref: Commands, time steps and views (Store views: resource stores can be inspected for every level and dimension of entity: Decided); Claims, ownership and territory (One scope model for overlays, stores and trees: Agreed, 30 Oct).
Goal: The household panel shows grain and wood for one household; nothing shows a village's or a culture's stores, or what a pawn is owed. The scope model (VB-043) makes one view serve every level. Depends on VB-043; if it is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria (draft, pending `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-092):
- [ ] A "Stores" block for the selected scope, from `inScope(st, scope)` with the dimension `owns`: grain, of it the seed reserve (VB-049), rations (VB-022) and flour (VB-009) where they exist, firewood, stone; for a village also its own store (`v.grain`, VB-039) apart from its households' sum. Later goods (hay, animals) are added by their tasks.
- [ ] Levels in the first version as answered in `QUESTIONS.md` (proposed: pawn, household, village and culture); a pawn shows its inheritance share held (`stake`) and owed (`owed`).
- [ ] Per member and per day: each store also shows days of food for the scope's members.
- [ ] The view reads only `st` (the lint of VB-056 holds) and costs under 5 ms to build for a culture on populous medium.
- [ ] The smoke test opens the view at each level with no console errors.
