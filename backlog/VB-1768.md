# VB-1768: A village's routines as levies: lots by a village custom, the household names its days, the alderman dates only the work done together

Goal: A village's work is a levy each household bears by its village's custom, and the small are not crushed by dates fixed for them
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A practice of the village, the size of its levy lots (by fields, the default; by adults; equal), in sim/customs' catalogue with its malleability; the meeting's routines lay one obligation of kind `days`, source `routine`, enforcer the village, a household and a season window, sized by it: at each season's turn the routine's days are the hours left of its season's tasks by the alderman's estimate over `OUTER.hours`, rounded up, split into whole adult-days by the largest remainder (ties in turn by household id against the year), at most `OUTER.lotMax` (3) a household a season, the rest to the others or left to the next season and to hands given; terms: the levied names, substitute, no commute, lapse at the season's end; days left when the work is done are released (design.md, Outer levies, The terms, the ladder and the breach: Lots).
- [x] Work done together (the fence closed, the commons cleared) is dated by the alderman through `callWorkday` at least three days ahead (the custom's day by default); its households' days are named by that date (terms `together`: a lot of two days is two adults that day; no other day, no commuting). Other routines' days are named by the households (VB-1767).
- [x] The village's plan holds its routines as steps of its year (author `custom`, the meeting's decision their `why`), and each levied day's `why` points to its routine, so `Sim.why` reaches the meeting.
- [x] A decision point added to VB-1757's list: a work day to call (the alderman, three days before the custom's day).
- [x] A household that does not work a named day breaches: the day's worth booked as goodwill its head owes the alderman, opinion following, told in the record with its reason; the work days' standing cost of today (`pawn.workdays.missed`) is replaced, or kept as the record of it, said in a line; a routine's work left at its window's end is named at the next meeting as a neglected stretch is, and weighs in its choice of routines.
- [x] Q11, the lots: a one-adult household's sowing lost to three days owed under lots by fields and by adults (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 3 years; the worst dates): reported.
- [x] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [x] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
