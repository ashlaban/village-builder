Goal: A village's work is a levy each household bears by its village's custom, and the small are not crushed by dates fixed for them
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A practice of the village, the size of its levy lots (by fields, the default; by adults; equal), in sim/customs' catalogue with its malleability; the meeting's routines lay one obligation of kind `days`, source `routine`, enforcer the village, a household and a season window, sized by it.
- [ ] Work done together (the fence closed, the commons cleared) is dated by the alderman through `callWorkday` at least three days ahead (the custom's day by default); its households' days are named by that date. Other routines' days are named by the households (VB-1767).
- [ ] A household that does not work a named day breaches: the day's worth booked as goodwill its head owes the alderman, opinion following, told in the record with its reason; the work days' standing cost of today (`pawn.workdays.missed`) is replaced, or kept as the record of it, said in a line.
- [ ] Q11, the lots: a one-adult household's sowing lost to three days owed under lots by fields and by adults (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 3 years; the worst dates): reported.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10): `npm run verify` green, a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose (never skipped or quarantined) and named in the PR; no stuck pawn (no task held 48 hours without progress, as VB-1546's script counts); the stocks' accounts close (`node tools/sim.js --report accounts`: `unexplained` no larger than before).
- [ ] No village dies out: `node tools/sim.js --scenario founders|populous --map small --seed 7|11|42 --years 10`, all six runs: no settlement extinct by hunger and no collapse of its people (the living at year 10 against year 0, in the PR).
- [ ] Reported in the PR, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in those six runs, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed), before and after.
