# VB-779: Fix: a household that dies out with timber at a building step throws (stores: dissolved holder)
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Work: opportunities, plans and tasks; backlog/VB-255.md
Goal: A household's plans are dropped while it can still hold their timber, so a run never throws when a building household dies out, and the timber lies on the farm's woodpile as VB-255 has it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `extinct` (`src/sim/heirs.js`) drops the household's plans before it marks the household dissolved (or `leftWood` puts the timber where a dissolved household's goods go: decide by what `Stores.close` does with the woodpile, and say which in the notes). No run throws `stores: missing or dissolved holder` from `leftWood`.
- [x] A check on a small constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): a household with a building plan whose open step holds timber at its house dies out; nothing throws, and the wood is where the notes say (the derelict farm's woodpile, or closed with the barn), counted, not lost unbooked.
- [x] The same order is checked for the other ways a household ends (`dissolve`, a merge at marriage, a move): each drops its plans while the holder still stands.

Notes / questions:
- 16 Nov 2026, found by VB-444's plan on a scratch copy (speed 8, the forested island, dear ground: `docs/plans/VB-444.md`, sections 4 and 5): `node tools/sim.js --scenario populous --seed 42 --map small --years 3` throws in year 2, at `killPawn` > `succession` > `extinct` > `dropPlan` > `dropAll` > `dropProject` > `leftWood` > `Stores.put`. `extinct` sets `hh.dissolved = true` and then calls `dropPlan`; `leftWood` (VB-255) then puts the step's timber into the dissolved household's store, which `Stores.type` refuses. The same run on today's values does not meet the case (no building household dies out), so it is latent on `main`, not caused by the scale; any run may meet it.

Implementation (16 Nov): `extinct` now calls `dropPlan` before `hh.dissolved = true`, so `leftWood` puts the step's timber into the household's wood while the holder stands. `Stores.close` clears only the ledgers (ledger, totals, ale, poured), not `wood`, so the timber stays with the derelict farm (its woodpile), booked as 'timber'. Chosen over changing `leftWood`: one line moved, no second route for goods of a dissolved holder.
Other endings: `extinct` is the only place `dissolved` is set (`dissolve`, a marriage merge at heirs.js:550 and service's wards all call it), so the order holds for all; a move (`moveTo`) dissolves nothing.
Check: `tests/extinctwood.js` (in suites): cart plan at the house with 7 timber, extinct, no throw, plan gone, hh.wood +7; fails without the fix.
