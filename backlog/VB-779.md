# VB-779: Fix: a household that dies out with timber at a building step throws (stores: dissolved holder)
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Work: opportunities, plans and tasks; backlog/VB-255.md
Goal: A household's plans are dropped while it can still hold their timber, so a run never throws when a building household dies out, and the timber lies on the farm's woodpile as VB-255 has it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `extinct` (`src/sim/heirs.js`) drops the household's plans before it marks the household dissolved (or `leftWood` puts the timber where a dissolved household's goods go: decide by what `Stores.close` does with the woodpile, and say which in the notes). No run throws `stores: missing or dissolved holder` from `leftWood`.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): a household with a building plan whose open step holds timber at its house dies out; nothing throws, and the wood is where the notes say (the derelict farm's woodpile, or closed with the barn), counted, not lost unbooked.
- [ ] The same order is checked for the other ways a household ends (`dissolve`, a merge at marriage, a move): each drops its plans while the holder still stands.

Notes / questions:

The same run on today's values does not meet the case (no building household dies out), so it is latent on `main`, not caused by the scale; any run may meet it.
