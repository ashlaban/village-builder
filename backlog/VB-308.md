# VB-308: Taken in before it starves: kin come, then a servant, when help falls short
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Households short of people (Taken in before it starves; Proposed, VB-283); Goods and the farming year (Help in hunger); backlog/VB-283.md (the survey)
Goal: A household with at most one member of 18 or more and fields (a lone adult, or a minor holder with one grown housemate) is hungry over 0.3 of its days in 56 of the 225 household-years over 0.3 on the ten full runs (34 households; VB-283's replay at `38b920d`). Today only a household with no adult gets a guardian (daily), and only a holder who lives alone gets a servant (yearly, at the moving round, from `st.demand` with 2 hands kept). Take such a household in when its seasonal ask for help falls short: a free relative comes first, else a servant, at once.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The sign: in `shareFood` (`src/sim/help.js`), when a household's ask leaves it short of `askDaysAt` days of food after the kin and the village store have given, and at most one of its members is 18 or more, and it holds fields, it is noted short of people (VB-307's queue or a call to `sim/service`; `sim/help` writes nothing of membership). Help itself is unchanged.
- [ ] Kin first (`sim/service`): the household's nearest relative in `st.demand` (kinship over 0, then the oldest, as `careForOrphans` chooses a guardian) who is fit to serve (`unfit` null: unmarried, 18 to 50, free) and whose household keeps a member of 18 or more without them, within the village or the service reach (`SERVICE.reach`, or VB-290's hours if it has landed), moves in as a member (not a servant), with a spouse and minor children if a later rule lets married kin come. An event and a log line (`atHousehold`) name the relative and the farm.
- [ ] Else a servant: `takeServants`' choice for this one household (village first, then the nearest village within reach, `SERVICE.keepHands`, the pawn's `serve` wish), through the `serve` command, now and not only at the moving round. The moving round's rule is unchanged.
- [ ] Nobody moves the land: the household keeps its farm, holder, fields and owner (`src/owners.json` unchanged for land). Nobody comes: nothing happens until VB-309 (Kim).
- [ ] Numbers, before and after (`docs/bench/lone-households.js` on the ten full runs, or populous small at seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 for 30 years and heirs 7 and 42 when time is short): household-years over 0.3 of households short of people (56 at `38b920d`, populous 3: 38, heirs 42: 9) fall by at least 10; none left of those that had a relative in `st.demand` or a servant within reach on their first short ask; relatives and servants moved a year; the households they left (none left short of people by it; their hungry share the next year); the bands' `hungry-households` and `hunger-deaths`, marriages a year and population at year 30 on populous 7 and 42, households and not only averages.
- [ ] `tests/golden.json` and `tests/counters.json` re-recorded, changed entries listed; a test on a constructed village (a lone holder with 3 fields and an empty larder, a sister unmarried at her parents' farm with a brother at home): after the first short ask the sister lives on the farm.
Cost (Rules for cost, 4 and 9): no new loop over households or days. The check rides on help's ask, which a household makes at most once a season, and only when its spare grain covers fewer than 3 days; the grown count is over its members. Only a household short of people searches: `st.demand` once with a kinship memo (as `careForOrphans`), then `takeServants`' list for one household. The replay has about 2 such household-years a run-year on populous small; at `medium-ref`, at most a few searches a season. Measured with `--report perf` on `small-y40` before and after.
Order: after VB-307. Before VB-309 (Kim). One at a time with VB-090 (service where labour is short, the same `sim/service` and the same placement: village first, then within reach; whichever lands second shares the other's helper) and VB-290 (the reach in walking hours). Independent of VB-034.

## Orchestrator's decisions for Kim to overturn
- Kin first, then service, at the sign of help falling short, as the plan's task asked (VB-270's proposed task 2, created on Kim's answers of 10 November). This extends two Decided rules of 1 November (the guardian for a household with no adult; the servant for a lone holder at the moving round) in their own direction: the same choice of person, a wider household (at most one grown member), and a second occasion (the short ask) beside the round. Nothing Decided is reversed: the guardian rule, the round's rule and land are as they were. VB-090's question 4 (Kim, 30 Oct: hunger sending a household's mouths into service waits for the food wrap-up) is the other direction, people sent out, and stays as answered.
- Help falling short is the sign, not the membership change itself: 2,490 lone household-years with fields on the ten runs, only 55 of them hungry over 0.3 (1 field: 34 of 1,106; 4 or more: 4 of 465), so taking in every lone household at the change would move many who are fine. The ask comes when 3 days of spare grain are left, before the hungry days.
- The relative comes from `st.demand` (those who want land), not from every adult: an heir in line at home is not pulled away. The replay's 14 rows with a free relative in reach counted every unmarried landless adult of 18 to 50, so it is an upper bound.
