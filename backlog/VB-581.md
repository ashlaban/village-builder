## Implementation notes (VB-581, reworked)
The first build put a road load back into the field or meadow: a shorter teleport the other way. Replaced by carrying. Each path to `release`:
- the working day's end (sim/clock), the planner's `work`, an occasion (`canGo`), the player's queue: already skip a hand with `phase === 'back'`
  until it has delivered (no change).
- a gift (`carryGift`): the `give` check now refuses a carrier on its way home with a load; another carrier or a later step takes it.
- `moved` (a move or a wedding at the round): a hand with a load on its way home keeps walking it to the old farm's barn; `agent.moving`
  ({old, carry}) holds the move, and `arrived` makes it after the deposit (`finishMove`), so the hand then walks, or sets out, from the barn.
- `release` itself: a hand standing in its field or meadow (`here` is the job's tile) sets the load back with the stooks or the mown hay
  (`fields.laidDown`, `meadows.hayLaid`; a kind's `setDown`): no teleport, as it is where it lies. Otherwise the old behaviour.
- death on the road (`stillAtDeath`) and a household dissolving (`stopWorkFor`): the hand cannot carry on, and a load lying on a tile needs new
  state (lying loads with owner, invariants, saves, stocks). The old behaviour is kept for these two only: the load is booked at the barn
  from the road, a teleport still. Noted: `teleport` and task VB-611 "a load lies where its carrier fell" (arch physical).
- Firewood: no `setDown`; it is felled at delivery in the simulation's own terms, so carried in as before, or (death) booked as before.
  Nothing cut in hand is lost.
- Decision: the move is deferred, not the load set down; the hours the carrier spends going by the barn first are real and the point.
- Tests: `tests/setdown.js` (move while carrying: the load arrives when the hand does, the hand is then free and goes; released in its field:
  back in the stooks; death: the load as it is kept, booked), `tests/service.js` as before. No new invariant for `moving` beyond the
  plain-data and saves checks (an agent field, saved with the agent).
- Numbers (small map, 6 years; `tools/sim.js --report hunger` and `tools/balance.js`, run branch tip against this change): populous 7 and 42,
  founders 7: households, people, hungry households (0 in all), median grain (784, 680, 838) and every table of `balance.js` (grain made, need,
  store, hungry days) are identical before and after: no grain is made, nothing is counted twice. Only the last two columns of the hunger
  table (a village-store figure) move slightly, e.g. populous 7 year 6: 210.9 to 171.9. The first build's 589 to 780 was the run branch moving
  under it (its base was older), not an effect of the change. Hours by kind: not re-run (the grain tables are identical).
