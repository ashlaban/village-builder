Goal: A teleport found by VB-473: release (VB-335) books a load on its way home at the farm's barn at once, wherever its carrier stands
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A carrier released on its way home with a load (a move or a wedding at the round, an occasion, the player's order, its household
  dissolving: `release` in `sim/agents`, VB-335) does not book the load at its farm's barn from the road: it walks the load home first,
  or sets it down where it stands as a load the task's next hand fetches; nothing is delivered where its carrier has not been.

- [x] Numbers before and after at populous small, seeds 7 and 42, and founders small, seed 7: grain, hungry household-days, the hours a
  household-year by kind.

## Implementation notes (VB-581)
- `release` (sim/agents) no longer calls `deposit` for a load on the road. A kind may have `setDown(st, j, a)`: haul (sheaves back into the
  field's stooks, `fields.laidDown`) and hay (back into the mown meadow, `meadows.hayLaid`). Firewood has none: the tree is felled only at
  delivery, so an undelivered load leaves it standing. Other kinds carry no `a.load`. The next hand fetches the load from the field, a
  trip a little longer or shorter than from the road: a close call, noted. `a.carry` is cleared.
- Tests: `tests/setdown.js` (new, in suites), `tests/service.js` updated (a dissolving household's loads are no longer booked in its barn).
- Numbers, small map, 6 years, before to after (tools/sim.js --report hunger / hours): populous 7: households 66, people 388 both,
  hungry households 0 both, median grain 589 to 780 (q1 412 to 542, q3 712 to 994); populous 42: same households and people, 0 hungry both,
  median 700 to 700; founders 7: 0 hungry both, median 844 to 844. Hours by kind: unchanged in the printed tables apart from idle (-0.1 to -2).
  The populous 7 gain is a divergence of the run after the first changed release, not attributed to a mechanism.
