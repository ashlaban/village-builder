# VB-277: Goods for the fair: quanta, three classes and keeping goods
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-183
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, The fair: barter between villages (10 Nov); Goods, Goods of place and side crafts (10 Nov); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, sections 4.3 and 5.2
Goal: Kim, 10 Nov: goods fall into classes, and custom says which exchanges across them are proper; keeping goods are accepted beyond need. VB-183 supplies the quanta and the worth table before this is ready.
Acceptance criteria (draft; VB-183 confirms the numbers):
- [ ] A registry entry per good the fair trades (grain, butter, salt meat, cheese, dried fish, tar, a cow, a sheep, wool, a day's work): its quantum, how it perishes, its class, and whether it is a keeping good. Declared before the registry seals; nothing of it in saved state.
- [ ] `Culture.norm('worth', good)` with fixed rates from VB-183's table, in rations as the one unit of account.
- [ ] `Culture.norm('proper', fromClass, toClass)`: within a class ordinary; food for an animal admired; an animal taken for food while the giver's household is below 60 days of need is a hard bargain, allowed and booked as one; land is in no deal.
- [ ] A keeping good is worth its customary worth to a taker with no need of it, up to a cap in rations a household (VB-183 names the cap).
- [ ] `tools/rules.js` lists the new rules and their owners; a test of each class rule; behaviour unchanged until VB-280 uses them (golden unchanged).
