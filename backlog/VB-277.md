# VB-277: The moving round a village a step: each village's marriages and handovers in its own six-minute step
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: between
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Leaving home: The moving round, A village's round: Decided, 10 Nov). Survey and cost: `backlog/VB-242.md`.
Goal: The round's marriages and handovers (`marriageRound`, `src/sim/lifecycle.js:160-255`, the round's phase 0 at noon of autumn day 11) run in one six-minute step for the whole island: every unmarried man of the island in one list, those with a place first, then the oldest. To stay near 60 ms the step keeps `roundHeirs` (`src/sim/heirs.js:103-147`), a view of heir rankings that tracks dependencies and is invalidated by reading the event record (`changedSince`, `lifecycle.js:181-197`). The view and the round's other memos are built by `matchRules` (`src/sim/matching.js:32-68`, VB-274), which the player's wish (`matchBar`) and its options (`sim/opportunities`) also call. Kim decided on 10 November that each village holds its own round, a village a step, in the order of village id. A village's round matches its own unmarried men with the unmarried women of any village, by today's score, and a lone farm is a village. VB-242's prototype ranks heirs afresh in each village's step: the longest step is 6 to 30 ms on the late saves.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The steps. On autumn day 11, the villages' steps run from noon, village k (id order) on step noon + k, while the island has at most 60 villages (noon to 18:00). With more villages, village k runs on step noon + floor(k × 60 / villages), so several share a step. Today's phases 1 to 3 (demand, claims and plots; settlement; service, what is owed and prestige) follow unchanged, on fixed steps at 18:00, 18:06 and 18:12. They are fixed because settlement founds villages, and steps counted from the number of villages would then move under a running day. `roundPhase(st, n)` stays the entry point for each step, so `tools/perf.js` and `tools/bench.js` ("Moving round, its call") read the longest step. In VB-241's calendar the round is one entry for the day, whose `run` reads the step of the day. `movingRound(st)` (the drawer's button, the tests) runs every village and then the three phases.
- [x] A village's step, `villageRound(st, v)`, holds today's `marriageRound` for one village. Its households fill the village store (`fillStores`, for this village only). The pool is the village's own unmarried men (`settlementIdOf` is v), under today's tests (age, single, not retired, fed), and the unmarried women of every village, as today. A woman married in an earlier village's step is no longer single. A woman held by an open offer (`st.offers`, VB-293) is taken. The men's order within the village, the score, `minScore`, `goAhead`, the means to marry and the put-off log per village are unchanged.
- [x] Heirs are ranked afresh. The step memoizes `heirRanking(st, hh)` by household, together with its other memos (`places`, `handed`, `means`, `housed`), and clears them all after each marriage or handover it makes. The memos and the `roundHeirs` call are `matchRules`'s (`src/sim/matching.js:32-33`), so the ranking memo goes there, and `matchBar` (the player's wish) reads the same fresh rankings; `tests/wishes.js` passes. Nothing reads the record: `changedSince`, `homes` and the sweep of `handed` by village (`lifecycle.js:238-241`) go. The marriages no longer call `roundHeirs`; VB-279 removes it.
- [x] Handovers. In a village's step: for the village's own farms (marriage and age 60, today's rule, `handover`, `lifecycle.js:260-269`), and for each home in another village that the step's marriages chose. It is the one function VB-293 made of that rule, which a marriage at an offer's close also calls (`closeOffer`).
- [x] Kin pressure, a village's own. At the end of its step: the plenty pull for the couples the step married, and the scarcity push for the village's unmatched men and unmarried women who are not held by an offer, each against the free pawns of the other sex in every village. A woman pushed at her village's step and married later the same day takes the pull of the step that married her instead (`wed` clears her push). The reset of `kinPush` stops walking `st.pawns` (`kinPressure`, `lifecycle.js:97`, Rules for cost, 1). The living's pushes are cleared at the first village's step. A pawn's push is cleared at its death (`killPawn`). The kin-barred log stays per village.
- [x] VB-293's offer is made in the man's village's step. A test in `tests/offers.js`: a woman held by an offer from village 0's step is not chosen by a man of village 1 the same day.
- [x] Saves: `tests/spread.js` checks that each village's step and each phase runs exactly once, with uneven advances. A run saved between villages 0 and 1, in the middle of the window, and between the last village and 18:00 goes on as the unbroken run. `tests/saves.js` passes. No progress is saved, since the step of the day says what is due.
- [x] Behaviour changes, so report before and after (targets: each line with its residual, none a gate):

The share of living women 45 to 54 never married within 2 percentage points. Each seed's never-married count is reported, and seeds 7 and 42 are reported.
  - The order of villages, over the same runs: marriages a year by the man's village's third (the first, middle and last third of village ids), and the share of marriages between two villages. VB-242's static study expects the first third's men to marry 44% of the times they ask, against 36% today. If the real runs show the same, VB-280 (the order turns by the year) has its numbers.
  - Households: households with an unmarried adult over 30 at year 30, before and after.
  - The golden file re-recorded, with the changed entries listed. `tests/counters.json` re-recorded, with `roundPairs`, `heirRank`, `roundRank` and `walkDaily` listed. The `tests/record.js` anchors, if they move. The round's rankings in all: about 1,900 at `medium-ref` against 521 today, and 1,700 against 396 at `small-y40`. The round's total time is spread over the window (73 to 132 ms at `medium-ref`, against 27 to 39 ms in one step today), a few ms a game day over the year. A step walks the living once to build its pool, which is about 26,000 pawn visits a year at `medium-ref`; count it in `VB.count.roundWalk`, a declared counter. Time it with `node tools/sim.js --load` on each fixture `--years 1 --report perf`.
