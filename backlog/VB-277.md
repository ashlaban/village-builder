# VB-277: Goods for the fair: quanta, three classes and keeping goods
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, The fair: barter between villages (10 Nov); Goods, Goods of place and side crafts (10 Nov); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, sections 4.3 and 5.2
Goal: Kim, 10 Nov: goods fall into classes, and custom says which exchanges across them are proper; keeping goods are accepted beyond need. The quanta and the worth table are Agreed (10 Nov).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A registry entry per good the fair trades (grain, butter, salt meat, dried fish, tar, ale, cattle by kind, a sheep; no wool or cheese, which are not goods yet, and no labour days, which are promises and come with slice 5): its quantum, how it perishes, its class, and whether it is a keeping good. Declared before the registry seals; nothing of it in saved state.
- [ ] `Culture.norm('worth', good)` with fixed rates, in rations as the one unit of account: grain, a sack of 10 rations, 10; salt meat, butter and dried fish, a lot of 10 rations, 15; a barrel of tar 8; a barrel of ale 14; a cow 40, an ox 50, a heifer or steer 20, a sheep 6.
- [ ] `Culture.norm('proper', fromClass, toClass)`: within a class ordinary; food for an animal admired; an animal taken for food while the giver's household is below 60 days of need is a hard bargain, allowed and booked as one; land is in no deal.
- [ ] A keeping good is worth its customary worth to a taker with no need of it, up to 30 rations' worth held at once by a household. Keeping goods: butter, salt meat, dried fish, tar, a sheep.
- [ ] `Needs.value` for these goods (`design.md`, The fair, Need): food 1 a ration when stores reach the next harvest with the reserve, in a line to 3 at empty stores and 0.5 beyond two years' need, and 1.2 times for a food the pot lacks, up to a fifth of the need; an animal 1.5 times its worth with none of its kind, 1 otherwise, 0 without hay for the winter; tar its worth when a boat or the house is due; ale its worth with an occasion ahead, half otherwise. It allocates nothing in an inner loop.
- [ ] `tools/rules.js` lists the new rules and their owners; a test of each class rule; behaviour unchanged until VB-280 uses them (golden unchanged).
