Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: backlog/VB-626.md; backlog/VB-331.md
Goal: the cause of the dry days in a household of one, and whether the water job's rank or its window is at fault
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] On solo, small, seeds 7 and 42, to the settler's death or 4 years: the dry days counted by why the job went undone, as VB-626 did (unclaimed with the hand free and what the hand did in its place, no hand in the window, claimed and not finished); a throwaway script.
