# VB-089: The heir-to-their-own-farm rule as a custom
Status: done (2026-10-30, SHA)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: expansion
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion, Succession (the own-farm rule is a custom: Decided, 29 Oct); Customs and culture, Custom dimensions ("Heir to their own parents' farm": options Decided, default and malleability Proposed); Customs, How attitudes move (Self-interest).
Goal: A relative who is first in line for their own parents' farm steps behind the other relatives in another farm's line, so farms are not combined. The design makes this a custom, but the code hard-codes "steps behind" in `heirRanking` (`src/sim/heirs.js:51`) and `DIMS` has no entry for it, so no village can ever change it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A `DIMS` entry `ownHeir`, "Heir to their own parents' farm", malleability 0.6, options `behind` "Steps behind other relatives" and `keeps` "Keeps their place", added after `feedOrder`; `oldNordic` in `ARCHETYPES` takes `behind`. Both heirs-scenario villages keep the default.
- [ ] `heirRanking` reads `cu.ownHeir`: `behind` is today's order; under `keeps` nobody steps behind. An heir who comes first in two lines takes the farm with more fields (on a tie, their own parents') and drops the other claim, which passes to the next in that line. The rule that someone who already holds a farm, or lives on a spouse's farm, is passed over stays, so no household ever holds two farms.
- [ ] Self-interest in `refreshInterests` (`src/sim/society.js`): the first heir of their home leans `keeps`; a landless adult leans `behind`.
- [ ] A test on a constructed family: a nephew who is first heir of his parents' farm, and a cousin with no farm, both in an uncle's line. Under `behind` the cousin is first heir of the uncle's farm; under `keeps` the nephew is.
- [ ] The customs drawer shows the new custom with no page change beyond what `DIMS` drives (checked in the jsdom smoke test).
- [ ] `node tests/calibrate.js natural 2 50` before and after: the change of the other customs stays within the ranges of the design's Pace table (reported, not asserted).
- [ ] Re-record the golden file (attitudes gain two entries) and list the changed entries in the PR.
