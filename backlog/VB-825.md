# VB-825: Betrothal: a match that waits for its place
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-697
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: acts
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (The round as offers); docs/plans/VB-682.md, section 4.1 (Betrothal)
Goal: A match may ride on a place rung (Kim, 16 Nov): a pair agreed on a match whose home is a place still asked for is betrothed, asks for land as a couple, and marries at the round after the place is theirs.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A `match` offer may name as its home a place asked for or coming: a farm under claim, a plot or a cottage under way, a party's new land. It is answered at the round as any match, but for the home and its means, which are read at the marriage. Agreed, it is a betrothal: `pawn.betrothed` (the other's id or null, the same on both sides; `@state`, owner, the save, an invariant: symmetric, both alive and unmarried), and neither is free to another offer.
- [ ] A betrothed pair makes its `land` or `party` ask as one, and counts as a couple in the claims custom's order (couples before singles, as `claimants` has it).
- [ ] At the first round after the place is theirs (the restored farm's household founded, the cottage finished, the settlers' farm founded) the two marry by `marry`, if the gate holds (the kin limit, the means of that home, hunger); the gate is not moved (Decided, 13 Nov). The betrothal lapses at the third round after it was agreed (`MARRIAGE.betrothal` 3, in rounds: the time a claim has to be mended in, `CLAIMS.years`), when either dies, or when the place is lost; a lapse is an event of both, and no memory.
- [ ] The pawn panel says "Betrothed to <name>, to marry when <the place> is theirs", for any pawn; `node tests/smoke.js`; a screenshot under `docs/img/`.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7 and 42, by the residence custom in force: betrothals made, married and lapsed; marriages a year per 1,000 and the mean age at first marriage by sex (expected up and down under "with whoever holds land", where a landless pair could not be a couple before); claims and plots that went to a betrothed pair; growth by principle "The island grows without the player". A target out is reported, not tuned beyond five short runs over `MARRIAGE.betrothal`.
- [ ] `tests/commands.js` and `tests/record.js` pass; tests on a constructed state: a pair betrothed on a plot marries the round after the cottage; a lapse; the couple's place in the custom's order.
