# VB-330: A home that took in a spouse at the round gives no settlers' provisions in it
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Moving to new land: Provisions). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Consistency review (10 Nov), pawns placed, question 3
Goal: At the moving round the first phase lets a couple marry only if their home holds food for 1.5 years of its members and one more (`affords`, `src/sim/lifecycle.js`), and the third phase may then take that home's grain as a settlers' provisions down to 42 days of food for those who stay (`provisions`, `src/sim/settle.js:66-79`). Kim, 10 Nov: a home that took in a spouse in this round gives no provisions in it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `provisions` skips, as a giver, any household that is the home of a union started in this round (a union of a member with `start` in the round's day, read from the members' unions; no new state). The party's own stakes count as today. A party short without it stays a year, and the `stayed` log line is unchanged.
- [x] A test (in `tests/settle.js` or beside it): a household that marries at the round and has a waiting sibling with a party gives nothing that round and gives the next; one that did not marry gives as today.
- [x] Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years): parties that left and that stayed for want of grain, by year; hungry household-days in the year after a round for homes that married in it; villages founded. Whether households, not only averages, are better off. The golden file is re-recorded if it changes and its changed entries listed.
