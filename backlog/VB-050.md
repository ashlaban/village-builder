# VB-050: Health: hunger drains it, and a starving pawn dies within a season
Status: needs-review
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: food
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs, Known gaps (hunger can kill, mostly infants and the old: Decided); Open questions (What hunger does). Kim, 2026-10-30: a hunger level of 1 should kill a pawn within a season; each pawn has a health stat 0–100 (a float) that hunger drains, fast at hunger 1.
Goal: Today hunger only multiplies the daily death risk by 1 + k(age) × h, with k = 0.3 for ages 15–59, so an adult eating nothing for years survives (review, 2026-10-30: 6 adults at hunger 1.0 for 7 years, none died).
Acceptance criteria (proposed numbers; Kim confirms in `QUESTIONS.md`):
- [ ] `p.health`, a float 0–100, starts at 100 for every pawn, newborns included; `@state` documented.
- [ ] Daily, after the meal: if hunger h > 0, health drops by `HEALTH.drain × h²` with `drain = 9` per day (so at h = 1 a healthy pawn dies in about 11 days, within a season of 12), times 1.5 for under 5 and 60 and over. If the pawn ate in full today, health rises by `HEALTH.recover = 3` per day, to at most 100.
- [ ] Health 0 kills the pawn; the cause is "starvation" (VB-051's cause table if it is done).
- [ ] Below 50, health adds to the daily death risk: the hazard is multiplied by 1 + (50 − health) / 25 (×3 at health 0). The existing hunger multiplier 1 + k(age) × h stays, so the agreed calibration of a hungry year (about twice the deaths) holds; `tests/calibrate.js` reports the ratios again with health in.
- [ ] Numbers in a `HEALTH` table in `src/sim/rules.js`.
- [ ] The pawn panel shows health; the household panel shows its lowest.
- [ ] Tests: a pawn at h = 1 from full health dies within a season: an adult on day 12, a child under 5 on day 8; a pawn at h = 0.25 loses 0.56 a day; full meals restore 3 a day.
- [ ] A founders run over 50 years at seeds 7 and 42 reports deaths by cause per decade (reported). Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
Notes / questions:
- Should low health also slow work (today hunger does, by up to 40%) or make illness deaths likelier? Proposed: not in this task.
