# VB-1639: Place the farmstead anywhere reachable: the goods, the cart and the herds stay at the landing and are fetched; a lone farm's village follows its house
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: L
Design ref: backlog/VB-1627.md; src/sim/camp.js (moveHouse, tileWhy, placeOwn); src/sim/lying.js; src/sim/droving.js (standAway, drove); src/sim/herds.js (houseOf, homeTile); src/sim/carts.js (strand); src/sim/territory.js (reckonWood, woodWhy, claimOutland); src/sim/planchains.js (CHAINS.farmstead); src/sim/walking.js
Goal: The settler may raise the farmstead wherever the land can be reached on foot, and pays for the distance in carrying and droving.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] No step bound for the played head: `placeWhy` refuses water, a river, a tile in use, trees standing, land owned or claimed by another, and "not reachable on foot from the camp" (walkable components reckoned once for a world and kept beside it, never in `st`, so that the check is cheap for every tile the page asks). The head's own rule (`placeOwn`) keeps its reach near the landing.
- [ ] Nothing teleports (the teleport recorded by VB-1627 is mended): placing on a tile other than the landing moves the house there and leaves at the landing tile, the household's own, lying loads (sim/lying) equal to the grain (by crop), the wood and the hay it held, those fields then 0; the cart standing there; every herd standing there (an optional tile a herd stands at, read where `herd.at` is read: sim/herds `houseOf`, sim/droving). The stocks are conserved (`tools/stocks.js`, the invariants). Placing on the landing tile moves nothing, as today.
- [ ] The farmstead task carries the move through with no further act, as steps of its chain found by their keys: the herds driven, the cart fetched, the loads fetched (their trips counted), then the timber and the raising. `planView` names each step and its `waits`; `Sim.agenda` lays them; the task's deadline is what the loads' own rule gives (a load left lying is lost at the autumn frost, sim/lying: grain, hay and wood by that rule; the cart and the herds are not lost: say how you kept them). The household eats from what has been fetched: the head takes one back load of grain with it at the move (its own walk), so the first night is not foodless.
- [ ] A lone farm's village follows its house: in a village of one farm and no green, `v.center` becomes the new house tile, the landing can be grazed and farmed again, `v.wood` is reckoned again from the new centre, and the next meeting claims outland round it. The writer of `v.center` is named in `src/owners.json`; an invariant: one farm, no green, then centre = house.
- [ ] A read for the page, plain data, writing nothing: `Sim.moveCost(st, tile)`: `{steps, trips: {cart, back}, hours, frost (whether the reckoned move ends after the frost the head expects)}`.
- [ ] Unchanged: the solo start without `hand`, founders, populous (`node tools/same.js --base REF`), and a placement on the landing tile. A save and a load in the middle of a move go on the same.
- [ ] Tests on small constructed states (`tests/camp.js` or a new script with its line in `tests/suites.js`): a move of 2 steps and of 10 or more; the refusals; the stocks conserved; the village followed. Report the hours from placing to the roof raised and the hungry days for a 10-step move. `npm run verify` passes; baselines do not rise.
