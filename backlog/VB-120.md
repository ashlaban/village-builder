# VB-120: Village work days
Status: needs-review
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: work
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Jobs, owners and roles: Village work days, Commons). Job review, second pass, 30 Oct 2026.
Goal: The village never works together except on a plot. Medieval villages set days for common work: closing the infield's roundpole fence before the animals went out (Upplandslagen), clearing the commons. A work day is a scene worth watching and a test of who turns up.
Acceptance criteria (proposed; see `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-120):
- [ ] At the autumn meeting each village with a fence sets two work days for the coming year: spring day 1 (the fence) and summer day 2 (the commons).
- [ ] On a work day from 6:00 the village posts its fence stretches below 0.9 (spring) or a clearing job on the commons wood's edge (summer) as village jobs with unlimited hands, at a priority of 8 for every adult whatever their role.
- [ ] Each adult who works 2 hours or more on the day gains 0.05 prestige for the year; a household with no adult at the work day loses 0.1 of its holder's prestige for the year (the prestige of `sim/society`).
- [ ] The village log notes the day and who stayed away.
- [ ] Populous medium at seed 7, 3 years: fence condition, adults at each work day, and idle shares in spring and summer, before and after (reported).
- [ ] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
