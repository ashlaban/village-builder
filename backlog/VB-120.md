# VB-120: Village work days
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Jobs, owners and roles: Village work days, Commons). Job review, second pass, 30 Oct 2026.
Goal: The village never works together except on a plot. Medieval villages set days for common work: closing the infield's roundpole fence before the animals went out (Upplandslagen), clearing the commons. A work day is a scene worth watching and a test of who turns up.
Acceptance criteria (Kim's answers of 10 November; `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-120"):
- [x] At the autumn meeting a village with a fence may choose work days as one of its routines (VB-257); chosen, it sets two work days for the coming year: spring day 1 (the fence) and summer day 2 (the commons).
- [x] On a work day from 6:00 the village posts its fence stretches below 0.9 (spring) or a clearing job on the commons wood's edge (summer) as village jobs with unlimited hands, at a priority of 8 for every adult whatever their role.
- [x] Each adult who works 2 hours or more on the day gains 0.05 prestige for the year; a household with no adult at the work day loses 0.1 of its holder's prestige for the year (the prestige of `sim/society`).
- [x] A memory as well (Kim): each other holder of the village who has a tie to the absent holder remembers "Stayed away from the work day", -0.05 a day missed, cap -0.15, fading over a year (`SOCIAL.memories`; the numbers are the agent's starting values). No tie is made for it, and the pass is over a village's holders on two days a year (holders × the absent, within the ties' cap).
- [x] The village log notes the day and who stayed away.
- [x] Populous medium at seed 7, 3 years: fence condition, adults at each work day, and idle shares in spring and summer, before and after (reported).
- [x] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR. (Retired by VB-408: no golden file; the numbers before and after are below.)

- 13 Nov 2026 (implementation, VB-120): a new module `sim/workdays` (social layer, after `sim/green`): the routine `workdays` of
  `VILLAGE_ROUTINES` (`sim/plans`; available while the village has a fence; `need` its occupied farms, as the green), which only a meeting
  sets (`meeting: true`: the founders' first routines hold none, so the first work days are those of year 2, after the first autumn
  meeting). Its rule (`ASK.village`) posts, on a work day until the end of work, `workfence` jobs for the stretches below
  `WORKDAY.fenceBelow` (0.9; 2.5 h each, as a mend; done, the stretch is whole) on spring day 1, and one `workclear` job on summer day 2 at
  the commons wood's edge (a tile of `v.wood` beside open land, nearest the green; `WORKDAY.clearHours` 3 an adult of the village). The
  village's plan is reviewed at 5:00 of a work day (`reviewWhy`, 'window'), so the jobs stand from 6:00. Both kinds are of a new work type
  `workday`, 8 for every role but the child (ROLES), from age 14 (`minAge`), `hands: Infinity` and `together` (a new kind flag: the
  matching's join factor does not fall for the hands already at the job, `sim/jobs`). Each step worked adds to the village's record of
  the day (`village.workday.hoursOf`, through the kind's `worked`, now passed `dt` and the pawn by `sim/agents`). At 18:00 of a work day
  (a new `sim/clock` entry, after everyone goes home) the day is judged: an adult with 2 h or more gains `WORKDAY.came` 0.05 for the year,
  a household none of whose adults worked their share is away, its holder `WORKDAY.missed` -0.1 for the year (both as `pawn.workdays`,
  read by `prestigeParts` while the year lasts; `sim/society` refreshes them at once on the event `workDay`); each other holder of the
  village with a tie to the absent holder remembers `stayedAway` (-0.05, cap -0.15, a year; no tie made); the village's log tells the
  day, how many of its adults turned out and the holders whose households stayed away (kind `workday`, routine: folded after 2 years);
  the day's jobs left free are taken down.
- Decisions (Deciding without Kim): (1) "at the work day" is read as having worked one's share: 2 hours, or the hours the village worked
  over its adults when that is less, so a short fence day does not make everyone absent; the prestige gain needs the full 2 hours as the
  criterion says. (2) The founders call no work days: the meeting sets them for the coming year (Kim's answer). (3) The commons' clearing
  changes nothing on the map: the scrub at a wood's edge is not modelled (the day is the work and the turning out); a follow-up could let
  it feed grazing or brushwood. (4) `together`: the join factor (VB-102) spreads hands over jobs; a work day is meant to be worked by all
  at once, so its kinds are exempt. (5) A day with no work (a whole fence, no wood) is not called and nobody is judged.
