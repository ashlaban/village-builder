# VB-904: Customs swing back within a decade: measure it against the Decided pace and mend the cause
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Design ref: Customs and culture (Pace; How attitudes move; Meetings, culture and splits)
Goal: A custom that passes holds on the scale of generations, as Pace has it, unless an effort as large moves it back.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Measured over founders small seeds 7 and 42 (80 years) and populous small seed 7 (40 years): each change of practice by village and of custom by culture, its dimension, the causes argued for it at the meeting that passed it, and whether it goes back within 10 and 30 years. A table in the notes; a throwaway script, or a report of `tools/sim.js` if it is worth keeping.
- [ ] The mechanism found and written in the notes: what support a passed practice keeps once the causes behind it lapse (Culture.P.causeEvery, 5 years), what pulls attitudes back (drift to the culture's custom, the founders' seeded attitudes), and how the culture's custom follows its villages' practice, including why one village of two changed the custom "across the Nordic culture" in the playtest, and whether that line says what happened.
- [ ] Mended by `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim 11 (enforcing a Decided point needs no question): where a formula computes something other than it says, fixed and reported before and after (principle 3); where the swing is a balance point, a named parameter with a meaning (a practice's hold once in force, a margin to undo it) tried in at most five short runs, the values and what each gave in the notes. A shortfall outside the task's reach is a row of `docs/tuning.md`.
- [ ] Reported before and after at the seeds above: changes per 30 years and the share reversed within 10, against Pace's table (natural drift: about 3 generations for the easiest custom; a coalition of 20% of the voice: 1.1 to 2.1 generations).

Notes / questions:
- 16 Nov 2026 (playtest, founders small seed 7, 80 years in the page). Each cause one pawn argued passed, and its custom went back after the cause lapsed: a daughter's share Equal (year 7), Half (12), Equal (23); when the heir takes over: at the holder's death (15), when the heir marries (21), when the holder turns 60 (42), when the heir marries (48), at the holder's death (61); claims on empty farms: villagers first (31), anyone (36), kin of the old family (43), the meeting assigns (53), kin first (67); cleared land: whoever it was cleared for (70), the village allots it (74). Every line read "Across the Nordic culture", in a culture of two villages and 20 to 40 people. Pace (Decided): customs change on the scale of generations, villages are stable and larger cultures slower.
- For the player a cause is the one act that reaches the village, and one that passes and lapses in five years teaches that it does not matter. Small cultures may rightly move faster (Pace: 100 people first change in about 2.2 generations); the measure says whether this is that, or a defect.
