# VB-051: Death causes by age, in their own module
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: food
Phase: implement
Design ref: People, households and family records (Mortality: Agreed; Life events).
Goal: `deathRoll` in `src/sim/hunger.js` returns 'old age' for every death not added by hunger, infants included, and the cause names live in the hunger module. Move the death roll and the causes into a module of their own, with causes by age.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A new module `src/sim/deaths.js` (`VB['sim/deaths']`, `@sys` header, listed in `src/manifest.json` after `sim/hunger`): `CAUSES`, a table of cause keys with their life-event and log texts, and `deathRoll(st, p)`, moved from `sim/hunger`. `sim/hunger` keeps only feeding and hunger.
- [ ] Causes for the base hazard by age: under 1 `infancy` ("Died in infancy"), 1–14 `childhood` ("Died of a childhood illness"), 15–59 `illness` ("Died of illness"), 60 and over `oldAge` ("Died of old age"). Added by hunger: `hunger`. Others: `childbirth` (from `sim/births`), `starvation` (VB-050), `killed` (the debug panel's kill, today 'died').
- [ ] `killPawn` takes a cause key and writes the texts from `CAUSES`; no cause string is written anywhere else.
- [ ] Same number of random draws as today, so only the texts change.
- [ ] A test: the cause of a death at ages 0, 5, 30 and 70 with no hunger.
- [ ] Re-record the golden file (event texts change) and list the changed entries in the PR.
