# VB-361: Facilities: a registry of built things, raised in stages
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The mill and the loom, the kiln and the quarry); `backlog/VB-185.md`
Goal: st.facilities holds built things on sites with an owner, a kind, a condition and building stages that are plan steps; the bloomery, the loom's house, kilns and mills use it. Slice 6's plan (VB-185, 11 Nov), from Kim's answers in the session.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `sim/facilities` owns `st.facilities` (ids are indexes, never reused): `{ id, kind, tile, owner (a household or a village), stage, done, condition, built }`, after target-v2 4.3's Facility, cut to what slice 6 needs; a live index by tile and by owner, a cache with one writer (rule 1); `@state`, `src/owners.json`, invariants, saves.
- [x] `FACILITY[kind]`: the stages of its building as a recipe (hours, loads of each good taken from the owner's stores through `sim/stores` with the reason `build`), where it may stand (a tile test), its capabilities and capacity. `found(st, kind, tile, owner)` checks and returns why not; a verb in `src/sim/commands.js`; each stage is a job kind `raise` whose hours and loads the stage names.
- [x] Nothing existing moves into it in this task: houses, barns, wells, fences and the fishing boat stay where they are (a task for each when a slice needs it). No kind is declared here but a test kind; each craft's task declares its own.
- [x] The page draws a facility in building (a plot of stakes) and built (a marker by kind, until each has art), and the tile panel names it, its owner and its stage.
- [x] Cost named (rule 9): facilities are few (one or two a household that crafts, one mill a village); no step walks them all; upkeep is offered on the owner's planning day.
- [x] The numbers that move are reported before and after at the seeds and maps named (Working rules); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
Notes:
- Numbers are Proposed by slice 6's plan (11 Nov) on Kim's answers to the shape; the hours are in the game's work hours (576 an adult a year) and, but for the textile shares, from general reading not checked by search: each task reports its numbers and the wrap-up tunes them.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim's answers after the plan of VB-702; section 14): A facility is built, not made: it has no grade of workmanship (the plan of VB-702, D22). Its condition is its quality.
- VB-361 (decisions): the job kinds are `raisestage` and `mendfacility`, since `raise` is already the stone foundation's kind (sim/building); the
  verbs are `found` and `upkeep`. Condition is kept as `condition` at `condAt` (closed form, as a booth's), the one field beyond the brief.
  `done` is the hours on the open stage, kept on the facility (a half-built one outlives a dropped plan; founding it again on its tile
  takes it up, the open step less those hours). The stages' loads go as project steps' `spends` (reserved at founding, taken with `build`).
  Upkeep: a household's on its planning day (planReviewed), a village's at its review (sim/routines villageSteps), below FACILITIES.mend 0.6.
  Test kinds only (tests/facilities.js); nothing moves in, so no run's numbers change (counters the same, facilityAsk 0 everywhere).
  The page's drawing is page/facilityart.js, its own concern, not page/settlement (at 715 lines it would have crossed 700).
