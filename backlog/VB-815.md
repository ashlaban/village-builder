# VB-815: Built ways as facilities: the track and the causeway, built along worn ways, kept by the village's custom
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-810
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: roads
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Climate and travel beyond walking (Built ways; Where a way is built; Upkeep); docs/architecture/target-v2.md, sections 4.2 (Worn and built ways) and 4.3 (Facilities); docs/plans/VB-190.md, section 5.5
Goal: Built ways are facilities with stretches and a condition (target-v2, 4.3), built where people already walk (desire lines) and kept up by a duty the village's custom names.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Two kinds of facility in VB-361's registry, each with stretches of tiles and a condition a stretch: a **track** (stumps and stones out, the way levelled: 8 hours a tile through pine or over hills, 12 through broadleaf forest, 3 over heath; a tile on a track costs what a lane costs whatever its wear, and carries an ox cart) and a **causeway** (logs laid over marsh: 16 hours and 2 loads of timber a tile; the tile costs 1.2 and carries carts). `WAYS` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each value with its meaning. The cost rule reads a built way and its condition (`Ways.factor` of target-v2, in the compiled arrays).
- [ ] Where: only along a worn way (a path or a lane), or to join two: the worn way is the survey. Once a year, before the meeting, the alderman (VB-314) reckons for the village's worn ways over dear ground what a way would save in a year: the crossings counted on the tile (`w.wear`), the loads that went on the back for want of a way (`wayMiss`, VB-810), times the hours saved a crossing. He proposes the stretch whose saving over `WAYS.payback` 5 years most exceeds its building hours, if any does; the meeting votes (VB-313) and the alderman carries it out (VB-673). It is his estimate (principle "The future is someone's estimate") and is logged with its figures. A head may build a track on its own holding to its own field as a household project, by the same reckoning. At most one way a village at a time.
- [ ] Building is village work, at the priority of the green and the well, by pawns who walk there, with timber felled in the village's wood and carried; the facility holds its progress a stretch.
- [ ] Upkeep: a track loses `wear` 10% of its condition a year (brush and ruts), a causeway 8% (rot); below 0.5 an ox cart does not pass, at 0 it is the worn way under it. A custom, `wayDuty` (principle "Customs may prescribe"; grounded in the Swedish farms' road lots, each farm keeping a stretch by its land, viewed at the hundred's court): `workdays` (the village mends its ways on its work days, `sim/workdays`: the start of every culture), `lots` (each farm keeps a stretch in proportion to its fields, as labour its head owes: an obligation of VB-310 when that exists, else a routine of its plan) and `builder` (who built it keeps it). A neglected stretch is named at the meeting by the alderman; a fine waits for the cases of VB-315.
- [ ] State, invariants (a stretch's tiles lie on land a way may cross; condition 0 to 1), saves, `tools/stocks.js` where it applies; the map draws a track and a causeway (`node tools/shot.js`, a screenshot under `docs/img/`).
- [ ] A constructed check: a village whose far field lies beyond 5 tiles of forest with a lane carting 40 loads a year proposes the track, builds it in the hours the rule gives, and its ox cart then passes; with 4 loads a year it proposes nothing; unmended for 6 years the track no longer carries the ox cart.
- [ ] Numbers before and after on populous medium at year 0, seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 (`node docs/plans/VB-190-water.js ROOT medium 3,7,11,42`, the plan's measures), and years on populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years, and founders small, seed 7, 50 years: ways proposed, built and kept, by kind and custom; hours of village work on ways; loads by ox cart; hours of carrying a farm; the hours between the villages a way joins; hungry days by household, growth. At most five short runs for `payback` and the hours a tile, the values tried in the task file, the rest as `tuning` notes.
- Cost (rule 9): villages (25 to 30) x once a year x its worn path and lane tiles over dear ground (hundreds), a sum of counts already kept; no search. Stretches: tens a village after decades.
