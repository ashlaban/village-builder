# VB-199: Bounded history: a pawn's events as an index, routine events folded
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: People, households and family records. `docs/roadmap.md`, sections 5.2 (save and load under 0.5 s each, Decided, Kim, 3 November), 5.3 (rule 9, bound history) and 5.4; VB-075 (the record of events).
Goal: History grows without bound, and the save with it. Measured on 3 November, populous small at seed 7:
- at year 40, `st.record` holds 110,278 events, and 81% of them are `friend` (52,201) and `fellOut` (36,598);
- each pawn record carries its own copy of its life events, 80.6 on average and 8.1 MB of the save;
- the save is 29 MB of JSON (pawns 14.1 MB, the record 11.9 MB);
- writing it takes 0.71 s to encode, 0.28 s to stringify and 0.61 s to gzip; reading it takes 0.62 s to parse and 0.28 s to load.
The budget is 0.5 s each way at the late reference, which has more people and 20 more years. Rule 9 (Decided): the record keeps notable events in full and folds routine ones into yearly counts after a generation; a pawn's events are an index into the record, not a second copy; save size grows with the living and a bounded history.
Decided (Kim, 10 November 2026; `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-199: bounded history"):
- Notable, kept in full: births, marriages, deaths (with their cause), inheritances and handovers, moves and leaving, farms, plots, cottages and meadows made, claimed or left, customs changed and causes argued at the meeting, and inheritance shares paid.
- Routine, folded: `friend`, `fellOut`, `gift`, `harvest`, `hay`, `frost`, `herdBirths`, `herdDeath`, `slaughter`, `spoiled` and `putOff`. A kind in neither list is notable until a task says otherwise; the two lists are one table beside the record's kinds, and `tests/record.js` checks every kind is in it.
- `friend` and `fellOut` fold 2 years after they were written; the other routine kinds after a generation (25 years).
- If the save is still over 0.5 s: this task measures each part and names the next step in its PR; building it is VB-226's.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A pawn's events are indexes into `st.record` (the event's position), not copies: `personEvent` writes one record event and pushes its index. The pawn panel and the family tree read them through `Sim.recordOf`, and a pawn's life reads the same before and after (a test compares the texts).
- [x] Routine kinds are folded into one count per kind, village and year in a new `st.recordYears`, at the ages decided above; notable events stay in full. Folding runs at the year's turn, spread over the year's first days a village at a time (rule 5). An index whose event was folded points to its count.
- [x] Save and load timed on `small-y40` and `medium-ref`, each part (encode, stringify, gzip; parse, decode), with the MB before and after. The line is Kim's (9 November): 0.5 s of main thread each at `medium-ref`, encode + stringify for the save; what size alone cannot reach is VB-226's.
- [x] `tests/record.js`, `tests/saves.js` and `tests/codec.js` pass; the new state field has its invariants in `checkState`.
- [x] This changes the state: re-record the golden file and list the changed entries.
Cost: the fold touches each routine event once, a generation after it was written; a pawn's events shrink from copies to numbers.

## Implementation, 10 November 2026
Impact note. Modules: `sim/records` writes the record, `pawn.events` (now positions in `st.record`), and the two new state fields
`st.recordYears` (the folded counts, `{year, kind, village, tally}`) and `st.recordFold` (the fold's positions and its pass in progress),
all three given to `sim/records` in `src/owners.json`. `HISTORY` (beside `record`) is the one table of kinds: 0 keeps a kind, otherwise the
years after which it is folded (`friend`, `fellOut` 2; the other routine kinds 25). `foldHistory` runs daily from `sim/clock` (hour 0): at
the year's turn it walks from its two cursors to the first event too young (the record is appended in time order), then folds a village a
day, then the events of no village; a folded slot becomes the index of its count, so no position ever moves (the notices' and the skip's
cursors, the history tab's stamp and the pawns' positions stay good). `personEvent` writes one record event and pushes its position; with
`false` its event is the pawn's own telling (`self: true`, with the pawn's village), which only the pawn's life reads: `notable`,
`skipStop`, `chronicle`, `villageLog`, `recordOf` and the round's `changedSince` (`sim/lifecycle`, so the run is unchanged) skip it. Births
and deaths point to the log's event (`lifeEvent`; `log` now returns the position). `recordOf` moved from `sim/records` to `sim/notable`,
which has `CAUSES`: `Sim.recordOf(st, {pawn, life: true})` (`lifeOf`) gives a pawn's life in its own words (a death as "Died of ...", a
birth with no text, as the copies had; a folded one as `{kind, folded: tally}`), read by the family panel (`src/page/family.js`) and by
the tools and tests that read `p.events`. `friend`/`fellOut` events now carry the pawn's village (`sim/relations`), so they fold by village.
`sim/saves` calls `indexEvents` on load: an older save's copies become positions (matched by pawn, time and text; a birth or death by
kind), and a copy told beside another is appended as a `self` event, but for routine ones (gifts given), which are dropped. New coupling:
`sim/clock` calls `foldHistory`; nothing else. New counter `recordFold` (slots passed by the fold; 0 in the 30-day counter and scaling
fixtures, which reach no year's turn). `tests/lib/fixtures.js` accepts a record longer than the stamp (the appended `self` events).
Behaviour: unchanged. Every state key but `pawns`, `cenv` (which holds the pawns), `record` and the two new ones hashes as at 7812253
(founders and heirs at seed 7, 3 years, pawn events and `self` events taken out).
