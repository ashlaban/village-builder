# VB-199: Bounded history: a pawn's events as an index, routine events folded
Status: needs-review
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: People, households and family records. `docs/roadmap.md`, sections 5.2 (save and load under 0.5 s each, Decided, Kim, 3 November), 5.3 (rule 9, bound history) and 5.4; VB-075 (the record of events).
Goal: History grows without bound, and the save with it. Measured on 3 November, populous small at seed 7:
- at year 40, `st.record` holds 110,278 events, and 81% of them are `friend` (52,201) and `fellOut` (36,598);
- each pawn record carries its own copy of its life events, 80.6 on average and 8.1 MB of the save;
- the save is 29 MB of JSON (pawns 14.1 MB, the record 11.9 MB);
- writing it takes 0.71 s to encode, 0.28 s to stringify and 0.61 s to gzip; reading it takes 0.62 s to parse and 0.28 s to load.
The budget is 0.5 s each way at the late reference, which has more people and 20 more years. Rule 9 (Decided): the record keeps notable events in full and folds routine ones into yearly counts after a generation; a pawn's events are an index into the record, not a second copy; save size grows with the living and a bounded history.
Questions: `QUESTIONS.md`, Priority 1, "VB-199: bounded history" (which kinds are notable; when `friend` and `fellOut` fold; save speed beyond size).
Acceptance criteria (draft, as proposed in the questions):
- [ ] A pawn's events are indexes into `st.record` (the event's position), not copies: `personEvent` writes one record event and pushes its index. The pawn panel and the family tree read them through `Sim.recordOf`, and a pawn's life reads the same before and after (a test compares the texts).
- [ ] Routine kinds are folded into one count per kind, village and year in a new `st.recordYears`; notable events stay in full. Folding runs at the year's turn, spread over the year's first days a village at a time (rule 5). An index whose event was folded points to its count.
- [ ] Save and load timed on `small-y40` and `medium-ref`, each part (encode, stringify, gzip; parse, decode), with the MB before and after. The line is Kim's (9 November): 0.5 s of main thread each at `medium-ref`, encode + stringify for the save; what size alone cannot reach is VB-226's.
- [ ] `tests/record.js`, `tests/saves.js` and `tests/codec.js` pass; the new state field has its invariants in `checkState`.
- [ ] This changes the state: re-record the golden file and list the changed entries.
Cost: the fold touches each routine event once, a generation after it was written; a pawn's events shrink from copies to numbers.
