# VB-1898: The storehouse and the barn: grain in the one, hay in the other
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Settlements and look (Farmstead, 27 Nov); Goods, production and animals; sim/ladder OUTBUILDINGS
Touches: sim/rules (BUILD); sim/hunger (barnLoss); sim/byre (spoilHay); sim/building (barnSteps, barnBuilt, KINDS); sim/aims (barnWhy); new sim/storehouse; sim/ladder (OUTBUILDINGS); sim/upgrade; sim/scenarios (makeState); page/settlement (drawFarm, YARD); page/ui2build; tests/aims.js; new tests/storehouse.js
Goal: A farm's grain and its hay each have a building of their own that the head weighs separately, by whether its farm leans on crops or on animals, and the map shows exactly the buildings the sim has (design.md, Farmstead, Decided 27 Nov).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `farmstead.storehouse` {built} and the making `storehouse` (a project of the household at its house, its stages walls and roof, its walls waiting for their timber), an outbuilding in sim/ladder OUTBUILDINGS from the cottage up: 20 timber, 60 work hours (Proposed, as the byre); a farm with one loses BUILD.storehouse.loss (0.5) of the grain to spoilage (`barnLoss` reads the storehouse, not the barn).
- [ ] The barn (`farmstead.barn`, VB-255) halves the hay lost only (`spoilHay`), no longer the grain; its cost stays 40 timber and 120 hours unless the task measures a reason to change it.
- [ ] The head's rule: each offered by the yearly project menu (sim/aims) under its own check; the storehouse weighed by the grain lost a year against its hours, the barn by the hay lost, the same function as the doing (One quantity, one reckoning). The played head builds either through a plan task `outbuilding` (sim/upgrade).
- [ ] Seeding: every farm a scenario seeds has both (as seedByres); a camp landed by hand has neither; a save from before reads its farms as having a storehouse and as having the barn they had.
- [ ] The page draws the storehouse and the barn only where the sim has them (page/settlement drawFarm: the always-drawn lada and härbre go, the second barn's place is the barn's); the farm view shows both outbuildings with icons first.
- [ ] Tests: each halving, seeding, an old save, and the drawing rule.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years): grain and hay lost a year, storehouses and barns built by year and which first, hungry household-days, idle hours.
- [ ] `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.

Notes:
- The storehouse is sim/storehouse (loaded before sim/hunger): its grainLoss (the day's loss without one, by grain's tiers) and storeLoss are the one reckoning of sim/hunger spoilGrain and of sim/aims' weighing; sim/byre hayLoss likewise for the barn. Kept out of sim/hunger's exports (lint, 15).
- The menu weighs each by what it would save a year over AIMS.keep (1, Proposed) times its hours, at most AIMS.heirs; none offered while it would save nothing. sim/outlook reckons the grain's spoilage with the storehouse, the hay's with the barn.
- Costs as the task proposed: the storehouse 20 timber, 40 + 20 hours, loss 0.5; the barn unchanged (nothing measured to move it).
- An older save: every farm reads as having a storehouse; a barn only where it had one (sim/storehouse storehousesLoaded).
- The page: the barn at the second barn's place (YARD.barn), the storehouse at the härbre's, each only where the farm has it (yardOut); the always-drawn lada is gone.
- Left: the threshed grain is still carried to the place `barn` (VB-2027).
