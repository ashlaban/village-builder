# VB-362: Spinning and weaving: fleece to yarn to cloth, a loom, and who does it
Status: done (2026-11-24)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The mill and the loom, the kiln and the quarry); `backlog/VB-185.md`
Goal: Fleece is spun into yarn as background work indoors and yarn woven into cloth on a loom the household builds; a practice says whose work it is. Slice 6's plan (VB-185, 11 Nov), from Kim's answers in the session.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Goods: `hh.yarn` (skeins) and `hh.cloth` (lengths), through `sim/stores` with reasons; cloth does not perish; the worth table gains a length of cloth at 40 rations and a skein at 12 (Proposed).
- [x] `spin`: background work (it is held beside minding the house, cooking and the byre, and worked while indoors), 40 work hours a skein from one fleece (`TEXTILE.spinHours`), skill spinning; posted while the household has fleeces and under `TEXTILE.yarnStock` (4) skeins.
- [x] The loom: `hh.loom`, built at home as long work, 12 hours and 2 loads of timber, once. `weave`: partial work at the loom, 12 work hours a length from 2 skeins, skill weaving; posted while the household has 2 skeins.
- [x] The practice "Spinning and weaving are" (`Culture`): women's work (default) or everyone's indoor work, malleability 0.4; it sets the role ranks of `spin` and `weave` (women's: wife 7, maid 7, daughter 6, retired woman 5, others 0; everyone's: the same, and men 4).
- [x] Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): the idle share by season and by sex, skeins and lengths made a household, fleeces left unspun. The hours are scaled to the game's 576 work hours a year so that dress for six takes about a third of one adult's year (acoup, "Clothing, How Did They Make It?", III: spinning about 85% of cloth's labour; checked by search on 3 Nov).
- [x] The numbers that move are reported before and after at the seeds and maps named (Working rules); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
Notes:

- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim's answers after the plan of VB-702; section 14): Cloth is a graded good (13.3): a length draws its grade at the weaving, by the weaver's skill. Yarn is a material, not graded (D20): spinning skill is rate only. Built before VB-761: every length sound.
- 18 Nov 2026, amended to agree with VB-1123 (plan mode; Kim's decisions of 18 Nov): as VB-121: spinning and weaving are actions at the house in plan mode; a daily stint is a chore (a pill under the member's name, handed over by dragging), a batch of half a day or more a bar.
- 23 Nov 2026 (VB-761, workmanship, landed): a made piece's grade is drawn by `VB['sim/workmanship'].draw(st, maker, craft)` (stream `making`; the maker is the pawn who finished the job, passed to a kind's `done` as its fifth argument); a graded good declares `graded: {work: [properties]}` in `declareGood`, and reads its row by grade from `Goods.kindRow(good, kind).grades[grade]` (`life`, `work`, `worth`, the properties scaled); the `worth` rule takes `grade` in its context; `jobs.allot` gives a making of a graded good to the household's most skilled hand at its craft; `expectedWorth(st, id, craft)` is the head's estimate. Cloth is a counted graded good: if it lands before pots (VB-366), it builds the five counts and the takes VB-761 left (see VB-366's note).
- 24 Nov 2026 (done; sim/textile): wool moved into sim/stores (`household.wool`, reason `shear`), with `yarn` and `cloth` (five counts by grade; a put names its grade, a take spends the least first): cloth is the first graded good in a store, so the counts VB-366 was to build are here, cloth's own, and pots can follow the shape. Decisions: (1) "partial work at the loom" is full work that keeps its progress when broken off: no kind of `partial` attention is worked by the machinery (its rate is 0). (2) Spinning `yields` (a new kind field, sim/attention offerWhy): a herder's background task bars work out of its reach, which with reach 0 would keep a spinner from the fields. (3) A kind may declare its own `rank` (sim/fit prioOf): the practice and the sex set it; a woman holding the farm ranks as the mistress, and a holder standing in for an absent mistress takes her rank (VB-1545). (4) Standing farms start with a loom (as their hand cart); a head wants one once it holds a length's yarn, so founders do not fell its timber in their first winter (tests/meadows.js, seed 42). (5) Cloth is not dealt at the fair yet; dress wears it (VB-363).
