# VB-1026: Bond badges: what a person is to another, as an icon on the portrait
Status: done (2026-11-18)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (15); design.md, People, households and family records
Goal: Wherever a person is shown in relation to another, the portrait carries the bond as an icon, the same icon everywhere, with the word as its label.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] One icon a bond (`src/page/icons.js`): the five kin bonds the simulation names (spouse, parent, child, sibling, housemate) and the four opinion bands (close friend, friend, dislikes, enemy); the word is the icon's label (husband, mother, enemy).
- [x] `faceImg(id, cls, of)` (`src/page/portraits.js`): the portrait of `id` as seen by `of` carries a kin badge and, where the opinion is not neutral, an opinion badge; without `of` it is drawn as before. The page only reads (`Sim.standing`, `Sim.opinionOf`, `Sim.opinionWord`).
- [x] Used in a person's Knows best rows and on the spouse's chip of its card; a browser check that the badges are there (`tests/browser-bonds.js`, extended tier).
- [x] No change to the simulation: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`.

- 17 Nov 2026 (Kim, the session on the life story): asked for, so that a portrait can say wife or child by itself, in the game and in a story made from it. Kim chose kin and opinions (not wider kin: grandparents and in-laws would need new kin rules), and the badge on the portrait.
- The kin label is still `standing` in the simulation; VB-703 renames it to `bond`, the word the page uses here already.

## Implementation (18 Nov 2026)
- `src/page/icons.js`: `BOND_PATHS` and `bondIcon`, nine icons: spouse (the two rings of the mood's `married`), parent (an elder with a staff), child (the swaddled child of the mood's `child`), sibling (two of one branch), housemate (two under one roof); friend (a heart), close friend (the heart filled), dislikes (the heart struck through), enemy (crossed blades).
- `src/page/portraits.js`: `bondsOf(of, id)` reads `Sim.standing`, `Sim.standingWord`, `Sim.opinionOf` and `Sim.opinionWord`; `faceImg(id, cls, of)` wraps the portrait and its badges in `.bonded`. Kin sits at the lower right, the opinion at the upper right, green or red by its band; the word is the badge's label and title. The dead keep their kin badge and have no opinion badge.
- `src/sim.js`: the facade gains `standing`, which `sim/relations` already exported (the facade is not counted for width). No simulation change.
- Used in Knows best (`src/page/words.js`) and on the spouse's chip (`pawnChip(id, as, of)`, `src/page/cards.js`). The row keeps its words beside the badge: the word is the icon's label (principle 15).
- Decisions, close calls: the opinion badge was first at the lower left, where a panel's edge cut it, so both badges are on the right. The opinion's icons are hearts and blades, not the two figures of the mood's `friend`, since two figures at 11 pixels read as the sibling's. Only the nine bonds the simulation already names: wider kin is a task of its own.
- Not done here, as tasks to come if wanted: the badges in the choice of whom to play, the successors' list and the family records.
