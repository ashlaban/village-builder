# VB-1026: Page: plan mode, the head's review as a place
Status: blocked
Waits for: Kim
Priority: 2
Size: L
Design ref: docs/mocks/plan-mode.html; backlog/VB-256.md; design.md section 7 (The plan panel); AGENTS.md, Deciding without Kim, 14 to 16
Goal: At its review the head sees what the plan costs in hands and days, what it changes against the custom's, and what came of the last one, and changes it on the map.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A moment. The game offers the mode, paused, on the played head's planning day and at each season's first review; the head may open it at any other time. Leaving it goes on with the plan as it stands. A pawn that is not a head can open it read-only, as the Plan tab is today.
- [ ] The constraint is shown. The season as its days, each routine with its window and the days it is estimated to take with the hands the household has; beside it the hand-days there are, those the daily chores take, and those the plan claims. A change to the order, a target or a project redraws this before it is confirmed, and what no longer fits is named (which routine slips, to when).
- [ ] The custom's plan is the proposal; the head's is a difference. The mode opens on what the head would do with nothing changed. Each change the head has made is listed, with its estimated cost and gain, and can be undone one at a time. Both plans can be looked at, one against the other.
- [ ] The map is the surface. With the mode open, the tiles that would take the next field or meadow carry the estimate of what each would give and the walk to it; each field and meadow carries its next step and window; the tile named and a project's tile are marked. A tile is chosen and changed there. When no tile would do, the reason is shown at once, not after a refused click (VB-1027 holds the Plan tab's case).
- [ ] The loop is closed. The mode shows, for the plan the last review made, what the head estimated beside what came of it (stores, the windows kept or missed, the hands), so that a player learns whether a choice mattered.
- [ ] The hands are part of the plan: who works this season, who left or joins and when, and where a hand could be asked for (the moving round, a match); each links to where that is done.
- [ ] Every estimate is the head's (principle 14): it says whose it is, it has a range where the head's judgement gives one, and a head of poorer judgement sees a poorer estimate. What has happened is shown as it was.
- [ ] Every change goes through a verb any head has (principle 16, `Sim.head`): the mode gives the player no choice the automatic head lacks. Where a requirement above needs a choice no verb covers, the task that builds it adds the verb for every head, or leaves the choice out and says so.
- [ ] One presentation (principle 15): a routine, a target and a field look the same here, in the Plan tab and in the timetable (VB-477); the season's strip and the day's are one object at two lengths, and a day of the season opens the day. Icons and meters before words.
- [ ] Cost (rules 7 and 9): the estimates are worked out on demand while the mode is open, never in a step, and the plan names their cost (households, times the season's routines, times the work of one estimate) and measures it on founders and on a late save.
- [ ] The mode's functional test in the page's tests; a screenshot in the PR; `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` if no verb was added.

What this leaves open, for the task that plans it:
- Whether the mode is a screen of its own, the farm's card grown large, or a layer over the map; the mock is one answer, not the requirement.
- How the days a routine takes are estimated (the timetable's ranges of VB-476 and VB-477 are the likely source), and at what grain: a household's pooled hand-days, as mocked, or each member's.
- Whether the Plan tab stays as the read-only summary, or goes.
- Whether this is one task or a plan task with implement tasks under it (size L says the second).
