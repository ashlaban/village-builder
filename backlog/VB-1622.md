# VB-1622: The plan's tile tasks made general: hay and harvest beside planting, and a marked tile begun in its turn
Status: done (2026-11-24)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: docs/ui2/HANDOFF.md (round 3, points 1 and 3); backlog/VB-1621.md; src/sim/playerplan.js; src/sim/agenda.js; src/sim/meadows.js; src/sim/fieldwork.js; src/sim/plans.js (plan.tiles, the head's verbs tile and project)
Goal: A player who plans by hand can make hay and bring in the harvest, each as one task at its tile.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] One rule for a task at a tile (the third case of its kind: AGENTS.md asks for the general rule): a chain is declared as data (its name, which tiles take it and why not, its steps in the order the work goes, when it is done, its deadline), and `plant` (VB-1621) becomes the first chain with its behaviour unchanged (`tests/plan.js` section 6 passes as it stands). A task of a chain is `{<chain>: tile, kind: '<chain>', site: tile}`, added by `Sim.plan(st, 'add', {<chain>: tile, at?})`; a tile holds one task a chain.
- [x] Chain `hay`: a meadow of the played pawn's household, or the place of its open project making a meadow: the steps from clearing the meadow (a project's) through mowing and whatever the sim's haymaking goes through after it (drying, raking, carrying the hay home: read `src/sim/meadows.js`) until this year's hay of that meadow is home. It may be added before the haymaking window opens: it stands, and is fed when its step is posted. It leaves when the hay is home or the window has closed with the meadow unmown (`plan.last.which`).
- [x] Chain `harvest`: a sown field of the household: reaping when ripe and carrying the sheaves in (not the threshing, which is indoor work for any day). It may be added while the crop grows: it stands until the reaping is posted. It leaves when the sheaves are in, or the crop is lost (`which`).
- [x] A marked tile: `plant` and `hay` are also accepted for the tile the head has marked for its next field or meadow (`plan.tiles`, sim/plans) while another project is open or none is begun; when the task's turn comes and the household has no open project, the feeder begins that project through the head's own verb (the played pawn is the head; refused for one that is not, with the reason), so that nothing moves without the pawn's act; until then the task stands.
- [x] `Sim.planView(st)` and `Sim.agenda(st)` treat every chain as they treat `plant` now: `step`, `stands`; in the agenda the hours of all steps left, `ends`, `deadline` (the haymaking window's end; the frost the head expects), `verdict`, `rough`, `steps`, laid into `days` step by step, a step not yet posted laid no earlier than its window opens or the crop is expected ripe.
- [x] A read for the page, on the facade: `Sim.planGaps(st)`: the windows that press with no task of the plan covering them, for the played pawn's household: `[{tile, chain, deadline {t, what}, days (left)}]`: a field unsown in its sowing window with no plant task, a meadow unmown in the haymaking window with no hay task, a field sown with no harvest task once it is ripe or within a few days of it (a tunable). Computed when asked; writes nothing.
- [x] `tests/plan.js` and `tests/agenda.js` extended on a small constructed state: a hay task carries a meadow from mowing to hay home with no further act; a harvest task added in summer waits and then brings the sheaves in; a marked tile's plant task begins its project when the first is done; the gaps listed and gone once planned; the refusals; a save and a load go on the same. A run with no played pawn is unchanged (`node tools/same.js --base REF`, founders and solo).
