# VB-113: Cluster villages: houses around the green, fields beyond the yards
Status: done (2026-10-30)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Settlements and look (Village; Generated villages: grown, not stamped; irregular cluster villages, *klungbyar*). Kim, 30 Oct 2026 (`QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "Paths, fords and village layout"): houses cluster around the green and may touch; each farm's fields form one connected block 2 to 4 steps from its house and never next to any house, so a ring of yards separates houses from fields; neighbouring blocks may adjoin into one fenced infield.
Goal: `buildVillage` keeps houses at least 2 steps apart and puts fields right next to them, so a generated village is a scatter of farms each ringed by its own fields: it does not look like a village that has grown. Real cluster villages kept their houses together on the village site (*tomt*) with the infield around them.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Houses: the first farm stands next to the green; each further house is grown on: it touches the green, an earlier house or an earlier house's yard (a tile next to a house), preferring tiles that touch more houses, nearer the green, off stony and wet ground, with a seeded random term, so villages differ. Houses may touch each other. A house never stands next to another farm's field.
- [x] Fields: `placeFields` (VB-109) never takes a tile next to any house or next to the green; it takes them within 4 steps of the house, so fields lie 2 to 4 steps out.
- [x] The fence goes round the village as a whole: houses, green, fields and the yards and gaps they enclose, not round each gap.
- [x] New fields made in play (founders, `chooseTile`) and plots (`findSite` in `src/sim/expansion.js`) keep the rule: no field next to a house; a plot's house may touch other houses.
- [x] A test on populous medium at seed 7: no field touches a house or the green; every village's houses form one connected group with its green, through houses and yards.
- [x] A screenshot of a generated village of each size band (reported in the PR); re-record the golden file and list the changed entries.
