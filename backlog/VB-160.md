# VB-160: Plan: what raised yields over the centuries, and a model for it
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: animals
Phase: plan
Design ref: Goods and the farming year (Yield over time: the soil's cap rises 0.1 a century of farming; Decided 30 Oct, rate Agreed 2 Nov 2026).
Goal: Yields are to rise slightly over a long game. The first rule (manure's cap on the soil, `SOIL.cap` 1.15, rising 0.1 a century of a village's years of farming; Agreed 2 Nov) was reopened by Kim on 5 November: a gain should follow what the farmers do, not a village's age, and its size needs evidence. Survey what raised grain yields in the medieval Nordic setting, and propose the model. No code.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `docs/review-yields.md`: the historical mechanisms, each with its evidence, its size (as a change in the yield ratio or in rations a field) and its timescale: manuring and the ratio of meadow and stock to arable, fallow and rotation (one-, two- and three-course), tools (ard to plough, iron shares), seed selection and crop mix, drainage and clearance of stones, and climate as the confound. Sources named; where the literature gives a range or disagrees, say so.
- [ ] For each mechanism, whether the game already holds the state it would act through (soil and manure in `src/sim/fields.js`, herds and hay, tools once slice 6 has them), and which are out of reach before a later slice.
- [ ] A proposed model in which a gain follows a change in farming, with its rate and cap, and what the player sees of it; set against the reopened village-age rule, with what each costs to build and to run (a per-field value read in closed form, no daily pass: Rules for cost, 3).
- [ ] Questions for Kim in `QUESTIONS.md`, each with a proposed answer; proposed text for `design.md`, Goods and the farming year (Yield over time).
- [ ] Once answered: the implement task or tasks written, with numbers and criteria, and VB-048's `Waits for:` line names them.
