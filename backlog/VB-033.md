# VB-033: Claiming an empty farm
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: expansion
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion, Empty farms (Claims: Agreed, next batch); Customs (Claims on empty farms); Claims, ownership and territory (claims on empty farms as tile claims; Birthright: Decided 1 Nov). Review: `docs/review-claims.md`.
Goal: Empty farms (`derelict` or `ruin`) are owned by their village once VB-003 is done, but nobody can take them. Let landless adults claim one, ordered by the village's "Claims on empty farms" custom (`claims`), which today has no effect, and restore it before they move in. Depends on VB-003, VB-030 and VB-042.
Acceptance criteria (Kim answered the VB-030 questions on restoration and the meeting on 2026-10-30):
- [ ] At the moving round, adults in `st.demand` may claim an empty farm of their own village, or of another village if the custom allows. Married couples come before singles.
- [ ] Order by `claims`: `kin` kin of the old family first (by `f.line`, closest degree first), then others; `villagers` members of the farm's village first; `anyone` any landless adult, by age; `assembly` the meeting assigns it to the claimant with most voice-weighted support among those with a voice, through their ties (by age until that is built).
- [ ] A claim posts a restoration job owned by the claimant's household. Its hours scale linearly with the building's condition, along straight lines through 0 h at 100%, 20 h at 40%, 60 h at 15% and 120 h at 0% (so about 3 h at 90% and 36 h at 30%); below 15% the job is the full rebuild of VB-032. Fields gone to grass are cleared again in 10 h, scrub in 20 h. Progress persists across days.
- [ ] The claimants move in when the roof is repaired; the farm passes from the village to their household (`setOwner`), and `f.state` becomes `occupied`.
- [ ] The heirs scenario: someone in village A claims A3 within a few years (reported).
- [ ] A test per `claims` option on a constructed village.
- [ ] `claimableFarm(st, pawnId)`, the stub VB-031 left returning null, returns the farm this pawn may claim under the custom, so a village clears a plot only when nothing is claimable.
- [ ] Birthright (Kim, 2026-11-01): when a farm empties, its last holder's family keeps a realised claim (`why` `birthright`, by the last holder as a pawn) on the farm's house for `CLAIMS.birthrightYears` = 20 years (`until`); under `kin`, the claimants of that family (kin of the last holder, closest first) come before all others while it stands, and after it lapses the farm is open to the rest in the custom's order. The claim shows on the Land overlay and in the tile panel.
- [ ] A claimant's claim on the farm is an unrealised claim on its house (`why` `farm`), renewed by the restoration work; it becomes the household's with the farm.
- [ ] Answers of 2026-11-01 (the proposed answers in `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`): the claimants' new household starts as a new plot's household (VB-032): their stakes as grain, made up from their old households' grain above their buffer and seed to seed for the farm's fields and 60 days of food, and firewood and rations as a founders household; ruins (below 15%) are left out of claims until the full rebuild exists; the claimants live and eat in their old household until they move in, take the restoration at priority 9, and their old households' members may help at 5; under `assembly`, the oldest claimant first.
