# VB-033: Claiming an empty farm
Status: done
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: expansion
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion, Empty farms (Claims: Agreed, next batch); Customs (Claims on empty farms); Claims, ownership and territory (claims on empty farms as tile claims; Birthright: Decided 1 Nov). Review: `docs/review-claims.md`.
Goal: Empty farms (`derelict` or `ruin`) are owned by their village once VB-003 is done, but nobody can take them. Let landless adults claim one, ordered by the village's "Claims on empty farms" custom (`claims`), which today has no effect, and restore it before they move in. Depends on VB-003, VB-030 and VB-042.
Acceptance criteria (Kim answered the VB-030 questions on restoration and the meeting on 2026-10-30):
- [x] At the moving round, adults in `st.demand` may claim an empty farm of their own village, or of another village if the custom allows. Married couples come before singles.
- [x] Order by `claims`: `kin` kin of the old family first (by `f.line`, closest degree first), then others; `villagers` members of the farm's village first; `anyone` any landless adult, by age; `assembly` the meeting assigns it to the claimant with most voice-weighted support among those with a voice, through their ties (by age until that is built).
- [x] A claim posts a restoration job owned by the claimant's household. Its hours scale linearly with the building's condition, along straight lines through 0 h at 100%, 20 h at 40%, 60 h at 15% and 120 h at 0% (so about 3 h at 90% and 36 h at 30%); below 15% the job is the full rebuild of VB-032. Fields gone to grass are cleared again in 10 h, scrub in 20 h. Progress persists across days.
- [x] The claimants move in when the roof is repaired; the farm passes from the village to their household (`setOwner`), and `f.state` becomes `occupied`.
- [x] The heirs scenario: someone in village A claims A3 within a few years (reported).
- [x] A test per `claims` option on a constructed village.
- [x] `claimableFarm(st, pawnId)`, the stub VB-031 left returning null, returns the farm this pawn may claim under the custom, so a village clears a plot only when nothing is claimable.
- [x] Birthright (Kim, 2026-11-01): when a farm empties, its last holder's family keeps a realised claim (`why` `birthright`, by the last holder as a pawn) on the farm's house for `CLAIMS.birthrightYears` = 20 years (`until`); under `kin`, the claimants of that family (kin of the last holder, closest first) come before all others while it stands, and after it lapses the farm is open to the rest in the custom's order. The claim shows on the Land overlay and in the tile panel.
- [x] A claimant's claim on the farm is an unrealised claim on its house (`why` `farm`), renewed by the restoration work; it becomes the household's with the farm.
- [x] Answers of 2026-11-01 (the proposed answers in `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`): the claimants' new household starts as a new plot's household (VB-032): their stakes as grain, made up from their old households' grain above their buffer and seed to seed for the farm's fields and 60 days of food, and firewood and rations as a founders household; ruins (below 15%) are left out of claims until the full rebuild exists; the claimants live and eat in their old household until they move in, take the restoration at priority 9, and their old households' members may help at 5; under `assembly`, the oldest claimant first.

- 2026-11-02 (done): built in `src/sim/farmclaims.js` (commits "VB-033 (1 of 2)" and "(2 of 2)"). When a farm empties (`extinct`), its last holder keeps a realised birthright on its house for 20 years; the heirs scenario's A3 and B2 get theirs from when they emptied. At the moving round, after `st.demand` is counted, `claimFarms` gives each derelict farm (ruins are left out) to its first claimant (`claimants`): `kin` puts kin of the birthright's holder first (closest civil degree, any village within reach) while it stands, then the farm's villagers; `villagers` the farm's villagers, then villages within reach; `anyone` all within reach; `assembly` the farm's villagers by voice-weighted support through ties (`support`, VB-042, now built), the oldest on a tie. Then couples before singles, then the oldest. Claimants from another village must live within `CLAIMS.farmReach` = 15 steps between village centres (as `SERVICE.reach`); this number is new and Proposed. The claimant's unrealised claim (`why` `farm`, by the pawn) stands on the house and is renewed by the work; the restoration job is owned by the claimant's household, at priority 9 for the couple and 5 for grown housemates (`CLAIMS.claimantPrio`, `helperPrio`), and its hours done are kept on `f.claim`, also across a change of household. A claimant leaves `st.demand` (`wantsOut` counts a claim under way as a prospect of a farm), so they neither get a plot nor leave as settlers. The farm stops decaying while claimed. When the roof is mended, `moveIn` makes the new household as a plot's: stakes, made up from the old households' grain above their seed and the holder's buffer to seed for the farm's fields and 60 days of food (`CLAIMS.farmFood`), as far as it goes; the founders' firewood and two days of rations. House, fields and meadows become the household's, and the claim and birthright are spent. Fields gone to grass or scrub are cleared by the new household after moving in (job `reclaim`, 10 or 20 hours), not as part of the claim. A claim ends when the claimant dies or takes land, or lapses after 3 years unworked. A last holder who claims their own farm takes up the birthright as the claim.
- Choices made: the birthright gives priority, not exclusivity; read as exclusive, A3 (no living kin of Ivar) would have stood empty 14 more years against the "within a few years" criterion. `claimableFarm` is wired into `startPlots` (late-bound, since `sim/farmclaims` loads after `sim/expansion`); as the claims are made first, it finds a farm only for pawns nobody chose.
