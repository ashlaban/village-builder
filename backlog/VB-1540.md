# VB-1540: Standing duties: the head's day remembers
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: `design.md` section 7, Standing duties, The day's work, Types of work; `docs/review-agency.md`, section 4; `docs/review-jobs.md`, section 5.2 (the long-lived assignment)
Goal: The head's day has no memory: each morning `allot` (`src/sim/jobs.js`) weighs every member for every task afresh and breaks ties by a task's id, so nobody is the one who sees to the wood. Build design.md's row Standing duties: the types of work (`KINDS[kind].type`) of the tasks the morning's allotment gives a member within its hours are its duties, kept on the plan's day (`plan.day.duties`, written by `allot` only); at the next morning's allotment a pair whose type the member holds as a duty has its fit times `MATCH.keep` (1.25, a tunable of `sim/roles` beside `join`). A duty no task of the morning answers lapses; a much more urgent task still takes the member (keep is under `switchRatio`). The head's verb `duty` (a member, a type of work, set or lift) pins one: a set duty stands until lifted, the member leaves or the head changes, and with no choice made the rule runs (parity: the planner's rule and the played head pass the same check). A member's card names its duties.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `plan.day.duties` as `@state` on the plan's day: plain data, saved (`tests/saves.js`, `tests/plaindata.js`), written only by `allot` and the verb; `MATCH.keep` a `@tunable` with its meaning. No random draw is added.
- [ ] A new test on a constructed household (two members, two tasks of equal fit of different types) holds: the member who did a type yesterday gets it today; with `keep` at 1 the order is as before; a task above the switch test still takes the member; a duty with no task lapses; a pinned duty survives a morning with no task of its type and goes when the head changes.
- [ ] The verb `duty` is registered by the module that owns the plan's choices (as `order` and `target`), returns why not (not the head, not a member, no such type), and is in the played head's Actions or the timetable's verbs; `tests/commands.js` passes.
- [ ] The member's card and the timetable name its duties, one presentation wherever shown.
- [ ] Reported at the result, populous small, seeds 7 and 11, 2 years, before and after: the share of member-days whose first task's type is the one of the day before (the measure this task is for; a counter or a report line added for it), hungry household-days, the harvest missed (fields lost or left standing), idle share by role. Whether households were better off is said in a line; if hungry days rise by more than a tenth at both seeds, try `keep` at 1.1 and 1.15 (at most five runs, recorded) and note the shortfall for the tuning run.
- [ ] `npm run format` and `npm run verify` pass; `sim/jobs` over 700 formatted lines opens or names its split task.
