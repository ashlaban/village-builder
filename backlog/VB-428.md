# VB-428: A household that would want a cow makes a meadow first
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: animals
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Goods, production and animals (cattle, meadows); backlog/VB-390.md
Goal: VB-390 gave a household with a meadow and no cattle the want of a cow, but wantsMeadow asks for a herd first, so a new farm without herds holds no meadow and never wants one: founders small was identical run for run. Decide by Deciding without Kim whether a household with the hands for a cow makes its first meadow at the land step, and build it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The rule decided and written here; a household with the hands and no herd makes a first meadow, or the reason it does not.
- [x] Numbers before and after (populous and founders small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): household-years without cattle, on new farms; meadows; hay; hungry household-years.

Decision (13 November, without Kim): yes. A household with no herd and no meadow that has the hands for a cow (`HERD.want.hands`, 2 working
hands of 14 to 59, not retired: the count of `cowWanted`, sim/exchange) starts one meadow at its land step, by the same project, the same
choice of tile and the same work as any meadow (`wantsFirstMeadow` beside `wantsMeadow` in `startMeadow`, sim/meadows). One meadow only: the
second waits for the herd, by `wantsMeadow` as before. Not at generation: generated farms without animals still start with none and make it
by work. Reasons: it extends a system and gives the player nothing new (Deciding without Kim, 4); `design.md` has no Decided point on who
makes a meadow (its "2 to 3 tiles to a farm that keeps animals" is marked Open); a peasant farm mowed its meadow before it could winter a
cow (5, 7); nothing appears without work (the meadow is cleared by its hours, the cow still led home by VB-390's deal). No new tunable: the
threshold is VB-390's `HERD.want.hands`. A lone hand makes none, as it wants no cow.

Implementation: `src/sim/meadows.js` (`wantsFirstMeadow`, read by `startMeadow`); no new state, export, counter or loop over a growing
collection (a household's members, capped). `tests/cowwant.js`: two hands, no herd, no meadow start a meadow project; one hand does not.
`tests/meadows.js`: the check that a household without animals holds no meadow is gone (the rule changed); the others stand.

Validation: `npm run format`, `npm test`, `npm run verify` (103/103 passed; 18 targets out, advisory; the base's count was not re-run on
the loaded machine, and no counter key was added or re-recorded; `tests/counters.js` passes). No page change, no screenshot.
