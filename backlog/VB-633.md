# VB-633: Who waits and why: the marriage valve's report, and the growth bands
Status: done (2026-11-16)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Balance loops and targets (The growth budget: The pace, The overshoot, The band); People, households and family records (Marriage, nursing and growth); backlog/VB-405.md
Goal: The marriage valve and the pace of growth are reported nightly and asserted nowhere: who waits to marry and for what, at what age people first marry, how many never do, how fast the island grows in each phase, when it passes 1,000, and how far growth swings between decades.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A report, `node tools/sim.js ... --report waiting` (its own file under `tools/`, with `--help`, a line in `docs/commands.md`): on the day before each moving round, every unmarried pawn of the least age to marry and under 45, not retired, is counted once under the best outcome over every such pawn of the other sex, in this order: too hungry; nobody free; only kin too close; no home for them; only matches the score refuses; the home lacks the means; free to marry. Summed by 20 years, with the living, the servants among those waiting, and the single-person households. It reads the state and the exports of `sim/matching` (`matchRules`, `kinOk`, `matchScore`) and changes nothing in `src/`; the research script it starts from is `docs/balance/mocks/measure/scripts/marriage-bars.js` (VB-405).
- [x] The same report gives, by 20 years: the mean age at first marriage by sex (founders and those seeded married left out); of those who reached 45, the share never married; marriages a year per 1,000 living; and the round's count of marriages put off for the means.
- [x] Bands (`tests/lib/bands.js`), all `described` or `report`, none asserted, shown by `node tools/bands.js`: `growth-phase`, yearly growth by 20 years and, apart, for the years before and after the first year in which a tenth of the adults of age wait for land (`st.demand`); `year-1000`, the first year with 1,000 living, and where the run ends short of it the years it would take at the last 20 years' rate, printed as arithmetic and marked so; `marry-age-f`, `marry-age-m`; `never-married`; the waiting shares by cause; and `growth-swing`, the widest difference in yearly growth between two decades of a run (the overshoot's measure: `design.md`, The growth budget, The overshoot).
- [x] The runs: the band's own (`FULL`: populous small, founders small, heirs small) report the new probes at once. Two runs on the medium map are added to `FULL`, four seeds each (3, 7, 11, 42): `populous-medium`, 30 years, which measures the year 1,000 is reached, and `founders-medium`, 50 years, where it is arithmetic. If the night's bands then run too long for the nightly workflow (say what was seen, as a description: others share the machine), the two are left out of the default and run by `--runs populous-medium,founders-medium`, and a line in `docs/commands.md` says at what cadence.
- [x] No criterion or band here rests on hungry household-days: over four seeds of populous small they swing by 20 to 50% between versions that differ little (`backlog/VB-485.md`; VB-571 makes the steadier measure).
- [x] Tests on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): one pawn under each cause in turn; a test of the probes on a hand-made list of samples (the phase split, the year of 1,000 found and not found, the swing). `tests/review-tools.js` or the suite that checks tools' `--help` knows the report; a line in `tests/suites.js` for the new test.
- [x] The first values, in this task's notes: populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 (VB-405 measured founders over 80 years: 47% of waiting years with nobody free, 22% kin, 6% the score, 1% no home, none the means; women first married at 20 to 22).

## Notes (VB-633, 16 November 2026)

**Impact.** Tools and tests only; nothing under `src/` changes. The new `tools/waiting.js` reads the state and the exports of `sim/matching`
(`matchRules`, `kinOk`, `matchScore`) and of `sim/records`. `tests/lib/bands.js` takes the census and the year's marriage facts in every
full and reduced run (two new fields on each yearly sample, `waiting` and `facts`). It also gains the probes and the described floors of the
group `growth`, and two runs on the medium map. A probe may now return `{value, note}` (the arithmetic year of 1,000), and
`tools/bands.js` prints and records the note. A run with `weekly` is left out of the default plan, and `tools/nightly.js` runs those on
Sundays. There is no new state field, loop in the simulation or coupling. A seed is the same run with or without the census:
`tests/waiting.js` compares the hash of the state on a tiny village, and populous small seed 3 gave the same yearly
births, marriages, living and invariants over 11 years with the census and without it.

**Implementation.**
- `tools/waiting.js`: `census(VB, st)` is the census of the pool. The pool is everyone alive, unmarried, not retired, of the least age to
  marry and under 45. Each member counts once, under the best outcome of a pair over every member of the other sex:
  `pairOutcome` judges hungry, kin, home, score, means and free, as the round's rules do, with the score's noise at its mean. Nobody
  free is counted when no member of the other sex is in the pool. `yearFacts` gives the year's first marriages by sex, those who reached
  45 and those of them never married by then, the marriages, the round's `putOff` events (summed over their `n`), the living, the
  households, the single-person households and the adults of age waiting for land. `byPeriods` sums the years by 20. `report` is the
  `--report waiting` of `tools/sim.js`, which cuts each year at the census.
- `tests/lib/bands.js`: the samples carry `waiting` and `facts`. There are 19 new described floors, all `reportOnly`, so none is asserted:
  - `growth-phase-1-20`, `-21-40`, `-41-60` and `-61-80` (those a run reaches); `growth-phase-free` and `growth-phase-scarce` (before
    the land-scarce year, and from it on); `land-scarce-year`;
  - `year-1000`, with the note `arithmetic: ...` where it is worked out; `growth-swing`;
  - `marry-age-f`, `marry-age-m`, `never-married`, `marriages-per-1000`;
  - `waiting-<cause>`, one for each of the seven causes.

  `populous-medium` (30 years) and `founders-medium` (50 years), seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42, report only this group, beside growth and the
  population ratio.
- `tests/waiting.js` (verify tier). On a constructed tiny village it puts one pair under each cause in turn and checks each against
  `matchBar`'s reason where the bar has one. It also checks that every pawn counts under its best outcome, that the census and the facts
  change nothing, that the report's year matches the plain run's, and the facts on a hand-made state. On a hand-made list of samples it
  checks the probes: the phase split, the scarce year never reached, too few adults to read, the first year left out, the year of 1,000
  found and worked out (and never reached at no growth), the swing, the marriage ages, the never married, the rate and the waiting
  shares. `tests/review-tools.js` checks both `--help` texts.

**Decisions** (Deciding without Kim; the close calls are `decision` notes):
- The census is taken at the start of the round's day (autumn day 11, 00:00), which is the end of the day before it. The meetings that
  may change a custom are over by then, and it is the research script's moment, so its numbers compare with VB-405's.
- A pair is too hungry when either of the two is, as `matchBar` has it. A pawn whose every match is hungry therefore counts as hungry.
  The widowed weight comes from `wasWidowed`, as in `matchBar`; the research script passed `false`. An open offer is not a cause of its
  own, since the round leaves those pawns out.
- "Adults of age" for the land-scarce year means the living of the least age to marry or over, and `st.demand` is read at the year's end.
  A year with fewer than 20 such adults is not read: one founders' servant waiting is a tenth of ten. Year 1 is not read either, because
  its round sorts out the placed start (populous medium seed 7: 47 of 360 adults waited at the end of year 1, 7 at the end of year 2).
- Never married counts those, founders left out, who were alive at their 45th birthday within the run and had no union before it.
- The year of 1,000 is worked out at the growth of the last 20 years (the whole run's when it is shorter). It is infinite when that growth
  is 0 or below.
- The swing compares whole decades counted from the start.
- The medium runs are weekly, not nightly. A populous medium run took 13 to 14 minutes of CPU (722 to 832 s, about an hour of wall time
  on a machine shared with three other runs and other agents). A populous small run took 4 to 5 minutes of CPU on the same machine. On
  four lanes the medium runs would add about a quarter of an hour of wall time to the night's bands, which ran in about 2 to 3 minutes on
  10 November, inside a workflow capped at 90 minutes. Founders medium is cheap (25 to 39 s of CPU), but it goes with its pair.

populous nearly everyone of the pool has some pair the rules allow on the eve. The census, which takes the best over every pawn of the
other sex, cannot see what the round does with them: one partner each, by score and village, `goAhead`, and the means of the one home
chosen. A follow-up task (`task` note) reports what became of those free on the eve.
