# VB-633: Who waits and why: the marriage valve's report, and the growth bands
Status: needs-review
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Balance loops and targets (The growth budget: The pace, The overshoot, The band); People, households and family records (Marriage, nursing and growth); backlog/VB-405.md
Goal: The marriage valve and the pace of growth are reported nightly and asserted nowhere: who waits to marry and for what, at what age people first marry, how many never do, how fast the island grows in each phase, when it passes 1,000, and how far growth swings between decades.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A report, `node tools/sim.js ... --report waiting` (its own file under `tools/`, with `--help`, a line in `docs/commands.md`): on the day before each moving round, every unmarried pawn of the least age to marry and under 45, not retired, is counted once under the best outcome over every such pawn of the other sex, in this order: too hungry; nobody free; only kin too close; no home for them; only matches the score refuses; the home lacks the means; free to marry. Summed by 20 years, with the living, the servants among those waiting, and the single-person households. It reads the state and the exports of `sim/matching` (`matchRules`, `kinOk`, `matchScore`) and changes nothing in `src/`; the research script it starts from is `docs/balance/mocks/measure/scripts/marriage-bars.js` (VB-405).
- [ ] The same report gives, by 20 years: the mean age at first marriage by sex (founders and those seeded married left out); of those who reached 45, the share never married; marriages a year per 1,000 living; and the round's count of marriages put off for the means.
- [ ] Bands (`tests/lib/bands.js`), all `described` or `report`, none asserted, shown by `node tools/bands.js`: `growth-phase`, yearly growth by 20 years and, apart, for the years before and after the first year in which a tenth of the adults of age wait for land (`st.demand`); `year-1000`, the first year with 1,000 living, and where the run ends short of it the years it would take at the last 20 years' rate, printed as arithmetic and marked so; `marry-age-f`, `marry-age-m`; `never-married`; the waiting shares by cause; and `growth-swing`, the widest difference in yearly growth between two decades of a run (the overshoot's measure: `design.md`, The growth budget, The overshoot).
- [ ] The runs: the band's own (`FULL`: populous small, founders small, heirs small) report the new probes at once. Two runs on the medium map are added to `FULL`, four seeds each (3, 7, 11, 42): `populous-medium`, 30 years, which measures the year 1,000 is reached, and `founders-medium`, 50 years, where it is arithmetic. If the night's bands then run too long for the nightly workflow (say what was seen, as a description: others share the machine), the two are left out of the default and run by `--runs populous-medium,founders-medium`, and a line in `docs/commands.md` says at what cadence.
- [ ] No criterion or band here rests on hungry household-days: over four seeds of populous small they swing by 20 to 50% between versions that differ little (`backlog/VB-485.md`; VB-571 makes the steadier measure).
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): one pawn under each cause in turn; a test of the probes on a hand-made list of samples (the phase split, the year of 1,000 found and not found, the swing). `tests/review-tools.js` or the suite that checks tools' `--help` knows the report; a line in `tests/suites.js` for the new test.
- [ ] The first values, in this task's notes: populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 (VB-405 measured founders over 80 years: 47% of waiting years with nobody free, 22% kin, 6% the score, 1% no home, none the means; women first married at 20 to 22).
