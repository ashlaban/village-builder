# VB-165: Inspect the map from the keyboard, and lists beside the canvas
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: Commands, time steps and views. External review of 2 November (`docs/review-external.md`).
Goal: Keys pan, zoom and set the speed (`src/page/input.js`), and the family tree takes the keyboard (`src/page/family.js`), but selecting a village, a household or a pawn needs the pointer, and everything the map shows is drawn on the canvas, out of reach of a screen reader. Make the map's things reachable from the keyboard and readable as text.
Questions: in `QUESTIONS.md`, "VB-162, VB-163 and VB-165: the external review's new tasks".
Acceptance criteria (draft, as proposed):
- [ ] A "Lists" panel (key `L`): villages, and under each its households and their people, as HTML buttons in a tree; Tab and the arrow keys move through it; Enter selects the thing on the map, centres the view on it and opens its panel, exactly as a click does.
- [ ] Keys on the map: `[` and `]` step to the previous and next village, `,` and `.` to the previous and next household in the selected village, `Escape` clears the selection; each is shown in the page's help.
- [ ] The selection's name and kind are announced through an `aria-live="polite"` region; the info panels are headed and labelled, and every button has a text label.
- [ ] The browser smoke test (`tests/browser.js`) drives it: from page load, keys alone select the first household of the first village and open its panel.
- [ ] The page only reads `st` (lint); behaviour unchanged (golden file unchanged).
