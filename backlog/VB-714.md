Goal: Hay from common meadows is shared by a rule of the culture (Kim, 16 Nov: later, after the alderman, VB-314). It gives the alderman's use of a common meadow (target-v2, trace 7.1, step 2) its thing.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] It starts with a short plan in the task file (size L): the village's meadow as a routine of the meeting beside the common field (`sim/commons` as the model: claimed and cleared on the village's land, raked and mown by the households on the routine's work days), the village's hay store as a place at the green, and the sharing; it is split into tasks of M or less, with reserved IDs of its own, if it does not fit one.
- [ ] `PRACTICES.hayShare` (a rule of the culture: not voted on, no drift): `work` ("Each takes what it mowed"; the base), `lots` ("Strips drawn by lot": each household with animals draws a strip each year and mows it), `herd` ("By the head wintered"), `need` ("To those short of hay first"). Under `herd` and `need` the alderman shares the hay, the day after the last of it is carried in, through a verb (principle 16; target-v2 trace 7.1, step 2).
- [ ] Nothing teleports: under `work` and `lots` the hay is carried from the meadow to the mower's barn, as a household's own is; under `herd` and `need` it is carried to the village's store by those who mowed, a share changes owner where it lies, and the household it is shared to fetches it, by the load (30 on the back, 90 with a hand cart, 300 with an ox cart).
- [ ] The autumn count reads the hay a household holds and its share not yet fetched; hay in the village's store spoils as barn hay does; `tools/stocks.js` gets the store's line (at none: nothing is shared) and `tests/lib/invariants.js` its invariants (not negative; the shares sum to at most the store).
- [ ] Numbers before and after, each option forced, at populous small seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 (15 years): hay a household by source, head wintered, emergency slaughters, hours mowing and carrying, hungry days a household (the distribution); who gains under each option.
- [ ] Cost (Rules for cost 9), to be confirmed by its plan: villages with a meadow (some tens at `medium-ref`) × once a year × the routine's tiles (mowing 16 h a tile, as on a household's meadow) and one sharing pass over the village's households in its meeting step, with a declared counter; carrying, one trip a load; no daily pass beyond the tasks it posts.
- [ ] `design.md`: the alderman's row (no land goes with the office; a common meadow's use waits for this task) and the row Hay from common meadows are brought up to date by this task's plan, by a design pass if it changes them.
