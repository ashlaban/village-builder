# VB-119: Mixed meals go further, and fresh food spoils
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-118
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Goods and the farming year: Mixed meals, Meals; Long work and processing: Milling at home). Job review, second pass, 30 Oct 2026: rations made from several food sources are more nutritious, combined with the flour mechanism (VB-009).
Goal: Fish and gathered food only matter if they change what a household needs. A ration is the unit and the cook makes meals from foods (Kim, 10 November; VB-259): a pot of several foods goes further, which rewards a household that fishes and gathers beside its fields; fresh food spoils, which keeps a summer's catch from feeding a winter; and a household with nothing cooked eats its grain raw, at a loss.
Decided (Kim, 10 November; `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-119"): variety is judged in the pot, at cooking; uncooked grain at a penalty.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Foods in `FOODS` (VB-259): fish (VB-117) and gathered food (VB-118) with a shelf life of 3 days, each its own source; grain and flour one source; milk and meat as their rows say.
- [ ] Fresh food is held in batches with the day it came in (`hh.fresh`, `@state`, invariants; at most 4 batches a food, since older ones are lost); a batch older than its shelf life is lost and noted in the ledger, through `sim/stores` with a reason. No daily pass over every household to spoil: a batch's loss is read from its day when the store is next touched, or falls due in the due-queue (Rules for cost, 3 and 4).
- [ ] The pot: the cook makes the next day's meals from the household's foods, what spoils first going in first (oldest batch first), then grain up to the need. A food is a source of the pot when it gives at least a fifth of the pot's rations. A pot of 2 sources makes 1 / 0.9 times its rations in meals, of 3 or more 1 / 0.8 (so the day's need is met with 10% or 20% less food, as decided on 30 October).
- [ ] People eat meals. A household with no meals eats grain uncooked at a penalty: 1.25 rations of grain for a ration of need (`ECON.rawGrain`; the number is the agent's starting value, to be confirmed in the PR). Fresh food that is marked eaten uncooked in `FOODS` (gathered food) is eaten before raw grain, at no penalty.
- [ ] Tests: a household with grain only, and the same with grain and fish at a quarter of the pot, over 12 days: the second uses 10% less food. A household that misses its cooking for 3 days uses 25% more grain on the days it has no meals.
- [ ] Populous small at seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42, 5 years, before and after: hungry household-days (the mean and the worst household), the share of pots with 2 and with 3 sources, days eaten raw, grain left at harvest, food spoiled. The penalty adds no hungry household-day at any seed in an ordinary year, or the PR names the households it does and why. Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries.
