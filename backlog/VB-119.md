# VB-119: Mixed meals go further, and fresh food spoils
Status: needs-review
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: food
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Goods and the farming year: Mixed meals, Meals; Long work and processing: Milling at home). Job review, second pass, 30 Oct 2026: rations made from several food sources are more nutritious, combined with the flour mechanism (VB-009).
Goal: Fish and gathered food only matter if they change what a household needs. A meal from several sources goes further, which rewards a household that fishes and gathers alongside its fields, and fresh food that spoils keeps a summer's catch from feeding a winter.
Acceptance criteria (proposed; see `QUESTIONS.md`, VB-119):
- [ ] Food sources: grain or flour (one source), fish (VB-117), gathered food (VB-118); milk and meat with the animals arch.
- [ ] Fresh food is held in batches with the day they came in (`hh.fresh`, `@state`, invariants); the daily meal takes fresh food first, oldest first, then cooked rations, then grain; a batch older than 3 game days is lost and noted in the ledger.
- [ ] A source counts for the day when it gives at least a fifth of what is eaten. With 2 sources the day's need is 90% of the rations, with 3 or more 80%.
- [ ] Built with VB-009 (milling): rations cooked from flour at 1:1 or grain at 1.25:1. VB-009's blocking problem (the cook's job missed on busy spring days) is solved first, for example by eating grain when there are no rations, as today.
- [ ] A test: a household with grain only, and the same household with grain and fish, over 12 days: the second eats 10% less in total.
- [ ] Populous medium and founders at seeds 7 and 42, 5 years, weather held at 1.0: hungry household-days, the share of meals with 2 and 3 sources, grain left at harvest and food spoiled, before and after (reported). The food balance of VB-080 is kept in an ordinary year.
