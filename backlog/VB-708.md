# VB-708: Followers: a pawn looks to the one it is most bound to, and being looked to is standing
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-705, VB-690
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (1, 8, 13, 16); design.md, Exchange, goodwill and obligations; Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Households and other bodies); backlog/VB-464.md
Goal: One relation, `looksTo`, over a table of bonds that later tasks add rows to; standing gains by followers; the lord has duties; pyramids form with no rule of their own.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `pawn.looksTo` (a pawn id or null; owner in `src/owners.json`, `@state`, the save, invariants: the one looked to lives, nobody looks to itself, no cycle) and a live index of followers by lord (rule 1). A household's members look where their head does unless they hold a bond of their own (a servant looks to its master).
- [ ] A table of bonds, each a row: what makes it (goodwill owed above `PATRON.above`; service; later a tenancy, VB-316, and a loan or days owed, VB-312 and VB-462, each adding its row), its weight, and when it ends. A pawn looks to the lord of its weightiest bond when it has none; it does not change lord while that bond holds (Kim: only when the tie breaks).
- [ ] The tie breaks when its bond ends (the balance fades under the threshold, the debt is met, the tenancy ends, the service year is over) or when the lord fails the follower: a follower in need asks its lord first after kin (VB-691's order), and a lord who refuses what he could give above his floor breaks the tie and commits a breach (VB-707, towards a follower). Events for a tie begun and ended, with why.
- [ ] Standing: a term by followers, each counted by its bond's weight and by its own followers at a discount (a named tunable), so a lord of lords stands higher; read lazily, refreshed where standing is (VB-705).
- [ ] What a follower gives beyond its bond's own dues: a lean towards its lord's side in the meeting's decisions and in the alderman's election (`PATRON.lean`; a lean, not a vote: Kim), and its yes to his asks weighed with the tie.
- [ ] Pyramids (Kim: wanted in place before the outside world arrives): a lord may himself look to another, in his village or beyond; mostly local follows from the bonds (a patron is of the village, service within a day's walk; only loans and tenancies made through the gathering cross villages). No cap on depth; cycles are refused.
- [ ] Reported, populous small and medium, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 40 years, and founders small seeds 7 and 42, 50 years: followers of the first, second and third pawn of each village by decade, and their share of the village's households (Kim expects about one or two big men a village: a description, and a row of `docs/tuning.md` if it is far off; principle 13, no count is set); ties' length, why they ended, lords who failed a follower; the depth of pyramids and ties across villages; whether the first is the alderman; hungry household-days of followers against others.
- [ ] The brakes are those planned, and none is added: goodwill's half-life, debtors' politics (VB-320), the alderman by turns, half a holder's goodwill passing to the heir. The report says which of them acted.
- [ ] Cost (rules 1, 2, 5, 9): one stored id a pawn; ties are looked at on the lord's planning day after harvest and where a bond ends, over a pawn's balances (at most 32); the standing term is a count kept by the index. Measured on `small-y40` and `medium-ref`.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim, the session on the social quantities): followers are dependants, not a new relation: the four dependencies the backlog plans (client, tenant, debtor, servant) share `looksTo`. VB-464, VB-316, VB-312, VB-462 and VB-634 each add or use a row.
