# VB-280: The order of villages turns by the year, so no village always asks first
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-277
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Leaving home: A village's round). `QUESTIONS.md`, "VB-242", question 1. Measurements: `backlog/VB-242.md` (The order of villages).
Goal: In village id order (VB-277), the men of the first villages ask before anyone else every year. A woman of a later village may be chosen by an earlier village's man before her own village's men ask, and never the other way round. VB-242's static study, on 38 states (populous small, seeds 1 to 6, years 5 to 30, plus `small-y40` and `medium-ref`), had the same men ask under three orders. Today the first third of village ids lose the woman they score best 38% of the times they ask; by village id order they lose her 21% of the times, and the middle third 48% (43% today). The first third marry 44% of the times they ask (36% today), the middle third 36% (41%) and the last 40% (45%). Turning the first village by the year spreads the advantage: 34%, 40% and 57%, varying with the year rather than the village. The proposed answer in `QUESTIONS.md` is the criteria below.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The villages' steps of the round (VB-277) and of the meetings (VB-278) start at village `year mod villages` and go on in id order, wrapping: village (s + j) mod villages on the window's j-th slot. `year` is `cal(st.t).year`, so the order is deterministic and draws nothing. The number of villages is the one the step reads (VB-277's window rule).
- [ ] `tests/spread.js`: each village's step runs exactly once a day in the turned order, and saves between steps go on, as before.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, as VB-277 reports them: marriages a year by the man's village's third (first, middle and last third of ids), and men's first-choice losses by third, over populous small at seeds 1 to 12 for 30 years. The thirds should be within the runs' spread of one another after. The demography gate's three measures, each reported with its residual. Customs over 20 years at seeds 7 and 42 (`--report customs`). The golden file re-recorded, with the changed entries listed.
- [ ] Cost: none. One modulo a step.
