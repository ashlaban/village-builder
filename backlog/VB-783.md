# VB-783: Sleeping away: a pawn has a bed, and sim/away holds the stays (the journeys move there)
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: far
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Moving to new land (Settling in steps; Sleeping away from home); docs/plans/VB-445.md, section 4; design.md, Between villages: distance and journeys (A journey; Provisions and the season)
Goal: A pawn can sleep at a place that is not its home and work from it, by the mechanism that already carries a journey, and nothing else is special.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `sim/away` (a new module; `src/owners.json`, the manifest, `docs/architecture/modules.md`) owns `st.away[]`, the open stays, each `{id, pawn, why, bed, roof, food, from, to}`, and `agent.away`, its index (a cache with one writer); the journeys' code leaves `sim/agents` for it (VB-422 planned that split: say in its file what is left of it). `st.journeys` and `st.journeySeq` go: a save with them loads as stays (`src/sim/saves.js`, `tests/saves.js`), and invariants cover the new fields (`tests/lib/invariants.js`: one stay a pawn, a stay's pawn alive, `agent.away` and `st.away` agree).
- [x] The bed: one function gives the tile a pawn's day starts from and ends at (its household's house; its stay's bed; on the road, where it stands), and what read the house for the walk home, a job's reach and the day's end reads it. List in the task file every reader changed.
- [x] A stay at a place, by two verbs any pawn has (`src/sim/commands.js`): `lodge` (walk to a tile and stay, with a `why` and a pack) and `comeHome`. The pack is taken as a journey's provisions are, a ration for each day planned, at most `AWAY.pack` (12), from the household's food above its seed; a household with nothing to spare sends nobody, and the verb says why.
- [x] Meals: a pawn away eats a ration a day from its pack and is left out of its household's meal by one test of `agent.away` (it replaces `sim/hunger`'s `onRoad` and `sim/help`'s copy); what is left of the pack returns to the household's store when it is home (the store kind 'journey' becomes 'away'; `tools/stocks.js` gains the pack's line).
- [x] The working day away: 6:00 to 18:00, on the jobs its stay names only; no chore of its house and no occasion (as a journey takes none today). It comes home when its work is done or none is open, when the pack is down to the walk home's ration, when it has gone hungry or is ill, and when its household changes; checked at its meal and when its job ends. `AWAY.walk` (4 hours there and back) is the tunable a caller reads to choose between walking daily and lodging.
- [x] A guest's bed: a stay may name a house of another household as its bed, with that household's head's yes (the check of `lodge`), roof 'house': what VB-289's visit and a late carrier need. Nothing sends a guest yet.
- [x] The journeys behave as before: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`, or the differences are listed with their cause; `tests/journeys.js` passes unchanged but for names.
- [x] Tests on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): a pawn lodges two hours from home with a pack of 3 rations, works there two days, is absent from its household's meals, walks home on the third with nothing left; a household at its seed sends nobody; a save in the middle plays as the unbroken run.
- [x] Cost (Rules for cost, 9): the pawns away are tens on an island of 1,000; a meal a day each and no loop over pawns. A counter `awayMeals` in `VB.COUNTERS`; `node tests/counters.js` before and after.

## Implementation (VB-783, 17 November 2026)

Impact note. New module `sim/away` (layer agency, loaded after `sim/agents`, before `sim/commands`) owns `st.away`, `st.awaySeq` and
`agent.away` (cache, one writer). It reads `sim/agents`' walking (`walkLeg`, `restOnRoad`, `turnTo`, `setDown`, `stop`, `waitIndoors`,
`standBy`, `placeAtHome`, `stayOf`), `sim/stores`, `sim/hunger` (`ate`, `rationOf`), `sim/exchange`, `sim/lying`, `sim/relations`
(`opinionOf`, the host's yes) and `sim/grazing`; it writes the stays, the packs (through `sim/stores`, reason `away`) and the gifts on the
road as the journeys did. No new coupling the other way: `sim/agents` tells it of a leg's end, a rest's end on the road, a death, a move made
once a load is in and an arrival at the bed by `sim/events` (`legEnd`, `legDue`, `stillAway`, `moveOn`, `atBed`), `sim/heirs` of a
changed household (`rehoused`, where it called `moved`), `sim/births` of a child to place (`placeBorn`); `sim/commands` reaches it
through `later`. So the upward calls fall by one (20 to 19: `sim/births -> sim/agents` went; `tests/layers-baseline.json` lowered).
`sim/agents` falls from 1,511 to 1,173 formatted lines; what is left of VB-422's split there is the attention slots (VB-251) and the
legs and awake set (VB-205, VB-473); its counted exports are 37 (the nine journey exports out, nine walking primitives and the bed in).

Every reader changed for the bed (`sim/agents` `bed(st, a)`: the household's house; a lodger's bed; on the road, where it stands; a
lodger turned for home, the house):
- `sim/agents` `goHome`: walks to the bed (the walk home, and the day's end: `sim/clock`'s 18:00 entry issues `goHome`).
- `sim/agents` `placeAtHome`, `placeByDoor` (dawn): by the door of the bed (`bedSpot`).
- `sim/jobs` `score` and `fit` (a job's reach, the distance term): from the bed.
- Kept on the household's house, on purpose: `homeSpot` for an indoor task (`start`) and a load brought to the door (`ended`): both are
  at the house whoever does them; `idleAbout`'s stroll (a lodger never idles about); `sim/occasions`' hours to a feast (a lodger goes to none).

The one test of `agent.away` (`a.away !== undefined`) replaced: `sim/hunger`'s `onRoad` (the meal), `sim/help`'s copy (who carries a
gift), `sim/offers`' copy, `sim/occasions`' phases (`canGo`), `sim/jobs`' `travelling` in `allot` (the household's day), and the fetcher's
`travelling` in `haulerOf`; for a journey it is the same set of pawns (agent.away is set exactly while phase is 'journey' or 'road').
`sim/agents`' `travelling` (the phases) stays for what is about the road itself (`placeByDoor`, `sim/clock`'s day end, `sim/lifecycle`'s
death on the road, the verb check `free`).

Decisions (Deciding without Kim):
- The pack is the whole pack or nobody goes: `lodge` is refused unless the household's grain above its seed covers every day planned (at
  most `AWAY.pack`), 'has no food to spare for the pack'. Reason: a half pack sends the worker home midway and wastes the walk; the design
  says a household with nothing to spare sends nobody (note `decision`).
- The walk home's ration is (ceil(walk / TRAVEL.dayWalk) - 1) rations: none for a walk home within the day, since it eats at home that
  night; a lodger turns home at the meal that leaves it no more than that. `walk` is the route's hours from the house to the bed when it set
  out (its estimate), kept on the stay.
- Ill is health below `HEALTH.riskBelow` (50), where the death roll begins to weigh it; gone hungry is a meal from the pack that fell short.
- The head's yes to a guest: the host household's holder does not dislike the guest (its opinion of it is not below 0). Nothing sends a
  guest yet (VB-289 will).
- "None is open" is checked whenever the lodger is free for work (6:00, and each time its task ends): a stay that names no open task ends
  then (a stay naming none, as the guest's test, ends at the next morning). The meal checks the rest.
- A lodger whose household changes turns for home at once (`moved`), not at its meal: to another village its stay becomes the journey,
  with what is left of its pack. The meal check covers a change it missed.
- `AWAY` is a tunable of `src/sim/rules.js` (pack 12, walk 4); nothing reads `AWAY.walk` yet (VB-784 and VB-781 will).
- The journeys' acts moved with their state, and the walking stayed in `sim/agents`, reached through primitives that write the agent's
  owned fields; `sim/agents` reaches `sim/away` only through `sim/events` (the module order forbids a later lookup).

Validation.
- `node tools/same.js --base cf5ea11` (founders and heirs, seeds 7 and 42, tiny, 2 years): differs only by names, checked leaf by leaf
  with `--keep`: `journeys` is `away` (each stay gains `bed: null`, `roof: 'none'`), `journeySeq` is `awaySeq`, the travellers' agents gain
  `away` (the stay's id), and the households' ledger entries of kind `journey` are `away`. Everything else is the same.
- `tests/journeys.js` passes with names only changed (`st.journeys` to `st.away`, the ledger kind). `tests/legs.js`, `tests/fetch.js`,
  `tests/help.js`, `tests/commons.js`, `tests/exchange.js`, `tests/grain.js`, `tests/commands.js` likewise.
- `tests/away.js` (new, on a tiny state): a pawn lodges 2.0 hours from home at 16:00 with a pack of 3 rations (4 grain taken, uncooked),
  sleeps at its bed, works the task its stay names on days 1 and 2, is out of its household's meal (the meals taken are the others'
  rations), eats a ration a day (2 `awayMeals` over the two days), turns home at the third meal with nothing left and walks home on the
  third morning, its stay over; a household at its seed sends nobody ('has no food to spare for the pack'); a guest sleeps at the host's
  house (roof 'house', a pack of `AWAY.pack` for 20 days asked), turns home by `comeHome` and brings what is left of its pack back into its
  household's meals; a save made at noon of the first day loads into a world of its own and plays on as the unbroken run.
- `tests/saves.js`: a save with `st.journeys` (an open journey, provisions of kind 'journey') loads as a stay with its index and plays on as
  the save made today. `tests/lib/invariants.js`: one stay a pawn, its pawn alive, `agent.away` and `st.away` agree, a lodger's bed, roof
  and host.
- Counters (`node tests/counters.js`, two villages, 30 days, 889 living pawn-days): every counter the same before and after; `awayMeals`
  0 (no journey and no lodger in the probe). Cost: a meal a day for each stay, over `st.away` (tens), and `dayAway` only when a stay is
  open; no loop over pawns added (the household's meal skips a member by its index).
- Balance: no change of behaviour on any run today (nothing lodges until VB-784), so no numbers before and after beyond `same`.

Found: a pawn who dies away loses its pack (the rations vanish with the stay, as the journeys did), and what is left of a pack comes
home as meals though it left as grain (a ration of grain is 1 / ECON.uncooked, so a quarter of it vanishes, and grain becomes meals with
no cook): noted as a teleport, with a task of the arch `physical` (VB-962).
