# VB-783: Sleeping away: a pawn has a bed, and sim/away holds the stays (the journeys move there)
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: far
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Moving to new land (Settling in steps; Sleeping away from home); docs/plans/VB-445.md, section 4; design.md, Between villages: distance and journeys (A journey; Provisions and the season)
Goal: A pawn can sleep at a place that is not its home and work from it, by the mechanism that already carries a journey, and nothing else is special.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `sim/away` (a new module; `src/owners.json`, the manifest, `docs/architecture/modules.md`) owns `st.away[]`, the open stays, each `{id, pawn, why, bed, roof, food, from, to}`, and `agent.away`, its index (a cache with one writer); the journeys' code leaves `sim/agents` for it (VB-422 planned that split: say in its file what is left of it). `st.journeys` and `st.journeySeq` go: a save with them loads as stays (`src/sim/saves.js`, `tests/saves.js`), and invariants cover the new fields (`tests/lib/invariants.js`: one stay a pawn, a stay's pawn alive, `agent.away` and `st.away` agree).
- [ ] The bed: one function gives the tile a pawn's day starts from and ends at (its household's house; its stay's bed; on the road, where it stands), and what read the house for the walk home, a job's reach and the day's end reads it. List in the task file every reader changed.
- [ ] A stay at a place, by two verbs any pawn has (`src/sim/commands.js`): `lodge` (walk to a tile and stay, with a `why` and a pack) and `comeHome`. The pack is taken as a journey's provisions are, a ration for each day planned, at most `AWAY.pack` (12), from the household's food above its seed; a household with nothing to spare sends nobody, and the verb says why.
- [ ] Meals: a pawn away eats a ration a day from its pack and is left out of its household's meal by one test of `agent.away` (it replaces `sim/hunger`'s `onRoad` and `sim/help`'s copy); what is left of the pack returns to the household's store when it is home (the store kind 'journey' becomes 'away'; `tools/stocks.js` gains the pack's line).
- [ ] The working day away: 6:00 to 18:00, on the jobs its stay names only; no chore of its house and no occasion (as a journey takes none today). It comes home when its work is done or none is open, when the pack is down to the walk home's ration, when it has gone hungry or is ill, and when its household changes; checked at its meal and when its job ends. `AWAY.walk` (4 hours there and back) is the tunable a caller reads to choose between walking daily and lodging.
- [ ] A guest's bed: a stay may name a house of another household as its bed, with that household's head's yes (the check of `lodge`), roof 'house': what VB-289's visit and a late carrier need. Nothing sends a guest yet.
- [ ] The journeys behave as before: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`, or the differences are listed with their cause; `tests/journeys.js` passes unchanged but for names.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`): a pawn lodges two hours from home with a pack of 3 rations, works there two days, is absent from its household's meals, walks home on the third with nothing left; a household at its seed sends nobody; a save in the middle plays as the unbroken run.
- [ ] Cost (Rules for cost, 9): the pawns away are tens on an island of 1,000; a meal a day each and no loop over pawns. A counter `awayMeals` in `VB.COUNTERS`; `node tests/counters.js` before and after.
