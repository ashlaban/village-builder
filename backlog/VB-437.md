# VB-437: Reach map mode: walking time from a selected tile, in bands the rules use
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Between villages: distance and journeys (Near and far); World and scale (Walking and terrain)
Goal: Kim can see what is within reach of a place: pick a tile, and the land is banded by walking time from it by the pawns' own route costs.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A button on the bar under the minimap (key H) turns the mode on and off; it stands beside the other overlays and shares nothing with them.
- [x] While it is on, the tile last selected by a click (land, a house, a green) is the origin; another click moves it. A selected person keeps it.
- [x] Land is coloured by one-way walking hours from the origin, by the route search's own costs (`reachCosts`, `sim/routes`), unladen.
- [x] The bands end at 15 min, 30 min, 1 h, `BUILD.stoneWalk` (3 h), `CLAIMS.farmHours` (6 h), `TRAVEL.dayWalk` (12 h) and `ROUTE.reach / SPEED` (16.7 h), read from the rules; land further by land is grey, land with no way by land has no colour.
- [x] A dark line runs between bands, at least 2 pixels wide at any zoom; a key lists the bands seen and what each hour means.
- [x] The page only reads: nothing is written to `st`.

## Decisions (Kim, 13 November, asked in the session)
- **Bands by the rules, not 6/12/24/48 h.** Walking is 12 tiles an hour on meadow and the route search stops at `ROUTE.reach` (16.7 h), so on
  today's maps 24 and 48 h lie beyond what the simulation treats as reachable and 6 h covers most of an island. Kim chose the rule-based edges.
  15 and 30 min and 1 h are no rule's: they give detail near the origin (1 h is about the next village; design.md, Two readings of one grid).
- **The origin follows the selection**, and **a band's time is one-way walking hours**, the number the rules compare against.

## Implementation notes
- `src/page/reach.js` (new, in `src/page.json` after `page/ground.js`), a layer `L.reach` above the ground's, `#reachBtn` and `#reachKey` in
  `src/body.html`, an icon, the shortcut H (W is a camera key). No module of the simulation changed and no state was added.
- One Dijkstra search over all passable tiles from the origin (`reachCosts` with no limit) when the origin changes and every 2 seconds
  while the mode is on (paths wear, bridges are built); the drawing is made again only when a tile's band changed. The search counts
  into `VB.count.reachSettled` as the simulation's do; counters are outside `st`.
- `tools/shot.js --reach` takes its screenshots with the mode on, measured from the largest village's green.
- Not done: hour labels along the lines on the map (the key carries them), the time to the tile under the pointer, the minimap.
