# VB-1155: Each farm owes its own share, and a farm's debt outlives its household
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, Handover and shares (Shares still owed)
Goal: A sibling owed shares by two farms is paid by each; today the farm that owes last takes over what the earlier farm owed (oweShare in src/sim/obligations.js, kept from pawn.owedFrom by VB-310), and a share whose debtor household dissolved is never paid though the farm lives on.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `oweShare` no longer transfers a sibling's open shares from other farms: each share keeps its debtor, and `payOwed` (`src/sim/cottage.js`) pays each farm's shares from that farm's grain above its reserve, in the order of the sibling's id, then the share's id.
- [ ] When a debtor household dissolves and another household takes its farm (heir, claim, trust's end), the open shares it owed pass to that household by `transfer` (the farm pays: design.md, Shares still owed); when the farm is abandoned they lapse, told once.
- [ ] Tests on a small constructed state (`tests/obligations.js`): two farms each pay their own; a farm taken over pays what it owed; an abandoned farm's shares lapse.
- [ ] A change to behaviour: numbers before and after at heirs and populous, seeds 7 and 42, small, 20 years (shares made, paid, lapsed, open at the end; living), and whether households are better off.
