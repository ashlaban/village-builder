# VB-1155: Each farm owes its own share, and a farm's debt outlives its household
Status: done (2026-11-19)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, Handover and shares (Shares still owed)
Goal: A sibling owed shares by two farms is paid by each; today the farm that owes last takes over what the earlier farm owed (oweShare in src/sim/obligations.js, kept from pawn.owedFrom by VB-310), and a share whose debtor household dissolved is never paid though the farm lives on.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `oweShare` no longer transfers a sibling's open shares from other farms: each share keeps its debtor, and `payOwed` (`src/sim/cottage.js`) pays each farm's shares from that farm's grain above its reserve, in the order of the sibling's id, then the share's id.
- [x] When a debtor household dissolves and another household takes its farm (heir, claim, trust's end), the open shares it owed pass to that household by `transfer` (the farm pays: design.md, Shares still owed); when the farm is abandoned they lapse, told once.
- [x] Tests on a small constructed state (`tests/obligations.js`): two farms each pay their own; a farm taken over pays what it owed; an abandoned farm's shares lapse.
- [ ] A change to behaviour: numbers before and after at heirs and populous, seeds 7 and 42, small, 20 years (shares made, paid, lapsed, open at the end; living), and whether households are better off.

Noted by VB-310, 18 November 2026.

Done 19 Nov 2026. Decisions: a dissolved debtor's shares are moved at the moving round (payOwed), not at the dissolution: the farm's new
household (f.household, not dissolved) takes them by transfer and pays from the next round; a farm with none, or derelict, lapses them (told
once a share, by Obligations.lapse); a farm empty but not yet derelict waits. A sibling's shares are paid farm by farm, in the share's id order.
