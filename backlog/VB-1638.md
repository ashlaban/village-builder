# VB-1638: The shelter: the ladder's first rung stands on the house tile, in place of the booth beside it, for the played settler and for settler households
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: backlog/VB-1627.md; backlog/VB-1631.md; backlog/VB-1632.md; src/sim/camp.js (placeFarmstead); src/sim/building.js (booths, raiseBooth, roofless, boothWork, stakedWork); src/sim/cottageplan.js (roofOn); src/sim/settle.js (settleFarm); src/sim/scenarios.js (landAlone); src/sim/ladder.js; src/sim/upgrade.js; src/sim/hunger.js (warmHouse, nightCost); design.md (Settling in steps; Sleeping away from home)
Goal: A farm's first roof is the first rung of its own house, on its own tile.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The first rung is a shelter on the house tile: `RUNGS.booth` becomes `shelter` (the same small cost to begin with: the timber and hours of `BOOTH`, read from there or moved to the rung's own tunables; the same roof against the cold and the same room, as the booth gives today), built at `f.house` itself, not on a neighbouring tile. The farmstead's plan task (`farmstead`, sim/camp) places the house and raises the shelter there; `placeWhy` no longer asks for open ground beside the tile. State: the farm's own record says the shelter stands (the rung stored or derived as the timber house's task decides if it has landed; else a field of the farm's with an owner, `@state`, an invariant, saved), and no entry is made in `st.booths` for it.
- [ ] Built up in place: the cottage's stages (`upgrade`, `beginCottage`) are worked on the same tile while the household lives in the shelter, and when the roof is on the shelter is gone (what `roofOn` does to `f.booth` today). `roofless`, the night's cold and damp, the cottage's waiting on a roof (`boothWork`) and `rungOf` read the shelter where they read the staked or raised booth of a farm.
- [ ] Settler households follow the ladder (Kim: one rule for every household): `settleFarm` (sim/settle) and the solo start without `hand` (`landAlone`) raise the shelter on their house tile by the same rule, where they stake a booth beside it today, and then build their cottage as today. Report before and after, founders seed 7 on the small map for 10 years and heirs or populous where settler households are founded (which scenario founds them: say): the nights in the open, the days to the first roof, the cottages built, the population. `node tools/same.js` will differ: say where and why.
- [ ] A traveller's shelter away from home stays as it is (`raiseBooth` by a pawn on a stay, `st.booths`, sim/away, VB-784): name in the notes what remains of the booth and that it is no longer a farm's.
- [ ] An older save with a farm's booth staked or raised loads and goes on (read as the shelter, or kept as a booth until its cottage is roofed: say which).
- [ ] What the page reads: say which fields tell a camp, a shelter being raised, a shelter standing and a cottage under way, and where the shelter is drawn (the house tile); `Sim.farmView().rung` reads `shelter`.
- [ ] Tests on small constructed states (`tests/camp.js`, `tests/upgrade.js`, `tests/ladder.js` updated; the settler households' in their own script); `npm run verify` passes; baselines do not rise.
