Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The kind `home` (declared by sim/projectmenu beside the farm for the heir): for a member who will need a place under the residence custom and is not the heir to the home farm; formed by the head's default author at its yearly review and when such a member turns 16, begins courting or is betrothed, on the weighing of aims.md 3.5 (`AIM.home`: a site in reach, the head's estimate of the house's hours against the budget's spare in the seasons until `by`, generosity, the food against the means to marry); `by` the custom's least age to marry, or the courting pair's round.
- [ ] Its means is today's farm for the heir (`heirFarm`): the plot begun ahead, `intended` the member; each season's step sized to the budget's spare after the targets the household lives by: the clearing, timber felled in winter and carried to the site as a pile with the household's title, the foundation, the walls and the roof. The menu's option `heir` goes: the aim replaces it.
- [ ] The roof waits for the member's yes when the member's wish ranks a place elsewhere above home (`waits: 'yes'`; the head asked); if the member leaves or marries elsewhere the aim fails and the plot goes to the village with its stages, as an unclaimed plot does today.
- [ ] A check on a constructed household over four game years: a second son courting at 17 with a site in reach: the aim is formed, its timber felled in winters, the cottage roofed before his round at 20 and the plot given him at it; and with a summer's spare taken by a poor harvest the aim is postponed, the card's reason says so, and the marriage waits for the house.
- [ ] Reported (populous small, seeds 7, 11 and 42, 10 years), before and after: homes built for a member ahead of a marriage, marriages that waited for a home, the age at first marriage by sex, plots begun and plots given back unused.
- [ ] Cost: one weighing at each of about 100 events a year and at the yearly review; the plot's work is the plot's, as today.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
