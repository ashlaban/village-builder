# VB-1506: Owners for w.biome, w.trees and w.stones
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: `docs/architecture/ownership.md`; `docs/architecture/target-v2.md` 4.3
Touches: sim/forest; new sim/ground; sim/spread; sim/layout; sim/fields; sim/meadows; sim/expansion; gen/cover (set); src/owners.json
Goal: `w.biome`, `w.trees`, `w.treesMax` and `w.stones` are written by about ten sim modules (forest, spread, layout, fields, meadows, expansion and others) and by the editor, and `src/owners.json` gives them no owner, so `tests/ownership.js` does not hold their writes. Found by the terrain review of 23 November; the split (VB-1501 to VB-1504) does not give them one.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `sim/forest` owns `w.trees`, `w.treesMax` and a wood's `w.biome`: felling, regrowth, spread and clearing write them through it. A new `sim/ground` (its `@sys`, `src/manifest.json`, `src/layers.json`) owns `w.stones` and a field's or yard's `w.biome`. Where `src/owners.json` holds one owner a field, `w.biome`'s is the one the more writes go through and the other calls it; the choice and its reason in the notes.
- [ ] Every sim writer of these fields (forest, spread, layout, fields, meadows, expansion and the others found by `node tools/writes.js`) goes through its owner; `tests/ownership.js` holds their writes, and its baseline does not rise.
- [ ] The editor writes through `gen/cover.set` before a run is seeded, never into a running state.
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`; `npm run verify` passes.
Notes:
