# VB-1142: A played heir takes up or renounces an inheritance itself
Status: done (2026-11-21)
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: acts
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Succession: Handed on, and taken up); docs/plans/VB-682.md, sections 2 and 4.6
Goal: At a death the played pawn in line is asked, as it is asked a handover in life, and its pawn's judgement answers only when it does not.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] When an inheritance falls to the played pawn, the farm is its own at once (a right), and it is asked whether it takes it up: an open choice with the wait of the basis `handover`, shown with its pawn's advice (principle 14); unanswered, its pawn's judgement answers, as today.
- [ ] Renounced, it falls to the next in line at once, and a `renounced` event tells it; taken up, the pawn moves by `move`, reason `heir`, with spouse and children.
- [ ] Until it answers the farm is held as a farm whose heir is on the way is held today (nobody else is offered it), and the test names what works its fields meanwhile.
- [ ] A test on a constructed state (`tests/handover.js`): a played heir renounces and the next takes it up; a played heir who does not answer takes it up at the wait's end.
