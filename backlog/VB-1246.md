# VB-1246: Illnesses that are survived
Status: done (2026-11-19)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: Principles for decisions (A wait is the thing waited for)
Goal: Ailing is not a sentence: some illnesses pass, so kin's worry is an estimate.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] An illness may be survived: a pawn falls ill at an incidence of its own (a named tunable by age), and recovers or dies by its health over the course; the deaths the death.hazard roll draws stay its share of those courses, so an ailing pawn no longer always dies (VB-1118 made every course fatal).
- [x] Kin's estimate on the sheet stays words, not a chance (principle 14).
- [x] Deaths by cause and age, founders and populous small, 30 years, before and after.

Noted by VB-1118, 19 November 2026.

Decisions (19 Nov 2026, the build):
- One draw a day, as before: below the roll's hazard a death (a course that kills, VB-1118); above it, up to `ILLNESS.incidence[cause]` a year, a course that passes (`pawn.ill.passes`). First values (Proposed): infancy 1, childhood 0.3, illness 0.1, oldAge 0.3 a year; a course kills about 2 in 5 at 0, 1 in 8 at 30, 2 in 3 at 80 (tests/illness.js measures the shares).
- The roll keeps running while a course passes, and a death drawn turns the course to one that kills (until = the later of its end and the cause's days from then): the roll's deaths are kept, not thinned by the days spent ailing (a game year is 48 days, so a course is long).
- A course that passes drains `ILLNESS.passing` (0.5) of `fall` and the pawn recovers at its end; one whose health gives out first dies of its illness, of starvation if it was hungry that day. No life event for a recovery (the record stays bounded).
- Kin's words are unchanged (ailing, gravely ill, by health): `ailingWords` names no chance.
- Numbers (after VB-1314 -> after VB-1246, deaths in 30 years): founders small seeds 7 and 42 the same (9, 16). Populous small seeds 3/7/11/42: 327 -> 359, 335 -> 361, 445 -> 433, 345 -> 338; by cause, illness (15-59) 117/113/119 -> 125/128/115, childhood 77/91/85 -> 78/82/108, infancy 103/111/132 -> 118/115/116, old age 18/12/21 -> 26/18/16, starvation 3/0/76 -> 1/6/72: within the runs' parting, the roll's deaths kept by construction (tests/illness.js).
