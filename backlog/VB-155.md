# VB-155: A tiny world from a text map, for fast tests, and a land report
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: tooling
Phase: implement
Design ref: none. Review: `docs/review-claims.md` (Testing it).
Goal: Most tests generate a whole island (280 × 200 tiles) and run years to reach the case they test; the later years are slow. Tests of claims and territory need a few villages placed exactly, on land drawn for the case, and run in milliseconds.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `tests/lib/tiny.js`: `tinyWorld(rows)` builds a world from a text map, one character a tile (`.` meadow, `h` heath, `m` marsh, `F` forest, `P` pine, `^` hills, `M` mountain, `~` sea, `o` lake), with every field the simulation reads (neighbours as the generator lays them, positions, biomes, water, rivers off, stones, trees, regions); `tinyState(w, villages, opts)` places villages at given tiles with the given number of farms through the real generators (`settlementGenerator`, `populousHouseholds`, `makeState`, `initCulture`), so the state is a real one.
- [ ] A test of the kit itself: neighbours match the generator's for an odd and an even row; a state of two villages on a 14 × 10 map seeds in under 100 ms and advances a day.
- [ ] `node tools/sim.js --report land`: per village, its tiles by kind (houses, fields, meadows, outland), claims held and in dispute, cairns, and the nearest other village's land; `--json` for the rows.
