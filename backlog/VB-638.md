# VB-638: The marriage valve measured: long runs against the growth budget
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Balance loops and targets (The growth budget); People, households and family records (Marriage, nursing and growth); docs/balance/cards.md, card 2; backlog/VB-405.md
Goal: Check the marriage valve as a whole against the growth budget with long runs: which lines the game spends its ceiling on, where the age at marriage and the share never marrying stand, how fast each scenario grows by phase, and what the means test moves.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Long runs with VB-633's report and bands: populous small, 50 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42; populous medium, four seeds, to the year 1,000 is reached and 30 years on; founders small, the band's sixteen seeds, 80 years. For each, against `design.md`, Balance loops and targets, The growth budget: growth by phase; the year 1,000 is reached; the age at first marriage by sex and by 20 years; the share unmarried at 45; the waiting years by cause; marriages put off for the means; servants as a share of the young; births while courting (VB-555); the birth interval after an infant's death (VB-632).
- [ ] The budget's lines that the game can show today (marriage by delay, never marrying, hunger), each as points of yearly growth, by a cohort calculation from the measured ages and shares whose script is kept beside the review's (`docs/balance/mocks/`); the ceiling measured again with VB-632 in (a run with the means at 0 and land free, or the cohort model with the game's numbers); the budget's table in `design.md` is corrected where the game differs from the mock, each change named.
- [ ] The setpoint's reach, measured and not applied: `MARRIAGE.means` at 1.2, 1.5 and 1.8, populous small, seeds 7 and 11, 50 years: the food produced over need late in the run (`node tools/balance.js`), the age at first marriage, the share never marrying, the land held by the largest tenth of households, growth. The review's mock gave +4.7% of the late food ratio for +10% of the means; say what the game gives, in the row of `docs/tuning.md` (a `tuning` note).
- [ ] `MARRIAGE.goAhead`: one run at 1 beside 0.75 (populous small, seeds 7 and 11, 30 years): the delay it adds to a first marriage and what it does to growth; and a `task` note that ends it if courting and the heads' answers by opinion now carry the judgement it stood for.
- [ ] The overshoot: the widest swing of growth between decades in each long run, and in one run with a shock made by hand (a harvest failed over the island in one year, by the weather's own parameters): the marriages put off and made up, and whether the wave shows a generation on. Reported; nothing damps it.
- [ ] Who never marries, as a description of households and not only of the average: by the land of the household born into, by birth order and by years served.
- [ ] Hunger is reported by VB-571's measure if it has landed; hungry household-days over four seeds are given as a description only and no conclusion rests on them (`backlog/VB-485.md`).
- [ ] The bands' ranges are written into `tests/lib/bands.js` as observed; `docs/balance/README.md` and card 2 of `docs/balance/cards.md` say what the game now does; the parameters a tuning run would move are named in `docs/tuning.md` rows (`tuning` notes), with what each means.
