# VB-101: Why a pawn is idle: bid reasons in the pawn panel and `tools/sim.js`
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: work
Phase: implement
Design ref: Economy and jobs (Jobs, owners and roles: Matching); Systems overview (Work: jobs, matching, walking). Jobs review, 30 Oct 2026 (REVIEW.md, Work and jobs).
Goal: Adults are idle for much of the working day: in populous medium, seed 7, year 1, farmers have no job for 35% of work hours in spring, 83% in summer, 56% in autumn and 69% in winter. Nothing says why: the pawn panel says "Resting" or "Strolling". The work arch's tasks need one way to see the reasons and to measure idleness before and after each change.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `explainIdle(st, pawnId)` in `sim/jobs`, exported through `Sim`: for each open job the pawn may take (`mayTake`), its noise-free bid, or the reason it gets none: `taken`, `not their work` (role priority 0), `poor fit, waits until HH:MM` (priority 2 or less, waited under 2 hours), `not worth the walk` (bid at most 0 even with the largest noise, 0.3). Sorted by bid, best first. It draws no random numbers and writes nothing to `st`.
- [ ] The pawn panel of an idle adult during work hours shows one line under its activity: the best reason, for example "No work for a farmer at Norrgården" (no job it may take), "Firewood: not worth the walk", "Threshing: waits until 09:40".
- [ ] `node tools/sim.js --report idle` prints, for the years run: the share of work hours (7–17, or the daylight hours once VB-105 is done) each role spends working, walking to a job, carrying home and idle, per season; the idle hours of adults split by reason (no job they may take, or each reason above); the median and 90th-percentile wait from posting to claim per job kind; the mean number of unclaimed jobs per kind at the end of the work day. `--json` gives the same as JSON.
- [ ] Behaviour unchanged: `npm run verify` passes and `tests/golden.json` is unchanged, apart from the API-key hash for the new `Sim` export (name it in the PR).
- [ ] A test in `tests/tools.js`: the idle report runs for 10 days of founders at seed 7 and its shares per role and season sum to 100%.
- [ ] Report in the PR the idle report for populous medium, seed 7, 1 year; the work arch's later tasks report against it.
Notes / questions:
- The numbers in the goal come from a scratch script of the jobs review (30 Oct); this task makes them a standard report.
