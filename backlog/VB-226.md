# VB-226: Save within 0.5 s of main thread at medium-ref, after the folds
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: Balance loops and targets (Performance budget). docs/roadmap.md, sections 5.2 and 5.3 (rule 8); docs/bench/closure.md (VB-203).
Goal: Kim's save line (9 November, VB-203): under 0.5 s of the page's main thread at `medium-ref`, counting encode and stringify; gzip may run in a worker and is reported beside it. On 9 November at `9f130e6` the save blocks the main thread 1.51 s at `medium-ref` (encode 0.95, stringify 0.56; 39.3 MB of JSON; gzip 0.58 s more), three times the line. Of the state's 36.8 MB: the households' ledgers 10.6 (VB-204 folds the routine 95% of their entries), the pawns 14.5 (their copied events 5.3, which VB-199 makes indexes; ancestry 1.4; attitudes 1.4), the record 7.9 (VB-199 folds the routine kinds), the path cache 1.2. By size alone the two folds should leave about 17 MB and about 0.65 s. Measure what is left once they land, and cut it to the line.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Measure first at `medium-ref`, after VB-204 and VB-199: the save's MB and its encode and stringify ms by part (each top-level field of the state, and each pawn field), the median of three runs.
- [ ] Cut the largest parts, by rule 8 (bound history) and by not saving what can be rebuilt: a cache rebuilt at load (the path cache, for example) is left out only if load stays under 0.5 s. A change of the save format bumps it in `src/sim/saves.js`; `tests/saves.js` and `tests/codec.js` pass, and the fixtures are remade by the nightly workflow.
- [ ] Encode + stringify under 0.5 s at `medium-ref` (`tools/bench.js`, the median of three runs), and parse + load still under 0.5 s; the MB before and after.
- [ ] The page's save compresses off the main thread (a worker or `CompressionStream`); if VB-214 has not done so, this task does.
- [ ] Golden file unchanged unless the state changes (then re-recorded, with the changed entries listed); `npm run verify` passes.
