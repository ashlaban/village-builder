# VB-043: One scope model: a membership graph of entities
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-184
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Claims, ownership and territory, One scope model for overlays, stores and trees (Proposed); Commands, time steps and views (Store views, Family tree: Decided).
Goal: The overlay, the store views and the family tree must each work for any level of entity (pawn, family, household, village, culture). Build the shared part once. Depends on VB-041; if it is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria (Kim, 2026-10-30: the scope model is adopted as the plan for the overlay, store views and family trees):
- [ ] A membership graph links pawn to household, household to village, village to culture, and pawn to family (the line of a farm, or descent from a founder), updated when people move, marry, die or a culture splits.
- [ ] A scope is `{ level, ids, dim }` with `dim` one of owns, claims, uses, works, is kin of. `inScope(st, scope)` returns the pawns, households, tiles or stores it covers.
- [ ] Unit tests on the heirs scenario: the scope "village A, owns" returns A's household and village tiles; "Lars's family, is kin of" returns his descendants.
- [ ] Cost: building the graph for a populous medium map takes under 50 ms (reported in the PR).
- 3 Nov 2026 (architecture review, `docs/review-architecture.md`, section 2, point 6): the review's multi-membership test (`docs/architecture/stress-tests.md`, section 4) has a pawn in several memberships at once: a household in one village, work for an employer or a guild in another, land elsewhere. Keep the graph's links as typed edges a pawn may have several of, not a chain pawn → household → village → culture, so a later employer, guild or parish is a new kind of edge, not a redesign.
