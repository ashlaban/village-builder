# VB-043: One scope model: a membership graph of entities
Status: blocked
Waits for: Kim
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Claims, ownership and territory, One scope model for overlays, stores and trees (Agreed, 30 Oct); Households and other bodies (Bodies: Open, measured 11 Nov); Commands, time steps and views (Store views, Family tree: Decided); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, section 4.4 (Membership)
Goal: The overlay, the store views and the family tree must each work for any level of entity (pawn, family, household, village, culture), and slice 5's bodies (a village with an alderman, a household that owes) need one way to say who belongs where. Build the membership graph once, and the scopes on it. Waits for Kim's answer on bodies (`QUESTIONS.md`, VB-184, question 7): if the graph is adopted as Membership's truth, this task makes it so; if each kind keeps its own domain, it builds the same graph as a derived view for the scopes only, and the second criterion falls.
Acceptance criteria (Kim, 2026-10-30: the scope model is adopted as the plan for the overlay, store views and family trees; rewritten by VB-184, 11 Nov 2026):
- [ ] A membership graph of typed edges (`{from: pawn or body, to: body, role, since, until}`): residence, service, holder, spouse, a household's village, a village's culture, the alderman (VB-314), and a pawn's family (the line of a farm, or descent from a founder); a pawn may hold several edges of one kind over time and several kinds at once (the multi-membership test below). Ended edges stay, outside the index of live edges that every step reads (rule 1).
- [ ] Under the graph as truth: Membership (`sim/records` and `sim/membership`, or a module that takes their membership part over) writes the edges, and derives `pawn.household`, `household.members`, `pawn.servant` and the role cache from them as caches it alone writes, checked against the edges in a test; behaviour unchanged, `tests/golden.json` unchanged.
- [ ] A scope is `{ level, ids, dim }` with `dim` one of owns, claims, uses, works, is kin of (owns and uses read VB-311's rights where it is done). `inScope(st, scope)` returns the pawns, households, tiles or stores it covers.
- [ ] Unit tests on the heirs scenario: the scope "village A, owns" returns A's household and village tiles; "Lars's family, is kin of" returns his descendants.
- [ ] Cost: building the graph for a populous medium map takes under 50 ms, and a move under 0.1 ms (reported in the PR). The probe of 11 Nov (VB-184; populous medium, seed 7, year 12, 1,031 living): 2,009 edges built in 0.55 ms, the household cache derived in 0.33 ms, 1,000 moves in 0.86 ms; saves grow by the ended edges, about 2 a pawn ever born, so bound them as history (rule 9: the dead's ended edges may fold into the record).
- 3 Nov 2026 (architecture review, `docs/review-architecture.md`, section 2, point 6): the review's multi-membership test (`docs/architecture/stress-tests.md`, section 4) has a pawn in several memberships at once: a household in one village, work for an employer or a guild in another, land elsewhere. Keep the graph's links as typed edges a pawn may have several of, not a chain pawn → household → village → culture, so a later employer, guild or parish is a new kind of edge, not a redesign.
