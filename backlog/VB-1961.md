# VB-1961: Split land: a dead heir's plot, and wanting land as a judgement
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, Handover and shares (Splitting land); Vision and scope, Principles for decisions (A judgement is never a die)
Touches: sim/heirland (wantsLand, divide); sim/allotment (reallot); tests/heirsplit.js
Goal: Split land founds households in play: siblings choose land by weighing, and a plot no heir will live in is not built
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A split house plot whose heir dies, or marries onto a farm, before it stands is dropped as a village project (its labour and timber not spent on a house no one will live in), or reallotted as an ordinary plot with a field to clear.
- [ ] `wantsLand` is the sibling's judgement, a threshold on what it weighs (land by its house against its share in goods, a match away), not a status test; a sibling whose weighing prefers goods takes no fields.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (heirs and populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years, split): households founded on split land (VB-034's run found 0 to 1 a run).
