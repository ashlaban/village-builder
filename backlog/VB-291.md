# VB-291: Kin help over distance: a gift from far kin is carried on a journey
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: between
Phase: implement
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Between villages: Kin help); Goods and the farming year (Help in hunger). `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-281", question 5 (Kim, 10 Nov: yes)
Goal: A hungry household asks its kin first, then its village's store (`shareFood`, `src/sim/help.js:60-79`; `kinHouseholds`, `:21-34`), and a gift from kin moves grain from one store to the other at once, wherever the two live. VB-281 counted one year's kin gifts from the fixtures by the walking hours between giver and receiver: at `medium-ref`, of 116, 53 within the village, 13 within 6 h, 16 within 6 to 12 h, 21 further and 13 over the sea; at `small-y40`, of 80, 21, 3, 7, 35 and 14. Kim decided on 10 November that distance governs what passes between villages and the pawn travels. Decided (Kim, 10 Nov; `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-281", question 5): kin within a day's walk give as today; kin further away are asked after the village store, and their gift is carried by one of their adults on a journey; kin over the sea are not asked until slice 10.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The order of asking: kin within a day's walk (`hoursBetween` at most `TRAVEL.dayWalk`, 12 h), closest kin first as today; then the village store; then kin beyond a day's walk and within reach, nearest first. Kin over the sea or beyond reach are not asked.
- [ ] A gift within a day's walk settles at once, as today (the walk there and back fits in a day, and is not modelled). A gift from further away is agreed at the ask (the `give` command and its allowance, as today) and carried: the giving household's youngest adult of 14 to 59 who is not on a journey sets out (VB-287, `why: 'carry'`), with the grain and his own provisions taken from his household at the start; the receiving household books the gift when he arrives, and he walks home. If no adult can go, the gift is not made and the ask goes on to the next. The help's goodwill and tie (`sim/exchange`, `receive`) are booked at arrival.
- [ ] The carrier's load: the grain carried counts against what one adult can carry, `TRAVEL.load` (a tunable in VB-287's `TRAVEL`, added by whichever of this task and VB-295 lands first; 30 rations: Kim, 10 Nov, one number with the fair's back load; `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-298"); a larger gift is cut to it, or takes a cart and two oxen where the household has them, 90.
- [ ] Events: the gift's events as today, at arrival; the journey's `setOut` and `arrived` (VB-287).
- [ ] Behaviour changes, so report before and after (targets with residuals): `tools/balance.js` (populous, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, small, 15 years): rations by source against need, and help received by the hours between giver and receiver; the hungry-households band (`node tests/bands.js`); gifts a year and carriers' days on the road at `medium-ref` and `small-y40`. Say whether households, not only averages, are better or worse off: a household whose only kin live over the sea loses its first source of help. The golden file and `tests/counters.json` re-recorded, the changes listed.
- [ ] Cost: one journey for each far gift: about 21 a year at `medium-ref` and 35 at `small-y40` by the counts above, each with a way back.
