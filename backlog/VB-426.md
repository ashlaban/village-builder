## Notes (17 Nov 2026)
Defect checked first on today's code (populous small seed 11, 30 years, a day at a time; scratch scripts, not committed). The four named villages no longer exist under those names (the map's history moved with the night's changes); the same defect shows in others.
- Cold household-days, all: seed 11 3,566 of 138,410 (2.58%), seed 42 4,933 of 158,295 (3.12%); deaths in cold houses 0 on both (counted from the record text " in a cold house"); growth 2.10% and 2.06% a year (324 -> 604, 347 -> 639).
- A measurement artefact dominates: 2,491 of seed 11's cold days (1,075 left when only households with members count) are one household with no members. hh25 (Tallberga) is an orphan's farm: its holder, a 9-year-old, lives as a ward in household 88; the house stands empty, its fire is reckoned cold for 48 winter days a year, no one is cold in it and nobody could cut for it. The `cold-households` band counts such a household-day as cold. On seed 42, 4,560 of 4,933.
- Real cold (occupied), seed 11: Andersvik 640 days (5 households, founded about year 9), Larsryd 212 (founded about 20), Hammaråker 204 (13 households, the last two years); seed 42: Berginge 166, Ullvi 108, the rest a few days each.
- Why, by the trace (every change of a village's wood, daily): Andersvik and Larsryd were founded with 4 to 13 wooded tiles (20 to 25 wanted) because older villages' woods already held the forest in reach: at year 22 Andersvik had 65 forest tiles within WOOD.far, 11 bare, 41 held by other villages' woods, 10 built on, 3 free. Its wood shrank 13 -> 8 -> 5 -> 1 -> 0 as the older villages kept their 5 tiles a household (each autumn meeting each re-takes the nearest tiles, a churn of about 5 a village) and its own few tiles were cut bare. So: the older villages' allotment, not their own cutting. Hammaråker is the other kind: 13 households on 45 tiles, 37 bare of the 155 forest tiles within far and 92 held by others; its own cutting emptied its wood (3 bare tiles lost on year 29 day 17).
- The existing system that answers it is the allotment size, WOOD.perHousehold (5): older villages hold more than they cut. One run at 4 (seed 11): occupied cold 1,075 -> 198 days (0.78% -> 0.14%), growth 2.10% -> 1.92% (a different history from the first autumn, so one seed shows only that the cold falls). Not adopted: one seed, and fewer tiles a household make older villages thinner; left as a tuning row (inbox).
