# VB-743: Whetstones and grindstones: slate for the keener edge, sandstone for the wheel
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-733, VB-761
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The metals and the stones: goods by purpose, kinds by property; Goods, recipes and facilities); `docs/plans/VB-702.md`; `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (17)
Goal: Slate and sandstone have a recurring good each that every farm with iron tools wants (Kim, 16 Nov: one good, two kinds; sandstone the only grindstone).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `whetstone/slate` and `whetstone/sandstone` (VB-733's kinds): cut from a load of the rock on the heap, 8 hours, 8 whetstones a load, skill stonework (VB-360). A household has one in use, `{kind, made}`: with it VB-701's wear of tools is multiplied by 0.75 (slate) or 0.9 (sandstone); it lasts 5 years (slate) or 3 (sandstone). Spare ones are counted by kind and are a good of the fair (worth, Proposed: 2 and 1 rations).
- [ ] `grindstone/sandstone`: hewn from a load of sandstone, 12 hours; one a household, lasting 20 years; with it the winter's mending of tools (VB-701, VB-121) takes 0.7 of the hours. Worth 8 rations (Proposed).
- [ ] A head chooses the kind it can get: its own rock first, the other by the fair; the target (VB-698) is one in use and one spare where it has the rock.
- [ ] All numbers Proposed and in the registry's rows, so that a third kind is a row.
- [ ] Numbers, populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years: tools' mean condition and field hours a ration by whether the village has slate, sandstone or neither within reach; whetstones carried between villages by kind; hours of mending a household.
- [ ] The numbers that move are reported before and after at the seeds and maps named (Working rules); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
Notes:
- Numbers are Proposed by the plan of VB-702 (16 Nov) on Kim's answers to the shape, from general reading not checked by search: the task reports its own, found in at most five short runs.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim's answers after the plan of VB-702; section 14): The good is declared graded (the plan of VB-702, 13.3): its making draws a grade by the maker's skill at the craft the criteria name, its numbers are the sound row, and the report gives pieces by grade. The wear a whetstone saves and the mending hours a grindstone saves take the grade's working; their years its life.
