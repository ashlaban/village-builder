Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Rake (*fagning*): spring day 1–10, a meadow in grass not yet raked posts `rake`, 2 h, any hands, harvest work; an unraked meadow gives 80%.
- [x] Mow: from summer day 1 to autumn day 3, a meadow in grass posts `mow`, 16 h, any hands, harvest work with the women's rank 4 and children 0 (Kim, 31 Oct, as P); urgency 1 rising by 1/12 a day of the window; done, the meadow is mown and holds 30 × site value × weather × raking × (0.9–1.1) hay.
- [x] Carry: a day after mowing, `hay`, 4 h, any hands, carried to the barn; delivered, `hh.hay` gains the meadow's hay and the meadow is aftermath. When a farm's last meadow is in, the log says how its hay stands against the winter's need.
- [x] Hay still lying at the autumn frost (autumn day 12) rots. Each spring every meadow is grass again.
- [x] Page: a mower swings a scythe; a carrier bears a bundle of hay; mown meadows show windrows and haycocks, aftermath short grass; a haystack by the barn in four sizes by hay against the winter's need.
