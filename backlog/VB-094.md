# VB-094: Worn paths
Status: done (2026-10-30)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: Systems overview, Presentation systems (Worn paths: lanes appear between farms, well and woods over years; one counter per tile, repainted with the chunk; Proposed); Settlements and look (Village: lanes).
Goal: Pawns walk the same ways every day, to the well, the woodlot and the fields, but the land shows no trace of it. Let walking wear the ground, so lanes appear over years where people go and fade where they stop going. Depends on VB-025 (the Chromium smoke test catches drawing errors); if it is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `w.wear` (Float32Array per tile, `@state` documented) counts crossings: each time a walking pawn enters a land tile that is not a house or a field, its wear rises by 1. Once a game day, wear decays with a half-life of one game year.
- [x] Three stages by wear: trodden at 50 or more, path at 200, lane at 800 (about 1, 4 and 16 crossings a game day, kept up). When a tile's stage changes it joins a dirty set, like `st.treeDirty`, and the page repaints only the chunks it touches.
- [x] Drawing: a worn tile shows a strip of bare, lighter ground towards each neighbour that is also worn (or is a house, green, well or field), wider and paler by stage, under trees and stones; trodden only at the closest zoom level.
- [x] Wear changes nothing in the simulation yet (the speed-up is VB-110): routes, walking speed and growth are unchanged, so the golden file changes only by the new `wear` key (and `pathDirty`).
- [x] Founders at seed 7 after 5 and 20 years: a screenshot from the Chromium test and the count of tiles per stage (reported in the PR).
- [x] The cost of counting and repainting on populous medium at seed 7 is reported: under 1 ms a game day for counting, and no frame over 16 ms from repainting at 10×.
