# VB-812: A boat carries: landings, the boat as a vehicle, and a journey's leg by water
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: roads
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Climate and travel beyond walking (Modes; Boats and landings); Goods of place and side crafts (The fishing boat); docs/architecture/target-v2.md, section 4.2 (Vehicle, legs); docs/plans/VB-190.md, section 5.4
Goal: People and goods cross water in a boat that someone rows and that lies where it was left (the hard rule), so that islands apart by water are joined by those who have boats.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A village's landing: the shore tile cheapest by land from its green within `LANDING.hours` 2, beside water a boat goes on (the sea within `BOATS.near` 3 tiles of land, a lake); found when the village is founded and when its ways change the answer at the yearly refresh, kept on the village (plain data, an owner, an invariant). A village with none is an inland village (the plan counted 10 to 20 landings among 22 to 30 villages). A household's fishing boat (VB-300) keeps its own shore tile, which is its landing.
- [ ] The boat as a vehicle: `household.boat` gains where it lies now (`at`, a shore tile), who is aboard and what it carries, as target-v2's `Vehicle` (find the owner in section 4.2: Movement holds place and passengers, Stores the title; say in the task file how `sim/drying`'s record becomes it). Goods aboard are a store operation with a good and a reason (`src/sim/stores.js`); `tools/stocks.js` gains its line. Invariants: a boat is at one shore tile or under way with a rower aboard; nobody fishes from a boat that is away.
- [ ] `BOATS` in `src/sim/rules.js`: a small boat carries its rower and 150 rations beyond, a passenger counting 40; it costs 0.5 a tile of water for its rower (twice a walker's pace on meadow) and `put` 3 to put out. A boat leg is a route over water from shore tile to shore tile, searched on the water alone and cached under the mode `boat`.
- [ ] A journey's legs (VB-287): walk to the boat, row, walk on. A verb in `src/sim/commands.js` for taking a boat on a journey, whose check holds for any pawn: the boat is its household's (or lent, when lending exists), lies where the pawn is, and the water is open. The boat stays at the far shore until it is rowed back: a journey that is a move (a bride, a servant) is rowed by a member of either household who rows home again, so the boat ends where it belongs; if nobody can, the move waits and says why.
- [ ] What a boat is used for first: a kin gift carried over water (VB-291's carrier takes the boat: 150 rations), and a move to a home across water (the cases VB-436 refused). Each is the existing act, now possible; the list of acts and what each needed is in the task file.
- [ ] A constructed check: two villages on islands 8 tiles of water apart, one with a boat: a gift of 100 rations crosses in one journey and the boat is back at its landing; with no boat the act is refused with its reason; a boat left at the far shore is not fished from until it is rowed back.
- [ ] Numbers before and after on populous medium, seeds 3, 11 and 42, and populous small, seed 42 (the islands apart by water), 20 years: journeys by water a year, moves and gifts across water, boats built and away-days, fish caught (a boat away is not fishing), hungry days by household, growth by island (principle 2).
- Cost (rule 9): a boat leg's search settles at most the water in reach of the landing (5,000 to 7,000 tiles on medium, the plan's measure), cached by pair of shore tiles; journeys by water are tens a year. A declared counter `boatSettled`.
