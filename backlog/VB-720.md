## Decisions (VB-720)
- A report of its own, tools/soil.js, not tools/balance.js: it needs a daily walk and the farm classes by cattle kept that day; balance.js reads food by source.
- Heap rot is the day's share of the heap (SOIL.rot), summed; spread loads read from tilth moving, split to cap or beyond by field.surplus. Descriptions, no target.
- Band `tilth-gain` (balancing, lo 0.03, hi 1; populous and founders full runs); reduced run asserts only gain > 0 (4 seeds: 0.006 to 0.012 at year 8).
- Numbers (populous small, seeds 3/7/11/42, years 30, decade rows; tilth none / 1-2 / 3+): decade 1: 0.0023-0.0029 / 0.009-0.010 / 0.013-0.015; decade 2: 0.002-0.005 / 0.014-0.019 / 0.026-0.031; decade 3: 0.005-0.014 / 0.014-0.023 / 0.030-0.044. Loads a field-year, 3+: 1.0-1.3 to the cap, 0.5-0.8 beyond; rot 10-16 loads a farm-year at 3+, 0.2-0.9 for none. Grain a field, 3+ against none: 109-116 vs 101-120 (decade 1), 96-106 vs 86-96 (2), 96-109 vs 78-88 (3).
- For VB-048: from tilth 0, 3+ farms gain about 0.015 a decade 1, 0.03 by 20, 0.04 by 30 (3% to 4% of harvest, near the reckoning); grain a field falls in every class over the thirty years (soil wear and the crowding, not tilth), but 3+ farms lose 10% and farms with no cattle 20-25%, so tilth plus dung keeps about 12% a field. Villages whose fields outran their meadows (under 0.5 meadow tiles a field; 3 to 7 a decade) held less tilth at decade 3 in seeds 7, 11 and 42 (0.0038, 0, 0 against 0.0164, 0.0176, 0.014), and more in seed 3 (0.0179 against 0.0144); the correlation of meadow ratio with tilth across villages is positive in 11 of 12 decade readings (0.05 to 0.70, one -0.04). So: they gain least, as the design says, though one seed in four shows otherwise.
- Owed: founders small, 50 years, and the long seeds, VB-662.
