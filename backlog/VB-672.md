# VB-672: Every pawn has wishes at the round: the player's wishes become the pawn's
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: acts
Phase: implement
Design ref: `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (15)
Goal: Each pawn who comes to the round holds its own wishes, the round reads them alike for all, and the player's are the same record on the pawn it plays.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The wishes live on the pawn (owner named in `src/owners.json`), written by the pawn's own choosing before the round (from ambition, opinion of a partner, its household's means) and by the `wish` verb for the played pawn; `wishOf` reads one place.
- [ ] The round's rules read the wish where they now read 'as the pawn would'; the default wish gives today's outcomes as nearly as the mechanism allows, and the differences are reported.
- [ ] VB-392 (the wish to settle) is built on this, as a wish every pawn has. Numbers before and after: marriages, moves out, claims and service, populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 15 years.
- 16 Nov 2026: Found by the consistency review of 16 November 2026 (code only); the lines named are leads, to be confirmed before work starts.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim, the session on the people side of the household boundary): a wish is a ranking of rungs, a rung being one answer to 'where do I live next year': stay, marry onto a holding, a plot or an empty farm, a place in service, new land; ranked by the pawn's traits and means (ambition towards land), which replaces the order of the round's steps; one open offer at a time.
