# VB-632: An infant's death ends the nursing gap
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, People, households and family records (Births; Marriage, nursing and growth: Nursing); Balance loops and targets (The growth budget); backlog/VB-405.md
Goal: Today a mother nurses for 48 days whether the child lives or not. The child's death ends the nursing (Kim, 13 Nov): it replaces losses and is the part of the birth interval that regulates itself; the length of nursing stays fixed.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A mother whose nursing child has died is no longer nursing: `sim/births` keeps the child she nurses (`pawn.nursling`, the child's id, set at the birth and null when the nursing ends), and its daily step skips a woman only while `nursingUntil` lies ahead and that child is alive; read lazily in the step that already walks the women, so there is no new loop and no call from `sim/lifecycle` into `sim/births`. `BIRTH.nursingDays` stays 48 and is not tuned here.
- [ ] `@state` line, `src/owners.json`, the save (an old save gains `nursling` null for every pawn: a mother then nursing nurses to her term) and invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): `nursling` is null or a child of hers born at most `nursingDays` ago, and null whenever `nursingUntil` is null or past.
- [ ] The pawn panel says whom she nurses ("nursing Karin"), and nothing once the child is dead; `node tests/smoke.js`.
- [ ] `expectedBirths`, the calibration without deaths, is unchanged, and `node tools/calibrate.js` (or the script that checks the starting targets: about 7 births for a woman married at 20 and 5.5 at 25) prints the same.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state, chances forced with `fix`: a mother whose infant dies on day 5 may conceive on day 6; one whose infant lives may not until day 48; a second child's death after the nursing ended changes nothing; a mother who dies in childbirth leaves no `nursling` dangling.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (Working rules), populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42: yearly growth; births a married woman-year; the mean days from a birth to the next conception after an infant's death and after a child that lived; the share of children dead before 1; marriages and marriages put off for the means. The review's mock expects about half a point of yearly growth; say what the game gives, as the ceiling's new value in `design.md`'s growth budget is to be read (a line in this task's notes for the wrap-up, VB-638). Whether households, not only averages, are better off: the completed family size of the mothers who lost an infant against those who did not. Hungry household-days are reported as a description only: over four seeds they swing by 20 to 50% between versions that differ little (`backlog/VB-485.md`).
- [ ] `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass; `node tests/counters.js` shows no new counter and no counter moved but by the births themselves.
