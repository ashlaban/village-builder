# VB-632: An infant's death ends the nursing gap
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, People, households and family records (Births; Marriage, nursing and growth: Nursing); Balance loops and targets (The growth budget); backlog/VB-405.md
Goal: Today a mother nurses for 48 days whether the child lives or not. The child's death ends the nursing (Kim, 13 Nov): it replaces losses and is the part of the birth interval that regulates itself; the length of nursing stays fixed.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A mother whose nursing child has died is no longer nursing: `sim/births` keeps the child she nurses (`pawn.nursling`, the child's id, set at the birth and null when the nursing ends), and its daily step skips a woman only while `nursingUntil` lies ahead and that child is alive; read lazily in the step that already walks the women, so there is no new loop and no call from `sim/lifecycle` into `sim/births`. `BIRTH.nursingDays` stays 48 and is not tuned here.
- [x] `@state` line, `src/owners.json`, the save (an old save gains `nursling` null for every pawn: a mother then nursing nurses to her term) and invariants (`tests/lib/invariants.js`): `nursling` is null or a child of hers born at most `nursingDays` ago, and null whenever `nursingUntil` is null or past. (`src/owners.json` lists no `pawn.nursingUntil` or `pregnant*` either, so `nursling` follows them with no line; `tests/ownership.js` passes. The invariant lets the step clear a finished term up to two days late.)
- [x] The pawn panel says whom she nurses ("nursing Karin"), and nothing once the child is dead; `node tests/smoke.js`. (smoke passes; no screenshot of the panel line: it is one phrase of the pawn card, and the shot tool cannot select a nursing mother.)
- [x] `expectedBirths`, the calibration without deaths, is unchanged, and `node tools/calibrate.js` (or the script that checks the starting targets: about 7 births for a woman married at 20 and 5.5 at 25) prints the same.
- [x] Tests on a constructed state, chances forced with `fix`: a mother whose infant dies on day 5 may conceive on day 6; one whose infant lives may not until day 48; a second child's death after the nursing ended changes nothing; a mother who dies in childbirth leaves no `nursling` dangling.
