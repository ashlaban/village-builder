# VB-065: People of a farm that dies out stay in their village
Status: done (2026-10-30, PENDING)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: Inheritance and expansion (Succession: no heir; Leaving home: service); REVIEW.md, Defects.
Goal: When a farm has no heir (`extinct` in `src/sim/heirs.js`), a member with no living kin elsewhere moves into the smallest household on the whole map, which can be another island; the comment and the design mean service in the same village.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A member with living kin in another household moves to the nearest such kin as today (kin anywhere).
- [x] Otherwise they move, as a servant, to the smallest undissolved household of the same village; if there is none (a one-farm village), to the smallest household of the village nearest by `hexSteps` between centres.
- [x] Their life event and the log line name the farm they moved to.
- [x] A test on the heirs scenario empties a farm whose members have no kin elsewhere and checks where they go.
- [x] Re-record the golden file if it changes and list the changed entries in the PR.
