# VB-062: Mood and its modifiers, shown in the pawn panel
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: talk
Phase: implement
Design ref: `design.md`, People, households and family records (Pawn interactions: "mood modifiers such as talked with a friend +5", Decided 30 Oct; Traits, talk and stories: Where pawns meet, Mood); `backlog/VB-186.md`. Kim, 2026-10-30: the pawn info screen shows active modifiers, for example "mood +5, recently talked to a friend".
Goal: Give each pawn a mood built from modifiers with a value and a label, so what happens in a pawn's life shows as feelings. In this first version mood has no effect on behaviour; it is shown, and later tasks may read it. Confirmed and rewritten by slice 7's plan (VB-186, 15 Nov) so that nothing is written per talk and nothing walks the pawns: a modifier for a state is read from the state, one for an event is written at the event, and "talked with a friend" is read from what talk already keeps.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `Mood.of(st, pawn)` (a module `sim/mood`, `@sys`, `src/manifest.json`) gives `{ mood, mods: [{ key, label, value }] }`, the mood being 50 plus the sum of the values, within 0 to 100. It is computed when asked and stored nowhere; nothing in the simulation calls it (a lint line or a test holds that, until a later task means to).
- [ ] Modifiers for a state, read from the state and never stored (`MOOD` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each value a starting value with its meaning): hungry, -20 × h while the pawn's hunger h is over 0.1; cold, -20 × `hh.cold` (VB-327); without water, -10 while `hh.dry` (VB-331; left out with a line here if that task has not landed); weak, -10 while health is under 50.
- [ ] Modifiers for an event, `pawn.mods`: a list of `{ key, until }` written by a subscriber to the event (`sim/events`) or at the act that makes the life event: newly married, +10 for 12 days; a child born, +5 for 12 days, both parents; grieving, -15 for 12 days at the death of a spouse, child or parent. An entry past its `until` is ignored where it is read and dropped when the list is next written; no daily pass (Rules for cost, 3 and 4). `@state`, `src/owners.json`, the save, an invariant (known keys, `until` finite, at most one entry a key).
- [ ] Talked with a friend, +5 for 2 days: read from the pawn's latest talk (`agent.talk`, which talk writes already: with whom and when), when the pawn's opinion of that pawn is at the page's friend line (0.25) or above. If the latest talk hides an earlier one with a friend too often to be seen (say how often, populous small, seed 7, a year), `agent.talk` gains one field, the hour of its last talk with a friend, written where `talk` already checks the pair's band: no new read of opinion a talk.
- [ ] The pawn panel shows mood as a number and a word (miserable under 25, low under 45, content under 65, happy) and each modifier in force with its sign, value and label; the page reads through the `Sim` API. `node tests/smoke.js` and the `tests/browser*.js` touched; a screenshot under `docs/img/`.
- [ ] Mood has no effect on any decision in this task: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` but for the new field `pawn.mods`; its line in the task file.
- [ ] A test on a constructed state: each modifier appears on its trigger and is gone on time; a save keeps the event modifiers; mood is within 0 to 100.
- [ ] Reported, as descriptions: the distribution of mood over a populous year (small, seed 7) by season, and the share of pawn-days each modifier is in force.
- [ ] Cost (rule 9): nothing a step; a write at a wedding, a birth and a death (a few hundred a year at 1,000 living); a read when a panel is open.
