# VB-062: Mood and its modifiers, shown in the pawn panel
Status: done (2026-11-16)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: talk
Phase: implement
Design ref: `design.md`, People, households and family records (Pawn interactions: "mood modifiers such as talked with a friend +5", Decided 30 Oct; Traits, talk and stories: Where pawns meet, Mood); `backlog/VB-186.md`. Kim, 2026-10-30: the pawn info screen shows active modifiers, for example "mood +5, recently talked to a friend".
Goal: Give each pawn a mood built from modifiers with a value and a label, so what happens in a pawn's life shows as feelings. In this first version mood has no effect on behaviour; it is shown, and later tasks may read it. Confirmed and rewritten by slice 7's plan (VB-186, 15 Nov) so that nothing is written per talk and nothing walks the pawns: a modifier for a state is read from the state, one for an event is written at the event, and "talked with a friend" is read from what talk already keeps.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `Mood.of(st, pawn)` (a module `sim/mood`, `@sys`, `src/manifest.json`) gives `{ mood, mods: [{ key, label, value }] }`, the mood being 50 plus the sum of the values, within 0 to 100. It is computed when asked and stored nowhere; nothing in the simulation calls it (a lint line or a test holds that, until a later task means to).
- [x] Modifiers for a state, read from the state and never stored (`MOOD` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each value a starting value with its meaning): hungry, -20 × h while the pawn's hunger h is over 0.1; cold, -20 × `hh.cold` (VB-327); without water, -10 while `hh.dry` (VB-331; left out with a line here if that task has not landed); weak, -10 while health is under 50.
- [x] Modifiers for an event, `pawn.mods`: a list of `{ key, until }` written by a subscriber to the event (`sim/events`) or at the act that makes the life event: newly married, +10 for 12 days; a child born, +5 for 12 days, both parents; grieving, -15 for 12 days at the death of a spouse, child or parent. An entry past its `until` is ignored where it is read and dropped when the list is next written; no daily pass (Rules for cost, 3 and 4). `@state`, `src/owners.json`, the save, an invariant (known keys, `until` finite, at most one entry a key).
- [x] Talked with a friend, +5 for 2 days: read from the pawn's latest talk (`agent.talk`, which talk writes already: with whom and when), when the pawn's opinion of that pawn is at the page's friend line (0.25) or above. If the latest talk hides an earlier one with a friend too often to be seen (say how often, populous small, seed 7, a year), `agent.talk` gains one field, the hour of its last talk with a friend, written where `talk` already checks the pair's band: no new read of opinion a talk.
- [x] The pawn panel shows mood as a number and a word (miserable under 25, low under 45, content under 65, happy) and each modifier in force with its sign, value and label; the page reads through the `Sim` API. `node tests/smoke.js` and the `tests/browser*.js` touched; a screenshot under `docs/img/`.
- [x] Mood has no effect on any decision in this task: `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` but for the new field `pawn.mods`; its line in the task file.
- [x] A test on a constructed state: each modifier appears on its trigger and is gone on time; a save keeps the event modifiers; mood is within 0 to 100.
- [x] Reported, as descriptions: the distribution of mood over a populous year (small, seed 7) by season, and the share of pawn-days each modifier is in force.
- [x] Cost (rule 9): nothing a step; a write at a wedding, a birth and a death (a few hundred a year at 1,000 living); a read when a panel is open.
Notes:
- Implementation (16 Nov 2026, VB-062):
  - `src/sim/mood.js` (`sim/mood`, social layer, after `sim/relations`): `of(st, id)` gives `{ mood, mods }` for a living pawn (null for the dead), each value rounded to a whole number, mood `MOOD.base` (50) plus their sum within 0 to 100; `word(m)` the four words; `feel(st, id, key)` writes an event modifier. `Sim.moodOf`, `Sim.moodWord` for the page. `MOOD` in `src/sim/rules.js`, the starting values of the criteria. `tests/mood.js` (fast tier) holds that no other module of `src/sim` names `sim/mood` or `pawn.mods` in its code.
  - Event modifiers (`pawn.mods`, owner `sim/mood`, absent until a pawn's first): three new events of `sim/events`, each a line where the act is: `wed` (`sim/lifecycle` wed), `born` (`sim/births` giveBirth: both parents) and `died` (`sim/lifecycle` killPawn, before the union ends: the spouse, the children and the parents grieve). One entry a key: a second grief, or a second child within 12 days, renews the entry. Expired entries are skipped at the read and dropped when the list is next written. Saved as the pawn is (plain data). Invariant `checkMods` (known keys, at most once, `until` finite and no further than its days ahead).
  - States read at the call: hungry `-20 × hunger` over 0.1; cold `-20 × hh.cold` (the 12 days' mean, VB-327); without water a flat -10 while `hh.dry` (its 12 days' mean) is above 0, so it lasts while a dry day is in the household's last 12; weak -10 under health 50.
  - Talked with a friend: the latest talk alone hid an earlier talk with a friend on 6,872 of 10,115 pawn-days that had one in the last two days (68%; populous small, seed 7, one year, sampled at noon). So `agent.talk` gained `friendAt`, the hour of the pawn's last talk with one it held at `SOCIAL.friend` (0.25) or above: talk now grows the two ties itself (grow's two lines) and keeps the opinions the two band checks (`touch`, which now returns the opinion it read) already compute; no new opinion read. With it, none is hidden. Invariant: `friendAt` finite and no later than now and a talk's bubble (two talks in one hour may be shown in either order).
  - The card (`src/page/words.js` pawnStats, so also the played pawn's sheet): mood as a stat on the one meter (VB-664), marked at the cut points 25, 45 and 65, the number and its word; under it each modifier in force as an icon and its signed value, the label its title and its screen reader's text (icons: hunger, cold, water and health for the states; new ones for mood, married, a child, grieving and a friend in `src/page/icons.js`). Screenshot: `docs/img/vb-062-mood.png` (populous tiny, seed 7, after 90 hours).
