# VB-327: An empty woodpile: a cold house, poorer health and no cooking
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (Goods and the farming year: Fuel). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, Consistency review (10 Nov)
Goal: The woodpile is read by nothing but the firewood job's urgency (`hh.wood`, `src/sim/kinds.js:138-140`): a household with none lives as one with a full pile. In populous small, seed 7, a village of 15 households had no wood for 40 years and nothing followed. Kim decided on 10 November: a household out of wood is cold, which lowers its members' mood and health, and it cannot cook.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Cold: at the end of each day (`endOfDay`, `src/sim/clock.js`, where the day's wood is burnt) a household whose pile does not cover the day's use burns what it has and is cold that day by the part it lacked times the season's use over winter's (`woodUse`: spring 0.22, summer 0.19, autumn 0.31, winter 1), so a summer day without wood barely counts. `hh.cold`, 0 to 1, is the mean of the last 12 days, kept as `hh.coldLog` like a pawn's `hungerLog` (state with an owner, `@state`, an invariant; a save without them loads with 0).
- [ ] Health: on a day its household is cold a pawn loses `HEALTH.cold` (2) times the day's cold, times `HEALTH.frail` (1.5) under 5 and from 60, in `updateHealth` (`src/sim/hunger.js`), beside hunger's drain of 4 times hunger squared, and a full meal restores nothing that day (it restores 3 on a warm one; with the meal's 3 kept, a fed adult would gain 1 a day in a cold house and the cold would cost nothing). A winter wholly without wood so costs a fed adult 24 of 100 and an infant or an old pawn 36; with hunger it kills. The death hazard reads health as today; the death of a pawn whose household was cold is recorded with `cold: true` and told ("died in a cold house"), and no new cause is added.
- [ ] No cooking: the cook job is not offered (`cookWanted`, `src/sim/kinds.js:51`) while the pile is empty, and cooking burns no wood of its own (the day's use is the hearth's). Grain eaten uncooked feeds `ECON.uncooked` (0.75) of a ration: `feedHousehold` counts the grain it takes beyond the cooked meals at that worth, so the household eats more grain for the same need or goes a quarter short. Cooked meals already in store are eaten as today; the larder's food (milk, meat) is not changed.
- [ ] Mood: the cold is one of VB-062's modifiers (a note there); this task only keeps `hh.cold` for it to read and names it in the pawn panel beside hunger ("cold: no firewood"), and in the household's card.
- [ ] The work answers it: the firewood job's urgency under `woodLow` stays 2.2, and a cold household's is `woodColdUrgency` (3), above cooking's, so the first free hand goes for wood; a village with an empty allotment (VB-326) cannot, and the yearly log line says its houses are cold.
- [ ] Help: nothing new. Firewood is not a good that moves between households yet (the fair's goods are VB-299's); the open point is in `design.md`, Known gaps.
- [ ] Bands (`tests/lib/bands.js`): a report of cold household-days as a share of household-days in the full runs, and no assert until Kim gives a target. Tests (`tests/cold.js`, in `npm test`): a household with an empty pile in winter is cold, its frail members' health falls, no cook job is posted and its grain feeds 0.75; with wood, a run is as before.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous and founders, small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years; and one year from `small-y40`): cold household-days by season, deaths and health by age in cold households, grain eaten uncooked, firewood hours; whether households, not only averages, are worse off, and which. The golden file is re-recorded and its changed entries listed.
- [ ] Cost (rule 9): one comparison a live household a day in the pass that already burns the wood; no new loop.
