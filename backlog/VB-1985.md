# VB-1985: A night's lodging and a load carried for hire at a landing
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-1984
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: town
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Settlements, Towns and regions (Lodging and carrying at a landing; The town's size); Inheritance (Sleeping away from home); docs/plans/VB-1924.md, section 8
Touches: sim/hosting; new sim/carryhire; sim/offers; new tests/carryhire.js
Goal: The landing's households earn by lodging and by carrying, deals between households, so a town holds people besides its makers
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A night's lodging: a boat-borne visitor (VB-1984) from beyond a day there and back sleeps at a house within 2 steps of the landing (Inheritance, Sleeping away from home; `sim/hosting`), chosen among at most 20 by the host's room and the visitor's ties; it gives `LODGE.night` 1 ration's worth a night, in kind, as a swap that both remember (Exchange, The fair). A host may refuse when its house is full. No label is read: the household that hosts most is the alehouse only in the report.
- [ ] Carrying for hire: a head whose loads waited for a boat (VB-814's trigger) offers `HIRE.share` a fifth of the load to a household with a boat at its landing, as an offer answered by that head's own estimate of the rowing hours against what the fifth is worth to it; the rower rows the load and rows home, as a journey (VB-812). Settling families rowed from the harbour (VB-636) use the same offer.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state: a visitor lodged and paying; a full house refusing; a load carried for its fifth; an offer refused when the rowing outweighs the share.
- [ ] Reported in `tools/sim.js --report boats` (VB-1984): nights lodged and loads carried a year by landing, and the households whose largest non-farm income they are, populous medium with boats, seeds 7 and 42, 60 years: the plan expects 1 to 3 lodging households and a few boat households at the leading landing (design.md, Towns and regions, Lodging and carrying at a landing; The town's size), as a description.
- [ ] Cost (rule 9): a few hundred nights and offers a year, each looking at at most 20 houses; with VB-1984 under 0.2 ms a game day, a year's mean, counted by a counter in `tests/counters.json`.

Noted by VB-1924, 28 November 2026.
