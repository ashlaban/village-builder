# VB-459: The harvest home: a yearly feast at the farm of a household with plenty
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Exchange, goodwill and obligations (The gift: The harvest home); backlog/VB-407.md
Goal: Weddings and funerals are too few to turn a good year's surplus into goodwill: the harvest home is the occasion plenty itself calls (VB-407's plan; Kim, 13 November).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A kind `harvest` in `OCCASIONS` (`src/sim/occasions.js`), 16 to 21 as a wedding, not overriding work; called on the host's first planning day after its last grain field is reaped, for an evening `FEAST.notice` (3) days ahead, if the household is then above the feast's threshold (VB-458). At most one a household a year, kept as the year it last held one (plain data, in the `@state` line, with its invariant).
- [ ] Whom it asks: the holder's kin households, then the households of the pawns the holder has the strongest ties to, nearest first, within `REACH` and with a route by land (VB-436's rule), until 20 guests are asked; `guests()` is extended, not copied. A pawn already bound for an occasion that day is not asked (one feast a day).
- [ ] The feast itself is VB-458's: food served, goodwill booked, ale poured for standing. The log line names the kind (`harvest`), the host and the farm.
- [ ] A test on a constructed state: a household above the threshold calls one and only one in a year; one below calls none; guests over the sea or beyond a day's walk are not asked; two hosts on one evening share no guest.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, as VB-458's seeds and years: harvest homes a village-year, the share of households that host and that are guests, rations feasted and rotted as shares of the harvest, goodwill outstanding a household, evening hours spent walking to feasts. If more than half the households host every year, try `FEAST.above` 2.5 and 3 (within VB-458's five runs or its own) and report; the value stays Kim's unless a run shows a collapse.
- [ ] Cost (rule 9), named before building: hosts (about half the households on the plan's rough count, so 50 to 60 a year on populous small and 100 to 120 at `medium-ref`) × 20 guests × one journey and one booking, through the occasions' due-queue (rule 4), spread over the autumn by each host's own planning day (rule 5). Measured at `small-y40` and on a late save (`--report perf`): `occasionsDue`, route lookups, and the step's longest time.
