# VB-175: Refactor: household membership in `sim/records`, and one act that founds a household
Status: done
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: ownership
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (code structure). VB-172's map, sections 4, 5.4 and 7.2; Kim's answers of 3 November (`QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-172: the ownership map", questions 1 and 6).
Goal: Household membership has five writer modules: births, the cottage and a death write past `moveTo`. A household is founded in three places with copies of the same steps (`farmclaims.moveIn`, `cottage.finishCottage`, `settle.settleFarm`), and two of them make dependency cycles through lookups into later modules (`farmclaims.js:181`, `expansion.js:172`). Give membership one owner, and founding one act. Depends on VB-174, which edits the same founding lines for the grain.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `sim/records` owns `pawn.household`, `household.members` and `pawn.servant`, through `joinHousehold` (as now) and a new `leaveHousehold`. `moveTo` (`sim/heirs`) stays the act that also releases the agent and places it at home, and calls them. Births (`births.js:27`), the cottage (`cottage.js:45`) and a death (`lifecycle.js:36`) use them.
- [x] `node tools/writes.js --field household.members` (and `pawn.household`, `pawn.servant`) lists only `sim/records`, apart from seeding and the save codec.
- [x] One act founds a household on a farm: make it, push it, own the house (and the fields and meadows of an empty farm), move each member, refresh the roles and mark the page. `farmclaims.moveIn`, `cottage.finishCottage` and `settle.settleFarm` keep their own steps (the claim, the plot, the booth) and call it.
- [x] farmclaims and expansion reach it without a lookup into a later module other than `issue` (Kim's answer 6), for example through a verb in `sim/commands`; the lookups at `farmclaims.js:181` and `expansion.js:172` are gone, and `tools/writes.js` lists two cycles fewer. A new verb is covered by `tests/commands.js`.
- [x] Refactor rules of `BACKLOG.md`: behaviour unchanged, `tests/golden.json` unchanged, `npm run verify` passes; no renames. The PR says what fell (the three copies, two cycles).

Implementation notes (6 November 2026):
- Impact: membership and service writes move from births, lifecycle, heirs, cottage, service and settle to `sim/records`.
  `sim/membership.foundHousehold` reads farm fields/meadows and party members; it writes the new household through records,
  supplies through stores, land through ownership, and agents through the existing move act. One new coordination module, no new state.
- The existing founding work is shared by restoration, cottages and settlers. Cottage stakes stay booked before the move;
  settlers' provisions and followers keep their existing treatment. Death preserves its historical household address and service.
- Two direct late-module lookups are replaced by checked, plain-data commands. Two forbidden cycle witnesses disappear;
  routing expansion through `issue` adds one permitted command cycle (five total witnesses before, four after).
  The strongly connected component loses heirs and cottage. VB-176 retains the plot-or-farm filter lookup.
- `tools/writes.js --field household.members`, `pawn.household` and `pawn.servant` now list only records.
  Ownership allowances fall from 20 to 7; the seven remaining allowances concern roles, outside this task.
- Common founding replaces three copies; no runtime framework or new persistent state is introduced. Work remains proportional
  to the moving party and farm tiles plus the existing role refresh, with no pass over history and no additional role refresh.
- `CLAUDE.md` moved to `AGENTS.md`, with Markdown references updated as requested.
- `sim/membership` is the one new module: the shared founding act belongs to Membership in target-v2 section 4.4,
  while heirs was already near the enforced 400-line limit. Its existing consumers import it after heirs; the restoration verb
  reaches it through commands. It owns no new fields. Cottage's special treatment of unhoused members is retained explicitly.
- Verification: `VB_TEST_JOBS=4 VB_SOFTWARE_RENDER=1 npm run verify` passes all 84 scripts in 258.6 s,
  including three real Chromium checks, all four late-game fixtures, five invariant runs and exact save/load continuity.
  Golden states, work counters and cost-growth baselines are unchanged. Build freshness, lint and `git diff --check` pass.
  `tests/tools.js` now asserts the single membership owner and absence of the two direct founding lookups; its old assertions
  deliberately expected the pre-refactor writers and cycle. Membership tests preserve death's historical address and service,
  and command refusals remain mutation-free. No presentation behavior changed, so screenshots do not apply.
