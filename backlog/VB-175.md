# VB-175: Refactor: household membership in `sim/records`, and one act that founds a household
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: ownership
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (code structure). VB-172's map, sections 4, 5.4 and 7.2; Kim's answers of 3 November (`QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-172: the ownership map", questions 1 and 6).
Goal: Household membership has five writer modules: births, the cottage and a death write past `moveTo`. A household is founded in three places with copies of the same steps (`farmclaims.moveIn`, `cottage.finishCottage`, `settle.settleFarm`), and two of them make dependency cycles through lookups into later modules (`farmclaims.js:181`, `expansion.js:172`). Give membership one owner, and founding one act. Depends on VB-174, which edits the same founding lines for the grain.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `sim/records` owns `pawn.household`, `household.members` and `pawn.servant`, through `joinHousehold` (as now) and a new `leaveHousehold`. `moveTo` (`sim/heirs`) stays the act that also releases the agent and places it at home, and calls them. Births (`births.js:27`), the cottage (`cottage.js:45`) and a death (`lifecycle.js:36`) use them.
- [ ] `node tools/writes.js --field household.members` (and `pawn.household`, `pawn.servant`) lists only `sim/records`, apart from seeding and the save codec.
- [ ] One act founds a household on a farm: make it, push it, own the house (and the fields and meadows of an empty farm), move each member, refresh the roles and mark the page. `farmclaims.moveIn`, `cottage.finishCottage` and `settle.settleFarm` keep their own steps (the claim, the plot, the booth) and call it.
- [ ] farmclaims and expansion reach it without a lookup into a later module other than `issue` (Kim's answer 6), for example through a verb in `sim/commands`; the lookups at `farmclaims.js:181` and `expansion.js:172` are gone, and `tools/writes.js` lists two cycles fewer. A new verb is covered by `tests/commands.js`.
- [ ] Refactor rules of `BACKLOG.md`: behaviour unchanged, `tests/golden.json` unchanged, `npm run verify` passes; no renames. The PR says what fell (the three copies, two cycles).
