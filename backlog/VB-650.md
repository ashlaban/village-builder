# VB-650: Teleport: a household that dies out leaves its barn to no one
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: physical
Phase: implement
Design ref: VB-611
Goal: The stores of a household that dies out stay in its barn on its farm and pass with the farm, or are fetched by whoever the rights go to; none vanishes
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] When a household dies out or dissolves, what its barn, woodpile and stores held does not vanish: it stays at the farmstead as the estate's, and passes by the rules that already move rights (the heir, or by custom the village), changing owner where it lies (the hard rule's exemption for rights), to be fetched or used there by whoever takes the farm.
- [ ] Find every place a store is closed with goods in it (`Stores.close` and its callers) and say for each what now happens to the goods; none is left that drops them.
- [ ] Goods nobody inherits are used up by a process that is named (rot at the frost, as lying loads and stooks), with an event of the record.
- [ ] Invariants, the save and an old save, and `tools/stocks.js` lines for the goods of a closed household.
- [ ] A test on a constructed state: a household dies out with grain in its barn; the grain is still there the next day, owned by its heir or the village, and is eaten or carried only by pawns who go there.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (`tools/balance.js`; grain made, used and lost a year), populous small seeds 7 and 42 and founders small seed 7, 15 years, and how much grain a year the old behaviour dropped.
- 16 Nov 2026 (orchestrator): the criteria are written from the goal and VB-611's notes; its note carried none.
