# VB-294: The gathering: villages choose where to meet, and one hosts
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: fair
Phase: implement
Design ref: Settlements, The gathering (10 Nov); `docs/review-markets.md`, sections 2.3 and 5
Goal: The first thing between villages: a yearly gathering at a host village's green. Villages offer to host and choose where to go, so hosts compete; the villages of one gathering are a district.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] At its autumn meeting (day 10) a village with a green may offer to host; every village then chooses the gathering it will attend that winter, a few days later (winter days 1 to 3), counted in hours of walking: the host with the fewest effective hours, if under 12 (`TRAVEL.dayWalk`). The walk is `hoursBetween(st, v, host)` (VB-286; by land only, so islands gather apart: Kim, 10 Nov); plus up to 4 for customs that differ (hard customs count double); less 0.5 for each household of kin in the villages that went there last year, at most 2; less or plus up to 2 for the disposition between the two villages (read from their holders' opinions and balances, never stored); less 1 if it was held last year; less up to 1 for the ale its host poured at last year's gathering (VB-305, merged: `Stores.pour`, `aleStanding`), so a host that poured nothing loses that pull the next year (carried from VB-305). A host's own walk is 0. Tunables in `src/sim/rules.js`. (`GATHERING`; the customs hours are not added: decision 2.)
- [x] A village attends one gathering. A gathering that fewer than three villages chose is not held; its villages choose again among those that stand, once. A village more than a day's walk (12 hours) from every host has none. A host lodges 10 guests a farm (Agreed, 10 Nov): a village whose expected goers (3 a household) the host cannot lodge beside those already coming, nearest first, goes to its next gathering; villages within 2 hours' walk need no lodging. (Choosing again is repeated, the smallest first, not once: decision 1.)
- [x] The choice is village state with an owner (`src/owners.json`), in the `WORLD` list of `src/sim/saves.js`, with invariants: the host is an inhabited village with a green; every attending village is within reach. (`village.gathering`, owned by `sim/gathering`; saved with `st.villages`, not in `WORLD`, which lists the world's tile arrays: decision 7. Inhabited is checked at the settling, in the test: decision 6.)
- [x] The district is a derived view (the villages of one gathering), never stored apart from the choice. (`district(st, host)`, `gatherings(st)`.)
- [x] A village's choice is a routine of its meeting (VB-257), so the player's pawn argues for it by advocacy as for any cause. (A question of the meeting of its own, made as the routines are: QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, "VB-294"; its verb `gathering`.)
- [ ] Cost: reads of `st.villageHours` (VB-286), villages × hosts once a year, in each village's own meeting step (VB-278); no search of its own; a counter; measured on `medium-ref`. (Built: four counters, villages × hosts in each meeting step, no search; not measured on `medium-ref`: no fixture in this worktree, owed in VB-1008.)
- [ ] Reported (`tools/sim.js --report`): gatherings, their hosts and villages, by year; over 40 populous years on small and medium, how many stand, how often a host changes, and the share of villages with no gathering (islands apart is accepted; the number is for slice 10). (`--report gathering` built; 10 years run, seeds 7 and 42, below; the 40-year runs are owed in VB-1008.)
- [x] A test; behaviour otherwise unchanged until VB-295 sends anyone (`node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same`, or what changed is listed). (`tests/gathering.js`; what changed is listed below.)
