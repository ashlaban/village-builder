# VB-258: Plans and the cache: a standing plan per household and village, golden unchanged
Status: done (2026-11-10)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Work: opportunities, plans and tasks (design.md, section 7); docs/review-work.md, sections 2, 4 and 5 (task 1)
Goal: `refreshJobs` (`src/sim/jobs.js`) runs over every occupied farm and village 14 times a game day and posts what each kind's `available` reports; no one holds a plan. Give each household and village a standing plan whose review is that same pass, so that later tasks can change what a review chooses and when it runs, with nothing changed yet.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A new module `sim/plans`, the one writer of `st.plans` (`src/owners.json`, the `WORLD` list of `src/sim/saves.js`, `@sys` and `@state` lines, the manifest after every module it reads; under 400 lines): one standing plan per occupied household and per village, plain data: `{holder, kind: 'standing', nextReview}` and no more until a later task needs it. A plan is made when its household or village is, and dropped with it (no plan of a dissolved household stays: Rules for cost, 1).
- [x] `refreshJobs` becomes the review of each holder's standing plan, called every hour for every holder as today; `st.jobs` keeps its name, its keys and its posting order.
- [x] Work counters `oppAsk` (calls of an `available` rule) and `planReview` in `VB.COUNTERS`; `node tools/sim.js --years 1 --report counts` shows them, and the PR gives the split of "jobs refresh and matching" between refresh and matching at populous small, seed 7, 2 years (10.2 ms a game day together on 10 November) and at `medium-ref`.
- [x] Invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js`: every occupied household and every village has exactly one standing plan, and no plan names a dissolved household. `tests/saves.js` and `tests/plaindata.js` pass.
- [x] `tests/golden.json` is unchanged; `tests/counters.json` changes only by the two new counters (listed in the PR). `npm run verify` passes.
- [x] The PR starts with the impact note: `sim/plans` reads households and villages and writes only `st.plans`; the one new call is from the hourly refresh.
Cost: one record per holder; no new loop (the review is today's pass).

Implementation (10 Nov 2026).
- `st.plans` is `{households: Map, villages: Map}` of id to `{holder: {type, id}, kind: 'standing', nextReview}`: two maps by number,
  so the hourly lookup allocates nothing, and a dissolved household's entry is deleted (Rules for cost, 1).
- `st.plans` is saved with the state like `st.jobs`; the `WORLD` list of `src/sim/saves.js` holds only the world's tile arrays, so it is
  not listed there (the criterion's wording); the load builds plans for older saves instead.
- `tests/golden.js` leaves `plans` out of the fingerprint (its `DERIVED` list): it holds nothing yet but one plan per live holder and the
  next review hour, both functions of the hashed state; the task that gives a plan a choice should hash it. `tests/golden.json` unchanged.
- Counters: `oppAsk` (calls of a kind's `available` rule by the review; `sim/opportunities`' own asks for the page are not counted) and
  `planReview`. `tests/counters.json` gains only these two: oppAsk 38.79 and planReview 3.73 per living pawn-day. (The other entries
  were already off HEAD's runs by up to 10%, inside the tolerance, and are left as they were; HEAD and this branch give identical
  values for all of them.)

Numbers (`tools/sim.js --report perf`, with the group split locally into refreshJobs and callCooks + lookUp + planRound; noisy machine):
- populous small seed 7, 2 years: refresh 2.97 to 3.95 ms a game day, matching 3.46 to 4.36 (two runs; HEAD the same way: refresh
  4.26, matching 4.81). Counts: oppAsk 35.2 and planReview 3.18 per living pawn-day (11,549 and 1,042 a game day).
- `medium-ref` (year 12 to 13): refresh 14.11 ms a game day, matching 17.23 (HEAD: 14.74 and 17.39). Counts: oppAsk 39.1 and
  planReview 3.30 per living pawn-day (39,611 and 3,348 a game day).
So the refresh, the plans' review, is about 45% of "jobs refresh and matching" at both; the plans themselves add nothing measurable.
No behaviour changes: households are exactly as before (golden and every other counter identical).
- Cost growth: `tests/history.js` now measures the two counters, and both exceed the 1.25 limit from `small-y5` to `small-y40` (oppAsk
  1.354, planReview 1.342 per living pawn-day): the review is per live holder, and households per living pawn grow over history (smaller
  households). The work is today's pass, not new; it is recorded as two ceilings in the `history` section of `tests/cost-baseline.json`
  (which may only fall). Kim: the refresh's cost per pawn-day grows with the number of households, a line for the planning-day task.
