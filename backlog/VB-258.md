# VB-258: Plans and the cache: a standing plan per household and village, golden unchanged
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Work: opportunities, plans and tasks (design.md, section 7); docs/review-work.md, sections 2, 4 and 5 (task 1)
Goal: `refreshJobs` (`src/sim/jobs.js`) runs over every occupied farm and village 14 times a game day and posts what each kind's `available` reports; no one holds a plan. Give each household and village a standing plan whose review is that same pass, so that later tasks can change what a review chooses and when it runs, with nothing changed yet.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A new module `sim/plans`, the one writer of `st.plans` (`src/owners.json`, the `WORLD` list of `src/sim/saves.js`, `@sys` and `@state` lines, the manifest after every module it reads; under 400 lines): one standing plan per occupied household and per village, plain data: `{holder, kind: 'standing', nextReview}` and no more until a later task needs it. A plan is made when its household or village is, and dropped with it (no plan of a dissolved household stays: Rules for cost, 1).
- [ ] `refreshJobs` becomes the review of each holder's standing plan, called every hour for every holder as today; `st.jobs` keeps its name, its keys and its posting order.
- [ ] Work counters `oppAsk` (calls of an `available` rule) and `planReview` in `VB.COUNTERS`; `node tools/sim.js --years 1 --report counts` shows them, and the PR gives the split of "jobs refresh and matching" between refresh and matching at populous small, seed 7, 2 years (10.2 ms a game day together on 10 November) and at `medium-ref`.
- [ ] Invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js`: every occupied household and every village has exactly one standing plan, and no plan names a dissolved household. `tests/saves.js` and `tests/plaindata.js` pass.
- [ ] `tests/golden.json` is unchanged; `tests/counters.json` changes only by the two new counters (listed in the PR). `npm run verify` passes.
- [ ] The PR starts with the impact note: `sim/plans` reads households and villages and writes only `st.plans`; the one new call is from the hourly refresh.
Cost: one record per holder; no new loop (the review is today's pass).
