- 19 Nov 2026, done. Decision: the norm is the village's mean attitude of its adults (age 12 and over), not its practice in force: the practice is one option a meeting chose, the mean is what the people hold, and it is what the daily drift's `oblique` pull already reads. `Culture.P.upbringing` 0.3 (as tests/calibrate.js); `Culture.inherit(mother, father, members)`, called by sim/births. A newborn of no village keeps its parents' mean.
- Measure (throwaway script, yearly samples of practices and customs, founders small, 80 years; changes, of them back within 10 years): upbringing 0 -> 0.3. Seed 7: customs 13 (4) -> 6 (1), practices 22 (7) -> 14 (1). Seed 42: customs 3 (0) -> 5 (1), practices 15 (2) -> 15 (2). One run each; seed 42 is not better, so the gain is on seed 7 only.
