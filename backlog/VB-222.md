# VB-222: Help in hunger asks once a season, until the next harvest
Status: done (2026-11-08)
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: kin-help
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, goodwill and obligations (The gift: Lumps and occasions, Decided 8 Nov); Goods, production and animals (Help in hunger: once a season, until the harvest, Kim 8 Nov); `QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "VB-217", questions 5, 6 and 8.
Goal: Help in hunger is a trickle: a household under 3 days' food asks for 12 days and may ask again within days, so it gets 2.3 to 3.1 gifts a year from 1.4 to 1.8 givers, of about 20 rations each (populous 7 small, 40 years). At year 40 that is 4,664 gift events in the record (649 KB), 582 KB in the pawns' events and 2,170 ledger entries (199 KB). Kim (8 Nov): make gifts rarer and more substantial, by the principle Lumps and occasions; help is its first occasion. At most once a season, a household short of food asks for enough to reach the next harvest.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The rule (`sim/help`, `shareFood`): a household whose spare grain covers fewer than `HELP.askBelow` (3) days, and that has not asked this season, asks for enough to reach the next harvest, the start of autumn, and at least a season's need (`HELP.askDays`, 12 days); in autumn, a season's need. That is from 12 days (summer's end, autumn) to 36 (winter's start). Kin first, closest first, each giving what lies above its floor and reserve (VB-216), then the village store. If the gifts fall short, it does not ask again until the next season.
- [x] State: `household.askedIn`, the season (`Math.floor(st.t / (SEASON_DAYS * DAY))`) of its last ask, or absent; owned by `sim/help` (`src/owners.json`, an `@state` line), saved, with an invariant in `checkState` (an integer no later than this season).
- [x] Tests: `tests/help.js` updated for the new rule (one ask a season; the amount until the harvest; the store covers the rest; a second shortfall in the same season asks nothing), in `npm test`.
- [x] Numbers before and after, here and in the PR: `tools/balance.js --scenario populous --seeds 3,7,11,42 --map small --years 15` (rations by source against need); `tools/sim.js` (founders 7 small, 10 years; populous 7 small, 15 years) yearly lines (deaths by cause, hungry household-days); and, over 40 years of populous 7 small, as measured for this task's goal: gifts a year and per living pawn, their median size, gifts and givers per receiving household, the record's gift events and the ledgers' gift entries, goodwill pairs and their bytes in the save. Say whether households, not only averages, are better or worse off: hungry household-days and deaths from hunger by household, not only in total.
- [x] If hunger deaths rise beyond the noise of four seeds, say so with the numbers, and propose the change (a question in `QUESTIONS.md`), rather than tuning on your own.
- [x] The golden file re-recorded, the changed entries listed in the PR; counters re-recorded if they move, and listed.
- [x] Cost: one check a household a day, as today (a season number compared); fewer gifts, bookings, events and ledger entries.
- [x] `npm run verify` passes.
Notes:
- `askedIn` goes on the household, not the holder, since help is the household's ask; a new household starts without one.
- The asks of a household that dissolves mid-season simply go with it.
