# VB-778: The scenario "The market town": a dense place as a test bed, known to decay
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: town
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Starting scenarios (The market town, a test bed); Balance loops and targets (Deaths; The urban sink); docs/plans/VB-189.md, sections 3 and 5.7
Goal: Crowding and the place's label need a dense place to be measured on, whatever becomes of it (the orchestrator's close call, 16 Nov: the scenario is kept as a test bed, known to decay, with a line saying it is not evidence of emergence).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A scenario "The market town" (`src/sim/scenarios.js`), built by the generators every scenario uses: a populous island with one place set dense, `TESTBED.plots` 8 plot households on plots within 2 steps of each other at the village with most people within a day's walk, each with what a plot household can hold (a cow, nets, a field if one is free). It is **a test bed, known to decay** (the second mock: 4.6 maker households at year 10, 1.4 at year 60), is not offered on the start screen, and its description and every report of it say that it is not evidence that a town emerges.
- [x] The world it starts with is placed, not carried (the hard rule's exemption); nothing after the start holds the place together: no rule reads that it is the test bed.
- [ ] Reported, small and medium, seeds 7 and 42, 40 years: the crowding factor of its plot households by decade (VB-766) and their deaths per 1,000 against the farms' (the budget's "about 1.5 times"); the place's people by decade and its sink, `M / (d - b)` and `S / c` (VB-765, if it exists); how long it stays denser than a village; once VB-771 exists, its measures and label by decade.
- [x] A reduced, asserted band where one can be shown on a tiny map and a few years (`tests/lib/bands.js`): in the packed place deaths per head exceed the farms'; it may fail outside its band.
- [x] `tests/saves.js` and the scenario list's tests cover it; the night's fixtures are not extended here (VB-662).
- Cost (rule 9): a scenario's start, once; no new loop.

Noted by VB-189, 16 November 2026.
