# VB-357: The ground: bedrock, clay and ore deposits in the generated world, and the Ground overlay
Status: done (2026-11-11)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: World, climate and travel (World and scale: Ground); Goods, Goods of place and side crafts (In the ground)
Goal: Kim, 11 Nov: the terrain generation adds clay and a number of minerals, for a setting of Europe 1300 to 1700, continental and Scandinavian. Generation and representation only: a per-tile layer over the biomes, deposits finite with a chance of sources that do not run out, tuned pure chance by island so that an island with everything is uncommon and islands commonly differ.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Bedrock on every land tile (`w.rock`): granite, limestone, sandstone or slate in provinces, with lenses of marble (high limestone) and
      soapstone (upland granite or slate).
- [x] Deposits (`w.deposit`, `w.depositLeft`), one kind a tile, as veins: clay (low, damp ground), bog iron (marsh, lake shores), iron, copper
      and lead-silver (uplands), tin (upland granite only), gold (the high mountains). Finite, in loads; a vein of clay, iron, copper,
      lead-silver or tin may hold one tile that does not run out (`ENDLESS`).
- [x] Each island draws which kinds it has, a small island seldom any: no island with every kind on the maps measured, and two islands of a
      map share about a third of their kinds (numbers below).
- [x] From its own random source: the rest of the world is as it was. Golden file: `world/7` and `world/42` gain `rock`, `deposit` and
      `depositLeft`; no other entry changes.
- [x] Shown: the tile panel names the bedrock and the deposit with its loads; a Ground overlay (the map bar, key O) tints the bedrock and
      marks the deposits, with a key of the kinds the map has.
- [x] `node tools/geology.js` reports the shares by map and island; `tests/geology.js` (npm test) checks the layers.
- [x] `design.md` (World and scale: Ground; Goods of place: In the ground) and `QUESTIONS.md` (what is still open) record it.
Notes:
- Kim's answers in the session (11 Nov): generation and representation only; every kind proposed may exist, none need be on every map; a
  per-tile layer over the biomes; tuned pure chance, an island with everything uncommon and islands commonly different; finite, with a
  chance of sources that are in effect endless, to be weighed when the play is balanced; silver a little commoner than first tuned
  (chance 0.2 to 0.3); a map overlay.
- History behind the list (Europe, 1300 to 1700). Iron in two forms: bog and lake ore, smelted by peasants across Scandinavia, and rock
  ore mined in districts (Bergslagen, Styria, the Siegerland). Copper from a few great mines (Falun, Mansfeld, Neusohl). Silver nearly
  always won from lead ore (Sala, Kongsberg, Freiberg, Kutná Hora), so one deposit gives both. Tin only in Cornwall and the Erzgebirge, with
  granite; gold marginal. Clay in the lowlands, for pots, daub, tiles and brick. Granite and gneiss the Scandinavian ground and
  fieldstone; limestone and sandstone the building stones; slate for roofs, soapstone for pots and carving, marble rare.
- Implementation. `src/gen/geology.js` (new, `gen/geology`, kernel layer, before `gen.js`, which calls `lay` with its arrays, a random
  source of its own, `seed ^ 0x6e0106`, and the biome codes). Bedrock: `PROVINCES` scatters a centre for every 2,200 map tiles, each with
  a rock by weight (granite 45, limestone 22, sandstone 18, slate 15); a tile takes its nearest centre's rock, the border made ragged by
  noise. `VEINS`, laid island by island, the rarest first: a kind is on an island with `chance`, less in proportion on one with fewer
  than `full` host tiles; then a vein for every `per` host tiles, each grown through host tiles from a random one. Amounts are
  placeholders until a good uses them. `src/page/ground.js` (new) draws the overlay once a world; `page/tileinfo` adds the two lines.
  `gen.js` keeps its 547 lines (the call is in the returned object; `hp` is declared with `landIdx`).
- Impact: reads the generator's arrays only; writes three new world fields, which nothing in the simulation reads or writes, so they are
  not in `WORLD` of `src/sim/saves.js` (a loaded run regenerates them from its seed) and have no invariant in `checkState` yet: the
  first task that digs adds both. No new coupling between systems.
- Numbers (`node tools/geology.js`), share of maps and of islands of 60 land tiles or more with each kind. Small map, seeds 1 to 20, 101
