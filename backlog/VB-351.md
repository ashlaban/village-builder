# VB-351: The head chooses what to sow, by foresight
Status: done (2026-11-22)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: crops
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods and the farming year, Crops and rotations (design.md, section 8)
Goal: How the head chooses what to sow: layers 2 and 3 of the plan for crops. Skill acts on choices and timing only (Kim, 11 Nov): no multiplier on yield.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Foresight from the stand-in traits (wits, husbandry, years as head), read through `sim/plans`' existing estimate: the lowest fifth keeps the rotation it took over and, in a hungry year, sows its fallow field with whatever seed it has; the middle holds the usual rotation, rests a field whose soil is below 0.85, and after a failed crop sows more of the other; the highest fifth compares the rotations it has seed for against its estimate of need and hands at the year's turn, rests the poorest field first and puts rye on fresh land. The numbers are rules.
- [x] The bias toward or against a crop is drawn once when the pawn becomes head, from the stream `traits`, and is plain data on the pawn (`@state`).
- [x] Timing: a good head opens hoeing and sowing on the window's first day; nothing else is added (planning days by diligence already spread the rest).
- [x] A trial is a project of VB-255's menu: targets met, a field's seed of a crop it does not grow, weighted by ambition; it puts one field under a rotation with that crop.
- [x] A test asserts that two fields of one crop, soil, sowing day and weeding yield the same under a poor and a good head. Numbers as in the rotations task, with heads' foresight on: the share of farms by rotation at years 5, 15 and 30, and how long a crop takes to become a village's usual one. Barley-only runs do not move (`node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` with every village on barley): say so.
Cost: a holder a year at its turn: at most five rotations × its fields, on its planning day. No new loop over households.
