# VB-522: Venues: the ale evening at a farm with a barrel to spare, and the season's rite on the green
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: talk
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, People, households and family records (Traits, talk and stories: Where pawns meet); Goods, production and animals (Ale and standing); Settlements (The gathering); backlog/VB-186.md
Goal: People of different households and villages meet too seldom for news, friendship or courting to cross between them: 0.35 friends in another village a pawn. Two occasions people go to by their own wish: a holder opens its house on a barrel of its own ale, to its village and the villages within an hour and a half, and three times a year a village holds a rite on its green, led by its most devout respected holder.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The ale evening (`design.md`, Where pawns meet): an occasion of kind `ale` in `sim/occasions`, at the host's farm from the end of work to 22. `ALE_EVENING` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each with its meaning: `call` 0.1 (a holder's chance a day), `reach` 1.5 (walking hours), `come` 0.25, `guests` 20, `mix` 0.5.
- [ ] Who calls: at its planning day's review (no new pass), a holder whose household holds a barrel of ale that no wedding, funeral ale, feast or gathering of its own within the barrel's keeping needs, at chance `call` × (0.5 + its sociability) × (0.5 + its ambition), scaled to the days since its last review; at most one ale evening a village a day, the first to call. It is a verb of `sim/commands` (`callAle`), which checks the barrel and returns why not, so the player as holder calls one by the same verb (the action bar's Household group; parity of command).
- [ ] Who is asked and who comes: the adults of the host's village and of the villages within `reach` walking hours of it (the table of walking hours, no search); each comes by its own wish at chance `come` × (0.5 + sociability) × (1 + its opinion of the host) × (1 − its walk / `reach`), counted until `guests` have said yes; a pawn at urgent work, hungry at 0.5 or more, or on a journey stays. The counter `occasionAsked` covers it.
- [ ] Nothing teleports: guests walk there and home (the occasion's own setting out, with the walk home after dark allowed for it); the ale poured is the host's barrel, taken through `sim/stores` with the reason `ale`, by `pourAle` as at a wedding (the guests' memory `ale`, the host's standing, the event `alePoured`). Nothing is poured for those who did not come. If VB-458 has landed, what is drunk is booked as a feast books it, by the same rule.
- [ ] The season's rite: an occasion of kind `rite` on the green of each village with one, on spring day 1, summer day 6 and winter day 7 at noon for `RITE.hours` 2, entered in the calendar (`sim/clock` CALENDAR) and the due-queue. Each adult of the village, and of a lone farm within `RITE.reach` 2 walking hours of a green, comes at chance `RITE.come` 0.3 + 0.6 × its piety; work is not overridden.
- [ ] The one who leads: the holder present with the highest piety × prestige, read at the rite's hour and stored nowhere but in its event (`rite`, by `log`, with its kind, the leader and the village: how many came). Each who came gains `SOCIAL.ties.rite` 0.25 to the leader, one way and to the leader only (no loop over pairs). `Encounters.said` is called with the leader, for VB-523 to make its story known to all present.
- [ ] Both kinds are venues of VB-521's table (`occasion`, with `mix` 0.5 for `ale` and 0.2 for `rite`), so guests talk with those present, strangers among them.
- [ ] The page: an ale evening and a rite show as the wedding and the meeting do (people walking in, standing, bubbles); the village log has their lines; the played pawn's sheet offers "Go to the ale evening at <farm>" and "Go to the rite" as acts, with the reason when refused. `node tests/smoke.js` and the `tests/browser*.js` touched; a screenshot of each under `docs/img/`.
- [ ] Numbers, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, and founders small, 50 years, seeds 7, 42, before and after: ale evenings a village-year and guests an evening, the share of guests from another village, barrels poured against brewed and against grain rotted, rites and the share of adults who come; ties and friends across villages a pawn (6.6 and 0.35 on 15 November, populous small, seed 7, year 3); hours of work lost (the hours report); growth, marriages and hungry household-days. Whether households, not only averages, are better off: hosts' standing against others'.
- [ ] Parameterisation (at most five short runs, seeds and map named): `call` and `reach`, for an ale evening in a village with grain to spare about once in 6 to 12 days and a fifth to a third of its guests from other villages where one lies within reach; the values tried and what each gave. The rest to `docs/tuning.md` by a `tuning` note (the wrap-up fits the fair-village band on `reach` and `come`).
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state: no barrel, no evening; a barrel kept for a wedding is not poured; a second host the same day waits; a guest 2 hours away does not come; the barrel leaves the store once and only if someone came; the rite's leader is the most devout respected holder present and only it gains the ties. Invariants for the new occasion kinds.
- [ ] Cost (rule 9): at most one ale evening a village a day × the asked (the village and its neighbours within reach, each one chance) × up to 20 who walk; 3 rites a year × villages × those who come; both through the due-queue, nothing polled between. Counters before and after.
