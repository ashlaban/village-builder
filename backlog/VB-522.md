# VB-522: Venues: the ale evening at a farm with a barrel to spare, and the season's rite on the green

Design ref: design.md, People, households and family records (Traits, talk and stories: Where pawns meet); Goods, production and animals (Ale and standing); Settlements (The gathering); backlog/VB-186.md
Goal: People of different households and villages meet too seldom for news, friendship or courting to cross between them: 0.35 friends in another village a pawn. Two occasions people go to by their own wish: a holder opens its house on a barrel of its own ale, to its village and the villages within an hour and a half, and three times a year a village holds a rite on its green, led by its most devout respected holder.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The ale evening (`design.md`, Where pawns meet): an occasion of kind `ale` in `sim/occasions`, at the host's farm from the end of work to 22. `ALE_EVENING` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each with its meaning: `call` 0.1 (a holder's chance a day), `reach` 1.5 (walking hours), `come` 0.25, `guests` 20, `mix` 0.5.
- [x] Who calls: at its planning day's review (no new pass), a holder whose household holds a barrel of ale that no wedding, funeral ale, feast or gathering of its own within the barrel's keeping needs, at chance `call` × (0.5 + its sociability) × (0.5 + its ambition), scaled to the days since its last review; at most one ale evening a village a day, the first to call. It is a verb of `sim/commands` (`callAle`), which checks the barrel and returns why not, so the player as holder calls one by the same verb (the action bar's Household group; parity of command).
- [x] Who is asked and who comes: the adults of the host's village and of the villages within `reach` walking hours of it (the table of walking hours, no search); each comes by its own wish at chance `come` × (0.5 + sociability) × (1 + its opinion of the host) × (1 − its walk / `reach`), counted until `guests` have said yes; a pawn at urgent work, hungry at 0.5 or more, or on a journey stays. The counter `occasionAsked` covers it.
- [x] Nothing teleports: guests walk there and home (the occasion's own setting out, with the walk home after dark allowed for it); the ale poured is the host's barrel, taken through `sim/stores` with the reason `ale`, by `pourAle` as at a wedding (the guests' memory `ale`, the host's standing, the event `alePoured`). Nothing is poured for those who did not come. If VB-458 has landed, what is drunk is booked as a feast books it, by the same rule.
- [x] The season's rite: an occasion of kind `rite` on the green of each village with one, on spring day 1, summer day 6 and winter day 7 at noon for `RITE.hours` 2, entered in the calendar (`sim/clock` CALENDAR) and the due-queue. Each adult of the village, and of a lone farm within `RITE.reach` 2 walking hours of a green, comes at chance `RITE.come` 0.3 + 0.6 × its piety; work is not overridden.
- [x] The one who leads: the holder present with the highest piety × prestige, read at the rite's hour and stored nowhere but in its event (`rite`, by `log`, with its kind, the leader and the village: how many came). Each who came gains `SOCIAL.ties.rite` 0.25 to the leader, one way and to the leader only (no loop over pairs). `Encounters.said` is called with the leader, for VB-523 to make its story known to all present.
- [x] Both kinds are venues of VB-521's table (`occasion`, with `mix` 0.5 for `ale` and 0.2 for `rite`), so guests talk with those present, strangers among them.
- [x] The page: an ale evening and a rite show as the wedding and the meeting do (people walking in, standing, bubbles); the village log has their lines; the played pawn's sheet offers "Go to the ale evening at <farm>" and "Go to the rite" as acts, with the reason when refused. `node tests/smoke.js` and the `tests/browser*.js` touched; a screenshot of each under `docs/img/`.

  - The ale evening: a holder decides at its planning day's review (`planReviewed`, why `day`; no pass of its own), while its household holds a barrel, at `call` × (0.5 + sociability) × (0.5 + ambition) a day, compounded over the days to its next planning day; it issues the verb `callAle` (`sim/commands`), whose check (`cannotCallAle`) says why not: not the holder, no farm of its own, after the end of work, no barrel, its ale kept for an occasion called at its farm (`spareAle`: a barrel for each 20 asked or come of each occasion live at the farm), or another farm of the village has its evening tonight. `callAle` walks the live households once, the host's village and those within `reach` hours by the table of walking hours (`hoursBetween`), nearest first; each adult (16 or over) not on a journey and not hungry at 0.5 draws once, at `come` × (0.5 + sociability) × (1 + opinion of the host) × (1 − walk / reach), until 20 have said yes; the host's household comes too. None said yes: nothing is called. The guests set out by the occasion's own `asked` (their walk before the start; one at work then waits, as at a wedding), stand in the yard and go home after 22 (`finish`). At its end, if a guest of another household stands there, the host pours one barrel (`pourAle`, at most 1: the guests' memory `ale`, `alePoured`, the host's standing) and each guest household with a living holder owes the host's holder its share of the barrel's grain, by the feast's booking (`owing`, `bookAll`, now shared with the feast). Event `aleEvening` (log, the host, `atFarm`, n = its guests; SHORT on the record, as a routine kind).
  - The rite: a calendar entry (`sim/clock`, a day entry guarded by `riteDay`, on `RITE.days`: spring day 1, summer day 6, winter day 7) calls at midnight a rite on each village green (`v.green`); one pass over the live households, each adult drawing once at `come` + `piety` × its piety; a village with no green goes to the nearest green within `RITE.reach` 2 hours. At its end, before those present leave, the leader is read (`lead`): the holder standing there highest in piety × prestige; `Encounters.said` gives those who heard it, each of whom gains `SOCIAL.ties.rite` to the leader only (`addTie`, one way). Event `rite` (log, the leader, the village; n = those present; kept a generation).
  - Encounters: `ENCOUNTERS.occasion.kinds` reads `ALE_EVENING` and `RITE`, so their `mix` (0.5, 0.2) is the floor for strangers there.

- Decisions (Deciding without Kim; noted in the inbox):
  1. A barrel "that no wedding, funeral ale, feast or gathering needs" is one beyond what the occasions already called at its farm need; the custom stock (`BREW.stock`) is not held back, since the household brews to its target again, and a wedding or a death is not foreseen. (The gathering does not exist yet; when VB-294 lands, its occasion at the farm's village counts the same way.)
  2. `ALE_EVENING.reach` 2, not 1.5 (Parameterisation, below): at 1.5 the share of guests from other villages was a twelfth, the target a fifth to a third.
  3. What is drunk at an ale evening is booked as the feast books it (the criterion's "if VB-458 has landed"): each guest household owes the host's holder its share of the barrel's grain. A wedding's ale with no feast still books nothing (VB-458's decision 3): the two differ, and the design's Ale and standing line should say which.
  4. A village with no green, not only a lone farm, goes to the nearest green within `RITE.reach`. With no holder standing at a rite, the most devout respected of those present leads, so a rite is never told without one.
  5. The rite's days are the season's common days (spring 1, summer 6, winter 7) as the criterion says; each village's own days (Kim, 16 Nov) are VB-775's.
  6. Adults are 16 and over (a guest of an ale evening, one coming to the rite); the household's own children of talking age stand at its evening as at a wedding.
