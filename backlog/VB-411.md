# VB-411: Tuning: the carts' numbers, for a run Kim starts
Status: blocked
Waits for: Kim
Priority: 3
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Carts; docs/tuning.md
Goal: Each cart number has a measured effect and a proposed value in docs/tuning.md
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] For each number below, a measured effect at founders and populous small over at least four seeds (carts a household by year, misses a year, households with no cart, hours walking and carrying by kind from `--report hours`, hay in by the frost, firewood short in winter), with the runs named.
- [ ] `docs/tuning.md` has a row for each number that moves something, with what it means, what was measured and a proposed value; a number that moves nothing is said to.
- [ ] No value is changed by the task: the rows wait for the tuning run Kim starts (AGENTS.md, Deciding without Kim, 3).
- [ ] The real loads and paces are checked against a source (they are from memory): a back load, a hand cart's, an ox cart's, and an ox team's pace.

The numbers (`TRAVEL` in `src/sim/rules.js`, `CART` in `src/sim/carts.js`): loads of 30 rations on the back, 90 on a hand cart, 300 on
an ox cart; a laden pawn's pace 0.9, 0.8 and 0.6; hay at 0.2 units a ration of load (6, 18 and 60 a trip); wear 0.02 a trip; mended
below 0.5 in 3 hours; 6 misses in a year for another cart; 12 hours and 3 loads of timber for a hand cart, 30 and 6 for an ox cart;
dung carted 1.5 and 2.5 times as fast; a further load of hay loaded in 0.5 hour.

Notes (the distance review, 13 November): measured once, at seed 7 on the small map. Founders built 6 hand carts in 12 years and none
wore out (mean condition about 0.75, so mending keeps up and a cart never ends). Populous built 32 in 6 years and 1 of 71 households had
none. At 12 tiles an hour carts save little walking: 179 hours a year of walking and carrying on a 4-field farm became 171. A household
with no cart now brings a meadow's hay home in five trips, where it was one. Questions the run should answer: whether a cart should ever
wear out while it is mended; whether 6 misses is reached too easily (nearly every household builds one); whether the ox cart is worth
its 30 hours when the hand cart carries a field's sheaves in two trips.
