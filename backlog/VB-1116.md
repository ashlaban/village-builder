## Findings (VB-1116, 18 Nov)
- Criterion 1: no, the plan does not cap what is sown. `targets.fields` (sim/plans 399, 686, 825; `fieldsWanted`/`fieldsWorkable`, sim/fields 50-72) is read only to decide whether to make a new field (plans 864, aims 66/106) and as the `target` verb's bound (plans 890-916). Sowing is a job each field posts when it is `plowed` and in window (`kinds.js` sow `available`, 227-231; sowWhy checks seed and window only); plow likewise posts per stubble field. Every field a household holds is worked while hours last, however many hands. Land held beyond the target comes by inheritance, a deal or a widow keeping a farm.
- Criterion 2, founders small, 50 years (script run once, not kept), per household-year, by working adults at the frost's eve:
