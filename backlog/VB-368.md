# VB-368: Kilns: brick and tiles from clay, and lime from limestone
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The mill and the loom, the kiln and the quarry); `backlog/VB-185.md`
Goal: Two facility kinds a household or a village builds: a brick kiln fires clay into brick and roof tiles, a lime kiln burns limestone to lime; both eat wood. Slice 6's plan (VB-185, 11 Nov), from Kim's answers in the session.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Facility kinds `brickKiln` and `limeKiln`: 30 hours and 6 loads of stone each. `burnBrick`: 6 loads of clay and 8 of wood, 16 work hours, gives 4 loads of brick or of tiles (the owner's plan says which). `burnLime`: 6 loads of limestone (quarried, VB-332's heap by kind) and 10 of wood, 16 hours, gives 4 loads of lime. Skill pottery for brick, building for lime.
- [ ] `hh.brick`, `hh.tiles`, `hh.lime` through `sim/stores`; the worth table gains a load of brick or tiles at 10 rations and of lime at 14 (Proposed). A kiln is built only by a holder whose project needs its product (VB-369, VB-370), or to sell it at the fair.
- [ ] Numbers (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 30 years): kilns built, loads burnt, wood eaten against the village's wood.
- [ ] The numbers that move are reported before and after at the seeds and maps named (Working rules); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
Notes:
- Numbers are Proposed by slice 6's plan (11 Nov) on Kim's answers to the shape; the hours are in the game's work hours (576 an adult a year) and, but for the textile shares, from general reading not checked by search: each task reports its numbers and the wrap-up tunes them.
- 16 Nov 2026 (Kim's answers after the plan of VB-702; section 14): Brick, tiles and lime are materials and a house is built: none is graded (the plan of VB-702, D20, D22). 'Fine' in 'the fine timber house' is a kind of house, not a grade of workmanship (D29).
