# VB-058: The village green and well, built by the village
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: year
Phase: implement
Design ref: Settlements and look (Village: farmsteads around a green with a well sweep, brunnsvinge: Decided look, Agreed layout); Inheritance and expansion. Kim, 2026-10-30: when a second farmstead appears in a one-farm village, the village buildings become something it can build; they are not created automatically.
Goal: After VB-003, a one-farm village has no green and no well. When it gains a second farm (VB-032 or VB-033), it can lay out a green and dig a well as village work, and is then drawn as a village. Depends on VB-003 and VB-032; if either is not done, mark this blocked.
Acceptance criteria (Kim, 2026-10-30: the proposed values are accepted as starting values):
- [x] A village with two or more occupied farms and no well posts a village-owned project: choose the green's tile, clear it, dig the well.
- [x] The green's tile: a free, passable tile between the farms, as close as possible to their mean position; it becomes the village's `center` and is owned by the village.
- [x] Jobs and hours (proposed): clear the green 10 h; dig and line the well 40 h; raise the well sweep 10 h. Adults of the village take them as a village duty, by role priority: farmhand and son 6, farmer 5, wife, maid and daughter 3.
- [x] When the well is done, `v.green` and `v.well` are set; water is drawn there; the map draws the green, the well sweep and the village label.
- [x] A test: a one-farm village that gains a second farm builds its green and well within a year; a one-farm village never starts the project.
- [x] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR. (Retired by VB-408: no golden file; the numbers before and after are below.)
