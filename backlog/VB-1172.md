Goal: Kim, 18 Nov 2026 (the session on VB-228): a booth is a temporary shelter, raised by a pawn for a pawn or a group so that they do not sleep rough for a season, and it should be a project. Today the verb raiseBooth only pushes a record to st.booths and sim/building's boothWork posts the wood and the raising by scanning every booth ever staked for each household (Rules for cost, 1); its check asks free (not on the road), copied from the walking verbs, though staking takes no time; and nothing in play issues it (only tests/booth.js).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Staking a booth makes a project plan held by the pawn who stakes it (`sim/plans` `makeProject`, holder type `pawn`): the wood, then the raising, each step opening when the one before is done; `boothWork`'s scan of `st.booths` goes, and the cost is reported before and after on a late save.
- [x] The verb registers where the plan puts it; its check is `living` and the ground's (`raiseBoothWhy`), not `free`: a pawn on the road may stake one.
- [x] A pawn in play stakes one by its own judgement when it would otherwise sleep rough for a season (a settler, a worker lodging away); the player's pawn has the same verb (principle 16).
- [x] Giving it up is the project's `dropProject`; a booth's record keeps only what stands (built, wood, condition).
- [x] Numbers before and after at founders seed 7 small: nights slept rough, booths raised.
