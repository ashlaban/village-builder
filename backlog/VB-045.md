## Implementation notes (VB-045)
- `src/page/land.js`: one `PIXI.Graphics` per chunk of `LV[0].size` (512 units; about 190 chunks on a medium map), holding the tiles whose centre lies in
  it; `landIndex()` buckets the tiles per world, `resetLand()` (called by the loader) destroys the graphics. `refreshLand()` runs each frame while the
  overlay is on (`animate.js`: `landOn || landDirty.size`): it turns `st.landDirty` into stale chunk keys (the tile's chunk and its neighbours' chunks,
  for an edge or a border) and clears the set, then draws the stale chunks in view (view plus two hexes): a first drawing at once, a redraw of a drawn
  chunk at most twice a second, within 10 ms a frame (one chunk at least); the other chunks wait stale and hidden. Toggling on marks all stale, so the
  chunks in view draw first and the rest as the camera reaches them. Claims, cairns and wood are looked up per tile of the chunk (no loop over all claims).
- The chunk size is that of level 0 of `chunks.js` (`LV[0]`); the overlay needs no canvas, only a grid of the same units.

## Validation notes
- `npm test`: 111 scripts passed (4 targets out, none of them near the page); `tests/browser.js`, `browser-mobile.js`, `smoke.js` run singly: green.
  No sim change: golden and counters untouched.
- Screenshots `docs/img/vb045-{before,after}-{island,close1}.jpg` (small map, populous seed 7, 3 years, overlay on), compared by pixel difference
  (PIL): island 21 pixels of 1.3 M differ by more than 24/255, close-up 366 (0.03 %), all in clouds and moving pawns; the overlay looks the same by eye.
- Cost, medium populous seed 7 after 3 years (844 living, 121 claims), headless Chromium on software rendering (about 1 frame a second, 4 shared
  cores; timed in the page with `performance.now` around `refreshLand`; one tile of the first village marked dirty with the overlay on; a few samples,
  noisy because the throttle runs on page time, which the slow frames stretch): before, a whole redraw: village view 37.9, 23.9, 9.3 ms; island view 9.7,
  19.4, 15.3 ms. After, a chunk redraw: village view 2.9 ms, island view 0.9 ms (other calls are an idle check of 0.1 to 0.2 ms). Frame time itself (rAF)
  is dominated by the software renderer (600 to 900 ms either way) and shows no difference. A first drawing at the island view costs 3 frames of 70 ms
  mean (up to 130 ms) after, against one whole-map stall before. The runs were not during a moving round at game speed: the slow frames let the game
  advance only a few days in 20 s, so there was no natural dirt in the before run; hence the marked tile. The large map was not tried (generation too slow).

## Orchestrator's decisions for Kim to overturn

- Draw order across chunks differs from the single object: a chunk's fills may overlay a neighbour's outline stroke where they meet (strokes extend
  half their width past the hex); not visible in the screenshots.
- Redraw throttle kept at the old 0.5 s of page time, with a 10 ms draw budget a frame.
