# VB-289: The suitor's visit: a match beyond a day's walk needs a winter journey first
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-287, VB-288
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Inheritance, expansion and other bodies (Between villages: The suitor's visit, Provisions and the season). QUESTIONS.md, VB-281, question 1
Goal: After VB-288 the round prefers near women but may still choose one two days' walk away whenever no nearer woman is free: the round's lowest accepted score (`MARRIAGE.minScore`, -1.5) lets any pair within about 8 years of the preferred age gap through, so the distance term ranks but does not cost. Kim decided on 10 November that preparing for a long trip is not cheap. Proposed (`QUESTIONS.md`, "VB-281", question 1): a woman of a village beyond a day's walk (more than 12 hours) can be chosen only by a man who has visited her village since the last round; the visit is a journey there and back in winter, the farm's slack season, at his household's cost in food and days.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The pool narrows: a man's round offers him the women of villages within a day's walk of his (`hoursBetween` at most `TRAVEL.dayWalk`, 12 h) and of the village he visited since the last round. VB-281 measured 52 pairs of villages within 12 h at `medium-ref` (each village has a median of 4 such neighbours; 3 villages have none) and 38 at `small-y40` (median 3; none without).
- [ ] Who sets out: at his village's round, an unmarried man of 20 or more who was in the pool (single, not retired, fed) and found no woman he could ask (none allowed, or none reaching `minScore`), whose home has the means to marry (`affords`) and the visit's food above them, is given a visit to the nearest village beyond a day's walk and within reach that holds a free woman he could marry (the round's rules: age, kin limit; the score with its noise at the mean reaching `minScore`). One visit a man a year. The round stores it as a journey (VB-287) with `why: 'visit'`, `village` and a way back, due at 6:00 of winter day 1 + (his household id mod 6), so departures spread over six days (Rules for cost, 5).
- [ ] The journey there and back: he walks to the other village's green, stays one day (he is its guest: no job, he may talk with its people), and walks home. Days away: `2 × ceil(h / 12) + 1`, so 5 days for 13 to 24 hours, 9 for 41 hours (the median pair at `medium-ref`), 13 for 67. Provisions: one ration a day away, from his household's food above its seed, at the start (VB-287's provisions). His household does without his hands for those days. On arrival he records the visit: `p.visited = {village, year}` (one field a pawn, overwritten by the next visit, cleared at marriage or death), which the round reads.
- [ ] Not made: in a household that would fall below its seed or its means by the visit's food; when the man marries, dies or takes a farm before the due day (the journey is dropped). The log says why a man does not set out, once a man a year (the put-off line's pattern).
- [ ] Events: `visited` in his record when he arrives at the other village (notable: kept, as `offered` is), naming the village; the setting out and the return are VB-287's `setOut` and `arrived`.
- [ ] Behaviour changes, so report before and after (targets with residuals), as VB-288 does: the demography (populous small, 30 years, seeds 1 to 12), marriages by walking hours, customs at seeds 7 and 42, households with an unmarried adult over 30 at year 30. And visits a year, days away and rations spent, at `medium-ref`, `small-y40` and the populous runs; the share of visits that end in a marriage at the next round. The golden file and `tests/counters.json` re-recorded, the changes listed.
- [ ] Tests (in `tests/journeys.js`): a man with no woman within a day's walk sets out on his winter day, walks there, stays a day, walks home, and at the next round can choose a woman of that village and of no other village beyond a day's walk; a household short of food keeps him home and logs it; a save during the visit plays as the unbroken run.
- [ ] Cost (Rules for cost, 9): visits a year are at most the round's unmatched men with the means: at `medium-ref`, 27 unmarried men of 20 or more at the fixture, of whom 1 had no free woman of 18 to 45 within 12 h and 2 none within 6 h. Each visit is two journeys' legs (VB-287), one route search each way. The narrower pool lowers `roundPairs` again. Nothing is polled: the visits are due entries.
