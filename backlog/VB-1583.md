# VB-1583: A site's water is weighed by the site search and shown in the leader's choice
Status: done (2026-11-25)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Water by the site); backlog/VB-1543.md; design.md, Moving to new land (The new site; Settling in steps)
Goal: the player and the leader see what a site's water will cost each day before the house is placed
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The site search weighs a site's water (design.md, Goods and the farming year, Water by the site, The site's access): the daily fetch in hours for the party's people, the least of the walk to fresh water (`w.freshWalk`), the walk to the well of the village the site would join, and a well of its own where the ground has one (its hours at the band's middle spread over `WELL.payYears`); the score loses `SETTLE.perFetch` (2.4) a daily hour. No site is refused for its water.
- [ ] The played leader's choice among the sites shown (`SETTLE.shown`) carries each site's water as the leader's estimate: the source and the walk there and back, the daily fetch in hours and as a share of one hand's day, and the well's band with its range of hours. The solo scenario's siting (VB-1544) shows the same. (Unticked: the estimate is built and carried, `sim/settle` `sites` and `Sim.siteWater`, worded by `siteWaterWords` of `src/page/water.js`, and the tile card shows the day's fetch; the choice itself is VB-788's, which waits for VB-787, and the solo siting is VB-1544's, neither built: VB-1595 shows it there.)
- [x] A check on a small constructed state: of two sites alike but for their water the search ranks the nearer first; a site with no source and no well is still offered when it is the only one.
- [x] Measured once, before and after (populous and founders, small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): where new farms are founded (the walk to water at founding, by quartile; the share at the waterside), the farms founded a year, and the dry days of new households in their first two years.
- [x] Cost, named before building: an array read a candidate (at most `SETTLE.candidates`, 40, a searching village a round) and one route for a candidate that would join a village; a round, not a day.
