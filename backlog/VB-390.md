# VB-390: The Culture pane replaces the customs drawer
Status: done (2026-11-12)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: Kim, session of 12 November 2026 (customs pane review)
Goal: With the customs on the village card, the dock's pane tells of the culture island-wide; the old drawer, its select and its comparison table go.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The dock's button and its pane are named Culture (the key stays C); the pane is titled by the culture ("Nordic culture") and says its villages, people and centre. Where the island has several cultures, chips choose one; it opens on the culture of the played pawn's village, or of the village selected.
- [x] Three tabs. Villages (first): a row a village, those set to change, contested or with ways of their own first; a row says "Keeps the custom throughout" or names each custom it holds its own way, with the custom's name, and what is set to change or contested; a click opens that village's card at its Customs tab. The custom: the culture's own customs as the charter of VB-389, a phrase tinted where some village has changed it; a phrase opens the options with the villages that hold each, as chips that open the village. Meetings: the meeting log by year, years in which nothing changed folded into one line.
- [x] The Customs tab of a village's card (VB-389) names its culture as a link that opens this pane at that culture.
- [x] The old customs drawer goes: its village select, cards, support bars and comparison table, their styles, and `customVillage`, `customsStamp`, `comparisonOpen`; the Debug drawer is unchanged.
- [x] A long list of villages that keep the custom throughout folds ("7 villages keep the custom throughout") once there are more than 6 of them.
- [x] Tests: `tests/smoke.js`, `tests/browser.js` and `tests/browser-mobile.js` updated from the old drawer's checks to the pane's (tabs, rows, a phrase opened, the links both ways, the sheet fits on a phone); `npm run verify`'s hard checks pass; `index.html` rebuilt.
