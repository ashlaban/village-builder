# VB-390: A new household with a meadow wants a cow
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: animals
Phase: implement
Design ref: Animals (Herds). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-284, question 5; docs/bench/food-balance.md
Goal: Households without cattle are 69% of household-seasons and all of the growth, and make 0.71 of their need on 3 fields (with cattle 1.33); new farms hold 0.9 meadows against 5.9. The animals' yields stay; the herd spreads to new households.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A household of 2 or more hands that holds a meadow and no cattle has a cow as a want of its standing plan; it is met from kin's herd at a marriage (a heifer as part of the start in life, where the old household's herd can spare one above its own plan) or by a deal, as cattle change hands today. `HERD.milk` and `SOIL.perLoad` are unchanged.
- [ ] No new state beyond the plan's want; the cost is named before building (households without cattle, at their planning day) and measured on a late save.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 30 years; founders small at four seeds): the share of household-years without cattle after year 16 (0.75 today), herds, hay and meadows taken, manure on new farms' fields, deals in cattle, hungry household-years, and growth by `tools/bands.js`. Whether the old households that give are worse off.
