# VB-390: A new household with a meadow wants a cow
Status: done (2026-11-13)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: animals
Phase: implement
Design ref: Animals (Herds). QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-284, question 5; docs/bench/food-balance.md
Goal: Households without cattle are 69% of household-seasons and all of the growth, and make 0.71 of their need on 3 fields (with cattle 1.33); new farms hold 0.9 meadows against 5.9. The animals' yields stay; the herd spreads to new households.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A household of 2 or more hands that holds a meadow and no cattle has a cow as a want of its standing plan; it is met from kin's herd at a marriage (a heifer as part of the start in life, where the old household's herd can spare one above its own plan) or by a deal, as cattle change hands today. `HERD.milk` and `SOIL.perLoad` are unchanged.
- [x] No new state beyond the plan's want; the cost is named before building (households without cattle, at their planning day) and measured on a late save.
- [x] Numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42, 30 years; founders small at four seeds): the share of household-years without cattle after year 16 (0.75 today), herds, hay and meadows taken, manure on new farms' fields, deals in cattle, hungry household-years, and growth by `tools/bands.js`. Whether the old households that give are worse off.

- 13 Nov 2026, decisions (Deciding without Kim, 4: extending a system; the cattle change hands as they do today):
  - The want is `plan.want` of the household's standing plan, `{what: 'cow', from, kind, by}`, owned by `sim/exchange` (deals between parties)
    as `plan.reserved` is `sim/stores'`; made or dropped at each review (`reviewWant`, called by `sim/plans`' `reviewed`) by `cowWanted`:
    `HERD.want.hands` (2) working hands (14 to 59, not retired, as `fieldsWorkable` counts them), a meadow, no cattle.
  - "A deal, as cattle change hands today": a deal in this game is still the gift booked as goodwill (`sim/exchange`, "the gift for now";
    payment in goods waits for the fair, VB-302). On its planning day an open want asks the households of its village, kin first
    (`kinHouseholds`, moved from `sim/help` to `sim/exchange`, which `sim/help` now reads), for a heifer, else a cow, that the giver spares by
    `giveAnimal`'s rule (it keeps a cow, so its own want, and its winter's hay) and whose allowance toward the household (the gift's rule)
    covers the animal's customary worth (`Culture.norm('worth')`: heifer 20, cow 40). When the animal is home its worth is booked as goodwill
    the receiver's holder owes the giver's.
  - At a marriage (`wed`, `sim/lifecycle`), the old household of the spouse who moved in, in the same village, gives a heifer it spares as a
    start in life, not booked (as a new household's start at the cottage, VB-140).
  - No teleportation (Kim, 13 Nov): nothing moves at the deal. A job kind `lead` (record in `sim/exchange`, type husbandry, 0.5 h, urgency
    `other`) is posted at the receiver's farm while the want has a giver and the herds are out of the byre (an animal led home in winter would
    come to a barn without its hay); a member walks to the giver's byre and, back home, the animal moves (`payInAnimals`, now with the kinds it
    may pay in) with the hay above the giver's winter need. A giver that can no longer spare it leaves the want open. The page does not draw
    the led cow yet (carry `cow` has no sprite; follow-up below).
  - Same village only, for the deal and the start in life: the lead job is a walk within the work day.
