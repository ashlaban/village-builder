Noted by VB-777, 16 November 2026.

Result (tools/contact.js, populous small, seeds 7, 11, 13, 40 years; 2151 village-years of 5 adults or more): the top fifth by people met (8.7 or more
distinct, mean 9.8; the rest 5.9) drifts 0.0079 a key a year, the rest 0.0096: the busier villages drift slower, not faster (correlation -0.28; each seed
alike). The mean of more people moves less, so contact alone shows no extra speed here. A gathering's host (405 village-years, 7.6 met against 6.4) drifts 0.0082
against 0.0095 for the others: it does not stand apart. Hence the question to Kim, below; target-v2 4.4 is untouched. The tool steps the clock by the hour.
