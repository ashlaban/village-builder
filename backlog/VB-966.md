# VB-966: The village store's outlook, shown as a supply on the village card
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: backlog/VB-927.md; backlog/VB-667.md; backlog/VB-666.md
Goal: The village card shows its store as VB-667's supply cells against its target
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A village's store has an outlook like a household's (`Sim.supplyOutlook`): how long its grain lasts, against its target (VB-457), by the estimate of a named pawn (the alderman, or the viewer's), kept or computed when asked, with no loop over every record.
- [x] The village card (`showVillage`) shows the store as a supply row by VB-667's cells (`suppliesHTML`), and says whose estimate it is.
- [x] `node tests/smoke.js` checks the row; a screenshot under docs/img/.

Noted by VB-927, 17 November 2026.

Implementation (17 Nov 2026):
- `Sim.villageOutlook(st, vid, viewer)` (`sim/overview`'s `villageOutlookOf`, over `sim/outlook`'s `villageOutlook`): computed when asked and stored nowhere, shaped as a household's outlook (food; fuel and hay `none`), `village: true`, no loop over records (one pass over the village's farmsteads). Grain less the seed reserved, walked a day at a time with `ECON.villageStoreLoss`; need is the households' daily need; no intake is reckoned (VB-849: what is promised is not yet there), so `own` equals `with`. `parts.target` is `HELP.storeTarget` years of need (VB-457), told in the numbers under the row ("The store's target").
- Whose: `head` is the viewer (the played pawn) or else the village's eldest holder, as the overview's warnings (`viewerOf`); the card says "By X's reckoning, today". Decision: a village never estimates (16), so the pawn named is the viewer's or the eldest holder's, who speaks for it until an alderman exists.
- The card (`showVillage`) has a Store section with `suppliesHTML(..., ['food'])`; the i opens the numbers (the numbers click redraws a village card, which draws on a timer).
- Tests: `tests/outlook.js` (the store's outlook), `tests/smoke.js` (the row, its four cells, whose estimate, the target). Screenshot: docs/img/vb966-store.png (populous tiny seed 7, year 3, Sandsta).
- Nothing in the simulation reads it; `tools/same.js` against 984607d (see the result).
