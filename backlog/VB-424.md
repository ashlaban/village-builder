# VB-424: Hides from the hunt

Design ref: Animals (Herds: wild grazers give meat and hides when hunted); QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, slice 4's question 9 ("Furs wait for roe deer (VB-139)"); design.md, Goods of place and side crafts
Goal: VB-139 brings home a roe deer's meat; its hide is left on the ground, because no good would read it. A kill also gives a hide, a stored good of the household (its line in tools/stocks.js), with a first use grounded in a need or in prestige: proposed, leather for shoes and harness, or furs bartered at the fair.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A kill (sim/deer, hunt) gives one hide through sim/stores, recorded in the ledger; its use is read by something, so tools/stocks.js does not mark it read by nothing.
- [x] A test on a constructed state.
Notes / questions:
- 13 Nov 2026: opened by VB-139, which left the hides out: a good no system reads would be a stock read by nothing.

Implementation (VB-424): a kill puts DEER.hunt.hide (1) hide through Stores.put (reason 'hunt', folded into the yearly totals as the meat is) into household.hides (owner sim/stores), a good 'hide' (class wares, keeping, worth 6 in culture's table). Its use: leather for shoes and harness; sim/deer contributes a `need` term, a household wants ceil(members * DEER.leather (0.25)) hides, and what is lacking is worth the customary worth each, so it is taken at the fair or as a gift (the first use is the proposal's second reading: bartered, and the need read by deals). tools/stocks.js has the line 'hides'. Test: tests/deer.js (hide at the kill, ledger, need term at none and enough), tests/goods.js, invariants for the field and ledger.
Decision: no consuming job (shoes, harness wear) yet; the hide's use is a need that fair deals read, so the stock is read; a wearing process is left to a later task (note VB-911). Behaviour numbers before and after not taken (the hide changes no hunger path unless fair deals move it): owed to the nightly run.
