Touches: sim/levies (substitute, name, commute, breach, release, transfer); sim/obligations (transfer, forgive, lapse); sim/controller; tests/levies.js
Goal: A household under any levier answers a hard day by the same judgement, and the difference between leviers is in their terms
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Verbs `substitute`, `name` (another day), `ask` with the bases `release` and `transfer` (answered by the levier's agent by its need and opinion), `commute` (goods for the day at the terms' rate, from the household's store through sim/stores with a reason) and `breach` on the levied side; `release` and `transfer` on the levier's (the alderman lends a day of his keep to a household in need); each allowed only where the terms allow it, its check saying why not.
- [x] The default author tries them in that order; breach is the head's judgement by its own estimate: what keeping the day costs (`K`: the windowed work it displaces, in rations, reckoned as the head's demand reckons work at risk, weighed by `OUTER.hunger` (2) below the floor) against what this levier does (`B`: the day's worth as goodwill weighed by `OUTER.breach` (2), and for a debt the day's worth again, since it stays owed); the ladder starts when the one named is dead, ill or away or `K` passes the day's worth (`OUTER.worth`), and breach is `K > B` (design.md, Outer levies, The terms, the ladder and the breach). An ask for release is granted when `K × (1 + o) > W + G` (the agent's opinion, the day's worth to the levier, the goodwill already owed), a grant booked as the agent's goodwill; an agent holding an ask for hands may `transfer` instead. On its next planning day the agent lets a breach stand or `forgive`s it, by the same rule. For now every levier's `onBreach` is goodwill only (a day's worth owed to the levier's agent, opinion following; design.md, What a levier does; Kim, answer 14); a fine, a case, an estate's penalty and eviction are named in the terms and not built. No threshold of its own.
- [ ] A levied adult dead or ill on its day is substituted, or the ladder tried, at once; a levy about to lapse unworked is the levier's to carry over (where `carry`) or let lapse; a debt's days unworked within their year (a breached day stays owed) go to the meeting as Remedies says: under "labour, then land" a field passes for a debt left of half a field's worth, else what remains is laid as a new year's `debt` levy; a day commuted pays the debt. `sim/days`' `sendsNone` (no hand while below the floor or the harvest waits) gives way to this judgement.
