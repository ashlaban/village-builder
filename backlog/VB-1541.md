# VB-1541: A dry day's health loss is named for what it is: thirst, not a damp house, nor starvation at death
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Water); backlog/VB-331.md
Goal: a player who reads why health fell learns that no water was fetched
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The day's health record carries the water's cost under a name of its own (today `damp`, sim/hunger: `dry > 0 ? HEALTH.dry * frail`), and the played pawn's sheet words it as no water fetched (sim/player CAUSE_WORDS), not "a damp house".
- [ ] A death whose health was lost mostly to dry days is not recorded or shown as starvation: the cause named is the one that took the most health over the days kept, or the water is named beside it.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state (tests/tiny.js): a household with food and no water for the days kept reads the water as the cause on the sheet and at death.
- [ ] The mood row's sign and the sheet's words name the same cause.

## Notes
- Found by the solo playtest of 22 November (seed 42, small): health 16, hunger 8, "a damp house (-5)" every five days; the death toast read "starved to death".
- Words only: no rule or number changes. The role of water itself is under discussion with Kim (access by the farm's site, a well as a project); this task stands whichever way that goes, since a dry day keeps a cost.
