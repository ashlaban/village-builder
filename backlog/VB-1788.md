Goal: A head can back a member's craft or set aside a portion for it as plans of the household's, and a member can buy hours of its levy with goods
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The kind `trade` (a household aim for a member who holds a `craft` aim): its means a standing grant of the member's levy (VB-1763) and material given into the member's store; formed by the head's default author at its review and when the member asks a lighter levy citing its aim (aims.md 3.5); done when the member's craft aim is, and its grant ends with it.
- [ ] The kind `portion`: goods of `n` worth given from the household's store into the member's a season at a time, from what the targets leave, by `by` (the member's foreseen leaving); formed for a member of age who will leave (a daughter under the residence custom "the husband's", a son for service or new land); counted in the means to marry and a party's provisions through the store (VB-1782).
- [ ] The ask `lighter` (and leave) may carry goods from the member's store, at 3 rations for 8 hours (`COMMUTE.day`, the outer levies' rate); the head answers by its judgement (the goods' worth to it against the hours its season needs from the member); a yes moves the goods with a reason of their own and lowers the levy; a no is a memory.
- [ ] A check on a constructed household: a son with grain in his store buys 16 winter hours and is refused them at the harvest; a head whose son holds a craft aim forms `trade`, and the son's own hours rise by the grant.
- [ ] Reported (populous small, seeds 7, 11 and 42, 10 years): trade and portion aims by household, hours bought with goods, portions given.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
