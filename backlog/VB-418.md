# VB-418: Refactor: split sim/kinds: each domain declares its own job kinds
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: none (code structure). backlog/VB-228.md (the same for verbs); backlog/VB-359.md, backlog/VB-328.md (the kinds placed outside sim/kinds while it was full)
Goal: sim/kinds is 781 formatted lines, the fifth longest module. Most job kinds' records are declared in it; sim/dig and sim/grazing declare theirs as KIND and sim/building the cottage's stages as KINDS, registered in sim/kinds in a line each, two of the three because sim/kinds stood at the old cap of 400 lines. Decide the rule (proposed: a domain module declares its kinds beside the rules they call, and sim/kinds keeps the registry, the shared rules and the asking order, as VB-228 does for verbs), then move the kinds, a domain at a time, each move checked with tools/same.js.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The rule is written in `docs/architecture/modules.md` (Adding things: a new job kind) and `AGENTS.md`, and every kind follows it.
- [ ] Each domain's kinds moved in a commit of its own; `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` for each.
- [ ] `sim/kinds` is under 700 lines and leaves `tests/lint-baseline.json`.
- [ ] Added 15 Nov: `sim/kinds` exports no more names than before (VB-420's criterion), and the width baselines of the modules whose callbacks became private fall.
Notes / questions:
- 13 Nov 2026: opened by the pass on misplaced code after VB-413, with the user's yes. The fields', meadows', byre's and pot's rules are already exported to `sim/kinds` alone (8, 4, 5 and 3 functions that their own modules never call): the kinds' records would join the rules they name.
- 15 Nov 2026 (review of the open refactors, with Kim's yes): takes over VB-420 (split `sim/kinds` by concern), which asked for the same move from the lint's side and is closed; renamed to name `sim/kinds`, so the lint finds it as the module's split task. The rule proposed here is the general one: a domain declares what it contributes (its kinds, as its rules, goods and, with VB-228, verbs), and the central module keeps the registry and the order (`ASK`). If the field kinds take `sim/fields` over 700 lines, they go to a module of their own beside it and the PR says so; VB-434 and VB-435 wait for this task and are judged again after it.
