# VB-418: Refactor: each domain declares its own job kinds
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: none (code structure). backlog/VB-228.md (the same for verbs); backlog/VB-359.md, backlog/VB-328.md (the kinds placed outside sim/kinds while it was full)
Goal: sim/kinds is 781 formatted lines, the fifth longest module. Most job kinds' records are declared in it; sim/dig and sim/grazing declare theirs as KIND and sim/building the cottage's stages as KINDS, registered in sim/kinds in a line each, two of the three because sim/kinds stood at the old cap of 400 lines. Decide the rule (proposed: a domain module declares its kinds beside the rules they call, and sim/kinds keeps the registry, the shared rules and the asking order, as VB-228 does for verbs), then move the kinds, a domain at a time, each move checked with tools/same.js.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The rule is written in `docs/architecture/modules.md` (Adding things: a new job kind) and `AGENTS.md`, and every kind follows it.
- [ ] Each domain's kinds moved in a commit of its own; `node tools/same.js --base REF` prints `same` for each.
- [ ] `sim/kinds` is under 700 lines and leaves `tests/lint-baseline.json`.
