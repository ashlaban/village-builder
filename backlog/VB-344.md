# VB-344: Digging: clay and bog iron are dug into a household's store, and bog iron grows back
Status: done (2026-11-11)
Priority: 3
Size: M
Design ref: Goods, Goods of place and side crafts (In the ground); World, climate and travel (World and scale: Ground)
Goal: Kim, 11 Nov: build a first digging job now, before slice 6 gives the dug goods a use (VB-185's plan), with bog iron growing back as decided (QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md, VB-342, question 3). Nothing digs today: w.depositLeft never changes (VB-342, VB-343).
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] What is dug: clay and bog iron only (they show at the surface and need no facility). The hidden ores, peat, flint, amber and salt
      wait for their goods; an `ENDLESS` tile never falls.
- [x] The stores: `hh.clay` and `hh.bogOre`, in loads, state with an owner (`src/owners.json`, `@state`), an invariant, and moved only
      through a `src/sim/stores.js` operation with a good and a reason (`dig`); a save without them loads with 0. The farm card shows them
      beside the stone heap.
- [x] The job: a kind `dig` in `src/sim/kinds.js` (and a verb in `src/sim/commands.js` if a pawn or the round can choose it), `DIG.hours`
      (3) a load, one load a trip carried home as firewood is, on a deposit tile of the household's own land, its village's outland or
      the wilderness within `DIG.reach` (12) walking steps, the nearest; it lowers `w.depositLeft[tile]` by 1, and a tile at 0 keeps
      its `w.deposit` kind (it is spent, not gone). The reach search is made when the want opens and kept, not each day (rule 4).
- [x] The want: a household digs only in its slack (the urgency of upkeep-type work) and only up to `DIG.stock` (4) loads of each good,
      since nothing uses them until slice 6: heaps must not grow without use. A household with no deposit in reach never posts the job.
- [x] Bog iron grows back: a worked bog iron tile regains `DIG.regrow` (1) load a year, up to what the generator gave it, read in closed
      form from the amount and the time it was last dug (rule 3: no yearly pass). The new world fields (the tile's first amount, the time
      of its last digging) are documented with `@state`, written only by their owner, and in `WORLD` of `src/sim/saves.js` with
      `depositLeft`. Clay does not grow back.
- [x] Views: the tile panel shows the loads left as they are now (the closed form for bog iron); the Ground overlay needs no change.
- [x] Tests (`tests/dig.js`, in `npm test`): a load dug lowers the tile and raises the store through the ledger; the stock cap stops the
      want; an endless tile stays endless; bog iron regains a load a year up to its first amount and clay nothing; a save keeps all of it.
- [x] Cost named (rule 9) and numbers before and after (populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years): households that dig, loads dug by
      good, hours spent, idle work hours before and after, and that rations by source do not move (`tools/balance.js`). The golden file
      is re-recorded and its changed entries listed.

- Impact. New module `sim/dig` (material layer, after `sim/stores`): reads `w.deposit`, `w.depositLeft`, `st.owner`, `st.used` (through
  `isOutland`), the house tile, and `hh.clay`/`hh.bogOre`; writes `w.depositLeft` (its owner now), `w.depositFull`, `w.dugDay` and
  `hh.digAt` (its digging ground, a cache); its job kind's delivery calls `sim/stores` `dug` (reason `dig`), the one writer of `hh.clay`
  and `hh.bogOre`. `sim/kinds` registers the kind in one line (`KINDS.dig = VB['sim/dig'].KIND`) and asks it at the farm after the plans

  there too). The page reads `Sim.depositLeftNow` (the closed form) in the tile panel; the farm card shows the dug loads under the stone.
- Decided here, Proposed: the urgency is the house's upkeep's (`DIG.urgency` 0.5, as `MATCH.urgency.repair`, type `upkeep`), kept in
  `DIG` so `src/sim/rules.js` is untouched; one job at a time a household, of the good it holds fewer loads of; the load is carried to
  the door in a basket (`carry: 'basket'`, `anim: 'hoe'`); a load carried home when the stock is already full is not dug. Bog iron's
  regrowth counts whole years since the last digging, and a digging restarts the count (a part-year is lost: at most 4 loads are dug
  before the want closes, so it matters little). `w.depositFull` and `w.dugDay` are made at the first digging of a run (a world never
  dug has neither, and an older save loads as never dug). No verb in `sim/commands.js`: the job is matched like firewood, not chosen.
- The reach search (`hh.digAt`): made when a household is first asked with a good wanted, kept for its house, and made again only for
  a new house, a new game year with a good not found, or a tile no longer to be dug (spent, or taken by another). Owner rules:
  own land, its village's outland (not in use, not the green), or wilderness.
- Cost (rule 9): live households (about 100 on populous small) times at most one search a year of a 12-step walking reach (at most 469
  tiles; counter `digGround`, tiny probe 2.8 a pawn-day over the first 30 days, all of it the first search) plus O(1) a review (two
  comparisons, and a check of the kept tile). Delivery is O(1). No pass over tiles: regrowth is read in closed form (rule 3).

  2 households in 3 years. Clay is in reach of about a fifth to a half of households. Slice 6 may want a longer reach for bog iron, or
  the generator more bog near lakes.
