# VB-1118: What a command achieved, and a death that comes with warning
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player and what they see (What the player sees); Principles for decisions (The future is someone's estimate)
Goal: The player sees what its commands bring, and a death is not a bolt from the blue.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The played pawn's sheet says what its last act yielded (the catch, the load, the field done) and, when a level moved over the last days (health, hunger, the store), what moved it: work, food, illness, cold.
- [ ] An illness shows before it kills: the illness's course is read (`sim/health` or wherever the death roll lives), and where death by illness is a draw with no course, decide by principle 19 (a wait is the thing waited for) whether the illness gets one, a few days ailing that health shows; write the decision and its numbers (deaths by cause and age before and after, founders and populous small, 30 years).
- [ ] What kin can see of it is shown as their estimate (principle 14): "Gunnar is ailing" on the sheet of the played pawn's household, not a probability.
- [ ] A check on a small constructed state for the first criterion: a queued fishing trip ends; the sheet names the catch.
