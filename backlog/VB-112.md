# VB-112: Rivers are crossed only at fords and bridges
Status: done (2026-10-30)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: World and scale (River courses: "Crossing is at fords and, later, bridges"; Proposed); Open questions ("Whether rivers can be waded anywhere or only at fords, and when bridges come"). Kim, 30 Oct 2026 (`QUESTION_ARCHIVE.md`, "Paths, fords and village layout"): rivers block walking except at fords and bridges; the world generates a few fords along every course, brooks included; bridges need construction (VB-115); village generation respects walking, so no farm stands on a far bank it cannot reach.
Goal: Pawns walk across rivers as if they were not there (VB-111 left walking unchanged). A river should divide the land: people cross it at a ford, or later at a bridge they have built, and villages are laid out so their farms can reach the green, their fields and their wood.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] `w.crossing` (Uint8Array per tile, `@state` documented): 0 none, 1 ford, 2 bridge. `src/gen/rivers.js` places the fords with the world, from the river stream only, so every other field of `w` is unchanged.
- [x] Fords: on every reach (from a source or a confluence down to the next confluence, a lake or the sea), in stretches of `FORDS.every` (10) land river tiles, the stretch's best ford site becomes a ford; a stretch of under `FORDS.least` (3) tiles at the end of a course joins the stretch before it. A site is a land river tile with dry land on two separate sides (it joins two banks), not next to a lake or the sea; the best is the narrowest (least catchment) and flattest (least drop to the next tile), with a seeded random term of up to `FORDS.noise`.
- [x] Walking: a river tile is passable only at a ford or a bridge (water, snowfields and mountains as before VB-096: water closed). A ford costs `TERRAIN.ford` (2) to cross, a bridge 1. `passable(w, i)` in `src/sim/routes.js` is the one rule, used by the route search and by every generator and site search that measures reach: village and lone-farm sites, house sites, fields, the commons wood and woodlots, new fields and plots.
- [x] No house, field or green is placed where it cannot be reached on foot from its village's green within the search's reach; populous medium at seed 7 and 42: every farm reaches its green, fields and woodlot by a found route (no fallback), tested.
- [x] The map draws a ford as stepping stones and pale shallows across the river at the closest two zoom levels; the tile card says "Ford".
- [x] A test (`tests/rivers.js`): fords are deterministic, each joins two banks, every reach of 5 or more land tiles has one; a route between two banks takes a ford.
- [x] Re-record the golden file and list the changed entries in the PR.
