# VB-761: Workmanship: five grades on made goods, drawn by the maker's skill
Status: done (2026-11-23)
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: mill
Phase: implement
Design ref: Goods, production and animals (The metals and the stones: Workmanship; The mill and the loom: Skills); `docs/plans/VB-702.md`, section 13; `AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim (17)
Goal: A made good has a grade of workmanship (shoddy, sound, good, fine, masterwork), drawn at its making by odds that shift with the maker's skill, which scales how long it lasts, how well it works and its worth (Kim, 16 Nov; principle 17): the one task that owns grades, which every graded good reads.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Tunables `WORKMANSHIP` (`sim/rules.js`), all Proposed: the five grades (shoddy, sound, good, fine, masterwork); `life` 0.7, 1, 1.1, 1.25, 1.5; `work` 0.85, 1, 1.05, 1.12, 1.25; `odds` at three levels of skill, in hundredths: novice 60, 35, 5, 0, 0; practised 15, 45, 33, 6, 1; master 2, 23, 50, 20, 5; `hands` 0.2. Custom's worth by grade, `Culture.norm('workmanship', grade)`: 0.6, 1, 1.25, 2, 4, one table for every culture until norms learn.
- [x] The draw, in `sim/skills` (VB-360): the grade of a piece made by a pawn at a craft, one draw from a new stream `making` (`SYSTEMS`, `src/sim/rng.js`); the odds are read in a straight line between the three levels at the pawn's `level(st, p, craft)`, moved by the hands trait as `hands` times (trait less a half) of skill once slice 7's traits exist. One draw a piece (each pot of a firing). Tests: `fix` forces each grade; over many draws a practised maker's mean life and working are 1 within 0.02, and the shares at 0, 0.5 and 1 are the table's.
- [x] The registry (`sim/stores`, or VB-416's module): a good declares that it is graded and which of its kinds' properties `life` scales (years, a share broken a year, hours of wear) and which `work` scales (the effect its use reads, measured from having none); compiled at load into one frozen row a good, kind and grade, so a reader allocates nothing. The grade is not part of a good's id; the `worth` rule takes the grade in its context and multiplies custom's worth of the good and kind by custom's multiplier for the grade.
- [ ] State, plain data, no record a piece and no maker's name: a graded good's count of a kind is five counts, `{[kind]: [n, n, n, n, n]}`; a durable's record is `{kind, grade, made}`. `put` names the grade; a take names a cell (kind and grade), or "least" (any cell, least customary worth first, in an order compiled for each good when custom's table is set, never sorted at the take), or "best" (for the household's own use: best by what the use reads, then by worth). The ledger and the yearly totals book the good only, never its kind or grade (their key is year, reason and good: `fold` in `sim/stores`). `@state`, invariants (five whole counts, none negative; a grade 0 to 4), `tools/stocks.js`, saves (an old save's pieces are sound).
- [x] The graded goods that exist when this lands are graded here, each with numbers before and after: the quern (`household.quern` gains its grade: grinding speed by `work`, its 20 years by `life`; hewn with the skill stonework), and, if built, pots (VB-366: the share broken a year divided by `life`), cloth and dress (VB-362, VB-363: a length's grade drawn at the weaving, `pawn.dress` carries it, `DRESS.years` times `life`). Goods built later declare themselves (the plan's 13.3 lists them). Not graded, by the plan's D20 to D22: materials, food and ale, the thing of worth, and what is built.
- [ ] The head's rules (the plan's 13.5), each a verb's choice any pawn has: own use takes the best, and a piece in use is used to its end; what is owed is paid in the least; a gift for standing is given in the best; dress is given out by rank, the holder and spouse first. The making is taken by the household's most skilled hand at the craft among those whose role allows it. `expectedWorth(st, p, craft)`, the mean of the odds' row at a pawn's skill in closed form, is offered to VB-698 and VB-699 as the head's estimate (principle 14) of what its own hand's piece will be worth.
- [x] Cost (rule 9; the plan's 13.7): nothing in a step; a grade is one number on records that exist and five counts for a kind held. Reported: `node tests/counters.js` unchanged but for intended lines; save size at the end of the runs, before and after; the bytes of a household's ledger and totals unchanged.
- [x] Numbers, populous small, seeds 7 and 42, 15 years, and founders small seed 7: pieces made by good, grade and the maker's skill (in quarters of skill); the share of each grade among pieces held at the end; the mean life and working of what households hold (a start of novices is expected near 0.83 and 0.91: report it, and a `tuning` row if the odds or multipliers should move); households with no working quern, the share of grain eaten as flour, hungry household-days, before and after. Up to five short runs for the tunables, each written down.
- [x] VB-366, VB-362, VB-363, VB-743, VB-744, VB-739, VB-740, VB-745, VB-093, VB-762 and VB-763 are told, in the task file and as inbox notes, what they read from this.
- [x] The numbers that move are reported before and after at the seeds and maps named (Working rules); `npm run verify` passes its hard checks.
