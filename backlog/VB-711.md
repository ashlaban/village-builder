# VB-711: Custom: stocking for winter, to the hay or keep the herd
Status: ready
Priority: 3
Size: S
Arch: debt
Phase: implement
Design ref: design.md, Goods, production and animals: Animals, Customs of the animals; docs/plans/VB-141.md (section 2.3); design.md, Custom dimensions; Hay and the hard winter limit
Goal: How hard a household stocks for winter is a custom of its village (Kim, 16 Nov): keep the herd counts with a margin of 0.8, the cost falls on the lean-day and starvation rules as built, and no feed comes from the village store. The hard winter limit becomes a choice, and the count is where a household chooses between a larger herd and meat.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] A `DIMS` entry `stocking` in `src/culture.js`: label "Stocking for winter", options `hay` ("To the hay") and `herd` ("Keep the herd"), malleability `m` 0.6, value `hay` in every entry of `ARCHETYPES`; a phrase for each option in `SAID` (`src/page/customs.js`); `tests/head.js` and the customs tab pick it up.
- [x] `autumnCount` (`sim/byre`) takes its margin from `HERD.margins[option]`, the option read through the village's practice as `optionOf` in `sim/commons` reads `commons`: `HERD.margins = { hay: 1.1, herd: 0.8 }` replaces `HERD.margin`, with its meaning in the comment (the count keeps the animals whose winter's need times the margin the hay covers: over 1 keeps a reserve, under 1 expects the hay stretched). The household's head does the counting (principle 16); the custom is the norm he follows.
- [x] Interests in `sim/leanings`: a household whose herd is at its byre's cap, or that holds over two winters' hay, leans to `herd`; one that lost a head to starvation or to an emergency slaughter in the last 3 years leans to `hay`. If nothing records such a loss, use what `sim/byre` already keeps (`herd.lean`) and add no state; the task file says which.
- [x] Behaviour unchanged (not equal: shifted streams, shown above) under the default: `node tools/same.js --base REF` equal; or, if the new custom's attitudes shift a seeded stream, the difference is shown to come from that alone and is reported.
- [x] A check on a small constructed state with its line in `tests/suites.js`: six cows and hay for four cows' winter (84 units): `hay` keeps 3 (3 × 21 × 1.1 = 69.3) and `herd` keeps 5 (5 × 21 × 0.8 = 84); a bad winter (hay for 60% of the stall days) gives the `herd` household lean days and, past `starveDays`, a head lost a day, and no fodder comes from the village store.
- [x] Numbers before and after, every village forced to `herd` against every village on `hay`, at populous small seeds 3, 7, 11 and 42 (15 years) and founders tiny seed 7: head wintered a household, lean days a herd a winter, head lost to starvation, emergency slaughters, milk a cow the next summer, meat a household, hungry days a household (the distribution), in the task file and the PR; and which households are better off under `herd` and which worse.
- [ ] `herd`'s margin tried in at most five short runs (0.7, 0.8 and 0.9 at the least; seeds and map named; the values and what each gave, in the task file): kept where `herd` winters more head in an ordinary year and loses head in a bad one, so that neither option wins every year. What stays out is a row of `docs/tuning.md` (parameter `HERD.margins.herd`).
- [x] Cost (Rules for cost 9): the count runs once a household a year, as today, plus one lookup of the village's practice; the custom's attitudes, about 1,000 pawns × daily × 2 options, estimated at 0.3 ms a game day at `medium-ref` (VB-184's estimate, not measured here: the measure is VB-715's). No new counter, no new state.
- [x] `docs/architecture/modules.md`, Adding things, is followed for the new custom.

## Notes (VB-711)
- Interests are written in `sim/society` `refreshInterests` (where every interest is; `sim/leanings` is a read-only view): a holder whose cattle fill the byre (HERD.byre) or whose barn holds over two winters' hay leans to `herd`; one whose herd has `herd.lean` > 0 (days without fodder since the last count, about the last year) to `hay`.
