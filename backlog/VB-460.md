# VB-460: The good-year gift: a holder with plenty carries a load to kin or a friend who is short
Status: done (2026-11-18)
Priority: 2
Size: S
Design ref: design.md, Exchange, goodwill and obligations (The gift: The good-year gift, Allowance); backlog/VB-407.md
Goal: Help today only answers an ask from a hungry household. Kim, 13 November: a household may give in a good year without being asked, kin first, so that generosity is a strategy (VB-407's plan).
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] On a holder's first planning day after its harvest, if its household is above the feast's threshold (`FEAST.above`, VB-458): it looks among the households it has a tie to (kin first, then by the tie's strength), within a day's walk and by land, for the first that holds under `GIFT.shortBelow` (1) year of its need above its seed, and gives `min(allowance, TRAVEL.load, what brings the receiver to a year)`; under `GIFT.least` (10 rations, a sack) no gift is made. One gift a household a year.
- [ ] The gift is a verb of `src/sim/commands.js` (`issue(st, {actor, verb: 'giveUnasked', ...})`), which returns why not (no one short, allowance under a sack, no carrier, no route); it is the player's gift's command where that fits, not a second one.
- [ ] Nothing teleports: the grain is reserved in the giver's store and carried by one of the giver's own on a journey, as far kin's help is (`carrierOf`, VB-291); it changes owner on arrival, and goodwill is booked then, by the gift's rule. If VB-461 has landed, its fetching rule is used in place of this one where the two differ.
- [ ] An event `gift` in the log, with giver, receiver and rations; the two panels show the balance.
- [ ] A test on a constructed state: a holder with a reserve of 40 days and little above the threshold gives nothing (the allowance is under a sack); one with no reserve gives a load; kin is chosen before a stronger tie that is not kin; nobody short, nobody given; the grain is on the carrier's back between the two farms and in neither store.
- [ ] Numbers before and after, as VB-458's seeds and years: gifts a village-year, rations given, the share of holders that give in a year by their reserve (a description, no target), hungry household-days, households helped by an ask (does the unasked gift come before the ask?), goodwill outstanding. Cost (rule 9): one pass over a holder's ties (at most 32) on one planning day a year.
