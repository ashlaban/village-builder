# VB-192: Work counters in `VB.count`, asserted at tiny scale
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: S
Arch: yardstick
Phase: implement
Design ref: none (measurement). `docs/roadmap.md`, section 6 (work counters; how counters stay out of the state); `CLAUDE.md`, Rules for cost, rule 10 (Decided, Kim, 3 November).
Goal: Timing is noisy and needs long runs. A count of the work a loop does is exact, needs no reference machine and never flakes. Count the work of the loops that grow with history or with pairs, outside `st`, and assert the counts at tiny scale in `npm test`, so a change that makes a loop do more work fails when it lands, not when the late game freezes. Every later check of slice 0 reads these counters.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `src/vb.js` creates `VB.count`, a plain object of numbers outside `st`, and `VB.COUNTERS`, the declared names with one line of meaning each; `VB.resetCounts()` sets them to 0. Counters are never saved and never read by the simulation (rule 10).
- [ ] The first counters, one increment where the work happens; in an inner loop a local tally is added once per call, not once per pass:
  - `routeSearch` (A* searches, `findRoute`), `routeExpand` (tiles taken off its heap), `routeHit` (routes served from `st.pathCache`), `routeRefresh` (cached routes `refreshRoutes` tests);
  - `talkWeighed` (partners weighed in `talkHour`), `contactPairs` (pairs compared in `noteContacts`);
  - `walkStep` (agents the 6-minute step walks, `advance` in `sim/clock`), `walkDaily` (pawn records walked by `dailyLife`'s loops and by `Culture.dailyStep`), `walkTies` (ties decayed in `dailyLife`);
  - `roundPairs` (pairs of a man and a woman the moving round weighs), `roundRank` (heir rankings computed during the round: `heirRanking` calls inside `movingRound`).
- [ ] The lint (`tests/lint.js`): in the modules, `VB.count` appears only as `VB.count.<name> += <expr>` or `VB.count.<name>++` with a declared name. Anything else fails with its line: a read, a branch on a counter, an undeclared name. This is the one allowance for state outside `st`, and `tests/lintrules.js` plants a read and sees it fail.
- [ ] `tests/counters.js` in `npm test`, under 3 s: a tiny world (`tests/lib/tiny.js`) of two villages for 30 game days, then one moving round. Each counter per living pawn-day is checked against `tests/counters.json` and fails outside ±10% of the recorded value. `--update` re-records it: a change that moves a counter on purpose re-records it and lists the counters in its PR, as for the golden file (add the rule to `BACKLOG.md`'s golden rule).
- [ ] Counting costs at most 2% of a game day: `tests/perf.js` with the increments and with them commented out, reported in the PR.
- [ ] `tools/sim.js --report counts` prints each counter per game day and per living pawn-day for the run (and with `--json`).
- [ ] `tests/golden.json` unchanged: counting changes nothing in `st`.
- [ ] `CLAUDE.md`: a line in Commands for `tests/counters.js` and `--report counts`; a line in Conventions that a new loop over a growing collection, or over pairs, adds a counter.
- [ ] When done: sets VB-088 `ready` if VB-193 is done too.
Cost: one addition per call of each counted function; 3 s in `npm test`, in parallel with the other scripts.
