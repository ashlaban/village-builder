# VB-519: Traits: twelve numbers a pawn, passed on at birth and set at 15, and a culture's values
Status: blocked

Design ref: design.md, People, households and family records (Traits, talk and stories: The twelve traits; How a trait is passed on); Customs and culture (A culture's values); docs/architecture/target-v2.md, section 4.4 (Persons); backlog/VB-186.md
Goal: The twelve traits Kim decided on 3 November replace the five uniform stand-ins: values from those who raised a child and from its culture, temperament and aptitudes partly from its parents by birth, a hungry childhood paid for in body and wits. Each culture holds the mean of its people's values, read from them and never set. No rule reads a new trait yet: this task is the state, the passing on, the labels and the door.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `sim/traits` (made by VB-254 for its five stand-ins) owns `pawn.traits`: twelve numbers in 0.02 to 0.98, in the order of `TRAITS.names` (generosity, piety, ambition, honour; sociability, temper, diligence, openness; body, hands, husbandry, wits), read only through `Traits.of(st, pawn, name)`. The five stand-ins and `pawn.diligence` become these (one home; an old save keeps the values it has and draws the rest). `@state`, `src/owners.json`, invariants in `tests/lib/invariants.js` (twelve finite numbers within the bounds, for the living and the dead), the save.
- [ ] One equation passes every trait on (`design.md`, How a trait is passed on): `mean + b × (the two parents' mean − mean) + chance`, the chance normal with spread `TRAITS.sd × √(1 − b²/2)`, drawn from the stream `traits`. `TRAITS` in `src/sim/rules.js`, each with its meaning: `sd` 0.15, `b` 0.45 (temperament), 0.5 (aptitudes), 0.6 (values), `setAge` 15, `lean` { body: 0.2, wits: 0.1, days: 96 }.
- [ ] At birth: temperament and aptitudes from the father and mother by birth around 0.5, for good; values from them around the culture's mean, as a child's value. A parent not on record (a founder's) counts as the mean.
- [ ] At 15, once, where the pawn's age already rolls over (no new pass over pawns): the values are drawn again from those who raised it, the holder and the mistress of the household it lives in (one of them alone if there is one; the culture's mean if none), around its culture's mean; and a hungry childhood takes from body and wits: `lean.body` and `lean.wits` times min(1, the child's hungry days / `lean.days`). The hungry days are one number a child, `pawn.leanDays`, counted where its household's hunger is already written for its members (hunger of 0.5 or more), and dropped at 15.
- [ ] A culture's values (`design.md`, Customs and culture, A culture's values): `culture.values`, four means, 0.5 but honour 0.6 for Old Nordic; founders and placed pawns draw from it with spread `sd`. At the culture's yearly reckoning it becomes the mean of its living adults' values, in the walk the reckoning already makes; a culture that splits takes its own adults' mean at once. `sim/saves.js` keeps it; an old save starts at the archetype's.
- [ ] Labels: `Traits.labelsOf(st, pawn)` gives the words of `design.md`'s table for traits under 0.345 or over 0.655 (`TRAITS.label`); the pawn panel shows them and no number. The page reads through the `Sim` API. A screenshot under `docs/img/`.
- [ ] Neutral on average: no rule reads a new trait in this task (VB-520 does), so `node tools/same.js --base REF` differs only by the draws of the stream `traits`; say which leaves differ. Where VB-254's stand-ins were uniform and are now normal, report its numbers (hungry household-days by the head's skill in thirds, populous small, 30 years, seeds 3, 7, 11, 42) before and after.
- [ ] Tests on a constructed state (`tests/tiny.js`, `tests/lib/probe.js`), with `fix` on the stream: a child of two high parents lies between them and the mean; the spread of 2,000 children of random pairs is within 0.14 to 0.16 for each group; a child raised in another house takes its values from that house and its temperament from its birth parents; a hungry childhood lowers body and wits and nothing else; a split culture's mean is its members'.
- [ ] Cost (rule 9): written at birth and at 15, read as an index; no daily or yearly pass but the reckoning's own walk. Twelve numbers a pawn in the save: name the bytes against the save's line (report the JSON's size on a 30-year populous small save before and after; the bench is the night's).
