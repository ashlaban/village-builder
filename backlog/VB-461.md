# VB-461: Help is carried: grain from near kin and from the village store does not arrive at once
Status: done (2026-11-16)
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: physical
Phase: implement
Design ref: AGENTS.md, Deciding without Kim (the hard rule); design.md, Goods and the farming year (Help in hunger); backlog/VB-291.md
Goal: `src/sim/help.js` (the season's asks, about line 92): kin within `TRAVEL.dayWalk` give at once and so does the village store, so grain moves between farmsteads with no one carrying it; only far kin send a carrier (VB-291). By the hard rule a gift changes owner where it lies and waits there until someone fetches it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Help from kin within a day's walk: the grain is set aside in the giver's store as the receiver's (ownership moves at once, as rights do) and a member of the hungry household fetches it, up to `TRAVEL.load` a journey, or the giver's carrier brings it, whichever VB-291's carrier rule gives; it is eaten only once it has arrived. The same for a gift of the village store, fetched from where the store stands.
- [ ] Households of one farmstead are exempt (a farmstead is one place).
- [ ] The yearly filling of the village store is checked the same way (`fillStores`): if a household's quarter arrives with no journey, it is carried at the moving round, or a second task of this arch is opened for it and named here.
- [ ] `tests/lib/invariants.js`: grain in transit is on a pawn; a count of grain moved between farmsteads with no carrier stays at 0.
- [ ] Numbers before and after (the hard rule asks for them, since goods that take time to arrive change hunger): populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 and founders small seeds 7, 42, 30 years: hungry household-days, the days between an ask and the meal, journeys a year for help, growth.
- [ ] Cost (rule 9): asks a season (2 to 4 households a village-year are helped today) × one journey each; no new loop.
