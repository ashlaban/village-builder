# VB-461: Help is carried: grain from near kin and from the village store does not arrive at once
Status: done (2026-11-16)
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: physical
Phase: implement
Design ref: AGENTS.md, Deciding without Kim (the hard rule); design.md, Goods and the farming year (Help in hunger); backlog/VB-291.md
Goal: `src/sim/help.js` (the season's asks, about line 92): kin within `TRAVEL.dayWalk` give at once and so does the village store, so grain moves between farmsteads with no one carrying it; only far kin send a carrier (VB-291). By the hard rule a gift changes owner where it lies and waits there until someone fetches it.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Help from kin within a day's walk: the grain is set aside in the giver's store as the receiver's (ownership moves at once, as rights do) and a member of the hungry household fetches it, up to `TRAVEL.load` a journey, or the giver's carrier brings it, whichever VB-291's carrier rule gives; it is eaten only once it has arrived. The same for a gift of the village store, fetched from where the store stands.
- [x] Households of one farmstead are exempt (a farmstead is one place).
- [x] The yearly filling of the village store is checked the same way (`fillStores`): if a household's quarter arrives with no journey, it is carried at the moving round, or a second task of this arch is opened for it and named here. Opened: VB-849.
- [x] `tests/lib/invariants.js`: grain in transit is on a pawn; a count of grain moved between farmsteads with no carrier stays at 0.
- [x] Numbers before and after (the hard rule asks for them, since goods that take time to arrive change hunger): populous small seeds 3, 7, 11, 42 and founders small seeds 7, 42, 30 years: hungry household-days, the days between an ask and the meal, journeys a year for help, growth.
- [x] Cost (rule 9): asks a season (2 to 4 households a village-year are helped today) × one journey each; no new loop.

Implementation (16 Nov 2026):
- The verb `give` (`sim/commands` giftOf) has a gift to a household from another farmstead, or from a village store, fetched: it names
  the fetcher (`sim/agents` fetcherOf: a member of the receiving household already fetching, else its youngest of 14 or more not on
  the road nor carrying a load home), and refuses with "no one of the household can fetch it" when there is none (the ask goes on).
  A gift between households of one farmstead still settles at once; a far gift with a named carrier is VB-291's journey, unchanged.
- `fetchGift`: the grain leaves the giver's store at the ask and lies, set aside, as a stop of the fetcher's journey (`why: 'fetch'`,
  `gift: {to, held, parts, stops: [{at, from, village, name, left, parts}]}`): at the giver's house, or on the green (`v.center`) for
  the store. The gift's records (events, tie, goodwill: `sim/exchange` booked, split out of receive) are booked at the ask, since rights
  move at once. The fetcher walks to the stop, takes up `TRAVEL.load` at most (by crop), walks home and puts it in his household's
  store (ledger `gift`, `from` the giver), and goes again while any lies; a second gift the same day (the store after the kin) is a
  further stop of the same journey. He takes a day's meal at the start and before each further trip (a pawn working away carries it).
- What lies at a stop goes back to its giver's store (a dissolved giver's: its village's) when the fetcher dies, or leaves the household,
  on his way out; a load on his back is brought in first. A load on the back of one who dies is lost, as VB-291's: VB-850 opened.
- `VB.count.giftUncarried` (`src/vb.js`, `sim/exchange` settleGift): grain settled at once between two farmsteads or from a store to a
  household; `tests/lib/invariants.js` holds it at 0 and checks a fetch: its pawn of the receiving household, the grain held on him
  bound for its house, none held on the way out to its first stop, each stop's grain by crop. `tools/stocks.js`: a line `giftGrain`.
- The pawn panel says "Going for grain at X" / "Carrying grain home from X" for a fetcher (`describe`).
- Tests: `tests/fetch.js` (new, fast tier): set aside at once, fetched in loads of 30 (three from kin, one from the store on the same
  journey), all of it in, invariants each half hour; a fetcher who dies on his way out leaves the grain the giver's. `tests/grain.js`:
  the player's gift is fetched before its crops are checked. `tests/relations.js`: the company check sets `SOCIAL.seek` past the
  sociability trait (4, not 1), so it no longer rests on the draw of the run before it.

Decisions (Deciding without Kim; inbox notes):
1. Near kin and the village store are fetched by the hungry household, not brought by the giver's carrier: the hard rule says the gift
   waits where it lies until someone fetches it, and the store has no household to send one. Far kin's gift is still brought (VB-291).
   A fetch has no upper age (a walk within a day is not VB-291's multi-day journey); one fetcher collects all of the day's gifts.
2. The rule is the verb's, so the player's gift is fetched as well (enforcing is not asking; every act is a pawn's).
3. A fetched gift is booked at the ask and its grain at each arrival; a far carried gift still books at arrival (left as VB-291 built).
4. `fillStores` left at once: VB-849 opened (the filling carried to the green).
