# VB-229: Skip to the next event that concerns the player's pawn
Status: needs-review
Priority: 2
Size: S
Arch: life
Phase: implement
Design ref: The player and what they see (Skip to the next event: Decided as a principle, 9 Nov; what stops it Proposed); Time and calendar (Speeds).
Goal: A run lasts 40 to 80 hours and a pawn's adult life many of them, most of it routine. Kim decided on 9 November (gameplay review) that the player may skip until the next event. The page runs the simulation at the uncapped speed until something happens that concerns the played pawn, and pauses there with the camera on it.
Acceptance criteria:
Acceptance criteria below are a draft, pending `QUESTIONS.md`, "VB-229".
- [ ] A "Skip" control beside the speeds, offered while the player plays a pawn: the simulation runs at the uncapped speed (VB-084) until a stop, then the page pauses, moves the camera to what happened and says why it stopped.
- [ ] What stops it comes from one function in the simulation over the record of events and `st.player`, shared with VB-098's `notable` (proposed list in the questions); the page only reads it.
- [ ] A skip never changes what the simulation does: a run skipped to a day has the same golden hashes as one played to that day (rule 7 of the Rules for cost).
- [ ] A limit, so a skip always ends (proposed: the next moving round at the latest).
- [ ] `tests/browser*.js`: play a pawn, skip, and see the page paused at a named event with no console errors.
