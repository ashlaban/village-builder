Goal: Slice 1's first step, with behaviour unchanged: help in hunger gives through Exchange's first mechanism, the gift, behind a command verb, and a gift goes ahead only when it keeps the giver at or above its floor and within its allowance. The registry gets its second rule, `allowance`, with the two terms that reproduce today's rule. Help keeps its own rules: whom it asks, in what order, and for how much.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] The floor: `floorOf(st, hh)` in `sim/hunger`, the seed reserve and `HELP.floorDays` (20, a new tunable in `src/sim/rules.js`) days of `dailyNeed`.
- [x] The rule `allowance` (`sim/registry`), declared by `sim/relations`, `combine: 'sum'`, clamped at 0 below, in rations, for a context `{giver, receiver, from, to}` (pawn ids or null, households or a village) that the caller reuses, so `value` allocates nothing:
  - `slack` (source `sim/hunger`): the giving household's grain above its floor, 0 or more;
  - `reserve` (source `sim/help`; a stand-in for generosity until traits, slice 7): minus the holder's `buffer` beyond `HELP.floorDays`, times the household's need; for a household without a holder, today's `HELP.bufferDays[1]`; from −40 days of need to 0.
  Ranges are bounds the registry's `explain` checks, so give `slack` a finite bound above any store (say 1e7 rations) and say so in a comment.

Notes:
- The golden file holds only if each gift's size is today's to the last bit. Today's size is `min(want, surplus)` with `surplus = grain - seedReserve - buffer * need` (`help.js:9`); the allowance's sum `(grain - floor) + -(buffer - floorDays) * need` can differ from it in the last bits. So help proposes today's size, and `assess` checks it against the allowance within 1e-9, rather than help asking for the allowance; say in the implementation notes which way was taken, and that the golden file held.
- The store's gift books nothing and has no agent (The gift, Who books: Agreed 8 Nov); VB-217 books kin gifts between the holders.
- Runs before VB-204 or after it, not beside it: both touch `sim/stores`' callers. Slice 2's tasks touch `sim/commands` too (VB-169); merge with care.

## Implementation notes (8 Nov 2026)

- Commits: `bb1d45c` (the floor, `allowance` with `slack` and `reserve`), `bd7e86f` (`sim/exchange`, the verb `give`, help through it),
  `8c619d9` (`tests/exchange.js`).
- `src/sim/exchange.js` (53 lines) owns no state. Its functions are `assessGift` and `settleGift`, not target-v2's `assess` and
  `settle`: `sim/claims` already defines a `settle`, and `tests/commands.js` finds the acts that only verbs may call by their bare names,
  so `settleGift` is in `WRAPPED` and `sim/commands` destructures it.
- The allowance is checked whenever a household gives, the yearly filling and households without a holder included, not only when both
  sides have an agent as first written: the `reserve` term already covers a household without a holder (today's 60 days), and checking
  only its floor would let it give down to 20 days if asked for more. A village store has neither a floor nor an allowance. Help proposes
  today's size (`min(want, surplus)`) and `assessGift` checks it within 1e-9, so sizes are today's to the last bit; on populous 7 (tiny)
  after a year, the allowance equals `surplus` exactly on all 25 households.
- The verb's actor: the giving household's holder (`holdingOf`), `{household}` for one without a holder (refused when it has one), or
  `{village}` for the store; its target a household or `{village}`. Grain only; a positive, finite amount; not to oneself.
- `allowance`'s terms call `dailyNeed` and `seedReserve`, which allocate small arrays; the registry's `value` itself allocates nothing.
  It is read about twice a game day at `small-y40`, so this is not a hot path. If slice 4's fair reads it in a loop, make those two
  helpers non-allocating first.
- `tests/registry.js` finds `death.hazard` by its id, since `allowance` (declared in `sim/relations`, which loads earlier) is now listed
  first; it also checks `allowance`'s two terms.
