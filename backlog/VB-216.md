# VB-216: Refactor: help's gifts through Exchange, the floor and `allowance`
Status: ready
Priority: 1
Size: M
Arch: kin-help
Phase: implement
Design ref: Exchange, goodwill and obligations (The gift: the gift and Acceptance, Proposed; The floor, Allowance, Agreed 8 Nov); Goods, production and animals (Help in hunger); `docs/architecture/target-v2.md`, sections 4.1 (rules, commands), 4.5 (Exchange) and 5.1; VB-182's plan.
Goal: Slice 1's first step, with behaviour unchanged: help in hunger gives through Exchange's first mechanism, the gift, behind a command verb, and a gift goes ahead only when it keeps the giver at or above its floor and within its allowance. The registry gets its second rule, `allowance`, with the two terms that reproduce today's rule. Help keeps its own rules: whom it asks, in what order, and for how much.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] The floor: `floorOf(st, hh)` in `sim/hunger`, the seed reserve and `HELP.floorDays` (20, a new tunable in `src/sim/rules.js`) days of `dailyNeed`.
- [ ] The rule `allowance` (`sim/registry`), declared by `sim/relations`, `combine: 'sum'`, clamped at 0 below, in rations, for a context `{giver, receiver, from, to}` (pawn ids or null, households or a village) that the caller reuses, so `value` allocates nothing:
  - `slack` (source `sim/hunger`): the giving household's grain above its floor, 0 or more;
  - `reserve` (source `sim/help`; a stand-in for generosity until traits, slice 7): minus the holder's `buffer` beyond `HELP.floorDays`, times the household's need; for a household without a holder, today's `HELP.bufferDays[1]`; from −40 days of need to 0.
  Ranges are bounds the registry's `explain` checks, so give `slack` a finite bound above any store (say 1e7 rations) and say so in a comment.
- [ ] A new module `src/sim/exchange.js` (`VB['sim/exchange']`, `@sys` line, after `sim/hunger` in `src/manifest.json`, under 400 lines), owning no state (target-v2: Exchange owns no goods and no balances), with the gift only:
  - `assess(st, offer)` for `{from, to, good, n, source}`: `{floorOk, allowance, accept, why}`; `accept` needs `floorOk` and `n` no larger than the allowance (with a tolerance of 1e-9, see the notes). With no agent on one side (the village store, a household without a holder), only the floor is checked. Source `player` passes below the floor and is not bound by allowance (The player's gift: Decided 3 Nov, Agreed 8 Nov); the warning that names who goes hungry is VB-221's.
  - `settle(st, offer)`: `gift`'s moves and records as they are today, moved from `help.js:29`: the grain through `sim/stores` with the same reasons, the two holders' `gift` events, the ties gain (`HELP.tieGain`, through `strengthenTie`), in today's order of writes.
- [ ] A verb `give` in `src/sim/commands.js`: actor the giving holder, or `{household}` for a household without one, or `{village}` for the store (as the meeting's verbs name a settlement); target the receiving household, or `{village}` for the yearly filling; args `{good, n}`; refused with `assess`'s reason when not accepted. `settle` joins the acts in `WRAPPED` (`tests/commands.js`), called only by the verb; `tests/commands.js` checks a gift carried out and its refusals.
- [ ] `sim/help` issues `give` (source `help`) for kin gifts, the store's gifts and the yearly filling, and keeps `kinHouseholds`, `shareFood` and `fillStores` as its own rules; `surplus` stays its name for what a household would spare (Refactor rules: no renames).
- [ ] A test, `tests/exchange.js` in `npm test`: a gift within the allowance and above the floor settles, with an entry on each side and the ties raised; one that would take the giver below its floor is refused with a reason naming the floor, unless its source is `player`; one larger than the allowance is refused; `explain(allowance)` names `slack` and `reserve`, and its value equals today's `surplus` within 1e-9 on every household of populous 7 (tiny map) at the end of year 1. `tests/help.js` passes unchanged.
- [ ] Cost line (roadmap, section 5.2): an `allowance` value for each kin household asked, at most a few hundred a game day at `medium-y60`: under 0.05 ms a game day. Report `node tools/sim.js --load` on `small-y40` with `--years 1 --report perf` before and after (the hunger and meals line).
- [ ] Refactor rules of `BACKLOG.md`: `tests/golden.json` unchanged, `npm run verify` passes. Work counters: only `ruleValue` may change; record it with `node tests/counters.js --update` and list it in the PR. `node tests/dependencies.js` and `node tools/writes.js` show no new writer of food or ties outside `sim/stores` and `sim/relations`.
- [ ] Impact note in the PR (a new module): `sim/exchange` reads stores, the floor and the registry, and writes only through `sim/stores` and `sim/relations`; `sim/help` now reaches the gift only through `issue`.
Notes:
- The golden file holds only if each gift's size is today's to the last bit. Today's size is `min(want, surplus)` with `surplus = grain - seedReserve - buffer * need` (`help.js:9`); the allowance's sum `(grain - floor) + -(buffer - floorDays) * need` can differ from it in the last bits. So help proposes today's size, and `assess` checks it against the allowance within 1e-9, rather than help asking for the allowance; say in the implementation notes which way was taken, and that the golden file held.
- The store's gift books nothing and has no agent (The gift, Who books: Agreed 8 Nov); VB-217 books kin gifts between the holders.
- Runs before VB-204 or after it, not beside it: both touch `sim/stores`' callers. Slice 2's tasks touch `sim/commands` too (VB-169); merge with care.
