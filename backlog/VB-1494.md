Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Making a field); Kim, 23 Nov: no rule mandates the order of land use
Goal: A head breaks the land it judges best, by estimated yield against distance and work, whatever its ground.
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Kim, 23 November 2026: no rule mandates the order of land use. `chooseTile` (`src/sim/fieldplans.js`) loses the term that puts open meadow before any other ground (`w.biome[t] === B.MEADOW ? 0 : 2`); `design.md`, Making a field, is already rewritten to this (the orchestrator's commit of 23 Nov): do not edit it.
