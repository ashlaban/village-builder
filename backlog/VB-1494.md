# VB-1494: A field's site is chosen by its estimated yield: no order of land use
Status: ready
Priority: 2
Size: M
Design ref: design.md, Goods and the farming year (Making a field); Kim, 23 Nov: no rule mandates the order of land use
Goal: A head breaks the land it judges best, by estimated yield against distance and work, whatever its ground.
Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Kim, 23 November 2026: no rule mandates the order of land use. `chooseTile` (`src/sim/fieldplans.js`) loses the term that puts open meadow before any other ground (`w.biome[t] === B.MEADOW ? 0 : 2`); `design.md`, Making a field, is already rewritten to this (the orchestrator's commit of 23 Nov): do not edit it.
- [x] The head prefers the tile with the best estimated yield for what it costs: a score the head could reckon (principle 14: the head's own estimate, as `harvestEstimate` and `sim/yields`' estimate are), from the field's expected grain a year (`fertility`, the soil it would start at, fresh ground's bonus), the hours to make it (`clearHours` with its ground's term, VB-1492, and `BREAK_HOURS`) spread over a named horizon of years, and the walk to it over the year's trips (distance in steps today; hours when VB-781 lands). Named tunables, in `sim/fieldplans` or beside the rules they join (`docs/architecture/modules.md`, Adding things); no magic numbers in the score.
- [x] The block stands (Decided, 30 Oct): a farm's fields lie together within `REACH` steps of the house, and a tile touching the block is preferred as before; `fieldBar`'s checks are unchanged. `pickField`'s second pass over contestable stakes follows the same score.
- [x] The search allocates nothing new a tile, and adds no loop: it is the one BFS within `REACH` it was. If the score reads the household's yield estimate, it is read once a search, not a tile.
- [x] A check on a small constructed state: with a far stony meadow and a near clean hill tile, the head takes the one whose score is higher, and the test states both scores; with equal distance and stones, meadow (1) beats hills (0.7).
- [x] Numbers before and after, founders and populous, small, seeds 7 and 42, 10 years: fields made by ground, their mean distance from the house, grain a field, hungry days a household (the distribution), growth. Whether households were better off.
- [x] `node tests/counters.js --update` if a counter moves, named; `docs/review-balance.md` is history and is not edited.

Notes:
- `siteScore` (sim/fieldplans): barley's grain a year (`fertility` x mean soil over SITE.years from SOIL.start or SOIL.fresh, worn to the floor) over its hours a year (its stage hours, weeds counted, + (clearHours + BREAK_HOURS) / SITE.years + 2 x SITE.trips x steps / SPEED); SITE = {years 5, trips 10, together 2}; touching the block multiplies the score by `together` (the old +1 on a score near 1). Terms computed once a search (siteTerms); nothing allocated a tile.
- For VB-1495: a ground's extra hours enter through GROUND.clear (clearHours); a one-off gain enters as grain / SITE.years in the numerator.
- The household's yield memory is not read: it would scale every tile alike (a ratio), so it changes nothing until a one-off gain is weighed against it.
- Close call: the walk now weighs less than FIELD_NEAR 0.3 a step did (4 steps cost ~10% of the score, not 1.2 of ~1); fresh hills (0.7 x 1.24) now beat meadow farmed before (1 x 0.85).
