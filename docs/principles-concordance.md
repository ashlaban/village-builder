# Principles: citing them, and the old numbers

Principles are cited **by name**, never by a bare number: `principle "The future is someone's estimate"`, or for target-v2's
architecture principles `target-v2, principle 7` (that list is numbered and live). A bare "principle 14" read three ways at once
(`docs/review-principles-2026-11-24/synthesis.md`, verification 3).

## The old numbers (`AGENTS.md`, Deciding without Kim, 12 to 20 November 2026)

From 12 November until the consolidation of 20 November (commit `bf435eae`), `AGENTS.md` numbered the principles 1 to 21. The
consolidation moved most of them, unnumbered, into `design.md`, Principles for decisions, and the rest into `AGENTS.md`'s prose.
Task files, plans, reviews, PRs and the archives written in that time cite the old numbers; they are history and are not rewritten.
