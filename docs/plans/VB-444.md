# VB-444: the journey scale (begun 16 November 2026)

The plan of VB-444, written in batches in a session with Kim. This file holds Kim's answers, the measures and the decisions as they
are made; `design.md` is written from it when the numbers after are in. The measure is `docs/plans/VB-444-geo.js`, a throwaway
script, not game code: `node docs/plans/VB-444-geo.js $PWD populous medium 3,7,11,42`.

## 1. Kim's answers (16 November 2026)

1. **A reach is a judgement, not a radius.** Whether a tile is worth working is to be the household head's judgement, part of its
   plan: if the head judges the travel time worth it, the tile is worked. Until that is built the reaches stay as they are, in
   steps, read as what they are: a distance. So this plan changes no reach of daily work; it lists them (section 3) and writes the
   task that replaces them.
2. **The forest's spread by seed is widened**, to reach from about 30% to about 60% of the land, about half at the median seed.
3. **Populous seats 800 as a target** and does not fail where the island holds fewer (as built on 15 November). So the scenario
   clears no forest, and the generator is not asked to keep meadow for it: a forested island may seat fewer.
4. **No nightly-scale runs for the plan.** The geography is measured at every seed and map named; years are run on the small map
   only, at two or three seeds; growth on medium is left to the nightly run after the implement tasks.

- **Seating holds.** Seven islands of eight seat as many as today or more; medium seed 7, the island with least meadow (5%),
  seats 775 of 800, as the session found. By answer 3 that is a target missed and nothing more.
- **More villages, smaller.** On medium the same people sit in 22 to 30 villages where today they sit in 19 to 23: the meadow lies
  in smaller patches, and a village is the size of its patch. Whether that is wanted is a close call surfaced for Kim: it suits
  "nobody is a stranger" less (more neighbours), and place more (9: a village is where its land is).
- **The cover alone moves the hours little**, as the session found: the median pair moves by up to 5 hours either way with the
  seating, and the nearest neighbour stays under 2 hours. The forest lengthens journeys only once it is dear to cross (section 5).

Two things the criterion asks that this proposal does not give:
- **Meadow on low wet ground.** In the generator meadow is the band of moisture next above heath, 0.07 to 0.2 of the map: the
  second driest low ground, not the wet. Lowering the two thresholds makes meadow scarcer and drier still, while the wet low ground
  becomes broadleaf forest and marsh. Meadow on wet ground (by the rivers, on low land) is a change to the generator's rule, so it
  goes with the spread into the generator's task. The first crops and the hay rest on it (river banks already grow hay well).
- **The history is still from memory.** The task's figures (forest about 45% of Gotland, 60% of Åland, 69% of Sweden today;
  ploughland 2 to 3% of Sweden around 1700) are not checked here; half forest with under a tenth of open meadow sits between the
  cleared south and the Swedish mainland. Owed: a check by search, in the last batch.
