# VB-455 event log split

## Ownership

| Module | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `sim/records` | The register of persons and households. It maintains the membership, kinship and live indexes and supplies the settlement lookup. |
| `sim/eventlog` | The typed history of the run. It appends events, indexes each pawn's history, folds routine events into yearly counts and migrates legacy history on load. |

Move `record`, `log`, `personEvent`, `lifeEvent`, `indexEvents`, `atHousehold`, `atFarm`, `startFold`, `foldHistory` and
`HISTORY` to `sim/eventlog`, together with their private helpers and tables. Give that module ownership of `st.record`,
`st.recordYears`, `st.recordFold` and `pawn.events`. Scenario seeding and pawn construction still initialize those fields.
Keep the saved keys, the event order and the public `Sim` API. The clock and save loader call the event log for folding and legacy
indexing. Update the other callers to import directly from the owner and keep the read-only histories in `sim/notable`.

## Why this boundary

An event records what occurred and can be compacted as it ages. The register answers who belongs where and maintains live indexes
as people and households change. Their only dependency across this boundary is the event log's use of `settlementIdOf` to record
where a pawn told an event in its own words. The event log can therefore load immediately after the register in the kernel,
with no callback or new dependency from the register. Keep the custom and familiarity reads in the register until VB-231 and
VB-430 address their callers. VB-456 owns the register's rename.

## What size tells us

At 841 formatted lines, the length check flags the module for review. The actual seam is around 260 lines of event handling
with a single register dependency. The register will still be wide because its callers need distinct queries about people and
households. A further cut should follow a change in who owns membership or kinship.

## Verification

Run the record, saves, dependency and ownership checks, then `npm run verify`. Compare the generated worlds and simulation state
at seeding and after each year against the starting commit using `tools/same.js`. Update the module map and ratchet the line and
export baselines down after the move.
