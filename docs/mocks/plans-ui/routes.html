The season as a route map

Plan · season after PERT / critical-path charts and transit route maps
Scales to / strains at

Scales to one household's season: five chains, about twenty stations, one critical path that reads at a glance (the thick yellow barley line), slack as one signed number a station. The forward and backward pass is trivial at this size and runs live under a drag. Moving the fence day shows its displaced cost on the line it hits, before the act.

Strains at the home tile: every chain ends there (threshing, targets, cutting), so home is an interchange of six stations in one hex; with ten members it is fourteen and the stubs cross. Window bars on every station double the ink; "critical only" is the readable setting. Route colours by chain run out at about seven (the stress lines' cyan and pink sit near the own-hours teal and the risk red). Dates live only on the bars and the line diagram below: the land carries no time axis, so a station slides only a few pixels a day and a shift of three days is easy to miss. The village fan (19 households into one fence station) is a hairball: the network is not the view for the village; the roster is.

Model: duration = mid hours / (hands × 10 h); a levy day takes its pawn's hand from any step it holds that day; slack = latest start − start. Resource levelling beyond that is not modelled. New: the "cut and resumed" state badge (a broken line bridged).
