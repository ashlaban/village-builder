The hour budget

Ledger · season · autumn day 4 after: envelope budgets · Sankey · burndown
Scales to / strains at

Scales to: four or five targets on their tiles, one envelope a tile (rye sits on the long field, where its hours go: the barn at home would stack three on one tile); a household of 5 to 10 in the side list; the Sankey at 5 members, or at 10 aggregated by position.

Strains at: envelopes count hours, not days. The budget shows 10 h free while the barley is 8 h short: the free hours fall on days 11 and 12, after its window. Only the burndown shows that, so the envelope alone misleads. The Sankey at 10 by member: breach bands of 1 to 4 h are slivers, and the labels collide. The estate's winter days have no tile and no target, so they live only in the flows and the budget. A village of 20 households: an envelope per target per household cannot stand on shared tiles. That needs a muster table, not this.
