The settler's blocks

Pawn · settler borrows from time-blocking calendars and personal task managers: Today / Upcoming / Someday, an inbox, recurring habits
Scales to / strains at
  • Scales to one pawn's day: 13 blocks with walks inside stay legible at 40 px an hour; a ½ h block keeps one line.
  • The sequence model (blocks follow one another, walks recomputed) makes overlap impossible by construction; a fixed hour (a meeting) would need a pin and then gaps.
  • Strains at ¼ h necessities (a sliver); a household (one column a member is the strip again); days-of-work estimates have no pill of their own in the grammar (the rounded "d" here is new).
  • Upcoming holds only dated things, so it is mostly empty; the field's progress is a window bar, not blocks: the player may want to see "break" on d4-d6 anyway.
