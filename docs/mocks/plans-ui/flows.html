Scales to / strains at

Scales to five members on a dozen tiles: each colour reads as a person, the shared track home to the Long field is a readable bundle of 4, the meeting is one station, and the two breaches show as a line bending away and one pulled back. Minard's width tells at a glance who spends the day where. Small multiples carry six days fine with one simplified line each.

Strains at ten: ten hues cannot be told apart (the lodger's sienna against Anna's, the servant's grey against the ink, a child's teal against the "own hours" rim), and seven parallel lines on one hex edge widen to 60 px and cover the tiles under them. Bundling into cables (×n) restores the land but loses who, which is the point of the view. The day's time is weak: travel takes no time in the data, so the hour ticks sit at departures, not along a scale. Work at home (cooking, spinning, mending) has no line at all: the home station carries most of the day as a count. A pawn's whole household moving on one land is the ideal; a village is twenty of these, never drawn at once.

Stress cases: 1 Nils' firewood (the stray pile at Hagen wood, the hand-off pile → saw); 2 the meeting at the Long field, Nils' 14:00 detour and Per's 16:30 pull; 3 the fence day in days ahead (day 6, one to name); 4 the barley station nobody's line reaches, with remedies and the rule's default; 5 Per's authored barley at risk.
