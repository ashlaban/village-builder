## Found more than once
- Home is the collision point (routes, trail, quests, placement): whatever draws time or sequence on the land dies at the yard. The
  strip owns time.
- Walks belong inside blocks: a walk is part of the task's leg, never a lane item.
- The levy line is a place on the clock; it moves only on a grant; taken hours need the torn corner, since the line cannot show them.
- Days, not hours, beyond today.
- A cut needs a state: routes and trail invented the same bridged break.
- Knowledge limits found three times (zoom's "?", timesheet's "not told", quests' rumour), with no model behind any of them.
- A fourth decision surface keeps growing (the chat, the Someday inbox, the journal): each is the tray.
- Grammar gaps and proposals: an estimate in days (a "d" pill with a hatched end); a countdown (tray only); cut (the bridged break);
  failed (a slash); offered (a dashed frame with "?"); rumour (a "~" badge, never dashing); slack (a signed day pill, Season lanes
  only); taken back (the author corner flips to the rule, with a return arrow).

## The synthesis spec
**Regions.** Land (centre: state, problems, where; one sign per tile with a count pip; nothing temporal at rest). Strip (bottom, 56 px
collapsed, ~220 px open: the only time surface; its rows are lanes). Tray (right rail: only the played pawn's decision points, at
once / this hour / by deadline; countdowns only here). Card (at the tapped thing, one at a time: bubble, acts, why). Breadcrumb (top
left: Nils › Backa › Byn › Sparre).

**Two zooms, independent.** Body: the breadcrumb, or tapping a pawn, a yard, the green; the map follows with hard thresholds.
Horizon: zooming the strip, Today | Ahead | Season | Year. Paired defaults: pawn-Today, household-Ahead, village-Season, estate-Year.

**One component, the lane:** one body's time in the horizon's unit. Its children are the body's parts (a pawn's lane expands to its
task's step row; a household's to its members plus "unheld"; a village's to its households, the roster; an estate's to its
villages). The same marks at every level (whose fill, torn corner, author and state corners, solid turning hatched at the estimate's
end). A parent lane is the sum of its children. Left of now every lane shows the six parts, exact: the Ledger is the strip scrolled
back.

**Modes by question.** Look (default: what and why; strip collapsed, tray live); Plan (strip open and editable); Build. Muster is not a
mode: it is the village body in Plan, with acts only for an alderman.

**How the parts fit.** Why: the card unfolds the thread upward (block → task → step → target → levy or aim), "moves" downward; the
chain's lanes and places light. Remedies: in the card, each with its cost, the rule's default marked with its deadline. Playbook:
member-coloured arrows on the land in Plan only, for focused members on the cursor day; hue is "whose" at every other time. Task
instructions: the pawn lane's child row and the card's expanded state, the live method only, "other ways" folded. Aims: shields on
the land, lanes at Season and Year, key results in the card; no log.

**What knowledge hides.** Other households' lanes: only days named at the meeting. A member's view of targets: only what was told.
Others' aims: "~" rumour. Others' answers: never as odds.
