Aims as a quest log

Pawn · aims after an RPG quest journal, OKRs (objective, key results) and the old-map cartouche
Scales to / strains at

Scales to a pawn with two to four aims: one shield a place, one dotted path from the pawn, the cartouche naming the placeless ones. Key results as measures (a count against an estimate's hatched end, an opinion on a signed meter, stages as pips) read better than labels and are the same meter everywhere. "What moves this aim" as links into the day and the season plan answers the reverse why in one tap. Failed and offered quests read at once (struck card, slashed shield; a dashed card with take / leave).

Strains at Per's seven: the journal has to fall to one-line cards (key results a tap away), the markers crowd the farm's tiles and the paths fan from one pawn into a hairball; two aims on one tile need the count pip. Others' aims known by rumour use a dashed shield, which collides with the dashed "offered" quest. The year strip's gates (sowing, the gathering, the moving round) hold, but conflicts are a day's matter and the year line cannot show them. Units: "own hours a week" for Nils when a game day already stands for a week; Ola's hours are per week of six days to match, which hides his necessities' daily rhythm. A quest's "next objective" pretends to a single next step: courting's next is today's taken visit, but the gathering is the one that matters.

New marks: state badges failed (slash), offered (?), rumour (~); the clay pit tile (1,4). Hatching = an estimate's range only.
