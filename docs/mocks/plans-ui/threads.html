Scales to / strains at

Scales to one chain of 4 to 8 nodes at a time; reasons with places read at a glance (the levy at the fence, the target at the barn); a target's fan-down of up to about six feeders.

Strains at many threads at once: the whole household's day drawn together is a hairball, so only one chain shows. Reasons with no place (the year's food, the winter, the village, the custom) pile into the edge column, far from the thing, and their threads cross the map; the ladder fixes that but loses the land. Nodes sharing a tile (the yard holds the store, the task and three targets) need hand-set offsets. A fan-down from the barn across a whole autumn would be dozens of threads.

Stress cases: 1 Nils (knot = the cut, the pile at Hagen wood); 2 Nils at 14:00 and Per's 16:30 demand; 3 Erik's fence day (levy, terms, ladder of remedies); 4 the barley vs the labour budget, Nils' aim; 5 the frayed barley thread. New glyph: the knot (a cut task).
