The alderman's muster

Muster · Byn · autumn day 4 borrows: shift roster, resource scheduler, RACI, choropleth
Scales to / strains at

Scales to: one village of 20 households, one season of 12 days, one routine and the work done together: the roster reads like a shift rota, the map like a choropleth of who is behind. The year zoom (4 × 20) holds every routine and the estate's winter days.

Strains at: 60 households (an estate's view): the map's farms fall below a readable sign (tint only, no icon), the roster loses its icons and becomes a heatmap of ~18 px rows that still scrolls about one and a half screens, sorted by urgency across villages, so his own Byn rows scatter among farms he cannot act on; hover-to-farm still works the three maps shrink to 11 px hexes and lose every sign, and the lot toggle hurts 16 farms of 60 under 'equal' with no room to say which village. Two levies on one day in a cell (a fence day on the bridge day) show only the more urgent. The ladder's "reason as he knows it" is guesswork the design must supply (gossip, word sent): most cells will read "not known".
