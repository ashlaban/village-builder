Look · Sparre › Byn › Backa · autumn day 4, 10:00 borrows: cartographic generalisation (a map redraws, not rescales), semantic zoom of map apps
Scales to / strains at

Scales to: one village of 20 farms: at the village scale each household is one ring (its lot days), one meter and one sign, about 40 px apart, and the hierarchy reads as one whole because the village glyph is literally the households' rings summed, and the household glyph grows into the yard's members and the places of work. The strip changing rows with the zoom (villages, households, members, hours) kept the time out of the map at every stop.

Strains at: 60 households: between the estate and the village scale the three villages' 60 rings fall to about 20 px apart, below the ring's readable size; the crossfade band (0.30 to 0.45 px per world px) then shows half-faded village glyphs over half-faded rings, which reads as mud. The honest fix is a hard threshold per village (a village switches whole, by its own on-screen size), not a global fade. Two thirds of the 60 are "?" to Per, so the far villages carry almost nothing but their named-day count. The roster strip of 60 rows scrolls three screens. A pawn stop exists only for Backa's members; a stranger's farm stops at the household with "?" dots.
