# Round 2 review (Fable, 25 November): strip, flows, timeland, muster, ledger

An independent review of the five round-2 mocks, condensed. The decisions are the owner's.

## Carry forward
- strip: the deadline tray (at once / this hour / by deadline, one icon and one countdown each); the remedy card with costs and "the
  rule will"; the "unheld" lane for a step nobody holds.
- flows: the meeting station ("4 meet"); the hand-off callout; "nobody's line, -8h", an unstaffed step as a station no line reaches;
  dragging a line to a station to `assign`. A focus view (one member coloured, the rest dimmed), not an overview.
- timeland: scrubbing previews the land; the forecast cone on the barley; the "displaced" line with its cost when a levy day is
  dropped on a day.
- muster: the roster (a row a household, a column a day, sorted by urgency); the ladder with struck rungs as the alderman knows it;
  the lots toggle that rings the farms a custom hurts; `callWorkday` options with a turnout estimate.
- ledger: the sovereignty pair bar (own by right against as lived) and the household's spread; the burndown with the budget band.

## Dead ends
Pawn-carried day bars on the map (a smudge at 34 px); duties as season-long bars; days-ahead lanes filled edge to edge (false
precision: 31% of hours can be foreseen); hour pills on lines; Minard width; small multiples of the land; the almanac ticks; the
permanent right margin; the muster's year zoom of identical columns; envelopes counting hours (the binding unit is days in a
window); a Sankey of the days ahead (the six parts are for past days only).

## The grammar v2 in use
Frames, torn corner, author and state corners hold at 40 px. Collisions: member hues against whose hues (Erik's blue is the
village's, Karin's plum near the estate's purple, Anna's sienna near at-risk red); three time numbers (countdown, day of season,
hours short); red overloaded (risk, breach, decision rim, now, displaced); hatching overloaded (estimate, to-name window, beyond the
horizon); pennants and the double rim vanish under about 28 px. Missing: a state for cut and resumed (flows invented a knot).

## The synthesis proposed: one page, four regions
- **Land** (centre): state and problems only; one sign per tile with a count pip; nothing temporal at rest.
- **Strip** (bottom, 56 px collapsed, about 220 px open): the only time surface. Today (hours), Ahead (days), Season (hours left
  against the days in the window). Rows: the selected body's members, or a village's households (the roster is this strip).
- **Tray** (right rail): the played pawn's decision points only, by deadline.
- **Card** (at the tapped thing, one at a time): bubble, acts with costs and the rule's default, then "why", unfolding the thread
  inside the card; the chain's places light on the land.
- Modes by question: Look (default: what and why), Plan (strip open; the focused member's playbook lines on the land for the
  strip's cursor day), Build, Muster (the roster in the strip, the real land tinted), Ledger (a panel, the past).
- Scale: tapping a pawn, a farmstead or the green sets the strip's body; a breadcrumb Per > Backa > Byn > Sparre.
- Interactions: the tray flies to the place and opens the card; scrubbing the strip previews the land; drag a slip or a line to
  `assign` (past the levy line it says demand, with the likely answer); drag a levy block to a day to `name`, the displaced cost shown
  before release; tap a member chip to focus.

## Decisions for the owner (the reviewer's recommendation first)
1. Planning unit follows the zoom: hours today, days ahead, the season as hours left against days in the window. Or hours everywhere.
2. Colour: whose hours owns hue; members are a dot, an initial and row order, coloured only when focused. Or a member palette of up
   to 6 off the whose hues, whose moved to line style.
3. Time: the strip owns the plan, the land previews under the scrub. Or Plan mode shows small multiples.
4. Ahead lanes: duties, dates and dated steps only; unfilled space says "as the day brings". Or a projected fill.
5. Tray: the played pawn's points only; others' choices show as state. Or all of them.
6. Muster map: tint the real land. Or a schematic inset that survives 60 households.
7. Ledger: a panel mode, past only. Or fold the sovereignty bars into the member chips.
8. Numbers: a countdown only in the tray; a sign carries a day or hours by its frame.
