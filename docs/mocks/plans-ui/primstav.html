Calendar · days ahead to year
Scales to / strains at
Scales to: one household of 5 to 7 rows; dated things that are a day or a span (levies, leaves, steps, windows); the same stave zooms days, season and year with one notch grammar. Map link via hover rings and stakes works because each item has one place.
Strains at: within a day (stress 1 and 2) the stave only opens a mini day from today's notch; hours inside a day do not fit a notch. Two things on one member-day overlap. An unassigned step (the barley) needs its own lane above the members, which breaks "one row per member". In B the unfurled stave covers the lower map, so the place being linked can sit under it. Season meters are a panel more than a carving.
