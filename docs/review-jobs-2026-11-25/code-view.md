# The job system as implemented (code-only review; parity lens)

Commit 81cf8e8. Everything below is from `src/sim/*` unless stated. Counts of KINDS fields come from loading `VB['sim/kinds']` and tallying.

## 1. The model in code

Work is a **board of posted jobs** (`st.jobs`, keyed; `st.jobsBy` per owner), filled by *availability rules*. Each kind's `available(st, holder, at, c, post, f)` reports "where this kind could be done now" (`kinds.js:34-45`). `refreshJobs` (`jobs.js:92`) asks the rules in the fixed order `ASK` (`kinds.js:262-291`) for each holder whose plan is due a review. A household's **head** (`sim/day.allot`, `day.js:76`) then weighs every member against every job the household may take (`fit.score`, `fit.js:92`) and fills per-member task lists (`plan.day.lists`). Idle pawns take the first task on their list through `takeDay` (`day.js:~395`).

The one carry-out path is the **`work` verb** (`agents.js:596`). It is called through `issue(st, {actor, verb:'work', target:key, source})` (`commands.js:77`) by the planner, the evening call, the dutyRound and the player alike. The verb runs `release` and `start`: join `j.claims`, walk, `arrived` sets phase `work`, and progress is closed-form (`progress.doneOf`) rather than stepped. There is no per-pawn "chooser" object. The choice is the head's allotment, and for the player it is a queue plus an optional plan.

## 3. A job's life, function by function

1. **Availability.** Per-kind `available` is called inside `refreshJobs`' local `ask` (`jobs.js:98-105`) and calls `put(key, site, extra, kind, owner)`. `put` goes to `jobs.post` (`jobs.js:38`), which creates `{id: ++st.jobSeq, …}`, applies `K.draw`, adds to `st.jobsBy` and notes the owner in `changed`. Every posted job is marked `j.live=true`. Before a review, an owner's jobs are set `live=false`; afterwards `takeDown` → `sweep` drops those still not live and unclaimed (`jobs.js:73-77`). A land-project job is dropped at once, hands freed (`KINDS[k].project`).
2. **Which holders ask.** Three cadences, all in the `refreshJobs` hourly body (clock `every:'hour', hour [WORK0-1, WORK1+1]`, `clock.js:146-152`):
   - The **due-queue** (`plandays.dueNow/again`): kinds with a `window` (8 kinds) are asked per household from their hour and again every hour while they give work. A job opened is `slotIn`'d into the day (`day.js:~245`) rather than re-allotting.
   - **Plan reviews**: `reviewWhy` (`plandays.js:~100`) is 'day' (`plan.nextReview`, cadence 1/2/3 days by the head's diligence, offset by id), 'window' (closing windows/fields due/workday), or 'store' (stock crossed a target, once a day).
   - **Island asks** (`ASK.island`) every review hour for plots, restores and booths (`islandJob`, `jobs.js:62`).
3. **Allotment.** `allotChanged` (`day.js:~330`) calls `allot` for each household whose tasks changed, and for every household at 05:00 and at `MATCH.day.afternoon`. `allot` → `weigh` (each member × each job: `eligible`, then `score`) → sort pairs → `man` (give hands until `Estimate.workFor` hours covered, each member to its `dayHours` cap; `first`/`late` kinds go to the list head) → `plan.day` written → `Hands.shortOf` asks for help in the morning → `call` (a deadline task, or a much more urgent top task, may pull a pawn off work via `issue(work,'planner')`).
4. **Taking.** `clock.step` (`clock.js:745-775`): `playerStep`; `stepAgents` (due work ends, due legs, offers idle pawns); `dutyRound` (owed/lent); `takeDay`; `lookForWork` (member with no task asks the household's non-windowed kinds, once a household a day, `mayLook`); then `takeDay` again; `ownTime` for the rest. `takeDay` issues `work` for the first list entry where `canTake` (= `!matchBar`: open, `eligible`, side/reach).
5. **Execution** (`agents.js`): `start` (join claims, `K.inside` → home spot, `K.setOut`, `walkTo` job site/`workSpot`) → `walkEnd` → `arrived`: phase 'go'→'work', `workAt` registers the work leg and progress, `rerate`. Hours elapse closed-form (`doneOf = done + ticks*rate`). Due-queue `dueWork(st, ended, sideEnded)` → **`ended`** (`agents.js:344`): if `K.load/stack/carry` → load, `phase='back'`, walk to door/woodpile/site/house; else `complete`. A pawn arriving home with the load → `deposit` (`K.deliver`); `again(j)` leaves the job open for the next load.
6. **Completion/release.** `complete` → `freeHands`, `K.done`, `emit('yielded')` for the played pawn, `dropJob`. `release` is how a pawn leaves a job unfinished (keeps `j.done`). At WORK[1] every free pawn is released and sent home, except `carriedOn` (vital, begun) ones (`clock.js:~183-189`). The evening (`eveningCall`) lets a household's `evening` kind (water, firewood) call one home hand for 2 hours.

## 4. The day and the plans

- **Standing plan** (per household and village): not a task list but *targets* (`plan.targets`, `st.plans.seasons`: hay winters, fields, wood loads, cow, made goods) plus rules. The kind rules read stores against targets, which decides which jobs exist. `sim/seasonal` (373 lines) makes the seasonal plan and targets. The head's choices are verbs registered in `plans.js:374-513` (`target`, `tile`, `order`, `project`, `dropProject`, `letField`, `rotation`, `sowOutOfTurn`, `courses`). Their check, `heads()` (`plans.js:337`), is the same for every pawn: the actor must be the household's holder (or `{household}` when headless).
- **Projects** (`sim/projects`, `sim/projectplans`, `sim/aims`, `sim/makings`): land steps and small constructions are step-lists with goods reservations. A step becomes a job through `landProject`/`plotWork`/etc. in ASK. NPC heads begin them through `issue(project, source:'pawn')` (`aims.js:189`, `plans.js:189`, `upgrade.js:105`) once a year/at a review. The player's `project` calls the same verb.
- **Day**: allot at 05:00 and 14:00, and when tasks change; `slotIn` for due-queue arrivals. The head's verbs on the day (`dayOrder/dayGive/dayCall`, `day.js:~455-545`) are registered and checked by the same `heads` rule, but no non-player code issues them (grep: only `player.js:393-395`).
- **Estimate** (`sim/estimate`): hours from the *estimating pawn's* skill, so the head's allotment can be wrong. `timetable`/`agenda`/`reasons` are views, written nowhere.
- **Cadences**: each 6-minute step: `playerStep`, `stepAgents`, takeDay/lookForWork/ownTime. Each hour (05:00–19:00): `refreshJobs`. At 17:00 (WORK[1]): reckon, release all. 17–`MATCH.day.evening`: `eveningCall`. Each day: lifecycle, hunger (`clock.js` table). The page's `ui2` is not the sim.

## 5. The KINDS record

94 kinds are joined in `kinds.js:72-145` (≈20 spread across `Object.assign` from domain modules). The record has **51 distinct fields** (tallied). Per-field use among 94 kinds: required 9: `verb go name at needs gives label hours type` (94 each, load-time validated, `kinds.js:~212`); then `anim 91, hands 91, spot 75, available 68, done 66, urgency 58, back/carry/to/deliver 27, project 22, field 13, load 13, craft 13, inside 11, worked 11, again 10, window 8, fetch 7, stages 6, vital 6, minAge 4, draw 3` and a long tail of 1–2 uses: `setDown 2, evening 2, together 2, attention 2, reach 2, rate 2, rank 2, pace, stack, first, trip, setOut, late, reload, words, carted, held, yields`. Only 68 of 94 have `available`; the rest (stage jobs, fetch kinds, lying loads) are posted by a **shared rule** keyed in ASK (`[null, rule]`, e.g. `Building.boothWork`, `landProject`, `plotWork`), so the posting site is not always the kind.

**Special cases in the matcher/allotment (kind or flag checks in core):**
- `fit.prioOf` (`fit.js:44-50`): `j.plot` → `plotPrio`; `j.claim` → `farmclaims.claimPrio`; `j.common` → `commons.commonPrio`; `K.rank` → kind's own; else role rank. Three job flags in the core scoring.
- `fit.score`: `K.vital && carriedOn` removes the walk; `K.minAge`; `joined`/`together`.
- `day.weigh`: `makesGraded(kind)` → only the best-skilled maker may take it (craft+graded good).
- `day.man`: `K.first`, `K.late`, `K.trip`, `attentionOf !== 'full'` (side tasks bypass hours).
- `jobs.islandJob`: names `'restore'`, `'booth'`, `'boothwater'` and `j.plot` literally (`jobs.js:62`).
- `hands.js:63-66`: the help window by `j.kind === 'plow'|'sow'|'reap'|'haul'`. `eligibility.helps` uses `MATCH.help.kinds`.
- `agents.complete`: `if (j.plot) { finishStep; dropJob }`; `agents.ended`: `K.stack` (firewood) and `K.load` cart logic inline; `j.common` destination. `withOxen`/`teamKind` special-case the plow/hoe kinds.
- `eligibility` is a clean ordered table of 6 bases (owed, servant, member, villager, helper, self; `eligibility.js:11-25`); duty/lent pawns get only one basis.
- Roles' ranks: each ROLE gets `prio[kind]` and `ranks[mask]` (stand-in roles) computed at load (`kinds.js:220-240`); `ASK`'s `fieldAt` is derived from `stages`.

## 6. The player's path, and parity

**Same code:** the job board, availability, eligibility (`BASES`), score (`fit.appraise` is used by both the matcher and the Actions list `opportunities.work`, `opportunities.js:60-95`), the `work` verb, `start`/`arrived`/`ended`/`complete`, closed-form progress, the head's verbs (`heads()` accepts a pawn actor), the day lists, and the `issue` path with `source`. The player's pawn, **if `auto` is true and no queue**, goes through `takeDay` exactly like an NPC (the clock filters only `unplanned` = queue non-empty or `!auto`, `records.js:781-784`).

**Divergence, in layers:**
1. *Gate.* `unplanned(st,id)` (`records.js:781`) removes a pawn from the planner: out of `fit.allotted` (`fit.js:58`), the clock's `idle` list, `hands`, `help`, `loans`, `gathering`, `away`. It is the only isPlayer-style branch in the planner and is spread over ≥10 modules.
2. *Choosing.* NPC: head allots `plan.day.lists` by `score`/`estimate`. Player: (a) `Sim.queue` — ≤8 commands, any verb whose `sources` includes 'player', actor must equal `pl.pawn` (`player.js:~165`); `playerStep` performs the head, using the verb's `active` reference (`commands.js:54`); (b) with `auto=false`, `feed`→`nextOf` (`plannext.js:157`) picks, in order: sleep, routine kinds not done today (first job of kind k the `work` check admits, `routineJob`), then `plan.tasks` in order via `taskCmd`. Output is the same `work` command, with `source:'player'` and a `plan` mark.
3. *The plan is a different data structure.* `st.player.plan` (routine/sleep/tasks/chains) has no NPC counterpart. NPCs have `plan.targets` + `plan.day`. There is no shared "task list": the NPC's list is derived output, the player's is authored input. Chain tasks (`planchains.js`, 514 lines: plant/hay/harvest/fell/firewood/farmstead/foundation/upgrade/outbuilding) address *tiles* and run multi-step sequences (move, droving, shelter). NPCs get the same effects only by emergent availability rules.
4. *Player state leaks into shared rules.* Rules read `st.player.plan` or `played()`: `chores.js:34,155` (the fell job `wood:h:t<tile>` is posted only for the played household's plan tasks, with `chosen:true`); `building.js:72` `played()` (cottage is built when the plan says, not in "slack"), `building.js:448` `foundationAsked`; `ladder.js:114,266-283` (`farmView`: `hand` → `upgradeWhy` etc. vs `WHY[what]` for non-player); `projects.js:106 heldOff` (auto-off player holds off every NPC-style project); `camp.js`, `upgrade.js` (`ownRung` for a camp "left to itself"), `outlook.js:148-152`. The "head's own rule" and "the player's plan task's check" are two parallel rule sets for farm rungs (`ladder.farmView`'s "for the played head ok and why are its plan task's check; for any other household the head's own rule's").
5. *Acts the player has and NPCs do not use.* `dayOrder/dayGive/dayCall` (exist for any head, used only via `head()` HEAD_VERBS, `player.js:389-403`); `Sim.plan` ops; `setAuto`; `wish` and `propose` (`player.js`) are not verbs, they write `pawn.wish`/`pawn.proposal` directly via the module. `sleep` verb is shared (`sources: ['player','pawn']`, `sleepers.js:83`, with `ownRule` for NPCs and `!auto` for the player).
6. *Acts NPCs have that the player has no equivalent for.* `askFor` (help) skips the player (`hands.js:200`); `voice` (leave asks) and `days.callDays` are planner-sourced.

**API richness for an LLM-driven NPC.** What exists generically: `Sim.command(st, cmd)` = `commands.issue` (any actor/verb/source: `sim.js:68,104`); `Sim.opportunities(st, id)` = `forPawn` for any pawn (the actor view is currently `st`: "every pawn knows everything", `opportunities.js:~25`); `Sim.timetable`, `reasons`. What is single-pawn: `queue`, `unqueue`, `setAuto`, `plan`, `head`, `agenda`, `planView`, `planGaps`, `buildMenu`, `sheetNotes` all read `st.player` and check `actor === pl.pawn` (`player.js:165-175`). So an external agent can *act* for an NPC via `command`, but cannot get a queue, the plan's tile-addressed chains, the waits ("why a task waits"), `agenda` or `buildMenu`. It would also need to turn the NPC's planner off (`unplanned` exists only for `st.player`), or it would compete with `takeDay`/`allot`. In short, the verbs are parity-ready, the planning layer (plan/queue/agenda/chains) is player-singleton.

## 7. Cost (perf: populous, seed 7, small, 2 years, 96 game days, 171 ms/day)

- `jobs refresh and matching` 7247 ms = **44%** (75 ms/day; 31,795 calls; longest `refreshJobs` 52 ms). Next `agents: walking and working` 15%, `talk and contacts` 12.8%, rest-of-advance 12.6%, `daily life` 6%, `idle strolls` 5%.
- Loops: `refreshJobs` ask: rules × (fields + farm + farmAfterPlans ≈ 45 rules) per reviewed holder (`VB.count.oppAsk`); `allot`: **jobs × members** pairs per allotment (`VB.count.dayPair`), at 05:00 and 14:00 for *every* household plus on every change; `weigh` evaluates `eligible` twice for graded kinds; `takeDay` iterates lists (`dayTake`); `eveningCall` walks every live household's jobs × members (`eveningAsk`) two hours a day; `st.jobs.values()` is scanned on every review hour to reset islandJobs and again to sweep (`jobs.js:~208-215`: O(all jobs)/hour). `dequeue` rebuilds `plans.due` by filter. Counters are declared in `src/vb.js:145-155`; `tests/counters.js` ratchets them.
- Mitigations in code: `urgency` memo per allotment (`fit.js:30`); `jobsFor` merges two Sets only when both non-empty; `Due.take` heaps; closed-form progress; legs queue (no per-pawn stepping); cadences staggered by id.

## 8. Code-level observations

- **Size/width.** Lint baseline: only `records.js` (733), `rigs.js`, `treelayout.js`. Over 700 lines of formatted code by the lint's scale: none of the job modules (kinds 297, jobs 269, day 566, fit 164, agents 717 — slightly over, but not in the baseline; `building.js` 915, `opportunities.js` 608, `agenda.js` 619). Width baseline lists `sim/agents: 28` exports and `sim/plans: 16`, `sim/projects: 16`; `agents` is the widest job module. `building.js` at 915 lines is the largest domain and carries the cottage/booth/staked/foundation logic plus plan-aware branches.
- **Coupling.** `sim/kinds` imports ~20 domain modules and hand-assembles KINDS in 4 styles (spread, `Object.assign`, named property, `Lying.fetchKind` generator). `ASK` is built in the same file via 10+ `push` lines after the literal (`kinds.js:~270`), so the order lives in two forms. `fit`, `day`, `agents`, `clock`, `eligibility` import `Days`, `Hands`, `Voice` (three "this pawn is not in its own day" states) and each tests them separately (`fit.allotted`, `eligibility.eligible`, `dutyRound`).
- **Duplication.** The player/NPC pairs: `ladder.farmView` `hand ? upgradeWhy : WHY`, `chores` plan-fell post vs `wood:h` post, `building.played()`/`slack`, `plannext.routineJob` vs `takeDay`, `playerplan.askCutting/askFarmstead` (emit `planAsks` to wake a review) vs the household's own cadence 1–3 days; `workCmd` is defined in `plannext.js:14` and `fit.workCommand` (differing `args: null`).
- **Special cases.** Core files name kinds (`'restore'`, `'booth'`, `'boothwater'`, `'plow'`, `'sow'`, `'reap'`, `'haul'`, `'fell'`, `'firewood'`) despite KINDS flags; `j.plot/claim/common` are three ad-hoc job-record flags read by core fit/agents. `kinds.js` documents its own fields in a comment (~50 lines) rather than a schema, validated only for `at/needs/gives`.
- **Fragile.** `st.plans.due` stores indexes into `WINDOWED`, which is derived from KINDS order (a reordering corrupts saves, `kinds.js:73`). Job id = posting order and matching is id-ordered, so ASK order is saved behaviour. The module-level `let changed` in `jobs.js:30` is state kept in a closure (documented "null between calls"), contrary to the "no state in closures" rule, but cleared per call. `lint-allow: owner` appears only in `agents.js:483`, `camp.js:37/95/134`, `plans.js:134`; no TODO/FIXME in the job modules. `jobs.refreshJobs` is ~150 lines mixing the due-queue, reviews, village and island.
- **Parity code smell**: `records.unplanned` is the only "is this pawn the player's" gate for the planner, but ≥12 other modules read `st.player` (list from grep: advice, asking, building, camp, chores, hands, hunger, ladder, matching, offers, projects, round, sleepers, outlook, timetable, notable...). The architecture treats the player as *a source of commands with a queue* (`sim/player` header), which is clean, but the *plan* layer (`playerplan`/`plannext`/`planchains`/`planview`/`agenda`/`camp`/`upgrade`, ≈3000 lines) is a second, player-only planner that also pokes back into shared posting rules.
