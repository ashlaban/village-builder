## 1. The intended model

Work is *available, not posted* (target-v2 4.5; design s7 intro, Decided 3 Nov). What could be done is an **opportunity**, computed from rights and stores by each job kind's `available`, never a stored list. A household's **head** is its *holder*; it keeps a **plan** (a standing plan of routines, a seasonal plan with targets, and projects), reviewed on a staggered **planning day**, and the plan makes **tasks** when a step is open. The head **allots the household's day** (morning, 14:00, on review, when tasks change): every member is weighed for every task it may take (role priority x urgency, less the walk), each gets an ordered list, and the member works down it. Who may take a task is **eligibility** by *basis* (member, servant, villager, helper, self). A pawn executes a task as a chain of **legs** (walk, work, wait) with ends known at their start. Every choice is a command: a **verb** with a check that holds for any pawn alike, issued by planner, meeting, round or player. The player is "a source" (target-v2 principle 15) and, as head, may make every choice the plan makes (design s7 "The player as head"); the automatic head chooses as well as its cost allows (QUESTION_ARCHIVE 24 Nov, Q9, cited in design s1 "Every act is a pawn's").

## 3. Flows (year -> hour)

- **Year:** culture's template and the yearly meeting set village routines afresh ("decided afresh each year", at most four; failure -> practice "nothing" by default) (s7 "Village routines"; target-v2 4.5). The alderman allots the village wood (`allotWood`, VB-781, Proposed). Moving round answers offers (VB-682).
- **Season:** head's first review of a season compiles the reach map and keeps ranked places per kind (`REACH.keep` 3) (s7 "What bounds the search", Proposed); seasonal plan/targets; supply outlook re-made.
- **Planning day:** holder reviews: estimate/outlook, targets, opportunities -> tasks; project step opened; reservations; "step made impossible is reviewed at once".
- **Day:** head allots at morning, 14:00, review, task/village change, and when a member looks for work; due-queue work (milking, meal, fodder) slotted alone into existing lists (s7 "The day's work").
- **Hour / step:** a member takes the first task with room on its list, issues verb `work` (ownership.md 5.1); legs run from a queue; a leg's end is known; an urgent task, call, night or death cuts a leg; 18:00 releases except `vital` kinds; no task -> pastime (`ownTime`).
- **Task -> act:** opportunity (kind `available`) -> task (plan or standing or self) -> eligibility (basis) -> day list -> verb `work` -> legs (walk, work, carry) -> kind's `done`/`deliver` through the owner (VB-418: "a record's `done` must change state through its owner"; "Hours reported to whoever owns the progress", target-v2 4.5).
- **Player:** queue of up to 8 commands goes before the pawn's own logic; with automatic behaviour on and no commands "an inhabited pawn does exactly what it would if no one played it" (s15 "The player's commands", Decided 7 Nov). From 24 Nov (HANDOFF; not design): automatic planning off, a plan (routine of kinds, sleep hours, ordered tasks) feeds the queue via `playerStep`.

## 4. Ownership and layering

- Layers (target-v2 3.1/3.2; modules.md Layers): kernel (rules, roles, due-queue), material (kinds in `sim/fieldwork`, `chores`, `byre`...), social, agency (`sim/legs`, `work`, `agents`, `jobs`, `fit`, `day`, `estimate`, `plans`, `seasonal`, `eligibility`, `player`, `pastimes`). `sim/kinds` is the registry (pull, not push: VB-418 5.3). Reads free everywhere; calls only down; three upward hooks: rule contribution, escalation target, procedure choice.
- Principles 1-4 (target-v2 s2): domains own truth, processes coordinate; one writer per field; opportunity/plan/task/activity are four things; **nothing is posted**. Plan state is `sim/plans`, `sim/seasonal`, `sim/projectplans` (single writer); `plan.day` is written only by `sim/day`'s `allot`; `agent.*` by `sim/agents`/`legs`; `st.player` by `sim/player` (VB-228 s4); job fields `claims`/`done` by agents, `live` by jobs (ownership.md 3.2 "job", proposed owners).
- A verb lives in the module of its act; planner, meeting, round, player, debug are *sources* (VB-228; target-v2 4.5 Decisions "decide aggregates, commands carry out", principle 10). Hourly single-voice choices stay in Work, not Decide (4.5).
- Walk judgement: `sim/estimate` for what a trip brings; verb check reads world, not estimate (s7 "The estimate").

## 5. Player path vs household path; parity assessment

**What the documents say (strong, repeated).** s15 "Parity of command" (Decided 10 Nov): every act a pawn may take is the player's, "a pawn's own logic and the player choose among the same verbs, and each verb's check binds both... a pawn never has an act the player lacks. A new act is not done until the player can reach it." design s1 "Every act is a pawn's" (24 Nov): same verbs, same checks; "whatever state the played pawn has, every pawn has, and `st.player` holds only the frame"; an unplayed head "chooses as well as its cost allows: the player's edge is foresight and attention, not a head made weaker on purpose." target-v2 principle 15 and 4.7: same four doors; "traits act on what happens to the player's pawn, never on what the player chooses."

**Where the written design honours it.**
- The head's choices (`target`, `tile`, `order`, `project`, `ground`, `allotWood`, `pastime`, `leave`) are verbs usable by any head and the player; "With no choice made the holder's rule runs" (s7 "The player as head", "The player"). One substrate for the *chooser-over-verbs*.
- Opportunities for one pawn (`Opportunities.forPawn`, s15 "What it may do here") are the same list the kinds' `available` yield, with the planner's fit.
- Sleep: every pawn owes sleep and has the `sleep` verb (VB-1645, backlog; `pawn.sleep`), explicitly "the player's pawn is no exception (principle 15)".
- Estimate, timetable, reasons, outlook: views of a pawn's own judgement, so the player sees what its pawn would believe, not omniscience.
- A non-head played pawn "acts on opportunities and cannot change the plan; it asks the holder" via offers (s7), same as any member.

**Where it is two systems, or unspecified.**
1. **Two command paths for the same work.** The planner's output is a *day list* per member that the pawn walks (s7 "The day's work"); the player's is a *queue of up to 8 commands* that overrides it (s15). s15 says commands are the unification, but the household's tasks are chosen by `allot` and taken by `takeDay` (ownership.md 5.1), the player's by hand or the feeder. They meet at verb `work`, not earlier.
2. **The player's plan is a second plan structure.** `st.player.plan` {routine kinds, sleep, ordered task list} (HANDOFF; modules.md `sim/playerplan`) is not the head's plan (`plan.day`, standing/seasonal/projects). HANDOFF: "How a manual and an automatic plan meet is for later"; Kim 24 Nov: "There is no 'automatic plan': there could be automatic planning, and it is turned off." That sentence is the parity reading (one plan, an automatic planner that can be off), but the written design has no row saying the household's plan *is* that structure, nor that an unplayed pawn has a routine/sleep/task list in the same form. Per "whatever state the played pawn has, every pawn has", `st.player.plan` should be per-pawn or per-head; the documents do not say so (sleep was moved to the pawn by VB-1645; the plan was not mentioned).
3. **Player's task list is ordered per pawn; the head's day is per household.** The player-as-head plans for one pawn by hand; who allots the rest of the household while the player plans by hand is unstated in the design (HANDOFF: nothing the head would have planned may be counted on; stores count what is in the plan).
4. **Automatic-behaviour switch.** s15: "the player may turn that automatic behaviour off" (the pawn's own logic). No unplayed pawn has the equivalent (nothing to switch). Under the lens it is fine, but the written justification is missing ("unless well-motivated", s15).
5. **Player-only views/aids** (day strip, `Sim.agenda`, `planGaps`, pulsing pins, "Do it now") are presentation and "foresight and attention" aids; consistent with parity if they read the same estimate (HANDOFF: the agenda's estimate "assumes the pawn's own hands only", unlike `sim/estimate`'s team-aware one: a divergence from "one quantity, one reckoning", s1).
6. **Chains.** `CHAINS` (plant, hay, harvest, building upgrade) are tile-task chains declared as data (modules.md), a player-side convenience of "a task that follows its project through". The head's equivalent is project steps and `plan` steps (s7 "projects", "A plan makes tasks when its next step is open"); the documents do not say CHAINS and project steps are one mechanism.
7. **Eligibility of "the player alone".** `self` basis ("or the pawn itself") and "A member may act on the household's opportunities without the plan, but prefers the plan's tasks" (4.5 Decided 3 Nov) is the parity hinge: the plan is a preference, not a gate.

**Verdict.** Parity of *verbs and checks* is strongly specified and mostly honoured; parity of *plan state and planning substrate* is asserted as a principle (state parity, 24 Nov) but the player's plan (the 24 Nov UI direction) is documented only in HANDOFF and tasks, not in design.md, and as built looks like a second structure feeding a queue. Whether an LLM-controlled pawn could produce a routine/sleep/task list in the same form is not specified.

## 6. Cost model assumed

- Rules (cost.md 1.3): cost follows activity not history; no pairs; decay lazily; schedule via due-queues; stagger per-holder work on planning day; compile hot rules; bound caches; name cost (entities x cadence x work).
- Work-specific: planning day staggered by id; the day allotment weighs every member x every task (VB-1554 measured 4.4 allotments/household-day; design s7 revised to morning/14:00/review/change; due work slotted alone to avoid re-weighing pairs); leg ends in one queue, "nothing is done to the pawn in between" (s7 "A leg"; cost rule 4); outlook made at review, not stepped; Reasons/timetable/opportunities computed when asked; "a pawn's own chooser may weigh fewer [verbs] to keep its cost down" (s1) is the sanctioned asymmetry.
- Walk weighed: one compiled reach map per house per season, <=1,000 tiles, ~0.8-1.6 M tiles/year on medium, staggered 1/7 a day over first week (s7 "What bounds the search"; worst day ~30 maps); "held for that review only, never in `st`".
- Cost.md 1.1 (10 Nov) records "Jobs refresh and matching" at 13-16% of a game day and "idle strolls" as a large part of agents' cost; the later design (own time, head's day) is the response, but no document restates the current budget for work.
- Performance budget in s15: 200 ms a game day at `medium-ref`.

## 7. Gaps, contradictions, ambiguities (within the documents)

1. **"Nothing is posted" vs the job board.** target-v2 p4 and s7 intro say work is not posted, tasks come from plans; yet modules.md lists `sim/jobs` as "the job board: `post`, the review hour's asks of the kinds' rules in `refreshJobs`" and ownership.md 5.1 has "the fields post their work", `job.live`, `st.jobs` (the 'store that st.jobs becomes'). The design's own statement that `st.jobs` "becomes" the task store, and that Opportunity computation moves "almost line for line" from `refreshJobs` to `available` (4.5), is partly done; the docs do not say what remains of posting. 11.3 item 6 steps (posting rules into KINDS, matching through Eligibility, village routines, seasonal plans) are the migration; their completion is not marked in the documents I read.
2. **s7 intro says v0.3's "Jobs, owners and roles" rules stand "where this table does not replace them, with 'job' read as 'task'"**, and that section's rows are marked "revised 10 Nov (the table above)" with no per-row indication of which are replaced. "Matching" row notes revision; "Who may take a job" (members/adults) is superseded by "Who may take a task" (bases) but both stand. Target-v2 4.5 "Work" paragraph still says "keeps the matcher as built (best fit first, **the two-hour rule for poor fits**, the urgency switch)" while design s7 "Matching" says the two-hour rule is gone (revised 15 Nov) and bids remain only for help. target-v2 4.5 says "Hourly single-voice choices (which job next) stay in Work" while s7 moved choice to the head's day. Target-v2 is a snapshot older than these.
3. **Two sets of status labels, mid-state.** s7 "Under revision" is used for fixed hours in "Long work and processing" (Harvest, Tilling: s7 "The time taken"); s8's table still says "Decided, Agreed (hours)" and shows 20 h hoe, 6 h thresh, with no Under-revision marking in that table itself.
4. **"The time taken" row marks itself the thing that revises s8's fixed hours**; "Mishaps" is a draw while principle "A judgement is never a die" and "Nothing... draws" apply to decisions only (mishaps are for nobody-decides, so consistent, but explicit reconciliation is absent).
5. **Walk weighed (VB-781) is Proposed but its rows "replace" Agreed rows** ("Reaches of daily work" s2, `WOOD.reach`) "until each lands, the steps stand"; the estimate row says the head's trip estimate may overrun and "the record says the trip took longer" while the verb check is "the world's". The verb `ground` and `allotWood` exist in design only. "Plan mode (VB-1123, in place of the plan panel)" conflicts in time with HANDOFF where "the plan bar" and "Today/Plan sheets removed" (4th batch) are different UI than "plan mode".
6. **"Plan panel" vs "plan bar".** s7 "The plan panel" row (Agreed 10 Nov; timetable proposed VB-256) and "The player" row (plan mode, VB-1123) and HANDOFF's `renderPlan`/plan bar are three accounts of the player's plan UI; HANDOFF says "later one representation, possibly edited from several places".
7. **"Player's plan" has no design home.** Neither s7 nor s15 define routine/sleep/ordered task list; target-v2 4.7 names "a plan as holder" as a `Decisions.answer` door but no plan-as-queue. design s15 "The queue" (up to 8 commands, Proposed VB-079) is the only player-order structure written, and it is not the 24 Nov plan.
8. **Terms used two ways.** "plan" = holder's plan (standing/seasonal/project), a project plan (`projectplans`), the day (`plan.day`), the player's plan (`st.player.plan`), plan mode/panel/bar (UI). "Task" = the household's task, the player's plan task keyed by job key, a backlog task. "Job" survives in code (`st.jobs`, `jobs.js`) and in design s7 "Jobs, owners and roles". "Holder" = head of household for the plan (s7) vs holder of land (s15 "head of a household, a holder of land"). "Routine" = a plan's standing routines (s7), the village's routines, and the player's daily routine of job kinds. "Agenda" = the village meeting's agenda (4.5 `agenda`) vs `Sim.agenda` (queue vs deadlines).
9. **Standing duties** idea Decided, mechanism Open; "Member's voice: duty" waits on it; target-v2 4.5 says the mechanism "would be the plan's" while s7 says it "should give the played head a choice that weighs".
10. **Target-v2 changes awaiting a design pass** (target-v2 11.1): "Jobs, owners and roles" (Decided 30 Oct) -> opportunities/plans/tasks and Eligibility replacing "members of the owning household or adults of the owning village": s7 covers it, yet the "Jobs, owners and roles" table is retained verbatim, so the 11.1 note is not retired. Section 11 "Proposed tasks" item 6 four steps still listed as proposals.
11. **Role of `Under revision`**: design.md Status says each Under revision names its slice plan task; only rows in s7 "The time taken" use it; "The estimate" supersedes "Mishaps"? Both sit in the same table without cross-reference.
12. **Cost statements:** cost.md 1.1 numbers (10 Nov) are stale relative to the day-allotment redesign; the walk-weighed row gives its own cost; no single place reports the job system's current ms.
13. **Parity wording:** s15 "The player may be offered more options than a pawn's own logic ever uses"; s1 "a pawn's own chooser may weigh fewer of them... and chooses as well as that cost allows"; Q9 "the player's edge is foresight and attention". Consistent, but "foresight" is defined by s1 "The future is someone's estimate" (nothing of the future is known to anyone), so what edge the player has in foresight is not defined.

## 8. Open questions the documents leave for the owner

3. Mechanism for standing duties and for "duty" in the member's voice (s7 Open).
4. How large projects are limited and ranked (VB-1472); weights of project menu (Open).
5. Whether Mishaps (a draw) stay under "a judgement is never a die"/"time taken is computed".
6. When VB-781's `ground`/`allotWood` and the reach map land, which of the Agreed reaches (s2) are retired, and who judges for a village with no alderman.
7. Whether `CHAINS` and project steps should be one mechanism; whether hay, building and trade should be plannable by hand (HANDOFF gaps 1, 3).
