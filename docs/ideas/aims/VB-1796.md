# VB-1796: A household's need of pots at the fair, and make or buy by the head's estimate
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-366, VB-1774
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: plans
Phase: implement
Design ref: docs/plans/four-scales/aims.md, sections 5.5 and 7.3, decision 18; design.md section 7, Aims and personal goods (Make or buy; Trade); section 8, The metals and the stones (Choosing among grades); The mill and the loom (Pots)
Touches: sim/exchange (needValue); sim/pottery; sim/workmanship (expectedWorth); tests/exchange.js; new tests/makeorbuy.js
Goal: A household short of pots buys them from a better maker when its head reckons that better than firing its own, so a maker's custom grows with its skill
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] Make or buy, at the head's review when its household is under `POTS.want`: making is weighed as its own hand's hours (at `COMMUTE.hour` times the share of the season's labour budget its targets already take, so an idle winter's hours are cheap), its clay and the life of the grade its hand would make (`expectedWorth`); buying as the customary worth of a better maker's pot in reach, by the grade it has seen placed, and its longer life. A household with no clay in its digging ground always buys. The firing is posted only when the head makes.
- [ ] At the fair a household that buys values pots by grade at customary worth up to its shortfall: a contribution to the rule `need`, read by `needValue`.
- [ ] A check on three constructed households: one without clay buys; a novice's with clay in an idle winter makes; one short of hands at the harvest buys; and a household whose own hand is a novice buys a master's pot where its longer life pays.
- [ ] Reported from the gate's six runs: households that bought pots and that made them, pots by grade.
- [ ] Cost: a few reads at a head's review while it is short of pots.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
