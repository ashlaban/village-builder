# VB-1783: A task a pawn owns, its material by its household's right, and the custom of a member's making

Touches: sim/jobs (post); sim/work; sim/dig (KIND, inReach); sim/chores (KINDS); sim/pottery (KINDS); sim/customs; new tests/ownmaking.js
Goal: A pawn can dig, fetch and make in its own hours for itself, across as many days as it takes, and keeps what it makes less what its village's custom gives the household
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A task may be owned by a pawn (`{type: 'pawn', id}`, taken on the basis `self` by that pawn alone); its hours are booked `kept` (VB-1761); its good goes into the owner's store at the work (The good is made where the work is done), with a reason of its own.
- [ ] Across days: a task a pawn owns is taken up again in its next own hours, its clay and wood lying at the pit as piles with its title and the firing's hours on the firing (Progress lives in the world); a check: a firing of 6.7 hours over three days of 2.5 own hours ends with the pots in the store and nothing lost.
- [ ] Material by the household's right: a pawn's own digging (sim/dig) and wood fetched take where its household may (its digging ground, the outland, the village's wood), and what they take is the pawn's; the household's stored clay and wood it uses only when given (VB-1792).
- [ ] The custom `memberMaking` (sim/customs, read through its one door; a row of design.md's Custom dimensions): `maker` (the default), `share` (`HOUSESHARE`, 0.25 of each own making to the household's store at the making, with its reason), `household` (all of it while the pawn is an unmarried member); a check of each.
- [ ] Firing pots for oneself (VB-366's kind, owned by the pawn) practises pottery (`practise` of sim/skills) as the household's firing does, and its pots are graded by the maker's skill (VB-761): a check that forces the stream `making`.
- [ ] Cost: own makings are tasks like any, inside the jobs line; no pass of their own.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
