# VB-1793: The aims' review: their own due day, the estimate, at risk, waiting, and the decision points
Status: blocked
Waits for: VB-1784, VB-1755, VB-1757, VB-1778
Priority: 2
Size: M
Arch: plans
Phase: implement
Design ref: docs/plans/four-scales/aims.md, sections 3.3, 3.4 and 3.5, decisions 5 and 6; design.md section 7, Aims and personal goods (How an aim becomes work; Progress and the estimate; At risk, waiting, dropped; Choosing an aim; Cost); section 15, Decision points
Touches: sim/aims; sim/due; sim/decisions; sim/plans (reviewed); tests/aims.js
Goal: Every aim is looked at once a season by its holder, measured and estimated, written into the season's steps, and at risk answered in a fixed order, never ended by its age
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A pawn's aims are reviewed on a due day of their own (sim/due, the asking day's formula, every pawn from 16 with own hours), not on sim/wishes' queue of those who seek, which is untouched; a household's at its head's first review of a season.
- [ ] Each review: the measure; the holder's estimate (a centre and a range, the head's lean, the pawn's own); then under way (its kind's `season` writes its steps, each with `why` the aim, sized to the spare of aims.md 3.3), waiting (`waits` named, no steps), or at risk (a decision point for its author; unanswered by its next review the default author, in this order: drops it under `AIM[kind].keep`; scales it down if its kind's lesser form meets `by`; else postpones `by` to the estimate's hi; a kept aim left). A check of each branch on a constructed state with the test kind.
- [ ] No aim ends by a count of years: a check that an aim whose estimate never reaches its `by` is postponed, keeps its `waits`, and is dropped only when its score falls under `keep`.
- [ ] Decision points added to VB-1757's list: an aim to form, an aim at risk, a member's aim bearing on the head's plan for it.
- [ ] Cost: about 700 pawns × 4 aim days a year and 200 households × 4 reviews, tens of reads each (under 0.1 ms a game day at the late reference); ended aims leave the live list at the season's fold; a work counter in `VB.COUNTERS`.
- [ ] With only the test kind no behaviour changes: `node tools/same.js --base REF` differs only in the aims' due queue's leaves, listed in the PR; `npm test` and `npm run verify` green.
