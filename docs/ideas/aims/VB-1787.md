# VB-1787: A pawn's goods at the fair: its lot, its accept rule, and its income from others
Status: blocked

Design ref: docs/plans/four-scales/aims.md, section 5.5, decision 17; design.md section 7, Aims and personal goods (Trade); section 9, The fair: barter between villages (Deals between strangers; Keeping goods); section 11, The gathering (Who goes)
Touches: sim/fair; sim/gathering; sim/exchange (needValue); sim/offers; sim/relations; sim/stores (record); tests/fair.js
Goal: A pawn can deal its own goods at the gathering by a rule of what its goods are for, and its year's income from others is known
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] A lot of `pawn.load` names its owner (a pawn or a household); a pawn with goods in its store over its target for exchange is among who goes, with that reason; a member who does not go may ask its household's dealer to carry its lot (answered by the dealer's opinion), each swap booked to the owner's store; a pawn does not swap with its own household's dealer; goodwill as change is booked between the persons who dealt.
- [ ] A pawn's accept rule, a contribution to the rule `need` for a pawn holder: food by the household's food curve up to `PORTION.food` (`MARRIAGE.means` years of one adult's need, 72 rations), the keeping goods beyond it at customary worth up to a back load (Keeping goods), its own pieces at nothing beyond its target for exchange (0 until VB-1786), its floor the material of its next firing.
- [ ] No swap gives a pawn an animal or a good that does not wear.
- [ ] `pawn.trade` (`@state`, owner sim/stores): `{year, got: [this, last], gave: [this, last], placed: {[good]: [this, last]}}`, folded at the year's turn; read by `Sim` for the card.
- [ ] A check on a constructed gathering: a son's six pots swapped with a household given a need of pots (VB-1796's when landed) for grain to his portion and butter beyond it, his income booked, nothing made or lost by the stocks' accounts.
- [ ] Reported from the gate's six runs: pawn lots at the fair, the worth pawns dealt against households'.
- [ ] Cost: a few dozen pawn lots a gathering beside the households'.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
