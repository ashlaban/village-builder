Goal: A pawn holds title to goods in a store of its own, the servant's portion its grain; the holder pair holds none; a member eats of its own when its household's meal falls short
Acceptance criteria:
- [ ] `pawn.stake` becomes `pawn.goods` (`@state`, owner sim/stores): the fields a household's store uses for the goods a pawn may hold (grain; pots by grade once VB-366 lands; clay, wood, cloth, butter and the keeping foods), read through the stores' reads as any holder's; every reader of the stake reads it (the wage, the means to marry, a party's provisions, a founding, the settlers' walk); `KEEPING.stake` goes and "stake" no longer names a pawn's grain. `FORMAT` raised (sim/saves): a save of an older build is refused; no migration is written.
- [ ] The means to marry and a party's provisions count a store's grain and keeping foods, never its pots or cloth.
- [ ] A pawn holds no animal and no good that does not wear (silver, gold, amber, a bronze pot): a put of one into a pawn's store is refused with its reason.
- [ ] The holder pair holds none: a pawn that becomes its household's holder or the holder's spouse (a founding, a handover, a marriage onto the holding) puts its store into the household's, title at once where it lies; a check of each of the three.
- [ ] A member eats of its own: when its household's meal falls short (sim/hunger), a member with grain or keeping foods in its store eats of its own first, an operation with its reason; a check on a constructed household short of food.
- [ ] `tools/stocks.js` counts the pawns' stores (in, out, where held), and the stocks' `unexplained` does not grow on founders small seed 7, 10 years.
- [ ] Cost: about 700 stores of a few numbers at the late reference, read where a wage, a meal short, a marriage or a founding already runs; nothing in a step; the save's growth reported.
- [ ] The gate (`docs/plans/four-scales/README.md`, 3.10), by one command: `npm run verify` green (a test that asserted the old behaviour updated on purpose, never skipped or quarantined, and named in the PR), and `node tools/gate.js --base <the last recorded result>` passing (VB-1774): no settlement extinct by hunger or collapsed (VB-1774's bound), no more tasks held over 48 hours than the base, the stocks' `unexplained` no larger.
- [ ] Reported in the PR from the same output, not gated: hungry household-days and deaths by hunger in the six runs, before and after, and the work counters (`node tests/counters.js`, `--update` with the entries listed); the result kept (`--out`) as the next task's base.
